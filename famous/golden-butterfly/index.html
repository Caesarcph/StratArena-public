Golden Butterfly Portfolio: does it still work? Replicated through 2026

We re-ran it on S&P 500, Gold from 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12 (16.9 years, before costs): +7.4% a year, Sharpe 0.97, worst drawdown -14.6%. The benchmark, S&P 500 buy-and-hold, did +13.9% a year (Sharpe 0.85, worst drawdown -33.7%).

Verdict: Smoother ride than its benchmark (higher Sharpe), but a lower return. Historical, hypothetical results before costs, taxes and fees. Not investment advice.

Adapted Adapted: this is not the real thing

This is our adapted version, not the real portfolio or fund. Read what is lost before comparing it with anything published about the original.

What is lost

  • Both Treasury sleeves (20% long-term, 20% short-term) are cash earning 0%.
  • The 20% small-cap value sleeve is folded into SPY, so the value/size tilt is gone.
Bond sleeve held as cash

40% of the original allocation is government bonds. We have no bond data, so that 40% is held as cash earning 0%.

What this means: no coupon income, no price gains when yields fall or stocks crash, and none of the losses bonds had when yields rose (2022). Read the results as the non-bond part plus idle cash. The real portfolio would have done differently, better in some years and worse in others.

Where it comes from

Attributed toTyler of Portfolio Charts
SourcePortfolio Charts - Golden Butterfly Portfolio (year approximate) (c. 2015)

A popular Permanent Portfolio variant with a stock tilt that became a staple of the 'lazy portfolio' community.

Rules, weights and thresholds are given as commonly cited. We have not re-checked them against the primary text.

The original rules

20% each in total US stock market, small-cap value stocks, long-term Treasuries, short-term Treasuries and gold, rebalanced annually.

How we replicate it

What we changed

Small-cap value is folded into SPY (40% SPY total) and both bond sleeves (40%) become cash; gold stays 20%.

The exact rule we run

Buy 40% SPY (20% total market plus 20% standing in for small-cap value) and 20% GLD and keep 40% in cash (0%). Rebalance at the last close of each year.

Target weights: 40.0% S&P 500, 20.0% Gold, 40.0% Cash (0%).

Conventions: Position decided at a close earns the next day's return; no look-ahead; before costs; cash earns 0%; starts 2010-01-04, when all its assets have data; weights drift between rebalances (annually); rebalanced at the last close of the period.

How much to trust it

  • A fixed allocation has no signals, so it is one path of history (16.9 years), not a test.
  • This is one backtest on one path of history, before costs and with hindsight on the choice of strategy. It does not show the strategy will work in future.

Honest caveat

Loses the small-cap value factor and bonds, and the publication year is approximate, so it only resembles the original.
