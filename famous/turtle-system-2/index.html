Turtle Trading System 2 (long-only): does it still work? Replicated through 2026

We re-ran it on 10 markets from 2010-03-19 to 2026-11-12 (16.7 years, before costs): +8.0% a year, Sharpe 0.99, worst drawdown -13.4%. The benchmark, Equal-weight of 10 markets, did +9.4% a year (Sharpe 0.64, worst drawdown -43.1%).

Verdict: Smoother ride than its benchmark (higher Sharpe), but a lower return. Historical, hypothetical results before costs, taxes and fees. Not investment advice.

What is lost

  • The volatility-based unit sizing, pyramiding and short selling.

The slower sibling of the famous Turtle experiment: fewer trades, longer trends.

Rules, weights and thresholds are given as commonly cited. We have not re-checked them against the primary text.

The original rules

System 2 is the slower Turtle system: enter on a 55-day high/low breakout and exit on a 20-day opposite breakout, with the same ATR-based unit sizing, up to 4 units per market, long and short, as commonly described by Curtis Faith.

How we replicate it

What we changed

Same simplifications as System 1: long-only, close-only, ten markets instead of futures, a full fixed slot (1/10 of equity) per market instead of ATR units, no pyramiding, no shorts.

The exact rule we run

For each of the 10 markets, at the close: enter long when the close is above the highest close of the prior 55 days; exit to cash when it is below the lowest close of the prior 20 days. Each market has a fixed 1/10 slot of equity.

Conventions: Position decided at a close earns the next day's return; no look-ahead; before costs; cash earns 0%; starts 2010-03-19, the first day a signal exists; every market has an equal slot of equity; indicators use each market's own trading days.

Growth of 1 vs the benchmark

Benchmark: Equal-weight of 10 markets, same period. Equal slots in S&P 500, Gold, Silver, Oil, Natural gas, Commodities, Agriculture, EUR/USD, Bitcoin, Ether, rebalanced daily; a market without data yet stays in cash.
