Classic 60/40 (as SPY/cash): does it still work? Replicated through 2026

We re-ran it on S&P 500 from 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12 (16.9 years, before costs): +8.5% a year, Sharpe 0.86, worst drawdown -20.6%. The benchmark, S&P 500 buy-and-hold, did +13.9% a year (Sharpe 0.85, worst drawdown -33.7%).

Verdict: About the same risk-adjusted return as its benchmark. Historical, hypothetical results before costs, taxes and fees. Not investment advice.

Adapted Adapted: this is not the real thing

This is our adapted version, not the real portfolio or fund. Read what is lost before comparing it with anything published about the original.

What is lost

  • The whole 40% bond sleeve is cash earning 0%: no coupon income, no price gains in equity sell-offs.
  • This is NOT a real 60/40; it is a 60% equity, 40% cash mix.
Bond sleeve held as cash

40% of the original allocation is government bonds. We have no bond data, so that 40% is held as cash earning 0%.

What this means: no coupon income, no price gains when yields fall or stocks crash, and none of the losses bonds had when yields rose (2022). Read the results as the non-bond part plus idle cash. The real portfolio would have done differently, better in some years and worse in others.

The original rules

How we replicate it

What we changed

Clearly labelled substitute: the 40% bond sleeve is cash at 0%, so this is effectively a 60% equity exposure with no bond diversification or income.

The exact rule we run

Buy 60% SPY and keep 40% in cash (0%). Rebalance at the last close of each year.

Target weights: 60.0% S&P 500, 40.0% Cash (0%).

Conventions: Position decided at a close earns the next day's return; no look-ahead; before costs; cash earns 0%; starts 2010-01-04, when all its assets have data; weights drift between rebalances (annually); rebalanced at the last close of the period.

How much to trust it

  • A fixed allocation has no signals, so it is one path of history (16.9 years), not a test.
  • This is one backtest on one path of history, before costs and with hindsight on the choice of strategy. It does not show the strategy will work in future.

Honest caveat

Without bonds this is not a true 60/40; it is a low-risk SPY/cash mix and is labelled as such.
