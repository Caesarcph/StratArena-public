Ray Dalio All Weather (public approximation): does it still work? Replicated through 2026

We re-ran it on S&P 500, Gold, Commodities from 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-09 (16.8 years, before costs): +5.3% a year, Sharpe 0.90, worst drawdown -12.5%. The benchmark, S&P 500 buy-and-hold, did +13.9% a year (Sharpe 0.85, worst drawdown -33.7%).

Verdict: About the same risk-adjusted return as its benchmark. Historical, hypothetical results before costs, taxes and fees. Not investment advice.

Adapted Adapted: this is not the real thing

This is our adapted version, not the real portfolio or fund. Read what is lost before comparing it with anything published about the original.

What is lost

  • The 55% Treasury sleeve (long and intermediate) is cash earning 0%.
  • No leverage and no risk parity: Bridgewater's fund levers the bonds, this does not.
  • Weights are the widely quoted Robbins version, not the fund's holdings.
Bond sleeve held as cash

55% of the original allocation is government bonds. We have no bond data, so that 55% is held as cash earning 0%.

What this means: no coupon income, no price gains when yields fall or stocks crash, and none of the losses bonds had when yields rose (2022). Read the results as the non-bond part plus idle cash. The real portfolio would have done differently, better in some years and worse in others.

Where it comes from

Attributed toRay Dalio / Bridgewater
SourceMoney: Master the Game (Tony Robbins interview with Dalio) (2014)

Dalio's risk-balanced portfolio is the best-known 'any economic environment' allocation.

Rules, weights and thresholds are given as commonly cited. We have not re-checked them against the primary text.

The original rules

As published by Robbins: 30% stocks, 40% long-term Treasuries, 15% intermediate Treasuries, 7.5% gold, 7.5% commodities.

How we replicate it

What we changed

The 55% bond sleeve is held as cash (0% yield), so the portfolio is far less defensive in deflation/recession; stocks, gold and broad commodities (DBC) map directly.

The exact rule we run

Buy 30% SPY, 7.5% GLD, 7.5% DBC and keep 55% in cash (0%). At the close of the last trading day of each year, rebalance back to those weights; in between the weights drift with prices.

Target weights: 30.0% S&P 500, 7.5% Gold, 7.5% Commodities, 55.0% Cash (0%).

Conventions: Position decided at a close earns the next day's return; no look-ahead; before costs; cash earns 0%; starts 2010-01-04, when all its assets have data; weights drift between rebalances (annually); rebalanced at the last close of the period.

How much to trust it

  • A fixed allocation has no signals, so it is one path of history (16.8 years), not a test.
  • This is one backtest on one path of history, before costs and with hindsight on the choice of strategy. It does not show the strategy will work in future.

Honest caveat

The weights are the widely quoted Robbins version, not Bridgewater's actual leveraged risk-parity fund, and with bonds replaced by cash the result differs greatly from the original.
