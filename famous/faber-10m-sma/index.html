Faber 10-Month SMA Timing (GTAA): does it still work? Replicated through 2026

We re-ran it on S&P 500, Gold, Commodities from 2010-10-29 to 2026-11-11 (16.0 years, before costs): +5.9% a year, Sharpe 0.71, worst drawdown -16.3%. The benchmark, Equal-weight of 3 markets, did +8.4% a year (Sharpe 0.74, worst drawdown -29.4%).

Verdict: About the same risk-adjusted return as its benchmark. Historical, hypothetical results before costs, taxes and fees. Not investment advice.

Adapted Adapted: this is not the real thing

This is our adapted version, not the real portfolio or fund. Read what is lost before comparing it with anything published about the original.

What is lost

  • Foreign stocks, bonds and REITs are missing: only 3 of the 5 classes remain, so the portfolio is more concentrated and more volatile.
  • GLD and DBC are ETFs, not the indices used in the paper, and the sample is shorter.

Where it comes from

Attributed toMebane Faber
SourceA Quantitative Approach to Tactical Asset Allocation, Journal of Wealth Management (2007)

The most-downloaded tactical allocation paper and the basis of the 'timing' wave in ETF investing.

Rules, weights and thresholds are given as commonly cited. We have not re-checked them against the primary text.

The original rules

Across five asset classes (US stocks, foreign stocks, bonds, REITs, commodities) equally weighted: at each month-end hold the class if its price is above its 10-month simple moving average, otherwise hold cash/T-bills.

How we replicate it

What we changed

Three of the five asset classes exist here: SPY, GLD (as a real-asset proxy) and DBC. Each gets a 1/3 slot of equity and is held only while its month-end close is above the average of its last 10 month-end closes, otherwise the slot stays in cash.

The exact rule we run

On the last trading day of each month, for each of SPY, GLD and DBC: if the month-end close is above the average of the last 10 month-end closes, that market is ON for the next month, else OFF. Each market has a fixed 1/3 slot; OFF slots stay in cash.

Conventions: Position decided at a close earns the next day's return; no look-ahead; before costs; cash earns 0%; starts 2010-10-29, the first day a signal exists; every market has an equal slot of equity; indicators use each market's own trading days.
