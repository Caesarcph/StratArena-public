Weinstein 30-Week MA Stage Analysis: does it still work? Replicated through 2026

We re-ran it on 6 markets from 2010-09-03 to 2026-11-12 (16.2 years, before costs): +13.8% a year, Sharpe 1.28, worst drawdown -15.5%. The benchmark, Equal-weight of 6 markets, did +14.2% a year (Sharpe 0.92, worst drawdown -36.4%).

Verdict: Better risk-adjusted return than its benchmark over this period. Historical, hypothetical results before costs, taxes and fees. Not investment advice.

Adapted Adapted: this is not the real thing

This is our adapted version, not the real portfolio or fund. Read what is lost before comparing it with anything published about the original.

What is lost

  • The chart-pattern judgement (base, breakout, volume, relative strength) is replaced by a mechanical average-and-slope test.
  • 30 weeks is approximated by 150 trading days; only six markets.

Where it comes from

Attributed toStan Weinstein
SourceSecrets for Profiting in Bull and Bear Markets (1988)

Weinstein's four-stage framework is one of the most widely taught chart-based trend methods.

Rules, weights and thresholds are given as commonly cited. We have not re-checked them against the primary text.

The original rules

Buy only in Stage 2 (price above a rising 30-week moving average after a base); sell when price falls into Stage 3/4 below the declining average.

How we replicate it

What we changed

30 weeks is approximated by 150 trading days; chart-pattern judgment, volume and relative strength are dropped, leaving a mechanical MA-and-slope rule on six markets.

The exact rule we run

For each of SPY, GLD, SLV, DBC, DBA and BTC-USD, at the close: enter when the close is above its 150-day average and that average is above its own value 20 trading days earlier; exit when the close falls below the average. Fixed 1/6 slot per market, OFF slots in cash.

Conventions: Position decided at a close earns the next day's return; no look-ahead; before costs; cash earns 0%; starts 2010-09-03, the first day a signal exists; every market has an equal slot of equity; indicators use each market's own trading days.

Growth of 1 vs the benchmark

Benchmark: Equal-weight of 6 markets, same period. Equal slots in S&P 500, Gold, Silver, Commodities, Agriculture, Bitcoin, rebalanced daily; a market without data yet stays in cash.

Honest caveat
