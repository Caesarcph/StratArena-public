Turtle Trading System 1 (long-only): does it still work? Replicated through 2026

We re-ran it on 10 markets from 2010-01-29 to 2026-11-11 (16.8 years, before costs): +8.3% a year, Sharpe 0.96, worst drawdown -15.9%. The benchmark, Equal-weight of 10 markets, did +9.4% a year (Sharpe 0.64, worst drawdown -43.1%).

Verdict: Smoother ride than its benchmark (higher Sharpe), but a lower return. Historical, hypothetical results before costs, taxes and fees. Not investment advice.

Adapted Adapted: this is not the real thing

This is our adapted version, not the real portfolio or fund. Read what is lost before comparing it with anything published about the original.

What is lost

  • The volatility-based unit sizing, which was a core part of the Turtles' risk control.
  • Pyramiding, short selling and the skip-after-a-winner filter.
  • Futures markets: our ETFs, FX and crypto behave differently.

Where it comes from

Attributed toRichard Dennis & William Eckhardt (rules published by Curtis Faith)
SourceWay of the Turtle (rules of the original Turtle program) (2007)

The Turtles' story, in which Dennis trained novices into a hugely profitable trend-following team, is the most famous experiment in trading.

Rules, weights and thresholds are given as commonly cited. We have not re-checked them against the primary text.

The original rules

System 1: enter on a 20-day high/low breakout and exit on a 10-day opposite breakout; position size is 1% of equity per N (20-day ATR), up to 4 units per market, long and short, across ~20 futures markets.

How we replicate it

What we changed

Long-only and close-only (no highs and lows), on ten ETF, FX and crypto markets instead of ~20 futures. Position sizing is simplified to a full fixed slot (1/10 of equity) per market: no ATR-based units, no pyramiding up to 4 units, and the filter that skips a breakout after a winning one is dropped. Shorts are lost.

The exact rule we run

For each of the 10 markets, at the close: enter long when the close is above the highest close of the prior 20 days; exit to cash when it is below the lowest close of the prior 10 days. Each market has a fixed 1/10 slot of equity.

Conventions: Position decided at a close earns the next day's return; no look-ahead; before costs; cash earns 0%; starts 2010-01-29, the first day a signal exists; every market has an equal slot of equity; indicators use each market's own trading days.

Growth of 1 vs the benchmark

Benchmark: Equal-weight of 10 markets, same period. Equal slots in S&P 500, Gold, Silver, Oil, Natural gas, Commodities, Agriculture, EUR/USD, Bitcoin, Ether, rebalanced daily; a market without data yet stays in cash.

How much to trust it

  • 1402 position changes over 16.8 years.
  • This is one backtest on one path of history, before costs and with hindsight on the choice of strategy. It does not show the strategy will work in future.

Honest caveat

Long-only, close-based and no pyramiding on a very different market set, so only the breakout idea survives.
