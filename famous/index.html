Famous strategies, replicated

22 well-known portfolios and rule sets, re-run on the same ten markets (stocks, gold, silver, oil, gas, commodities, agriculture, EUR/USD, Bitcoin, Ether) under one rule set, from 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10. Every page says what is the real rule and what we had to change.

How close is each one to the original?

Short answer: none of these is the real fund or the real portfolio. We have ten markets, no bond data and no futures, so each replica is labelled by how much of the original survives.

Rule as published5Rule as published

The published rule is applied as written, on the instrument we have (for example SPY instead of an index). Thresholds may still vary between editions.

Adapted14Adapted

Part of the original is missing: instruments, bonds, shorts, position sizing or stock selection. The page lists exactly what is lost.

Our interpretation3Our interpretation

The originator never published a numeric rule. The parameters are ours, an illustration of the idea, not a replica.

Bonds: held as cash. We have no bond data, so wherever an original allocation holds bonds (6 of these strategies) that sleeve is cash earning 0%. That removes coupon income and the gains bonds made in equity sell-offs, and the losses they made in 2022, so results can be lower or higher than the real thing.

One rule set for everyone

  • Results are before trading costs, taxes and fund fees; cash earns 0%.
  • A position decided at a close earns the next day's return; indicators use closes up to that day only.
  • Markets: SPY, GLD, SLV, USO, UNG, DBC, DBA, EUR/USD, BTC-USD, ETH-USD. Fixed-weight portfolios start when all their assets have data; weights drift between rebalances.
  • Rules and weights are given as commonly cited. We have not re-checked them against the primary texts: read the source before relying on any detail.
