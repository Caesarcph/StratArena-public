Clenow Trend Following (50/100 MA + 50-day breakout): does it still work? Replicated through 2026

We re-ran it on 10 markets from 2010-05-21 to 2026-11-12 (16.5 years, before costs): +5.5% a year, Sharpe 1.08, worst drawdown -8.9%. The benchmark, Equal-weight of 10 markets, did +9.8% a year (Sharpe 0.65, worst drawdown -43.1%).

Verdict: Smoother ride than its benchmark (higher Sharpe), but a lower return. Historical, hypothetical results before costs, taxes and fees. Not investment advice.

Adapted Adapted: this is not the real thing

This is our adapted version, not the real portfolio or fund. Read what is lost before comparing it with anything published about the original.

What is lost

  • Risk-based sizing: every market gets the same slot regardless of its volatility.
  • The short side and the hundred-plus futures markets of the original.

Where it comes from

Attributed toAndreas Clenow
SourceFollowing the Trend: Diversified Managed Futures Trading (2012)

A modern, openly documented description of how managed-futures funds trade trends.

Rules, weights and thresholds are given as commonly cited. We have not re-checked them against the primary text.

The original rules

Long when the 50-day average is above the 100-day average and price makes a new 50-day high; exit on a trailing stop several ATRs below the high or when the trend filter turns off; positions are risk-sized by ATR across a diversified futures portfolio.

How we replicate it

What we changed

Long-only on ten markets, close-only: N (the ATR proxy) is the 100-day mean absolute close-to-close change. Risk-based position sizing is replaced by a fixed 1/10 slot per market, and the futures breadth and short side are lost.

The exact rule we run

For each of the 10 markets, at the close: when flat, enter if the 50-day average is above the 100-day average and the close is at or above the highest close of the prior 50 days. Exit when the 50-day average falls below the 100-day average or the close is more than 3 N below the highest close since entry. Fixed 1/10 slot per market.

Conventions: Position decided at a close earns the next day's return; no look-ahead; before costs; cash earns 0%; starts 2010-05-21, the first day a signal exists; every market has an equal slot of equity; indicators use each market's own trading days.

Benchmark: Equal-weight of 10 markets, same period. Equal slots in S&P 500, Gold, Silver, Oil, Natural gas, Commodities, Agriculture, EUR/USD, Bitcoin, Ether, rebalanced daily; a market without data yet stays in cash.
