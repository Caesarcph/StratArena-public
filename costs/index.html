How costs are measured

  • All metrics use every original full-precision daily record; sampled curves are for display only.
  • A basket and a single market remain separate even when their display symbol is identical. Asset order, observation dates and input identity are retained for exact comparisons.
  • After each gross close return, the fee is charged on drift-adjusted one-way risky turnover: (1 + gross return) × (1 − turnover × bp / 10,000). Initial entry is charged; there is no forced terminal liquidation.
  • Fees paid are actual synthetic charges in account-capital units, not the difference between gross and net ending wealth. Full history uses initial capital; shorter windows use capital at the first included close and exclude charges at that anchor close.
  • The matched benchmark uses the same asset returns, dates and cost model: long buy-and-hold for a single market, or a daily equal-weight long basket. Sharpe and volatility use 252 observations per year and a zero risk-free rate.
  • This is a synthetic exposure-cost model, not broker fills or exact traded notional. Fees do not feed back into targets or weight drift. Financing, borrow, funding, taxes and market impact are excluded.
