摘要

该EA运用统计方法分析价格数据，基于价格偏离统计期望值的程度生成交易信号。它利用均值、标准差和Z分数等统计指标来识别超买超卖状态，在价格显著偏离统计常态时入场交易。策略系统性地基于统计概率阈值开仓，并在价格回归均值时平仓。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是统计套利，基于价格围绕均值波动的统计规律进行交易。入场条件为价格偏离统计均值达到一定阈值（如Z分数超过设定标准），认为此时价格有回归均值的概率优势，从而在偏离方向的反方向开仓。出场条件包括价格回归至均值附近、达到止盈目标或触发止损。仓位管理可能基于统计置信度动态调整，偏离越大仓位越重，或采用固定手数方式。资金管理方面，EA可能根据账户余额和风险比例计算每笔交易的风险敞口。整体策略依赖统计学原理，假设价格分布具有均值回归特性，但在趋势行情中可能面临连续亏损的风险。

Summary

This EA applies statistical methods to analyze price data and generate trading signals based on statistical deviations from expected values. It uses statistical measures such as mean, standard deviation, and z-scores to identify overbought and oversold conditions, entering trades when price deviates significantly from its statistical norm. The strategy systematically opens positions based on statistical probability thresholds and exits when price reverts toward the mean.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is statistical arbitrage, trading based on the statistical principle that price tends to revert to its mean. Entry conditions trigger when price deviates from the statistical mean beyond a defined threshold (e.g., z-score exceeding a set standard deviation level), operating on the probabilistic advantage that price will revert. Exit conditions include price returning to the mean vicinity, reaching take-profit targets, or hitting stop-loss levels. Position sizing may dynamically adjust based on statistical confidence levels, with larger deviations potentially warranting larger positions, or it may employ fixed lot sizing. Money management likely calculates risk exposure per trade based on account balance and a risk percentage. The overall strategy relies on statistical principles assuming mean-reverting price distribution, but may face consecutive losses during trending markets where price persistently drifts away from the mean.

优势

  • 基于统计学原理，交易逻辑有数学理论基础支撑
  • 均值回归策略在震荡行情中表现稳健，胜率较高
  • 参数化设计允许用户根据不同品种和市场条件优化
  • 利用Z分数等标准化指标，可在不同价格水平的品种间通用

劣势

  • 均值回归策略在强趋势行情中容易持续亏损，价格可能长期不回归
  • 缺乏趋势过滤机制，可能在单边行情中逆势加仓
  • 固定手数未考虑波动率变化，在不同市场状态下风险敞口不均匀
  • 统计分布假设可能失效，极端行情下正态分布模型不适用

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤器（如ADX或长周期均线），在趋势行情中暂停或减少交易
  • 引入动态仓位管理，根据当前波动率或Z分数大小调整仓位
  • 添加非正态分布处理机制，如使用中位数和四分位距替代均值和标准差

Strengths

  • Grounded in statistical theory, the trading logic is supported by a mathematical foundation
  • Mean reversion strategy performs robustly in ranging markets with a relatively high win rate
  • Parameterized design allows users to optimize for different instruments and market conditions
  • Standardized metrics like z-scores make the strategy portable across instruments at different price levels

Weaknesses

  • Mean reversion strategies are prone to sustained losses in strong trending markets where price may never revert
  • Lacks a trend filter mechanism, potentially adding positions against the trend in directional markets
  • Fixed lot sizing does not account for volatility changes, leading to uneven risk exposure across market regimes
  • Statistical distribution assumptions may break down; normal distribution models are unreliable during extreme market events

Improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., ADX or long-period moving average) to pause or reduce trading during trending markets
  • Introduce dynamic position sizing that adjusts based on current volatility or z-score magnitude
  • Implement non-normal distribution handling, such as using median and interquartile range instead of mean and standard deviation
