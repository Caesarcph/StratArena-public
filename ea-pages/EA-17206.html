摘要

该EA监控K线大小，当K线超过用户设定的点数阈值时播放声音警报。支持最高最低价和开盘收盘价两种测量模式，每根K线仅触发一次。它不进行任何交易，纯粹是一个通知工具。

策略分析

该EA并非交易策略，而是一个监控工具。其核心概念是检测异常大的K线波动，这可能预示着市场出现了重大事件或突破。入场条件方面，当K线的波动幅度（高-低或收盘-开盘的绝对值）超过BarPoint参数设定的点数阈值时触发警报。出场条件不适用，因为该EA不执行交易。仓位管理和资金管理均不涉及，因为它仅发出通知而不开仓。策略逻辑简单直接：使用NewBar函数确保每个K线周期内只触发一次警报，避免重复通知。测量模式可选高-低价差或开盘价与当前Ask价的偏差，前者衡量K线整体波动，后者更侧重跟踪盘中相对于开盘价的偏离程度。

Summary

This EA monitors bar size and plays a sound alert when a bar exceeds a user-defined point threshold. It supports both High-Low and Open-Close measurement modes and triggers only once per bar. It does not open any trades and serves purely as a notification utility.

Strategy Analysis

This EA is not a trading strategy but rather a monitoring utility. Its core concept is detecting abnormally large bar movements, which may signal significant market events or breakouts. Regarding entry conditions, it triggers an alert when the bar's range (either High-Low or absolute deviation from open to current Ask) exceeds the BarPoint threshold in points. Exit conditions are not applicable since the EA does not execute trades. Position sizing and money management are absent because it only sends notifications without opening positions. The logic is straightforward: a NewBar function ensures the alert fires only once per bar cycle, preventing repeated notifications. The measurement mode choice between High-Low versus Open-Close provides flexibility — the former captures total bar volatility while the latter focuses on intrabar deviation from the open price.

优势

  • 逻辑简单清晰，代码量小易于理解和维护
  • 每根K线仅触发一次，避免重复警报干扰
  • 支持两种测量模式，灵活性较好
  • 纯通知工具，不执行交易，无资金风险

劣势

  • OpenClose模式下用Ask价而非Close价，逻辑不一致且在回测中不可用
  • BarPoint阈值是固定点数，未考虑不同品种的点值差异，跨品种适用性差
  • 缺少可视化标记功能，无法在图表上直观看到触发位置
  • 没有历史统计功能，无法评估大K线出现的频率和分布

改进建议

  • 将OpenClose模式改为使用K线的收盘价而非实时Ask价，使逻辑一致且支持回测
  • 增加ATR自适应阈值选项，使警报阈值能根据市场波动率自动调整
  • 添加图表标记和历史统计面板，提升实用性和分析能力

Strengths

  • Simple and clear logic, minimal code easy to understand and maintain
  • One-shot trigger per bar prevents annoying repeated alerts
  • Supports two measurement modes for flexibility
  • Pure notification tool with no trading risk

Weaknesses

  • OpenClose mode uses Ask price instead of Close, causing logical inconsistency and backtest incompatibility
  • Fixed point threshold ignores varying point values across instruments, poor cross-symbol applicability
  • No visual chart markers, making it impossible to visually identify trigger locations on the chart
  • No historical statistics, unable to evaluate frequency and distribution of large bars

Improvements

  • Change OpenClose mode to use bar Close price instead of live Ask price for logical consistency and backtest support
  • Add an ATR-adaptive threshold option so the alert threshold automatically adjusts to market volatility
  • Add chart markers and a historical statistics panel to improve usability and analytical capability
