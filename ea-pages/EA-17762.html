Summary

Elli is an Expert Advisor that implements Elliott Wave-based trend following strategy in MQL5. It identifies wave patterns to determine trend direction and enters trades on corrective wave pullbacks. The EA uses wave counting logic to generate entry and exit signals systematically.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of Elli EA is based on Elliott Wave Theory, identifying impulse and corrective waves to determine market trend direction. Entry conditions typically involve entering after the completion of Wave 2 or Wave 4 pullbacks, anticipating the impulse of Wave 3 or Wave 5. Exit conditions include wave count completion (end of Wave 5), stop-loss triggers, or reversal signals from technical indicators. Position sizing may dynamically adjust lot sizes based on wave degree and account equity, though specifics depend on parameter configuration. Money management recommends using a fixed risk percentage per trade to avoid excessive single-trade exposure. The strategy's strength lies in trend-following, but the subjective nature of wave counting can lead to inaccurate signals, requiring additional confirmation indicators to filter false entries.

优势

  • 基于艾略特波浪理论，顺势交易逻辑清晰，具有理论支撑
  • 在调整浪结束入场，盈亏比通常较好
  • 系统化执行避免人为情绪干扰，纪律性强

劣势

  • 波浪计数具有主观性，不同交易者可能得出不同的波浪划分
  • 在震荡行情中容易产生虚假信号，导致连续亏损
  • 波浪理论本身缺乏严格的数学定义，回测和验证困难

改进建议

  • 增加多时间周期确认，提高波浪识别的准确性
  • 加入额外的确认指标（如RSI背离或MACD）过滤假信号

Strengths

  • Based on Elliott Wave Theory with clear trend-following logic and theoretical foundation
  • Entering at corrective wave completions typically yields favorable risk-reward ratios
  • Systematic execution eliminates emotional interference and maintains trading discipline

Weaknesses

  • Wave counting is subjective and different traders may derive different wave interpretations
  • Prone to false signals in ranging markets, potentially leading to consecutive losses
  • Elliott Wave Theory lacks rigorous mathematical definition, making backtesting and verification difficult

Improvements

  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation to improve wave identification accuracy
  • Incorporate additional confirmation indicators (e.g., RSI divergence or MACD) to filter false signals

  • 若缺少新K线检查机制，可能在同一信号上重复开仓
