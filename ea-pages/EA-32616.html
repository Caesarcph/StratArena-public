摘要

该EA在斐波那契50%和61%回撤位放置限价挂单，止损根据相邻斐波那契水平加减点差偏移计算，止盈统一设为目标价位。支持牛市区和熊市区两种市场方向选择，并实现了价格到达目标后减仓一半并将止损移至保本的部分平仓机制。仓位按风险比例计算，50%交易分配0.7%账户余额风险，61%交易分配剩余风险比例。

策略分析

该EA基于斐波那契回撤理论，在50%和61.8%回撤位设置限价挂单入场，假设价格在这些关键回撤位会获得支撑或阻力反转。入场条件是当市场方向设为牛市时放置BuyLimit挂单在50%和61%水平，熊市则放置SellLimit挂单，挂单仅在两个交易标志位均为0时一次性下达。止损设置方面，50%交易的止损设在61%水平下方3个点差处，61%交易的止损设在61%与100%水平中点再加减3个点差处。仓位管理采用固定风险比例法，根据账户余额的设定风险百分比和止损点数自动计算手数，50%交易分配0.7%风险，61%交易分配剩余风险。出场策略为统一止盈到dTarget2目标位，并且当卖价超过目标位时触发部分平仓——平掉一半仓位并将止损移至入场价实现保本。该策略的主要缺陷是止损和止盈水平全部硬编码为输入参数，缺乏动态计算和自适应能力，且部分平仓逻辑只在价格超过目标后触发，没有移动止盈或追踪止损机制。

Summary

This EA places pending limit orders at Fibonacci 50% and 61% retracement levels, with stop losses calculated using spread-adjusted offsets from adjacent Fibonacci levels and a common take-profit target. It supports both bullish and bearish market bias selections, and implements a partial-close mechanism that halves position size and moves stop-loss to breakeven when price reaches the target. Lot sizing is risk-based, allocating 0.7% of account balance to the 50% trade and the remaining risk percentage to the 61% trade.

Strategy Analysis

This EA is based on Fibonacci retracement theory, placing limit orders at the 50% and 61.8% retracement levels under the assumption that price will find support or resistance at these key levels and reverse. Entry conditions are straightforward: when the market bias is set to Bull, BuyLimit orders are placed at the 50% and 61% price levels; when set to Bear, SellLimit orders are placed instead, and both orders are submitted only once when the trade flags are both zero. For stop-loss placement, the 50% trade's SL is set 3 spreads below the 61% level, while the 61% trade's SL is set 3 spreads beyond the midpoint between the 61% and 100% levels. Position sizing uses a fixed percentage risk model, calculating lot size based on account balance, risk percentage, and stop-loss distance in pips, with 0.7% risk allocated to the 50% trade and the remainder of the user-specified risk percentage allocated to the 61% trade. The exit strategy uses a unified take-profit at the dTarget2 level, and when the ask price exceeds this target, a partial close is triggered — halving the position and moving the stop-loss to the entry price for a breakeven scenario. A major drawback is that all Fibonacci levels and targets are hardcoded as input parameters requiring manual entry, with no dynamic calculation or market-adaptive capability, and the partial close logic only triggers after price exceeds the target rather than using trailing stops or dynamic profit management.

优势

  • 基于风险百分比自动计算仓位大小，资金管理逻辑合理
  • 采用限价挂单入场，避免了市价滑点，且在关键回撤位入场符合技术分析理论
  • 部分平仓加保本止损的出场策略锁定了部分利润，降低了盈利回吐风险

劣势

  • 所有斐波那契水平和目标价格都是硬编码输入参数，必须手动计算和填写，缺乏自动识别回撤水平的能力
  • 部分平仓逻辑存在严重缺陷：仅检查dAsk>dTarget2但使用PositionGetTicket遍历持仓时索引可能因平仓而变化导致跳过持仓
  • 缺乏挂单有效期管理、挂单删除、重置机制等，一旦挂单未成交或条件变化，EA无法重新调整
  • 止损计算中3倍点差的偏移量在高点差品种上可能导致止损过宽或过窄，缺乏对不同品种的适应性

改进建议

  • 增加自动斐波那契水平计算功能，通过识别最近的高点和低点自动绘制回撤位，而非依赖手动输入
  • 修复持仓遍历逻辑，使用倒序遍历或按持仓编号收集后再操作，避免部分平仓导致索引错位；增加挂单状态检测和超时取消机制

Strengths

  • Risk-based position sizing automatically calculates lot size using account balance percentage, providing sound money management
  • Limit order entries avoid market slippage and align with established Fibonacci retracement technical analysis principles
  • Partial close with breakeven stop-loss modification locks in partial profits and reduces the risk of giving back gains

Weaknesses

  • All Fibonacci levels and target prices are hardcoded input parameters requiring manual calculation and entry, with no automatic swing detection or dynamic level calculation
  • The partial close logic has a critical bug: iterating PositionsTotal() with PositionGetTicket while closing positions can cause index shifts leading to skipped positions
  • Lacks pending order lifecycle management — no expiration, deletion, or reset mechanism, so the EA cannot adapt if orders go unfilled or market conditions change
  • The 3-spread offset in stop-loss calculation may result in disproportionately wide or narrow stops on high-spread or low-spread instruments, lacking instrument adaptability

Improvements

  • Add automatic Fibonacci level calculation by detecting recent swing highs and lows to dynamically compute retracement levels instead of requiring manual input
  • Fix the position iteration logic by using reverse-order traversal or collecting position tickets before operating, avoiding index shift from partial closes; add pending order status monitoring and timeout cancellation mechanisms
