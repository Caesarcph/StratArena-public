摘要

New Martin是一个基于马丁格尔策略的智能交易系统，每次亏损后将手数翻倍，试图用一次盈利挽回之前所有亏损。它通常使用简单的方向性入场信号，依靠马丁格尔递进而非高胜率策略逻辑来实现净盈利。在连续亏损期间，该EA面临严重的账户回撤或爆仓风险。

策略分析

策略概念基于经典的马丁格尔资金管理方法，即在每次亏损后将下一笔交易的手数翻倍，使得一次盈利即可覆盖之前所有累计亏损并产生净收益。入场条件通常较为简单，可能基于单一指标信号（如移动平均线交叉或价格突破）或固定方向的重复开仓。出场条件主要依赖止盈目标，当累计亏损被一次盈利覆盖时平仓了结，部分版本可能带有止损但马丁格尔逻辑下止损往往被弱化。仓位管理采用指数级递增方式，手数按2的幂次增长（如0.01、0.02、0.04、0.08...），这导致持仓规模在连续亏损后急剧膨胀。资金管理方面缺乏有效保护机制，没有最大回撤限制或最大步数限制，在极端行情下可能导致账户爆仓。整体而言，该策略的核心风险在于亏损序列的不可控性，当市场持续反向运动时，账户净值将快速下降直至无法承受保证金要求。

Summary

New Martin is a Martingale-based Expert Advisor that opens trades and doubles the lot size after each consecutive loss, aiming to recover all previous losses with a single winning trade. It typically uses a simple directional entry signal and relies on the Martingale progression to achieve net profitability rather than high win-rate strategy logic. The EA carries significant risk of account drawdown or margin call during prolonged losing streaks.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the classic Martingale money management method, which doubles the lot size after each consecutive loss so that a single winning trade can recover all accumulated losses and produce a net profit. Entry conditions are typically simplistic, often relying on a single indicator signal such as a moving average crossover or price breakout, or even repeating entries in the same direction regardless of market conditions. Exit conditions primarily depend on a take-profit target that closes all positions when the cumulative loss is covered by a single win; some versions may include stop-losses but these are often weakened under the Martingale logic. Position sizing follows an exponential progression where lots grow by powers of two (e.g., 0.01, 0.02, 0.04, 0.08...), causing position sizes to balloon dramatically after a series of consecutive losses. Money management lacks effective protective mechanisms such as maximum drawdown limits or maximum step limits, which can lead to account liquidation during extreme market movements. The core risk of this strategy lies in the uncontrollable nature of losing streaks—when the market moves persistently against the position, account equity will decline rapidly until margin requirements can no longer be met.

优势

  • 逻辑简单直观，易于理解和调整参数
  • 在震荡行情中回撤后盈利概率较高，短期表现可能较好
  • 代码结构通常较为简洁，便于快速部署和修改

劣势

  • 连续亏损时手数指数增长，极易导致爆仓
  • 缺乏有效的市场趋势判断，逆势加仓风险巨大
  • 没有最大回撤保护机制，账户安全完全依赖运气和市场震荡

改进建议

  • 增加最大回撤百分比限制，达到阈值后停止加仓并强制平仓
  • 加入趋势过滤指标（如大周期均线或ADX），避免逆势加仓
  • 设置最大马丁格尔步数限制，超出后重置手数而非继续倍增

Strengths

  • Simple and intuitive logic that is easy to understand and adjust parameters for
  • Higher probability of recovery after drawdowns in ranging markets, potentially good short-term performance
  • Typically concise code structure that allows quick deployment and modification

Weaknesses

  • Lot sizes grow exponentially during consecutive losses,easilyleading to margin calls and account blow-up
  • Lacks effective market trend assessment, creating enormous risk from adding positions against the trend
  • No maximum drawdown protection mechanism; account safety relies entirely on luck and market oscillation

Improvements

  • Add a maximum drawdown percentage limit that stops adding positions and forces closure when the threshold is reached
  • Incorporate trend filtering indicators (e.g., higher timeframe moving average or ADX) to avoid adding positions against the prevailing trend
  • Set a maximum Martingale step limit that resets lot size instead of continuing to double beyond the threshold
