摘要

New Martin是一个基于马丁格尔策略的EA，每次亏损后将手数翻倍，试图通过一次盈利交易来覆盖之前的所有亏损。它基于简单的方向判断入场，通过递增仓位而非止损来管理风险。该策略依赖于价格最终会反转的假设，但在极端行情下可能导致账户爆仓。

策略分析

New Martin采用的是经典马丁格尔策略，核心理念是在每次亏损后将下一笔交易的手数翻倍，从而在一次盈利中收回所有之前的亏损并获利。入场条件通常基于简单的趋势判断或信号指标，比如均线方向或价格突破。出场条件主要依赖获利目标平仓，而不是设置止损，这意味着如果市场持续朝不利方向运动，仓位和风险将不断累积。仓位管理完全遵循马丁格尔倍增规则，起始手数较小，但连续亏损后手数呈指数增长。资金管理方面缺乏最大回撤限制和账户权益保护机制，在极端单边行情下可能导致追加保证金甚至爆仓。整体而言，该策略在震荡市中可能表现良好，但在趋势行情中风险极高。

Summary

New Martin is a Martingale-based Expert Advisor that doubles the lot size after each losing trade, aiming to recover previous losses with a single winning trade. It enters positions based on a simple directional bias and uses progressive position sizing rather than stop-losses for risk management. The strategy relies on the assumption that price will eventually reverse in the trader's favor before the account is depleted.

Strategy Analysis

New Martin employs the classic Martingale strategy, where the core idea is to double the lot size after each losing trade, aiming to recover all previous losses plus profit from a single winning trade. Entry conditions are typically based on simple directional signals such as moving average crossovers or price breakouts. Exit conditions primarily rely on a take-profit target rather than stop-losses, meaning that if the market continues moving against the position, both the exposure and risk accumulate exponentially. Position sizing strictly follows the Martingale doubling rule, starting with a small initial lot but growing exponentially after consecutive losses. Money management lacks maximum drawdown limits and account equity protection mechanisms, which can lead to margin calls or account blowouts during strong trending markets. Overall, this strategy may perform well in ranging markets but carries extreme risk during sustained directional moves.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中表现良好，能够在价格回调时快速回本并获利
  • 参数设置灵活，可以调整倍增系数和最大订单数来控制风险敞口
  • 逻辑简单直观，易于理解和部署，适合初学者学习马丁格尔原理

劣势

  • 手数呈指数增长，连续亏损后风险急剧放大，可能导致账户爆仓
  • 缺乏有效的止损机制，完全依赖价格回归假设，在强趋势行情中极其危险
  • 没有考虑点差和手续费的累积成本，实际盈利条件比理论更苛刻

改进建议

  • 增加账户权益保护机制，设置最大回撤百分比限制，达到阈值后强制平仓并重置马丁格尔序列
  • 引入市场状态识别（如ATR或波动率过滤），在强趋势行情中暂停加仓或缩小倍增系数

Strengths

  • Performs well in ranging markets, capable of quickly recovering losses and profiting when price reverts
  • Flexible parameter configuration allows adjusting the multiplier and maximum order count to control risk exposure
  • Simple and intuitive logic, easy to understand and deploy, suitable for beginners learning Martingale principles

Weaknesses

  • Lot sizes grow exponentially, causing risk to escalate dramatically after consecutive losses, potentially leading to account blowout
  • Lacks effective stop-loss mechanisms, entirely relies on the mean-reversion assumption, which is extremely dangerous in strong trending markets
  • Does not account for accumulated spread and commission costs, making actual profit conditions more demanding than theoretical ones

Improvements

  • Add account equity protection with a maximum drawdown percentage limit, forcing closure and Martingale sequence reset when the threshold is reached
  • Introduce market regime detection (e.g., ATR or volatility filter) to pause position doubling or reduce the multiplier during strong trending conditions

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:DANGEROUS

风险点

  • 马丁格尔倍增仓位在连续亏损时呈指数增长，缺乏硬性止损保护，极端行情下可迅速耗尽账户资金
  • 无市场状态过滤机制，在单边趋势行情中持续逆势加仓，风险敞口不可控

Risk Assessment

Overall:DANGEROUS

Risk Points

  • Martingale position doubling causes exponential growth during consecutive losses without hard stop-loss protection, potentially rapidly depleting account funds in extreme markets
  • No market regime filter, continuously adding positions against strong trends, leading to uncontrolled risk exposure
