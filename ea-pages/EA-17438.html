摘要

New Martin是一个基于马丁格尔策略的EA，每次亏损后将手数翻倍，盈利后重置为初始手数。它基于简单信号开仓，依赖递增仓位来恢复亏损，而非使用止损进行风险管理。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略概念是马丁格尔法则：亏损后加倍下注，盈利后回归初始仓位。入场条件通常基于简单的技术指标信号（如移动平均线交叉或价格突破）来决定首次开仓方向。出场条件为达到盈利目标时平仓，亏损时不设止损而是反向加倍开仓。仓位管理采用经典马丁格尔倍增法，每次亏损手数乘以2（或可配置的倍数），直到总体头寸获利平仓后重置。资金管理方面没有任何最大回撤限制或最大层数限制，仅依赖账户余额承受连续亏损。这种策略在趋势行情中可能面临连续逆势加仓导致爆仓的极端风险，属于高风险高回报类型。

Summary

New Martin is a Martingale-based Expert Advisor that doubles the lot size after each losing trade while resetting to the initial lot after a win. It opens positions based on a simple signal and relies on probability recovery through increasing position sizes rather than stop-loss based risk management.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy concept of this EA is the Martingale principle: double the bet after each loss and reset to the initial size after a win. Entry conditions typically rely on a simple technical indicator signal such as a moving average crossover or price breakout to determine the initial trade direction. Exit conditions close all positions when a profit target is reached, while losses trigger a doubling of lot size rather than a stop-loss cut. Position sizing follows the classic Martingale doubling method, multiplying lot size by 2 (or a configurable multiplier) after each loss, resetting only when the overall position achieves profit. Money management lacks any maximum drawdown limit or maximum layer count, relying solely on account balance to absorb consecutive losses. This strategy faces extreme risk of account blowup during strong trending markets with consecutive adverse positions, making it a high-risk, high-reward approach.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中能够稳定盈利，通过加倍仓位覆盖前期亏损
  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和调试
  • 参数可配置，允许调整初始手数和倍增系数以适应不同账户规模

劣势

  • 连续亏损时仓位指数级增长，极易导致爆仓
  • 没有设置最大加仓层数限制，风险完全不可控
  • 在单边趋势行情中几乎必然亏损，缺乏止损保护机制

改进建议

  • 增加最大马丁格尔层数限制，防止无限加倍导致爆仓
  • 加入总仓位最大风险百分比控制，当账户回撤超过阈值时停止交易

Strengths

  • Can achieve consistent profits in ranging markets by doubling position size to recover prior losses
  • Strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and debug
  • Configurable parameters allow adjustment of initial lot size and multiplier to suit different account sizes

Weaknesses

  • Position size grows exponentially during consecutive losses, easily leading to account blowup
  • No maximum layer limit is set, making risk completely uncontrolled
  • Almost certain to lose in trending markets, lacking stop-loss protection mechanisms

Improvements

  • Add a maximum Martingale layer limit to prevent infinite doubling leading to account blowup
  • Implement a maximum total position risk percentage control that halts trading when account drawdown exceeds a threshold
