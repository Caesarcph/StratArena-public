Exp_Trend_Intensity_Index

This EA implements the Trend Intensity Index (TII) strategy, a trend-following indicator that measures the strength and direction of a trend by comparing the deviation of price from its moving average. It generates buy signals when the TII rises above a threshold indicating strong uptrend momentum, and sell signals when it falls below a threshold indicating strong downtrend momentum.

Strategy analysis

The Trend Intensity Index (TII) strategy core concept quantifies trend strength by calculating how much price deviates from its moving average. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when TII crosses above the buy threshold (typically 80), indicating strong uptrend momentum; a sell signal is generated when TII crosses below the sell threshold (typically 20), indicating strong downtrend momentum. Exit conditions typically include closing positions on reverse signal triggers, as well as stop-loss and take-profit levels. Position sizing is managed through configurable lot size parameters, potentially supporting both fixed lot and equity-based dynamic sizing. Money management incorporates stop-loss parameters to control per-trade risk exposure, though the specific risk control mechanism depends on parameter configuration. The overall strategy is a classic trend-following system that performs well in clear trending markets but may generate numerous false signals during choppy or ranging conditions.

Strengths

  • Clear quantification of trend strength with a 0-100 scale that intuitively reflects trend intensity
  • Adjustable thresholds allow traders to fine-tune entry levels based on market volatility and trading style
  • Clean and simple strategy logic based on a single indicator reduces the risk of overfitting
  • Performs well in clearly trending markets, effectively capturing medium to long-term trend moves

Weaknesses

  • Prone to false signals in ranging or choppy markets, leading to frequent losing trades from whipsaws
  • Default stop-loss and take-profit are zero, meaning no risk control unless manually configured
  • Lacks multi-timeframe confirmation, limiting signal reliability from a single timeframe perspective
  • Does not account for trading costs (spread, commission), potentially resulting in net losses on low reward-to-risk signals
  • Position sizing is simplistic, lacking volatility-based or account-risk-based dynamic adjustment

Possible improvements

  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation, e.g., determine trend direction on H4 before entering on H1
  • Implement ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit that adjusts to market volatility automatically
  • Add a trend filter (such as ADX) to disable trading when trend strength is insufficient, reducing false signals in ranging markets
  • Integrate a money management module supporting dynamic position sizing based on account equity percentage

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Default stop-loss and take-profit are zero, leaving trades completely unprotected if users do not manually configure them
  • Trend indicator generates frequent false signals in ranging markets, potentially causing consecutive losses
  • Lacks position count checks, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction and amplifying risk
