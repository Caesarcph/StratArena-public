摘要

该EA结合MACD和CCI指标产生交易信号，当MACD确认向上动量且CCI显示从超卖恢复时做多，反之做空。这是一个基于双指标共振的系统性动量策略，使用固定止损止盈管理仓位。

策略分析

策略核心概念是利用MACD的动量方向与CCI的超买超卖状态形成共振信号进行交易。入场条件为：做多时要求MACD主线在信号线上方或MACD柱状图为正，同时CCI从超卖区域上穿或处于中性区域；做空条件相反。出场条件依赖预设的固定止损和止盈水平，没有追踪止损或动态出场机制。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，未根据账户余额或波动率动态调整仓位大小。资金管理方面缺乏风险比例控制，未实现基于账户权益的百分比风险计算。整体策略逻辑简单清晰，但缺少对市场状态（趋势/震荡）的适应性判断。

Summary

This EA combines MACD and CCI indicators to generate trading signals, entering long positions when MACD confirms upward momentum and CCI indicates oversold-to-neutral recovery, and entering short positions on the opposite conditions. It is a systematic momentum-based strategy that relies on dual-indicator confluence for trade entries. The EA manages positions with fixed stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy concept uses MACD momentum direction combined with CCI overbought/oversold levels to form confluence trading signals. Entry conditions: for long positions, MACD histogram must be positive or the MACD main line above the signal line, while CCI crosses up from oversold territory or sits in the neutral zone; short conditions are the reverse. Exit conditions rely on preset fixed stop loss and take profit levels, with no trailing stop or dynamic exit mechanism. Position sizing uses fixed lots without dynamic adjustment based on account balance or market volatility. Money management lacks percentage-based risk calculation tied to account equity. Overall the strategy logic is simple and clear, but it lacks adaptability to different market states (trending vs ranging).

优势

  • 双指标共振过滤假信号，提高入场质量
  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和调优
  • 使用固定止损止盈，风险可预期
  • MACD与CCI互补，MACD捕捉趋势方向，CCI捕捉极端回归机会
  • 代码结构简洁，适合作为学习参考或二次开发基础

劣势

  • 固定手数仓位管理，未根据账户风险动态调整
  • 缺少市场状态识别，在震荡市中可能频繁亏损
  • 无追踪止损机制，可能无法充分捕获大趋势利润
  • 未设置点差过滤，在高点差环境下入场成本偏高
  • 缺少新K线检查，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓

改进建议

  • 增加基于ATR或账户权益百分比的动态仓位管理
  • 加入趋势/震荡市场状态识别，在非趋势行情中减少或暂停交易

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator confluence filters false signals and improves entry quality
  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and optimize
  • Fixed stop loss and take profit make risk predictable
  • MACD and CCI complement each other — MACD catches trend direction while CCI catches extreme reversals
  • Clean code structure suitable as a learning reference or base for further development

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot position sizing without dynamic adjustment based on account risk
  • No market state identification, likely to suffer frequent losses in ranging markets
  • No trailing stop mechanism, may not fully capture profits from strong trends
  • No spread filter, entry costs may be excessive in high-spread environments
  • No new bar check, potentially opening multiple positions on the same candle

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on ATR or account equity percentage
  • Implement trend/range market state detection to reduce or pause trading in non-trending conditions
