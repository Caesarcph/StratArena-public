Exp_AFIRMA

This EA trades based on the AFIRMA (Adaptive FIR Moving Average) custom indicator, entering long when the indicator slopes upward and short when it slopes downward. It uses a windowed FIR filter with configurable taps and window functions to smooth price data and generate trend-following signals. Positions are managed with fixed Stop Loss and Take Profit levels, and trades are executed only on new bars to avoid over-trading.

摘要

该EA基于AFIRMA（自适应FIR移动平均）自定义指标进行交易，当指标向上倾斜时做多，向下倾斜时做空。它使用带有可配置抽头和窗函数的FIR滤波器来平滑价格数据并生成趋势跟踪信号。仓位通过固定的止损和止盈进行管理，仅在新K线形成时执行交易以避免过度交易。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于AFIRMA自适应FIR移动平均指标的趋势跟踪系统。入场条件为：当指标值在相邻两根K线上递增（Value[0]>Value[1]且Value[1]<Value[2]）时开多仓；当指标值递减（Value[0]<Value[1]且Value[1]>Value[2]）时开空仓。出场条件依赖固定的止损和止盈点位，同时也允许通过反向信号平仓（如出现卖出信号时平多仓）。仓位管理支持多种模式，包括基于可用保证金、余额、亏损比例的动态计算以及固定手数模式。资金管理通过MM参数控制每笔交易占用的存款比例，默认为0.1即10%。策略使用窗函数（矩形窗、汉宁窗、布莱克曼窗等）对FIR滤波器进行优化，以减少频谱泄漏，提高信号平滑度。

Summary

This EA trades based on the AFIRMA (Adaptive FIR Moving Average) custom indicator, entering long when the indicator slopes upward and short when it slopes downward. It uses a windowed FIR filter with configurable taps and window functions to smooth price data and generate trend-following signals. Positions are managed with fixed Stop Loss and Take Profit levels, and trades are executed only on new bars to avoid over-trading.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is a trend-following system based on the AFIRMA (Adaptive FIR Moving Average) custom indicator. Entry conditions are: a long position is opened when the indicator values increase across consecutive bars (Value[0]>Value[1] and Value[1]<Value[2]), and a short position is opened when values decrease (Value[0]<Value[1] and Value[1]>Value[2]). Exit conditions rely on fixed Stop Loss and Take Profit levels in points, with reverse-signal exits also supported (e.g., closing long positions when a sell signal appears). Position sizing supports multiple modes including dynamic calculation based on free margin, balance, loss share ratios, or fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter which determines the share of deposit per deal, defaulting to 0.1 or 10%. The strategy leverages window functions (Rectangular, Hanning, Blackman, Blackman-Harris) to optimize the FIR filter, reducing spectral leakage and improving signal smoothness for more reliable trend detection.

优势

  • 使用FIR滤波器配合多种窗函数，信号平滑度高，有效减少虚假信号
  • 仅在新K线形成时执行交易，避免逐笔报价的过度交易问题
  • 支持多种仓位管理模式，灵活适应不同风险偏好的交易者

劣势

  • 作为趋势跟踪策略，在震荡行情中容易产生频繁的虚假信号和连续亏损
  • 缺少追踪止损功能，固定的止损止盈可能无法适应市场波动性变化
  • 依赖自定义指标AFIRMA，指标文件缺失时EA将无法正常运行
  • 没有交易时间过滤，可能在流动性低的时段产生不利成交

改进建议

  • 增加震荡行情过滤器（如ADX指标），在非趋势行情中暂停交易以减少虚假信号
  • 添加追踪止损功能和波动性自适应止损（如ATR止损），提高盈利保护能力

Strengths

  • Uses FIR filter with multiple window functions for high signal smoothness, effectively reducing false signals
  • Executes trades only on new bar formation, avoiding over-trading on every tick
  • Supports multiple position sizing modes, flexibly accommodating traders with different risk preferences

Weaknesses

  • As a trend-following strategy, it is prone to frequent false signals and consecutive losses in ranging markets
  • Lacks trailing stop functionality; fixed SL/TP may not adapt to changing market volatility
  • Depends on the custom AFIRMA indicator file; EA will fail to run if the indicator is missing
  • No trading session filter, potentially leading to unfavorable fills during low-liquidity periods

Improvements

  • Add a ranging market filter (e.g., ADX indicator) to pause trading during non-trending conditions and reduce false signals
  • Implement trailing stop and volatility-adaptive stops (e.g., ATR-based) to better protect profits

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 趋势跟踪策略在震荡市中可能产生连续亏损，缺少震荡行情过滤器
  • 固定止损止盈不适应市场波动性变化，可能在剧烈波动时止损过近或止盈过远

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Trend-following strategy may produce consecutive losses in ranging markets; lacks a ranging market filter
  • Fixed SL/TP does not adapt to market volatility changes; stop may be too close or take-profit too far during sharp moves
