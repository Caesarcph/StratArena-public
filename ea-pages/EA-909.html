摘要

该EA是一个基于对话框的工具面板，用于展示外汇三角套利/对冲的合成货币对关系。它显示13组预定义的货币对三元组合及其合成方向（多头/空头），并通过自定义GUI对话框在每个tick更新价格。

策略分析

该EA本身不执行任何交易，而是一个对冲辅助工具面板。策略概念基于外汇三角关系，即将一个货币对拆解为另外两个货币对的合成等价物，例如EURUSD = EURGBP × GBPUSD。程序预定义了13组三角货币对关系，每组包含目标货币对、两个合成成分货币对以及合成方向（L=多头合成，S=空头合成）。入场条件和出场条件由用户自行判断，EA仅提供信息展示功能，不自动下单。仓位管理和资金管理完全依赖用户手动操作，EA不涉及任何自动仓位计算或风控逻辑。工具的价值在于帮助交易者快速识别合成价格与实际价格之间的偏差，从而发现潜在的三角套利或对冲机会。

Summary

This EA is a dialog-based utility panel that displays synthetic symbol relationships for forex triangular arbitrage/hedging. It shows 13 predefined currency pair triplets with their Long/Short synthesis direction, updating prices on each tick via a custom GUI dialog.

Strategy Analysis

This EA does not execute any trades itself but serves as a hedging assistance utility panel. The strategy concept is based on forex triangular relationships, decomposing one currency pair into a synthetic equivalent of two other pairs, e.g., EURUSD = EURGBP × GBPUSD. The program predefines 13 triangular currency pair groups, each containing a target pair, two synthetic component pairs, and a synthesis direction (L=long synthesis, S=short synthesis). Entry and exit conditions are entirely determined by the user; the EA only provides informational display without automatic order placement. Position sizing and money management rely completely on manual user operation, with no automated lot calculation or risk control logic. The tool's value lies in helping traders quickly identify discrepancies between synthetic and actual prices, thereby uncovering potential triangular arbitrage or hedging opportunities.
