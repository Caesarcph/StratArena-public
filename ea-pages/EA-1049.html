摘要

该EA基于价格通道（唐奇安式支撑/阻力）的突破进行交易。当价格突破阻力位时开多仓，突破支撑位时开空仓，使用ATR止损和追踪止损功能。仓位可采用固定手数或基于风险的动态计算，并在连续亏损后减小手数。

策略分析

策略概念基于价格通道突破，使用指定周期（rChannel）的最高价和最低价定义阻力位和支撑位。入场条件为：当最高价触及或超过阻力位且阻力位未发生变化时开多仓，或收盘价在阻力位下方但高于枢轴点时开多仓；做空条件对称。出场条件包括：持有多仓时若最高价触及阻力位且阻力位不变则平仓，做空同理，同时支持追踪止损。仓位管理支持固定手数和基于账户自由保证金与最大风险比例（MaxR）的动态手数计算。资金管理包含亏损后手数递减机制（DcF参数），连续亏损越多手数越小，以控制风险。止损基于ATR指标计算，做多止损为支撑位减ATR值，做空止损为阻力位加ATR值加点差。此外，EA在开仓前会检查止损距离是否满足经纪商最小止损水平要求。

Summary

This EA trades breakouts of a price channel (Donchian-style support/resistance). It opens buy positions when price breaks above resistance and sell positions when price breaks below support, with ATR-based stop loss and a trailing stop feature. Position sizing can be fixed or risk-based, with a lot reduction mechanism after consecutive losses.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy is based on price channel breakouts, using the highest high and lowest low over a specified period (rChannel) to define resistance and support levels. Entry conditions are: open a buy when the period high touches or exceeds resistance and resistance has not changed, or when the close is below resistance but above the pivot point; sell conditions are symmetric. Exit conditions include closing a buy position when the high touches resistance and resistance is unchanged, with symmetric logic for sells, plus a configurable trailing stop. Position sizing supports both fixed lots and dynamic calculation based on account free margin and a maximum risk ratio (MaxR). Money management includes a lot reduction mechanism (DcF parameter) that decreases lot size after consecutive losses—the more consecutive losses, the smaller the lot, to control drawdown. Stop loss is calculated using the ATR indicator: for buys it is support minus ATR, and for sells it is resistance plus ATR plus spread. Additionally, the EA checks whether the stop loss distance meets the broker's minimum stop level before placing an order.

优势

  • 具有ATR自适应止损机制，能根据市场波动性动态调整止损距离
  • 包含连续亏损后手数递减机制，有助于控制回撤风险
  • 在开仓前检查止损距离是否满足经纪商最小止损水平要求，避免订单被拒绝
  • 支持基于风险的动态仓位计算，可控制每笔交易的风险敞口
  • 使用新K线判断避免同一K线内重复交易

劣势

  • 开仓信号与平仓信号存在逻辑矛盾——开多仓条件和平多仓条件完全相同，可能导致开仓后立即平仓
  • 没有止盈（Take Profit）设置，盈利完全依赖追踪止损，可能在震荡行情中频繁止损
  • CopyLow返回值检查存在bug，l1的条件判断写成了==1而非==-1，可能导致错误数据未被检测
  • 缺乏点差过滤，在点差较大时仍可能开仓，增加交易成本
  • 追踪止损仅在价格超过开仓价后才会启动，浮盈未达开仓价时没有任何保护机制

改进建议

  • 修复开仓与平仓逻辑矛盾，区分趋势确认信号与反转平仓信号，避免开仓后立即触发平仓
  • 修复defPcChannel函数中CopyLow的返回值检查bug（==1改为==-1）
  • 增加点差过滤机制，在点差超过阈值时禁止开仓

Strengths

  • Features ATR-based adaptive stop loss that dynamically adjusts to market volatility
  • Includes a lot reduction mechanism after consecutive losses to help control drawdown risk
  • Checks stop loss distance against broker minimum stop level before placing orders, avoiding rejected orders
  • Supports risk-based dynamic position sizing to control risk exposure per trade
  • Uses new bar detection to prevent repeated trading within the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Logical contradiction between entry and exit signals—buy open and buy close conditions are identical, which could cause immediate closure after opening
  • No Take Profit is set, profit relies entirely on trailing stop, which may cause frequent stop-outs in choppy markets
  • Bug in CopyLow return value check: l1 condition uses ==1 instead of ==-1, potentially allowing corrupted data to pass undetected
  • Lacks spread filtering, may open positions during high spread conditions increasing trading costs
  • Trailing stop only activates when price exceeds the open price, providing no protection when profit is below the entry level

Improvements

  • Fix the logical contradiction between entry and exit signals by differentiating trend confirmation signals from reversal exit signals to prevent immediate closure after opening
  • Fix the CopyLow return value check bug in defPcChannel function (change ==1 to ==-1)
  • Add a spread filter mechanism to prevent opening positions when the spread exceeds a threshold
