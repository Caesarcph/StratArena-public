摘要

该EA基于价格通道（唐奇安通道风格）突破进行交易，使用ATR设置止损。当价格突破阻力位时开多单，跌破支撑位时开空单，并在连续亏损后缩减仓位。还包含追踪止损和邮件通知功能。

策略分析

策略核心基于价格通道突破：利用rChannel周期的最高价和最低价构建支撑和阻力线，当价格触及或突破阻力且阻力值未变时产生买入信号，当价格触及或跌破支撑且支撑值未变时产生卖出信号。入场条件包含两种情况：一是高点突破阻力位，二是收盘价在阻力与中枢位之间且阻力位稳定不变。出场条件与入场逻辑相关：买单在价格触及阻力时平仓，卖单在价格触及支撑时平仓。仓位管理采用两种模式：固定手数和基于可用保证金与最大风险比例的动态手数。资金管理引入了亏损递减因子DcF，在连续亏损后按比例缩减仓位，但该逻辑仅在losses>1时触发，单次亏损不会调整手数。止损基于ATR指标计算，买单位于支撑减ATR，卖单位于阻力加ATR加点差，同时检查止损距离是否满足经纪商的最小止损水平要求。

Summary

This EA trades breakouts of a price channel (Donchian-style) with ATR-based stop losses. It opens buy positions when price breaks above resistance and sell positions when price breaks below support, using a lot reduction mechanism after consecutive losses. Trailing stop and email notifications are included as auxiliary features.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is based on price channel breakout: it builds support and resistance lines using the highest and lowest prices over the rChannel period. Buy signals are generated when price touches or breaks above resistance and the resistance value remains unchanged; sell signals when price touches or breaks below support and support remains unchanged. Entry conditions include two scenarios: first, the high breaks the resistance level; second, the close is between resistance and pivot with resistance stable. Exit conditions mirror entry logic: buy positions close when price reaches resistance, sell positions close when price reaches support. Position sizing uses two modes: fixed lots or dynamic lots calculated from free margin multiplied by the MaxR risk ratio. Money management introduces a drawdown reduction factor DcF that proportionally reduces lot size after consecutive losses, but this logic only triggers when losses exceed 1, meaning a single loss does not adjust the lot. Stop losses are ATR-based: buy SL at support minus ATR, sell SL at resistance plus ATR plus spread, with checks against the broker's minimum stop level.

优势

  • 基于价格通道突破的经典策略逻辑，具有明确的结构化入场和出场规则
  • 使用ATR动态止损，能根据市场波动性自适应调整止损距离
  • 连续亏损后自动缩减仓位，有助于控制回撤风险
  • 止损设置前检查经纪商最小止损水平，避免因违规被拒单
  • 追踪止损功能可锁定浮动利润，保护已盈利仓位

劣势

  • 出场逻辑存在问题：买入开仓条件之一（高点突破阻力）与买入平仓条件相同，可能导致刚开仓就立即平仓
  • 平仓后LastTime才更新，但开仓和同bar平仓在同一个tick中处理，逻辑可能产生开平仓冲突
  • 亏损递减逻辑仅在losses>1时触发，单次亏损后不调整仓位，递减机制不够灵敏
  • 没有止盈设置（TP=0），完全依赖平仓信号或追踪止损来退出盈利仓位
  • 价格通道函数中CopyLow的返回值检查有一个bug：l1==1应为l1==-1

改进建议

  • 修复isOpenBuy与isCloseBuy的逻辑冲突，将入场和出场信号区分开，避免同bar开平仓
  • 修复defPcChannel中CopyLow返回值检查bug（l1==1应为l1==-1），并添加更健壮的数据拷贝错误处理
  • 增加止盈逻辑或至少基于ATR的动态止盈，而非完全依赖信号平仓
  • 加入点差过滤，在点差过大时避免开仓，特别是突破策略在新闻时段容易被滑点影响

Strengths

  • Classic price channel breakout logic with clearly structured entry and exit rules
  • ATR-based dynamic stop loss adapts to current market volatility automatically
  • Automatic lot reduction after consecutive losses helps control drawdown risk
  • Broker minimum stop level check before placing stops prevents order rejection
  • Trailing stop feature locks in floating profits and protects winning positions

Weaknesses

  • Exit logic flaw: one of the buy entry conditions (high breaks resistance) is identical to the buy close condition, potentially causing immediate close after opening
  • LastTime is updated after both open and close logic execute in the same bar tick, creating potential open-then-immediately-close conflict
  • Drawdown reduction only triggers when losses>1, so single losses do not adjust lot size, making the mechanism insufficiently responsive
  • No take profit is set (TP=0), relying entirely on close signals or trailing stop to exit profitable positions
  • Bug in defPcChannel: CopyLow for l1 checks ==1 instead of ==-1, so a failed copy would not be caught

Improvements

  • Fix the logic conflict between isOpenBuy and isCloseBuy by differentiating entry and exit signals to avoid same-bar open-then-close
  • Fix the CopyLow return value check bug in defPcChannel (l1==1 should be l1==-1) and add more robust data copy error handling
  • Add take profit logic or at least ATR-based dynamic take profit instead of relying entirely on signal-based exits
  • Add spread filter to avoid opening positions when spread is too wide, especially since breakout strategies are vulnerable to slippage during news periods

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Overlapping open and close signal logic may cause immediate open-then-close trades with spread costs
  • Bug in CopyLow return value check in defPcChannel could lead to using uninitialized array data
  • No take profit means winning positions rely entirely on trailing stop or close signals, risking significant profit giveback during fast reversals
  • Only linear lot reduction after consecutive losses rather than pausing trading, which may lead to sustained losses in extreme market conditions
