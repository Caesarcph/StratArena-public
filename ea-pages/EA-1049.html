TradeChannel

A price channel breakout EA that identifies support and resistance levels using a Donchian-style channel, then opens buy/sell positions when price breaks through these levels. It includes ATR-based stop loss, a martingale-like lot reduction after consecutive losses, and a simple trailing stop mechanism.

Strategy analysis

The EA uses a Donchian-style price channel with a configurable period (rChannel) to identify support and resistance levels. Entry signals come in two forms: first, when price breaks the channel boundary (high touching resistance for buy, low touching support for sell) with the current channel level matching the previous period's level, indicating channel stability; second, when the close price is inside the channel butbiases toward the pivot direction. Exit conditions mirror the breakout signals: closing longs when the high touches resistance, and closing shorts when the low touches support. Position sizing offers two modes: fixed lots or dynamic calculation based on a maximum risk percentage of free margin, with a DcF parameter that reduces lot size after consecutive losses. Stop loss is ATR-based: for buys it's support minus ATR, for sells it's resistance plus ATR plus the spread. The trailing stop feature moves the stop loss to breakeven or better once profit exceeds the Trailing parameter in points.

Strengths

  • ATR-based dynamic stop loss adapts to market volatility automatically
  • Includes stop loss protection and trailing stop functionality to lock in profits
  • Lot reduction mechanism after consecutive losses helps control drawdown risk
  • Channel breakout strategy logic is clear, easy to understand and tune parameters
  • Checks minimum stop level requirements before opening positions

Weaknesses

  • isCloseBuy and isOpenBuy share identical signal logic, causing a newly opened long position to potentially close on the very next bar
  • Symmetric entry/exit signals lack differentiation; no take-profit target, relying solely on stop loss and reversal signals for exits
  • The DcF lot reduction mechanism decreases size after losses rather than increasing, which is conservative but may miss recovery opportunities
  • Bug in defPcChannel: CopyLow return value check uses ==1 instead of ==-1, potentially missing data fetch failures
  • Missing spread filter and trading session filter, may result in unfavorable fills during low liquidity or high spread periods

Possible improvements

  • Differentiate entry and exit signal logic, e.g., use different channel periods or add confirmation conditions to avoid immediate close after open
  • Fix the CopyLow return value check bug in defPcChannel (change ==1 to ==-1), and add take-profit targets and spread filter
  • Add trading session filter and spread check to avoid opening positions under unfavorable conditions

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Identical entry and exit signals may cause frequent open-close cycles leading to commission losses
  • CopyLow return value check bug in defPcChannel may cause use of uninitialized data
  • No take-profit target means profits rely entirely on trailing stop or reversal signals, risking repeated stop-outs in ranging markets
  • Lot reduction after consecutive losses reduces risk but also diminishes recovery capacity
