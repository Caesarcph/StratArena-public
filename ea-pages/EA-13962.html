摘要

该EA基于XKRI_Histogram自定义指标进行交易，该指标结合了KRI（关键反转指标）与XMA平滑算法。当直方图上升时开多仓，当直方图下降时开空仓，使用可配置的资金管理和止损/止盈水平。

策略分析

策略核心基于XKRI_Histogram自定义指标，该指标融合了KRI动量计算与多种平滑算法（如JJMA、JurX、T3等）。入场条件为：当直方图前两根柱值递减且当前柱值大于前一柱值时，触发买入信号；当直方图前两根柱值递增且当前柱值小于前一柱值时，触发卖出信号。出场条件依赖固定的止损和止盈设置，同时也有基于指标反向信号的平仓机制。仓位管理支持五种模式：基于可用保证金、基于余额、基于亏损后可用保证金、基于亏损后余额、以及固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1表示使用10%的资金比例。信号仅在新的K线出现时计算，避免重复交易，同时使用SignalBar参数允许在指定K线位置获取信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on the XKRI_Histogram custom indicator, which combines a KRI (Key Reversal Indicator) with XMA smoothing. It opens buy positions when the histogram rises and sell positions when the histogram falls, using configurable money management and stop-loss/take-profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is based on the XKRI_Histogram custom indicator, which fuses KRI momentum calculation with multiple smoothing algorithms (such as JJMA, JurX, T3, etc.). Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when the histogram values of the prior two bars are decreasing and the current bar value exceeds the previous bar value; a sell signal triggers when the prior two bars are increasing and the current bar value is below the previous bar value. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop-loss and take-profit settings, along with a position-closing mechanism based on indicator reversal signals. Position sizing supports five modes: based on free margin, balance, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled via the MM parameter, defaulting to 0.1 which represents using 10% of the capital ratio. Signals are only calculated on new bar formation to avoid repeated trades, while the SignalBar parameter allows obtaining signals from a specified bar position.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于直方图动量变化进行交易，信号规则明确
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，灵活性高，适应不同风险偏好
  • 提供了完整的买入/卖出开仓和平仓开关，可灵活控制交易方向
  • 使用新K线检测机制避免重复信号，减少过度交易
  • 指标参数丰富，可针对不同市场条件进行优化调整

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点的默认值偏大，对大多数品种来说风险过高
  • 缺少点差过滤机制，在买卖价差较大时可能不利入场
  • 没有持仓检查逻辑，可能在已有同方向持仓时重复开仓
  • 指标依赖外部自定义指标XKRI_Histogram，增加了部署复杂性和依赖风险
  • 缺少最大持仓数量和最大回撤限制等风控措施

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓
  • 添加点差过滤和最大回撤保护机制
  • 优化默认止损止盈参数，使其更适应不同品种的波动性
  • 增加追踪止损功能，让盈利头寸有更好的出场策略

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on histogram momentum changes with well-defined signal rules
  • Supports multiple money management modes with high flexibility to adapt to different risk preferences
  • Provides complete buy/sell open and close switches for flexible trading direction control
  • Uses new bar detection mechanism to avoid duplicate signals and reduce overtrading
  • Rich indicator parameters allow optimization for different market conditions

Weaknesses

  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments, posing high risk
  • Lacks spread filter mechanism, potentially entering at unfavorable prices when spread is wide
  • No position check logic, may repeatedly open positions in the same direction
  • Depends on external custom indicator XKRI_Histogram, increasing deployment complexity and dependency risk
  • Lacks risk controls such as maximum position count and maximum drawdown limits

Improvements

  • Add position check logic to avoid duplicate openings in the same direction
  • Add spread filter and maximum drawdown protection mechanisms
  • Optimize default stop loss and take profit parameters to better adapt to different instrument volatilities
  • Add trailing stop functionality for better exit strategies on profitable positions
