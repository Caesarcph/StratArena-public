摘要

该EA基于FRASMAv2（分形自适应简单移动平均线）自定义指标信号进行交易。当指标从中性状态转为上涨信号时开多仓，转为下跌信号时开空仓，并可在信号反转时选择平仓。

策略分析

策略核心基于FRASMAv2分形自适应移动平均指标，该指标通过分形维度调整移动平均线的速度以适应市场波动变化。入场条件为：当指标缓冲区值从0（中性）变为正值（Value[0]>0且Value[1]==0）时触发买入信号；当值从2（下跌趋势）变为小于2的值（Value[0]<2且Value[1]==2）时触发卖出信号。出场条件依赖信号反转：买入平仓在下跌信号出现时触发，卖出平仓在上涨信号出现时触发，且买卖平仓可分别通过BuyPosClose和SellPosClose参数独立控制。仓位管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，并支持五种资金管理模式（可用保证金、余额、亏损保证金、亏损余额、固定手数）。止损和止盈分别固定为1000点和2000点，但缺乏动态调整机制。策略使用IsNewBar机制限制每根K线仅计算一次信号，避免重复交易，同时通过SignalBar参数允许选择信号来源K线。

Summary

This EA trades based on the FRASMAv2 (Fractal-adapted Simple Moving Average) custom indicator signals. It opens buy positions when the indicator transitions from a neutral state to an uptrend signal and sell positions on transitions to a downtrend signal, with optional position closing on signal reversals.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is based on the FRASMAv2 fractal-adapted moving average indicator, which adjusts the moving average speed using fractal dimension to adapt to market volatility changes. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when the indicator buffer value transitions from 0 (neutral) to a positive value (Value[0]>0 and Value[1]==0), and a sell signal triggers when the value transitions from 2 (downtrend) to a value below 2 (Value[0]<2 and Value[1]==2). Exit conditions rely on signal reversals: buy positions close when a downtrend signal appears, and sell positions close when an uptrend signal appears, with each independently controllable via BuyPosClose and SellPosClose parameters. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter as a risk ratio, supporting five money management modes (free margin, balance, loss free margin, loss balance, and fixed lot). Stop loss and take profit are fixed at 1000 and 2000 points respectively, with no dynamic adjustment mechanism. The strategy uses the IsNewBar mechanism to limit signal calculation to once per bar, preventing duplicate trades, while the SignalBar parameter allows selecting the signal source bar.

优势

  • 使用分形自适应移动平均线，能够根据市场波动率动态调整灵敏度，比传统MA更灵活
  • 买卖开仓和平仓信号独立可控，提供灵活的交易逻辑配置
  • 采用IsNewBar新K线检测机制，避免同一根K线内重复计算信号和重复开仓
  • 支持五种资金管理模式，适应不同风险偏好的交易者
  • 止损止盈参数明确，风险控制基础框架完整

劣势

  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未考虑市场波动率（ATR）动态调整，在不同市场条件下可能不适用
  • 缺乏持仓检查机制，可能在已有同方向持仓时重复开仓
  • 依赖自定义指标FRASMAv2，指标逻辑不透明，无法验证信号质量
  • 没有点差过滤机制，在高点差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • 缺少追踪止损功能，盈利仓位可能在回撤中损失大量利润

改进建议

  • 引入ATR动态止损止盈机制，根据市场波动率自动调整止损止盈距离
  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓，并支持加仓策略
  • 增加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免不利成交条件下的交易

Strengths

  • Uses fractal-adapted moving average that dynamically adjusts sensitivity based on market volatility, more flexible than traditional MAs
  • Buy/sell open and close signals are independently controllable, providing flexible trading logic configuration
  • Employs IsNewBar new bar detection mechanism, preventing duplicate signal calculation and repeated position opening within the same bar
  • Supports five money management modes, accommodating traders with different risk preferences
  • Clear stop loss and take profit parameters with a complete basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Fixed stop loss and take profit in points without ATR-based dynamic adjustment, may be unsuitable across varying market conditions
  • Lacks position existence check, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Depends on the custom FRASMAv2 indicator with opaque logic, making signal quality unverifiable
  • No spread filter mechanism, may result in unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • Missing trailing stop functionality, profitable positions may lose significant gains during pullbacks

Improvements

  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit mechanism to automatically adjust distances based on market volatility
  • Add position existence check logic to prevent duplicate openings in the same direction, and support position-adding strategies
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid trading under unfavorable execution conditions
