摘要

Lucky是一个突破剥头皮EA，检测相对于上一tick价格的突然价格跳跃，并反向开仓，预期价格回归。它在盈利时或亏损超过固定点数限制时平仓。该策略是一个简单的跳跃检测反转系统，风险控制较少。

策略分析

Lucky EA的核心策略是检测价格跳跃后进行反向交易，本质上是一种短期均值回归/突破反转策略。入场条件：当当前Ask价相对于上一tick的Ask价上涨超过Shift个点时，开空单；当当前Bid价相对于上一tick的Bid价下跌超过Shift个点时，开多单。这是一种逆势入场逻辑，假设价格跳跃后会回撤。出场条件有两个：一是当持仓浮盈大于0时立即平仓，二是当亏损点数超过Limit参数时止损平仓。仓位管理方面，仅使用固定手数（Lots参数），没有任何加仓、减仓或马丁格尔逻辑。资金管理完全缺失，没有基于账户余额的动态仓位计算，也没有最大回撤控制。该EA仅在无持仓时才开新仓，每次只持有一个方向的仓位。

Summary

Lucky is a breakout scalping EA that detects sudden price jumps relative to the previous tick's prices and opens positions in the opposite direction, expecting a mean reversion. It closes positions when profitable or when the loss exceeds a fixed point limit. The strategy is a simple jump-detection reversal system with minimal risk controls.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of Lucky EA is to detect price jumps and trade in the opposite direction, essentially a short-term mean reversion breakout-reversal system. Entry conditions: when the current Ask price rises more than Shift points above the previous tick's Ask, a sell position is opened; when the current Bid price falls more than Shift points below the previous tick's Bid, a buy position is opened. This is a counter-trend entry logic assuming price will retrace after a sudden jump. Exit conditions are twofold: first, close immediately when the position shows any positive floating profit; second, close when the loss exceeds the Limit parameter in points. Position sizing uses only a fixed lot size (Lots parameter) with no scaling, martingale, or pyramid logic. Money management is entirely absent, with no dynamic lot calculation based on account balance and no maximum drawdown control. The EA only opens new positions when no existing position is held, maintaining at most one position at a time in a single direction.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和调试
  • 每次只持有一个仓位，避免了复合风险
  • 设有止损限制参数，防止单笔亏损无限扩大

劣势

  • 仅在浮盈大于零时平仓，没有止盈目标，可能错失更大利润
  • 使用tick级别比较而非新K线，在快速市场中可能频繁触发虚假信号
  • 缺乏资金管理，固定手数不适应不同账户规模
  • 没有考虑点差和滑点对剥头皮策略的严重影响
  • 止损Limit仅基于点数而非ATR等波动率指标，在不同市场条件下效果差异大

改进建议

  • 增加基于ATR的动态止损和止盈，替代固定点数参数
  • 添加新K线检测机制，避免tick级别噪音导致的虚假信号
  • 实现基于账户余额的动态仓位管理，替代固定手数

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and debug
  • Holds only one position at a time, avoiding compounding risk
  • Has a stop-loss limit parameter to prevent unlimited single-trade losses

Weaknesses

  • Closes only when floating profit is positive with no take-profit target, potentially missing larger gains
  • Uses tick-level comparison instead of new bar detection, may trigger false signals frequently in fast markets
  • Lacks money management with fixed lots unsuitable for different account sizes
  • Does not account for spread and slippage impact critical to scalping strategies
  • Stop-loss Limit is point-based only without ATR or volatility metrics, performing inconsistently across market conditions

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit instead of fixed point parameters
  • Implement new bar detection to avoid false signals from tick-level noise
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account balance instead of fixed lots

总体 / Overall:CAUTION

风险点

  • 剥头皮策略未考虑点差过滤，高点差环境下可能频繁亏损
  • 浮盈即平仓逻辑在点差较大时可能导致刚开仓就因微小浮盈平仓再重新开仓的循环
  • tick级别信号在没有新K线检查时可能产生大量虚假交易信号

Risk Assessment

Overall:CAUTION

Risk Points

  • Scalping strategy without spread filter may incur frequent losses in high-spread environments
  • Close-on-any-profit logic with large spreads may cause open-close-reopen cycling on tiny profits
  • Tick-level signals without new bar checks may generate numerous false trade signals
