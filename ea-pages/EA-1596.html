摘要

该EA基于自定义TrendContinuation指标的趋势延续信号进行交易。通过检测指标上下两条线的交叉来开仓做多或做空，支持可配置的资金管理、止损和止盈。交易信号仅在指定时间周期的新K线形成时生成，以避免重复入场。

策略分析

策略概念基于趋势延续，即当TrendContinuation指标的两条线发生交叉时，认为当前趋势将会延续。入场条件为：当上线上穿下线时开多单，当上线下穿下线时开空单，信号在指定SignalBar上检测并通过前一根K线的比较确认交叉。出场条件包括固定点数的止损和止盈，以及反向信号平仓（多单在卖出信号时平仓，空单在买入信号时平仓）。仓位管理支持五种模式：按自由保证金比例、按余额比例、按自由保证金亏损比例、按余额亏损比例和固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制投入比例，MMMode参数决定具体的资金分配方式。策略使用了多种平滑算法（SMA、EMA、JJMA、T3等）对价格进行预处理，提高了信号的平滑度。

Summary

This EA trades trend continuation signals generated by the custom TrendContinuation indicator. It detects crossovers between the indicator's upper and lower buffer lines to open buy or sell positions, with configurable money management, stop loss, and take profit. Trading is restricted to new bar formation on the specified timeframe to avoid redundant signals.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on trend continuation, where crossovers of the TrendContinuation indicator's two buffer lines signal that the current trend will persist. Entry conditions: a buy position opens when the upper line crosses above the lower line, and a sell position opens when the upper line crosses below the lower line; signals are confirmed by comparing the current and previous bar values at the specified SignalBar index. Exit conditions include fixed-point stop loss and take profit levels, plus reverse-signal closing where long positions close on sell signals and short positions close on buy signals. Position sizing supports five modes: free margin share, balance share, loss share from free margin, loss share from balance, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter for deposit share and MMMode parameter for the specific allocation method. The strategy employs multiple smoothing algorithms (SMA, EMA, JJMA, T3, etc.) for price preprocessing, which improves signal smoothness and reduces noise.

优势

  • 使用新K线检测机制，避免同一根K线内重复开仓，减少过度交易
  • 支持多种平滑算法和价格类型，策略灵活度高，可适应不同市场环境
  • 提供买卖方向独立的开关控制，可灵活控制交易方向和平仓逻辑
  • 止损止盈设置明确，风险可控
  • 反向信号平仓机制提供额外的趋势反转保护

劣势

  • 依赖自定义TrendContinuation指标，指标源码不可见时难以验证信号逻辑的可靠性
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值偏大，在短线交易中可能造成较大亏损
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时间过滤，在高点差时段或非活跃时段可能产生不利成交
  • 没有追踪止损功能，盈利头寸可能在回调中大幅回吐利润
  • 信号仅在K线交叉时触发，在剧烈行情中可能错过快速入场时机

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免高点差和低流动性时段的不利成交
  • 增加追踪止损功能，在趋势延续时锁定更多利润
  • 增加仓位检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓
  • 提供更细粒度的信号确认机制，如增加成交量或动量过滤

Strengths

  • New bar detection mechanism prevents redundant trades within the same bar, reducing overtrading
  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithms and price types, offering high flexibility for different market conditions
  • Independent on/off switches for buy/sell directions and close permissions provide granular control
  • Explicit stop loss and take profit settings make risk controllable
  • Reverse-signal closing mechanism provides additional protection against trend reversals

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom TrendContinuation indicator whose source code is not visible, making signal logic reliability hard to verify
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are large, potentially causing significant losses in short-term trading
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, which may result in unfavorable fills during high-spread or illiquid periods
  • No trailing stop functionality, so profitable positions may give back significant gains during pullbacks
  • Signals only trigger on bar crossovers, which may miss fast entry opportunities during volatile market moves

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid unfavorable fills during high-spread and low-liquidity periods
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in more profit during trend continuation
  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Provide more granular signal confirmation mechanisms such as volume or momentum filters
