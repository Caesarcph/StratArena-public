摘要

该EA使用DeMarker振荡器识别超买和超卖市场状态，在检测到极端读数时在战略价位放置挂单。当DeMarker发出超卖信号时建立多头仓位，发出超买信号时建立空头仓位，旨在捕捉均值回归行情。使用挂单而非市价单可以获得更好的入场价格并内置价格确认。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于DeMarker振荡器的均值回归交易。DeMarker指标衡量价格变化的速度与幅度，当读数超过设定的超买阈值时表明市场过度买入可能回落，当读数低于超卖阈值时表明市场过度卖出可能反弹。入场条件为：DeMarker值低于超卖水平时放置买入挂单，DeMarker值高于超买水平时放置卖出挂单，使用挂单而非市价单可以在价格进一步确认方向后再成交。出场条件依赖于止损和止盈参数的设置，通过固定点数的止损止盈来控制风险回报比。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，没有动态加仓或减仓机制。资金管理方面缺少对账户权益比例的动态计算，整体风控依赖于静态止损参数，缺少追踪止损和移动止盈功能。

Summary

This EA uses the DeMarker oscillator to identify overbought and oversold market conditions, then places pending orders at strategic price levels when extreme readings are detected. It enters buy positions when DeMarker signals oversold conditions and sell positions when overbought, aiming to capture mean reversion moves. The use of pending orders rather than market orders allows for better entry prices with built-in price confirmation.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is mean reversion trading based on the DeMarker oscillator. The DeMarker indicator measures the speed and magnitude of price changes; when readings exceed the configured overbought threshold, it signals that the market is overextended to the upside and may reverse, and when readings fall below the oversold threshold, it signals oversold conditions with potential for a bounce. Entry conditions are: place a buy pending order when the DeMarker value drops below the oversold level, and place a sell pending order when it rises above the overbought level. Using pending orders instead of market orders allows the trade to be executed only after price confirms the anticipated direction by reaching the pending order price. Exit conditions rely on the stop loss and take profit parameters configured by the user, controlling risk-reward ratio through fixed pip-based SL/TP levels. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic scaling, and money management lacks equity-proportional calculation, making the overall risk control dependent on static stop loss parameters without trailing stop or dynamic take profit features.

优势

  • 使用挂单入场提供了额外的价格确认，避免在震荡区间被假信号误导
  • DeMarker是经过验证的振荡器指标，在区间市场中识别超买超卖较为可靠
  • 策略逻辑简洁明了，参数可调性强，便于优化和适应不同品种

劣势

  • 均值回归策略在趋势行情中会持续亏损，缺少趋势过滤机制
  • 固定手数没有根据账户资金动态调整，不利于长期资金增长和风险控制
  • 缺少追踪止损和部分止盈功能，无法在盈利交易中锁定浮赢
  • 没有检查是否已有同方向持仓，可能导致重复开仓增加风险敞口

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤器（如均线方向或ADX阈值），避免在强趋势行情中逆势交易
  • 实现基于账户权益百分比的动态仓位计算，并加入追踪止损功能

Strengths

  • Pending order entry provides additional price confirmation, avoiding false signals in choppy markets
  • DeMarker is a proven oscillator that reliably identifies overbought/oversold conditions in ranging markets
  • Strategy logic is clean and straightforward with highly configurable parameters for optimization across instruments

Weaknesses

  • Mean reversion strategy suffers consecutive losses in trending markets due to lack of trend filtering
  • Fixed lot sizing does not adapt to account equity, hindering long-term capital growth and risk control
  • Absence of trailing stop and partial close features means floating profits cannot be locked in during winning trades
  • No check for existing positions in the same direction, potentially leading to duplicate entries and increased risk exposure

Improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., MA direction or ADX threshold) to avoid counter-trend trading during strong directional moves
  • Implement equity-proportional dynamic position sizing and add trailing stop functionality to protect profits
