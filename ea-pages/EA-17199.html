摘要

这是一个资金管理工具型EA，根据账户余额的固定风险百分比和预定的止损点数来计算仓位大小（手数）。它本身不生成交易信号或开仓，而是为手动或半自动交易决策提供固定风险的仓位计算。

策略分析

该EA的核心功能是根据固定风险金额来计算合适的交易手数。用户设定账户风险百分比和止损点数，EA据此计算出每笔交易的最大亏损金额对应的仓位大小。策略概念是基于固定分数法（Fixed Fractional）的资金管理原则，确保每笔交易的风险敞口不会超过账户的预设比例。入场条件不适用，因为这是一个工具型EA而非策略型EA。出场条件同样不适用。仓位管理是其核心功能，通过将账户余额乘以风险百分比再除以止损点数对应的价值来计算手数。资金管理方面，它严格遵循固定风险比例原则，防止单笔交易造成过大损失，但缺少对最小手数和最大手数的边界检查。

Summary

This is a money management utility EA that calculates position size (lot) based on a fixed risk percentage of the account balance and a predetermined stop-loss distance in points. It does not generate trading signals or open positions on its own; instead it provides fixed-risk position sizing for manual or semi-automated trading decisions.

Strategy Analysis

The core function of this EA is to calculate appropriate trade lot sizes based on a fixed risk amount. The user sets an account risk percentage and a stop-loss distance in points, and the EA computes the position size that corresponds to the maximum allowable loss per trade. The strategy concept is rooted in the Fixed Fractional money management principle, ensuring each trade's risk exposure does not exceed a preset proportion of the account. Entry conditions are not applicable since this is a utility EA rather than a strategy EA. Exit conditions are similarly not applicable. Position sizing is its core feature, calculating lots by multiplying the account balance by the risk percentage and dividing by the monetary value of the stop-loss points. In terms of money management, it strictly follows the fixed risk ratio principle to prevent any single trade from causing excessive loss, though it may lack boundary checks for minimum and maximum lot sizes.

优势

  • 严格遵循固定风险比例原则，有效控制单笔交易最大亏损
  • 使用简单，参数少，易于理解和配置
  • 帮助交易者避免情绪化过度仓位，实现纪律性资金管理

劣势

  • 仅提供仓位计算功能，不包含入场或出场策略逻辑
  • 缺少对经纪商最小/最大手数限制的检查，可能导致下单失败
  • 未考虑点差、佣金和掉期等交易成本对实际风险的影响

改进建议

  • 增加经纪商手数限制检查（最小手数、最大手数、手数步长），确保计算结果可执行
  • 增加对点差和佣金的考虑，使实际风险计算更加精确

Strengths

  • Strictly follows the fixed risk percentage principle, effectively controlling maximum loss per trade
  • Simple to use with few parameters, easy to understand and configure
  • Helps traders avoid emotional over-positioning and enables disciplined money management

Weaknesses

  • Only provides position sizing functionality without any entry or exit strategy logic
  • Lacks checks for broker minimum/maximum lot size limits, which may cause order placement failures
  • Does not account for trading costs such as spread, commission, and swap that affect actual risk

Improvements

  • Add broker lot size limit checks (minimum lot, maximum lot, lot step) to ensure calculated sizes are executable
  • Incorporate spread and commission into the risk calculation for more accurate actual risk estimation
