摘要

该EA基于自定义BrakeParb指标的交易信号进行操作，当指标产生趋势变化信号时开仓买入或卖出，趋势反转时平仓。采用多周期方法，可配置资金管理、止损和止盈。这是一个系统化的趋势跟踪策略，对多头和空头的入场与出场分别设有权限控制。

策略分析

策略概念基于BrakeParb自定义指标的趋势跟踪，该指标通过参数A、B和biginShift计算趋势方向和信号。入场条件为：当UpSignal信号触发时开多仓，当DnSignal信号触发时开空仓，同时分别在趋势持续时关闭反向仓位。出场条件包括止损和止盈的固定点数设置，以及趋势反转时的信号平仓。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示固定手数，正值表示按存款比例计算手数。资金管理采用单笔交易风险控制模式，但没有动态调整或 trailing stop 机制。策略使用IsNewBar机制确保每个K线只计算一次信号，避免重复交易，并通过Recount标志在数据不足时重新计算。

Summary

This EA trades based on signals from the custom BrakeParb indicator, opening buy/sell positions when the indicator generates trend change signals and closing positions when the trend reverses. It uses a multi-timeframe approach with configurable money management, stop loss, and take profit. The strategy is a systematic trend-following system with separate permissions for entry and exit on both sides.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is trend following based on the custom BrakeParb indicator, which calculates trend direction and signals using parameters A, B, and biginShift. Entry conditions: a buy position is opened when UpSignal triggers, and a sell position is opened when DnSignal triggers, while opposing positions are closed when the trend persists. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit in points, as well as signal-based closing when the trend reverses. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the deposit per deal. Money management uses a single-trade risk control model but lacks dynamic adjustment or trailing stop mechanisms. The strategy employs the IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are calculated only once per bar, preventing repeated trades, and uses a Recount flag to recalculate when data is insufficient.

优势

  • 使用IsNewBar机制防止同一K线内重复交易，逻辑严谨
  • 买入和卖出的开仓与平仓权限可独立控制，灵活性高
  • 有固定止损和止盈设置，风险可控
  • 使用自定义指标的多缓冲区设计，区分趋势信号和反转信号

劣势

  • 止损和止盈为固定点数，未考虑市场波动率（如ATR），适应性差
  • 没有移动止损（Trailing Stop）机制，无法锁定浮动利润
  • 依赖外部自定义指标BrakeParb，指标逻辑不透明且无法验证
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生不利成交

改进建议

  • 引入基于ATR的动态止损止盈，替代固定点数设置，提高策略对不同波动环境的适应性
  • 添加移动止损功能以在趋势延续时逐步锁定利润
  • 增加点差过滤和交易时间过滤，避免在不利条件下开仓

Strengths

  • Uses IsNewBar mechanism to prevent duplicate trades within the same bar, ensuring logical rigor
  • Buy and sell open/close permissions can be independently controlled, offering high flexibility
  • Has fixed stop loss and take profit settings for controllable risk
  • Uses multi-buffer design of the custom indicator, distinguishing between trend signals and reversal signals

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss and take profit are fixed in points without considering market volatility (e.g., ATR), resulting in poor adaptability
  • No trailing stop mechanism to lock in floating profits
  • Depends on the external custom BrakeParb indicator whose logic is opaque and unverifiable
  • Lacks spread filtering, which may result in unfavorable fills in high-spread environments

Improvements

  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit to replace fixed point settings, improving adaptability to different volatility environments
  • Add trailing stop functionality to progressively lock in profits as the trend continues
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid opening positions under unfavorable conditions
