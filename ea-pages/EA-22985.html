摘要

Tengri是一个趋势跟踪型智能交易系统，通过技术指标识别并跟随已建立的市场趋势方向进行交易。当趋势条件确认后入场，并使用定义的风险参数管理交易。该EA旨在捕捉多个时间周期上的持续方向性行情。

策略分析

Tengri策略的核心概念是趋势跟踪，通过技术指标判断市场当前趋势方向并在趋势确认后顺势入场。入场条件基于趋势指标的共振信号，当多个指标同时指向同一方向时触发交易信号。出场条件包括固定止损和止盈设置，以及趋势反转信号出现时的动态出场机制。仓位管理采用固定手数或基于账户余额的比例计算方式，确保单笔交易风险可控。资金管理方面，EA通过止损距离和账户风险百分比来计算每笔交易的下单量，避免过度杠杆。整体策略追求在趋势行情中获取大波段利润，以较小的试错成本捕捉大趋势。

Summary

Tengri is a trend-following Expert Advisor that identifies and trades in the direction of established market trends using technical indicators. It enters positions when trend conditions are confirmed and manages trades with defined risk parameters. The EA aims to capture sustained directional moves across multiple timeframes.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of the Tengri strategy is trend following, using technical indicators to determine the current market trend direction and entering positions after trend confirmation. Entry conditions are based on confluence signals from trend indicators, triggering trade signals when multiple indicators align in the same direction. Exit conditions include fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels, as well as dynamic exit mechanisms when trend reversal signals appear. Position sizing employs either fixed lot sizes or proportional calculations based on account balance, ensuring controlled risk per trade. In terms of money management, the EA calculates order size based on stop-loss distance and account risk percentage to avoid excessive leverage. The overall strategy aims to capture large trend moves while keeping trial-and-error costs small, following the classic trend-following philosophy of letting profits run and cutting losses short.

优势

  • 趋势跟踪策略逻辑清晰，在明显趋势行情中表现优异
  • 具有止损和追踪止损机制，能有效控制单笔交易风险
  • 参数可调性强，交易者可根据不同市场环境优化设置
  • 多指标共振确认入场信号，减少虚假信号干扰
  • 支持多时间周期运行，适应不同交易风格

劣势

  • 在震荡盘整行情中容易产生频繁的虚假信号导致连续亏损
  • 趋势跟踪策略天然具有较低胜率，需要较强的心理承受能力
  • 缺少自适应机制，市场状态变化时参数可能不再适用
  • 固定止损止盈未考虑市场波动率的动态变化
  • 缺乏交易时段过滤，可能在流动性不足的时段产生不利成交

改进建议

  • 增加市场状态识别模块，在震荡行情中自动暂停交易或切换策略
  • 引入ATR动态止损止盈，根据市场波动率自适应调整出场水平
  • 添加交易时段过滤和新闻事件过滤，避免低流动性时段和重大数据发布时交易

Strengths

  • Clear trend-following logic that performs well in strong trending markets
  • Stop-loss and trailing stop mechanisms effectively control per-trade risk
  • Highly configurable parameters allow optimization for different market conditions
  • Multi-indicator confluence for entry signals reduces false signal interference
  • Supports multiple timeframes, accommodating different trading styles

Weaknesses

  • Prone to frequent false signals and consecutive losses in ranging/choppy markets
  • Trend-following strategies inherently have low win rates, requiring strong psychological endurance
  • Lacks adaptive mechanisms; parameters may become unsuitable when market regime changes
  • Fixed stop-loss and take-profit do not account for dynamic changes in market volatility
  • No trading session filter, potentially leading to unfavorable fills during low-liquidity periods

Improvements

  • Add market regime detection to automatically pause trading or switch strategies during ranging conditions
  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit for adaptive exit levels based on market volatility
  • Implement trading session and news event filters to avoid trading during low-liquidity periods and major data releases
