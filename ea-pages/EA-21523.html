摘要

该EA实现了一种剥头皮策略，基于短期价格运动信号分别开立买单和卖单。每次交易目标利润较小，采用紧止盈设置，是典型的低时间框架剥头皮交易方式。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念是基于短期价格偏离或动量信号进行剥头皮交易。入场条件可能依赖于价格相对于某个基准（如移动平均线）的偏离程度，当偏离超过阈值时入场，预期价格回归或继续沿动量方向运动。出场条件设置了固定的止盈和止损水平，止盈较小以快速锁定利润。仓位管理使用固定手数或基于资金的简单计算方式，没有复杂的动态仓位调整。资金管理方面缺乏对账户整体风险的统筹考虑，没有最大回撤限制或账户权益保护机制。策略的核心风险在于剥头皮交易对点差和滑点极其敏感，在没有点差过滤的情况下可能导致频繁的虚假信号触发。

Summary

This EA implements a scalping strategy that opens separate buy and sell trades based on fast price movement signals, likely using short-term momentum or price deviation from a mean. It targets small profits per trade with tight take-profit levels, typical of a scalping approach on lower timeframes.

Strategy Analysis

The EA's strategy concept is based on scalping using short-term price deviation or momentum signals. Entry conditions likely rely on the degree of price deviation from a baseline (such as a moving average), entering when deviation exceeds a threshold in expectation of either mean reversion or momentum continuation. Exit conditions use fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, with small take-profit to quickly lock in gains. Position sizing employs fixed lots or a simple account-balance-based calculation without sophisticated dynamic adjustment. Money management lacks holistic account risk consideration, with no maximum drawdown limit or equity protection mechanism. The core risk lies in scalping's extreme sensitivity to spread and slippage, which without a spread filter can lead to frequent false signal triggers and net losses even on nominally winning trades.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单明了，易于理解和调试
  • 独立的买卖信号机制提供了双向交易机会，不局限于单边行情
  • 使用固定止盈止损，每笔交易风险可控且预先确定

劣势

  • 剥头皮策略对点差极为敏感，未设置点差过滤可能导致亏损
  • 缺乏账户级别的资金管理和回撤保护机制
  • 没有新K线检查，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓导致过度交易

改进建议

  • 添加点差过滤机制，当点差超过阈值时禁止开仓
  • 增加新K线检查，避免在同一根K线上重复开仓，并添加最大同时持仓数量限制

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and debug
  • Separate buy/sell signal mechanism provides bidirectional trading opportunities, not limited to one-sided markets
  • Fixed take-profit and stop-loss ensure controllable and pre-determined risk per trade

Weaknesses

  • Scalping strategy is extremely sensitive to spread; lack of spread filter can cause net losses on trades
  • No account-level money management or drawdown protection mechanism
  • No new bar check may cause repeated entries on the same bar, leading to overtrading

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter mechanism to prevent opening positions when spread exceeds a threshold
  • Add a new bar check to avoid repeated entries on the same bar, and implement a maximum concurrent position limit

风险点

  • 剥头皮策略缺少点差过滤，高波动时段点差扩大可能导致频繁止损
  • 未检测新K线，可能短时间内重复开仓造成仓位堆积风险
  • 固定止损止盈未考虑市场波动性变化，低波动时止盈过远高波动时止损过近

Risk Points

  • No new bar detection may cause repeated position stacking in short timeframes, increasing exposure
  • Fixed SL/TP doesn't account for market volatility changes; TP too far in low volatility, SL too close in high volatility
