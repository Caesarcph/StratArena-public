摘要

该EA基于BinaryWave复合指标进行交易，该指标将七个加权技术指标（MA、MACD、OsMA、CCI、动量、RSI、ADX）合并为一个二进制波动信号。支持两种入场模式——零线突破和方向变化——并使用固定止损/止盈与可配置的资金管理。

策略分析

策略概念是将七个经典技术指标的信号归一化为±1后加权求和，形成一个综合动量波形。入场条件有两种模式：突破模式（breakdown）下，当BinaryWave值从零下方穿越到零上方时买入，从零上方穿越到零下方时卖出；转向模式（twist）下，当波形从下降转为上升时买入，从上升转为下降时卖出。出场条件包括固定止损和止盈，以及反向信号平仓（如持多头时出现卖出信号则平多）。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值为固定手数，正值为存款比例。信号经过JJMA等平滑算法过滤以减少噪音，且仅在出现新K线时计算信号以避免重复交易。各指标的权重可独立调节，允许灵活调整策略偏向。

Summary

This EA trades based on the BinaryWave composite indicator, which combines seven weighted technical indicators (MA, MACD, OsMA, CCI, Momentum, RSI, ADX) into a single binary wave signal. It supports two entry modes—zero-line breakout and direction change—and uses fixed stop-loss/take-profit with configurable money management.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept normalizes seven classic technical indicators into ±1 signals and sums them with configurable weights to form a composite momentum wave. Entry conditions have two modes: in breakdown mode, a buy signal triggers when BinaryWave crosses above zero and a sell signal triggers when it crosses below zero; in twist mode, a buy triggers when the wave changes from declining to rising and a sell triggers when it changes from rising to declining. Exit conditions include fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels, plus counter-signal exits (e.g., closing longs when a sell signal appears). Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter—negative values represent fixed lot sizes while positive values represent a share of the deposit. Signals are filtered through JJMA or other smoothing algorithms to reduce noise, and trading signals are only calculated on new bar formation to prevent duplicate entries. Each indicator's weight can be independently adjusted, allowing flexible tuning of the strategy's directional bias.

优势

  • 多指标综合信号降低了单一指标误判的风险，提高了信号的可靠性
  • 支持两种入场模式（零线突破和方向变化），适应不同市场环境
  • 各指标权重可独立调节，策略灵活性高，可针对不同品种优化
  • 使用JJMA等高级平滑算法过滤噪音，信号质量较好
  • 内置新K线检测机制，避免同根K线内重复交易

劣势

  • 七个指标全部使用默认参数且权重均为1.0，缺乏针对性优化，可能导致信号平庸化
  • 无追踪止损机制，固定止盈止损可能无法适应波动性变化
  • 无点差过滤和交易时间过滤，在流动性差或点差大的时段可能产生不利成交
  • 依赖外部自定义指标BinaryWave和SmoothAlgorithms.mqh库，部署复杂性增加
  • 止损1000点/止盈2000点的默认值过大，对多数品种不合适

改进建议

  • 增加自适应止损机制（如ATR止损）和追踪止损功能，以适应市场波动性变化
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段控制，避免在高点差或低流动性时段交易
  • 引入指标权重的自适应优化或预设方案，针对不同品种提供推荐参数组合

Strengths

  • Multi-indicator composite signal reduces the risk of false signals from any single indicator, improving reliability
  • Two entry modes (zero-line breakout and direction change) adapt to different market conditions
  • Independently adjustable indicator weights provide high strategy flexibility for instrument-specific optimization
  • Advanced smoothing algorithms like JJMA filter noise effectively, producing cleaner signals
  • Built-in new bar detection prevents duplicate trades within the same bar

Weaknesses

  • All seven indicators use default parameters with equal weights of 1.0, lacking targeted optimization and potentially producing mediocre signals
  • No trailing stop mechanism; fixed stop-loss and take-profit cannot adapt to changing volatility
  • No spread filter or trading session filter, which may lead to unfavorable fills during low-liquidity or high-spread periods
  • Depends on external custom indicator BinaryWave and SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library, increasing deployment complexity
  • Default stop-loss of 1000 points and take-profit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments

Improvements

  • Add adaptive stop-loss (e.g., ATR-based) and trailing stop functionality to adjust for changing market volatility
  • Implement spread filtering and trading session controls to avoid trading during high-spread or low-liquidity periods
  • Introduce adaptive weight optimization or preset profiles with recommended parameter combinations for different instruments

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 固定止损止盈参数默认值过大（1000/2000点），若不调整可能导致单笔大额亏损
  • 无点差过滤，在不利交易条件下可能产生滑点损失
  • 无仓位检查和最大持仓限制，在极端情况下可能过度暴露
  • MM参数为负数时表示固定手数，用户可能误解导致仓位过大

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Default fixed stop-loss/take-profit values are very large (1000/2000 points) and may cause significant single-trade losses if not adjusted
  • No spread filter may result in slippage losses under unfavorable trading conditions
  • No position check or maximum exposure limit could lead to overexposure in extreme scenarios
  • Negative MM parameter means fixed lot size, which users may misunderstand leading to oversized positions
