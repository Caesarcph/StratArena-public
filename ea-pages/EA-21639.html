Dematus

Dematus is a trend-following Expert Advisor that identifies market direction using a combination of moving averages and momentum indicators. It enters trades in the direction of the prevailing trend when momentum confirms the move, and manages positions with trailing stops. The EA applies systematic position sizing based on account equity and risk parameters.

Strategy analysis

The EA employs a trend-following strategy whose core concept uses moving average alignment across multiple periods to determine market trend direction, identifying an uptrend when short-term MAs sit above long-term MAs and vice versa. Entry conditions require a clear trend direction confirmed by momentum indicators such as MACD or RSI, preventing entries when trend strength is waning. Exit conditions include a triple mechanism of fixed stop loss, fixed take profit, and trailing stop, where the trailing stop locks in additional profits as the trend extends. Position sizing follows a fixed-risk model based on a percentage of account equity, where the risk amount per trade is a set proportion of total equity. Money management calculates lot size from the stop loss distance and pip value, ensuring maximum loss per trade does not exceed the preset risk percentage. Additionally, the EA incorporates risk controls such as maximum position count limits and same-direction duplicate entry checks to prevent overexposure.

Strengths

  • Multi-confirmation entry with trend direction plus momentum confirmation reduces false signal impact
  • Fixed risk percentage position sizing ensures controllable per-trade risk, preventing account blowup from market volatility
  • Trailing stop mechanism maximizes profits in trending markets while protecting accumulated gains
  • Fully parameterized design allows users to flexibly adjust strategy for different market conditions
  • Includes maximum position limits and duplicate entry checks, effectively preventing overtrading and redundant risk exposure

Weaknesses

  • Trend-following strategy prone to frequent false signals and consecutive losses in ranging/choppy markets
  • Fixed pip stop loss doesn't account for volatility differences across instruments, potentially too tight for volatile pairs
  • Lacks time filter and news event filter, may generate unfavorable trades during low-liquidity sessions or major news events
  • Trailing stop parameters require optimization per instrument; fixed settings may not suit all markets
  • Lacks drawdown control and account-level risk management, cannot automatically reduce exposure during significant account drawdowns

Possible improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss instead of fixed pip stops to adapt to different instruments' volatility
  • Add account-level drawdown control that automatically reduces position size or pauses trading when drawdown exceeds threshold
  • Add time filter and news event filter to avoid opening positions during unfavorable trading sessions

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Lack of ATR dynamic stop loss may result in stops being too small for high-volatility instruments
  • No account-level drawdown control means potential losses beyond expectations during extreme market conditions
  • No trading session filter may lead to increased slippage from wider spreads during low-liquidity periods
