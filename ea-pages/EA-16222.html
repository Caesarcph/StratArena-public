Exp_RVIDiff

This EA trades based on the RVIDiff custom indicator, which applies additional smoothing (T3 by default) to the Relative Vigor Index. It opens buy positions when the RVI value rises above its prior value during a downtrend in the RVI, and opens sell positions when the RVI falls below its prior value during an uptrend, with configurable stop-loss and take-profit levels.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on momentum reversal signals from the RVIDiff indicator, which smooths the standard RVI with an additional averaging method (T3 by default). Entry conditions: a buy signal is triggered when RVI[1] < RVI[2] (downtrend) and RVI[0] >= RVI[1] (momentum turning upward); a sell signal is triggered when RVI[1] > RVI[2] (uptrend) and RVI[0] <= RVI[1] (momentum turning downward). Exit conditions include a fixed 1000-point stop-loss, 2000-point take-profit, and closing positions on opposing signals. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter with multiple margin modes including balance-based, free margin-based, and fixed lot sizing. Money management leverages the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library for standardized position calculation. The strategy operates on H6 timeframe by default, using SignalBar=1 to reference the previous bar's values, avoiding look-ahead bias.

Strengths

  • Uses SignalBar=1 to avoid look-ahead bias, making backtesting results more reliable
  • Offers multiple smoothing algorithms (10 methods), providing high strategy flexibility
  • Includes complete stop-loss, take-profit mechanism and slippage control
  • Buy/sell open and close switches are independently configurable, offering granular control
  • Leverages standardized TradeAlgorithms library for position sizing, ensuring good code reuse

Weaknesses

  • Lacks spread filtering, which may cause unnecessary losing entries during high-spread periods
  • No position check logic, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Fixed 1000-point SL and 2000-point TP do not adapt to market volatility changes
  • Depends on iCustom calling external RVIDiff indicator; EA fails if the indicator file is missing
  • No time filter or trading session control, may trade during low-liquidity periods

Possible improvements

  • Add a spread filter to pause trading when spread exceeds a threshold, avoiding unfavorable entries
  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate same-direction entries that increase leverage exposure
  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit that automatically adjusts to market volatility

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No spread filter; widened spreads during high-volatility periods may cause unfavorable entries
  • No position check; duplicate entries may cause risk exposure to exceed expectations
  • Fixed SL/TP does not account for market volatility; SL too wide in low volatility, TP insufficient in high volatility
