摘要

Disaster是一个基于马丁格尔策略的EA，每次亏损后将仓位翻倍，试图通过一次盈利来弥补所有损失。它基于简单的方向信号入场，依赖马丁格尔加仓而非技术分析来获利。该EA在连续亏损期间存在爆仓的极端风险。

策略分析

策略概念基于经典的马丁格尔加倍法：初始下注亏损后，下一笔交易将手数翻倍，期望一次盈利覆盖所有累计亏损并获利。入场条件通常依赖简单的方向判断信号，如单一指标或固定逻辑，缺乏复杂的多条件过滤。出场条件为固定止盈目标，当累计利润达到设定值时平仓，但止损机制薄弱或缺失。仓位管理采用指数级递增方式，每亏损一次手数乘以2或设定倍数，这在连续亏损时会以几何级数膨胀仓位。资金管理方面没有最大持仓限制或回撤保护，账户资金在不利行情下极易被迅速消耗。整体策略逻辑简单粗暴，完全依赖概率回归假设，忽视了市场趋势延续性和资金有限性的现实约束。

Summary

Disaster is a martingale-based EA that doubles lot size after each losing trade, aiming to recover losses with a single winning trade. It enters positions based on simple directional signals and relies on the martingale progression rather than technical analysis for profitability. The EA carries extreme risk of account blow-up during extended losing streaks.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy is based on the classic martingale doubling principle: after an initial bet loss, the next trade doubles the lot size, expecting a single win to cover all cumulative losses and produce profit. Entry conditions typically rely on simple directional signals such as a single indicator or fixed logic, lacking complex multi-condition filtering. Exit conditions use a fixed take-profit target that closes positions when cumulative profit reaches a set value, but stop-loss mechanisms are weak or absent. Position sizing follows exponential progression, multiplying lot size by 2 or a set multiplier after each loss, which causes positions to balloon geometrically during losing streaks. Money management lacks maximum position limits or drawdown protection, making account capital extremely vulnerable to rapid depletion under adverse market conditions. The overall strategy logic is simplistic and crude, relying entirely on the probability reversion assumption while ignoring the reality of trending markets and finite capital.

优势

  • 代码结构简单，逻辑清晰易读
  • 马丁格尔在短期震荡市中可能表现良好
  • 参数可调，用户可以自定义倍数和初始手数

劣势

  • 马丁格尔策略在连续亏损时存在爆仓风险
  • 缺少有效止损机制，亏损可能无限扩大
  • 没有账户资金检查和最大回撤保护
  • 入场信号过于简单，缺乏技术分析支撑
  • 仓位指数增长对保证金要求极高

改进建议

  • 增加最大回撤保护和账户资金检查机制
  • 加入有效止损和最大持仓数限制
  • 改进入场信号，加入多指标确认过滤

Strengths

  • Simple and clean code structure that is easy to read and understand
  • Martingale approach may perform well in short-term ranging markets
  • Configurable parameters allow users to customize multiplier and initial lot size

Weaknesses

  • Martingale strategy carries catastrophic account blow-up risk during consecutive losses
  • Lacks effective stop-loss mechanism, allowing losses to grow indefinitely
  • No account equity check or maximum drawdown protection
  • Entry signals are overly simplistic without solid technical analysis support
  • Exponential position growth demands extremely high margin requirements

Improvements

  • Add maximum drawdown protection and account equity check mechanism
  • Implement effective stop-loss and maximum position count limits
  • Improve entry signals with multi-indicator confirmation filtering
