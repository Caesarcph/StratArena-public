摘要

Disaster是一个基于马丁格尔策略的智能交易系统，每次亏损后将手数翻倍，试图用一次盈利来回收所有损失。它基于简单的方向信号开仓，依赖马丁格尔加仓而非技术分析来获利。由于指数级增仓，该EA存在爆仓的极高风险。

策略分析

该EA采用马丁格尔策略核心概念，即在每次亏损后将交易手数翻倍，期望一次盈利覆盖所有先前损失。入场条件通常基于简单的价格方向判断或基础指标信号，没有复杂的技术分析过滤。出场条件依赖整体持仓盈利目标，当累计利润达到设定阈值时全部平仓。仓位管理采用经典的马丁格尔倍增法，初始手数较小但随连续亏损呈指数增长。资金管理方面几乎没有保护机制，不会设置止损，也不会限制最大加仓次数，在极端行情中极易导致账户资金全部损失。该策略在震荡市中可能短期盈利，但在单边趋势中面临毁灭性风险。

Summary

Disaster is a Martingale-based Expert Advisor that doubles the lot size after each losing trade, aiming to recover losses with a single winning trade. It opens positions based on a simple directional signal and relies on the Martingale progression rather than technical analysis for profitability. The EA carries significant risk of account blow-up due to its exponential position sizing.

Strategy Analysis

The EA employs the classic Martingale concept, doubling the lot size after each consecutive loss in hopes that a single win recovers all prior losses. Entry conditions are typically based on simple directional price signals or basic indicator readings without sophisticated technical analysis filters. Exit conditions rely on an overall profit target across all positions, closing everything when cumulative profit reaches a predefined threshold. Position sizing follows the pure Martingale progression where the initial lot is small but grows exponentially with each consecutive loss. Money management is essentially non-existent as there is no stop-loss and no maximum martingale step limit, making account blow-up highly probable during trending markets. The strategy may show short-term profits in ranging markets but faces catastrophic risk during sustained directional moves.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中可以持续获利，短期回撤可控
  • 逻辑简单直观，参数少易于理解和配置
  • 理论上只要资金无限大终将回本

劣势

  • 手数指数增长极易导致爆仓，风险极高
  • 没有止损保护，极端行情中损失不可控
  • 对资金量要求极高，小账户几乎必然失败
  • 长时间连续亏损时心理压力极大

改进建议

  • 增加最大马丁格尔步骤限制，防止无限加仓
  • 加入止损和资金管理模块，控制单次最大亏损比例
  • 添加趋势过滤指标，避免在强单边行情中逆势加仓

Strengths

  • Can generate consistent profits in ranging markets with controlled short-term drawdown
  • Simple and intuitive logic with few parameters making it easy to understand and configure
  • Theoretically guarantees recovery with unlimited capital

Weaknesses

  • Exponential lot growth can easily blow the account with extreme risk of total loss
  • No stop-loss protection means losses are unbounded during extreme market moves
  • Requires extremely large capital and small accounts are almost guaranteed to fail
  • Enormous psychological pressure during prolonged losing streaks

Improvements

  • Add a maximum Martingale step limit to prevent infinite position scaling
  • Implement stop-loss and money management module to cap maximum loss per cycle as a percentage of equity
  • Add trend filtering indicators to avoid adding positions against strong directional moves
