摘要

该EA基于布林带均值回归信号并结合DEMA趋势方向确认，可同时交易多达6个货币对。在上升趋势中价格刺破布林带下轨后反转向上时买入，在下降趋势中价格刺破布林带上轨后反转向下时卖出。包含权益止盈、移动止损、优化数据挖掘及远程服务器验证功能。

策略分析

策略概念是基于布林带的均值回归交易，当价格触及布林带上下轨后回归均值时入场，同时利用DEMA（双指数移动平均线）判断大周期趋势方向作为过滤条件。入场条件为：买入信号需满足前一根K线开盘价低于下轨且收盘价高于下轨（穿回带内）、前一根低点高于再前一根低点（更高低点）、更前一根低点低于再前一根低点（更低低点，即双重底形态），且DEMA呈上升趋势；卖出信号为对称逻辑。出场条件包括固定止盈止损、权益止盈（当权益超过平衡的指定倍数时平掉所有仓位）、移动止损以及信号反向平仓。仓位管理支持三种模式：按风险百分比计算手数、固定手数、按权益与除数比例计算手数。资金管理方面，风险可控但存在不足，如未设置最小止损距离、无点差过滤、且依赖远程服务器验证的代码已过时。

Summary

This EA trades Bollinger Band mean-reversion signals confirmed by DEMA trend direction across up to 6 currency pairs simultaneously. It enters buy when price pierces below the lower Bollinger Band and reverses upward in an uptrend, and sells when price pierces above the upper band and reverses downward in a downtrend. It includes equity take-profit, trailing stop-loss, optimization data mining, and a remote server verification system.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is Bollinger Band mean reversion, entering when price pierces outside the bands and reverses back, with DEMA trend direction on a higher timeframe serving as a trend filter. Buy entry requires the previous bar to open below the lower band and close above it (piercing back inside), a higher low on the previous bar versus the one before, and a lower low two bars back (double bottom pattern), all confirmed by an ascending DEMA; sell signals follow the symmetrical logic. Exit conditions include fixed take-profit and stop-loss in pips, an equity take-profit that closes all positions when equity exceeds a multiple of balance, a trailing stop-loss step mechanism, and optional signal-based position closing. Position sizing supports three modes: risk-percentage-based lot calculation, fixed lot size, or equity-divided lot sizing. Money management has gaps including no minimum stop-loss distance, no spread filter, no maximum position limit per symbol, and the remote server verification code is obsolete and potentially insecure.

优势

  • 多货币对同时交易能力，最多支持6个货币对，分散风险
  • 结合布林带均值回归与DEMA趋势过滤，避免逆势交易
  • 多种仓位管理模式（风险百分比、固定手数、权益比例），灵活适应不同需求
  • 包含权益止盈机制，防止浮盈回吐
  • 支持优化数据挖掘和远程结果上传，便于策略回测分析

劣势

  • 每次BarLogic调用都重新创建指标句柄（iBands、iDEMA），造成严重资源泄漏和性能问题
  • 使用已废弃的MQL4函数如MarketInfo()、StrToDouble()、DoubleToStr()，兼容性差
  • 依赖远程服务器验证（coensio.com已失效），EA可能无法启动
  • 缺少点差过滤、最大持仓限制和滑点保护，在剧烈行情下风险较大
  • 硬编码的URL和访问码存在安全隐患，且版权保护逻辑有误（判断用户名为coensio时反而拒绝）

改进建议

  • 将指标句柄创建移至Init()函数中，BarLogic()仅负责CopyBuffer读取数据，避免资源泄漏
  • 移除远程服务器验证和wininet.dll依赖，替换为本地许可证验证机制
  • 添加点差过滤、最大持仓数量限制和新闻事件时间过滤，提高策略稳健性
  • 将所有废弃的MQL4函数替换为MQL5标准函数，修复EACheck中的逻辑反转错误

Strengths

  • Multi-currency simultaneous trading capability supporting up to 6 pairs for risk diversification
  • Combines Bollinger Band mean reversion with DEMA trend filter to avoid counter-trend trades
  • Multiple position sizing modes (risk percentage, fixed lot, equity ratio) for flexible risk management
  • Equity take-profit mechanism prevents floating profit drawdowns
  • Built-in optimization data mining and remote result uploading for systematic backtesting analysis

Weaknesses

  • Indicator handles (iBands, iDEMA) are recreated on every BarLogic call causing severe resource leaks and performance degradation
  • Uses deprecated MQL4 functions like MarketInfo(), StrToDouble(), DoubleToStr() resulting in poor compatibility
  • Depends on remote server verification (coensio.com is defunct) which may prevent the EA from starting
  • Missing spread filter, maximum position limits, and slippage protection exposing risk during volatile markets
  • Hardcoded URLs and access codes pose security concerns, and the copyright check logic is inverted (rejects when username equals 'coensio')

Improvements

  • Move indicator handle creation to Init() and only call CopyBuffer in BarLogic() to eliminate resource leaks
  • Remove remote server verification and wininet.dll dependency, replace with local license validation
  • Add spread filter, maximum open position limits, and news event time filtering to improve robustness
  • Replace all deprecated MQL4 functions with MQL5 equivalents and fix the inverted logic in EACheck()
