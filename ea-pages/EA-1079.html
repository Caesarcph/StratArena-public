摘要

这是一个三均线交叉EA，当快速MA穿越中速MA时开仓，同时要求快速和中速MA均在慢速MA之上（买入）或之下（卖出）。包含移动止损、固定止盈止损以及连续亏损后缩减仓位的功能。该策略是经典的三均线趋势跟踪系统。

策略分析

策略概念基于三条简单移动平均线（SMA）的排列与交叉来识别趋势方向。入场条件：买入信号要求快速MA向上穿越中速MA，且当前和前一根K线的快速MA及中速MA均高于慢速MA；卖出信号则相反，要求快速MA向下穿越中速MA，且快速和中速MA均低于慢速MA。出场条件包括固定点数的止盈（50点）和止损（1100点），以及可配置的移动止损（100点）。仓位管理方面，当Lots参数为0时，根据账户可用保证金和风险比例计算仓位，否则使用固定手数。资金管理包含DecreaseFactor参数，在连续亏损后逐步缩减仓位大小，以控制回撤风险。移动止损功能会在价格有利方向移动时跟进止损，但受限于经纪商的止损水平限制。

Summary

A triple moving average crossover EA that trades on the fast MA crossing the middle MA, confirmed by both fast and middle MAs being above/below the slow MA. It includes a trailing stop, fixed take profit/stop loss, and a lot reduction mechanism after consecutive losses. The strategy is a classic trend-following system using three SMAs with configurable periods.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the alignment and crossover of three Simple Moving Averages (SMA) to identify trend direction. Entry conditions: a buy signal requires the fast MA to cross above the middle MA, with both the current and previous bar's fast and middle MAs above the slow MA; sell signals are the mirror opposite requiring the fast MA to cross below the middle MA with all fast and middle MA values below the slow MA. Exit conditions include a fixed take profit (50 points), a fixed stop loss (1100 points), and a configurable trailing stop (100 points). Position sizing uses a risk-based calculation from free margin when Lots is set to 0, or a fixed lot size otherwise. Money management incorporates a DecreaseFactor that progressively reduces lot size after consecutive losing trades to control drawdown. The trailing stop follows price movement in the favorable direction but is constrained by the broker's stop level requirements.

优势

  • 三重MA确认机制减少了假突破信号，入场条件较为严格
  • 包含连续亏损后自动缩减仓位的资金管理功能，有助于控制回撤
  • 移动止损功能可以锁定盈利，同时考虑了经纪商止损水平限制
  • 止损远大于止盈（1100 vs 50点），表明策略追求大趋势中的高胜率交易

劣势

  • 止盈止损比极不合理（1:22），单次亏损需要22次盈利才能弥补，严重依赖高胜率
  • DecreaseFactor默认为0即禁用，多数用户可能不会启用该风控功能
  • 仅支持单品种单持仓，无法在多个时间框架或品种上同时运行
  • 所有MA均使用SMA且偏移量相同（6），缺乏对不同MA类型的灵活配置
  • 没有交易时间过滤、点差过滤等保护机制，可能在不利时段开仓

改进建议

  • 调整止盈止损比例至更合理的范围（如1:2或1:3），或引入动态止盈机制跟随趋势延长盈利
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免在高点差或低流动性时段交易
  • 添加魔法号（MagicNumber）以区分不同图表上的EA实例，支持多品种运行

Strengths

  • Triple MA confirmation mechanism reduces false breakout signals with strict entry conditions
  • Built-in lot reduction after consecutive losses helps control drawdown risk
  • Trailing stop locks in profits while respecting broker stop level constraints
  • Large stop loss relative to take profit (1100 vs 50 points) indicates pursuit of high win-rate trades in strong trends

Weaknesses

  • Extremely unfavorable risk-reward ratio (1:22), requiring 22 winning trades to offset one loss, heavily dependent on high win rate
  • DecreaseFactor defaults to 0 (disabled), so most users may not enable this risk management feature
  • Only supports single symbol and single position, cannot run across multiple timeframes or instruments simultaneously
  • All MAs use SMA with identical shift values (6), lacking flexibility for different MA types
  • No trading session filter, spread filter, or other protective mechanisms, may open positions during unfavorable conditions

Improvements

  • Adjust risk-reward ratio to a more reasonable range (e.g., 1:2 or 1:3) or implement dynamic take profit to ride trends longer
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid trading during high spread or low liquidity periods
  • Add MagicNumber to distinguish EA instances on different charts, enabling multi-symbol operation

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 止盈止损比极差（50:1100点），单次亏损可能抵消大量盈利
  • 缺少点差过滤，高波动时段点差扩大可能导致不必要的入场
  • MA偏移量（Shift=6）使得信号滞后，可能在趋势末期才入场
  • 没有检查当前是否已有挂单或其他EA的持仓，可能导致意外叠加仓位

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Extremely poor risk-reward ratio (50:1100 points), a single loss can wipe out many winning trades
  • No spread filter, widening spreads during volatile periods may lead to unfavorable entries
  • MA shift of 6 causes signal lag, potentially entering late in the trend cycle
  • No check for existing pending orders or positions from other EAs, risking unintended position stacking
