摘要

该EA使用三重移动平均线交叉系统（快线、中线、慢线）识别趋势方向。当快MA向上穿越中MA且两者均在慢MA之上时开多仓，反向条件开空仓。包含追踪止损、基于风险比例的仓位计算以及连亏后减仓机制。

策略分析

策略概念基于三重SMA交叉的趋势跟踪系统，通过快线（24周期）、中线（60周期）和慢线（120周期）的相对位置判断趋势。入场条件：做多需快线向上穿越中线，同时快线和中线均持续位于慢线之上（当前和前一根K线均需满足）；做空为镜像条件。出场条件包括固定止盈（50点）、固定止损（1100点）以及追踪止损（100点）。仓位管理支持固定手数和基于自由保证金与风险比例的动态手数两种模式。资金管理方面，DecreaseFactor参数实现了连亏后逐步减仓的保守机制，通过对历史成交记录的回溯统计连续亏损次数来调整下一笔交易的手数。

Summary

This EA uses a triple moving average crossover system with three SMA lines (fast, middle, slow) to identify trend direction. It opens buy positions when the fast MA crosses above the middle MA while both are above the slow MA, and sells on the inverse condition. It includes a trailing stop, lot sizing based on risk percentage, and a lot reduction mechanism after consecutive losses.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy is a triple SMA crossover trend-following system that determines trend direction via the relative positions of fast (24-period), middle (60-period), and slow (120-period) moving averages. Entry conditions for long: the fast MA must cross above the middle MA while both the fast and middle MAs remain consistently above the slow MA on both the current and previous bars. Short entry is the exact mirror condition. Exit conditions include a fixed take profit (50 points), a fixed stop loss (1100 points), and a trailing stop (100 points) that only moves the stop in the profitable direction. Position sizing supports both fixed lots and dynamic lots calculated from free margin multiplied by the risk percentage divided by 1000. The DecreaseFactor parameter implements a conservative lot reduction mechanism after consecutive losing trades by iterating through deal history to count sequential losses and reducing the next trade size proportionally.

优势

  • 三重MA交叉提供多层过滤，减少虚假信号，入场条件相对严谨
  • 连亏减仓机制（DecreaseFactor）有助于控制回撤期间的资金损失
  • 追踪止损功能可以锁定浮动利润，同时考虑了StopsLevel限制避免被拒单
  • 使用标准库（CTrade/CSymbolInfo等）规范了交易操作和品种信息获取

劣势

  • 止损（1100点）远大于止盈（50点），盈亏比严重失衡约为1:22，需要极高胜率才能盈利
  • 新K线检测逻辑（LastTime）在OnTick末尾赋值，但追踪止损在NewBar判断之外每tick执行，逻辑不一致
  • 缺乏点差过滤，在小TP（50点）策略中点差波动可能显著侵蚀利润甚至导致亏损
  • 仅支持单品种单仓位，无法在多品种上同时运行，灵活性有限

改进建议

  • 调整止盈止损比例，建议将盈亏比改善至至少1:2以上，或引入动态ATR止损替代固定点数止损
  • 添加点差过滤机制，当点差超过阈值时暂停开仓，避免在高点差环境下交易

Strengths

  • Triple MA crossover provides multi-layer filtering to reduce false signals with relatively strict entry conditions
  • The lot reduction mechanism after consecutive losses (DecreaseFactor) helps control capital drawdown during losing streaks
  • Trailing stop function locks in floating profits while respecting the broker's StopsLevel to avoid order rejection
  • Uses standard MQL5 trade libraries (CTrade, CSymbolInfo, etc.) for proper trade operations and symbol info handling

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss (1100 points) vastly exceeds take profit (50 points), creating an unfavorable risk-reward ratio of about 1:22 that requires extremely high win rates to be profitable
  • New bar detection (LastTime) is assigned at the end of OnTick but trailing stop runs every tick outside the NewBar check, creating inconsistent logic flow
  • No spread filter is present; with a small TP of 50 points, spread fluctuations can significantly erode profits or even cause losses
  • Only supports single-symbol single-position trading, limiting flexibility for multi-instrument deployment

Improvements

  • Adjust the take-profit to stop-loss ratio to achieve at least a 1:2 risk-reward ratio, or replace fixed-point stops with ATR-based dynamic stops
  • Add a spread filter that pauses trade entry when the spread exceeds a configurable threshold, avoiding trading in high-spread conditions

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 盈亏比极度失衡（止盈50点 vs 止损1100点），单次亏损可抵消大量盈利交易
  • 固定点数止盈止损未考虑市场波动率变化，在低波动率时止损过大、高波动率时止盈过小
  • 动态手数计算公式（自由保证金×风险/1000）缺乏与ATR或品种特性的关联，可能在不同品种上产生过大或过小的仓位

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Extremely unbalanced risk-reward ratio (TP 50 points vs SL 1100 points), a single loss can wipe out many winning trades
  • Fixed point-based TP/SL does not adapt to market volatility changes; SL may be too large in low-volatility conditions and TP too small in high-volatility conditions
  • Dynamic lot formula (free margin × risk / 1000) lacks correlation with ATR or instrument characteristics, potentially producing oversized or undersized positions on different symbols
