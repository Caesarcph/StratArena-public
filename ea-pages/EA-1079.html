摘要

该EA是一个三重均线交叉趋势跟踪系统。使用三条SMA（快线24、中线60、慢线120），当快线上穿中线且快线和中线均在慢线之上时做多，反之做空。仓位通过固定止损、止盈和可选移动止损进行管理。

策略分析

策略概念基于三重均线交叉趋势跟踪，通过快中慢三条SMA的相对位置关系判断趋势方向。入场条件：做多时要求快线SMA上穿中线SMA，且快线和中线在当前和前一根K线均高于慢线，确保强势趋势环境；做空条件为镜像对称。出场条件包括固定止损1100点、止盈50点，以及可选的100点移动止损，移动止损仅在盈利超过开仓价后激活并只向有利方向移动。仓位管理支持固定手数或基于账户自由保证金和风险比例的动态计算，并通过DecreaseFactor参数实现连亏后的减仓机制，连续亏损越多手数越小。资金管理方面，风险敞口由MaximumRisk参数控制在5%，但止损1100点与止盈50点的盈亏比仅为1:0.045，需要极高的胜率才能盈利。

Summary

This EA is a triple moving average crossover trend-following system. It uses three SMAs (fast 24, middle 60, slow 120) to generate buy signals when the fast MA crosses above the middle MA while both fast and middle MAs remain above the slow MA, and vice versa for sell signals. Positions are managed with fixed stop loss, take profit, and an optional trailing stop.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a triple moving average crossover trend-following approach, using the relative positioning of fast, middle, and slow SMAs to determine trend direction. Entry conditions for buy require the fast SMA crossing above the middle SMA, while both fast and middle SMAs must be above the slow SMA on both the current and previous bars, confirming a strong trending environment; sell conditions are the mirror image. Exit conditions include a fixed stop loss of 1100 points, take profit of 50 points, and an optional 100-point trailing stop that only activates after the position is in profit and only moves in the favorable direction. Position sizing supports either fixed lots or dynamic calculation based on account free margin and risk percentage, with a DecreaseFactor mechanism that reduces lot size after consecutive losses. Money management controls exposure via the 5% MaximumRisk parameter, but the risk-reward ratio of 1100:50 (approximately 1:0.045) is extremely unfavorable and would require an exceptionally high win rate to be profitable.

优势

  • 三重均线过滤机制有效减少震荡行情中的虚假信号
  • 移动止损功能保护已有利润，止损只向有利方向移动
  • 连亏减仓机制（DecreaseFactor）有助于控制回撤期风险
  • 代码结构清晰，模块化良好，入场/出场/仓位管理分离
  • 使用MQL5标准交易类库（CTrade等），代码规范

劣势

  • 盈亏比极差（止损1100点对止盈50点，约1:0.045），需要超过95%胜率才能盈利
  • 所有均线偏移量均为6，造成信号严重滞后，可能错过最佳入场时机
  • 缺少震荡行情过滤器，在横盘市场中会产生大量亏损信号
  • 没有点差过滤，在宽点差环境下频繁交易会侵蚀利润
  • DecreaseFactor为0时默认禁用，且连亏减仓逻辑在止损远大于止盈的情况下效果有限

改进建议

  • 调整盈亏比，建议将止盈提高至至少止损的1/3以上或缩小止损范围
  • 增加ADX或波动率过滤器以避免在横盘震荡行情中开仓
  • 增加点差过滤，当点差过大时暂停交易
  • 考虑将均线偏移量减小或设为0以减少信号滞后
  • 增加多时间框架确认，例如更高周期趋势方向一致才允许入场

Strengths

  • Triple MA filtering mechanism effectively reduces false signals in ranging markets
  • Trailing stop protects profits and only moves in the favorable direction
  • DecreaseFactor lot reduction mechanism helps control drawdown during losing streaks
  • Clean modular code structure with separated entry/exit/position management logic
  • Uses standard MQL5 trade libraries (CTrade etc.) following best practices

Weaknesses

  • Extremely poor risk-reward ratio (SL 1100 vs TP 50, approximately 1:0.045) requiring over 95% win rate to be profitable
  • All MA shifts set to 6, causing significant signal lag and potentially missing optimal entry timing
  • No ranging market filter, generating numerous losing signals in sideways markets
  • No spread filter, frequent trading in wide spread environments erodes profits
  • DecreaseFactor defaults to 0 (disabled), and the lot reduction logic is limited given the disproportionate SL/TP sizes

Improvements

  • Adjust risk-reward ratio by increasing take profit to at least 1/3 of stop loss or reducing stop loss range
  • Add ADX or volatility filter to avoid opening positions in sideways/ranging markets
  • Add spread filter to pause trading when spreads are excessively wide
  • Consider reducing or zeroing MA shift values to minimize signal lag
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation, only entering when higher timeframe trend aligns
