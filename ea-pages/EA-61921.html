Strategy analysis

This EA employs a multi-strategy combination approach, integrating trend-following, momentum, and breakout strategies into a unified trading system. Entry conditions are based on confluence confirmation from multiple technical indicators, including moving average crossovers, RSI overbought/oversold levels, and MACD signal line crossovers. Exit conditions combine fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, trailing stops, and indicator reversal signals, providing multiple exit mechanisms. Position sizing uses either fixed lot sizes or risk-based calculation as a percentage of account balance, with support for single-strategy and multi-strategy position allocation. In terms of money management, the EA sets maximum position limits and maximum drawdown protection, but the coordination mechanism between strategies is insufficient, which may lead to overleveraging when multiple strategies trigger simultaneously.

Strengths

  • Multi-strategy combination improves adaptability across different market conditions; when one strategy fails, others can still profit
  • Individual strategies can be independently enabled or disabled, providing high flexibility for backtesting and optimization
  • Includes trailing stop and maximum position limits, providing a basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Lacks position coordination mechanism when multiple strategies trigger simultaneously, potentially leading to overly concentrated risk exposure
  • Missing spread filter and new bar check, which may generate duplicate signals during fast markets or high volatility periods
  • Strategy weight allocation and dynamic adjustment mechanism is not intelligent enough to automatically adjust weights based on recent performance

Possible improvements

  • Add an inter-strategy position coordinator that dynamically adjusts the total position cap when multiple strategies trigger simultaneously
  • Add new bar check and spread filter to prevent duplicate entries on the same bar and entries during excessive spread conditions
  • Implement dynamic strategy weight adjustment that automatically increases or decreases each strategy's weight based on recent profit/loss performance

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Positions may stack too large when multiple strategies trigger simultaneously, lacking total exposure control
  • Missing new bar check may cause duplicate entries on the same bar, increasing slippage and spread costs
