摘要

该EA利用FORTS（俄罗斯衍生品）市场上出现的负价差条件进行套利交易。当检测到买价高于卖价（负价差）时，放置卖出限价单开仓，待价差恢复正常后买入平仓。策略依赖短暂的市场微观结构失衡来获取近乎无风险的套利利润。

策略分析

策略核心概念是利用市场深度（Order Book）中偶尔出现的负价差异常进行统计套利。入场条件为：当从市场深度获取的最低卖价(ask)低于最高买价(bid)时，即ask-bid<0，判定为负价差，此时以bid价格放置卖出限价单(SELL_LIMIT)开仓。出场条件为：当持仓存在时，以ask价格放置买入限价单(BUY_LIMIT)平仓，等待价差恢复正常后成交获利。仓位管理采用固定手数(Lots)方式，无动态调仓机制。资金管理方面，设置了固定的止损和止盈点数(SlPips/TpPips)，但止损止盈的计算方式在两个版本中不一致——FORTS版本直接用点数加减价格，而普通版本则用点数乘以跳动大小。此外，EA在等待订单成交时采用忙等待循环（while循环），这可能导致CPU占用过高或陷入死循环。

Summary

This EA exploits negative spread conditions on the FORTS (Russian derivatives) market by placing limit orders when the bid exceeds the ask. It opens a SELL when a negative spread is detected, then closes the position by buying back when the spread normalizes. The strategy relies on fleeting order book imbalances to capture risk-free arbitrage profits.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of this strategy is statistical arbitrage exploiting occasional negative spread anomalies in the market depth (Order Book). The entry condition is: when the lowest ask price retrieved from the market depth is lower than the highest bid price, i.e., ask-bid<0, a negative spread is detected, and a SELL_LIMIT order is placed at the bid price to open a position. The exit condition is: when a position exists, a BUY_LIMIT order is placed at the ask price to close the position, profiting when the spread normalizes. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (Lots) with no dynamic adjustment mechanism. For money management, fixed stop loss and take profit levels are set (SlPips/TpPips), but the calculation method is inconsistent between the two versions — the FORTS version directly adds/subtracts pips to the price, while the standard version multiplies pips by tick size. Additionally, the EA uses busy-wait loops (while loops) while waiting for order execution, which can lead to excessive CPU usage or infinite loops.

优势

  • 利用负价差套利，理论上属于低风险策略，因为买卖价差本身提供了利润空间
  • 直接读取市场深度数据，响应速度快，能捕捉瞬时的微观结构失衡
  • FORTS版本使用挂单而非市价单，可以更精确地控制入场价格

劣势

  • 忙等待循环（while循环等待成交）可能导致CPU满载甚至死循环，严重影响系统稳定性
  • 负价差出现频率极低，且持续时间极短，实际交易中可能几乎无法触发
  • FORTS版本中止损止盈计算直接加减点数而非乘以跳动大小，在非点数对齐的品种上会导致价格无效
  • 缺乏最大持仓数量限制和资金比例控制，单笔风险敞口不可控
  • 两个版本代码逻辑不一致，FORTS版本缺少对BUY_LIMIT订单的止盈设置

改进建议

  • 将忙等待循环替换为事件驱动机制（如OnTradeTransaction回调），避免CPU空转和潜在死循环
  • 统一止损止盈计算方式，使用点数乘以跳动大小（tick size）来确保价格有效性
  • 增加最大持仓数限制、单日最大亏损限制等风险控制参数
  • 添加价差过滤器，避免在正常正价差时频繁检查和发送无效订单

Strengths

  • Exploits negative spread arbitrage which is theoretically low-risk since the bid-ask spread itself provides the profit margin
  • Reads market depth data directly for fast response, capturing fleeting microstructure imbalances
  • The FORTS version uses limit orders instead of market orders, allowing more precise entry price control

Weaknesses

  • Busy-wait loops (while loops waiting for execution) can cause 100% CPU usage or infinite loops, severely impacting system stability
  • Negative spreads occur very rarely and last for extremely short durations, making actual trade triggers almost nonexistent in practice
  • The FORTS version calculates SL/TP by directly adding/subtracting pips instead of multiplying by tick size, producing invalid prices on non-pip-aligned instruments
  • No maximum position count limit or equity ratio control, leaving single-trade risk exposure unchecked
  • Inconsistent logic between the two versions; the FORTS version lacks take profit setting for BUY_LIMIT close orders

Improvements

  • Replace busy-wait loops with event-driven mechanisms (e.g., OnTradeTransaction callback) to avoid CPU spinning and potential infinite loops
  • Unify SL/TP calculation by using pips multiplied by tick size to ensure price validity across all instruments
  • Add risk control parameters such as maximum position count and daily maximum loss limit
  • Add a spread filter to avoid frequent checks and invalid order submissions during normal positive spread conditions

风险点

  • 忙等待循环可能导致终端卡死或无响应，属于严重的程序设计风险
  • 止损止盈计算方式在FORTS版本中不正确，可能导致止损止盈价格无效而被服务器拒绝

Risk Assessment

Overall:CAUTION

Risk Points

  • Busy-wait loops can cause terminal freezing or unresponsiveness, representing a severe program design risk
  • SL/TP calculation is incorrect in the FORTS version, potentially resulting in invalid prices rejected by the server
  • Insufficient order state checking; OrderSelect may fail if the order has already been filled
  • Negative spread conditions are extremely rare; the EA mostly idle-checks, generating excessive unnecessary OnBookEvent calls
