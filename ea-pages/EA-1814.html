EA-1814 · Trend Following

MA.mq5

A dual moving average crossover EA that uses a fast 3-period LWMA and a slow 57-period EMA to generate trend-following signals. It enters buy positions when both MAs are rising and price closes above the slow MA, and sell positions when both MAs are falling and price closes below the slow MA, with fixed SL/TP and 5-digit broker adjustment.

Strategy analysis

This EA employs a dual moving average trend-following strategy, using the directional consistency of a fast 3-period LWMA and a slow 57-period EMA along with price relative position to identify trends. Buy entry requires four conditions: the slow MA rising for 3 consecutive bars, the fast MA rising for 3 consecutive bars, the close price above the previous slow MA value, and the slow MA above the fast MA. Sell entry mirrors this with declining conditions. Exit relies entirely on fixed stop loss and take profit of 100 points each (auto-adjusted by 10x for 5-digit brokers). Position sizing is a fixed 1.0 lot with no dynamic sizing or money management logic. The strategy only executes on new bars to avoid redundant signals, and it restricts to one position at a time per direction.

Strengths

  • New bar detection prevents redundant trade entries within the same bar, reducing overtrading
  • Automatic 5/3-digit broker adjustment ensures correct SL/TP values across different broker types
  • Clear and concise strategy logic with dual MA directional consistency filter reduces false signals
  • Position check mechanism avoids duplicate entries in the same direction

Weaknesses

  • Fixed 1.0 lot with no money management logic poses excessive risk for large accounts and insufficient capital for small ones
  • 1:1 risk-reward ratio is suboptimal for trend following which typically needs wider profit targets
  • ORDER_FILLING_FOK fill mode may cause order rejections during low liquidity periods
  • No spread filter means entries during high-spread periods may be unfavorable
  • Error handling is incomplete - OrderSend checks retcode but lacks handling for partial failure scenarios

Possible improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account balance and risk percentage instead of fixed lots
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid unfavorable entries during high-spread or inactive periods
  • Consider variable SL/TP or trailing stop to give trend-following trades more room for profit
  • Improve order filling mode by preferring ORDER_FILLING_IOC with a retry mechanism

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Fixed 1.0 lot without money management leads to uncontrolled risk exposure across different account sizes
  • No spread filter means entry prices can significantly deviate from expectations in high-spread environments
  • Single position check without magic number filtering may conflict with other EAs on the same symbol
