Exp_WPR

This EA trades based on the Williams' Percent Range (%R) indicator, entering long positions when %R indicates oversold conditions and short positions when it indicates overbought conditions. It implements a classic momentum mean-reversion strategy using WPR extremes as entry signals with configurable thresholds for buy and sell zones. The EA includes basic risk management with stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy analysis

Exp_WPR is a momentum mean-reversion trading system based on the Williams' Percent Range (%R) oscillator. Entry conditions are: open a long position when the WPR indicator enters the oversold zone (below a configurable buy threshold), and open a short position when WPR enters the overbought zone (above a configurable sell threshold). Exit conditions rely on preset stop loss and take profit levels, and may also close positions when WPR returns to neutral territory. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic position adjustment or money management algorithms. The strategy concept is rooted in mean reversion—expecting price to revert toward the mean when it reaches extreme levels. The EA lacks a new bar check mechanism, which could lead to multiple entries on the same candle, and it has no spread filter, making it vulnerable to slippage and false signals in high-volatility markets.

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic based on the classic WPR indicator, easy to understand and verify
  • Configurable overbought/oversold thresholds allow traders to flexibly adjust based on market conditions
  • Includes stop loss and take profit settings, providing basic risk protection
  • Uses magic number to distinguish orders, supporting concurrent operation with other EAs
  • WPR is a long-validated oscillator indicator that performs well in range-bound markets

Weaknesses

  • Lacks new bar check mechanism, potentially opening multiple positions on the same candle leading to overtrading
  • No spread filter, which may result in unfavorable fills in high-spread market conditions
  • Fixed lot sizing without money management logic cannot dynamically adjust based on account size and risk
  • WPR tends to generate consecutive false signals in trending markets, leading to successive losses
  • No trailing stop or partial close mechanism to lock in floating profits

Possible improvements

  • Add a new bar check to prevent repeated entries on the same candle, reducing overtrading risk
  • Introduce dynamic position sizing based on account balance, automatically calculating lot size according to risk percentage

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Missing new bar check may cause multiple entries on the same candle, increasing overtrading risk
  • No spread filter may result in unfavorable fills in high-spread conditions
  • Fixed lot sizing doesn't adapt to different account sizes, potentially exposing small accounts to excessive risk
