摘要

该EA基于Directed Movement自定义指标进行交易，该指标使用双重平滑的RSI生成买卖信号。当指标的上行线和下行线交叉时开仓，并可选择在信号反转时平掉反向仓位。资金管理支持多种保证金模式，可配置手数大小。

策略分析

策略概念基于Directed Movement指标的双线交叉，该指标对RSI进行两次平滑处理（第一次使用可配置的平滑方法如SMA，第二次默认使用JJMA），生成上行线和下行线。入场条件为：当上行线上穿下行线时做多，当下行线上穿下行线时做空，且仅在前一根K线两线未分离时才触发信号，确保捕捉趋势启动点。出场条件分为两种：一是止损/止盈出场，二是在反向信号出现时平掉对应仓位。仓位管理通过MM参数和MMMode枚举实现，支持固定手数、基于余额百分比、基于可用保证金百分比等多种资金管理模式。资金管理框架来自TradeAlgorithms.mqh，提供了灵活但未内置风险限制的仓位计算方式。策略默认在H4周期运行，使用SignalBar=1即前一根已完成K线的信号，避免未完成K线的虚假信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on the Directed Movement custom indicator, which uses a doubly-smoothed RSI to generate buy and sell signals. It opens positions when the indicator's up and down lines cross, and optionally closes opposite positions on signal reversal. Money management supports multiple margin modes with configurable lot sizing.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the crossover of two lines from the Directed Movement indicator, which applies double smoothing to RSI — the first smoothing uses a configurable method (default SMA) and the second uses JJMA by default, producing an Up line and a Down line. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when the Up line crosses above the Down line, and a sell signal triggers when the Up line crosses below the Down line, with the additional requirement that the previous bar showed no separation between the lines, ensuring entries capture trend initiation points. Exit conditions are twofold: fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit levels, and optional closing of opposite positions when a reversal signal occurs. Position sizing is controlled via the MM parameter and MMMode enumeration, supporting fixed lot size, balance-based percentage, free margin percentage, and other money management modes. The money management framework comes from TradeAlgorithms.mqh, providing flexible but unconstrained position calculation. The strategy defaults to H4 timeframe and uses SignalBar=1 (the last completed bar) to avoid false signals from unfinished bars.

优势

  • 双重平滑RSI减少了虚假信号，提高了信号质量
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，灵活性高
  • 买卖方向均可独立开关，便于单向交易或对冲测试
  • 使用已完成K线信号（SignalBar=1），避免重绘问题
  • 止损止盈参数完善，风险可控

劣势

  • 依赖外部自定义指标Directed_Movement和两个包含文件，缺少独立运行能力
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值偏大，可能不适用于所有品种
  • 没有持仓检查逻辑，可能在同方向重复开仓
  • 缺少点差过滤，在点差较大的品种上可能产生不利入场
  • 代码中注释为乱码（编码问题），可读性差

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，防止同方向重复开仓
  • 增加点差过滤，避免在点差过大时入场
  • 修复源文件编码，使中文注释可读
  • 增加追踪止损功能以保护浮动利润

Strengths

  • Double-smoothed RSI reduces false signals and improves signal quality
  • Supports multiple money management modes for flexibility
  • Buy and sell directions can be independently enabled/disabled for directional or hedging tests
  • Uses completed bar signal (SignalBar=1) to avoid repainting issues
  • Stop loss and take profit parameters are provided for risk control

Weaknesses

  • Depends on external custom indicator Directed_Movement and two include files, lacking standalone capability
  • Default SL 1000 and TP 2000 points are large and may not suit all instruments
  • No position check logic — may open duplicate positions in the same direction
  • No spread filter — unfavorable entries possible on high-spread instruments
  • Comments are garbled due to encoding issues, hurting readability

Improvements

  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Add spread filter to avoid entering when spread is too wide
  • Fix source file encoding to make Chinese comments readable
  • Add trailing stop functionality to protect floating profit
