EA-14314 · Momentum

Exp_ColorBears.mq5

This EA trades based on the ColorBears custom indicator, which uses dual-smoothed bearish momentum to generate buy and sell signals. It opens buy positions when the indicator transitions from bearish to bullish color (0→2) and sell positions when it transitions from bullish to bearish color (2→0), with configurable position closing on signal reversals.

Strategy analysis

The EA's strategy concept is based on the ColorBears indicator's dual-smoothed momentum assessment, using two layers of different smoothing algorithms (the first for the indicator itself, the second for the signal line) to filter market noise. Entry conditions: a buy position opens when indicator buffer 1 at SignalBar equals 0 (no bearish momentum) and buffer 0 equals 2 (bullish signal color); a sell position opens when buffer 1 equals 2 (bearish momentum) and buffer 0 equals 0 (no bullish momentum). Exit conditions rely on signal reversal: when an opposite signal appears, positions close based on BuyPosClose and SellPosClose parameters, with fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit levels. Position sizing supports five modes: fixed lot, balance-based, equity-based, free margin-based, and loss-adjusted balance calculation. Money management is controlled via the MM parameter for risk ratio and MMMode for the specific calculation method, but there is no dynamic adjustment or scale-in/scale-out mechanism.

Strengths

  • Dual smoothing algorithm effectively filters market noise and reduces false signals
  • Supports 10 smoothing methods and 10 price types for high flexibility
  • Buy and sell directions can be independently controlled for both opening and closing
  • Uses IsNewBar() for new bar detection, preventing repeated trades within the same bar
  • Provides 5 money management modes to accommodate different risk preferences

Weaknesses

  • Lacks spread filter, which may trigger false signals frequently in high-spread environments
  • Default SL 1000/TP 2000 points are excessively wide without dynamic ATR-based adjustment
  • No position count check, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • ColorBears is a custom indicator requiring external files, making deployment complex
  • Missing drawdown control and maximum position limits as risk protection mechanisms

Possible improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss/take profit that auto-adjusts based on market volatility
  • Add spread filter and maximum position check to improve stability under adverse market conditions
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in profits during trend continuation

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No position count limit, potentially over-exposing in the same direction with duplicate positions
  • Fixed SL/TP does not adapt to market volatility changes, potentially too wide in low volatility or too narrow in high volatility
  • Depends on custom indicator file; if indicator fails to load, EA cannot run but lacks auto-protection
