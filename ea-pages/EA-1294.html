摘要

该EA基于平滑ADX的DI+和DI-线交叉进行交易。当DI+上穿DI-时开多仓，当DI+下穿DI-时开空仓，入场和出场均可配置权限。仓位管理采用存款比例模型，交易以固定止损止盈管理。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于ADX_Smoothed自定义指标的DI+与DI-线交叉来判断趋势方向。入场条件为：当DI+[1]大于DI-[1]且DI+[0]小于等于DI-[0]时产生买入信号（即DI+从下方穿越DI-），反之DI+从上方穿越DI-时产生卖出信号。出场条件与入场信号互相关联，买入信号同时触发空头平仓，卖出信号同时触发多头平仓。仓位管理采用MM参数控制，负值代表固定手数，正值代表存款比例。资金管理使用固定点数的止损和止盈，分别默认为1000点和2000点。策略仅在新区块出现时计算信号，避免重复交易，同时使用SignalBar参数允许在当前K线上即时交易或等待确认。

Summary

This EA trades based on the crossover of smoothed ADX DI+ and DI- lines. It opens buy positions when DI+ crosses above DI- and sell positions when DI+ crosses below DI-, with configurable permissions for entry and exit. Position sizing uses a deposit share model, and trades are managed with fixed Stop Loss and Take Profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the crossover of DI+ and DI- lines from the custom ADX_Smoothed indicator to determine trend direction. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when DI+[1] is greater than DI-[1] and DI+[0] is less than or equal to DI-[0] (i.e., DI+ crosses above DI- from below), while a sell signal is generated when the opposite crossover occurs. Exit conditions are linked to entry signals — a buy signal simultaneously triggers closing of short positions, and a sell signal triggers closing of long positions. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the deposit. Money management uses fixed Stop Loss and Take Profit in points, defaulting to 1000 and 2000 points respectively. The strategy only recalculates signals on new bars to avoid redundant trades, while the SignalBar parameter allows either immediate trading on the current bar or waiting for bar confirmation.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于经典的DI线交叉趋势跟踪方法
  • 支持独立控制买入卖出的开仓和平仓权限，灵活性好
  • 使用新区块检测机制避免重复信号，减少过度交易
  • 平滑ADX参数可调，允许优化指标灵敏度
  • 止损止盈参数明确，提供基础风险保护

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标ADX_Smoothed，缺少该指标则EA无法运行
  • 止盈止损使用固定点数，未考虑市场波动率变化
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时间过滤，可能在不利条件下开仓
  • 信号逻辑仅在DI+与DI-交叉时触发，震荡行情中可能频繁亏损
  • 缺少持仓检查和重复开仓保护，可能产生多余持仓

改进建议

  • 添加ADX强度阈值过滤，仅在趋势足够强时才开仓，减少震荡市亏损
  • 增加点差过滤和交易时段限制，避免高成本和低流动性时段交易

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on the classic DI line crossover trend-following method
  • Independent control over buy/sell open and close permissions offers good flexibility
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate signals and reduces overtrading
  • Adjustable ADX smoothing parameters allow optimization of indicator sensitivity
  • Explicit Stop Loss and Take Profit parameters provide basic risk protection

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom ADX_Smoothed indicator; EA cannot run without it
  • Fixed point-based Stop Loss and Take Profit do not account for changing market volatility
  • No spread filter or trading session filter; may open positions under unfavorable conditions
  • Signal logic only triggers on DI+/DI- crossovers, likely causing frequent losses in ranging markets
  • Lacks position check and duplicate entry protection, potentially creating redundant positions

Improvements

  • Add an ADX strength threshold filter to only enter trades when the trend is sufficiently strong, reducing losses in ranging markets
  • Add spread filtering and trading session restrictions to avoid high-cost and low-liquidity periods
