Exp_KalmanFilter

This EA trades based on signals from the KalmanFilter custom indicator, which uses a Kalman filter algorithm to estimate price trends. It opens buy positions when the indicator signals an upward trend and sell positions on downward trends, with configurable entry/exit switches and fixed stop-loss/take-profit levels. The strategy supports two signal modes: trend-based and Kalman-based, operating on a user-defined timeframe.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy is based on the Kalman Filter indicator, which uses a recursive optimal estimation algorithm to extract trend signals from noisy price data. Entry conditions: when indicator buffer 1 equals 1 (uptrend) and the previous value was 0, a buy signal is triggered; when the value equals 0 (downtrend) and the previous value was 1, a sell signal is triggered. Exit conditions rely on independent buy/sell close switches that trigger position closure upon trend reversal. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter and MMMode enumeration, supporting five modes: free margin-based, balance-based, loss-free margin, loss-balance, and fixed lot size. For money management, the EA uses fixed stop-loss (default 1000 points) and take-profit (default 2000 points), yielding a 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio with positive expectancy. The strategy also implements a new bar detection mechanism (IsNewBar) to prevent repeated trading on the same bar, and allows users to select the signal bar offset via the SignalBar parameter for backtesting flexibility.

Strengths

  • Kalman filter algorithm provides superior noise filtering and trend tracking compared to simple moving averages
  • Five position sizing modes and independent buy/sell switches offer flexible risk control for traders
  • 2:1 take-profit to stop-loss ratio provides positive expectancy, combined with new bar detection to avoid overtrading
  • Clear signal logic based on state transitions (0→1 or 1→0) reduces false signals in choppy markets

Weaknesses

  • Depends on external custom indicator KalmanFilter; EA fails to run if the indicator file is missing
  • Fixed point-based stop-loss and take-profit do not adapt to market volatility changes, risking premature stops in volatile periods and distant targets in quiet periods
  • No spread filter or trading session filter, potentially leading to unfavorable fills during high-spread periods
  • No trailing stop mechanism to lock in floating profits during trending moves
  • No explicit check for existing positions or duplicate same-direction orders, posing risk of repeated entries

Possible improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit to make risk management adaptive to market volatility
  • Implement spread filter and trading session restrictions to avoid opening positions under unfavorable conditions
  • Add trailing stop functionality to progressively lock in profits during trending markets

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Dependency on external indicator file causes initialization failure but no financial loss
  • Fixed 1000-point stop-loss may be insufficient during extreme market moves, with no slippage protection cap
  • No duplicate same-direction position check may lead to multiple entries when signal persists across bars
