摘要

该EA基于DSS Bressert指标进行交易，该指标是一种双平滑随机指标，用于衡量动量。当平滑随机指标交叉转牛时开多仓，转熊时开空仓，并使用固定的止损和止盈水平。

策略分析

策略概念基于DSS Bressert双平滑随机指标的动量交叉信号。入场条件为：当指标上线上穿下线时产生买入信号，当指标下线上穿上线时产生卖出信号，且要求前一根K线的指标值未交叉而当前K线发生交叉，确认动量反转。出场条件包括两条路径：一是固定的止损和止盈（分别默认为1000点和2000点），二是基于反向交叉信号的主动平仓——当买入信号出现时平空仓，卖出信号出现时平多仓。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值直接指定手数大小，正值表示占用存款比例。资金管理采用固定比例或固定手数模式，未包含动态加仓或马丁格尔逻辑。策略在每个新K线形成时计算信号，避免逐Tick重复交易，同时使用Recount机制确保数据复制失败时重新尝试。

Summary

This EA trades based on the DSS Bressert indicator, a double-smoothed stochastic that measures momentum. It opens buy positions when the smoothed stochastic crossover turns bullish and sell positions when it turns bearish, with fixed stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on momentum crossover signals from the DSS Bressert double-smoothed stochastic indicator. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the upper line crosses above the lower line, and a sell signal when the lower line crosses above the upper line, with the additional requirement that the previous bar did not show a crossover while the current bar does, confirming a momentum reversal. Exit conditions include two paths: fixed stop loss and take profit (default 1000 and 2000 points respectively), and proactive position closing based on reverse crossover signals — closing shorts when a buy signal fires and closing longs when a sell signal fires. Position sizing is controlled via the MM parameter, where negative values directly specify lot size and positive values represent the share of the deposit used per deal. Money management uses either a fixed proportion or fixed lot mode, with no dynamic position scaling or martingale logic included. The strategy calculates signals only on new bar formation to avoid per-tick redundant trading, and employs a Recount mechanism to retry when indicator data copying fails.

优势

  • 使用双平滑随机指标过滤噪声，信号质量优于普通随机指标
  • 新K线机制避免逐Tick重复交易，减少过度交易风险
  • 同时具备固定止损止盈和信号反转平仓双重出场机制
  • 提供买卖开关控制，灵活性较高
  • Recount机制确保数据复制失败时不会遗漏信号

劣势

  • 缺乏点差过滤，在流动性较差时段可能产生不利成交
  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未考虑市场波动率（ATR）动态调整
  • 未检查是否已有同向持仓，可能产生重复开仓
  • 仅依赖单一指标，缺乏多维度确认和过滤机制
  • MM参数正负值含义不同，容易造成用户混淆误用

改进建议

  • 增加ATR动态止损止盈，根据市场波动率自适应调整
  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，避免同向重复开仓和过度杠杆

Strengths

  • Double-smoothed stochastic filters noise better than standard stochastic, yielding higher-quality signals
  • New bar check prevents per-tick redundant trading, reducing overtrading risk
  • Dual exit mechanism combining fixed SL/TP with signal-based reversal closing
  • Buy/sell permission switches provide flexible control over trading direction
  • Recount mechanism ensures signals are not missed when indicator data copying fails

Weaknesses

  • No spread filter, potentially leading to unfavorable fills during low-liquidity periods
  • Fixed SL/TP in points without ATR-based dynamic adjustment to market volatility
  • No check for existing same-direction positions, risking duplicate entries
  • Relies solely on a single indicator without multi-dimensional confirmation or filtering
  • MM parameter's dual meaning (positive=share, negative=lot) is confusing and error-prone for users

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit that adapts to market volatility
  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate same-direction entries and excessive leverage
