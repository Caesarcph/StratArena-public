摘要

该EA实现了一种简单对冲策略：当现有持仓亏损达到预设阈值时，开立反向头寸进行对冲。通过反向交易覆盖亏损头寸来中和回撤，当两笔交易的净盈利达到目标时同时平仓。逻辑极简，不使用任何指标，纯粹基于持仓状态的对冲操作。

策略分析

策略概念是基于亏损触发对冲：当一笔交易浮亏超过设定的亏损阈值(InPips)时，自动开立等量或加倍的反向头寸，以对冲风险敞口。入场条件为现有持仓亏损达到InPips点数，无需任何技术指标信号，纯粹依赖浮亏状态触发。出场条件为当对冲组合的净盈利达到TakeProfit目标时，同时关闭两个头寸。仓位管理方面，对冲单的手数可能与原仓相同或按倍数设置，缺乏明确的自适应仓位调整机制。资金管理较为薄弱，没有最大回撤限制或账户权益保护机制，持续亏损可能导致反复开仓对冲，最终耗尽保证金。整体策略缺乏趋势判断和止损保护，在单边行情中可能陷入多层对冲的恶性循环。

Summary

This EA implements a straightforward hedging strategy by opening an opposite-direction trade whenever an existing position moves into loss by a predefined amount. It aims to neutralize drawdown by covering the losing position with a counter-trade, then closes both when the net profit reaches a target. The logic is minimalistic — no indicators, just pure position-based hedging.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is loss-triggered hedging: when an existing position's floating loss exceeds a predefined threshold (InPips), the EA automatically opens an opposite-direction trade to hedge the risk exposure. Entry conditions are purely based on floating loss exceeding InPips — no technical indicators or market analysis is involved, just position P&L status. Exit conditions trigger when the hedged pair's combined net profit reaches the TakeProfit target, at which point both positions are closed simultaneously. Position sizing for the hedge order typically mirrors the original lot size or applies a multiplier, but there is no adaptive sizing mechanism based on account equity or volatility. Money management is weak — there is no maximum drawdown limit or account equity protection, and sustained adverse moves can cause repeated hedging layers that eventually exhaust margin. The overall strategy lacks trend awareness and hard stop-loss protection, risking a vicious spiral of multiple hedge layers in trending markets.

优势

  • 逻辑极其简单，代码易读易维护，适合初学者理解对冲概念
  • 在震荡市中能有效减少单边浮亏，起到一定保护作用
  • 对冲后净敞口降低，账户回撤在短期内得到控制

劣势

  • 没有止损，单边趋势中可能无限加仓对冲导致爆仓
  • 缺乏技术指标判断，纯粹基于浮亏触发，入场时机不优化
  • 没有最大对冲层数限制和账户权益保护，资金管理薄弱

改进建议

  • 增加最大对冲层数限制和账户最大回撤保护
  • 加入技术指标过滤，避免在强趋势中盲目对冲
  • 实现动态仓位管理，根据账户权益和波动率调整对冲手数

Strengths

  • Extremely simple logic that is easy to read and maintain, suitable for beginners learning hedging concepts
  • Can effectively reduce unilateral floating loss in ranging markets, providing some protection
  • Net exposure is reduced after hedging, keeping short-term account drawdown under control

Weaknesses

  • No stop-loss; in a trending market, unlimited hedging layers can lead to margin call
  • Lacks technical indicator analysis — entry is purely loss-triggered with no timing optimization
  • No maximum hedge layer limit or account equity protection, making money management inadequate

Improvements

  • Add maximum hedge layer limits and account maximum drawdown protection
  • Incorporate technical indicator filters to avoid blind hedging during strong trends
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account equity and market volatility
