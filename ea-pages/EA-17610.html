摘要

该EA在当前价格上下方设定距离处放置挂单（Buy Stop和Sell Stop），当价格突破区间时触发成交。它根据市场条件管理挂单的修改或删除，并包含基本的止损和止盈风险管理设置。

策略分析

该EA采用突破策略，在当前价格的上下方设置Buy Stop和Sell Stop挂单，等待价格突破后成交。入场条件是价格突破用户设定的距离阈值，挂单自动触发进场。出场条件依靠固定的止损和止盈参数，没有动态追踪止盈机制。仓位管理通过手数参数直接设定，没有根据账户余额动态调整仓位的功能。资金管理方面仅依赖止损止盈比例控制风险，缺少最大持仓限制和账户风险比例控制。整体策略逻辑简单直接，适合趋势突破行情，但在震荡市中可能频繁触发挂单后被打止损。

Summary

This EA places pending orders (Buy Stop and Sell Stop) at a user-defined distance from the current price, triggered when price breaks out of a range. It manages the pending orders by modifying or deleting them based on market conditions and includes basic risk management with stop loss and take profit settings.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a breakout strategy by placing Buy Stop and Sell Stop pending orders at a user-defined distance above and below the current price, triggering entries when price breaks through these levels. Entry conditions are based on price exceeding the configured distance threshold, with pending orders automatically executing upon breakout. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit parameters without any dynamic trailing stop mechanism. Position sizing is handled through a direct lot size parameter without dynamic scaling based on account balance or equity. Money management is limited to stop loss and take profit ratio control, lacking maximum position limits or account risk percentage constraints. The overall strategy logic is straightforward and suitable for trending breakout markets, but may suffer frequent stop-outs in ranging or choppy conditions.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单明了，易于理解和参数调优
  • 挂单方式可以避免在震荡区间内频繁开仓，等待突破确认后才进场
  • 支持双向挂单，能捕捉上涨和下跌两个方向的突破行情

劣势

  • 缺少动态仓位管理，固定手数无法根据账户风险自动调整
  • 在震荡行情中挂单容易被触发后打止损，造成连续亏损
  • 没有移动止损或追踪止盈功能，无法锁定浮动利润

改进建议

  • 增加基于账户余额的动态仓位计算，按风险比例自动调整手数
  • 添加ATR指标动态设定挂单距离和止损止盈，适应市场波动性变化

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and tune parameters
  • Pending order approach avoids frequent entries in ranging markets, only entering after breakout confirmation
  • Supports both buy and sell pending orders, capturing breakout opportunities in both directions

Weaknesses

  • Lacks dynamic position sizing; fixed lot size cannot automatically adjust based on account risk
  • Pending orders are easily triggered then stopped out in choppy markets, causing consecutive losses
  • No trailing stop or dynamic take profit feature to lock in floating profits

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account balance, automatically adjusting lot size by risk percentage
  • Incorporate ATR indicator to dynamically set pending order distance and stop loss/take profit, adapting to market volatility changes

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 固定手数不根据账户规模调整，大仓位可能导致单笔亏损过大
  • 没有最大持仓限制或最大回撤保护，连续亏损时风险不可控

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed lot size does not scale with account size, large positions may cause excessive single-trade losses
  • No maximum position limit or maximum drawdown protection, risk becomes uncontrolled during consecutive losses
