Exp_FatlSatlOsma

This EA trades based on the FatlSatlOsma custom indicator, which is an OSMA-style oscillator derived from FATL (Fast Adaptive Trend Line) and SATL (Slow Adaptive Trend Line) digital filters. It opens long positions when the oscillator values show increasing momentum (Value[0] > Value[1] > Value[2] is not required, but Value[1] < Value[2] with Value[0] > Value[1]) and short positions on decreasing momentum, with configurable stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on an OsMA-style oscillator derived from the difference between FATL (Fast Adaptive Trend Line, 39-period) and SATL (Slow Adaptive Trend Line, 65-period) digital filters, detecting momentum direction changes via oscillator slope transitions. Entry conditions: go long when Value[1]<Value[2] (oscillator was declining) and Value[0]>Value[1] (current value starts rising), indicating momentum shifting from bearish to bullish; go short when Value[1]>Value[2] (oscillator was rising) and Value[0]<Value[1] (current value starts falling), indicating momentum shifting from bullish to bearish. Exit conditions rely on fixed StopLoss (1000 points) and TakeProfit (2000 points) parameters, while also allowing reverse-signal exits (e.g., closing longs when a sell signal appears). Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter governing capital allocation ratio, supporting both LOT and MARGIN modes via the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library functions. Money management defaults to MM=0.1 (10% of available funds), but lacks maximum position limits or overall exposure controls. The strategy operates on the H12 timeframe by default, classifying it as a medium-to-long-term momentum reversal system, with signals taken from a configurable SignalBar to avoid repainting issues.

Strengths

  • Uses FATL/SATL digital filters which produce smoother signals than standard MA crossovers, reducing false signals
  • Has complete stop loss and take profit mechanism with controllable risk per trade
  • Supports independent on/off switches for long and short entries/exits, providing high trading flexibility
  • Implements new bar detection to prevent repeated trading within the same bar
  • Configurable SignalBar parameter avoids indicator repainting issues

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom FatlSatlOsma indicator file; EA cannot run without it
  • Default StopLoss of 1000 points and TakeProfit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments and require manual optimization
  • No position count check exists, potentially allowing duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Lacks spread filtering, which may cause unfavorable entries during high-spread conditions
  • Money management only controls per-trade allocation ratio with no overall portfolio exposure limit
  • No trailing stop or dynamic take-profit mechanism to lock in floating profits

Possible improvements

  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction and limit maximum concurrent positions
  • Implement a trailing stop feature to protect floating profits during trend continuation
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid entering under unfavorable conditions

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No position count check may allow duplicate entries leading to excessive exposure
  • Default SL/TP values are very large; without optimization they can cause significant losses or missed profits
  • Dependency on external custom indicator and TradeAlgorithms library; missing any file causes initialization failure
