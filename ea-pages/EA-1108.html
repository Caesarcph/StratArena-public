摘要

该EA使用基于虚拟交易的自学习算法，将市场价格模式编码为二进制形式并估算方向性概率。仅当学习得到的概率超过可配置阈值时才开立真实仓位，止损止盈大小由虚拟交易参数决定。遗忘因子逐渐衰减旧的学习数据以适应市场变化。

策略分析

策略概念：该EA通过将最近NN根K线的价格变化编码为二进制模式（上涨为1，下跌为0），识别出2^(NN-1)种不同的市场形态。入场条件：对每种形态和不同的delta参数组合，EA进行虚拟交易——当价格变动超过虚拟止损/止盈时记录方向概率；当上涨概率超过Probab阈值且虚拟交易次数大于10次时开多仓，当下跌概率超过阈值时开空仓。出场条件：当反向概率显著上升（超过上次保存值0.05以上）时平仓，或在持仓盈利超过半个止损距离且反向概率超过0.6时平仓。仓位管理：固定手数Lots开仓，止损止盈相等且由虚拟交易中表现最佳的参数组合决定。资金管理：无动态资金管理，固定手数交易，可通过ReplaceStops参数根据新信号调整止损止盈，还有可选的跟踪止损功能。

Summary

This EA uses a self-learning algorithm based on virtual trading to classify binary market patterns and estimate directional probabilities. It opens real positions only when the learned probability exceeds a configurable threshold, with stop-loss and take-profit sized from the virtual trade parameters. A forgetting factor gradually discounts old learning data to adapt to changing market conditions.

Strategy Analysis

Strategy concept: The EA encodes the price changes of the most recent NN bars into binary patterns (1 for up, 0 for down), identifying up to 2^(NN-1) distinct market configurations. Entry conditions: For each pattern and delta parameter combination, the EA conducts virtual trades — recording directional probability when price movement exceeds virtual stop-loss/take-profit levels; a buy position opens when the up probability exceeds the Probab threshold and virtual trade count exceeds 10, and a sell opens when the down probability exceeds the threshold. Exit conditions: Positions close when the reverse probability rises significantly (exceeding the last saved value by more than 0.05), or when the position is profitable by more than half the stop-loss distance and the reverse probability exceeds 0.6. Position sizing: Fixed lot size (Lots) for all trades, with equal stop-loss and take-profit determined by the best-performing virtual trade parameter combination. Money management: No dynamic money management; fixed lot trading, with optional ReplaceStops to adjust stops based on new signals, and an optional trailing stop feature.

优势

  • 自学习机制能适应市场变化，不依赖固定指标参数
  • 虚拟交易验证策略有效性后才开真实仓位，降低盲目入场风险
  • 遗忘因子使模型持续更新，避免过时数据干扰
  • 多delta参数和多止损参数组合提供丰富的模式覆盖
  • 支持学习历史的保存和加载，实现跨会话的持续学习

劣势

  • 固定手数无资金管理，无法根据账户余额或风险动态调整仓位
  • 二进制模式编码过于简化，仅区分涨跌而忽略价格变动幅度，信息损失严重
  • 大数组（5000x30x3）占用大量内存，且Store_TypesCount最大可达4096，实际可能访问越界
  • 虚拟交易仅基于止损/止盈命中统计，不考虑时间衰减和部分盈利场景
  • 缺少点差过滤和滑点保护，在高点差环境下可能频繁止损

改进建议

  • 增加动态资金管理，根据账户风险和胜率动态调整开仓手数
  • 将二进制模式扩展为多级量化编码，保留价格变动幅度信息以提升模式区分度
  • 增加点差过滤和新K线检查，避免在点差过大时开仓以及同一K线内重复交易

Strengths

  • Self-learning mechanism adapts to changing market conditions without relying on fixed indicator parameters
  • Virtual trade validation before opening real positions reduces the risk of blind entries
  • Forgetting factor keeps the model updated, preventing stale data from distorting signals
  • Multiple delta and stop parameter combinations provide rich pattern coverage
  • Support for saving and loading learning history enables continuous learning across sessions

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing with no money management; cannot dynamically adjust position size based on account balance or risk
  • Binary pattern encoding is oversimplified — only distinguishes up/down, ignoring price move magnitude, resulting in significant information loss
  • Large arrays (5000x30x3) consume significant memory, and Store_TypesCount can reach 4096, potentially causing out-of-bounds access
  • Virtual trades are based solely on stop-loss/take-profit hit statistics, ignoring time decay and partial profit scenarios
  • No spread filter or slippage protection; may trigger frequent stop-losses in high-spread environments

Improvements

  • Add dynamic money management to adjust lot size based on account risk and win rate
  • Extend binary pattern encoding to multi-level quantization to preserve price move magnitude information and improve pattern discrimination
  • Add spread filter and new-bar check to avoid opening positions during wide spreads and prevent repeated trading within the same bar

风险点

  • 无新K线检查，同一K线内可能重复开仓导致过度交易
  • 缺少点差过滤，高点差环境下止损易被触发
  • 大数组存在越界访问风险，当NN=12时2^11=2048种模式可能接近数组边界
  • 文件读写无异常处理，文件不存在或损坏时可能导致EA崩溃

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • No new-bar check; may repeatedly open positions within the same bar leading to overtrading
  • No spread filter; stop-losses easily triggered in high-spread environments
  • Large arrays pose out-of-bounds access risk; with NN=12, 2^11=2048 pattern types may approach array boundaries
  • No exception handling for file I/O; missing or corrupted files may cause the EA to crash
