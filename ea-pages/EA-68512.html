Trend Momentum EA

This EA trades momentum signals in the direction of the trend, defined by price being above or below both EMA 50 and EMA 200. It enters longs when RSI is deeply oversold (10-25) with a bullish stochastic crossover in an uptrend, and shorts when RSI is overbought (80-90) with a bearish stochastic crossover in a downtrend. Trading is restricted to London and New York sessions only.

摘要

该EA在趋势方向上进行动量交易，趋势由价格相对于EMA50和EMA200的位置定义。在上升趋势中RSI极度超卖（10-25）且随机指标金叉时做多，在下降趋势中RSI极度超买（80-90）且随机指标死叉时做空。交易仅限伦敦和纽约时段。

策略分析

策略概念是趋势过滤加动量反转入场——用EMA50和EMA200双均线判断大趋势方向，只在趋势方向上寻找入场机会。入场条件分为做多和做空：做多需价格收盘高于EMA50和EMA200、RSI在10-25超卖区间、随机指标%K从下方穿越%D、最后一根闭合K线为阳线；做空条件对称相反。出场条件完全依赖固定止损和止盈，止损300点止盈600点，盈亏比2:1，没有移动止损或部分平仓机制。仓位管理采用固定手数（0.1手），不考虑账户余额或波动率动态调整。资金管理方面缺乏风险百分比计算，也没有最大持仓限制或回撤控制。策略只在伦敦和纽约交易时段运行，避免低流动性时段。

Summary

This EA trades momentum signals in the direction of the trend, defined by price being above or below both EMA 50 and EMA 200. It enters longs when RSI is deeply oversold (10-25) with a bullish stochastic crossover in an uptrend, and shorts when RSI is overbought (80-90) with a bearish stochastic crossover in a downtrend. Trading is restricted to London and New York sessions only.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept combines trend filtering with momentum reversal entry — using EMA 50 and EMA 200 to determine the major trend direction, only seeking entries in the trend's direction. Buy entry requires price closing above both EMAs, RSI in the deeply oversold 10-25 zone, a bullish stochastic %K crossing above %D, and the last closed candle being bullish; sell conditions are symmetrically opposite. Exit conditions rely entirely on fixed stop loss (300 points) and take profit (600 points) with a 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio, with no trailing stop or partial close mechanism. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (0.1 lots) without any dynamic adjustment based on account balance or volatility. Money management lacks risk-percentage calculation, maximum position limits, or drawdown controls. The strategy restricts trading to London and New York sessions, avoiding low-liquidity periods.

优势

  • 趋势过滤逻辑合理，双EMA确认趋势方向后只在趋势方向交易，减少逆势风险
  • 使用已完成K线（index[1]）而非当前K线产生信号，避免信号闪烁和重绘问题
  • 指标句柄验证完整，初始化失败时正确返回INIT_FAILED，防止运行时崩溃
  • 交易时段过滤有效避免低流动性时段的滑点和假信号
  • 固定盈亏比2:1，具有正期望值的理论基础

劣势

  • RSI买入区间（10-25）极度超卖，在上升趋势中极少触发，可能导致长时间无交易
  • 固定手数不随账户规模调整，无法进行合理的资金管理，大账户风险不足、小账户风险过大
  • 没有新K线检测机制，每个tick都执行完整逻辑，浪费计算资源且可能在同一K线上重复尝试下单
  • 止损止盈以点数计算而非ATR动态调整，不同波动率环境下风险暴露差异巨大
  • 仅允许单持仓，没有加仓或分批出场机制，限制了策略的灵活性

改进建议

  • 增加新K线检测机制（如使用静态datetime变量记录上一根K线时间），避免每个tick重复计算和下单尝试
  • 用ATR动态计算止损止盈，适应不同波动率环境，同时将固定手数改为基于账户余额和风险百分比的自适应仓位
  • 扩大RSI入场区间（如30-45做多、55-70做空）或增加RSI从超卖区回升的确认条件，提高交易频率
  • 增加点差过滤器，避免在高点差时段（尤其是伦敦-纽约重叠前）入场

Strengths

  • Sound trend filtering logic — dual EMA confirms trend direction and only trades with the trend, reducing counter-trend risk
  • Uses completed candle (index[1]) instead of current candle for signals, avoiding signal flickering and repainting issues
  • Complete indicator handle validation with proper INIT_FAILED return on failure, preventing runtime crashes
  • Session filtering effectively avoids low-liquidity periods that cause slippage and false signals
  • Fixed 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio provides a theoretical positive expectancy foundation

Weaknesses

  • RSI buy zone (10-25) is extremely oversold, rarely triggered in an uptrend, potentially causing long periods without trades
  • Fixed lot size doesn't scale with account size, making proper money management impossible — under-risked for large accounts, over-risked for small ones
  • No new bar detection mechanism — full logic runs every tick, wasting computation and potentially retrying orders on the same candle
  • Stop loss and take profit are in fixed points rather than ATR-based, creating vastly different risk exposure across varying volatility environments
  • Single position only — no scaling in/out or partial close mechanism, limiting strategy flexibility

Improvements

  • Add new bar detection (e.g., static datetime variable tracking last bar time) to avoid redundant computation and order attempts every tick
  • Use ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit to adapt to different volatility environments, and replace fixed lot size with risk-percentage-based position sizing
  • Widen RSI entry zones (e.g., 30-45 for buys, 55-70 for sells) or add RSI confirmation of recovery from oversold levels to increase trade frequency
  • Add a spread filter to avoid entering during high-spread periods, especially before the London-New York overlap

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 无新K线检测可能导致同一K线多次尝试下单，虽然PositionSelectByMagic限制了多持仓但可能产生不必要的订单请求
  • 固定点数止损在不同品种和波动率环境下实际风险差异大，300点在EURUSD和XAUUSD上含义完全不同
  • 缺少点差过滤，高点差时段可能使实际止损远大于预期
  • 单持仓限制加上极度严格的RSI区间可能导致长期无交易，资金闲置

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • No new bar detection may cause multiple order attempts on the same candle — while PositionSelectByMagic limits multiple positions, it generates unnecessary order requests
  • Fixed point stop loss creates vastly different actual risk across instruments and volatility — 300 points means very different things on EURUSD vs XAUUSD
  • Lack of spread filter means high-spread periods could make actual stop loss much larger than expected
  • Single position limit combined with extremely strict RSI zones may lead to long periods without trades, leaving capital idle
