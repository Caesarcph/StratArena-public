EA-1152 · Mean Reversion

Exp_ColorBBCandles

This EA trades based on the ColorBBCandles custom indicator, which uses Bollinger Band deviation levels to classify candle colors. It opens buy positions when the indicator signals an upward bias (value > 5) and sell positions when it signals a downward bias (value < 5), while closing all positions when a flat/neutral signal (value == 5) is detected.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on multi-level classification of Bollinger Band deviation levels, using the ColorBBCandles indicator to categorize candles into bullish, bearish, and flat states. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when indicator buffer 4's previous value exceeds 5 and the current value is below 6; a sell signal triggers when the previous value is below 5 and the current value exceeds 4. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit in points, plus closing all positions when the indicator value equals 5 (flat/neutral market). Position sizing is controlled via the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent deposit-proportional sizing. Money management relies on functions from the included TradeAlgorithms.mqh file, supporting both proportional allocation and fixed lot modes. The strategy uses a multi-timeframe architecture where the indicator can operate on a different timeframe than the chart, defaulting to H4.

Strengths

  • Uses multi-level Bollinger Band deviation classification, effectively distinguishing between trending and flat market states
  • Supports both deposit-proportional and fixed lot position sizing, offering good flexibility
  • Provides independent toggles for buy/sell opening and closing, allowing flexible directional control
  • Implements new bar detection to prevent repeated trading within the same bar
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit parameters provide a basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Signal logic relies on hardcoded thresholds (5, 4, 6) without adaptive mechanisms, requiring re-tuning for different instruments
  • Depends on external TradeAlgorithms.mqh file, making it hard to independently audit the complete risk profile
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are large, potentially creating excessive risk exposure on some instruments
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, potentially executing unfavorable trades during low-liquidity or high-spread periods
  • Indicator value boundary conditions (Value[1]>5 and Value[0]<6) have ambiguous logic; signals in the 4-6 range may be unstable

Possible improvements

  • Make hardcoded signal thresholds (4, 5, 6) configurable input parameters to improve strategy adaptability and tunability
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid entering during high-spread and low-liquidity periods
  • Add position check and duplicate order protection to prevent excess positions from network latency or anomalies

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Hardcoded signal thresholds may produce false signals under varying market conditions, leading to consecutive losses
  • No spread filter may result in unfavorable execution during high-spread periods
  • Dependency on external TradeAlgorithms.mqh with unaudited implementation may contain risk vulnerabilities
  • Large default SL/TP values may expose unnecessary risk on low-volatility instruments
