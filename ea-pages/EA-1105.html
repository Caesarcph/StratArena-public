摘要

该EA基于连续K线实体递增形态进行交易，当出现N根阴线且实体逐渐增大时开多仓，当出现N根阳线且实体逐渐增大时开空仓。使用固定手数，可配置止损止盈点数，仅在新K线开盘时执行交易。

策略分析

策略概念基于连续K线实体递增形态的反转交易逻辑，认为连续同方向且实体增大的K线暗示趋势过度延伸即将反转。入场条件为：买入信号要求最近N根K线全部为阴线（收盘价低于开盘价），且从第2根开始每根实体绝对值大于前一根，即up_1==N且up_2==N-1；卖出信号则相反，要求N根阳线且实体递增。出场条件仅有止损止盈，没有主动平仓信号（SignalCloseBuy和SignalCloseSell始终返回false）。仓位管理使用固定手数Lots参数，SolveLots函数直接返回输入的Lots值，没有动态仓位调整机制。资金管理方面没有任何风险管理逻辑，不支持基于账户余额或风险百分比的仓位计算。止损止盈使用点数计算并通过StopsLevel检查合规性，但止损700点止盈1200点的设置较为宽泛。

Summary

This EA trades based on a consecutive candlestick body pattern, opening buy positions when N bearish candles appear with increasing body sizes, and sell positions when N bullish candles appear with increasing body sizes. It uses fixed lot sizing with configurable stop loss and take profit in points, and executes trades only once per bar on new bar detection.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a counter-trend reversal logic using consecutive candlestick bodies that increase in size, reasoning that a sequence of same-direction candles with growing bodies signals an overextended move due for reversal. Entry conditions: a buy signal requires the last N candles to all be bearish (close < open) with each successive body larger than the previous one (up_1==N and up_2==N-1); a sell signal requires N consecutive bullish candles with increasing bodies. Exit conditions rely solely on stop loss and take profit, as both SignalCloseBuy and SignalCloseSell always return false, meaning there is no active signal-based position closing. Position sizing uses a fixed Lots input parameter with no dynamic adjustment; the SolveLots function simply returns the hardcoded Lots value. Money management is essentially absent, with no risk-based lot calculation tied to account equity or percentage risk. Stop loss and take profit are calculated in points and validated against the broker's StopsLevel, but the default 700-point SL and 1200-point TP are quite wide, and the strategy lacks any trailing stop or breakeven mechanism.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简洁清晰，基于K线实体递增形态，易于理解和验证
  • 设有新K线检测机制（LastTime），避免同一根K线内重复开仓
  • 止损止盈计算经过StopsLevel合规检查，防止因距离过近被经纪商拒绝

劣势

  • 没有主动平仓信号，完全依赖止损止盈出场，缺乏灵活的退出策略
  • 固定手数没有资金管理机制，无法根据账户风险动态调整仓位
  • 策略假设连续实体递增后必然反转，但强趋势市场中该假设失效会导致连续亏损
  • 没有最大持仓限制或连续亏损后的保护机制

改进建议

  • 增加主动平仓信号逻辑，例如当反转K线出现或技术指标背离时主动平仓
  • 引入基于账户风险百分比的资金管理，替代固定手数，并添加最大回撤保护

Strengths

  • Clean and simple strategy logic based on increasing candle body patterns, easy to understand and backtest
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate trade entries within the same bar
  • Stop loss and take profit calculations include StopsLevel validation to avoid broker rejection

Weaknesses

  • No active closing signals; exits rely entirely on stop loss and take profit with no trailing or signal-based exits
  • Fixed lot sizing with no money management or risk-based position sizing mechanism
  • The reversal assumption fails in strong trending markets, leading to consecutive losses when the trend continues
  • No maximum position limit or drawdown protection mechanism after consecutive losses

Improvements

  • Add active closing signal logic, such as closing on reversal candle patterns or indicator divergence
  • Introduce risk-percentage-based money management to replace fixed lots, and add maximum drawdown protection

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 固定手数无资金管理，小账户可能因单笔亏损占比过大而遭受严重损失
  • 缺乏主动平仓信号，在极端行情中只能依赖止损，若止损滑点可能造成超出预期的亏损
  • 反转策略在强趋势中会产生连续亏损信号，没有连亏保护机制

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed lots without money management can cause disproportionately large losses relative to small account sizes
  • Lack of active exit signals means extreme market conditions rely solely on stop loss, with slippage risk causing losses beyond expectations
  • Reversal strategy generates consecutive losing signals in strong trends with no loss-streak protection
