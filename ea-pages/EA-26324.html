Reversing Martingale

摘要

基于马丁格尔策略的EA，在亏损后反转交易方向并将手数翻倍，旨在通过一次盈利交易回收之前所有亏损。当前一笔交易亏损平仓后，它会以指数级增加的仓位量反向开仓。该策略依赖价格最终均值回归来收回累积亏损并获取小幅利润。

策略分析

该EA采用反向马丁格尔策略，核心概念是在每次亏损后将交易方向反转并加倍仓位，期望一次盈利覆盖所有前序亏损。入场条件为：当上一笔交易以止损平仓后，立即在反方向以双倍手数开仓；若为首笔交易则按初始手数和默认方向入场。出场条件依赖固定止盈和止损，止盈通常较小而止损相对较大，形成高胜率低盈亏比的结构。仓位管理采用经典马丁格尔倍投法，每次亏损后手数乘以2（或可配置倍数），盈利后重置为初始手数。资金管理方面缺乏最大回撤限制和最大连亏次数约束，在极端行情下可能导致指数级仓位增长，存在爆仓风险。

Summary

A Martingale-based EA that reverses trade direction after a loss and doubles the lot size, aiming to recover previous losses with a single winning trade. It enters new positions when the previous one closes at a loss, increasing volume exponentially. The strategy relies on eventual mean reversion to recoup accumulated losses plus a small profit.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a reversing Martingale strategy where the core concept is to flip trade direction and double position size after each loss, expecting a single win to recover all prior losses. Entry condition: when the previous trade closes at a loss (stop loss hit), the EA immediately opens a reverse position with double the lot size; for the first trade it uses the initial lot and a default direction. Exit conditions rely on fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, with typically a small TP and relatively larger SL, creating a high win-rate but low reward-to-risk ratio structure. Position sizing follows the classic Martingale doubling method, multiplying lot size by 2 (or a configurable multiplier) after each loss, and resetting to the initial lot after a win. Money management lacks maximum drawdown limits and maximum consecutive loss constraints, which under extreme market conditions can lead to exponential position growth and account blow-up risk.

劣势

改进建议

  • 增加最大连续亏损次数限制，达到阈值后重置手数并暂停交易
  • 引入基于账户权益的动态仓位调整和最大回撤百分比熔断机制

Strengths

  • Simple and clear logic, easy to understand and deploy
  • Performs well in ranging markets with limited drawdowns, profitable in short term
  • Direction reversal mechanism is more adaptive than one-directional Martingale, catching trend shifts

Weaknesses

  • Exponential position growth during consecutive losses can easily lead to account blow-up
  • Lacks maximum drawdown and maximum consecutive loss protection mechanisms
  • High win rate masks tail risk of extreme losses, giving traders a false sense of security

Improvements

  • Add maximum consecutive loss count limit, reset lot size and pause trading when threshold is reached
  • Introduce equity-based dynamic position sizing and maximum drawdown percentage circuit breaker mechanism
