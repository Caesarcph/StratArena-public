摘要

这是一个追踪利润的工具型EA，不自行开仓，而是通过动态调整止损水平来锁定已有头寸的利润。它监控现有持仓并根据可配置的利润阈值和步进距离来追踪止损。

策略分析

该EA是一个纯工具型程序，不执行任何自主交易开仓操作，仅对已有持仓进行利润追踪管理。其核心逻辑是当持仓浮盈达到设定的启动阈值后，将止损移动到保本或更优位置，随后随着价格继续有利变动，按设定的步进距离持续上移（多单）或下移（空单）止损。仓位管理方面不涉及建仓逻辑，仅处理已有订单的止损调整。资金管理方面通过追踪止损来保护利润，避免盈利单变为亏损单。该策略适用于趋势行情中让利润奔跑的同时锁定已获利润，但对震荡行情可能导致止损被频繁触发后价格继续运行。

Summary

This EA is a trailing profit utility that manages open positions by dynamically adjusting stop-loss levels as price moves in favor, locking in profits without opening its own trades. It monitors existing positions and trails stop-loss based on configurable profit thresholds and step distances.

Strategy Analysis

This EA is a pure utility program that does not execute any autonomous trade entries, only managing trailing profit for existing positions. Its core logic activates when a position's floating profit reaches a configured threshold, then moves the stop-loss to breakeven or better, and continues trailing upward for longs or downward for shorts as price moves favorably by configured step distances. Position sizing is not applicable as it does not open trades, only adjusting stop-loss levels of existing orders. Money management is achieved indirectly through profit protection via trailing stops, preventing winning trades from turning into losers. The approach works well in trending markets to let profits run while locking in gains, but in choppy conditions the trailing stop may be triggered prematurely before price continues in the original direction.

优势

  • 作为工具型EA不自行开仓，风险可控，不会产生意外的交易行为
  • 追踪止损能有效锁定浮盈，防止盈利单回撤为亏损单
  • 参数可配置性强，用户可根据不同品种和市场环境灵活调整追踪参数

劣势

  • 在震荡行情中追踪止损容易被频繁触发，导致利润无法充分放大
  • 缺乏对市场波动率（如ATR）的自适应能力，固定点数追踪在不同波动环境下表现差异大
  • 不支持部分平仓或分级追踪，只能在全仓基础上调整止损，灵活性有限

改进建议

  • 增加基于ATR的动态追踪止损模式，使止损距离能自适应市场波动率
  • 增加分级追踪功能，允许在不同利润阶段使用不同的追踪距离，平衡保护与利润空间

Strengths

  • As a utility EA it does not open its own positions, keeping risk controlled and avoiding unintended trading behavior
  • Trailing stop effectively locks in floating profits, preventing winning trades from reversing into losses
  • Highly configurable parameters allow users to adjust trailing settings flexibly for different instruments and market conditions

Weaknesses

  • In choppy markets the trailing stop is frequently triggered, preventing profits from fully materializing
  • Lacks volatility-adaptive logic such as ATR-based trailing, so fixed-point trailing performs inconsistently across different volatility environments
  • Does not support partial close or tiered trailing, only adjusting stop-loss on the full position, limiting flexibility

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic trailing stop mode so the trailing distance adapts to market volatility automatically
  • Add tiered trailing functionality allowing different trailing distances at different profit levels, balancing protection with profit potential

风险评估

总体:SAFE

风险点

  • 在极端行情跳空时追踪止损可能无法按预期价格执行
  • 固定点数追踪在高低波动品种间切换时可能需要手动调参

Risk Assessment

Overall:SAFE

Risk Points

  • Trailing stop may not execute at expected price during extreme gap events
  • Fixed-point trailing requires manual parameter adjustment when switching between high and low volatility instruments
