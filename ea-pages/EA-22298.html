摘要

该EA实现了双向Skyscraper趋势跟踪策略，根据Skyscraper指标识别的主趋势方向开仓。具有固定手数、止损止盈管理功能，支持双向交易模式，根据指标方向允许做多和做空。

策略分析

该EA基于Skyscraper指标进行趋势跟踪交易，核心策略是当指标判断上升趋势时做多，判断下降趋势时做空。入场条件依赖Skyscraper指标的方向信号，当指标从看空转为看多时开多单，从看多转为看空时开空单。出场条件通过设定的止损和止盈参数实现，每个仓位都有明确的风险控制目标。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，交易者通过Lots参数设定每笔交易的手数，不进行动态加仓或减仓。资金管理方面，EA依赖止损止盈比例来控制单笔风险，但没有基于账户余额的动态仓位计算。双向(Duplex)模式允许EA在同一时间根据信号方向分别处理多头和空头仓位，增强了策略的灵活性。

Summary

This EA implements a duplex (bidirectional) Skyscraper trend-following strategy that opens positions in the direction of the prevailing trend identified by the Skyscraper indicator. It features fixed lot sizing, stop loss and take profit management, and trades on trend signals with a duplex mode allowing both long and short entries based on indicator direction.

Strategy Analysis

This EA implements a trend-following strategy based on the Skyscraper indicator, opening long positions when the indicator signals an uptrend and short positions when it signals a downtrend. Entry conditions rely on the Skyscraper indicator's directional signals - a bullish shift triggers buy orders while a bearish shift triggers sell orders. Exit conditions are managed through configurable stop loss and take profit parameters, providing each position with defined risk and reward targets. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach where the trader specifies the lot size via the Lots parameter, without dynamic position scaling. Money management depends on the stop loss/take profit ratio to control per-trade risk, but lacks account-balance-based dynamic position sizing. The Duplex mode allows the EA to independently handle both long and short positions based on signal direction, adding flexibility to the strategy execution.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，基于趋势跟踪原理，易于理解和维护
  • 具有明确的止损止盈设置，每笔交易风险可控
  • 双向Duplex模式允许灵活应对多空两个方向的趋势信号

劣势

  • 固定手数不根据账户资金动态调整，无法实现比例化资金管理
  • 缺乏新的K线检查机制，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓
  • 没有点差过滤器，在高位差环境下可能产生不利入场

改进建议

  • 增加基于账户余额的动态仓位计算，实现比例化资金管理
  • 添加新K线检查逻辑，避免同一根K线上重复开仓
  • 加入点差过滤和交易时段过滤，提高入场质量

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic based on trend-following principles, easy to understand and maintain
  • Defined stop loss and take profit settings provide controlled risk per trade
  • Duplex mode allows flexible response to both bullish and bearish trend signals

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing does not dynamically adjust based on account equity, preventing proportional money management
  • Lacks new bar check mechanism, potentially opening duplicate positions on the same bar
  • No spread filter, which may lead to unfavorable entries in high-spread environments

Improvements

  • Add account-balance-based dynamic position sizing for proportional money management
  • Implement new bar check logic to prevent duplicate position opening on the same bar
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to improve entry quality
