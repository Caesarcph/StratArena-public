摘要

该EA实现了一种配对交易/统计套利策略，当两个相关货币对的相关系数达到阈值时，同时开立相反方向的仓位。它监控两个仓位的合计利润，达到目标利润后同时平仓，同时以止损/止盈作为兜底退出。自动模式根据交易时段的货币强度假设来确定交易方向。

策略分析

策略概念：该EA基于两个货币对之间的相关性进行统计套利，当相关系数接近设定阈值(-0.98±0.003)时开仓。入场条件：相关系数CorrelationBuffer[0]落入Open_Value±Demis_Value范围内，且当日为允许交易的星期、没有持仓时触发入场。自动模式下，根据GMT时段判断主流货币的强弱趋势来决定买卖方向（如欧洲时段EUR/CHF/GBP为强势货币），直接模式(default AutoD)两对货币开反向仓，镜像模式(AutoM)两对货币开同向仓。出场条件：双仓合计利润达到Profit参数(默认20)时同时反向平仓，或任一仓位触发止损/止盈后等待另一仓位也被止损/止盈。仓位管理：两对货币分别使用独立手数Lot1和Lot2，默认1和1.3，不设动态加仓或减仓逻辑。资金管理：无动态资金管理，固定手数交易，止损止盈均为5000点，属于较宽的止损设置。

Summary

This EA implements a pairs trading / statistical arbitrage strategy by simultaneously opening opposite-direction positions on two correlated currency pairs when their correlation reaches a threshold. It monitors combined profit of both positions and closes them when the total profit target is hit, while also relying on StopLoss/TakeProfit as fallback exits. Auto mode determines trade direction based on session-specific currency strength assumptions.

Strategy Analysis

Strategy concept: This EA performs statistical arbitrage based on the correlation between two currency pairs, opening positions when the correlation coefficient approaches a threshold (-0.98 ± 0.003). Entry conditions: triggered when CorrelationBuffer[0] falls within Open_Value ± Demis_Value range, the current day is an allowed trading day, and no positions are open. In auto mode, trade direction is determined by session-based currency strength assumptions (e.g., EUR/CHF/GBP are dominant during European session); in default mode (AutoD) the two pairs open opposite directions, while mirror mode (AutoM) opens same-direction positions. Exit conditions: both positions are closed simultaneously when combined profit reaches the Profit parameter (default 20), or when one position hits SL/TP, the EA waits for the other to also be stopped out or take profited. Position sizing: two independent lot sizes Lot1 and Lot2 (default 1 and 1.3), with no dynamic scaling logic. Money management: no dynamic money management; fixed lot trading with wide SL/TP of 5000 points each.

优势

  • 配对交易策略逻辑清晰，利用相关性进行统计套利，具有一定的理论基础
  • 支持自动模式根据交易时段货币强度判断方向，增加了策略灵活性
  • 设有双仓合计利润目标出场机制，有利于锁定套利收益
  • 提供交易日过滤功能，可以避开不活跃或风险较高的交易日

劣势

  • 使用Sleep()函数阻塞OnTick线程，会导致EA无法响应其他事件，严重降低平台稳定性
  • 止损/止盈后仅通过轮询历史交易记录判断是否另一仓位也被止损/止盈，存在死循环风险且逻辑脆弱
  • 当任一仓位被SL/TP后，另一仓位可能长期浮亏无人管理，存在巨大风险敞口
  • 相关性判断依赖外部自定义指标Correlation，若指标不存在则EA无法正常运行且无错误提示
  • 代码编码问题导致俄语注释乱码，严重影响可读性和可维护性
  • 使用StringLen()==3来判断Buy/Sell方向非常脆弱，依赖硬编码字符串长度而非内容比较

改进建议

  • 移除Sleep()阻塞调用，改用状态机模式在OnTick中分步处理订单生命周期，避免阻塞主线程
  • 增加单边仓位被止损/止盈后的另一仓位应急平仓逻辑，防止单边风险敞口无限扩大
  • 使用PositionSelect或PositionGetTicket替代轮询历史记录的方式来管理持仓状态
  • 将StringLen()==3的方向判断替换为字符串内容比较(如StringCompare)，提高代码健壮性
  • 添加Correlation自定义指标的存在性检查，指标不可用时输出错误提示并禁止交易

Strengths

  • Clear pairs trading logic based on correlation with theoretical foundation for statistical arbitrage
  • Auto mode adds flexibility by determining trade direction based on session-specific currency strength
  • Combined profit target exit mechanism helps lock in arbitrage gains from paired positions
  • Trading day filter allows avoiding inactive or high-risk trading sessions

Weaknesses

  • Sleep() calls block the OnTick thread, preventing the EA from responding to other events and severely degrading platform stability
  • After SL/TP hit, polling history deals in an infinite loop to detect the other position's exit is fragile and risks infinite hangs
  • When one position hits SL/TP, the other may run with unlimited floating loss — a significant risk exposure gap
  • Correlation signal depends on an external custom indicator with no existence check or error handling if missing
  • Garbled encoding of Russian comments severely hurts readability and maintainability
  • Using StringLen()==3 to detect Buy vs Sell is extremely fragile, relying on hardcoded string length instead of content comparison

Improvements

  • Remove Sleep() blocking calls and replace with a state-machine approach in OnTick to handle order lifecycle without blocking the main thread
  • Add emergency close logic for the remaining position when one side hits SL/TP, preventing unlimited unilateral risk exposure
  • Replace history deal polling with PositionSelect/PositionGetTicket for proper position state management
  • Replace StringLen()==3 direction check with content-based comparison (e.g., StringCompare) for robustness
  • Add existence check for the Correlation custom indicator and disable trading with error message if missing

Risk Assessment

Risk Points

  • No protection for the remaining position after one side hits SL/TP — unlimited floating loss possible
  • SL/TP detection uses while(1) infinite loop with Sleep polling, creating deadlock risk

  • No validation for external Correlation indicator — failed load may produce incorrect signals
