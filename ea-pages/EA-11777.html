摘要

一个基于相关性的配对交易EA，当两个货币对的相关性达到阈值时同时开仓。它利用时段逻辑根据活跃货币时段自动决定交易方向，并在组合利润达到目标时平掉两个仓位。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是统计套利配对交易，通过自定义相关性指标计算两个货币对之间的相关性，当相关性达到预设阈值（Open_Value±Demis_Value）时触发交易信号。入场条件为两个货币对的相关性进入指定区间，同时在允许交易的星期内。EA同时开两个仓位（货币对1和货币对2），方向可以手动设定或根据时段自动判断：自动模式下，根据GMT时段判断哪个货币处于活跃时段来确定趋势方向。仓位管理使用固定手数（Lot1和Lot2），两个仓位手数可以不同以平衡波动率差异。出场条件有两种：一是组合利润达到Profit目标时手动平仓，二是止损或止盈触发后等待另一仓位也被触发。资金管理方面没有动态调整，完全依赖固定手数和固定止损止盈。

Summary

A correlation-based pair trading EA that simultaneously opens positions on two currency pairs when their correlation reaches a threshold value. It uses time-of-day session logic to auto-determine trade direction based on which currency's session is active, and closes both positions when combined profit reaches a target.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy is statistical arbitrage pair trading, using a custom correlation indicator to calculate the correlation between two currency pairs and triggering trade signals when the correlation reaches a preset threshold (Open_Value ± Demis_Value). Entry conditions require the correlation to enter the specified range and the current day of week to be enabled for trading. The EA simultaneously opens two positions (currency pair 1 and currency pair 2), with direction either manually set or auto-determined based on trading sessions: in auto mode, the active GMT session determines which currency is trending and sets direction accordingly. Position sizing uses fixed lots (Lot1 and Lot2), with different lot sizes allowed to balance volatility differences between the two pairs. Exit conditions are twofold: first, manual close when combined profit reaches the Profit target; second, waiting for the other position to also hit SL/TP after one is triggered. Money management lacks dynamic adjustment, relying entirely on fixed lot sizes and fixed stop loss/take profit levels.

优势

  • 配对交易策略具有市场中性特征，降低单边方向风险
  • 时段逻辑根据各货币活跃时段判断趋势方向，有一定合理性
  • 支持手动和自动两种方向判断模式，灵活性较好
  • 组合利润目标出场机制比单一止损止盈更适合配对交易
  • 允许不同手数配置两个货币对，可平衡波动率差异

劣势

  • 使用Sleep()在OnTick中轮询等待，严重阻塞主线程，可能导致平台卡顿
  • 止损/止盈后等待另一仓位也被触发的逻辑存在死循环风险，无超时保护
  • 没有检查是否已有持仓就开仓，可能重复开仓
  • 时段判断逻辑过于简单，仅根据货币名称位置（0或3）判断方向，不够准确
  • 没有考虑点差、滑点和手续费对配对交易利润的影响
  • 相关性指标使用iCustom加载，依赖外部自定义指标文件，部署不便

改进建议

  • 将Sleep轮询改为事件驱动模式，使用OnTradeTransaction处理仓位变化，避免阻塞主线程
  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，在开仓前确认没有同方向的已有仓位，防止重复开仓
  • 为SL/TP等待逻辑增加超时保护机制，避免无限循环
  • 加入点差过滤和佣金成本考量，提高配对交易的实际盈利能力
  • 将相关性计算内置到EA中，移除对外部自定义指标的依赖

Strengths

  • Pair trading strategy has market-neutral characteristics, reducing directional risk
  • Session-based logic for trend direction based on active currency hours has sound reasoning
  • Supports both manual and auto direction modes, providing good flexibility
  • Combined profit target exit mechanism is more suitable for pair trading than individual SL/TP
  • Allows different lot sizes for two pairs, enabling volatility-adjusted position sizing

Weaknesses

  • Using Sleep() in OnTick for polling severely blocks the main thread and may cause platform lag
  • Logic waiting for the other position to hit SL/TP after one triggers has infinite loop risk with no timeout protection
  • No check for existing positions before opening new ones, risk of duplicate positions
  • Session-based direction logic is overly simplistic, determining direction only by currency name position (0 or 3)
  • Does not account for spread, slippage, and commission impact on pair trading profit
  • Correlation indicator loaded via iCustom depends on external custom indicator file, making deployment inconvenient

Improvements

  • Replace Sleep polling with event-driven model using OnTradeTransaction for position change handling, avoiding main thread blocking
  • Add position check logic before opening, confirm no existing same-direction positions to prevent duplicates
  • Add timeout protection to SL/TP waiting logic to avoid infinite loops
  • Incorporate spread filtering and commission cost consideration to improve actual pair trading profitability
  • Build correlation calculation into the EA to remove dependency on external custom indicators
