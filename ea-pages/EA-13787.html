摘要

该EA基于iAnchMom（锚定动量）自定义指标进行交易，当动量向上转向时开多仓，动量向下转向时开空仓。指标内部结合SMA和EMA来检测动量变化，并应用可配置的资金管理及止损止盈。交易信号在新K线形成时生成，以避免重复入场。

策略分析

策略核心基于锚定动量指标iAnchMom，该指标内部通过SMA（默认34周期）和EMA（默认20周期）计算动量值。入场条件：当指标前值小于更前值（动量下降趋势）且当前值大于等于前值（动量开始回升）时，触发买入信号；当指标前值大于更前值（动量上升趋势）且当前值小于等于前值（动量开始回落）时，触发卖出信号。出场条件通过独立的布尔开关控制，买入平仓在动量下降时触发，卖出平仓在动量上升时触发。仓位管理采用TradeAlgorithms.mqh中定义的多种模式，包括基于自由保证金、余额、亏损保证金、亏损余额和固定手数五种方式。止损默认1000点，止盈默认2000点，风险回报比为1:2。信号仅在新K线形成时计算，避免逐tick重复触发，但SignalBar=0时每个tick都会重新计算。

Summary

This EA trades based on the iAnchMom (Anchored Momentum) custom indicator, opening buy positions when momentum turns upward and sell positions when momentum turns downward. It uses a combination of SMA and EMA within the indicator to detect momentum shifts and applies configurable money management with stop loss and take profit. Trading signals are generated on new bar formation to avoid redundant entries.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy is centered on the iAnchMom (Anchored Momentum) custom indicator, which internally uses SMA (default period 34) and EMA (default period 20) to compute momentum values. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when the indicator's previous value is below the one before it (declining momentum) and the current value is greater than or equal to the previous value (momentum starting to recover); a sell signal triggers when the previous value exceeds the prior one (rising momentum) and the current value is less than or equal to the previous value (momentum starting to decline). Exit conditions are controlled by separate boolean switches—buy positions close on declining momentum and sell positions close on rising momentum. Position sizing supports five modes from TradeAlgorithms.mqh: based on free margin, balance, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, or fixed lot size. The default stop loss is 1000 points and take profit is 2000 points, yielding a 1:2 risk-reward ratio. Signals are computed only on new bar formation to prevent tick-by-tick redundancy, though setting SignalBar=0 causes recalculation on every tick.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简洁明了，基于动量反转的经典思路，易于理解和维护
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，灵活性较高，适应不同风险偏好
  • 具备独立的买卖开仓和平仓开关，可灵活控制单向或双向交易
  • 止损止盈比例1:2，具有正期望的风险回报比设计
  • 使用新K线检测机制避免信号重复触发，减少过度交易

劣势

  • 依赖外部自定义指标iAnchMom和TradeAlgorithms.mqh库，缺少这些文件则无法编译运行
  • 没有扩展点数过滤，在流动性低或点差大的品种上可能产生不利入场
  • SignalBar=0时每个tick都会重新计算信号，可能导致频繁闪烁和错误交易
  • 没有对持仓方向的检查，可能在同方向已有持仓时重复开仓
  • 代码中俄语注释乱码，可读性和可维护性较差

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤机制，当点差超过阈值时暂停交易以避免不利入场
  • 在开仓前检查是否已有同方向持仓，避免重复开仓导致仓位过大
  • 将SignalBar=0的tick级重算逻辑改为仅在新K线时计算，统一信号触发机制
  • 添加追踪止损功能以在趋势延续时锁定更多利润
  • 修复源文件编码问题，确保注释可正常显示

Strengths

  • Clean and straightforward strategy logic based on classic momentum reversal, easy to understand and maintain
  • Supports multiple money management modes with high flexibility to suit different risk preferences
  • Independent buy/sell open and close switches allow flexible unidirectional or bidirectional trading control
  • Stop loss to take profit ratio of 1:2 provides a positive-expectancy risk-reward design
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents redundant signal triggers and reduces overtrading

Weaknesses

  • Depends on external custom indicator iAnchMom and TradeAlgorithms.mqh library—without them the EA cannot compile or run
  • No spread filter, which may lead to unfavorable entries on instruments with low liquidity or wide spreads
  • When SignalBar=0, signals recalculate on every tick, potentially causing frequent flickering and erroneous trades
  • No check for existing position direction, which may result in duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Russian comments display as garbled text due to encoding issues, hurting readability and maintainability

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter to pause trading when the spread exceeds a threshold, avoiding unfavorable entries
  • Check for existing positions in the same direction before opening new ones to prevent overexposure
  • Change the SignalBar=0 tick-level recalculation logic to only compute on new bars, unifying the signal trigger mechanism
  • Add a trailing stop feature to lock in more profit during trend continuation
  • Fix the source file encoding so that comments display correctly
