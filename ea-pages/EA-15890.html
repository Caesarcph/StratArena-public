摘要

一个半自动交易面板EA，通过比较当前和前一K线的OHLC价格在21个时间周期上计算买卖信号强度。用户可通过面板按钮手动交易，也可在聚合信号百分比超过可配置阈值时自动开仓，并支持基于分形的止损设置。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念基于多周期价格比较信号：对每个时间周期，比较当前K线与前一根K线的开盘价、最高价、最低价、收盘价及其组合均值（共7项比较），统计看涨和看跌信号的数量。入场条件为：当所有时间周期的买入信号百分比超过开仓阈值（默认85%）时自动买入，卖出信号同理。出场条件为：当持仓方向的信号百分比低于平仓阈值（默认55%）时自动平仓。仓位管理支持固定手数或按净值百分比动态计算手数，最小手数为0.01。资金管理方面，当启用止损功能时，使用分形指标寻找最近的分形低点（多单）或分形高点（空单）作为止损位。策略未考虑点差、滑点和市场波动性过滤，存在在低流动性环境下频繁开平仓的风险。

Summary

A semi-automated trade panel EA that calculates buy/sell signal strength across 21 timeframes by comparing the current and previous bar's OHLC prices. It can either let the user manually trade via panel buttons or auto-trade when the aggregated signal percentage exceeds a configurable threshold, with optional fractal-based stop loss placement.

Strategy Analysis

The EA's strategy concept is based on multi-timeframe price comparison signals: for each timeframe, it compares the current bar's Open, High, Low, Close and their composite averages against the previous bar's values (7 comparisons total), tallying bullish and bearish votes. Entry conditions: when the aggregated buy signal percentage across all timeframes exceeds the opening threshold (default 85%), it auto-opens a buy position; sell signals work symmetrically. Exit conditions: when the signal percentage for the held position's direction falls below the closing threshold (default 55%), it auto-closes the position. Position sizing supports either a fixed lot or a dynamic lot calculated as a percentage of account equity, with a minimum of 0.01 lots. Money management includes optional fractal-based stop loss placement: for buy positions, the nearest lower fractal below current price is used as SL; for sell positions, the nearest upper fractal above current price is used. The strategy does not account for spread, slippage, or volatility filtering, risking frequent open/close cycles in low-liquidity environments.

优势

  • 多周期信号聚合提供更全面的市场方向判断，减少单一周期噪声
  • 半自动设计灵活，用户可选择手动面板交易或自动驾驶模式
  • 基于分形的止损设置具有自适应市场结构的特点，比固定点数止损更合理
  • 可视化面板直观显示买卖信号百分比和强度条，便于实时监控
  • 支持固定手数和净值比例两种仓位管理模式，适应不同资金管理需求

劣势

  • 信号计算仅基于简单的前后K线OHLC价格比较，缺乏对市场结构和趋势强度的深入分析
  • 未设置点差过滤和滑点保护，在高点差或低流动性环境下可能产生不利成交
  • 只允许同时持有单一品种的一个仓位，无法对冲但也没有多仓位风控
  • 分形止损仅在OnTrade事件中设置，开仓时止损和获利均为0，存在短暂无保护期
  • 85%的开仓阈值可能过于严格导致错失机会，而55%的平仓阈值可能过于宽松导致回吐利润

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和最小波动阈值，避免在高点差或低波动环境下频繁交易
  • 在开仓时直接设置分形止损而非等待OnTrade事件回调，消除无保护持仓窗口期
  • 增加趋势强度过滤器（如ADX或移动平均线方向），避免在震荡行情中频繁开平仓
  • 增加新K线检测机制，避免在同一根K线上重复计算信号和重复交易

Strengths

  • Multi-timeframe signal aggregation provides a more comprehensive market direction assessment, reducing single-timeframe noise
  • Semi-automated design offers flexibility: users can choose manual panel trading or autopilot mode
  • Fractal-based stop loss placement adapts to market structure, which is more reasonable than fixed-pip stops
  • Visual panel intuitively displays buy/sell signal percentages and strength bars for real-time monitoring
  • Supports both fixed lot and equity-percentage position sizing modes to accommodate different money management needs

Weaknesses

  • Signal calculation is based solely on simple bar-over-bar OHLC price comparison, lacking deeper analysis of market structure and trend strength
  • No spread filter or slippage protection, which may result in unfavorable fills during high-spread or low-liquidity conditions
  • Only allows a single position per symbol with no hedging capability and no multi-position risk control
  • Fractal stop loss is only set in OnTrade events; entry orders have zero SL/TP, creating a brief unprotected period
  • The 85% opening threshold may be too strict and miss opportunities, while the 55% closing threshold may be too loose and give back profits

Improvements

  • Add spread filtering and minimum volatility thresholds to avoid frequent trading in high-spread or low-volatility environments
  • Set fractal-based stop loss at order submission time instead of waiting for OnTrade callback, eliminating the unprotected position window
  • Add a trend strength filter (e.g., ADX or moving average direction) to avoid frequent open/close cycles in choppy markets
  • Implement new bar detection to prevent redundant signal calculation and repeated trading on the same bar

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Entry orders have zero SL/TP, relying on OnTrade callback to set fractal stop loss, creating an unprotected position window
  • No new bar detection, potentially triggering trading logic multiple times on the same bar
  • No maximum holding period limit, potentially leading to prolonged losing positions
  • Fractal stop loss may be placed too far from entry, resulting in larger-than-expected single-trade losses
