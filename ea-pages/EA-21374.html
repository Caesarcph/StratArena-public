EA-21374 · Pivot Point

SimplePivot

SimplePivot is an Expert Advisor that calculates classical pivot point levels (Support/Resistance) based on the previous period's High, Low, and Close prices, and enters trades when price breaks through these pivot levels. It uses pivot-based support and resistance zones as entry signals with predefined take-profit targets at the next pivot level. The EA systematically trades breakouts from pivot levels with fixed lot sizing.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy of this EA is the classic pivot point calculation method, computing the central pivot along with S1/S2/S3 support and R1/R2/R3 resistance levels from the previous period's High, Low, and Close prices. Entry conditions trigger when price breaks through pivot support or resistance levels — going long on resistance breakout and going short on support breakdown. Exit conditions use the next pivot level as take-profit targets, for example targeting R2 after breaking R1. Position sizing employs a fixed lot approach without any dynamic adjustment based on account equity or risk percentage. Money management is notably deficient as there is no explicit stop-loss mechanism, relying instead on the opposite pivot level breakout for risk control, which presents a significant vulnerability. The overall strategy logic is straightforward and simple, but risk management is inadequately implemented.

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is clear and simple, easy to understand and verify
  • Pivot points are a classic and widely-used technical analysis method with market psychology backing
  • Multiple support/resistance levels provide a structured trading framework with clear entry and exit targets

Weaknesses

  • Lack of explicit stop-loss mechanism, risk control is severely inadequate
  • Fixed lot sizing without dynamic adjustment ignores account equity proportion
  • Pivot breakouts generate frequent false signals in ranging markets leading to repeated losses

Possible improvements

  • Add a dynamic stop-loss mechanism based on ATR or pivot levels
  • Introduce a money management module to calculate lot size dynamically based on account risk percentage
  • Add a trend filter indicator (e.g., moving average direction) to reduce false breakout signals in ranging markets

Risk assessment

Overall risk: High risk

  • No stop-loss set, a single trade may incur far greater loss than expected
  • Fixed lot size ignores account size, small accounts may blow up from a single excessive loss
  • Pivot breakouts frequently fail in ranging markets, potentially causing consecutive losses
