摘要

该EA基于X2MACandle自定义指标的交易信号进行操作，该指标通过双重均线交叉系统生成K线式信号。它内置了带有仓位恢复（MMRec）逻辑的资金管理模块，在亏损后调整手数以尝试恢复权益。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略基于X2MACandle指标，该指标本质上是一个双重移动平均线交叉系统的可视化表达，以K线形态呈现趋势方向。入场条件为：当X2MACandle指标出现看涨K线时开多单，出现看跌K线时开空单，属于典型的趋势跟踪策略。出场条件依赖于反向信号的出现或止损/止盈的触发。仓位管理方面，EA集成了MMRec（资金管理恢复）模块，当交易出现亏损时，后续交易会增大手数以尝试恢复之前的亏损，这是一种渐进式加仓的恢复机制。资金管理包含固定手数和基于账户余额的百分比计算两种模式，MMRec模块则在此基础上叠加了亏损恢复逻辑。整体而言，该策略在趋势行情中表现较好，但在震荡市中可能因频繁假信号和恢复性加仓而遭受较大回撤。

Summary

This EA trades based on signals from the X2MACandle custom indicator, which generates candlestick-style signals from a double moving average crossover system. It incorporates money management with position recovery (MMRec) logic, adjusting lot sizes after losses to attempt recovery of equity.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the X2MACandle indicator, which is essentially a visual representation of a dual moving average crossover system rendered in candlestick form to show trend direction. Entry conditions are straightforward: a buy position is opened when the X2MACandle indicator produces a bullish candle, and a sell position when it produces a bearish candle, making it a classic trend-following approach. Exit conditions rely on the appearance of a reverse signal or the triggering of stop-loss/take-profit levels. For position sizing, the EA integrates an MMRec (Money Management Recovery) module that increases lot sizes after losing trades to attempt equity recovery, which is a progressive position enlargement mechanism. Money management supports both fixed lot and percentage-of-balance calculation modes, with the MMRec module overlaying a loss recovery logic on top. Overall, the strategy performs well in trending markets but may suffer significant drawdowns in choppy conditions due to frequent false signals compounded by recovery-based position enlargement.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰简洁，基于双重均线交叉的信号易于理解和验证
  • 内置多种资金管理选项，提供固定手数和百分比风险两种模式
  • 支持追踪止损功能，可以在趋势行情中锁定浮动利润

劣势

  • MMRec恢复性加仓机制在连续亏损时会显著增大风险敞口，可能导致严重回撤
  • 止损和止盈默认值为0，用户如果不手动设置则完全没有风险控制
  • 双重均线交叉系统在震荡行情中会产生大量假信号导致频繁亏损

改进建议

  • 增加震荡行情过滤器（如ADX或波动率指标），在震荡市中减少或暂停交易以避免假信号
  • 为MMRec模块设置最大手数上限和最大连续恢复次数限制，防止亏损后无限加仓

Strengths

  • Clear and simple strategy logic based on dual MA crossover signals that are easy to understand and verify
  • Built-in multiple money management options providing both fixed lot and percentage-based risk modes
  • Trailing stop support allows locking in floating profits during trending markets

Weaknesses

  • The MMRec recovery-based position enlargement significantly increases risk exposure during consecutive losses, potentially causing severe drawdowns
  • Stop-loss and take-profit default to 0, meaning no risk control at all if the user does not manually configure them
  • The dual MA crossover system generates numerous false signals in ranging markets leading to frequent losses

Improvements

  • Add a ranging market filter (such as ADX or volatility indicator) to reduce or pause trading during choppy conditions and avoid false signals
  • Set a maximum lot size cap and maximum consecutive recovery count limit for the MMRec module to prevent unlimited position enlargement after losses

  • MMRec恢复性加仓机制在连续亏损时会呈指数级增大仓位，存在爆仓风险

Risk Points

  • The MMRec recovery-based position enlargement increases positions exponentially during consecutive losses, creating a risk of account blowout
  • Stop-loss and take-profit default to 0, relying entirely on reverse signals for exit when not configured, which can result in massive single-trade losses
