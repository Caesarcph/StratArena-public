摘要

该EA基于自定义TSI_WPR指标的交叉信号进行交易，该指标结合了真实强度指数和威廉百分比范围。当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿时开空仓，支持可配置的资金管理和平仓选项。

策略分析

策略概念基于TSI_WPR指标的动量交叉，该指标将WPR（威廉百分比范围）通过多次平滑算法处理后生成主线和信号线。入场条件为：当主线大于信号线且前一周期主线小于等于信号线时触发买入信号，反之触发卖出信号。出场条件通过指标交叉反向信号平仓，同时设有固定的StopLoss和TakeProfit。仓位管理支持多种模式，包括基于自由保证金、余额、亏损后保证金或固定手数的资金管理方式。该策略使用多周期框架（默认H4），并允许用户选择多种平滑算法（SMA、EMA、JJMA、JurX等）来调整指标灵敏度。信号检测仅在NewBar时触发，避免同一K线内重复交易，但缺少点差过滤和加仓限制。

Summary

This EA trades based on crossover signals from a custom TSI_WPR indicator, which combines the True Strength Index with Williams' Percent Range. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above its signal line and sell positions on the reverse crossover, with configurable money management and position closing options.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on momentum crossover of the TSI_WPR indicator, which processes WPR (Williams' Percent Range) through multiple smoothing algorithms to generate a main line and a signal line. Entry conditions trigger a buy signal when the main line crosses above the signal line (Ind[1]>Sign[1] and Ind[0]<=Sign[0]), and a sell signal on the reverse crossover. Exit conditions use reverse crossover signals for closing positions, along with fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit levels. Position sizing supports multiple margin modes including free margin-based, balance-based, loss-adjusted margin, or fixed lot size. The strategy operates on a multi-timeframe basis (default H4) and allows selection of various smoothing algorithms (SMA, EMA, JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, AMA) to adjust indicator sensitivity. Signal detection only triggers on new bars via IsNewBar() to prevent repeated trades within the same candle, but lacks spread filtering and position accumulation limits.

优势

  • 使用多层平滑算法对WPR进行处理，有效减少虚假信号
  • 支持多种资金管理模式和平滑算法选择，灵活性强
  • 新K线检测机制避免同一K线内重复交易
  • 买卖开仓和平仓可独立控制，便于策略优化
  • 固定止损止盈设置，风险可控

劣势

  • 缺少点差过滤，在高位差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • 没有检查是否已有同方向持仓，可能导致重复开仓
  • 依赖自定义指标TSI_WPR，部署时需确保指标文件存在
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值过大，可能不适合所有品种
  • 没有追踪止损或移动止损功能，无法锁定浮动利润

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤机制，在点差过大时暂停交易
  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓
  • 增加追踪止损功能以更好地保护利润

Strengths

  • Multi-layer smoothing of WPR effectively reduces false signals
  • Supports multiple money management modes and smoothing algorithms for high flexibility
  • New bar detection prevents repeated trades within the same candle
  • Buy/sell open and close can be independently controlled for strategy optimization
  • Fixed stop loss and take profit provide defined risk boundaries

Weaknesses

  • Lacks spread filtering, may result in unfavorable fills during high-spread conditions
  • No check for existing same-direction positions, potentially causing duplicate entries
  • Depends on custom TSI_WPR indicator file, requiring proper deployment
  • Default SL of 1000 and TP of 2000 points are very large and may not suit all instruments
  • No trailing stop feature to lock in floating profits

Improvements

  • Add spread filtering to pause trading when spread exceeds a threshold
  • Add position checking logic to prevent duplicate same-direction entries
  • Add trailing stop functionality to better protect floating profits
