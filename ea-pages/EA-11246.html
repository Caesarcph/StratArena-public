摘要

该EA基于TSI_WPR自定义指标的交叉信号进行交易，该指标结合了真实强度指数平滑与威廉百分比范围动量。当指标线从上方穿越信号线时开多仓，从下方穿越时开空仓，并提供可配置的资金管理和平仓逻辑。

策略分析

该EA的策略核心是基于TSI_WPR指标的动量交叉系统。TSI_WPR指标对威廉百分比范围（WPR）进行多重平滑处理，生成一条主线和一条信号线。入场条件为：当主线上方穿越信号线后，若在信号K线处主线回穿至信号线下方，则触发买入信号；反之当主线下方穿越信号线后回穿至上方，则触发卖出信号。出场条件通过独立的买卖平仓开关控制，当反向交叉信号出现时平掉对应仓位。仓位管理支持五种模式：按可用保证金比例、按余额比例、按净可用保证金比例、按净余额比例以及固定手数。止损和止盈以点数设定，分别为1000和2000点。该策略仅在新的K线形成时计算信号，避免逐笔重复计算，并使用Recount标志在指标数据读取失败时重新计算。

Summary

This EA trades based on crossover signals from the TSI_WPR custom indicator, which combines True Strength Index smoothing with Williams Percent Range momentum. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses below the signal line from above, and sell positions when it crosses above from below, with configurable money management and position closing logic.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is a momentum crossover system based on the TSI_WPR indicator. The TSI_WPR indicator applies multiple smoothing passes to Williams Percent Range (WPR), generating a main line and a signal line. Entry conditions: when the main line was above the signal line and then crosses below it at the signal bar, a buy signal is triggered; conversely, when the main line was below and crosses above, a sell signal is triggered. Exit conditions are controlled by independent buy/sell close switches, closing the corresponding position when a reverse crossover signal appears. Position sizing supports five modes: percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of net free margin, percentage of net balance, and fixed lot size. Stop loss and take profit are set in points at 1000 and 2000 respectively. The strategy only calculates signals on new bar formation to avoid per-tick recalculation, and uses a Recount flag to retry when indicator data copy fails.

优势

  • 多重平滑算法选择丰富，适应不同市场状态
  • 独立控制买卖开平仓开关，灵活配置交易方向
  • 新K线检测机制避免逐笔重复计算信号，减少冗余交易
  • 支持五种仓位管理模式，满足不同风险偏好
  • 指标数据读取失败时有重算机制，增强鲁棒性

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值较大，可能不适合所有品种
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生不利入场
  • 未检查当前是否已有同方向持仓，可能重复开仓
  • 依赖外部自定义指标TSI_WPR，独立运行时需要该指标文件存在
  • 注释为乱码（编码问题），严重影响代码可读性和维护性

改进建议

  • 添加点差过滤机制，避免在高点差时入场
  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，防止同方向重复开仓
  • 将止损止盈改为自适应ATR计算，适应不同品种波动性

Strengths

  • Rich selection of multiple smoothing algorithms adaptable to different market conditions
  • Independent buy/sell open/close switches for flexible trading direction configuration
  • New bar detection mechanism avoids per-tick signal recalculation, reducing redundant trades
  • Supports five position sizing modes to accommodate different risk preferences
  • Recount mechanism on indicator data read failure enhances robustness

Weaknesses

  • Default SL of 1000 and TP of 2000 points are large and may not suit all instruments
  • No spread filter, potentially leading to unfavorable entries in high-spread environments
  • No check for existing same-direction positions, potentially causing duplicate entries
  • Depends on external custom indicator TSI_WPR, requiring the indicator file to be present
  • Comments are garbled due to encoding issues, severely hurting code readability and maintainability

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter mechanism to avoid entering during high-spread conditions
  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate same-direction entries
  • Change SL/TP to adaptive ATR-based calculation to suit different instrument volatilities
