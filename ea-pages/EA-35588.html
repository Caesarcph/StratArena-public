摘要

该EA使用三个随机振荡器和一个加速振荡器组合产生入场信号，使用动量振荡器管理出场。仅在已完成K线上交易，采用固定手数、可配置的止损止盈（点数），以及可选的追踪止损机制。支持按星期和时段过滤交易，并在时段结束或周五强制平仓。

策略分析

策略概念基于多指标动量确认，通过三个不同参数设置的随机振荡器交叉和超买超卖区域来确认趋势方向。入场条件要求：Stochastic Signal的主线高于信号线（多头）或低于信号线（空头）；Stochastic指标1的%K值高于20水平（多头）或低于80水平（空头）；Stochastic指标2的%K值低于75水平（多头）或高于25水平（空头）；加速振荡器从负转正穿越0.0002水平（多头）或从正转负穿越-0.0002水平（空头）。出场条件由动量振荡器驱动：当AO从上方穿越0.0013水平向下时平多仓，从下方穿越-0.0013水平向上时平空仓。仓位管理采用固定手数入场，支持追踪止损（基于前一根K线的最高/最低价），止损止盈按点数计算。资金管理方面仅使用固定手数，没有根据账户余额或风险比例动态调整仓位大小的机制。

Summary

This EA uses a combination of three Stochastic oscillators and an Accelerator Oscillator to generate entry signals, and an Awesome Oscillator to manage exits. It trades on completed bars only, with fixed lot sizing, configurable stop loss and take profit in pips, and an optional trailing stop mechanism. Sessions can be filtered by day of week and time of day, with forced closes at session or Friday boundaries.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on multi-indicator momentum confirmation, using three Stochastic oscillators with different parameter settings along with crossover and overbought/oversold zones to confirm trend direction. Entry conditions require: Stochastic Signal main line above signal line (long) or below (short); Stochastic 1 %K value above level 20 (long) or below level 80 (short); Stochastic 2 %K value below level 75 (long) or above level 25 (short); Accelerator Oscillator crossing upward through 0.0002 (long) or crossing downward through -0.0002 (short). Exit conditions are driven by the Awesome Oscillator: close long when AO crosses below 0.0013 from above, close short when AO crosses above -0.0013 from below. Position management uses fixed lot sizing for entries, supports a trailing stop based on the previous bar's high/low, with stop loss and take profit calculated in pips. Money management relies solely on fixed lots with no dynamic position sizing based on account balance or risk percentage.

优势

  • 多指标确认入场，三重随机振荡器加加速振荡器组合过滤，降低虚假信号
  • 仅在已完成K线上交易，避免盘中噪音和重复信号
  • 具有完善的会话管理功能，支持按星期和时段过滤交易并强制平仓

劣势

  • 固定手数无动态仓位管理，无法根据账户风险自动调整手数
  • 三个随机振荡器参数高度相似（均为40/10/10），存在指标冗余，实质性多样性不足
  • 追踪止损逻辑中当价格触及止损时直接平仓而非真正追踪，可能在剧烈波动时造成不利成交
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下入场成本可能过高

改进建议

  • 引入基于账户权益或风险比例的动态仓位计算，替代固定手数
  • 为三个随机振荡器设置差异化参数或替换其中一个为不同类型的指标，增加策略多样性
  • 添加点差过滤条件，避免在点差过大时入场

Strengths

  • Multi-indicator confirmation with triple Stochastic plus Accelerator Oscillator combination reduces false signals
  • Trades only on completed bars, avoiding intrabar noise and duplicate signals
  • Comprehensive session management with day-of-week and time filtering plus forced close at session boundaries

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing with no dynamic position management, cannot automatically adjust lots based on account risk
  • Three Stochastic oscillators have highly similar parameters (all 40/10/10), indicating indicator redundancy and insufficient diversity
  • Trailing stop logic closes position when price hits the trailing level instead of properly trailing, which may cause unfavorable fills during sharp moves
  • No spread filter, entry costs may be excessive in high-spread environments

Improvements

  • Introduce dynamic position sizing based on account equity or risk percentage instead of fixed lots
  • Differentiate the three Stochastic parameters or replace one with a different indicator type to increase strategy diversity
  • Add a spread filter to avoid entering trades when spread is excessively wide
