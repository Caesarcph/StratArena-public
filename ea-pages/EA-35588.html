摘要

该EA使用三个随机指标和一个加速震荡指标组合产生入场信号，使用动量震荡指标管理出场。当所有指标满足超卖/看涨交叉条件时开多仓，满足超买/看跌交叉条件时开空仓。策略基于新K线触发交易，支持止损止盈和时段控制。

策略分析

该策略属于多指标共振型均值回归系统，核心概念是在多个震荡指标同时确认超买或超卖区域时入场。入场条件方面，做多需要四个条件同时满足：Stochastic Signal的%K线上穿%D线、Stochastic主线上穿20水平线、第二个Stochastic下穿75水平线（超卖区）、Accelerator Oscillator从负值区域上穿0.0002水平。做空则为完全对称的反向条件。出场条件使用Awesome Oscillator，当持仓方向与AO交叉信号相反时平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数（默认0.01手），不使用加仓或减仓逻辑。资金管理方面，止损40点、止盈70点，盈亏比约1:1.75，同时支持追踪止损功能但默认关闭。策略还包含时段过滤，周五可强制平仓，周日默认不交易。

Summary

This EA uses a combination of three Stochastic oscillators and an Accelerator Oscillator to generate entry signals, and an Awesome Oscillator to manage exits. It opens buy positions when all indicators align with oversold/bullish crossover conditions and sell positions when overbought/bearish crossover conditions are met. The strategy operates on bar-open events with configurable stop loss, take profit, and session-based trading windows.

Strategy Analysis

This strategy is a multi-indicator confluence mean-reversion system, with the core concept of entering when multiple oscillators simultaneously confirm overbought or oversold zones. For entry conditions, a long requires four conditions to be met simultaneously: Stochastic Signal %K crossing above %D, Stochastic main line crossing above level 20, a second Stochastic crossing below level 75 (oversold zone), and Accelerator Oscillator crossing above the 0.0002 level from negative territory. Short conditions are perfectly symmetrical in reverse. Exit conditions use the Awesome Oscillator, closing positions when the AO crossover signal opposes the held position direction. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (default 0.01), with no scaling in or out logic. For money management, the stop loss is 40 pips and take profit is 70 pips, yielding a reward-to-risk ratio of approximately 1:1.75, while trailing stop functionality is supported but disabled by default. The strategy also includes session filtering with forced Friday closing and Sunday trading disabled by default.

优势

  • 多指标共振过滤减少了假信号，四个入场条件同时满足才开仓，入场门槛较高
  • 盈亏比1:1.75合理，止盈大于止损有利于长期正期望值
  • 具备完善的交易基础设施：订单重试机制、填充类型自动适配、时段过滤、周五强制平仓
  • 使用sigma容差比较浮点数避免了浮点精度问题
  • 追踪止损功能提供了额外的盈利保护选项

劣势

  • 三个Stochastic指标参数完全相同（%K=40,%D=10,Slowing=10），仅水平线不同，存在指标冗余，Ind1和Ind2实际上可能高度相关
  • 固定手数无资金管理逻辑，不根据账户余额或风险动态调整仓位
  • 追踪止损默认关闭且isTrailingStop初始值为false，在OnInit中被恒为false的表达式覆盖，实际永远无法启用
  • 缺少点差过滤，在H4周期虽影响较小但非零风险
  • Awesome Oscillator出场条件参数0.0013对于不同货币对可能需要大幅调整，缺乏自适应性

改进建议

  • 修复追踪止损功能：将isTrailingStop改为input参数允许用户启用，并优化追踪止损逻辑使其更实用
  • 减少Stochastic指标冗余：考虑使用不同周期参数或合并Ind1和Ind2的功能，避免高度相关指标占用资源
  • 加入动态仓位管理：根据账户余额和风险比例自动计算手数，替代固定0.01手
  • 增加点差过滤和滑点保护，避免在高点差时段开仓

Strengths

  • Multi-indicator confluence filtering reduces false signals with four entry conditions required simultaneously, setting a high entry bar
  • Reasonable reward-to-risk ratio of 1:1.75 with take profit exceeding stop loss, favoring long-term positive expectancy
  • Robust trading infrastructure: order retry mechanism, filling type auto-adaptation, session filtering, and Friday forced closing
  • Uses sigma tolerance for floating-point comparison, avoiding precision-related bugs
  • Trailing stop functionality provides an additional profit protection option

Weaknesses

  • Three Stochastic indicators share identical parameters (%K=40, %D=10, Slowing=10) differing only in level thresholds, creating indicator redundancy where Ind1 and Ind2 are likely highly correlated
  • Fixed lot sizing with no money management logic, does not dynamically adjust position size based on account balance or risk
  • Trailing stop is permanently disabled - isTrailingStop is initialized to false and the OnInit expression 'isTrailingStop && Stop_Loss > 0' always evaluates to false
  • No spread filter, though impact is smaller on H4 timeframe it remains a non-zero risk
  • Awesome Oscillator exit level parameter of 0.0013 may require significant adjustment for different currency pairs, lacking adaptivity

Improvements

  • Fix trailing stop functionality: change isTrailingStop to an input parameter to allow user control, and improve the trailing stop logic for practical use
  • Reduce Stochastic indicator redundancy: consider using different period parameters or merging Ind1 and Ind2 functionality to avoid highly correlated indicators wasting resources
  • Add dynamic position sizing: automatically calculate lot size based on account balance and risk ratio instead of fixed 0.01 lots
  • Add spread filter and slippage protection to avoid opening positions during high-spread periods
