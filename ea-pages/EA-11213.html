摘要

该EA基于HullTrend自定义指标进行交易，利用Hull移动平均线交叉信号判断趋势方向。当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿时开空仓，具有可配置的资金管理和止损止盈功能。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是利用HullTrend指标的趋势跟踪交叉信号进行交易。入场条件为：当指标线(Ind)大于信号线(Sign)且前一根柱出现上穿交叉时开多仓，当指标线小于信号线且前一根柱出现下穿交叉时开空仓。出场条件包括：反向交叉信号触发平仓（多头持仓在空头信号出现时平仓，反之亦然），以及固定的止损和止盈。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，支持多种保证金模式包括固定手数、按余额百分比和按可用保证金百分比等方式。资金管理采用MMMode参数选择模式，MM参数控制资金比例。策略使用IsNewBar机制确保每个K线周期只计算一次信号，避免重复交易，同时通过SignalBar参数允许在当前K线即时交易或等待K线收盘确认。

Summary

This EA trades based on the HullTrend custom indicator, which uses Hull Moving Average crossover signals to determine trend direction. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions when it crosses below, with configurable money management and stop loss/take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA trades based on trend-following crossover signals from the HullTrend indicator. Entry conditions: a buy position opens when the indicator line crosses above the signal line on the previous bar, and a sell position opens when the indicator line crosses below the signal line on the previous bar. Exit conditions include: reverse crossover signals trigger position closure (long positions close on bearish signals and vice versa), plus fixed stop loss and take profit levels. Position sizing is controlled via the MM parameter, supporting multiple margin modes including fixed lot size, percentage of balance, and percentage of free margin. Money management uses the MMMode parameter to select the sizing mode, with MM controlling the proportion of funds. The strategy uses the IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are calculated only once per bar cycle, preventing duplicate trades, while the SignalBar parameter allows trading on the current bar immediately or waiting for bar close confirmation.

优势

  • 使用Hull移动平均线减少滞后性，信号比传统MA更及时
  • 支持多种平滑算法和价格类型，策略灵活性高
  • 具有IsNewBar新K线检测机制，避免重复开仓
  • 买卖方向可独立开关控制，适应不同市场观点
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，风险控制选项丰富

劣势

  • 依赖外部自定义指标HullTrend，缺少该指标文件则EA无法运行
  • 没有点差过滤机制，在高点差环境下可能产生虚假信号
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值较大，不适合所有品种
  • 缺少多时间周期确认机制，单一周期信号可能产生假突破
  • 没有持仓数量检查，可能在已有持仓时重复开仓

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和交易时间过滤，避免不利市场条件下的交易
  • 添加多时间周期趋势确认，提高信号可靠性并减少假信号

Strengths

  • Uses Hull Moving Average to reduce lag, providing more timely signals than traditional MAs
  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithms and price types, offering high strategy flexibility
  • Implements IsNewBar new bar detection mechanism to prevent duplicate position openings
  • Buy and sell directions can be independently toggled, accommodating different market views
  • Supports multiple money management modes with rich risk control options

Weaknesses

  • Depends on external custom indicator HullTrend; EA cannot run without the indicator file
  • No spread filter mechanism, which may produce false signals in high-spread environments
  • Default SL 1000 and TP 2000 points are large and not suitable for all instruments
  • Lacks multi-timeframe confirmation, single timeframe signals may produce false breakouts
  • No position count check, potentially opening duplicate positions when already holding

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid trading in unfavorable market conditions
  • Add multi-timeframe trend confirmation to improve signal reliability and reduce false signals
