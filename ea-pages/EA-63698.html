摘要

RSI Engine是一个基于RSI多信号的智能交易系统，通过看涨/看跌背离和超买超卖反转信号进行交易，支持中心线确认过滤。集成了动态仓位管理的风险管理、移动止损、每日盈亏限制、新闻过滤和可配置交易时段功能。该EA同时只持有一个仓位，通过RSI水平穿越或移动止损管理出场。

策略分析

策略核心是基于RSI指标的均值回归交易，利用价格与RSI之间的背离现象捕捉潜在反转机会。入场条件有两种：一是RSI背离信号（价格创新低但RSI未创新低为看涨背离，价格创新高但RSI未创新高为看跌背离），二是超买超卖反转信号（RSI从超卖区上穿或从超买区下穿）。可选的50中心线确认要求RSI穿越50水平后才入场，提高信号可靠性。出场条件包括RSI到达对立极端区域时平仓，以及移动止损在利润达到触发阈值后跟踪价格保护利润。仓位管理支持固定手数和基于风险百分比的动态手数计算，根据权益和止损距离自动确定交易量。资金管理方面设有每日盈亏限制，达到目标利润或亏损上限后停止当日交易，同时具备新闻时段过滤和交易时间过滤来规避高风险时段。

Summary

The RSI Engine is a multi-signal RSI-based Expert Advisor that trades bullish/bearish divergences and overbought/oversold reversals with optional centerline confirmation. It includes risk management with dynamic lot sizing, trailing stops, daily profit/loss limits, news filtering, and configurable trading hours. The EA opens one position at a time and manages exits via RSI level crosses or trailing stops.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy is centered on mean reversion trading using the RSI indicator, exploiting divergences between price and RSI to capture potential reversal opportunities. Entry conditions come in two forms: RSI divergence signals where price makes a new low but RSI does not (bullish divergence) or price makes a new high but RSI does not (bearish divergence), and overbought/oversold reversal signals where RSI crosses out of extreme zones. An optional 50-centerline confirmation requires RSI to cross the 50 level before entry, improving signal reliability. Exit conditions include closing positions when RSI reaches the opposite extreme zone and a trailing stop that locks in profits once a trigger threshold is met. Position sizing supports both fixed lots and dynamic lot calculation based on risk percentage, equity, and stop loss distance. Money management features daily profit/loss limits that halt trading when targets are reached, along with news time and trading hour filters to avoid high-risk periods.

优势

  • 多信号融合：结合RSI背离、超买超卖反转和中心线确认，提高信号质量
  • 风险管理体系完善：包含动态仓位计算、每日盈亏限制、移动止损和保证金检查
  • 过滤机制全面：新闻时段过滤和交易时间过滤有效规避高风险时段
  • 代码结构清晰：函数模块化设计，逻辑分层明确，易于维护和扩展
  • 新K线检测机制：避免同一根K线内重复交易，减少不必要的信号触发

劣势

  • 背离检测逻辑过于简单：仅比较前后半段最低/最高点，可能产生大量假信号
  • 只支持单仓位交易：无法同时持有多单和空单，限制了策略灵活性
  • IsPositionOpen仅检查魔术号不检查品种：多品种运行时可能冲突
  • 新闻过滤只支持单一新闻事件时间，无法处理一天内的多个重要新闻
  • 每日盈亏计算仅累加第一个匹配魔术号的持仓浮动盈亏，忽略多仓位场景
  • 缺少点差过滤：作为均值回归策略在低流动性时段入场可能因高点差受损

改进建议

  • 改进背离检测算法：引入RSI局部极值点识别和趋势线拟合，而非简单的前后半段最高/最低比较
  • 添加点差过滤：在入场前检查当前点差是否超过阈值，避免高成本交易
  • 支持多仓位管理和多品种符号检查，使IsPositionOpen和每日盈亏计算更准确
  • 增强新闻过滤功能：支持多个新闻事件时间点或接入经济日历API
  • 添加最大滑点控制和订单重试机制，提高订单执行的鲁棒性

Strengths

  • Multi-signal fusion combining RSI divergence, overbought/oversold reversal, and centerline confirmation improves signal quality
  • Comprehensive risk management including dynamic lot sizing, daily P/L limits, trailing stops, and margin checks
  • Thorough filtering mechanisms with news time and trading hour filters to avoid high-risk periods
  • Clean code architecture with modular functions, clear logic layering, and easy maintainability
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate trades within the same bar, reducing unnecessary signal triggers

Weaknesses

  • Oversimplified divergence detection only compares the lowest/highest points of two halves, potentially generating many false signals
  • Single position limitation prevents holding both long and short simultaneously, reducing strategy flexibility
  • IsPositionOpen checks magic number without symbol filtering, which may cause conflicts when running on multiple instruments
  • News filter only supports a single news event time per day, unable to handle multiple important news releases
  • Daily P/L calculation only adds floating profit from the first matching magic number position, ignoring multi-position scenarios
  • No spread filter: as a mean reversion strategy, entering during low-liquidity periods can suffer from high spread costs

Improvements

  • Improve divergence detection by identifying RSI local extremes and trendline fitting rather than simple half-period highest/lowest comparison
  • Add spread filtering to check current spread against a threshold before entry, avoiding high-cost trades
  • Support multi-position management and symbol-specific checks for more accurate position tracking and daily P/L calculation
  • Enhance news filter to support multiple news event times or integrate with the economic calendar API
  • Add maximum slippage control and order retry mechanism to improve execution robustness

  • Overbought/oversold reversal signals will continuously trade against strong trends, potentially causing consecutive losses
  • Daily P/L floating profit calculation only considers the first matching position, inaccurate when multiple positions exist
  • No spread filter means entering during low-liquidity periods can result in actual costs far exceeding expectations
  • ManageOpenTrades uses SelectByMagic which only selects the first matching position, leaving other positions unmanaged
