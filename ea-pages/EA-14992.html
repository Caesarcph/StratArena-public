摘要

该EA在每个新的M15K线时在布林带下轨放置BuyLimit挂单，在上轨放置SellLimit挂单，当价格触及布林带中轨SMA时平仓。不设止损和止盈，完全依赖价格回归布林带中轨来退出交易。

策略分析

该策略基于布林带均值回归概念，假设价格在触及布林带上下轨后会回归中轨。入场条件为：每根新K线形成时，在下轨放置BuyLimit买入限价单，在上轨放置SellLimit卖出限价单。出场条件为：当价格触及布林带中轨SMA时，分别平掉多头和空头仓位。仓位管理采用固定0.01手微型仓位，没有动态仓位调整机制。资金管理方面完全没有止损和止盈设置，这意味着如果价格单边运行不回归中轨，亏损将无限扩大。每根新K线会先删除所有未成交挂单再重新下单，避免挂单堆积。但该策略没有检查是否已有同方向持仓，可能导致重复开仓。

Summary

This EA places BuyLimit and SellLimit pending orders at the Bollinger Band lower and upper bands respectively on each new M15 candle, and closes open positions when price touches the middle SMA band. It uses no stop loss or take profit, relying entirely on the mean reversion to the Bollinger middle band for exits.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy is based on the Bollinger Band mean reversion concept, assuming that price will revert to the middle band after touching the upper or lower bands. Entry conditions: on each new candle, a BuyLimit order is placed at the lower band and a SellLimit order is placed at the upper band. Exit conditions: when price touches the Bollinger SMA middle band, long positions are closed if price is above SMA and short positions are closed if price is below SMA. Position sizing uses a fixed 0.01 micro lot with no dynamic adjustment mechanism. Money management is completely absent of stop loss and take profit, meaning if price trends in one direction without reverting to the middle band, losses can grow unbounded. Each new candle first deletes all pending orders before placing new ones to prevent order accumulation. However, the strategy does not check for existing open positions in the same direction, which could lead to duplicate entries.

优势

  • 逻辑简洁明了，基于经典的布林带均值回归策略
  • 每根新K线重置挂单，避免过期挂单堆积
  • 使用固定0.01微仓，降低了单笔交易风险
  • 使用魔数1221标识订单，避免与其他EA冲突
  • 在价格回归中轨时及时平仓，符合均值回归逻辑

劣势

  • 完全没有止损设置，单边趋势行情中可能造成巨大亏损
  • 没有止盈设置，盈利空间仅依赖价格回归中轨
  • 没有检查已有同方向持仓，可能重复开仓增加风险
  • CloseAllPending函数没有过滤魔数，会删除所有EA的挂单
  • 使用iVolume检测新K线不够可靠，在非M15周期回测时可能失效
  • OrderSymbol()在无选中订单时可能返回空值，BuyLimitExecute和SellLimitExecute中存在潜在bug

改进建议

  • 添加止损和止盈参数，在挂单下单时设置保护性止损，防止单边趋势造成无限亏损
  • 在CloseAllPending函数中添加魔数过滤，避免误删其他EA的挂单
  • 使用更可靠的新K线检测方法（如比较Time[0]与上次记录时间），替代不稳定的iVolume检测
  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，在开仓前检查是否已有同方向持仓，避免重复开仓
  • 将固定手数改为可配置参数，并考虑基于ATR或账户余额的动态仓位管理

Strengths

  • Simple and clear logic based on the classic Bollinger Band mean reversion strategy
  • Resets pending orders on each new candle to prevent stale order accumulation
  • Uses fixed 0.01 micro lot size which reduces per-trade risk exposure
  • Uses magic number 1221 to identify orders and avoid conflicts with other EAs
  • Closes positions promptly when price reverts to the middle band, consistent with mean reversion logic

Weaknesses

  • No stop loss at all, which can lead to catastrophic losses in trending markets
  • No take profit set, profit potential relies entirely on price reverting to the middle band
  • Does not check for existing positions in the same direction, potentially leading to duplicate entries and increased risk
  • CloseAllPending function does not filter by magic number, which could delete pending orders from other EAs
  • Using iVolume to detect new candles is unreliable and may fail during backtesting on non-M15 timeframes
  • OrderSymbol() may return empty when no order is selected, creating a potential bug in BuyLimitExecute and SellLimitExecute

Improvements

  • Add stop loss and take profit parameters to set protective stops when placing pending orders, preventing unlimited losses in trending markets
  • Add magic number filtering in CloseAllPending to avoid accidentally deleting pending orders from other EAs
  • Use a more reliable new bar detection method (e.g., comparing Time[0] with a stored timestamp) instead of the unstable iVolume check
  • Add position check logic before opening new trades to check for existing same-direction positions and avoid duplicate entries
  • Change the fixed lot size to a configurable parameter and consider dynamic position sizing based on ATR or account balance

  • BuyLimitExecute and SellLimitExecute use OrderSymbol() instead of NULL, which may return incorrect values when no order is selected
  • When Close[0] exactly equals BB_SMA, both CloseAllBuy and CloseAllSell are triggered due to overlapping >= and <= conditions
