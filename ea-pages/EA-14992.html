摘要

该EA在每个新的M15蜡烛上，在布林带下轨放置BuyLimit挂单，在上轨放置SellLimit挂单，当价格回归中轨时平仓。每根新K线开始时删除所有挂单并以更新的布林带价位重新下单。这是一个典型的布林带均值回归策略，没有止损和止盈。

策略分析

策略概念基于布林带均值回归，假设价格触及上下轨后会回归中轨。入场条件为：每根新M15蜡烛开盘时，在下轨放置BuyLimit挂单做多，在上轨放置SellLimit挂单做空。出场条件为：当价格触及布林带中轨（SMA）时，分别平掉所有买入或卖出仓位。仓位管理采用固定0.01手迷你仓，没有任何动态仓位调整机制。资金管理方面完全缺失——没有止损、没有止盈、没有最大持仓限制、没有回撤控制。挂单管理方面，每根新K线都会删除所有未成交挂单并重新放置，确保挂单价格与最新布林带值同步，但这也意味着频繁下单可能受到经纪商限制。

Summary

This EA places BuyLimit and SellLimit pending orders at the Bollinger Bands lower and upper bands respectively on every new M15 candle, closing positions when price reverts to the middle band (SMA). It deletes all pending orders at the start of each new bar and replaces them with fresh ones at the updated band levels. The strategy is a classic Bollinger Band mean-reversion system with no stop loss or take profit.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on Bollinger Band mean reversion, assuming price will revert to the middle band after touching the upper or lower band. Entry conditions: on each new M15 candle open, a BuyLimit pending order is placed at the lower band and a SellLimit pending order is placed at the upper band. Exit conditions: when the close price reaches the BB middle band (SMA), all open buy positions are closed if price is above SMA, and all sell positions are closed if price is below SMA. Position sizing uses a fixed 0.01 lot micro size with no dynamic lot adjustment mechanism whatsoever. Money management is completely absent — there is no stop loss, no take profit, no maximum position limit, and no drawdown control. Pending order management deletes all unfilled pending orders at each new bar and replaces them at updated band levels, ensuring synchronization with current BB values, but frequent order placement may be restricted by brokers.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简洁明了，基于经典的布林带均值回归原理，易于理解和验证
  • 使用挂单入场而非市价单，可以获得更有利的入场价格，减少滑点影响
  • 每根新K线更新挂单价格，确保挂单始终与最新布林带值同步，避免过期挂单成交
  • 固定小仓位0.01手，在测试阶段可以控制单笔风险暴露
  • 使用Magic Number 1221区分本EA的订单，避免与其他EA冲突

劣势

  • 完全没有止损和止盈设置，一旦市场出现趋势性行情，亏损可能无限扩大
  • CloseAllPending函数没有检查Magic Number，可能误删其他EA的挂单
  • OrderSend中使用OrderSymbol()获取交易品种而非NULL或明确的Symbol()，在多品种图表上可能出错
  • 没有检查是否已有同方向挂单或持仓就重复下单，可能导致重复挂单
  • iVolume==1的新K线检测方式不可靠，在回测和实盘中行为不一致，应该用时间比较法
  • 没有点差过滤、没有交易时间限制、没有最大持仓数量控制

改进建议

  • 添加止损和止盈参数，至少设置基于布林带宽度或ATR的动态止损，防止趋势行情中的巨额亏损
  • CloseAllPending函数应添加Magic Number过滤条件，避免误删其他EA的挂单
  • 使用时间比较法（比较当前bar时间与上次记录时间）替代iVolume==1来检测新K线，确保回测和实盘行为一致
  • 在下单前检查是否已存在同方向挂单或持仓，避免重复下单
  • 添加最大持仓数量限制和回撤控制，提高资金管理安全性

Strengths

  • Clean and simple strategy logic based on the classic Bollinger Band mean reversion principle, easy to understand and verify
  • Uses pending limit orders instead of market orders for better entry prices and reduced slippage impact
  • Updates pending order prices on each new bar to ensure synchronization with current BB values, preventing stale order fills
  • Fixed micro lot size of 0.01 limits per-trade risk exposure during testing
  • Uses Magic Number 1221 to distinguish its own orders, avoiding conflicts with other EAs

Weaknesses

  • No stop loss or take profit at all — losses can grow unbounded during trending markets
  • CloseAllPending function does not check Magic Number, potentially deleting pending orders from other EAs
  • OrderSend uses OrderSymbol() instead of NULL or explicit Symbol(), which may fail when no order is pre-selected
  • No check for existing orders or positions before placing new pending orders, potentially causing duplicate orders
  • iVolume==1 for new bar detection is unreliable and behaves differently in backtesting vs live, should use time comparison
  • No spread filter, no trading session restrictions, no maximum position count control

Improvements

  • Add stop loss and take profit parameters, at minimum implement ATR-based or BB-width-based dynamic stops to prevent catastrophic losses in trending markets
  • Add Magic Number filter in CloseAllPending to avoid accidentally deleting other EAs' pending orders
  • Replace iVolume==1 new bar detection with time comparison method (compare current bar time with last recorded time) for consistent behavior in backtesting and live trading
  • Check for existing same-direction pending orders or positions before placing new orders to prevent duplicates
  • Add maximum position count limits and drawdown controls for better money management safety

  • iVolume==1 new bar detection may trigger on every tick in backtesting, causing excessive order placement and deletion
