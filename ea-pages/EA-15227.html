摘要

该EA基于Sidus指标进行交易，该指标利用双EMA和双LWMA交叉产生买入和卖出信号。它在新的K线信号上系统地开仓和平仓，具有可配置的资金管理、止损和止盈水平。

策略分析

策略概念基于Sidus指标，通过快速EMA与慢速EMA的交叉以及快速LWMA与慢速LWMA的交叉来确认趋势方向。入场条件为：当指标缓冲区0（UpValue）有非零且非EMPTY_VALUE的信号时，开多单并平空单；当缓冲区1（DnValue）有信号时，开空单并平多单。出场条件包括止损和止盈的设置，以及反向信号触发时的平仓逻辑。仓位管理通过MM参数和MMMode枚举控制，支持固定手数、按余额百分比和按可用保证金百分比等多种模式。资金管理还包含了对买卖仓位独立控制的开仓和平仓开关，允许灵活配置交易方向。此外，EA在新K线形成时才计算信号，避免重复交易，但在同时允许开仓和平仓时，会向前回溯历史K线确认是否已有反向平仓信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on the Sidus indicator, which uses dual EMA and dual LWMA crossovers to generate buy and sell signals. It opens and closes positions systematically on new bar signals with configurable money management, stop loss, and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the Sidus indicator, which confirms trend direction through the crossover of a fast EMA with a slow EMA and a fast LWMA with a slow LWMA. Entry conditions: when indicator buffer 0 (UpValue) produces a non-zero, non-EMPTY_VALUE signal, a buy position is opened and sell positions are closed; when buffer 1 (DnValue) signals, a sell position is opened and buy positions are closed. Exit conditions include configurable stop loss and take profit levels, as well as position closure triggered by opposing signals. Position sizing is controlled via the MM parameter and MMMode enumeration, supporting fixed lot size, balance percentage, and free margin percentage modes. Money management also features independent buy/sell open and close switches, allowing flexible directional trading configuration. Additionally, the EA only calculates signals on new bar formation to avoid repeated trades, and when both open and close permissions are enabled, it retrospectively scans historical bars to confirm whether an opposing close signal already exists.

优势

  • 采用双均线交叉确认趋势，信号相对可靠，减少了虚假突破的可能性
  • 独立的买卖开仓和平仓控制开关，提供了极大的灵活性
  • 新K线检查机制避免了同一K线内重复交易，降低了过度交易风险
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，适应不同风险偏好的交易者
  • 使用iCustom调用外部指标，逻辑与指标分离，便于维护和替换指标

劣势

  • 止损设置为1000点、止盈为2000点，数值偏大，在波动性较低的市场中可能导致持仓时间过长
  • 没有点差过滤机制，在点差较大的品种上交易可能造成不必要的损失
  • 历史回溯确认平仓信号的逻辑可能遍历大量K线，存在性能瓶颈
  • 依赖外部TradeAlgorithms.mqh库和Sidus指标文件，缺少这些文件时EA无法运行
  • 没有持仓数量检查或最大持仓限制，可能在极端情况下过度开仓

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤机制，当点差超过阈值时不执行交易，避免在高点差环境下入场
  • 添加最大持仓数量限制和资金回撤保护，提升风险控制能力
  • 优化历史K线回溯逻辑，设置最大回溯K线数量上限，避免性能问题

Strengths

  • Dual moving average crossover confirmation reduces false breakout signals and improves reliability
  • Independent buy/sell open and close switches provide great flexibility in strategy configuration
  • New bar check mechanism prevents repeated trades within the same bar, reducing overtrading risk
  • Multiple money management modes accommodate traders with different risk preferences
  • Uses iCustom to call external indicator, separating logic from indicator implementation for easier maintenance

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are large values that may result in excessively long holding periods in low-volatility markets
  • No spread filter mechanism, which may cause unnecessary losses on instruments with wide spreads
  • The historical bar retrospective logic for confirming close signals could iterate over many bars, creating performance concerns
  • Depends on external TradeAlgorithms.mqh library and Sidus indicator file; EA cannot run without them
  • No position count check or maximum position limit, potentially leading to overexposure in extreme conditions

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter to skip trading when the spread exceeds a threshold, avoiding entries in high-spread environments
  • Implement maximum position count limits and equity drawdown protection to enhance risk management
  • Optimize the historical bar retrospective logic by setting a maximum lookback bar limit to avoid performance issues
