Previous Candle Breakdown

This EA trades breakouts of the previous candle's high and low. When price breaks above the previous candle's high, it opens a buy; when price breaks below the previous candle's low, it opens a sell. It is a simple structural breakout system that relies on momentum continuation after a range expansion.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on structural breakout of the previous candle, assuming that price continuation follows a breach of the prior candle's extremes. Entry conditions: buy when current price breaks above the previous completed candle's high, sell when current price breaks below the previous completed candle's low. Exit conditions likely include fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, or possibly ATR-based dynamic stops. Position sizing may use fixed lots or account-balance-proportional sizing. Money management is moderately controlled if no martingale or grid is employed, but consecutive false breakouts in ranging markets can cause drawdowns. The strategy performs best in trending markets but suffers from frequent whipsaws during consolidation periods.

Strengths

  • Simple and clear logic, easy to understand and verify
  • No complex indicators needed, reducing overfitting risk
  • Captures breakout opportunities promptly in trending markets
  • Well-defined entry rules without subjective judgment
  • Applicable across multiple timeframes

Weaknesses

  • Frequent false breakouts in ranging markets lead to losses
  • Lacks trend filter, prone to repeated entries during consolidation
  • Fixed stop-loss/take-profit may not suit varying market volatility
  • Does not account for trading session and liquidity differences
  • Lack of position management may lead to overtrading

Possible improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., MA direction or ATR threshold) to filter false breakouts
  • Add trading session filter to avoid low-liquidity periods
  • Use ATR-based dynamic stop-loss/take-profit to adapt to market volatility
  • Add maximum consecutive loss limit and cooldown period
  • Add position sizing module based on account risk percentage

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Lacks trend filter leading to frequent losses in ranging markets
  • May lack new bar check causing repeated entries on same breakout
  • Fixed stop-loss may not adapt to current market volatility
