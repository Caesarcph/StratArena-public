摘要

该EA基于价格与简单移动平均线(SMA)的交叉信号进行交易，价格穿越MA时触发开仓和平仓。它包含根据连续亏损缩减仓位的功能，并集成了FrameGenerator用于在回测优化期间可视化结果。

策略分析

策略概念是基于价格与简单移动平均线(SMA)的关系判断趋势方向。入场条件为：当K线开盘价在MA上方且收盘价在MA下方时做空，开盘价在MA下方且收盘价在MA上方时做多，即价格穿越MA线时入场。出场条件为：持有多单时价格从下方穿越MA到上方则平仓，持有空单时价格从上方穿越MA到下方则平仓。仓位管理采用基于账户自由保证金和最大风险比例的动态手数计算，并根据历史连续亏损次数按DecreaseFactor因子递减仓位。资金管理通过MaximumRisk参数控制每笔交易最大风险为账户净值的2%，同时遵守品种的最小/最大手数和手数步长限制。该策略仅在新区块的第一笔tick时交易，并要求K线数量大于100作为额外安全检查。

Summary

This EA trades based on a simple Moving Average crossover signal where price crossing the SMA triggers entries and exits. It includes optimized lot sizing that reduces position size after consecutive losses, and integrates MetaTrader's FrameGenerator for visualizing optimization results during backtesting.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the relationship between price and a Simple Moving Average (SMA) to determine trend direction. Entry conditions are: sell when a bar opens above the MA and closes below it, buy when a bar opens below the MA and closes above it — essentially trading on price crossover of the MA line. Exit conditions are: close a long position when price crosses from below to above the MA, close a short position when price crosses from above to below the MA. Position sizing uses a dynamic lot calculation based on account free margin and the maximum risk percentage, with lot size decreased according to the number of consecutive historical losses divided by the DecreaseFactor. Money management controls maximum risk per trade at 2% of account equity via the MaximumRisk parameter, while also respecting the symbol's minimum/maximum volume and volume step constraints. The strategy only trades on the first tick of a new bar and requires more than 100 bars of history as an additional safety check.

优势

  • 具有动态仓位管理，根据账户余额和风险比例自动调整手数
  • 连续亏损后自动缩减仓位，有助于控制回撤
  • 集成FrameGenerator优化可视化，便于参数优化分析
  • 遵守品种手数限制（最小/最大/步长），防止无效订单
  • 仅在新区块首笔tick交易，避免重复信号

劣势

  • 没有设置止损和止盈，仓位完全依赖信号平仓，存在大幅亏损风险
  • 策略逻辑过于简单，单一MA交叉信号容易产生频繁假突破
  • 没有考虑点差和滑点对交易成本的影响
  • 平仓时使用固定的3点滑点可能不足，在剧烈行情中可能无法平仓
  • DecreaseFactor递减逻辑在连续亏损次数为1时不生效，存在逻辑边界问题

改进建议

  • 添加止损和止盈参数，为每笔交易设置明确的风险边界
  • 增加多重确认条件（如成交量、RSI或第二周期MA过滤）以减少假信号
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时间过滤，避免高成本时段交易

Strengths

  • Dynamic position sizing that automatically adjusts lot size based on account balance and risk percentage
  • Automatic lot reduction after consecutive losses helps control drawdown
  • Integrated FrameGenerator for optimization visualization, facilitating parameter analysis
  • Respects symbol volume constraints (min/max/step), preventing invalid orders
  • Trades only on the first tick of a new bar, avoiding duplicate signals

Weaknesses

  • No stop-loss or take-profit set; positions rely entirely on signal-based closing, creating significant loss risk
  • Overly simple strategy logic; a single MA crossover signal prone to frequent false breakouts
  • Does not account for spread and slippage impact on trading costs
  • Position close uses a fixed 3-point slippage which may be insufficient during volatile markets
  • DecreaseFactor reduction logic does not activate when consecutive losses equal 1, creating a logic edge case

Improvements

  • Add stop-loss and take-profit parameters to set explicit risk boundaries for each trade
  • Add multiple confirmation conditions (e.g., volume, RSI, or second-period MA filter) to reduce false signals
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid high-cost trading periods
