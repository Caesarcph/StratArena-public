摘要

该EA基于FatlMacd自定义指标进行交易，该指标是FATL数字滤波器与类MACD振荡器的结合。当FatlMacd柱状图转向上时开多仓，转向下时开空仓，并可在反向信号时平仓。

策略分析

策略概念基于FatlMacd指标的方向变化，该指标通过FATL数字滤波器计算价格再用可选平滑方法（如T3、JJMA、JurX等）进行二次平滑处理，本质上是一个高级动量振荡器。入场条件为：当指标值Value[1]<Value[2]（下降趋势中）且Value[0]>Value[1]（开始上升）时触发买入信号；当Value[1]>Value[2]（上升趋势中）且Value[0]<Value[1]（开始下降）时触发卖出信号。出场条件包括固定点数的止损和止盈，以及反向信号平仓选项。仓位管理支持多种资金管理模式，包括基于自由保证金、余额、亏损后保证金比例计算手数，以及固定手数模式。默认止损1000点、止盈2000点，止盈是止损的两倍，体现了趋势跟踪中让利润奔跑的思路。策略使用SignalBar参数允许在当前K线或指定偏移K线上判断信号，并有新K线检测机制避免重复开仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on the FatlMacd custom indicator, which is a smoothed digital filter (FATL) combined with a MACD-like oscillator. It opens buy positions when the FatlMacd histogram turns upward and sell positions when it turns downward, with configurable position closing on opposite signals.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on directional changes in the FatlMacd indicator, which applies a FATL digital filter to price and then smooths it with a selectable method (T3, JJMA, JurX, etc.), functioning as an advanced momentum oscillator. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when Value[1]<Value[2] (downtrend) and Value[0]>Value[1] (histogram turns up); a sell signal triggers when Value[1]>Value[2] (uptrend) and Value[0]<Value[1] (histogram turns down). Exit conditions include fixed stop-loss and take-profit in points, plus optional opposite-signal position closing. Position sizing supports multiple money management modes: based on free margin percentage, balance percentage, loss-adjusted margin, or fixed lot size. Default stop-loss of 1000 points and take-profit of 2000 points reflects a trend-following philosophy of letting profits run. The SignalBar parameter allows signal detection on the current or offset bar, and a new bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate entries.

优势

  • 使用FATL数字滤波器结合高级平滑算法，信号质量优于普通MACD
  • 支持多种资金管理模式和灵活的买卖开平仓开关，可精细控制交易行为
  • 新K线检测机制避免同一K线内重复开仓，减少过度交易风险
  • 止损止盈比例2:1，符合趋势跟踪策略的盈利期望
  • 支持多种平滑算法选择（T3、JJMA、JurX等），可针对不同市场优化

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标FatlMacd，若指标文件缺失EA将无法运行
  • 缺少持仓检查逻辑，可能在已有同向持仓时重复开仓
  • 默认时间周期H12较小众，流动性可能不足导致滑点增大
  • 缺少移动止损/追踪止损功能，盈利头寸可能大幅回吐
  • 未考虑点差过滤，在高点差品种上频繁信号可能导致亏损

改进建议

  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，避免同向重复开仓导致仓位过重
  • 增加移动止损/追踪止损功能，保护浮动盈利
  • 添加点差过滤和最大持仓数量限制，提升风险管理能力

Strengths

  • Uses FATL digital filter combined with advanced smoothing algorithms, yielding higher quality signals than standard MACD
  • Supports multiple money management modes and flexible buy/sell open/close switches for fine-grained trade control
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate entries within the same bar, reducing overtrading risk
  • Stop-loss to take-profit ratio of 1:2 aligns with trend-following profit expectancy
  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithm choices (T3, JJMA, JurX, etc.) for market-specific optimization

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom FatlMacd indicator; EA fails if the indicator file is missing
  • Lacks position existence check, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Default H12 timeframe is uncommon, potentially leading to poor liquidity and increased slippage
  • No trailing stop functionality, so profitable positions may give back significant gains
  • No spread filter, so frequent signals on high-spread instruments may lead to losses

Improvements

  • Add position existence check to avoid duplicate same-direction entries and over-concentration
  • Implement trailing stop functionality to protect floating profits
  • Add spread filter and maximum position count limit to enhance risk management
