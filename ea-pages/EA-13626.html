摘要

该EA基于FatlMacd自定义指标进行交易，该指标结合了数字滤波器(FATL)和平滑MACD信号。当指标上升时开多仓，下降时开空仓，具有可配置的资金管理、止损和止盈功能。

策略分析

策略概念基于FatlMacd指标的方向变化，该指标使用FATL数字滤波器结合多种平滑算法（如T3、JJMA、JurX等）对选定价格进行平滑处理。入场条件为：当指标值从下降转为上升（Value[0]>Value[1]且Value[1]<Value[2]）时开多仓；当指标值从上升转为下降（Value[0]<Value[1]且Value[1]>Value[2]）时开空仓。出场条件包括止损和止盈的固定点数设置，以及反向信号触发平仓。仓位管理支持五种模式：按可用保证金比例、按余额比例、按扣除亏损后的可用保证金比例、按扣除亏损后的余额比例、以及固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，MMMode参数选择计算方式，默认使用固定手数模式。

Summary

This EA trades based on the FatlMacd custom indicator, which applies a digital filter (FATL) combined with a smoothed MACD-style signal. It opens buy positions when the indicator rises and sell positions when it falls, with configurable money management, stop loss, and take profit.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on directional changes in the FatlMacd indicator, which uses a FATL digital filter combined with multiple smoothing algorithms (such as T3, JJMA, JurX, etc.) to smooth the selected price. Entry conditions: a buy position opens when the indicator value turns from declining to rising (Value[0]>Value[1] and Value[1]<Value[2]); a sell position opens when it turns from rising to declining (Value[0]<Value[1] and Value[1]>Value[2]). Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit in points, as well as reverse signal-triggered closing. Position sizing supports five modes: by free margin percentage, by balance percentage, by loss-adjusted free margin, by loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled via the MM parameter for risk proportion and the MMMode parameter for calculation method, defaulting to fixed lot mode.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于FatlMacd指标的方向变化产生信号，交易规则简洁明了
  • 支持多种平滑算法和价格类型，灵活性强，可适应不同市场环境
  • 具备完善的资金管理系统，支持五种仓位计算模式，风险控制能力较好
  • 止损止盈配置合理，默认止盈是止损的两倍，盈亏比有利
  • 提供了买卖开关控制，可以单独启用或禁用多空方向

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点的默认值偏大，可能在某些品种上不合适
  • 没有点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能导致不必要的交易损失
  • 指标计算依赖于自定义指标FatlMacd，增加了部署复杂性和依赖性
  • 缺乏对现有持仓数量的检查，可能在同一方向重复开仓
  • 默认使用H12周期，回测数据量可能不足，策略验证困难

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤，当点差超过阈值时跳过交易信号
  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免同一方向重复开仓
  • 增加移动止损功能，在盈利达到一定程度后锁定利润
  • 将止损止盈参数改为ATR动态计算，以适应不同品种的波动特性

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on FatlMacd indicator directional changes with simple and transparent trading rules
  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithms and price types, offering high flexibility for different market conditions
  • Comprehensive money management system with five position sizing modes for good risk control
  • Reasonable stop loss and take profit configuration with default 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio
  • Provides buy/sell toggle controls allowing independent enabling or disabling of long/short directions

Weaknesses

  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points may be excessively large for some instruments
  • No spread filter, which may lead to unnecessary trading losses in high-spread environments
  • Depends on the custom FatlMacd indicator, increasing deployment complexity and external dependencies
  • Lacks check for existing position count, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Default H12 timeframe means limited backtesting data availability, making strategy validation difficult

Improvements

  • Add spread filter to skip trading signals when spread exceeds a threshold
  • Add position check logic to avoid duplicate opening in the same direction
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in profits after reaching a certain gain level
  • Change stop loss and take profit to ATR-based dynamic calculation to adapt to different instruments' volatility characteristics

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 没有点差过滤，在高波动或高点差时段可能产生滑点损失
  • 依赖自定义指标FatlMacd，如果指标文件缺失或计算错误会导致EA无法正常运行
  • 止损和止盈为固定点数，未考虑市场波动率变化，可能在低波动时止损过大或高波动时止损过小
  • 没有显式的持仓数量检查，可能存在重复开仓风险

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • No spread filter, may incur slippage losses during high volatility or high spread periods
  • Dependency on custom FatlMacd indicator; missing or erroneous indicator file will prevent EA from functioning
  • Fixed stop loss and take profit in points does not account for market volatility changes; stop may be too wide in low volatility or too narrow in high volatility
  • No explicit position count check, posing risk of duplicate position opening
