摘要

一个多货币对马丁格尔式EA，同时交易10个货币对，不使用任何技术指标。当持仓盈利时增加仓位，当持仓亏损时反转方向，当整体权益偏离余额超过设定阈值时平掉所有仓位。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念是纯价格行为驱动的多货币对交易，不依赖任何技术分析指标。入场条件为：当某货币对没有持仓且可用保证金超过阈值时，按照当前方向（BUY或SELL）以最小手数开仓。出场条件有三个：一是当整体浮亏超过loss参数时全部平仓，二是当整体浮盈超过profit参数时全部平仓，三是当单笔持仓盈利超过minLot*k_closse时单独平仓。仓位管理采用类马丁格尔方法：当持仓盈利超过Lot[i]*k_change时，在相同方向追加minLot手数，同时将Lot[i]递增记录累计仓位。当持仓亏损超过-Lot[i]*k_change时，反转交易方向（Type[i]取反）并平掉当前仓位，下次将以反方向重新开仓。资金管理通过全局equity阈值控制：浮亏超loss或浮盈超profit时一键清仓所有货币对，但没有任何止损止盈设置。

Summary

A multi-currency Martingale-style EA that trades 10 currency pairs simultaneously without any technical indicators. It increases lot size when a position is profitable and reverses direction when a position is losing, closing all positions when overall equity deviates beyond configured thresholds from balance.

Strategy Analysis

The EA's strategy concept is purely price-action driven multi-currency trading without any technical analysis indicators. Entry conditions: when a currency pair has no open position and free margin exceeds the threshold, it opens a position at the minimum lot size in the current direction (BUY or SELL). Exit conditions are threefold: first, close all positions when overall floating loss exceeds the loss parameter; second, close all when floating profit exceeds the profit parameter; third, close an individual position when its profit exceeds minLot*k_closse. Position sizing employs a Martingale-like method: when a position's profit exceeds Lot[i]*k_change, it adds minLot lots in the same direction while incrementing Lot[i] to track cumulative size. When a position's loss exceeds -Lot[i]*k_change, it reverses the trading direction (Type[i] flipped) and closes the current position, re-entering in the opposite direction next time. Money management is controlled via global equity thresholds: all positions are liquidated when floating loss exceeds loss or floating profit exceeds profit, but there are no stop-loss or take-profit levels set on any individual trade.

优势

  • 多货币对分散风险，同时交易10个品种降低单一品种依赖
  • 有全局浮亏和浮盈保护机制，防止极端行情下过度亏损
  • 盈利加仓策略在趋势行情中可以放大收益

劣势

  • 类马丁格尔加仓策略在单边亏损行情中会导致仓位越积越大，风险极高
  • 没有任何止损设置，单笔亏损可能无限扩大直到触发全局阈值
  • 反转方向后立即重新开仓没有等待确认，可能在震荡行情中频繁反复亏损
  • 10个货币对同时运行且共用同一个on_trade开关，一个品种触发全局平仓会影响所有品种
  • 编码不规范，变量命名混乱，注释乱码（非UTF-8编码），k_closse拼写错误

改进建议

  • 为每笔交易设置独立的止损和止盈，不依赖全局阈值作为唯一保护
  • 加入技术指标或趋势过滤器避免在震荡行情中反复反转亏损
  • 将全局平仓开关改为按货币对独立控制，避免单一品种影响全局

Strengths

  • Multi-currency diversification reduces reliance on any single instrument by trading 10 pairs simultaneously
  • Global floating loss and profit protection mechanisms prevent excessive drawdowns during extreme market moves
  • Profit-adding position sizing can amplify returns during trending markets

Weaknesses

  • Martingale-like position adding causes positions to grow increasingly large during sustained losing moves, creating extreme risk
  • No stop-loss on any individual trade means losses can expand indefinitely until the global threshold triggers
  • Direction reversal with immediate re-entry lacks confirmation, causing frequent whipsaw losses in ranging markets
  • 10 currency pairs share a single on_trade switch, so one pair triggering global closure affects all positions
  • Poor coding practices: inconsistent variable naming, garbled comments (non-UTF-8 encoding), and typo in k_closse parameter name

Improvements

  • Add individual stop-loss and take-profit for each trade instead of relying solely on global equity thresholds for protection
  • Incorporate technical indicators or trend filters to avoid repeated direction reversals in choppy/ranging markets
  • Change the global closure switch to per-pair independent control so one pair doesn't trigger closure of all positions

风险点

  • 马丁格尔式加仓策略在持续亏损时仓位无限扩大，可能导致爆仓
  • 无单笔止损，完全依赖全局浮亏阈值作为风控，一旦快速跳空可能来不及平仓
  • 方向反转后无等待期无确认机制，震荡市中会产生连续亏损
  • 多品种共用全局开关，一个品种触发清仓导致其他品种被强制平仓

Risk Assessment

Overall:DANGEROUS

Risk Points

  • Martingale-style position adding can cause positions to grow infinitely during sustained losses, potentially leading to margin call
  • No individual stop-loss means risk control depends entirely on global floating loss threshold, which may fail during fast gap moves
  • Direction reversal with no wait period or confirmation causes consecutive losses in choppy markets
  • Multi-pair shared global switch means one pair triggering closure forces all other positions to close
