摘要

这是一个多货币对马丁格尔式EA，同时交易10个货币对，不使用任何技术指标。当持仓盈利时加仓，亏损时反转方向，当整体投资组合盈亏达到阈值时平掉所有仓位。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念是基于多货币对的网格马丁格尔变体：不使用任何技术分析指标，而是通过盈亏反馈来决定交易方向和仓位大小。入场条件为：当没有持仓且可用保证金超过阈值时，按预设方向（BUY或SELL）以最小手数开仓。出场条件包括三种：一是当整体权益与余额之差超过止损线(loss)或止盈线(profit)时关闭所有仓位；二是当单个持仓盈利超过Lot[i]*k_change时加仓；三是当单个持仓亏损超过-Lot[i]*k_change时反转方向并平仓。仓位管理采用类马丁格尔方式：盈利时加仓（Lot递增minLot），方向反转时重置为最小手数，这实际上是一种反马丁格尔策略（盈利加仓）。资金管理通过全局止损/止盈参数和保证金门槛来控制，但缺乏单笔止损保护，风险敞口可能无限放大。

Summary

A multi-currency martingale-style EA that simultaneously trades 10 currency pairs without any technical indicators. It increases position size when a trade is profitable and reverses direction when losing, closing all positions when overall portfolio profit or loss thresholds are hit.

Strategy Analysis

The EA's strategy concept is a multi-currency anti-martingale variant that uses profit/loss feedback rather than technical indicators to drive trading decisions. Entry conditions: when no position exists for a symbol and free margin exceeds the threshold, it opens a position in the predetermined direction (BUY or SELL) at minimum lot size. Exit conditions include three mechanisms: first, when the portfolio-wide equity-balance difference exceeds the loss or profit threshold, all positions across all 10 pairs are closed simultaneously; second, when an individual position's profit exceeds Lot[i]*k_change, a new minimum-lot trade is added in the same direction (position scaling); third, when an individual position's loss exceeds -Lot[i]*k_change, the direction is reversed and the position is closed. Position sizing follows an anti-martingale approach: the lot size increases by minLot each time the position is profitable enough, and resets to minLot upon direction reversal. Money management relies on global portfolio-level stop-loss and take-profit thresholds plus a margin requirement for new entries, but lacks per-trade stop-loss protection, allowing risk exposure to grow significantly during adverse market conditions.

优势

  • 多货币对分散风险，不依赖单一货币对走势
  • 反马丁格尔策略在趋势行情中可实现盈利加仓放大收益
  • 全局止损和止盈机制提供组合级别的风险边界
  • 无需技术指标，逻辑简单直观，便于理解和调试

劣势

  • 反马丁格尔在震荡市中反复反转会导致频繁亏损和手续费侵蚀
  • 缺乏单笔止损保护，单品种亏损可能远超预期
  • 加仓机制可能使持仓手数不断增大，导致保证金不足或爆仓风险
  • 所有货币对使用相同的参数，未考虑各品种的波动率差异
  • 全局止损阈值1900在组合10个货币对时可能过小，容易误触发

改进建议

  • 为每个货币对添加独立的止损和止盈，而非仅依赖全局阈值
  • 根据各货币对的ATR或波动率动态调整k_change和k_closse参数
  • 添加新K线检查（new bar check），避免每个tick都执行交易逻辑导致过度交易
  • 加入点差过滤和最大持仓手数限制，防止极端行情下的过度敞口

Strengths

  • Multi-currency diversification reduces dependence on any single pair's movement
  • Anti-martingale approach amplifies gains during trending markets by scaling into winners
  • Portfolio-level stop-loss and take-profit provide overall risk boundaries
  • No technical indicators required; logic is simple and straightforward for debugging

Weaknesses

  • Anti-martingale causes repeated reversals and commission drain in ranging markets
  • No per-trade stop-loss protection; individual position losses can far exceed expectations
  • Position scaling can cause lot sizes to grow uncontrollably, risking margin calls
  • Same parameters applied to all 10 pairs without accounting for individual volatility differences
  • Global stop-loss of 1900 may be too tight for a 10-pair portfolio, causing premature liquidation

Improvements

  • Add per-symbol stop-loss and take-profit instead of relying solely on portfolio-level thresholds
  • Dynamically adjust k_change and k_closse based on each pair's ATR or volatility characteristics
  • Add new bar check to prevent excessive trading on every tick
  • Implement spread filtering and maximum position size limits to prevent overexposure in extreme markets

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:DANGEROUS

风险点

  • 反马丁格尔加仓机制可导致持仓手数无限增长，在极端行情下面临爆仓风险
  • 缺乏单笔止损，单品种最大亏损仅由k_change反转条件控制，但反转后立即反向开仓可能再次亏损
  • 全局止损可能被跳空缺口穿越，实际亏损可能远超设定值
  • 10个货币对同时持仓且无关联性风控，可能在黑天鹅事件中遭受系统性亏损

Risk Assessment

Overall:DANGEROUS

Risk Points

  • Anti-martingale scaling can cause unlimited lot growth, risking account blowup in extreme markets
  • No per-trade stop-loss; maximum per-symbol loss is only controlled by k_change reversal, but immediate re-entry in opposite direction can compound losses
  • Global stop-loss can be gapped through during illiquid periods, resulting in actual losses far exceeding the threshold
  • Simultaneous 10-pair positions without correlation risk management can cause systemic losses during black swan events
