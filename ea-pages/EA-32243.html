摘要

该EA实现了基于马丁格尔策略的交易，在亏损后增大仓位规模，通常通过加倍手数来回收之前的亏损。它根据初始信号开仓，然后在后续亏损头寸上递增手数。由于仓位呈指数增长，该策略内在风险极高。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略概念是马丁格尔法，即在每次亏损后将下一笔交易的手数倍增，以期通过一次盈利覆盖所有之前的累积亏损。入场条件通常基于简单的技术指标信号或固定条件触发首笔交易，后续入场则由前一笔交易的亏损状态决定。出场条件为当累计盈利达到目标时平仓所有头寸，或者当账户资金不足以支撑继续加仓时被动停止。仓位管理采用倍增模式，首仓使用初始手数，每亏损一次手数翻倍（或按设定乘数增加），这是策略最关键也最危险的环节。资金管理方面缺乏硬性止损和最大回撤限制，理论上在无限资金的假设下可以盈利，但实际交易中连续亏损会导致账户爆仓。整体而言，该策略的数学期望并不正向，长期来看存在极高的账户清零风险。

Summary

This EA implements a Martingale-based trading strategy where position size is increased after losing trades, typically doubling the lot size to recover previous losses. It opens trades based on initial signals and then escalates lot sizes on subsequent losing positions. The strategy inherently carries high risk due to exponential position growth.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy concept of this EA is the Martingale method, which doubles the lot size after each losing trade, aiming to recover all accumulated losses with a single winning trade. Entry conditions are typically based on simple technical indicator signals or fixed conditions to trigger the first trade, with subsequent entries determined by the loss status of the preceding trade. Exit conditions involve closing all positions when cumulative profit reaches a target, or passively stopping when account funds are insufficient to support further position increases. Position sizing follows a multiplication model: the first position uses an initial lot size, and each subsequent loss doubles (or increases by a set multiplier) the lot size, which is both the most critical and most dangerous aspect of the strategy. Money management lacks hard stop-losses and maximum drawdown limits; theoretically profitable under the assumption of infinite capital, but in practice, consecutive losses can lead to account blow-up. Overall, the mathematical expectation of this strategy is not positive, and it carries extremely high risk of account wipeout in the long run.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单直观，易于理解和部署
  • 在震荡行情中短期内可能获得稳定小利
  • 代码结构清晰，参数可调节性强

劣势

  • 马丁格尔策略在连续亏损时手数指数增长，极易爆仓
  • 缺乏硬性止损保护，极端行情下账户风险不可控
  • 长期数学期望为负，不具备持续盈利能力
  • 未考虑点差和手续费对递增仓位的累积侵蚀效应

改进建议

  • 增加全局最大回撤限制和硬性止损，防止账户清零
  • 加入趋势过滤指标，避免在单边行情中逆势加仓
  • 限制马丁格尔层数并设置资金占用比例上限

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is simple and intuitive, easy to understand and deploy
  • May achieve stable small profits in ranging markets in the short term
  • Clear code structure with highly adjustable parameters

Weaknesses

  • Martingale strategy leads to exponential lot growth during consecutive losses,极易 causing account blow-up
  • Lacks hard stop-loss protection, account risk is uncontrollable in extreme market conditions
  • Long-term mathematical expectation is negative, lacks sustainable profitability
  • Does not account for the cumulative erosion effect of spreads and commissions on escalating positions

Improvements

  • Add global maximum drawdown limits and hard stop-losses to prevent account wipeout
  • Add trend filter indicators to avoid adding positions against strong trends
  • Limit Martingale levels and set maximum capital occupation ratio caps
