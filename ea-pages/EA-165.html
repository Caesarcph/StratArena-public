EA-165 Martingale / 马丁格尔 DANGEROUS 交易 EA ★☆☆☆☆

tootrix

This EA uses TriX indicator crossovers to generate buy/sell signals with a martingale position sizing system. It enters long when the fast TriX turns up and the slow TriX is rising, and enters short on the inverse condition, applying lot doubling after losses. Fixed stop loss and take profit levels are set on each trade.

摘要

该EA使用两个不同周期的TriX指标生成交易信号，将动量反转检测与马丁格尔仓位管理系统相结合。当TriX呈现V形反转形态且第二个TriX确认动量方向时开仓，亏损后通过内置马丁格尔库加倍仓位。

策略分析

策略概念基于TriX指标的三重平滑动量反转检测。入场条件：快速TriX出现V形底反转（t[0]>t[1]且t[1]<t[2]）且慢速TriX上升时做多；快速TriX出现倒V形顶反转（t[0]<t[1]且t[1]>t[2]）且慢速TriX下降时做空。出场条件依赖固定止损和止盈，止损500点止盈1500点，盈亏比3:1。仓位管理采用马丁格尔策略，通过Martingail库在亏损后加倍手数，DML参数控制初始手数大小，Ud参数控制加倍次数。资金管理方面，使用全局变量保存马丁格尔状态，确保EA重启后能恢复加仓进度。该策略仅在无持仓时开仓，每次只持有一个头寸。

Summary

This EA uses two TriX indicators with different periods to generate trading signals, combining momentum reversal detection with a martingale position sizing system. It opens buy/sell positions when TriX shows a V-shaped reversal pattern and the second TriX confirms momentum direction, while doubling lot size after losses via the included Martingail library.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on TriX indicator triple-smoothed momentum reversal detection. Entry conditions: go long when the fast TriX shows a V-bottom reversal (t[0]>t[1] and t[1]<t[2]) and the slow TriX is rising; go short when the fast TriX shows an inverted V-top reversal (t[0]<t[1] and t[1]>t[2]) and the slow TriX is falling. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit, with 500-point SL and 1500-point TP, yielding a 3:1 reward-to-risk ratio. Position sizing uses a martingale strategy via the Martingail library, doubling lot size after losses, where DML controls initial lot sizing and Ud controls the number of doubling steps. Money management persists the martingale state using global variables so the EA can resume its progression after restarts. The strategy only opens positions when no existing position is held, maintaining at most one position at a time.

优势

  • 使用TriX三重平滑指标能有效过滤市场噪音，减少假信号
  • 止盈止损比3:1较为合理，单笔盈利期望值较高
  • 双TriX确认机制增加信号可靠性，快速周期抓反转、慢速周期确认趋势

劣势

  • 马丁格尔策略在连续亏损时会导致仓位指数级增长，存在爆仓风险
  • 快速和慢速TriX周期均为9，缺乏差异化确认，两个指标实质相同
  • 仅限单持仓交易，在震荡行情中可能频繁止损后加倍导致资金快速消耗
  • 没有点差过滤和交易时间过滤，在不利条件下仍会开仓

改进建议

  • 将快速和慢速TriX周期设置为不同值（如Fast=9, Slow=21），实现真正的多周期确认
  • 加入最大连续亏损次数限制和最大仓位上限，防止马丁格尔失控
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免在不利条件下开仓

Strengths

  • TriX triple-smoothing indicator effectively filters market noise and reduces false signals
  • 3:1 reward-to-risk ratio is reasonable with high expected value per winning trade
  • Dual TriX confirmation mechanism increases signal reliability: fast period catches reversals, slow period confirms trend

Weaknesses

  • Martingale sizing causes exponential lot growth during losing streaks, risking account blowup
  • Both Fast and Slow TriX periods are set to 9, providing no differentiated confirmation — both indicators are effectively identical
  • Single-position limitation means frequent stop-outs in choppy markets can rapidly deplete capital through doubling
  • No spread filter or trading session filter, so positions open even under unfavorable conditions

Improvements

  • Set different periods for fast and slow TriX (e.g., Fast=9, Slow=21) to achieve genuine multi-timeframe confirmation
  • Add maximum consecutive loss count and maximum position size cap to prevent martingale from spiraling out of control
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid opening positions under unfavorable conditions
