摘要

该EA使用两个不同周期的TriX指标产生入场信号，并结合马丁格尔仓位管理在连续亏损后加倍手数。它根据TriX交叉形态开立买入或卖出仓位，仅通过固定止损和止盈管理出场。马丁格尔组件通过全局变量在重启后保持状态。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念基于双TriX指标的动量交叉：快周期TriX(Fast=9)检测短期动量反转，慢周期TriX(Slow=9)确认中期趋势方向。买入条件为快TriX呈现V形反转（t[0]>t[1]且t[1]<t[2]，即触底回升）且慢TriX上升（k[1]>k[0]）；卖出条件相反，快TriX呈倒V形（触顶回落）且慢TriX下降。出场条件完全依赖固定止损（Stop=500点）和止盈（Tp=1500点），止盈是止损的3倍，体现趋势跟踪的盈亏比设计。仓位管理采用马丁格尔策略，通过Martingail类实现：初始手数由DML=1000控制（Shape参数），每次亏损后按Ud=1次翻倍手数。资金管理方面，EA在OnDeinit中保存马丁格尔状态到全局变量，在OnInit中恢复，确保持仓状态跨重启持久化。OnTester函数使用盈利次数除以亏损次数再除以最大连续亏损系列作为优化目标，体现对风险调整收益的关注。

Summary

This EA uses two TriX indicators with different periods to generate entry signals, combined with a martingale lot-sizing system that doubles the lot size after consecutive losses. It opens buy or sell positions based on TriX crossover patterns and manages exits purely via fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels. The martingale component persists state across restarts using global variables.

Strategy Analysis

The EA's strategy concept is based on dual TriX indicator momentum crossovers: a fast TriX (Fast=9) detects short-term momentum reversals while a slow TriX (Slow=9) confirms the medium-term trend direction. The buy condition requires the fast TriX to form a V-shaped reversal (t[0]>t[1] and t[1]<t[2], i.e., bouncing off a bottom) with the slow TriX rising (k[1]>k[0]); the sell condition is the inverse, requiring an inverted-V pattern on the fast TriX with the slow TriX declining. Exit conditions rely entirely on fixed stop-loss (Stop=500 points) and take-profit (Tp=1500 points) levels, with the take-profit being 3x the stop-loss, reflecting a trend-following risk-reward design. Position sizing employs a martingale strategy via the Martingail class: the initial lot size is controlled by DML=1000 (Shape parameter), and the lot doubles after each consecutive loss with DoublingCount=Ud=1. For money management, the EA persists martingale state to global variables in OnDeinit and restores it in OnInit, ensuring position state survives restarts. The OnTester function uses profit count divided by loss count, further divided by max consecutive loss series as the optimization metric, reflecting attention to risk-adjusted returns.

优势

  • TriX指标是去趋势化的动量振荡器，能有效过滤价格噪音，识别真实的动量反转
  • 止盈是止损的3倍（1500:500），具有正向期望的盈亏比设计
  • 马丁格尔状态通过全局变量持久化，防止重启后丢失仓位递增信息
  • OnTester自定义优化指标考虑了最大连续亏损，比单纯利润更科学

劣势

  • 马丁格尔策略在连续亏损后指数级加大手数，可能导致爆仓风险极高
  • 快慢TriX使用相同周期（均为9），实际上两个指标完全相同，失去了双重确认的意义
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下短线信号容易被滑点和成本侵蚀
  • 仅检查是否有持仓（PositionSelect），不区分多空方向，可能错过同方向加仓或反向切换的灵活性
  • tick_volume>1的过滤条件在不同经纪商间表现不一致，可能错过有效信号

改进建议

  • 将快慢TriX设置为不同周期（如Fast=9, Slow=21），使双指标确认机制真正发挥作用
  • 移除马丁格尔仓位管理，改用固定比例或基于ATR的动态仓位管理，避免指数级亏损风险
  • 添加点差过滤和新的K线检查（isNewBar），避免在不利条件下重复入场
  • 区分多空持仓方向，允许同时管理多头和空头仓位

Strengths

  • TriX is a detrended momentum oscillator that effectively filters price noise and identifies genuine momentum reversals
  • The 3:1 take-profit to stop-loss ratio (1500:500) provides a positive-expectancy risk-reward design
  • Martingale state is persisted via global variables, preventing loss of lot-escalation info after restarts
  • The custom OnTester optimization metric accounts for max consecutive loss series, more scientific than pure profit

Weaknesses

  • The martingale strategy exponentially increases lot size after consecutive losses, creating extremely high risk of account blow-up
  • Both fast and slow TriX use the same period (9), making them identical and defeating the purpose of dual confirmation
  • No spread filter is present, so short-term signals can be eroded by slippage and costs in high-spread environments
  • Only checks for any open position (PositionSelect) without distinguishing direction, limiting flexibility for same-direction adds or directional switches
  • The tick_volume>1 filter behaves inconsistently across different brokers and may miss valid signals

Improvements

  • Set different periods for fast and slow TriX (e.g., Fast=9, Slow=21) to make the dual-confirmation mechanism actually functional
  • Remove martingale position sizing and replace with fixed fractional or ATR-based dynamic sizing to avoid exponential loss risk
  • Add spread filter and new bar check (isNewBar) to prevent repeated entries under unfavorable conditions
  • Distinguish between long and short positions to allow independent management of buy and sell positions

风险点

  • 马丁格尔策略在连续亏损后加倍手数，可快速耗尽账户资金导致爆仓

  • 缺少最大连续翻倍次数限制，理论上手数可无限增长

Risk Assessment

Overall:DANGEROUS

Risk Points

  • Martingale lot doubling after consecutive losses can rapidly deplete account funds leading to margin call
  • Fixed 500-point stop-loss may result in actual losses far exceeding expectations due to slippage in volatile markets
  • No maximum consecutive doubling limit, theoretically allowing lot size to grow infinitely
