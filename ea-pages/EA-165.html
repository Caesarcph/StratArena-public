摘要

该EA使用TriX指标交叉产生买卖信号，并采用马丁格尔仓位管理系统。快速TriX转向上且慢速TriX上升时做多，反向条件做空，亏损后加倍下注。每笔交易设置固定止损和止盈。

策略分析

策略概念基于TriX（三重指数平滑）指标的动量反转信号进行交易。入场条件：做多要求快速TriX在最近3根K线形成V型反转（t[0]>t[1]且t[1]<t[2]）且慢速TriX上升（k[1]>k[0]），做空则相反。出场条件采用固定止损500点和止盈1500点，盈亏比为3:1。仓位管理使用马丁格尔系统，亏损后按Ud参数设定的次数加倍仓位，初始资金由DML参数控制为1000。资金管理通过全局变量保存和恢复马丁格尔状态，确保持仓倍数在EA重启后不丢失。策略仅在没有持仓时开仓，且只在tick_volume>1时跳过，缺乏新K线检测。

Summary

This EA uses TriX indicator crossovers to generate buy/sell signals with a martingale position sizing system. It enters long when the fast TriX turns up and the slow TriX is rising, and enters short on the inverse condition, applying lot doubling after losses. Fixed stop loss and take profit levels are set on each trade.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on momentum reversal signals from the TriX (Triple Exponential Smoothing) indicator. Entry conditions: a long signal requires the fast TriX to form a V-shaped reversal over 3 bars (t[0]>t[1] and t[1]<t[2]) while the slow TriX is rising (k[1]>k[0]); a short signal requires the inverse pattern. Exit conditions use a fixed stop loss of 500 points and take profit of 1500 points, giving a reward-to-risk ratio of 3:1. Position sizing employs a martingale system that doubles the lot size after losses, with the number of doubling steps controlled by the Ud parameter and the initial sizing based on DML=1000. Money management persists the martingale state via global variables, ensuring the doubling factor survives EA restarts. The EA only opens a new position when no existing position is selected, but lacks a new bar check, potentially firing signals on every tick.

优势

  • 盈亏比设定为3:1，单笔盈利目标远大于止损
  • 使用TriX指标过滤假信号，减少震荡行情中的频繁交易
  • 通过全局变量保存马丁格尔状态，EA重启后可恢复

劣势

  • 马丁格尔仓位管理在连续亏损时会指数级增加仓位，风险极高
  • 快速和慢速TriX周期均为9，缺乏差异化，信号质量差
  • 缺少新K线检测，每个tick都可能触发信号判断
  • 没有点差过滤，可能在点差较大时仍开仓
  • 仅支持单品种单持仓，无法分散风险

改进建议

  • 移除马丁格尔系统，改用固定仓位或基于ATR的动态仓位管理
  • 区分快速和慢速TriX周期（如Fast=9, Slow=21），并添加新K线检测和点差过滤

Strengths

  • The reward-to-risk ratio is set at 3:1, meaning each winning trade targets far more than the stop loss
  • TriX indicator filtering reduces false signals and limits frequent trading in choppy markets
  • Martingale state is persisted via global variables, allowing recovery after EA restarts

Weaknesses

  • Martingale position sizing exponentially increases lot size after consecutive losses, creating extreme risk
  • Both fast and slow TriX periods default to 9, providing no real differentiation and poor signal quality
  • Lacks new bar detection, potentially evaluating signals on every tick instead of once per bar
  • No spread filter means the EA may open positions even when spreads are wide
  • Only supports single-symbol single-position trading, no risk diversification

Improvements

  • Remove the martingale system and replace with fixed lot sizing or ATR-based dynamic position management
  • Differentiate fast and slow TriX periods (e.g., Fast=9, Slow=21) and add new bar detection plus spread filtering

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:DANGEROUS

风险点

  • 马丁格尔系统在连续亏损后仓位指数级放大，可能导致爆仓
  • 没有点差过滤和滑点保护不足，大点差环境下损失加剧
  • 缺少新K线检测可能导致重复信号和过度交易

  • No spread filter and insufficient slippage protection increase losses in wide-spread environments
  • Lack of new bar detection may cause repeated signals and overtrading
