摘要

该EA使用经过移动平均平滑处理的MFI（资金流量指数）作为动量信号过滤器，在MQL5标准库Expert框架内运行。根据CH_HM_MFI信号的阈值开仓和平仓，采用固定手数资金管理，无追踪止损。

策略分析

策略概念基于资金流量指数（MFI）的动量判断，通过将MFI值经过移动平均（MA）平滑处理后生成交易信号。入场条件是当CH_HM_MFI过滤器的信号强度超过开仓阈值（默认40）时开仓，信号强度低于平仓阈值（默认20）时平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数方式（默认0.1手），不根据账户余额动态调整。资金管理使用MoneyFixedLot模块，设定百分比为10%，固定手数为0.1手。止损和止盈均设为0，意味着不使用固定的止损止盈，完全依赖信号反转来平仓。挂单有效期为4根K线周期，未设置追踪止损，风险控制主要依赖信号阈值过滤。

Summary

This EA uses the MFI (Money Flow Index) smoothed by a Moving Average as a momentum-based signal filter within the MQL5 Standard Library Expert framework. It opens and closes trades based on threshold values applied to the CH_HM_MFI signal, with fixed lot money management and no trailing stop.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on momentum judgment using the Money Flow Index (MFI), smoothed by a Moving Average to generate trading signals. Entry conditions trigger when the CH_HM_MFI filter's signal strength exceeds the opening threshold (default 40), and positions close when signal strength falls below the closing threshold (default 20). Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach (default 0.1 lots) without dynamic adjustment based on account balance. Money management employs the MoneyFixedLot module with a percentage setting of 10% and a fixed lot size of 0.1. Both Stop Loss and Take Profit are set to 0, meaning no fixed stop-loss or take-profit is used, relying entirely on signal reversal for exit. Pending order expiration is set to 4 bars, and no trailing stop is configured, with risk control primarily depending on signal threshold filtering.

优势

  • 使用MFI结合MA平滑，减少虚假信号干扰，提高信号质量
  • 基于MQL5标准库Expert框架开发，代码结构规范，模块化程度高
  • 信号阈值可调，提供灵活的入场/出场控制，适应不同市场环境

劣势

  • 止损和止盈均设为0，完全依赖信号平仓，在极端行情中可能造成重大损失
  • 固定手数策略不根据账户规模和波动率动态调整，资金管理过于简单
  • 未设置追踪止损，无法在趋势行情中锁定浮动利润

改进建议

  • 添加止损和止盈参数，建议至少设置基于ATR的动态止损以控制单笔风险
  • 引入追踪止损模块替代TrailingNone，以便在趋势行情中保护利润

Strengths

  • MFI combined with MA smoothing reduces false signals and improves signal quality
  • Built on the MQL5 Standard Library Expert framework with well-structured, modular code architecture
  • Adjustable signal thresholds provide flexible entry/exit control adaptable to different market conditions

Weaknesses

  • Stop Loss and Take Profit are both set to 0, relying entirely on signal reversal for exits, which can cause significant losses in extreme market conditions
  • Fixed lot sizing does not dynamically adjust based on account size or volatility, making money management overly simplistic
  • No trailing stop configured, unable to lock in floating profits during trending markets

Improvements

  • Add Stop Loss and Take Profit parameters; recommend at least ATR-based dynamic stop-loss to control per-trade risk
  • Replace TrailingNone with a trailing stop module to protect profits during trending markets
