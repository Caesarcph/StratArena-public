Exp_ColorRsiMACD

This EA trades based on the ColorRsiMACD custom indicator, which applies RSI smoothing with configurable averaging methods before computing a MACD-style oscillator. It offers four trading modes: MACD zero-line breakdown, MACD direction twist, signal line twist, and MACD-vs-signal disposition crossover, opening and closing positions accordingly.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy constructs a MACD indicator from RSI values processed through configurable smoothing algorithms (JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, AMA, etc.), generating trading signals from this hybrid oscillator. Four entry modes are available: breakdown mode triggers on MACD crossing the zero line; MACDtwist mode triggers on MACD direction change (declining-to-rising for buys, rising-to-declining for sells); SIGNALtwist mode triggers on signal line direction change; and MACDdisposition mode triggers on MACD/signal line crossover. Exit conditions mirror entries—reverse signals close positions, supplemented by configurable StopLoss and TakeProfit levels. Position sizing is controlled via the MM parameter and MMMode enum, supporting fixed lots or percentage-of-balance sizing. Money management leverages the TradeAlgorithms library, and each buy/sell open/close action can be independently enabled or disabled.

Strengths

  • Offers four flexible trading modes adaptable to different market conditions and trading styles
  • Employs multiple advanced smoothing algorithms on RSI, reducing lag and noise compared to traditional MACD
  • Buy/sell open/close can each be independently enabled, providing high configuration flexibility
  • New bar check mechanism prevents duplicate trades within the same candle
  • Stop loss and take profit parameters are provided, giving basic risk control

Weaknesses

  • Fixed-pip stop loss and take profit ignore ATR-based dynamic volatility, which may be unsuitable in low- or high-volatility regimes
  • No spread filter; while less critical on H4, switching to lower timeframes could incur losses from excessive spreads
  • TradeAlgorithms.mqh is included twice (lines 12 and 105), indicating redundant code
  • The RSI+MACD combination remains a momentum indicator prone to frequent false signals in ranging markets
  • No position count check or maximum position limit, risking over-exposure in extreme market conditions

Possible improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit that auto-adjusts to market volatility for better adaptability
  • Add a spread filter and maximum position count check to strengthen risk control and robustness

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No spread filter could cause losses on lower timeframes due to wide spreads
  • No maximum position limit could lead to over-exposure from consecutive signals
  • Fixed-pip stops may be insufficiently flexible during extreme volatility
