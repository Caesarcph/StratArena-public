摘要

OpenTicks是一个基于Tick数据的剥头皮EA，通过实时分析逐笔报价数据来捕捉微小的价格波动。它根据Tick级别的短期价格动量而非K线指标来快速开仓和平仓，专为高流动性品种和低延迟环境设计。

策略分析

OpenTicks的策略核心是基于Tick数据的超短线剥头皮交易。入场条件依赖于实时Tick流的方向性动量：当连续的Tick价格变化累积超过某个阈值时，EA判断短期动量方向并顺势开仓。出场条件包括固定点数的止盈和止损，以及基于Tick反向动量的动态平仓机制。仓位管理采用固定手数或基于账户余额的简单比例计算，没有复杂的仓位缩放逻辑。资金管理方面，EA依赖紧密的止损来控制单笔风险，但由于是Tick级别交易，滑点和点差对实际盈亏影响极大。整体策略逻辑简单直接，属于典型的高频低利剥头皮风格，对经纪商的执行质量和交易成本高度敏感。

Summary

OpenTicks is a tick-based scalping EA that aims to capture small price movements by analyzing incoming tick data in real time. It opens and closes positions rapidly based on short-term price momentum derived from tick-level changes rather than bar-based indicators. The EA is designed for high-liquidity instruments and low-latency environments.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of OpenTicks is ultra-short-term tick-based scalping. Entry conditions rely on directional momentum derived from the real-time tick stream: when the cumulative price change across consecutive ticks exceeds a defined threshold, the EA identifies short-term momentum direction and opens a position in that direction. Exit conditions include a fixed take-profit and stop-loss in points, as well as a dynamic closing mechanism triggered by opposing tick momentum. Position sizing uses either a fixed lot size or a simple proportional calculation based on account balance, without complex scaling logic. For money management, the EA relies on tight stop-losses to control per-trade risk, but since it operates at the tick level, slippage and spread have an outsized impact on actual profitability. The overall strategy logic is straightforward, embodying a classic high-frequency low-margin scalping style that is highly sensitive to broker execution quality and transaction costs.

优势

  • Tick级别分析能捕捉极短期的价格变动，反应速度远快于K线指标
  • 策略逻辑简洁清晰，参数少，易于理解和调优
  • 内置点差过滤和魔术编号管理，具备基本的风险控制意识

劣势

  • Tick级剥头皮对经纪商执行质量极其敏感，滑点可能完全吞噬利润
  • 缺乏新K线检查机制，可能在同一根K线上反复开仓导致过度交易
  • 没有马丁格尔或网格机制但也缺少仓位验证和重复订单检查，存在风险敞口

改进建议

  • 增加新K线检查和持仓检查逻辑，避免重复开仓和过度交易
  • 加入更精细的资金管理模块，如基于ATR的动态止损止盈和风险百分比仓位计算

Strengths

  • Tick-level analysis captures extremely short-term price movements with reaction speed far exceeding bar-based indicators
  • The strategy logic is concise and clear with few parameters, making it easy to understand and tune
  • Built-in spread filter and magic number management demonstrate basic risk control awareness

Weaknesses

  • Tick-level scalping is extremely sensitive to broker execution quality; slippage can completely erode profits
  • Lacks a new bar check mechanism, potentially opening repeated positions on the same bar leading to overtrading
  • No Martingale or grid mechanism but also lacks position verification and duplicate order checks, leaving risk exposure

Improvements

  • Add new bar check and position verification logic to prevent duplicate entries and overtrading
  • Implement a more sophisticated money management module, such as ATR-based dynamic SL/TP and risk-percentage position sizing
