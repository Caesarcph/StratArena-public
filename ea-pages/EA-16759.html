摘要

该EA是一个全局止损管理工具，监控账户整体权益或浮亏，当累计浮亏超过预设阈值时自动平掉所有持仓。它不自行开仓，仅作为账户级风险防护手段，在亏损达到设定值时强制清仓。

策略分析

策略概念：该EA并非交易策略，而是账户级止损保护工具。入场条件：无入场逻辑，EA不主动开仓。出场条件：当账户总浮亏达到或超过参数设定的止损金额时，EA会遍历所有持仓并逐一平仓，实现全局止损。仓位管理：不涉及仓位管理，EA仅负责平仓操作。资金管理：核心功能是资金保护，通过设定最大允许亏损额来限制账户风险暴露。该工具适合同时运行多个策略的账户，作为最后一道防线防止极端行情下的巨额亏损。

Summary

This EA is a global stop-loss management utility that monitors the overall account equity or profit and closes all positions when a predefined loss threshold is reached. It does not open any trades on its own but acts as an account-level risk safeguard by liquidating all open positions if the cumulative floating loss exceeds the configured parameter.

Strategy Analysis

Strategy concept: This EA is not a trading strategy but an account-level stop-loss protection tool. Entry conditions: None — the EA does not open any positions. Exit conditions: When the total account floating loss reaches or exceeds the configured stop-loss threshold, the EA iterates through all open positions and closes them one by one, executing a global stop-out. Position sizing: Not applicable — the EA only handles position closure. Money management: The core function is capital protection, limiting account risk exposure by setting a maximum allowable loss amount. This tool is suitable for accounts running multiple strategies simultaneously, serving as a last line of defense against catastrophic losses during extreme market conditions.

优势

  • 作为账户级安全网，能有效防止极端行情下的爆仓风险
  • 实现简单直观，用户只需设置一个止损金额参数即可使用
  • 可与其他EA配合使用，不影响原有策略运行，独立性强

劣势

  • 仅在浮亏达到阈值后才平仓，无法在亏损较小时进行早期预警或分批减仓
  • 平仓时未考虑滑点和流动性问题，在剧烈波动时可能无法按预期价格平仓
  • 单一止损金额参数缺乏灵活性，无法根据账户规模动态调整或按百分比设置

改进建议

  • 增加按账户余额百分比设置止损的功能，使风险控制更具适应性
  • 添加分批平仓机制和预警通知功能，在接近止损阈值时先减仓或发出警报
  • 加入滑点保护和重试逻辑，提高在极端行情下的平仓可靠性

Strengths

  • Acts as an account-level safety net, effectively preventing margin call risk during extreme market moves
  • Simple and intuitive implementation — users only need to set one stop-loss amount parameter
  • Can work alongside other EAs without interfering with existing strategies, maintaining strong independence

Weaknesses

  • Only closes positions after floating loss reaches the threshold — no early warning or partial position reduction at smaller loss levels
  • Does not account for slippage and liquidity issues — may fail to close at expected prices during violent market moves
  • Single stop-loss amount parameter lacks flexibility — cannot dynamically adjust based on account size or set percentage-based thresholds

Improvements

  • Add percentage-based stop-loss relative to account balance for more adaptive risk control
  • Implement partial closing mechanism and alert notifications — reduce positions or trigger warnings before reaching the stop-loss threshold
  • Add slippage protection and retry logic to improve closure reliability during extreme market conditions

  • 在极端行情下平仓可能因滑点导致实际亏损超过设定的止损金额
  • 如果账户同时运行多个EA，全局平仓可能影响其他策略的正常运作

Overall:SAFE

Risk Points

  • During extreme market conditions, slippage may cause actual losses to exceed the configured stop-loss amount
  • If the account runs multiple EAs simultaneously, the global close-all may disrupt other strategies' normal operations
