Waddah_Attar_Win

Waddah Attar Win is a trend-following EA based on the Waddah Attar Explosion indicator, which combines MACD histogram analysis with Bollinger Band and moving average convergence to identify explosive trend movements. It enters trades when the indicator detects a strong directional breakout confirmed by expanding momentum and trend alignment, aiming to capture large trend moves from early breakout stages.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the Waddah Attar Explosion indicator, a composite indicator that combines MACD momentum analysis with Bollinger Band volatility analysis. Entry conditions require the MACD histogram to cross the zero line and expand while Bollinger Band width expansion confirms a trend explosion, with indicator line color and direction confirming the trend direction. Long entries need the indicator showing positive values with increasing upward momentum, while short entries need negative values with increasing downward momentum. Exit conditions typically include the appearance of a reversal signal or fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels. Position sizing supports fixed lot trading mode, with some versions including dynamic position calculation based on account balance. Money management is relatively simple, relying on the user to set reasonable stop-loss distances and lot size ratios, lacking automatic risk percentage calculation mechanisms.

Strengths

  • Dual confirmation combining MACD momentum and Bollinger Band volatility reduces false signals
  • Clear trend explosion identification logic captures early major trend moves
  • Dead zone filtering mechanism effectively filters noise signals during market consolidation

Weaknesses

  • Stop-loss and take-profit default to 0, lacking mandatory risk control protection, posing significant capital risk
  • Missing new bar check mechanism, potentially opening repeated positions on the same bar
  • May generate consecutive losing signals in ranging markets, lacking market state adaptive switching

Possible improvements

  • Add mandatory stop-loss setting and new bar check mechanism to prevent repeated entries and unlimited losses
  • Incorporate ranging/trending market state identification to automatically reduce position size or pause trading during consolidation

Risk assessment

Overall risk: High risk

  • Stop-loss defaults to 0, exposing the user to unlimited loss risk if not manually configured
  • Missing new bar check may cause multiple position openings on the same bar leading to overexposure
