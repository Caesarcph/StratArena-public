摘要

该EA实现了基于双均线交叉的趋势跟踪策略，使用Fast2自定义指标的两条平滑移动平均线来生成买卖信号。当快线上穿慢线时做多，反之下穿时做空，并支持可配置的止损和止盈。两条均线均支持多种平滑算法（SMA、EMA、JJMA、JurX、ParMA、T3、VIDYA、AMA）。

策略分析

该策略基于双平滑移动平均线交叉的经典趋势跟踪方法，使用Fast2自定义指标提供两条可配置平滑方式的均线。入场条件为：当Ma1从下方上穿Ma2时开多仓，当Ma1从上方下穿Ma2时开空仓，交叉判断通过比较前后两根K线的均线相对位置实现。出场条件包括止损和止盈，同时支持反向信号平仓，即出现空头信号时平多仓、出现多头信号时平空仓。仓位管理采用基于账户余额百分比的资金管理方式（MM参数），支持固定手数和百分比两种模式（LOT和FREEMARGIN）。策略在新K线形成时才进行信号判断，避免同一根K线内重复开仓，并使用IsNewBar机制进行控制。

Summary

This EA implements a dual moving average crossover strategy using two smoothed moving averages (Fast2 indicator) to generate buy and sell signals. It enters long when the faster MA crosses above the slower MA and enters short on the opposite crossover, with configurable stop loss and take profit levels. The EA supports multiple smoothing algorithms (SMA, EMA, JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, AMA) for both moving average lines.

Strategy Analysis

This strategy is based on the classic dual smoothed moving average crossover trend-following method, using the Fast2 custom indicator which provides two configurable smoothing algorithm lines. Entry conditions: open long when Ma1 crosses above Ma2, open short when Ma1 crosses below Ma2, with crossover detection implemented by comparing the relative positions of the two MAs on the current and previous bars. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit levels, as well as reverse signal closure where a bearish signal closes long positions and a bullish signal closes short positions. Position sizing uses a percentage-of-account money management approach via the MM parameter, supporting both fixed lot and percentage modes (LOT and FREEMARGIN). The strategy only evaluates signals on new bar formation to avoid repeated entries within the same bar, controlled by the IsNewBar mechanism.

优势

  • 支持多达10种平滑算法，策略灵活性极高，可适应不同市场环境
  • 买卖开仓和平仓均可独立开关控制，便于精细化管理策略行为
  • 采用新K线判断机制，避免同根K线内重复交易，减少过度交易风险
  • 止损止盈参数可配置，具备基本的风险控制框架
  • 使用已收盘K线（SignalBar=1）判断信号，避免未收盘K线的信号闪烁

劣势

  • 均线交叉策略在震荡市中容易产生频繁虚假信号，导致连续亏损
  • 缺乏趋势过滤器（如ADX或更高周期趋势判断），在非趋势行情中无法有效过滤假信号
  • 止损1000点、止盈2000点的默认值过大，在大多数品种上风险敞口过高
  • 没有点差过滤机制，在高点差环境下开仓可能导致不必要的亏损
  • 缺乏回撤控制和最大持仓限制，极端行情下可能造成严重损失
  • min_rates_total计算被后续赋值覆盖（第84-85行），前面的累加逻辑实际无效

改进建议

  • 添加趋势过滤器（如ADX或更高周期均线方向），仅在趋势行情中开仓以减少震荡市中的假信号
  • 添加点差过滤和最大回撤限制，提高策略在不同市场条件下的鲁棒性
  • 修复min_rates_total的计算逻辑（删除第85行的覆盖赋值），使均线起始数据需求正确生效
  • 考虑添加移动止损（Trailing Stop）功能，在趋势行情中更好地保护浮盈

Strengths

  • Supports up to 10 smoothing algorithms providing high strategy flexibility to adapt to different market conditions
  • Buy/sell open and close can be independently toggled, enabling fine-grained control over strategy behavior
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated trades within the same bar, reducing overtrading risk
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit parameters provide a basic risk control framework
  • Uses closed bar (SignalBar=1) for signal detection, avoiding signal flickering on uncompleted bars

Weaknesses

  • MA crossover strategy generates frequent false signals in ranging markets, leading to consecutive losses
  • Lacks a trend filter (e.g., ADX or higher timeframe trend confirmation), unable to effectively filter false signals in non-trending conditions
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large, creating oversized risk exposure on most instruments
  • No spread filter mechanism, opening positions in high-spread environments can lead to unnecessary losses
  • Lacks drawdown control and maximum position limits, extreme market conditions could cause severe losses
  • min_rates_total calculation is overwritten by subsequent assignment (lines 84-85), making the earlier accumulation logic effectively dead code

Improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., ADX or higher timeframe MA direction) to only enter trades in trending markets, reducing false signals in ranging conditions
  • Add spread filter and maximum drawdown limit to improve strategy robustness under varying market conditions
  • Fix the min_rates_total calculation logic (remove the overriding assignment on line 85) so the MA start bar requirements take effect correctly
  • Consider adding a trailing stop feature to better protect unrealized profits during trending moves
