摘要

一个采用马丁格尔式多手数递进策略的剥头皮EA，在亏损交易后加倍或增大手数以回收损失。它基于简单的技术信号入场，并依靠递增的仓位在市场反转时最终获利平仓。该EA设计用于小获利目标的短期交易，但由于其马丁格尔仓位管理方式而承担着显著的风险。

策略分析

策略概念基于马丁格尔原理：每次亏损后增大手数，期望一次赢利覆盖之前所有亏损。入场条件可能依赖简单指标信号如移动平均线交叉或RSI超买超卖来判断短期价格反转点。出场条件通常为固定的止盈点数目标，止损可能缺失或设置较宽以避免过早被止损出局。仓位管理是核心特征：初始手数较小，每次亏损后按倍数或固定比例增加手数，形成1-2-4-8的经典马丁格尔序列。资金管理方面缺乏有效的最大持仓限制或最大回撤保护，在连续亏损行情中账户面临爆仓风险。整体策略在震荡行情中可能表现良好，但在单边趋势中会造成灾难性损失。

Summary

A scalping EA that employs a martingale-style multi-lot progression strategy, doubling or increasing lot sizes after losing trades to recover losses. It enters positions based on simple technical signals and relies on the increasing position sizes to eventually close in profit when the market reverses. The EA is designed for short-term trades with small take-profit targets but carries significant risk due to its martingale position sizing.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the martingale principle: increasing lot size after each loss, expecting a single win to recover all previous losses. Entry conditions likely rely on simple technical indicators such as moving average crossovers or RSI overbought/oversold levels to identify short-term price reversal points. Exit conditions typically use a fixed take-profit in pips target, while stop-loss may be absent or set very wide to avoid premature stop-outs. Position sizing is the core feature: starting with a small initial lot, each loss triggers a multiplication or fixed-ratio increase in lot size, forming the classic 1-2-4-8 martingale sequence. Money management lacks effective maximum position limits or maximum drawdown protection, exposing the account to margin call risk during consecutive losing streaks. The overall strategy may perform well in ranging markets but can cause catastrophic losses during strong trending conditions.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中可以稳定获利，小TP目标容易达成
  • 马丁格尔策略理论上可以回收之前所有亏损
  • 参数简单易懂，用户可以快速配置和调整

劣势

  • 马丁格尔策略在单边行情中可能导致爆仓
  • 缺少止损设置使风险无限放大
  • 手数指数增长会很快触及经纪商最大手数限制或账户保证金不足
  • 连续亏损时心理和资金压力极大

改进建议

  • 增加严格的最大回撤保护机制，当回撤超过阈值时停止交易
  • 添加有效的止损逻辑替代纯马丁格尔依赖
  • 加入趋势过滤器，在单边行情中暂停马丁格尔加仓

Strengths

  • Can generate steady profits in ranging markets where small TP targets are easily hit
  • Martingale progression theoretically recovers all previous losses with a single winning trade
  • Simple and intuitive parameters allow users to quickly configure and adjust settings

Weaknesses

  • Martingale strategy can lead to account blow-up during strong trending markets
  • Absence of stop-loss exposes the account to unlimited downside risk
  • Exponential lot growth quickly hits broker maximum lot limits or insufficient margin
  • Extreme psychological and financial pressure during consecutive losing streaks

Improvements

  • Add strict maximum drawdown protection that halts trading when drawdown exceeds a threshold
  • Implement effective stop-loss logic instead of relying purely on martingale recovery
  • Incorporate a trend filter to pause martingale position increases during strong trending markets
