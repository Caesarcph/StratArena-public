摘要

该EA是一个剥头皮交易系统，采用马丁格尔仓位管理方式，在每次亏损后加倍手数，同时追求小额止盈目标。它基于简单价格行为或指标信号开立市价单，依赖马丁格尔恢复逻辑而非止损来进行风险管理。策略每笔交易追求小幅点差收益，假设最终会有一次盈利交易来回收之前的亏损。

策略分析

该EA的策略核心是马丁格尔仓位加倍法，在每次亏损后将下一笔交易的手数翻倍，试图通过一次盈利来覆盖所有之前的损失。入场条件通常基于简单的价格突破或基础指标信号（如移动平均线交叉），在剥头皮时间框架上寻找小幅波动机会。出场条件仅设有一个较小的固定止盈点数，而止损则依赖于马丁格尔机制本身而非传统止损单。仓位管理采用倍增模式，初始手数较小但经过连续亏损后会指数级增长，存在账户爆仓的极端风险。资金管理方面几乎没有保护机制，没有最大连续亏损次数限制，也没有账户权益回撤保护。整体而言，这种策略在连续亏损行情中极其危险，可能导致账户资金迅速耗尽。

Summary

This EA is a scalping system that uses a martingale position sizing approach, doubling lot size after each losing trade while aiming for small take-profit targets. It opens market orders based on simple price action or indicator signals and relies on the martingale recovery logic rather than stop-losses for risk management. The strategy targets small pip gains on each trade, assuming that a winning trade will eventually recover previous losses.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is a martingale lot-doubling approach, where the lot size is doubled after each losing trade, attempting to recover all previous losses with a single win. Entry conditions are typically based on simple price breakouts or basic indicator signals such as moving average crossovers, seeking small price movements on scalping timeframes. Exit conditions only include a small fixed take-profit in pips, while stop-loss relies on the martingale mechanism itself rather than traditional stop-loss orders. Position sizing follows an exponential doubling pattern, starting with a small initial lot but growing exponentially after consecutive losses, creating an extreme risk of account blow-up. Money management has virtually no protective mechanisms—there is no limit on maximum consecutive losses and no equity drawdown protection. Overall, this strategy is extremely dangerous during losing streaks and can rapidly deplete account funds.

优势

  • 代码逻辑简单直观，易于理解和修改
  • 在小幅震荡行情中可能获得稳定的小额盈利
  • 马丁格尔在理论上能保证最终盈利（前提是资金无限）

劣势

  • 马丁格尔仓位加倍在连续亏损时会导致手数指数级增长，极易爆仓
  • 缺乏止损机制，单笔亏损可能极大
  • 没有最大手数上限和账户回撤保护，资金管理极其薄弱
  • 在趋势行情中会持续逆势加仓，亏损不断放大
  • 对经纪商点差和滑点敏感，剥头皮策略成本高

改进建议

  • 增加最大连续亏损次数限制和最大手数上限，防止爆仓
  • 添加真实止损而非仅依赖马丁格尔恢复机制
  • 加入账户权益回撤保护，达到最大回撤时停止交易

Strengths

  • Simple and intuitive code logic, easy to understand and modify
  • May achieve consistent small profits in ranging/sideways markets
  • Martingale theoretically guarantees eventual profit (assuming infinite capital)

Weaknesses

  • Martingale lot doubling causes exponential position growth during losing streaks,极易导致账户爆仓
  • Lacks stop-loss mechanism, single trade loss can be enormous
  • No maximum lot size cap or account drawdown protection, money management is extremely weak
  • Continues adding positions against the trend in trending markets, compounding losses
  • Sensitive to broker spread and slippage, scalping strategy costs are high

Improvements

  • Add maximum consecutive loss count limit and maximum lot size cap to prevent account blow-up
  • Add actual stop-loss instead of relying solely on martingale recovery mechanism
  • Implement equity drawdown protection that stops trading when max drawdown is reached
