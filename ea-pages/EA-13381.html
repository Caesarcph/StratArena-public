摘要

该EA基于ColorMaRsi-Trigger自定义指标进行交易，该指标结合了双周期RSI和双周期MA来生成买卖信号。当指标上穿零轴时开多仓，下穿零轴时开空仓，并在反向信号时可选择平仓。资金管理支持多种保证金模式，包括固定手数。

策略分析

该策略的核心概念是利用双时间维度的RSI与MA组合来捕捉动量变化，短周期RSI(3)与长周期RSI(13)的差值经过MA平滑后产生信号线。入场条件为：当指标值从零轴下方上穿至正区间时触发做多信号，同时平掉空头仓位；当指标值从零轴上方下穿至负区间时触发做空信号，同时平掉多头仓位。出场条件依赖反向信号平仓以及固定的止损(1000点)和止盈(2000点)设置。仓位管理支持五种模式：基于可用保证金、基于余额、基于扣除浮亏后的可用保证金、基于扣除浮亏后的余额、以及固定手数模式。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1手，止损止盈比例1:2，体现了趋势动量跟踪的思路。

Summary

This EA trades based on the ColorMaRsi-Trigger custom indicator, which combines dual-period RSI with dual-period MA to generate buy and sell signals. It opens buy positions when the indicator crosses above zero and sell positions when it crosses below zero, with configurable position closing on opposite signals. Money management supports multiple margin modes including fixed lot sizing.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of this strategy uses a dual-timeframe RSI and MA combination to capture momentum shifts, where the difference between short-period RSI(3) and long-period RSI(13) is smoothed by MAs to generate a signal line. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when the indicator crosses from below zero to positive territory, simultaneously closing sell positions; a sell signal triggers when the indicator crosses from above zero to negative territory, simultaneously closing buy positions. Exit conditions rely on opposite signal closures plus fixed stop loss (1000 points) and take profit (2000 points) settings. Position sizing supports five modes: based on free margin, balance, free margin minus floating loss, balance minus floating loss, and fixed lot mode. Money management is controlled via the MM parameter at a default of 0.1 lots, with a stop-loss-to-take-profit ratio of 1:2, reflecting a trend-momentum-following approach.

优势

  • 双周期RSI与MA组合提供多维度动量确认，减少假信号
  • 止损止盈比例1:2，具有正期望的风险回报比
  • 新K线检测机制避免同一根K线内重复交易
  • 买卖开关独立控制，可灵活配置只做单边行情
  • 多种仓位计算模式适应不同风险偏好

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点固定值过大，未根据市场波动率动态调整，在高波动品种上可能不够
  • 缺少点差过滤机制，在买卖点差较大时可能导致不必要的亏损入场
  • 依赖自定义指标ColorMaRsi-Trigger，增加了部署复杂性和依赖性
  • 没有追踪止损机制，盈利仓位可能因市场回撤而大幅缩水后才触及止盈
  • 指标缓冲区读取仅在两根K线上，信号判断维度较单一

改进建议

  • 添加ATR动态止损止盈功能，根据市场波动率自动调整止损止盈距离
  • 增加点差过滤和追踪止损机制，提高入场质量和盈利保护能力

Strengths

  • Dual-period RSI and MA combination provides multi-dimensional momentum confirmation, reducing false signals
  • Stop-loss to take-profit ratio of 1:2 offers a positive risk-reward expectancy
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated trades within the same candle
  • Independent buy/sell toggle switches allow flexible single-direction trading configuration
  • Multiple position sizing modes accommodate different risk preferences

Weaknesses

  • Fixed stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large and not dynamically adjusted for market volatility
  • No spread filter mechanism, potentially leading to unfavorable entries when bid-ask spread is wide
  • Depends on the custom ColorMaRsi-Trigger indicator, increasing deployment complexity and dependency
  • No trailing stop mechanism, profitable positions may give back significant gains before reaching take profit
  • Indicator buffer reading on only two bars provides a relatively one-dimensional signal assessment

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit to automatically adjust distance based on market volatility
  • Add spread filter and trailing stop mechanism to improve entry quality and profit protection
