摘要

该EA基于ColorMaRsi-Trigger自定义指标进行交易，该指标结合了双周期RSI和双周期移动平均线来产生买卖信号。当指标缓冲区从零或负值转为正值时开多仓，从零或正值转为负值时开空仓，并支持反向信号平仓功能。

策略分析

策略核心是结合短期RSI(周期3)和长期RSI(周期13)分别经过短期MA(周期5)和长期MA(周期10)平滑后的差值来判断市场动量方向。入场条件：当指标缓冲区1的当前值大于0且前一值小于等于0时产生买入信号；当当前值小于0且前一值大于等于0时产生卖出信号。出场条件：可通过反向信号触发平仓（BuyPosClose和SellPosClose参数控制），同时设有固定的止损和止盈（默认分别为1000和2000点）。仓位管理支持五种模式：按自由保证金比例、按余额比例、按扣除亏损后的自由保证金比例、按扣除亏损后的余额比例、以及固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制交易规模比例，默认为0.1手。策略在每个新K线形成时计算信号，避免逐tick重复交易。

Summary

This EA trades based on the ColorMaRsi-Trigger custom indicator, which combines dual-period RSI with dual-period moving averages to generate buy and sell signals. It opens buy positions when the indicator buffer transitions from zero/negative to positive, and sell positions when it transitions from zero/positive to negative, with configurable close-on-reverse logic.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core combines a short-term RSI (period 3) and a long-term RSI (period 13), each smoothed by a short-term MA (period 5, EMA) and a long-term MA (period 10, EMA) respectively, using their difference to determine market momentum direction. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when indicator buffer 1's current value is above zero and the previous value was zero or below; a sell signal is generated when the current value is below zero and the previous value was zero or above. Exit conditions: positions can be closed on reverse signals (controlled by BuyPosClose and SellPosClose parameters), with fixed stop loss and take profit (default 1000 and 2000 points respectively). Position sizing supports five modes: by free margin percentage, by balance percentage, by loss-adjusted free margin, by loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter which sets the trading size ratio, defaulting to 0.1 lots. The strategy calculates signals only on new bar formation, avoiding redundant per-tick trading.

优势

  • 双周期RSI配合双周期MA平滑，有效过滤短期噪音，信号质量较高
  • 提供灵活的资金管理模式，支持从固定手数到基于保证金比例的多种仓位计算方式
  • 买卖开仓和平仓均可独立开关控制，策略配置灵活度高
  • 新K线检测机制避免逐tick重复交易，减少资源消耗和不必要操作
  • 止损止盈参数完整，风险控制基础框架健全

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标ColorMaRsi-Trigger，缺乏对该指标内部逻辑的透明度，黑盒风险较高
  • 默认止损1000点和止盈2000点数值偏大，在多数品种上可能导致单笔亏损过大
  • 缺少点差过滤和波动率过滤，在点差较大或市场剧烈波动时可能产生不利成交
  • 没有仓位检查逻辑，可能在已有同向持仓时重复开仓
  • 指标计算周期（RSI周期3和13）偏短，容易产生频繁假信号

改进建议

  • 添加点差过滤条件，当点差超过阈值时不进行交易，避免不利成交
  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，防止同向重复开仓导致仓位过重
  • 引入ATR动态止损止盈，根据市场波动率自适应调整止损止盈距离
  • 增加指标源码集成或详细说明，消除对自定义指标的黑盒依赖
  • 添加最大持仓数量限制和最大回撤控制，增强整体风险管控

Strengths

  • Dual-period RSI with dual-period MA smoothing effectively filters short-term noise, producing higher quality signals
  • Flexible money management modes support lot sizing from fixed lots to margin-ratio-based calculations
  • Buy/sell opening and closing can each be independently toggled, providing high configuration flexibility
  • New bar detection mechanism avoids per-tick redundant trading, reducing resource consumption and unnecessary operations
  • Complete stop loss and take profit parameters provide a sound risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom ColorMaRsi-Trigger indicator, lacking transparency into its internal logic, creating black-box risk
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are large values that may cause excessive single-trade losses on most instruments
  • Lacks spread filter and volatility filter, which may result in unfavorable fills during high-spread or volatile market conditions
  • No position check logic, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Indicator calculation periods (RSI periods 3 and 13) are relatively short, prone to generating frequent false signals

Improvements

  • Add spread filter condition to prevent trading when spread exceeds a threshold, avoiding unfavorable fills
  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate same-direction positions that over-concentrate exposure
  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop loss/take profit to adapt exit levels to market volatility
  • Integrate indicator source code or provide detailed documentation to eliminate black-box dependency on the custom indicator
  • Add maximum position count limit and maximum drawdown control to enhance overall risk management
