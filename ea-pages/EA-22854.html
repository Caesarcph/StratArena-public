摘要

该EA使用移动平均线和MACD指标的组合来生成交易信号，然后采用仓位加仓（马丁格尔式）策略，在价格反向运动时以预设间隔追加仓位。它基于MA/MACD交叉信号开立初始仓位，并在价格不利时逐步加大仓位，期望市场反转时回收损失。

策略分析

策略概念基于MA与MACD双重确认的趋势跟踪入场，结合马丁格尔式加仓法进行仓位管理。入场条件为MA方向与MACD信号线交叉一致时开仓，确保双重指标确认减少假信号。出场条件依赖整体仓位达到盈利目标或触及止损，而非单笔交易盈亏。仓位管理采用加仓模式，每次加仓间距固定点数，且加仓手数递增（通常倍增或按系数增加），这是典型的马丁格尔策略特征。资金管理方面，通过设定最大加仓次数和总止损来限制极端风险，但加仓递增的本质使得亏损时风险呈指数增长。该策略在震荡行情中表现较好，但在单边大趋势中可能因持续加仓而导致巨额亏损。

Summary

This EA uses a combination of Moving Average and MACD indicators to generate trade signals, then employs a position averaging (martingale-style) strategy by adding to losing positions at predetermined intervals. It opens initial positions based on MA/MACD crossover signals and progressively increases lot size as price moves against the position, aiming to recover losses when the market reverses.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept combines a dual-confirmed trend-following entry using MA and MACD indicators with a martingale-style position averaging approach for position management. Entry conditions require alignment between the MA direction and MACD signal line crossover, providing dual indicator confirmation to reduce false signals. Exit conditions depend on the overall portfolio reaching a profit target or hitting a stop loss, rather than individual trade profit/loss. Position sizing employs an averaging-down model where additional positions are opened at fixed pip intervals with increasing lot sizes (typically doubling or scaling by a coefficient), which is a classic martingale characteristic. Money management attempts to limit extreme risk by setting a maximum number of averaging steps and a total stop loss, but the inherently escalating lot sizes cause risk to grow exponentially during losing streaks. The strategy performs well in ranging markets but can suffer catastrophic losses in strong trending markets where price continuously moves against the accumulated position.

优势

  • 双重指标确认入场减少假信号概率
  • 加仓策略在震荡行情中能有效摊薄成本并获利
  • 参数化设计完善，加仓次数和间距可调便于优化
  • 有最大加仓次数限制避免无限加仓风险
  • MA长周期过滤有效减少逆势交易

劣势

  • 马丁格尔式加仓在单边趋势中风险呈指数增长
  • 止损设为0意味着极端行情下无保护机制
  • 加仓手数递增导致爆仓风险随加仓次数急剧上升
  • 缺乏账户资金检查可能在保证金不足时仍尝试加仓
  • 没有考虑点差和手续费对加仓策略盈利门槛的影响

改进建议

  • 添加强制总止损或账户最大回撤限制作为安全网
  • 加入账户可用保证金检查确保加仓前有足够资金
  • 增加点差过滤和手续费计算避免小盈利被交易成本吞噬

Strengths

  • Dual indicator confirmation reduces false signal probability
  • Averaging strategy effectively reduces average cost and profits in ranging markets
  • Well-parameterized design with adjustable averaging count and step for optimization
  • Maximum averaging count limit prevents infinite position accumulation
  • Long-period MA filtering effectively reduces counter-trend trades

Weaknesses

  • Martingale-style averaging causes exponentially growing risk in trending markets
  • Stop loss set to 0 means no protection during extreme market moves
  • Escalating lot sizes dramatically increase margin call risk with each averaging step
  • Lack of account balance check may attempt averaging even with insufficient margin
  • Does not account for spread and commission impact on breakeven threshold

Improvements

  • Add a mandatory total stop loss or maximum account drawdown limit as a safety net
  • Implement available margin check before opening averaging positions to ensure sufficient funds
  • Add spread filter and commission calculation to prevent small profits from being consumed by trading costs
