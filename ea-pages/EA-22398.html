摘要

该EA使用两条简单移动均线（SMA5和SMA25）生成买入信号，入场条件基于短期均线与收盘价的偏离值及均线上斜条件。它在新K线事件上运行，以固定止损和均线目标价执行市价买单，并以最小手数交易。卖出逻辑被完全注释掉，使其成为一个仅做多的剥头皮系统。

策略分析

策略概念基于短期均线与价格之间的偏离作为波动性信号，当偏离值超过阈值（-0.0015）且短期均线呈上斜趋势时触发买入。入场条件为：val[0]（MA5与收盘价之差）小于-0.0015，且MA_Val1[0]大于MA_Val1[2]，表示价格低于均线且均线上升。出场条件依赖固定止损（15点，5/3位小数经纪商自动调整）和以MA5当前值作为止盈。仓位管理使用SymbolInfoDouble获取最小交易手数，未实现任何加仓或仓位调整逻辑。资金管理部分存在严重缺陷：margin变量未初始化即与free_margin比较，实际上条件判断形同虚设，几乎总是通过。卖出逻辑被完全注释掉，EA只做多不做空，策略不完整。

Summary

This EA uses two Simple Moving Averages (SMA 5 and SMA 25) to generate buy signals based on the deviation between the short-period MA and close price, combined with an upward MA slope condition. It operates on new bar events, executes market buy orders with fixed stop loss and MA-based take profit, and trades at minimum volume. The sell logic is entirely commented out, making it a buy-only scalping system.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept uses the deviation between a short-period SMA (period 5) and the close price as a volatility signal, triggering a buy when the deviation exceeds a threshold (-0.0015) and the MA is sloping upward. Entry conditions require val[0] (MA5 minus close) to be less than -0.0015, and MA_Val1[0] greater than MA_Val1[2], indicating the price is below the moving average while the MA is rising — a mean-reversion-within-trend approach. Exit conditions rely on a fixed stop loss of 15 points (auto-adjusted for 5/3-digit brokers) and the current MA5 value as take profit. Position sizing uses the broker's minimum lot size with no scaling or accumulation logic. Money management has a critical flaw: the margin variable is declared but never initialized before comparison with free_margin, making the margin check effectively useless since an uninitialized double could be anything. The sell logic is entirely commented out, leaving the EA as buy-only, making the strategy incomplete and asymmetric.

优势

  • 使用新K线判断避免同一根K线重复交易，逻辑合理
  • 包含数据同步检查和重试机制，对历史数据加载有一定容错
  • 止损和止盈参数可配置，5/3位小数经纪商自动乘10调整
  • 交易前检查是否已有同方向持仓，防止重复开仓

劣势

  • 卖出逻辑被完全注释掉，EA只做多，策略严重不完整
  • margin变量未初始化即用于条件判断，资金管理检查形同虚设
  • EA_Magic参数未在买单中使用（硬编码15489），参数与实际行为不一致
  • TakeProfit参数在买单中未使用（止盈设为MA值），参数名与功能不符
  • 偏离阈值-0.0015硬编码，对不同品种和价格水平不具适应性
  • 仅检查单品种单持仓，不支持多单同时存在的情况

改进建议

  • 修复margin变量初始化问题，使用OrderCalcMargin正确计算所需保证金后再比较
  • 恢复卖出逻辑并使用EA_Magic参数，使策略完整且参数一致
  • 将偏离阈值改为可配置参数，或根据ATD/ATR动态计算以适应不同品种

Strengths

  • Uses new bar detection to prevent repeated trades on the same bar, which is logically sound
  • Includes data synchronization checks and retry mechanism for historical data loading
  • Stop loss and take profit are configurable with auto-adjustment for 5/3-digit brokers
  • Checks for existing positions before opening new ones, preventing duplicate entries

Weaknesses

  • Sell logic is entirely commented out, making the EA buy-only and the strategy severely incomplete
  • The margin variable is never initialized before comparison, rendering the money management check useless
  • EA_Magic parameter is not used in buy orders (hardcoded as 15489), parameter does not match actual behavior
  • TakeProfit parameter is unused in buy orders (TP set to MA value instead), parameter name is misleading
  • The deviation threshold -0.0015 is hardcoded and not adaptive to different instruments or price levels
  • Only checks for a single position per symbol, does not support scenarios with multiple concurrent orders

Improvements

  • Fix the margin variable initialization — use OrderCalcMargin() to properly calculate required margin before comparison with free margin
  • Restore the sell logic and use the EA_Magic parameter consistently, making the strategy complete and parameters coherent
  • Make the deviation threshold a configurable input parameter or dynamically calculate it based on ATR to adapt to different instruments
