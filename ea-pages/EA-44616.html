摘要

该EA监控账户净值，当净值达到用户设定的盈利目标或亏损阈值时自动平掉所有持仓。它作为组合级别的净值监控工具运行，本身不开仓，纯粹是风险管理工具。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念基于账户净值监控而非行情信号，不涉及任何入场条件因为它不主动开仓。出场条件是当账户净值达到设定的盈利目标（Equity Profit Target）或亏损限额（Equity Loss Limit）时，自动平掉所有持仓。仓位管理方面不适用，因为该EA不管理仓位大小。资金管理方面，它通过净值阈值来保护整体账户资金，是一种全局性的资金保护机制。EA在OnTick中持续监控当前净值与设定目标的偏差，一旦触发阈值便遍历所有持仓并逐个平仓。该工具适合同时运行多个策略的交易者，用于在整体账户层面控制盈亏。

Summary

This EA monitors account equity and automatically closes all open positions when equity reaches a user-defined profit target or loss threshold. It acts as a portfolio-level equity monitor and does not open any trades itself, functioning purely as a risk management utility.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept of this EA is based on account equity monitoring rather than market signals, and it does not involve any entry conditions because it does not open trades. The exit condition is triggered when account equity reaches a user-defined profit target or loss limit, at which point all open positions are closed automatically. Position sizing is not applicable since this EA does not manage position sizes. For money management, it protects overall account capital through equity thresholds, serving as a global capital protection mechanism. The EA continuously monitors the deviation between current equity and set targets in OnTick, and once a threshold is triggered, it iterates through all positions and closes them one by one. This tool is suitable for traders running multiple strategies simultaneously, providing account-level profit and loss control.

优势

  • 提供账户级别的整体风险控制，适合多策略同时运行的场景
  • 逻辑简单清晰，不引入额外的交易风险
  • 同时支持盈利目标和止损限额，双向保护账户资金

劣势

  • 无法区分不同策略或不同品种的持仓，平仓时一刀切全部关闭
  • 缺乏细粒度控制，不能按品种、魔数或方向选择性平仓
  • 如果设定的阈值不合理（如过小的止损），可能在正常波动中被频繁触发导致不必要的平仓

改进建议

  • 增加按品种、魔数或方向过滤平仓的功能，避免一刀切
  • 增加净值追踪的统计和日志功能，帮助用户优化阈值设定

Strengths

  • Provides account-level risk control, ideal for scenarios with multiple strategies running simultaneously
  • Simple and clear logic without introducing additional trading risk
  • Supports both profit target and loss limit, providing dual-direction account capital protection

Weaknesses

  • Cannot distinguish positions from different strategies or symbols, closing all positions indiscriminately
  • Lacks granular control, cannot selectively close by symbol, magic number, or direction
  • Unreasonable thresholds (e.g., very tight stop) may trigger frequently during normal volatility causing unnecessary closures

Improvements

  • Add filtering capability to close positions by symbol, magic number, or direction instead of all-at-once
  • Add equity tracking statistics and logging to help users optimize threshold settings

风险评估

总体:SAFE

风险点

  • 平仓执行时可能出现滑点导致实际净值与预期不一致
  • 在极端行情下批量平仓可能面临流动性不足的问题

Risk Assessment

Overall:SAFE

Risk Points

  • Slippage during position closure may cause actual equity to differ from expected
  • Batch closing during extreme market conditions may face insufficient liquidity
