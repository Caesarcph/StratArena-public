摘要

该EA基于自定义的AsimmetricStochNR指标（非对称噪声过滤随机指标）的交叉信号进行交易。当随机指标主线向上穿越信号线时开多仓，向下穿越时开空仓，入场和出场权限可独立配置。

策略分析

该EA使用非对称噪声过滤随机指标（AsimmetricStochNR）作为核心策略引擎，该指标融合了短周期（默认5）和长周期（默认12）的%K线计算，并通过可配置的平滑方法（默认SMA）生成%D信号线。入场条件为：当Stoch[1]>Sign[1]且Stoch[0]<Sign[0]时，主线从下方穿越信号线向上，触发买入信号；反之Stoch[1]<Sign[1]且Stoch[0]>Sign[0]时触发卖出信号。出场条件独立于入场，通过BuyPosClose和SellPosClose参数控制是否允许平仓，当反向交叉信号出现时平掉对应仓位。仓位管理通过MM参数实现，负值表示固定手数，正值表示按存款比例计算手数。止损和止盈分别默认为1000点和2000点，风险回报比为1:2。该策略运行在H4周期上，仅在新K线出现时计算信号，避免逐tick重复交易。噪声过滤参数Sens默认为7点，有助于减少虚假信号。超买超卖水平（80/20）传入指标但未在EA逻辑中直接用于过滤。

Summary

This EA trades based on crossover signals from the custom AsimmetricStochNR indicator, which is an asymmetric Stochastic with noise reduction. It opens buy positions when the Stochastic main line crosses above the signal line and sell positions on the reverse crossover, with independent permissions for entry and exit.

Strategy Analysis

The EA uses the AsimmetricStochNR indicator as its core strategy engine, which combines short-period (default 5) and long-period (default 12) %K calculations and generates a %D signal line via a configurable smoothing method (default SMA). Entry conditions: when Stoch[1]>Sign[1] and Stoch[0]<Sign[0], the main line crosses above the signal line from below, triggering a buy signal; conversely, Stoch[1]<Sign[1] and Stoch[0]>Sign[0] triggers a sell signal. Exit conditions are independent from entries, controlled by BuyPosClose and SellPosClose parameters—when a reverse crossover occurs, the corresponding position is closed. Position sizing is handled via the MM parameter: negative values represent fixed lot size, while positive values represent deposit share percentage. StopLoss defaults to 1000 points and TakeProfit to 2000 points, yielding a 1:2 risk-reward ratio. The strategy operates on H4 timeframe by default and only calculates signals on new bars to avoid tick-level redundant trading. The noise reduction parameter Sens defaults to 7 points, helping reduce false signals. Overbought/oversold levels (80/20) are passed to the indicator but not directly used as entry filters in the EA logic.

优势

  • 使用噪声过滤随机指标，减少虚假交叉信号，提高信号质量
  • 独立控制入场和出场权限，交易逻辑灵活可配置
  • 仅在新K线时计算信号，避免逐tick重复开仓，节省计算资源
  • 止损止盈均设置，风险回报比1:2合理
  • 支持多种平滑方法（JJMA/VIDIA/AMA等），适应不同市场环境

劣势

  • 超买超卖水平传入指标但未在EA入场逻辑中作为过滤器使用，可能在极端区域仍开仓
  • 依赖自定义指标AsimmetricStochNR，指标代码不透明，无法验证其内部逻辑
  • 没有点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • 止损1000点较大，可能导致单笔亏损过大
  • 没有持仓检查逻辑，可能在已有同向仓位时重复开仓

改进建议

  • 在入场逻辑中增加超买超卖区域过滤，避免在极端区域追涨杀跌
  • 增加点差过滤和持仓检查，防止高点差环境不利成交和重复开仓
  • 增加移动止损或保本止损功能，在浮盈达到一定比例后锁定利润

Strengths

  • Uses noise-reduced stochastic indicator, reducing false crossover signals and improving signal quality
  • Independent entry and exit permission controls provide flexible and configurable trading logic
  • Calculates signals only on new bars, avoiding tick-level redundant entries and saving computational resources
  • Both stop loss and take profit are configured with a reasonable 1:2 risk-reward ratio
  • Supports multiple smoothing methods (JJMA/VIDIA/AMA etc.), adapting to different market conditions

Weaknesses

  • Overbought/oversold levels are passed to the indicator but not used as entry filters in the EA logic, potentially opening positions in extreme zones
  • Depends on the custom AsimmetricStochNR indicator whose internal logic is opaque and cannot be verified
  • No spread filter, potentially resulting in unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • Stop loss of 1000 points is relatively large, potentially causing significant single-trade losses
  • No existing position check logic, may repeatedly open positions in the same direction

Improvements

  • Add overbought/oversold zone filtering in entry logic to avoid chasing trends in extreme areas
  • Add spread filter and position check to prevent unfavorable fills in high-spread environments and duplicate position opening
  • Add trailing stop or breakeven functionality to lock in profits after reaching a certain floating profit threshold
