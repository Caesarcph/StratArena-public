摘要

该EA基于自定义Force_DiverSign指标生成的背离信号进行交易，检测价格与强力指标之间的背离。在看涨背离信号时开多仓，在看跌背离信号时开空仓，具有可配置的仓位管理和资金管理选项。

策略分析

策略核心概念是基于Force Index（强力指标）与价格之间的背离来产生交易信号。入场条件：当Force_DiverSign指标在指定信号柱上输出非零且非EMPTY_VALUE的上行信号时开多仓，下行信号时开空仓。出场条件包括两种方式：一是通过反向背离信号平仓（如检测到看跌背离时平多仓），二是通过固定止损和止盈参数出场。仓位管理通过MMMode参数支持多种模式，包括基于自由保证金、余额、亏损自由保证金、亏损余额和固定手数的资金管理方式。策略还包含历史回溯机制，当同时启用开仓和平仓时，会向后遍历历史柱寻找最近的反向信号以确认平仓条件。整体策略逻辑清晰，依赖单一自定义指标信号，属于典型的背离交易系统。

Summary

This EA trades based on divergence signals generated by the custom Force_DiverSign indicator, which detects divergence between price and the Force Index. It opens buy positions on bullish divergence signals and sell positions on bearish divergence signals, with configurable position management and money management options.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy concept is based on detecting divergence between the Force Index and price to generate trading signals. Entry conditions: a buy position is opened when the Force_DiverSign indicator outputs a non-zero, non-EMPTY_VALUE value on buffer 1 at the specified signal bar, and a sell position is opened when buffer 0 triggers similarly. Exit conditions include two methods: closing via reverse divergence signals (e.g., closing a buy when a bearish divergence is detected) and using fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit parameters. Position sizing supports multiple modes through the MMMode parameter, including money management based on free margin, balance, loss-free margin, loss-balance, and fixed lot sizing. The strategy also incorporates a historical lookback mechanism that scans backward through historical bars to find the nearest opposing divergence signal for confirming close conditions when both opening and closing are enabled. Overall, the strategy logic is clear and relies on a single custom indicator signal, representing a typical divergence trading system.

优势

  • 背离策略具有较好的市场逻辑基础，能捕捉趋势转折点
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，灵活性较高
  • 买卖开平仓可独立控制，便于策略组合和回测优化

劣势

  • 依赖单一自定义指标Force_DiverSign，缺乏多信号确认机制
  • 历史回溯平仓逻辑可能遍历大量历史柱，在长无信号期间效率低下
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值偏大，未根据ATR或市场波动动态调整

改进建议

  • 增加ATR动态止损止盈，替代固定点数止损止盈
  • 增加多时间周期确认或额外指标过滤，减少假信号

Strengths

  • Divergence strategy has sound market logic and can capture trend reversal points
  • Supports multiple money management modes providing good flexibility
  • Buy/sell open and close controls are independent, enabling strategy combination and optimization

Weaknesses

  • Relies on a single custom Force_DiverSign indicator without multi-signal confirmation
  • Historical lookback close logic may iterate through many bars, inefficient during long no-signal periods
  • Default StopLoss 1000 and TakeProfit 2000 points are large and not dynamically adjusted by ATR or volatility

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit instead of fixed point values
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation or additional indicator filtering to reduce false signals
