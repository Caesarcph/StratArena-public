摘要

该EA基于自定义Force_DiverSign指标的发散信号进行交易，识别Force Index中的牛熊背离。在牛背离信号时开多仓，在熊背离信号时开空仓，并可通过反向信号平仓来管理仓位。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是利用Force Index（强力指数）的背离信号进行交易。Force Index由价格变动幅度和成交量计算得出，结合两条不同周期的移动平均线（默认EMA3和EMA7）来识别背离。入场条件为：当指标buffer 1输出非零非空值时产生牛背离信号，开多仓并可选平空仓；当指标buffer 0输出非零非空值时产生熊背离信号，开空仓并可选平多仓。出场条件依赖反向背离信号平仓或固定的止损止盈（默认SL=1000点，TP=2000点）。仓位管理支持五种模式：固定手数、按自由保证金比例、按余额比例、按扣除浮亏后的自由保证金比例、按扣除浮亏后的余额比例。额外逻辑会在当前bar无平仓信号时，向后搜索历史bar查找平仓信号，确保不遗漏反向信号。默认在H4周期运行，SignalBar=1表示使用前一根已完成K线的信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on divergence signals from the custom Force_DiverSign indicator, which identifies bullish and bearish divergences in the Force Index. It opens buy positions on bullish divergence signals and sell positions on bearish divergence signals, with configurable close-on-opposite-signal logic for position management.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy trades divergence signals from the Force Index, which combines price change magnitude with volume. The Force_DiverSign indicator uses two moving averages (default EMA period 3 and 7) to detect divergence between price and Force Index. Entry conditions: when indicator buffer 1 outputs a non-zero, non-EMPTY_VALUE, a bullish divergence is detected, triggering a buy open and optional sell close; when buffer 0 triggers, a bearish divergence opens sell positions and optionally closes buys. Exit conditions rely on opposite divergence signals or fixed StopLoss (1000 pts) and TakeProfit (2000 pts). Position sizing supports five modes: fixed lot, percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin minus floating loss, and percentage of balance minus floating loss. An additional logic scans historical bars backward for close signals when the current bar has none, ensuring opposite divergence signals are not missed. Default timeframe is H4 with SignalBar=1, using the most recently completed bar for signal evaluation.

优势

  • 背离策略具有较高的盈亏比潜力，能捕捉趋势反转的早期信号
  • 提供五种仓位管理模式，资金管理灵活度高
  • 买入/卖出/平仓开关独立可配，策略组合灵活
  • 新K线检测机制避免同一信号重复触发交易
  • 支持止损止盈设置，具备基本风险控制

劣势

  • 默认止损1000点过大，在多数品种上风险敞口过高
  • 没有点差过滤，背离信号可能在宽点差时段造成不利成交
  • 反向搜索历史bar平仓的逻辑可能产生延迟平仓，导致不必要的持仓时间
  • 依赖自定义指标Force_DiverSign，指标文件缺失时EA无法运行
  • 没有持仓数量限制，多个背离信号可能累积过多仓位

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和最大持仓数量限制，降低不利成交风险和过度持仓风险
  • 将止损止盈改为ATR动态计算，适应不同品种和波动环境

Strengths

  • Divergence strategy has high reward-to-risk potential, capturing early trend reversal signals
  • Five position sizing modes provide flexible money management
  • Independent toggles for buy/sell open/close allow strategy combination flexibility
  • New bar detection prevents duplicate trade triggers from the same signal
  • StopLoss and TakeProfit parameters provide basic risk control

Weaknesses

  • Default 1000-point StopLoss is excessively large, creating high risk exposure on most instruments
  • No spread filter means divergence signals may execute at unfavorable prices during wide spread periods
  • Backward historical bar scanning for close signals may cause delayed exits and unnecessary holding time
  • Depends on custom Force_DiverSign indicator; EA fails if the indicator file is missing
  • No position count limit, multiple divergence signals could accumulate excessive positions

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and maximum position count limit to reduce adverse execution risk and over-exposure
  • Replace fixed StopLoss/TakeProfit with ATR-based dynamic calculation to adapt to different instruments and volatility regimes
