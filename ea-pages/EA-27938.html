摘要

该EA在日线图上识别十字星K线形态，并在十字星高低点两侧放置两个对向止损挂单。使用ATR确定止损距离，并通过固定追踪止损管理风险。当一侧挂单成交后，自动删除另一侧挂单。

策略分析

策略概念基于十字星(Doji)K线形态的突破交易，十字星表示市场犹豫不决，随后的突破方向可能决定趋势方向。入场条件是当日线出现十字星（实体不超过整根K线3%）时，在前一K线高点放置Buy Stop挂单，低点放置Sell Stop挂单，形成双向突破策略。止损设置在十字星低点减去1.75倍ATR（买入）或高点加上1.75倍ATR（卖出），止损距离较大以容纳市场噪音。出场条件依赖固定追踪止损（2700点），当价格朝有利方向移动且距离超过固定点数时，止损跟随前一日收盘价移动，但不会在亏损时移动止损。仓位管理使用固定手数0.10手，没有动态仓位计算。资金管理方面有保证金检查和手数规范化处理，但缺少最大持仓限制和回撤控制。

Summary

This EA identifies Doji candlestick patterns on the daily chart and places two opposing pending stop orders around the Doji bar's high and low. It uses ATR for stop loss placement and manages risk with a fixed trailing stop. When one pending order fills, the other is automatically deleted.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on breakout trading from Doji candlestick patterns, where a Doji signals market indecision and the subsequent breakout direction may determine the trend. Entry conditions trigger when a Doji appears on the daily chart (body size is 3% or less of the total bar range), placing a Buy Stop at the Doji high and a Sell Stop at the Doji low, creating a dual-direction breakout setup. Stop loss is set at the Doji low minus 1.75x ATR for buys or the Doji high plus 1.75x ATR for sells, providing wide stops to accommodate market noise. Exit conditions rely on a fixed trailing stop of 2700 points, where the stop loss follows the previous day's close once the position is profitable beyond the spread, but the stop never moves when the position is unprofitable. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size of 0.10 with no dynamic calculation based on account equity or risk percentage. Money management includes margin checking and lot normalization but lacks maximum position limits, equity drawdown controls, or daily loss limits.

优势

  • 双向挂单突破策略逻辑清晰，能捕捉十字星后的方向性突破
  • 止损基于ATR动态计算，能根据市场波动率自适应调整
  • OnTradeTransaction中自动删除未成交挂单，避免多余敞口
  • 有止损级别检查(CheckStopsLevel)和保证金检查，符合规范

劣势

  • 固定手数0.10不做动态仓位管理，无法根据账户规模和风险调整
  • 追踪止损使用固定点数而非ATR比例，在不同波动率品种上表现差异大
  • 没有止盈(TP)设置，完全依赖追踪止损出场，可能回吐大量利润
  • SelectPosition使用PositionSelect而非遍历所有仓位，多仓位场景可能出错
  • 缺少最大回撤、最大持仓等资金管理保护机制

改进建议

  • 将追踪止损改为基于ATR倍数的动态追踪，以适应不同波动率环境
  • 添加基于账户风险百分比的动态仓位计算，替代固定手数
  • 增加止盈逻辑或基于指标的出场信号，避免完全依赖追踪止损
  • 添加最大回撤保护和每日亏损限制机制

Strengths

  • Dual pending order breakout logic is clear and captures directional moves after Doji indecision
  • Stop loss based on ATR adapts to market volatility dynamically
  • OnTradeTransaction automatically deletes the unfilled pending order when the other fills, preventing extra exposure
  • Includes stops level checking and margin verification following MQL5 best practices

Weaknesses

  • Fixed 0.10 lot size with no dynamic position sizing fails to adapt to account size and risk tolerance
  • Trailing stop uses fixed points instead of ATR-based calculation, performing inconsistently across instruments with different volatility
  • No take profit (TP) is set, relying entirely on trailing stop for exit which may give back substantial profits
  • SelectPosition uses PositionSelect instead of iterating all positions, which can fail in multi-position scenarios
  • Lacks equity drawdown limits, maximum position controls, or daily loss limits for capital protection

Improvements

  • Replace fixed-point trailing stop with ATR-based trailing to adapt to varying volatility environments
  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account risk percentage instead of fixed lot size
  • Add take profit logic or indicator-based exit signals to avoid relying solely on trailing stop
  • Add maximum drawdown protection and daily loss limit mechanisms

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 双向挂单同时存在时可能因极端行情（如跳空）导致两侧都成交，形成对冲但双倍风险
  • 无止盈设置可能导致盈利单在反转时回吐过多利润
  • 固定手数不随账户变化，大账户风险过小而小账户风险过大
  • 止损距离1.75倍ATR较大，单笔亏损可能较为显著

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Dual pending orders can both fill on extreme moves like gaps, creating double exposure
  • No take profit means winning trades may give back significant profits on reversals
  • Fixed lot size doesn't scale with account, under-risking large accounts and over-risking small ones
  • 1.75x ATR stop loss distance is wide, making individual trade losses potentially significant
