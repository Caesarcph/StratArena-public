摘要

该EA结合分形指标和强力指数指标来生成交易信号。当分形确认向上动力时做多，当分形确认向下动力时做空。该策略旨在捕捉由这两个指标趋同所识别的动量转换。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略概念是将威廉姆斯分形指标与埃尔德强力指数相结合，利用分形识别市场结构的转折点，再用强力指数确认价格背后的资金流向强度。入场条件为：做多时需要出现向上分形突破且强力指数为正值，做空时需要出现向下分形突破且强力指数为负值，两者同时满足才触发信号。出场条件主要依赖固定止损和止盈参数，也可能在反向分形出现时平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，通过输入参数设置交易量。资金管理方面相对简单，没有动态仓位计算或基于账户余额的自适应手数调整，风险完全依赖用户手动设置合理的止损距离和手数。

Summary

This EA combines the Fractal indicator with the Force Index to generate trading signals. It enters long positions when fractals confirm an upward force and short positions when fractals confirm a downward force. The strategy aims to capture momentum shifts identified by the convergence of these two indicators.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy concept combines Williams' Fractal indicator with Elder's Force Index, using fractals to identify structural turning points in the market and the Force Index to confirm the strength of money flow behind price moves. Entry conditions require both indicators to align: a long signal triggers when an upward fractal breakout occurs and the Force Index is positive, while a short signal triggers when a downward fractal breakout occurs and the Force Index is negative. Exit conditions primarily rely on fixed stop-loss and take-profit parameters, with potential position closing when opposing fractals appear. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach controlled by an input parameter, without dynamic calculation. Money management is relatively simplistic, lacking adaptive lot sizing based on account equity or risk percentage, leaving risk control entirely to the user's manual configuration of stop-loss distance and lot size.

优势

  • 双指标确认机制减少假信号，分形提供结构判断而强力指数提供动量确认
  • 策略逻辑清晰易懂，基于经典技术分析理论（威廉姆斯分形和埃尔德强力指数），便于理解和优化
  • 使用魔术编号管理订单，可与其他EA并行运行而不产生冲突

劣势

  • 固定手数交易缺乏动态仓位管理，无法根据账户风险自动调整交易规模
  • 没有回撤控制或最大连续亏损限制，在不利行情中可能持续亏损
  • 强力指数和分形指标在震荡市中容易产生频繁的虚假信号，缺乏市场状态过滤器

改进建议

  • 增加基于ATR的动态止损止盈，根据市场波动性自动调整止损距离
  • 加入市场状态识别过滤器（如ADX或波动率阈值），在震荡行情中暂停交易以减少假信号

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator confirmation mechanism reduces false signals — fractals provide structural judgment while Force Index provides momentum confirmation
  • Clear and understandable strategy logic based on classic technical analysis theory (Williams Fractals and Elder's Force Index), making it easy to comprehend and optimize
  • Uses magic number for order management, allowing parallel operation with other EAs without conflicts

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing lacks dynamic position management, unable to automatically adjust trade size based on account risk
  • No drawdown control or maximum consecutive loss limits, potentially leading to sustained losses in unfavorable markets
  • Force Index and Fractal indicators tend to generate frequent false signals in ranging markets, lacking a market state filter

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit that automatically adjusts stop distances based on market volatility
  • Incorporate a market state identification filter (such as ADX or volatility threshold) to pause trading during ranging conditions and reduce false signals
