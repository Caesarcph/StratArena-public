摘要

该EA实施周末跳空交易策略，在周五晚间根据最近N根K线的最高价和最低价放置买入止损和卖出止损挂单，捕捉周一开盘跳空突破行情，达到固定美元盈利目标后平仓，周一重置所有挂单。

策略分析

策略概念是基于周末跳空（Weekend Gap）现象，在周五收盘前根据最近LookbackBars根K线的最高价和最低价分别放置Buy Stop和Sell Stop挂单，预期周一开盘时价格突破区间。入场条件为周五指定时间（默认23:00-23:15），找到回顾周期内的最高价加一个点作为Buy Stop价格，最低价减一个点作为Sell Stop价格。出场条件为持仓盈利达到SecureProfitTarget（默认5美元）时平仓，止损为固定点数（默认60点），无止盈设置。仓位管理采用固定手数（LotSize=0.1），仅允许单方向各开一单（MaxOpenTrade=1但代码未实际使用该参数限制）。资金管理方面仅有保证金检查，无最大回撤控制或仓位比例管理，止损固定且无动态调整机制。

Summary

This EA implements a weekend gap trading strategy by placing Buy Stop and Sell Stop pending orders at the highest high and lowest low of the last N bars on Friday evening. It targets the Monday opening gap breakout and closes positions when a fixed USD profit target is reached, resetting all pending orders on Monday.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the Weekend Gap phenomenon, placing Buy Stop and Sell Stop pending orders before Friday close at the highest high and lowest low of the last LookbackBars bars, expecting a Monday opening breakout. Entry conditions trigger on Friday at the specified time (default 23:00-23:15), finding the highest high plus one point as the Buy Stop price and the lowest low minus one point as the Sell Stop price. Exit conditions close positions when profit reaches the SecureProfitTarget (default $5 USD), with a fixed stop loss (default 60 points) and no take profit set. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (LotSize=0.1), allowing only one order per direction, though MaxOpenTrade parameter is declared but never actually enforced in code. Money management only includes a margin check, lacking drawdown control or proportional position sizing, and the stop loss is fixed without any dynamic adjustment mechanism.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于周末跳空这一经典市场现象，具有统计学依据
  • 使用挂单（Buy Stop/Sell Stop）而非市价单，避免滑点且只在突破时成交
  • 有保证金检查和止损设置，基本风险控制存在
  • 会话标志防止重复下单，周一自动清理挂单，避免过期订单残留
  • 手数规范化处理考虑了最小手数、手数步长和最大手数限制

劣势

  • MaxOpenTrade参数声明但未实际使用，无法限制同时持仓数量
  • Sell Stop订单未设置止损，存在单边风险失控的严重隐患
  • 止损固定为60点，未考虑市场波动率（ATR），在不同货币对和时段下适应性差
  • 无止盈设置（TP=0），仅依赖美元盈利目标平仓，可能在大幅盈利后回撤
  • 缺乏点差过滤，周五收盘和周一开盘时点差可能极大导致不利成交
  • lowest_low初始值为999999.9的硬编码不够优雅，可能在极端行情下出问题

改进建议

  • 实现MaxOpenTrade参数的实际限制逻辑，并在开仓前检查当前持仓数量
  • 为Sell Stop订单添加止损计算，与Buy Stop保持对称的风险管理
  • 引入ATR动态止损替代固定点数止损，并添加点差过滤条件
  • 添加最大回撤限制和按账户余额比例的动态仓位管理
  • 增加周一夜单超时清理机制，避免挂单长时间未成交后市场环境变化带来的风险

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on the weekend gap phenomenon which has statistical backing in forex markets
  • Uses pending orders (Buy Stop/Sell Stop) instead of market orders, avoiding slippage and only filling on breakout
  • Includes margin check and stop loss, providing basic risk control
  • Session flag prevents duplicate order placement, and Monday auto-cleanup removes stale pending orders
  • Lot size normalization properly handles minimum lot, lot step, and maximum lot constraints

Weaknesses

  • MaxOpenTrade parameter is declared but never enforced in code, failing to limit concurrent positions
  • Sell Stop orders have no stop loss set (SL=0 for SELL), creating a critical one-sided risk exposure
  • Fixed 60-point stop loss ignores market volatility (ATR), making it poorly adaptive across pairs and timeframes
  • No take profit set (TP=0), relying solely on the USD profit target which may let large gains reverse
  • No spread filter - Friday close and Monday open spreads can be extremely wide causing unfavorable fills
  • Hardcoded lowest_low initial value of 999999.9 is inelegant and could theoretically fail in extreme scenarios

Improvements

  • Implement actual MaxOpenTrade enforcement logic and check current position count before placing orders
  • Add stop loss calculation for Sell Stop orders, ensuring symmetric risk management with Buy Stop
  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop loss instead of fixed points, and add spread filter conditions
  • Add maximum drawdown limit and balance-proportional dynamic position sizing
  • Add a timeout mechanism for Monday gap orders to cancel unfilled pending orders after a set period

风险点

  • Sell Stop订单未设置止损，若周一跳空方向相反可能造成无限亏损
  • 周五收盘和周一开盘点差可能极大，导致挂单在不利价格成交
  • 无最大回撤保护和动态仓位管理，连续亏损时风险不可控
  • MaxOpenTrade参数未生效，可能导致超预期持仓
  • 固定止损在不同波动率环境下可能过小（被打损）或过大（亏损过多）

Risk Assessment

Overall:CAUTION

Risk Points

  • Sell Stop orders have no stop loss, potentially causing unlimited losses if Monday gaps in the opposite direction
  • Friday close and Monday open spreads can be extremely wide, causing fills at unfavorable prices
  • No maximum drawdown protection or dynamic position sizing, making consecutive losses uncontrolled
  • MaxOpenTrade parameter is not enforced, potentially leading to more positions than expected
  • Fixed stop loss may be too tight (triggered by noise) or too wide (excessive loss) across different volatility environments
