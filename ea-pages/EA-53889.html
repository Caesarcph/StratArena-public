摘要

CloseAgent是一个仅用于平仓的工具EA，基于布林带和RSI超买超卖信号来关闭已有持仓。它不会开新仓，而是在价格高于布林带上轨且RSI大于70时平多仓，在价格低于布林带下轨且RSI小于30时平空仓。它还提供了账户总利润目标功能，当账户利润超过设定值时关闭所有持仓。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念是基于均值回归原理，利用布林带和RSI指标判断价格是否已偏离均值过远，从而在极端区域平仓锁定利润。出场条件分为两种：一是针对多单，当Bid价高于布林带上轨且RSI大于70且Bid高于开仓价时平仓；二是针对空单，当Ask价低于布林带下轨且RSI小于30且Ask低于开仓价时平仓。此外还有一个全局出场条件，当账户总利润超过CloseAll设定值时关闭所有持仓。该EA没有仓位管理功能，因为它不开仓。资金管理方面仅有账户利润阈值一个参数，缺乏单笔止损机制和回撤控制。EA通过CloseMode参数可以选择只平手动单、算法单或全部，提供了灵活性。

Summary

CloseAgent is an exit-only utility EA that closes existing positions based on Bollinger Bands and RSI overbought/oversold signals. It does not open new trades but manages the closing of buy positions when price is above the upper Bollinger Band with RSI above 70, and sell positions when price is below the lower Bollinger Band with RSI below 30. It also features a global profit target that closes all positions when account profit exceeds a threshold.

Strategy Analysis

The EA's strategy concept is based on mean reversion principles, using Bollinger Bands and RSI to determine when price has deviated too far from the mean, closing positions at extreme zones to lock in profit. Exit conditions are twofold: for buy positions, it closes when Bid is above the upper Bollinger Band, RSI is above 70, and Bid is above the entry price; for sell positions, it closes when Ask is below the lower Bollinger Band, RSI is below 30, and Ask is below the entry price. Additionally, a global exit condition closes all positions when account profit exceeds the CloseAll threshold. The EA has no position sizing functionality since it does not open trades. Money management is limited to a single account profit threshold parameter, lacking per-trade stop loss mechanisms and drawdown controls. The CloseMode parameter allows selective closing of manual trades, algorithmic trades, or both, providing flexibility.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，使用布林带和RSI双重确认避免假信号
  • 要求平仓时价格必须优于开仓价，确保只在盈利时平仓
  • 支持按手动/算法/全部模式选择性平仓，灵活度高

劣势

  • EA初始化时acp变量只读取一次账户利润，之后不会更新，导致CloseAll功能失效
  • 指标句柄在每次循环迭代中重复创建（iRSI和iBands），造成严重的资源泄漏
  • 没有任何止损保护机制，如果价格不触及布林带极端区域，亏损仓位将无限持有

改进建议

  • 将acp变量移至MyAlgo函数内部每次调用时更新，或直接使用AccountInfoDouble实时获取账户利润
  • 在OnInit中创建指标句柄并缓存，避免在循环中重复创建导致资源泄漏和性能下降
  • 增加单笔止损参数和最大回撤限制，防止亏损仓位无限持有

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic with Bollinger Bands and RSI dual confirmation to avoid false signals
  • Requires price to be better than entry price when closing, ensuring only profitable exits
  • Supports selective closing by manual/algorithmic/all mode, offering high flexibility

Weaknesses

  • The acp variable (account profit) is only read once during initialization and never updated, rendering the CloseAll feature non-functional
  • Indicator handles (iRSI and iBands) are created repeatedly inside each loop iteration, causing serious resource leaks
  • No stop loss protection mechanism exists; losing positions will be held indefinitely if price never reaches Bollinger Band extremes

Improvements

  • Move the acp variable update inside MyAlgo or call AccountInfoDouble(ACCOUNT_PROFIT) in real-time instead of caching it at initialization
  • Create indicator handles in OnInit and cache them, avoiding repeated creation inside loops that causes resource leaks and performance degradation
  • Add per-trade stop loss parameter and maximum drawdown limit to prevent losing positions from being held indefinitely

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • acp变量在初始化时赋值后永不更新，CloseAll功能形同虚设，可能导致预期之外的大额亏损
  • 指标句柄每次循环重新创建会造成内存泄漏，长时间运行可能导致终端崩溃

Risk Assessment

Risk Points

  • Indicator handles are recreated every loop iteration causing memory leaks; prolonged operation may crash the terminal
