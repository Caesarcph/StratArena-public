摘要

该EA基于ZPF（零相位滤波器）自定义指标进行交易，当指标从零轴下方穿越至上方时做多，从零轴上方穿越至下方时做空。它支持多种平滑算法生成信号，并包含可配置的止损、止盈和仓位管理功能。交易在指定时间周期的新K线形成时执行。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于ZPF指标的零轴穿越信号进行动量交易。入场条件为：当ZPF指标值从零或负值转为正值时开多仓，从零或正值转为负值时开空仓，采用穿越确认机制避免假信号。出场条件包括：止损和止盈点位平仓，以及反向信号触发时平掉对应仓位（多头信号平空仓，空头信号平多仓）。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示固定手数，正值表示按存款比例计算仓位。资金管理依赖固定的止损止盈比（默认1:2），但没有动态调整机制或 trailing stop。策略使用了10种平滑算法选项，用户可以灵活选择以适应不同市场环境，但默认的SMA可能在快速市场中产生滞后。

Summary

This EA trades based on the ZPF (Zero-Phase Filter) custom indicator, entering long when the indicator crosses above zero and short when it crosses below zero. It uses multiple smoothing methods (SMA, EMA, JJMA, JurX, T3, VIDYA, AMA, etc.) for signal generation and includes configurable stop-loss, take-profit, and position sizing. Trading is executed on new bar formation at the specified timeframe.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is momentum trading based on zero-line crossover signals from the ZPF indicator. Entry conditions: open long when the ZPF indicator value crosses from zero or negative to positive, and open short when it crosses from zero or positive to negative, using a crossover confirmation mechanism to avoid false signals. Exit conditions include stop-loss and take-profit levels, as well as closing positions on opposing signals (close short on bullish signal, close long on bearish signal). Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the deposit. Money management relies on a fixed stop-loss to take-profit ratio (default 1:2), but lacks dynamic adjustment mechanisms or trailing stops. The strategy offers 10 smoothing algorithm options for flexible adaptation to different market conditions, though the default SMA may produce lag in fast-moving markets.

优势

  • 支持10种平滑算法，灵活适应不同市场环境
  • 止损止盈比1:2，风险收益比合理
  • 提供买卖开仓和平仓的独立开关，灵活控制交易方向
  • 新K线检查机制避免重复信号和过度交易
  • 使用自定义ZPF指标，零相位滤波减少信号滞后

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点偏大，可能导致较大单笔亏损
  • 缺乏动态止损和移动止损机制，无法锁定浮动利润
  • 没有点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生不利入场
  • 单一指标依赖，缺乏多因子确认或过滤机制
  • MM参数为负值时直接作为手数使用，缺少最小手数和手数步长校验

改进建议

  • 添加移动止损（Trailing Stop）功能以保护浮动利润
  • 增加点差过滤和交易时间过滤，避免不利市场条件下的入场

Strengths

  • Supports 10 smoothing algorithms for flexible adaptation to different market conditions
  • Stop-loss to take-profit ratio of 1:2 provides a reasonable risk-reward ratio
  • Independent switches for buy/sell opening and closing offer flexible directional control
  • New bar check mechanism prevents repeated signals and overtrading
  • Uses custom ZPF indicator with zero-phase filtering to reduce signal lag

Weaknesses

  • Stop-loss of 1000 points and take-profit of 2000 points are large, potentially leading to significant single-trade losses
  • Lacks dynamic stop-loss and trailing stop mechanisms, unable to lock in floating profits
  • No spread filter, may result in unfavorable entries in high-spread environments
  • Relies on a single indicator without multi-factor confirmation or filtering
  • When MM is negative it's used directly as lot size without minimum lot or lot step validation

Improvements

  • Add trailing stop functionality to protect floating profits
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid entries in unfavorable market conditions
