摘要

该EA基于自定义ZPF（零相位滤波器）指标进行交易，当指标从零或负值上穿正值时做多，从正值下穿负值时做空。它支持多种可配置的平滑方法（SMA、EMA、JJMA、JurX等），可应用于不同价格类型和成交量，并使用固定止损止盈水平。交易信号仅在新K线出现时生成，避免重复入场。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于ZPF指标的趋势跟踪，ZPF指标通过多层平滑算法（支持SMA、EMA、SMMA、LWMA、JJMA、JurX、ParMA、T3、VIDYA、AMA共10种方法）对选定价格类型进行零相位滤波处理，生成正负值信号。入场条件为：当指标buffer值从零或负值变为正值时开多单，从正值变为负值时开空单，即捕捉趋势方向的转换点。出场条件包括固定点数的止损（默认1000点）和止盈（默认2000点），以及反向信号平仓——当出现空头信号时平掉多头仓位，反之亦然。仓位管理采用资金份额模式（MM参数），负值表示固定手数，正值表示按存款比例计算手数。EA还提供了买卖开仓和平仓的权限开关，允许灵活控制交易方向。信号检测仅在新K线形成时触发，通过IsNewBar机制防止同一根K线上重复计算，同时使用SignalBar参数允许在当前K线上获取信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on the custom ZPF (Zero-Phase Filter) indicator, entering long when the indicator crosses from zero or negative to positive and entering short when it crosses from positive to negative. It uses configurable smoothing methods (SMA, EMA, JJMA, JurX, etc.) applied to various price types and volumes, with fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels. Trading is restricted to new bar events on the specified timeframe to avoid redundant signals.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is trend following based on the ZPF indicator, which applies zero-phase filtering through multi-layer smoothing algorithms (supporting 10 methods: SMA, EMA, SMMA, LWMA, JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, AMA) to the selected price type, generating positive/negative signals. Entry conditions are: open a long position when the indicator buffer value crosses from zero or negative to positive, and open a short position when it crosses from positive to negative, capturing trend direction transitions. Exit conditions include fixed stop-loss (default 1000 points) and take-profit (default 2000 points), plus counter-signal exits — closing long positions on bearish signals and vice versa. Position sizing uses a money management share model (MM parameter), where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values calculate lots as a proportion of the deposit. The EA also provides buy/sell open and close permission switches, allowing flexible control over trading directions. Signal detection is triggered only on new bar formation through the IsNewBar mechanism, preventing redundant calculations on the same bar, while the SignalBar parameter allows signal retrieval on the current bar when set to zero.

优势

  • 支持10种平滑算法和10种价格类型，策略参数化程度高，灵活性强
  • 具有买卖开平仓权限开关，可以只做单边交易或仅用信号平仓
  • 新K线检测机制避免重复信号，减少过度交易风险
  • 资金管理支持按存款比例和固定手数两种模式，适应不同需求
  • 反向信号平仓机制提供额外的趋势反转保护

劣势

  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未考虑市场波动率（ATR）动态调整，在不同行情下可能不适配
  • 缺少移动止损（追踪止损）功能，盈利仓位可能大幅回撤后才被止盈或反向信号平仓
  • 无点差过滤和滑点保护机制，在高点差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • 指标缓冲区读取仅使用2个值，信号判断较为简单，可能产生虚假信号
  • 未对仓位是否存在进行检查，可能在无持仓时仍尝试平仓

改进建议

  • 增加基于ATR的动态止损止盈计算，使风险管理适应市场波动率变化
  • 添加移动止损（追踪止损）功能，锁定浮动利润并减少盈利回撤

Strengths

  • Supports 10 smoothing algorithms and 10 price types, offering high parameterization and flexibility
  • Buy/sell open and close permission switches allow one-direction trading or signal-only closing
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate signals and reduces overtrading risk
  • Money management supports both deposit-proportion and fixed lot modes, adapting to different needs
  • Counter-signal closing mechanism provides additional protection against trend reversals

Weaknesses

  • Fixed stop-loss and take-profit in points without ATR-based dynamic adjustment, potentially misaligned across different market conditions
  • No trailing stop functionality, profitable positions may retrace significantly before hitting TP or being closed by counter-signal
  • No spread filter or slippage protection, may result in unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • Indicator buffer reading uses only 2 values for signal judgment, which is simplistic and may generate false signals
  • No position existence check before attempting to close, may attempt to close non-existent positions

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit calculation to adapt risk management to market volatility changes
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in floating profits and reduce profitable position drawdowns

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 固定止损止盈不考虑波动率，在低波动行情中止损过大，高波动行情中止盈可能不够
  • 无点差过滤，在剧烈行情或流动性差时可能导致不利成交
  • SignalBar=0时在当前K线上交易，信号可能随价格变化而消失导致虚假入场

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed SL/TP ignores volatility — stop may be too wide in low-volatility markets and TP insufficient in high-volatility conditions
  • No spread filter may lead to unfavorable fills during volatile or low-liquidity periods
  • When SignalBar=0, trading on the current bar means signals can disappear as price changes, causing false entries
