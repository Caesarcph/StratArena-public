摘要

Multi Arbitration是一个统计套利EA，利用多个相关品种之间的价格偏差进行交易。它同时监控和交易多个相关性较高的货币对，当相关资产价格偏离时开立对冲仓位，从价差的均值回归中获利。

策略分析

该EA采用统计套利策略，核心概念是基于多个相关金融工具之间的价差偏离进行交易。入场条件为当两个或多个相关品种的价差偏离历史均值达到一定阈值时，在偏离方向上开立对冲仓位——做多低估品种同时做空高估品种。出场条件是当价差回归到均值或达到止盈目标时平仓，同时设有止损以防止价差持续偏离造成的损失。仓位管理通常根据价差的统计特性（如标准差）来确定开仓规模，以控制单笔交易的风险敞口。资金管理方面，EA可能采用固定比例或基于波动率的方法来分配每笔交易的风险。该策略依赖品种间相关性的稳定性，一旦相关性结构发生突变（如市场危机期间），策略可能面临较大回撤风险。

Summary

Multi Arbitration is a statistical arbitrage EA that exploits price divergence across multiple correlated instruments. It simultaneously monitors and trades multiple currency pairs to capture convergence opportunities when correlated assets drift apart, opening opposing positions to profit from mean reversion of the spread.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a statistical arbitrage strategy based on the core concept of trading price spread deviations between multiple correlated financial instruments. Entry conditions trigger when the spread between two or more correlated instruments deviates from its historical mean by a predetermined threshold, at which point hedging positions are opened—going long on the undervalued instrument while shorting the overvalued one. Exit conditions are met when the spread reverts to its mean or reaches a take-profit target, with stop-losses also in place to protect against sustained spread divergence. Position sizing is typically determined by the statistical properties of the spread (such as standard deviation) to control risk exposure per trade. Money management may use fixed fractional or volatility-based methods to allocate risk per trade. The strategy relies on the stability of inter-instrument correlations; if the correlation structure breaks down (e.g., during market crises), the strategy may face significant drawdowns.

优势

  • 市场中性策略，不依赖单边趋势方向，在震荡市和趋势市中均可获利
  • 通过对冲仓位降低了系统性风险暴露，理论上具有较低的市场Beta
  • 多品种监控提高了交易机会频率，分散了单一品种的风险集中度

劣势

  • 高度依赖品种间相关性的稳定性，相关性崩塌时可能导致巨大损失
  • 统计套利的利润空间通常较小，交易成本（点差、滑点、佣金）可能侵蚀大部分利润
  • 多品种同时交易增加了复杂性，需要同时管理多个仓位和资金分配

改进建议

  • 增加动态相关性监测机制，当相关性低于阈值时自动暂停交易以避免策略失效
  • 引入自适应仓位管理，根据价差波动率动态调整开仓规模而非固定手数

Strengths

  • Market-neutral strategy that does not depend on directional trends, capable of profiting in both ranging and trending markets
  • Hedging positions reduce systemic risk exposure, theoretically maintaining low market Beta
  • Multi-instrument monitoring increases trade opportunity frequency and diversifies concentration risk away from single instruments

Weaknesses

  • Highly dependent on the stability of inter-instrument correlations; correlation breakdowns can lead to significant losses
  • Statistical arbitrage profit margins are typically narrow, and trading costs (spreads, slippage, commissions) can erode most of the profit
  • Simultaneous multi-instrument trading increases complexity, requiring concurrent management of multiple positions and capital allocation

Improvements

  • Add dynamic correlation monitoring that automatically pauses trading when correlations fall below threshold to prevent strategy failure
  • Introduce adaptive position sizing that dynamically adjusts trade size based on spread volatility rather than using fixed lots

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 相关性结构突变风险：在市场极端事件中，历史相关性可能完全失效，导致对冲仓位同时亏损
  • 滑点和流动性风险：多品种同时开平仓时，滑点可能显著增加，尤其在流动性不足的品种上

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Correlation regime shift risk: during extreme market events, historical correlations may completely fail, causing both legs of hedged positions to incur losses simultaneously
  • Slippage and liquidity risk: simultaneous opening/closing across multiple instruments may significantly increase slippage, especially on less liquid instruments
