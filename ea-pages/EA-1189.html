摘要

该EA结合JBrainTrendSig1和UltraRSI两个指标生成交易信号，提供三种运行模式：JBrainSig1过滤、UltraRSI过滤及双重组合过滤。基于信号交叉和趋势确认来开立买卖仓位，并配置了止损和止盈水平。策略采用多周期分析，指标时间周期可独立于图表周期设置。

策略分析

该策略核心概念是将JBrainTrendSig1趋势信号与UltraRSI动量信号结合，通过三种模式灵活组合入场条件。在JBrainSig1Filter模式下，UltraRSI提供入场信号而JBrainSig1作为趋势过滤器；在UltraRSIFilter模式下则相反；Composition模式下两者互相确认即可入场。JBrainSig1通过遍历历史K线寻找最近的趋势方向信号，UltraRSI则通过相邻两根K线的缓冲区数值交叉产生买卖信号。出场条件要求两个指标同时发出平仓信号，确保双重确认。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值代表固定手数，正值代表存款比例。止损1000点、止盈2000点的设置表明该策略偏向中长线趋势跟踪，风险回报比为1:2。

Summary

This EA combines JBrainTrendSig1 and UltraRSI indicators to generate trading signals with three operational modes: JBrainSig1-filtered, UltraRSI-filtered, or a composition of both. It opens buy/sell positions based on signal crossovers and trend confirmations, with configurable stop loss and take profit levels. The strategy uses multi-timeframe analysis with a user-defined indicator timeframe separate from the chart timeframe.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept combines JBrainTrendSig1 trend signals with UltraRSI momentum signals, offering three flexible modes for entry conditions. In JBrainSig1Filter mode, UltraRSI provides entry signals while JBrainSig1 acts as a trend filter; in UltraRSIFilter mode the roles are reversed; in Composition mode either indicator's cross-confirmation triggers entry. JBrainSig1 determines the most recent trend direction by iterating through historical bars until a signal is found, while UltraRSI generates buy/sell signals via buffer value crossovers between two adjacent bars. Exit conditions require both indicators to simultaneously signal position closure, ensuring dual confirmation before exiting. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent deposit share percentages. The stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points indicate a medium-to-long-term trend-following approach with a 1:2 risk-reward ratio.

优势

  • 双指标组合提供更强的信号确认，减少虚假信号
  • 三种运行模式灵活适应不同市场环境
  • 止损和止盈明确设置，风险回报比1:2较为合理
  • 多周期分析能力，指标可在不同时间框架计算
  • 买卖开关独立控制，可单独关闭某方向交易

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标JBrainTrendSig1和UltraRSI，缺乏标准指标可验证性
  • JBrainSig1信号通过遍历历史K线获取，在数据量大时可能影响性能
  • 没有点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生不利入场
  • 出场要求双指标同时确认，可能导致平仓延迟增加浮亏
  • MM参数负值代表固定手数的逻辑不够直观，容易误操作

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤机制，避免在点差过大时入场
  • 优化JBrainSig1信号获取逻辑，避免全量遍历历史K线
  • 增加移动止损或保本止损功能，保护已有利润

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator combination provides stronger signal confirmation and reduces false signals
  • Three operational modes offer flexibility to adapt to different market conditions
  • Explicit stop loss and take profit with a reasonable 1:2 risk-reward ratio
  • Multi-timeframe analysis capability with independent indicator timeframe setting
  • Independent buy/sell permission switches allow directional trading control

Weaknesses

  • Depends on custom indicators JBrainTrendSig1 and UltraRSI which lack standard verifiability
  • JBrainSig1 signal retrieval iterates through historical bars which may impact performance with large data
  • No spread filter, potentially leading to unfavorable entries in high-spread environments
  • Dual-confirmation exit requirement may delay position closure and increase floating losses
  • Negative MM value meaning fixed lot size is unintuitive and prone to misconfiguration

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter to prevent entries when spread exceeds a threshold
  • Optimize JBrainSig1 signal retrieval logic to avoid iterating all historical bars
  • Add trailing stop or break-even functionality to protect accumulated profits
