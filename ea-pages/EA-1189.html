EA-1189 · Multi-Timeframe

Exp_JBrainSig1_UltraRSI

This EA combines two indicators — JBrainTrendSig1 and UltraRSI — to generate trading signals. It offers three operational modes: using JBrainSig1 as a filter for UltraRSI signals, using UltraRSI as a filter for JBrainSig1 signals, or a composition mode where either indicator's open signal paired with the other's close signal triggers a trade. Entry signals require cross-confirmation from both indicators before opening buy or sell positions.

Strategy analysis

The EA's core strategy is a dual-indicator cross-confirmation system combining the trend-detection JBrainTrendSig1 indicator and the momentum oscillator UltraRSI. Entry conditions depend on the Mode parameter: in JBrainSig1Filter mode, UltraRSI generates open signals but JBrainSig1 must confirm direction; in UltraRSIFilter mode the roles are reversed; in Composition mode, either indicator's open signal paired with the other's close signal triggers entry. Exit conditions require both indicators to simultaneously emit close signals (SELL_Close1 && SELL_Close2 or BUY_Close1 && BUY_Close2). Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent deposit-proportional sizing. Each trade includes StopLoss at 1000 points and TakeProfit at 2000 points, yielding a 1:2 risk-reward ratio. The strategy operates on the H4 timeframe as a medium-to-long-term trading system, relying on a new bar detection mechanism to prevent repeated trading within the same bar.

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator cross-confirmation reduces false signal probability and improves entry quality
  • Three flexible operational modes adapt to different market conditions
  • StopLoss and TakeProfit are configured with a 1:2 risk-reward ratio, providing solid risk control
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated entries within the same bar
  • Independent buy/sell direction control offers high trading flexibility

Weaknesses

  • Composition mode allows either indicator's open signal to trigger a trade, potentially making entry conditions too loose
  • JBrainTrendSig1 uses a loop-back search for signals, which may impact performance with large historical data
  • No spread filter or trading session filter, risking unfavorable fills during wide spread or low liquidity periods
  • StopLoss of 1000 points is relatively large, potentially enduring significant floating drawdown on some instruments
  • Depends on custom indicator files (JBrainTrend1Sig and UltraRSI), missing either causes runtime failure

Possible improvements

  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid opening positions under unfavorable conditions
  • Optimize the JBrainTrendSig1 signal loop-back logic by limiting the maximum number of bars to search, preventing performance issues

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No spread filter — wide spreads during high volatility periods can lead to unfavorable fills
  • JBrainTrendSig1 loop-back has no upper limit — extreme cases may cause lag or timeout
  • Dependency on external custom indicators — missing or incompatible indicator files cause EA failure
