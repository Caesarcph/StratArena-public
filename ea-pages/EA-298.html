摘要

该EA使用CDC_PL_CCI信号过滤器，结合CCI（商品通道指标）与移动平均线在MQL5标准库EA框架内生成交易信号。它采用固定手数资金管理且无追踪止损，完全依赖CCI/MA动量信号进行入场和出场决策。

策略分析

策略概念基于CCI指标与移动平均线的组合动量信号，CCI用于识别价格偏离统计均值的程度，MA则用于平滑和确认趋势方向。入场条件由CCI和MA的交叉或阈值突破触发，信号需达到开仓阈值（默认40）和平仓阈值（默认20）才执行交易。出场条件依赖信号强度的反向衰减，当信号值降至平仓阈值以下时平仓，未设置硬性止损和止盈（均为0）。仓位管理采用固定手数模式（默认0.1手），不根据账户余额或波动性动态调整。资金管理使用CMoneyFixedLot类，按固定百分比（10%）和固定手数进行资金分配，缺乏自适应的仓位调整机制。

Summary

This EA uses the CDC_PL_CCI signal filter, which combines CCI (Commodity Channel Index) with a Moving Average to generate trade signals within the MQL5 Standard Library Expert Advisor framework. It employs fixed-lot money management and no trailing stop, relying entirely on the CCI/MA momentum signal for entry and exit decisions.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a combined momentum signal from the CCI indicator and a Moving Average, where CCI identifies the degree of price deviation from its statistical mean and MA smooths and confirms trend direction. Entry conditions are triggered by CCI and MA crossover or threshold breakthroughs, with signals required to reach the open threshold (default 40) and close threshold (default 20) before executing trades. Exit conditions rely on the reverse decay of signal strength, closing positions when the signal value drops below the close threshold, with no hard stop loss or take profit configured (both set to 0). Position sizing uses a fixed lot model (default 0.1 lots), which does not dynamically adjust based on account balance or market volatility. Money management employs the CMoneyFixedLot class with a fixed percentage (10%) and fixed lot size allocation, lacking an adaptive position sizing mechanism.

优势

  • 使用MQL5标准库Expert框架，代码结构清晰规范，模块化程度高
  • CCI与MA组合信号可有效过滤假突破，减少频繁交易
  • 信号阈值机制提供了灵活的开平仓控制，可调节策略灵敏度
  • 固定手数降低了复利风险，适合小资金账户测试

劣势

  • 止损和止盈均设为0，没有硬性风险控制，极端行情下可能造成重大亏损
  • 无追踪止损，盈利头寸可能因回撤而转为亏损
  • 固定手数不考虑账户规模和波动性变化，缺乏自适应资金管理
  • 仅使用单一信号过滤器，策略多样性不足，容易在震荡市中产生连续亏损
  • 未设置新K线检查（EveryTick=false但无bar检查），可能在新bar内多次计算

改进建议

  • 添加止损和止盈参数，提供基本的风险保护机制
  • 引入动态仓位管理，根据ATR或账户风险比例自动调整手数
  • 增加额外的信号过滤器或时间过滤，提高策略的稳健性和适应性
  • 添加追踪止损功能，保护已获浮盈

Strengths

  • Uses the MQL5 Standard Library Expert framework with clean, well-structured, and highly modular code
  • CCI and MA combination signal effectively filters false breakouts and reduces over-trading
  • Signal threshold mechanism provides flexible open/close control and adjustable strategy sensitivity
  • Fixed lot sizing reduces compounding risk, suitable for small account testing

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss and take profit are both set to 0, lacking hard risk controls which could cause significant losses in extreme market conditions
  • No trailing stop means profitable positions may reverse into losses due to price retracements
  • Fixed lot sizing ignores account size and volatility changes, lacking adaptive money management
  • Uses only a single signal filter, lacking strategy diversity and prone to consecutive losses in ranging markets
  • No explicit new bar check despite EveryTick=false, potentially recalculating multiple times within a bar

Improvements

  • Add stop loss and take profit parameters to provide basic risk protection mechanisms
  • Introduce dynamic position sizing based on ATR or account risk ratio for automatic lot adjustment
  • Add additional signal filters or time filters to improve strategy robustness and adaptability
  • Add trailing stop functionality to protect unrealized profits
