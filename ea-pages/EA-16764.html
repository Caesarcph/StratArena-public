摘要

该EA结合支点(Pivot Point)水平和平均K线(Heiken Ashi)过滤来生成交易信号。当价格与支点支撑/阻力位互动且平均K线确认趋势方向时，识别入场机会。系统在支点水平突破或反弹并得到平均K线颜色确认时下单。

策略分析

策略概念基于经典支点计算方法，通过前一日的高、低、收盘价计算出中心支点及多级支撑阻力位。入场条件为：当价格突破支点阻力位且Heiken Ashi蜡烛图由绿转红（多头信号）时做多；当价格跌破支点支撑位且Heiken Ashi由红转绿（空头信号）时做空。出场条件包括触及下一个支点目标位、固定止损或Heiken Ashi颜色反转信号。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，未发现动态仓位调整或基于ATR的自适应手数逻辑。资金管理方面，止损设置相对明确但缺乏最大回撤保护或账户风险比例控制。整体策略逻辑清晰，将支撑阻力与趋势确认结合，但缺乏对震荡行情的过滤机制和动态风险管理。

Summary

This EA combines Pivot Point levels with Heiken Ashi candlestick filtering to generate trade signals. It identifies entry opportunities when price interacts with pivot support/resistance levels and Heiken Ashi confirms trend direction. The system places trades at pivot level breakouts or bounces with Heiken Ashi color confirmation.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the classic pivot point calculation method, deriving the central pivot and multiple support/resistance levels from the previous day's high, low, and close prices. Entry conditions: go long when price breaks above a pivot resistance level and Heiken Ashi candles turn from green to red (bullish signal); go short when price breaks below a pivot support level and Heiken Ashi turns from red to green (bearish signal). Exit conditions include reaching the next pivot target level, a fixed stop-loss, or a Heiken Ashi color reversal signal. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach with no dynamic sizing or ATR-based adaptive lot logic detected. Money management has defined stop-losses but lacks maximum drawdown protection or account risk percentage controls. The overall strategy logic is clear, combining support/resistance with trend confirmation, but it lacks filtering for ranging markets and dynamic risk management.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，将支点支撑阻力与Heiken Ashi趋势确认有效结合
  • 使用经典支点计算方法，支撑阻力位具有统计可靠性
  • 入场有双重确认机制（支点水平+蜡烛图颜色），减少假信号
  • 止损止盈设置明确，每笔交易风险可控
  • 代码结构相对简洁，参数可调节性较好

劣势

  • 固定手数交易，未根据账户规模或波动性动态调整仓位
  • 缺乏震荡行情过滤机制，盘整市中可能频繁假突破
  • 未设置最大回撤限制或账户级别风险控制
  • 支点仅基于前一日数据计算，未考虑周线或月线支点
  • 未对滑点和点差进行过滤，在流动性差时可能造成额外损失

改进建议

  • 加入ATR动态止损止盈和仓位管理，根据市场波动性自适应调整
  • 增加ADX或波动率过滤器，在震荡行情中减少交易频率
  • 加入账户级别最大回撤保护和每日最大亏损限制

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic effectively combining pivot support/resistance with Heiken Ashi trend confirmation
  • Classic pivot calculation method provides statistically reliable support/resistance levels
  • Dual confirmation mechanism (pivot level + candle color) reduces false signals
  • Defined stop-loss and take-profit keep per-trade risk controlled
  • Relatively clean code structure with good parameter adjustability

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing without dynamic adjustment based on account size or volatility
  • Lacks ranging market filter, prone to frequent false breakouts in sideways conditions
  • No maximum drawdown limit or account-level risk control implemented
  • Pivots calculated from only the previous day, ignoring weekly or monthly pivot levels
  • No spread or slippage filter, potentially causing extra losses in low liquidity

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss/take-profit and position sizing for volatility-adaptive adjustment
  • Add ADX or volatility filter to reduce trading frequency in ranging markets
  • Implement account-level maximum drawdown protection and daily loss limits
