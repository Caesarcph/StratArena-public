HarVesteR

HarVesteR is a trend-following Expert Advisor that harvests profits from directional market moves using technical indicator combinations. It identifies trend direction and enters positions on pullbacks or momentum confirmations, with built-in profit harvesting through trailing stops and partial closures. The EA is designed for medium-term timeframes and aims to capture sustained trend movements.

Strategy analysis

The core concept of HarVesteR's strategy is trend following combined with profit harvesting, trading in the direction of established market trends. Entry conditions are typically based on moving average crossovers or price breakouts of key levels, confirmed by momentum indicators such as RSI or MACD to filter out false signals. Exit conditions include fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, trailing stop tracking, and partial position closing mechanisms for phased profit realization. Position sizing employs either fixed lot sizes or balance-proportional methods to control per-trade risk exposure. Money management includes maximum position limits and account risk percentage controls, though the strictness of implementation depends on parameter configuration. The overall strategy logic is clear, but it lacks explicit filtering for ranging market conditions, which may lead to frequent false signals and losing trades during sideways markets.

Strengths

  • Clear trend-following logic with favorable win rate when trading with the trend
  • Built-in trailing stop and partial closing mechanisms effectively protect profits
  • Highly configurable parameters suitable for optimization across different market conditions
  • Multi-timeframe support provides good operational flexibility
  • Magic number for order identification prevents conflicts with other EAs

Weaknesses

  • Lacks ranging market filter, prone to consecutive losses during sideways markets
  • Position sizing may not be refined enough, lacks ATR-adaptive stop-loss
  • No built-in spread filter, entry quality degrades under high-spread conditions
  • Over-reliance on parameter optimization risks curve-fitting
  • Lacks drawdown protection mechanism, significant risk during extreme market conditions

Possible improvements

  • Add ranging market identification filter (e.g., ADX or Bollinger Band width) to reduce ineffective trades during consolidation
  • Introduce ATR-adaptive stop-loss and take-profit to dynamically adjust risk parameters based on market volatility

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Lacks ranging market filter, may generate frequent losing trades during consolidation
  • No built-in spread filter or slippage protection increases execution risk under extreme market conditions
  • Position sizing lacks account-level maximum drawdown protection mechanism
