摘要

该EA基于William Blau的Ergodic MDI（均值偏差指标）进行交易，提供三种信号模式：零线突破、柱状图方向变化和信号云颜色交叉。它根据指标的动量信号系统地开仓和平仓，并配有可配置的止损和止盈水平。

策略分析

策略概念基于William Blau的Ergodic MDI动量指标，该指标通过多级平滑处理价格偏差来识别趋势动量。入场条件有三种模式可选：零线突破模式（柱状图穿越零线时入场）、方向变化模式（柱状图方向反转时入场）和信号云变色模式（信号线上下轨交叉时入场）。出场条件由反向信号触发平仓，同时配合固定的止损和止盈参数。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，可选择固定手数或按存款比例计算手数。资金管理支持LOT和MARGIN两种模式，由MMMode枚举决定。信号仅在出现新K线时计算，避免逐tick重复触发，SignalBar参数允许选择当前K线或已确认K线作为信号源。

Summary

This EA trades based on William Blau's Ergodic MDI (Mean Deviation Indicator) using three distinct signal modes: zero-line breakout, histogram direction change, and signal cloud color crossover. It opens and closes positions systematically on the indicator's momentum signals with configurable stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on William Blau's Ergodic MDI momentum indicator, which identifies trend momentum through multi-stage smoothing of price deviation. Entry conditions offer three selectable modes: breakdown mode (histogram crossing the zero line), twist mode (histogram direction reversal), and cloudtwist mode (signal cloud upper/lower band crossover). Exit conditions are triggered by opposite signals closing existing positions, supplemented by fixed stop loss and take profit parameters. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, supporting either fixed lot size or deposit share-based calculation. Money management supports LOT and MARGIN modes as determined by the MMMode enumeration. Signals are calculated only on new bar formation to avoid per-tick repeated triggers, and the SignalBar parameter allows selecting between the current or confirmed bar as the signal source.

优势

  • 提供三种灵活的信号模式，适应不同市场环境和交易偏好
  • 使用多级平滑的Blau Ergodic MDI指标，减少了噪音信号和虚假触发
  • 仅在新K线形成时计算信号，避免逐tick过度交易
  • 支持独立的买卖开平仓权限控制，灵活性高
  • 止损止盈参数完善，提供基本风险保护

劣势

  • 缺少点差过滤，在点差较大的品种上可能产生不利入场
  • 没有加仓或减仓逻辑，仓位管理较为简单单一
  • 依赖iCustom自定义指标，指标文件缺失时EA无法运行
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值过大，对多数品种不合适
  • 缺少回撤控制和最大持仓限制等高级风控功能

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和最大点差限制，避免在高点差环境下开仓
  • 添加动态止损止盈机制，如ATR自适应止损或移动止损，替代固定点数止损止盈
  • 增加回撤控制和每日最大亏损限制，提升资金安全性

Strengths

  • Offers three flexible signal modes adaptable to different market environments and trading preferences
  • Uses multi-stage smoothed Blau Ergodic MDI indicator which reduces noise signals and false triggers
  • Calculates signals only on new bar formation, avoiding per-tick over-trading
  • Supports independent buy/sell open/close permission controls for high flexibility
  • Complete stop loss and take profit parameters provide basic risk protection

Weaknesses

  • Lacks spread filter which may cause unfavorable entries on high-spread instruments
  • No position scaling logic; position management is simple and single-layered
  • Depends on iCustom custom indicator; EA fails to run if the indicator file is missing
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments
  • Lacks advanced risk controls such as drawdown limits and maximum position restrictions

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and maximum spread limit to avoid opening positions in high-spread environments
  • Implement dynamic stop loss/take profit mechanisms such as ATR-adaptive stops or trailing stops to replace fixed point values
  • Add drawdown control and daily maximum loss limits to enhance capital safety
