摘要

该EA实现了MT5网格交易策略，在当前市价上下按固定间距放置买卖挂单。通过调整网格层级、在整体网格持仓达到目标利润时平仓来管理网格，并可选择在后续网格层级使用类马丁格尔加仓方式。

策略分析

该EA采用纯网格交易策略，在价格上下按设定间距布置买卖挂单网格。入场条件是当价格触及网格某一层级时自动成交对应挂单，没有趋势判断或指标信号驱动。出场条件是当整个网格的浮盈达到设定的获利目标时全部平仓，或达到最大亏损限制时止损。仓位管理采用递增手数方式，每一层网格的手数可按倍数或固定增量递增，本质上带有马丁格尔属性。资金管理方面，EA允许设置最大网格层数、起始手数和手数递增系数来控制总仓位风险，但缺乏基于账户净值的动态调整机制。整体策略在震荡行情中表现良好，但在单边趋势中会因持续加仓而面临巨大风险。

Summary

This EA implements a grid trading strategy for MetaTrader 5, placing buy and sell pending orders at fixed price intervals around the current market price. It manages the grid by adjusting order levels, taking profit when the overall grid position reaches a target, and optionally using martingale-like lot scaling on subsequent grid levels.

Strategy Analysis

This EA implements a pure grid trading strategy, placing buy and sell pending orders at predefined intervals above and below the current price. Entry conditions are purely mechanical — orders are triggered when price reaches a grid level, with no trend or indicator-based signal generation. Exit conditions are met when the cumulative floating profit of the entire grid reaches a configurable take-profit target, or when a maximum loss threshold is hit. Position sizing uses incremental lot scaling where each subsequent grid level can increase the lot size by a multiplier or fixed increment, giving the strategy an inherent martingale-like character. Money management relies on configurable parameters such as maximum grid levels, starting lot size, and lot multiplication factor, but lacks dynamic adjustment based on account equity. The strategy performs well in ranging markets but faces significant risk of catastrophic drawdown in strong trending conditions due to continuously adding positions against the trend.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中能持续获利，利用价格来回波动获利
  • 参数可配置性强，可灵活调整网格间距、手数和层级
  • 同时布置买卖双向网格，能捕捉双向波动机会

劣势

  • 单边趋势行情中持续逆势加仓，可能导致巨大亏损甚至爆仓
  • 手数递增机制本质上是马丁格尔变种，风险指数级增长
  • 缺乏趋势过滤机制，在任何市场状态下都机械地布置网格

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤指标（如均线或ADX），在明显趋势中减少或暂停逆势网格
  • 增加基于账户净值或风险的动态仓位调整，限制最大暴露风险
  • 增加网格层级止损机制，当达到最大层数时强制全部平仓而非仅依赖浮盈/浮亏阈值

Strengths

  • Generates consistent profits in ranging markets by capitalizing on price oscillations within the grid
  • Highly configurable parameters allow flexible adjustment of grid spacing, lot sizes, and levels
  • Bi-directional buy and sell grids can capture opportunities from price movements in either direction

Weaknesses

  • Continuously adding positions against the trend in strong directional markets can lead to catastrophic losses or account blowout
  • The lot increment mechanism is essentially a martingale variant, causing risk to grow exponentially with grid depth
  • No trend filtering mechanism — the grid is placed mechanically regardless of market regime

Improvements

  • Add trend filtering indicators (e.g., moving averages or ADX) to reduce or pause counter-trend grid placement during strong trends
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account equity or risk limits to cap maximum exposure
  • Add a hard stop-loss mechanism at maximum grid levels that forces full closure instead of relying solely on floating P/L thresholds
