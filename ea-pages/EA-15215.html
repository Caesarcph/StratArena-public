Exp_Karacatica

This EA trades breakout signals generated by the custom 'Karacatica' indicator, which is based on ATR. It opens buy positions on upward breakout signals and sell positions on downward breakout signals, with optional position closing on opposite signals. Money management is configurable with multiple margin modes including fixed lot sizing.

Strategy analysis

The EA's strategy concept is based on breakout signals generated by the Karacatica indicator, which internally uses ATR (Average True Range) to measure market volatility and produce breakout signals. Entry conditions are: open a buy position when indicator buffer 1 (UpValue) produces a non-empty, non-zero signal, and open a sell position when buffer 0 (DnValue) produces a non-empty, non-zero signal. Exit conditions are: close a buy position when a downward breakout signal appears, and close a sell position when an upward breakout signal appears, with configurable fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit levels in points. For position management, the EA uses the IsNewBar mechanism to ensure at most one signal per bar, preventing repeated entries on the same candle. Money management supports five modes: free margin percentage, balance percentage, loss-adjusted free margin percentage, loss-adjusted balance percentage, and fixed lot mode. The EA also includes an additional historical lookback check logic: when both opening and closing are enabled but no close signal exists on the current signal bar, it traverses forward through historical bars to find any missed closing signals.

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on ATR breakout signals that adapts to market volatility changes
  • Rich money management modes offering five different position sizing methods for high flexibility
  • Built-in IsNewBar mechanism prevents duplicate entries on the same bar, avoiding signal repetition
  • Independent toggle switches for buy/sell opening and closing provide high configuration flexibility
  • Historical lookback logic for closing signals reduces the risk of missing exit signals

Weaknesses

  • Fixed point StopLoss/TakeProfit instead of ATR-based dynamic calculation leads to inconsistent risk-reward ratios across different volatility environments
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, which may result in unfavorable fills during high-spread or low-liquidity periods
  • The historical lookback closing signal check iterates through all historical bars, causing potential performance issues with large bar counts
  • Depends on external custom indicator Karacatica via iCustom call, making backtesting and optimization difficult without the indicator source code
  • No global risk controls such as maximum position count limits or maximum drawdown protection

Possible improvements

  • Replace fixed-point StopLoss/TakeProfit with ATR-multiple-based dynamic stops to make risk-reward ratios self-adaptive to market volatility
  • Add spread filter and trading session controls to avoid opening positions under unfavorable conditions, and implement global risk controls like maximum position count and maximum drawdown limits
  • Optimize the historical lookback closing signal check logic by setting a maximum lookback bar count limit to avoid performance issues

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Fixed 1000-point SL and 2000-point TP may be too large for low-volatility instruments and too small for high-volatility ones
  • Historical lookback closing check has no bar count limit, potentially iterating through all history causing lag
  • No spread filter may result in unfavorable fills during news events or low-liquidity sessions when spreads widen
  • SignalBar=1 introduces one-bar signal delay, but SignalBar=0 may generate false signals on uncompleted bars
