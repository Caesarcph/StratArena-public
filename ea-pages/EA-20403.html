Crossing of two iMA v2

This EA trades based on the crossover of two Moving Averages (iMA). It opens a buy position when the fast MA crosses above the slow MA and a sell position when the fast MA crosses below the slow MA, closing opposite positions on signal reversal.

Strategy analysis

This strategy is a classic dual moving average crossover trend-following system. Entry conditions: a buy is triggered when the fast moving average crosses above the slow moving average, and a sell is triggered when the fast MA crosses below the slow MA. Exit conditions: when a reversal crossover signal occurs, the EA closes the current position and may open a reverse position. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size approach, with the user setting the trade volume via input parameters. For money management, the EA provides stop loss and take profit parameters for the user to configure, but lacks dynamic position sizing or account-balance-proportional money management. The overall strategy logic is simple and clear, suitable for strongly trending markets, but is prone to frequent false signals and consecutive losses in choppy or ranging markets.

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and verify
  • Classic trend-following approach that performs well in strong trending markets
  • Highly configurable parameters allow optimization for different instruments and timeframes

Weaknesses

  • Prone to frequent false signals and consecutive losses in choppy/ranging markets
  • Lacks dynamic position sizing; fixed lot size does not adapt to different account sizes
  • Default stop loss and take profit are 0, risking significant losses if not manually configured
  • No new bar check mechanism, potentially opening multiple positions within the same bar

Possible improvements

  • Add new bar detection logic to prevent repeated trading within the same bar
  • Implement ATR-based dynamic stop loss or trailing stop to improve risk management
  • Add a trend filter (e.g., higher timeframe MA direction confirmation) to reduce false signals in ranging markets

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Default SL/TP of 0 exposes the account to unlimited loss risk if user forgets to set them
  • Lack of new bar check may cause repeated position opening within the same bar
  • Fixed lot size without proportional money management may lead to overleveraging on small accounts
