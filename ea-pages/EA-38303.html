摘要

MAGrid是一个网格交易EA，以移动平均线为中心线，在固定价格间距上下对称布置买卖挂单。它通过价格在区间内来回震荡时系统性地开仓和平仓来获利，利用市场波动的均值回归特性捕捉利润。

策略分析

MAGrid策略的核心概念是以移动平均线作为网格的中心锚点，在价格上下方按固定间距布置多层买卖挂单，形成对称的网格结构。入场条件是当价格触及网格某一层时，对应的限价单被触发成交，买入单在均线之下、卖出单在均线之上。出场条件通常为当价格回归到中心线或达到设定的止盈目标时平仓获利。仓位管理采用固定手数或可配置的每层手数，网格层数和间距均可通过参数调整。资金管理方面，由于网格策略会同时持有多个仓位，需要确保账户保证金足以覆盖所有挂单成交后的持仓，否则可能面临爆仓风险。该策略在震荡行情中表现良好，但在单边趋势行情中可能导致一侧网格全部成交且浮亏不断扩大。

Summary

MAGrid is a grid trading Expert Advisor that places buy and sell pending orders at fixed price intervals around a moving average center line. It aims to profit from price oscillations within a range by systematically opening and closing grid levels as the market moves back and forth. The EA uses a moving average as the anchor point and builds a symmetric grid of limit orders on both sides.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of MAGrid is using a moving average as the central anchor point and placing symmetric buy and sell limit orders at fixed intervals above and below it, forming a structured trading grid. Entry conditions are triggered when price reaches a grid level, causing the corresponding pending order to be filled — buy orders below the MA and sell orders above it. Exit conditions typically involve closing positions when price reverts to the center line or when a configured take-profit target per level is reached. Position sizing uses either fixed lots or configurable lot sizes per grid level, with the number of grid layers and spacing both adjustable via input parameters. Money management is critical since the grid can hold multiple simultaneous positions, requiring sufficient margin to cover all filled orders or risking a margin call. The strategy performs well in ranging markets but can suffer significant drawdowns in strong trending conditions where one side of the grid fills completely with accumulating unrealized losses.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中能稳定获利，利用价格回归均值特性反复捕捉小利润
  • 参数可配置性强，网格间距、层数、手数均可灵活调整适应不同品种
  • 逻辑清晰易懂，系统化执行减少人为情绪干扰
  • 以移动平均线为动态中心，比固定价格网格更能适应市场趋势变化

劣势

  • 单边趋势行情中一侧网格全部成交后浮亏会持续扩大，存在爆仓风险
  • 如果止损设为0则每层无止损保护，极端行情下损失不可控
  • 网格层数过多时占用保证金大，资金利用率低且风险集中
  • 缺乏趋势过滤机制，在明显单边行情中仍会不断在逆势方向建仓

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤器（如ADX或大周期MA方向判断），在强趋势行情中暂停逆势网格建仓
  • 添加整体账户风险控制，包括最大浮亏限制和总持仓手数上限，防止极端行情下爆仓

Strengths

  • Consistently profits in ranging markets by capturing small gains from price mean-reversion movements
  • Highly configurable parameters allow grid spacing, levels, and lot sizes to be adapted to different instruments
  • Clear and logical strategy with systematic execution reducing emotional trading decisions
  • Dynamic MA center line adapts better to market trend shifts compared to fixed-price grids

Weaknesses

  • Sustained trending markets fill one side of the grid completely, causing mounting unrealized losses and potential margin calls
  • Zero stop-loss setting means each level has no protection, making losses unbounded during extreme market moves
  • Too many grid levels consume excessive margin, leading to poor capital efficiency and concentrated risk
  • Lacks trend filtering — continues placing counter-trend orders during strong directional moves

Improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., ADX or higher-timeframe MA direction) to pause counter-trend grid placement during strong trending conditions
  • Implement account-level risk controls including maximum floating loss limits and total position size caps to prevent margin calls in extreme scenarios

Risk Assessment

Overall:HIGH

Risk Points

  • Default stop-loss of zero means each grid level has no protection, risking catastrophic losses during black swan events
  • Multiple simultaneous positions consume significant margin; poor capital management easily triggers forced liquidation
