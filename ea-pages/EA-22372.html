摘要

该EA基于Skyscraper趋势指标与ColorAML（自适应移动线性回归）过滤器组合，采用固定手数及资金管理建议。当ColorAML斜率确认Skyscraper趋势方向时入场，使用基于ATR或固定止损/止盈水平出场。MMRec组件提供仓位管理的资金管理建议。

策略分析

策略核心是结合Skyscraper趋势指标和ColorAML自适应移动线性回归线进行双重过滤的趋势跟踪系统。入场条件为：Skyscraper指标显示上升趋势且ColorAML斜率为正时做多，Skyscraper显示下降趋势且ColorAML斜率为负时做空，双重确认降低了假信号的概率。出场条件包括固定止损止盈和ATR动态止损，能够在趋势反转时及时退出。仓位管理采用固定手数模式，但MMRec模块根据账户余额和风险比例提供手数建议，帮助控制每笔交易的风险敞口。资金管理方面，EA建议每笔交易风险不超过账户余额的1-2%，通过合理的手数计算来平衡收益与风险。整体策略逻辑清晰，双指标过滤有效减少了震荡行情中的虚假信号。

Summary

This EA is based on the Skyscraper trend-following indicator combined with a ColorAML (Adaptive Moving Linear Regression) filter and fixed lot sizing with money management recommendations. It enters trades in the direction of the Skyscraper trend when confirmed by the ColorAML slope, and uses ATR-based or fixed stop loss/take profit levels for exits. The 'MMRec' component provides money management recommendations for position sizing.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is a dual-filter trend following system combining the Skyscraper trend indicator with the ColorAML (Adaptive Moving Linear Regression) line. Entry conditions: go long when Skyscraper shows an uptrend AND ColorAML slope is positive; go short when Skyscraper shows a downtrend AND ColorAML slope is negative, with the dual confirmation reducing false signal probability. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss/take profit and ATR-based dynamic stops, allowing timely exits on trend reversals. Position sizing uses a fixed lot mode, but the MMRec module provides lot size recommendations based on account balance and risk ratio, helping control risk exposure per trade. In terms of money management, the EA recommends risking no more than 1-2% of account balance per trade, balancing returns and risk through proper lot calculation. The overall strategy logic is clear, and the dual-indicator filtering effectively reduces false signals during ranging markets.

优势

  • 双指标过滤系统有效减少震荡行情中的虚假信号和频繁交易
  • MMRec资金管理模块根据账户风险比例动态建议手数，有助于长期资金安全
  • ATR动态止损机制能根据市场波动性自动调整止损距离，适应不同行情环境
  • 趋势跟踪策略在明确趋势行情中表现优异，盈亏比合理

劣势

  • 趋势跟踪策略在震荡盘整行情中容易产生连续亏损，缺乏横盘过滤机制
  • 固定手数与MMRec建议手数可能存在冲突，用户可能忽略推荐而使用过大的固定手数
  • 缺少新K线检查机制，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓导致风险集中
  • 没有点差过滤，在大幅点差时仍会入场，增加交易成本

改进建议

  • 增加横盘/震荡市场识别过滤器（如ADX或布林带宽度），在无明显趋势时暂停交易
  • 添加新K线检查和点差过滤机制，避免重复开仓和高成本交易
  • 将固定手数改为默认使用MMRec动态手数，强制执行风险比例控制

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator filtering system effectively reduces false signals and overtrading during ranging markets
  • MMRec money management module dynamically recommends lot sizes based on account risk ratio, supporting long-term capital safety
  • ATR-based dynamic stop loss adjusts stop distance according to market volatility, adapting to different market conditions
  • Trend following strategy performs excellently in clear trending markets with reasonable risk-reward ratios

Weaknesses

  • Trend following strategy tends to produce consecutive losses in ranging/sideways markets, lacking a consolidation filter
  • Conflict between fixed lot size and MMRec recommended lots may lead users to ignore recommendations and use oversized fixed lots
  • Lacks new bar check mechanism, potentially opening repeated positions on the same bar leading to concentrated risk
  • No spread filter, entering trades even during wide spreads increases transaction costs

Improvements

  • Add a ranging market filter (such as ADX or Bollinger Band width) to pause trading when no clear trend exists
  • Implement new bar check and spread filter mechanisms to prevent repeated entries and high-cost trades
  • Change default from fixed lots to MMRec dynamic lot sizing, enforcing risk percentage control
