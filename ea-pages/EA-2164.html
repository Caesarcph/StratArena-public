EA-2164 · Trend Following

Exp_AltrTrend_Signal_v2_2

This EA trades based on signals from the AltrTrend_Signal_v2_2 custom indicator, which identifies trend direction changes. It opens buy positions on upward trend signals and sell positions on downward trend signals, with configurable stop loss, take profit, and position sizing based on a share of the deposit.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy of this EA is a trend-following trading system based on the AltrTrend_Signal_v2_2 custom indicator. Entry conditions: when indicator buffer 1 (UpValue) shows a non-zero, non-EMPTY_VALUE value on the specified signal bar, a buy signal is generated; when indicator buffer 0 (DnValue) shows a valid value, a sell signal is generated. Exit conditions operate in two ways: first, through reverse signal closing (a buy signal triggers short position closure, a sell signal triggers long position closure), and second, through fixed stop loss and take profit levels in points. Position sizing uses a deposit share-based method controlled by the MM parameter, supporting both LOT and MARGIN calculation modes. For money management, the EA searches backward through historical bars for the most recent reverse signal to determine whether position closing is needed when no direct reverse signal exists on the current bar. The strategy includes a new bar detection mechanism via the CIsNewBar class to prevent repeated trading within the same bar, but lacks scaling in or scaling out logic for position management.

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on trend-following signals with well-defined direction
  • Flexible position sizing that dynamically adjusts lot size based on deposit share
  • Independent configuration of buy/sell open and close permissions for granular trade control
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated entries within the same bar
  • SignalBar parameter allows flexible selection of signal confirmation delay

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom AltrTrend_Signal_v2_2 indicator which must be available, making backtesting and deployment less portable
  • Lacks spread filtering, which may result in unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • Backward bar traversal to search for reverse signals may be inefficient on long historical data
  • Default StopLoss of 1000 points and TakeProfit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments
  • Lacks multi-timeframe confirmation and additional filtering conditions, making it prone to false signals

Possible improvements

  • Add spread and volatility filters to reduce unfavorable fills and false signals
  • Optimize the historical reverse signal search logic by setting a maximum bar search limit to prevent infinite traversal

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No spread filter, which may cause slippage losses on high-spread instruments
  • Default stop loss and take profit point values are very large, potentially leading to excessive per-trade losses
  • Unbounded historical bar traversal may cause execution delays in extreme cases
