摘要

该EA基于PPO_Cloud自定义指标进行交易，该指标是百分比价格振荡器(PPO)的变体，支持多种可配置的平滑算法。当PPO线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿信号线时开空仓，并支持反向信号平仓。

策略分析

策略概念基于百分比价格振荡器(PPO)云图指标，通过快慢两条平滑线的差值与信号线的关系判断动量方向。入场条件为：当指标线在信号线上方且前一根柱指标线低于或等于信号线时，产生买入信号；当指标线在信号线下方且前一根柱指标线高于或等于信号线时，产生卖出信号，本质上是一个动量穿越策略。出场条件包括止损和止盈的固定点数设置，以及反向信号触发的平仓功能。仓位管理支持多种模式，包括按自由保证金比例、按余额比例、按净自由保证金比例、按净余额比例以及固定手数五种模式。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1即10%的资金用于交易。该策略使用可配置的新K线检测机制(SignalBar=1时逐Tick检测)，支持多种平滑算法(SMA/EMA/SMMA/LWMA/JJMA/JurX/ParMA/T3/VIDYA/AMA)增强指标灵活性。

Summary

This EA trades based on the PPO_Cloud custom indicator, which is a Percentage Price Oscillator variant with configurable smoothing algorithms. It opens buy positions when the PPO line crosses above the signal line and sell positions when it crosses below, with optional position closing on reverse signals.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the PPO_Cloud indicator, which uses the relationship between a fast-slow smoothed difference line and a signal line to determine momentum direction. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the indicator line is above the signal line and the previous bar's indicator line was at or below the signal line; a sell signal is generated when the indicator line is below the signal line and the previous bar's indicator line was at or above the signal line, making it fundamentally a momentum crossover strategy. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit in points, plus optional position closing on reverse crossover signals. Position sizing supports five modes: by free margin percentage, by balance percentage, by net free margin percentage, by net balance percentage, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter, defaulting to 0.1 (10% of funds for trading). The strategy uses a configurable new bar detection mechanism (tick-by-tick when SignalBar=1), and supports multiple smoothing algorithms (SMA/EMA/SMMA/LWMA/JJMA/JurX/ParMA/T3/VIDYA/AMA) for enhanced indicator flexibility.

优势

  • 支持9种平滑算法，指标参数高度可定制，适应不同市场环境
  • 具备完整的止损止盈机制和反向信号平仓功能，风险控制较全面
  • 提供5种仓位管理模式，从固定手数到多种按比例计算模式，灵活性好
  • 使用新K线检测机制避免重复信号，逻辑严谨
  • 买卖开仓和平仓均可独立开关控制，策略组件化程度高

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标PPO_Cloud，指标文件缺失时EA无法运行
  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未考虑ATR等波动率自适应机制，在不同品种上需手动调整
  • 无持仓检查逻辑，可能在不同条件下重复开仓
  • 默认止损1000点和止盈2000点数值偏大，对小账户或低波动品种可能不合适
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时间过滤，在高点差时段可能不利

改进建议

  • 增加基于ATR的动态止损止盈计算，使策略自适应不同品种的波动率
  • 增加持仓检查和最大持仓数限制，避免过度开仓
  • 增加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，提高执行质量

Strengths

  • Supports 9 smoothing algorithms with highly customizable indicator parameters for different market conditions
  • Includes complete stop loss/take profit mechanism and reverse signal closing for comprehensive risk control
  • Offers 5 position sizing modes ranging from fixed lots to various proportional calculation methods for flexibility
  • Uses new bar detection mechanism to avoid duplicate signals with rigorous logic
  • Buy/sell opening and closing can be independently toggled, providing high strategy modularity

Weaknesses

  • Depends on custom PPO_Cloud indicator; EA fails to run if the indicator file is missing
  • Stop loss and take profit use fixed points without ATR-based volatility adaptation, requiring manual adjustment per instrument
  • No open position check logic, potentially leading to repeated position opening under certain conditions
  • Default SL 1000 and TP 2000 points are large, potentially unsuitable for small accounts or low-volatility instruments
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, which may be disadvantageous during high-spread periods

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss/take profit calculation for volatility-adaptive strategy across instruments
  • Add position check and maximum position limit to prevent over-opening
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to improve execution quality
