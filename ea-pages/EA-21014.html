摘要

该EA基于William Blau的TVI（成交量指标）动量震荡器进行交易，当TVI穿越其信号线时产生买入/卖出信号。它包含时间管理过滤器以限制在特定时段交易，并支持固定手数和基于资金管理的仓位计算。

策略分析

策略概念基于William Blau的TVI（Tick Volume Indicator）动量指标，该指标通过将成交量数据进行双平滑处理来衡量价格动量的方向和强度。入场条件为TVI主线与其信号线发生交叉——TVI上穿信号线时开多单，TVI下穿信号线时开空单。出场条件包括固定的止盈止损，以及反向信号出现时平仓。仓位管理支持固定手数和基于账户余额百分比的动态手数计算两种模式。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，允许交易者根据账户规模调整每笔交易的风险敞口。此外，EA内置了时间管理功能，可以限制仅在指定的交易时段内开仓，避免在低流动性或高波动时段交易。EA还提供了止损和止盈的独立参数设置，以及尾盘平仓功能。

Summary

This EA trades based on William Blau's TVI (Tick Volume Indicator) momentum oscillator, generating buy/sell signals when the TVI crosses its signal line. It includes a time management filter to restrict trading to specific hours and supports both fixed lot sizing and money-management-based position sizing.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on William Blau's TVI (Tick Volume Indicator) momentum oscillator, which measures the direction and strength of price momentum by double-smoothing tick volume data. Entry conditions are triggered when the TVI main line crosses its signal line — a bullish crossover generates a buy signal while a bearish crossover generates a sell signal. Exit conditions include fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, as well as position closure upon receiving a reverse signal. Position sizing supports both fixed lot mode and dynamic lot calculation based on a percentage of account balance. Money management is controlled through the MM parameter, allowing traders to adjust risk exposure per trade according to account size. Additionally, the EA incorporates a time management feature that restricts new position openings to specified trading sessions, avoiding low-liquidity or high-volatility periods. The EA also provides independent parameter settings for stop-loss and take-profit, along with an end-of-day position closure feature.

优势

  • 使用Blau TVI动量指标，理论基础扎实，双平滑处理减少虚假信号
  • 内置时间管理过滤器，避免在不利时段交易，提高信号质量
  • 支持固定手数和动态资金管理两种模式，灵活性高

劣势

  • 止盈止损默认为0，如果不手动设置则交易无保护，存在较大风险
  • TVI基于成交量数据，在成交量数据质量较差的市场或经纪商上可能表现不稳定
  • 缺乏新K线检查机制，可能在同一K线上多次开仓导致重复交易

改进建议

  • 增加新K线检查机制，确保每根K线只产生一次交易信号，避免重复开仓
  • 增加点差过滤器，在点差过大时禁止开仓，保护交易免受滑点和高成本影响

Strengths

  • Uses Blau's TVI momentum indicator with solid theoretical foundation; double-smoothing reduces false signals
  • Built-in time management filter avoids trading during unfavorable sessions, improving signal quality
  • Supports both fixed lot and dynamic money management modes, providing high flexibility

Weaknesses

  • Stop-loss and take-profit default to 0, leaving trades unprotected if not manually configured — significant risk
  • TVI relies on tick volume data which may perform inconsistently on brokers or markets with poor volume data quality
  • Lacks a new bar check mechanism, potentially allowing multiple entries on the same bar causing duplicate trades

Improvements

  • Add a new bar check mechanism to ensure only one signal per bar, preventing duplicate position openings
  • Add a spread filter to prohibit opening positions when spread is excessive, protecting trades from slippage and high costs
