摘要

该EA基于FineTuningMA自定义指标进行交易，当指标值显示上升趋势时开多仓，显示下降趋势时开空仓。它支持可配置的资金管理模式、止损止盈，并且仅在新K线形成时处理信号以提高效率。

策略分析

策略概念基于FineTuningMA自定义指标的趋势跟踪，该指标通过三个rank参数和三个shift参数对移动平均线进行精细调节。入场条件为：当Value[1]<Value[2]且Value[0]>Value[1]时开多仓，表示指标从下降转为上升即趋势向上反转；当Value[1]>Value[2]且Value[0]<Value[1]时开空仓，表示趋势向下反转。出场条件依赖固定的止损止盈设置，同时也通过反向信号触发平仓（BuyPosClose/SellPosClose）。仓位管理支持五种模式：基于自由保证金、余额、扣除亏损后的自由保证金、扣除亏损后的余额以及固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认为0.1，配合MarginMode选择具体的计算方式。策略仅在新K线形成时计算信号，避免逐tick重复计算，提高了运行效率。

Summary

This EA trades based on the FineTuningMA custom indicator, opening buy positions when the indicator values show an uptrend (Value[0]>Value[1]<Value[2]) and sell positions when a downtrend is detected (Value[0]<Value[1]>Value[2]). It uses configurable money management modes, stop loss/take profit, and processes signals only on new bars for efficiency.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is trend following based on the FineTuningMA custom indicator, which fine-tunes a moving average using three rank parameters and three shift parameters for adjustable smoothing. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when Value[1]<Value[2] and Value[0]>Value[1], indicating the indicator has turned from declining to rising (uptrend reversal); a sell signal triggers when Value[1]>Value[2] and Value[0]<Value[1], indicating a downtrend reversal. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit settings, with additional exit signals triggered by opposing trend signals (BuyPosClose/SellPosClose). Position sizing supports five modes: based on free margin, balance, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter (default 0.1) combined with the MarginMode selection for specific calculation methods. The strategy only calculates signals on new bar formation rather than every tick, improving computational efficiency and reducing unnecessary trade operations.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于指标拐点判断趋势反转，逻辑可解释性强
  • 支持五种仓位管理模式，灵活适配不同风险偏好
  • 仅在新K线时计算信号，避免逐tick重复交易，运行效率高
  • 买卖开仓和平仓可独立控制，便于单向交易或仅信号平仓
  • 使用SignalBar=1避免未完成K线的信号闪烁问题

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点设置偏大，可能不适用于所有品种和波动环境
  • 缺少点差过滤，在点差较大的品种上可能产生不利入场
  • 没有持仓检查逻辑，可能在已有同向仓位时重复开仓
  • 依赖外部自定义指标FineTuningMA，指标文件缺失时EA无法运行
  • 缺少追踪止损功能，盈利仓位可能因趋势回撤而大幅回吐

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓
  • 增加点差过滤器，当点差超过阈值时暂停开仓
  • 增加追踪止损功能，保护已有浮盈

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on indicator inflection points for trend reversal detection, with strong interpretability
  • Supports five position sizing modes, flexibly adapting to different risk preferences
  • Only calculates signals on new bar formation, avoiding redundant per-tick trading and improving efficiency
  • Buy/sell open and close can be independently controlled, enabling one-directional trading or signal-only exits
  • Uses SignalBar=1 to avoid signal flickering from uncompleted bars

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are large and may not suit all instruments or volatility environments
  • No spread filter, potentially leading to unfavorable entries on high-spread instruments
  • No position check logic, may open duplicate positions in the same direction when already holding
  • Depends on the external custom indicator FineTuningMA; EA fails if the indicator file is missing
  • No trailing stop functionality, profitable positions may give back significant gains on trend pullbacks

Improvements

  • Add position check logic to avoid opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Add a spread filter to pause opening when spread exceeds a threshold
  • Add trailing stop functionality to protect accumulated profits
