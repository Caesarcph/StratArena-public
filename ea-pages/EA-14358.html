摘要

该EA使用自定义LeManTrend指标的快慢信号线交叉来产生买卖信号。它在交叉趋势方向上开仓，并可选地平掉反向持仓。交易管理包括可配置的止损、止盈和资金管理选项。

策略分析

策略概念基于LeManTrend指标的双线交叉系统，当快速线在上一根K线上高于慢速线且当前K线快线低于等于慢线时，确认上升趋势结束并产生买入信号（趋势回调入场逻辑）。入场条件为快速线与慢速线发生交叉，具体为快线从下方穿越慢线时卖出开仓，快线从上方穿越慢线时买入开仓，同时关闭反向持仓。出场条件依赖固定止损和止盈点数，以及反向信号触发时平仓，没有移动止损或追踪止损机制。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，支持手数模式和保证金比例模式两种资金管理方式。资金管理采用固定比例或固定手数方式，缺乏根据波动率或账户权益动态调整仓位大小的自适应机制。

Summary

This EA uses the custom LeManTrend indicator's fast and slow signal line crossovers to generate buy and sell signals. It opens positions in the direction of the crossover trend and optionally closes opposing positions. Trade management includes configurable stop loss, take profit, and money management options.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a dual-line crossover system using the LeManTrend custom indicator, which combines three moving average periods (Min=13, Midle=21, Max=34) smoothed by an EMA of period 3. Entry conditions trigger when the fast line crosses above the slow line for buy signals and below for sell signals, with an additional confirmation that the previous bar showed the opposite relationship, ensuring a genuine crossover rather than a temporary touch. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit values in points, as well as closing opposing positions when a reverse signal occurs, though there is no trailing stop mechanism. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, supporting both fixed lot size and margin-based percentage modes via the MMMode input. Money management is static rather than adaptive, lacking volatility-based or equity-curve-based dynamic position sizing, which limits its ability to adjust risk exposure to changing market conditions.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于经典均线交叉原理，易于理解和验证
  • 提供灵活的开仓和平仓开关，可以单独控制买入和卖出方向
  • 具有新K线检测机制，避免同一根K线内重复开仓
  • 使用可配置的止损止盈，基本风险控制到位
  • 指标计算与EA主逻辑分离，模块化设计合理

劣势

  • 缺乏移动止损或追踪止损机制，无法锁定浮动利润
  • 固定止损止盈不考虑市场波动率变化，在高低波动环境下表现不一致
  • 没有持仓检查逻辑，可能在已有同向持仓时重复开仓
  • LeManTrend为自定义指标，依赖外部文件，增加了部署复杂度
  • 没有点差过滤或交易时间过滤，在高点差时段可能产生不利成交

改进建议

  • 增加移动止损功能以保护浮动利润
  • 加入基于ATR的动态止损止盈，适应不同波动环境
  • 添加持仓检查避免同向重复开仓
  • 增加交易时段和点差过滤器

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on classic moving average crossover principles, easy to understand and verify
  • Flexible open/close switches allow independent control of buy and sell directions
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate entries within the same bar
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit provide basic risk control
  • Indicator calculation separated from EA main logic with modular design

Weaknesses

  • No trailing stop mechanism to lock in floating profits as the trade moves favorably
  • Fixed SL/TP does not account for market volatility changes, leading to inconsistent performance across volatility regimes
  • No position check logic, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • LeManTrend is a custom indicator requiring an external file, increasing deployment complexity
  • No spread filter or trading session filter, may execute at unfavorable prices during high-spread periods

Improvements

  • Add trailing stop functionality to protect floating profits
  • Implement ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit for volatility adaptation
  • Add position check to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Add trading session and spread filters to avoid unfavorable executions
