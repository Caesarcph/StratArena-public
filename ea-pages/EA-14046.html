摘要

该EA基于LSMA（最小二乘移动平均线）角度指标进行交易，当LSMA角度向上穿越阈值时开多仓，向下穿越时开空仓。它使用带有可配置角度阈值和偏移周期的自定义指标生成趋势跟踪入场信号，并通过固定的止损和止盈水平进行出场管理。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是利用LSMA（最小二乘移动平均线）的斜率角度来判断趋势方向和强度。入场条件为：当指标buffer1的值从小于4变为等于4时触发买入信号，表示上升趋势角度突破了阈值；当值从等于0变为大于0时触发卖出信号，表示下降趋势角度突破了阈值。出场条件为：买入平仓当指标值小于2时，卖出平仓当指标值大于1时，这意味着趋势角度减弱到一定程度即退出。仓位管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，支持多种保证金模式包括固定手数模式。资金管理采用百分比风险管理方式，可以根据自由保证金、余额或扣除浮亏后的资金计算仓位大小。该策略还包含新K线判断逻辑，确保每个信号只在一个新柱上执行一次，避免重复开仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on the LSMA (Least Squares Moving Average) Angle indicator, opening buy positions when the LSMA angle crosses upward past a threshold and sell positions when it crosses downward. It uses a custom indicator with configurable angle thresholds and shift periods to generate trend-following entry signals, with fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels for exit management.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA uses the slope angle of the LSMA (Least Squares Moving Average) to determine trend direction and strength. Entry conditions are: a buy signal is triggered when indicator buffer1 value changes from less than 4 to equal to 4, indicating the uptrend angle has crossed the threshold; a sell signal is triggered when the value changes from equal to 0 to greater than 0, indicating the downtrend angle has crossed the threshold. Exit conditions are: buy positions close when the indicator value drops below 2, and sell positions close when it rises above 1, meaning positions are exited when the trend angle weakens to a certain degree. Position sizing is controlled through the MM parameter which sets the risk percentage, supporting multiple margin modes including a fixed lot mode. Money management uses a percentage-based risk approach that can calculate position size based on free margin, balance, or equity after deducting floating losses. The strategy also includes a new bar detection mechanism to ensure each signal is executed only once per new candle, preventing duplicate entries.

优势

  • 使用LSMA角度判断趋势，比简单均线交叉更精确地衡量趋势强度
  • 具备完整的新K线检测机制，避免同一信号重复开仓
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，灵活适应不同风险偏好
  • 入场和出场开关独立控制，可灵活组合多空策略
  • 包含止损和止盈设置，有基本的风险控制框架

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点设置过大，在大多数品种上可能导致巨大亏损
  • 缺乏点差过滤和滑点保护，在高点差环境下容易被止损
  • 依赖自定义指标LSMA_Angle，指标不可用时EA无法运行且错误处理有限
  • 没有对现有持仓的检查，可能在同方向重复开仓
  • 角度阈值固定，未考虑不同品种波动率的差异

改进建议

  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓导致过度杠杆
  • 增加动态角度阈值，根据ATR或品种波动率自适应调整
  • 加入点差过滤和交易时间过滤，提高信号质量

Strengths

  • Uses LSMA angle for trend detection, which measures trend strength more precisely than simple MA crossovers
  • Includes a proper new bar detection mechanism to prevent duplicate entries on the same signal
  • Supports multiple money management modes, providing flexibility for different risk preferences
  • Independent enable/disable switches for entry and exit in both directions allow flexible strategy combinations
  • Includes stop loss and take profit settings providing a basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large, potentially leading to huge losses on most instruments
  • Lacks spread filter and slippage protection, making it vulnerable to being stopped out in high-spread environments
  • Depends on the custom LSMA_Angle indicator, and the EA cannot run with limited error handling when the indicator is unavailable
  • No check for existing positions, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Fixed angle threshold does not account for volatility differences across instruments

Improvements

  • Add position checking logic to avoid duplicate entries in the same direction causing excessive leverage
  • Implement dynamic angle thresholds that self-adjust based on ATR or instrument volatility
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to improve signal quality
