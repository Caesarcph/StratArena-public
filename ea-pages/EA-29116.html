摘要

多货币环形对冲EA，从3至8种货币中构建三角货币对组合（最多56个环），对每个环的三对同时开立买/卖仓位组合（正套与反套），利用临时价格失衡获利，支持步进递进、手数递进及篮子/单笔平仓逻辑。

策略分析

策略核心是三角套利/环形对冲：从用户指定的货币中按组合数学生成所有三元组，每个三元组构成一个'环'（3对货币对）。对每个环同时开立'Plus套组'（Buy/Sell/Buy）和'Minus套组'（Sell/Buy/Sell），本质是对冲三对间的汇率关系偏差。入场条件为初始开仓后，若浮亏达到设定步进值则加仓（支持静态、几何、指数步进）。出场支持单笔获利平仓和篮子获利平仓两种模式，也可设定亏损平仓。仓位管理支持手动手数、自动手数（基于风险因子）、公平手数分配，以及静态/几何/指数/递减手数递进。资金管理包含最大手数限制、最大订单数限制、点差过滤、交易时段控制和滑点控制。

Summary

A multi-currency ring hedging EA that creates triangular currency pair groups from 3-8 currencies, generating up to 56 ring combinations. It opens simultaneous buy/sell positions across each ring's three pairs (Plus and Minus suites) to exploit temporary pricing imbalances, with configurable step progression, lot progression, and basket/single close logic.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy is triangular arbitrage/ring hedging: from user-specified currencies it generates all 3-currency combinations (rings), each containing 3 currency pairs. For each ring it simultaneously opens a 'Plus suite' (Buy/Sell/Buy) and a 'Minus suite' (Sell/Buy/Sell), essentially hedging exchange rate relationship deviations across the three pairs. Entry involves initial positions, then adding positions when floating loss reaches a configured step threshold (supporting static, geometric, and exponential step progression). Exit supports both single-ticket and basket-ticket profit closing, with optional loss closing. Position sizing supports manual lot, auto lot (based on risk factor), fair lot distribution, and static/geometric/exponential/decreasing lot progression. Money management includes maximum lot limits, maximum order limits, spread filtering, trading session control, and slippage control.

优势

  • 多货币环形对冲设计在理论上能对冲单一货币对方向性风险，降低单边暴露
  • 支持3至8种货币灵活配置，最多56个环组，组合覆盖面广
  • 提供丰富的仓位管理和资金管理选项（步进递进、手数递进、自动手数、公平手数）
  • 有订单数上限、点差过滤、交易时段控制、滑点控制等多层风险防护
  • Plus/Minus双套组设计允许用户选择单向或双向对冲，灵活度较高

劣势

  • 亏损加仓机制（步进加仓）本质是网格/马丁类策略，在极端行情下可能导致账户爆仓
  • 默认不设止损（TypeCloseInLoss=Not_Close_In_Loss），浮亏可能无限扩大
  • 同时操作最多56个环组×3对×2方向=336个仓位，对账户保证金和点差成本要求极高
  • 大量全局变量和硬编码数组（MaxGroups=57）使代码可维护性差，容易出错
  • 缺乏真正的套利逻辑——没有检测三对间定价偏差的指标，入场时机完全依赖加仓步进而非市场信号

改进建议

  • 引入三角套利定价偏差指标（如合成汇率与实际汇率之差）作为入场信号，而非纯靠浮亏加仓
  • 强制设置止损或最大浮亏限制，防止极端行情下账户爆仓
  • 将全局变量重构为结构体/类，减少命名冲突风险并提升可读性和可维护性

Strengths

  • Multi-currency ring hedging design theoretically hedges directional risk of individual pairs, reducing unilateral exposure
  • Supports flexible 3-8 currency configuration with up to 56 ring groups for broad combination coverage
  • Rich position sizing and money management options (step progression, lot progression, auto lot, fair lot sizing)
  • Multi-layer risk protection including order limit, spread filter, session control, and slippage control
  • Plus/Minus dual suite design allows users to choose one-directional or bi-directional hedging flexibly

Weaknesses

  • Loss-adding mechanism (step-based position increase) is essentially a grid/martingale strategy that can blow accounts in extreme markets
  • Default no stop-loss (TypeCloseInLoss=Not_Close_In_Loss) allows unlimited floating loss expansion
  • Operating up to 56 groups × 3 pairs × 2 directions = 336 positions simultaneously demands extremely high margin and spread costs
  • Massive global variables and hardcoded arrays (MaxGroups=57) make code poorly maintainable and error-prone
  • Lacks true arbitrage logic—no indicator detecting pricing deviation among three pairs; entry timing relies entirely on step-based adding rather than market signals

Improvements

  • Introduce triangular arbitrage pricing deviation indicator (e.g., synthetic vs actual rate difference) as entry signal instead of pure loss-based adding
  • Enforce mandatory stop-loss or maximum floating loss limit to prevent account blow-up in extreme markets
  • Refactor global variables into structs/classes to reduce naming collision risk and improve readability/maintainability

风险点

  • 亏损加仓（网格/马丁式）在趋势行情中可导致指数级仓位增长和账户爆仓
  • 默认不设止损，浮亏无上限保护

  • 环形对冲在极端相关性破裂时（如闪崩）三对可能同时亏损，对冲失效

Risk Assessment

Overall:DANGEROUS

Risk Points

  • Loss-adding (grid/martingale style) can cause exponential position growth and account blow-up in trending markets
  • Default no stop-loss means unlimited floating loss exposure with no cap protection
  • Simultaneously managing up to 336 positions consumes enormous margin and may trigger margin calls
  • Ring hedging fails when extreme correlation breakdowns (e.g., flash crashes) cause all three pairs to lose simultaneously
