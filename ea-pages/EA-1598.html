NarrowestRangeSignal EA

摘要

该EA基于NarrowestRangeSignal自定义指标的窄幅整理突破信号进行交易。当检测到窄幅整理形态时，在区间上下方设置买入止损和卖出止损挂单，其中一单触发后删除另一侧挂单。

策略分析

策略概念基于窄幅整理区间突破，当NarrowestRangeSignal指标在指定检查周期内识别到最近N根K线形成最窄波动范围时产生信号。入场条件为指标在上一根K线输出非零非空值，且区间最高价和最低价不在最近的K线上（即区间已形成），随后在区间最高价加偏移处挂Buy Stop单，在区间最低价减偏移处挂Sell Stop单。出场条件通过TakeProfit止盈实现，止损设置为对侧区间边界加减偏移。仓位管理支持固定手数和固定百分比两种模式，固定百分比模式根据可用保证金和杠杆计算手数。资金管理通过CheckLotValue函数规范化手数，确保符合经纪人最小最大手数和手数步长要求。

Summary

This EA trades breakout signals from the NarrowestRangeSignal custom indicator. When a narrow range pattern is detected, it places buy-stop and sell-stop pending orders on both sides of the range with a configurable indent. Once one order triggers, the opposite pending order is deleted.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on narrow range breakout, where the NarrowestRangeSignal indicator identifies the narrowest price range over the past N bars within a check period. Entry conditions require the indicator to output a non-zero, non-empty value on the previous bar, and the range high/low must not occur on the most recent bar (confirming the range is formed), then a Buy Stop is placed above the range high plus indent and a Sell Stop below the range low minus indent. Exit conditions are handled via TakeProfit, with the stop loss set to the opposite side of the range boundary plus or minus indent. Position sizing supports both fixed lot and fixed percentage modes, where the fixed percentage mode calculates lot size based on free margin and account leverage. Money management is enforced through the CheckLotValue function which normalizes lot sizes to comply with broker minimum, maximum, and step requirements.

优势

  • 双边挂单突破策略逻辑清晰，利用窄幅整理后的突破方向不确定性进行双向捕捉
  • 一单触发后删除对侧挂单，避免反向持仓风险
  • 手数规范化处理完善，考虑了经纪人最小最大手数和步长限制

劣势

  • 止损设置不合理，Buy Stop的止损设为min_low-indent而非区间低点，Sell Stop同理，缩小了止损空间
  • 缺少滑点和点差过滤，窄幅区间突破后点差扩大可能导致不必要的入场
  • DeletePendingOrders和CheckOrders中各自有独立的static prev_time，可能导致时间控制不同步
  • 固定百分比模式的仓位计算过于简化，未考虑品种的点值和保证金要求

改进建议

  • 修复止损逻辑，Buy Stop止损应为区间最低价减偏移，Sell Stop止损应为区间最高价加偏移，避免止损过小
  • 添加点差过滤和滑点保护，在点差过大时跳过下单
  • 统一DeletePendingOrders和CheckOrders的时间控制逻辑，避免因时间不同步导致的重复操作或遗漏

Strengths

  • Clear dual-pending-order breakout logic that captures breakout direction from narrow range consolidation
  • Deletes the opposite pending order once one side triggers, avoiding opposing position risk
  • Robust lot size normalization handling broker minimum, maximum, and step constraints

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss placement is flawed: Buy Stop SL is set to min_low-indent rather than the range low, and vice versa for Sell Stop, narrowing the stop distance
  • No spread or slippage filter; widening spreads after narrow ranges can cause unfavorable entries
  • DeletePendingOrders and CheckOrders each have their own static prev_time, potentially causing time-control desynchronization
  • Fixed percentage position sizing is oversimplified, not accounting for tick value and margin requirements per symbol

Improvements

  • Fix stop loss logic: Buy Stop SL should be range low minus indent and Sell Stop SL should be range high plus indent to avoid excessively tight stops
  • Add spread filter and slippage protection to skip order placement when spreads are too wide
  • Unify time control logic between DeletePendingOrders and CheckOrders to prevent duplication or missed operations from desynchronized time checks
