NarrowestRangeSignal EA

The Expert Advisor based on the NarrowestRangeSignal indicator. If there is no position, stop orders will be set in both sides of the range by the signal of the indicator. When either of orders triggers, the opposite order is deleted.

摘要

该EA基于NarrowestRangeSignal指标识别窄幅价格区间后，在区间高低点两侧设置止损挂单（买止损和卖止损）。当一侧挂单成交后，删除另一侧挂单，实现窄幅整理后的OCO突破策略。

策略分析

策略概念基于窄幅整理突破，当NarrowestRangeSignal指标检测到指定周期内出现最窄K线范围时发出信号。入场条件为指标发出信号后，在区间最高价加上indent偏移处挂买止损单，在区间最低价减去indent偏移处挂卖止损单，形成双向突破挂单。出场条件依靠止盈参数平仓，止损设在区间对侧（买止损单的止损在最低价下方，卖止损单的止损在最高价上方）。仓位管理支持固定手数和固定百分比两种模式，百分比模式根据可用保证金和杠杆计算手数。资金管理方面，CalculateLot函数会检查最小最大手数限制并规范化手数精度，但缺少止损的独立风险控制，且没有追踪止损或移动止损机制。

Summary

This EA places bracket stop orders (buy stop above and sell stop below) when the NarrowestRangeSignal indicator identifies a narrow price range. When one side triggers, the opposite pending order is deleted, implementing an OCO (One-Cancels-Other) breakout strategy from narrow range consolidation.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on narrow range breakout, where the NarrowestRangeSignal indicator detects the narrowest bar range within a specified period to generate a signal. Entry conditions involve placing a buy stop order above the range high plus an indent offset and a sell stop order below the range low minus an indent offset when the indicator fires, creating bracket stop orders on both sides. Exit conditions rely on a fixed take profit parameter for closing positions, with stop loss set on the opposite side of the range (buy stop SL below the low, sell stop SL above the high). Position sizing supports two modes: fixed lot and fixed percentage, where the percentage mode calculates lot size based on available free margin and account leverage. Money management includes lot validation against broker min/max limits and step normalization via CheckLotValue, but lacks independent risk control for stop loss sizing, and there is no trailing stop or break-even mechanism.

优势

  • 窄幅整理突破策略逻辑清晰，利用市场低波动期蓄势后的突破捕捉趋势
  • OCO挂单机制合理，一侧成交后自动删除对侧挂单，避免双向持仓风险
  • 支持固定手数和固定百分比两种仓位管理模式，灵活性较好

劣势

  • 缺少追踪止损和保本机制，盈利仓位可能大幅回撤后才被止损出场
  • 止损设在区间对侧但减去了indent偏移，实际止损可能过小或过大，未考虑ATR等动态指标
  • DeletePendingOrders函数中使用static变量做新K线检查与CheckOrders中的逻辑重复且可能冲突，每tick都创建新的CTrade对象效率低下

改进建议

  • 增加基于ATR的动态止损和追踪止损机制，替代固定偏移和固定止盈，使策略适应不同市场波动环境
  • 优化代码结构，将CTrade对象声明为全局变量避免重复创建，统一新K线检查逻辑避免static变量冲突

Strengths

  • Clear and logical narrow range breakout strategy that captures momentum after low-volatility consolidation periods
  • Reasonable OCO bracket order mechanism that automatically deletes the opposite pending order when one side triggers, avoiding dual-position risk
  • Supports both fixed lot and fixed percentage position sizing modes, offering good flexibility

Weaknesses

  • Lacks trailing stop and break-even mechanisms, so winning positions may give back significant profits before being stopped out
  • Stop loss is placed at the opposite side of the range minus indent, which may result in stops that are too tight or too wide without dynamic ATR-based adjustment
  • DeletePendingOrders uses a static variable for new bar checking that duplicates and potentially conflicts with CheckOrders logic, and creating a new CTrade object each tick is inefficient

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss and trailing stop mechanisms to replace fixed indent and fixed take profit, making the strategy adaptive to different market volatility conditions
  • Optimize code structure by declaring CTrade as a global variable to avoid repeated instantiation, and unify the new bar checking logic to prevent static variable conflicts between functions

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 止损计算不够稳健，区间对侧减去indent可能导致止损过窄容易被假突破打掉
  • 没有最大持仓数量限制和单日最大亏损控制，连续信号可能频繁开仓造成资金损失
  • DeletePendingOrders和CheckOrders中的static prev_time可能导致新K线判断不同步，存在逻辑漏洞

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Stop loss calculation is not robust enough; subtracting indent from the opposite range side may result in stops too tight and easily triggered by false breakouts
  • No maximum position count limit or daily maximum loss control, so consecutive signals may cause frequent trading and capital loss
  • Static prev_time variables in both DeletePendingOrders and CheckOrders may cause new bar detection to be out of sync, presenting a logic flaw
