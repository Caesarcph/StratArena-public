摘要

该EA基于两条CCI指标（快速CCI周期6和慢速CCI周期14）的交叉信号进行交易，即Woodies CCI模式。当快速CCI上穿慢速CCI时开多仓，反之开空仓，支持可配置的平仓、止损和止盈。

策略分析

该策略基于Woodies CCI系统，使用快速CCI（周期6）和慢速CCI（周期14）两条线的交叉来判断市场动量方向。入场条件：当快速CCI从下方上穿慢速CCI时产生买入信号（BUY_Open=true），同时触发卖仓平仓信号（SELL_Close=true）；当快速CCI从上方下穿慢速CCI时产生卖出信号（SELL_Open=true），同时触发买仓平仓信号（BUY_Close=true）。出场条件通过反向交叉信号触发平仓，同时可设置固定止损（1000点）和止盈（2000点）。仓位管理支持五种模式：自由保证金比例、余额比例、扣除亏损后的自由保证金比例、扣除亏损后的余额比例以及固定手数，默认使用固定手数模式（LOT=0.1）。策略还提供了信号反转功能（Invert参数），可以将买卖信号互换。系统仅在新K线形成时计算信号，避免重复计算，并使用SignalBar参数指定用于信号判断的K线索引。

Summary

This EA trades based on the crossover of two CCI indicators (fast CCI with period 6 and slow CCI with period 14), known as the Woodies CCI pattern. It opens buy positions when the fast CCI crosses above the slow CCI and sell positions on the reverse crossover, with configurable position closing, stop loss, and take profit.

Strategy Analysis

This strategy is based on the Woodies CCI system, using the crossover of two CCI lines (fast period 6 and slow period 14) to determine market momentum direction. Entry conditions: a buy signal (BUY_Open=true) is generated when the fast CCI crosses above the slow CCI, simultaneously triggering a sell position close signal (SELL_Close=true); a sell signal (SELL_Open=true) is generated when the fast CCI crosses below the slow CCI, simultaneously triggering a buy position close signal (BUY_Close=true). Exit conditions are triggered by reverse crossover signals, with configurable fixed stop loss (1000 points) and take profit (2000 points). Position sizing supports five modes: free margin percentage, balance percentage, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size, defaulting to fixed lot mode (LOT=0.1). The strategy also provides a signal inversion feature (Invert parameter) to swap buy and sell signals. The system only calculates signals on new bar formation to avoid redundant calculations, and uses the SignalBar parameter to specify the bar index for signal evaluation.

优势

  • 基于经典的Woodies CCI系统，策略逻辑清晰且有广泛的市场验证
  • 提供五种仓位管理模式，资金管理灵活度高
  • 新K线判断机制避免重复计算，提高执行效率

劣势

  • 缺乏点差过滤，在窄点差品种以外可能出现滑点问题
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点为固定值，未根据市场波动性动态调整
  • 依赖自定义指标CCI_Woodies，若该指标缺失则EA无法运行

改进建议

  • 增加基于ATR的动态止损止盈机制，根据市场波动性自适应调整
  • 添加点差过滤和多时间框架确认，提高信号质量并减少假突破

Strengths

  • Based on the well-known Woodies CCI system with clear strategy logic and broad market validation
  • Offers five position sizing modes providing high flexibility in money management
  • New bar detection mechanism avoids redundant calculations and improves execution efficiency

Weaknesses

  • Lacks spread filtering which may cause slippage issues on instruments with wider spreads
  • Stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are fixed values not dynamically adjusted for market volatility
  • Depends on the custom indicator CCI_Woodies; if missing the EA cannot run

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit mechanism to adapt to market volatility
  • Add spread filter and multi-timeframe confirmation to improve signal quality and reduce false breakouts
