Rabbit3

Rabbit3 is a breakout EA that trades based on daily price range levels, entering positions when price breaks above or below calculated support and resistance levels derived from the previous day's High, Low, and Close prices. The EA typically places pending orders at these breakout levels at the start of each trading day and manages them with take profit and stop loss. It is designed to capture intraday momentum moves triggered by range expansion from established daily levels.

Strategy analysis

Rabbit3's core strategy concept is based on calculating multiple breakout levels from the previous day's price range (commonly referred to as Rabbit levels), entering trades when price breaks through these calculated support and resistance zones. Entry conditions: at the start of each trading day, the EA computes upper and lower breakout levels from the previous day's High, Low, and Close; a buy is triggered when price breaks above the upper level, and a sell when price breaks below the lower level. Exit conditions include fixed-pip take profit and stop loss targets, along with an end-of-day closing logic to avoid overnight exposure. Position sizing typically uses fixed lots or an equity-proportional lot calculation method. Money management relies on stop loss to control per-trade maximum loss, but lacks overall account-level risk controls such as maximum drawdown limits or position adjustment after consecutive losses. The strategy belongs to the range breakout category and performs well in trending markets, but is vulnerable to frequent false breakout signals in choppy or ranging conditions.

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on daily support/resistance level breakout calculation with statistical justification
  • Uses pending orders for entry, avoiding chase pricing and achieving better entry prices
  • Includes trading session filter to avoid false breakouts during low liquidity periods
  • Recalculates levels daily, adapting to market changes without over-reliance on historical parameters

Weaknesses

  • Vulnerable to frequent stop-outs from false breakouts in ranging/choppy markets
  • Lacks account-level money management such as maximum drawdown protection or lot adjustment after consecutive losses
  • Stop loss setting may be inflexible; fixed-pip SL doesn't account for volatility differences across currency pairs
  • Lacks trailing stop or break-even mechanisms to lock in floating profits

Possible improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss/take profit to adapt SL/TP distances based on market volatility
  • Add account-level risk controls including maximum drawdown limits and lot reduction after consecutive losses
  • Add trailing stop functionality to automatically track price and lock in profits after reaching a profit threshold

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Fixed-pip stop loss doesn't account for market volatility; may get stopped out frequently during high volatility periods
  • Lacks account-level maximum drawdown protection; significant capital loss possible during extreme market conditions
  • Intraday breakout strategy has low win rate in ranging markets, potentially leading to consecutive losses
