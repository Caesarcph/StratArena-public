摘要

基于ONNX机器学习模型的EA，将市场结构分类为4个类别，当模型置信度超过阈值时，在斐波那契回撤位挂限价单入场。结合50周期均线趋势过滤，并使用基于风险回报比的移动止损。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是利用ONNX模型对市场结构进行四分类预测（标签0-3），其中标签3和2代表看涨信号，标签1和0代表看跌信号。入场条件要求模型置信度超过阈值（默认0.65），同时价格需与50周期均线方向一致：看涨信号需价格在均线上方，看跌信号需价格在均线下方。入场方式为挂限价单（BuyLimit/SellLimit），入场价基于最近枢轴点之间的斐波那契0.618回撤位，止损设在枢轴低点/高点外加15点缓冲，止盈按2倍风险回报比计算。仓位管理采用固定手数，有最小/最大手数和手数步长校验。移动止损在利润达到0.8RR时激活，将止损移至开仓价加30%浮盈距离处。挂单设有4小时有效期。

Summary

An ONNX-based Expert Advisor that uses a machine learning model to classify market structure into 4 categories, then places Fibonacci-based pending limit orders when model confidence exceeds a threshold. It combines ML predictions with a 50-period MA trend filter and employs RR-based trailing stops.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy uses an ONNX model to classify market structure into four categories (labels 0-3), where labels 3 and 2 indicate bullish signals and labels 1 and 0 indicate bearish signals. Entry requires model confidence above a configurable threshold (default 0.65) and alignment with the 50-period MA trend: bullish signals need price above the MA, bearish signals need price below. Entries are placed as pending limit orders at the 0.618 Fibonacci retracement of the last pivot high-low range, with stop loss beyond the pivot plus a 15-point buffer and take profit at a 2x risk-reward ratio. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size with validation against broker min/max lot and lot step constraints. A trailing stop activates when profit reaches 0.8RR, moving the stop to the open price plus 30% of the floating profit distance. Pending orders have a 4-hour expiration.

优势

  • 结合机器学习与经典斐波那契回撤策略，逻辑层次分明
  • 有置信度阈值过滤低质量信号，避免盲目入场
  • 挂限价单而非市价单入场，获得更好的入场价格
  • 具有RR基础移动止损和挂单过期机制，风控较完善
  • 新K线检查避免重复信号，防止同根K线多次开仓

劣势

  • ONNX模型为黑箱，无法审查训练质量和泛化能力，策略可靠性完全依赖模型质量
  • InpWindowSize参数已声明但在代码中未使用，存在死代码
  • 枢轴点识别仅回溯100根K线且窗口固定为5，在剧烈行情中可能找不到有效枢轴
  • 移动止损仅锁定30%浮盈，保护力度偏弱，可能在大波动中回吐过多利润
  • 没有点差过滤和滑点保护，在流动性差的环境中挂单可能以不利价格成交
  • GetLastPivot的窗口判断逻辑中j遍历范围可能越界（i+5超过可用K线数）

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和波动率过滤，避免在高点差或低流动性时段交易
  • 将移动止损的锁定比例改为可配置参数，并考虑分步移动策略
  • 修复GetLastPivot中的数组越界风险，加入Bars()边界检查
  • 移除未使用的InpWindowSize参数，或在特征计算中实际使用它
  • 增加对ONNX模型加载失败的更详细日志输出，便于排查问题

Strengths

  • Combines machine learning with classical Fibonacci retracement for a multi-layered approach
  • Confidence threshold filters low-quality signals, preventing blind entries
  • Uses limit orders instead of market orders for better entry prices
  • RR-based trailing stop and pending order expiration provide solid risk controls
  • New bar check prevents duplicate signals on the same candle

Weaknesses

  • ONNX model is a black box — strategy reliability depends entirely on model quality with no way to audit training or generalization
  • InpWindowSize parameter is declared but never used in the code, creating dead code
  • Pivot identification only looks back 100 bars with a fixed window of 5, may fail to find valid pivots in volatile markets
  • Trailing stop only locks in 30% of floating profit, which is weak protection and may give back too much in large swings
  • No spread filter or slippage protection — limit orders may fill at unfavorable prices in low-liquidity environments
  • GetLastPivot loop may access out-of-range bars when i+5 exceeds available history

Improvements

  • Add spread and volatility filters to avoid trading during high-spread or low-liquidity periods
  • Make the trailing stop lock-in percentage configurable and consider a step-trailing approach
  • Fix potential array out-of-bounds in GetLastPivot by adding Bars() boundary checks
  • Remove unused InpWindowSize parameter or actually incorporate it into feature calculation
  • Add detailed logging for ONNX model load failures to aid troubleshooting
