摘要

该EA是一个半自动交易面板，基于简单的多价格比较动量启发式方法生成买卖信号。它提供图形界面，包含手动买入/卖出/平仓按钮，以及一个自动驾驶模式，当信号偏向超过对方时自动执行交易。策略通过比较最近两根K线的开盘价、最高价、最低价、收盘价等来统计方向投票数，并将结果显示为百分比条。

策略分析

策略概念基于多重价格分量动量投票：比较最近两根K线的开盘价、最高价、最低价、收盘价以及它们的组合均值，共7个维度进行方向性判断。入场条件为：当买入信号数大于卖出信号数时做多，反之做空；在自动驾驶模式下，如果当前无持仓则开新仓，如果持有反向仓位则先平仓再反转。出场条件仅在自动驾驶模式下通过反向信号触发平仓和反手，手动模式下仅能通过面板的CLOSE按钮平仓。仓位管理使用固定手数0.01，没有任何递增或递减逻辑。资金管理完全缺失，没有止损和止盈设置，所有交易的SL和TP均为0，存在无限亏损风险。

Summary

This EA is a semi-automated trading panel that generates buy/sell signals based on a simple multi-price comparison momentum heuristic across Open, High, Low, Close prices. It provides a graphical interface with manual Buy/Sell/Close buttons and an autopilot mode that automatically executes trades when the signal bias exceeds the opposing direction. The strategy compares the last two bars' price components to count directional votes, displaying the result as percentage bars on the chart.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a multi-price-component momentum voting system: it compares the last two bars across Open, High, Low, Close and their derived averages (7 dimensions total) to determine directional bias. Entry conditions: when buy signals exceed sell signals, a long position is opened; when sell signals exceed buy signals, a short is opened. In autopilot mode, if no position exists a new one is opened; if an opposing position exists, it is closed first and then reversed. Exit conditions are only triggered in autopilot mode via opposing signal reversal, or manually via the CLOSE button on the panel. Position sizing uses a fixed lot of 0.01 with no scaling logic whatsoever. Money management is entirely absent — all trades are opened with SL=0 and TP=0, creating unlimited loss exposure.

优势

  • 提供直观的图形化面板，支持手动和自动两种交易模式
  • 信号计算逻辑简单透明，基于7个维度的投票机制便于理解
  • 固定小手数0.01降低了单笔交易的风险敞口
  • 支持一键平仓和方向反转，操作便捷

劣势

  • 完全没有止损和止盈设置，单笔交易亏损无上限
  • 信号逻辑过于简单，仅比较两根K线的价格分量，缺乏统计显著性
  • 没有新K线检查，每个tick都会重新评估信号，可能导致频繁交易
  • 没有点差过滤，在M5周期上容易因高点差造成滑点损失
  • 仓位管理僵硬，固定手数无法根据账户规模或波动率调整

改进建议

  • 添加止损和止盈参数，并在每笔交易中强制设置，防止无限亏损
  • 添加新K线检查逻辑，避免在同一根K线上重复开仓和平仓
  • 增加点差过滤和时间过滤，避免在高点差时段和低流动性时段交易
  • 将固定手数改为基于账户余额或ATR的动态仓位管理

Strengths

  • Provides an intuitive graphical panel supporting both manual and automated trading modes
  • Signal calculation logic is simple and transparent, based on a 7-dimension voting mechanism that is easy to understand
  • Fixed small lot size of 0.01 limits per-trade risk exposure
  • Supports one-click position closing and directional reversal for convenient operation

Weaknesses

  • No stop-loss or take-profit at all — single trade loss is unlimited
  • Signal logic is overly simplistic, comparing only two bars' price components with no statistical significance
  • No new bar check — signals are re-evaluated every tick, potentially causing excessive trading frequency
  • No spread filter — on M5 timeframe, high spreads can easily cause slippage losses
  • Rigid position sizing with fixed lots that cannot adapt to account size or volatility

Improvements

  • Add stop-loss and take-profit parameters and enforce them on every trade to prevent unlimited losses
  • Add new bar check logic to avoid repeated opening and closing on the same bar
  • Add spread filter and time filter to avoid trading during high-spread and low-liquidity periods
  • Replace fixed lot size with dynamic position sizing based on account balance or ATR

  • No new bar check may cause repeated open/close on the same bar leading to overtrading
  • Autopilot reversal trades (close then open) have execution risk — position open may proceed even if close fails
