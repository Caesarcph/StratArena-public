摘要

一个基于面板的半自动交易EA，通过比较当前K线与前一根K线的开盘价、最高价、最低价和收盘价来生成买卖动量信号（0-7分）。具有GUI面板，支持手动买卖平仓按钮，以及根据信号强度自动开仓或反转仓位的自动驾驶模式。

策略分析

策略概念基于简单的价格动量比较：将当前K线的开盘价、最高价、最低价、收盘价分别与前一根K线对比，以及计算(H+L)/2、(H+L+C)/3、(H+L+C+C)/4的复合均值进行对比，共7项指标投票决定多空方向。入场条件为：手动模式下点击Buy/Sell按钮开仓，或自动驾驶模式下当买入信号数大于卖出信号数时做多，反之做空。出场条件仅有手动平仓按钮和自动驾驶模式下的仓位反转（先平后开反向仓），没有任何止损或止盈设置。仓位管理采用固定0.01手数，不根据账户余额或风险动态调整。资金管理完全缺失，无止损止盈、无最大持仓限制、无回撤控制，风险暴露极高。

Summary

A panel-based semi-automated trading EA that compares the current bar's Open, High, Low, and Close prices against the previous bar to generate a buy/sell momentum signal (0-7 score). It features a GUI panel with manual Buy/Sell/Close buttons and an autopilot mode that automatically opens or reverses positions based on the signal strength.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on simple bar-to-bar price momentum comparison: it compares the current bar's Open, High, Low, and Close against the previous bar, plus three composite averages ((H+L)/2, (H+L+C)/3, (H+L+C+C)/4), for a total of 7 directional votes. Entry conditions are: manual Buy/Sell button clicks on the GUI panel, or in autopilot mode, a BUY when buy votes exceed sell votes and a SELL when sell votes exceed buy votes. Exit conditions are limited to a manual Close button and autopilot position reversals (close then open opposite direction), with absolutely no stop-loss or take-profit levels. Position sizing is fixed at 0.01 lots regardless of account balance, equity, or risk parameters. Money management is entirely absent — there are no stop-losses, take-profits, maximum position limits, or drawdown controls, creating extremely high risk exposure.

优势

  • 界面直观，提供可视化面板显示买卖信号百分比和进度条
  • 支持手动和自动两种交易模式，灵活性较好
  • 信号计算逻辑简单透明，易于理解和验证
  • 提供一键平仓功能，方便快速退出市场

劣势

  • 完全没有止损和止盈设置，亏损可能无限扩大
  • 固定手数0.01不做任何资金管理调整，不考虑账户风险承受能力
  • 信号逻辑过于简单，仅比较相邻两根K线的价格大小，缺乏统计学支撑
  • 自动驾驶模式下只做反转不设止损，频繁开平仓会导致大量点差成本
  • 没有新K线检查机制，每个tick都会执行交易逻辑可能造成重复交易

改进建议

  • 添加止损和止盈参数，至少设置默认止损以控制最大亏损
  • 增加新K线检查机制，确保每根K线只交易一次，避免tick级别重复开仓
  • 优化信号逻辑，引入技术指标（如移动平均线、RSI）作为辅助确认，提高信号可靠性

Strengths

  • Intuitive GUI panel displaying buy/sell signal percentages and progress bars visually
  • Supports both manual and automated trading modes for flexibility
  • Signal calculation logic is simple and transparent, easy to understand and verify
  • Provides one-click close position button for quick market exit

Weaknesses

  • No stop-loss or take-profit at all — losses can grow indefinitely
  • Fixed 0.01 lot size with no money management adjustment, ignoring account risk capacity
  • Signal logic is overly simplistic, only comparing adjacent bar prices without statistical support
  • Autopilot mode only reverses positions without stops, frequent open/close leads to high spread costs
  • No new bar check mechanism, trading logic executes every tick which may cause duplicate trades

Improvements

  • Add stop-loss and take-profit parameters with at least a default stop-loss to control maximum loss
  • Implement a new bar check to ensure only one trade per bar, avoiding tick-level duplicate entries
  • Enhance signal logic by introducing technical indicators (e.g., moving averages, RSI) as confirmation to improve reliability

风险评估

总体:HIGH

风险点

  • 无止损止盈，单笔亏损无上限
  • 无新K线检查，每个tick可能触发交易导致频繁开仓
  • 自动驾驶模式下的反转交易不设止损，连续错误信号会造成连环亏损
  • 无仓位检查保护（如最大持仓数限制），极端行情下可能过度交易

Risk Assessment

Overall:HIGH

Risk Points

  • No stop-loss or take-profit, single trade loss has no ceiling
  • No new bar check, every tick can trigger a trade causing frequent position opening
  • Autopilot reversal trades lack stops, consecutive wrong signals cause cascading losses
  • No position protection (e.g., max position limit), may over-trade in extreme markets
