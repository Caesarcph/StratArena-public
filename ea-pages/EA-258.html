摘要

该EA基于MQL5标准库Expert框架，实现了以MA交叉信号并经ADX趋势强度确认的趋势跟踪策略。当移动平均线交叉且ADX值超过最低阈值时入场交易，采用固定手数仓位管理，无移动止损。

策略分析

策略概念是基于移动平均线交叉与ADX趋势强度过滤相结合的趋势跟踪系统。入场条件为移动平均线发生交叉信号，且ADX指标值必须高于设定的最低阈值（默认22.0），以此确保只在趋势足够明显时才开仓。出场条件依赖固定的止损（400点）和止盈（900点）设置，止盈目标是止损的2.25倍，体现了较好的盈亏比设计。仓位管理采用固定手数模式（默认0.1手），不根据账户余额动态调整。资金管理使用CMoneyFixedLot类，虽然配置了百分比参数（10%），但实际为固定手数模式，缺乏动态风险调控。该策略未使用移动止损（TrailingNone），意味着无法在趋势运行中锁定浮动利润，存在利润回吐风险。

Summary

This EA uses the MQL5 Standard Library Expert framework to implement a trend-following strategy based on MA crossover confirmed by ADX strength. It enters trades when a moving average crossover occurs and the ADX value exceeds a minimum threshold, with fixed lot sizing and no trailing stop.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is a trend-following system combining moving average crossover signals with ADX trend strength filtration. Entry conditions require a moving average crossover to occur and the ADX indicator value must exceed a configurable minimum threshold (default 22.0), ensuring trades are only opened when the trend is sufficiently strong. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss (400 points) and take profit (900 points) levels, with a reward-to-risk ratio of 2.25:1, reflecting a solid profit factor design. Position sizing uses a fixed lot mode (default 0.1 lots) that does not dynamically adjust based on account balance or equity. Money management employs the CMoneyFixedLot class, which despite having a percentage parameter configured (10%), operates in fixed lot mode and lacks dynamic risk scaling. The strategy does not use any trailing stop (TrailingNone), meaning it cannot lock in floating profits during trending moves and risks significant profit givebacks.

优势

  • MA交叉结合ADX过滤有效减少震荡行情中的虚假信号
  • 止盈止损比例2.25:1设计合理，有利于长期正期望值
  • 基于MQL5标准库Expert框架开发，代码结构清晰规范
  • ADX最低阈值参数可调，适应不同市场波动环境

劣势

  • 无移动止损机制，趋势反转时无法锁定已获利润
  • 固定手数仓位管理无法根据账户规模动态调整风险暴露
  • 缺少新K线检查机制（Expert_EveryTick=false部分缓解但非彻底解决），可能在同一根K线重复开仓
  • 仅依赖单一MA周期交叉，缺少多时间框架确认
  • 无点差过滤机制，在高位差环境下可能不利

改进建议

  • 增加移动止损模块（如TrailingFixedPct或TrailingStopPct），在趋势运行中锁定利润
  • 引入多时间框架确认机制，例如在H4趋势方向下使用H1信号入场
  • 将固定手数改为动态仓位管理，根据ATR或账户风险百分比自动调整手数

Strengths

  • MA crossover combined with ADX filtration effectively reduces false signals in ranging markets
  • Stop loss to take profit ratio of 2.25:1 is well-designed for long-term positive expectancy
  • Built on MQL5 Standard Library Expert framework with clean and standardized code structure
  • Configurable ADX minimum threshold parameter allows adaptation to different market volatility environments

Weaknesses

  • No trailing stop mechanism means profits cannot be locked in during trend reversals
  • Fixed lot position sizing cannot dynamically adjust risk exposure based on account size
  • Lacks new bar check mechanism (Expert_EveryTick=false partially mitigates but does not fully resolve), potential for repeated entries on the same bar
  • Relies on single MA period crossover without multi-timeframe confirmation
  • No spread filter mechanism, which may be detrimental in high-spread environments

Improvements

  • Add a trailing stop module (e.g., TrailingFixedPct or TrailingStopPct) to lock in profits during trending moves
  • Introduce multi-timeframe confirmation, such as entering on H1 signals only when aligned with H4 trend direction
  • Replace fixed lot sizing with dynamic position management that auto-adjusts lot size based on ATR or account risk percentage

风险点

  • 无移动止损导致盈利交易可能大幅回吐
  • 固定手数不随账户变化调整，在连亏后风险比例被动放大
  • 缺少点差过滤在高波动或低流动性时段可能产生不利成交

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • No trailing stop causes winning trades to potentially give back significant profits
  • Fixed lot sizing does not adjust with account changes, causing risk ratio to passively increase after consecutive losses
  • Lack of spread filter may result in unfavorable fills during high volatility or low liquidity periods
