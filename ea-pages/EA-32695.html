摘要

该EA结合布林带、RSI和移动均线来识别均值回归交易机会。当价格触及布林带下轨且RSI超卖时买入，当价格触及布林带上轨且RSI超买时卖出，移动均线作为趋势过滤器。当价格回归均值或出现反向信号时平仓。

策略分析

策略核心是布林带均值回归，利用价格偏离布林带上下轨后回归均值的特性进行交易。入场条件：做多时要求价格触及或跌破布林带下轨，同时RSI处于超卖区域（低于阈值），且快速均线在慢速均线下方或价格接近均值；做空反之。出场条件包括价格回归布林带中轨、RSI离开超买超卖区、或出现反向交叉信号。仓位管理采用固定手数或基于资金比例的手数计算方式。资金管理方面设置了止损和止盈，止损通常设在布林带外轨之外，止盈设在中轨附近。整体策略逻辑清晰，属于经典的超买超卖均值回归体系，但在强趋势市场中可能面临连续亏损的风险。

Summary

This EA combines Bollinger Bands, RSI, and Moving Averages to identify mean reversion trading opportunities. It enters buy positions when price touches the lower Bollinger Band and RSI is oversold, and sell positions when price touches the upper Bollinger Band and RSI is overbought, with Moving Averages serving as trend filters. Exits are triggered when price reverts to the mean or when opposing signals occur.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy is Bollinger Band mean reversion, exploiting the tendency of price to revert to the mean after touching the upper or lower bands. Entry conditions: for buy signals, price must touch or cross below the lower Bollinger Band while RSI is in oversold territory (below threshold), with Moving Averages acting as directional filters; sell signals are the mirror opposite. Exit conditions include price reverting to the Bollinger Band middle band, RSI leaving overbought or oversold zones, or opposing crossover signals appearing. Position sizing uses either fixed lots or a balance-proportional lot calculation method. Money management incorporates stop loss and take profit levels, with stops typically placed beyond the outer Bollinger Band and take profit targets near the middle band. The overall strategy logic is clear and follows a classic overbought-oversold mean reversion framework, but it carries the risk of consecutive losses during strong trending markets where mean reversion fails.

优势

  • 多指标组合确认信号，减少假信号概率，提高入场质量
  • 布林带均值回归策略逻辑经典成熟，在震荡行情中表现良好
  • RSI与布林带搭配使用，双重超买超卖确认增强可靠性

劣势

  • 均值回归策略在强趋势市场中容易遭受连续亏损，缺乏趋势识别保护
  • 默认止损止盈为零，存在未设置止损直接交易的风险
  • 缺少新K线检查机制，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤机制，在强趋势行情中暂停均值回归交易或反转策略
  • 强制要求设置止损，默认值不应为零，以防止极端行情下大幅亏损
  • 添加新K线检查和持仓检查逻辑，避免重复开仓和过度交易

Strengths

  • Multi-indicator confirmation reduces false signals and improves entry quality
  • Bollinger Band mean reversion is a proven, classic strategy that performs well in ranging markets
  • Dual overbought-oversold confirmation from both RSI and Bollinger Bands enhances signal reliability

Weaknesses

  • Mean reversion strategy is vulnerable to consecutive losses in strong trending markets without adequate trend protection
  • Default stop loss and take profit values are zero, posing risk of trading without protective orders
  • Lacks new bar check mechanism, potentially opening duplicate positions on the same candle

Improvements

  • Add trend filtering mechanism to pause mean reversion trades or switch strategy during strong trending markets
  • Enforce mandatory stop loss with non-zero default values to prevent large losses during extreme market moves
  • Implement new bar check and position check logic to prevent duplicate entries and overtrading
