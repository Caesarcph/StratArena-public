Intersection 2 iMA

This EA trades based on the intersection (crossover) of two Moving Average indicators (iMA). It opens buy positions when the fast MA crosses above the slow MA and sell positions when the fast MA crosses below the slow MA, implementing a classic trend-following strategy.

Strategy analysis

The core concept of this strategy is dual moving average crossover, a classic trend-following approach. Entry conditions are triggered when the fast moving average crosses the slow moving average — a golden cross signals a buy, while a death cross signals a sell. Exit conditions typically rely on the opposite crossover signal or fixed stop-loss/take-profit levels. Position sizing may use fixed lots or dynamic lot calculation based on account equity. Money management is relatively basic, usually relying on parameterized stop-loss and take-profit to control per-trade risk. The strategy performs well in strongly trending markets but suffers from frequent false signals during ranging or choppy conditions. The inherent lag of moving average crossover strategies means signals are generated after a significant portion of the move has already occurred.

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and maintain
  • Classic trend-following approach with stable performance in trending markets
  • Few parameters with large optimization space, adaptable to different instruments and timeframes

Weaknesses

  • Moving average crossover signals have significant lag, entry points are often suboptimal
  • Frequent false signals in ranging markets lead to consecutive losses
  • Lacks market state (trending/ranging) filtering mechanism

Possible improvements

  • Add a trend filter (such as ADX or ATR) to reduce false signals in ranging markets
  • Implement a trailing stop to protect accumulated profits
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation to improve signal reliability

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Lack of position existence check may lead to duplicate openings
  • Stop-loss set to 0 exposes the account to unlimited loss risk
  • No new bar check may cause repeated trading on the same bar
