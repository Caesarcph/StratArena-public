RSI RFTL EA

This EA uses the RSI (Relative Strength Index) indicator combined with RFTL (Recursive Fast Trend Line) to generate trading signals. It enters long positions when RSI is oversold and the RFTL confirms an upward trend, and enters short positions when RSI is overbought and RFTL confirms a downward trend. The strategy aims to capture momentum reversals filtered by a trend-confirmation digital filter.

Strategy analysis

The EA's strategy concept is based on a dual-confirmation mechanism combining RSI momentum reversal with RFTL trend direction filtering. Entry conditions are: go long when RSI is below the oversold threshold and RFTL value is positive (confirming uptrend), go short when RSI is above the overbought threshold and RFTL value is negative (confirming downtrend). Exit conditions typically include fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, or closing positions when RSI returns to the neutral zone. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach with no dynamic lot calculation or equity-based sizing. Money management relies on the user manually setting the lot size, lacking automatic risk-per-trade calculation. The overall strategy logic is clear but lacks adaptability to market volatility and spread considerations.

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator confirmation reduces false signals; RSI and RFTL complement each other to improve signal reliability
  • Strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and optimize parameters
  • RFTL as a digital filter effectively removes market noise and provides smooth trend identification

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot size lacks dynamic position management, unable to automatically adjust trade size based on account risk
  • No spread filter mechanism included, which may cause unnecessary losses in high-spread environments
  • RSI can produce persistent overbought/oversold signals in strong trending markets, leading to premature counter-trend entries

Possible improvements

  • Add equity-percentage-based risk calculation for dynamic position sizing
  • Add spread filter and session filter to avoid trading during high-spread and low-liquidity periods

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Fixed lot size does not scale with account balance, potentially leading to overtrading or insufficient risk
  • Lacks new bar check mechanism, may repeatedly open positions on the same bar
