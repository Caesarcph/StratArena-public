Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept of ExpertClor_v01 is based on color-signal-driven momentum trading, capturing shifts in trend momentum through color changes of technical indicators. Entry conditions likely depend on indicator color transitions from one state to another (e.g., moving average turning from red to green signaling upward momentum building), opening positions upon color switch confirmation. Exit conditions may use reverse color signals as position-closing triggers, or combine fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels for position management. Regarding position sizing, as an early v01 version, it likely employs fixed lot sizes or simple money management rules, lacking advanced dynamic position adjustment mechanisms. For money management, as an early version, it may lack comprehensive risk control logic such as maximum position limits, drawdown protection, or equity-based safeguards. The overall strategy logic depends on the timeliness and accuracy of indicator color signals, carrying inherent lag risk inherent to indicator-based systems.
