Percentage_Crossover_Channel_EA

This EA trades based on a percentage-based channel that dynamically adjusts to price movements. It enters buy positions when price crosses below the lower channel boundary and sell positions when price crosses above the upper channel boundary, aiming to capture mean reversion moves within the channel range.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy concept is a dynamically calculated percentage-based channel, where the upper and lower boundaries are constructed as a percentage offset from price, forming an envelope similar to Bollinger Bands. Entry conditions are: open a buy position when price crosses below the lower channel boundary, expecting mean reversion toward the channel center; open a sell position when price crosses above the upper channel boundary, expecting price to fall back. Exit conditions rely on the reversal of the crossover signal — positions are held until price crosses the opposite channel boundary. Position sizing uses a fixed lot parameter with no dynamic adjustment based on account equity or volatility. Money management lacks explicit maximum position limits and drawdown controls, relying on channel mean reversion assumptions without hard stop-loss protection. The strategy performs well in ranging markets but may suffer consecutive losses in strong trending conditions where price persistently deviates from the channel.

Strengths

  • Percentage-based channel dynamically adapts to price changes, offering more flexibility than fixed ranges
  • Simple and clear strategy logic with few parameters, easy to understand and optimize
  • Mean reversion logic has statistical edge in ranging markets

Weaknesses

  • Lacks hard stop-loss, which can cause severe losses in strong trending markets
  • Fixed lot position sizing cannot adapt to market volatility changes
  • No new bar check mechanism, potentially opening duplicate positions on the same bar

Possible improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit mechanism to improve risk control
  • Implement new bar detection logic to prevent duplicate signal entries on the same bar

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No hard stop-loss protection, losses can expand indefinitely in strong trends
  • No new bar check may cause duplicate position openings on the same bar, increasing risk exposure
  • Fixed lot size ignores account equity changes, making leverage risk uncontrollable in extreme markets
