摘要

该EA基于UltraWPR自定义指标的交叉信号进行交易，UltraWPR对威廉指标(WPR)进行了多阶段平滑处理。当平滑后的WPR上行线上穿下行线时开多仓，反向下穿时开空仓，并支持配置入场和出场的权限控制。

策略分析

策略概念基于UltraWPR指标的双线交叉，该指标是对WPR(威廉百分比范围)经过多阶段自适应平滑处理后的产物。入场条件为：当UpValue线从下方穿越DnValue线时产生买入信号，当DnValue线从下方穿越UpValue线时产生卖出信号，具体判断逻辑是比较相邻两根K线的上下线关系变化。出场条件包括：止损和止盈以点数设定(默认止损1000点、止盈2000点)，以及反向信号触发平仓(BuyPosClose/SellPosClose)。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示固定手数，正值表示按存款比例计算仓位。资金管理依赖外部TradeAlgorithms.mqh库中的BuyPositionOpen/SellPositionOpen函数实现，支持最大价格偏差控制。该策略使用多周期框架(默认H4)进行信号生成，并在新K线出现时才执行交易逻辑以避免重复信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on crossover signals from the UltraWPR custom indicator, which applies multi-stage smoothing to the Williams Percent Range (WPR). It opens buy positions when the smoothed WPR upward line crosses above the downward line and sell positions on the reverse crossover, with configurable permissions for entry and exit.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on dual-line crossover signals from the UltraWPR indicator, which applies multiple stages of adaptive smoothing (JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, AMA options) to the Williams Percent Range (WPR). Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the UpValue line crosses above the DnValue line (detected by comparing values across two consecutive bars), and a sell signal on the reverse crossover. Exit conditions include fixed Stop Loss (default 1000 points) and Take Profit (default 2000 points), plus directional close signals triggered when the opposite crossover occurs. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the deposit. Money management is delegated to the external TradeAlgorithms.mqh library functions (BuyPositionOpen/SellPositionOpen), which also handle maximum price deviation control. The strategy operates on a configurable timeframe (default H4) and only evaluates trading logic on new bar formation to prevent duplicate signals within the same bar.

优势

  • 使用多阶段自适应平滑算法减少WPR指标的噪音和假信号，提高信号质量
  • 支持丰富的平滑方法选择(JJMA/JurX/ParMA/T3/VIDYA/AMA等)，策略灵活性高
  • 买卖开仓和平仓权限可独立控制，便于精细化管理交易行为

劣势

  • 止损1000点止盈2000点的固定比例缺乏根据市场波动率动态调整的机制
  • 没有点差过滤和波动率过滤，在高点差或低波动环境下可能频繁触发虚假信号
  • 核心交易逻辑依赖外部库TradeAlgorithms.mqh和SmoothAlgorithms.mqh，代码可审计性和可移植性较差

改进建议

  • 增加基于ATR的动态止损止盈机制，取代固定点数设置，以适应不同市场波动环境
  • 添加点差过滤和最小波动率阈值，避免在交易成本过高或市场过于平静时入场

Strengths

  • Multi-stage adaptive smoothing algorithms reduce WPR noise and false signals, improving signal quality
  • Rich selection of smoothing methods (JJMA/JurX/ParMA/T3/VIDYA/AMA) provides high strategy flexibility
  • Independent permission controls for buy/sell open and close operations enable fine-grained trade management

Weaknesses

  • Fixed 1000-point SL and 2000-point TP lack dynamic adjustment based on market volatility (e.g. ATR-based)
  • No spread filter or volatility filter means potential frequent false signals in high-spread or low-volatility environments
  • Core trading logic depends on external libraries (TradeAlgorithms.mqh, SmoothAlgorithms.mqh), reducing code auditability and portability

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss/take profit mechanism instead of fixed point values to adapt to varying market volatility
  • Add spread filter and minimum volatility threshold to avoid entering trades when transaction costs are too high or the market is too quiet

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 固定止损止盈不随市场波动率调整，在极端行情下可能造成过大亏损或过早止盈
  • MM参数为负值时使用固定手数，缺乏对账户净值变化的动态响应，可能导致过度杠杆

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed stop loss/take profit does not adjust with market volatility, potentially causing excessive losses or premature profit-taking in extreme conditions
  • When MM is negative (fixed lot), there is no dynamic response to account equity changes, potentially leading to over-leveraging
