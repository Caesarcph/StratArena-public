摘要

该EA基于均线偏离信号入场，并采用深度马丁格尔仓位管理策略，在连续亏损后加倍下注以恢复回撤。当价格偏离均线时开仓，在浮亏头寸上逐步加码，期望市场回归时以保本或盈利平仓。核心逻辑依赖在亏损头寸上递增加仓，本质上风险极高。

策略分析

策略概念基于价格与均线的偏离：当价格远离均线时判断为过度偏离，逆势开仓期待回归。入场条件为价格偏离MA达到设定阈值后首次建仓，若价格继续不利方向运行，则按马丁格尔倍数加仓（如1.5倍或2倍递增），每层加仓间距由参数控制。出场条件通常为当整体持仓组合达到目标利润或保本时全部平仓，而不设单个订单的止损。仓位管理采用马丁格尔递增模式，首仓较小，后续加仓量按倍数增长，使得整体均价向有利方向靠近只需较小的回撤即可解套。资金管理方面缺乏固定止损和最大回撤限制，严重依赖市场最终回归均线的假设。该策略在震荡市中可能表现良好，但在单边趋势行情中会面临巨大风险，可能导致账户爆仓。

Summary

This EA uses a moving average-based entry signal and employs a deep martingale position sizing strategy that increases lot size after consecutive losses to recover drawdowns. It opens positions when price deviates from the MA and doubles down on losing positions, aiming to close all trades at a breakeven or profit level when the market reverts. The core concept relies on averaging into losing positions with progressively larger lots, which is inherently risky.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on price deviation from a moving average: when price moves far from the MA, the EA judges it as an over-extension and opens a counter-trend position expecting mean reversion. Entry conditions trigger the first position when price deviates beyond a configured threshold from the MA, and if price continues moving against the position, additional orders are placed at fixed intervals with progressively larger lot sizes following a martingale multiplier (e.g., 1.5x or 2x). Exit conditions typically close all positions in the basket when the combined portfolio reaches a target profit or breakeven level, without individual stop-losses on each order. Position sizing follows a martingale progression where the initial lot is small but subsequent layers grow exponentially, so that the overall average entry price only needs a small favorable move to break even. Money management lacks fixed stop-losses and maximum drawdown limits, heavily relying on the assumption that price will eventually revert to the mean. This strategy may perform acceptably in ranging markets but faces catastrophic risk during strong trending conditions that could wipe out the entire account.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中能稳定获利，利用均值回归特性
  • 参数化程度高，用户可调整MA周期、倍数、间距等关键参数
  • 组合止盈机制确保整体持仓盈利时统一出场，避免贪心
  • 代码结构相对简单，逻辑清晰便于理解和修改
  • 首仓可控，小手数起步降低了初始风险暴露

劣势

  • 马丁格尔策略在单边趋势中会导致指数级亏损增长
  • 缺乏硬性止损保护，极端行情可能爆仓
  • 加仓倍数递增使得后期每层风险急剧放大
  • 过度依赖均值回归假设，趋势市场中价格可能长期不回归
  • 最大加仓层数限制在极端波动下仍不足以控制风险

改进建议

  • 增加全局最大回撤限制和账户权益保护机制，达到阈值强制平仓
  • 加入趋势过滤器（如ADX或更高周期MA方向），在强趋势中禁止逆势加仓

Strengths

  • Profitable in ranging markets by exploiting mean reversion characteristics
  • Highly parameterized allowing users to adjust MA period, multiplier, step distance and other key settings
  • Basket take-profit mechanism ensures unified exit when the combined position is profitable, avoiding greed
  • Relatively simple code structure with clear logic for easy understanding and modification
  • Controllable initial lot size with small starting position reducing initial risk exposure

Weaknesses

  • Martingale strategy leads to exponentially growing losses during strong trending markets
  • Lacks hard stop-loss protection; extreme market moves can blow the account
  • The multiplying lot size causes risk to escalate dramatically in later layers
  • Over-reliance on mean reversion assumption; price may not revert for extended periods in trending markets
  • Maximum order limit is still insufficient to control risk under extreme volatility conditions

Improvements

  • Add a global maximum drawdown limit and account equity protection mechanism that forces closure when thresholds are breached
  • Incorporate a trend filter (e.g., ADX or higher timeframe MA direction) to prohibit counter-trend pyramiding during strong trends
