EA-1100 · Trend Following

Exp_BrakeExp

This EA trades based on the custom BrakeExp indicator, opening buy positions on upward signals and sell positions on downward signals while closing opposing positions. It uses a trend-following approach with configurable money management, stop loss, and take profit levels, operating on a user-specified timeframe.

Strategy analysis

The EA's core strategy uses the BrakeExp custom indicator to identify trend direction and trade signals. Entry conditions are: open a buy position when the UpSignal buffer has a non-zero value, and open a sell position when the DnSignal buffer has a non-zero value. Exit conditions include closing opposing positions when trend buffers are active (close sells when UpTrend is present, close buys when DnTrend is present), plus stop loss and take profit levels. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the deposit. The EA employs a new bar detection mechanism to avoid duplicate signals, evaluating trade conditions only when a new bar forms. The strategy supports independent permission controls for opening and closing both buy and sell positions, providing flexible directional trading control.

Strengths

  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate entries within the same bar
  • Stop loss and take profit parameters are complete, making risk controllable
  • Independent buy/sell permission controls provide high strategy flexibility
  • Supports both fixed lot size and deposit share position sizing modes
  • Signal bar index parameter allows avoiding signal lag or overfitting

Weaknesses

  • Depends on a custom BrakeExp indicator whose source is unavailable, making strategy logic opaque
  • No spread filter, which may result in unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • No position check before opening, which may cause repeated entries and position stacking
  • Stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large for all instruments
  • Lacks maximum position count limits and overall risk control mechanisms

Possible improvements

  • Add position check logic to prevent repeated same-direction entries causing excessive position stacking
  • Add spread filter and maximum position count limits to improve risk management

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No position count check, potentially allowing repeated same-direction entries
  • Large stop loss and take profit values may result in prolonged open positions on low-volatility instruments
