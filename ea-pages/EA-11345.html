摘要

该EA基于自定义MaByMa指标的双均线交叉策略，当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿时开空仓。支持可配置的平仓选项和资金管理方式。

策略分析

策略概念基于双平滑均线的交叉信号，使用MaByMa自定义指标生成两条线——指标线和信号线。入场条件为：当指标线从下方穿越信号线时产生买入信号，当指标线从上方穿越信号线时产生卖出信号，仅在信号Bar或新Bar出现时计算以避免重复交易。出场条件包括固定的止损和止盈设置，以及反向交叉信号触发的平仓逻辑，买卖平仓均可独立开关控制。仓位管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，支持五种资金管理模式：自由保证金比例、余额比例、扣除亏损后自由保证金比例、扣除亏损后余额比例及固定手数。策略使用了九种平滑算法（SMA、EMA、SMMA、LWMA、JJMA、JurX、ParMA、T3、VIDYA、AMA）供两条均线分别选择，灵活性很高。

Summary

This EA implements a dual moving average crossover strategy using two smoothed MA lines (indicator and signal) from the custom MaByMa indicator. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions when it crosses below, with configurable position closing and money management options.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a dual smoothed moving average crossover system using the custom MaByMa indicator, which produces two lines: an indicator line and a signal line. Entry conditions trigger a buy signal when the indicator line crosses above the signal line, and a sell signal when the indicator line crosses below the signal line, with calculations performed only on new bars or the signal bar to prevent duplicate trades. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit levels, plus reverse crossover-based position closing, where buy and sell closing can be independently enabled or disabled. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter and supports five money management modes: free margin percentage, balance percentage, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. The strategy offers high flexibility by allowing independent selection of nine smoothing algorithms (SMA, EMA, SMMA, LWMA, JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, AMA) for each of the two moving average lines.

优势

  • 支持九种平滑算法选择，策略灵活性极高
  • 买卖开仓和平仓可独立控制，便于精细化管理
  • 内置五种资金管理模式，适应不同风险偏好
  • 使用新Bar检测机制避免信号重复计算
  • 止损止盈参数完整，风险控制基本到位

劣势

  • 依赖外部自定义指标MaByMa，独立性较差
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生虚假信号
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值偏大，可能不适合所有品种
  • 未做持仓检查，可能在同方向重复开仓
  • 两条均线使用相同默认周期7，交叉信号可能过于频繁

改进建议

  • 添加持仓方向检查，避免同方向重复开仓
  • 增加点差过滤条件，在点差过大时暂停交易

Strengths

  • Supports nine smoothing algorithms, offering extremely high strategy flexibility
  • Buy/sell opening and closing can be independently controlled for fine-grained management
  • Built-in five money management modes adapting to different risk preferences
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate signal calculations
  • Complete stop loss and take profit parameters provide basic risk control

Weaknesses

  • Depends on external custom indicator MaByMa, reducing self-contained portability
  • Lacks spread filter, may generate false signals in high-spread environments
  • Default SL of 1000 and TP of 2000 points are large and may not suit all instruments
  • No position existence check before opening, potentially causing duplicate same-direction positions
  • Both MAs use the same default period of 7, which may produce overly frequent crossover signals

Improvements

  • Add position direction check to avoid opening duplicate same-direction positions
  • Add spread filter to pause trading when spread exceeds a threshold
