摘要

该EA基于自定义'MaByMa'指标的交叉信号进行交易，该指标用一条均线平滑另一条均线。当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿时开空仓，支持可配置的资金管理和止损止盈设置。

策略分析

策略核心是双重平滑均线交叉系统，使用MaByMa指标将一条均线再进行一次平滑处理，从而减少假信号。入场条件为：当指标线Ind上穿信号线Sign时产生买入信号，下穿时产生卖出信号，仅在信号发生交叉时才触发新交易。出场条件依赖固定点数的止损和止盈，同时也支持反向信号平仓——即买入信号时平卖仓，卖出信号时平买仓。仓位管理支持五种模式：固定手数、基于余额百分比、基于可用保证金百分比、基于亏损后余额百分比和基于亏损后可用保证金百分比。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1。策略使用SignalBar参数决定在哪根K线检测信号，默认为1即前一根已收盘K线，这有助于避免未收盘K线的信号闪烁问题。

Summary

This EA trades based on crossover signals from a custom 'MaByMa' indicator, which smooths one moving average by another. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with configurable money management and stop-loss/take-profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is a dual-smoothed moving average crossover system using the MaByMa indicator, which applies a second smoothing to one moving average to reduce false signals. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the indicator line Ind crosses above the signal line Sign, and a sell signal on the opposite crossover, with trades only triggered on actual crossovers. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop-loss and take-profit in points, and also support signal-based exits where a buy signal closes sell positions and vice versa. Position sizing supports five modes: fixed lot, balance percentage, free margin percentage, loss-adjusted balance percentage, and loss-adjusted free margin percentage. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter with a default of 0.1, representing the risk fraction. The strategy uses the SignalBar parameter to determine which bar to check for signals, defaulting to 1 (the last closed bar), which helps avoid signal flickering from unclosed bars.

优势

  • 双重平滑均线设计有效减少传统单均线交叉的假信号，提高信号质量
  • 支持10种平滑算法（SMA/EMA/SMMA/LWMA/JJMA/JurX/ParMA/T3/VIDYA/AMA），灵活度高
  • 提供5种仓位管理模式和可配置的止损止盈，资金管理较为完善
  • 使用SignalBar=1检测已收盘K线信号，避免未收盘K线的信号闪烁
  • 买卖开平仓开关独立可控，便于灵活控制策略行为

劣势

  • 默认止损1000点、止盈2000点对于H12周期来说可能偏大，缺乏基于ATR的动态止损机制
  • 没有扩展点差过滤，在流动性较差的时段可能以较大滑点入场
  • 双重平滑均线本质仍是滞后指标，在趋势反转时入场延迟较大
  • 缺少追踪止损功能，在趋势行情中无法锁定浮动利润
  • 依赖外部自定义指标MaByMa和两个头文件，部署复杂度较高

改进建议

  • 增加基于ATR的动态止损止盈机制，根据市场波动性自动调整止损距离
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免在不利条件下入场

Strengths

  • Dual-smoothed moving average design effectively reduces false signals common in single MA crossovers, improving signal quality
  • Supports 10 smoothing algorithms (SMA/EMA/SMMA/LWMA/JJMA/JurX/ParMA/T3/VIDYA/AMA), offering high flexibility
  • Provides 5 position sizing modes and configurable stop-loss/take-profit, with relatively complete money management
  • Uses SignalBar=1 to detect signals on closed bars, avoiding signal flickering from unclosed bars
  • Independent on/off switches for buy/sell open/close operations, allowing flexible strategy behavior control

Weaknesses

  • Default 1000-point SL and 2000-point TP may be oversized for H12 timeframe, lacking ATR-based dynamic stop-loss
  • No spread filter, potentially entering at large slippage during low-liquidity sessions
  • Dual-smoothed MAs are inherently lagging indicators, causing significant entry delay on trend reversals
  • No trailing stop functionality, unable to lock in floating profits during trending markets
  • Depends on external custom indicator MaByMa and two include files, increasing deployment complexity

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit mechanism to automatically adjust stop distances based on market volatility
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid entering under unfavorable conditions

风险点

  • 固定止损止盈缺乏对市场波动率变化的适应性，在高波动期可能止损过近
  • 没有最大持仓或最大回撤限制，在极端行情下可能持续亏损

Risk Assessment

Overall:LOW

Risk Points

  • Fixed stop-loss/take-profit lacks adaptability to changing market volatility; stops may be too close during high-volatility periods
  • No maximum position or maximum drawdown limit, potentially sustaining continuous losses in extreme markets
