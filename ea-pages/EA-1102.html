摘要

该EA基于牛力与熊力指标的交叉信号进行交易，当牛力超过熊力时做多，当熊力超过牛力时做空。它使用可配置的平滑算法生成信号，并包含固定的止损/止盈水平和仓位平仓逻辑。交易执行仅限于指定时间周期的新K线形成时。

策略分析

策略概念基于市场买卖力量的对比，使用BullsBears自定义指标来判断多空力量转换。入场条件为：当上一根K线的上升趋势线值大于下降趋势线值，且前一周期上升趋势线小于等于下降趋势线时开多单；反之当上升趋势线值小于下降趋势线值且前一周期上升趋势线大于等于下降趋势线时开空单。出场条件包括固定止损和止盈，以及反向信号触发时平仓（如多头信号出现时平空单，空头信号出现时平多单）。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示固定手数，正值表示存款比例。资金管理没有动态调整机制，止损1000点和止盈2000点属于较宽的范围，适合中长期趋势跟踪。

Summary

This EA trades based on the crossover of Bulls and Bears power indicators, entering long when bulls overpower bears and short when bears overpower bulls. It uses customizable smoothing algorithms for signal generation and includes fixed stop-loss/take-profit levels with position closing logic. Trade execution is restricted to new bar formation on the specified timeframe.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on comparing market buying and selling power using the custom BullsBears indicator to detect shifts in bullish/bearish momentum. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the UpTrend line exceeds the DnTrend line on the previous bar and the prior bar showed the opposite or equal relationship; a sell signal is the mirror opposite. Exit conditions include fixed stop-loss at 1000 points and take-profit at 2000 points, plus directional close signals where a new bullish crossover closes short positions and a bearish crossover closes long positions. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the deposit. Money management lacks dynamic adjustment mechanisms, and the wide 1000-point SL with 2000-point TP suits medium-to-long-term trend following rather than short-term scalping.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于买卖力量对比的交叉信号，易于理解和验证
  • 支持多种平滑算法（LWMA等），参数灵活可调，适应性较强
  • 包含独立的买卖开仓和平仓权限控制，可灵活配置交易方向
  • 仅在新K线形成时计算信号，避免了逐tick重复计算的性能浪费
  • 使用2:1的盈亏比（止盈2000点/止损1000点），具有正期望值潜力

劣势

  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生虚假信号导致不必要的交易
  • 止损1000点非常宽泛，在剧烈行情下可能承受较大亏损
  • 没有追踪止损机制，无法在有利行情中锁定浮动利润
  • OnInit中声明了局部CXMA对象但未持久化，min_rates_total计算后该对象即销毁，设计不够优雅
  • 缺乏多重时间框架确认，仅依赖单一指标信号，容易产生假突破交易

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和波动率过滤（如ATR），避免在不利市场条件下开仓
  • 添加追踪止损功能，在趋势有利时逐步锁定利润，提高盈亏比
  • 引入多时间框架确认机制，如大周期趋势方向一致时才允许交易，减少假信号

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on bulls/bears power crossover signals, easy to understand and verify
  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithms (LWMA etc.) with flexible parameters for adaptability
  • Independent buy/sell open and close permission controls allow flexible directional configuration
  • Signal calculation only on new bar formation avoids redundant per-tick computation overhead
  • 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio (TP 2000 / SL 1000 points) provides positive expectancy potential

Weaknesses

  • No spread filter, which may generate false signals and unnecessary trades in high-spread environments
  • The 1000-point stop-loss is very wide, potentially leading to large losses during volatile markets
  • No trailing stop mechanism to lock in floating profits during favorable moves
  • OnInit declares a local CXMA object that is destroyed after min_rates_total calculation, which is inelegant design
  • Lacks multi-timeframe confirmation, relying on a single indicator signal prone to false breakout trades

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and volatility filter (e.g., ATR) to avoid opening positions under unfavorable market conditions
  • Implement trailing stop functionality to progressively lock in profits during favorable trends, improving reward-to-risk ratio
  • Introduce multi-timeframe confirmation, only allowing trades when the higher timeframe trend aligns, reducing false signals

风险点

  • 止损点数高达1000点，单笔亏损可能较大，缺乏动态止损调整
  • 没有检查现有持仓数量，可能存在同方向重复开仓的风险
  • MM参数为负值时直接作为手数，缺乏最小手数和最大手数边界检查

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Stop-loss of 1000 points is very large, single trade loss can be significant with no dynamic SL adjustment
  • No check on existing position count, risking duplicate openings in the same direction
  • When MM is negative it is used directly as lot size with no minimum/maximum lot boundary validation
