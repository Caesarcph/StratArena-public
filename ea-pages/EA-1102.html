摘要

该EA基于自定义的BullsBears指标进行交易，通过比较多空趋势强度来生成信号。当多头趋势上穿空头趋势时开多仓，反之开空仓，并设有可配置的止损止盈水平。

策略分析

策略概念基于牛熊力量指标的多空趋势交叉，属于典型的趋势跟踪策略。入场条件为：当UpTrend线从下方穿越DnTrend线时产生买入信号，当UpTrend线从上方穿越DnTrend线时产生卖出信号。出场条件包括止损和止盈的硬性设定，以及趋势反转时的平仓信号——即当多头持仓时出现卖出信号则平多仓，空头持仓时出现买入信号则平空仓。仓位管理通过MM参数实现，负值表示固定手数，正值表示按存款比例计算仓位大小。资金管理方面，该EA使用了SignalBar参数控制信号延迟，并在新K线出现时才进行信号判断以避免重复交易。策略还使用了多种平滑算法（LWMA、JJMA、VIDIA、AMA）对牛熊力量进行平滑处理，以减少噪音干扰。

Summary

This EA trades based on the BullsBears custom indicator, which compares bullish and bearish trend strengths. It opens buy positions when the bullish trend crosses above the bearish trend and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with configurable stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the crossover of bullish and bearish trend strengths from the BullsBears custom indicator, representing a classic trend following approach. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the UpTrend line crosses above the DnTrend line, and a sell signal is generated when the UpTrend line crosses below the DnTrend line, with the crossover confirmed by comparing the current and previous bar values. Exit conditions include hardcoded stop loss and take profit levels, plus trend reversal closing — long positions are closed when a sell signal appears and short positions are closed when a buy signal appears. Position sizing is managed via the MM parameter, where negative values specify a fixed lot size and positive values represent a share of the deposit per deal. Money management also incorporates the SignalBar parameter to control signal timing delay, and trade signals are only evaluated on new bars to prevent duplicate entries. The strategy employs multiple smoothing algorithms (LWMA, JJMA, VIDIA, AMA) to smooth the bulls/bears power values, reducing market noise in the signals.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰简洁，基于趋势交叉易于理解和验证
  • 支持多种平滑算法选择，交易者可根据市场特性灵活调整
  • 设有止损止盈和滑点控制，基本风险管理到位
  • 新K线判断机制有效防止同一根K线内重复交易
  • 买卖开仓和平仓权限独立控制，灵活性高

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点的默认值偏大，可能导致较大亏损
  • 缺乏点差过滤，在高点差环境下交易成本可能侵蚀利润
  • 没有持仓检查，可能在已有同方向持仓时重复开仓
  • 依赖自定义指标BullsBears，增加了部署和调试的复杂度
  • MM参数的正负值逻辑不直观，容易混淆
  • 缺乏回撤控制和最大持仓限制等高级风控机制

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤机制，当点差超过阈值时暂停交易
  • 添加持仓方向检查，避免同方向重复开仓
  • 引入移动止损或保本止损机制，保护已获利润
  • 增加回撤控制和最大连续亏损次数限制
  • 优化MM参数的接口设计，将固定手数和比例手数分离为两个独立参数

Strengths

  • Clear and concise strategy logic based on trend crossover, easy to understand and verify
  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithms, allowing traders to adapt to different market conditions
  • Includes stop loss, take profit, and slippage control for basic risk management
  • New bar detection mechanism effectively prevents duplicate trades within the same bar
  • Independent permission controls for buy/sell opening and closing offer high flexibility

Weaknesses

  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are large, potentially leading to significant losses
  • No spread filter, trading costs in high-spread environments may erode profits
  • No position check before opening, risk of duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Depends on the custom BullsBears indicator, adding deployment and debugging complexity
  • MM parameter's positive/negative value logic is unintuitive and error-prone
  • Lacks advanced risk controls such as drawdown limits and maximum position restrictions

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter to pause trading when spread exceeds a threshold
  • Add position direction check to avoid duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Introduce trailing stop or break-even mechanism to protect unrealized profits
  • Add drawdown control and maximum consecutive loss limits
  • Redesign the MM parameter interface by separating fixed lot and percentage lot into two independent parameters
