10 pips EURUSD

This EA is a scalping system designed for EURUSD that targets small pip gains (approximately 10 pips) per trade. It enters positions based on short-term price momentum signals and exits quickly with a tight take-profit. The strategy is optimized for low-spread environments and high-frequency, small-profit trading.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy concept of this EA is scalping, accumulating profits by capturing small price movements on EURUSD. Entry conditions are typically based on short-term momentum indicators or price action signals, such as fast moving average crossovers or short-term overbought/oversold reversals. Exit conditions set a tight take-profit target (approximately 10 pips), paired with a relatively small stop-loss to control individual trade losses. In terms of position sizing, the EA likely uses either fixed lot sizes or balance-proportional lot calculation, but as a scalping strategy, it typically does not employ martingale or position-adding methods. Money management relies on the combination of high win rate and small take-profit, accumulating returns through frequent trading, though the tight stop-loss means spreads and slippage significantly impact profitability. Overall, the strategy's success is highly dependent on low-latency execution environments and low-spread conditions.

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and verify
  • Small profit target keeps per-trade risk controllable
  • Suitable for low-spread accounts, frequent trading can accumulate significant profits
  • Scalping strategy performs well in ranging markets

Weaknesses

  • Scalping strategy is extremely sensitive to spreads and slippage; high-cost environments can lead to losses
  • 10-pip take-profit target is very small; a single adverse tick can trigger stop-loss
  • Lacks news event filtering, exposing trades to severe slippage during major data releases
  • High-frequency trading may lead to overtrading, accumulating transaction costs

Possible improvements

  • Add a spread filter to pause trading when spread exceeds a threshold
  • Integrate economic calendar event filtering to avoid trading around major news releases
  • Add a new bar check mechanism to prevent repeated entries within the same bar

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Caution

  • Scalping without spread filter makes take-profit hard to achieve in high-spread conditions
  • Tight stop-loss is easily triggered by market noise, causing frequent losses
  • Lacks news event filtering, creating significant slippage risk
