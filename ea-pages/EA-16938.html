摘要

该EA结合分形突破信号与RSI动量过滤来生成交易入场信号。当看涨分形确认且RSI支持向上动量时开多仓，在看跌分形确认且RSI确认向下压力时开空仓。通过设定的止损和止盈水平管理出场。

策略分析

策略核心概念是基于Bill Williams分形结构识别价格突破方向，并辅以RSI指标过滤假突破信号。入场条件为：看涨分形向上突破时RSI高于阈值（如50或超卖回升）则做多，看跌分形向下突破时RSI低于阈值（如50或超买回落）则做空。出场条件依赖固定止损止盈比，部分版本可能加入移动止损逻辑。仓位管理采用固定手数或基于资金的百分比计算方式。资金管理方面未发现复杂的马丁格尔或网格机制，属于常规固定仓位策略。整体策略逻辑清晰，但分形信号滞后性较强，RSI过滤条件相对简单，在震荡行情中可能产生较多假信号。

Summary

This EA combines Fractal breakout signals with RSI momentum filtering to generate trade entries. It opens buy positions when a bullish fractal is confirmed and RSI supports upward momentum, and sells on bearish fractals with RSI confirming downward pressure. Exits are managed via stop-loss and take-profit levels set at entry.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept relies on Bill Williams fractal structures to identify price breakout directions, supplemented by RSI momentum filtering to reduce false breakouts. Entry conditions: buy when a bullish fractal breaks upward and RSI confirms momentum (above a threshold such as 50 or recovering from oversold); sell when a bearish fractal breaks downward and RSI confirms downside pressure (below 50 or declining from overbought). Exit conditions depend on fixed stop-loss and take-profit ratios set at order placement, with some implementations potentially including trailing stop logic. Position sizing uses either fixed lot sizes or a percentage-of-equity calculation method. Money management does not employ complex Martingale or grid mechanisms, falling into a conventional fixed-lot approach. The overall strategy logic is clear, but fractal signals carry significant lag and RSI filtering is relatively simplistic, which may produce numerous false signals during ranging markets.

优势

  • 双指标过滤减少假信号，分形结构识别关键支撑阻力位
  • 策略逻辑简洁明了，参数较少便于优化和理解
  • 固定止损止盈比提供了明确的风险回报控制框架

劣势

  • 分形信号本质滞后，突破确认时价格已运行一段距离，入场点不够理想
  • RSI阈值过滤条件过于简单，缺乏对市场状态（趋势/震荡）的自适应机制
  • 未包含仓位递减或加仓逻辑，在连续亏损时缺乏资金保护机制

改进建议

  • 增加ADX或波动率过滤器以区分趋势和震荡行情，减少震荡市中的假信号
  • 加入移动止损和分批止盈逻辑，提升盈利交易的收益保护能力

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator filtering reduces false signals; fractals identify key support/resistance levels effectively
  • Strategy logic is clean and straightforward with few parameters, making it easy to optimize and understand
  • Fixed stop-loss/take-profit ratio provides a clear risk-reward control framework

Weaknesses

  • Fractal signals are inherently lagging; by the time breakout is confirmed, price has already moved significantly, leading to suboptimal entry points
  • RSI threshold filtering is overly simplistic, lacking adaptive logic for market state (trending vs ranging)
  • No position scaling or loss-streak protection mechanism, exposing capital to sustained drawdowns during adverse conditions

Improvements

  • Add ADX or volatility filter to distinguish trending from ranging markets, reducing false signals during consolidation
  • Implement trailing stop and partial profit-taking logic to better protect gains on winning trades
