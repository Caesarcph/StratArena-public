Exp_Fractal_RSI

This EA combines Fractal breakout signals with RSI momentum filtering to generate trade entries. It identifies fractal highs and lows as support/resistance levels, then uses RSI to confirm momentum direction before opening positions. The strategy aims to capture trend continuation moves following fractal breakouts validated by RSI readings.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy concept is a dual-confirmation entry system based on Williams Fractal breakout combined with RSI momentum filtering. An upside fractal breakout indicates price has broken above a prior swing high resistance, while a downside fractal breakout indicates price has broken below a prior swing low support. Entry conditions: long entry requires price breaking above a fractal high with RSI above a configured threshold (e.g., 50 or higher) confirming upward momentum; short entry requires price breaking below a fractal low with RSI below a configured threshold confirming downward momentum. Exit conditions typically include fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, or position closure triggered by opposing fractal signals. Position sizing supports fixed lot trading, with some versions potentially including balance-based proportional sizing. Money management relies on stop-loss orders for per-trade risk control but lacks portfolio-level risk management and maximum drawdown limits.

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator confirmation (Fractal + RSI) reduces false breakout signals and improves entry quality
  • Fractal breakout is inherently trend-following, performing well in trending markets
  • Clear and understandable strategy logic with highly adjustable parameters, facilitating optimization and backtesting

Weaknesses

  • Fractal breakouts frequently fail in ranging markets, leading to consecutive stop-loss hits
  • RSI as a momentum filter may lag, causing late entries and missing part of the move
  • Lacks money management and maximum position control, leaving risk exposure uncontrolled during losing streaks

Possible improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit that adapt to market volatility for better risk parameter adjustment
  • Add maximum drawdown limit and maximum consecutive loss count control to improve capital safety

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Fractal breakouts produce many false signals in ranging markets without a market regime filter
  • Fixed stop-loss and take-profit do not account for volatility changes—stop too wide in low volatility, take-profit too tight in high volatility
