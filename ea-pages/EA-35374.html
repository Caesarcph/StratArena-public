摘要

该EA基于MACD背离结合RSI过滤进行MT5交易。它检测价格与MACD指标之间的牛背离和熊背离，然后利用RSI水平确认入场信号后开仓。

策略分析

策略核心是MACD背离检测，当价格创新低而MACD不创新低时形成牛背离做多信号，当价格创新高而MACD不创新高时形成熊背离做空信号。入场条件需要RSI作为过滤器，多头要求RSI处于超卖区域附近，空头要求RSI处于超买区域附近，以增加背离信号的可靠性。出场条件通常包含固定的止盈止损设定，也可能根据反向背离信号平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，未见动态仓位调整或基于账户余额的比例仓位管理。资金管理方面较为薄弱，缺乏追迹止损、部分平仓等高级风控机制，整体依赖背离信号的准确性来控制风险。

Summary

This EA trades based on MACD divergence combined with RSI filtering on MT5. It detects bullish and bearish divergences between price and the MACD indicator, then uses RSI levels to confirm entry signals before opening positions.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy's core concept is MACD divergence detection: a bullish divergence forms when price makes a lower low but MACD makes a higher low, signaling a long entry; a bearish divergence forms when price makes a higher high but MACD makes a lower high, signaling a short entry. Entry conditions require RSI as a confirming filter — long signals need RSI near oversold levels, and short signals need RSI near overbought levels, enhancing the reliability of divergence signals. Exit conditions typically involve fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, and may also close positions on opposing divergence signals. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic adjustment or account-balance-proportional sizing. Money management is relatively weak, lacking advanced risk controls such as trailing stops or partial closes, relying primarily on the accuracy of divergence signals for risk control.

优势

  • MACD背离是经典且经过验证的反转交易策略，具有较高的理论可靠性
  • RSI作为二次过滤有效减少了虚假背离信号导致的错误入场
  • 策略逻辑清晰易懂，参数可调性强，适合不同市场环境优化
  • 背离检测结合动量过滤的双重确认机制降低了交易频率但提高了信号质量

劣势

  • 背离检测在震荡市中容易产生大量虚假信号导致连续亏损
  • 固定手数交易缺乏动态仓位管理，无法根据市场波动调整风险敞口
  • 未明确包含追迹止损机制，盈利头寸可能在回撤中损失大部分利润
  • RSI过滤参数在不同品种和时间周期上需要重新优化，泛化能力有限

改进建议

  • 增加追迹止损功能以保护盈利头寸，减少盈利回吐
  • 引入基于ATR的动态止损止盈，根据市场波动性自适应调整
  • 增加背离强度量化指标，过滤弱背离信号提高胜率
  • 加入账户风险比例仓位管理，替代固定手数方式

Strengths

  • MACD divergence is a classic and well-validated reversal trading strategy with high theoretical reliability
  • RSI as a secondary filter effectively reduces false divergence signals that lead to incorrect entries
  • Clear and understandable strategy logic with adjustable parameters suitable for optimization across different market conditions
  • The dual confirmation mechanism of divergence detection combined with momentum filtering reduces trade frequency but improves signal quality

Weaknesses

  • Divergence detection can generate numerous false signals in ranging markets, leading to consecutive losses
  • Fixed lot trading lacks dynamic position sizing and cannot adjust risk exposure based on market volatility
  • No explicit trailing stop mechanism means profitable positions may lose most gains during pullbacks
  • RSI filter parameters require re-optimization for different instruments and timeframes, limiting generalization ability

Improvements

  • Add trailing stop functionality to protect profitable positions and reduce profit givebacks
  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit that self-adjusts based on market volatility
  • Add divergence strength quantification to filter weak divergence signals and improve win rate
  • Implement account-risk-proportional position sizing to replace the fixed lot approach
