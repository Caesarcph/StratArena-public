摘要

该EA实现零填充止损策略，通过开立对冲仓位来将持仓均价拉向当前市场价格，从而填补亏损缺口至零。它动态管理反向仓位以使整体持仓达到盈亏平衡，然后在净利润达到零或目标时平仓。该策略是一种无需传统止损的成本均摊对冲方式。

策略分析

策略概念基于零填充原理：当持仓出现浮亏时，EA通过在反方向开仓来改变整体持仓的均价，使其逐步接近当前价格，从而缩小浮亏。入场条件是当现有持仓的浮亏超过一定阈值时，EA自动在相反方向开立对冲单，利用对冲单的盈利来抵消原始持仓的亏损。出场条件是当所有持仓的净利润达到零或预设目标时，全部平仓。仓位管理采用逐步加码方式，每次对冲的仓位大小可能根据浮亏程度动态调整。资金管理方面，该策略没有传统止损，而是依赖对冲机制来控制风险，但这可能导致持仓量不断累积。整体策略的核心风险在于市场单边行情时，对冲仓位也会产生浮亏，导致总仓位和风险敞口持续扩大。

Summary

This EA implements a zero-filling stop strategy that opens hedging positions to average the entry price toward the current market price, effectively 'filling' the gap to zero loss. It dynamically manages opposing positions to bring the overall position breakeven, then closes all positions once the net profit reaches zero or a target. The strategy is a form of cost-averaging hedging without traditional stop-loss orders.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the zero-filling principle: when existing positions are in floating loss, the EA opens hedging positions in the opposite direction to shift the average entry price closer to the current market price, thereby reducing the floating loss. Entry conditions trigger when the floating loss of existing positions exceeds a certain threshold, at which point the EA automatically opens a hedge order in the opposite direction, using the hedge's profit to offset the original position's loss. Exit conditions are met when the net profit of all positions reaches zero or a preset target, at which point all positions are closed. Position sizing uses a progressive scaling approach, where the size of each hedging position may be dynamically adjusted based on the degree of floating loss. Money management relies on the hedging mechanism rather than traditional stop-losses to control risk, but this can lead to continuous accumulation of positions. The core risk of the overall strategy is that during sustained trending markets, hedging positions also generate floating losses, causing total exposure and risk to expand continuously.

优势

  • 无需传统止损，通过动态对冲机制控制风险
  • 在震荡行情中表现良好，能够有效将浮亏拉回零
  • 策略逻辑清晰，实现成本均摊的思路独特

劣势

  • 在单边趋势行情中，对冲仓位也会产生浮亏，导致总风险敞口不断扩大
  • 没有传统止损保护，极端行情下可能造成严重亏损
  • 仓位可能不断累积，对资金要求较高，容易触发保证金不足

改进建议

  • 增加最大浮亏止损限制，作为最后防线防止账户爆仓
  • 加入趋势过滤器，在强趋势行情中暂停对冲操作或减少仓位

Strengths

  • No traditional stop-loss needed; risk is managed through a dynamic hedging mechanism
  • Performs well in ranging markets, effectively pulling floating losses back to zero
  • Clear strategy logic with a unique approach to cost averaging

Weaknesses

  • In sustained trending markets, hedging positions also generate floating losses, causing total risk exposure to expand continuously
  • No traditional stop-loss protection; extreme market conditions can lead to severe losses
  • Positions can accumulate continuously, requiring high margin and risking margin calls

Improvements

  • Add a maximum floating loss stop as a last line of defense to prevent account blowout
  • Incorporate a trend filter to pause hedging operations or reduce position sizes during strong trending markets

风险点

  • 对冲仓位可能无限累积，导致保证金不足和爆仓风险
  • 单边趋势行情中对冲策略失效，双方向均产生浮亏

Risk Assessment

Overall:HIGH

Risk Points

  • No traditional stop-loss mechanism; risk amplifies infinitely during extreme market conditions
  • Hedging positions can accumulate without limit, leading to margin shortage and account blowout risk
  • Hedging strategy fails during sustained trending markets, generating floating losses in both directions
