EA Close

EA Close is a utility expert advisor designed to close all open positions and/or pending orders on the account either immediately or based on configurable conditions such as profit/loss thresholds, magic number, symbol, or time. It does not open any new trades and serves solely as a position/order management tool for risk control and account cleanup.

Strategy analysis

EA Close is fundamentally not a trading strategy EA but rather a position management utility tool. Its core function is to batch-close positions or delete pending orders based on user-configured parameters. Entry conditions are not applicable since it does not open positions; exit conditions include closing by total profit threshold, total loss threshold, per-position profit/loss threshold, magic number filter, symbol filter, time-based scheduled closing, and more. In terms of position management, the EA does not involve position-opening logic and only handles closing operations, with options to close long positions, short positions, or all positions. For money management, it protects account equity by setting profit/loss thresholds to prevent excessive drawdown or lock in realized profits. The EA is typically used as a supplementary tool alongside other trading strategy EAs, executing one-click or conditional closing when specific criteria are met.

Strengths

  • Simple and intuitive operation with one-click batch closing for improved execution efficiency
  • Supports multiple filter conditions (magic number, symbol, direction) for flexible closing scope control
  • Profit/loss threshold closing effectively protects account equity by locking profits or limiting drawdown
  • Highly compatible as a supplementary tool that works with any trading strategy EA
  • Scheduled closing capability useful for session-based trading or automatic liquidation before major events

Weaknesses

  • As a utility EA it generates no trading signals and is only meaningful when combined with other EAs or manual trading
  • Batch closing during extreme market volatility may result in slippage causing actual fill prices to deviate from expected levels
  • Lacks advanced risk management features such as partial closing or trailing stops, making functionality relatively limited
  • Frequent polling to check conditions may increase server load, especially with large numbers of positions
  • Some default parameter values may not suit all trading environments and require user adjustment based on actual conditions

Possible improvements

  • Add partial closing functionality to allow gradual position reduction by percentage to minimize slippage impact
  • Add a graphical interface panel for real-time position monitoring and one-click closing operations
  • Add email/push notification to alert users after closing operations are executed

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Safe

  • Batch closing during extreme market conditions may result in significant slippage due to insufficient liquidity
  • Improper parameter settings (e.g., profit threshold too small) may trigger premature closing before optimal profit levels are reached
