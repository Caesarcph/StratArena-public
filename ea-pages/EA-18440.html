Crossing of two iMA

This EA implements a classic dual moving average crossover strategy, generating buy signals when a fast moving average crosses above a slow moving average and sell signals when the fast MA crosses below the slow MA. It uses the built-in iMA indicator of MQL5 for both moving average calculations. Trades are opened on crossover signals with configurable lot size, stop loss, and take profit levels.

Strategy analysis

This EA employs a dual moving average crossover trend-following strategy, where the core concept is using the intersection of two moving averages with different periods to detect trend direction changes. Entry conditions are straightforward: a buy signal is generated when the fast MA crosses above the slow MA, and a sell signal is triggered when the fast MA crosses below the slow MA, which essentially captures trend reversal moments. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit parameters, closing positions when price hits the preset SL or TP levels, and reverse crossover signals may also trigger position closure. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size specified by the Lots parameter, with no dynamic adjustment mechanism based on account equity or balance. Money management is relatively simplistic, relying solely on the stop loss to take profit ratio to control the risk-reward profile of each trade, lacking account-level risk controls such as maximum drawdown limits or percentage-based position sizing. The overall strategy logic is clear but basic, representing a typical entry-level trend-following approach.

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and verify, suitable for beginners to learn from and improve
  • Uses MQL5 built-in iMA indicator handles for efficient calculation consistent with platform standards
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit parameters provide a basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing cannot dynamically adjust position size based on account size and volatility, making money management crude
  • MA crossover strategies generate numerous false signals in ranging/choppy markets leading to frequent losses
  • Lacks new bar detection mechanism, potentially opening redundant trades on the same bar

Possible improvements

  • Add new bar detection logic to ensure each crossover signal triggers only one trade, avoiding redundant entries
  • Implement ATR-based dynamic stop loss and position sizing to adaptively adjust risk parameters based on market volatility

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Lack of new bar detection may cause repeated entries on the same signal, increasing unnecessary risk exposure
  • Frequent false signals in ranging markets may lead to consecutive stop losses and accumulated losses
  • Fixed lot size does not adjust with account balance, potentially unable to sustain trading after significant drawdown
