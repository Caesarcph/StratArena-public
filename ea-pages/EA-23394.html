摘要

FitFul 13是一个均值回归EA，基于布林带极端位置和RSI超买超卖水平交易，在价格显著偏离均值时逆势入场。它利用波动率和动量过滤器的组合来捕捉统计极端值的反转机会，预期价格触及布林带边界后回归均值。

策略分析

FitFul 13的策略核心是均值回归，当价格触及布林带上轨或下轨且RSI处于超买或超卖区域时，系统判定市场处于统计极端位置，预期价格将回归均值从而逆势开仓。入场条件要求布林带带宽达到一定阈值以确认波动率足够大，同时RSI必须处于极端区域以确认动量衰竭。出场条件包括价格回归布林带中轨时止盈，以及固定止损点数作为风控底线。仓位管理采用固定手数模式，未引入动态仓位调整或基于账户权益的比例仓位计算。资金管理方面，EA依赖固定止损来控制单笔风险，但缺乏整体账户层面的风险控制机制如最大回撤限制或连亏止损。该策略在震荡行情中表现较好，但在趋势行情中可能因持续逆势交易而产生连续亏损。

Summary

FitFul 13 is a mean reversion EA that trades based on Bollinger Band extremes and RSI overbought/oversold levels, entering against the prevailing trend when price deviates significantly from its mean. It targets reversals from statistical extremes using a combination of volatility and momentum filters. The EA systematically opens positions expecting price to revert to the mean after reaching Bollinger Band boundaries.

Strategy Analysis

FitFul 13's core strategy is mean reversion, entering contrarian positions when price reaches Bollinger Band upper or lower bands while RSI sits in overbought or oversold zones, identifying statistically extreme market conditions where price is expected to revert to its mean. Entry conditions require Bollinger Band width to exceed a minimum threshold confirming sufficient volatility, and RSI must be in extreme territory confirming momentum exhaustion. Exit conditions include take-profit when price reverts to the Bollinger middle band, plus a fixed stop-loss in points as a risk control backstop. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic sizing or equity-proportional calculation. Money management relies on the fixed stop-loss for per-trade risk control, but lacks account-level safeguards such as maximum drawdown limits or consecutive loss circuit breakers. The strategy performs well in ranging markets but may suffer consecutive losses in trending conditions due to persistent counter-trend trading.

优势

  • 均值回归逻辑清晰，在震荡市中有统计学优势
  • 布林带和RSI双重过滤降低了虚假信号的数量
  • 固定止损为每笔交易提供了明确的风险边界
  • 布林带带宽过滤确保只在波动率足够时交易，避免窄幅震荡中的频繁信号

劣势

  • 逆势交易在强趋势行情中容易产生连续亏损
  • 固定手数无法根据账户权益和市场波动率动态调整仓位
  • 缺乏账户级风控机制如最大回撤限制或连亏熔断
  • 止盈目标设为布林带中轨，在部分回归行情中可能过早平仓
  • 未包含点差过滤，在高点差环境下逆势入场成本较高

改进建议

  • 增加趋势方向过滤器（如大周期均线），在趋势方向上禁止逆势开仓以减少趋势行情中的连亏
  • 引入动态仓位管理，基于ATR或账户权益百分比计算手数，使风险随市场波动率自适应调整

Strengths

  • Clear mean reversion logic with statistical edge in ranging markets
  • Dual Bollinger Band and RSI filter reduces false signal frequency
  • Fixed stop-loss provides a defined risk boundary per trade
  • Bollinger Band width filter ensures trading only when volatility is sufficient, avoiding whipsaws in narrow ranges

Weaknesses

  • Counter-trend trading prone to consecutive losses in strong trending markets
  • Fixed lot sizing cannot dynamically adjust based on account equity or market volatility
  • Lacks account-level risk controls such as max drawdown limits or consecutive loss circuit breakers
  • Take-profit at Bollinger middle band may exit prematurely in partial reversion scenarios
  • No spread filter included, making contrarian entries costly in high-spread environments

Improvements

  • Add a trend direction filter (e.g., higher timeframe moving average) to prohibit counter-trend entries along the trend direction, reducing consecutive losses during trending markets
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on ATR or account equity percentage to make risk self-adjusting with market volatility

  • 固定手数在高波动时风险敞口偏大，低波动时偏小
  • 缺乏账户级风控如最大持仓数、最大回撤止损

  • Fixed lot sizing creates oversized risk in high volatility and undersized exposure in low volatility
  • No account-level risk controls such as max open positions or max drawdown stop
