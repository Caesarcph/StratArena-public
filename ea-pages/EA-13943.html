Exp_TEMA_CUSTOM

This EA trades based on a Triple Exponential Moving Average (TEMA) custom indicator with configurable smoothing methods for each of the three MA layers. It opens buy positions when the TEMA value is rising (Value[0]>Value[1] with Value[1]<Value[2]) and sell positions when falling, closing opposite positions on signal reversal.

摘要

该EA基于自定义三重指数移动平均线(TEMA)指标进行交易，支持为三个MA层分别配置平滑方法。当TEMA值上升时开多仓并平空仓，当TEMA值下降时开空仓并平多仓，属于典型的趋势跟踪策略。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于自定义TEMA指标的趋势跟踪，TEMA通过对三层数据分别应用不同的平滑算法来减少滞后性，从而更灵敏地捕捉趋势变化。入场条件为：当指标值从下降转为上升（Value[1]<Value[2]且Value[0]>Value[1]）时开多仓，当指标值从上升转为下降（Value[1]>Value[2]且Value[0]<Value[1]）时开空仓。出场条件包括止损和止盈的硬性设置，以及反向信号触发时平掉对应仓位。仓位管理方面，EA支持多种资金管理模式，包括按自由保证金比例、按余额比例、按亏损后自由保证金比例、按亏损后余额比例以及固定手数。EA使用了IsNewBar机制确保每个K线只计算一次信号，避免逐tick重复交易，同时通过SignalBar参数允许用户选择使用哪根K线的信号。风险控制依赖于固定的止损止盈点数，但没有考虑滑点过滤、点差过滤和持仓数量限制等额外风控措施。

Summary

This EA trades based on a Triple Exponential Moving Average (TEMA) custom indicator with configurable smoothing methods for each of the three MA layers. It opens buy positions when the TEMA value is rising (Value[0]>Value[1] with Value[1]<Value[2]) and sell positions when falling, closing opposite positions on signal reversal.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy is trend following based on a custom TEMA indicator, which applies three separate smoothing algorithms to reduce lag and more sensitively capture trend changes. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the indicator transitions from declining to rising (Value[1]<Value[2] and Value[0]>Value[1]), and a sell signal when it transitions from rising to declining (Value[1]>Value[2] and Value[0]<Value[1]). Exit conditions include hard stop-loss and take-profit levels in points, as well as closing opposite positions when a reversal signal is triggered. Position sizing supports multiple money management modes: percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin after losses, percentage of balance after losses, and fixed lot size. The EA employs an IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are calculated only once per bar, preventing repeated trades on every tick, and the SignalBar parameter allows users to choose which bar's signal to use. Risk control relies on fixed stop-loss and take-profit points, but lacks additional safeguards such as spread filtering, slippage protection beyond Deviation_, and maximum position count limits.

优势

  • 支持多种平滑算法（SMA/EMA/SMMA/LWMA/JJMA/JurX/ParMA/T3/VIDYA/AMA），灵活性强
  • 使用IsNewBar机制避免逐tick重复信号，减少不必要的交易操作
  • 止损止盈比例1:2，具备合理的风险回报比设计
  • 资金管理模式多样，适应不同风险偏好
  • 买入和卖出方向可独立开关，便于单向交易或测试

劣势

  • 缺少点差过滤，在点差较大时可能导致不必要的亏损交易
  • 没有最大持仓数量限制，可能在震荡行情中频繁开仓平仓造成损失
  • 依赖外部自定义指标TEMA_CUSTOM，若指标文件缺失则EA无法运行
  • 没有移动止盈/追踪止损功能，趋势行情中可能无法最大化利润
  • 信号逻辑较为简单（仅三根K线比较），在震荡市中容易产生虚假信号

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和时间过滤机制，避免在点差过大或流动性低的时段交易
  • 加入移动止盈和追踪止损功能，以在趋势行情中锁定更多利润
  • 增加震荡行情识别过滤（如ADX指标），在无明显趋势时暂停交易
  • 增加最大同时持仓数量限制，防止震荡市中过度交易

Strengths

  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithms (SMA/EMA/SMMA/LWMA/JJMA/JurX/ParMA/T3/VIDYA/AMA) for high flexibility
  • Uses IsNewBar mechanism to avoid repeated tick-level signals, reducing unnecessary trading operations
  • Stop-loss to take-profit ratio of 1:2, providing a reasonable risk-reward design
  • Multiple money management modes accommodate different risk preferences
  • Buy and sell directions can be independently toggled, convenient for unidirectional trading or testing

Weaknesses

  • Lacks spread filtering, which may lead to unnecessary losing trades during high-spread conditions
  • No maximum position count limit, potentially causing frequent open/close cycles in choppy markets
  • Depends on external custom indicator TEMA_CUSTOM; EA fails if the indicator file is missing
  • No trailing stop functionality, potentially failing to maximize profits in trending markets
  • Signal logic is simplistic (only three-bar comparison), prone to false signals in ranging markets

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and time filter to avoid trading during high-spread or low-liquidity sessions
  • Implement trailing stop and trailing take-profit to lock in more profit during trends
  • Add ranging market detection filter (e.g., ADX indicator) to pause trading when no clear trend exists
  • Add maximum simultaneous position limit to prevent over-trading in choppy markets
