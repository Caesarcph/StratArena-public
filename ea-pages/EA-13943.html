摘要

该EA基于TEMA_CUSTOM（三重指数自定义平滑移动平均线）指标进行交易。当TEMA值拐头向上时开多仓，拐头向下时开空仓，通过连续K线值的三重平滑移动平均线交叉来检测趋势方向变化。

策略分析

策略概念基于三重自定义平滑移动平均线(TEMA_CUSTOM)的趋势跟踪系统。该指标使用三层嵌套平滑算法，第一层平滑用于初始价格序列，第二层平滑用于第一层结果，第三层平滑用于第二层结果，从而产生低延迟的趋势信号。入场条件为：当Value[1]<Value[2]（即前期指标值下降趋势中）且Value[0]>Value[1]（当前值开始上升）时，产生买入信号；当Value[1]>Value[2]且Value[0]<Value[1]时，产生卖出信号，这本质上是一个趋势拐点检测逻辑。出场条件方面，买入信号的拐头同时触发卖出平仓，卖出信号的拐头同时触发买入平仓，形成完全对称的反转式出场机制。仓位管理支持多种模式，包括固定手数、基于余额百分比、基于可用保证金百分比以及基于净额的保证金模式。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例(默认0.1)，并结合止损1000点和止盈2000点的固定点位设置来控制单笔风险。

Summary

This EA trades based on the TEMA_CUSTOM (Triple Exponential Moving Average with custom smoothing) indicator. It opens buy positions when the TEMA value turns upward and sell positions when it turns downward, using a triple-smoothed moving average crossover of consecutive bar values to detect trend direction changes.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a trend-following system using the Triple Exponential Custom Smoothing Moving Average (TEMA_CUSTOM) indicator. This indicator employs a triple-nested smoothing algorithm where the first layer smooths the initial price series, the second layer smooths the first layer result, and the third layer smooths the second layer result, producing low-lag trend signals. Entry conditions: when Value[1]<Value[2] (prior indicator values in downtrend) and Value[0]>Value[1] (current value starts rising), a buy signal is generated; when Value[1]>Value[2] and Value[0]<Value[1], a sell signal is generated, which is essentially a trend reversal detection logic. For exit conditions, the buy signal turning point simultaneously triggers sell position closing, and the sell signal turning point simultaneously triggers buy position closing, forming a fully symmetric reversal-style exit mechanism. Position sizing supports multiple modes including fixed lot, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin, and net margin-based modes. Money management uses the MM parameter to control risk ratio (default 0.1) combined with a fixed 1000-point stop loss and 2000-point take profit to control per-trade risk.

优势

  • 三层嵌套平滑算法大幅减少滞后性，信号响应速度快
  • 支持多达9种平滑算法（SMA/EMA/SMMA/LWMA/JJMA/JurX/ParMA/T3/VIDYA/AMA），灵活度高
  • 模块化设计良好，交易逻辑与指标逻辑分离，代码结构清晰
  • 提供多种仓位管理模式和买卖开关，适应不同交易需求
  • 使用SignalBar=1基于已完成K线信号，避免重绘问题

劣势

  • 止损止盈使用固定点数，未考虑市场波动率（ATR），在不同品种上表现差异大
  • 缺乏点差过滤机制，在高位差品种上可能造成不必要的入场成本
  • 反转式出场完全依赖信号拐头，没有独立的移动止损或保本机制，盈利回撤风险大
  • 1000点止损配合2000点止盈的1:2盈亏比看似合理，但固定值不适应不同市场环境
  • 代码中注释为乱码（编码问题），可读性和可维护性差

改进建议

  • 将固定止损止盈改为基于ATR的动态止损止盈，以适应不同品种的波动率特征
  • 增加移动止损（Trailing Stop）和保本机制，在趋势延续时锁定利润
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免高成本和低流动性时段入场

Strengths

  • Triple-nested smoothing algorithm significantly reduces lag for faster signal response
  • Supports up to 9 smoothing methods (SMA/EMA/SMMA/LWMA/JJMA/JurX/ParMA/T3/VIDYA/AMA) offering high flexibility
  • Well-modularized design with clear separation between trading logic and indicator logic
  • Multiple position sizing modes and buy/sell toggle switches accommodate diverse trading needs
  • Uses SignalBar=1 based on completed bar signals, avoiding repainting issues

Weaknesses

  • Fixed stop loss and take profit in points without ATR-based volatility adaptation performs inconsistently across instruments
  • No spread filter mechanism, potentially causing unnecessary entry costs on high-spread instruments
  • Reversal-style exit relies entirely on signal turning points with no independent trailing stop or breakeven mechanism, risking profit drawdowns
  • The 1000-point SL with 2000-point TP appears as 1:2 risk-reward but fixed values cannot adapt to different market conditions
  • Source code comments are garbled due to encoding issues, severely hurting readability and maintainability

Improvements

  • Replace fixed stop loss/take profit with ATR-based dynamic levels to adapt to different instruments' volatility characteristics
  • Add trailing stop and breakeven mechanisms to lock in profits during trend continuation
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid high-cost and low-liquidity entries
