This EA implements a simple momentum strategy by comparing the close price of a user-specified current bar against a user-specified comparable bar. When price rises over the lookback period it opens a buy; when price falls it opens a sell, always closing the opposite position first. It includes configurable stop loss, take profit, trailing stop with step, and margin checks before order placement.

摘要

该EA实现了简单的动量策略，通过比较用户指定的当前K线收盘价与可比较K线收盘价来判断方向。价格上涨时开多单并平空单，价格下跌时开空单并平多单。EA包含可配置的止损、止盈、追踪止损及保证金检查功能。

策略分析

策略概念基于纯动量比较：如果当前K线收盘价高于N根K线前的收盘价则判定为上涨动量开多，反之判定为下跌动量开空。入场条件为close_array[InpCurrentBar] > close_array[InpComparableBar]时做多，close_array[InpCurrentBar] < close_array[InpComparableBar]时做空，仅在新K线诞生时执行交易逻辑。出场条件包括固定止损止盈（默认各50点）以及追踪止损（默认5点最小距离加5点步长），开反向仓前会先平掉同品种同魔数的反向持仓。仓位管理采用固定手数模式（默认1手），通过CheckVolumeValue函数验证手数是否符合品种最小/最大手数及步长要求。资金管理方面在开仓前调用FreeMarginCheck检查可用保证金是否充足，不足则拒绝开仓。整体策略逻辑非常简单直接，缺乏趋势过滤器、波动率过滤和多时间框架确认。

Summary

This EA implements a simple momentum strategy by comparing the close price of a user-specified current bar against a user-specified comparable bar. When price rises over the lookback period it opens a buy; when price falls it opens a sell, always closing the opposite position first. It includes configurable stop loss, take profit, trailing stop with step, and margin checks before order placement.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on pure price momentum comparison: if the close price of a user-designated current bar is higher than the close price of a comparable bar N periods ago, the EA detects upward momentum and opens a buy; if lower, it detects downward momentum and opens a sell. Entry conditions are straightforward — close_array[InpCurrentBar] > close_array[InpComparableBar] triggers a long entry, and the reverse triggers a short entry, with trading logic executed only on new bar birth. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit (both defaulting to 50 pips), plus a trailing stop mechanism with configurable minimum distance (5 pips) and step (5 pips); opposite positions are closed before opening a new directional position. Position sizing uses a fixed lot mode (default 1.0 lot) with validation through CheckVolumeValue to ensure compliance with symbol minimum/maximum lot size and lot step requirements. Money management incorporates a FreeMarginCheck call before order placement to verify sufficient available margin, rejecting orders if margin is insufficient. The overall strategy is very simple and direct, lacking trend filters, volatility filters, multi-timeframe confirmation, or any advanced momentum indicators like RSI or MACD.

优势

  • 代码结构清晰，使用MQL5标准库（CTrade、CSymbolInfo等）规范开发，具有良好的代码质量
  • 包含保证金检查、手数合法性验证和止损止盈保护，具备基础风险管理框架
  • 追踪止损功能可帮助锁定浮动利润，防止盈利回吐
  • 仅在新K线诞生时执行交易逻辑，避免tick级别过度交易
  • 支持3/5位小数点品种自动适配，兼容性良好

劣势

  • 策略逻辑过于简单，仅比较两个K线收盘价缺少过滤条件，在震荡行情中会产生大量虚假信号和频繁交易
  • 固定手数缺乏动态仓位管理，无法根据账户规模或市场波动率调整风险敞口
  • 止损止盈默认仅50点且比例1:1，风险回报比不佳，追踪止损5点过小容易被市场噪音触发
  • 缺少点差过滤、波动率过滤和多时间框架确认，在高点差或低流动性环境下容易亏损
  • 每次信号都反向平仓再开仓，在频繁震荡时会产生大量手续费和滑点损失

改进建议

  • 增加动量确认指标（如RSI、MACD或ADX）作为过滤条件，减少震荡行情中的虚假信号
  • 实现动态仓位管理（如基于ATR的风险百分比计算手数），并根据波动率调整止损止盈距离

Strengths

  • Clean code structure using MQL5 standard library (CTrade, CSymbolInfo, etc.) following proper development conventions with good code quality
  • Includes margin check, lot size validation, and stop loss/take profit protection providing a basic risk management framework
  • Trailing stop functionality helps lock in floating profits and prevents profit giveback
  • Executes trading logic only on new bar birth, avoiding excessive tick-level trading
  • Automatic adaptation for 3/5 digit brokers ensuring good compatibility

Weaknesses

  • Strategy logic is overly simplistic, comparing only two bar close prices without filters, generating numerous false signals and frequent trades in ranging markets
  • Fixed lot sizing lacks dynamic position management, unable to adjust risk exposure based on account size or market volatility
  • Default stop loss and take profit of only 50 pips with 1:1 ratio provides poor risk-reward, and 5-pip trailing stop is too tight and easily triggered by market noise
  • Lacks spread filter, volatility filter, and multi-timeframe confirmation, prone to losses in high-spread or low-liquidity environments
  • Closes opposite positions and re-opens on every signal, incurring significant commission and slippage costs during frequent whipsaws

Improvements

  • Add momentum confirmation indicators (such as RSI, MACD, or ADX) as filters to reduce false signals in ranging markets
  • Implement dynamic position sizing (e.g., ATR-based risk percentage lot calculation) and adjust stop loss/take profit distances based on volatility
