摘要

一个基于MACD指标线的简单专家顾问，通过MACD线与信号线的交叉产生交易信号。当MACD上穿信号线时做多，下穿信号线时做空，支持自定义手数、止损和止盈参数。该EA设计为基础动量跟踪系统，适用于趋势市场。

策略分析

该EA采用经典的MACD交叉策略，属于动量跟踪类交易系统。入场条件为MACD主线与信号线发生交叉：上穿做多，下穿做空，这是最基础的MACD用法。出场条件依赖预设的止损和止盈水平，没有使用移动止损或信号反转出场机制。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，没有根据账户资金或波动率进行动态调整。资金管理方面缺乏风险评估，没有检查账户余额是否足以支撑当前仓位。策略整体逻辑简单直接，但在震荡市中容易频繁产生虚假信号导致连续亏损。

Summary

A straightforward MACD-based Expert Advisor that generates trade signals from MACD line and signal line crossovers. It enters long when MACD crosses above its signal line and enters short when MACD crosses below its signal line, with configurable lot sizing and stop loss/take profit levels. The EA is designed as a basic momentum-following system suitable for trending markets.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs the classic MACD crossover strategy, belonging to the momentum-following category of trading systems. Entry conditions rely on MACD main line crossing the signal line: going long on upward crossover and short on downward crossover, which is the most fundamental MACD application. Exit conditions depend on pre-set stop loss and take profit levels, with no trailing stop or signal-reversal exit mechanism. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic adjustment based on account equity or market volatility. Money management lacks risk assessment, as there is no check whether the account balance is sufficient to support the current position size. The overall strategy logic is simple and straightforward, but it is prone to frequent false signals in ranging markets leading to consecutive losses.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和维护
  • 使用经典MACD指标，信号具有统计学依据
  • 参数可配置，便于回测和优化

劣势

  • MACD交叉在震荡市中产生大量虚假信号
  • 固定手数缺乏动态资金管理，无法适应不同账户规模
  • 没有新K线检查机制，可能同一根K线内重复开仓

改进建议

  • 增加新K线检查，避免同一根K线内重复交易
  • 加入市场趋势过滤器（如ADX或大周期EMA），仅在趋势行情中交易

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and maintain
  • Uses the classic MACD indicator with statistical basis for signals
  • Configurable parameters allow for backtesting and optimization

Weaknesses

  • MACD crossovers generate numerous false signals in ranging markets
  • Fixed lot sizing lacks dynamic money management, cannot adapt to different account sizes
  • No new bar check mechanism, potentially opening duplicate positions within the same bar

Improvements

  • Add new bar check to prevent duplicate trading within the same bar
  • Add a trend filter (e.g., ADX or higher timeframe EMA) to only trade in trending conditions
