摘要

该EA基于自定义2MoHLC指标进行交易，该指标通过两个OHLC均线形成云带通道。支持两种模式：云带变色（趋势反转）和云带突破（价格突破通道边界），并提供可配置的买卖权限和平仓规则。

策略分析

该EA的策略核心是利用2MoHLC指标生成的上下轨云带来判断市场方向。在CloudColor模式下，当上轨从下穿下轨变为上穿下轨时产生买入信号，反之产生卖出信号，本质是均线交叉的趋势跟踪策略。在Breakdown模式下，当收盘价突破云带上轨时买入，跌破下轨时卖出，属于经典的通道突破策略。入场信号在SignalBar指定的K线上检测，且仅在新K线出现时重新计算，避免重复交易。出场方面，EA在产生反向信号时平仓，同时设有固定点数的止损和止盈。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示固定手数，正值表示账户资金比例。策略使用了静态变量保存信号状态，并在数据拷贝失败时设置Recount标志重试，增加了鲁棒性。

Summary

This EA trades based on the custom 2MoHLC indicator, which creates a cloud channel from two moving averages of OHLC data. It supports two modes: cloud color change (trend reversal) and cloud breakout (price breaking through the channel boundaries), with configurable buy/sell permissions and position closing rules.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy uses the 2MoHLC indicator which generates upper and lower cloud bands from two smoothed moving averages of OHLC data. In CloudColor mode, a buy signal is generated when the upper band crosses above the lower band (and vice versa for sell), essentially a moving average crossover trend-following approach. In Breakdown mode, a buy signal fires when the close price breaks above the cloud's upper boundary and a sell signal when it breaks below the lower boundary, which is a classic channel breakout strategy. Entry signals are evaluated at the bar specified by SignalBar and only recalculated on new bars to prevent duplicate trades. For exits, the EA closes positions on opposing signals and uses fixed stop loss and take profit in points. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the account deposit. The strategy uses static variables to preserve signal states and sets a Recount flag on data copy failures for retry logic, adding robustness.

优势

  • 双模式设计灵活，可选择趋势跟踪或突破策略
  • 新K线检测机制避免同一K线重复交易
  • 买卖权限和平仓权限可独立控制，适应不同市场环境
  • 数据拷贝失败时自动重试，提高稳定性

劣势

  • 缺少点差过滤，突破模式下可能因高点差产生虚假信号
  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未考虑ATR等动态波动指标
  • 没有持仓检查和最大持仓限制，可能在同一方向重复开仓
  • 依赖外部TradeAlgorithms.mqh库，核心交易逻辑不可见

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和波动率自适应止损止盈
  • 添加最大同向持仓数限制和持仓状态检查
  • 加入回撤控制和资金管理最大风险限制

Strengths

  • Dual-mode design offers flexibility between trend-following and breakout strategies
  • New bar detection prevents duplicate trades on the same bar
  • Independent buy/sell and close permissions allow adaptation to different market conditions
  • Automatic recount on data copy failure improves reliability

Weaknesses

  • No spread filter, breakout mode may generate false signals in high-spread conditions
  • Stop loss and take profit use fixed points without dynamic ATR-based adjustment
  • No position check or maximum position limit, potentially opening repeated trades in the same direction
  • Depends on external TradeAlgorithms.mqh library with invisible core trading logic

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and ATR-based adaptive stop loss and take profit
  • Add maximum same-direction position limit and position state checking
  • Implement drawdown control and maximum risk limits for money management
