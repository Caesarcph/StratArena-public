摘要

Sidus是一个基于Sidus交易系统的趋势跟踪EA，利用均线交叉和RSI过滤相结合来判断趋势方向和入场时机。当快速均线上穿慢速均线且RSI确认时产生买入信号，反之产生卖出信号。该EA旨在通过简单直接的信号策略捕捉中期趋势行情。

策略分析

Sidus EA的核心策略是基于均线交叉的趋势跟踪系统，通常使用两条不同周期的移动平均线（如快速EMA和慢速EMA）的交叉来判断趋势方向。入场条件为快速均线上穿慢速均线且RSI位于特定区间确认多头趋势时做多，快速均线下穿慢速均线且RSI确认空头趋势时做空。出场条件通常依赖反向交叉信号或固定的止损止盈设置。仓位管理方面，EA使用固定手数或基于资金的简单仓位计算方式，没有复杂的仓位缩放机制。资金管理相对简单，缺乏动态风险调整或基于波动率的仓位控制，整体风控依赖于预设的止损止盈参数。

Summary

Sidus is a trend-following EA based on the Sidus trading system, which uses a combination of moving average crossovers and RSI filtering to identify trend direction and entry points. It generates buy signals when fast moving averages cross above slow ones with RSI confirmation, and sell signals on the reverse. The EA aims to capture medium-term trend movements with a straightforward signal-based approach.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of the Sidus EA is a trend-following system based on moving average crossovers, typically employing two moving averages of different periods (fast and slow EMA) whose crossovers determine trend direction. Entry conditions require the fast MA crossing above the slow MA with RSI confirmation in a specific zone for buy signals, and the reverse for sell signals. Exit conditions generally rely on opposing crossover signals or fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels. Position sizing uses fixed lot sizes or a simple account-balance-based calculation, without sophisticated position scaling mechanisms. Money management is relatively basic, lacking dynamic risk adjustment or volatility-based position control, with overall risk management depending on user-configured stop-loss and take-profit parameters.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和调参，适合初学者使用
  • 结合均线交叉与RSI双重过滤，减少假信号干扰
  • 趋势跟踪策略在强趋势市场中表现良好，能捕捉较大波段利润

劣势

  • 在震荡盘整市场中容易产生频繁的虚假信号导致连续亏损
  • 缺乏动态止损机制如追踪止损，可能在趋势反转时回吐大量利润
  • 资金管理过于简单，固定手数不考虑账户波动率和风险承受能力

改进建议

  • 增加市场状态识别机制（如ADX过滤），在震荡市中减少交易频率
  • 引入追踪止损和动态仓位管理，根据ATR调整止损距离和下单手数

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and optimize, suitable for beginners
  • Dual filtering with MA crossover and RSI reduces false signal interference
  • Trend-following approach performs well in strong trending markets, capturing significant swing profits

Weaknesses

  • Prone to frequent false signals and consecutive losses in ranging or choppy markets
  • Lacks dynamic exit mechanisms like trailing stops, potentially giving back significant profits on trend reversals
  • Overly simplistic money management with fixed lot sizes ignores account volatility and risk capacity

Improvements

  • Add market regime detection (e.g., ADX filter) to reduce trading frequency in ranging markets
  • Implement trailing stops and dynamic position sizing based on ATR for adaptive risk management
