Strategy analysis

The strategy core is a dual trend-following approach combining the WAMI indicator with a cloud-based filtering system. The WAMI indicator measures market movement strength and direction through weighted accumulation calculations, generating long signals when the indicator crosses from negative to positive and short signals when crossing from positive to negative. The cloud system serves as a trend filter, accepting long signals only when price is above the cloud and short signals only when price is below the cloud, which effectively reduces false signals during ranging markets. Exit conditions likely include WAMI reverse crossovers, price breaching cloud boundaries, or fixed take-profit and stop-loss targets. Position sizing may use fixed lots or account-balance proportional calculations. For money management, the X2 version likely incorporates trailing stops to lock in profits while setting maximum position limits to prevent excessive directional risk exposure.

Strengths

  • Dual trend filtering system (WAMI + cloud) effectively reduces false signals and improves entry quality
  • Cloud filter identifies market structure, avoiding frequent trades in ranging zones
  • X2 version as an improved iteration likely has optimizations in signal stability and risk control
  • Systematic trading logic is clear with well-defined rules, facilitating backtesting and optimization
  • WAMI indicator's weighted accumulation characteristic is relatively sensitive in capturing market movement direction

Weaknesses

  • Trend-following strategies tend to produce consecutive losses in sideways ranging markets, lacking market state identification mechanism
  • While dual filtering reduces false signals, it can also cause entry delays, missing early trend profits
  • If stop-loss is improperly set or zero, a single trade maysustain significant losses
  • Lacks volatility adaptation mechanism, using the same parameters in high and low volatility environments may be suboptimal
  • WAMI indicator may lag in fast reversal markets, causing delayed exits

Possible improvements

  • Add market state identification module (e.g., ADX or volatility filter) to pause trading or switch strategy during sideways markets
  • Introduce dynamic stop-loss mechanism using ATR-adaptive stop distances to suit different volatility environments
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation, only opening positions when the higher timeframe trend direction aligns

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Trend-following strategy may produce consecutive small losses in ranging markets, accumulating significant drawdown
  • If default stop-loss is zero and trailing stop is not enabled, single trade risk exposure is uncontrolled
  • Lacks money management safety nets such as maximum drawdown control and daily loss limits
