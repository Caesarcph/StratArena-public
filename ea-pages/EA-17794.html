NUp1Down

This EA trades based on a consecutive candle counting pattern — it enters a position when N consecutive up candles are followed by a down candle (or vice versa), anticipating either a reversal or continuation. It is a systematic candlestick pattern strategy that opens trades on signal detection without manual intervention.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on a consecutive candle counting pattern: when N consecutive directional candles (e.g., N up candles) appear followed by 1 opposite candle, it is interpreted as a trend exhaustion signal and a reversal trade is entered. Entry conditions: after N consecutive bullish candles followed by a bearish candle, a sell (or buy reversal) is placed; after N consecutive bearish candles followed by a bullish candle, a buy (or sell reversal) is placed, with N being a configurable parameter. Exit conditions rely on fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, with some versions potentially including a trailing stop. Position sizing typically uses fixed lots or a percentage-based calculation relative to account equity. Money management is generally simple with a fixed risk ratio, lacking dynamic adjustment mechanisms. The overall strategy logic is simple and intuitive, representing a typical candlestick pattern reversal trading system, but it may suffer frequent counter-trend losses in strong trending markets.

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is simple and intuitive, easy to understand and verify
  • Configurable parameters allow N-value optimization for different markets and timeframes
  • Candlestick pattern reversal approach performs well in ranging markets

Weaknesses

  • Frequent counter-trend trades in strong trending markets lead to consecutive losses
  • Lacks trend filter to distinguish between ranging and trending markets
  • Position sizing is overly simplistic without dynamic risk adjustment mechanism

Possible improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., MA or ADX) to pause reversal trades or switch to trend-following in trending markets
  • Implement a dynamic money management module that adjusts position size based on consecutive losses
  • Add time and volatility filters to avoid trading during low-liquidity or high-volatility sessions

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Lack of trend filter leads to counter-trend losses in strong trends
  • Fixed stop-loss may not adapt to current market volatility — too wide in low volatility, too narrow in high volatility
