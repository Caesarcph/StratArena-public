Exp_FigurelliSeries

This EA trades based on the Figurelli Series indicator, which uses a basket of Moving Averages with varying periods to determine trend direction. It opens positions at a specified start time and closes them either by opposing signals or at a specified stop time. Position sizing supports multiple margin modes including fixed lot, free margin, and balance-based calculations.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on the Figurelli Series indicator, which calculates multiple Moving Averages with varying periods (starting from iStartPeriod, incrementing by iStep, totaling iTotal MAs) to synthesize trend direction. Entry conditions: when the indicator value is greater than 0, a buy signal is generated; when less than 0, a sell signal is generated, but actual trade execution only occurs at the user-specified opening time (StartHour:StartMinute). Exit conditions include two mechanisms: closing on opposing indicator signals, and forced closing of all positions at the specified stop time (StopHour:StopMinute). Position sizing uses the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library functions, supporting both fixed lot sizes and dynamic lot calculation based on margin ratios. Money management is controlled via the MM parameter which sets the deposit share per deal, with multiple margin calculation modes available (FREEMARGIN, BALANCE, LOSSFREEMARGIN, LOSSBALANCE, LOT). The EA also implements stop loss and take profit parameters in points, along with a maximum price deviation setting to control slippage risk.

Strengths

  • Uses a basket of multiple Moving Averages for trend determination, producing relatively robust signals that mitigate single MA lag effects
  • Provides complete time window control to restrict trading to specified sessions, avoiding low-liquidity periods
  • Supports multiple money management modes with stop loss and take profit settings, offering fairly comprehensive risk controls

Weaknesses

  • Indicator signals are only executed at the specified opening time, potentially missing optimal entry points and limiting strategy timeliness
  • The time-based closing logic has an operator precedence issue (tm.hour==StopHour && tm.min>=StopMinute || tm.hour>StopHour) that could cause unintended behavior
  • Depends on a custom iCustom indicator; if the FigurelliSeries indicator is not properly installed, the EA cannot run, increasing deployment complexity

Possible improvements

  • Fix the operator precedence issue in the time judgment logic by adding parentheses to ensure correct condition grouping
  • Add spread filtering and minimum profit point checks to avoid entering trades when transaction costs are too high

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Operator precedence issue in time-based closing logic could cause positions to be closed at unintended times
  • Lack of spread filtering may result in unnecessary losing trades in high-spread environments
  • Stop loss of 1000 points is relatively large and could cause significant single-trade losses in extreme market conditions
