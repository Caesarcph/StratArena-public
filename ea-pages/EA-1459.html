摘要

该EA基于自定义MBKAsctrend3指标的交易信号进行操作，该指标融合了三个不同周期的加权威廉百分比范围（WPR）振荡器来检测动量变化。它在向上动量信号时开多仓，在向下动量信号时开空仓，支持可配置的止损、止盈以及基于资金份额或固定手数的仓位管理。

策略分析

策略核心是利用三个不同周期（9、33、77）的WPR指标的加权组合来判断市场动量方向。入场条件为：当MBKAsctrend3指标的上行信号线在指定K线位置输出非零且非EMPTY_VALUE时，产生买入信号；当下行信号线输出有效值时，产生卖出信号。出场条件分为两层：第一层是反向信号触发平仓（买入信号平空仓，卖出信号平多仓）；第二层是当没有反向信号时，通过回溯历史K线查找最近的反向信号来确定平仓方向。仓位管理采用资金百分比模式或固定手数模式，由MM参数控制，负值为固定手数，正值为存款比例。资金管理通过止损止盈参数控制单笔风险，但缺乏整体账户风险控制机制。策略还使用了新K线检测机制避免同根K线重复交易。

Summary

This EA trades based on signals from the custom MBKAsctrend3 indicator, which combines three weighted Williams Percent Range (WPR) oscillators across different periods to detect momentum shifts. It opens buy positions on upward momentum signals and sell positions on downward momentum signals, with configurable stop loss, take profit, and position sizing based on deposit share or fixed lot size.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core utilizes a weighted combination of three WPR indicators with different periods (9, 33, 77) to determine market momentum direction. Entry conditions: when the MBKAsctrend3 indicator's up-signal buffer outputs a non-zero, non-EMPTY_VALUE at the specified signal bar, a buy signal is generated; when the down-signal buffer outputs a valid value, a sell signal is generated. Exit conditions operate on two levels: first, opposite signals trigger position closure (buy signal closes shorts, sell signal closes longs); second, when no opposite signal appears on the current bar, the EA backscans historical bars to find the most recent opposite signal to determine the closing direction. Position sizing uses either a deposit percentage mode or a fixed lot mode, controlled by the MM parameter where negative values represent fixed lots and positive values represent deposit share. Money management controls per-trade risk through stop loss and take profit parameters, but lacks overall account-level risk controls. The strategy also employs a new bar detection mechanism to prevent repeated trading on the same bar.

优势

  • 三周期WPR加权组合提供多层次的动量分析，减少了单一指标的误判
  • 新K线检测机制避免同一根K线内重复交易，降低过度交易风险
  • 买卖开仓和平仓权限独立控制，灵活性高，可单独测试多头或空头策略
  • 支持资金比例和固定手数两种仓位管理模式，适应不同交易需求

劣势

  • 回溯历史K线查找平仓信号的循环没有上限限制，可能在深度回撤时导致性能问题
  • 缺少点差过滤和最大持仓检查，在流动性差的市场可能产生不利成交
  • WPR指标在震荡市中容易产生频繁的虚假信号，导致连续亏损
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点的默认值过大，不适合所有品种和市场条件

改进建议

  • 为历史K线回溯循环添加最大搜索范围限制，防止性能瓶颈
  • 增加点差过滤和最大持仓数量限制，提升风险控制能力

Strengths

  • The triple-period WPR weighted combination provides multi-layered momentum analysis, reducing false signals from a single indicator
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated trading within the same bar, reducing overtrading risk
  • Independent control of buy/sell open and close permissions provides high flexibility for testing long-only or short-only strategies
  • Supports both deposit-percentage and fixed-lot position sizing modes, adapting to different trading needs

Weaknesses

  • The historical bar backscan loop for finding closure signals has no upper limit, potentially causing performance issues during deep drawdowns
  • Lacks spread filter and maximum position checks, which may lead to unfavorable fills in low-liquidity markets
  • WPR indicators tend to generate frequent false signals in ranging markets, leading to consecutive losses
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large and unsuitable for all instruments and market conditions

Improvements

  • Add a maximum search range limit to the historical bar backscan loop to prevent performance bottlenecks
  • Add spread filtering and maximum position count limits to enhance risk control capabilities
