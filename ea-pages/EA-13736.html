摘要

该EA基于i-KlPrice自定义指标进行交易，该指标对KlPrice偏差振荡器进行双重平滑处理（SMA+JJMA），并设有超买/超卖水平。当指标从下水平上穿时开多仓，从上水平下穿时开空仓，并配有可配置的平仓逻辑。

策略分析

策略概念基于KlPrice偏差振荡器的双重平滑信号，通过第一层SMA平滑和第二层JJMA平滑来减少噪音产生交易信号。入场条件：当指标值从0上穿（Value[1]==0且Value[0]>0）时开空单，当指标值从4下穿（Value[1]==4且Value[0]<4）时开多单，这是典型的振荡器极值反转+趋势跟踪混合策略。出场条件：当指标值低于2时平多单，当指标值高于2时平空单，采用部分水平交叉的渐进出场方式。仓位管理支持五种模式：自由保证金比例、余额比例、扣除亏损后自由保证金比例、扣除亏损后余额比例以及固定手数模式。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1表示使用10%的可用资金，同时设有1000点止损和2000点止盈，风险收益比为1:2。

Summary

This EA trades based on the i-KlPrice custom indicator, which applies double smoothing (SMA + JJMA) to a KlPrice deviation-based oscillator with overbought/oversold levels. It opens buy positions when the indicator signals an upward crossover from the lower level and sell positions on a downward crossover from the upper level, with configurable position closing logic.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a double-smoothed KlPrice deviation oscillator signal, using a first-layer SMA smoothing and a second-layer JJMA smoothing to reduce noise and generate trading signals. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when the indicator value crosses down from level 4 (Value[1]==4 and Value[0]<4), and a sell signal triggers when the value crosses up from level 0 (Value[1]==0 and Value[0]>0), which is a hybrid oscillator-extreme-reversal and trend-following approach. Exit conditions: long positions close when the indicator value drops below 2, and short positions close when it rises above 2, using a gradual exit approach based on partial level crossovers. Position sizing supports five modes: free margin percentage, balance percentage, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled via the MM parameter defaulting to 0.1 (10% of available capital), with a 1000-point stop loss and 2000-point take profit, yielding a 1:2 risk-reward ratio.

优势

  • 双重平滑（SMA+JJMA）有效减少信号噪音，提高信号质量
  • 支持五种仓位管理模式，灵活适配不同风险偏好
  • 设有独立的买卖开关，可灵活控制单边交易方向
  • 止损止盈比例1:2，具有正期望值的风险管理
  • 新K线检测机制避免同一根K线重复开仓

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标i-KlPrice，指标逻辑不透明且难以回测验证
  • 指标值为整数级别（0/2/4）的离散判断，信号可能过于稀疏或不够精确
  • 缺少点差过滤，在点差较大时可能产生虚假信号
  • 止损1000点较大，单笔亏损可能过高
  • 未检查是否已有同方向持仓，可能重复开仓

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤机制，避免高点差环境下开仓
  • 添加同方向持仓检查，防止重复开仓导致过度敞口
  • 增加移动止损或追踪止盈功能，锁定浮动利润

Strengths

  • Double smoothing (SMA+JJMA) effectively reduces signal noise and improves signal quality
  • Supports five position sizing modes, flexibly adapting to different risk preferences
  • Independent buy/sell switches allow flexible control over single-direction trading
  • 1:2 stop-loss to take-profit ratio provides positive expectancy risk management
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate entries on the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Depends on custom i-KlPrice indicator with opaque logic, making backtesting and verification difficult
  • Discrete integer-level signal values (0/2/4) may produce too sparse or imprecise signals
  • No spread filter, which may generate false signals during high-spread conditions
  • 1000-point stop loss is large, potentially causing high per-trade losses
  • No check for existing same-direction positions, potentially leading to duplicate entries

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter mechanism to avoid opening positions during high-spread conditions
  • Add same-direction position checking to prevent duplicate entries causing overexposure
  • Add trailing stop or trailing take-profit functionality to lock in floating profits
