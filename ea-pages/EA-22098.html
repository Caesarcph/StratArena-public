摘要

Ketty是一个趋势跟踪型智能交易系统，利用移动平均线交叉和动量确认沿趋势方向入场交易。每个仓位采用固定手数并设置止损和止盈，在指定时间周期上全自动运行。

策略分析

Ketty EA的核心策略是基于移动平均线的交叉来判断趋势方向，当快速均线上穿慢速均线时做多，下穿时做空。入场条件还包括动量指标的确认，避免在震荡行情中频繁开仓。出场条件为预设的止损和止盈价位，同时也支持反向信号平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，不根据账户余额动态调整。资金管理方面较为基础，缺少加仓和减仓逻辑，整体风险可控但灵活性不足。

Summary

Ketty is a trend-following Expert Advisor that uses moving average crossovers and momentum confirmation to enter trades in the direction of the prevailing trend. It employs fixed lot sizing with stop loss and take profit levels for each position. The EA operates systematically on specified timeframes without manual intervention.

Strategy Analysis

Ketty EA's core strategy uses moving average crossovers to determine trend direction, going long when the fast MA crosses above the slow MA and short on the reverse cross. Entry conditions include momentum indicator confirmation to avoid frequent trades in ranging markets. Exit conditions rely on preset stop loss and take profit levels, with reverse signal exits also supported. Position sizing uses fixed lots without dynamic adjustment based on account equity. Money management is basic, lacking scaling in or out logic, making overall risk controllable but inflexible.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和调优
  • 设有止损止盈，风险可控
  • 使用动量确认过滤假信号，减少震荡行情中的亏损交易

劣势

  • 固定手数不随账户规模变化，资金管理灵活性差
  • 均线交叉策略在震荡市中容易产生频繁虚假信号导致连续亏损
  • 缺少加仓减仓机制和移动止损，无法锁定浮动利润

改进建议

  • 增加动态仓位管理，根据账户余额和ATR自动调整手数
  • 添加移动止损功能以保护已获利润，并在趋势延续时让利润奔跑

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and optimize
  • Stop loss and take profit are set, keeping risk controllable
  • Momentum confirmation filters false signals, reducing losing trades in ranging markets

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing doesn't adapt to account size, poor money management flexibility
  • MA crossover strategy tends to generate frequent false signals in ranging markets leading to consecutive losses
  • Lacks scaling and trailing stop mechanisms, unable to lock in floating profits

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing that adjusts lots based on account equity and ATR
  • Implement trailing stop functionality to protect profits while allowing gains to run during trend continuation

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 均线交叉信号滞后，可能在趋势末期才入场导致止损触发
  • 固定手数在大额账户下风险敞口过小，小额账户下可能过大
  • 缺少新K线检查机制，可能同根K线多次开仓

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • MA crossover signals lag, potentially entering at trend end and triggering stop loss
  • Fixed lots create disproportionate risk exposure for small accounts and under-utilization for large accounts
  • Lacks new bar check, potentially opening multiple positions on the same candle
