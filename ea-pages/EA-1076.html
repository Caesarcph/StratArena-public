摘要

该EA使用同周期的EMA和LWMA双均线交叉系统来生成趋势跟踪信号。当LWMA高于EMA且两者均上升时开多仓，当LWMA低于EMA且两者均下降时开空仓。仓位管理包含可选的亏损递减机制，在连续亏损后逐步缩小仓位。

策略分析

策略概念基于LWMA与EMA的交叉判断趋势方向，LWMA对近期价格赋予更高权重，因此比EMA更快反应价格变化，两者交叉可作为趋势转折信号。入场条件：做多要求LWMA高于EMA（短期动能向上）且LWMA和EMA各自高于前一期值（趋势确认），做空则反之。出场条件：做多持仓在LWMA跌破EMA时平仓，做空持仓在LWMA升破EMA时平仓，属于反转式出场而非固定止盈止损。仓位管理提供两种模式：固定手数或基于自由保证金和风险比例的动态手数。资金管理中的DecreaseFactor功能会在连续亏损后按比例缩减仓位，亏损次数越多缩减越大，有助于控制回撤，但该机制缺乏止损保护，完全依赖指标信号出场。

Summary

This EA uses a dual moving average crossover system combining EMA and LWMA of the same period to generate trend-following signals. It opens buy positions when LWMA is above EMA and both are rising, and sell positions when LWMA is below EMA and both are falling. Position sizing includes an optional loss-based lot reduction mechanism to scale down after consecutive losing trades.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the crossover of LWMA and EMA of the same period to determine trend direction; LWMA assigns higher weight to recent prices, making it more responsive than EMA, and their crossover serves as a trend reversal signal. Entry conditions: going long requires LWMA above EMA (short-term momentum upward) and both LWMA and EMA above their previous values (trend confirmation), while going short is the mirror condition. Exit conditions: long positions are closed when LWMA falls below EMA, and short positions are closed when LWMA rises above EMA, representing a reversal-based exit rather than fixed stop-loss or take-profit levels. Position sizing offers two modes: fixed lots or dynamic lots calculated from free margin multiplied by the risk percentage. The DecreaseFactor mechanism in money management progressively reduces lot size after consecutive losing trades—the more losses, the greater the reduction—which helps control drawdown, but the strategy lacks explicit stop-loss protection, relying entirely on indicator signals for exits.

优势

  • 双均线确认机制（交叉+同向上行/下行）减少了假信号，比简单均线交叉更可靠
  • DecreaseFactor亏损递减功能有助于在连续亏损期控制风险暴露
  • 代码结构清晰，信号判断、仓位管理、交易执行各模块分离良好
  • 新K线检查机制（LastTime）避免了同一根K线内重复开仓

劣势

  • 没有设置止损和止盈，完全依赖指标反转信号出场，可能导致单笔亏损过大
  • 仓位计算公式（自由保证金×风险/1000）缺乏理论依据，未考虑ATR或点值
  • DecreaseFactor的递减逻辑在losses刚好大于1时可能导致过小的仓位
  • 没有持仓检查时的多单空单互斥逻辑可能导致信号冲突时无法正确处理

改进建议

  • 增加止损和止盈参数，至少设置一个基于ATR的止损来限制单笔最大亏损
  • 改进仓位计算公式，使用基于ATR或点值的规范风险计算方法替代当前的简单除以1000的方式
  • 增加对交易时间段的过滤，避免在低流动性时段产生错误信号

Strengths

  • Dual MA confirmation (crossover + same-direction movement) reduces false signals compared to simple MA crossover
  • DecreaseFactor loss-reduction mechanism helps control risk exposure during consecutive losing streaks
  • Clean code structure with well-separated modules for signal logic, position sizing, and trade execution
  • New bar check mechanism (LastTime) prevents duplicate entries within the same bar

Weaknesses

  • No stop-loss or take-profit set—exits rely entirely on indicator reversal signals, which can lead to excessive single-trade losses
  • Lot calculation formula (free margin × risk / 1000) lacks theoretical justification and does not account for ATR or pip value
  • DecreaseFactor reduction logic when losses barely exceed 1 may result in excessively small position sizes
  • The mutual exclusion logic between buy and sell signals may fail to handle conflicting signals correctly

Improvements

  • Add stop-loss and take-profit parameters, at minimum an ATR-based stop-loss to cap maximum per-trade loss
  • Improve the lot calculation formula to use a proper risk-based approach accounting for ATR or pip value instead of the arbitrary division by 1000
  • Add trading session filters to avoid generating false signals during low-liquidity periods
