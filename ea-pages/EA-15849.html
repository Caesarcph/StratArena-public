摘要

该EA按固定时间间隔交替下买单和卖单，形成对冲持仓系统。每笔交易设有止损和止盈，每次定时器触发时交易方向在买入和卖出之间交替切换。

策略分析

策略概念是基于定时器的时间驱动交易，每隔设定秒数自动开仓，交替执行买入和卖出操作，形成对冲持仓。入场条件完全依赖时间触发，无任何技术指标或价格分析，定时器到期即开仓。出场条件依赖固定的止损和止盈点数，止损较小（10点）而止盈较大（50点），风险收益比为1:5。仓位管理使用固定手数（TradeValue参数），没有根据账户资金或波动率进行动态调整。资金管理完全缺失，没有检查账户余额、已持仓数量或最大持仓限制，可能导致无限累积对冲仓位。

Summary

This EA places alternating Buy and Sell orders on a fixed timer interval, creating a hedging position system. Each trade includes a Stop Loss and Take Profit, and the direction alternates between buy and sell on each timer trigger.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is purely time-driven trading based on a timer, automatically opening positions at fixed intervals with alternating buy and sell directions to create a hedging layout. Entry conditions rely entirely on the timer event with no technical indicators or price analysis — a trade is placed every time the timer fires. Exit conditions depend on fixed Stop Loss (10 points) and Take Profit (50 points) levels, giving a risk-reward ratio of 1:5. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (TradeValue parameter) with no dynamic adjustment based on account equity or market volatility. Money management is completely absent — there is no check on account balance, existing position count, or maximum position limits, which could lead to unlimited accumulation of hedged positions.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单直观，易于理解和部署
  • 止损止盈比例1:5，单笔交易风险收益比较好
  • 交替买卖形成自然对冲，一定程度上降低了单边风险
  • 使用CTrade标准库，代码简洁规范

劣势

  • 完全没有技术分析，入场纯粹依赖时间，无视市场状态
  • 没有持仓数量检查，定时器持续触发会导致仓位无限累积
  • 止损仅10点过于紧密，极易被市场噪音触发止损
  • 没有检查新柱或交易时段过滤，可能在低流动性时段开仓
  • Bug：EventSetTimer参数使用TimerTime*100，实际间隔远超预期值

改进建议

  • 增加最大持仓数量限制和账户资金检查，防止仓位无限累积
  • 添加技术指标作为入场过滤条件，避免在不利市场环境下开仓
  • 修正EventSetTimer的参数错误，应为TimerTime而非TimerTime*100
  • 增加交易时段过滤和点差检查，避免在不利条件下交易

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is simple and intuitive, easy to understand and deploy
  • Stop Loss to Take Profit ratio of 1:5 provides a favorable risk-reward per trade
  • Alternating buy/sell creates natural hedging, partially reducing directional risk
  • Uses the standard CTrade library for clean and conventional order execution

Weaknesses

  • No technical analysis whatsoever — entries are purely time-based, ignoring market conditions entirely
  • No position count check — the timer continuously firing leads to unlimited position accumulation
  • Stop Loss of only 10 points is extremely tight and easily triggered by market noise
  • No new bar check or trading session filter — may open positions during low liquidity periods
  • Bug: EventSetTimer uses TimerTime*100, making actual interval far longer than the intended value

Improvements

  • Add maximum position count limit and account equity check to prevent unlimited position accumulation
  • Add technical indicators as entry filters to avoid trading in unfavorable market conditions
  • Fix the EventSetTimer parameter bug — should be TimerTime instead of TimerTime*100
  • Add trading session filter and spread check to avoid trading under adverse conditions
