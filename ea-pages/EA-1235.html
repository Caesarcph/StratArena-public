摘要

该EA基于自定义Extremum指标识别价格极值点（摆动高低点）进行交易。当确认上升极值信号时开多仓，确认下降极值信号时开空仓，入场和出场权限可独立控制。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于Extremum自定义指标识别价格的极值点（即摆动高低点）来进行突破交易。入场条件为：当指标的第5缓冲区（UpValue）在信号K线上出现上穿信号，且第3缓冲区（DnValue）在前一根K线上确认不存在下降信号时，触发买入信号；反之，当DnValue出现下穿信号且UpValue确认不存在上升信号时，触发卖出信号。出场条件依赖独立的买卖平仓权限控制，当出现反向信号时平掉对应仓位，同时使用固定的StopLoss和TakeProfit进行风险管理。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示直接指定手数大小，正值表示按存款比例计算。资金管理采用固定止损止盈方式，止损1000点、止盈2000点，风险回报比为1:2。策略在新K线形成时才计算信号，避免重复交易，但缺少对持仓状态的检查，可能造成重复开仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on the custom Extremum indicator, which identifies price extremes (swing highs and lows). It opens buy positions when an upward extremum signal is confirmed and sell positions when a downward extremum signal is confirmed, with optional permissions for entry and exit independently controlled.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is a breakout-style system based on the custom Extremum indicator, which identifies swing highs and lows in price action. Entry conditions require that the UpValue buffer (index 5) signals an upward extremum on the signal bar while the DnValue buffer (index 3) does not show a downward extremum on the previous bar, triggering a buy; the mirror logic applies for sell entries. Exit conditions are controlled through independent permission flags (BuyPosClose/SellPosClose), where a reverse extremum signal closes the opposing position, supplemented by fixed StopLoss (1000 points) and TakeProfit (2000 points) for risk management. Position sizing is governed by the MM parameter: negative values specify a fixed lot size directly, while positive values represent a share of the deposit per deal. Money management uses a fixed stop-loss and take-profit approach with a risk-reward ratio of 1:2, which is reasonable but lacks trailing stop or dynamic adjustment capabilities. The strategy only recalculates on new bar formation to avoid redundant signals, but notably lacks an explicit check for existing open positions before opening new ones, which could lead to accumulated or duplicate positions.

优势

  • 独立控制买入和卖出的开仓及平仓权限，灵活性高
  • 采用新K线检测机制避免同一K线内重复计算信号
  • 风险回报比为1:2，止盈大于止损，符合趋势跟踪的基本原则
  • 使用自定义指标识别极值点，逻辑清晰且与价格结构直接相关
  • 支持按存款比例或固定手数两种仓位管理模式

劣势

  • 缺少对已有持仓的检查，可能在同方向重复开仓导致仓位累积
  • 没有点差过滤，在高点差环境下止损可能更容易被触发
  • 止损1000点过大，可能导致单笔亏损金额过高
  • 依赖自定义指标Extremum，但该指标的具体算法不在本文件中，透明度不足
  • 没有移动止损或动态止盈机制，无法锁定浮动利润

改进建议

  • 添加持仓状态检查，在开仓前确认同方向无已有仓位，避免重复开仓
  • 增加移动止损（Trailing Stop）功能，在趋势有利时逐步锁定利润并减少回撤风险

Strengths

  • Independent permission flags for buy/sell entry and exit provide high flexibility in controlling trading direction
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents redundant signal calculations within the same bar
  • Risk-reward ratio of 1:2 with take-profit larger than stop-loss aligns with sound trend-following principles
  • Custom Extremum indicator identifies swing points directly tied to price structure with clear logic
  • Supports both deposit-share percentage and fixed lot size position sizing modes

Weaknesses

  • No existing position check before opening new trades, potentially causing duplicate or accumulated positions in the same direction
  • No spread filter, making the EA vulnerable to slippage and premature stop-loss triggers in high-spread environments
  • Stop-loss of 1000 points is very large and could result in significant per-trade losses
  • Depends on the custom Extremum indicator whose algorithm is not included in this file, reducing transparency
  • No trailing stop or dynamic take-profit mechanism, meaning floating profits cannot be locked in during favorable moves

Improvements

  • Add an existing position check before opening new trades to confirm no same-direction positions are already open, preventing duplicate entries
  • Implement a trailing stop mechanism to progressively lock in profits during favorable trends and reduce drawdown risk
