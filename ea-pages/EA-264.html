摘要

该EA使用双EMA交叉信号，并通过ITF（日内时间过滤器）模块进行过滤，限制交易仅在特定的分钟、小时和星期进行。它结合快慢EMA交叉与基于ATR的波动率限制，采用固定手数资金管理，下达带有止损、止盈和过期时间的挂单。

策略分析

策略概念基于双EMA交叉趋势跟踪，快EMA周期为5，慢EMA周期为30，当快线上穿慢线时做多，下穿时做空。入场条件不仅依赖EMA交叉，还通过ATR指标（周期7）和Limit参数（1.2）过滤低波动率行情，避免在震荡区间入场。出场条件使用固定点数的止损（5.0）和止盈（8.0），并通过订单过期时间（4根K线）自动取消未成交挂单。仓位管理采用固定手数模式（0.1手），不根据账户余额动态调整。资金管理使用CMoneyFixedLot类，设置了10%的百分比参数但实际手数为固定值，没有动态仓位缩放机制。ITF时间过滤器允许用户指定好的交易分钟/小时/星期和排除坏的时段，有效避免低流动性或高波动时段的假信号。策略没有追踪止损，使用CTrailingNone表示持仓后不动态调整止损位。

Summary

This EA uses a dual EMA crossover signal filtered by an ITF (Intraday Time Filter) module, which restricts trading to specific minutes, hours, and days. It combines fast/slow EMA crossover with ATR-based volatility limits and fixed-lot money management, placing pending orders with defined stop-loss, take-profit, and expiration.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a dual EMA crossover trend-following approach with a fast EMA period of 5 and a slow EMA period of 30, going long when the fast EMA crosses above the slow EMA and short on the reverse crossover. Entry conditions are further filtered by an ATR indicator (period 7) with a Limit parameter (1.2) to avoid entering during low-volatility consolidation phases. Exit conditions use fixed stop-loss (5.0) and take-profit (8.0) levels, with pending orders automatically cancelled after an expiration of 4 bars if not filled. Position sizing uses a fixed lot model (0.1 lots) that does not dynamically scale based on account equity or balance. Money management employs the CMoneyFixedLot class with a 10% percent parameter, but the actual lot size remains fixed without any dynamic position scaling mechanism. The ITF (Intraday Time Filter) module allows users to specify good and bad trading minutes, hours, and days of the week, effectively avoiding false signals during low-liquidity or high-volatility sessions. The strategy uses no trailing stop (CTrailingNone), meaning stop-loss levels are not dynamically adjusted once a position is opened.

优势

  • ITF时间过滤器有效避免低流动性时段的假信号，提高入场质量
  • ATR波动率过滤防止在震荡行情中频繁开仓，减少无效交易
  • 使用MetaQuotes标准库架构，代码结构清晰，模块化程度高
  • 挂单过期机制避免未成交订单长期占用保证金

劣势

  • 固定手数不根据账户规模动态调整，资金利用率低
  • 没有追踪止损，盈利仓位无法锁定浮盈，存在盈利回吐风险
  • 5/30 EMA周期组合在震荡市中容易产生频繁假交叉信号
  • 止损止盈使用固定点数而非ATR倍数，未考虑市场波动率变化

改进建议

  • 将止损止盈改为ATR倍数计算，使风险参数自适应市场波动率
  • 添加追踪止损模块替代CTrailingNone，实现盈利保护
  • 将固定手数改为基于ATR和账户风险的动态仓位管理

Strengths

  • The ITF time filter effectively avoids false signals during low-liquidity sessions, improving entry quality
  • ATR volatility filtering prevents frequent entries during ranging markets, reducing ineffective trades
  • Uses MetaQuotes standard library architecture with clear code structure and high modularity
  • Pending order expiration mechanism prevents unfilled orders from occupying margin indefinitely

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing does not dynamically adjust based on account size, resulting in low capital utilization
  • No trailing stop means profitable positions cannot lock in floating gains, risking profit givebacks
  • The 5/30 EMA combination is prone to frequent false crossover signals in ranging markets
  • Stop-loss and take-profit use fixed point values instead of ATR multiples, ignoring volatility changes

Improvements

  • Change stop-loss and take-profit to ATR-multiple calculations for volatility-adaptive risk parameters
  • Add a trailing stop module to replace CTrailingNone for profit protection
  • Replace fixed lot sizing with dynamic position management based on ATR and account risk
