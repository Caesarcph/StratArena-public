摘要

该EA基于平滑蜡烛图(Heiken Ashi)方向进行交易，当检测到连续三根蜡烛趋势加速形态时开仓。它使用自定义平滑蜡烛指标获取开盘价和收盘价缓冲区，通过比较连续差值确认动量增强后才入场。仓位采用固定手数管理，止损取自当前平滑蜡烛开盘价，反向信号触发平仓反转。

策略分析

策略概念基于平滑蜡烛图(Heiken Ashi)的趋势跟踪，通过分析连续三根HA蜡烛的开盘价递进关系和收盘价与开盘价的相对位置来判断趋势方向和强度。入场条件为多头时要求连续三根HA收盘价均高于对应开盘价(e>a, f>b, g>c)，且开盘价差值逐根放大(w>x>y>0)，表明上升趋势加速；空头反之。出场条件包括反向信号出现时平仓反转，以及止损设在最新HA开盘价处。仓位管理采用固定手数0.1手，并通过P参数限制同方向最大持仓数量(默认3)。资金管理方面仅在保证金水平低于120%时禁止开仓，缺乏独立的风险控制机制如最大回撤限制或动态仓位调整。

Summary

This EA trades based on Heiken Ashi candlestick direction, opening positions when a consistent three-bar trend acceleration pattern is detected. It uses a custom Heiken Ashi indicator to derive open and close buffer values, comparing successive differences to confirm strengthening momentum before entering. Positions are managed with a fixed lot size and a stop loss derived from the current Heiken Ashi open price, while opposing signals trigger position reversal.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is trend following based on Heiken Ashi smoothed candlesticks, analyzing the progressive relationship of open prices across three consecutive HA bars and the relative position of close versus open prices to determine both trend direction and strength. Entry conditions for a long require three consecutive HA closes above their respective opens (e>a, f>b, g>c), with open price differences increasing bar over bar (w>x>y>0), signaling accelerating uptrend momentum; short conditions are the mirror opposite. Exit conditions include position reversal on opposing signals and a stop loss set at the latest HA open price. Position sizing uses a fixed lot of 0.1, with the P parameter capping the maximum number of same-direction positions (default 3). Money management only blocks new entries when the margin level falls below 120%, lacking independent risk controls such as maximum drawdown limits or dynamic position sizing.

优势

  • 利用平滑蜡烛图过滤市场噪音，减少虚假信号，趋势判断更稳定
  • 要求连续三根蜡烛动量递增才入场，入场条件严格，避免震荡市频繁交易
  • 有反向信号自动平仓并反转机制，能快速响应趋势变化

劣势

  • 固定手数无动态仓位调整，无法根据账户风险或波动率自适应
  • 止损仅设在HA开盘价且受Spp最小距离限制，在趋势初期止损可能过近导致易被扫损
  • 变量命名极度过简短(V, D, Cnt, xP等)，代码可读性极差，维护困难

改进建议

  • 增加动态仓位管理，根据ATR或账户权益比例调整手数，改善资金管理
  • 添加止盈逻辑和追踪止损机制，当前只有止损没有止盈，盈利空间受限
  • 增强代码可读性，将单字母变量替换为有意义的名称，添加详细注释

Strengths

  • Heiken Ashi smoothing filters market noise effectively, yielding more stable trend signals and fewer false entries
  • Requires three consecutive bars of accelerating momentum before entry, providing strict confirmation that avoids frequent trading in ranging markets
  • Automatic position reversal on opposing signals enables quick response to trend changes

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing with no dynamic adjustment prevents adaptation to account risk or market volatility
  • Stop loss placed at the HA open price with a minimum distance check (Spp) can be too tight early in a trend, leading to premature stop-outs
  • Extremely terse variable naming (V, D, Cnt, xP, etc.) makes the code nearly unreadable and very difficult to maintain

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on ATR or account equity percentage to improve money management
  • Implement take-profit logic and a trailing stop mechanism; currently there is only a stop loss with no take-profit, limiting profit potential
  • Improve code readability by replacing single-letter variables with meaningful names and adding detailed comments

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Stop loss distance may be too small (Spp*Point is only 300 points), easily stopped out during high volatility
  • No maximum drawdown or daily loss limit; the EA will keep opening positions during sustained losses
  • Position count (Cnt) relies on a global variable rather than recalculating from actual positions, causing potential state inconsistency after program restart
