摘要

该EA使用快速SMA(6)和慢速SMA(54)的双均线交叉系统来生成趋势跟踪的买卖信号。仅在无持仓时开仓，交叉信号出现时先平反向仓，并对持仓应用可配置步长的移动止损和固定止盈。

策略分析

策略概念基于双均线交叉的趋势跟踪方法，使用快速SMA(6)和慢速SMA(54)判断趋势方向变化。入场条件为：当无持仓时，慢线上穿快线（金叉）做多，慢线下穿快线（死叉）做空。出场条件包括两方面：一是有持仓时出现反向交叉信号则平仓，二是设有固定点数的止盈目标。仓位管理采用固定手数（InpLots），不考虑账户余额或风险比例的动态调整。资金管理方面没有止损设置，开仓时止损为0，仅依赖移动止损和反向信号平仓来控制风险。移动止损逻辑为：盈利超过追踪止损加步长距离后才调整止损位，且每次调整步长由InpTrailingStep控制。

Summary

This EA uses a dual moving average crossover system with a fast SMA(6) and slow SMA(54) to generate trend-following buy and sell signals. It opens positions only when no existing positions are open, closes opposing positions on crossover signals, and applies a trailing stop with configurable step for open position management. A fixed take-profit in pips is also applied on entry.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a dual moving average crossover trend-following approach, using fast SMA(6) and slow SMA(54) to detect trend direction changes. Entry conditions: when no positions exist, a buy is placed on golden cross (slow crosses above fast) and a sell on death cross (slow crosses below fast). Exit conditions include closing opposing positions on crossover signals and a fixed pip take-profit target on entry. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (InpLots parameter), with no dynamic adjustment based on account balance or risk percentage. Money management is weak because there is no stop-loss on entry—positions open with SL=0, relying solely on trailing stop and reverse signal closure for risk control. The trailing stop logic only adjusts the stop-loss when profit exceeds the trailing stop distance plus step, and each adjustment moves the stop by the configurable InpTrailingStep amount.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简洁清晰，双均线交叉是经典且易于理解的趋势跟踪方法
  • 包含移动止损功能，在趋势行情中能够有效锁定浮动利润
  • 使用新K线检测机制（PrevBars），避免同一根K线内重复交易，减少过度交易风险
  • 参数验证完善，初始化时检查均线周期和手数合法性
  • 支持3/5位小数点自动调整，兼容不同经纪商的报价精度

劣势

  • 开仓时不设止损（SL=0），如果市场剧烈波动且未触发移动止损条件，可能导致巨大亏损
  • 固定手数不考虑账户资金比例，缺乏资金管理策略，小账户容易爆仓
  • 仅允许同时持有一个方向的单子，在震荡行情中会频繁开平仓产生大量亏损点差成本
  • 没有点差过滤，在小TP（10点）设置下点差可能吞噬大部分利润甚至导致亏损
  • 没有交易时间过滤，可能在低流动性时段或重大新闻时段产生不利成交

改进建议

  • 增加开仓止损设置，建议根据ATR或固定点数设置初始止损，以限制最坏情况下的单笔亏损
  • 实现基于账户风险比例的动态仓位计算（如每笔风险账户余额的1-2%），替代固定手数
  • 增加点差过滤器，当点差超过阈值时暂停开仓，保护小TP策略的盈利能力
  • 增加交易时段过滤，避免在低流动性时段交易
  • 考虑加入ADX或波动率过滤器，在震荡市中减少无效交叉信号导致的亏损交易

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is clean and straightforward—dual MA crossover is a classic, well-understood trend-following method
  • Includes trailing stop functionality that effectively locks in floating profits during trending markets
  • New bar detection mechanism (PrevBars) prevents repeated trades within the same bar, reducing overtrading risk
  • Thorough parameter validation at initialization checks MA periods and lot size validity
  • Automatic 3/5 digit point adjustment ensures compatibility with different broker quote precisions

Weaknesses

  • No stop-loss on entry (SL=0)—if the market moves sharply before trailing stop activates, losses can be catastrophic
  • Fixed lot size with no account equity-based sizing means poor money management; small accounts risk blowup
  • Only one direction at a time is allowed, causing frequent open/close cycles in ranging markets with high spread costs
  • No spread filter—with a small TP of 10 pips, spreads can consume most profits or even cause net losses
  • No trading session filter, potentially leading to unfavorable fills during low-liquidity or news event periods

Improvements

  • Add a stop-loss on entry, ideally based on ATR or a fixed pip distance, to limit worst-case per-trade loss
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account risk percentage (e.g., 1-2% equity risk per trade) instead of fixed lots
  • Add a spread filter that suspends new entries when spread exceeds a threshold, protecting the profitability of small-TP strategies
  • Add trading session filters to avoid trading during low-liquidity periods
  • Consider adding an ADX or volatility filter to reduce false crossover signals in ranging markets
