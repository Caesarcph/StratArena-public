Strategy Analysis

The core concept of this strategy is a percentage-based channel breakout trading system where the upper and lower channel bands are calculated as percentage offsets from price, forming dynamic support and resistance zones. Entry conditions trigger a buy when price breaks above the upper channel band and a sell when price breaks below the lower band, following classic breakout logic. Exit conditions likely include closing positions when price retreats back inside the channel, using fixed take-profit/stop-loss levels, or closing on a reverse channel breakout. Position sizing details cannot be confirmed from the filename alone, but typical implementations use either fixed lot sizes or equity-based lot calculation. Money management aspects may include configurable stop-loss and take-profit parameters, though the specific risk controls require source code review to verify. The percentage-based channel approach has an inherent advantage over fixed-pip channels by self-adjusting to different price levels and volatility conditions across various market environments.
