摘要

该EA结合移动平均线(iMA)和抛物线SAR(iSAR)指标生成趋势跟踪交易信号。当价格位于均线上方且SAR位于K线下方时做多，当价格位于均线下方且SAR位于K线上方时做空。EA通过可配置的手数和止损/止盈水平管理仓位。

策略分析

策略概念基于双重趋势确认：移动平均线判断大趋势方向，抛物线SAR确认短期趋势转折点。入场条件为做多时价格需在均线之上且SAR翻转至K线下方，做空时价格需在均线之下且SAR翻转至K线上方，两个指标必须同向共振才触发信号。出场条件依赖SAR指标的反转信号或预设的止损止盈水平。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，没有根据账户资金动态调整的机制。资金管理方面缺乏追踪止损和分批平仓功能，风险控制完全依赖静态止损止盈。整体策略逻辑简单清晰，但在震荡行情中容易产生频繁的虚假信号导致连续亏损。

Summary

This EA uses a combination of the Moving Average (iMA) and Parabolic SAR (iSAR) indicators to generate trend-following trade signals. It enters long positions when the price is above the MA and the SAR is below the candle, and enters short positions when the price is below the MA and the SAR is above the candle. The EA manages positions with configurable lot sizing and stop loss/take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on dual trend confirmation: the Moving Average determines the overall trend direction while the Parabolic SAR confirms short-term trend reversal points. Entry conditions require both indicators to align - for longs, price must be above the MA and SAR must flip below the candle; for shorts, price must be below the MA and SAR must flip above the candle. Exit conditions rely on SAR reversal signals or pre-configured stop loss and take profit levels. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic adjustment based on account equity or volatility. Money management lacks trailing stops and partial close functionality, relying entirely on static stop loss and take profit for risk control. The overall strategy logic is simple and clear, but it is prone to generating frequent false signals during ranging markets, leading to consecutive losses.

优势

  • 双指标共振过滤减少了虚假信号，提高了入场质量
  • 策略逻辑简单直观，易于理解和调试
  • SAR指标自带追踪特性，能适应趋势发展动态调整止损
  • 参数可配置性强，适合不同市场环境优化
  • 趋势行情中表现良好，能捕捉较长的趋势波段

劣势

  • 震荡行情中SAR频繁翻转导致大量虚假信号和连续亏损
  • 固定手数交易缺乏资金管理，无法根据账户风险动态调整仓位
  • 缺少追踪止损功能，盈利仓位可能在回撤中返还大量利润
  • 没有新K线检查机制，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时段过滤，不利条件下仍会入场

改进建议

  • 增加ATR或波动率过滤器，在低波动震荡行情中暂停交易
  • 实现基于账户风险的动态仓位计算，替代固定手数
  • 加入追踪止损机制锁定浮动利润，防止盈利回撤过大

Strengths

  • Dual indicator confluence reduces false signals and improves entry quality
  • Simple and intuitive strategy logic, easy to understand and debug
  • SAR has built-in trailing特性 that adapts stop loss dynamically with trend development
  • Highly configurable parameters suitable for optimization across different market conditions
  • Performs well in trending markets, capable of capturing extended trend moves

Weaknesses

  • SAR frequent reversals in ranging markets generate numerous false signals and consecutive losses
  • Fixed lot sizing lacks money management, unable to dynamically adjust positions based on account risk
  • No trailing stop functionality, profitable positions may give back substantial profits during retracements
  • No new bar check mechanism, potentially opening multiple positions on the same bar
  • Lack of spread filter and trading session filter, entries occur even under unfavorable conditions

Improvements

  • Add ATR or volatility filter to pause trading during low-volatility ranging conditions
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account risk instead of fixed lots
  • Add trailing stop mechanism to lock in floating profits and prevent significant profit givebacks
