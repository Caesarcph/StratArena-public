摘要

一个基于时间的突破EA，在特定时间放置挂单，旨在捕获高波动时段的剧烈价格变动。EA在当前价格的计算距离处设置买入止损和卖出止损订单，由预定时间事件触发。它似乎专为利用突然的市场行情而设计，类似于波动的'定时炸弹'引爆。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于时间触发的大幅突破交易。入场条件是在指定时间点，在当前价格上下方一定距离处同时放置买入止损和卖出止损挂单，形成一个突破区间。出场条件可能包括止盈和止损设置，当价格触及任一方向即成交。仓位管理方面，EA可能使用固定手数或基于ATR的动态手数调整。资金管理方面，由于采用双向突破挂单策略，如果未设置合理的止损，可能面临较大风险。整体策略概念是捕捉市场在特定时段的突发性突破行情，如新闻发布或开市时段的剧烈波动。

Summary

A time-based breakout EA that places pending orders at specific times, aiming to capture explosive price movements during high-volatility sessions. The EA sets buy stop and sell stop orders at a calculated distance from the current price, triggered by a scheduled time event. It appears designed to capitalize on sudden market moves, similar to a 'time bomb' detonation of volatility.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is a time-triggered breakout trading system. Entry conditions involve placing buy stop and sell stop pending orders at a calculated distance above and below the current price at a specified time, creating a breakout zone. Exit conditions likely include take profit and stop loss settings, where the order fills when price touches either direction. Position sizing may use fixed lots or ATR-based dynamic lot adjustment. Money management is a concern as the dual-direction breakout pending order strategy can face significant risk without proper stop losses. The overall strategy concept aims to capture sudden breakout movements during specific time periods such as news releases or market open sessions with explosive volatility.

  • 策略逻辑简单明了，易于理解和参数优化
