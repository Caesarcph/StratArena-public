摘要

这是一个多周期趋势跟踪EA，在三个时间框架上使用五个不同周期的SMA结合加速振荡器信号来生成综合评分。当汇总得分超过可配置阈值时开立多头或空头仓位，内置止损、止盈和移动止损功能。

策略分析

策略概念基于多时间框架SMA趋势方向投票与加速振荡器（AC）确认的叠加评分系统。在三个时间框架（默认M15、H1、H4）上各计算5条SMA（周期5/8/13/21/34）的当前值与前一根K线值的大小关系，每条SMA向上赋1分、向下赋1分、持平0分。AC指标在强趋势确认时额外赋3分权重，每个时间框架的最高总分为8分，乘以12.5得到0-100的百分比评分。入场条件为三个时间框架的评分均超过阈值（>50为普通信号，>=75为强信号），Signal值1或2开多，-1或-2开空。出场条件为反向信号触发平仓，也可设置止盈止损平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数，无加仓减仓逻辑。资金管理方面没有动态仓位调整或风险百分比计算，仅使用固定手数参数。移动止损仅在盈利超过开仓价后激活，逐步跟踪保护利润。

Summary

A multi-timeframe trend-following EA that uses five SMA periods across three timeframes combined with Accelerator Oscillator signals to generate a composite score. It opens buy or sell positions when the aggregated score exceeds configurable thresholds, with built-in stop loss, take profit, and trailing stop functionality.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a composite scoring system combining multi-timeframe SMA trend direction voting with Accelerator Oscillator confirmation. On each of three timeframes (default M15, H1, H4), five SMAs (periods 5/8/13/21/34) are compared between current and previous bar values, assigning 1 point for upward direction, 1 point for downward, and 0 for flat. The AC indicator adds an extra 3-point weight when a strong trend is confirmed, making the maximum total score 8 per timeframe, multiplied by 12.5 to yield a 0-100 percentage. Entry conditions require all three timeframe scores to exceed thresholds (above 50 for a normal signal, at or above 75 for a strong signal), with Signal values of 1 or 2 triggering buys and -1 or -2 triggering sells. Exit conditions include reverse signal closure and configurable stop loss/take profit levels. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size with no scaling or accumulation logic. Money management lacks dynamic position sizing or risk percentage calculation, relying solely on a fixed lot parameter. The trailing stop activates only when in profit beyond the open price, gradually trailing to protect gains.

优势

  • 多时间框架确认提高了信号的可靠性，减少了虚假突破
  • 评分系统将多个指标信号量化为0-100百分比，逻辑清晰直观
  • 内置止损止盈和移动止损，具备基本的风险控制机制
  • 使用费波纳奇数列作为SMA周期，符合技术分析主流理论
  • 加速振荡器（AC）的3分额外权重能有效区分强弱趋势

劣势

  • 代码大量使用全局变量，变量命名晦涩（如u1x5v、d3acv），可读性极差
  • 固定手数没有动态仓位管理，无法根据账户资金或波动性调整风险敞口
  • SMA比较逻辑重复冗余，5个周期×3个时间框架×上下方向共30段几乎相同的代码应抽象为循环
  • 平仓操作使用3倍滑点容忍度，在高波动市场可能导致滑点过大
  • 没有点差过滤，在点差较大的品种上可能频繁止损或入场成本过高

改进建议

  • 将重复的SMA方向判断和AC指标逻辑重构为循环或函数调用，消除代码冗余
  • 加入动态仓位管理，基于账户余额百分比或ATR波动率计算手数
  • 增加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免在高点差或低流动性时段交易

Strengths

  • Multi-timeframe confirmation improves signal reliability and reduces false breakouts
  • The scoring system quantifies multiple indicator signals into a 0-100 percentage, making logic clear and intuitive
  • Built-in stop loss, take profit, and trailing stop provide basic risk control mechanisms
  • Fibonacci sequence SMA periods align with mainstream technical analysis theory
  • The Accelerator Oscillator's 3-point extra weight effectively distinguishes strong from weak trends

Weaknesses

  • Excessive use of global variables with cryptic naming (e.g. u1x5v, d3acv) makes code extremely hard to read and maintain
  • Fixed lot sizing lacks dynamic position management, unable to adjust risk exposure based on account equity or volatility
  • The SMA comparison logic is highly repetitive; 5 periods × 3 timeframes × up/down directions yield 30 near-identical code blocks that should be abstracted into loops
  • Position close uses 3x spread deviation tolerance, which may cause excessive slippage in volatile markets
  • No spread filter means the EA may suffer frequent stop outs or high entry costs on wide-spread instruments

Improvements

  • Refactor the repetitive SMA direction comparison and AC indicator logic into loops or function calls to eliminate code redundancy
  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account equity percentage or ATR volatility for better risk management
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid trading during high-spread or low-liquidity periods
