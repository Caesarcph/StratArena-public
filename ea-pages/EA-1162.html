摘要

该EA使用基于3个时间周期上5条SMA（周期5、8、13、21、34）和加速振荡器的多周期评分系统来生成买卖信号。每个指标贡献一个分数，当综合评分超过可配置阈值时开仓。包含止损、止盈、追踪止损和基本仓位管理功能。

策略分析

策略概念是综合多时间周期多均线方向与加速振荡器的评分系统。入场条件为：三个时间周期（M15、H1、H4）的上涨评分或下跌评分均超过50（OpenLevel=0时）则开仓，超过75则为强信号。每个时间周期中，5条SMA若当前值大于前值则贡献1分（上涨），反之为0分；AC指标若符合特定连续递增/递减模式则贡献3分，总分乘以12.5得到百分比评分。出场条件为：反向评分超过CloseLevel阈值时平仓，也可设置止损止盈出场。仓位管理采用固定手数（默认0.1手），无动态调仓机制。资金管理方面缺乏风控逻辑，无最大回撤限制或账户权益保护机制。追踪止损功能仅在价格超过开仓价后才开始生效，属于保本型追踪。

Summary

This EA uses a multi-timeframe scoring system based on 5 SMAs (periods 5,8,13,21,34) across 3 timeframes and the Accelerator Oscillator to generate buy/sell signals. Each indicator contributes a score, and positions are opened when the aggregate score exceeds configurable thresholds. It includes stop loss, take profit, trailing stop, and basic position management.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is a multi-timeframe, multi-moving-average scoring system combined with the Accelerator Oscillator. Entry conditions require that all three timeframes (M15, H1, H4) show directional consensus — the aggregate up-score or down-score must exceed 50 on each timeframe simultaneously, with scores above 75 indicating stronger signals. Within each timeframe, 5 SMAs each contribute 1 point if rising (current > previous), and the AC indicator contributes 3 points if it shows a specific sequential momentum pattern, with the total multiplied by 12.5 to produce a percentage. Exit conditions trigger when the opposite-direction score exceeds the CloseLevel threshold, or via fixed stop loss and take profit levels. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (default 0.1) with no dynamic adjustment based on equity or volatility. Money management is essentially absent — there is no drawdown limit, no equity protection, and no lot-scaling logic. The trailing stop only activates after price moves beyond the open price, functioning as a breakeven-style trailing mechanism.

优势

  • 多时间周期共识机制降低了假信号概率，三个周期同向才开仓
  • 评分系统设计合理，将多个指标方向量化为百分比便于理解
  • 使用已成形K线（Shift=1）判断信号，避免当前K线变化导致的信号闪烁
  • 包含止损止盈和追踪止损，提供基本风险控制手段

劣势

  • 代码结构极度冗余，大量重复的全局变量和if-else判断可用循环和数组替代
  • 固定手数无资金管理，不根据账户余额或波动率调整仓位
  • 止损止盈使用点数而非ATR动态计算，在不同波动率环境下可能不适配
  • 追踪止损仅在盈利超过开仓价后触发，缺乏亏损时的保护机制
  • SolveLots函数实际上是空壳，未实现任何有意义的仓位计算逻辑

改进建议

  • 用数组和循环替代大量重复的全局变量和if-else代码块，大幅提升可读性和可维护性
  • 实现基于账户余额或ATR波动的动态仓位管理，替代当前的固定手数方案
  • 增加最大回撤限制、最大同时持仓数和日内亏损限制等资金管理保护机制

Strengths

  • Multi-timeframe consensus mechanism reduces false signals by requiring all three timeframes to align
  • Well-designed scoring system quantifies multiple indicator directions into an intuitive percentage
  • Uses confirmed bars (Shift=1) for signal evaluation, avoiding signal flickering on the current bar
  • Includes stop loss, take profit, and trailing stop providing basic risk control

Weaknesses

  • Extremely redundant code structure with massive duplication — global variables and repetitive if-else blocks could be replaced by loops and arrays
  • Fixed lot sizing with no money management — no adjustment based on account equity or volatility
  • Stop loss and take profit use fixed points instead of ATR-based dynamic calculation, which may be unsuitable across different volatility environments
  • Trailing stop only activates after price exceeds the open price, lacking protection during losing phases
  • The SolveLots function is essentially an empty shell with no meaningful position sizing logic implemented

Improvements

  • Replace the massive number of redundant global variables and repetitive if-else blocks with arrays and loops to dramatically improve readability and maintainability
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account equity or ATR volatility instead of the current fixed lot approach
  • Add money management safeguards such as maximum drawdown limits, maximum simultaneous positions, and daily loss limits
