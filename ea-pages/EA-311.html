摘要

该EA使用CBH_BH_CCI自定义信号过滤器，结合商品通道指标(CCI)与移动平均线(MA)生成交易信号，基于MQL5标准库EA框架运行。采用固定手数资金管理，无追踪止损，依赖CCI-MA交叉动量信号进行入场和出场决策。

策略分析

策略概念基于CCI动量指标与MA平滑滤波的组合，通过CCI指标识别价格偏离统计均值的程度，再用MA对CCI值进行平滑处理以减少假信号。入场条件由CBH_BH_CCI过滤器生成买卖信号，当CCI上穿其MA均线时产生做多信号，下穿时产生做空信号，信号强度需超过阈值40才开仓、超过阈值20才平仓。出场条件依赖信号阈值机制，当反向信号强度达到平仓阈值时关闭仓位，同时支持挂单并在4根K线后过期。仓位管理使用固定手数模式（默认0.1手），不考虑账户余额动态调整仓位大小。资金管理采用CMoneyFixedLot固定手数管理，百分比参数设为10%但实际交易量由固定手数决定，缺乏动态风险调整机制。

Summary

This EA uses the CBH_BH_CCI custom signal filter, which combines the Commodity Channel Index (CCI) with a Moving Average to generate trading signals within the MQL5 Standard Library Expert Advisor framework. It employs fixed lot money management with no trailing stop, relying on the CCI-MA crossover momentum signals for entry and exit decisions.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on combining the CCI momentum indicator with a Moving Average filter, where CCI identifies the degree of price deviation from its statistical mean and MA smooths the CCI values to reduce false signals. Entry conditions are generated by the CBH_BH_CCI filter, producing buy signals when CCI crosses above its MA line and sell signals when it crosses below, with signal strength required to exceed a threshold of 40 for opening and 20 for closing positions. Exit conditions rely on the signal threshold mechanism, closing positions when the reverse signal strength reaches the closing threshold, with support for pending orders that expire after 4 bars. Position sizing uses a fixed lot model (default 0.1 lots), which does not dynamically adjust position size based on account balance or equity. Money management employs CMoneyFixedLot with a percentage parameter set to 10%, but actual trade volume is determined by the fixed lot size, lacking dynamic risk adjustment mechanisms.

优势

  • 使用MQL5标准库框架，代码结构清晰规范，模块化设计便于维护和扩展
  • CCI与MA组合有效减少单一指标的假信号，平滑处理提高了信号可靠性
  • 信号阈值机制（开仓40/平仓20）提供了灵活的信号过滤，避免弱信号交易
  • 支持挂单及过期机制，可以在特定条件下等待更好的入场价格

劣势

  • 止损和止盈均设为0，意味着没有硬性风险保护，极端行情下可能遭受巨大亏损
  • 固定手数资金管理不考虑账户规模变化，无法根据风险动态调整仓位
  • 无追踪止损（TrailingNone），盈利仓位无法锁定浮盈，可能面临利润回吐
  • 仅依赖单一信号过滤器，缺乏多时间框架或多指标确认机制，策略深度不足

改进建议

  • 添加止损和止盈参数的合理默认值，确保每笔交易都有硬性风险保护
  • 将固定手数改为动态仓位管理（如基于ATR或账户风险的仓位计算），提高资金利用效率和风险控制能力
  • 引入追踪止损机制，在盈利时保护浮盈，防止盈利仓位转为亏损

Strengths

  • Uses the MQL5 Standard Library framework with clean, modular code structure that is easy to maintain and extend
  • CCI combined with MA smoothing effectively reduces false signals from a single indicator, improving signal reliability
  • Signal threshold mechanism (open 40/close 20) provides flexible signal filtering, avoiding trades on weak signals
  • Supports pending orders with expiration, allowing entry at better prices under specific conditions

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss and take profit are both set to 0, meaning no hard risk protection exists, potentially leading to massive losses in extreme market conditions
  • Fixed lot money management ignores account size changes and cannot dynamically adjust positions based on risk
  • No trailing stop (TrailingNone), profitable positions cannot lock in floating profits and may suffer profit drawdowns
  • Relies on a single signal filter, lacking multi-timeframe or multi-indicator confirmation, resulting in insufficient strategy depth

Improvements

  • Add reasonable default values for stop loss and take profit parameters to ensure every trade has hard risk protection
  • Replace fixed lot sizing with dynamic position management (e.g., ATR-based or account risk-based calculation) to improve capital efficiency and risk control
  • Introduce a trailing stop mechanism to protect floating profits and prevent winning positions from turning into losses
