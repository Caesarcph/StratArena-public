摘要

这是一个多货币智能交易系统，使用Unity指标检测货币的超买/超卖状态，并采用马丁格尔仓位管理策略进行交易。支持突破和回撤两种信号模式，包含移动止损、时段调度以及基于R平方准则的优化报告系统。通过策略池架构可同时管理多个货币对。

策略分析

策略基于Unity指标的多货币相对强弱分析，当某一货币在指标缓冲区中处于最大值（超买）而另一货币处于最小值（超卖）时产生交易信号。入场条件分为两种模式：突破模式（BREAKOUT）在信号方向直接入场，回撤模式（PULLBACK）则反转信号方向入场，即逆势交易。出场条件包括止损/止盈触发、反向信号出现时平仓、以及交易时段结束时强制平仓。仓位管理采用马丁格尔策略：盈利后重置为基础手数，亏损后将手数乘以Factor倍数，直到达到Limit设定的最大倍数限制后重置为基础手数。资金管理方面包含自由保证金检查，当保证金不足时自动回退到基础手数，并在交易错误后按SkipTimeOnError设定的周期暂停交易。系统还支持TrailingStop移动止损，并在测试器中实时计算权益回撤以评估策略表现。

Summary

A multi-currency expert advisor that uses the Unity indicator to detect overbought/oversold currency conditions and trades with a martingale lot-sizing strategy. It supports both breakout and pullback signal modes, includes trailing stop functionality, scheduled trading hours, and a sophisticated optimization reporting system with R-squared criterion. The EA can simultaneously manage multiple currency pairs through a strategy pool architecture.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy is based on the Unity indicator's multi-currency relative strength analysis, generating trade signals when one currency is at the maximum (overbought) and another at the minimum (oversold) among the indicator buffers. Entry conditions come in two modes: BREAKOUT mode enters in the signal direction directly, while PULLBACK mode reverses the signal direction, trading against the extreme. Exit conditions include stop loss/take profit triggers, position closure on opposing signals, and forced closure when outside scheduled trading hours. Position sizing employs a martingale strategy: after a profit, lot size resets to the base lots; after a loss, the lot is multiplied by the Factor, continuing until the Limit of multiplications is reached, at which point it resets to base lots. Money management includes free margin checks that automatically fall back to base lots when margin is insufficient, and a pause mechanism after trade errors based on the SkipTimeOnError timeout. The system also supports TrailingStop functionality and calculates equity drawdown on-the-fly in the tester to evaluate strategy performance.

优势

  • 多货币架构设计优秀，通过策略池可同时管理多个品种的策略实例
  • 包含完整的优化报告系统，支持R平方准则和HTML报告生成，便于策略评估
  • 代码结构清晰，使用面向对象设计（接口、继承、智能指针），可维护性强

劣势

  • 马丁格尔仓位管理策略在连续亏损时风险呈指数级增长，可能导致爆仓
  • 当检测到多个持仓时仅发出警告而未处理，存在持仓管理漏洞
  • WorkSymbols解析逻辑中'-'符号与负数可能产生歧义，解析鲁棒性不足

改进建议

  • 增加最大回撤限制或账户权益保护机制，在达到危险水平时强制停止交易并平仓
  • 将马丁格尔策略改为反马丁格尔或固定比例仓位管理，降低连续亏损时的风险暴露

Strengths

  • Excellent multi-currency architecture with a strategy pool that can manage multiple symbol instances simultaneously
  • Comprehensive optimization reporting system with R-squared criterion and HTML report generation for strategy evaluation
  • Clean code structure using OOP design (interfaces, inheritance, smart pointers) for high maintainability

Weaknesses

  • Martingale position sizing creates exponential risk growth during consecutive losses, potentially leading to account blowup
  • When multiple positions are detected, only an alert is issued without handling them, leaving a position management gap
  • The WorkSymbols parser's use of '-' symbol may conflict with negative numbers, reducing parsing robustness

Improvements

  • Add maximum drawdown limits or account equity protection that forces trading stop and position closure at dangerous levels
  • Replace martingale with anti-martingale or fixed fractional position sizing to reduce risk exposure during losing streaks

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:DANGEROUS

风险点

  • 马丁格尔策略在连续亏损时手数指数增长，Factor=2和Limit=5意味着最大手数可达初始的32倍
  • 虽然有Limit限制最大加倍次数，但单次最大手数交易仍可能造成巨大亏损
  • 多品种同时运行马丁格尔策略时，关联性风险可能同时触发多个品种的加倍操作

Risk Assessment

Overall:DANGEROUS

Risk Points

  • Martingale lot doubling creates exponential growth - Factor=2 and Limit=5 means max lot can reach 32x the initial size
  • Although Limit caps the number of multiplications, a single max-lot trade can still cause catastrophic loss
  • Running martingale on multiple correlated symbols simultaneously may trigger doubling across all pairs at once
