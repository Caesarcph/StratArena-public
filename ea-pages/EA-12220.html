摘要

该EA实现了马丁格尔加仓策略，根据RSI和价格方向开仓后，当持仓浮亏超过动态点数阈值时，以递增手数加仓摊薄成本。动态点数基于近期K线高低点差值计算，适应市场波动。包含多重出场机制：加权均价止盈、权益止损、CCI指标止损、时间止损和移动止损。

策略分析

该EA采用典型的马丁格尔加仓策略，核心概念是通过不断加仓来摊薄加权均价，等待价格回归后获利出场。入场条件分两种：首次入场依赖RSI指标和最近K线方向判断，RSI低于30且价格上涨时做多，RSI高于70且价格下跌时做空；加仓入场则取决于上一笔持仓的浮亏是否超过动态点数阈值，动态点数由近期Glubina根K线的最高价与最低价之差除以DEL系数计算，并受DefaultPips上下限约束。出场条件包括：加权均价加上TakeProfit点数止盈、CCI指标绝对值超过CCILevel时平仓、持仓时间超过MaxTradeOpenHours时平仓、权益回撤超过TotalEquityRisk百分比时平仓，以及可选的移动止损。仓位管理采用LotExponent指数递增手数，每次加仓手数为初始手数乘以LotExponent的(当前持仓数+1)次方，归一化到lotdecimal位小数。资金管理方面提供了权益止损和经典止损两种保护机制，但经典止损默认关闭，且与移动止损互斥。整体策略在趋势行情中风险极高，加仓可能导致巨额亏损。

Summary

This EA implements a Martingale-style averaging strategy that opens an initial position based on RSI and price direction, then adds increasingly larger positions (lot multiplication) when the previous position is in loss beyond a dynamic pip threshold. It includes multiple exit mechanisms: take-profit at a weighted average price, equity stop, CCI-based stop, time-based stop, and a trailing stop. The dynamic pip step is calculated from the High-Low range of recent bars, adapting grid spacing to market volatility.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a classic Martingale averaging strategy where the core concept is to continuously add positions to lower the weighted average entry price, expecting a price reversion to exit profitably. Entry conditions are twofold: the first position uses RSI and recent bar direction (buy when RSI>30 and price rising, sell when RSI<70 and price falling), while subsequent averaging positions open when the latest position's floating loss exceeds a dynamic pip threshold calculated from the High-Low range of the last 'Glubina' bars divided by DEL, bounded by DefaultPips. Exit mechanisms include: take-profit at weighted average price plus TakeProfit pips, CCI-based closure when CCI absolute value exceeds CCILevel, time-based closure after MaxTradeOpenHours, equity drawdown stop when loss exceeds TotalEquityRisk% of peak equity, and an optional trailing stop. Position sizing uses exponential lot multiplication where each new position's lot equals the base lot times LotExponent raised to the power of (current position count + 1), normalized to lotdecimal decimal places. Money management provides equity stop and classic stop-loss options, but classic stop-loss is disabled by default and mutually exclusive with trailing stop. The strategy carries extreme risk in trending markets where continuous averaging can lead to catastrophic drawdowns.

优势

  • 动态点数间距根据市场波动自适应调整，比固定间距更灵活
  • 提供多种出场机制（CCI止损、权益止损、时间止损、移动止损），风险控制选项丰富
  • 代码结构清晰，模块化程度高，使用面向对象的 HedgePosition 框架
  • RSI结合价格方向的入场条件简单有效，避免逆势开仓
  • 可视化绘制功能完善，便于在图表上直观观察交易状态

劣势

  • 马丁格尔加仓策略在单边趋势中风险极高，可能导致账户爆仓
  • 经典止损默认关闭，且与移动止损互斥，多数场景下缺乏基础止损保护
  • 加仓手数按指数递增，10次加仓后手数可达初始的28倍以上，资金消耗极快
  • 没有考虑点差和手续费对加权均价的影响，实际止盈距离可能不足
  • 首次入场仅依赖RSI和最近两根K线方向，信号过于简单缺乏过滤

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤机制（如均线方向判断），避免在强趋势行情中逆势加仓
  • 将经典止损与移动止损改为可同时启用，并设置总持仓最大亏损硬止损
  • 加入点差过滤和交易时间过滤，避免在高点差时段和低流动性时段交易

Strengths

  • Dynamic pip spacing adapts to market volatility using recent High-Low range, more flexible than fixed intervals
  • Multiple exit mechanisms (CCI stop, equity stop, time stop, trailing stop) provide layered risk control options
  • Clean modular code structure using object-oriented HedgePosition framework for position management
  • RSI combined with price direction for entry is simple and effective, avoiding counter-trend initial entries
  • Comprehensive visual drawing functionality for intuitive trade observation on the chart

Weaknesses

  • Martingale averaging strategy carries extreme risk in trending markets, potentially leading to account blow-up
  • Classic stop-loss is disabled by default and mutually exclusive with trailing stop, lacking basic protection in most scenarios
  • Exponential lot multiplication means 10 averaging positions can reach 28x+ the initial lot size, rapidly depleting capital
  • Spread and commission costs are not factored into the weighted average price calculation, making take-profit targets potentially insufficient
  • Initial entry relies solely on RSI and last two bar direction, overly simplistic signal without additional filters

Improvements

  • Add trend filter (e.g., moving average direction) to avoid counter-trend averaging during strong trending markets
  • Allow classic stop-loss and trailing stop to work simultaneously, and implement a hard maximum portfolio loss stop
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid trading during high-spread and low-liquidity periods

Risk Points
