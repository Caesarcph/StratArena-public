摘要

该EA基于CenterOfGravityOSMA自定义指标进行交易，该指标结合了重心概念与OSMA风格的平滑处理。当振荡器从下降转为上升时开多仓，从上升转为下降时开空仓，并使用可配置的资金管理和止损/止盈水平。

策略分析

该策略基于CenterOfGravityOSMA振荡器的动量反转信号进行交易。入场条件：当指标值macd[1]<macd[2]（下降趋势中）且macd[0]>macd[1]（开始上升）时触发买入信号；当macd[1]>macd[2]（上升趋势中）且macd[0]<macd[1]（开始下降）时触发卖出信号。出场条件：买入平仓信号在卖出开仓条件触发时产生，卖出平仓信号在买入开仓条件触发时产生，同时配合固定点数的止损和止盈。仓位管理采用基于保证金比例（MM参数）的动态手数计算，支持固定手数和百分比模式。资金管理通过MMMode参数控制，可选择固定手数或根据账户余额百分比计算。策略使用新K线检测机制避免重复信号，仅在新柱形成时计算信号，减少噪音干扰。

Summary

This EA trades based on the CenterOfGravityOSMA custom indicator, which is an oscillator combining the Center of Gravity concept with OSMA-style smoothing. It opens buy positions when the oscillator turns upward from a decline and sell positions when it turns downward from a rise, using configurable money management and stop-loss/take-profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy trades on momentum reversal signals from the CenterOfGravityOSMA oscillator. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when macd[1]<macd[2] (downtrend) and macd[0]>macd[1] (upturn begins); a sell signal triggers when macd[1]>macd[2] (uptrend) and macd[0]<macd[1] (downturn begins). Exit conditions: a buy close signal occurs when the sell open condition is met, and a sell close signal occurs when the buy open condition is met, supplemented by fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels in points. Position sizing uses dynamic lot calculation based on the margin ratio (MM parameter), supporting both fixed lot and percentage modes. Money management is controlled via the MMMode parameter, allowing selection between fixed lot size or percentage-based calculation relative to account balance. The strategy employs a new bar detection mechanism to avoid repeated signals, calculating signals only on new bar formation to reduce noise.

优势

  • 使用双重平滑的振荡器信号，能有效过滤市场噪音
  • 具备完整的止损止盈机制和滑点控制，风险管理基础完善
  • 信号检测采用新K线机制，避免同一根K线内重复交易
  • 买卖方向可独立开关，灵活性高，支持仅做多或仅做空
  • 指标周期和参数均可配置，适应性较强

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标CenterOfGravityOSMA，缺少该指标时EA无法运行
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生不利入场
  • 没有持仓检查逻辑，可能在不同信号间重复开仓
  • 止损止盈使用固定点数，未考虑市场波动性（ATR）动态调整
  • 平仓信号仅依赖反向信号，在横盘市场中可能长期持仓不平

改进建议

  • 添加点差过滤机制，避免在点差过大时入场
  • 加入ATR动态止损止盈，根据市场波动性自适应调整
  • 增加持仓数量检查，防止同方向重复开仓导致风险集中

Strengths

  • Uses a double-smoothed oscillator signal that effectively filters market noise
  • Includes complete stop-loss/take-profit mechanisms and slippage control, providing solid risk management fundamentals
  • Signal detection uses new bar mechanism to prevent repeated trades within the same bar
  • Buy and sell directions can be independently enabled or disabled, offering high flexibility for long-only or short-only configurations
  • Indicator periods and parameters are fully configurable, providing good adaptability

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom CenterOfGravityOSMA indicator; the EA cannot run without it
  • Lacks spread filtering, which may result in unfavorable entries during high-spread conditions
  • No position check logic, potentially leading to repeated position openings across different signals
  • Stop-loss and take-profit use fixed point values without dynamic adjustment based on market volatility (ATR)
  • Exit signals rely solely on opposing signals, which may result in prolonged open positions during ranging markets

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter to avoid entering trades when the spread is excessively wide
  • Implement ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit for self-adaptive adjustment to market volatility
  • Add position count checking to prevent repeated same-direction openings that concentrate risk
