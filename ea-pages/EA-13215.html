Exp_CenterOfGravityOSMA

This EA trades based on the Center of Gravity OSMA indicator, which is an oscillator-derived signal combining center of gravity calculations with double-smoothed moving averages. It opens buy positions when the OSMA histogram turns upward from a declining state and sell positions when it turns downward from a rising state, with configurable stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on momentum reversal signals from the CenterOfGravityOSMA indicator, which generates oscillator values through center of gravity price calculations processed with two rounds of smoothing to produce a MACD-like histogram. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when macd[1]<macd[2] (prior decline) and macd[0]>macd[1] (current recovery), indicating an upward momentum shift; a sell signal is generated when macd[1]>macd[2] (prior advance) and macd[0]<macd[1] (current decline), indicating a downward momentum shift. Exit conditions are controlled by BuyPosClose and SellPosClose boolean parameters that determine whether opposite signals close existing positions, combined with fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit levels for risk management. Position sizing is managed through the MM parameter controlling lot size ratio, with MMMode selecting between fixed lot size or margin-based percentage calculation. Money management relies on the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library providing standardized order placement functions with slippage control (Deviation parameter) and signal timestamps to prevent duplicate orders. The strategy is fundamentally a momentum reversal system capturing short-term oscillator turning points.

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on oscillator turning point momentum reversal trading with definite directional signals
  • Supports independent buy/sell open and close controls, offering high flexibility to enable or disable based on market conditions
  • Uses NewBar check to prevent repeated trading within the same bar, reducing overtrading and signal noise
  • Includes fixed stop loss and take profit settings, providing a basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss and take profit use fixed points rather than ATR-based dynamic calculation, poor adaptability across different volatility environments
  • Lacks position addition and trailing stop mechanisms, profitable trades may lose gains due to pullbacks
  • Many indicator parameters without self-adaptive mechanism, prone to failure when market regime changes
  • Does not account for spread and commission impacts on trading costs, which may erode profits in shorter timeframes
  • Double smoothing may cause significant signal lag, missing optimal entry timing

Possible improvements

  • Replace fixed stop loss/take profit with ATR-based dynamic calculation to adapt to different market volatility environments
  • Add trailing stop functionality to protect profitable positions and allow profits to run further
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid opening positions during high-spread and unfavorable periods
  • Consider adding self-adaptive optimization for indicator parameters, dynamically adjusting smoothing periods based on recent market conditions

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Fixed SL 1000/TP 2000 points are large values that may exceed reasonable ranges on some instruments, resulting in practically no protection
  • Oscillator reversal signals can produce frequent false signals during trending markets, leading to consecutive losses
  • Lacks maximum drawdown control and daily loss limit, potentially suffering significant losses in extreme market conditions
  • Depends on custom indicator CenterOfGravityOSMA; if the indicator file is missing, EA initialization fails
