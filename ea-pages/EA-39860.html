Two pending orders 2

This EA places two pending orders (a Buy Stop and a Sell Stop) at a configurable distance from the current price at the start of each new bar. It uses an OCO (One-Cancels-the-Other) logic where once one pending order is triggered, the opposite pending order is deleted, capturing breakout moves in either direction.

Strategy analysis

This EA implements a dual-direction breakout strategy by placing a Buy Stop and a Sell Stop order at a configurable distance above and below the current price at the start of each new bar. Entry conditions are based on price breaking through a predefined distance from the current price level, which is a classic breakout approach. Exit conditions rely on stop-loss and take-profit parameters, along with the OCO (One-Cancels-the-Other) mechanism where the opposite pending order is deleted once one side is triggered, preventing opposing positions. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size determined by the user through input parameters, with no dynamic position sizing or risk-based lot calculation. Money management is minimal, lacking maximum position limits, drawdown protection, or percentage-of-balance sizing, leaving risk control entirely to the user's manual configuration of stop-loss and take-profit values. The strategy is well-suited for volatile market conditions but may suffer frequent stop-outs during ranging or choppy markets.

Strengths

  • OCO mechanism ensures only one directional position is held, avoiding hedging risk
  • Dual pending order design captures breakouts in either direction without missing major moves
  • Strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and optimize
  • Pending orders are re-placed at each new bar, dynamically adapting to price changes
  • Customizable stop-loss and take-profit provide transparent and controllable risk parameters

Weaknesses

  • Lacks dynamic position sizing; fixed lot size ignores account equity changes
  • No ranging market filter, leading to frequent stop-outs during sideways markets
  • Missing money management features like max position count and drawdown protection
  • No spread filter, making stop-loss and take-profit potentially unreasonable during high-spread conditions
  • Deleting and re-placing pending orders every bar may increase trading costs and slippage risk

Possible improvements

  • Add ATR or volatility-based dynamic adjustment for pending order distance and stop-loss/take-profit to adapt to varying market volatility
  • Incorporate a trend filter (e.g., moving average direction) or oscillator to avoid frequent entries during ranging markets
  • Implement equity-proportional dynamic position sizing to improve capital efficiency and safety

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Fixed lot size without equity-proportional sizing can lead to significant losses with oversized positions
  • Lack of ranging market filter may cause cumulative losses from frequent stop-outs during choppy conditions
  • OCO mechanism may fail to delete the opposite pending order in extreme market conditions such as price gaps
