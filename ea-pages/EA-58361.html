StellarLite_ICT_EA

This EA implements ICT (Inner Circle Trader) concepts — specifically the Silver Bullet and 2022 Model strategies — for the Stellar Lite 5K prop firm challenge. It uses higher timeframe MA bias, liquidity sweeps, market structure shifts, fair value gaps, and NDOG/NWOG detection to find entries, with partial take-profits at 1:1, 2:1, and 3:1 R:R and trailing stop management.

Strategy analysis

The strategy is based on ICT concepts, using a higher timeframe (H1) 200-period SMA to determine trend direction, only trading in alignment with the trend. Entry requires five simultaneous conditions: a liquidity sweep (price piercing a key liquidity level then reversing), market structure shift (MSS confirming trend reversal), a fair value gap (FVG providing the entry zone), NDOG/NWOG confirmation (the current candle's range is within an ATR-based threshold), and price retracing into the FVG zone. The Silver Bullet strategy only trades during the NY AM Killzone (10:00-11:00), while the 2022 Model trades any time but uses inverse liquidity sweeps (inducement) for additional confirmation. Exits use partial take-profits: 50% closed at TP1 with SL moved to breakeven, 25% closed at TP2 with a trailing stop activated, and the remaining 25% held to TP3. Position sizing is based on a fixed risk percentage (0.25%) with lot size automatically calculated from the stop-loss distance. Money management enforces a 10% total drawdown and 5% daily drawdown hard limit, complying with prop firm challenge rules.

Strengths

  • Strict money management: 0.25% risk per trade, 10% total drawdown and 5% daily drawdown limits fully compliant with prop firm challenge rules
  • Well-structured partial take-profit: closes 50% at TP1 locking profit and moving SL to breakeven, then 25% at TP2 activating trailing stop, balancing profit protection with trend following
  • Multi-condition entry filter: requires liquidity sweep + MSS + FVG + NDOG/NWOG five-fold confirmation, significantly reducing false signal entries
  • Higher timeframe trend filter: uses H1 200 SMA for directional bias, avoiding counter-trend trades

Weaknesses

  • ATR handle memory leak: CheckNDOG_NWOG creates and releases an iATR handle on every call, causing resource waste and potential performance issues with frequent invocations
  • Overly simplified liquidity detection: FindNearestLiquidityLevel only finds the highest/lowest point in the lookback period without identifying true structural swing highs/lows, easily producing false signals
  • Flawed MSS (Market Structure Shift) logic: only compares close/high/low of the last 3 bars without considering the magnitude or time span of structural breaks, prone to misjudgment
  • Inverted FVG detection logic: the buy FVG condition checks rates[i].high < rates[i-2].low, which describes a bearish gap rather than a bullish FVG, inconsistent with the actual ICT definition
  • Daily drawdown baseline uses equity instead of balance, inconsistent with most prop firm rules which typically use the day's opening balance
  • Single position limitation: positionInfo.SelectByMagic only checks for existence of a position with the magic number, unable to manage multiple simultaneous positions

Possible improvements

  • Move ATR handle initialization to OnInit() to avoid repeated creation/destruction in CheckNDOG_NWOG, improving performance and preventing resource leaks
  • Improve liquidity level detection: implement proper swing high/low identification (e.g., fractal or ZigZag-based methods) instead of simply finding extremums in the lookback period
  • Fix FVG detection logic: bullish FVG should be rates[i-2].high < rates[i].low (gap between bar i-2 high and bar i low unfilled by the middle bar), consistent with ICT definition
  • Enhance MSS detection: add magnitude thresholds for structural breaks (e.g., ATR multiple beyond previous structure point) and consider longer time-span structural shifts
  • Support multi-position management: use position iteration instead of single-position SelectByMagic, allowing the strategy to manage multiple trade signals simultaneously

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Inverted FVG detection logic may cause entries in the wrong direction, leading to unnecessary losses
  • Overly simplistic MSS detection may produce false structural break signals, increasing erroneous trade probability
  • Repeated ATR indicator handle creation in CheckNDOG_NWOG on every tick may cause resource exhaustion in high-frequency tick environments
  • Daily drawdown baseline uses equity instead of opening balance, which may violate actual prop firm evaluation rules
  • String comparison for time filtering (timeStr >= SB_StartTime) may fail across midnight boundaries
