Gaps

This EA trades price gaps (window gaps) that occur between candle closes and opens, entering positions in the direction of gap fill expectation. It identifies gaps on the current timeframe and places market orders expecting the gap to be closed by price retracement.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on the gap fill theory, which posits that price tends to retrace and fill gaps that occur between candle closes and opens. Entry conditions detect a significant price gap between the current candle open and the previous candle close — an upward gap triggers a sell order expecting gap fill, while a downward gap triggers a buy order. Exit conditions typically involve take-profit at the gap fill level, with stop-loss placed at a calculated distance beyond the gap. Position sizing generally uses fixed lot sizes or a percentage of account equity. Money management may include basic stop-loss and take-profit ratio controls but lacks sophisticated money management modules. The core assumption is that gaps have a high probability of being filled, but in strong trending markets gaps may persist without being filled, leading to losses.

Strengths

  • Gap fill is a statistically validated phenomenon with relatively high win rate across most market conditions
  • Strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and verify, with few parameters for optimization
  • Entry conditions are objective and well-defined, free from subjective judgment, suitable for systematic trading

Weaknesses

  • In strong trending markets gaps may not fill, leading to significant losses
  • Lacks multi-timeframe confirmation, potentially trading false gaps on the wrong timeframe
  • Does not account for the erosion of gap fill profits by trading costs and slippage

Possible improvements

  • Add multi-timeframe gap confirmation mechanism to filter low-quality gap signals
  • Incorporate a trend filter to avoid counter-trend gap fill trading during strong trends

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Gap non-fill risk: In strong trends, gaps may widen instead of filling, triggering stop-losses
  • Weekend gap risk: Monday opening gaps may suffer severe slippage due to low liquidity, making stop-losses ineffective
