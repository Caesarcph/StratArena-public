This EA trades based on the MovingAverage_FN custom indicator, which applies a configurable Fourier noise filter to a smoothed moving average. It opens buy positions when the filtered MA turns upward and sell positions when it turns downward, with optional position closing on reverse signals. 摘要

该EA基于MovingAverage_FN自定义指标进行交易，该指标对平滑移动平均线应用可配置的傅里叶噪声滤波器。当滤波后的MA向上转折时开多仓，向下转折时开空仓，并在反转信号时可选平仓。这是一个使用多种平滑方法和基于傅里叶降噪滤波器的趋势跟踪系统。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于傅里叶噪声滤波的移动平均线趋势跟踪。入场条件为：当指标值在SignalBar处呈现上升趋势（Value[0]>Value[1]且Value[1]<Value[2]）时开多仓，当指标值呈下降趋势（Value[0]<Value[1]且Value[1]>Value[2]）时开空仓。出场条件包括止损和止盈（默认1000点和2000点），以及反向信号时的平仓权限控制。仓位管理通过MM参数实现，负值代表固定手数，正值代表按存款比例计算仓位。资金管理采用百分比或固定手数模式，但没有加仓或减仓的动态调整机制。策略使用CXMA类支持多达10种平滑方法（SMA、EMA、SMMA、LWMA、JJMA、JurX、ParMA、T3、VIDYA、AMA），并通过filter_mode枚举选择4到60阶傅里叶滤波器，具有较强的降噪能力。

Summary

This EA trades based on the MovingAverage_FN custom indicator, which applies a configurable Fourier noise filter to a smoothed moving average. It opens buy positions when the filtered MA turns upward and sell positions when it turns downward, with optional position closing on reverse signals. The strategy is a trend-following system using multiple smoothing methods and a Fourier-based noise reduction filter.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is trend following based on a Fourier noise-filtered moving average. Entry conditions are: open a long position when the indicator values show an uptrend at the SignalBar (Value[0]>Value[1] and Value[1]<Value[2]), and open a short position when the values show a downtrend (Value[0]<Value[1] and Value[1]>Value[2]). Exit conditions include StopLoss (default 1000 points) and TakeProfit (default 2000 points), plus optional closing on reverse signals controlled by BuyPosClose and SellPosClose parameters. Position sizing is managed through the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the deposit per deal. The money management uses either percentage-based or fixed lot modes, but lacks dynamic position adjustment mechanisms like scaling in or out. The strategy leverages the CXMA class supporting up to 10 smoothing methods (SMA, EMA, SMMA, LWMA, JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, AMA) and selects Fourier filter orders from 4 to 60 via the filter_mode enumeration, providing strong noise reduction capability.

优势

  • 傅里叶滤波器有效降低市场噪声，减少虚假信号
  • 支持10种平滑方法和多种价格类型，灵活度高
  • 独立的买卖开仓和平仓权限控制，便于精细化策略管理

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点的默认值较大，可能在剧烈行情中承受较大回撤
  • 缺乏加仓、减仓或移动止损机制，资金管理较为单一
  • 依赖iCustom调用外部指标MovingAverage_FN，增加了部署复杂性和依赖性

改进建议

  • 增加移动止损功能以锁定浮动利润，降低回撤风险
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段限制，避免高成本和低流动性时段交易

Strengths

  • Fourier noise filter effectively reduces market noise and minimizes false signals
  • Supports 10 smoothing methods and multiple price types, offering high flexibility
  • Independent buy/sell open and close permission controls allow fine-grained strategy management

Weaknesses

  • Default StopLoss of 1000 points and TakeProfit of 2000 points are large, potentially exposing to significant drawdowns during volatile markets
  • Lacks scaling in/out or trailing stop mechanisms, making money management relatively simplistic
  • Depends on iCustom call to external MovingAverage_FN indicator, increasing deployment complexity and dependency

Improvements

  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in floating profits and reduce drawdown risk
  • Add spread filter and trading session restrictions to avoid high-cost and low-liquidity periods

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 固定止损点数不适应不同品种的波动率差异，可能在低波动品种上止损过宽
  • MM参数为负值时直接作为手数使用，缺少最小手数和最大手数限制校验
  • 无点差过滤机制，在点差较大时可能仍会开仓

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed stop loss in points does not adapt to volatility differences across instruments, potentially being too wide for low-volatility assets
  • When MM is negative, it is used directly as lot size without minimum/maximum lot validation
  • No spread filter mechanism, so positions may still be opened during high-spread conditions
