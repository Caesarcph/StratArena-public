摘要

Hercules A.T.C. 2006 是为2006年自动交易锦标赛开发的趋势跟踪EA。它使用均线交叉逻辑结合动量过滤器，在主趋势方向上入场交易，采用固定手数和止损/止盈管理。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略基于均线交叉系统，通过快速均线与慢速均线的金叉和死叉来判断趋势方向并产生交易信号。入场条件要求均线交叉确认，同时可能结合ADX或类似动量指标过滤假信号，只在趋势明确时开仓。出场条件包括固定的止损和止盈，以及均线反向交叉时平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，未根据账户余额或波动率动态调整。资金管理方面相对简单，每次交易使用相同手数，缺乏风险比例控制机制，这在锦标赛环境中常见但长期使用风险较高。

Summary

Hercules A.T.C. 2006 is a trend-following Expert Advisor built for the Automated Trading Championship 2006. It uses moving average crossover logic combined with momentum filters to enter trades in the direction of the prevailing trend, with fixed lot sizing and stop-loss/take-profit management.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on a moving average crossover system, generating trade signals through golden cross and death cross of fast and slow moving averages. Entry conditions require confirmation from the MA crossover, potentially combined with ADX or similar momentum indicators to filter out false signals, only opening positions when the trend is clearly established. Exit conditions include fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels, as well as closing positions when the moving averages cross in the opposite direction. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic adjustment based on account balance or volatility. Money management is relatively simple, using the same lot size for every trade without risk-proportional control, which is common in championship environments but risky for long-term use.

优势

  • 趋势跟踪逻辑清晰易懂，均线交叉是经过验证的经典策略
  • 结合动量过滤器减少假信号，提高入场质量
  • 设有止损和止盈，具备基本的风险控制框架

劣势

  • 固定手数不随账户规模调整，长期使用风险不均
  • 均线交叉在震荡市中频繁产生虚假信号导致连续亏损
  • 缺乏仓位检查和新K线检查，可能重复开仓或过度交易

改进建议

  • 增加动态仓位管理，根据账户余额和ATR波动率调整手数
  • 添加震荡市过滤器和扩展止损机制，减少盘整期的虚假信号

Strengths

  • Clear and easy-to-understand trend-following logic based on proven MA crossover strategy
  • Momentum filter reduces false signals and improves entry quality
  • Has stop-loss and take-profit settings providing basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot size doesn't adjust with account size, creating uneven risk over long-term use
  • MA crossover generates frequent false signals in ranging markets leading to consecutive losses
  • Lacks position check and new bar check, potentially causing duplicate positions or overtrading

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account balance and ATR volatility to adjust lot size
  • Add ranging market filter and extended stop mechanisms to reduce false signals during consolidation
