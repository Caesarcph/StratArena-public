摘要

该EA基于彩色PEMA数字信号进行交易，配合时间过滤器和双工马丁格尔资金管理恢复系统。当PEMA信号触发时开仓，通过时段过滤控制交易时间，亏损后加倍手数以恢复资金。双工架构暗示两个并行交易通道或双向交易各有独立恢复逻辑。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于彩色PEMA（周期指数移动平均线）的数字信号进行趋势判断入场。PEMA信号通过颜色变化或数字交叉来识别趋势转折点，产生买入或卖出信号。入场条件为PEMA数字信号发生方向性变化，且当前时间在允许的交易时段内。出场条件包括止盈止损以及反向信号出现时平仓。仓位管理采用马丁格尔加倍恢复机制，即在亏损后下一单手数加倍以弥补损失。双工(Duplex)设计意味着可能同时处理多空两个方向的交易，各自独立运行资金管理恢复逻辑。时间过滤器(Tm)限制了交易时段，避免低流动性或高波动时段交易。MMRec（资金管理恢复）是核心风险模块，通过几何级数加仓试图在下一笔盈利交易中覆盖前序亏损。

Summary

This EA trades based on a Color PEMA (Period EMA) Digit signal with time filtering and a duplex Martingale money management recovery system. It opens positions on PEMA crossover/digit signals, applies time-based session filters, and doubles lot size after losses to recover equity. The duplex architecture suggests two parallel trading channels or bidirectional trading with independent recovery logic.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on Color PEMA (Period Exponential Moving Average) digit signals for trend identification and trade entry. PEMA signals identify trend reversal points through color changes or digit crossovers, generating buy or sell signals. Entry conditions require a directional change in the PEMA digit signal combined with the current time being within the allowed trading session filter. Exit conditions include take profit, stop loss, and position closure on reverse signal appearance. Position sizing employs a Martingale doubling recovery mechanism where the lot size is doubled after each loss to recover equity. The Duplex design suggests simultaneous handling of both long and short trading directions, each running its own independent money management recovery logic. The time filter (Tm) restricts trading to specific sessions, avoiding low liquidity or high volatility periods. MMRec (Money Management Recovery) is the core risk module, attempting to cover previous losses through geometric progression position sizing on the next profitable trade.

优势

  • 双工架构允许同时处理多空方向，提高交易机会覆盖率
  • 时间过滤器有效避免非理想交易时段，降低低流动性风险
  • PEMA信号相对传统EMA更平滑，减少虚假信号

劣势

  • 马丁格尔资金管理在连续亏损时会导致手数呈几何级数增长，存在爆仓风险
  • 双工设计增加了策略复杂度，多空方向同时运行马丁格尔可能加速资金消耗
  • 缺乏对点差和市场条件的动态过滤，在高点差环境下可能频繁触发止损

改进建议

  • 将马丁格尔恢复机制替换为固定比例或抗马丁格尔(Anti-Martingale)策略，在盈利时加仓而非亏损时加仓
  • 增加点差过滤器和市场波动性检查（如ATR阈值），避免在不利条件下入场

Strengths

  • Duplex architecture allows simultaneous handling of both long and short directions, improving trade opportunity coverage
  • Time filter effectively avoids non-ideal trading sessions, reducing low liquidity risk
  • PEMA signals are smoother than traditional EMA, reducing false signals

Weaknesses

  • Martingale money management causes lot sizes to grow geometrically during consecutive losses, creating account blowup risk
  • Duplex design increases strategy complexity; running Martingale on both directions simultaneously can accelerate capital depletion
  • Lacks dynamic spread and market condition filtering, potentially triggering stop losses frequently in high spread environments

Improvements

  • Replace Martingale recovery mechanism with fixed fractional or Anti-Martingale strategy, increasing position size on wins rather than losses
  • Add spread filter and market volatility check (e.g., ATR threshold) to avoid entering under unfavorable conditions

  • 双工马丁格尔同时运行两个方向的恢复逻辑，加速资金消耗
  • 缺乏最大回撤保护或账户权益熔断机制
