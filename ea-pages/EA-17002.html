摘要

The MasterMind 3是一个多周期EA，在较高时间框架上检测趋势方向，在较低时间框架上寻找入场信号。它结合移动平均线、RSI和MACD来识别多周期动量一致的共振区域，并通过移动止损和分批平仓来锁定利润并跟踪趋势。

策略分析

该EA的策略核心是跨时间框架的趋势共振交易。在较高时间框架（如H4或D1）上，通过长期和短期移动平均线的排列判断主趋势方向，只有在主趋势明确时才允许交易。入场信号在较低时间框架（如M15或H1）上生成，要求RSI处于超卖回调区（趋势向上时RSI回落至30-50区间）或超买回调区（趋势向下时RSI反弹至50-70区间），同时MACD柱状图出现方向一致的拐头信号。仓位管理采用固定手数或基于账户余额的百分比计算，每次交易风险控制在账户的1-2%以内。出场条件包括：固定止盈目标、反向均线交叉信号、以及动态移动止损。当价格朝有利方向移动一定点数后，EA会启动移动止损跟踪，并在达到第一目标位时分批平仓一半仓位，剩余仓位用更宽松的移动止损继续跟踪趋势。资金管理方面，EA支持最大同时持仓数限制和每日最大亏损限制，以防止极端行情下的过度亏损。

Summary

The MasterMind 3 is a multi-timeframe EA that combines trend detection on higher timeframes with entry signals on lower timeframes. It uses a blend of moving averages, RSI, and MACD to identify confluence zones where momentum aligns across timeframes. The EA manages positions with trailing stops and partial closes to lock in profits while riding trends.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is cross-timeframe trend confluence trading. On higher timeframes (such as H4 or D1), the main trend direction is determined by the alignment of long-term and short-term moving averages, and trades are only permitted when the main trend is clearly established. Entry signals are generated on lower timeframes (such as M15 or H1), requiring RSI to be in a pullback zone (RSI falling back to 30-50 in an uptrend, or bouncing to 50-70 in a downtrend) while the MACD histogram shows a directional turn consistent with the higher timeframe trend. Position sizing uses either fixed lots or a percentage of account balance calculation, keeping each trade's risk within 1-2% of the account. Exit conditions include fixed take-profit targets, reverse moving average crossover signals, and a dynamic trailing stop. Once price moves favorably by a specified number of points, the EA activates a trailing stop and partially closes half the position at the first profit target, letting the remainder ride the trend with a wider trailing stop. For money management, the EA supports maximum simultaneous position limits and daily maximum loss limits to prevent excessive drawdowns during extreme market conditions.

优势

  • 多时间框架共振确认有效过滤假信号，提高入场质量
  • 分批平仓机制兼顾了锁定利润和跟踪趋势的需求
  • 每日最大亏损限制和最大持仓数控制提供了账户级别的风险保护
  • RSI回调入场而非追涨杀跌，能获得更好的入场价格
  • 移动止损机制在趋势行情中能有效保护浮盈

劣势

  • 多时间框架分析增加了逻辑复杂度，参数优化空间大容易导致过拟合
  • 在震荡行情中均线和MACD信号频繁切换可能产生连续亏损
  • 缺少点差过滤机制，在流动性差的时间段可能以不利价格成交
  • 固定点数止损未考虑市场波动率变化，在高低波动期风险暴露不均
  • 分批平仓后剩余仓位的止损可能过宽导致回吐过多利润

改进建议

  • 将固定点数止损替换为基于ATR的动态止损，使风险暴露随市场波动率自适应调整
  • 添加点差过滤和市场时段过滤，避免在流动性差和点差扩大的时段交易

Strengths

  • Multi-timeframe confluence confirmation effectively filters false signals and improves entry quality
  • Partial close mechanism balances profit locking with trend riding capability
  • Daily maximum loss limit and maximum position count provide account-level risk protection
  • RSI pullback entries avoid chasing price, yielding better entry prices
  • Trailing stop mechanism effectively protects floating profits during trending markets

Weaknesses

  • Multi-timeframe analysis increases logic complexity, and the large parameter optimization space can lead to overfitting
  • In choppy markets, frequent MA and MACD signal switching may produce consecutive losses
  • Lack of spread filter may result in unfavorable fills during low-liquidity periods
  • Fixed-pip stop loss doesn't account for market volatility changes, creating uneven risk exposure across volatility regimes
  • The wider stop on remaining position after partial close may give back too much profit

Improvements

  • Replace fixed-pip stop loss with ATR-based dynamic stops to adapt risk exposure to market volatility
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid trading during low-liquidity and wide-spread periods
