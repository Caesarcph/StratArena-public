摘要

该EA基于重心指标(CenterOfGravity)进行交易，这是一种振荡类指标，用于识别超买和超卖状态。当信号线上穿时开多仓，下穿时开空仓，配有可配置的止损止盈水平。EA使用自定义重心指标句柄，在指定时间周期上通过新K线检测来处理交易信号。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略基于CenterOfGravity指标，这是一种均值回归型振荡器，通过计算价格的重心来判断市场的超买超卖状态。入场条件为：当指标缓冲区1的值从2变为1时产生买入信号（信号线上穿上行线），当值从1变为2时产生卖出信号（信号线下穿）。出场条件依赖于反向信号触发平仓以及预设的止损止盈点位。仓位管理采用固定手数或基于存款比例的资金管理方式，通过MM参数控制，负值表示固定手数，正值表示存款占比。EA使用IsNewBar机制确保每个K线只计算一次信号，避免重复开仓。同时提供了买入/卖出开关以及平仓权限开关，允许灵活控制交易方向和出场行为。

Summary

This EA trades based on the Center of Gravity indicator, which is an oscillator-type indicator that identifies overbought and oversold conditions. It opens buy positions when the signal line crosses upward and sell positions when it crosses downward, with configurable stop loss and take profit levels. The EA uses a custom CenterOfGravity indicator handle and processes signals on a specified timeframe with new bar detection.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the Center of Gravity indicator, a mean reversion oscillator that identifies overbought and oversold market conditions by calculating the center of gravity of price. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when buffer 1 value transitions from 2 to 1 (signal line crossing upward), and a sell signal is generated when the value transitions from 1 to 2 (signal line crossing downward). Exit conditions rely on reverse signal triggers for position closure plus configurable stop loss and take profit levels in points. Position sizing uses either fixed lot size or deposit-percentage-based money management controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lots and positive values represent deposit share. The EA employs the IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are calculated only once per bar, preventing duplicate entries. It also provides buy/sell permission switches and close position toggles for flexible control over trading direction and exit behavior.

优势

  • 使用新K线检测机制，避免同一根K线重复开仓，减少过度交易风险
  • 止损止盈参数可配置，默认1:2的风险回报比合理
  • 提供买入/卖出/平仓权限开关，交易控制灵活度高
  • 信号线平滑处理减少了虚假信号的产生
  • 支持多种价格类型和均线方法，策略适应性较强

劣势

  • 缺乏点差过滤机制，在高位差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • 没有仓位检查逻辑，可能在已有同方向持仓时重复开仓
  • 指标依赖自定义CenterOfGravity指标，若该指标不可用则EA完全失效
  • 止损止盈以点数计算，未考虑市场波动率（ATR）动态调整
  • 缺少最大回撤控制和最大持仓数量限制

改进建议

  • 添加点差过滤和最大持仓数量检查，提高交易安全性
  • 引入ATR动态止损止盈，使风险管理适应市场波动率变化

Strengths

  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate entries on the same bar, reducing overtrading risk
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit with a default 1:2 risk-reward ratio
  • Buy/sell/close permission switches provide flexible trade control
  • Signal line smoothing reduces false signal generation
  • Supports multiple price types and MA methods for strategy adaptability

Weaknesses

  • No spread filter, which may lead to unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • No position check logic, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Depends on a custom CenterOfGravity indicator; EA fails completely if the indicator is unavailable
  • Stop loss and take profit are fixed in points without ATR-based dynamic adjustment
  • Lacks maximum drawdown control and maximum position count limits

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and maximum position count check to improve trading safety
  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit for volatility-adaptive risk management
