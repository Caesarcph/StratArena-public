Strategy analysis

This EA employs a scalping strategy focused on capturing small price movements on lower timeframes. Entry conditions are likely based on short-term momentum indicators or price breakout signals, entering trades when rapid price movement is detected. Exit conditions typically feature tight take-profit targets and relatively close stop-loss levels to maintain a high win rate. Position sizing may use fixed lot sizes or a simple proportion based on account balance. Money management relies on high-frequency trading to accumulate profits since each individual trade yields a small gain. The core risk of this strategy lies in spread and slippage eroding profits, especially during periods of low market liquidity which can lead to consecutive losses.

Strengths

  • Scalping strategy can profit frequently in ranging markets without requiring a directional trend
  • Small take-profit targets lead to a relatively high win rate and lower psychological pressure
  • Suitable for lower timeframe trading with high capital utilization efficiency

Weaknesses

  • Scalping is heavily impacted by spread and slippage; trading costs can erode profits significantly
  • High-frequency trading demands fast execution; VPS latency can lead to losses
  • Lacks trend filtering mechanism, which may cause frequent stop-losses in strong trending markets

Possible improvements

  • Add a spread filter to pause trading when the spread is too wide to protect profits
  • Add a trend filter indicator (e.g., EMA200) to prohibit opening positions against the prevailing trend

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Caution

  • Scalping strategy lacks a spread filter, which may cause continuous losses in high-spread environments
  • No explicit new bar check mechanism, potentially leading to repeated position openings on the same bar
