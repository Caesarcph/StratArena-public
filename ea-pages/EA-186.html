摘要

该EA使用双MACD交叉信号生成交易入场，并采用马丁格尔仓位管理策略，在亏损后增加手数。它基于MACD柱状图拐点并由第二个MACD确认来开仓做多或做空，设有固定止损和止盈水平。马丁格尔组件在连续亏损时加大手数以试图回本。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念是利用两个不同参数的MACD指标进行交叉确认入场，属于动量反转类策略。入场条件为：MACD1柱状图出现拐点（t[0]>t[1]且t[1]<t[2]表示由跌转涨做多，反之做空），同时MACD2的当前值需与前一值方向一致确认（k[1]>k[0]做多，k[1]<k[0]做空）。出场条件完全依赖固定止损（500点）和止盈（1500点），没有移动止损或其他动态出场机制。仓位管理采用马丁格尔策略，通过Martingail类对象控制，首笔手数由DML参数决定，连续亏损后按Ud参数加倍手数。资金管理方面，EA使用全局变量保存和恢复马丁格尔状态（GVarSet/GVarGet），确保EA重启后能继续之前的马丁格尔序列。值得注意的是，EA仅在无持仓时开仓，且过滤了tick_volume>1的报价，试图只在新K线开始时交易。

Summary

This EA uses dual MACD crossover signals to generate trade entries and applies a martingale position sizing strategy that increases lot size after losing trades. It opens buy or sell positions based on MACD histogram turning points confirmed by a second MACD, with fixed stop loss and take profit levels. The martingale component doubles (or scales) the lot size on consecutive losses to recover previous losses.

Strategy Analysis

The EA's strategy concept uses two MACD indicators with different parameters for crossover confirmation entries, belonging to a momentum-reversal category strategy. Entry conditions: MACD1 histogram shows a turning point (t[0]>t[1] and t[1]<t[2] signals bottom reversal for buy, opposite for sell), while MACD2 current value must confirm direction (k[1]>k[0] for buy, k[1]<k[0] for sell). Exit conditions rely entirely on fixed stop loss (500 points) and take profit (1500 points), with no trailing stop or other dynamic exit mechanism. Position sizing employs a martingale strategy controlled by the Martingail class object, with the initial lot determined by the DML parameter, and lot size scaling up after consecutive losses based on the Ud parameter. For money management, the EA uses global variables to save and restore martingale state (GVarSet/GVarGet), ensuring the martingale sequence continues after EA restarts. Notably, the EA only opens positions when no position exists and filters out ticks with volume >1, attempting to trade only on new bar beginnings.

优势

  • 双MACD确认减少虚假信号，提高入场质量
  • 固定止损止盈比例1:3，风险收益比合理
  • 使用全局变量持久化马丁格尔状态，EA重启不丢失序列
  • tick_volume过滤减少重复报价噪音
  • OnTester自定义优化指标考虑了最大连续亏损和盈亏比

劣势

  • 马丁格尔策略在连续亏损时指数级增加手数，可能导致爆仓
  • 无新K线检测机制，tick_volume>1过滤不可靠且可能在回测中失效
  • 止损500点相对较小，容易在波动行情中被扫损后触发马丁格尔加倍
  • 仅允许单持仓，马丁格尔加倍后风险集中无分散
  • 缺少点差过滤，在点差较大时入场可能不利
  • HistoryOrdersTotal索引逻辑可能越界或取到错误订单

改进建议

  • 替换马丁格尔策略为固定比例或凯利公式仓位管理，避免指数级风险增长
  • 添加真正的新K线检测函数替代tick_volume过滤，确保每根K线只交易一次
  • 添加点差过滤和最大连续亏损停止机制，保护账户资金

Strengths

  • Dual MACD confirmation reduces false signals and improves entry quality
  • Fixed SL:TP ratio of 1:3 provides a reasonable risk-reward ratio
  • Global variables persist martingale state across EA restarts without losing sequence
  • Tick volume filtering reduces noise from repetitive quotes
  • Custom OnTester metric accounts for max consecutive loss and profit/loss ratio for optimization

Weaknesses

  • Martingale strategy exponentially increases lot size on consecutive losses, risking account blowup
  • No proper new bar detection; tick_volume>1 filter is unreliable and may fail in backtesting
  • 500-point stop loss is relatively tight, easily triggered in volatile markets then activating martingale doubling
  • Single position only concentrates all martingale risk without diversification
  • No spread filter means entries may be unfavorable during high-spread conditions
  • HistoryOrdersTotal indexing logic may cause out-of-bounds or reference wrong orders

Improvements

  • Replace martingale with fixed fractional or Kelly criterion position sizing to avoid exponential risk growth
  • Add proper new bar detection function instead of tick_volume filtering to ensure one trade per bar
  • Add spread filter and maximum consecutive loss stop mechanism to protect account equity

  • No maximum lot size or maximum loss limit, creating uncapped risk exposure
  • History order indexing logic is fragile and may reference wrong orders causing incorrect decisions
