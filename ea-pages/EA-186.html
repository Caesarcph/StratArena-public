摘要

该EA使用双MACD交叉信号作为入场条件，并采用马丁格尔仓位管理策略，在亏损后加倍下注。它同时只持有一个仓位，依赖固定止损和止盈出场，马丁格尔模块通过全局变量在重启间持久化状态。

策略分析

策略概念基于双MACD指标的交叉信号判断趋势转折点，快MACD（5/20）和慢MACD（10/15）需同时确认方向才开仓。入场条件为快MACD主线发生转折（t[0]>t[1]且t[1]<t[2]表示向上转折）且慢MACD方向一致（k[1]>k[0]表示上升），做空则相反。出场条件完全依赖固定止损（500点）和止盈（1500点），没有移动止损或其他动态出场机制。仓位管理采用马丁格尔策略，通过Martingail类实现，初始手数由DML参数控制，亏损后按Ud参数设定的倍数加倍仓位。资金管理方面，马丁格尔策略在连续亏损后会指数级增加仓位，风险极高；虽然有止损保护单笔亏损，但加倍后的仓位可能导致爆仓。该EA还包含OnTester函数用于回测评估，通过利润与亏损比除以最大连续亏损来评分。

Summary

This EA uses dual MACD crossover signals for trade entries and applies a Martingale position sizing system that increases lot size after losses. It only opens one position at a time and relies on fixed stop-loss and take-profit for exits, with the Martingale module persisting state via global variables across restarts.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on dual MACD crossover signals to identify trend reversal points, requiring both a fast MACD (5/20) and a slow MACD (10/15) to confirm direction simultaneously. Entry conditions require the fast MACD main line to show a reversal (t[0]>t[1] and t[1]<t[2] indicates upward reversal) with the slow MACD confirming the direction (k[1]>k[0] indicates rising), and vice versa for shorts. Exit conditions rely entirely on fixed stop-loss (500 points) and take-profit (1500 points), with no trailing stop or other dynamic exit mechanisms. Position sizing employs a Martingale system implemented via the Martingail class, where the initial lot is controlled by the DML parameter and the lot is multiplied after losses based on the Ud (doubling count) parameter. Money management is extremely risky as the Martingale strategy exponentially increases position size after consecutive losses; while stop-losses protect individual trades, the doubled positions can lead to account blowout. The EA also includes an OnTester function for backtesting evaluation, scoring by dividing the profit-to-loss ratio by the maximum consecutive loss series.

优势

  • 双MACD确认机制减少了假信号，快慢指标交叉验证提高了入场质量
  • 止损止盈比例3:1（1500:500），单笔交易的盈亏比合理
  • 马丁格尔状态通过全局变量持久化，EA重启后不会丢失加倍状态
  • OnTester函数提供自定义回测评估指标，便于策略优化

劣势

  • 马丁格尔策略在连续亏损后指数级加大仓位，存在爆仓风险，是最危险的资金管理方式之一
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时段过滤，在流动性差或点差大的时候仍会开仓
  • 仅依赖固定止损止盈出场，没有移动止损机制来锁定浮动利润
  • tick_volume>1的过滤条件不够可靠，无法确保每个K线只交易一次
  • 历史订单逻辑复杂且脆弱，通过比较最后两笔订单的开仓价来判断盈亏容易出错

改进建议

  • 替换马丁格尔仓位管理为固定比例或凯利公式，或至少设置最大加倍次数和最大仓位上限
  • 添加新K线检测逻辑替代tick_volume过滤，确保每个K线只执行一次交易逻辑
  • 增加点差过滤和交易时段控制，避免在不利条件下开仓

Strengths

  • Dual MACD confirmation mechanism reduces false signals, with fast and slow indicator cross-validation improving entry quality
  • Stop-loss to take-profit ratio of 3:1 (1500:500) provides a reasonable risk-reward ratio per trade
  • Martingale state is persisted via global variables, so the EA does not lose doubling state after restarts
  • OnTester function provides a custom backtesting evaluation metric for strategy optimization

Weaknesses

  • Martingale strategy exponentially increases position size after consecutive losses, carrying blowout risk and being one of the most dangerous money management approaches
  • No spread filter or trading session filter, so orders can be opened during low liquidity or high spread periods
  • Exits rely solely on fixed stop-loss and take-profit with no trailing stop mechanism to lock in floating profits
  • The tick_volume>1 filter is unreliable and cannot guarantee only one trade per bar
  • History order logic is complex and fragile, comparing the last two order open prices to determine profit/loss is error-prone

Improvements

  • Replace Martingale position sizing with fixed fractional or Kelly criterion, or at minimum set a maximum doubling count and maximum position cap
  • Add new bar detection logic to replace the tick_volume filter, ensuring trade logic executes only once per bar
  • Add spread filter and trading session control to avoid opening positions under unfavorable conditions
