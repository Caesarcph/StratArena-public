摘要

该EA使用DeMarker振荡指标来识别动量变化，并在DeMarker值显示动量增强时开仓。它结合基于成交量的仓位管理（第二版），根据市场成交量调整交易规模。策略在DeMarker显示超卖动量反转时买入，在超买动量反转时卖出。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略基于DeMarker振荡指标，DeMarker是一种衡量价格动量的技术指标，通过比较当前最高价与前一期最高价来评估买方压力。入场条件为：当DeMarker值从超卖区域（低于0.3或类似阈值）向上穿越时开多单，当DeMarker值从超买区域（高于0.7或类似阈值）向下穿越时开空单。出场条件依赖固定止盈止损或指标反向信号。仓位管理采用基于成交量的方法，第二版增加了成交量确认逻辑，只有在成交量放大时才开仓，以过滤假信号。资金管理方面，EA使用固定手数或根据账户余额比例计算手数，但没有明显的马丁格尔或网格成分。整体策略属于动量反转类型，在极端动量区域寻找反转机会，并利用成交量确认提高信号可靠性。

Summary

This EA uses the DeMarker oscillator indicator to identify momentum shifts and enters positions when the DeMarker value signals gaining momentum. It incorporates volume-based position sizing (volume 2) to scale trade size according to market volume. The strategy buys when DeMarker indicates oversold momentum reversal and sells when overbought momentum reversal is detected.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the DeMarker oscillator, a technical indicator that measures price momentum by comparing the current high price with the previous period's high to assess buying pressure. Entry conditions: open a long position when the DeMarker value crosses upward from the oversold zone (below approximately 0.3), and open a short position when the DeMarker value crosses downward from the overbought zone (above approximately 0.7). Exit conditions rely on fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels or indicator reversal signals. Position sizing employs a volume-based approach, where version 2 adds volume confirmation logic — trades are only opened when volume increases, filtering out false signals. Money management uses fixed lot sizes or calculates lots based on account balance proportion, with no martingale or grid components detected. The overall strategy is a momentum reversal type, seeking reversal opportunities at extreme momentum zones and using volume confirmation to improve signal reliability.

优势

  • 使用DeMarker指标结合成交量确认，双重过滤减少假信号
  • 在动量极端区域寻找反转，具有较高的盈亏比潜力
  • 策略逻辑清晰，参数可调性强，便于优化和适应不同市场

劣势

  • DeMarker在趋势行情中容易产生连续错误信号，导致频繁止损
  • 成交量确认逻辑在低流动性时段可能失效，产生错误判断
  • 缺乏对整体趋势方向的过滤，可能在逆势中频繁交易

改进建议

  • 增加大周期趋势过滤器（如均线方向），避免逆势交易
  • 加入动态止损和移动止盈，根据市场波动率调整出场水平

Strengths

  • Dual filtering with DeMarker indicator and volume confirmation reduces false signals
  • Seeking reversals at extreme momentum zones offers high reward-to-risk ratio potential
  • Clear strategy logic with adjustable parameters facilitates optimization and market adaptation

Weaknesses

  • DeMarker tends to generate consecutive false signals during trending markets, leading to frequent stop-loss hits
  • Volume confirmation logic may fail during low-liquidity sessions, producing incorrect judgments
  • Lack of overall trend direction filter may cause frequent counter-trend trading

Improvements

  • Add a higher timeframe trend filter (e.g., moving average direction) to avoid counter-trend trades
  • Implement dynamic stop-loss and trailing take-profit adjusted by market volatility (ATR-based)
