Expert_ABC_WS_CCI

This EA uses a CCI (Commodity Channel Index) indicator smoothed by a Moving Average as its core signal filter within the MQL5 Standard Library Expert Advisor framework. It opens and closes positions based on threshold-based signal logic, employs no trailing stop, and uses fixed lot money management. The strategy relies on CCI momentum shifts confirmed by MA smoothing to generate trade signals.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on combining the CCI momentum indicator with MA smoothing to capture directional momentum shifts in the market. Entry conditions are triggered when the CCI (period 37 by default) generates a momentum value that is smoothed by a Moving Average (period 13 by default), and the composite signal strength exceeds the opening threshold (default 40 on a 0-100 scale). Exit conditions occur when the signal strength falls below the closing threshold (default 20), with optional stop loss and take profit levels that default to zero (disabled). Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach (default 0.1 lots) without dynamic adjustment based on account equity or volatility. Money management relies on the CMoneyFixedLot class configured with a fixed percentage (default 10%) and fixed lot size, lacking any dynamic risk scaling mechanism. The overall strategy is a momentum-following system within the MQL5 Standard Library framework, with clear logic but limited adaptability and no dynamic risk controls.

Strengths

  • Built on the MQL5 Standard Library framework with clean, well-structured code that is easy to maintain and extend
  • CCI + MA dual filtering mechanism smooths out CCI noise and reduces false signal triggers
  • Separate threshold values for opening and closing positions provide flexible entry/exit control
  • Modular design with decoupled signal, trailing, and money management components makes it easy to swap or upgrade individual modules

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss and take profit default to zero, creating unlimited loss risk with no basic risk protection
  • Fixed lot money management ignores account size and volatility, unable to dynamically adjust risk exposure
  • No trailing stop used (CTrailingNone), so floating profits cannot be locked in and winning trades may experience significant drawdowns
  • Lacks a strict new bar check mechanism (Expert_EveryTick=false is not the same as proper new bar detection), potentially processing signals repeatedly on the same bar
  • CCI period 37 and MA period 13 are not thoroughly optimized or validated, may not suit all market conditions

Possible improvements

  • Add non-zero default stop loss and take profit parameters, and implement ATR-based dynamic stop loss to adapt to market volatility
  • Replace CTrailingNone with a trailing stop module to lock in profits during trend continuation and reduce drawdown risk
  • Add strict new bar detection and spread filtering to avoid repeated position opening and high-cost trading under unfavorable conditions

Risk assessment

Overall risk: High risk

  • Stop loss and take profit both default to zero with no price protection, exposing each trade to unlimited loss potential
  • Fixed lot sizing does not adjust with account balance, causing risk ratio to increase during consecutive losses
  • Absence of trailing stop means profitable trades can fully retrace into loss territory
