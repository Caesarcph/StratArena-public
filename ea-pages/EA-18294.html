摘要

一个简单的反转剥头皮EA，在买入和卖出仓位之间交替交易。当一个仓位被止盈或止损平仓后，立即在相反方向开立新仓位。使用固定手数，可配置点数止盈和止损。

策略分析

策略概念是基于反转逻辑的持续交易：每当一笔交易被平仓后，立即在相反方向开仓，形成买卖交替的循环。入场条件完全依赖于上一笔交易的平仓方向——如果是买入平仓（DEAL_ENTRY_OUT + DEAL_TYPE_BUY），则下一笔开卖出；如果是卖出平仓，则下一笔开买入。出场条件仅依靠固定点数的止盈和止损，没有任何技术指标辅助判断。仓位管理采用固定手数（InpLots=0.1），不做任何加仓或减仓操作。资金管理方面存在严重缺陷，没有最大持仓限制、没有亏损累计控制、没有资金比例计算，仅依赖CheckVolume检查保证金是否充足。策略本质上是一个永远在场的反转系统，无论市场趋势如何都会持续交易。

Summary

A simple reversal scalping EA that alternates between buy and sell positions. After a position is closed by take-profit or stop-loss, it immediately opens a new position in the opposite direction. It uses fixed lot sizing with configurable take-profit and stop-loss in pips.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is a persistent reversal-based trading loop: whenever a position is closed, a new position is immediately opened in the opposite direction, creating a continuous buy-sell alternation. Entry conditions rely entirely on the direction of the previous closed trade — if a buy position is closed out (DEAL_ENTRY_OUT + DEAL_TYPE_BUY), the next trade opens a sell, and vice versa. Exit conditions use only fixed pip-based take-profit and stop-loss values with no technical indicator confirmation whatsoever. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (InpLots=0.1) with no scaling, martingale, or proportional sizing logic. Money management is severely lacking — there is no maximum position limit, no cumulative loss control, no percentage-of-equity sizing, relying solely on CTrade.CheckVolume to verify margin sufficiency. The strategy is essentially an always-in-market reversal system that trades continuously regardless of market trend or conditions.

优势

  • 策略逻辑极其简单，代码易于理解和维护
  • 使用标准库CTrade和CSymbolInfo，交易执行较为规范
  • 包含3/5位小数点的点值调整，适应不同报价精度
  • 交易前通过CheckVolume检查保证金充足性，避免因资金不足导致的订单失败

劣势

  • 完全没有技术指标或市场分析，纯粹依赖反转逻辑盲目交易
  • 没有点差过滤，剥头皮策略在高点差环境下极易亏损
  • 止损和止盈仅10点，点差很容易超过止盈目标导致持续亏损
  • 没有最大回撤控制或连续亏损限制，可能在剧烈行情中持续亏损
  • 没有新K线检查，每个tick都可能触发交易逻辑导致重复下单风险

改进建议

  • 添加技术指标过滤（如均线趋势方向、RSI超买超卖），避免逆势交易
  • 增加点差过滤和最大回撤控制，在不利市场条件下暂停交易
  • 添加新K线检查机制，防止在同一根K线上重复开仓

Strengths

  • Extremely simple strategy logic, code is easy to understand and maintain
  • Uses standard MQL5 Trade library classes (CTrade, CSymbolInfo) for proper trade execution
  • Includes point value adjustment for 3/5 digit brokers, handling different quote precisions correctly
  • Checks margin sufficiency via CheckVolume before placing orders, avoiding insufficient-fund errors

Weaknesses

  • No technical indicators or market analysis whatsoever — trades blindly based on pure reversal logic
  • No spread filter, which is critical for a scalping strategy that can easily lose money in high-spread conditions
  • Stop-loss and take-profit of only 10 pips mean spread can easily exceed the profit target, leading to consistent losses
  • No maximum drawdown control or consecutive loss limit, risking sustained losses during volatile markets
  • No new bar check, so trade logic could trigger on every tick creating risk of duplicate orders

Improvements

  • Add technical indicator filters (e.g., MA trend direction, RSI overbought/oversold) to avoid trading against the trend
  • Add spread filter and maximum drawdown control to pause trading under unfavorable market conditions
  • Add new bar check mechanism to prevent duplicate order placement on the same candle
