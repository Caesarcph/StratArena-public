摘要

该EA基于自定义Oracle指标进行交易，提供三种算法模式（突破、扭转、交叉）来检测趋势信号。根据Oracle指标信号线相对于主线或零水平的行为来开仓和平仓，并支持可配置的资金管理和止损止盈设置。

策略分析

该EA的策略核心是自定义Oracle指标，该指标产生主线和信号线两组数据，用于判断市场趋势方向。入场条件分为三种模式：breakdown模式检测信号线突破零水平，twist模式检测信号线方向变化，disposition模式检测信号线与主线的交叉。出场条件依赖固定的止损止盈设置以及反向信号触发平仓，当出现反向信号时若允许平仓则自动关闭对应仓位。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值代表固定手数，正值代表按存款比例计算仓位大小。资金管理采用百分比或固定手数方式，结合止损止盈进行风险控制，但缺少加仓、减仓或追踪止损等高级资金管理功能。

Summary

This EA trades based on the custom Oracle indicator, offering three algorithmic modes (breakdown, twist, disposition) to detect trend signals. It opens and closes positions based on the Oracle indicator's signal line behavior relative to its main line or zero level, with configurable money management and stop loss/take profit settings.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA revolves around the custom Oracle indicator, which generates a main line and a signal line to determine market trend direction. Entry conditions are split into three modes: breakdown mode detects when the signal line crosses the zero level, twist mode detects changes in the signal line's direction, and disposition mode detects crossovers between the signal line and the main line. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit settings, along with reverse signal triggers that close the corresponding position when permitted. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a percentage of the deposit. Money management uses either a percentage-based or fixed-lot approach combined with stop loss and take profit for risk control, but lacks advanced features such as scaling in/out or trailing stops.

优势

  • 提供三种不同的入场算法模式，策略灵活性较高
  • 支持按存款比例或固定手数两种仓位管理方式
  • 买卖开仓和平仓权限可独立控制，便于灵活配置
  • 使用新K线检测机制避免同根K线重复交易

劣势

  • 依赖自定义Oracle指标，指标逻辑不透明且可能存在重绘问题
  • 缺少追踪止损功能，盈利保护能力有限
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值较大，点值成本高且不适合所有品种
  • 未对持仓进行数量或风险敞口上限检查，存在过度交易风险

改进建议

  • 增加追踪止损功能以更好地保护已获利润
  • 添加持仓数量上限和最大风险敞口检查，防止过度交易
  • 增加点差过滤条件，避免在点差过大时开仓

Strengths

  • Offers three different entry algorithm modes, providing good strategic flexibility
  • Supports both percentage-of-deposit and fixed lot position sizing methods
  • Buy/sell open and close permissions can be independently controlled for flexible configuration
  • Uses new bar detection to prevent repeated trading on the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Relies on a custom Oracle indicator whose logic is opaque and may have repainting issues
  • Lacks trailing stop functionality, limiting profit protection capability
  • Default 1000-point SL and 2000-point TP are large and may not suit all instruments
  • No check on maximum number of positions or risk exposure, posing overtrading risk

Improvements

  • Add trailing stop functionality to better protect unrealized profits
  • Add maximum position count and risk exposure checks to prevent overtrading
  • Add spread filter to avoid opening positions when spread is too wide

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • Recount_参数设为true时指标可能重绘，导致信号回溯不可靠
  • 缺少持仓上限检查，极端行情可能连续开仓导致风险敞口过大
  • 固定止损止盈未考虑市场波动性变化，低波动时止损过宽、高波动时止损过窄

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • When Recount_ is set to true, the indicator may repaint, making signals unreliable in hindsight
  • No position limit check; extreme market conditions could lead to consecutive openings and excessive risk exposure
  • Fixed SL/TP does not account for market volatility changes—SL too wide in low volatility, too narrow in high volatility
