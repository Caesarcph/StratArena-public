摘要

该EA基于内包线(Insider Bar)K线形态结合锯齿价位确认系统进行交易。当价格突破前一根K线的高点或低点时，在内包线形态和时间过滤确认后开立多头或空头仓位，支持可选的止损和止盈设置。

策略分析

策略核心基于内包线(Inside Bar)K线形态识别，即当前K线的高低点完全包含在前一根K线范围内。系统定义了白色内包线（收盘高于开盘）和黑色内包线（收盘低于开盘），以及连续内包线形态（白色飞鸟和黑色飞鸟）作为过滤条件。入场条件为：买入信号需当前K线高点突破前一根高点、确认买入条件满足（当前低点触及锯齿买入价位或时间超过8点）、非黑色飞鸟和非白色内包线、且当前低点高于前一根低点；卖出信号反之。出场条件为：持有多头时若出现卖出信号且当前高点低于前一根高点则平仓；持有空头时若出现买入信号且当前低点高于前一根低点则平仓。仓位管理使用固定手数，无动态仓位调整或资金管理逻辑。止损止盈为固定点数计算，需满足经纪商最小止损水平要求，否则拒绝开仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on inside bar (insider) candlestick patterns combined with a zigzag price level confirmation system. It opens buy or sell positions when price breaks above or below the previous bar's high/low, confirmed by inside bar patterns and time-based filters, with optional stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy is based on Inside Bar candlestick pattern recognition, where the current bar's high and low are completely contained within the previous bar's range. The system defines White Insider (close above open) and Black Insider (close below open) patterns, as well as consecutive insider patterns called White Bird and Black Bird, which serve as filtering conditions. Entry conditions: a buy signal requires the current bar's high to break above the previous bar's high, buy confirmation satisfied (current low touches the zigzag buy level or time is past 8:00), not a Black Bird and not a White Insider, and current low above previous low; sell signals are the mirror opposite. Exit conditions: a long position is closed when a sell signal appears with current high below previous high; a short position is closed when a buy signal appears with current low above previous low. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size with no dynamic position adjustment or money management logic. Stop loss and take profit are calculated as fixed point values from entry price, and must satisfy the broker's minimum stop level requirement or the trade is rejected.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于经典的内包线突破形态，易于理解和验证
  • 内置止损止盈检查机制，确保订单符合经纪商最小止损水平要求
  • 支持信号反转功能，便于在不同市场环境下灵活调整
  • 使用全局变量防止同一K线重复开仓，避免过度交易
  • 代码结构模块化，函数划分合理，便于维护和扩展

劣势

  • 无动态仓位管理，固定手数无法根据账户余额或波动率调整风险
  • 止损止盈默认为零，实际使用中可能完全不设止损，风险极高
  • 没有新K线检查机制，每个tick都可能触发信号计算，效率低下且可能重复交易
  • 内包线形态在高时间框架出现频率低，在低时间框架假突破多，策略适用性有限
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生不利成交

改进建议

  • 添加新K线检测机制，仅在新的K线形成时计算信号，避免tick级别重复计算
  • 引入动态仓位管理，基于账户余额百分比或ATR波动率调整手数
  • 增加点差过滤和滑点保护，在高点差环境下禁止开仓
  • 加入追踪止损功能，在盈利时锁定利润而非仅依赖固定止盈
  • 增加多时间框架确认，例如在H1内包线突破时检查H4趋势方向

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on the classic inside bar breakout pattern, easy to understand and verify
  • Built-in stop loss and take profit validation ensures orders meet broker minimum stop level requirements
  • Signal inversion feature allows flexible adaptation to different market conditions
  • Uses global variables to prevent repeated entries on the same bar, avoiding overtrading
  • Modular code structure with well-separated functions, easy to maintain and extend

Weaknesses

  • No dynamic position sizing; fixed lots cannot adjust risk based on account balance or volatility
  • Stop loss and take profit default to zero, potentially running without any stop loss at all — extremely risky
  • No new bar check mechanism; signal calculation runs on every tick, which is inefficient and may cause duplicate trades
  • Inside bar patterns occur infrequently on higher timeframes and produce many false breakouts on lower timeframes, limiting strategy applicability
  • Missing spread filter; unfavorable fills may occur in high-spread environments

Improvements

  • Add new bar detection to calculate signals only when a new bar forms, avoiding redundant tick-level computation
  • Introduce dynamic position sizing based on account equity percentage or ATR volatility to adjust lot size
  • Add spread filter and slippage protection to prevent opening positions in high-spread environments
  • Implement trailing stop functionality to lock in profits during favorable moves rather than relying solely on fixed take profit
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation, e.g., check H4 trend direction when trading H1 inside bar breakouts
