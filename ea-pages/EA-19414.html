RSI Eraser

RSI Eraser is an Expert Advisor that trades mean reversion signals based on the RSI indicator reaching extreme overbought or oversold levels. It enters counter-trend positions when RSI exits extreme zones, expecting price to revert to the mean. The EA includes configurable RSI periods, extreme thresholds, and basic risk management parameters.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy concept of RSI Eraser is mean reversion, using the RSI indicator to identify extreme market conditions. Entry conditions are: buy when RSI crosses upward from the oversold zone (below the configured lower threshold), and sell when RSI crosses downward from the overbought zone (above the configured upper threshold), essentially entering counter-trend positions at market extremes. Exit conditions rely on configurable take-profit and stop-loss levels, and may also include closing logic when RSI returns to neutral territory. Position sizing supports fixed lot trading, with some implementations potentially offering dynamic lot calculation based on account balance. Money management depends on stop-loss settings and per-trade risk control, though the rigor of implementation varies with parameter configuration. The strategy performs well in ranging markets but faces the risk of consecutive counter-trend losses during strong trending conditions.

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is clean and straightforward, based on the classic RSI overbought/oversold principle, making it easy to understand and verify
  • Mean reversion approach has high win rate and stable profit potential in ranging markets
  • Highly configurable parameters allow traders to flexibly adjust RSI thresholds and periods for different instruments and market conditions

Weaknesses

  • Pure counter-trend strategy generates frequent false signals during strong trending markets, leading to consecutive losses
  • Lacks trend filter mechanism, unable to distinguish between ranging and trending markets, prone to repeated counter-trend entries during trends
  • Default stop-loss of 0 means no stop-loss may be set, creating extreme risk of unlimited loss on a single trade

Possible improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., moving average direction or ADX indicator) to enable counter-trend signals only in ranging markets
  • Mandate stop-loss settings and add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit calculation to adapt to different volatility environments

Risk assessment

Overall risk: High risk

  • Default stop-loss of 0 means a single trade without stop-loss can cause catastrophic losses
  • Lacks trend filter and multi-confirmation mechanism, making counter-trend trading extremely risky during strong trends
  • No new bar check mechanism may cause repeated position opening on the same signal
