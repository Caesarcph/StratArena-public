摘要

Spreader是一个统计套利EA，通过交易两个相关品种之间的价差来获利。当价差偏离历史均值超过设定阈值时，在两个品种上开立对冲仓位，等待价差回归均衡时平仓获利。该EA监控价差比率，在偏离过大时入场，在价差正常化或触及止损时出场。

策略分析

Spreader采用配对交易（Pairs Trading）策略，属于统计套利类别。其核心概念是寻找两个历史上高度相关的金融品种，计算它们之间的价差（spread），并假设价差具有均值回归特性。入场条件是当价差偏离其移动均值超过设定的标准差倍数（阈值）时，在价差偏大的品种上做空、在偏小的品种上做多，形成对冲仓位。出场条件包括价差回归到均值附近时获利平仓，以及价差继续恶化触及止损时亏损平仓。仓位管理方面，EA支持在两个品种上分别开仓，且可以根据品种的波动率差异调整各自的仓位比例以实现中性对冲。资金管理方面，EA提供了手数参数供用户设置每笔交易规模，但缺乏基于账户余额或波动率的动态仓位调整机制。整体策略逻辑依赖协整关系的持续性，若品种间相关性发生结构性断裂，可能导致双边亏损。

Summary

Spreader is a statistical arbitrage Expert Advisor that trades the price spread between two correlated instruments. It opens opposing positions on two symbols when the spread deviates from its historical mean, profiting from convergence back to equilibrium. The EA monitors the spread ratio, enters when deviation exceeds a threshold, and exits when the spread normalizes or hits stop-loss levels.

Strategy Analysis

Spreader employs a Pairs Trading strategy falling under the statistical arbitrage category. The core concept identifies two historically correlated financial instruments, calculates the spread between them, and assumes mean-reverting properties of that spread. Entry conditions trigger when the spread deviates beyond a configurable threshold (standard deviation multiplier) from its moving average, opening opposing positions — short on the overvalued instrument and long on the undervalued one — forming a hedged pair. Exit conditions include profitable closure when the spread reverts to its mean, and stop-loss closure when the spread continues to diverge beyond the stop level. Position sizing supports opening separate positions on each symbol, with the ability to adjust lot ratios based on instrument volatility differences to achieve delta-neutral hedging. Money management provides user-configurable lot size parameters but lacks dynamic position sizing based on account equity or volatility. The overall strategy logic relies on the persistence of cointegration relationships; a structural break in correlation between instruments could lead to losses on both legs.

优势

  • 市场中性策略，不受单边趋势方向影响，在震荡和趋势市场中均可运作
  • 对冲仓位降低了单边风险暴露，双边持仓互为保护
  • 利用统计规律，有明确的均值回归逻辑支撑，入场和出场条件量化清晰
  • 可扩展性强，用户可自定义配对品种和参数阈值，适应不同市场环境

劣势

  • 过度依赖历史相关性假设，若品种间协整关系发生结构性断裂，可能导致双边亏损
  • 缺少动态仓位调整机制，固定手数设置无法适应账户权益变化和品种波动率变化
  • 未考虑交易成本（点差、佣金、滑点）对价差交易利润的侵蚀，小价差回归利润可能被成本吞噬
  • 缺乏新K线检查机制，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓

改进建议

  • 增加协整性检验（如ADF检验），动态监控品种间相关性的稳定性，在相关性衰减时暂停交易
  • 引入基于ATR或账户风险的动态仓位计算，替代固定手数设置
  • 增加交易成本过滤器，确保预期利润超过点差和佣金之和才执行交易
  • 添加新K线检测逻辑，防止同一根K线上重复开仓

Strengths

  • Market-neutral strategy that is independent of overall market direction, functioning in both trending and ranging environments
  • Hedged positions reduce directional risk exposure, with each leg providing protection for the other
  • Grounded in statistical principles with clear mean-reversion logic and quantified entry/exit conditions
  • High extensibility allows users to customize instrument pairs and threshold parameters for different market conditions

Weaknesses

  • Over-reliance on historical correlation assumptions; structural breaks in cointegration between instruments can lead to simultaneous losses on both legs
  • Lacks dynamic position sizing; fixed lot sizes cannot adapt to changes in account equity or instrument volatility
  • Does not account for transaction costs (spreads, commissions, slippage) eroding spread-trading profits; small mean-reversion gains may be consumed by costs
  • Missing new bar check mechanism, potentially allowing repeated entries on the same bar

Improvements

  • Add cointegration testing (e.g., ADF test) to dynamically monitor the stability of the correlation and pause trading when the relationship decays
  • Introduce ATR-based or risk-based dynamic position sizing to replace fixed lot sizes
  • Add a transaction cost filter to ensure expected profit exceeds the sum of spread and commission before executing trades
  • Add new bar detection logic to prevent repeated entries within the same bar
