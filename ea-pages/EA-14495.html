摘要

该EA基于ColorMETRO_WPR自定义指标的交叉信号进行交易，该指标是阶梯式威廉百分比范围振荡器。当快速阶梯线上穿慢速阶梯线时开多仓，反之开空仓，具有可配置的入场/出场开关和基本的止损/止盈管理。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略基于ColorMETRO_WPR指标的信号线交叉。ColorMETRO_WPR是一个对WPR（威廉百分比范围）指标进行阶梯化处理后的自定义指标，包含快速步长（StepSizeFast）和慢速步长（StepSizeSlow）两条信号线。入场条件为：当MPlus线从下方穿越MMinus线时产生买入信号，反之产生卖出信号；同时要求前一根K线上两条线的关系确认趋势方向。出场条件依赖止损和止盈参数，以及可选的反向信号平仓功能。仓位管理通过MM参数和MMMode枚举支持多种模式，包括固定手数、基于余额百分比、基于可用保证金百分比等方式。资金管理方面提供了四种模式选择，但缺少加仓、减仓或追踪止损等高级资金管理功能。策略在新K线形成时或SignalBar为0时才计算信号，避免同一根K线内重复交易。

Summary

This EA trades based on the crossover signals from the ColorMETRO_WPR custom indicator, which is a stepped (metro-style) Williams Percent Range oscillator. It opens buy positions when the fast metro line crosses above the slow metro line and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with configurable entry/exit switches and basic stop-loss/take-profit management.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the crossover of two signal lines from the ColorMETRO_WPR indicator, which applies a step-filter (metro-style) transformation to the Williams Percent Range oscillator. The indicator produces two lines: a fast metro line (MPlus, StepSizeFast=5) and a slow metro line (MMinus, StepSizeSlow=15). Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when MPlus crosses above MMinus from below, confirmed by the previous bar showing MPlus below MMinus; a sell signal triggers on the opposite crossover. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop-loss and take-profit values in points, with optional reverse-signal closing controlled by BuyPosClose and SellPosClose switches. Position sizing supports multiple modes via the MMMode enum: fixed lot size, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin, percentage of balance minus floating loss, and percentage of free margin minus floating loss. The strategy recalculates signals only on new bar formation or when SignalBar is 0, preventing repeated trades within the same bar. However, there is no trailing stop, break-even, or partial close functionality for advanced money management.

优势

  • 使用阶梯化WPR指标过滤噪声，信号更稳定
  • 提供多种资金管理模式选择，灵活性高
  • 支持独立控制买卖方向的入场和出场，可灵活配置
  • 新K线检测机制避免同根K线重复交易
  • 止损止盈参数可配置，具备基本风险控制

劣势

  • 依赖外部自定义指标ColorMETRO_WPR，缺乏内嵌备份方案
  • 缺少追踪止损和保本止损功能
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点的默认值偏大，可能导致较大浮亏
  • 没有点差过滤，在大幅点差行情中可能不利
  • 指标加载失败时仅打印日志，无重试机制

改进建议

  • 添加追踪止损功能以保护浮盈
  • 增加点差过滤和重试加载指标的机制

Strengths

  • Stepped WPR indicator filters out market noise for more stable signals
  • Multiple money management modes provide flexibility in position sizing
  • Independent buy/sell entry and exit controls allow flexible configuration
  • New bar detection prevents repeated trades within the same bar
  • Configurable stop-loss and take-profit provide basic risk control

Weaknesses

  • Depends on external custom indicator ColorMETRO_WPR with no fallback if missing
  • No trailing stop or break-even functionality for profit protection
  • Default SL=1000 and TP=2000 points are large, potentially leading to significant floating losses
  • No spread filter, which may be detrimental during high-spread conditions
  • Indicator handle failure only logs a message with no retry mechanism

Improvements

  • Add trailing stop functionality to protect floating profits
  • Add spread filter and indicator loading retry mechanism
