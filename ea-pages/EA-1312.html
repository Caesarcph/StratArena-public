Exp_ColorStepXCCX

This EA trades based on the ColorStepXCCX custom indicator, which uses step-filtered moving averages with dual smoothing. It enters long when the fast line crosses above the slow line and short when it crosses below, with configurable permissions for opening and closing positions independently.

Strategy analysis

The EA's core strategy is trend following based on the ColorStepXCCX indicator, which combines dual smoothing algorithms — price smoothing (e.g., JJMA) and deviation smoothing (e.g., T3) — then generates signals through fast and slow step sizes. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when buffer 0 (MPlus) crosses above buffer 1 (MMinus), and a sell signal triggers on the opposite crossover, with the additional requirement that the prior bar showed no crossover (confirming a fresh cross). Exit conditions: reverse crossover signals close positions, supplemented by fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit values in points for risk control. Position sizing is governed by the MM parameter: negative values specify a fixed lot size, while positive values represent a share of the deposit per deal. Money management relies on the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library functions (BuyPositionOpen/SellPositionOpen), which include deviation control to manage slippage. The strategy also uses an IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are calculated only once per bar, preventing duplicate trades within the same bar.

Strengths

  • Dual smoothing algorithms (JJMA+T3) produce higher quality signals with reduced noise
  • Independent control over buy/sell open and close permissions provides flexible configuration
  • IsNewBar mechanism prevents redundant signal calculation and duplicate trades within the same bar
  • Reasonable StopLoss/TakeProfit defaults with a 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio favoring long-term profitability
  • Step filtering mechanism effectively reduces frequent false signals in choppy markets

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom ColorStepXCCX indicator which must be installed separately, increasing deployment complexity
  • No spread filter, potentially leading to unfavorable entries in high-spread conditions
  • No explicit position check logic, potentially allowing duplicate position openings under certain conditions
  • StopLoss 1000 and TakeProfit 2000 points may be too large or small for some instruments without adaptive adjustment
  • The positive/negative MM parameter logic is unintuitive and prone to misconfiguration

Possible improvements

  • Add a spread filter to pause trading when spread exceeds a threshold, avoiding unfavorable slippage
  • Add position checking to prevent duplicate same-direction opens and allow max position limits
  • Split the MM parameter into separate position sizing mode and lot/ratio parameters for better readability

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No spread filter means entry prices may be unfavorable during high-spread periods
  • The negative MM value for fixed lots is error-prone and could accidentally use oversized lots
  • Dependency on external custom indicator means EA fails silently if indicator file is missing
