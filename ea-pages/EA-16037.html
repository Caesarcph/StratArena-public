摘要

该EA基于极化分形效率(PFE)指标进行交易，当PFE向上转折时开多仓，向下转折时开空仓。它使用平滑算法（默认JJMA）过滤PFE信号，并通过新K线检查避免重复下单。仓位管理支持多种保证金模式，可配置止损和止盈。

策略分析

策略核心是极化分形效率(PFE)指标，该指标衡量价格运动的分形效率，值越高表示趋势越强越顺畅。入场条件：当PFE值从前一周期到当前周期上升（Value[0]>Value[1]）且前一周期相对更早的值下降（Value[1]<Value[2]）时，产生买入信号；反向则产生卖出信号。出场条件分为两部分：信号反转时平仓（如持多仓时PFE转向下则平多），以及止损止盈触发时平仓。仓位管理支持五种模式：基于可用保证金、基于余额、基于扣除亏损后的可用保证金、基于扣除亏损后的余额、以及固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1。策略使用JJMA平滑算法过滤噪声信号，XPhase参数控制平滑的相位特性。SignalBar参数允许回溯检查历史K线上的信号，默认为1即前一根已收盘K线。

Summary

This EA trades based on the Polarized Fractal Efficiency (PFE) indicator, opening buy positions when PFE turns upward and sell positions when it turns downward. It uses smoothing algorithms (JJMA by default) to filter the PFE signal and employs a new-bar check to avoid redundant trade execution. Position sizing supports multiple margin modes with configurable stop-loss and take-profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy centers on the Polarized Fractal Efficiency (PFE) indicator, which measures the fractal efficiency of price movement — higher values indicate a stronger, smoother trend. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when PFE rises from the previous period to the current one (Value[0]>Value[1]) while the prior period showed a decline (Value[1]<Value[2]), and conversely for sell signals. Exit conditions are twofold: signal-based closing (e.g., closing a long when PFE turns downward) and fixed stop-loss/take-profit levels. Position sizing supports five margin modes: based on free margin, balance, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, or a fixed lot size. Money management uses the MM parameter to control risk proportion, defaulting to 0.1 (10%). The strategy applies JJMA smoothing to filter noisy PFE signals, with the XPhase parameter controlling the smoothing phase characteristic. The SignalBar parameter allows checking signals on historical bars, defaulting to 1 (the last closed bar), which helps avoid repainting issues.

优势

  • 使用PFE分形效率指标，理论基础扎实，能有效识别趋势强度和方向转折
  • 支持多种平滑算法（10种）和多种价格类型（11种），灵活性极高
  • 设有止损止盈和滑点控制，风险管理基础设施完善
  • 使用SignalBar=1基于已收盘K线信号交易，避免了重绘问题
  • 开仓和平仓信号可独立开关控制，策略配置灵活

劣势

  • PFE周期仅为5且平滑长度也为5，参数偏小可能导致信号过于频繁和噪声较多
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点在H4周期上可能过大或不适配所有品种，缺乏动态调整机制
  • 没有仓位检查逻辑，可能在已有同方向持仓时重复开仓
  • 没有点差过滤，在点差较大的品种上可能因滑点导致不利入场
  • 依赖外部指标文件PFE和头文件，部署依赖性较强，不利于独立运行

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓，可通过检查当前持仓方向和数量实现
  • 增加点差过滤和波动率适配，根据ATR动态调整止损止盈，使策略适应不同品种和市场状态
  • 优化PFE和平滑参数的默认值，建议增大周期以减少虚假信号，并增加参数验证防止不合理输入

Strengths

  • Uses the well-grounded PFE indicator which effectively identifies trend strength and directional turning points
  • Supports 10 smoothing algorithms and 11 applied price types, offering exceptional flexibility
  • Implements stop-loss, take-profit, and slippage control, providing solid risk management infrastructure
  • Uses SignalBar=1 to trade on closed-bar signals, avoiding indicator repainting issues
  • Buy/sell open and close signals can be independently toggled, allowing flexible strategy configuration

Weaknesses

  • PFE period of only 5 and smoothing length of 5 are small values that may produce frequent, noisy signals
  • Stop-loss of 1000 points and take-profit of 2000 points on H4 may be too wide or unsuitable for all instruments, lacking dynamic adjustment
  • No position check logic — may open duplicate positions in the same direction when one already exists
  • No spread filter — on high-spread instruments, unfavorable entries may occur due to slippage
  • Depends on external indicator file PFE and include headers, creating deployment fragility and reducing portability

Improvements

  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate same-direction entries by verifying existing open positions before opening new ones
  • Add spread filtering and volatility-adaptive mechanisms — use ATR for dynamic stop-loss/take-profit adjustment to suit different instruments and market conditions
  • Optimize default PFE and smoothing parameters — increase periods to reduce false signals and add input validation to prevent unreasonable parameter combinations
