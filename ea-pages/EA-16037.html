摘要

该EA基于极化分形效率(PFE)指标进行交易，PFE上升时做多，PFE下降时做空。使用CXMA平滑算法库生成信号，支持包括固定手数和百分比在内的多种资金管理模式。EA基于新K线触发交易，并分别控制开仓和平仓操作。

策略分析

策略概念基于极化分形效率(PFE)指标，该指标衡量价格运动的分形效率，值越高表示趋势越强越顺畅。入场条件为：当PFE前一值小于前两值（上升趋势）且当前值大于前一值（动量加速）时开多仓；当PFE前一值大于前两值（下降趋势）且当前值小于前一值（动量加速）时开空仓。出场条件依赖固定止损止盈，同时支持趋势反转时反向平仓（如持多仓时PFE转跌则平多）。仓位管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，MMMode选择资金管理模式（余额、净值、固定手数等）。资金管理支持五种模式：自由保证金比例、余额比例、扣除亏损后自由保证金比例、扣除亏损后余额比例、固定手数。信号仅在新生成K线时计算，避免逐tick重复开仓，但SignalBar为0时会在每个tick计算信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on the Polarized Fractal Efficiency (PFE) indicator, entering long when PFE rises and short when PFE falls. It uses the CXMA smoothing library for signal generation and supports configurable money management modes including lot-based and percentage-based position sizing. The EA operates on a new bar basis with separate controls for opening and closing positions.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the Polarized Fractal Efficiency (PFE) indicator, which measures the fractal efficiency of price movement—higher values indicate a stronger, more efficient trend. Entry conditions: a long position is opened when the previous PFE value is less than the one before it (uptrend forming) and the current value exceeds the previous one (momentum accelerating); a short position is opened when the opposite conditions hold. Exit conditions rely on fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit values in points, with additional trend-reversal closes where a long is closed when PFE turns down and vice versa. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter specifying risk percentage, while MMMode selects from five money management modes: free margin percentage, balance percentage, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. Signals are computed only on new bars to prevent repeated tick-level entries, though setting SignalBar to zero triggers per-tick signal recalculation which could increase trade frequency undesirably.

优势

  • 使用新K线检测机制避免同一K线内重复开仓，减少过度交易风险
  • 开仓和平仓条件独立可控，灵活性高，可单独启用或禁用买卖方向
  • 止损止盈参数明确设置，TakeProfit为StopLoss的2倍，具有正期望收益比特征
  • 支持多种平滑算法和价格类型，策略参数化程度高，便于优化
  • 资金管理模式丰富，从固定手数到百分比模式均有覆盖

劣势

  • SignalBar=0时会在每个tick重新计算信号，可能导致频繁交易和过度开仓
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下止损可能容易被触发
  • 依赖自定义指标PFE，如果指标文件缺失EA将无法初始化
  • 没有持仓数量检查，可能在同方向重复开仓
  • 止损止盈为固定点数而非ATR动态计算，不同波动率环境下适应性差

改进建议

  • 增加持仓方向检查，避免同方向重复开仓导致仓位过重
  • 使用ATR动态计算止损止盈，使策略在不同波动率环境下具有更好的适应性
  • 增加点差过滤和时间过滤，避免在流动性差或点差过大时交易

Strengths

  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated position opening within the same bar, reducing overtrading risk
  • Independent control for open and close operations in both directions provides high flexibility
  • Explicit StopLoss and TakeProfit with a 1:2 risk-reward ratio characteristic of positive expectancy systems
  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithms and price types, offering high parameterization for optimization
  • Comprehensive money management modes covering fixed lot to various percentage-based approaches

Weaknesses

  • When SignalBar=0, signals are recalculated on every tick which may cause excessive trading and over-positioning
  • No spread filter means trades can be opened during high-spread conditions where StopLoss is more easily triggered
  • Depends on the custom PFE indicator file; if missing, the EA fails to initialize
  • No check for existing positions in the same direction, potentially leading to duplicate entries
  • Fixed-point StopLoss and TakeProfit lack ATR-based dynamic adaptation to varying volatility regimes

Improvements

  • Add position direction checking to prevent duplicate entries that over-concentrate risk in one direction
  • Implement ATR-based dynamic StopLoss and TakeProfit for better adaptation across different volatility environments
  • Add spread filter and session/time filter to avoid trading during low-liquidity or high-spread periods

  • 缺少同方向持仓检查，可能重复开仓累积过大仓位
  • 固定止损止盈不适应市场波动率变化，低波动时止损过宽，高波动时止损过窄

  • Lack of same-direction position checking may cause duplicate entries and excessive position accumulation
  • Fixed StopLoss/TakeProfit does not adapt to volatility changes—too wide in low volatility, too narrow in high volatility
