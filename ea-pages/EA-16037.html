摘要

该EA基于极化分形效率(PFE)指标进行交易，当PFE向上转折时开多仓，向下转折时开空仓。它使用CXMA平滑算法库对信号进行优化，并支持包括固定手数和百分比仓位在内的多种资金管理模式。

策略分析

策略概念基于极化分形效率(PFE)指标的方向性转折来捕捉趋势动量变化。入场条件为：当PFE指标前一根值低于前两根值且当前值高于前一值时触发做多信号；当PFE前一根值高于前两根值且当前值低于前一值时触发做空信号。出场条件通过独立的买入平仓和卖出平仓开关控制，在反向信号出现时平仓。仓位管理支持五种模式：基于可用保证金、基于余额、基于扣除亏损后的可用保证金、基于扣除亏损后的余额，以及固定手数模式。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1。止损和止盈分别默认设为1000和2000点，提供了较好的风险回报比。策略使用新K线检测机制避免同一根K线内重复交易，并设置了滑点容忍度以应对市场波动。

Summary

This EA trades based on the Polarized Fractal Efficiency (PFE) indicator, opening buy positions when PFE turns upward and sell positions when PFE turns downward. It uses the CXMA smoothing library for signal refinement and supports configurable money management modes including lot-based and percentage-based sizing.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on directional reversals of the Polarized Fractal Efficiency (PFE) indicator to capture trend momentum shifts. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when PFE's previous bar value is below the bar before it AND the current value is above the previous value; a sell signal triggers when PFE's previous bar value is above the bar before it AND the current value is below the previous value. Exit conditions are controlled by independent buy-close and sell-close switches, closing positions when opposing signals appear. Position sizing supports five modes: based on free margin, balance, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter with a default risk ratio of 0.1. Stop loss and take profit default to 1000 and 2000 points respectively, providing a reasonable risk-reward ratio. The strategy uses a new bar detection mechanism to prevent repeated trades within the same bar and sets a slippage tolerance to handle market volatility.

优势

  • 使用PFE极化分形效率指标，能有效识别趋势动量方向变化
  • 支持多种资金管理模式和灵活的仓位控制，适应不同风险偏好
  • 具备新K线检测机制，避免同根K线内重复入场
  • 止损止盈设置合理，默认1:2的风险回报比
  • 买入和卖出的入场与出场开关独立控制，灵活性高

劣势

  • 仅依赖单一指标PFE，缺乏多维度信号确认，容易产生虚假信号
  • 没有仓位检查逻辑，可能在已有同方向持仓时重复开仓
  • 缺乏点差过滤机制，在高位差环境下可能不利入场
  • PFE指标通过iCustom调用，依赖外部自定义指标文件，部署时需额外文件
  • 没有移动止损或追踪止损功能，盈利头寸可能大幅回撤

改进建议

  • 增加多指标确认机制，如结合趋势过滤器（MA/ADX）过滤震荡行情中的虚假信号
  • 添加仓位检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓，并实现移动止损保护浮盈

Strengths

  • Uses the PFE indicator which effectively identifies trend momentum directional changes
  • Supports multiple money management modes and flexible position control for different risk preferences
  • Implements new bar detection to prevent repeated entries within the same bar
  • Reasonable stop loss and take profit with a default 1:2 risk-reward ratio
  • Independent switches for buy/sell entry and exit provide high flexibility

Weaknesses

  • Relies solely on the PFE indicator without multi-dimensional signal confirmation, prone to false signals
  • Lacks position check logic, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • No spread filter, may enter trades under unfavorable high-spread conditions
  • PFE indicator called via iCustom depends on external custom indicator file, requiring additional deployment files
  • No trailing stop functionality, profitable positions may experience significant drawdowns

Improvements

  • Add multi-indicator confirmation mechanism, such as combining trend filters (MA/ADX) to filter false signals in ranging markets
  • Add position check logic to avoid duplicate same-direction entries and implement trailing stop to protect floating profits
