摘要

该EA基于KaufWMAcross自定义指标的交叉信号进行交易，该指标结合了考夫曼自适应移动平均线(AMA)和线性加权移动平均线(LWMA)。在向上交叉信号时开多仓，向下交叉信号时开空仓，支持可配置的平仓逻辑和资金管理模式。

策略分析

策略核心是考夫曼自适应移动平均线(AMA)与线性加权移动平均线(LWMA)的交叉系统。入场条件为：当AMA上穿LWMA时产生UpValue信号，开多仓并平空仓；当AMA下穿LWMA时产生DnValue信号，开空仓并平多仓。出场条件依赖反向交叉信号或止损/止盈，同时EA会回溯历史柱检查是否已存在同向平仓信号以避免重复操作。仓位管理支持五种模式：按自由保证金比例、按余额比例、按扣除浮亏后自由保证金比例、按扣除浮亏后余额比例、固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1手或10%资金。该策略使用自适应均线，理论上在趋势市中能更快跟随价格变化，在震荡市中自动减速减少假信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on crossover signals from the KaufWMAcross custom indicator, which combines Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average (AMA) with a weighted moving average (LWMA). It opens buy positions on upward crossover signals and sell positions on downward crossover signals, with configurable position closing logic and money management modes.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy is a crossover system between the Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average (AMA) and a Linear Weighted Moving Average (LWMA). Entry conditions: when AMA crosses above LWMA, an UpValue signal is generated to open buy positions and close sell positions; when AMA crosses below LWMA, a DnValue signal is generated to open sell positions and close buy positions. Exit conditions rely on reverse crossover signals or stop-loss/take-profit levels, and the EA also retrospectively checks historical bars for existing closing signals to avoid redundant operations. Position sizing supports five modes: by free margin percentage, by balance percentage, by free margin after floating loss, by balance after floating loss, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter, defaulting to 0.1 lots or 10% of capital. The strategy uses an adaptive moving average which theoretically tracks price faster during trending markets and automatically slows down during choppy markets to reduce false signals.

优势

  • 使用考夫曼自适应均线(AMA)，能根据市场波动率自动调节灵敏度，趋势市快速跟随，震荡市减少假信号
  • 提供多种资金管理模式选择，灵活性高，从固定手数到基于余额/保证金的比例仓位均支持
  • 设置了止损止盈(SL=1000, TP=2000)，盈亏比2:1较为合理，有基本的风险控制
  • 使用SignalBar=1确认信号，基于已收盘K线判断，避免未收盘柱信号反复变化
  • 支持独立控制买卖方向的开仓和平仓，策略配置灵活

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点数值过大，对大多数品种而言可能不切实际，风险参数需要根据具体品种调整
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时间过滤，在点差较大或流动性差的时段可能产生不利成交
  • 依赖自定义指标KaufWMAcross，指标文件不可用则EA无法运行，且指标逻辑不可直接审查
  • 回溯历史柱查找平仓信号的循环（第135-157行）在柱数很多时可能影响性能
  • 没有显式的持仓检查逻辑，可能存在同方向重复开仓的风险

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，在开仓前验证是否已存在同方向持仓，避免重复开仓
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，减少不利市场条件下的滑点和亏损风险
  • 优化回溯平仓信号循环，设置最大回溯柱数限制，避免在历史数据过长时影响性能
  • 增加追踪止损(Trailing Stop)功能，在趋势延续时锁定利润

Strengths

  • Uses Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average (AMA) which automatically adjusts sensitivity based on market volatility — fast in trends, slow in choppy markets to reduce false signals
  • Provides multiple money management modes with high flexibility, from fixed lots to percentage-based sizing on balance or margin
  • Implements stop-loss and take-profit (SL=1000, TP=2000) with a reasonable 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio for basic risk control
  • Uses SignalBar=1 for signal confirmation based on closed bars, avoiding signal flickering on unconfirmed bars
  • Supports independent control of buy/sell opening and closing directions for flexible strategy configuration

Weaknesses

  • Stop-loss of 1000 points and take-profit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments and need instrument-specific calibration
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, which may result in unfavorable fills during high-spread or low-liquidity periods
  • Depends on the custom indicator KaufWMAcross — if the indicator file is unavailable the EA cannot run, and the indicator logic cannot be directly audited
  • The historical bar loop for finding closing signals (lines 135-157) may impact performance when bar count is large
  • No explicit position check logic before opening new trades, potentially risking duplicate positions in the same direction

Improvements

  • Add position check logic before opening trades to verify no existing positions in the same direction, preventing duplicate entries
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to reduce slippage and losses during unfavorable market conditions
  • Optimize the historical bar loop for closing signals by setting a maximum lookback bar limit to prevent performance degradation with long history
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in profits when trends continue in favor
