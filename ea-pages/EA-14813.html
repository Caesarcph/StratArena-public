摘要

该EA基于自定义BSI（买卖指标）进行交易，当指标发出看涨动量信号时开多仓，发出看跌动量信号时开空仓。它支持可配置的资金管理模式、止损/止盈水平，并允许独立控制买入/卖出的入场和出场逻辑。

策略分析

策略概念基于BSI自定义指标的动量信号，该指标结合了范围周期、平滑和平均周期来计算买卖力量。入场条件为：当指标缓冲区3的值为2时产生买入信号（前值不为2时开多仓），值为0时产生卖出信号（前值不为0时开空仓）。出场条件为：买入信号出现时同时平空仓，卖出信号出现时同时平多仓，即反向信号即为止损出场。仓位管理通过TradeAlgorithms.mqh库实现，支持多种资金管理模式包括固定手数、按余额百分比、按净值百分比等。资金管理参数MM控制仓位大小，MMMode决定计算方式，默认为固定手数模式。策略使用新K线检测机制避免重复信号，并通过SignalBar参数允许在当前K线或信号K线上执行交易。

Summary

This EA trades based on the BSI (Buy Sell Indicator) custom indicator, opening buy positions when the indicator signals bullish momentum and sell positions when it signals bearish momentum. It uses configurable money management modes, stop loss/take profit levels, and allows independent control of buy/sell entry and exit logic.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on momentum signals from the BSI (Buy Sell Indicator) custom indicator, which combines range period, slowing, and averaging periods to calculate buying and selling pressure. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when indicator buffer 3 value equals 2 (and previous value was not 2), opening a long position; a sell signal is generated when the value equals 0 (and previous value was not 0), opening a short position. Exit conditions: a buy signal simultaneously closes short positions, and a sell signal simultaneously closes long positions, meaning a reversal signal serves as the exit for the opposite direction. Position sizing is implemented through the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library, supporting multiple money management modes including fixed lot, percentage of balance, percentage of equity, and percentage of free margin. The MM parameter controls position size while MMMode determines the calculation method, defaulting to fixed lot mode. The strategy uses a new bar detection mechanism to prevent duplicate signals and allows execution on either the current bar or a signal bar via the SignalBar parameter.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰简洁，基于BSI指标的动量信号进行交易，易于理解和维护
  • 提供灵活的资金管理模式，支持固定手数、按余额、按净值等多种方式
  • 买入和卖出的入场与出场逻辑可独立控制，灵活性高
  • 使用新K线检测机制避免重复信号，减少不必要的交易
  • 止损止盈参数可配置，具备基本的风险控制框架

劣势

  • 依赖自定义BSI指标，该指标的具体算法不透明，难以验证信号有效性
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点的默认值较大，可能在某些品种上不合适
  • 缺少点差过滤和最大持仓检查，在高点差或波动剧烈时可能产生不利成交
  • 没有回撤控制机制，连续亏损时无保护措施
  • 指标缓冲区使用魔数（0和2）判断信号，可读性差且容易出错

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤机制，避免在点差过大时开仓
  • 增加最大回撤限制和连续亏损保护机制
  • 添加移动止损或追踪止盈功能，更好地保护利润

Strengths

  • Clear and concise strategy logic based on BSI indicator momentum signals, easy to understand and maintain
  • Flexible money management modes supporting fixed lot, balance-based, equity-based and other methods
  • Independent control for buy/sell entry and exit logic provides high flexibility
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate signals and reduces unnecessary trades
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit parameters provide a basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Depends on a custom BSI indicator whose internal algorithm is opaque, making signal validity hard to verify
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are large and may be unsuitable for some instruments
  • Lacks spread filter and maximum position checks, potentially leading to unfavorable fills during high spread or volatility
  • No drawdown control mechanism to protect against consecutive losses
  • Uses magic numbers (0 and 2) from indicator buffer for signal detection, reducing readability and increasing error risk

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter mechanism to avoid opening positions when spread is excessively wide
  • Add maximum drawdown limits and consecutive loss protection mechanisms
  • Implement trailing stop or trailing take profit functionality to better protect profits
