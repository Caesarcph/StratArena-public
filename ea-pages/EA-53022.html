EA-53022 · Martingale

Smart Trend Follower

This EA is a trend-following martingale system that uses either MA crossover or MA+Stochastic confirmation to generate trade signals. On losing positions, it opens additional layers at fixed pip distances with multiplied lot sizes (martingale), then sets a common break-even TP/SL across all positions of the same direction.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on trend following with two entry signal modes: one is dual MA crossover (fast MA crossing above slow MA for buy, below for sell), and the other uses MA trend direction confirmation combined with Stochastic oversold/overbought zone filtering and candle direction confirmation. Exit conditions use a weighted average break-even price across all same-direction positions plus a fixed pip take-profit, with optional stop-loss. Position sizing employs a martingale approach where the first position uses the initial lot size, and each subsequent layer multiplies the lot by a configurable multiplier (default 2x), with layers spaced at a fixed 200-pip distance. Money management includes lot normalization, minimum/maximum lot limits, lot step alignment, and margin checking, but lacks overall risk exposure limits. The EA detects signals on each new candle and continuously modifies TP/SL for all positions to maintain a unified exit target.

Strengths

  • Provides two entry signal modes (MA crossover and MA+Stochastic trend confirmation), adding strategy flexibility
  • Includes thorough margin checking and lot normalization logic to prevent invalid orders
  • Unified TP/SL management for same-direction positions based on weighted average break-even price, with clear logic

Weaknesses

  • Martingale strategy carries extreme risk; consecutive layering causes exponential lot growth that can rapidly blow the account
  • Default stop-loss is 0, meaning martingale positions accumulate without any safety net, making risk uncontrollable
  • Bug in getTotalVolume: uses PositionGetDouble instead of OrderGetDouble for pending orders volume
  • CopyBuffer return values are not checked, so failed indicator data reads can produce wrong signals

Possible improvements

  • Mandate a stop-loss or add a maximum layer count limit to control martingale risk exposure
  • Fix the getTotalVolume bug using OrderGetDouble for pending orders instead of PositionGetDouble, and check CopyBuffer return values
  • Add maximum drawdown protection that forces position closure when floating loss reaches a configurable percentage of account balance

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Dangerous

  • Martingale lot sizing grows exponentially; consecutive layering can rapidly deplete account funds
  • Default stop-loss of 0 means multi-layer positions have no protection, and a sustained trend can cause massive losses
  • No maximum layer count limit means risk exposure is unbounded in extreme market conditions
