摘要

该EA基于自定义KPrmSt指标的两个缓冲区交叉信号进行交易。当第一缓冲区上穿第二缓冲区时开多仓，反之开空仓，并在反转信号时可选平仓。

策略分析

该EA采用基于KPrmSt自定义指标的双线交叉趋势跟踪策略。入场条件为：当指标缓冲区0从下方穿越缓冲区1时产生买入信号，从上方穿越时产生卖出信号，具体判断通过比较Ma1[1]与Ma2[1]的大小关系以及Ma1[0]与Ma2[0]的交叉来确认。出场条件包括：反向交叉信号触发平仓（可通过BuyPosClose/SellPosClose参数控制），以及固定点数的止损和止盈。仓位管理通过MM参数控制下单手数，支持固定手数和基于保证金比例两种模式。资金管理方面，MMMode参数可选择LOT固定手数模式或其他保证金比例模式。策略使用IsNewBar机制确保每个K线只计算一次信号，避免重复开仓，同时通过SignalBar参数指定信号来源K线位置以减少虚假信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on the crossover of two buffers from the custom KPrmSt indicator. It opens buy positions when the first buffer crosses above the second and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with optional position closing on reverse signals.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a dual-line crossover trend-following strategy based on the custom KPrmSt indicator. Entry conditions are triggered when indicator buffer 0 crosses above buffer 1 for a buy signal, or crosses below for a sell signal, confirmed by comparing Ma1[1] vs Ma2[1] for trend direction and Ma1[0] vs Ma2[0] for crossover confirmation. Exit conditions include reverse crossover signals for closing positions (controllable via BuyPosClose/SellPosClose parameters) and fixed StopLoss/TakeProfit levels in points. Position sizing is managed through the MM parameter, supporting both fixed lot size and margin-based proportional sizing modes. Money management offers flexibility via the MMMode parameter, allowing selection between LOT (fixed lots) and margin-proportional allocation. The strategy uses an IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are calculated only once per bar, preventing duplicate entries, while the SignalBar parameter specifies which bar to read signals from to reduce false signals.

优势

  • 使用IsNewBar机制避免同一K线重复开仓，减少过度交易
  • 提供独立的买入/卖出开仓和平仓开关，灵活性高
  • 止损止盈均可配置，具备基本风控
  • 使用SignalBar=1确认信号，避免当前K线未完成时的虚假信号
  • 支持多种仓位计算模式，适应不同资金规模

劣势

  • 依赖自定义KPrmSt指标，指标逻辑不透明，难以验证策略有效性
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值过大，可能导致单笔亏损过高
  • 没有点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能频繁虚假触发
  • 没有持仓检查机制，可能重复开仓
  • 缺乏移动止盈或追踪止损功能，无法锁定浮动利润

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免在已有同方向仓位时重复开仓
  • 添加点差过滤和最大持仓数限制，提升风控能力

Strengths

  • IsNewBar mechanism prevents duplicate entries within the same bar, reducing overtrading
  • Independent buy/sell open and close switches provide high flexibility
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit provide basic risk control
  • SignalBar=1 confirms signals on completed bars, avoiding false signals from unfinished bars
  • Multiple position sizing modes accommodate different account sizes

Weaknesses

  • Depends on custom KPrmSt indicator whose logic is opaque, making strategy validation difficult
  • Default StopLoss of 1000 points and TakeProfit of 2000 points are very large, risking significant per-trade losses
  • No spread filter, prone to false triggers in high-spread environments
  • No explicit position check before opening, risking duplicate positions
  • Lacks trailing stop or breakeven functionality to lock in floating profits

Improvements

  • Add position check logic to avoid opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Add spread filter and maximum open position limit to enhance risk management
