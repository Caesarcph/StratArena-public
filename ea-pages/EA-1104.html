EA-1104 · Machine Learning

ArtificialIntelligence

This EA uses a linear perceptron model with four weights applied to Accelerator Oscillator (AC) indicator values at shifts 0, 7, 14, and 21 bars to generate buy/sell signals. When a signal opposes an existing profitable position, it performs a position reversal with increased volume; otherwise it trails the stop loss to breakeven plus spread. All positions require a stop loss and there is no take profit.

Strategy analysis

The strategy is based on a linear perceptron that multiplies four AC indicator values at different bar shifts by optimizable weights and sums them; a positive output signals a buy and a negative output signals a sell. Entry conditions require the perceptron signal to trigger and the calculated stop loss to meet the broker's minimum stop level distance. Exit conditions rely primarily on stop loss, but when a position is profitable by more than twice the stop loss distance plus spread, the EA either reverses the position with increased volume on an opposing signal or trails the stop to breakeven if no opposing signal exists. Position sizing adds the new lot size to the existing position volume during reversals, which effectively increases exposure. Money management uses only fixed lot sizing with no dynamic adjustment based on equity or risk percentage. The strategy entirely lacks a take profit mechanism, depending solely on stop loss and breakeen trailing to manage the risk-reward ratio.

Strengths

  • The perceptron model applies weighted combinations to AC indicator values, offering more flexibility than single-indicator signals
  • Includes a breakeven stop loss trailing mechanism that locks in profit once the position moves sufficiently in favor
  • Stop loss is mandatory, ensuring every trade has a risk exit in place
  • Position reversal with increased volume can amplify gains when the market genuinely reverses direction

Weaknesses

  • Position reversal increases exposure by adding volume, which can lead to rapid losses on consecutive false signals
  • Completely lacks a take profit mechanism, meaning profitable positions can only exit via breakeen trailing or reversal, potentially giving back significant gains
  • Perceptron weights require optimization but have no adaptive mechanism, so they may become ineffective after market regime changes
  • Fixed lot sizing with no money management means risk cannot be dynamically adjusted based on account size

Possible improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing that adjusts lot size based on account equity and a risk percentage parameter
  • Implement a take profit or trailing take profit mechanism to prevent significant drawdowns on winning positions
  • Introduce an adaptive optimization mechanism for perceptron weights so they can adjust to changing market conditions

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Caution

  • The reversal with added volume doubles risk per trade, and consecutive reversals can compound losses rapidly
  • No take profit means relying on breakeen trailing, but sharp price reversals may give back profits before the stop can be moved
  • High risk of perceptron weight overfitting, as historically optimized parameters may fail in live markets
