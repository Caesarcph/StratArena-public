摘要

该EA使用线性感知器模型，将加权后的AC（加速振荡器）指标值作为输入来生成买卖信号。感知器输出为正时开多仓，为负时开空仓，均设止损但无止盈。EA实现了仓位反转机制——在盈利且出现反向信号时加大手数反向开仓，若无反向信号则将止损移至保本加价差水平。

策略分析

策略核心是基于AC指标的线性感知器，从4个不同偏移（Shift、Shift+7、Shift+14、Shift+21）提取AC值，分别乘以可优化权重(x1-100, x2-100, x3-100, x4-100)后求和作为感知器输出。入场条件为：感知器输出大于0开多仓，小于0开空仓。出场条件依赖止损平仓，没有止盈设置。仓位管理采用反转加仓模式：当持仓盈利超过2倍止损加价差时，若出现反向信号则以当前仓位手数加新开手数反向开仓（类似马丁加仓），若无法反向开仓则直接平仓。资金管理方面，固定手数开仓，反转时手数为原持仓手数加初始手数，存在逐步加仓风险。止损管理方面，无反向信号时将止损移至保本水平（盈利等于价差），但未设止盈，完全依赖止损和反转逻辑退出。

Summary

This EA uses a linear perceptron model with weighted AC (Accelerator Oscillator) indicator values to generate buy/sell signals. When the perceptron output is positive, it opens a buy position; when negative, it opens a sell position, both with a stoploss but no takeprofit. The EA implements a position reversal mechanism that increases lot size when an opposite signal occurs while in profit, and trails the stoploss to breakeven-plus-spread when no reversal signal is present.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is a linear perceptron based on the AC indicator, extracting AC values from 4 different shifts (Shift, Shift+7, Shift+14, Shift+21), each multiplied by optimizable weights (x1-100, x2-100, x3-100, x4-100) and summed as the perceptron output. Entry conditions: perceptron output greater than 0 opens a buy position, less than 0 opens a sell position. Exit conditions rely entirely on stoploss — there is no takeprofit. Position management uses a reversal-and-scale-up model: when the position profit exceeds 2x stoploss plus spread and an opposite signal appears, the EA opens a reverse position with volume equal to current volume plus the initial lot size (resembling martingale-style scaling). If the reverse position cannot be opened due to stoplevel constraints, the position is closed directly. Money management uses fixed lot sizing for initial entries and cumulative lot sizing on reversals, which creates progressive position scaling risk. Stoploss management moves the stoploss to breakeven-plus-spread when no reversal signal is present, but the absence of takeprofit means exits are entirely dependent on stoploss and reversal logic.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简洁清晰，基于感知器的信号生成方式易于理解和优化
  • 具有止损保护机制，每笔交易都有明确的止损设置
  • 实现了盈利时移动止损至保本的机制，能锁定部分利润
  • 仓位反转逻辑在趋势转变时可以快速调整方向
  • AC指标多周期采样（间隔7根K线）能捕捉不同时间尺度的动量变化

劣势

  • 反转时加仓机制类似马丁格尔，连续反转会导致手数不断增大，风险敞口快速扩大
  • 没有止盈设置，盈利完全依赖止损移动和反转逻辑，可能错过最佳出场时机
  • 感知器权重需要外部优化，默认参数很可能过度拟合历史数据
  • 缺少点差过滤和滑点保护，在高点差环境下止损可能被频繁触发
  • 没有最大持仓手数限制，反转加仓可能超出账户承受能力

改进建议

  • 增加最大持仓手数限制和最大连续反转次数限制，防止仓位无限扩大
  • 添加止盈逻辑或动态止盈（如基于ATR的止盈），避免完全依赖止损出场
  • 增加点差过滤器，当点差过大时暂停开仓，保护免受高点差环境损失

Strengths

  • Clean and simple strategy logic based on a perceptron model, easy to understand and optimize
  • Has stoploss protection with a defined stop for every trade
  • Implements a breakeven stoploss trailing mechanism when in profit, locking in some gains
  • Position reversal logic allows quick direction changes when trend reverses
  • Multi-lag AC sampling (7-bar intervals) captures momentum at different time scales

Weaknesses

  • Reversal scaling resembles martingale — consecutive reversals increase lot size progressively, rapidly expanding risk exposure
  • No takeprofit means exits rely entirely on stoploss trailing and reversal logic, potentially missing optimal exit points
  • Perceptron weights require external optimization; default parameters are likely overfitted to historical data
  • Lacks spread filter and adequate slippage protection; stoplosses may be frequently triggered in high-spread environments
  • No maximum position size limit; reversal scaling could exceed account capacity

Improvements

  • Add maximum position size limit and maximum consecutive reversal count to prevent unlimited position scaling
  • Add takeprofit logic or dynamic takeprofit (e.g., ATR-based) to avoid relying solely on stoploss for exits
  • Add a spread filter to pause trading when spread is too wide, protecting against high-spread environment losses

风险点

  • 无止盈设置，在震荡行情中可能反复触发止损和反转导致累计亏损
  • 固定止损点数不适应不同波动率环境，低波动时止损过大，高波动时止损过小

  • No takeprofit means choppy markets can repeatedly trigger stoploss and reversal, causing cumulative losses
  • Fixed stoploss points don't adapt to varying volatility — too wide in low volatility, too tight in high volatility
