摘要

该EA基于CMO（钱德动量震荡指标）进行交易，当CMO上穿零轴时做多，下穿零轴时做空。它使用可配置平滑类型和价格的自定义CMO指标，并支持基于存款比例或固定手数的仓位管理。

策略分析

该EA的策略核心是利用CMO指标的零轴交叉来判断市场动量方向。入场条件为：当CMO值从负变正（上穿零轴）时开多仓，当CMO值从正变负（下穿零轴）时开空仓，信号通过比较相邻两根K线的CMO值来确认交叉。出场条件包括止损和止盈的固定点数设置，以及反向信号触发平仓——当出现做空信号时平掉多头，出现做多信号时平掉空头。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示固定手数，正值表示占用存款比例。策略在新K线出现时才检测信号，避免重复交易，同时使用权限开关控制开仓和平仓方向。

Summary

This EA trades based on the CMO (Chande Momentum Oscillator) indicator, entering long positions when CMO crosses above zero and short positions when it crosses below zero. It uses a custom CMO indicator with configurable smoothing and price inputs, and supports position sizing based on deposit share or fixed lot size.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy uses the CMO (Chande Momentum Oscillator) zero-line crossover to determine market momentum direction. Entry conditions: a long position is opened when CMO crosses above zero (Value[1]<0 and Value[0]>0), and a short position is opened when CMO crosses below zero (Value[1]>0 and Value[0]<0), with the crossover confirmed by comparing two consecutive bar CMO values. Exit conditions include fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit in points, plus reverse-signal exits where a sell signal closes longs and a buy signal closes shorts. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent the share of deposit per deal. The strategy only checks for signals on new bars to prevent repeated trades, and includes permission switches to independently control opening and closing of long and short positions.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰简洁，基于动量零轴交叉，易于理解和维护
  • 具有止损和止盈设置，基本风险控制到位
  • 新K线检测机制避免同一根K线内重复交易
  • 开仓和平仓方向可独立控制，灵活性高
  • 使用自定义CMO指标，支持多种平滑和价格参数配置

劣势

  • 缺乏移动止损（追踪止损）机制，盈利头寸可能大幅回撤后才平仓
  • 没有考虑点差和手续费对交易成本的影响，尤其是短线交易时
  • CMO零轴交叉在震荡市中容易产生频繁虚假信号导致连续亏损
  • 依赖外部TradeAlgorithms.mqh库文件，缺少该文件则无法编译运行
  • 仓位管理中正值比例模式缺乏最大仓位限制，可能过度加仓

改进建议

  • 增加追踪止损功能，在趋势有利时锁定利润
  • 添加过滤器（如ADX或ATR）以在震荡市中减少虚假信号
  • 增加点差过滤和交易时间限制，降低交易成本影响

Strengths

  • Clear and concise strategy logic based on momentum zero-line crossover, easy to understand and maintain
  • Has StopLoss and TakeProfit settings providing basic risk control
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated trades within the same bar
  • Independent permission switches for opening and closing long/short positions offer flexibility
  • Uses a custom CMO indicator with configurable smoothing and price parameters

Weaknesses

  • No trailing stop mechanism, profitable positions may experience significant drawdown before exit
  • Does not account for spread and commission costs, which can erode profits especially on shorter timeframes
  • CMO zero-line crossovers generate frequent false signals in choppy/ranging markets leading to consecutive losses
  • Depends on external TradeAlgorithms.mqh include file; missing it prevents compilation
  • The positive-value deposit share mode lacks a maximum position limit, risking over-leveraging

Improvements

  • Add a trailing stop feature to lock in profits when the trend moves favorably
  • Add filters such as ADX or ATR to reduce false signals in ranging markets
  • Add spread filtering and trading session restrictions to minimize cost impact
