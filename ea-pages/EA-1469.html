摘要

该EA基于ADX_Cross_Hull_Style指标的交叉信号和UltraXMA指标的趋势过滤进行交易。当ADX交叉信号与UltraXMA趋势方向一致时开仓，在反向信号出现时平仓。完整版还增加了UltraXMA作为额外的交易方向确认过滤。

策略分析

该策略结合了ADX交叉信号和UltraXMA平滑趋势过滤，属于多指标确认的趋势跟踪系统。入场条件为：ADX_Cross_Hull_Style指标产生上穿信号（UpValue非零且非空值）时做多，产生下穿信号时做空；完整版还要求UltraXMA的上轨和下轨关系确认趋势方向，若UpUXMA<DnUXMA则禁止做多并强制平多仓，反之亦然。出场条件包括：反向ADX交叉信号触发平仓，以及当初始信号未触发平仓时，通过遍历历史K线寻找最近的反向信号来决定平仓。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示固定手数，正值表示按存款比例计算仓位。止损和止盈分别固定为1000点和2000点，通过TradeAlgorithms.mqh库函数执行交易操作。策略使用了丰富的平滑算法（JJMA、JurX、ParMA、T3、VIDYA、AMA等）来减少信号噪音。

Summary

This EA trades based on signals from the ADX_Cross_Hull_Style indicator for entry direction and the UltraXMA indicator as a trend filter. It opens buy/sell positions when ADX crossover signals align with the UltraXMA trend direction, and closes positions on opposing signals or historical signal searches. The full version also incorporates UltraXMA as an additional confirming filter for trade direction.

Strategy Analysis

This strategy combines ADX crossover signals with UltraXMA smoothed trend filtering, forming a multi-indicator confirmed trend-following system. Entry conditions: ADX_Cross_Hull_Style generates a bullish crossover signal (UpValue non-zero and non-empty) for buy entries and bearish crossover for sell entries; the full version additionally requires UltraXMA trend confirmation where UpUXMA<DnUXMA prohibits longs and forces long closes, and vice versa. Exit conditions include: opposing ADX crossover signals trigger position closes, and when the initial signal doesn't trigger a close, the EA searches historical bars backwards to find the most recent opposing signal for closing decisions. Position sizing is controlled via the MM parameter where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent deposit-share-based sizing. Stop loss and take profit are fixed at 1000 and 2000 points respectively, with trade execution handled through the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library functions. The strategy employs a rich set of smoothing algorithms (JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, AMA, etc.) to reduce signal noise across multiple smoothing layers.

优势

  • 双指标确认机制降低假信号，ADX交叉信号加UltraXMA趋势过滤提高了入场质量
  • 支持丰富的平滑算法选择（JJMA、JurX、ParMA、T3、VIDYA、AMA等），灵活性强
  • 包含买卖开仓和平仓的独立开关，可精细控制交易方向和出场逻辑
  • 新K线检测机制避免同一K线重复交易，减少了过度交易风险
  • 历史K线反向搜索逻辑提供了额外的平仓保障机制

劣势

  • 固定止损止盈不根据市场波动性（ATR）动态调整，在不同市场条件下可能不适配
  • 缺乏点差过滤，在小时间周期或高波动时段可能因点差过大导致不利成交
  • 历史K线遍历搜索平仓信号效率低，在K线数量大时可能影响性能
  • 依赖自定义指标ADX_Cross_Hull_Style和UltraXMA，若这些指标不可用则EA无法运行
  • MM参数设计混淆，负值为手数正值为比例，不够直观易误操作

改进建议

  • 增加ATR动态止损止盈功能，根据市场波动性自动调整止损止盈距离
  • 添加点差过滤和时间交易过滤，避免在高点差时段和非理想时段开仓
  • 优化历史K线搜索逻辑，限制最大搜索K线数量避免性能问题

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator confirmation mechanism reduces false signals; ADX crossover plus UltraXMA trend filtering improves entry quality
  • Supports a rich set of smoothing algorithms (JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, AMA) offering high flexibility
  • Independent switches for buy/sell opening and closing enable fine-grained control over trade direction and exit logic
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated trading on the same bar, reducing overtrading risk
  • Historical bar reverse-search logic provides an additional safeguard for position closing

Weaknesses

  • Fixed stop loss and take profit do not dynamically adjust based on market volatility (ATR), potentially misaligned under different market conditions
  • Lacks spread filtering, which may lead to unfavorable fills during high-spread periods or on lower timeframes
  • Historical bar iteration for searching closing signals is inefficient and may impact performance with large bar counts
  • Depends on custom indicators ADX_Cross_Hull_Style and UltraXMA; EA cannot run if these are unavailable
  • MM parameter design is confusing: negative values for lots and positive for deposit share ratio, easy to misconfigure

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss/take profit to automatically adjust distances based on market volatility
  • Add spread filter and session/time filter to avoid trading during high-spread periods and suboptimal hours
  • Optimize historical bar search logic by capping the maximum number of bars to search, preventing performance issues

风险点

  • 固定止损止盈无法适应不同市场波动环境，极端行情下止损可能过小或过大
  • MM参数的负值/正值逻辑容易导致误操作，可能意外使用过大手数
  • 历史K线遍历无上限约束，可能长时间阻塞OnTick执行
  • 缺乏持仓检查，可能在已有同方向仓位时重复开仓

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed stop loss/take profit cannot adapt to different market volatility environments; stops may be too small or too large in extreme conditions
  • MM parameter's negative/positive logic is error-prone and may accidentally result in excessively large lot sizes
  • Historical bar iteration has no upper bound, potentially blocking OnTick execution for extended periods
  • Lacks position existence checks before opening, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
