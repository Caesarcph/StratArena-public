MASTER_WINNERFX_GOLD_MT5

This EA is a grid trading system for gold (XAUUSD) that uses EMA trend direction and RSI momentum for initial entries, then adds up to 3 martingale-scaled grid positions at fixed pip intervals when price moves against the trade. All positions are closed when combined profit reaches a target or loss exceeds a maximum risk threshold.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is a trend-following grid pyramiding system that combines EMA for trend direction and RSI for momentum confirmation before initial entry. Entry conditions are: go long when price is above EMA and RSI is above 55, or go short when price is below EMA and RSI is below 45. Exit conditions are twofold: close all positions when total combined profit reaches TargetProfit, or close all when total loss exceeds MaxRisk. Position sizing uses dynamic lots calculated from account balance at LotPer1000 per $1000, and grid additions use a Multiplier factor to increase lot size progressively, making this a martingale-style pyramiding approach. Money management checks free margin via CanOpenTrade before each order and normalizes lot size via GetSafeLot, but the maximum grid level is hard-coded to 3 layers. Grid spacing is fixed at GridStep points, and no individual stop-loss or take-profit price levels are set on any order, relying entirely on combined P&L triggers for exits.

Strengths

  • Uses dual EMA and RSI filters for initial entry direction, avoiding counter-trend openings
  • Dynamic lot sizing automatically adjusts based on account balance, providing proportional risk management
  • MaxRisk parameter caps maximum allowed loss, preventing total account blowout
  • Pauses trading during news hours and market close hour, avoiding high-volatility periods
  • Uses MagicNumber to tag positions, preventing conflicts with other EAs

Weaknesses

  • Grid pyramiding uses a 1.2x martingale multiplier, causing increasingly large positions against the trend which amplifies losses exponentially
  • No individual stop-loss prices are set on any order, relying entirely on programmatic closing via OnTick which fails if the terminal disconnects
  • RSI thresholds are counterintuitive with buy threshold at 55 and sell threshold at 45, the narrow gap may cause frequent misjudgment of direction
  • Maximum grid layers are hard-coded to 3 with no parameter to adjust, limiting flexibility
  • News pause is based only on a fixed hour, unable to adapt to different economic calendars or actual news release times
  • No new bar check is implemented, every tick triggers evaluation which could lead to redundant or rapid-fire order attempts

Possible improvements

  • Add new bar detection logic to prevent repeated entries and grid additions within the same candle
  • Set stop-loss price levels on each individual order to protect against terminal disconnection scenarios where programmatic closing cannot execute
  • Make maximum grid layers a configurable parameter and add a total exposure lot size cap check
  • Add spread filtering to avoid opening positions during high-spread conditions, especially before/after news and during low-liquidity sessions

Risk assessment

Overall risk: High risk

  • Martingale-style grid pyramiding rapidly amplifies losses in adverse markets, with 3 layers at 1.2x multiplier reaching 1.728x base lot on the final layer
  • No stop-loss prices on any order means if the MT5 terminal disconnects from the server, programmatic closing logic cannot execute, leaving positions fully exposed to unlimited market risk
  • No new bar check mechanism could cause rapid-fire repeated entry triggers within extremely short timeframes
  • MaxRisk is set as a fixed dollar value rather than a percentage, causing the risk proportion to vary as the account grows or shrinks
