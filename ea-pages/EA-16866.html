摘要

该EA实现了经典的海龟交易系统，基于唐奇安通道突破策略。当价格突破N日高点时做多，突破N日低点时做空，使用ATR进行仓位计算并采用金字塔加仓方式。出场依靠反向突破信号或基于较短周期的跟踪止损。

策略分析

海龟交易策略是一种经典的趋势跟踪突破系统，由Richard Dennis和William Eckhardt开发。入场条件为：当价格突破过去20日（可配置）最高价时开多单，突破过去20日最低价时开空单。系统支持金字塔加仓，每突破0.5个ATR即可加仓一次，最多加仓4个单位。仓位管理基于ATR指标，每个交易单位的风险控制在账户净值的1%-2%之间，通过将1个ATR对应的点值除以账户风险比例来确定每手交易量。出场条件使用较短周期（通常为10日）的反向突破，即持有多单时若价格跌破10日最低价则平仓，持有空单时若价格涨破10日最高价则平仓。此外还设有基于2个ATR的止损，防止单笔交易亏损过大。整体策略逻辑清晰、规则明确，属于典型的系统性趋势跟踪策略。

Summary

This EA implements the classic Turtle Trading system based on Donchian Channel breakouts. It enters long when price breaks above the N-day high and enters short when price breaks below the N-day low, using ATR-based position sizing and a pyramiding approach. Exits are triggered by opposite breakout signals or trailing stops based on a shorter period.

Strategy Analysis

The Turtle Trading strategy is a classic trend-following breakout system developed by Richard Dennis and William Eckhardt. Entry conditions: go long when price breaks above the highest high of the past 20 days (configurable), and go short when price breaks below the lowest low of the past 20 days. The system supports pyramiding, adding one unit each time price moves 0.5 ATR in the trade's favor, up to a maximum of 4 units per position. Position sizing is based on the ATR indicator, with each unit's risk controlled to 1-2% of account equity, calculated by dividing the dollar value of 1 ATR by the account risk percentage. Exit conditions use a shorter period (typically 10 days) for reverse breakouts: close longs if price falls below the 10-day low, close shorts if price rises above the 10-day high. Additionally, a stop-loss at 2 ATR from the entry point prevents excessive single-trade losses. The overall strategy logic is clear and rule-based, representing a textbook systematic trend-following approach.

优势

  • 策略逻辑经典且经过长期实盘验证，海龟交易法是量化交易史上的标杆系统
  • ATR动态仓位管理使风险随市场波动自适应调整，有效控制单笔亏损
  • 金字塔加仓机制能在趋势行情中最大化盈利，同时通过单位上限控制风险敞口
  • 双向突破入场兼顾多空机会，不会错过任何大级别趋势
  • 短周期出场规则使利润回吐控制在合理范围内

劣势

  • 趋势跟踪策略在震荡市中会产生频繁的虚假突破信号，导致连续亏损
  • 没有内置的震荡市过滤器（如ADX或波动率收缩判断），无法区分趋势行情和盘整行情
  • 止损设为2倍ATR可能过宽，在高波动品种上单笔亏损金额较大
  • 金字塔加仓在快速反转行情中可能导致严重回撤，所有仓位同时止损出场
  • 缺少滑点和点差的过滤，在低流动性时段突破入场可能获得较差成交价

改进建议

  • 增加ADX或波动率收缩指标作为趋势过滤器，在震荡市中暂停交易以减少虚假信号
  • 加入加仓仓位独立止损机制，每层加仓使用各自的止损位而非统一止损，降低反转风险

Strengths

  • The strategy logic is classic and battle-tested over decades; the Turtle system is a benchmark in systematic trading history
  • ATR-based dynamic position sizing adapts risk to market volatility, effectively controlling per-trade losses
  • Pyramiding mechanism maximizes profits during trending markets while capping exposure through unit limits
  • Dual-direction breakout entries capture both long and short opportunities without missing major trends
  • Shorter-period exit rules keep profit givebacks within reasonable bounds

Weaknesses

  • Trend-following strategies generate frequent false breakout signals in ranging markets, leading to consecutive losses
  • No built-in ranging market filter (such as ADX or volatility contraction detection) to distinguish trending from consolidating conditions
  • The 2x ATR stop-loss may be too wide on highly volatile instruments, resulting in large per-trade losses
  • Pyramiding can cause severe drawdowns during sharp reversals when all units are stopped out simultaneously
  • Lacks slippage and spread filtering; breakout entries during low-liquidity sessions may get poor fills

Improvements

  • Add an ADX or volatility contraction indicator as a trend filter to suspend trading during ranging markets and reduce false signals
  • Implement independent stop-losses for each pyramided unit rather than a unified stop, reducing reversal risk exposure
