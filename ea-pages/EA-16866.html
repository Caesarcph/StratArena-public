摘要

TurtleTrader是基于理查德·丹尼斯和威廉·埃克哈特开发的经典海龟交易系统的专家顾问。它在价格突破唐奇安通道上轨时做多，突破下轨时做空，使用基于ATR的仓位管理和加仓策略。出场条件为价格突破反向通道或触及基于ATR的固定止损。

策略分析

该EA实现了经典的海龟交易策略，核心概念是基于唐奇安通道（Donchian Channel）的突破交易系统。入场条件为：当价格突破N日最高价时做多，突破N日最低价时做空，通常使用20日通道作为短期入场、55日通道作为长期入场。出场条件为：当价格跌破10日最低价时平多仓，突破10日最高价时平空仓，同时设有基于2倍ATR的止损作为第二道出场保护。仓位管理采用ATR归一化方法，每个头寸的波动风险限定为账户权益的固定百分比（通常1%-2%），即手数=（账户权益×风险百分比）/（ATR×每点价值）。资金管理方面支持金字塔加仓，每在有利方向波动0.5倍ATR时加仓一个单位，最多加仓4次，总持仓风险可控。整体策略强调截断亏损、让利润奔跑的趋势跟踪哲学。

Summary

TurtleTrader is an Expert Advisor based on the classic Turtle Trading system developed by Richard Dennis and William Eckhardt. It enters long positions on breakouts above the Donchian Channel high and short positions on breakouts below the Donchian Channel low, using ATR-based position sizing and pyramiding. Exits are triggered when price breaks the opposite channel or when a fixed ATR-based stop loss is hit.

Strategy Analysis

This EA implements the classic Turtle Trading strategy, whose core concept is a breakout system based on the Donchian Channel. Entry conditions are: go long when price breaks above the N-day high, and go short when price breaks below the N-day low, typically using a 20-day channel for short-term entries and a 55-day channel for long-term entries. Exit conditions are: close long positions when price breaks below the 10-day low, close short positions when price breaks above the 10-day high, with a 2x ATR stop loss serving as a secondary protective exit. Position sizing uses ATR normalization, where each position's volatility risk is limited to a fixed percentage of account equity (typically 1%-2%), calculated as lots = (account equity × risk percentage) / (ATR × point value). Money management supports pyramiding — adding one unit each time price moves 0.5x ATR in the favorable direction, up to a maximum of 4 additional units, keeping total position risk manageable. The overall strategy emphasizes the trend-following philosophy of cutting losses short and letting profits run.

优势

  • 采用经过数十年验证的经典海龟交易策略，逻辑清晰、理论基础扎实
  • ATR归一化的仓位管理使风险与市场波动性挂钩，自动适应不同品种和行情
  • 金字塔加仓机制能够在趋势行情中充分获利，同时通过最大单位数限制总风险

劣势

  • 趋势跟踪策略在震荡市中会产生频繁的虚假突破亏损，回撤期可能很长
  • 缺乏市场状态过滤器（如ADX或波动率筛选），在非趋势行情中仍会持续交易
  • 固定参数的唐奇安通道无法适应不同品种和周期的波动特征差异

改进建议

  • 增加市场状态过滤器（如ADX>25才允许交易），减少震荡市中的虚假突破亏损
  • 引入自适应通道周期（如根据波动率动态调整N值），提高不同品种和周期的适用性

Strengths

  • Based on the decades-proven classic Turtle Trading strategy with clear logic and solid theoretical foundation
  • ATR-normalized position sizing links risk to market volatility, automatically adapting to different instruments and market conditions
  • Pyramiding mechanism captures full trend moves while capping total risk through maximum unit limits

Weaknesses

  • Trend-following strategy generates frequent false breakout losses in ranging markets, with potentially long drawdown periods
  • Lacks a market regime filter (such as ADX or volatility screening), continuing to trade in non-trending conditions
  • Fixed-parameter Donchian Channel cannot adapt to volatility characteristic differences across instruments and timeframes

Improvements

  • Add a market regime filter (e.g., only trade when ADX>25) to reduce false breakout losses in ranging markets
  • Introduce adaptive channel periods (e.g., dynamically adjusting N based on volatility) to improve applicability across instruments and timeframes
