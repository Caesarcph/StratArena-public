摘要

HelloSmart是一个马丁格尔风格的网格EA，按固定价格间隔开仓，当持仓总量超过BigLot时将手数乘以倍增系数。达到盈利目标或亏损限额时平掉所有仓位并重置手数。

策略分析

策略概念：该EA采用马丁格尔加仓策略，在价格每移动Step个点后加仓，试图通过增大仓位来摊薄成本并在回调时获利。入场条件：当BuySell=1时，若无持仓或当前Ask比上次开仓价格低Step个点，则买入；当BuySell=2时，若无持仓或当前Bid比上次开仓价格高Step个点，则卖出。出场条件：当累计浮动盈利超过Profit美元或亏损超过Loss美元时，平掉该品种所有仓位。仓位管理：初始使用Lot手数开仓，当累计持仓量达到BigLot时，手数乘以Mnogitel倍增系数，若手数超过MaxLots则重置为Lot。资金管理：仅通过Profit和Loss两个美元阈值控制盈亏，无止损止盈设置，且平仓后手数重置为初始值。该策略在单边行情中会持续加仓，风险极大。

Summary

HelloSmart is a Martingale-style grid EA that opens positions at fixed price intervals (Step) and multiplies lot size by a Mnogitel factor when total position volume exceeds BigLot. It closes all positions when a profit target or loss limit is reached, then resets lot size to the initial value.

Strategy Analysis

Strategy concept: This EA employs a Martingale position-sizing approach, adding positions at fixed price intervals (Step points) and scaling up lot size to average down entry price, aiming to profit on price reversals. Entry conditions: When BuySell=1, it buys if there are no positions or if Ask has dropped Step points below the last entry price; when BuySell=2, it sells if there are no positions or if Bid has risen Step points above the last entry price. Exit conditions: All positions on the symbol are closed when cumulative floating profit exceeds Profit dollars or loss exceeds Loss dollars. Position sizing: Starts with Lot size, multiplies by Mnogitel when total volume reaches BigLot, and resets to Lot if the computed lot exceeds MaxLots. Money management: Relies solely on dollar-based profit/loss thresholds for closure, with no individual stop-loss or take-profit set on any position. The strategy continuously adds positions in trending markets, creating extreme drawdown risk.

优势

  • 逻辑简单直观，易于理解和修改
  • 有全局止盈止损机制，限制最大亏损
  • 手数倍增后设有MaxLots上限和重置机制，防止无限加仓
  • 使用新K线判断避免逐tick重复开仓

劣势

  • 马丁格尔策略在单边行情中风险极高，亏损限额5100美元相对盈利60美元过大
  • 无单笔止损止盈，完全依赖全局平仓机制
  • SymbProfit和Lots函数存在逻辑错误：pos.Select(Symb)未使用循环变量i，导致仅检查同一持仓
  • CloseAll函数同样存在pos.Select索引错误，无法正确遍历所有仓位
  • 无滑点、点差、保证金检查，实盘可能开仓失败
  • 手数倍增系数10倍过大，容易迅速达到MaxLots上限

改进建议

  • 修复pos.Select调用逻辑，应使用pos.SelectByIndex(i)或pos.Select(pos.Ticket(i))来正确遍历持仓
  • 添加单笔止损止盈，降低单方向持续加仓的风险
  • 增加点差过滤和保证金检查，提高实盘可靠性
  • 将Mnogitel倍增系数降低至合理范围（如1.5-2），并采用更保守的加仓策略

Strengths

  • Simple and intuitive logic, easy to understand and modify
  • Has global profit/loss thresholds to limit maximum drawdown
  • MaxLots cap and lot reset mechanism prevents infinite lot escalation
  • Uses new bar detection to avoid repeated tick-level entries

Weaknesses

  • Martingale strategy carries extreme risk in trending markets; the loss limit of $5100 vs profit target of $60 has terrible risk-reward ratio
  • No individual stop-loss or take-profit, relying entirely on global closure mechanism
  • SymbProfit and Lots functions have a critical bug: pos.Select(Symb) ignores loop variable i, only checking the same position repeatedly
  • CloseAll function has the same index bug, unable to correctly iterate all positions
  • No slippage, spread, or margin checks; real trading may fail to open positions
  • Mnogitel factor of 10x is excessively aggressive, quickly reaching MaxLots cap

Improvements

  • Fix pos.Select call logic — use pos.SelectByIndex(i) to correctly iterate through positions instead of selecting by symbol name inside the loop
  • Add individual stop-loss and take-profit to reduce risk of continuous one-direction position accumulation
  • Add spread filter and margin check to improve real-trading reliability
  • Reduce Mnogitel multiplier to a reasonable range (1.5-2x) and adopt a more conservative position-sizing approach
