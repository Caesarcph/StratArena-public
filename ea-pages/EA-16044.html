摘要

该EA基于极化分形效率(PFE)指标进行交易，当PFE向上穿越上阈值时开多仓，向下穿越下阈值时开空仓。它使用可配置的平滑方法处理PFE值，并通过资金管理规则确定仓位大小。策略在出现新信号时平掉反向仓位，并支持分别开关入场和出场逻辑。

策略分析

策略核心基于极化分形效率(PFE)指标，该指标衡量价格运动的分形效率，正值表示上升趋势效率高，负值表示下降趋势效率高。入场条件为：当PFE值从下方穿越上阈值(+0.5默认)时触发买入信号，当PFE值从上方穿越下阈值(-0.5默认)时触发卖出信号，这本质上是动量突破策略。出场条件包括：固定止损(1000点)和止盈(2000点)，以及当反向信号出现时平掉对应仓位（如PFE高于上阈值时平空仓）。仓位管理支持五种模式：基于可用保证金、基于余额、基于亏损后可用保证金、基于亏损后余额、以及固定手数，默认使用固定手数模式。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例(默认0.1)，结合MMMode选择具体的仓位计算方式。策略还引入了新K线检测机制，避免同一根K线内重复开仓，并在信号确认使用SignalBar参数允许回看指定数量的K线。

Summary

This EA trades based on the Polarized Fractal Efficiency (PFE) indicator, opening buy positions when PFE crosses above the upper level and sell positions when it crosses below the lower level. It uses a smoothed PFE value with configurable moving average methods and applies money management rules for position sizing. The strategy closes opposite-direction positions on new signals and supports separate toggles for entry and exit logic.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is based on the Polarized Fractal Efficiency (PFE) indicator, which measures the fractal efficiency of price movement — positive values indicate high uptrend efficiency while negative values indicate high downtrend efficiency. Entry conditions trigger a buy signal when PFE crosses above the upper threshold (+0.5 by default) from below, and a sell signal when PFE crosses below the lower threshold (-0.5 by default) from above, making this essentially a momentum breakout strategy. Exit conditions include a fixed stop loss (1000 points) and take profit (2000 points), plus closing opposite-direction positions when a reverse signal fires (e.g., closing sells when PFE is above the upper level). Position sizing supports five modes: based on free margin, balance, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size, with the fixed lot mode as default. Money management is controlled via the MM parameter for risk ratio (default 0.1), combined with MMMode to select the specific lot calculation method. The strategy also implements a new bar detection mechanism to prevent duplicate entries within the same bar, and uses the SignalBar parameter to allow looking back a specified number of bars for signal confirmation.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于PFE指标的动量穿越，入场条件明确且可量化验证
  • 提供丰富的参数配置，包括10种平滑方法、9种价格类型和5种仓位管理模式，灵活度高
  • 止损止盈设置合理(1:2风险回报比)，且有反向信号平仓机制提供额外保护
  • 支持分别控制做多做空的入场和出场开关，便于灵活调整策略方向
  • 使用新K线检测机制防止同根K线重复开仓，避免过度交易

劣势

  • 默认止损1000点和止盈2000点数值过大，在大多数品种上可能导致单笔风险过高
  • 没有持仓检查机制，可能在新信号出现时重复开仓导致仓位叠加
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时段过滤，在点差较大或低流动性时段可能产生不利成交
  • PFE指标参数(周期5)较短，容易产生频繁的假信号导致反复开平仓
  • 没有追踪止损或动态止损机制，持仓期间风险敞口无法随盈利缩减

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，在开仓前确认同方向无已有仓位，防止仓位叠加风险
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段控制，避免在不利条件下开仓
  • 引入追踪止损机制，在盈利达到一定比例后自动收紧止损保护利润
  • 优化PFE参数默认值，使用更长周期减少假信号，或增加信号确认过滤条件

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on PFE indicator momentum crossover with well-defined, quantifiable entry conditions
  • Rich parameter configuration including 10 smoothing methods, 9 price types, and 5 position sizing modes for high flexibility
  • Reasonable risk-reward ratio (1:2 with SL/TP) plus reverse signal closing mechanism for additional protection
  • Separate toggle switches for buy/sell entry and exit, enabling flexible directional strategy adjustments
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate entries within the same bar, avoiding overtrading

Weaknesses

  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large, potentially leading to high per-trade risk on most instruments
  • No position check mechanism — may open duplicate positions on repeated signals causing position stacking
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, which may result in unfavorable fills during high-spread or low-liquidity periods
  • Short PFE period (5) is prone to generating frequent false signals causing repeated open/close cycles
  • No trailing stop or dynamic stop mechanism, so risk exposure cannot be reduced as profit accumulates during a trade

Improvements

  • Add position check logic before opening trades to confirm no existing same-direction positions, preventing position stacking risk
  • Implement spread filter and trading session controls to avoid opening positions under unfavorable conditions
  • Introduce a trailing stop mechanism that automatically tightens the stop loss after reaching a certain profit threshold
  • Optimize default PFE parameters with longer periods to reduce false signals, or add signal confirmation filters
