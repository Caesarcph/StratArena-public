摘要

TrendCapture是一个趋势跟踪EA，利用抛物线SAR和ADX指标识别交易入场信号。当收盘价高于SAR且ADX低于阈值时开多仓，收盘价低于SAR且ADX低时开空仓。EA还实现了保本功能，并利用最近一笔平仓交易的盈亏方向来过滤新入场。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于抛物线SAR指标判断趋势方向，结合ADX指标过滤低趋势强度环境下的交易信号。入场条件为：收盘价高于SAR值且ADX主线低于设定阈值(默认20)时开多仓，收盘价低于SAR值且ADX主线低于阈值时开空仓。ADX低于阈值表示市场趋势不强但SAR已有方向信号，这是一种在趋势初期入场的逻辑。出场条件主要依赖固定的止损(1800点)和止盈(500点)，同时配有保本功能，当持仓浮盈超过50点时将止损移至开仓价。仓位管理方面，当Lots参数为0时根据账户自由保证金和风险比例动态计算手数，否则使用固定手数。该EA还引入了一个独特的方向过滤机制CheckAllowedDir，通过检查最近一笔历史平仓交易的盈亏情况来决定是否允许当前方向的开仓——如果上笔盈利的交易方向与当前信号方向一致则允许，反之则阻止，这是一种基于历史交易结果的方向过滤策略。

Summary

TrendCapture is a trend-following EA that uses Parabolic SAR and ADX indicators to identify trade entry signals. It opens buy positions when the close price is above SAR and ADX is below a threshold, and sell positions when the close price is below SAR with low ADX. The EA also implements a breakeven function and uses the last closed deal's profit direction to filter new entries.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the Parabolic SAR indicator to determine trend direction, combined with the ADX indicator to filter trade signals in low-trend-strength environments. Entry conditions are: open a buy position when the close price is above the SAR value and the ADX main line is below the set threshold (default 20), and open a sell position when the close price is below the SAR value and ADX is below the threshold. ADX below the threshold indicates weak market trend while SAR already shows a directional signal, which is a logic for entering at the early stage of a trend. Exit conditions primarily rely on fixed stop loss (1800 points) and take profit (500 points), along with a breakeven function that moves the stop loss to the entry price when floating profit exceeds 50 points. For position sizing, when the Lots parameter is 0, the lot size is dynamically calculated based on account free margin and risk ratio; otherwise, a fixed lot size is used. The EA also introduces a unique directional filtering mechanism called CheckAllowedDir, which checks the profit/loss of the most recent historical closed deal to decide whether to allow opening in the current direction — if the last profitable trade direction matches the current signal direction, it is allowed; otherwise, it is blocked. This is a directional filtering strategy based on historical trade results.

优势

  • 使用SAR和ADX双指标组合，既有趋势方向判断又有趋势强度过滤
  • 实现了保本功能，在浮盈达到一定点数后保护本金安全
  • 引入历史交易方向过滤机制，避免在连续亏损方向上重复开仓
  • 支持动态手数计算，根据账户余额和风险比例自动调整仓位
  • 使用CTrade等标准库类进行交易操作，代码结构相对规范

劣势

  • 止损(1800点)远大于止盈(500点)，盈亏比仅约1:0.28，需要极高胜率才能盈利
  • ADX低于阈值时才允许交易意味着在趋势较弱时入场，与趋势跟踪策略理念矛盾
  • CheckAllowedDir逻辑仅基于最近一笔交易结果判断方向，样本量过小容易产生错误过滤
  • 缺少移动止盈/追踪止损功能，趋势行情中可能过早止盈而无法充分捕获利润
  • 没有考虑点差和滑点对短线交易的影响，缺乏交易时间过滤和市场环境判断

改进建议

  • 优化盈亏比，建议将止盈提高或缩小止损，使盈亏比至少达到1:1以上
  • 将ADX过滤逻辑改为ADX高于阈值时才允许交易，以真正捕捉趋势行情
  • 增加追踪止损功能以替代固定止盈，在趋势行情中获取更大利润
  • 增加点差过滤和时间过滤，避免在高点差时段和不利市场条件下交易

Strengths

  • Combines SAR and ADX dual indicators, providing both trend direction identification and trend strength filtering
  • Implements a breakeven function that protects capital when floating profit reaches a certain threshold
  • Introduces a historical trade direction filtering mechanism to avoid repeated entries in consistently losing directions
  • Supports dynamic lot size calculation based on account balance and risk ratio for automatic position sizing
  • Uses standard library classes like CTrade for trading operations, resulting in relatively structured code

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss (1800 points) is far larger than take profit (500 points), resulting in a risk-reward ratio of only about 1:0.28, requiring extremely high win rate to be profitable
  • Allowing trades only when ADX is below the threshold means entering during weak trends, which contradicts the trend-following strategy philosophy
  • The CheckAllowedDir logic judges direction based on only the most recent trade result, with too small a sample size prone to incorrect filtering
  • Lacks trailing stop functionality, potentially exiting too early in trending markets and failing to capture full profit potential
  • Does not account for spread and slippage impact on trading, and lacks trading session filters and market environment assessment

Improvements

  • Optimize the risk-reward ratio by increasing take profit or reducing stop loss to achieve at least 1:1 or better
  • Change the ADX filter logic to allow trading only when ADX is above the threshold, to truly capture trending markets
  • Add a trailing stop function to replace the fixed take profit, allowing capture of larger profits in trending markets
  • Add spread filter and time filter to avoid trading during high-spread periods and unfavorable market conditions

  • ADX低于阈值入场逻辑可能导致在震荡市中频繁虚假信号

  • Lacks over-leveraging protection, MaximumRisk parameter may calculate excessively large lot sizes
