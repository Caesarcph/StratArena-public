摘要

TrendCapture是一个基于抛物线SAR的趋势跟踪EA，使用ADX作为过滤器在低波动趋势条件下交易。当收盘价高于SAR且ADX低于阈值时做多，收盘价低于SAR且ADX低于阈值时做空。保本功能在持仓达到足够盈利后将止损移至入场价，历史交易方向过滤器则倾向于跟随最近盈利交易的方向进行后续交易。

策略分析

策略概念基于抛物线SAR判断趋势方向，结合ADX低于阈值确认市场处于温和趋势状态而非强趋势或震荡。入场条件为：做多需收盘价高于SAR值且ADX低于设定水平（默认20），做空需收盘价低于SAR值且ADX低于设定水平，同时受到历史交易方向过滤器的限制——如果上一笔盈利交易是卖出，则禁止做多，反之亦然。出场条件依靠固定止损（1800点）和止盈（500点），止损远大于止盈，风险回报比约3.6:1，属于典型的大止损小止盈趋势跟踪策略。仓位管理支持固定手数或根据可用保证金和风险比例动态计算手数。保本功能在持仓盈利达到50点时将止损移至开仓价，提供了一定的利润保护机制。

Summary

TrendCapture is a Parabolic SAR-based trend following EA that uses ADX as a filter to trade in low-volatility trending conditions. It enters buy when close is above SAR and ADX is below a threshold, and sell when close is below SAR and ADX is below threshold. A break-even function moves stop loss to entry price once a position reaches sufficient profit, and a historical deal direction filter biases future trades toward the direction of the last profitable deal.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the Parabolic SAR for trend direction identification, combined with an ADX below-threshold filter to confirm the market is in a mild trending condition rather than a strong trend or choppy consolidation. Entry conditions require: buy when close is above SAR and ADX is below the set level (default 20), sell when close is below SAR and ADX is below the set level, additionally constrained by a historical deal direction filter that prohibits trading in the opposite direction of the last profitable deal. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss (1800 points) and take profit (500 points), with a risk-reward ratio of approximately 3.6:1, which is characteristic of a wide-stop narrow-profit trend following approach. Position sizing supports either fixed lots or dynamic lot calculation based on available free margin and a risk percentage parameter. The break-even function moves the stop loss to the entry price once the position profit reaches 50 points, providing a modest profit protection mechanism.

优势

  • 使用SAR和ADX双重过滤，逻辑清晰易懂
  • 具备保本功能，能在盈利后锁定风险
  • 支持固定手数和动态手数两种仓位管理模式
  • 有历史交易方向过滤器，避免盲目反手交易
  • 使用标准MQL5交易类库，代码结构规范

劣势

  • 风险回报比极差，止损1800点而止盈仅500点，约3.6:1
  • 历史交易方向过滤逻辑存疑——上一笔盈利方向不一定是未来趋势方向
  • 没有新K线检查机制（Shift=1时依赖时间比较但Shift=0时每个tick都执行）
  • ADX低于20才交易的设计可能错过强趋势行情
  • 没有点差过滤，在大幅点差环境下可能造成不必要的亏损

改进建议

  • 优化风险回报比，增加移动止盈或追踪止损替代固定止盈
  • 移除或重新设计历史交易方向过滤器，改为基于市场状态的过滤器
  • 添加点差过滤和新K线检查机制以提高执行质量

Strengths

  • Dual filter using SAR and ADX provides clear and understandable logic
  • Break-even function locks in risk after reaching profit threshold
  • Supports both fixed and dynamic position sizing modes
  • Historical deal direction filter prevents blind counter-trend entries
  • Uses standard MQL5 trade class libraries with well-structured code

Weaknesses

  • Extremely poor risk-reward ratio with 1800-point SL vs 500-point TP, approximately 3.6:1
  • Historical deal direction filter logic is questionable — last profitable direction does not predict future trend
  • No proper new bar check mechanism when Shift=0, causing per-tick execution
  • ADX-below-20 design may filter out strong trending opportunities
  • No spread filter, potentially causing unnecessary losses in high-spread environments

Improvements

  • Optimize risk-reward ratio by adding trailing stop or dynamic take profit instead of fixed TP
  • Remove or redesign the historical deal direction filter, replace with market-state-based filter
  • Add spread filter and proper new bar check mechanism to improve execution quality
