摘要

该EA基于抛物线SAR与价格的相对方向进行交易，并通过ADX低于阈值进行过滤（低趋势市场）。它使用基于历史交易结果的独特方向过滤器，偏向于最近盈利平仓方向的入场，并包含保本功能以保护利润。

策略分析

策略概念是基于抛物线SAR判断价格趋势方向，当收盘价高于SAR值时视为买入信号，低于时视为卖出信号。入场条件还需ADX值低于设定阈值（默认20），这意味着EA在趋势较弱时入场，本质上是一种反常规的趋势跟踪策略——在低ADX环境下追随SAR方向。出场条件依赖固定的止损和止盈点数，以及保本功能：当浮盈达到BreakEven点数时将止损移至开仓价。仓位管理支持固定手数或基于可用保证金和风险比例的动态计算。资金管理通过MaximumRisk参数控制风险敞口，但计算公式（FreeMargin*Risk/1000）缺乏严格的理论依据。独特的CheckAllowedDir函数根据最近一笔平仓交易的盈亏结果来限制下一笔交易方向——如果上次卖出盈利则只允许买入，如果上次卖出亏损则只允许卖出，这是一种经验性的方向过滤机制。

Summary

This EA trades based on Parabolic SAR direction relative to price, filtered by ADX being below a threshold (low-trending market). It uses a unique history-based direction filter that biases future entries toward the side of the last profitable closed deal, and includes a break-even function to protect profits.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on Parabolic SAR to determine price trend direction — when close is above SAR it signals a buy, when below it signals a sell. Entry conditions also require ADX to be below a set threshold (default 20), meaning the EA enters during weak-trending environments, which is counter-intuitive for a trend-following system. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit in points, plus a break-even function that moves the stop loss to the entry price when floating profit reaches the BreakEven threshold. Position sizing supports either fixed lots or dynamic calculation based on free margin and risk percentage. Money management uses the MaximumRisk parameter to control exposure, but the lot calculation formula (FreeMargin*Risk/1000) lacks rigorous theoretical justification. The unique CheckAllowedDir function restricts the next trade direction based on the last closed deal's profit/loss — if the last sell was profitable, only buys are allowed, and vice versa, serving as an empirical directional filter mechanism.

优势

  • 使用SAR和ADX双重过滤，具备一定的入场逻辑框架
  • 包含保本功能，能在浮盈达到阈值时保护本金
  • 动态手数计算可根据账户资金调整仓位大小
  • 有止损止盈设置，单笔风险可控
  • 新K线检查机制避免同一根K线重复开仓

劣势

  • ADX低于阈值才开仓的逻辑与趋势跟踪策略矛盾，SAR在低趋势环境下频繁翻转导致假信号
  • 止损1800点远大于止盈500点，风险收益比极差（1:0.28），需要极高胜率才能盈利
  • CheckAllowedDir方向过滤逻辑缺乏理论支撑，可能导致连续错过有效信号
  • LotsOptimized公式（FreeMargin*Risk/1000）不严谨，未考虑止损距离来计算真实风险
  • 没有点差过滤和滑点保护，在剧烈行情中可能以不利价格成交

改进建议

  • 将ADX过滤逻辑改为ADX高于阈值时才开仓，以顺应SAR的趋势跟踪本质
  • 改善风险收益比，缩小止损或增大止盈，使盈亏比至少达到1:1以上
  • 将动态手数计算改为基于止损距离的真实风险计算：Lot = RiskAmount / (StopLoss * TickValue)
  • 增加点差过滤，当点差过大时暂停开仓以避免滑点损失
  • 改进CheckAllowedDir逻辑，可考虑加入时间衰减或连续亏损计数器来动态调整方向偏置

Strengths

  • Uses dual filtering with SAR and ADX, providing a structured entry logic framework
  • Includes a break-even function that protects capital when floating profit reaches the threshold
  • Dynamic lot calculation adjusts position size based on account equity
  • Has stop loss and take profit settings, controlling per-trade risk
  • New bar check mechanism prevents repeated entries on the same bar

Weaknesses

  • The ADX-below-threshold entry logic contradicts trend-following principles — SAR frequently flips in low-trending environments, generating false signals
  • Stop loss of 1800 points vastly exceeds take profit of 500 points, yielding a poor risk-reward ratio (1:0.28) that requires extremely high win rates to be profitable
  • The CheckAllowedDir directional filter lacks theoretical justification and may cause consecutive missed valid signals
  • The LotsOptimized formula (FreeMargin*Risk/1000) is not rigorous — it ignores stop loss distance for calculating true risk exposure
  • No spread filter or slippage protection, which may result in unfavorable fills during volatile conditions

Improvements

  • Change ADX filter logic to require ADX above the threshold for entries, aligning with SAR's trend-following nature
  • Improve risk-reward ratio by reducing stop loss or increasing take profit to achieve at least 1:1 or better
  • Replace the dynamic lot formula with true risk-based sizing: Lot = RiskAmount / (StopLoss * TickValue)
  • Add a spread filter to pause entries when spread is excessively wide, avoiding slippage costs
  • Improve CheckAllowedDir logic by incorporating time decay or a consecutive loss counter to dynamically adjust directional bias
