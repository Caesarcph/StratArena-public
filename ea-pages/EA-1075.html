TrendCapture

TrendCapture is a trend-following EA that uses Parabolic SAR and ADX indicators to determine trade direction. It opens buy positions when price is above SAR and ADX is below a threshold, and sell positions when price is below SAR with low ADX. The EA also incorporates a breakeven function and uses historical deal outcomes to restrict trade direction based on the last closed position's result.

摘要

TrendCapture是一个基于抛物线SAR和ADX指标的趋势跟踪EA。当价格在SAR上方且ADX低于阈值时开多仓，价格在SAR下方且ADX低于阈值时开空仓。该EA还包含保本功能，并根据历史交易结果限制交易方向。

策略分析

策略概念基于抛物线SAR判断趋势方向，结合ADX指标确认市场是否处于趋势初期（ADX低于阈值表示趋势尚未强烈建立，此时入场可以捕捉趋势的早期阶段）。入场条件：做多需收盘价高于SAR值且ADX值低于设定水平；做空需收盘价低于SAR值且ADX值低于设定水平。出场条件依赖固定的止损和止盈点位，同时有保本功能——当浮盈达到BreakEven点数时，将止损移至开仓价。仓位管理支持固定手数和基于自由保证金的风险比例计算两种模式。资金管理通过LotsOptimized函数实现，当Lots=0时根据账户自由保证金和MaximumRisk比例动态计算手数，并经过规范化处理确保符合品种的最小/最大手数和步长限制。此外，CheckAllowedDir函数根据最近一笔平仓交易的盈亏结果限制下一笔交易的方向——盈利则继续同方向交易，亏损则反向交易，这是一种简单的结果依赖型方向过滤机制。

Summary

TrendCapture is a trend-following EA that uses Parabolic SAR and ADX indicators to determine trade direction. It opens buy positions when price is above SAR and ADX is below a threshold, and sell positions when price is below SAR with low ADX. The EA also incorporates a breakeven function and uses historical deal outcomes to restrict trade direction based on the last closed position's result.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the Parabolic SAR to determine trend direction, combined with the ADX indicator to confirm whether the market is in the early stage of a trend (ADX below the threshold suggests the trend has not yet strongly established, allowing entry at the trend's inception). Entry conditions: a buy is triggered when the close price is above the SAR value and ADX is below the set level; a sell is triggered when the close price is below the SAR value and ADX is below the set level. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit points, along with a breakeven function that moves the stop loss to the entry price when unrealized profit reaches the BreakEven threshold in points. Position sizing supports two modes: fixed lots and dynamic calculation based on free margin and the MaximumRisk percentage. Money management is implemented via the LotsOptimized function, which dynamically calculates lot size based on account free margin and MaximumRisk when Lots=0, with normalization to comply with the symbol's minimum/maximum volume and step size constraints. Additionally, the CheckAllowedDir function restricts the next trade direction based on the result of the most recent closed deal — continuing in the same direction after a profit and reversing direction after a loss, serving as a simple outcome-dependent directional filter.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，使用SAR和ADX两个经典指标组合，易于理解和调试
  • 具备完整的止损止盈和保本功能，风险控制体系较为完善
  • 手数计算支持动态模式，可根据账户资金自动调整仓位大小
  • 利用历史交易结果进行方向过滤，避免连续亏损后继续同方向交易
  • 指标句柄在OnDeinit中正确释放，资源管理规范

劣势

  • 止损1800点远大于止盈500点，风险收益比约为3.6:1，需要极高的胜率才能盈利
  • ADX低于阈值作为入场条件意味着在弱势趋势中交易，与趋势跟踪策略的理念存在矛盾
  • CheckAllowedDir仅依赖最近一笔交易结果决定方向，逻辑过于简单且缺乏统计学依据
  • 没有新K线检查机制（Shift=0时每次tick都尝试交易），可能导致重复信号处理
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时间过滤，在点差扩大或流动性低的时段仍会开仓
  • 仅使用单一仓位（Pos.Select），无法同时管理多品种交易

改进建议

  • 优化风险收益比，建议缩小止损或增大止盈，或引入移动止损替代固定止损以改善盈亏比
  • 将ADX入场条件改为ADX高于阈值时入场，这样才真正符合趋势跟踪策略的逻辑——在趋势已确立时顺势交易
  • 增加新K线检查机制，避免在同一K线上重复处理信号
  • 增加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免在不利条件下开仓
  • CheckAllowedDir逻辑应改为基于更长历史的统计胜率或连续亏损计数来决定方向，而非仅依赖单笔交易结果

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic using the classic SAR and ADX indicator combination, easy to understand and debug
  • Complete stop loss, take profit, and breakeven functionality providing a solid risk control framework
  • Dynamic lot sizing mode available, automatically adjusting position size based on account equity
  • Historical deal outcome filtering prevents continuing in the same direction after a loss
  • Indicator handles properly released in OnDeinit, following good resource management practices

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss of 1800 points far exceeds take profit of 500 points, resulting in a risk-reward ratio of approximately 3.6:1, requiring an extremely high win rate to be profitable
  • Using ADX below threshold as an entry condition means trading in weak trends, which contradicts the core philosophy of trend-following strategies
  • CheckAllowedDir relies solely on the most recent trade result to determine direction, which is overly simplistic and lacks statistical basis
  • No new bar check mechanism (when Shift=0, every tick attempts to trade), potentially leading to redundant signal processing
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, allowing entries during spread widening or low liquidity periods
  • Single position model only (Pos.Select), unable to manage multiple symbol trades simultaneously

Improvements

  • Optimize the risk-reward ratio by reducing stop loss or increasing take profit, or introduce trailing stop to replace fixed stop loss for better profitability
  • Change the ADX entry condition to require ADX above the threshold, which truly aligns with trend-following logic — entering when the trend is confirmed rather than when it is weak
  • Add a new bar check mechanism to avoid processing signals repeatedly on the same bar
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to prevent opening positions under unfavorable conditions
  • Improve CheckAllowedDir logic to use statistical win rate over a longer history or consecutive loss count rather than relying on a single trade result

  • 当Shift=0时缺少新K线检查，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓尝试
  • 动态手数计算基于自由保证金，在盈利时仓位可能快速增大，放大回撤风险
  • 没有点差过滤，高点差环境下止损止盈可能被滑点严重影响

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Stop loss far exceeds take profit (1800 vs 500 points), meaning a single loss is much larger than a single win, requiring a high win rate for positive expectancy
  • Lack of new bar check when Shift=0 may cause repeated trade attempts on the same bar
  • Dynamic lot sizing based on free margin may rapidly increase position size during winning streaks, amplifying drawdown risk
  • No spread filter means high spread environments can severely impact stop loss and take profit execution via slippage
