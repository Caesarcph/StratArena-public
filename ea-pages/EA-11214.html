摘要

该EA基于自定义TSI_MACD指标的交叉信号进行交易，该指标结合了真实强度指数(TSI)平滑与MACD式信号线逻辑。当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿时开空仓，支持可配置的仓位管理、止损和止盈。入场和出场由独立的布尔开关控制，允许灵活的信号过滤。

策略分析

策略核心是基于TSI_MACD指标的动量交叉系统。TSI（True Strength Index）是一种双重平滑动量指标，通过对价格动量进行两次平滑处理来减少噪音，然后用MACD式的信号线交叉产生交易信号。入场条件为：指标线Ind上穿信号线Sign时开多仓，Ind下穿Sign时开空仓，交叉判断通过比较相邻两根K线的Ind和Sign值大小关系实现。出场条件包括：反向交叉信号触发平仓（多仓在Ind下穿Sign时平仓，空仓在Ind上穿Sign时平仓），以及固定的StopLoss和TakeProfit止盈止损。仓位管理支持多种模式：按自由保证金比例、按余额比例、按净损保证金比例、按净损余额比例，以及固定手数模式。资金管理参数MM控制仓位比例，默认0.1即10%。策略使用IsNewBar机制确保每个K线只计算一次信号，避免重复开仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on crossover signals from the custom TSI_MACD indicator, which combines True Strength Index smoothing with MACD-style signal line logic. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with configurable position sizing, stop loss, and take profit. Trade entry and exit are controlled by separate boolean flags, allowing flexible signal filtering.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is a momentum crossover system based on the TSI_MACD indicator. TSI (True Strength Index) is a double-smoothed momentum indicator that reduces noise by applying two rounds of smoothing to price momentum, then generates trading signals using MACD-style signal line crossovers. Entry conditions: a buy position opens when the indicator line Ind crosses above the signal line Sign, and a sell position opens when Ind crosses below Sign, with crossover detection implemented by comparing Ind and Sign values across two consecutive bars. Exit conditions include: reverse crossover signals trigger position closing (long positions close when Ind crosses below Sign, short positions close when Ind crosses above Sign), along with fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit levels. Position sizing supports multiple margin modes: percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of net-loss free margin, percentage of net-loss balance, and fixed lot mode. The money management parameter MM controls the position ratio, defaulting to 0.1 (10%). The strategy uses the IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signal calculation occurs only once per bar, preventing duplicate entries.

优势

  • 使用TSI双重平滑动量指标，相比原始MACD噪音更少，信号更稳定
  • 入场和出场信号独立控制，可以只启用开仓或平仓，灵活性高
  • 采用IsNewBar机制避免同一K线内重复计算和重复开仓，逻辑严谨
  • 支持10种平滑算法选择，策略可定制性强
  • 有Recount机制处理指标数据未就绪的情况，增强了健壮性

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点设置过大，在非大周期或低波动品种上可能导致风险过高或资金利用率低
  • 缺少点差过滤和波动率过滤，在高点差或低波动环境下容易产生虚假信号
  • 没有多空仓位互斥检查，可能同时持有多空仓位形成对冲，浪费保证金
  • 依赖自定义外部指标TSI_MACD和外部头文件，部署依赖较多，不利于移植
  • SignalBar=1使用已完成K线信号，但交叉检测逻辑要求比较[1]和[0]两根bar，实际延迟可能大于预期

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和ATR自适应止损止盈，根据市场波动率动态调整风险参数
  • 增加持仓方向检查，避免同时持有多空仓位，或至少提供可选的对冲模式开关
  • 增加指标数据有效性校验和错误重试机制，提升在指标加载失败时的容错能力

Strengths

  • Uses TSI double-smoothed momentum indicator, producing less noisy and more stable signals compared to raw MACD
  • Entry and exit signals are independently controlled, allowing flexible enable/disable of buy/sell open and close operations
  • Implements IsNewBar mechanism to prevent redundant signal calculation and duplicate entries within the same bar
  • Supports 10 different smoothing algorithms, offering strong strategy customizability
  • Includes a Recount mechanism to handle cases when indicator data is not yet ready, enhancing robustness

Weaknesses

  • StopLoss of 1000 points and TakeProfit of 2000 points are excessively large, potentially causing high risk or low capital efficiency on non-large timeframe or low-volatility instruments
  • Lacks spread filter and volatility filter, making it prone to false signals in high-spread or low-volatility environments
  • No mutual exclusion check between long and short positions, potentially holding both simultaneously and wasting margin on hedging
  • Depends on custom external indicator TSI_MACD and external include files, creating deployment complexity and poor portability
  • SignalBar=1 uses completed bar signals, but the crossover detection logic compares bars [1] and [0], so actual signal delay may be larger than expected

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and ATR-adaptive stop loss/take profit to dynamically adjust risk parameters based on market volatility
  • Add position direction check to prevent simultaneously holding long and short positions, or at least provide a configurable hedging mode toggle
  • Add indicator data validity checks and improved error retry mechanisms to enhance fault tolerance when indicator loading fails

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 止损止盈为固定点数而非ATR自适应，在不同波动率环境下风险敞口不一致
  • 缺少点差过滤，高点差品种上可能因滑点导致实际亏损大于预期
  • 多空仓位可同时持有形成隐性对冲，占用双倍保证金但不一定有对冲意图

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed-point stop loss/take profit is not ATR-adaptive, resulting in inconsistent risk exposure across different volatility environments
  • No spread filter means high-spread instruments may incur actual losses greater than expected due to slippage
  • Long and short positions can be held simultaneously forming implicit hedging, consuming double margin without necessarily intending to hedge
