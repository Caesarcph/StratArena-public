摘要

该EA基于自定义TSI_MACD指标的金叉死叉信号进行交易，当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿时开空仓。它结合了真实强度指数(TSI)概念与MACD式信号线逻辑，并使用多种平滑算法，配有可配置的资金管理和仓位控制选项。

策略分析

策略概念基于TSI（真实强度指数）与MACD信号线的交叉，属于动量类交易系统。入场条件为：当指标线(Ind)高于信号线(Sign)且前一根K线指标线低于等于信号线时，开多仓；当指标线低于信号线且前一根K线指标线高于等于信号线时，开空仓。出场条件包括反向信号触发平仓以及固定点数的止损和止盈。仓位管理支持多种模式，包括固定手数、基于余额百分比、基于可用保证金百分比等模式，通过MM和MMMode参数控制。资金管理使用MarginMode枚举提供五种模式，从自由保证金到固定手数均有覆盖。该EA还提供了买卖方向的独立开关控制，允许只开启单向交易或单向平仓，增加了灵活性。

Summary

This EA trades based on crossover signals from a custom TSI_MACD indicator, opening buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions when it crosses below. It uses multiple smoothing algorithms applied to the True Strength Index (TSI) concept combined with MACD-style signal line logic, with configurable money management and position control options.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on crossover signals between the TSI (True Strength Index) indicator line and its MACD-style signal line, classifying it as a momentum trading system. Entry conditions: a buy position opens when the indicator line (Ind) is above the signal line (Sign) and the previous bar's indicator line was at or below the signal line; a sell position opens when the opposite crossover occurs. Exit conditions include reverse signal-triggered position closing and fixed StopLoss/TakeProfit levels in points. Position sizing supports multiple modes including fixed lot, balance percentage, and free margin percentage, controlled by the MM and MMMode parameters. Money management leverages the MarginMode enumeration offering five distinct modes ranging from free margin-based to fixed lot sizing. The EA also provides independent on/off switches for buy/sell direction trading and closing, allowing unidirectional-only operation for added flexibility.

优势

  • 支持十种平滑算法，灵活度高，适应不同市场环境
  • 买卖方向可独立控制，方便只做多或只做空
  • 具有NewBar检测机制，避免同一根K线重复开仓
  • 提供多种资金管理模式，从保守到激进均可配置

劣势

  • 依赖外部自定义指标TSI_MACD，缺乏内嵌计算逻辑，指标缺失时EA无法运行
  • 止损止盈参数值偏大（1000/2000点），可能在某些品种上不合适
  • 没有点差过滤机制，在价差较大时仍可能开仓
  • 没有回撤控制或最大持仓限制，极端行情下风险较大

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤，当点差超过阈值时禁止开仓
  • 加入回撤控制和最大同时持仓数限制

Strengths

  • Supports ten smoothing algorithms offering high flexibility to adapt to different market conditions
  • Independent buy/sell direction controls allow long-only or short-only operation
  • NewBar detection mechanism prevents duplicate position opening on the same bar
  • Multiple money management modes available from conservative to aggressive configurations

Weaknesses

  • Depends on external custom indicator TSI_MACD; EA cannot run if the indicator file is missing
  • StopLoss/TakeProfit default values are large (1000/2000 points) and may not suit all instruments
  • No spread filter mechanism; positions may open even when spread is wide
  • No drawdown control or maximum position limit; high risk during extreme market conditions

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter to prevent opening positions when spread exceeds a threshold
  • Add drawdown control and maximum concurrent position limits
