Exp_SilverTrend

This EA trades based on the SilverTrend_Signal custom indicator, opening buy positions on upward signals and sell positions on downward signals. It supports configurable money management modes, stop loss/take profit, and optional position closing on reverse signals. The strategy is a signal-based breakout system that enters on new bar formations when the indicator produces non-empty values.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy uses the SilverTrend_Signal indicator to detect trend breakout signals for trading. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when indicator buffer 1 (UpValue) produces a non-zero, non-EMPTY_VALUE value on the specified SignalBar; a sell signal triggers when buffer 0 (DnValue) produces a valid value. Exit conditions include hard stop loss and take profit, as well as reverse signal closing — buy signals close sell positions and sell signals close buy positions. Position sizing supports 5 money management modes including fixed lot, balance percentage, and free margin percentage. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter for risk ratio and MMMode for the calculation mode. The EA also includes an additional reverse signal search logic: when both open and close on reverse are enabled, it scans forward through historical bars to find earlier reverse signals for confirmation, which adds conservatism but may delay position closing.

Strengths

  • Supports multiple money management modes with high flexibility for different risk preferences
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit provide clear risk control mechanisms
  • Independent switches for opening and closing allow flexible strategy combinations such as open-only or close-only modes
  • Uses IsNewBar mechanism to control signal detection frequency, preventing repeated trades within the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the external custom SilverTrend_Signal indicator whose logic is opaque and hard to verify
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments, leading to high risk exposure
  • The reverse signal search logic iterates through all historical bars, potentially causing performance issues on long history data
  • Lacks spread filter, trading session filter, and market condition filter mechanisms
  • No position check logic — may open duplicate positions in the same direction

Possible improvements

  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate openings in the same direction
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to improve trade quality
  • Optimize reverse signal search logic by limiting maximum bar count to improve performance

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Default SL/TP of 1000/2000 points are excessively large, creating very high per-trade risk exposure
  • No same-direction position check may lead to duplicate openings amplifying risk
  • Dependency on external indicator with opaque logic makes signal reliability unverifiable
