摘要

该EA使用移动平均线和MACD指标生成交易信号，当持仓浮亏时采用加仓摊平（马丁格尔式）策略。通过在不利价格上不断加仓来降低持仓均价，期望价格反转时达到保本或小幅盈利。该策略将趋势判断与激进的摊平仓位管理相结合。

策略分析

策略概念基于MA（移动平均线）与MACD双重指标确认入场方向，当MA趋势方向与MACD信号一致时开仓。入场条件为MA金叉/死叉配合MACD柱状图方向确认，双重过滤减少假信号。出场条件依赖于整体持仓达到盈利目标或反转信号触发全部平仓，而非单笔止损。仓位管理是该EA的核心特征——当已有持仓浮亏达到设定阈值时，以加倍的仓位（或递增倍数）再次入场摊平均价，属于典型的马丁格尔加仓模式。资金管理方面，EA通过倍数递增手数来摊低成本，但未设定最大加仓次数或总仓位上限，存在爆仓风险。整体策略在趋势行情中可能有效，但在单边持续不利行情中将面临致命风险。

Summary

This EA uses Moving Average and MACD indicators to generate trade signals, then employs a position averaging (martingale-style) approach when trades go against it. It opens additional positions at worsening prices to lower the average entry price, aiming for a break-even or small profit on a reversal. The strategy combines trend detection with an aggressive cost-averaging position management system.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on dual indicator confirmation using Moving Average and MACD to determine entry direction, opening positions when the MA trend direction aligns with the MACD signal. Entry conditions require MA crossover aligned with MACD histogram direction confirmation, using dual filtering to reduce false signals. Exit conditions depend on the overall portfolio reaching a profit target or a reversal signal triggering closure of all positions, rather than individual stop-losses per trade. Position sizing is the EA's core characteristic — when an existing position is in a floating loss exceeding a set threshold, it re-enters with multiplied lot size to average down the entry price, which is a classic martingale position-averaging approach. In terms of money management, the EA uses progressively increasing lot multipliers to lower cost basis, but does not set a maximum number of averaging rounds or total position limit, creating a blow-up risk. The strategy may work in ranging or mean-reverting markets, but faces fatal risk during sustained one-sided adverse moves.

优势

  • 双重指标过滤（MA+MACD）减少假信号入场
  • 摊平策略在震荡行情中能够有效降低持仓成本并实现盈利
  • 设定了整体止盈目标，有明确的获利了结机制

劣势

  • 马丁格尔加仓模式在单边行情中面临爆仓风险，亏损可能呈指数级增长
  • 缺乏硬性止损保护，单次交易没有独立止损机制
  • 加仓次数和总仓位虽有参数限制但默认值仍较高，资金消耗速度极快
  • 未考虑点差和手续费对摊平策略的累积影响

改进建议

  • 增加硬性最大回撤止损，当总浮亏达到账户余额一定比例时强制全部平仓
  • 将固定倍数加仓改为基于ATR或波动率的动态加仓间距，避免在剧烈行情中密集加仓

Strengths

  • Dual indicator filtering (MA+MACD) reduces false signal entries
  • Averaging strategy can effectively lower cost basis and achieve profit in ranging markets
  • Has a clear overall take-profit target for closing all positions

Weaknesses

  • Martingale averaging approach faces blow-up risk in trending markets, losses can grow exponentially
  • Lacks hard stop-loss protection — no individual trade stop-loss mechanism
  • Although max orders parameter exists, the default is still high and capital depletion is extremely rapid
  • Does not account for cumulative impact of spreads and commissions on the averaging strategy

Improvements

  • Add a hard maximum drawdown stop — force close all positions when total floating loss reaches a percentage of account balance
  • Replace fixed multiplier averaging with dynamic step sizing based on ATR or volatility to avoid dense entries during violent moves
