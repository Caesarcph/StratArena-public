摘要

该EA结合移动平均线和MACD指标产生交易信号，当持仓亏损时采用加仓摊平（马丁格尔式）策略。它按设定的间距逐步加仓以降低均价，当整体持仓达到盈利目标时全部平仓。

策略分析

策略概念基于MA和MACD双重确认的趋势跟踪信号入场，当价格向不利方向运动达到设定的间距（Step）时，以更大的手数（乘数LotExponent）加仓摊平均价。入场条件为MA方向确认加上MACD信号线交叉或柱状图方向确认。出场条件为当所有同向持仓的整体浮盈达到TakeProfit目标时一次性全部平仓。仓位管理采用马丁格尔式递增，每次加仓手数为前一次的LotExponent倍，这意味着亏损越大仓位越重。资金管理方面没有硬性止损，完全依赖均价拉低后的反弹回本，存在爆仓风险。整体策略本质上是一个带技术指标入场的马丁格尔摊平系统，在单边行情中可能因持续加仓导致巨大亏损。

Summary

This EA uses a combination of Moving Average and MACD indicators to generate trade signals, then employs a position averaging (martingale-style) strategy when positions go into loss. It opens additional positions at a defined step distance to average down the entry price, closing all positions when the overall portfolio reaches a profit target.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on dual confirmation of MA trend direction and MACD signal line crossover or histogram direction for trade entry. When price moves against the position by a defined Step distance, the EA adds positions with increased lot size (multiplied by LotExponent) to average down the entry price. Entry conditions require MA direction confirmation combined with MACD signal crossover or histogram direction alignment. Exit conditions trigger when the total floating profit of all same-direction positions reaches the TakeProfit target, at which point all positions are closed simultaneously. Position sizing follows a martingale-style progression where each additional position's lot size is multiplied by LotExponent, meaning the larger the loss, the heavier the position becomes. Money management lacks a hard stop loss, relying entirely on price reverting after averaging down, which carries significant margin call risk in trending markets.

优势

  • MA+MACD双重过滤提高了入场信号的质量，相比纯马丁格尔策略入场更合理
  • 加仓摊平在震荡行情中回本效率较高，能够利用价格波动获利
  • 参数可配置性强，交易者可以调整加仓倍率、间距和最大次数来控制风险敞口
  • 整体止盈机制确保一旦行情回归即可锁定利润，逻辑清晰

劣势

  • 马丁格尔式加仓在单边行情中会导致仓位指数级增长，极易爆仓
  • 没有硬性止损保护，完全依赖价格回归，在强趋势中可能造成灾难性亏损
  • 加仓乘数LotExponent大于1时手数增长极快，资金消耗速度超出多数交易者预期
  • 缺少新K线检查机制，可能在同一根K线内重复开仓
  • 未考虑点差和佣金成本对摊平效果的影响，实际回本距离大于理论值

改进建议

  • 增加硬性止损机制，设置最大亏损金额或最大回撤比例限制，防止爆仓
  • 添加新K线检查和点差过滤，避免在不利条件下重复开仓和在高点差时交易
  • 限制最大持仓手数或最大风险敞口占比，加入账户净值监控作为安全阀
  • 优化加仓策略，考虑使用固定手数加仓而非倍数递增，降低指数级风险

Strengths

  • MA+MACD dual filter improves entry signal quality compared to pure martingale strategies
  • Position averaging recovers efficiently in ranging markets by exploiting price fluctuations
  • Highly configurable parameters allow traders to adjust multiplier, step distance, and max trades to control risk exposure
  • Overall take-profit mechanism ensures profits are locked once price reverts, with clear logic

Weaknesses

  • Martingale-style position sizing causes exponential lot growth in trending markets,极易leading to margin calls
  • No hard stop loss protection means the strategy relies entirely on price reversion, risking catastrophic losses in strong trends
  • LotExponent multiplier greater than 1 causes rapid lot size growth, draining capital faster than most traders anticipate
  • Lacks new bar check mechanism, potentially opening multiple positions within the same candle
  • Does not account for spread and commission costs on averaging effectiveness, making actual breakeven distance larger than theoretical

Improvements

  • Add hard stop loss mechanism with maximum loss amount or maximum drawdown percentage limit to prevent margin calls
  • Implement new bar check and spread filter to avoid repeated entries within the same candle and trading during high spread conditions
  • Cap maximum total lot size or maximum risk exposure as a percentage of account equity as a safety valve
  • Optimize the averaging strategy by considering fixed-lot increments instead of multiplicative scaling to reduce exponential risk
