Trend Me Leave Me

This EA identifies trend direction using moving averages and generates entry signals when the trend is confirmed, then manages trades with a trailing stop mechanism. It opens positions in the direction of the prevailing trend and exits when the trend reverses or the trailing stop is hit. The EA is designed to capture medium-to-long-term trend movements while protecting profits with dynamic stop management.

Strategy analysis

The strategy core is a trend following system based on moving averages, using the crossover of fast and slow MAs or the relationship between price and MAs to determine trend direction. Entry conditions require trend confirmation, with long entries when MAs are in bullish alignment and price is above the MA, and short entries when bearish alignment with price below the MA. Exit conditions include trend reversal signals (MA crossover in opposite direction) or trailing stop activation, where the trailing stop continuously moves upward as profit increases to lock in gains. Position sizing uses either fixed lots or a percentage-based calculation of account balance, without complex pyramiding logic. Money management relies on the trailing stop for single-trade risk control, but there is no explicit maximum drawdown limit or account-level risk management mechanism. The overall strategy logic is clear but relatively basic, lacking consolidation market filtering and multi-timeframe confirmation.

Strengths

  • Clear and simple trend following logic that is easy to understand and adjust
  • Trailing stop mechanism protects accrued profits and reduces giveback risk
  • Mature strategy concept with stable performance in trending markets
  • Highly configurable parameters allowing flexible adjustment for different instruments and timeframes
  • Fixed stop loss setting prevents excessive single-trade losses

Weaknesses

  • Pure trend strategy prone to frequent stop losses in ranging markets, producing consecutive losses
  • Lacks multi-timeframe confirmation, may enter on false breakouts
  • No trading session filter, may generate poor signals during low liquidity periods
  • Position sizing is simplistic without dynamic volatility-based adjustment
  • Missing account-level risk controls such as maximum drawdown limits or daily loss caps

Possible improvements

  • Add a ranging market filter (e.g., ADX or ATR threshold) to reduce trading frequency during low trend strength
  • Incorporate multi-timeframe confirmation logic to improve entry signal reliability
  • Dynamically adjust stop loss and trailing stop distances based on ATR to adapt to different market volatility environments

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Lacks ranging market filter, prone to consecutive losses in sideways markets
  • No account-level maximum drawdown limit, risk exposure can be large during extreme market conditions
  • Position sizing does not account for market volatility, risk may exceed expectations during high volatility
