摘要

Probe是一个基于动量的专家顾问，使用力度指标和均线收敛来检测市场脉冲运动。它在强动量转换方向上入场，当动量消退或触及追踪止损时出场。该EA专为主要货币对的中期交易而设计。

策略分析

Probe EA的策略核心是基于力度指标(Force Index)的动量突破交易。入场条件为：当力度指标突破其移动平均线且价格位于关键均线之上时做多，反之做空。出场条件包括：力度指标反向穿越其均线、固定止损/止盈触发、或追踪止损被触及。仓位管理采用固定手数模式，用户可通过参数调整每笔交易的风险比例。资金管理方面，EA提供了最大持仓限制和每日最大亏损限制，以防止连续亏损导致账户大幅回撤。整体策略偏向趋势跟踪与动量确认相结合的中周期交易方式。

Summary

Probe is a momentum-based Expert Advisor that uses the Force Index and moving average convergence to detect impulsive market moves. It enters trades in the direction of strong momentum shifts and exits when momentum fades or a trailing stop is hit. The EA is designed for medium-term trading on major currency pairs.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of Probe EA is momentum breakout trading based on the Force Index indicator. Entry conditions: a long position is opened when the Force Index crosses above its moving average and price is above a key moving average, and vice versa for short positions. Exit conditions include the Force Index crossing back below its moving average, fixed stop-loss or take-profit being triggered, or a trailing stop being hit. Position sizing uses a fixed lot model, with users able to adjust the risk ratio per trade via input parameters. For money management, the EA provides maximum position limits and daily maximum loss limits to prevent consecutive losses from causing significant account drawdowns. The overall strategy combines trend-following with momentum confirmation for medium-term trading.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于力度指标的动量确认具有统计学依据
  • 提供了多重出场机制（止损、止盈、追踪止损），风险控制较为全面
  • 参数设计合理，用户可根据自身风险偏好灵活调整
  • 每日最大亏损限制功能有效防止极端行情下的账户灾难性回撤
  • 代码结构简洁，适合学习和二次开发

劣势

  • 固定手数模式未根据账户余额动态调整，存在过度交易风险
  • 缺乏新K线检查机制，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓
  • 未包含点差过滤，在高点差环境下入场成本可能过高
  • 力度指标在震荡行情中容易产生虚假信号导致频繁亏损
  • 缺少交易时段过滤，可能在低流动性时段产生不利成交

改进建议

  • 增加基于账户余额百分比的动态仓位计算功能
  • 添加新K线检查和点差过滤以避免重复交易和高成本入场
  • 引入交易时段过滤和ATR自适应止损以适应不同市场环境

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on Force Index momentum confirmation with statistical justification
  • Multiple exit mechanisms (stop-loss, take-profit, trailing stop) providing comprehensive risk control
  • Well-designed parameters allowing flexible adjustment based on individual risk preferences
  • Daily maximum loss limit effectively prevents catastrophic account drawdowns during extreme market conditions
  • Clean code structure suitable for learning and further development

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing does not dynamically adjust based on account balance, posing over-trading risk
  • Lacks new bar check mechanism, potentially opening repeated positions on the same bar
  • No spread filter included, resulting in potentially high entry costs in high-spread environments
  • Force Index tends to generate false signals in ranging markets, leading to frequent losses
  • Missing trading session filter, which may cause unfavorable fills during low-liquidity periods

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account balance percentage for better risk scaling
  • Implement new bar check and spread filter to avoid duplicate trades and high-cost entries
  • Introduce trading session filter and ATR-adaptive stop-loss to adapt to varying market conditions
