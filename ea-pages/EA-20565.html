摘要

该EA基于'吸收'K线形态进行交易，当一根K线的实体完全包住前一根K线的实体时，视为潜在反转信号。形态确认后，沿吸收K线方向开仓。策略采用固定手数，无马丁格尔或网格成分。

策略分析

策略核心是经典的吸收（吞没）K线形态识别，即当前K线的实体完全覆盖前一根K线的实体，判断为市场力量反转信号。入场条件为：阳线吞没阴线时做多，阴线吞没阳线时做空，形态在K线收盘确认后触发交易。出场条件依赖止损和止盈参数的设定，若未设置则可能无明确退出机制。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，不做动态调整或加仓。资金管理方面没有基于账户余额或风险的动态计算，存在单笔风险不可控的问题。整体策略逻辑简单直观，但缺乏过滤条件和多重确认，容易在震荡行情中产生虚假信号导致频繁亏损。

Summary

This EA trades based on the 'Absorption' candlestick pattern, where a candle's body engulfs the previous candle's body, signaling a potential reversal. It enters trades in the direction of the absorbing candle after the pattern is confirmed. The strategy applies fixed lot sizing without martingale or grid components.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy centers on identifying the classic Absorption (Engulfing) candlestick pattern, where the current candle's body completely covers the previous candle's body, signaling a potential market reversal. Entry conditions are: a bullish candle engulfing a bearish candle triggers a buy, while a bearish candle engulfing a bullish candle triggers a sell, with trades executed after the pattern is confirmed at candle close. Exit conditions rely on user-configured stop loss and take profit parameters; if these are not set, there may be no explicit exit mechanism. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size without dynamic adjustment or position scaling. Money management lacks risk-based or account-equity-based dynamic calculation, creating uncontrolled per-trade risk exposure. The overall logic is straightforward but lacks filtering conditions and multi-confirmation, making it prone to false signals and frequent losses in ranging markets.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和验证
  • 基于经典K线形态，有广泛的技术分析理论支撑
  • 无马丁格尔或网格机制，不会因连续亏损而爆仓
  • 代码结构简洁，便于二次开发和优化

劣势

  • 单一K线形态缺乏过滤机制，震荡行情中假信号多
  • 默认止损止盈为0，未设置时风险极大
  • 固定手数不随账户资金动态调整，资金管理粗糙
  • 没有新K线检查机制，可能同一根K线上重复开仓
  • 缺乏趋势过滤、成交量确认等辅助条件

改进建议

  • 增加趋势方向过滤（如均线方向），只在顺势方向交易吸收形态
  • 加入基于ATR的动态止损止盈，替代固定点数或零值默认
  • 实现风险百分比仓位计算，根据账户余额动态调整手数
  • 添加新K线检查和持仓检查，防止重复开仓

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and verify
  • Based on a classic candlestick pattern with broad technical analysis support
  • No martingale or grid mechanism, avoiding account blow-up from consecutive losses
  • Clean code structure, convenient for further development and optimization

Weaknesses

  • Single candlestick pattern lacks filtering, generating many false signals in ranging markets
  • Default stop loss and take profit are 0, creating extreme risk when not configured
  • Fixed lot size without dynamic adjustment based on account equity is crude money management
  • No new bar check mechanism, potentially opening duplicate positions on the same bar
  • Lacks auxiliary conditions such as trend filtering or volume confirmation

Improvements

  • Add trend direction filtering (e.g., moving average direction) to trade absorption patterns only in the trend direction
  • Implement ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit instead of fixed values or zero defaults
  • Implement risk-percentage position sizing that dynamically adjusts lot size based on account equity
  • Add new bar check and position check to prevent duplicate entries
