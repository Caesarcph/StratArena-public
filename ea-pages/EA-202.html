摘要

该EA基于双EMA交叉并过滤RSI超买超卖水平进行交易，同时采用马丁格尔仓位管理系统在亏损后加倍手数。当快速EMA上穿慢速EMA且RSI低于超卖阈值时买入，反向交叉且RSI高于超买阈值时卖出。每笔交易设有固定止损止盈，马丁格尔手数通过全局变量在重启后保持状态。

策略分析

策略概念基于双均线交叉与RSI过滤的组合：快速EMA与慢速EMA的金叉死叉作为主信号，RSI超买超卖作为过滤条件，避免在极端区域追单。入场条件为：买入信号需快速EMA上穿慢速EMA且RSI低于超卖线（默认30），卖出信号需快速EMA下穿慢速EMA且RSI高于超买线（默认70）。出场条件依赖固定的止损（500点）和止盈（1500点），止盈是止损的3倍，追求高盈亏比。仓位管理采用马丁格尔系统，通过Martingail类管理手数，亏损后加倍手数（Ud参数控制加倍次数），初始手数由DML参数决定（1000为单位）。资金管理方面，使用全局变量保存马丁格尔状态，确保EA重启后能恢复上次的加仓进度，但没有最大回撤保护或账户余额比例仓位控制。

Summary

This EA trades based on a dual EMA crossover filtered by RSI overbought/oversold levels, and employs a Martingale position sizing system that doubles lot size after losses. It opens buy orders when the fast EMA crosses above the slow EMA with RSI below the oversold threshold, and sell orders on the reverse crossover with RSI above the overbought threshold. Positions include fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels, and the Martingale lot multiplier resets via global variables across restarts.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept combines a dual EMA crossover with RSI filtering: the fast and slow EMA golden/death cross serves as the primary signal, while RSI overbought/oversold levels act as filters to prevent chasing entries in extreme zones. Entry conditions require the fast EMA crossing above the slow EMA with RSI below the oversold level (default 30) for buys, and the reverse crossover with RSI above the overbought level (default 70) for sells. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop-loss (500 points) and take-profit (1500 points), achieving a 3:1 reward-to-risk ratio. Position sizing uses a Martingale system via the Martingail class, doubling lot size after losses with the Ud parameter controlling the number of doubling steps, and DML (default 1000) defining the base lot unit. Money management persists the Martingale state through global variables so the EA can resume its progression after restarts, but there is no maximum drawdown protection or account equity-proportional sizing.

优势

  • 双EMA交叉配合RSI过滤，避免在趋势末期追单，入场逻辑有一定合理性
  • 止盈是止损的3倍（1500:500），追求高盈亏比，符合趋势跟踪理念
  • 使用全局变量保存马丁格尔状态，EA重启后可恢复加仓进度，增强了鲁棒性
  • OnTester函数提供自定义测试指标（盈亏比/最大连亏），便于策略优化评估

劣势

  • 马丁格尔仓位管理极其危险，连续亏损后手数指数级增长，可能导致账户爆仓
  • 没有新K线检查，仅用tick_volume>1过滤，在非严格意义上会重复触发信号
  • 没有交易时间过滤、点差过滤和资金不足检查，实盘环境下容易出问题
  • 止损止盈使用固定点数而非ATR自适应，在不同波动率环境下效果差异大
  • 只允许同时持有一个仓位（PositionSelect），限制了策略的灵活性

改进建议

  • 移除马丁格尔仓位管理，改为基于账户风险比例的固定分数仓位管理，每笔交易风险不超过账户2%
  • 添加新K线检测（比较当前bar时间与上次交易时间），避免同一根K线重复开仓
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免在高点差或低流动性时段交易
  • 将止损止盈改为基于ATR的动态计算，适应不同市场波动率

Strengths

  • Dual EMA crossover with RSI filter avoids entering at trend exhaustion, providing reasonable entry logic
  • 3:1 reward-to-risk ratio (1500:500) aligns with trend-following principles for high reward trades
  • Global variables persist Martingale state across EA restarts, improving robustness of position sizing
  • Custom OnTester metric (profit-loss ratio divided by max consecutive loss) enables better strategy optimization

Weaknesses

  • Martingale position sizing is extremely dangerous — consecutive losses cause exponential lot growth that can blow the account
  • No new bar check; only tick_volume>1 filtering, which is unreliable for preventing repeated signal triggers
  • No spread filter, trading session filter, or insufficient margin check, creating risks in live trading
  • Fixed pip stop-loss and take-profit instead of ATR-adaptive levels perform inconsistently across volatility regimes
  • Only one position allowed at a time (PositionSelect), limiting strategy flexibility

Improvements

  • Remove Martingale sizing and replace with fixed-fractional risk management, risking no more than 2% of account per trade
  • Add new bar detection (compare current bar time vs last trade time) to prevent multiple entries on the same bar
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid trading during high-spread or low-liquidity periods
  • Change stop-loss and take-profit to ATR-based dynamic calculation for better adaptation to varying volatility
