EA-12581 · Trend Following

Exp_simple_trading_system

This EA implements a simple trend-following trading system based on a custom indicator called 'simple_trading_system' that generates up/down arrow signals. It opens buy positions on up signals and sell positions on down signals, with configurable stop loss and take profit levels, and supports multiple money management modes including lot-based and margin-based sizing.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on trend-following signals generated by the custom 'simple_trading_system' indicator, which internally uses EMA as its core calculation foundation. Entry conditions are: a buy position is opened when indicator buffer 1 (UpValue) produces a non-zero, non-EMPTY_VALUE on the specified signal bar, and a sell position is opened when buffer 0 (DnValue) produces such a value. Exit conditions include hard stop loss and take profit levels, plus a reverse-signal closing logic — when both opening and closing permissions are enabled and no close signal exists, the EA backtracks through historical bars to find the most recent opposite signal to determine whether to close positions. Position sizing supports five modes: free margin percentage, balance percentage, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled via the MM parameter for ratio or lot size and the MMMode parameter for selecting the specific calculation mode. The overall strategy logic is clear but relatively simple, lacking consideration for market volatility and trading costs such as spread.

Strengths

  • Clear and concise strategy logic that is easy to understand and maintain
  • Multiple money management modes provided for flexibility
  • Independent control of open and close permissions for each direction enables versatile configuration
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate entries within the same bar
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit parameters provide basic risk control

Weaknesses

  • Depends on an external custom indicator whose logic is invisible and cannot be independently verified
  • No spread filter, potentially resulting in unfavorable fills during high-spread conditions
  • Stop loss of 1000 points is excessively large, potentially causing high single-trade losses
  • MAPeriod=2 is extremely short, prone to generating frequent false signals
  • The backtrack loop for finding close signals may be inefficient, iterating through all bars in extreme cases

Possible improvements

  • Add a spread filter mechanism to pause trading when spread exceeds a threshold
  • Optimize the backtrack close logic by setting a maximum bar limit to prevent infinite loops
  • Provide more reasonable default MA period values and add signal confirmation to reduce false signals

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • 1000-point stop loss is excessively large, single-trade loss may account for too high a percentage of total capital
  • No spread filter, significant slippage risk during high-volatility periods
  • Custom indicator logic is unauditable, presenting black-box risk
  • MAPeriod=2 generates frequent signals that may lead to overtrading
