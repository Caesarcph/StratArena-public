摘要

该EA基于自定义指标simple_trading_system实现了一个简单的趋势跟踪交易系统，该指标通过可配置的移动平均线（位移、周期、方法、应用价格）产生上/下信号。在上升信号时开多仓，在下降信号时开空仓，支持在反向信号时平仓，并提供可配置的资金管理模式。

策略分析

策略概念基于移动平均线的方向性信号，通过自定义指标simple_trading_system判断趋势方向。入场条件为：当指标上信号线（buffer 1）在指定信号K线上出现非零且非EMPTY_VALUE的值时开多仓；当指标下信号线（buffer 0）出现有效值时开空仓。出场条件包括：止损和止盈（分别默认1000和2000点），以及反向信号平仓——当同时启用开仓和平仓选项时，EA会回溯历史K线寻找最近的反向信号来决定是否平仓。仓位管理支持五种模式：基于可用保证金、基于余额、基于可用保证金减亏损、基于余额减亏损、以及固定手数模式。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1，结合MMMode选择计算方式。EA采用新K线检测机制，仅在新K线形成时或需要重新计算时才更新信号，避免频繁交易。

Summary

This EA implements a simple trend-following trading system based on a custom indicator called simple_trading_system, which generates up/down signals using a moving average with configurable shift, period, method, and applied price. It opens buy positions on up signals and sell positions on down signals, with optional position closing on opposite signals and configurable money management modes.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on directional signals from a moving average, using the custom indicator simple_trading_system to determine trend direction. Entry conditions: a buy position is opened when the indicator's up signal line (buffer 1) shows a non-zero, non-EMPTY_VALUE value on the specified signal bar; a sell position is opened when the down signal line (buffer 0) shows a valid value. Exit conditions include Stop Loss (default 1000 points) and Take Profit (default 2000 points), as well as opposite signal closing — when both open and close options are enabled, the EA looks back through historical bars to find the most recent opposite signal to determine whether to close a position. Position sizing supports five modes: based on free margin, balance, free margin minus loss, balance minus loss, and fixed lot mode. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter (default 0.1 risk ratio) combined with MMMode for lot calculation. The EA employs a new bar detection mechanism, updating signals only when a new bar forms or a recount is needed, preventing excessive trading frequency.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简洁明了，基于移动平均线信号，易于理解和维护
  • 提供灵活的资金管理模式，支持五种不同的手数计算方式
  • 具有独立的买入/卖出的开仓和平仓开关，可灵活组合交易方向
  • 采用新K线检测机制，避免同一K线内重复开仓
  • 止损止盈设置齐全，默认1:2的风险回报比

劣势

  • 依赖外部自定义指标simple_trading_system和TradeAlgorithms.mqh库，缺少这些文件EA无法运行
  • 默认MAPeriod为2，周期过短容易产生频繁的虚假信号
  • 没有点差过滤，在小周期或高波动市场可能因点差过大而亏损
  • 反向信号平仓逻辑回溯所有历史K线，在K线数量多时可能影响性能
  • 缺乏对现有持仓的检查，可能在已有同向持仓时重复开仓

改进建议

  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓，并限制同时持仓数量
  • 增加点差过滤条件，在点差过大时暂停交易以保护资金
  • 优化反向信号平仓的历史回溯逻辑，设置最大回溯K线数以提升性能

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic based on moving average signals, easy to understand and maintain
  • Flexible money management with five different lot calculation modes
  • Independent buy/sell open and close switches allow flexible trading direction combinations
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate entries within the same bar
  • Complete Stop Loss and Take Profit settings with a default 1:2 risk-reward ratio

Weaknesses

  • Depends on external custom indicator simple_trading_system and TradeAlgorithms.mqh library; EA cannot run without them
  • Default MAPeriod of 2 is too short and prone to generating frequent false signals
  • No spread filter, which may cause losses on small timeframes or high-volatility markets with wide spreads
  • Opposite signal closing logic loops through all historical bars, which may degrade performance with many bars
  • Lacks existing position checking, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction

Improvements

  • Add position checking logic to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction and limit the number of simultaneous positions
  • Add a spread filter to pause trading when the spread is too wide, protecting capital
  • Optimize the opposite signal closing lookback logic by setting a maximum number of bars to review for better performance
