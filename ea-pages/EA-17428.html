摘要

MARE5.1是一个均值绝对回归EA，利用统计偏离度指标交易价格向均值回归的机会。它识别过度延伸的价格走势，并在预期价格回归均值时入场，配有可配置的偏离阈值用于入场和出场。该EA采用基于偏离幅度的系统化仓位管理和风险管理。

策略分析

MARE5.1的核心策略概念是基于均值回归原理，认为价格偏离均值后必然会回归。入场条件是当价格偏离均值超过设定阈值时开仓，做多条件为价格低于均值减去N倍标准差，做空条件为价格高于均值加上N倍标准差。出场条件包括价格回归到均值附近时平仓获利，以及止损设在偏离进一步扩大的位置。仓位管理根据偏离幅度动态调整，偏离越大仓位越重，但受最大仓位限制。资金管理采用固定比例风险模型，每笔交易的风险占总资金的百分比是可配置的。该策略还包含时间过滤和波动率过滤机制，避免在低流动性或极端波动时交易。

Summary

MARE5.1 is a Mean Absolute Reversion Expert Advisor that trades price reversals to the mean using statistical deviation measures. It identifies overextended price moves and enters positions anticipating a return to the mean value, with configurable deviation thresholds for entry and exit. The EA employs systematic position sizing and risk management based on the magnitude of deviation from the mean.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy concept of MARE5.1 is based on the mean reversion principle, assuming that prices deviating from the mean will inevitably revert. Entry conditions trigger when price deviates beyond a configurable threshold from the mean — long positions when price falls below mean minus N standard deviations, short positions when price rises above mean plus N standard deviations. Exit conditions include taking profit when price reverts near the mean and stop-loss placement at levels where deviation further increases. Position sizing dynamically adjusts based on deviation magnitude — larger deviations allow heavier positions, capped by maximum position limits. Money management employs a fixed fractional risk model where the percentage of total capital risked per trade is configurable. The strategy also incorporates time filters and volatility filters to avoid trading during low liquidity or extreme volatility periods.

优势

  • 均值回归策略具有统计学基础，在震荡市场中表现良好
  • 动态仓位管理根据偏离幅度调整，逻辑合理
  • 包含时间过滤和波动率过滤，避免不利交易环境
  • 参数可配置性强，适应不同市场条件
  • 系统化执行消除情绪干扰

劣势

  • 趋势市场中均值回归策略会持续亏损，抗趋势能力弱
  • 缺乏趋势过滤机制，可能在强趋势中反复逆势入场
  • 止损设置相对简单，未考虑ATR动态止损
  • 未包含加仓和减仓的渐进式逻辑
  • 回撤可能在单边行情中非常显著

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤器（如ADX或更长周期均线斜率），在强趋势时暂停交易或反转策略
  • 使用ATR动态调整止损和止盈，适应不同波动率环境

Strengths

  • Mean reversion strategy has solid statistical foundation and performs well in ranging markets
  • Dynamic position sizing based on deviation magnitude is logically sound
  • Incorporates time and volatility filters to avoid unfavorable trading conditions
  • High configurability of parameters adapts to different market conditions
  • Systematic execution eliminates emotional interference

Weaknesses

  • Mean reversion strategy suffers persistent losses in trending markets with poor trend resistance
  • Lacks trend filter mechanism, potentially entering repeatedly against strong trends
  • Stop loss is relatively simple without ATR-based dynamic stop consideration
  • No gradual position scaling logic for adding or reducing positions
  • Drawdowns can be significant during extended directional moves

Improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., ADX or longer-period MA slope) to pause trading or reverse strategy during strong trends
  • Use ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit to adapt to varying volatility environments
