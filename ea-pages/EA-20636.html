摘要

这是一个保本工具EA，当价格达到用户设定的触发点时，自动将止损移动到保本价（或指定的利润水平）。它本身不开仓交易，而是通过调整现有仓位的止损来保护利润。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念是保护已有浮盈，当价格朝有利方向移动达到设定点数后，将止损移至入场价或略高于入场价的位置。入场条件不适用于此EA，因为它不自行开仓。出场条件同样不适用，EA仅在已有持仓上操作止损调整。仓位管理方面，EA不涉及仓位大小计算，仅修改止损价格。资金管理方面，通过Inprofit参数可以设定触发后保本价上移的点数，实现锁定部分利润的功能。整体逻辑简单直接，属于持仓管理工具而非交易策略。

Summary

This EA is a breakeven utility that automatically moves stop loss to breakeven (or a specified profit level) when price reaches a user-defined trigger point. It does not open trades itself but manages existing positions by adjusting their stop losses to protect profits.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept of this EA is to protect existing floating profits by moving the stop loss to the entry price or slightly above when price moves favorably by a specified number of points. Entry conditions do not apply since this EA does not open trades on its own. Exit conditions are also not applicable as the EA only modifies stop losses on existing positions. Position sizing is not handled by this EA as it only adjusts stop loss prices on already-opened trades. Money management is achieved through the Inprofit parameter which allows setting the number of points above breakeven to lock in a small profit when the stop is moved. The overall logic is straightforward and simple, classifying it as a position management utility rather than a trading strategy.

优势

  • 逻辑简单清晰，功能单一明确，易于理解和使用
  • 可灵活设定触发点数和额外利润锁定，适应不同交易风格
  • 不自行开仓，仅管理已有持仓，风险可控
  • 帮助交易者自动化情绪管理，避免因贪婪或恐惧而未能及时移动止损
  • 可与任何手动或自动交易策略配合使用，兼容性好

劣势

  • 功能过于单一，仅提供保本功能，缺少移动止损（追踪止损）等更高级的持仓管理
  • 没有对点差和手续费的补偿计算，保本价可能未完全覆盖交易成本
  • 缺少对多品种多仓位的批量管理能力，可能需要多实例运行
  • 没有视觉化界面显示当前触发状态和剩余点数
  • 不支持部分仓位保本，只能对整个仓位操作

改进建议

  • 增加追踪止损功能，在保本后继续随价格移动止损以锁定更多利润
  • 加入点差和手续费的自动补偿计算，使保本价真正覆盖全部交易成本
  • 增加多仓位批量管理模式，支持按品种或魔术号筛选并同时管理多个持仓

Strengths

  • Simple and clear logic with a single well-defined function, easy to understand and use
  • Flexible trigger point and additional profit lock settings to adapt to different trading styles
  • Does not open trades on its own, only manages existing positions, keeping risk controllable
  • Helps traders automate emotional discipline by removing the need to manually move stops
  • Compatible with any manual or automated trading strategy, good interoperability

Weaknesses

  • Functionality is too limited, only provides breakeven without more advanced features like trailing stop
  • Does not account for spread and commission costs, so the breakeven price may not fully cover trading expenses
  • Lacks batch management for multiple symbols and positions, may require multiple instances
  • No visual interface to show current trigger status and remaining points to activation
  • Does not support partial position breakeven, can only operate on the entire position

Improvements

  • Add trailing stop functionality to continue moving the stop loss after breakeven to lock in more profit
  • Include automatic spread and commission compensation in breakeven price calculation for true cost coverage
  • Add batch position management mode supporting filtering by symbol or magic number for simultaneous multi-position management

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:SAFE

风险点

  • 保本触发点数设置过大可能导致浮盈回撤至亏损才被保护
  • Inprofit参数设置不当可能在保本时因点差和手续费仍产生实际亏损

Risk Assessment

Overall:SAFE

Risk Points

  • Setting the breakeven trigger too large may allow profits to retrace to loss before protection activates
  • Improper Inprofit setting may still result in actual losses at breakeven due to unaccounted spread and commission costs
