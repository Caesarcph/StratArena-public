摘要

该EA结合威廉分形和威廉百分比范围(WPR)指标来识别突破入场信号。当看涨分形突破并与WPR确认一致时开多仓，在看跌分形突破并获得WPR验证时开空仓。策略使用固定手数以及可配置的止损和止盈水平。

策略分析

该EA的策略核心是利用威廉分形指标识别价格突破点，并结合WPR指标进行超买超卖确认，从而过滤假突破信号。入场条件为：多头入场需价格突破上方分形且WPR未处于超买区域，空头入场需价格突破下方分形且WPR未处于超卖区域。出场条件依赖固定的止损和止盈设置，缺乏追踪止损或动态退出机制。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，没有根据账户余额或波动性进行动态调整。资金管理方面较为简单，仅依赖止损来控制单笔风险，缺少整体风险控制机制如最大持仓限制或回撤保护。

Summary

This EA combines Williams' Fractals and Williams' Percent Range (WPR) indicator to identify breakout entry signals. It opens buy positions when a bullish fractal breakout occurs alongside WPR confirmation, and sell positions on bearish fractal breakouts with WPR validation. The strategy uses fixed lot sizing with configurable stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA uses Williams' Fractals to identify price breakout points and combines the WPR indicator for overbought/oversold confirmation to filter false breakouts. Entry conditions require: long entries when price breaks above an upper fractal and WPR is not in overbought territory, short entries when price breaks below a lower fractal and WPR is not in oversold territory. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit settings, lacking trailing stops or dynamic exit mechanisms. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic adjustment based on account balance or market volatility. Money management is simplistic, relying solely on stop loss for per-trade risk control without overall portfolio risk controls such as maximum position limits or drawdown protection.

优势

  • 双指标组合过滤假突破信号，提高入场质量
  • 分形突破策略逻辑清晰，易于理解和参数优化
  • 使用魔术编号区分订单，避免与其他EA冲突

劣势

  • 固定手数不考虑账户规模和波动性，资金管理粗糙
  • 缺乏追踪止损和动态出场机制，可能错失利润或承受不必要回撤
  • 没有持仓检查和新K线检查，可能在同一信号上重复开仓

改进建议

  • 增加基于账户余额或ATR的动态仓位管理
  • 添加新K线检查防止同一信号重复入场，以及追踪止损保护利润
  • 增加最大持仓数和最大回撤限制等整体风控措施

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator combination filters false breakout signals, improving entry quality
  • Fractal breakout strategy logic is clear and easy to understand and optimize
  • Uses magic number to distinguish orders, avoiding conflicts with other EAs

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing ignores account size and volatility, resulting in crude money management
  • Lacks trailing stop and dynamic exit mechanisms, potentially missing profits or enduring unnecessary drawdowns
  • No position check or new bar check may cause repeated entries on the same signal

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account balance or ATR
  • Add new bar check to prevent repeated entries on the same signal, and trailing stop to protect profits
  • Add overall risk controls such as maximum position count and maximum drawdown limits
