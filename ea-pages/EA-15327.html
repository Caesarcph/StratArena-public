摘要

该EA基于BinaryWave_StDev自定义指标进行交易，该指标将七个加权技术指标（MA、MACD、OsMA、CCI、动量、RSI、ADX）组合成复合二值波信号并进行标准差过滤。它支持三种信号模式（点信号、直接信号、无信号）用于入场和出场，并使用可配置的资金管理及止损止盈。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是将七个经典技术指标（MA、MACD、OsMA、CCI、MOM、RSI、ADX）的信号通过可调权重加权合成一个复合二值波指标，再通过JJMA等平滑算法进行平滑处理，最后用标准差进行过滤以确认信号强度。入场条件分为三种模式：POINT模式通过指标缓冲区的信号点（非EMPTY_VALUE）触发；DIRECT模式通过二值波线的拐点（局部极值）触发；WITHOUT模式则不产生该方向的信号。出场条件同样支持三种模式，但DIRECT模式的出场逻辑与入场不同——出场仅需二值波线方向变化即可，无需拐点确认。仓位管理支持五种模式（自由保证金、余额、亏损自由保证金、亏损余额、固定手数），通过MM参数和MMMode参数控制。止损和止盈分别固定为1000和2000点，偏差控制在10点以内。策略使用IsNewBar机制确保每个K线只计算一次信号，避免重复开仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on the BinaryWave_StDev custom indicator, which combines seven weighted technical indicators (MA, MACD, OsMA, CCI, Momentum, RSI, ADX) into a composite binary wave signal with standard deviation filtering. It supports three signal modes (point, direct, without) for both entry and exit, and uses configurable money management with stop-loss and take-profit.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy combines seven classic technical indicators (MA, MACD, OsMA, CCI, MOM, RSI, ADX) through adjustable weights into a composite binary wave indicator, which is then smoothed using algorithms like JJMA and filtered by standard deviation to confirm signal strength. Entry conditions support three modes: POINT mode triggers on signal dots from indicator buffers (non-EMPTY_VALUE); DIRECT mode triggers on turning points (local extrema) of the binary wave line; WITHOUT mode disables signals for that direction. Exit conditions also support three modes, but DIRECT mode exit logic differs from entry—exits only require directional change of the binary wave line without needing turning point confirmation. Position sizing supports five modes (free margin, balance, loss-free margin, loss balance, fixed lot) controlled by MM and MMMode parameters. Stop-loss and take-profit are fixed at 1000 and 2000 points respectively, with slippage deviation controlled at 10 points. The strategy uses the IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are calculated only once per bar, preventing duplicate position openings.

优势

  • 多指标复合加权系统提高了信号的可靠性，单一指标产生虚假信号的概率降低
  • 灵活的三种信号模式（点信号、直接信号、无）允许用户分别精细控制入场和出场逻辑
  • 标准差过滤机制能有效过滤低波动市场中的噪声信号，减少不必要的交易
  • 新K线检测机制避免同一根K线内重复计算和重复开仓
  • 可调权重参数允许用户根据不同市场环境优化各指标的贡献度

劣势

  • 七个指标等权重默认配置可能导致信号冲突，缺乏指标间信号一致性的智能融合机制
  • 固定止损1000点和止盈2000点缺乏根据市场波动率（ATR）动态调整的能力
  • 没有点差过滤，在高点差环境下POINT模式的信号点可能被吞没
  • 依赖自定义指标BinaryWave_StDev，如果指标文件缺失则EA完全无法运行
  • 出场逻辑中DIRECT模式仅检查方向变化（2根K线），可能在震荡市中频繁触发平仓

改进建议

  • 引入ATR动态止损止盈机制，替代固定点数设置，使策略能自适应不同波动率的市场环境
  • 添加点差过滤和时间过滤（如避开重要新闻发布时段和低流动性时段），减少不利条件下的交易
  • 增加指标权重优化功能或预设配置文件，针对不同市场（趋势/震荡）使用不同权重组合

Strengths

  • Multi-indicator composite weighted system improves signal reliability by reducing the probability of false signals from any single indicator
  • Flexible three signal modes (point, direct, without) allow users to finely control entry and exit logic independently
  • Standard deviation filtering effectively removes noise signals in low-volatility markets, reducing unnecessary trades
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate calculations and duplicate position openings within the same bar
  • Adjustable weight parameters allow users to optimize each indicator's contribution based on different market conditions

Weaknesses

  • Default equal weighting of seven indicators may cause signal conflicts, lacking intelligent fusion mechanism for indicator consensus
  • Fixed stop-loss of 1000 points and take-profit of 2000 points lacks dynamic adjustment based on market volatility (ATR)
  • No spread filter; in high-spread environments, POINT mode signal dots may be absorbed by spread
  • Dependency on custom indicator BinaryWave_StDev means the EA is completely non-functional if the indicator file is missing
  • DIRECT mode exit logic only checks directional change (2 bars), which may trigger frequent closures in choppy markets

Improvements

  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit mechanism instead of fixed point values to adapt to different volatility environments
  • Add spread filter and time filter (e.g., avoid major news releases and low-liquidity sessions) to reduce trading under unfavorable conditions
  • Add indicator weight optimization feature or preset profiles for different market types (trending/ranging) with different weight combinations

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 固定止损止盈无法适应市场波动率变化，在低波动时止损可能过大，在高波动时止盈可能不足
  • 没有持仓数量检查，理论上可能在同方向重复开仓
  • 依赖外部自定义指标文件，指标缺失时OnInit返回INIT_FAILED但没有回退机制
  • 没有点差过滤，在流动性差的市场中滑点可能超过Deviation_设定值

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed stop-loss and take-profit cannot adapt to changing market volatility; SL may be too large in low volatility and TP insufficient in high volatility
  • No position count check; theoretically could open duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Dependency on external custom indicator file; if missing, OnInit returns INIT_FAILED with no fallback mechanism
  • No spread filter; in low-liquidity markets, slippage may exceed the Deviation_ setting
