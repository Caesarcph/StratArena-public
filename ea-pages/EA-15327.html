Exp_BinaryWave_StDev

This EA trades based on the BinaryWave_StDev custom indicator, which combines seven weighted technical indicators (MA, MACD, OsMA, CCI, Momentum, RSI, ADX) into a composite binary wave signal with standard deviation filtering. It supports three signal modes (point signals, direct line direction, or disabled) for both entry and exit, with configurable money management and lot sizing.

Strategy analysis

The core concept of this strategy is to combine seven classic technical indicators with configurable weights into a composite BinaryWave value, then filter it through standard deviation channels to produce more reliable trading signals. Entry conditions offer two modes: POINT mode detects arrow signals in indicator buffers (non-EMPTY_VALUE values), while DIRECT mode detects inflection points on the BinaryWave line (V-bottom for buy, inverted-V-top for sell). Exit conditions also support two modes: POINT mode detects opposite arrow signals, while DIRECT mode detects BinaryWave line direction changes (close long when line declines, close short when line rises). Position sizing supports five modes: free margin percentage, balance percentage, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. Money management uses the MM parameter to control risk ratio, combined with fixed stop loss and take profit in points for risk control. The strategy applies a configurable smoothing method for secondary smoothing of the BinaryWave, adjusting standard deviation channel width via dK1 and dK2 coefficients to reduce false signals.

Strengths

  • Multi-indicator weighted combination improves signal robustness, remaining effective even when individual indicators fail
  • Standard deviation channel filtering effectively reduces false signals, with dK1/dK2 dual coefficients allowing flexible channel width adjustment
  • Independent configurable entry and exit signal modes provide extremely high strategy flexibility
  • Multiple position sizing modes accommodate traders with different risk preferences
  • NewBar detection prevents redundant calculations and duplicate entries within the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Equal weighting for all seven indicators may be suboptimal, lacking an adaptive weight adjustment mechanism
  • Fixed stop loss and take profit in points rather than ATR-based dynamic levels leads to inconsistent performance across varying volatility environments
  • No spread filter mechanism, potentially causing unnecessary losses on high-spread instruments
  • Lacks position count checking, which could lead to over-opening positions in extreme conditions
  • Depends on the external custom indicator BinaryWave_StDev; EA will fail to run if the indicator file is missing

Possible improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit mechanism to replace fixed point settings
  • Add a spread filter to pause trading when spread is excessively wide, protecting capital
  • Implement adaptive weight optimization for indicators or at least provide weight presets

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Fixed stop loss/take profit in points creates unbalanced risk across volatility regimes — too wide in low volatility, too narrow in high volatility
  • No spread filter may cause slippage losses during low liquidity or news events
  • Indicator weights require manual optimization; incorrect weight configuration can render the strategy ineffective
