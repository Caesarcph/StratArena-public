摘要

该EA实现了一个简单的交替买卖策略，每次平仓后反转方向。当没有持仓时立即开仓，达到固定止盈或止损点数后平仓并翻转下一笔交易方向。不使用任何技术指标，入场完全基于上一笔交易的结果。

策略分析

该策略的核心概念是均值回归中的反转交易：假设价格在触及止盈或止损后会反向运动，因此每次平仓后立即反向开仓。入场条件极其简单——当没有持仓时，根据布尔变量bs决定买入或卖出，初始默认为买入。出场条件为固定点数的止盈（TP=46点）和止损（SL=31点），通过手动计算当前价格与开仓价的点差来判断。仓位管理仅使用固定手数（Lot=0.1），没有根据账户余额或波动率进行动态调整。资金管理完全缺失，没有最大回撤限制、没有交易次数限制、没有账户权益保护机制。策略不依赖任何技术指标或市场结构分析，属于纯粹的价格反转赌博式交易。

Summary

This EA implements a simple alternating buy/sell strategy that reverses direction after each position closes. It opens a position immediately when no position exists, then closes on reaching a fixed TP or SL in points, flipping the direction for the next trade. No technical indicators are used—entry is purely based on the previous trade's outcome.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of this strategy is a reversal-based mean reversion approach: it assumes that after hitting a take-profit or stop-loss, the price will move in the opposite direction, so it immediately opens a reverse position after each close. Entry conditions are extremely simplistic—when no position exists, it opens a buy or sell based on a boolean flag, defaulting to buy initially. Exit conditions use fixed point-based take-profit (TP=46 points) and stop-loss (SL=31 points), calculated manually by comparing the current price to the open price divided by _Point. Position sizing uses only a fixed lot size (Lot=0.1) with no dynamic adjustment based on account balance, equity, or market volatility. Money management is entirely absent—there is no maximum drawdown limit, no trade frequency cap, and no account equity protection. The strategy relies on zero technical indicators or market structure analysis, making it essentially a pure price-reversal gambling system.

优势

  • 逻辑极其简单，易于理解和维护
  • 每次只持有一个仓位，不存在过度持仓风险
  • 止盈大于止损（46:31），单笔盈亏比大于1，符合正向期望的基础要求

劣势

  • 没有任何技术指标或市场分析作为入场依据，入场本质上是随机方向
  • 止损止盈使用固定点数而非ATR等动态指标，无法适应不同市场波动环境
  • 没有点差过滤、新K线检查或交易时段过滤，可能在不利条件下频繁开仓
  • 反转逻辑在趋势行情中会连续亏损，缺乏趋势识别机制
  • 没有资金管理和风险控制机制，长期运行可能导致账户爆仓

改进建议

  • 增加技术指标确认入场方向（如均线趋势、RSI超买超卖），避免纯随机方向交易
  • 使用ATR动态调整止盈止损，并增加点差过滤和新K线检查机制

Strengths

  • Extremely simple logic, easy to understand and maintain
  • Only holds one position at a time, avoiding overexposure risk
  • Take-profit exceeds stop-loss (46:31), giving a reward-to-risk ratio greater than 1 per trade

Weaknesses

  • No technical indicators or market analysis for entry decisions—direction is essentially random
  • Fixed point-based TP/SL instead of dynamic levels like ATR, failing to adapt to varying volatility
  • No spread filter, new bar check, or session filter, potentially opening trades under adverse conditions
  • Reversal logic will produce consecutive losses in trending markets with no trend identification mechanism
  • No money management or risk control mechanisms, prolonged operation may lead to account blow-up

Improvements

  • Add technical indicator confirmation for entry direction (e.g., MA trend, RSI overbought/oversold) to avoid purely random direction trading
  • Use ATR for dynamic TP/SL adjustment, and add spread filter and new bar check mechanisms
