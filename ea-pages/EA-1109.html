摘要

该EA使用自定义BuySell指标（基于MA和ATR）生成趋势跟踪的买卖信号。当上升趋势信号出现且之前为下降趋势时开多仓，当下降趋势信号出现且之前为上升趋势时开空仓。在反向信号出现时平仓，并设有可配置的止损和止盈水平。

策略分析

该EA采用基于自定义BuySell指标的趋势跟踪策略，BuySell指标内部结合了移动平均线（MA）和平均真实波幅（ATR）来判断趋势方向。入场条件为：当UpTrend信号在当前Bar上触发且前一Bar为DnTrend信号时开多仓；当DnTrend信号在当前Bar上触发且前一Bar为UpTrend信号时开空仓，即要求趋势发生反转确认。出场条件为：收到反向信号时平仓（多仓在卖出信号时平仓，空仓在买入信号时平仓），同时设置了固定点数的止损和止盈。仓位管理方面，MM参数支持两种模式：负值为固定手数，正值为按存款比例计算手数。资金管理通过MM参数统一控制，止损止盈为固定点数方式，未考虑动态调整ATR相关的止损。策略仅在新的K线形成时计算信号，避免逐Tick重复计算，提高了效率。

Summary

This EA uses a custom BuySell indicator (based on MA and ATR) to generate trend-following buy and sell signals. It opens long positions when the uptrend signal appears after a prior downtrend, and opens short positions when the downtrend signal appears after a prior uptrend. Positions are closed on opposite signals, with configurable stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a trend-following strategy based on the custom BuySell indicator, which internally combines a Moving Average (MA) and Average True Range (ATR) to determine trend direction. Entry conditions are: a long position is opened when the UpTrend signal fires on the current bar and the previous bar was a DnTrend signal; a short position is opened when the DnTrend signal fires on the current bar and the previous bar was an UpTrend signal, requiring a confirmed trend reversal. Exit conditions are: positions are closed upon receiving an opposite signal (longs closed on sell signals, shorts closed on buy signals), with fixed stop loss and take profit levels configured in points. Position sizing is handled through the MM parameter which supports two modes: negative values specify a fixed lot size, while positive values calculate lot size as a proportion of the deposit. Money management is unified through the MM parameter, with stop loss and take profit set as fixed point values without dynamic ATR-based adjustment. The strategy only calculates signals on new bar formation, avoiding redundant per-tick calculations and improving efficiency.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于趋势反转确认入场，避免追高杀低
  • 支持双向交易，可独立控制买卖开关和平仓权限，灵活性高
  • 仅在新K线形成时计算信号，避免逐Tick重复运算，执行效率高
  • 仓位管理灵活，支持固定手数和按比例分配两种模式

劣势

  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未根据ATR动态调整，可能在不同波动环境下不适用
  • 没有点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生虚假信号导致亏损
  • 依赖自定义BuySell指标，指标代码不透明，难以验证信号逻辑的正确性
  • 缺少持仓检查和重复开仓保护，可能在同方向重复开仓

改进建议

  • 将固定止损止盈改为基于ATR的动态止损止盈，以适应不同市场波动环境
  • 添加点差过滤和持仓检查，避免高点差环境下入场和重复开仓问题
  • 增加追踪止损功能，在趋势延续时锁定更多利润

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on confirmed trend reversal entries, avoiding chasing highs and selling lows
  • Supports bi-directional trading with independent buy/sell and close permission toggles for high flexibility
  • Signals calculated only on new bar formation, avoiding redundant per-tick computation for high execution efficiency
  • Flexible position sizing supporting both fixed lot and deposit proportion modes

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss and take profit are fixed point values without dynamic ATR adjustment, potentially unsuitable across varying volatility environments
  • No spread filter, which may cause losses from false signals in high-spread conditions
  • Depends on a custom BuySell indicator whose code is not visible, making signal logic verification difficult
  • Lacks position existence checks and duplicate opening protection, potentially opening repeated positions in the same direction

Improvements

  • Replace fixed stop loss/take profit with ATR-based dynamic levels to adapt to different market volatility environments
  • Add spread filter and position existence checks to prevent entries in high-spread conditions and duplicate position openings
  • Add a trailing stop feature to lock in more profits during trend continuation

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 止损止盈为固定值，低波动市场止损可能过大而高波动市场止盈可能不足
  • 缺少持仓检查，可能导致同方向重复开仓增加风险敞口
  • 没有点差过滤，在流动性差的市场中可能因滑点和点差造成额外损失

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed stop loss/take profit may result in disproportionately large stops in low volatility or insufficient targets in high volatility
  • Lack of position existence checks may cause duplicate openings in the same direction, increasing risk exposure
  • No spread filter may cause additional losses from slippage and spreads in low-liquidity markets
