摘要

该EA监控账户浮动盈亏，当账户亏损超过用户设定的最大亏损阈值时，自动平掉所有品种的多头和空头仓位。它使用全局变量作为一次性触发标志，防止在阈值被突破后重复触发平仓操作。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念是一个账户级别的亏损保护工具，而非独立的交易策略。它没有入场条件，不自行开仓，仅在账户浮动亏损超过设定的MaxLoss参数时触发全部平仓。出场条件为：当AccountInfoDouble(ACCOUNT_PROFIT)小于等于-MaxLoss时，设置全局变量标志为1，随后在OnTick中检测到该标志后，遍历所有持仓并逐个平仓。仓位管理方面，该EA不涉及仓位大小计算或加仓逻辑。资金管理方面，仅依赖MaxLoss单一参数作为最大容忍亏损额，但没有考虑账户余额比例的风险控制。该EA使用全局变量作为状态锁，确保亏损触发后只执行一次平仓操作，直到所有仓位被关闭且全局变量被删除后才重置。然而，平仓时没有检查平仓是否成功，也没有重试机制，滑点设置为固定的10点。

Summary

This EA monitors the account's floating profit/loss and closes all open positions (both long and short) across all symbols when the account loss exceeds a user-defined maximum loss threshold. It uses a global variable as a one-time trigger flag to prevent repeated closing attempts once the threshold is breached.

Strategy Analysis

The EA's strategy concept is an account-level loss protection utility rather than an independent trading strategy. It has no entry conditions and does not open positions on its own; it only triggers a full position closure when the account's floating loss exceeds the configured MaxLoss parameter. The exit condition is: when AccountInfoDouble(ACCOUNT_PROFIT) is less than or equal to -MaxLoss, a global variable flag is set to 1, and upon detecting this flag in OnTick, the EA iterates through all positions and closes them one by one. In terms of position sizing, the EA does not involve lot size calculation or position scaling logic. For money management, it relies solely on the single MaxLoss parameter as the maximum tolerable loss amount, without considering risk control based on account balance percentage. The EA uses a global variable as a state lock to ensure the closing operation is executed only once after the loss threshold is triggered, resetting only after all positions are closed and the global variable is deleted. However, there is no check for whether the close operation succeeds, no retry mechanism, and the slippage is hardcoded to 10 points.

优势

  • 简单直观的亏损保护机制，防止账户遭受过大损失
  • 使用全局变量作为状态锁，避免在亏损状态下重复触发平仓逻辑
  • 当所有仓位平仓后自动重置触发标志，可以重新开始监控

劣势

  • 没有平仓成功检查和重试机制，网络延迟或流动性不足可能导致平仓失败
  • MaxLoss为固定金额而非账户余额比例，不同资金规模需要手动调整
  • 滑点硬编码为10点，不同品种和交易环境可能需要不同的滑点设置
  • 没有考虑点差和手续费对实际亏损的影响
  • 在快速行情中OnTick可能来不及平仓，缺乏定时器补充机制

改进建议

  • 增加平仓结果检查和重试机制，确保所有仓位真正被关闭
  • 将MaxLoss改为支持百分比模式（基于账户余额/净值），提高适应性

Strengths

  • Simple and intuitive loss protection mechanism that prevents excessive account drawdown
  • Uses a global variable as a state lock to avoid repeatedly triggering closure logic while in a loss state
  • Automatically resets the trigger flag when all positions are closed, allowing monitoring to restart

Weaknesses

  • No close order success check or retry mechanism; network latency or insufficient liquidity could cause closure failures
  • MaxLoss is a fixed amount rather than a percentage of account balance, requiring manual adjustment for different account sizes
  • Slippage is hardcoded to 10 points, while different symbols and trading environments may require different slippage settings
  • Does not account for the impact of spreads and commissions on actual losses
  • In fast-moving markets OnTick may not execute quickly enough; lacks a timer-based backup mechanism

Improvements

  • Add close order result checking and retry logic to ensure all positions are actually closed
  • Change MaxLoss to support percentage mode (based on account balance/equity) for better adaptability across different account sizes

  • 平仓操作无成功验证，极端行情下可能无法完全平仓导致亏损超出预期
  • 固定金额止损不随账户规模变化，小账户可能触发过晚，大账户可能触发过早

  • Fixed amount stop-loss does not scale with account size; small accounts may trigger too late while large accounts may trigger too early
