摘要

该EA监控账户浮动盈亏，当总未实现亏损超过用户设定的最大亏损阈值时，强制平仓所有持仓（多空均平）。它使用全局变量持久化触发状态，确保在亏损触发后不会重新开仓，直到确认所有仓位已关闭。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略概念是最大亏损保护，即当账户浮动亏损达到设定阈值时一次性平掉所有仓位。它没有入场条件，不主动开仓，仅作为已有持仓的风险管理工具使用。出场条件为账户总浮动利润低于负的MaxLoss值时触发全部平仓。仓位管理方面，该EA不涉及开仓逻辑，仅执行平仓操作，遍历所有持仓并逐个关闭。资金管理依赖于用户设定的MaxLoss参数作为最大可承受亏损限额，但该参数为固定金额而非百分比，未考虑账户余额变化。此外，该EA使用全局变量记录触发状态，防止反复触发平仓逻辑，并在所有仓位关闭后清除该标记。

Summary

This EA monitors the account's floating profit/loss and closes all open positions (both long and short) when the total unrealized loss exceeds a user-defined maximum loss threshold. It uses a global variable to persist the triggered state so that positions are not reopened after the loss level is hit until all positions are confirmed closed.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of this EA is maximum loss protection, closing all positions at once when the account's floating loss exceeds a user-defined threshold. It has no entry conditions and does not open trades; it serves purely as a risk management utility for existing positions. The exit condition triggers when the total account floating profit falls below the negative of the MaxLoss parameter value, at which point all long and short positions are closed. Position sizing is not applicable since the EA only performs closing operations, iterating through all open positions and closing them one by one. Money management relies on the MaxLoss parameter as a fixed monetary loss limit rather than a percentage of account equity, which fails to adapt to changing account balances. The EA uses a global variable to persist the triggered state, preventing repeated close attempts, and clears this flag only when all positions have been confirmed closed.

优势

  • 简单直观的最大亏损保护机制，有效防止账户出现灾难性亏损
  • 使用全局变量持久化触发状态，避免重复触发平仓逻辑
  • 同时平仓多空两侧持仓，确保亏损触发时无遗漏仓位
  • 平仓后自动清除全局变量标记，允许后续重新监控

劣势

  • MaxLoss为固定金额而非账户余额百分比，无法随资金变化自动调整
  • 没有滑点和部分平仓失败的处理机制，平仓可能不完整
  • 在OnTick中每次都调用PositionsTotal和遍历持仓，效率较低
  • 依赖外部头文件TradeAlgorithms.mqh中的函数，核心平仓逻辑不透明
  • 没有日志记录功能，无法追溯平仓触发时间和原因

改进建议

  • 将MaxLoss改为账户余额或净值的百分比，动态适应资金变化
  • 添加平仓失败的错误处理和重试机制，并记录日志以便追踪
  • 增加隔夜/周末风险检查及跳空缺口防护机制

Strengths

  • Simple and intuitive maximum loss protection mechanism that effectively prevents catastrophic account drawdowns
  • Uses a global variable to persist the triggered state, avoiding repeated close logic execution
  • Closes both long and short positions simultaneously, ensuring no positions are left open when loss threshold is hit
  • Automatically clears the global variable flag after all positions are closed, allowing resumed monitoring

Weaknesses

  • MaxLoss is a fixed monetary amount rather than a percentage of account equity, unable to adapt to changing account balances
  • No handling for slippage or partial close failures; position closure may be incomplete
  • Inefficient design calling PositionsTotal and iterating positions on every tick
  • Depends on external include file TradeAlgorithms.mqh for closing functions, making core logic opaque
  • No logging functionality, making it impossible to trace when and why closures were triggered

Improvements

  • Change MaxLoss from fixed amount to a percentage of account balance/equity for dynamic adaptation to capital changes
  • Add error handling and retry mechanisms for failed close operations, along with logging for traceability
  • Add overnight/weekend risk checks and gap protection mechanisms

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 固定金额止损无法适应不同账户规模，小额账户可能被过早触发或大额账户保护不足
  • 平仓函数依赖外部未验证的头文件，无法确认其错误处理和重试逻辑是否完善
  • 在市场剧烈波动时滑点可能导致实际亏损远超MaxLoss设定值

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed-amount stop loss cannot adapt to different account sizes; small accounts may trigger prematurely while large accounts may be under-protected
  • Closing functions depend on an unverified external include file; error handling and retry logic cannot be confirmed as robust
  • During extreme market volatility, slippage may cause actual losses to far exceed the MaxLoss threshold
