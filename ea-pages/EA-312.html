EA-312 · Momentum

Expert_ABH_BH_MFI

This EA uses the MFI (Market Facilitation Index) indicator smoothed by a Moving Average as a momentum signal filter within the MQL5 Standard Library Expert framework. It opens and closes positions based on threshold values applied to the CBH_BH_MFI signal, with fixed lot sizing and no trailing stop. The strategy relies on the MFI indicator's ability to detect market momentum shifts combined with a MA filter to reduce noise.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on the Market Facilitation Index (MFI) indicator, using a moving average of MFI to identify shifts in market momentum direction. Entry conditions trigger when the CBH_BH_MFI signal value exceeds the opening threshold (default 40), and exit conditions trigger when the signal drops below the closing threshold (default 20), employing a 0-100 threshold system for signal filtering. Exit conditions rely entirely on signal threshold-based closing with no Stop Loss (StopLevel=0) and no Take Profit (TakeLevel=0) configured, which is a significant risk deficiency. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach (default 0.1 lots) implemented by the CMoneyFixedLot class, without dynamic adjustment based on account balance or volatility. Money management uses a 10% percent parameter with fixed lots, lacking dynamic position sizing based on volatility or risk proportion. The overall strategy framework leverages the MQL5 Standard Library CExpert multi-filter signal architecture, but this EA only uses a single MFI filter, making the signal source relatively one-dimensional.

Strengths

  • Uses the MQL5 Standard Library CExpert framework with clean, well-structured code that is easy to maintain and extend
  • MFI indicator combined with MA smoothing effectively filters market noise and reduces false signals
  • Threshold system (0-100) provides flexible signal strength control for tuning entry/exit sensitivity
  • Framework supports multi-filter extension, making it easy to add additional signal sources for confirmation

Weaknesses

  • Both Stop Loss and Take Profit are set to 0, providing no hard risk protection which could lead to catastrophic losses in extreme market conditions
  • Relies on a single MFI signal filter only, making the signal source one-dimensional and lacking multi-dimensional market analysis
  • Fixed lot money management cannot dynamically adjust position size based on market volatility or account risk
  • No trailing stop mechanism (uses TrailingNone), meaning profitable positions cannot lock in floating gains
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, potentially opening positions under unfavorable conditions

Possible improvements

  • Add Stop Loss and Take Profit parameters with at least a default SL to prevent extreme losses; consider ATR-based dynamic stop distance
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation or additional indicator filters (e.g., trend-direction MA, volume confirmation) to improve signal reliability
  • Implement volatility-based (ATR) dynamic position sizing and trailing stop to replace the current fixed lot and no-trailing-stop approach

Risk assessment

Overall risk: High risk

  • Stop Loss and Take Profit are both 0, providing absolutely no hard risk protection
  • Single indicator signal source is prone to frequent false signals in ranging markets leading to consecutive losses
  • No spread filter may result in unfavorable entries under high spread conditions
  • Pending order expiration of only 4 bars may cause frequent triggering and expiration during low liquidity periods
