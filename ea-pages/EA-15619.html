摘要

该EA基于MaChannel自定义指标进行交易，该指标构建一条平滑移动平均通道。当价格突破上通道时开多仓，当价格突破下通道时开空仓，并在反向信号出现时可选平仓对手头寸。

策略分析

策略概念基于移动平均通道突破，使用可配置的平滑算法（SMA、EMA、JJMA、JurX、T3、VIDYA、AMA等）构建上下通道线。入场条件为：当上通道缓冲区在指定信号K线处出现非空值时触发买入信号，当下通道缓冲区出现非空值时触发卖出信号。出场条件包括：固定止损和止盈点数、反向通道信号触发平仓（可配置开关控制）。仓位管理支持五种模式：基于可用保证金、基于余额、基于亏损后可用保证金、基于亏损后余额、以及固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，MMMode参数选择资金管理模式。该策略还包含新K线检测机制，避免在同一根K线内重复开仓，并使用Recount标志确保数据获取失败时重新计算信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on the MaChannel custom indicator, which builds a smoothed moving average channel. It opens buy positions when price breaks above the upper channel and sell positions when price breaks below the lower channel, with optional counter-position closing on reverse signals.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a moving average channel breakout using a configurable smoothing algorithm (SMA, EMA, JJMA, JurX, T3, VIDYA, AMA, etc.) to build upper and lower channel lines. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when the upper channel buffer shows a non-empty value on the specified signal bar, and a sell signal triggers when the lower channel buffer shows a non-empty value. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit in points, and reverse channel signals that can close opposing positions (controlled by boolean switches). Position sizing supports five modes: based on free margin, based on balance, based on free margin after losses, based on balance after losses, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter for risk ratio and MMMode for selecting the money management mode. The strategy also includes a new bar detection mechanism to prevent repeated entries within the same bar, and a Recount flag to ensure signal recalculation when data retrieval fails.

优势

  • 支持多达10种平滑算法，策略灵活性高，可适应不同市场环境
  • 具备新K线检测机制，避免同一K线内重复开仓，减少过度交易风险
  • 资金管理模式多样，提供五种仓位计算方式，满足不同风险偏好
  • 买卖开仓和平仓均可独立开关控制，策略配置粒度细致
  • 使用信号K线参数，避免在未完成K线上产生虚假信号

劣势

  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未考虑ATR等波动率指标，在不同波动环境下可能不适用
  • 缺乏加仓和移动止损机制，盈利头寸无法通过追踪止损锁定更多利润
  • 依赖自定义MaChannel指标，指标逻辑不透明，增加了回测和调试的难度
  • 未对交易品种的点差进行过滤，在高点差品种上可能造成不必要的损失
  • Recount机制在数据获取失败时仅设置标志并返回，缺乏重试次数限制和错误日志

改进建议

  • 引入基于ATR的动态止损止盈，使风险管理适应市场波动率变化
  • 添加移动止损功能，在趋势延续时锁定更多利润并保护已获收益
  • 增加点差过滤条件，当点差超过阈值时暂停开仓，避免滑点成本过高

Strengths

  • Supports up to 10 smoothing algorithms, providing high strategy flexibility to adapt to different market conditions
  • Includes new bar detection mechanism to prevent repeated entries within the same bar, reducing overtrading risk
  • Diverse money management modes with five position sizing methods to suit different risk preferences
  • Buy/sell open and close can be independently toggled, offering fine-grained strategy configuration
  • Uses configurable signal bar parameter to avoid false signals on uncompleted bars

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss and take profit are fixed points without ATR or volatility consideration, which may be unsuitable across varying volatility environments
  • Lacks position addition and trailing stop mechanisms, preventing profitable positions from locking in more gains
  • Depends on a custom MaChannel indicator with opaque logic, increasing difficulty for backtesting and debugging
  • No spread filter for trading instruments, potentially causing unnecessary losses on high-spread symbols
  • The Recount mechanism only sets a flag and returns on data retrieval failure, lacking retry limits and error logging

Improvements

  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit to adapt risk management to market volatility changes
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in more profits and protect gains during trend continuation
  • Add spread filter condition to pause opening positions when spread exceeds a threshold, avoiding excessive slippage costs

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 固定止损止盈未考虑市场波动率，在低波动时期止损可能过大，高波动时期止盈可能不足
  • 缺乏最大持仓数量限制和账户整体风险控制，多品种同时运行可能导致过度暴露
  • 通道突破策略在震荡市中容易产生频繁假突破信号，导致连续亏损

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed stop loss and take profit without volatility consideration may result in oversized stops in low-volatility periods and insufficient targets in high-volatility periods
  • Lacks maximum position count limit and overall account risk control, running on multiple symbols simultaneously may lead to overexposure
  • Channel breakout strategy prone to frequent false breakouts in ranging markets, causing consecutive losses
