摘要

该EA基于MaChannel自定义指标进行交易，该指标构建一个平滑移动平均通道。价格突破上轨时开多仓，突破下轨时开空仓，同时产生反向平仓信号。

策略分析

策略概念基于移动平均通道突破，使用可配置的平滑算法（SMA、EMA、JJMA、T3、VIDYA、AMA等）构建通道上下轨。入场条件为：当上轨信号缓冲区（buffer 2）出现非空值时触发做多开仓和做空平仓信号，当下轨信号缓冲区（buffer 3）出现非空值时触发做空开仓和做多平仓信号。出场条件分为两类：一是反向信号平仓（趋势反转时平掉原有仓位），二是固定止损止盈平仓（StopLoss=1000点，TakeProfit=2000点）。仓位管理支持五种模式：按可用保证金比例、按余额比例、按扣除浮亏后的保证金比例、按扣除浮亏后的余额比例、以及固定手数模式。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1，配合MarginMode选择计算方式。该策略还增加了通道线值（buffer 0和1）作为额外的平仓确认条件，即在同时开启同方向开仓和平仓开关时，通道线信号也会触发平仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on the MaChannel custom indicator, which builds a smoothed moving average channel. It opens buy positions when price breaks above the upper channel and sell positions when price breaks below the lower channel, with opposite-direction close signals generated as well.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a moving average channel breakout system, using configurable smoothing algorithms (SMA, EMA, JJMA, T3, VIDYA, AMA, etc.) to construct upper and lower channel bands. Entry conditions: when the upper signal buffer (buffer 2) produces a non-empty value, a buy-open and sell-close signal is triggered; when the lower signal buffer (buffer 3) produces a non-empty value, a sell-open and buy-close signal is triggered. Exit conditions are twofold: first, reverse-signal closing (closing existing positions when the opposite signal appears), and second, fixed stop-loss and take-profit closing (StopLoss=1000 points, TakeProfit=2000 points). Position sizing supports five margin modes: percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin minus floating loss, percentage of balance minus floating loss, and fixed lot mode. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter (default 0.1) combined with the MarginMode selection. The strategy also incorporates channel line values (buffers 0 and 1) as additional close confirmation, meaning when both same-direction open and close switches are enabled, channel line signals can also trigger position closing.

优势

  • 支持多种平滑算法（T3、JJMA、VIDYA等），通道构建灵活可调
  • 开仓和平仓信号独立控制，可通过开关灵活配置交易方向
  • 内置新K线检测机制（CIsNewBar），避免同一根K线重复开仓
  • 提供五种仓位管理模式，资金管理方案完善
  • 使用反向信号平仓加固定止损止盈双重出场机制

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点固定值偏大，未根据市场波动率动态调整
  • 缺少点差过滤机制，通道突破可能在点差较大时产生虚假信号
  • 指标缓冲区读取失败时仅设置Recount标志并返回，无错误计数或告警机制
  • 未检查当前是否已有同方向持仓，可能重复开仓
  • 依赖自定义指标MaChannel和外部包含文件，移植性较差

改进建议

  • 增加ATR动态止损止盈功能，根据市场波动率自动调整止损止盈距离
  • 添加点差过滤和持仓检查逻辑，避免高滑点和重复开仓

Strengths

  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithms (T3, JJMA, VIDYA, etc.) for flexible channel construction
  • Independent open/close signal control with configurable direction switches
  • Built-in new bar detection (CIsNewBar) prevents duplicate entries on the same bar
  • Five position sizing modes provide comprehensive money management options
  • Dual exit mechanism combining reverse-signal closing with fixed stop-loss and take-profit

Weaknesses

  • Fixed 1000-point SL and 2000-point TP are large and not dynamically adjusted for market volatility
  • No spread filter mechanism; channel breakouts may produce false signals during high-spread conditions
  • Indicator buffer read failures only set a Recount flag without error counting or alerting
  • No check for existing same-direction positions, potentially leading to duplicate entries
  • Depends on custom MaChannel indicator and external include files, reducing portability

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit to automatically adjust distances based on market volatility
  • Add spread filtering and position checking logic to avoid high-slippage entries and duplicate positions

风险点

  • 固定止损止盈未考虑市场波动率，在低波动市场止损可能过大而在高波动市场止盈可能不足
  • 缺少点差过滤，突破信号可能在点差扩大时导致不利成交
  • 未检查现有持仓可能导致同方向重复开仓增加风险敞口

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed SL/TP does not account for market volatility; SL may be too wide in low-volatility markets and TP insufficient in high-volatility markets
  • No spread filter means breakout signals may lead to unfavorable fills when spreads widen
  • No existing position check may cause duplicate same-direction entries increasing risk exposure
