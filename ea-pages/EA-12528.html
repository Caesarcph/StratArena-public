摘要

该EA基于FisherCyberCycle自定义指标的交叉信号进行交易，该指标将Fisher变换应用于赛博周期振荡器。当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿时开空仓，支持可配置的仓位管理和止损止盈设置。

策略分析

该EA使用FisherCyberCycle指标作为核心策略引擎，该指标结合了赛博周期滤波器和Fisher变换，旨在将正态分布的价格数据转换为近似高斯分布，从而产生更清晰的交叉信号。入场条件为：当指标线(Ind)从下方穿越信号线(Sign)时触发买入信号，当指标线从上方穿越信号线时触发卖出信号，交叉信号需在SignalBar指定的K线上确认。出场条件包括：反向交叉信号触发平仓（买入平仓和卖出平仓可独立开关），以及固定的止损和止盈水平。仓位管理采用TradeAlgorithms.mqh中定义的多种模式，包括基于自由保证金、余额、损失后自由保证金、损失后余额和固定手数五种资金管理方式。EA使用IsNewBar机制确保每个K线仅计算一次信号，避免重复入场，同时通过Recount标志在数据复制失败时重新计算信号。止损设置为1000点，止盈设置为2000点，盈亏比为1:2。

Summary

This EA trades based on crossover signals from the FisherCyberCycle custom indicator, which applies a Fisher transform to a cyber cycle oscillator. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions when it crosses below, with configurable position sizing and stop-loss/take-profit management.

Strategy Analysis

This EA uses the FisherCyberCycle indicator as its core strategy engine, which combines a cyber cycle filter with a Fisher transform to convert normally distributed price data into an approximately Gaussian distribution, producing cleaner crossover signals. Entry conditions are triggered when the indicator line (Ind) crosses above the signal line (Sign) for a buy signal, and when it crosses below for a sell signal, with crossover confirmation required on the bar specified by SignalBar. Exit conditions include: reverse crossover signals triggering position closure (buy and sell close can be independently toggled), and fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels. Position sizing employs five money management modes defined in TradeAlgorithms.mqh: based on free margin, balance, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. The EA uses an IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are calculated only once per bar, avoiding duplicate entries, while a Recount flag forces signal recalculation if data copying fails. The stop-loss is set at 1000 points and take-profit at 2000 points, yielding a 1:2 risk-reward ratio.

优势

  • 使用Fisher变换将价格数据归一化，产生更清晰的交叉信号，减少虚假信号
  • 提供五种灵活的资金管理模式，适应不同风险偏好的交易者
  • 买卖开仓和平仓可独立控制，策略配置灵活性高
  • 设有1:2的盈亏比，长期期望值为正
  • 使用IsNewBar机制防止同一K线重复入场

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点可能过大，在某些品种上不适用
  • 没有点差过滤，在点差较大的品种上可能造成不必要的损失
  • 依赖外部自定义指标FisherCyberCycle，如果指标文件缺失EA将无法运行
  • 缺乏对已有持仓数量的检查，可能在同一方向重复开仓
  • 没有最大回撤或最大持仓限制等风险控制机制

改进建议

  • 增加持仓数量检查，防止同方向重复开仓导致过度杠杆
  • 添加点差过滤和最大回撤限制，提高风险控制能力
  • 将止损止盈改为ATR动态计算，使参数自适应不同品种的波动性

Strengths

  • Fisher transform normalizes price data for cleaner crossover signals with reduced false signals
  • Five flexible money management modes accommodate traders with different risk preferences
  • Buy/sell open and close can be independently toggled for high strategy configuration flexibility
  • 1:2 risk-reward ratio provides positive long-term expectancy
  • IsNewBar mechanism prevents duplicate entries on the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Stop-loss of 1000 points and take-profit of 2000 points may be too large for some instruments
  • No spread filter, which could cause unnecessary losses on high-spread instruments
  • Depends on external custom indicator FisherCyberCycle; EA fails if indicator file is missing
  • No check for existing position count, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Lacks risk control mechanisms such as maximum drawdown or maximum position limits

Improvements

  • Add position count check to prevent duplicate same-direction entries causing excessive leverage
  • Add spread filter and maximum drawdown limit to improve risk control
  • Replace fixed SL/TP with ATR-based dynamic calculation for better adaptability across instruments
