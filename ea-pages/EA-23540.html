摘要

Ilan iMA是一个基于iMA（移动平均线）指标判断方向的马丁格尔网格EA。它根据MA信号开仓，若价格逆向运行，则按固定点距加倍手数补仓，形成金字塔式网格。策略依赖价格最终回撤以小盈利平掉整个订单组合。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略概念是马丁格尔加仓法结合移动平均线方向过滤。入场条件为：价格位于MA之上时做多，位于MA之下时做空，首次入场按设定手数开仓。出场条件为：当整个订单组合（包括初始单和所有补仓单）的浮盈达到设定的利润目标时，全部平仓获利了结。仓位管理采用经典马丁格尔方式，每次补仓手数为前一次的倍数（默认2倍），补仓间距为固定点数。资金管理方面，没有硬性止损，完全依赖价格回撤和加仓摊平成本来解套，这在单边行情中极易爆仓。整体策略缺乏风险底线控制，属于高风险高回报类型。

Summary

Ilan iMA is a Martingale-style grid EA that uses the iMA (Moving Average) indicator to determine trade direction. It opens an initial position based on the MA signal and, if the trade goes against it, doubles the lot size at fixed pip intervals, creating a pyramiding grid. The strategy relies on price eventually retracing to close the entire basket of orders at a small profit.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy concept is a Martingale averaging-down approach combined with a Moving Average directional filter. Entry conditions: go long when price is above the MA, go short when price is below the MA, with the initial trade at a configured lot size. Exit conditions: when the floating profit of the entire order basket (initial plus all pyramid entries) reaches a configured profit target, all positions are closed simultaneously. Position sizing follows classic Martingale logic, doubling the lot size on each subsequent entry (default 2x multiplier), with entries spaced at fixed pip intervals. Money management lacks a hard stop-loss, relying entirely on price reversion and cost-averaging to exit profitably, which makes it extremely vulnerable to sustained trending moves. The strategy has no risk floor control, categorizing it as a high-risk, high-reward system.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中表现优秀，频繁小幅盈利积累可观收益
  • 参数可调性强，可根据品种特性优化补仓间距和倍数
  • MA方向过滤减少了逆势开仓的概率，比纯随机马丁更合理

劣势

  • 无硬性止损，单边趋势行情中可能爆仓
  • 马丁格尔加倍手数导致后期仓位呈指数级增长，资金需求极大
  • 补仓次数上限虽有限制但到达时往往已严重亏损，缺乏有效兜底机制

改进建议

  • 增加硬性止损或最大回撤比例限制，防止账户爆仓
  • 加入趋势强度过滤（如ADX）或波动率过滤（如ATR），在强趋势时暂停加仓
  • 将固定点距改为基于ATR的动态间距，适应不同市场波动环境

Strengths

  • Performs well in ranging markets, accumulating significant returns through frequent small profits
  • Highly configurable parameters allow optimization of grid spacing and multiplier for different instruments
  • MA directional filter reduces counter-trend entries, making it more logical than a purely random Martingale

Weaknesses

  • No hard stop-loss means the account can be blown during sustained trending markets
  • Martingale lot doubling causes exponential position growth in later stages, demanding enormous capital
  • Although MaxTrades limits the number of entries, reaching the cap usually means severe drawdown with no recovery mechanism

Improvements

  • Add a hard stop-loss or maximum drawdown percentage limit to prevent account blow-up
  • Incorporate a trend strength filter (e.g., ADX) or volatility filter (e.g., ATR) to pause grid entries during strong trends
  • Change fixed pip spacing to ATR-based dynamic spacing to adapt to varying market volatility conditions

  • 马丁格尔加倍仓位在持续单边行情中会导致指数级亏损，最终可能爆仓
  • 无止损设定，亏损敞口无限扩大
  • 达到最大加仓次数后无退出机制，浮亏可能超过账户余额

Risk Points

  • No stop-loss means unlimited downside exposure
  • No exit mechanism after reaching MaxTrades, unrealized losses can exceed account balance
