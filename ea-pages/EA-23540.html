Ilan iMA

Ilan iMA is a Martingale-style grid EA that uses the iMA (Moving Average) indicator as its entry signal. When a trade goes against the position, it opens additional orders with increasing lot sizes at fixed pip intervals, aiming to recover losses on a reversal. It is a classic Ilan-family EA adapted for MQL5 with MA-based trend filtering.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy of this EA is the Martingale position-sizing method, which doubles the lot size after each losing trade in hopes of recovering all losses on a single winning reversal. Entry conditions are based on the iMA (Moving Average) indicator to determine the trend direction, opening the first position when price aligns with the perceived trend. If the market moves against the position, the EA opens additional orders at a fixed pip interval (Step parameter), with lot sizes multiplied by the Multiplier factor each time. Exit conditions trigger when the combined floating profit of all same-direction positions reaches the TakeProfit target, at which point all positions are closed simultaneously. Position sizing follows the classic Martingale formula: Nth level lot = Initial Lot × Multiplier^(N-1). Money management lacks a hard stop-loss, relying entirely on eventual price mean-reversion to escape drawdowns, which can lead to account blowout during strong trending markets.

Strengths

  • Performs well in ranging/sideways markets, generating consistent profits
  • MA trend filter reduces the probability of counter-trend initial entries
  • Unified take-profit mechanism ensures all levels exit profitably together
  • Highly configurable parameters allow users to adapt to different instrument characteristics

Weaknesses

  • Martingale position sizing can lead to account blowout during strong trending markets
  • No hard stop-loss means risk is unbounded and uncontrollable
  • Margin requirements grow exponentially with each level, demanding very large account equity
  • Does not account for the erosion of profit by spreads and commissions
  • Drawdowns can be extreme, requiring significant psychological tolerance

Possible improvements

  • Add a hard stop-loss and maximum drawdown limit to prevent account blowout
  • Implement spread filter and trading session filter to avoid high-cost periods
  • Consider dynamic Step based on ATR/volatility instead of fixed pip intervals for adaptive spacing
  • Add a total portfolio risk percentage control to cap the maximum loss per trade series as a fraction of account equity

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Dangerous

  • Martingale lot multiplication grows exponentially during consecutive losses,easily leading to margin call
  • No hard stop-loss means unlimited losses in strong unidirectional markets
  • No spread filter causes rapidly escalating costs when adding positions during high-volatility periods
