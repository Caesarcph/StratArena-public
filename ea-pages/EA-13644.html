摘要

该EA基于FractalAMA_MBK自定义指标的交叉信号进行交易。当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿信号线时开空仓，支持可配置的止损、止盈和资金管理选项。EA以新K线为基础运行，使用TradeAlgorithms库进行仓位管理。

策略分析

该EA采用分形自适应移动平均线（FractalAMA）与信号线的交叉作为趋势跟踪策略的核心概念。入场条件为：当指标值从下方穿越信号线时产生买入信号，从上方穿越信号线时产生卖出信号，且信号需在相邻两根K线上确认交叉。出场条件依赖固定点数的止损（1000点）和止盈（2000点），同时当反向交叉信号出现时也触发平仓。仓位管理支持五种模式：按可用保证金比例、按余额比例、按扣除亏损后的保证金比例、按扣除亏损后的余额比例以及固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险敞口比例，默认0.1表示10%的资金分配。EA使用IsNewBar机制避免同一根K线内重复开仓，并通过SignalBar参数允许在历史K线上验证信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on crossover signals from the FractalAMA_MBK custom indicator. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions when it crosses below, with configurable stop loss, take profit, and money management options. The EA operates on a new bar basis and uses the TradeAlgorithms library for position management.

Strategy Analysis

This EA uses the crossover of the Fractal Adaptive Moving Average (FractalAMA) with its signal line as the core concept of a trend-following strategy. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the indicator value crosses above the signal line, and a sell signal when it crosses below, with the crossover confirmed across two adjacent bars. Exit conditions rely on a fixed stop loss (1000 points) and take profit (2000 points), while reverse crossover signals also trigger position closure. Position sizing supports five modes: percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin minus floating loss, percentage of balance minus floating loss, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter which sets the risk exposure ratio, defaulting to 0.1 (10% allocation). The EA uses the IsNewBar mechanism to prevent duplicate entries within the same bar and allows signal validation on historical bars via the SignalBar parameter.

优势

  • 使用分形自适应移动平均线，能根据市场波动性自动调整灵敏度，优于简单移动平均线
  • 新K线机制防止同一根K线内重复开仓，避免过度交易
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，灵活性高
  • 止损止盈配置合理（1:2风险回报比），有基本风控保障
  • 开关控制允许单独启用/禁用买卖方向，适应不同市场环境

劣势

  • 依赖外部自定义指标FractalAMA_MBK，若指标文件缺失则EA无法运行
  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未考虑市场波动率（ATR）动态调整
  • 缺乏点差过滤机制，在宽点差环境下可能不利
  • 未检查是否已存在同方向持仓，可能导致重复开仓
  • Recount静态变量在指标数据获取失败时仅设为true重试，但无最大重试限制

改进建议

  • 添加ATR动态止损止盈功能，根据市场波动率自适应调整止损距离
  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓导致风险过度集中
  • 添加点差过滤和最大重试次数限制，提高在不利市场条件下的健壮性

Strengths

  • Uses Fractal Adaptive Moving Average which auto-adjusts sensitivity based on market volatility, superior to simple moving averages
  • New bar mechanism prevents duplicate entries within the same bar, avoiding overtrading
  • Supports multiple money management modes offering high flexibility
  • Reasonable stop loss/take profit configuration (1:2 risk-reward ratio) providing basic risk control
  • Toggle switches allow independently enabling/disabling buy/sell directions to adapt to different market conditions

Weaknesses

  • Depends on external custom indicator FractalAMA_MBK; EA fails to run if the indicator file is missing
  • Fixed stop loss and take profit in points without ATR-based dynamic adjustment for market volatility
  • No spread filter mechanism, potentially disadvantageous in wide spread conditions
  • No check for existing positions in the same direction, potentially leading to duplicate entries
  • The Recount static variable retries on data fetch failure but has no maximum retry limit

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss/take profit to adapt stop distances to market volatility
  • Add position existence check to avoid duplicate same-direction entries that over-concentrate risk
  • Add spread filter and maximum retry count limit to improve robustness under adverse market conditions
