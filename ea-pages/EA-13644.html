摘要

该EA基于FractalAMA_MBK自定义指标的交叉信号进行交易，该指标是一种分形自适应移动平均线。当主线从下方穿越信号线时开多仓，从上方穿越时开空仓，支持可配置的资金管理、止损和止盈。

策略分析

策略概念基于FractalAMA（分形自适应移动平均）指标的趋势跟踪，利用指标主线与信号线的交叉作为趋势转折信号。入场条件：当指标主线大于信号线且前一根K线主线小于等于信号线时，产生做多信号；反之当主线小于信号线且前一根主线大于等于信号线时，产生做空信号。出场条件通过反向交叉信号触发平仓，同时配合固定的止损（1000点）和止盈（2000点）进行风险管理。仓位管理支持五种模式：按自由保证金比例、按余额比例、按扣除浮亏后的自由保证金比例、按扣除浮亏后的余额比例、以及固定手数模式。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1，MMMode默认为LOT固定手数模式。EA使用IsNewBar机制确保每个K线只计算一次信号，避免重复开仓，同时通过Recount标志在CopyBuffer失败时重新获取数据。

Summary

This EA trades based on the crossover signals of the FractalAMA_MBK custom indicator, which is a fractal-adaptive moving average. It opens buy positions when the main line crosses above the signal line and sell positions when it crosses below, with configurable money management, stop loss, and take profit.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is trend following based on the FractalAMA (Fractal Adaptive Moving Average) indicator, using crossovers between the indicator main line and signal line as trend reversal signals. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the main line is above the signal line and the previous bar's main line was below or equal to the signal line; conversely, a sell signal is generated when the main line is below the signal line and the previous bar's main line was above or equal to the signal line. Exit conditions are triggered by reverse crossover signals for position closure, combined with fixed stop loss (1000 points) and take profit (2000 points) for risk management. Position sizing supports five modes: percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin minus floating loss, percentage of balance minus floating loss, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter with a default of 0.1, and the MMMode defaults to LOT (fixed lot) mode. The EA uses an IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are calculated only once per bar, preventing duplicate entries, and employs a Recount flag to retry data retrieval when CopyBuffer fails.

优势

  • 使用分形自适应移动平均线，能根据市场波动性自动调节灵敏度，比传统MA更适应不同行情
  • 具备完整的资金管理模块，支持五种仓位计算模式，灵活度高
  • 采用IsNewBar机制防止同一K线内重复开仓，逻辑严谨
  • 止损止盈参数可配置，默认2:1的盈亏比设计合理
  • 买卖方向可独立开关控制，适应不同市场环境

劣势

  • 依赖外部自定义指标FractalAMA_MBK，若指标文件缺失EA将无法运行，增加了部署复杂度
  • 没有加仓和仓位调整机制，无法在趋势延续时增加仓位以放大盈利
  • 没有移动止损或追踪止损功能，可能导致盈利大幅回撤
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点的默认值过大，在短线交易中可能不适用
  • 缺少对交易时段和点差的过滤，可能在流动性差或点差大时产生不利成交

改进建议

  • 增加移动止损/追踪止损功能，在趋势行情中保护浮动利润
  • 增加点差过滤和交易时段控制，避免在不利条件下开仓

Strengths

  • Uses Fractal Adaptive Moving Average which automatically adjusts sensitivity based on market volatility, making it more adaptive than traditional MAs
  • Comprehensive money management module with five position sizing modes offering high flexibility
  • Implements IsNewBar mechanism to prevent duplicate entries within the same bar, ensuring logical rigor
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit with a default 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio which is well-designed
  • Buy and sell directions can be independently enabled or disabled, adapting to different market conditions

Weaknesses

  • Depends on external custom indicator FractalAMA_MBK; if the indicator file is missing the EA cannot run, increasing deployment complexity
  • No pyramiding or position adjustment mechanism, unable to add positions during trend continuation to maximize profits
  • Lacks trailing stop functionality, which may lead to significant profit drawdowns
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large and may not be suitable for short-term trading
  • Missing trading session and spread filters, which may result in unfavorable fills during low liquidity or high spread periods

Improvements

  • Add trailing stop functionality to protect floating profits during trending markets
  • Add spread filter and trading session controls to avoid opening positions under unfavorable conditions

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 缺少移动止损功能，盈利仓位可能因趋势反转而大幅回撤甚至转为亏损
  • 默认止损值很大（1000点），单笔亏损可能较大，尤其是在小周期上
  • 没有点差和流动性过滤，可能在新闻事件或低流动性时段产生滑点较大的成交

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Lack of trailing stop means profitable positions may experience significant drawdowns or even turn into losses on trend reversal
  • Large default stop loss value (1000 points) can result in significant single-trade losses, especially on lower timeframes
  • No spread or liquidity filter may cause large slippage entries during news events or low liquidity periods
