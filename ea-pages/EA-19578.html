摘要

该EA实现了'Puria方法'交易策略，利用带偏移的移动平均线组合来产生趋势跟踪入场信号。当价格上穿偏移均线时做多，下穿时做空，旨在捕捉中期趋势行情，MA偏移有助于过滤短期噪音。

策略分析

Puria方法的核心策略概念是利用移动平均线的偏移（shift/displacement）来平滑价格走势并产生延迟的趋势信号，从而过滤掉短期市场噪音。入场条件通常为：当价格从下方突破偏移移动平均线时开多仓，从上方跌破时开空仓；有些变体还要求多条不同周期的偏移均线交叉确认。出场条件依赖于反向交叉信号或者固定的止盈止损水平，部分实现会使用追踪止损来锁定利润。仓位管理方面，该EA通常采用固定手数交易，缺少基于账户余额或波动率的动态仓位调整机制。资金管理较为基础，虽然有止损设置但可能不够灵活，未考虑点差和滑点对短线交易的影响。整体而言，这是一个典型的趋势跟踪系统，在趋势明显的市场中表现较好，但在震荡市中容易产生频繁的虚假信号导致连续亏损。

Summary

This EA implements the 'Puria Method' trading strategy, which uses a combination of moving averages with a shift (displacement) to generate trend-following entry signals. It enters long when price crosses above the shifted MA and short when it crosses below, aiming to capture medium-term trend movements while the MA shift helps filter out short-term noise.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy concept of the Puria Method is to use shifted (displaced) moving averages to smooth price action and generate delayed trend signals, thereby filtering out short-term market noise. Entry conditions typically involve: opening a long position when price crosses above the displaced moving average from below, and opening a short position when price crosses below from above; some variants require confirmation from multiple shifted MAs with different periods. Exit conditions rely on reverse crossover signals or fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, with some implementations using trailing stops to lock in profits. In terms of position sizing, this EA typically uses fixed lot sizes and lacks dynamic position adjustment based on account equity or market volatility. Money management is relatively basic — while stop-losses are set, they may not be flexible enough, and the impact of spread and slippage on trading performance is not adequately considered. Overall, this is a typical trend-following system that performs well in strongly trending markets but is prone to frequent false signals and consecutive losses in choppy, range-bound conditions.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和调试，适合初学者使用和修改
  • MA偏移机制有效减少了短期市场噪音带来的虚假信号，提高了信号质量
  • 作为趋势跟踪策略，在明确趋势行情中能够捕获较大幅度的利润
  • 参数较少，优化空间明确，方便进行回测和参数调优
  • 固定止损止盈设置提供了基本的风险控制框架

劣势

  • 在震荡整理行情中会产生大量虚假信号，导致频繁止损和连续亏损
  • 固定手数交易缺乏动态仓位管理，无法根据市场波动率或账户风险自动调整
  • 没有点差过滤和交易时间过滤，在高点差时段交易会显著降低盈利能力
  • 缺少新K线检查机制，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓
  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未考虑ATR等波动率指标，在不同市场条件下适应性差

改进建议

  • 增加ATR动态止损止盈机制，根据市场波动率自动调整止损止盈距离，提高策略适应性
  • 添加ADX或波动率过滤器，在震荡行情中减少交易频率或暂停交易
  • 实现基于账户余额百分比的动态仓位管理，替代固定手数方案

Strengths

  • The strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and debug, suitable for beginners to use and modify
  • The MA shift mechanism effectively reduces false signals caused by short-term market noise, improving signal quality
  • As a trend-following strategy, it can capture substantial profits in clear trending markets
  • Fewer parameters with clear optimization space, convenient for backtesting and parameter tuning
  • Fixed stop-loss and take-profit settings provide a basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Generates numerous false signals in choppy/range-bound markets, leading to frequent stop-losses and consecutive losses
  • Fixed lot sizing lacks dynamic position management, unable to automatically adjust based on market volatility or account risk
  • No spread filter or trading session filter, trading during high-spread periods significantly reduces profitability
  • Lacks new bar check mechanism, potentially opening multiple positions on the same candle
  • Fixed pip-based stop-loss and take-profit without considering ATR or other volatility indicators, resulting in poor adaptability across varying market conditions

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit mechanism to automatically adjust SL/TP distances based on market volatility, improving strategy adaptability
  • Add ADX or volatility filter to reduce trading frequency or pause trading during range-bound market conditions
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account equity percentage to replace the fixed lot scheme

  • 没有点差过滤机制，在流动性差的时段交易可能因点差扩大而造成不必要的损失

Risk Assessment

Risk Points

  • Fixed stop-loss does not account for market volatility — too wide in low volatility wasting profit potential, too narrow in high volatility getting stopped out easily
  • No spread filter mechanism, trading during low-liquidity periods may cause unnecessary losses due to widened spreads
