摘要

该EA基于自定义LeManSignal指标的信号进行交易，在上涨动量信号时开多单，在下跌动量信号时开空单。它包含可配置的买入/卖出开仓和平仓逻辑，以及可选的止损和止盈水平，并通过百分比仓位管理参数(MM)进行资金管理。当同时出现买入和卖出信号时，EA会取消两个入场信号以避免冲突。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念基于LeManSignal自定义指标产生的动量信号进行趋势跟踪交易。入场条件为：当指标上轨(UpValue)出现非零且非空值时产生买入信号，当指标下轨(DnValue)出现非零且非空值时产生卖出信号。出场条件有两种：一是通过反向信号平仓（买入信号触发卖出平仓，卖出信号触发买入平仓），二是通过固定点数的止损和止盈平仓。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示按账户余额百分比计算手数，正值表示固定手数。资金管理方面，EA包含冲突信号处理逻辑——当同时出现买入和卖出信号时，所有开仓和平仓标志都会被重置为false，避免同时开多空仓。此外，当买入开仓和买入平仓同时启用（或卖出同理）但未检测到平仓信号时，EA会回溯历史K线寻找最近的反向信号来确定平仓条件，这是一种保守的风险控制机制。

Summary

This EA trades based on signals from the custom LeManSignal indicator, opening buy positions on upward momentum signals and sell positions on downward momentum signals. It includes configurable buy/sell opening and closing logic, with optional stop-loss and take-profit levels, plus money management via a percentage-based lot sizing parameter (MM). The EA also handles conflicting signals by canceling both entries when simultaneous buy and sell signals occur.

Strategy Analysis

The EA's strategy concept is based on momentum signals generated by the custom LeManSignal indicator for trend-following trading. Entry conditions are: a buy signal is generated when the indicator's upper buffer (UpValue) shows a non-zero and non-EMPTY_VALUE reading, while a sell signal is generated when the lower buffer (DnValue) shows a non-zero and non-EMPTY_VALUE reading. Exit conditions come in two forms: closing via opposing signals (a buy signal triggers sell position closure, and vice versa), or through fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels in points. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where a negative value calculates lot size as a percentage of account balance, and a positive value represents a fixed lot size. For money management, the EA includes a conflict resolution mechanism — when both buy and sell signals appear simultaneously, all open and close flags are reset to false, preventing contradictory positions. Additionally, when both buy-open and buy-close (or sell equivalents) are enabled but no close signal is detected on the signal bar, the EA loops back through historical bars to find the most recent opposing signal to determine the close condition, which serves as a conservative risk control mechanism.

优势

  • 具备冲突信号处理机制，同时出现买卖信号时取消所有操作，避免对冲风险
  • 支持灵活的资金管理方式，可通过MM参数选择固定手数或百分比仓位
  • 回溯历史K线寻找平仓信号的机制增强了策略的风险控制能力
  • 所有交易方向（买入开仓、卖出开仓、买入平仓、卖出平仓）均可独立开关，灵活性高

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标LeManSignal，指标源码不可见时无法验证信号逻辑的可靠性
  • 回溯历史K线查找平仓信号的循环可能在大数据量时影响性能
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时段过滤，可能在不利条件下开仓
  • 止损和止盈为固定点数，未考虑市场波动率（ATR）的动态调整

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免在高点差和非活跃时段交易
  • 将固定止损止盈改为基于ATR的动态止损止盈，适应不同市场波动环境
  • 在回溯循环中增加最大回溯K线数量限制，防止性能问题

Strengths

  • Conflict signal handling cancels all operations when both buy and sell signals appear simultaneously, avoiding hedging risk
  • Flexible money management supports both fixed lot size and percentage-based position sizing via the MM parameter
  • Historical bar lookback for close signal detection enhances risk control capability
  • All trading directions (buy open, sell open, buy close, sell close) can be independently toggled on/off for high flexibility

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom LeManSignal indicator whose source code is not visible, making signal logic reliability unverifiable
  • The historical bar lookback loop for close signal detection may impact performance with large bar counts
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, potentially opening positions under unfavorable conditions
  • Stop-loss and take-profit use fixed point values without dynamic adjustment based on market volatility (ATR)

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid trading during high-spread and inactive periods
  • Replace fixed stop-loss/take-profit with ATR-based dynamic levels to adapt to varying market volatility
  • Add a maximum lookback bar limit in the historical loop to prevent performance issues
