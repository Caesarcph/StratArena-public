MACD Stochastic 2

This EA combines MACD and Stochastic oscillator signals to generate trade entries. It uses MACD for trend direction confirmation and Stochastic for overbought/oversold momentum timing. Trades are opened when both indicators align, providing a dual-confirmation momentum trading system.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on a dual-confirmation momentum trading system combining MACD and Stochastic indicators. Entry conditions: for long trades, MACD main line must be above the signal line and Stochastic crosses up from the oversold zone; for short trades, MACD main line must be below the signal line and Stochastic crosses down from the overbought zone. Exit conditions rely on fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels or indicator reversal signals. Position sizing uses fixed lots without dynamic adjustment based on account equity. Money management lacks risk-proportional control and does not implement volatility-adaptive position sizing. The overall strategy logic is clear but lacks a market state filter (trending vs ranging) to avoid whipsaws in sideways markets.

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator confirmation reduces false signals and improves entry quality
  • MACD and Stochastic are complementary: MACD captures trend direction while Stochastic pinpoints entry timing
  • Strategy logic is intuitive and easy to understand, facilitating parameter optimization and backtesting

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing does not adapt to account size changes, making money management inflexible
  • Lacks market state filtering, prone to frequent losing signals in ranging markets
  • No new bar detection mechanism, potentially opening duplicate positions on the same bar

Possible improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit for volatility-adaptive exit levels
  • Add a trend filter (e.g., ADX or higher timeframe MA) to avoid frequent trading in ranging markets

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Lack of new bar detection may cause duplicate position openings on the same bar
  • Fixed lot size does not dynamically adjust based on account risk tolerance
  • No spread filter, entry costs may be too high in high-spread environments
