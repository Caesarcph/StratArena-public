摘要

VR SETKA 3是一个网格交易EA，在初始入场点周围按固定价格间隔放置一系列挂单（即'网格'）。它通过在区间内价格波动时逐个在止盈目标平仓来获利，但当市场沿网格对侧趋势运行时会累积仓位。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是网格交易（Setka在俄语中意为'网格'），在预设价格水平上等间距布置买单和卖单。入场条件基于初始价格位置，随后在上下方按固定步长自动布设挂单阵列。出场条件为每个网格层级独立设止盈，当价格触及某一层级的止盈时该单平仓获利，但缺乏全局止损机制。仓位管理采用固定手数或递增手数方式，部分版本支持马丁格尔加仓。资金管理方面风险较高，因为当市场单边趋势运行时会不断开仓，导致浮亏急剧扩大，且没有整体账户保护机制。

Summary

VR SETKA 3 is a grid trading Expert Advisor that places a series of pending orders at fixed price intervals (a 'setka' or grid) around an initial entry point. It profits from price oscillations within a range by closing individual grid levels at take-profit targets while accumulating positions when the market trends against the grid.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is grid trading ('Setka' means 'grid' in Russian), placing buy and sell orders at equal intervals around a central price level. Entry conditions are based on an initial price anchor, with pending orders automatically placed at fixed step distances above and below. Exit conditions use individual take-profit targets per grid level, closing each order when price reaches its TP, but there is no overall portfolio stop-loss. Position sizing may use fixed lots or incremental lots, with some variants supporting Martingale-style lot increases on losing positions. Money management is high-risk because the EA continuously opens new positions when the market trends in one direction, causing floating losses to accumulate rapidly without an account-level protection mechanism. The strategy inherently assumes mean-reverting market behavior and can suffer catastrophic drawdowns during strong trending moves.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中能持续获利，每次价格穿越网格层级都有收益
  • 策略逻辑简单直观，参数较少易于理解和调整
  • 无需判断市场方向，完全依靠价格波动本身获利

劣势

  • 单边趋势行情中浮亏会急剧累积，可能导致爆仓
  • 缺乏全局止损和账户级保护机制，风险不可控
  • 持续开仓会占用大量保证金，资金使用效率低
  • 在低流动性或跳空行情中网格可能全部被触发造成巨大损失

改进建议

  • 添加全局最大回撤止损，当浮亏达到账户余额一定比例时全部平仓
  • 加入趋势过滤指标（如均线或ADX），在强趋势时暂停或缩减网格开仓

Strengths

  • Profits consistently in ranging markets, earning on each price oscillation across grid levels
  • Simple and intuitive strategy logic with few parameters, easy to understand and tune
  • No need to predict market direction, profits purely from price volatility itself

Weaknesses

  • Floating losses accumulate rapidly during trending markets, potentially causing account blow-up
  • Lacks global stop-loss and account-level protection mechanisms, making risk uncontrollable
  • Continuous position opening consumes large margin, resulting in low capital efficiency
  • Grid orders can all be triggered during low liquidity or gap events, causing massive losses

Improvements

  • Add a global maximum drawdown stop that closes all positions when floating loss reaches a percentage of account balance
  • Incorporate a trend filter indicator (e.g., moving average or ADX) to pause or reduce grid opening during strong trends
