RobotPowerM5 meta4V12

RobotPowerM5 is an M5 timeframe momentum-based Expert Advisor that generates trade signals from a combination of oscillators and trend filters. It enters positions when momentum indicators confirm directional strength and exits via fixed take-profit or stop-loss levels. The EA includes lot sizing and basic risk controls but appears to lack advanced money management features.

Strategy analysis

This EA employs a momentum-based strategy operating on the M5 short-term timeframe, suitable for capturing short-term price movements. Entry conditions rely on confluence confirmation from multiple momentum-type indicators (such as RSI, MACD, Stochastic, etc.), triggering trade signals when indicators collectively point in the same direction. Exit conditions use fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, with no trailing stop or dynamic exit mechanism. Position sizing is based on fixed lots or a simple account percentage calculation, without employing martingale or grid strategies. Money management is relatively simple, lacking complex dynamic position adjustment or volatility-based lot calculation. The overall strategy logic is clear but basic, lacking adaptive capability to changing market conditions.

Strengths

  • Clear and understandable strategy logic with multi-indicator confluence reducing false signals
  • Uses fixed stop-loss, making risk per trade controllable and predefined
  • No martingale or grid strategy, avoiding extreme account blowout risk
  • M5 timeframe suits short-term trading and responds quickly to market changes
  • Magic number identifies orders, preventing conflicts with other EAs

Weaknesses

  • Lacks trailing stop mechanism, unable to lock in floating profits
  • M5 short-term trading is heavily affected by spread, no built-in spread filter
  • Position sizing is too simple, no dynamic lot adjustment based on volatility
  • Fixed TP/SL doesn't account for market volatility changes, may be maladaptive across different market conditions
  • Missing new bar check mechanism, may repeatedly open positions on the same bar

Possible improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic take-profit and stop-loss to adapt exit levels to market volatility
  • Implement trailing stop functionality to protect floating profits
  • Introduce spread filter to prohibit opening positions when spread is too wide
  • Add new bar check to ensure only one entry per bar
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account balance percentage

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • M5 scalping is heavily impacted by spread and slippage, no spread filter implemented
  • Missing new bar check may cause repeated position opening on the same bar
  • Fixed TP/SL may be inappropriate under varying volatility conditions
