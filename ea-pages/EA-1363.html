摘要

该EA基于CorrectedAverage修正均线指标实现突破交易系统。当价格突破均线上方指定点位时做多，跌破均线下方指定点位时做空。使用固定止损和止盈管理仓位，并可独立控制买卖方向的入场和出场权限。

策略分析

该EA采用基于CorrectedAverage修正均线的突破策略，核心概念是当价格偏离均线达到指定突破水平(Level点)时确认趋势启动并入场。入场条件为：当前K线收盘价高于均线值加突破水平时触发做多信号，前一根K线收盘价低于均线值加突破水平以确保是突破而非持续偏离；做空条件对称。出场条件包括固定止损(StopLoss)和止盈(TakeProfit)点位，以及反向信号触发平仓——即做多信号出现时若允许则平空仓，做空信号出现时若允许则平多仓。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值代表固定手数，正值代表按存款比例计算仓位。资金管理依赖TradeAlgorithms.mqh库函数执行，止损止盈以点数设定。策略仅在新K线形成时检测信号(SignalBar=0除外)，避免重复交易，同时使用Recount机制确保数据获取失败时重新计算。

Summary

This EA implements a breakout trading system based on the CorrectedAverage custom moving average indicator. It enters long when price breaks above the moving average by a specified level, and enters short when price breaks below. Positions are managed with fixed stop loss and take profit levels, with optional permissions for buy/sell entry and exit.

Strategy Analysis

This EA implements a breakout strategy based on the CorrectedAverage custom moving average indicator. The core concept is to confirm a trend initiation when price deviates from the moving average by a specified breakthrough level (Level points) and enter accordingly. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when the current bar's close exceeds the indicator value plus the breakthrough level, with the previous bar's close at or below that threshold to confirm a fresh breakout rather than a continued deviation; sell conditions are symmetrical. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit levels in points, plus counter-signal exits — a buy signal closes short positions if permitted, and a sell signal closes long positions if permitted. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter: negative values represent fixed lot sizes, while positive values represent a share of the deposit. Money management relies on the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library functions for execution. The strategy only checks for signals on new bar formation (unless SignalBar=0), preventing duplicate trades, and uses a Recount mechanism to retry data fetching on failure.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于均线突破的原理简单有效，易于理解和优化
  • 提供独立的买卖方向入场和出场权限控制，灵活性高，可适应不同市场观点
  • 使用新K线检测机制避免同一根K线内重复交易，减少过度交易风险
  • 止损止盈参数完整，配合反向信号平仓提供多重出场保护
  • Recount机制确保数据获取失败时不会丢失信号，增强了数据可靠性

劣势

  • 缺乏止损止盈的动态调整机制，固定点数止损可能在波动性变化时不够灵活
  • 没有过滤交易时段或市场波动性的机制，可能在低流动性或高波动时段产生不利交易
  • 未进行点差过滤，在点差较大的品种上可能导致突破信号被点差吞噬
  • MM参数的正负值含义不直观，容易造成用户误配置
  • 依赖外部自定义指标CorrectedAverage和TradeAlgorithms.mqh库，缺乏独立性，部署时需确保这些依赖可用

改进建议

  • 增加ATR或波动性自适应的止损止盈机制，替代固定点数，使风险管理随市场波动动态调整
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免在高点差或低流动性时段入场
  • 增加移动止损(Trailing Stop)功能，在趋势延续时锁定浮动利润

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on moving average breakout — simple, effective, easy to understand and optimize
  • Independent buy/sell entry and exit permission controls provide high flexibility to adapt to different market views
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate trades within the same bar, reducing overtrading risk
  • Complete stop loss and take profit parameters combined with counter-signal exits provide multiple exit protections
  • Recount mechanism ensures signals are not lost on data fetch failures, enhancing data reliability

Weaknesses

  • Lacks dynamic stop loss/take profit adjustment — fixed-point stops may be inflexible when volatility changes
  • No trading session or market volatility filter, potentially generating unfavorable trades during low liquidity or high volatility periods
  • No spread filter, so on high-spread instruments the breakout signal may be consumed by the spread
  • The MM parameter's dual meaning (negative=lot, positive=share) is unintuitive and prone to user misconfiguration
  • Depends on the external custom CorrectedAverage indicator and TradeAlgorithms.mqh library, reducing independence and requiring these dependencies at deployment

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based or volatility-adaptive stop loss/take profit instead of fixed points, enabling dynamic risk management that adjusts to market volatility
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid entries during high spread or low liquidity periods
  • Add a trailing stop feature to lock in floating profits when the trend continues
