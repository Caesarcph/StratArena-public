摘要

该EA基于SuperWoodiesCCI自定义指标进行交易，这是一种双周期CCI变体。当信号CCI缓冲区上穿零轴时开多仓，下穿零轴时开空仓，入场和出场权限均可配置。仓位管理支持固定手数或按存款比例计算。

策略分析

策略概念基于Woodies CCI系统，使用主CCI周期(50)和顺势CCI周期(10)的双CCI组合来判断市场动量方向。入场条件为：当指标缓冲区值从零轴下方上穿至上方时产生买入信号，从零轴上方下穿至下方时产生卖出信号，使用SignalBar参数指定信号获取的K线位置。出场条件包括：反向信号触发平仓（如买入信号时平空仓，卖出信号时平多仓），以及固定的止损(1000点)和止盈(2000点)设置。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示固定手数大小，正值表示存款比例。资金管理依赖TradeAlgorithms.mqh中封装的函数，支持偏差控制和信号时间标记，整体属于典型的动量反转交易系统。

Summary

This EA trades based on the SuperWoodiesCCI custom indicator, which is a dual-period CCI (Commodity Channel Index) variant. It opens buy positions when the signal CCI buffer crosses above zero and sell positions when it crosses below zero, with configurable permissions for entries and exits. Position sizing uses a money management parameter that can represent either a fixed lot size or a percentage of deposit.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy is based on the Woodies CCI system, using a dual CCI combination with a main CCI period (50) and a turbo CCI period (10) to determine market momentum direction. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the indicator buffer value crosses above zero, and a sell signal is generated when it crosses below zero, with the SignalBar parameter specifying which bar to read the signal from. Exit conditions include: reverse signal triggers position closure (e.g., buy signal closes short positions, sell signal closes long positions), plus fixed stop loss (1000 points) and take profit (2000 points). Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the deposit. Money management relies on functions encapsulated in TradeAlgorithms.mqh, supporting slippage control and signal time stamps, making this a typical momentum crossover trading system.

优势

  • 使用双CCI周期确认信号，减少了单CCI的虚假信号
  • 支持灵活的仓位管理（固定手数或按比例），适应不同资金规模
  • 买卖开仓和平仓权限独立控制，可灵活组合策略方向
  • 使用新K线检测机制避免重复信号，减少过度交易

劣势

  • 止损1000点且止盈2000点的固定比例不适合所有市场环境，缺乏动态调整机制
  • 没有点差过滤，在低流动性时段可能因大点差导致不利成交
  • 依赖外部TradeAlgorithms.mqh库文件，核心交易逻辑不透明，难以审计
  • SignalBar=1意味着使用已完成的K线信号，但信号检测逻辑中零轴穿越判断可能存在滞后

改进建议

  • 增加动态止损止盈机制，如基于ATR或波动率调整止损距离
  • 增加点差过滤和时间过滤，避免在点差过大或低流动性时段交易

Strengths

  • Dual CCI period confirmation reduces false signals compared to single CCI
  • Flexible position sizing (fixed lot or percentage-based) accommodates different account sizes
  • Independent buy/sell open and close permission controls allow flexible directional configuration
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate signals and reduces overtrading

Weaknesses

  • Fixed 1000-point SL and 2000-point TP ratio is not suitable for all market conditions, lacking dynamic adjustment
  • No spread filter, which can lead to unfavorable fills during low liquidity sessions
  • Depends on external TradeAlgorithms.mqh library, making core trading logic opaque and hard to audit
  • SignalBar=1 uses completed bar signals, but the zero-line crossover detection may have inherent lag

Improvements

  • Add dynamic stop loss/take profit based on ATR or volatility for adaptive risk management
  • Add spread filter and session time filter to avoid trading during high spread or low liquidity periods
