EA-17909 · Session Trading

Exp_ClosePositionsByTime

This EA is a utility tool that automatically closes all open positions at a specified time of day. It does not open any trades itself but acts as a time-based position manager to ensure no positions are left open past the user-defined closing time.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy concept of this EA is time-based position management with no trade signal generation or entry logic involved. Entry conditions: None; this EA does not execute any entry operations. Exit conditions: When server time reaches the user-defined closing time, it iterates through all open positions and closes them one by one. Position sizing: No dynamic position adjustment; it only executes a full close-all operation at the configured time. Money management: No money management logic is present; it does not involve risk control or lot size calculation. The EA is essentially a scheduled position liquidation tool suitable for traders who need to clear all positions at a specific time, such as before market close or before major news releases.

Strengths

  • Simple and intuitive operation — just set a closing time and it executes automatically
  • Effectively avoids overnight risk or position risk during specific time periods
  • Can be used alongside other trading strategies as a risk managementauxiliary tool

Weaknesses

  • Single-function utility that only supports scheduled closing, lacking more flexible conditional logic
  • Does not account for market liquidity and slippage, potentially resulting in unfavorable fills during low-liquidity periods
  • No logging or notification functionality, making it impossible for users to confirm execution status

Possible improvements

  • Add partial close functionality and a pre-close alert/notification mechanism
  • Include liquidity detection and slippage protection to avoid closing positions during low-liquidity periods

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Safe

  • Scheduled closing may result in actual fill prices deviating from expected due to slippage during extreme market conditions
  • If the closing time is set incorrectly, execution may fail during market-off hours
