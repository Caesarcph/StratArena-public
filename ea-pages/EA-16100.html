摘要

该EA基于RAVI Histogram指标进行交易，通过快慢均线的离散程度判断趋势强度。当RAVI值从下方穿越上限时开多仓，从上方穿越下限时开空仓。反向信号出现时平仓，支持多种资金管理模式包括固定手数。

策略分析

策略核心概念是RAVI（Range Action Verification Index）指标，它计算快速均线与慢速均线之间的百分比差值来衡量趋势强度。入场条件为：当RAVI直方图值高于上限（默认+0.1）且前一根K线的值从下方穿越上限时开多仓；当RAVI值低于下限（默认-0.1）且前一根K线的值从上方穿越下限时开空仓。出场条件为：当出现反向穿越信号时平掉对应仓位，即多仓在RAVI跌破下限时平仓，空仓在RAVI突破上限时平仓。仓位管理使用CXMA平滑算法库，支持SMA、EMA、JJMA、JurX、ParMA、T3、VIDYA、AMA等多种平滑方法。资金管理支持五种模式：按可用保证金比例、按余额比例、按扣除浮亏后的可用保证金比例、按扣除浮亏后的余额比例、以及固定手数模式。EA设有止损（默认1000点）和止盈（默认2000点），并对买卖开平仓操作分别设有开关控制。

Summary

This EA trades based on the RAVI (Range Action Verification Index) Histogram indicator, which measures the divergence between fast and slow moving averages to determine trend strength. It opens buy positions when the RAVI value crosses above the upper level from below, and sell positions when it crosses below the lower level from above. Positions are closed on opposing signals, and money management supports multiple margin modes including fixed lot sizing.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy concept is the RAVI (Range Action Verification Index) indicator, which calculates the percentage difference between fast and slow moving averages to measure trend strength. Entry conditions are: a buy position opens when the RAVI histogram value is above the upper level (default +0.1) and the previous bar's value crossed up through that level; a sell position opens when the RAVI value is below the lower level (default -0.1) and the previous bar's value crossed down through that level. Exit conditions are: positions are closed on opposing crossover signals, meaning long positions close when RAVI drops below the lower level and short positions close when RAVI rises above the upper level. Position sizing leverages the CXMA smoothing algorithm library, supporting multiple smoothing methods including SMA, EMA, JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, and AMA. Money management supports five modes: percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin minus floating loss, percentage of balance minus floating loss, and fixed lot mode. The EA sets a stop loss (default 1000 points) and take profit (default 2000 points), and provides separate enable/disable switches for buy/sell open and close operations.

优势

  • 支持多种高级平滑算法（JJMA、JurX、T3、VIDYA、AMA等），均线计算灵活性强
  • 资金管理模式丰富，提供五种仓位计算方式，适应不同风险偏好
  • 买卖开平仓操作分别独立控制，便于只做单方向交易或仅信号提醒
  • 设有新K线检测机制，避免同一根K线内重复开仓
  • 止损止盈参数完备，默认止盈为止损的两倍，具有正期望收益比的设计思路

劣势

  • 缺乏点差过滤，在高位价差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • 没有持仓数量检查，可能在不同信号周期内重复开仓导致过度集中
  • 指标依赖自定义iCustom调用，若RAVI_Histogram指标文件缺失则EA无法运行
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值偏大，在低波动品种上可能长期无法触发
  • 信号逻辑仅在穿越阈值边界时触发，在横盘震荡市中可能频繁假突破产生亏损

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和最大持仓数量限制，减少不利成交风险和过度集中风险
  • 添加趋势过滤机制（如更高时间周期的ADXR或ATR确认），在横盘市中减少假信号交易
  • 增加移动止损/追踪止盈功能，在趋势行情中锁定更多利润而非仅依赖固定止盈

Strengths

  • Supports multiple advanced smoothing algorithms (JJMA, JurX, T3, VIDYA, AMA), offering high flexibility in moving average calculation
  • Rich money management with five position sizing modes to accommodate different risk preferences
  • Buy/sell open and close operations are independently controllable, enabling directional-only trading or signal-only alerts
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated position opening within the same bar
  • Complete stop loss and take profit parameters with a default 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio suggesting positive expectancy design

Weaknesses

  • No spread filter, which may result in unfavorable fills during high-spread conditions
  • No position count check, potentially leading to over-concentration from repeated entries across signal periods
  • Depends on custom iCustom call for RAVI_Histogram indicator; EA fails to run if the indicator file is missing
  • Default stop loss of 1000 and take profit of 2000 points are large, potentially remaining untriggered on low-volatility instruments
  • Signal logic only triggers on threshold crossovers, prone to frequent false breakout losses in ranging markets

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and maximum position count limits to reduce unfavorable fill risk and over-concentration risk
  • Add a trend filter mechanism (e.g., higher-timeframe ADXR or ATR confirmation) to reduce false signal trades in ranging markets
  • Add trailing stop/breakeven functionality to lock in more profit during trending moves rather than relying solely on fixed take profit
