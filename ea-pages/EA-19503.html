摘要

该EA实现了Larry Connors的RSI-2策略，这是一个著名的均值回归系统。当RSI(2)低于6且价格在200 SMA上方时做多，当RSI(2)高于95且价格在200 SMA下方时做空。当价格穿越5周期SMA时触发出场，提供快速的均值回归退出机制。

策略分析

该策略基于Larry Connors的RSI-2均值回归系统，核心理念是在大趋势方向上捕捉短期超卖/超买的回撤入场机会。入场条件：做多要求RSI(2)低于6（极度超卖）且收盘价位于200周期SMA上方（确认长期上升趋势）；做空要求RSI(2)高于95（极度超买）且收盘价位于200周期SMA下方（确认长期下降趋势）。出场条件：做多持仓时，当收盘价上穿5周期SMA即平仓；做空持仓时，当收盘价下穿5周期SMA即平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数，没有根据账户资金或波动率进行动态调整。资金管理方面提供了止损和止盈选项，默认止损30点、止盈60点，但止盈逻辑与策略的均值回归本质存在冲突——策略原意是依赖SMA穿越出场而非固定止盈。此外，EA没有新K线检查机制，可能在同一根K线内重复触发信号。

Summary

This EA implements Larry Connors' RSI-2 strategy, a well-known mean reversion system. It enters long when RSI(2) is oversold below 6 while price is above the 200 SMA, and enters short when RSI(2) is overbought above 95 while price is below the 200 SMA. Exits are triggered when price crosses above or below the 5-period SMA, providing a quick mean-reversion exit.

Strategy Analysis

This strategy implements Larry Connors' RSI-2 mean reversion system, whose core concept is to capture short-term oversold/overbought pullback entries in the direction of the major trend. Entry conditions: long requires RSI(2) below 6 (extremely oversold) with close above the 200 SMA (confirming long-term uptrend); short requires RSI(2) above 95 (extremely overbought) with close below the 200 SMA (confirming long-term downtrend). Exit conditions: for long positions, close when the close price crosses above the 5-period SMA; for short positions, close when the close price crosses below the 5-period SMA. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size with no dynamic adjustment based on account equity or volatility. Money management provides optional stop loss and take profit with defaults of 30 and 60 pips respectively, though the fixed take-profit logic conflicts with the strategy's mean-reversion nature—Connors' original design relies on SMA crossover exits rather than fixed targets. Additionally, the EA lacks a new-bar check mechanism, risking repeated signal triggers within the same candle.

优势

  • 策略基于Larry Connors的量化研究，有历史回测数据支撑，逻辑清晰可靠
  • 使用200 SMA作为趋势过滤，有效避免逆势交易，提高胜率
  • 出场逻辑基于价格穿越短周期SMA，符合均值回归策略的快速退出原则
  • 提供了止损和止盈选项，有基本的风险控制框架
  • 代码结构清晰，入场和出场规则分离，易于理解和修改

劣势

  • 缺少新K线检查机制，同一根K线内每个tick都可能触发交易信号，导致重复开仓风险
  • 固定手数没有动态仓位管理，无法根据账户规模或市场波动率调整风险敞口
  • 止损/止盈与SMA穿越出场逻辑冲突——Connors原始策略不使用固定止盈，应依赖SMA穿越退出
  • 做空入场时使用currentAsk作为卖出价格而非currentBid，存在逻辑错误
  • 缺少点差过滤，在点差较大时入场可能导致不必要的滑点损失

改进建议

  • 添加新K线检查机制（IsNewBar函数），确保每根K线只执行一次信号判断，避免重复交易
  • 修正做空入场价格错误，将currentAsk改为currentBid作为卖出开仓价格
  • 实现动态仓位管理，基于账户权益百分比或ATR波动率调整手数，而非固定手数
  • 移除或默认关闭固定止盈，让SMA穿越作为主要出场机制，更忠实于Connors原始策略
  • 添加点差和交易时间过滤，避免在点差过大或流动性不足的时段交易

Strengths

  • Strategy is based on Larry Connors' quantitative research with historical backtesting support and clear logic
  • Uses 200 SMA as trend filter, effectively avoiding counter-trend trades and improving win rate
  • Exit logic based on price crossing the short SMA aligns with mean reversion's quick-exit principle
  • Provides stop loss and take profit options with a basic risk control framework
  • Clean code structure with separated entry and exit rules, easy to understand and modify

Weaknesses

  • Missing new-bar check mechanism—every tick within the same candle can trigger trading signals, risking duplicate entries
  • Fixed lot sizing without dynamic position management, unable to adjust risk exposure based on account size or market volatility
  • Stop loss/take profit conflicts with SMA crossover exit logic—Connors' original strategy does not use fixed take-profit, relying on SMA crossover exits instead
  • Sell entry uses currentAsk as the sell price instead of currentBid, which is a logic error
  • No spread filter—entering during high-spread periods may cause unnecessary slippage losses

Improvements

  • Add a new-bar check mechanism (IsNewBar function) to ensure signal evaluation occurs only once per candle, preventing duplicate trades
  • Fix the sell entry price error—change currentAsk to currentBid for sell position opening
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account equity percentage or ATR volatility instead of fixed lots
  • Remove or disable fixed take-profit by default, letting SMA crossover serve as the primary exit mechanism to stay faithful to Connors' original strategy
  • Add spread and trading session filters to avoid trading during high-spread or low-liquidity periods
