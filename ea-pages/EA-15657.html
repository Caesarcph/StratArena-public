摘要

该EA基于TSICloud指标进行交易，该指标使用双重平滑的动量指数(TSI)及可配置的平滑算法。当TSI上线穿越下线时开多仓，反向穿越时开空仓，支持信号反转和独立的反向平仓逻辑。

策略分析

策略概念基于TSI（真实力度指数）云图指标的双线交叉，属于动量类交易系统。入场条件：当上线从下方穿越下线时产生做多信号，当下线从上方穿越上线时产生做空信号，支持信号反转(Invert)功能。出场条件：通过反向信号触发平仓（BuyPosClose/SellPosClose参数控制），同时设有固定点数的止损和止盈。仓位管理支持多种模式：固定手数(LOT)、基于余额比例、基于可用保证金比例等，由MM和MMMode参数控制。资金管理使用TradeAlgorithms.mqh库中的标准化函数，允许0.1的风险比例配置。系统在每个新K线或指定信号柱上检测交叉信号，避免逐tick重复计算，但缺乏加仓和减仓机制。

Summary

This EA trades based on the TSI Cloud indicator, which uses double-smoothed momentum (TSI) with configurable smoothing methods. It opens buy positions when the upper TSI line crosses above the lower line and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with optional signal inversion and separate close-on-reversal logic.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the crossover of two TSI (True Strength Index) Cloud indicator lines, classifying it as a momentum-based trading system. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the upper line crosses above the lower line, and a sell signal on the opposite crossover, with an optional signal inversion (Invert) feature that swaps the buffer readings. Exit conditions are triggered by reversal signals controlled by BuyPosClose and SellPosClose parameters, along with fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels in points. Position sizing supports multiple modes including fixed lot size (LOT), balance-based percentage, and free margin-based percentage, governed by the MM and MMMode input parameters. Money management leverages standardized functions from the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library with a default risk ratio of 0.1. The system checks for crossover signals on each new bar or at a specified signal bar index, preventing per-tick redundant calculations, but it lacks scaling in/out or partial position management mechanisms.

优势

  • 支持10种平滑算法（SMA/EMA/JJMA/JurX/T3等），灵活适应不同市场条件
  • 独立的入场和出场开关设计，可以只开仓不平仓或只平仓不开仓，策略组合灵活
  • 使用IsNewBar()机制避免逐tick重复计算信号，降低计算负担和重复交易风险
  • 信号反转功能(Invert)方便在逆势策略中使用同一指标逻辑
  • 标准化的资金管理模块支持多种仓位计算模式

劣势

  • 缺乏点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生不利入场
  • 止损和止盈为固定点数，未考虑市场波动率(ATR)动态调整
  • 没有最大持仓数量限制，可能在震荡行情中连续开仓导致过度暴露
  • 依赖外部自定义指标TSICloud和两个include库，增加了部署复杂性和依赖风险
  • 信号仅在K线完成时检测，在快速行情中可能延迟入场

改进建议

  • 增加ATR动态止损止盈和点差过滤机制，提升在不同波动率环境下的适应性
  • 添加最大同时持仓数量限制和冷却期机制，防止震荡行情中的连续亏损开仓
  • 增加移动止损(Trailing Stop)功能，在趋势行情中锁定浮动利润

Strengths

  • Supports 10 smoothing algorithms (SMA/EMA/JJMA/JurX/T3 etc.), flexibly adapting to different market conditions
  • Independent entry and exit switches allow fine-grained control — can open-only, close-only, or both
  • Uses IsNewBar() mechanism to avoid per-tick redundant signal calculations, reducing computational load and repeat trade risk
  • Signal inversion feature (Invert) enables convenient use of the same indicator logic for counter-trend strategies
  • Standardized money management module supports multiple position sizing modes

Weaknesses

  • Lacks spread filtering, which may result in unfavorable entries in high-spread environments
  • Stop-loss and take-profit use fixed point values without ATR-based dynamic adjustment for volatility
  • No maximum position count limit, potentially leading to overexposure in choppy markets with consecutive entries
  • Depends on external custom indicator TSICloud and two include libraries, increasing deployment complexity and dependency risk
  • Signals are only detected on bar completion, which may delay entries during fast market moves

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss/take-profit and spread filtering to improve adaptability across volatility regimes
  • Implement maximum simultaneous position limits and cooldown periods to prevent consecutive losing entries in choppy markets
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in floating profits during trending markets
