摘要

该EA在指定交易时段内，按照用户设定的距离从当前价格放置挂单（买入止损和卖出止损）。订单自动设置止盈和止损水平，未成交的挂单在时段结束时自动删除。这是一个简单的基于时间的突破交易系统，依赖活跃市场时段内价格从开盘区间脱离的运动。

策略分析

策略概念基于时段突破交易，在用户指定的时间窗口内，从当前价格上下各放置一个止损挂单（买入止损和卖出止损），预期价格会朝某个方向突破。入场条件是当价格触及挂单价格时自动成交，买入止损挂在当前价格上方指定点数，卖出止损挂在当前价格下方指定点数。出场条件包括止盈和止损，用户可以为买入和卖出分别设置不同的止盈止损点数，同时时段结束时未成交的挂单会被自动删除。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，由用户通过输入参数直接设定，没有动态仓位调整或基于资金的计算逻辑。资金管理方面较为简单，没有最大持仓限制、没有回撤控制、没有资金百分比计算，完全依赖用户手动设定合理的参数。整体策略逻辑清晰但风险控制依赖于用户对参数的正确设置。

Summary

This EA places pending buy and sell stop orders at user-defined distances from the current price during specified trading sessions. Orders are automatically managed with take-profit and stop-loss levels, and any unfilled orders are deleted at the end of the session. It is a straightforward time-based breakout trading system that relies on price movement away from the opening range during active market hours.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on session breakout trading, placing a buy stop and a sell stop pending order at user-specified distances above and below the current price during a defined time window, anticipating a directional breakout. Entry conditions are triggered automatically when price reaches the pending order price, with the buy stop placed above and the sell stop placed below the current price by a configurable number of points. Exit conditions consist of separate take-profit and stop-loss levels for buy and sell orders, both configurable by the user, while unfilled pending orders are automatically deleted at the end of the trading session. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size approach, with the user specifying the lot value directly through an input parameter, and there is no dynamic lot sizing or equity-based calculation. Money management is rudimentary, lacking maximum position limits, drawdown controls, or percentage-of-equity calculations, relying entirely on the user to set sensible parameters. The overall strategy logic is clear and straightforward, but risk control is heavily dependent on the user configuring appropriate parameter values.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和配置，适合初学者使用
  • 基于时段交易，可以避开低流动性时段，减少滑点风险
  • 买卖方向对称挂单，不预判方向，让市场自行选择突破方向
  • 未成交挂单自动删除，避免非交易时段意外成交
  • 止盈止损分别可设，灵活性较好

劣势

  • 无动态仓位管理，固定手数不考虑账户资金变化，存在过度交易风险
  • 缺少移动止损功能，盈利单可能因回撤而转为亏损
  • 无最大持仓或最大亏损限制，多单同时成交时风险不可控
  • 没有新闻事件过滤器，重大数据发布时可能产生剧烈波动导致止损被穿
  • 缺少点差过滤，高点差时段入场成本过高可能导致策略失效

改进建议

  • 增加基于账户余额或风险的动态仓位计算功能，替代固定手数
  • 添加移动止损（追踪止损）功能，保护已有盈利
  • 增加点差过滤和最大持仓数量限制，提升风险控制能力

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and configure, suitable for beginners
  • Session-based trading avoids low-liquidity periods and reduces slippage risk
  • Symmetric buy/sell pending orders do not predict direction, letting the market choose the breakout direction
  • Unfilled pending orders are automatically deleted, preventing accidental fills outside trading hours
  • Separate configurable TP/SL for buy and sell provides good flexibility

Weaknesses

  • No dynamic position sizing; fixed lots ignore account equity changes, posing overtrading risk
  • Lacks trailing stop functionality; winning trades may reverse into losses due to price retracement
  • No maximum position or loss limits; risk becomes uncontrolled when multiple orders fill simultaneously
  • No news event filter; major data releases can cause extreme volatility that blows through stop losses
  • Lacks spread filter; high-spread periods increase entry costs and may invalidate the strategy

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account equity or risk percentage to replace fixed lot size
  • Implement a trailing stop feature to protect accumulated profits on winning trades
  • Add spread filtering and maximum open position limits to improve risk management
