Strategy Analysis

This EA is based on a moving average crossover strategy, which is a classic trend-following approach. Entry conditions are a golden cross (fast MA crossing above slow MA) for buy signals and a death cross (fast MA crossing below slow MA) for sell signals, triggered at the moment of crossover. Exit conditions typically rely on reverse crossover signals or fixed stop-loss/take-profit levels, with some versions potentially including trailing stop functionality. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach with a small default lot size, lacking dynamic adjustment based on account balance. Money management is relatively simple, with no dynamic risk ratio calculation or equity curve protection logic. The overall strategy logic is clear and easy to understand, but it tends to generate frequent false signals in ranging markets, leading to consecutive losses.
