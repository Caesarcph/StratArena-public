摘要

该EA实现了经典的马丁格尔策略，在每次亏损后将手数翻倍，盈利后则重置为初始手数。它基于简单的方向信号开仓，并依赖概率假设认为最终会出现盈利交易来回收所有前期亏损。

策略分析

策略核心概念是经典的马丁格尔倍投法：每次亏损后加倍下注，期望一次盈利能覆盖之前所有亏损。入场条件通常基于简单的趋势判断或固定方向信号，没有复杂的指标组合。出场条件为达到盈利目标时平仓并重置手数，亏损时则不平仓而是加倍开仓反向或同向订单。仓位管理采用几何级数递增（1x, 2x, 4x, 8x...），在连续亏损时手数增长极快。资金管理方面几乎没有保护机制，没有最大亏损限制或最大手数上限。该策略的最大风险在于连续亏损时账户资金指数级消耗，可能导致爆仓。整体而言，这是一种高风险的赌博式策略，不具备长期稳定盈利的能力。

Summary

This EA implements a classic Martingale strategy that doubles the lot size after each losing trade while resetting to the initial lot size after a win. It opens positions based on a simple directional signal and relies on the probabilistic assumption that a winning trade will eventually occur to recover all previous losses.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept is the classic Martingale doubling method: doubling the bet after each loss, expecting a single win to recover all previous losses. Entry conditions are typically based on simple directional signals or fixed direction assumptions without complex indicator combinations. Exit conditions trigger when a profit target is reached, closing the position and resetting the lot size; on losses, instead of closing, the EA doubles the lot size and opens a new position. Position sizing follows a geometric progression (1x, 2x, 4x, 8x...), causing lot sizes to grow extremely fast during consecutive losses. Money management has virtually no protective mechanisms, lacking maximum loss limits or maximum lot size caps. The greatest risk is that consecutive losses cause exponential depletion of account funds, potentially leading to a margin call or account blowout. Overall, this is a high-risk gambling-style strategy that lacks the capability for long-term stable profitability.

优势

  • 逻辑简单直观，易于理解和实现
  • 在震荡行情中短期内可能实现稳定小额盈利
  • 盈利后重置手数机制可锁定部分利润

劣势

  • 连续亏损时手数指数级增长，极易导致爆仓
  • 没有止损机制或最大亏损限制，资金管理极度缺失
  • 不适用于趋势行情，单边行情会造成毁灭性损失
  • 策略本质是赌博而非投资，长期数学期望为负

改进建议

  • 添加最大手数和最大连续亏损次数限制，防止爆仓
  • 增加止损机制，在达到最大亏损时强制平仓并重置
  • 结合技术指标判断入场时机，避免在强趋势中逆势加仓

Strengths

  • Simple and intuitive logic, easy to understand and implement
  • May achieve stable small profits in the short term during ranging markets
  • Lot size reset after profit can lock in partial gains

Weaknesses

  • Lot size grows exponentially during consecutive losses, extremely prone to account blowout
  • No stop-loss mechanism or maximum loss limit, money management is severely lacking
  • Not suitable for trending markets, one-sided moves cause catastrophic losses
  • Strategy is essentially gambling rather than investing, with negative long-term mathematical expectation

Improvements

  • Add maximum lot size and maximum consecutive loss count limits to prevent account blowout
  • Implement stop-loss mechanism to force close and reset when maximum loss is reached
  • Incorporate technical indicators for entry timing to avoid adding positions against strong trends
