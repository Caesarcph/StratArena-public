Exp_WPR_Histogram

This EA trades based on the WPR (Williams' Percent Range) Histogram indicator, opening buy positions when the histogram shows bullish momentum above the high level and sell positions when it shows bearish momentum below the low level. It uses a custom WPR_Histogram indicator with configurable overbought/oversold levels and signal bar selection, combined with money management modes for position sizing. Trade exits are triggered by opposing signals from the histogram.

Strategy analysis

This strategy trades momentum based on a histogram variant of the Williams' Percent Range (WPR) indicator. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the histogram color buffer (buffer 2) equals 0 and the histogram value (buffer 0) is greater than 0, indicating bullish momentum above the overbought threshold; a sell signal is generated when buffer 2 equals 2 and the histogram value is less than 2, indicating bearish momentum below the oversold threshold. Exit conditions: an opposing signal triggers position closure — a buy signal closes sell positions (SELL_Close=true) and a sell signal closes buy positions (BUY_Close=true), implementing a signal-reversal exit model. Position sizing is controlled via the MM parameter and MMMode enumeration, supporting five modes: free margin-based, balance-based, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. Money management features stop loss and take profit defaults of 1000 and 2000 points respectively, providing substantial room for price fluctuation. The strategy uses the SignalBar parameter to determine which bar to read signals from, defaulting to the previous closed bar (value=1), which avoids signal repaint issues from unclosed bars.

Strengths

  • Uses closed bar signal (SignalBar=1) to avoid signal repainting, improving signal reliability
  • Provides complete buy/sell open and close toggle switches with high flexibility for directional control
  • Built-in five money management modes to accommodate different risk preferences and account sizes
  • Includes new bar detection mechanism (IsNewBar) to prevent repeated entries within the same bar
  • Complete stop loss and take profit parameters providing a basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • No spread filter, which can lead to unfavorable entry prices during high spread conditions, especially impactful for short-term trading
  • Lacks position checking logic, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction when one already exists
  • Stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large, potentially preventing timely exits in low-volatility markets
  • Depends on custom WPR_Histogram indicator — if the indicator file is missing, the EA fails with insufficient error handling
  • No trailing stop functionality to lock in floating profits after favorable price movement

Possible improvements

  • Add a spread filter to pause new entries when spread exceeds a configurable threshold, avoiding unfavorable slippage
  • Add position checking logic before opening trades to confirm no existing same-direction positions, preventing duplicate entries
  • Implement a trailing stop feature to automatically adjust stop loss when price moves favorably, locking in profits

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No spread filter — widened spreads during high volatility periods can cause unnecessary losing entries
  • No position check — potentially building excessive exposure by opening duplicate same-direction positions
  • Fixed stop loss and take profit in points, not dynamically adjusted based on market volatility (ATR)
