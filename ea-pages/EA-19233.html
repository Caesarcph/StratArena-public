MACD Stochastic

This EA combines MACD and Stochastic oscillator signals to generate trade entries, requiring both indicators to confirm momentum direction before opening positions. It uses MACD for trend/momentum direction and Stochastic for overbought/oversold timing, creating a dual-confirmation trading system. The EA opens buy trades when MACD is bullish and Stochastic is oversold, and sell trades when MACD is bearish and Stochastic is overbought.

Strategy analysis

This EA employs a dual-indicator momentum confirmation strategy combining MACD and Stochastic oscillator, where the core concept uses MACD to identify medium-term trend direction and Stochastic to find entry timing in overbought/oversold zones. Entry conditions require: for buy signals, MACD main line above signal line (or positive histogram) combined with Stochastic crossing up from oversold territory; for sell signals, MACD main line below signal line (or negative histogram) combined with Stochastic crossing down from overbought territory. Exit conditions may include fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels or indicator reversal-based exits. Position sizing typically relies on a user-configured lot size parameter, with some versions potentially including simple account-balance-based dynamic lot calculation. Money management is relatively basic, depending on fixed lot sizes or simple proportional sizing without advanced mechanisms like Kelly criterion or risk-percentage position control. The overall strategy logic is clear, but dual-indicator confirmation may result in fewer entry signals, and the system can generate frequent false signals in choppy, range-bound markets.

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator confirmation reduces false signals and improves entry quality
  • MACD and Stochastic complement each other well — one identifies trend, the other identifies timing
  • Highly configurable parameters allow users to optimize indicator settings for different instruments and timeframes
  • Strategy logic is simple and intuitive, making it easy to understand and backtest

Weaknesses

  • Dual-indicator confirmation may result in scarce entry signals, missing some market opportunities
  • MACD can frequently flip in choppy markets, generating false trend judgments
  • Lacks advanced money management — fixed lot sizing cannot adapt to varying risk environments
  • Stochastic may stay in overbought/oversold zones for extended periods during strong trends, causing delayed signals

Possible improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., ADX or higher-timeframe moving average) to reduce false signals in choppy markets
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account risk percentage instead of fixed lot sizes
  • Add spread filtering and session control to avoid trading during high-cost and low-liquidity periods

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Stop-loss parameter defaults to 0 — if not set by user, positions have no protection and face unlimited loss risk
  • No spread filter mechanism — entries may occur under unfavorable high-spread conditions
  • No explicit new bar check logic — may open multiple positions within the same bar
