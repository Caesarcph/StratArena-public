摘要

Two PerBar是一个趋势跟踪型EA，每个K线最多产生两个交易信号，利用基于移动平均线的趋势检测来确定交易方向。当价格动量与其指标计算识别出的主导趋势一致时开仓。该EA采用系统化固定手数交易，并实现基本的止损止盈来管理仓位。

策略分析

策略概念基于每根K线最多产生两个交易信号的趋势跟踪系统。入场条件依赖于移动平均线交叉或趋势方向判断，当短期均线与长期均线关系确认趋势方向时入场做多或做空。出场条件通过预设的止损和止盈水平来实现，当价格触及目标或止损时平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，没有根据账户余额或风险比例进行动态调整。资金管理相对简单，没有包含复杂的资金管理算法或风险控制机制，主要依赖止损来控制单笔风险。

Summary

Two PerBar is a trend-following Expert Advisor that generates up to two trade signals per bar, using moving average-based trend detection to determine trade direction. It opens positions when price momentum aligns with the prevailing trend identified by its indicator calculations. The EA operates systematically with fixed lot sizing and implements basic stop loss and take profit for trade management.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a trend-following system that generates at most two trade signals per bar. Entry conditions rely on moving average crossover or trend direction determination, entering long or short when the relationship between short-term and long-term moving averages confirms the trend direction. Exit conditions are implemented through preset stop loss and take profit levels, closing positions when price reaches the target or stop. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic adjustment based on account balance or risk ratio. Money management is relatively simple, lacking complex money management algorithms or risk control mechanisms, primarily relying on stop losses to control per-trade risk. The two-signals-per-bar limit helps prevent overtrading while still allowing the system to capture intra-bar opportunities.

优势

  • 每根K线限制交易次数，有效防止过度交易
  • 使用趋势跟踪逻辑，顺势交易胜率较高
  • 具有明确的止损止盈设置，风险可控
  • 中文优势4

劣势

  • 固定手数缺乏动态仓位管理，无法根据市场波动调整
  • 趋势跟踪策略在震荡市中容易产生频繁亏损信号
  • 缺乏移动止损或保本机制，盈利可能大幅回吐
  • 缺少交易时间过滤，可能在低流动性时段交易

改进建议

  • 增加动态仓位管理，根据ATR或账户风险比例调整手数
  • 添加移动止损和保本功能以锁定浮盈
  • 增加震荡市过滤器，在区间市场中减少或暂停交易

Strengths

  • Per-bar trade limit effectively prevents overtrading
  • Trend-following logic trades with the prevailing direction for better win rate
  • Clear stop loss and take profit settings provide defined risk management
  • Simple and straightforward logic makes the EA easy to understand and modify

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing lacks dynamic position management unable to adjust to market volatility
  • Trend-following strategy tends to generate frequent losing signals in ranging markets
  • Lacks trailing stop or breakeven mechanism so profits can be significantly given back
  • No trading time filter may lead to trading during low liquidity sessions

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on ATR or account risk percentage
  • Implement trailing stop and breakeven functionality to lock in floating profits
  • Add range market filter to reduce or pause trading during sideways markets
