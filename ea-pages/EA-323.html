摘要

该EA使用MFI（市场促进指数）指标配合移动平均线平滑作为核心信号过滤器，基于MQL5标准库Expert框架构建。通过CMS_ES_MFI过滤器的阈值穿越信号来开仓和平仓，采用固定手数且无追踪止损。策略依赖MFI-MA组合检测的动量变化来产生交易决策。

策略分析

策略概念基于市场促进指数（MFI）与其移动平均线的关系来判断市场动量变化。入场条件为当CMS_ES_MFI滤波器的信号值超过开仓阈值（默认40）时开仓，信号值低于平仓阈值（默认20）时平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，默认为0.1手，不根据账户资金动态调整。资金管理使用MoneyFixedLot模块，设定10%的百分比参数但实际采用固定手数交易。出场条件仅依赖信号阈值平仓，未设置止损和止盈水平，也未使用追踪止损。挂单有效期为4根K线周期，整体策略缺乏主动的风险控制机制。

Summary

This EA uses the MFI (Market Facilitation Index) indicator smoothed by a Moving Average as its core signal filter within the MQL5 Standard Library Expert framework. It opens and closes positions based on threshold-crossing signals from the CMS_ES_MFI filter, with fixed lot sizing and no trailing stop. The strategy relies on momentum shifts detected by the MFI-MA combination to generate trade decisions.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the relationship between the Market Facilitation Index (MFI) and its Moving Average to detect momentum shifts in the market. Entry conditions trigger when the CMS_ES_MFI filter signal exceeds the open threshold (default 40), and exit conditions trigger when the signal falls below the close threshold (default 20). Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach with a default of 0.1 lots, without dynamic adjustment based on account equity. Money management employs the MoneyFixedLot module with a 10% percent parameter, but effectively trades with a fixed lot size. Exit conditions rely solely on the signal threshold for closing positions, with no stop loss or take profit levels configured, and no trailing stop is applied. Pending orders expire after 4 bars, and the overall strategy lacks proactive risk control mechanisms such as stop losses or trailing protection.

优势

  • 基于标准库Expert框架构建，代码结构清晰规范，易于维护和扩展
  • 使用MFI动量指标配合MA平滑，能有效过滤短期噪音信号
  • 信号开仓和平仓阈值分离设计，允许灵活控制进出场的敏感度

劣势

  • 未设置止损和止盈水平，存在严重的资金安全风险
  • 固定手数交易缺乏自适应仓位调整能力，无法根据市场波动性优化风险
  • 无追踪止损机制，盈利仓位无法锁定浮盈，可能大幅回撤
  • 仅使用单一信号过滤器，缺乏多维度确认，容易产生虚假信号

改进建议

  • 添加止损和止盈参数，为每笔交易设置硬性风险边界
  • 引入多时间框架确认或多指标过滤，减少虚假信号导致的亏损交易
  • 将固定手数改为动态仓位管理，根据ATR或账户风险比例调整手数

Strengths

  • Built on the MQL5 Standard Library Expert framework with clean, well-structured code that is easy to maintain and extend
  • Uses MFI momentum indicator smoothed by MA, which effectively filters short-term noise signals
  • Separate open and close threshold design allows flexible control over entry and exit sensitivity

Weaknesses

  • No stop loss or take profit levels configured, presenting a serious capital safety risk
  • Fixed lot sizing lacks adaptive position adjustment, unable to optimize risk based on market volatility
  • No trailing stop mechanism means profitable positions cannot lock in gains, potentially leading to significant drawdowns
  • Uses only a single signal filter without multi-dimensional confirmation, prone to false signals

Improvements

  • Add stop loss and take profit parameters to set hard risk boundaries for every trade
  • Introduce multi-timeframe confirmation or additional indicator filters to reduce losses from false signals
  • Replace fixed lot sizing with dynamic position management based on ATR or account risk percentage
