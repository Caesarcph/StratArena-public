摘要

ArbSint是一个统计套利EA，通过交易合成货币对与其理论价值之间的偏差来获利。它识别合成符号组合与实际市场价格之间的定价低效，当价差偏差超过阈值时入场，当价差回归均值时出场。

策略分析

ArbSint策略的核心概念是统计套利，即利用合成货币对（由两个或多个符号按比例组合而成）与实际交易品种之间的价差偏离进行交易。入场条件为当合成价格与目标品种价格之间的偏差超过设定的阈值时，EA会开仓做多或做空价差，预期价差将回归历史均值。出场条件则是当价差回归到均值或达到止盈/止损水平时平仓。仓位管理方面，EA根据偏差幅度和预设的风险参数来确定开仓手数，可能采用固定手数或基于波动率的动态手数。资金管理上，EA通常会设置止损以控制单笔交易的最大亏损，并可能包含最大持仓数量限制和最大回撤保护。该策略依赖于价差的均值回归特性，因此在市场结构发生根本性变化时可能面临持续亏损的风险。

Summary

ArbSint is a statistical arbitrage EA that trades synthetic pair deviations from their theoretical values. It identifies pricing inefficiencies between a synthetic combination of symbols and the actual market price, entering trades when the spread deviation exceeds a threshold and exiting when the spread reverts to mean.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of the ArbSint strategy is statistical arbitrage, exploiting price deviations between a synthetic pair (constructed from a proportional combination of two or more symbols) and the actual traded instrument. Entry conditions trigger when the spread deviation between the synthetic price and the target symbol exceeds a predefined threshold, at which point the EA opens a position betting on mean reversion of the spread. Exit conditions are met when the spread reverts to its historical mean or when take-profit/stop-loss levels are hit. Position sizing is determined based on the deviation magnitude and preset risk parameters, potentially using fixed lots or volatility-based dynamic sizing. Money management incorporates stop-losses to cap maximum loss per trade, and may include maximum position limits and drawdown protection. The strategy inherently relies on the mean-reverting property of the spread, making it vulnerable to sustained losses when market structure undergoes fundamental shifts that break historical correlation relationships.

优势

  • 统计套利策略具有市场中性特性，不依赖单边趋势方向
  • 利用价差均值回归逻辑，在正常市场条件下胜率较高
  • 同时交易多个相关品种，分散了单一品种的风险敞口
  • 通过合成价格与实际价格的对比，能够捕捉定价低效带来的套利机会

劣势

  • 价差的均值回归假设在市场结构突变时可能失效，导致持续亏损
  • 套利利润空间通常较小，交易成本（点差、佣金、滑点）对盈利影响较大
  • 相关性破裂时两个方向都可能亏损，对冲保护可能不足
  • 对执行速度和延迟敏感，在高延迟环境下套利窗口可能已关闭

改进建议

  • 增加动态偏差阈值，根据近期价差波动率自适应调整开仓条件
  • 引入相关性监控机制，当品种间相关系数低于安全阈值时暂停交易

Strengths

  • Statistical arbitrage strategy has market-neutral properties, not dependent on directional trends
  • Mean reversion logic on spreads provides relatively high win rates under normal market conditions
  • Trading multiple correlated symbols simultaneously diversifies single-instrument risk exposure
  • Comparing synthetic vs actual prices captures pricing inefficiencies for arbitrage opportunities

Weaknesses

  • Mean reversion assumption on spreads may fail during market structure shifts, leading to sustained losses
  • Arbitrage profit margins are typically thin, making trading costs (spread, commission, slippage) highly impactful
  • Both legs can lose when correlation breaks down, providing insufficient hedging protection
  • Sensitive to execution speed and latency; arbitrage windows may close before orders are filled in high-latency environments

Improvements

  • Add dynamic deviation thresholds that adapt entry conditions based on recent spread volatility
  • Introduce correlation monitoring that pauses trading when inter-symbol correlation drops below a safety threshold
