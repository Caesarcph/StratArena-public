摘要

ArbSint是一个统计套利EA，利用相关或协整工具之间的价格偏离进行交易。当价差偏离超过阈值时开立对冲仓位，当价差回归均值时平仓。该策略本质上是市场中性的，旨在从相对价值收敛中获利而非方向性走势。

策略分析

ArbSint策略的核心概念是统计套利，基于两个或多个相关金融工具之间的价差偏离和均值回归特性。入场条件是当两个相关品种的价差（或合成价格差）偏离其统计均值达到预设阈值时，同时在两个品种上开立方向相反的仓位，做多被低估的一方、做空被高估的一方。出场条件是当价差回归到均值或达到设定的目标利润时，同时平掉两个品种的仓位，实现套利收益。仓位管理通常采用等值配对方式，确保两边仓位的市场敞口基本对冲。资金管理方面，EA可能根据价差偏离程度和账户风险限额调整仓位大小，但统计套利策略本身的风险相对可控，因为始终持有多空对冲仓位。需注意的关键风险是协整关系可能破裂，导致两个品种的价格持续背离而不回归，造成双重亏损。

Summary

ArbSint is a statistical arbitrage EA that exploits price divergence between correlated or cointegrated instruments. It opens opposing positions when the spread deviates beyond a threshold and closes them when the spread reverts to the mean. The strategy is inherently market-neutral, aiming to profit from relative value convergence rather than directional movement.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of ArbSint is statistical arbitrage, based on the spread divergence and mean-reversion properties between two or more correlated financial instruments. Entry conditions trigger when the spread (or synthetic price difference) between two correlated symbols deviates beyond a predefined threshold from its statistical mean, simultaneously opening opposing positions—going long on the undervalued instrument and short on the overvalued one. Exit conditions are met when the spread reverts to its mean or reaches a specified target profit, at which point both positions are closed simultaneously to capture the arbitrage profit. Position sizing typically employs equal-value pairing to ensure the market exposure on both legs is essentially hedged. Money management adjusts lot sizes based on the degree of spread deviation and account risk limits, though statistical arbitrage inherently carries relatively controlled risk due to the always-hedged long-short positioning. A key risk to monitor is the potential breakdown of the cointegration relationship, which could cause the two instruments' prices to diverge persistently without reverting, resulting in losses on both legs.

优势

  • 市场中性策略，不受单边行情影响，降低了方向性风险
  • 统计套利基于可量化的均值回归原理，逻辑清晰且有数理支撑
  • 对冲持仓结构限制了最大亏损敞口，风险相对可控
  • 可在震荡市和趋势市中均有获利机会，策略适应性较强
  • 利用品种间相关性的结构性偏离，发现传统技术分析难以捕捉的机会

劣势

  • 协整关系可能在市场结构性变化时破裂，导致双腿同时亏损
  • 统计参数（均值、标准差）依赖历史数据，在非平稳市场中可能失效
  • 需要两个品种同时具有流动性和低点差，交易成本可能侵蚀套利利润
  • 价差回归时间不确定，可能长时间持仓占用保证金
  • 过度依赖历史相关性假设，黑天鹅事件中相关性可能趋近于1导致对冲失效

改进建议

  • 增加协整检验（如ADF检验）动态监控，当协整破裂时自动暂停交易
  • 加入自适应参数调整机制，根据市场波动率动态调整偏离阈值和仓位大小

Strengths

  • Market-neutral strategy immune to directional market risk, reducing exposure to trend bias
  • Statistical arbitrage grounded in quantifiable mean-reversion principles with clear mathematical justification
  • Hedged position structure limits maximum loss exposure, keeping risk relatively contained
  • Capable of generating profits in both ranging and trending markets, offering strong adaptability
  • Exploits structural divergence between correlated instruments, capturing opportunities invisible to traditional technical analysis

Weaknesses

  • Cointegration relationship may break down during structural market shifts, causing losses on both legs simultaneously
  • Statistical parameters (mean, standard deviation) rely on historical data and may fail in non-stationary markets
  • Requires both instruments to have sufficient liquidity and low spreads, as transaction costs can erode arbitrage profits
  • Spread reversion timing is uncertain, potentially tying up margin in prolonged positions
  • Over-reliance on historical correlation assumptions; during black swan events correlations may converge toward 1, rendering hedges ineffective

Improvements

  • Add dynamic cointegration testing (e.g., ADF test) to automatically pause trading when the cointegration relationship breaks down
  • Implement adaptive parameter adjustment to dynamically calibrate deviation thresholds and position sizes based on prevailing market volatility

  • 持仓时间风险：价差回归时间不确定，长期持仓占用大量保证金并增加隔夜风险

  • Cointegration breakdown risk: spread between two instruments may persistently diverge without reverting, causing hedged positions to lose on both legs
  • Holding period risk: uncertain spread reversion timing may tie up significant margin and increase overnight exposure
