摘要

该EA基于快速和慢速简单移动平均线之间的背离进行交易。当背离处于正阈值范围内时开多仓，当背离处于负阈值范围内时开空仓，并配备可配置的止损、止盈、移动止损和保本功能。

策略分析

策略核心是利用两条不同周期的SMA均线之间的背离度来捕捉动量变化。入场条件：当快速MA与慢速MA的差值减去前一期差值的变化量（即背离度）处于DVBuySell到DVStayOut之间时开多仓，当背离度处于负DVStayOut到负DVBuySell之间时开空仓。出场条件：止损和止盈以点数设定，同时支持移动止损和保本止损功能，但信号平仓函数始终返回false，意味着没有基于信号的主动平仓逻辑，完全依赖止损止盈和移动止损出场。仓位管理支持固定手数交易，可通过MultyOpen参数允许加仓，并设置MaxVolume限制最大持仓量。资金管理方面提供了账户级别的篮子止盈和止损功能，当整个账户的盈利或亏损达到设定阈值时关闭所有仓位。整体策略缺乏信号驱动的平仓机制，且背离度计算方式较为简单，未考虑价格与指标的背离。

Summary

This EA trades based on the divergence between a fast and slow Simple Moving Average. It opens buy positions when divergence is within a positive threshold range and sell positions when divergence is within a negative threshold range, with configurable stop loss, take profit, trailing stop, and breakeven functionality.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core uses divergence between two SMA lines of different periods to capture momentum shifts. Entry conditions: a buy is opened when the divergence measure falls between DVBuySell and DVStayOut, and a sell is opened when it falls between negative DVStayOut and negative DVBuySell. The divergence is calculated as the current MA spread minus the change in MA spread from the previous bar. Exit conditions: stop loss and take profit are set in points at order opening, with optional trailing stop and breakeven functionality; however, both signal-based close functions always return false, meaning there is no signal-driven exit logic and the EA relies entirely on SL/TP and trailing for exits. Position sizing uses fixed lots with an optional MultyOpen feature that allows adding to positions up to a MaxVolume cap. Money management includes account-level basket profit and loss closing, which closes all positions across all symbols when the account reaches a specified profit or loss threshold. The strategy lacks signal-driven closing and the divergence calculation is simplistic, not accounting for price-indicator divergence in the traditional sense.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于均线背离度的动量捕捉思路有理论依据
  • 具备完整的止损止盈、移动止损和保本止损等风险控制功能
  • 提供账户级别的篮子止盈止损，能有效控制整体账户风险
  • 加仓功能设有最大持仓量限制，防止过度杠杆
  • 使用标准库CTrade等类进行交易操作，代码规范性好

劣势

  • 信号平仓函数始终返回false，缺乏基于策略信号的主动平仓机制
  • 背离度计算并非传统意义上的价格与指标背离，仅是均线差值的变化量
  • DVBuySell和DVStayOut参数值非常小，需要针对不同品种反复优化，泛化能力差
  • SolveLots函数固定返回输入手数，没有动态仓位计算能力
  • 篮子止损关闭所有品种仓位，可能误伤其他策略的持仓

改进建议

  • 实现基于背离度反转的信号平仓逻辑，而非仅依赖止损止盈出场
  • 增加动态仓位计算功能，根据账户余额和风险比例自动调整手数
  • 将背离度阈值参数改为自适应或基于ATD/ATR的动态值，提高跨品种适应性

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic with theoretical basis for momentum capture via MA divergence
  • Complete risk control with stop loss, take profit, trailing stop, and breakeven functionality
  • Account-level basket profit/loss closing effectively controls overall account risk
  • MultyOpen feature includes MaxVolume cap to prevent excessive leverage
  • Uses standard library CTrade classes for trade operations, following good coding conventions

Weaknesses

  • Signal close functions always return false, lacking any signal-driven active exit mechanism
  • Divergence calculation is not traditional price-indicator divergence, merely the change in MA spread
  • DVBuySell and DVStayOut values are very small and require per-instrument optimization with poor generalization
  • SolveLots function returns a fixed input lot with no dynamic position sizing capability
  • Basket loss closing affects all symbols, potentially closing positions from other strategies

Improvements

  • Implement signal-based closing logic based on divergence reversal instead of relying solely on SL/TP exits
  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account balance and risk ratio instead of fixed lots
  • Make divergence thresholds adaptive or based on ATR for better cross-instrument compatibility

风险点

  • 缺乏信号平仓机制，完全依赖止损止盈，在剧烈行情中可能滑点较大
  • 篮子止损功能关闭所有品种仓位，可能影响其他策略
  • 背离度阈值固定且数值极小，在不同波动率环境下可能失效

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • No signal-based closing mechanism means full reliance on SL/TP, which can suffer significant slippage in volatile markets
  • Basket loss closing affects all positions across all symbols, potentially disrupting other EAs
  • Fixed divergence thresholds with very small values may fail under different volatility regimes
