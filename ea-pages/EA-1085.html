摘要

该EA基于快速和慢速简单移动均线之间的背离进行交易。当背离值在正值定义范围内时开多仓，在负值定义范围内时开空仓。包含移动止损、保本止损、账户整体盈亏平仓以及加仓功能。

策略分析

策略概念基于两条不同周期的SMA（默认7和88）之间的背离值，通过计算当前背离与上一期背离的变化率来判断市场动能的衰减，属于均值回归逻辑。入场条件为：买入信号要求背离值在DVBuySell(0.0011)到DVStayOut(0.0079)之间，卖出信号要求背离值在负DVStayOut到负DVBuySell之间，即只在中度背离时入场，过度背离时观望。出场条件依赖固定止损止盈（默认550点），另支持移动止损和保本止损功能，但SignalCloseBuy和SignalCloseSell始终返回false，缺少主动平仓信号。仓位管理使用固定手数（默认0.1手），MultyOpen允许加仓但受MaxVolume（0.5手）限制且仅允许同方向加仓。资金管理方面有账户整体盈亏平仓功能（BasketProfit/BasketLoss），可在达到指定盈利或亏损时关闭所有持仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on the divergence between a fast and slow Simple Moving Average. It opens buy positions when divergence is positive within a defined range and sell positions when divergence is negative within a mirrored range. It includes trailing stop, breakeven, basket profit/loss closing, and multi-lot position scaling features.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the divergence between two SMAs of different periods (default 7 and 88), calculating the change rate of current divergence versus the previous bar's divergence to detect momentum deceleration, which follows a mean-reversion logic. Entry conditions: buy signals require divergence between DVBuySell (0.0011) and DVStayOut (0.0079), sell signals require divergence between negative DVStayOut and negative DVBuySell, meaning positions are only opened at moderate divergence levels while excessive divergence triggers a stay-out zone. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit (default 550 points each), with optional trailing stop and breakeven functionality, but SignalCloseBuy and SignalCloseSell always return false, lacking active closing signals based on strategy logic. Position sizing uses fixed lots (default 0.1), with MultyOpen allowing additional positions in the same direction capped by MaxVolume (0.5 lots). Money management includes basket profit/loss closing functionality that can close all account positions when a specified profit or loss threshold is reached.

优势

  • 背离区间设计合理，设有观望区间（DVStayOut），避免在极端背离时盲目入场
  • 包含完整的止损止盈检查机制，确保订单符合交易所最小止损距离要求
  • 账户整体盈亏保护功能可防止极端行情下的过度亏损或锁定整体利润
  • 代码结构清晰，函数分工明确，易于理解和修改
  • 使用MQL5标准库类（CTrade、CSymbolInfo等），符合现代MQL5编程规范

劣势

  • SignalCloseBuy和SignalCloseSell始终返回false，缺少基于策略逻辑的主动平仓信号，完全依赖止损止盈出场
  • 背离计算方式不够直观，dv1-dv2的公式实际等价于maF2[0]-maS2[0]，即上一期的快慢均线差值，可能非作者本意
  • 固定手数没有根据账户余额或风险比例动态调整，缺乏动态仓位管理
  • 没有点差过滤，在高点差环境下频繁信号可能导致不必要的亏损
  • SolveBuySL和SolveBuyTP使用Sym.Ask()计算，但在OnTick中可能已过时，应使用Sym.RefreshRates()后的最新值

改进建议

  • 实现基于背离反转的主动平仓信号，例如当背离从正值转为负值时平掉多仓
  • 修正Divergence()函数的计算逻辑，使其真正反映背离的变化率，并添加注释说明计算含义
  • 添加动态仓位管理，根据账户余额和风险比例自动计算手数
  • 增加点差过滤器，在点差过大时暂停开仓以避免滑点损失

Strengths

  • Well-designed divergence zone with stay-out threshold (DVStayOut) prevents entering during extreme divergence conditions
  • Complete stop loss and take profit validation ensures orders comply with exchange minimum stop level requirements
  • Basket profit/loss protection prevents excessive drawdown and locks in overall account profits during extreme market conditions
  • Clean code structure with clear function separation makes the EA easy to understand and modify
  • Uses MQL5 standard library classes (CTrade, CSymbolInfo, etc.) following modern MQL5 programming conventions

Weaknesses

  • SignalCloseBuy and SignalCloseSell always return false, lacking strategy-based active closing signals and relying entirely on stop loss/take profit for exits
  • Divergence calculation is unintuitive — dv1-dv2 simplifies to maF2[0]-maS2[0] (previous bar's fast-slow MA difference), which may not be the author's intended logic
  • Fixed lot sizing without dynamic adjustment based on account balance or risk percentage lacks proper position management
  • No spread filter means frequent signals in high-spread environments can lead to unnecessary losses
  • SolveBuySL and SolveBuyTP use Sym.Ask() which may be stale if called after Sym.RefreshRates() timing is not synchronized

Improvements

  • Implement strategy-based active closing signals, such as closing buy positions when divergence reverses from positive to negative
  • Fix the Divergence() function calculation logic so it truly reflects the rate of divergence change, and add comments explaining the calculation
  • Add dynamic position sizing that automatically calculates lot size based on account balance and risk percentage
  • Add a spread filter to pause opening new positions when the spread is too wide, avoiding slippage losses
