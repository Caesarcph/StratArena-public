Momentum-M15

This EA trades on the M15 timeframe using momentum-based indicators to identify trend strength and direction. It enters positions when momentum signals align with trend direction and exits based on momentum reversal or fixed take-profit/stop-loss levels. The strategy is fully automated with no discretionary input required.

Strategy analysis

This EA trades based on a momentum strategy, with the core concept of entering positions in the direction of market momentum. Entry conditions typically rely on momentum indicators (such as RSI, MACD, or CCI) crossing key thresholds or producing directional signals, potentially combined with moving averages to confirm trend direction. Exit conditions include momentum reversal signals, fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, and optionally a trailing stop mechanism. Position sizing employs either fixed lots or an account-balance-proportional approach. Money management is handled through stop-loss parameters controlling maximum loss per trade, though the actual risk exposure depends on user-configured settings. The overall strategy logic is clear and suitable for trending markets, but may generate frequent false signals and consecutive losses in range-bound or choppy conditions.

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and optimize
  • Performs well in strong trending markets, capable of capturing medium-to-long-term trend moves
  • Provides stop-loss and trailing stop functionality with basic risk control capability

Weaknesses

  • Prone to frequent false signals and consecutive losses in range-bound or choppy markets
  • Lacks multi-timeframe confirmation, relying solely on M15 which may misjudge trend direction
  • Position sizing is simplistic, lacking dynamic lot adjustment or volatility-adaptive mechanisms

Possible improvements

  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation such as H4 trend direction combined with M15 entry signals to improve signal reliability
  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit that adapt to market volatility, avoiding the limitation of fixed pip values across different volatility regimes

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Single timeframe trend judgment without multi-period verification carries misjudgment risk
  • Fixed pip stop-loss and take-profit may be inappropriate in high or low volatility markets, leading to premature stops or insufficient profit capture
