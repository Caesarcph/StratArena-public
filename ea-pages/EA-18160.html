EA-18160 · Trend Following

Exp_Kolier_SuperTrend_X2

This EA implements a dual SuperTrend indicator strategy for trend-following trading. It uses two SuperTrend indicators with different periods/multipliers to generate entry signals when both align in the same direction, and exits when either SuperTrend flips direction. The system applies the ATR-based SuperTrend logic to capture medium-to-long-term trends while filtering out noise through the double confirmation mechanism.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on a dual SuperTrend indicator trend-following system where two SuperTrend indicators with different periods and multipliers are used simultaneously. Entry conditions require both SuperTrend indicators to align in the same direction — a long signal is generated when both turn bullish, and a short signal when both turn bearish, providing stronger trend confirmation and filtering out false breakouts in choppy markets. Exit conditions trigger when either SuperTrend indicator flips direction, which ensures early exit when a trend reversal may be forming. Position sizing supports both fixed lot size and equity-based dynamic lot calculation. Money management includes stop loss configuration, though the SuperTrend's inherent ATR-based mechanism already provides dynamic stop loss functionality. The overall strategy logic is clear, and the dual confirmation mechanism effectively reduces false signals, though it may cause delayed entries at the beginning of new trends.

Strengths

  • Dual SuperTrend confirmation effectively filters false signals and improves entry quality
  • ATR-based dynamic stop loss adapts to market volatility changes, self-adjusting across different market conditions
  • Clear and concise strategy logic with highly adjustable parameters, easy to optimize for different instruments

Weaknesses

  • Dual confirmation causes delayed entry at trend inception, potentially missing the initial portion of trend profit
  • May still generate frequent false signals in ranging/choppy markets leading to consecutive losses
  • Lacks advanced money management modules such as maximum drawdown control and daily loss limits

Possible improvements

  • Add a trend strength filter (e.g., ADX) to avoid frequent entries during weak trend or ranging conditions
  • Implement trailing take-profit and partial close mechanisms to better lock in trend profits

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Default stop loss and take profit parameters are 0, lack of hard stop protection may lead to excessive single-trade losses
  • No maximum position or maximum drawdown control mechanism, risk exposure is uncontrolled during extreme market conditions
