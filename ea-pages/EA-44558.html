MartingaleEA-5 Levels

This EA implements a 5-level martingale strategy that adds to losing positions at predefined distance intervals with multiplied volume. It opens additional buy/sell positions when price moves against the initial trade by specified pip distances, doubling (or multiplying) the lot size each level. All positions of the same direction are closed when total profit or loss reaches a threshold.

Strategy analysis

The strategy is based on the classic martingale principle: doubling down after losses in hopes of recovering all losses with one winning trade. Entry conditions: when an existing buy (or sell) position is in loss and price has moved against it by cumulative distance thresholds (e.g., Level 1 at 300 pips, Level 2 at 700 pips, etc.), a new same-direction position is opened with volume multiplied by the MartingaleVolumeMultiplier. Exit conditions: all positions of the same direction are closed when total profit reaches MartingaleTakeProfit ($200) or total loss hits MartingaleStopLoss (-$500), after which counters and volumes reset. Position sizing follows exponential growth — each level multiplies the lot by the configured factor (default 2x), reaching 32x the initial volume by level 5. Money management relies on the total-loss threshold, but this stop-loss can be overwhelmed by slippage and the exponentially growing exposure across levels. A critical flaw exists in the counting logic — BCount and SCount are never reset between ticks properly, and the EA iterates all positions every tick without new-bar checks, risking duplicate entries.

Strengths

  • Simple and intuitive code structure that makes the martingale logic easy to understand
  • 5-level progressively increasing distance intervals give the market room to retrace before next entry
  • Total profit/loss threshold closing mechanism provides a basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • BCount/SCount are never reset between ticks in OnTick, causing martingale level counting to malfunction after first cycle
  • No new-bar detection — iterates all positions every tick, risking duplicate orders or unexpected behavior
  • VOL variable is never assigned a value; BMartingaleVol/SMartingaleVol initialize to 0, which may cause zero-volume trades or logic failures
  • Martingale strategy inherently carries blow-up risk — at 2x multiplier over 5 levels, final level is 32x initial size with exponential loss potential
  • Using Point() for distance calculation doesn't account for symbol digit differences, producing incorrect distances on certain instruments

Possible improvements

  • Add new-bar detection (IsNewBar) to prevent trade logic from firing every tick
  • Fix VOL initialization — add an input parameter for initial lot size and properly initialize counting variables in OnInit
  • Refactor the 5 repeated if-blocks into a loop structure for better maintainability and extensibility

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Dangerous

  • Martingale exponential position sizing can blow up the account in extreme market conditions
  • Uninitialized VOL variable may cause zero-volume trades or unpredictable behavior
  • BCount/SCount not reset in OnTick, making re-entry logic after closing completely broken
  • Stop-loss threshold of -$500 is likely insufficient for 5-level 2x martingale — actual max loss can far exceed this
