摘要

The MasterMind 2 是一个基于马丁格尔策略的EA，在每次亏损后加倍仓位试图回本。它基于简单的动量或趋势信号入场，依赖马丁格尔加仓来实现净盈利。该EA缺乏有效的止损保护，在连续亏损期间存在账户爆仓的极高风险。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是马丁格尔加仓法：初始以固定手数开仓，若交易亏损则下一次交易手数翻倍，以此类推直到盈利后重置为初始手数。入场条件通常基于简单的技术指标信号（如移动平均线交叉或价格突破），但并非策略的重点——重点在于资金管理。出场条件为盈利达到预设目标时平仓，亏损时不设止损而是依赖加仓回本。仓位管理采用经典的倍增模式（1→2→4→8→16...），在连续亏损时手数呈指数级增长。资金管理方面，没有最大加仓次数限制，也没有账户权益保护机制。该策略在理论上可以盈利，前提是交易者拥有无限资金且市场没有极端波动，但在实际交易中，连续亏损很快就会导致保证金不足而爆仓。

Summary

The MasterMind 2 is a Martingale-based Expert Advisor that doubles lot size after each losing trade in an attempt to recover losses. It enters positions based on simple momentum or trend signals and relies on the Martingale progression to achieve net profitability. The EA lacks meaningful stop-loss protection and carries extreme risk of account blowout during extended losing streaks.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is the Martingale position sizing method: it opens an initial position with a fixed lot size, and after each losing trade, the lot size is doubled in an attempt to recover previous losses. Entry conditions are typically based on simple technical indicator signals such as moving average crossovers or price breakouts, but the entry logic is secondary to the money management approach. Exit conditions trigger when a profit target is reached, at which point the lot size resets to the initial value; losing trades have no stop-loss and instead rely on the next doubled-position trade to recover. Position sizing follows the classic doubling pattern (1→2→4→8→16...), causing lot sizes to grow exponentially during consecutive losing streaks. Money management lacks a maximum number of Martingale steps and has no account equity protection mechanism. The strategy is theoretically profitable only if the trader has unlimited capital and markets avoid extreme moves, but in practice, a moderate losing streak quickly depletes margin and blows the account.

优势

  • 逻辑简单直观，易于理解和部署
  • 在短期震荡行情中可能实现稳定的小额盈利
  • 盈利后重置手数，单次盈利即可覆盖前期所有亏损（理论上）

劣势

  • 马丁格尔策略在连续亏损时手数呈指数增长，极易爆仓
  • 不设止损意味着单笔亏损可能无限扩大
  • 没有最大加仓次数限制，缺乏资金安全网
  • 对账户资金要求极高，小额账户无法承受多步加仓
  • 在趋势行情中可能持续逆势加仓导致巨额亏损

改进建议

  • 增加最大马丁格尔步数限制，防止无限加仓
  • 添加硬止损作为最后的资金保护线
  • 加入账户权益百分比保护，当浮亏超过账户权益一定比例时强制平仓

Strengths

  • Simple and intuitive logic, easy to understand and deploy
  • May achieve steady small profits in ranging markets over the short term
  • A single winning trade at a doubled lot size can recover all previous losses (theoretically)

Weaknesses

  • Martingale lot sizing grows exponentially during losing streaks,极易 causing account blowout
  • No stop-loss means a single position's loss can grow indefinitely
  • No maximum Martingale step limit, lacking a capital safety net
  • Requires extremely large account balance; small accounts cannot sustain multiple doubling steps
  • May keep adding positions against the trend during trending markets, leading to massive losses

Improvements

  • Add a maximum Martingale step limit to prevent infinite position doubling
  • Implement a hard stop-loss as a last-resort capital protection line
  • Add account equity percentage protection that forces closure when floating loss exceeds a threshold of account equity
