摘要

该EA基于时区支点水平进行交易，当价格突破或跌破由可配置时区会话计算的支点支撑和阻力位时开仓。它采用开放系统逻辑并带有跟踪止损修改（Tm Plus）功能，旨在捕捉支点突破和反转形态的日内波动。

策略分析

策略核心基于时区支点计算，根据用户指定时区的开盘价、最高价、最低价和收盘价计算经典支点水平（PP、S1-S3、R1-R3）。入场条件为价格突破阻力位做多或跌破支撑位做空，属于支点突破与反转结合的策略。出场条件包括达到止盈目标、触发止损或通过Tm Plus跟踪止损机制动态平仓。仓位管理通过手数参数控制，支持固定手数或基于资金比例的下单方式。资金管理方面，EA依赖支点位的统计有效性进行交易，但未见明显的动态仓位调整或风险比例控制机制，止盈止损设置相对静态。

Summary

This EA trades based on time-zone pivot point levels, opening positions when price breaks above or below pivot-derived support and resistance levels calculated from a configurable time zone session. It incorporates an open system logic with trailing modification (Tm Plus) for dynamic stop management, aiming to capture intraday moves from pivot breakout and reversal setups.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is based on time-zone pivot point calculation, computing classic pivot levels (PP, S1-S3, R1-R3) from the open, high, low, and close prices of a user-specified time zone session. Entry conditions trigger long positions when price breaks above resistance levels and short positions when price breaks below support levels, combining pivot breakout and reversal logic. Exit conditions include reaching take-profit targets, hitting stop-loss levels, or dynamic closure through the Tm Plus trailing stop modification mechanism. Position sizing is controlled via lot size parameters, supporting fixed lot or balance-proportional ordering. Money management relies on the statistical validity of pivot levels for trade decisions, but lacks obvious dynamic position adjustment or risk-proportion control mechanisms, with relatively static take-profit and stop-loss settings.

优势

  • 时区支点计算使策略适应不同市场的交易时段，具有较强的灵活性
  • 支点水平基于前一日价格数据计算，具有统计学上的可靠性和市场共识性
  • Tm Plus跟踪止损机制有助于锁定浮动利润，减少回吐风险

劣势

  • 止损和止盈默认值为0，若不手动设置则交易无保护，风险极高
  • 支点策略在震荡市中容易产生频繁假突破信号导致连续亏损
  • 缺乏动态仓位管理和资金比例控制，无法根据账户风险自动调整下注规模

改进建议

  • 增加动态止损止盈计算，根据ATR或支点间距自动设置合理的止损止盈水平
  • 添加趋势过滤器（如均线方向）避免在逆势方向交易支点突破信号

Strengths

  • Time-zone pivot calculation adapts the strategy to different market trading sessions with strong flexibility
  • Pivot levels computed from previous day price data offer statistical reliability and market consensus
  • The Tm Plus trailing stop mechanism helps lock in floating profits and reduces give-back risk

Weaknesses

  • Default stop-loss and take-profit values are 0, meaning trades have no protection if not manually set, posing extreme risk
  • Pivot strategies are prone to frequent false breakout signals in ranging markets leading to consecutive losses
  • Lacks dynamic position sizing and risk-proportion control, unable to automatically adjust bet size based on account risk

Improvements

  • Add dynamic stop-loss and take-profit calculation using ATR or pivot spacing to automatically set reasonable SL/TP levels
  • Add a trend filter (e.g., moving average direction) to avoid trading pivot breakout signals against the prevailing trend
