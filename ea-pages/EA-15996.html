摘要

该EA基于SMI Correct指标进行交易，SMI Correct是随机动量指数的一种多层级平滑变体。当SMI主线从下方穿越信号线时生成买入信号，反向穿越时生成卖出信号，并支持可配置的仓位管理和止损止盈管理。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略基于SMI Correct指标的主线与信号线交叉。入场条件为：当指标主线从下方穿越信号线时开多仓，从上方穿越信号线时开空仓。出场条件包括止损和止盈，分别由StopLoss_和TakeProfit_参数控制，同时支持基于信号反向交叉的平仓。仓位管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，并支持多种资金管理模式（可用保证金、余额、亏损后保证金、固定手数）。指标采用三重平滑（EMA、JJMA等），第一层平滑处理极值周期数据，第二层平滑主线，第三层平滑信号线。策略还使用了新K线判断机制（IsNewBar），避免在同一根K线内重复交易。

Summary

This EA trades based on the SMI Correct indicator, which is a Stochastic Momentum Index variant with multi-layer smoothing. It generates buy signals when the SMI main line crosses above the signal line and sell signals on the reverse crossover, with configurable position sizing and stop loss/take profit management.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the crossover of the SMI Correct indicator's main line and signal line. Entry conditions: a buy position opens when the main line crosses above the signal line, and a sell position opens when the main line crosses below the signal line. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit controlled by StopLoss_ and TakeProfit_ parameters, along with signal-based closing when the crossover reverses direction. Position sizing is managed through the MM parameter which controls the risk percentage, supporting multiple margin modes including free margin, balance, loss-adjusted margin, and fixed lot size. The indicator employs triple smoothing (EMA, JJMA, etc.), where the first layer smooths the extremum period data, the second smooths the main line, and the third smooths the signal line. The strategy also uses a new bar detection mechanism (IsNewBar) to prevent repeated trading within the same bar.

优势

  • 采用三重平滑的SMI指标，信号较为平滑，减少虚假交叉
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，灵活性高
  • 使用新K线判断机制，避免同根K线重复开仓
  • 买卖开关独立控制，可以只做多或只做空
  • 止损止盈均可配置，风险控制有基本保障

劣势

  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能不利
  • 没有仓位检查机制，可能重复开仓
  • 指标依赖自定义外部文件SMI_Correct，部署复杂
  • 没有追踪止损机制，盈利保护不足
  • SMI参数众多，优化困难且容易过拟合

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤机制，避免在高点差时开仓
  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，防止同一方向重复开仓
  • 增加追踪止损功能，更好地保护浮动盈利

Strengths

  • Triple-smoothed SMI indicator produces cleaner signals with fewer false crossovers
  • Multiple money management modes provide high flexibility in position sizing
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated entries within the same bar
  • Independent buy/sell enable switches allow directional-only trading
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit provide basic risk management

Weaknesses

  • No spread filter, which may be detrimental in high-spread environments
  • No position check before opening, potentially leading to duplicate positions
  • Depends on custom external indicator file SMI_Correct, making deployment complex
  • No trailing stop mechanism, insufficient profit protection
  • Numerous SMI parameters make optimization difficult and prone to overfitting

Improvements

  • Add spread filtering to avoid opening positions during high-spread conditions
  • Add position checking logic to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Add trailing stop functionality for better protection of floating profits
