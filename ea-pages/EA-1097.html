摘要

该EA基于自定义Arrows_Curves指标的交易信号进行买卖操作，买入箭头出现时开多仓，卖出箭头出现时开空仓。支持可配置的资金管理、止损止盈以及独立的入场和出场权限控制。策略还会遍历历史K线寻找止损信号，在没有直接平仓信号时关闭现有持仓。

策略分析

策略概念基于Arrows_Curves自定义指标的箭头信号进行趋势跟踪交易。入场条件为：当指标缓冲区1（Buy缓冲区）在指定信号K线上产生非空值时开多仓，当缓冲区0（Sell缓冲区）产生非空值时开空仓。出场条件分为两种：一是反向信号触发平仓（买入信号平空仓，卖出信号平多仓），二是遍历历史K线检查止损缓冲区（缓冲区2为SellStop，缓冲区3为BuyStop）是否产生止损信号来决定平仓。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示固定手数，正值表示按存款比例计算手数。资金管理采用固定止损止盈点数方式，止损默认1000点，止盈默认2000点，盈亏比为2:1。策略包含新K线检测机制，避免同一K线内重复交易，同时设置偏差参数控制滑点容忍度。

Summary

This EA trades based on signals from the custom Arrows_Curves indicator, opening buy positions on buy arrows and sell positions on sell arrows. It supports configurable money management, stop loss/take profit, and separate permissions for entry and exit. The strategy also searches historical bars for stop signals to close existing positions when no direct close signal is generated.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is trend-following based on arrow signals generated by the custom Arrows_Curves indicator. Entry conditions: a buy position opens when indicator buffer 1 (Buy buffer) produces a non-empty, non-zero value on the specified signal bar, and a sell position opens when buffer 0 (Sell buffer) produces such a value. Exit conditions are twofold: first, opposite signals trigger position closes (buy signal closes shorts, sell signal closes longs); second, the EA iterates through historical bars checking stop buffers (buffer 2 for SellStop, buffer 3 for BuyStop) to find stop signals for closing positions when no direct close signal is present. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the deposit per deal. Money management uses fixed stop loss and take profit in points, with a default SL of 1000 points and TP of 2000 points, yielding a 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio. The strategy includes a new bar detection mechanism to prevent repeated trades within the same bar and a deviation parameter to control slippage tolerance.

优势

  • 盈亏比设计合理（2:1），有利于长期正期望值
  • 买卖开仓和平仓权限独立控制，灵活性高
  • 具有新K线检测机制，避免同一K线重复交易
  • 支持存款比例和固定手数两种仓位管理模式
  • 止损和止盈参数可配置，风险控制明确

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标Arrows_Curves，指标逻辑不透明，无法独立评估信号质量
  • 历史K线遍历搜索止损信号可能效率低下，在大量历史数据时存在性能风险
  • 缺乏点差过滤机制，在高点差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • SignalBar=1使用已完成K线信号，但止损信号搜索循环中重新读取Buy/Sell缓冲区存在逻辑冗余
  • 没有最大持仓数量限制和最大回撤保护机制

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和最大持仓数量限制，提升风险控制能力
  • 优化止损信号搜索逻辑，避免在循环中重复读取相同缓冲区数据，提升性能和代码清晰度

Strengths

  • Reasonable reward-to-risk ratio of 2:1, supporting long-term positive expectancy
  • Independent permission controls for buy/sell opening and closing provide high flexibility
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate trades within the same bar
  • Supports both deposit share and fixed lot position sizing modes
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit parameters provide clear risk control

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom Arrows_Curves indicator whose internal logic is opaque, making signal quality impossible to evaluate independently
  • Historical bar iteration for stop signal search may be inefficient with large datasets, posing performance risks
  • No spread filter mechanism, which could lead to unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • Re-reading Buy/Sell buffers inside the stop signal search loop is logically redundant and wastes resources
  • No maximum position count limit or maximum drawdown protection mechanism

Improvements

  • Add spread filtering and maximum position count limits to enhance risk management capabilities
  • Optimize the stop signal search logic by avoiding redundant buffer reads inside the loop, improving both performance and code clarity
