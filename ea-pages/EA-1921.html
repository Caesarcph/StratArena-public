摘要

该EA基于简单移动平均线(SMA)交叉信号进行交易。当K线开盘价在MA下方且收盘价在MA上方时开多仓，反之开空仓。仓位根据账户可用保证金和最大风险比例动态计算，并在连续亏损后缩减仓位。

策略分析

策略概念是基于价格与简单移动平均线(SMA)的交叉关系来判断趋势方向并跟随趋势交易。入场条件为：当一根K线的开盘价低于MA且收盘价高于MA时产生买入信号，当开盘价高于MA且收盘价低于MA时产生卖出信号，这实际上是一种单K线穿越MA的突破入场方式。出场条件采用对称逻辑：持有多仓时若出现卖出信号则平仓，持有空仓时若出现买入信号则平仓，本质上是一个反转策略——平仓即反向开仓。仓位管理采用基于账户可用保证金的比例风险模型，根据MaximumRisk参数计算初始手数，并使用DecreaseFactor在连续亏损后递减仓位以控制回撤。资金管理方面，手数经过步长规范化处理并受最小/最大手数限制，但策略未设置止损和止盈，完全依赖反向信号平仓，风险较大。

Summary

This EA trades based on a simple moving average (SMA) crossover signal. It opens a buy position when the bar opens below and closes above the MA, and opens a sell when the bar opens above and closes below the MA. Position sizing is dynamically calculated based on account free margin and a maximum risk percentage, with lot reduction after consecutive losses.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the crossover relationship between price and a Simple Moving Average (SMA) to determine trend direction and follow the trend. Entry conditions are: a buy signal is generated when a bar opens below the MA and closes above it, and a sell signal is generated when a bar opens above the MA and closes below it — effectively a single-bar MA breakout entry. Exit conditions use symmetric logic: a long position is closed when a sell signal appears, and a short position is closed when a buy signal appears, making this essentially a reversal strategy where closing and opening are coupled. Position sizing uses a percentage-risk model based on account free margin with the MaximumRisk parameter, and the DecreaseFactor reduces lot size after consecutive losses to control drawdown. Money management normalizes lot size to the broker's volume step and enforces min/max volume limits, but the strategy has no explicit stop-loss or take-profit, relying entirely on reverse signals for exits, which poses significant risk.

优势

  • 仓位管理考虑了连续亏损递减机制，有助于控制回撤
  • 使用账户可用保证金比例计算手数，风险与账户规模动态匹配
  • 仅在新区块首根Tick交易，避免重复信号和过度交易
  • 代码结构清晰简洁，易于理解和修改

劣势

  • 没有设置止损和止盈，完全依赖反向信号平仓，极端行情下可能造成巨大亏损
  • 单根K线穿越MA的信号过于简单，容易产生频繁的假突破和虚假信号
  • DecreaseFactor递减逻辑在losses>1时才生效且除以DecreaseFactor而非指数递减，递减力度有限
  • 没有考虑点差和滑点过滤，在波动较小或点差较大时可能亏损入场
  • OnDeinit函数未释放指标句柄，存在资源泄漏

改进建议

  • 添加明确的止损和止盈设置，至少应有止损保护以防止极端亏损
  • 增加点差过滤和多周期确认机制，减少虚假信号和不利条件下的入场
  • 在OnDeinit中调用IndicatorRelease释放指标句柄，避免资源泄漏
  • 考虑添加移动止损(Trailing Stop)来锁定盈利而非完全依赖反向信号平仓

Strengths

  • Position sizing includes a consecutive-loss reduction mechanism that helps control drawdown
  • Lot size is dynamically calculated as a percentage of free margin, scaling risk with account size
  • Trades only on the first tick of a new bar, preventing duplicate signals and overtrading
  • Clean and concise code structure that is easy to understand and modify

Weaknesses

  • No stop-loss or take-profit is set, relying entirely on reverse signals for exits, which can lead to catastrophic losses in extreme markets
  • The single-bar MA crossover signal is overly simplistic and prone to frequent false breakouts and whipsaws
  • The DecreaseFactor reduction only activates when losses>1 and uses linear division rather than exponential reduction, limiting its effectiveness
  • No spread or slippage filter, potentially entering losing trades when spreads are wide or volatility is low
  • OnDeinit does not release the indicator handle (IndicatorRelease), causing a resource leak

Improvements

  • Add explicit stop-loss and take-profit levels, at minimum a stop-loss to protect against extreme losses
  • Add spread filtering and multi-timeframe confirmation to reduce false signals and avoid entry under unfavorable conditions
  • Call IndicatorRelease in OnDeinit to properly release the indicator handle and prevent resource leaks
  • Consider adding a trailing stop to lock in profits rather than relying entirely on reverse signal exits
