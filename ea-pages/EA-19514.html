摘要

该EA实现了基于马丁格尔策略的交易系统，在每次亏损后加倍仓位大小并保持相同方向。它基于方向性偏见入场，并通过马丁格尔递进方式试图用一次盈利交易来恢复所有损失。由于指数级仓位递增，该策略具有极高的风险。

策略分析

策略概念基于经典马丁格尔原理：亏损后加倍下注，期望一次盈利覆盖所有前期亏损。入场条件通常是固定方向或简单信号触发首次开仓，之后每次止损后以加倍手数同方向再次入场。出场条件依赖于价格回撤至整体持仓的加权平均成本之上实现盈利平仓，即所有同向持仓的总体浮亏转为浮盈时全部平仓。仓位管理采用固定倍率递增（通常为2倍），首次手数由用户参数设定，后续手数为前一次的倍数。资金管理方面，该策略没有硬性的最大手数或最大层数限制，完全依赖账户余额来支撑递增的保证金需求，在连续亏损情况下极易爆仓。整体而言，该策略在震荡行情中可能表现稳定，但在单边趋势中面临毁灭性风险。

Summary

This EA implements a Martingale-based trading strategy that doubles lot size after each losing trade while maintaining the same direction. It enters positions based on a directional bias and uses the Martingale progression to recover losses through a single winning trade. The strategy carries significant risk due to exponential position sizing.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the classic Martingale principle: double the bet after each loss, expecting a single win to recover all previous losses. Entry conditions typically involve a fixed directional bias or simple signal for the initial trade, followed by doubling lot size in the same direction after each stop loss. Exit conditions rely on price retracing to a level where the weighted average cost of all positions becomes profitable, closing all positions when the net floating profit turns positive across the entire basket. Position sizing uses a fixed multiplier progression (typically 2x), with the initial lot size set by user parameters and subsequent lots being a multiple of the previous. Money management lacks hard caps on maximum lot size or maximum number of layers, relying entirely on account balance to sustain increasing margin requirements, making it extremely vulnerable to margin calls during consecutive losses. The strategy may perform stably in ranging markets but faces catastrophic risk in strong trending conditions where price fails to retrace sufficiently.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中回撤较小，盈利概率较高
  • 逻辑简单直观，易于理解和调参
  • 一次盈利可覆盖多次亏损，心理上具有恢复感

劣势

  • 指数级仓位增长在连续亏损时可导致爆仓
  • 缺乏最大层数或最大手数硬限制
  • 单边趋势行情中极度危险，可能快速耗尽全部资金

改进建议

  • 增加最大马丁层数限制和最大手数上限，防止无限递增
  • 加入趋势过滤器，在强趋势中暂停开仓或反转方向
  • 加入资金管理模块，根据账户余额动态调整初始手数和最大层数

Strengths

  • Performs well in ranging markets with small drawdowns and high win rate
  • Simple and intuitive logic that is easy to understand and configure
  • A single winning trade can recover multiple consecutive losses, providing psychological recovery

Weaknesses

  • Exponential lot size growth can lead to margin call during consecutive losses
  • Lacks hard limits on maximum layers or maximum lot size
  • Extremely dangerous in trending markets, potentially depleting entire account rapidly

Improvements

  • Add maximum Martingale layer limit and maximum lot size cap to prevent infinite progression
  • Incorporate a trend filter to pause or reverse direction during strong trends
  • Add a money management module that dynamically adjusts initial lot size and maximum layers based on account equity
