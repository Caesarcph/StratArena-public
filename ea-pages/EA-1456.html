摘要

该EA基于TMA（三角移动平均线）通道突破进行交易。当价格收盘价超过上轨指定点数时开多仓，低于下轨指定点数时开空仓，配有可配置的止损和止盈。

策略分析

策略核心是基于TMA通道的突破交易。入场条件为：当最新收盘价高于TMA值加上上轨突破点数时产生买入信号，且前一根K线的收盘价未超过该阈值（确认突破刚刚发生）；卖出信号同理，收盘价低于TMA值减去下轨突破点数时触发。出场条件包括止损和止盈的固定点数设置，以及反向信号平仓——买入信号触发时平空仓，卖出信号触发时平多仓。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示固定手数，正值表示按存款比例计算。策略使用多周期框架，指标在H4周期计算而交易在当前周期执行，通过IsNewBar机制确保每个K线只计算一次信号，避免重复入场。

Summary

This EA trades breakouts from the TMA (Triangular Moving Average) channel. It opens buy positions when price closes above the upper band by a specified level and sell positions when price closes below the lower band, with configurable stop loss and take profit.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy is centered on TMA channel breakout trading. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the latest close exceeds the TMA value plus the upper breakout level in points, and the previous bar's close was at or below that threshold (confirming a fresh breakout); sell signals are symmetric, triggered when close falls below TMA value minus the lower breakout level. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit in points, plus counter-signal exits: long positions are closed on sell signals and short positions are closed on buy signals. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter: negative values represent fixed lot sizes while positive values represent a share of the deposit. The strategy operates on a multi-timeframe basis with the indicator calculated on H4 while trading executes on the current chart period. The IsNewBar mechanism ensures signals are calculated only once per bar, preventing duplicate entries.

优势

  • 使用新K线检测机制避免重复信号，逻辑清晰
  • 止损止盈参数完整，风险可控
  • 买卖和平仓权限独立控制，灵活性强
  • 突破确认逻辑（前一K线未突破）减少假信号
  • 多周期框架设计，指标与交易周期分离

劣势

  • 没有扩展点差过滤，突破交易在新闻时段容易受滑点影响
  • TMA指标使用iCustom调用，依赖外部自定义指标文件，部署不便
  • 止损1000点相对较大，在小周期上可能风险偏高
  • 没有持仓检查和最大持仓限制，可能在趋势行情中过度加仓
  • 反向信号平仓与止损止盈可能冲突，没有优先级处理逻辑

改进建议

  • 添加点差过滤和最大持仓数量限制，降低极端行情风险
  • 将TMA指标逻辑内嵌到EA中，消除对外部自定义指标文件的依赖
  • 增加移动止损（Trailing Stop）功能，在趋势行情中锁定利润

Strengths

  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate signals with clear logic
  • Complete stop loss and take profit parameters for controllable risk
  • Independent permission controls for buy/sell open and close provide flexibility
  • Breakout confirmation logic (previous bar not yet broken) reduces false signals
  • Multi-timeframe design separates indicator and trading periods

Weaknesses

  • No spread filter; breakout trades are vulnerable to slippage during news events
  • TMA indicator uses iCustom call, depending on external custom indicator file, making deployment inconvenient
  • 1000-point stop loss is relatively large and may pose high risk on smaller timeframes
  • No position check or maximum position limit; may over-add positions in trending markets
  • Counter-signal close and stop loss/take profit may conflict without priority handling logic

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and maximum position count limit to reduce extreme market risk
  • Embed TMA indicator logic inside the EA to eliminate dependency on external custom indicator files
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in profits during trending moves
