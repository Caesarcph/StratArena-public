摘要

该EA基于TMA（三角移动平均）通道突破进行交易。当价格收于TMA值加上上轨水平之上时开多仓，当价格收于TMA值减去下轨水平之下时开空仓，配有可配置的止损和止盈。

策略分析

策略概念基于TMA通道突破，当价格偏离TMA中心线超过指定点数时视为突破信号。入场条件为：买入信号需当前K线收盘价高于TMA值加上上轨水平，且前一K线收盘价未突破该水平；卖出信号反之，需收盘价低于TMA值减去下轨水平且前一K线未突破。出场条件包括止损和止盈的固定点数设置，以及反向信号触发平仓（如买入信号出现时平掉空头仓位）。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示固定手数，正值表示按存款比例计算手数。资金管理使用TradeAlgorithms.mqh中封装的函数，支持按存款份额或固定手数两种模式。策略默认在H4周期运行，使用SignalBar=1即前一根已收盘K线作为信号源，避免未收盘K线的信号漂移问题。

Summary

This EA trades breakouts from the TMA (Triangular Moving Average) channel. It opens buy positions when price closes above the TMA plus an upper level, and sell positions when price closes below the TMA minus a lower level, with configurable stop loss and take profit.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on TMA channel breakouts, where price deviation beyond specified point levels from the TMA centerline triggers entry signals. Entry conditions: a buy signal requires the current bar's close to be above the TMA value plus the upper level, while the previous bar's close was at or below that threshold, confirming a fresh breakout; sell signals are the mirror opposite. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit in points, plus reverse-signal closes (e.g., a buy signal triggers closing of short positions). Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter: negative values represent fixed lot sizes, while positive values represent a share-of-deposit calculation. Money management uses functions from the TradeAlgorithms.mqh include file, supporting both fixed-lot and percentage-of-balance modes. The strategy defaults to H4 timeframe and uses SignalBar=1, meaning it reads from the most recently closed bar to avoid signal repainting from unclosed bars.

优势

  • 使用已收盘K线（SignalBar=1）作为信号源，避免未收盘K线的信号漂移问题
  • 具备独立的买卖开关控制，灵活性强，可单独禁用某个方向的交易
  • 止损止盈均有明确设置，风险控制参数完备
  • 采用IsNewBar机制限制信号检测频率，减少不必要的重复计算

劣势

  • TMA指标为中心对称算法，存在未来函数重绘问题，历史回测结果可能不可靠
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下突破信号可能因点差成本而失效
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点设置过大，在H4周期下回撤可能很深
  • 没有持仓检查逻辑，可能在同一信号上重复开仓

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓
  • 添加点差过滤和时间过滤，避免高点差时段和低流动性时段交易
  • 考虑使用非重绘版本的TMA指标替代当前可能重绘的版本
  • 增加移动止损或保本止损功能，在盈利达到一定幅度后锁定利润

Strengths

  • Uses closed bar (SignalBar=1) for signal generation, avoiding repainting issues from unclosed bars
  • Independent buy/sell permission switches provide flexibility to disable trading in either direction
  • Explicit stop loss and take profit settings provide defined risk control parameters
  • IsNewBar mechanism limits signal detection frequency, reducing unnecessary redundant calculations

Weaknesses

  • TMA indicator uses centered symmetric algorithm which repaints historical values, making backtesting results potentially unreliable
  • No spread filter — breakout signals may be invalidated by spread costs in high-spread environments
  • Stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are very large, potentially leading to deep drawdowns on H4
  • No position check logic — may repeatedly open positions on the same signal

Improvements

  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Add spread filter and session filter to avoid trading during high-spread and low-liquidity periods
  • Consider using a non-repainting version of the TMA indicator to replace the current potentially repainting one
  • Add trailing stop or break-even functionality to lock in profits after reaching a certain gain threshold

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • TMA指标可能存在重绘问题，导致回测与实盘表现不一致
  • 止损止盈点数较大，单笔交易风险敞口较高
  • 缺少持仓检查可能导致同方向重复开仓放大风险

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • TMA indicator may repaint, causing inconsistency between backtest and live performance
  • Large stop loss/take profit point values result in high per-trade risk exposure
  • Missing position check may cause duplicate entries amplifying directional risk
