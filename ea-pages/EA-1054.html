摘要

该EA基于前一日的最高价、最低价和收盘价计算经典支点水平进行交易。当价格穿越关键支撑/阻力位时开仓做多或做空，提供5种可配置的入场、止损和止盈变体组合。包含连亏减仓、可选的日内交易模式以及保本止损修改功能。

策略分析

策略概念基于经典支点计算，利用前一交易日的最高价、最低价和收盘价计算出Pivot中轴及R1-R3/S1-S3共7个关键价位。入场条件为：当K线低点跌破买入水平或收盘价低于买入水平且开盘价在该水平之上，同时当前收盘价回到该水平之上时做多；卖出信号反之。出场条件依赖支点水平设定的止损和止盈，可选日内23:00强制平仓，以及当价格到达第一目标位时将止损移至保本。仓位管理采用固定手数或基于自由保证金比例的动态手数计算，并在连续亏损时按DcF因子递减手数。资金管理通过MaxR参数控制最大风险占比，fLotsNormalize函数确保手数符合品种最小/最大手数和步长规范。策略还提供了第二套备选止损止盈方案，当第一套方案因距离过近不满足最小止损要求时启用。

Summary

This EA trades based on classic pivot point levels calculated from the previous day's high, low, and close prices. It opens buy or sell positions when price crosses below/above key support/resistance levels, with 5 configurable variants for entry, stop loss, and take profit based on different pivot level combinations. It includes lot reduction on consecutive losses, optional intraday-only trading, and a breakeven stop-loss modification feature.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on classic pivot point calculation, deriving the Pivot midpoint and R1-R3/S1-S3 levels from the previous day's high, low, and close prices. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when the previous bar's low dips below the buy level or its close is at/below the buy level while its open was above, and the current bar's close returns to or above the buy level; sell signals are the mirror image. Exit conditions rely on pivot-based stop loss and take profit levels, with an optional forced close at 23:00 for intraday trading, and a breakeven stop-loss modification when price reaches the first target level. Position sizing uses either a fixed lot size or dynamic calculation based on free margin proportion controlled by MaxR, with a lot reduction mechanism that decreases size proportionally after consecutive losses using the DcF factor. Money management is enforced through fLotsNormalize which ensures lot sizes conform to the symbol's minimum/maximum volume and step size. The strategy also provides a fallback SL/TP scheme activated when the primary levels fail minimum stop-distance validation checks.

优势

  • 基于经典支点理论，逻辑清晰透明，易于理解和验证
  • 提供5种不同的支点水平变体，交易者可根据市场波动性灵活选择
  • 包含连亏减仓机制，有助于控制连续亏损风险
  • 有第二套备选止损止盈方案，提高了开仓成功率
  • 包含最小止损距离检查，避免因止损过近被拒单

劣势

  • 仅基于单一时间框架的支点计算，缺乏多周期确认，容易产生假信号
  • 没有扩展点差过滤，在流动性差的时段可能以不利价格成交
  • 止损修改逻辑中卖出方向使用Bid与目标比较存在潜在逻辑错误，应使用Ask
  • 第二套卖出备选方案中SL/TP添加了点差偏移但买入方案未添加，处理不一致
  • 缺少回撤控制和最大持仓时间限制等额外风控措施

改进建议

  • 增加多时间框架确认机制，例如在H1支点信号与H4趋势方向一致时才开仓
  • 修正卖出方向止损修改逻辑中的Bid/Ask使用问题，确保正确比较价格

Strengths

  • Based on classic pivot point theory with clear and transparent logic, easy to understand and verify
  • Offers 5 different pivot level variants, allowing traders to flexibly choose based on market volatility
  • Includes a lot reduction mechanism on consecutive losses, helping control drawdown risk
  • Provides a fallback SL/TP scheme, increasing the chance of successful order placement
  • Includes minimum stop-distance validation checks, preventing rejected orders due to stops too close

Weaknesses

  • Relies on single-timeframe pivot calculation without multi-timeframe confirmation, prone to false signals
  • No spread filter, potentially executing at unfavorable prices during low-liquidity sessions
  • Potential logic error in SL modification for sell positions using Bid instead of Ask for target comparison
  • Inconsistent handling in fallback SL/TP: sell variant adds spread offset but buy variant does not
  • Lacks additional risk controls such as drawdown limits or maximum position holding time

Improvements

  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation, e.g., only enter when H1 pivot signals align with H4 trend direction
  • Fix the Bid/Ask usage in the sell-side stop-loss modification logic to ensure correct price comparison

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 连亏减仓机制虽然存在但DcF参数默认值较小（3），在极端行情下减仓幅度可能不足以防止大幅回撤
  • 卖出方向CheckForSLMod函数中使用Sym.Bid()与目标价比较，对于卖出仓位应使用Sym.Ask()，可能导致止损修改时机判断错误
  • 没有最大回撤限制或账户净值保护机制，在支点水平失效的趋势行情中可能持续亏损

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • The lot reduction mechanism exists but DcF default (3) may be insufficient to prevent significant drawdown in extreme market conditions
  • CheckForSLMod uses Sym.Bid() for sell position target comparison where Sym.Ask() should be used, potentially causing incorrect SL modification timing
  • No maximum drawdown limit or account equity protection, risking sustained losses during trending markets where pivot levels fail
