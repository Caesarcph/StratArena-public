摘要

该EA基于日线支点水平进行交易，当价格触及支撑位时开多仓，触及阻力位时开空仓。提供5种基于支点支撑1-3和阻力1-3的入场/出场组合方案，并支持连续亏损后缩仓和保本止损修改功能。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于前一日的高、低、收盘价计算经典支点及三个支撑位和三个阻力位。入场条件为：买入信号要求前一根K线的低点或收盘价跌破买入支撑位且开盘价在该位之上，同时当前收盘价回到该位上方；卖出信号为镜像逻辑。出场条件通过TgtProfit参数提供5种止盈止损组合，例如方案1为在Support1买入、Support2止损、Resist1止盈。若首选方案因止损或止盈过近无法下单，则尝试第二套方案。仓位管理方面，当Lots为0时根据可用保证金和MaxR比例计算仓位，且DcF参数实现了连续亏损后递减仓位。资金管理还包含fLotsNormalize函数确保手数符合品种最小步长和限制。此外，ModSL参数启用后可在价格达到第一目标位时将止损移至保本。isTradeDay参数支持日内交易模式，在23:00强制平仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on daily pivot point levels, opening buy positions when price touches support levels and sell positions when price touches resistance levels. It offers five variants of entry/exit level combinations based on pivot-derived Support1-3 and Resist1-3, with optional lot reduction after consecutive losses and breakeven stop-loss modification.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy calculates classic pivot points along with three support and three resistance levels from the previous day's high, low, and close prices. Entry conditions: a buy signal requires the previous bar's low or close to break below the buy level while its open was above it, and the current close returns above that level; sell signals follow the mirror logic. Exit conditions are determined by the TgtProfit parameter offering five combinations of stop-loss and take-profit levels, e.g., variant 1 buys at Support1 with stop at Support2 and target at Resist1. If the primary variant cannot be placed due to stops being too close, a second alternative set of SL/TP is attempted. Position sizing defaults to a margin-based calculation using MaxR when Lots is zero, and the DcF parameter implements lot reduction after consecutive losing trades. The fLotsNormalize function ensures lot sizes conform to symbol volume step and limits. Additionally, the ModSL parameter enables moving the stop-loss to breakeven when the first target level is reached. The isTradeDay parameter supports intraday-only mode by forcing position closure at 23:00.

优势

  • 基于经典支点策略，逻辑清晰且广泛使用，支撑阻力位具有市场心理意义
  • 提供5种不同的止盈止损组合方案，交易者可根据风险偏好灵活选择
  • 具有连续亏损后缩仓机制，有助于控制回撤风险
  • 第二套SL/TP备选方案增加了订单成交概率，避免因止损过近而错失交易机会
  • 保本止损功能（ModSL）可在第一目标达成后锁定风险

劣势

  • 仅使用前一日数据计算支点，在波动剧烈或趋势行情中支撑阻力位可能过于狭窄或失效
  • 同一时间只允许持有一个方向的仓位，且无对冲机制，在震荡行情中可能频繁止损
  • 止损和止盈依赖于固定支点水平，未考虑ATR等波动率指标，在不同波动环境下风险收益比不一致
  • 23:00强制平仓的实现仅检查小时而未考虑服务器时间与交易时段的对应关系
  • 代码中存在冗余赋值（如SolveSellSLTP2中先赋0再重新赋值），且变量命名可读性较差

改进建议

  • 引入ATR或波动率乘数动态调整止损止盈距离，使风险收益比适应不同市场波动环境
  • 增加多时间框架确认（如H4或日线趋势方向过滤），避免在逆势支点信号上入场
  • 完善日内平仓逻辑，应根据品种交易时段而非固定23:00来判断是否需要强制平仓
  • 增加最大回撤限制和最大连续交易次数限制，作为额外的风控层

Strengths

  • Based on the classic pivot point strategy with clear logic; support/resistance levels carry psychological market significance
  • Five different SL/TP level combinations give traders flexibility to match their risk appetite
  • Lot reduction mechanism after consecutive losses helps control drawdown risk
  • A second fallback SL/TP variant increases order fill probability, avoiding missed trades due to stops being too close
  • The breakeven stop-loss modification (ModSL) locks in risk-free trades after the first target is reached

Weaknesses

  • Pivot levels derived only from the previous day's data may be too narrow or invalid during volatile or trending markets
  • Only one directional position at a time with no hedging; frequent stop-outs likely in ranging markets
  • SL/TP based on fixed pivot levels without ATR or volatility adjustment leads to inconsistent risk-reward ratios across varying volatility environments
  • The 23:00 forced close only checks the hour without accounting for server time versus actual trading session alignment
  • Code contains redundant assignments (e.g., SolveSellSLTP2 assigns 0 then overwrites) and poor variable naming readability

Improvements

  • Introduce ATR or volatility multipliers to dynamically adjust SL/TP distances for consistent risk-reward across varying market conditions
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation (e.g., H4 or daily trend filter) to avoid entering against the dominant trend at pivot levels
  • Improve intraday close logic to use instrument trading sessions rather than a fixed 23:00 hour check
  • Add maximum drawdown limit and maximum consecutive trade count as additional risk management layers

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 支点水平在低波动或单边趋势行情中可能失效，导致连续亏损
  • 第二套SL/TP方案（如SolveBuySLTP2使用Support2/Resist3）的止损距离可能远大于首选方案，风险敞口不一致
  • DcF缩仓机制虽然降低了亏损后的仓位，但不会阻止交易本身，连续亏损仍可能累积较大回撤
  • 无滑点保护机制，仅使用Sym.Spread()*3作为成交偏差可能不足

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Pivot levels may fail in low-volatility or strong trending markets, leading to consecutive losses
  • The second SL/TP variant (e.g., SolveBuySLTP2 using Support2/Resist3) can have significantly larger stop distances than the primary variant, creating inconsistent risk exposure
  • The DcF lot reduction mechanism reduces size but doesn't prevent trading itself; consecutive losses can still accumulate significant drawdown
  • No slippage protection beyond Sym.Spread()*3 deviation, which may be insufficient in fast markets
