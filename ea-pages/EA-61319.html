摘要

该EA在当前价格上下方按固定间距放置一系列买入止损和卖出止损挂单，形成对称网格。当价格触及挂单时自动进场，通过级联入场捕捉趋势行情，每个挂单设有独立的止盈止损。

策略分析

该策略采用网格交易方法，在价格上下方对称放置买入止损和卖出止损挂单。入场条件为价格突破网格层级触发挂单成交，无需额外技术指标判断方向。出场条件依赖每笔订单独立的止盈和止损设置，当价格达到TP或SL时平仓。仓位管理通过初始手数参数和网格层数控制总仓位，每层挂单使用相同手数。资金管理方面，EA提供了最大滑点和魔法编号等参数，但缺少根据账户余额动态调整手数的功能。整体而言，策略依赖市场波动触发网格订单获利，在趋势行情中可能连续触发多单或空单获取利润，但在震荡行情中可能因止损频繁触发而亏损。

Summary

This EA places a grid of pending orders (buy stops and sell stops) at fixed intervals from the current price, creating a symmetric grid around the market. When price hits a pending order, it enters a position and the grid captures directional moves through cascading entries while using take-profit and stop-loss levels for each order.

Strategy Analysis

This strategy employs a grid trading approach by symmetrically placing buy stop and sell stop pending orders above and below the current price. Entry conditions are purely mechanical - price movement triggers pending orders at each grid level, with no technical indicator used for directional bias. Exit conditions rely on individual take-profit and stop-loss levels set for each order in the grid. Position sizing is controlled through an initial lot parameter and the number of grid levels, with each level using the same fixed lot size. Money management includes parameters for maximum slippage and magic number for order identification, but lacks dynamic lot sizing based on account equity or balance. The strategy profits from trending markets where multiple orders in one direction get triggered consecutively, but suffers in ranging markets where frequent stop-loss hits erode capital.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单明了，参数少易于理解和配置
  • 网格结构能在趋势行情中通过级联触发获取较大利润
  • 每笔订单设有独立止损，单笔风险有上限控制

劣势

  • 对称网格无方向性判断，在震荡行情中止损频繁触发导致持续亏损
  • 缺少动态仓位调整，无法根据账户资金或波动率自适应手数
  • 多层级挂单同时持仓时总风险敞口较大，可能超出账户承受能力
  • 缺少新K线判断机制，可能在同一根K线上重复下单

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤指标（如均线或ATR），使网格具有方向偏向，减少逆势挂单
  • 添加根据账户余额和ATR动态调整手数和网格间距的资金管理模块

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic with few parameters, easy to understand and configure
  • Grid structure can capture significant profits in trending markets through cascading order triggers
  • Each order has an independent stop-loss, capping per-trade risk

Weaknesses

  • Symmetric grid lacks directional bias, leading to frequent stop-loss triggers and sustained losses in ranging markets
  • No dynamic position sizing - cannot adapt lot size based on account equity or market volatility
  • Multiple grid levels held simultaneously create large total risk exposure that may exceed account capacity
  • No new bar check mechanism, potentially placing duplicate orders on the same bar

Improvements

  • Add a trend filter indicator (e.g., moving average or ATR) to give the grid a directional bias and reduce counter-trend pending orders
  • Implement a dynamic money management module that adjusts lot size and grid spacing based on account balance and ATR volatility

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:HIGH

风险点

  • 网格多层级同时持仓导致总风险敞口不可控，极端行情下可能全部止损
  • 缺少账户级别整体止损或最大回撤保护，持续震荡可能缓慢消耗账户资金
  • 未检查已有持仓数量，可能重复下单导致仓位叠加

Risk Assessment

Overall:HIGH

Risk Points

  • Multiple simultaneous grid positions create uncontrolled total risk exposure; extreme market moves could trigger all stop-losses
  • No account-level overall stop-loss or maximum drawdown protection; prolonged ranging markets can slowly deplete account funds
  • No check on existing position count, potentially leading to duplicate orders and position stacking
