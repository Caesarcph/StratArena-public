摘要

该EA实现了经典的马丁格尔策略，在每次亏损交易后将手数翻倍，而在盈利后重置为初始手数。它基于简单信号（通常是单一指标或价格行为条件）开仓，并通过不断加大的仓位来弥补损失。

策略分析

策略概念基于经典马丁格尔理论：亏损后加倍下注，一次赢利即可覆盖之前所有亏损。入场条件通常依赖单一技术指标信号（如移动平均线交叉或RSI超买超卖）判断方向开仓。出场条件为达到止盈目标时平仓获利，若亏损则在下一笔交易中加倍手数。仓位管理采用等比递增方式，每次亏损后手数乘以倍数系数（通常为2），盈利后回归初始手数。资金管理方面缺乏硬性止损，账户风险随连续亏损呈指数级增长，在极端行情下可能导致爆仓。该策略在震荡市中短期可能有效，但面对单边趋势行情时存在巨大风险。

Summary

This EA implements a classic Martingale strategy, doubling the lot size after each losing trade while resetting to the initial lot after a win. It opens positions based on a simple signal (typically a single indicator or price action condition) and relies on the Martingale progression to recover losses through increasingly larger positions.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the classic Martingale theory: doubling the bet after each loss so that a single win can recover all previous losses. Entry conditions typically rely on a single technical indicator signal (such as moving average crossover or RSI overbought/oversold) to determine trade direction. Exit conditions close the position when a take-profit target is reached, while a loss triggers a doubled lot size on the next trade. Position sizing follows a geometric progression, multiplying the lot by a coefficient (typically 2) after each loss, and resetting to the initial lot after a win. Money management lacks a hard stop-loss, meaning account risk grows exponentially with consecutive losses, potentially leading to a margin call under extreme market conditions. The strategy may appear effective in ranging markets short-term but carries enormous risk during sustained trending moves.

优势

  • 逻辑简单易懂，新手也能快速理解运行机制
  • 在震荡行情中短期表现可能较好，能持续获利
  • 盈利后自动重置手数，锁定利润避免过度加仓

劣势

  • 连续亏损时手数呈指数增长，极易导致爆仓
  • 缺乏有效止损机制，单边行情中亏损无上限
  • 不适应趋势行情，逆势加仓会放大损失
  • 没有考虑点差和手续费对马丁格尔序列的侵蚀效应

改进建议

  • 增加最大连续亏损次数限制和最大手数硬性上限，防止无限加仓
  • 引入硬性止损和账户风险百分比控制，保护本金安全
  • 结合趋势过滤指标，避免在强趋势行情中逆势加仓

Strengths

  • Simple and easy-to-understand logic, accessible even for beginners
  • May perform well short-term in ranging/sideways markets, generating consistent small profits
  • Automatically resets lot size after a win, locking in profits and avoiding over-leveraging

Weaknesses

  • Lot size grows exponentially during consecutive losses,极易 leading to margin call
  • Lacks effective stop-loss mechanism, losses are uncapped in trending markets
  • Poorly suited for trending markets, adding to losing positions amplifies losses
  • Does not account for the erosion effect of spreads and commissions on the Martingale sequence

Improvements

  • Add a maximum consecutive loss count limit and a hard cap on maximum lot size to prevent unlimited position scaling
  • Introduce a hard stop-loss and account risk percentage control to protect capital
  • Incorporate a trend filter indicator to avoid adding positions against strong trending markets
