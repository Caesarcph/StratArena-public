摘要

该EA基于BBSqueeze（布林带挤压）指标进行交易，检测波动率收缩后的突破信号。当上直方图值显示看涨动能时开多仓，当下直方图值显示看跌动能时开空仓，并配有可配置的止损和止盈水平。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于布林带挤压（Bollinger Band Squeeze）突破理念，当波动率收缩后价格突破方向产生交易信号。入场条件为：当上直方图值（UpValue[1]）非零且非空值时，若下直方图前值（DnValue[0]）也存在，则触发买入开仓信号；反之当下直方图值存在且上直方图前值存在时触发卖出开仓信号。出场条件包括止损和止盈的固定点数设置，以及基于反向信号的平仓逻辑——当卖出信号出现时关闭多头，当买入信号出现时关闭空头。仓位管理采用基于存款份额的动态手数计算（MM参数为负值时为固定手数，正值时为按资金比例计算）。该策略仅在新的K线形成时才重新计算信号，避免了逐tick的频繁交易。指标使用了可配置的平滑方法（支持SMA、EMA、JJMA等多种算法），默认使用EMA作为布林带的平滑方法，周期为20，偏差为2.0。

Summary

This EA trades based on the BBSqueeze (Bollinger Band Squeeze) indicator, detecting volatility contraction breakouts. It opens long positions when the upper histogram value signals bullish momentum and opens short positions when the lower histogram value signals bearish momentum, with configurable stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy is based on the Bollinger Band Squeeze breakout concept, generating trading signals when price breaks out after a period of volatility contraction. Entry conditions are: when the upper histogram value (UpValue[1]) is non-zero and not EMPTY_VALUE, and the previous lower histogram value (DnValue[0]) also exists, a buy signal is triggered; conversely, when the lower histogram value exists and the previous upper histogram value exists, a sell signal is triggered. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit levels in points, as well as reverse signal closing logic — long positions are closed when a sell signal appears and short positions are closed when a buy signal appears. Position sizing uses a deposit share-based dynamic lot calculation (the MM parameter: negative values represent fixed lot size, positive values represent percentage of deposit). The strategy only recalculates signals on new bar formation, avoiding excessive per-tick trading. The indicator uses configurable smoothing methods (supporting SMA, EMA, JJMA, and various other algorithms), defaulting to EMA as the Bollinger Band smoothing method with a period of 20 and deviation of 2.0.

优势

  • 基于布林带挤压突破策略，逻辑清晰，在波动率收缩后捕捉趋势启动点
  • 使用新K线检测机制避免频繁交易，减少过拟合和滑点影响
  • 提供灵活的仓位管理，支持固定手数和资金比例两种模式
  • 支持多种平滑算法（SMA/EMA/JJMA等），适应不同市场环境
  • 买卖开关可独立控制开仓和平仓，策略配置灵活

劣势

  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下止损止盈可能失效
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点设置较大，对小账户风险较高
  • 依赖自定义指标BBSqueeze，指标不可用时仅打印日志而不阻止EA运行
  • 未检查当前持仓状态，可能重复开仓导致仓位叠加
  • SignalBar默认值为1，存在一定程度的信号延迟

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤器，在点差过大时禁止开仓以保护资金
  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，避免在同一信号上重复开仓导致仓位叠加
  • 增加追踪止损功能，在趋势行情中锁定利润而非仅依赖固定止盈

Strengths

  • Based on the Bollinger Band Squeeze breakout strategy with clear logic, capturing trend initiation after volatility contraction
  • Uses new bar detection mechanism to avoid frequent trading, reducing overfitting and slippage impact
  • Provides flexible position sizing supporting both fixed lot size and deposit percentage modes
  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithms (SMA/EMA/JJMA etc.), adaptable to different market conditions
  • Buy/sell switches can independently control opening and closing, offering flexible strategy configuration

Weaknesses

  • Lacks spread filter, stop loss and take profit may fail in high spread environments
  • Stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are large, posing high risk for small accounts
  • Depends on custom BBSqueeze indicator; if unavailable, only logs a warning but does not prevent EA from running
  • Does not check current open positions, potentially leading to repeated position stacking
  • SignalBar defaults to 1, introducing some signal lag

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter to prohibit opening positions when spread is too wide, protecting capital
  • Add position check logic to avoid repeated position opening on the same signal causing position stacking
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in profits during trending markets rather than relying solely on fixed take profit
