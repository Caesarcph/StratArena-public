Exp_MFI

This EA trades based on the Money Flow Index (MFI) indicator, entering long positions when MFI is oversold and exiting when overbought, and vice versa for short positions. It uses fixed lot sizing and implements a simple momentum-based strategy with customizable MFI periods and threshold levels. The EA applies the MFI oscillator to identify potential reversal points driven by volume-weighted price action.

Strategy analysis

This EA uses the Money Flow Index (MFI) as its sole trading signal source, implementing a classic momentum reversal strategy. Entry conditions are: go long when MFI crosses above the oversold threshold and go short when MFI crosses below the overbought threshold. Exit conditions rely on MFI reaching the opposite extreme zone to trigger position closure, or using stop-loss/take-profit parameters to control per-trade risk. Position sizing employs a fixed lot approach without dynamic adjustment or percentage-based money management. Money management is relatively primitive, depending solely on user-specified lot sizes without adaptive mechanisms based on account balance or volatility. The overall strategy logic is clear but singular, lacking multi-timeframe confirmation and filtering mechanisms, which makes it prone to frequent false signals and consecutive losses in choppy markets.

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and debug
  • MFI incorporates both price and volume information, providing more reference value than pure price indicators
  • High configurability with user-adjustable MFI period and overbought/oversold thresholds

Weaknesses

  • Lacks multi-timeframe confirmation, a single indicator easily generates false signals
  • Fixed lot sizing without dynamic money management or risk control mechanisms
  • No duplicate order check or new bar detection, potentially opening multiple positions on the same signal
  • Missing spread filter, high spread entries increase costs and hurt profitability
  • Strategy is overly simplistic, lacks trend filter to distinguish ranging and trending markets

Possible improvements

  • Add multi-timeframe trend confirmation, e.g., only execute M15 MFI signals aligned with H1 trend direction
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account balance percentage instead of fixed lots
  • Add new bar detection to prevent duplicate entries on the same bar, and incorporate a spread filter

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Fixed lot sizing without money management, large positions can cause excessive single-trade losses
  • No duplicate order check, potentially stacking multiple positions on the same signal amplifying risk
  • Stop-loss defaults to 0, if user doesn't set it then relies entirely on MFI signal for exit, risking large drawdowns
