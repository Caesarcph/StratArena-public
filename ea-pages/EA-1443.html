摘要

该EA基于自定义'Beginner'指标进行交易，该指标通过检测带有可配置偏移和周期的新分形高低点来识别突破信号。当出现向上信号时开多仓，出现向下信号时开空仓，并可通过参数控制入场和出场权限。EA包含按存款比例或固定手数的资金管理功能，并对所有交易设置止损和止盈。

策略分析

策略核心基于Beginner自定义指标，该指标通过检测价格突破前高或前低来产生交易信号。入场条件为：当指标上轨缓冲区出现非空值时产生买入信号，当指标下轨缓冲区出现非空值时产生卖出信号。出场条件包括止损和止盈的硬性设置，以及反向信号触发平仓——当检测到买入信号时同时触发空头平仓，检测到卖出信号时触发多头平仓。仓位管理采用MM参数控制，负值代表固定手数，正值代表按存款比例计算手数。EA还通过历史回溯检测最近的交易方向来补充平仓信号，确保在单向持仓模式下也能正确平仓。整体策略属于突破类系统，利用价格突破关键点位产生趋势跟踪信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on the custom 'Beginner' indicator, which identifies breakout signals by detecting new fractal-like highs and lows with a configurable offset and period. It opens buy positions when an upward signal appears and sell positions when a downward signal appears, with configurable permissions for entry and exit. The EA includes money management via deposit share or fixed lot sizing, and applies stop loss and take profit to all trades.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is based on the custom Beginner indicator, which generates trading signals by detecting price breakouts of previous highs and lows. Entry conditions: a buy signal is triggered when the indicator's upper buffer shows a non-empty value, and a sell signal is triggered when the lower buffer shows a non-empty value. Exit conditions include hard stop loss and take profit settings, plus reverse-signal closing — a buy signal also triggers closing of short positions, and a sell signal triggers closing of long positions. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent deposit share-based lot calculation. The EA also performs a historical bar lookup to find the last trading direction for supplementary close signals, ensuring proper position closing even in single-direction holding mode. The overall strategy falls under the breakout category, using price breakouts of key levels to generate trend-following signals.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于突破信号入场，符合趋势跟踪的交易理念
  • 提供了完整的买入/卖出开仓和平仓权限控制，灵活性高
  • 使用NewBar检测避免同一根K线重复交易，减少过度交易风险
  • 包含止损和止盈设置，具备基本的风险控制能力
  • 资金管理支持固定手数和存款比例两种模式，适应不同交易需求

劣势

  • Beginner指标为自定义指标，依赖外部文件，缺少该指标则EA无法运行
  • 没有点差过滤，在高点差环境下突破信号可能导致不利成交
  • 历史回溯搜索平仓信号可能遍历大量K线，存在性能隐患
  • 缺乏交易时间过滤，可能在不利时段（如低流动性时段）开仓
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值较大，对不同品种可能不适用

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤条件，避免在点差过大时开仓
  • 添加交易时段过滤，限制仅在流动性充足的时段交易
  • 对历史回溯搜索设置最大K线数量限制，防止性能问题

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on breakout signals, aligning with trend-following trading principles
  • Complete buy/sell open and close permission controls provide high flexibility
  • NewBar detection prevents repeated trading on the same bar, reducing overtrading risk
  • Stop loss and take profit settings provide basic risk control
  • Money management supports both fixed lot and deposit share modes, accommodating different trading needs

Weaknesses

  • The Beginner indicator is custom and requires an external file; the EA cannot run without it
  • No spread filter; breakout signals in high-spread environments may lead to unfavorable fills
  • Historical bar lookup for close signals may iterate through many bars, posing performance concerns
  • Lacks trading session filter; may open positions during unfavorable low-liquidity periods
  • Default SL of 1000 and TP of 2000 points are large and may not suit all instruments

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter condition to avoid opening positions when the spread is too wide
  • Add trading session filtering to restrict trading to high-liquidity periods only
  • Set a maximum bar count limit for the historical lookup search to prevent performance issues

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 止损和止盈点数固定，未根据ATR或波动率动态调整，可能不适应当前市场波动
  • 没有点差过滤，突破信号在新闻事件或低流动性时可能产生滑点
  • MM参数为负值时直接作为手数使用，无最小手数校验，可能导致过小或过大的仓位

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed stop loss and take profit in points without ATR-based dynamic adjustment may not suit current market volatility
  • No spread filter; breakout signals during news events or low liquidity may cause slippage
  • When MM is negative, it is used directly as lot size without minimum lot validation, potentially leading to excessively small or large positions
