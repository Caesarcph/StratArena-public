Price Rollback

Price Rollback is a mean reversion EA that identifies overextended price movements and enters trades anticipating a rollback or reversion to the mean. It monitors price deviation from a moving average or range and opens positions when price has moved too far too fast, expecting a corrective pullback.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on mean reversion principles, assuming that when price deviates significantly from its statistical mean, a rollback is expected. Entry conditions typically involve price moving a certain ATR multiple or percentage away from a moving average followed by a reversal candlestick pattern, or price touching the outer Bollinger Band then closing back inside. Exit conditions include take-profit when price returns near the mean line, fixed pip take-profit, and ATR-based dynamic stop-loss. Position sizing generally uses fixed lots or risk-percentage-based calculation, dynamically adjusted according to stop-loss distance. Money management carries potential risk as mean reversion strategies can suffer consecutive losses during trending markets, which is dangerous without maximum position limits or consecutive loss protection mechanisms.

Strengths

  • Mean reversion logic performs well in ranging markets with relatively high win rate
  • Uses ATR to dynamically measure price deviation, adapting to different instrument volatility characteristics
  • High parameter adjustability allows users to optimize for different market conditions

Weaknesses

  • Mean reversion strategy can suffer continuous counter-trend losses during strong trending markets
  • Lack of trend filter may lead to frequent entries and stop-outs during trending markets
  • Faces risk of significant single-trade loss if rollback does not materialize

Possible improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., ADX or large-period MA direction) to pause trading or reverse strategy during trending markets
  • Add consecutive loss protection mechanism to pause trading or reduce position size after N consecutive losses
  • Add spread filter to avoid entering during high spread periods

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Mean reversion can incur consecutive losses during trending markets without trend filter protection
  • Improper stop-loss placement may lead to excessive single-trade losses
