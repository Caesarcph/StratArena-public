摘要

该EA基于i_Trend自定义指标进行交易，该指标结合了基于移动平均线的趋势线和布林带信号线。当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿时开空仓，并具有可配置的资金管理和止损止盈设置。

策略分析

该EA使用i_Trend自定义指标作为核心策略引擎，该指标由两个缓冲区组成：趋势线（Ind）和信号线（Sign）。趋势线基于移动平均线计算，信号线则基于布林带逻辑生成，两者结合形成趋势判断依据。入场条件为：当趋势线从下方穿越信号线时产生做多信号，反之则产生做空信号；具体判断使用当前K线和前一根K线的交叉关系。出场条件包括止损和止盈的硬性设定，以及反向交叉信号触发平仓。仓位管理采用TradeAlgorithms.mqh库中的多种模式，包括固定手数、按余额比例、按可用保证金比例等。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，MMMode参数选择具体的仓位计算方式。该策略还包含新K线检测机制，避免在同一根K线上重复开仓，同时使用SignalBar参数允许回溯检测信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on the i_Trend custom indicator, which combines a moving average-based trend line with Bollinger Band-derived signal line. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with configurable money management and stop-loss/take-profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

This EA uses the i_Trend custom indicator as its core strategy engine, which consists of two buffers: a trend line (Ind) and a signal line (Sign). The trend line is calculated based on a moving average, while the signal line is derived from Bollinger Band logic, combining both to form a trend assessment. Entry conditions are: a buy signal is generated when the trend line crosses above the signal line, and a sell signal on the opposite crossover, using the relationship between the current and previous bar values. Exit conditions include hard stop-loss and take-profit levels, as well as closing positions on reverse crossover signals. Position sizing leverages the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library with multiple modes including fixed lot, balance-proportional, and free margin-proportional sizing. Money management is controlled via the MM parameter for risk ratio and MMMode for the specific lot calculation method. The strategy also includes a new bar detection mechanism to avoid repeated entries on the same bar, and the SignalBar parameter allows signal detection on a user-specified bar offset.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，使用趋势线与信号线交叉判断，易于理解和验证
  • 具备完善的开仓和平仓独立控制开关，灵活性高
  • 使用新K线检测机制防止重复开仓，避免同根K线多次交易
  • 止损止盈比例2:1（TP=2000, SL=1000），具有正期望收益特征
  • 支持多种仓位管理模式，适应不同风险偏好

劣势

  • 依赖i_Trend自定义指标，指标源码不可见，策略黑盒风险
  • 缺乏点差过滤机制，在流动性差的市场中可能因滑点造成损失
  • 止损1000点数值偏大，可能在剧烈行情中造成较大单笔亏损
  • 没有持仓数量检查，可能在已有持仓时重复开仓
  • 缺少时间过滤和交易日限制，可能在非理想时段交易

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓
  • 添加点差过滤和时间过滤，提高交易质量
  • 增加移动止损或保本止损功能，保护浮盈
  • 将止损止盈参数改为ATR动态计算，适应市场波动性

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic using trend/signal line crossover, easy to understand and verify
  • Independent on/off switches for opening and closing buy/sell positions, providing high flexibility
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated entries on the same bar, avoiding multiple trades per candle
  • Stop-loss to take-profit ratio of 1:2 (SL=1000, TP=2000), exhibiting positive expectancy characteristics
  • Supports multiple position sizing modes, accommodating different risk preferences

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the i_Trend custom indicator whose source is not visible, creating a black-box strategy risk
  • Lacks spread filtering, potentially suffering from slippage in low-liquidity markets
  • Stop-loss of 1000 points is relatively large, potentially causing significant single-trade losses during volatile moves
  • No open position count check, potentially opening duplicate positions when existing ones are already active
  • No time or trading session filter, may trade during suboptimal market hours

Improvements

  • Add open position checking logic to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Add spread filter and time/session filter to improve trade quality
  • Implement trailing stop or break-even functionality to protect floating profits
  • Make stop-loss and take-profit dynamic using ATR to adapt to market volatility
