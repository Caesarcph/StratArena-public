摘要

该EA基于i_Trend自定义指标进行交易，该指标结合移动平均线和布林带组件来判断趋势方向。当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿时开空仓，并设有可配置的止损止盈。仓位管理支持多种保证金模式。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念基于i_Trend指标的趋势判断，该指标内部融合了移动平均线（MA）和布林带（BB）两个组件来生成两条线——指标线和信号线。入场条件为：当指标线大于信号线时处于上升趋势，若前一根K线的指标线小于等于信号线而当前K线指标线大于信号线，则触发买入开仓信号；下降趋势反之触发卖出开仓信号。出场条件包括：止损和止盈的硬性设置（默认止损1000点、止盈2000点），以及反向信号触发的平仓机制——上升趋势中若开启买仓平仓则会在下降信号出现时平掉多头。仓位管理通过MM参数和MMMode枚举支持五种模式，包括固定手数、按余额百分比、按可用保证金百分比等多种资金管理方式。EA还实现了新K线检测机制，避免在同一根K线内重复交易，并在指标数据不足时自动跳过，具有一定的鲁棒性设计。

Summary

This EA trades based on the i_Trend custom indicator, which combines Moving Average and Bollinger Band components to determine trend direction. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with configurable stop loss and take profit levels. Position sizing is handled via a money management module supporting multiple margin modes.

Strategy Analysis

The EA's strategy concept is based on the i_Trend indicator, which internally integrates Moving Average (MA) and Bollinger Band (BB) components to generate two lines — the indicator line and the signal line. Entry conditions: when the indicator line is above the signal line, the market is in an uptrend; if the previous bar's indicator line was below or equal to the signal line and the current bar shows indicator above signal, a buy open signal is triggered; the reverse logic triggers sell open signals in a downtrend. Exit conditions include hard stop loss and take profit settings (default SL 1000 points, TP 2000 points), plus a signal-based close mechanism — in an uptrend with buy close enabled, long positions are closed when a down signal appears, and vice versa. Position sizing is managed through the MM parameter and MMMode enumeration supporting five modes: fixed lot, balance-based percentage, free margin percentage, loss-adjusted balance, and loss-adjusted free margin. The EA also implements a new bar detection mechanism to avoid repeated trades within the same bar, and skips processing when indicator data is insufficient, demonstrating reasonable robustness design.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于指标线与信号线的交叉判断趋势方向，简单易懂
  • 具备完善的新K线检测机制，避免同一根K线内重复开仓
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，灵活适应不同交易需求
  • 止损止盈参数可配置，具备基本的风险控制框架
  • 买卖开仓和平仓均可独立开关控制，策略配置灵活度高

劣势

  • 依赖外部自定义指标i_Trend和TradeAlgorithms.mqh库，可移植性和独立性较差
  • 缺少点差过滤机制，在高点差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点的默认值较大，可能不适合所有品种和波动环境
  • 没有持仓检查逻辑，可能在已有同向持仓时重复开仓
  • i_Trend指标的具体计算逻辑不透明，使用者难以理解信号生成原理

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免在已有同向仓位时重复开仓
  • 添加点差过滤和时间过滤机制，提高入场质量

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on indicator/signal line crossover for trend determination, easy to understand
  • Proper new bar detection mechanism prevents repeated trade entries within the same bar
  • Supports multiple money management modes, flexibly adapting to different trading needs
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit parameters provide a basic risk control framework
  • Buy/sell open and close can be independently toggled, offering high strategy configuration flexibility

Weaknesses

  • Depends on external custom indicator i_Trend and TradeAlgorithms.mqh library, poor portability and independence
  • Lacks spread filter mechanism, may result in unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • Default SL 1000 and TP 2000 points are large, may not suit all instruments and volatility conditions
  • No existing position check logic, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • i_Trend indicator's internal calculation logic is opaque, making it hard for users to understand signal generation

Improvements

  • Add existing position check logic to avoid duplicate entries when same-direction positions already exist
  • Add spread filter and session time filter mechanisms to improve entry quality

  • 无点差过滤，高点差时入场成本高
