摘要

该EA基于自定义i_Trend指标进行交易，该指标结合移动平均线和布林带生成趋势信号。当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿时开空仓，并在信号反转时支持可配置的平仓操作。资金管理支持多种模式，包括固定手数和基于保证金的仓位计算。

策略分析

策略概念基于i_Trend指标的趋势跟踪，该指标由两个缓冲区组成：指标线(Ind)和信号线(Sign)，底层结合了移动平均线和布林带。入场条件为：当Ind[1]>Sign[1]（上根K线指标线在信号线上方）且Ind[0]<=Sign[0]（当前K线指标线在信号线下方或交叉）时触发买入信号，即指标线从下方穿越信号线上方；卖出信号反之。出场条件依赖信号反转：当趋势由多转空时自动平多仓，由空转多时自动平空仓，同时设有固定点数的止损和止盈。仓位管理支持五种模式：自由保证金比例、余额比例、含浮亏的自由保证金比例、含浮亏的余额比例和固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1，并结合止损止盈点数进行风险控制。策略还包含新K线检测机制，避免在同一根K线内重复交易，并使用Recount标志在数据拷贝失败时重新计算信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on the custom i_Trend indicator, which combines a moving average with Bollinger Bands to generate trend signals. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with configurable position closing on signal reversal. Money management supports multiple modes including lot-based and margin-based sizing.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is trend following based on the custom i_Trend indicator, which consists of two buffers: the indicator line (Ind) and the signal line (Sign), combining a moving average with Bollinger Bands under the hood. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when Ind[1]>Sign[1] (indicator above signal on the previous bar) and Ind[0]<=Sign[0] (indicator crosses above signal on the current bar), meaning the indicator line crosses upward through the signal line; sell signals are the mirror opposite. Exit conditions rely on signal reversal: long positions close when the trend shifts from bullish to bearish, and short positions close on the opposite shift, with configurable fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit in points. Position sizing supports five modes: free margin percentage, balance percentage, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter (default 0.1) combined with stop loss and take profit distances for risk control. The strategy also includes a new bar detection mechanism to prevent repeated trades within the same bar, and uses a Recount flag to recalculate signals when buffer copy operations fail.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于指标线与信号线的交叉判断趋势方向，易于理解和验证
  • 提供多种仓位管理模式，适应不同风险偏好和账户规模
  • 具有新K线检测机制，避免同一K线内重复开仓，减少过度交易
  • 买卖开仓和平仓均可独立控制，灵活性高
  • 使用布林带和移动平均线双重指标组合，信号可靠性优于单一指标

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标i_Trend，指标文件缺失时EA无法运行，降低了可移植性
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值偏大，可能不适合所有品种和波动环境
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • 无多重时间框架确认机制，单一周期信号可能产生较多假突破
  • 没有回撤控制或最大连亏限制，极端行情下风险敞口较大

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤机制，当点差超过阈值时暂停交易，避免高点差环境下的不利成交
  • 添加多时间框架确认，例如在H4生成信号时检查日线趋势方向，减少假信号
  • 增加最大回撤限制和连续亏损次数限制，加强极端行情下的风险控制

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on indicator/signal line crossover for trend direction, easy to understand and verify
  • Multiple position sizing modes available, accommodating different risk appetites and account sizes
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated entries within the same bar, reducing overtrading
  • Buy/sell opening and closing can be independently controlled, providing high flexibility
  • Dual indicator combination of Bollinger Bands and moving average offers better signal reliability than a single indicator

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom i_Trend indicator; the EA cannot run if the indicator file is missing, reducing portability
  • Default StopLoss of 1000 points and TakeProfit of 2000 points are large and may not suit all instruments and volatility conditions
  • No spread filter, which may lead to unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • No multi-timeframe confirmation, single timeframe signals may produce frequent false breakouts
  • No drawdown control or maximum consecutive loss limit, leaving significant risk exposure during extreme market conditions

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter to pause trading when spread exceeds a threshold, avoiding unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation, e.g., checking daily trend direction when H4 generates a signal, reducing false signals
  • Add maximum drawdown limit and consecutive loss count limit to strengthen risk control during extreme market conditions
