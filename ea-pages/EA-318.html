Expert_AML_Stoch

This EA uses a custom CML_Stoch indicator (a modified Stochastic oscillator combined with a moving average filter) to generate trading signals within the MQL5 Standard Library Expert Advisor framework. It opens and closes positions based on configurable signal thresholds, employing fixed lot sizing with no trailing stop mechanism.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the CML_Stoch indicator, a custom signal filter that combines a traditional Stochastic oscillator with a moving average. Entry conditions are governed by Signal_ThresholdOpen (default 40) and Signal_ThresholdClose (default 20), opening positions when signal strength exceeds the open threshold and closing when it drops below the close threshold. The Stochastic parameters are K-period 3, D-period 36, and slowing period 25, which means the indicator is sensitive to short-term price movements while the smoothed line reacts slowly, tending to capture reversal signals in overbought/oversold zones, classifying it as a mean reversion strategy. Position sizing uses fixed lots (MoneyFixedLot, default 0.1 lots) without dynamic adjustment based on account balance. Money management sets a 10% percent parameter but actually uses fixed lot sizing, and both stop loss and take profit levels are set to 0, relying on signal thresholds for exit rather than hard stops, which represents a significant risk management deficiency.

Strengths

  • Built on the standard CExpert framework with clean, well-structured code that is easy to maintain and extend
  • Uses a custom CML_Stoch indicator combining Stochastic and moving average, offering some strategic innovation
  • Signal threshold mechanism provides flexible entry/exit control adaptable to different market conditions

Weaknesses

  • Both stop loss and take profit are set to 0, relying entirely on signal thresholds for exit, which can cause massive losses during extreme market conditions
  • Fixed lot sizing does not account for account size changes and cannot dynamically adjust risk exposure
  • No trailing stop mechanism (uses TrailingNone), profitable positions cannot lock in floating profits
  • Missing spread filter, which can erode profits in a mean reversion strategy where high spreads are detrimental
  • No new bar check mechanism (EveryTick=false mitigates this somewhat but is not rigorous enough)

Possible improvements

  • Add hard stop loss and take profit settings as a safety net beyond signal-based exits to prevent uncontrolled losses during extreme market conditions
  • Implement dynamic position sizing that adjusts lot size based on account balance and ATR volatility
  • Add a trailing stop module to replace TrailingNone, protecting floating profits and improving risk-reward ratio

Risk assessment

Overall risk: High risk

  • Stop loss and take profit are both 0, providing no hard protection during extreme market conditions, potentially leading to massive losses
  • Fixed lot size does not adapt to account changes, automatically increasing risk ratio after losses, posing over-leveraging risk
  • Missing spread filter and slippage protection, may execute at unfavorable prices in low-liquidity markets
