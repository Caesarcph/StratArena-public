摘要

该EA基于百分比交叉策略进行交易，通过比较当前价格相对于移动平均线或参考水平的百分比位置来生成信号。当价格上穿设定百分比阈值时产生买入信号，下穿时产生卖出信号，实现系统化的趋势跟踪方法。

策略分析

策略概念基于价格与移动平均线之间的百分比偏离度交叉，当百分比偏离度从负转正时做多，从正转负时做空。入场条件为百分比指标线穿越零轴或阈值线，确认趋势方向转变后开仓。出场条件包括反向交叉信号、固定止损止盈或跟踪止损。仓位管理采用固定手数或基于资金比例的动态手数计算方式。资金管理方面允许设置最大持仓数和风险百分比限制，但缺乏根据波动率自适应调整仓位的机制。整体策略逻辑清晰，属于典型的趋势跟踪系统，但在震荡市中可能产生频繁的虚假信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on a percentage crossover strategy, comparing the current price's percentage position relative to a moving average or reference level. It generates buy signals when the price crosses above a defined percentage threshold and sell signals when crossing below, implementing a systematic trend-following approach with configurable parameters.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the percentage deviation crossover between price and a moving average, going long when the percentage deviation crosses from negative to positive and short when it crosses from positive to negative. Entry conditions rely on the percentage indicator line crossing the zero axis or a configurable threshold, confirming a trend direction change before opening positions. Exit conditions include reverse crossover signals, fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels, or trailing stop mechanisms. Position sizing uses either fixed lot sizes or dynamic lot calculation based on account equity percentage. Money management allows setting maximum open positions and risk percentage limits, but lacks volatility-adaptive position sizing. The overall strategy logic is clear and follows a typical trend-following system, though it may generate frequent false signals in ranging markets.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰易懂，百分比偏离度概念直观反映价格与均线的相对关系
  • 参数可配置性强，用户可根据不同品种和时间周期灵活调整阈值和均线周期
  • 包含止损止盈和跟踪止损机制，具备基本的风险控制能力

劣势

  • 趋势跟踪策略在震荡行情中容易产生频繁虚假信号导致连续亏损
  • 缺乏市场状态识别机制，无法自动区分趋势行情和震荡行情
  • 仓位管理较为简单，未考虑根据市场波动率自适应调整仓位大小

改进建议

  • 增加市场状态过滤器（如ADX指标），在震荡行情中减少交易频率或暂停交易
  • 引入基于ATR的动态止损止盈和仓位管理，使风险控制随市场波动率自适应调整

Strengths

  • Clear and intuitive strategy logic where percentage deviation directly reflects the relative relationship between price and moving average
  • Highly configurable parameters allowing users to flexibly adjust thresholds and MA periods for different instruments and timeframes
  • Includes stop-loss, take-profit, and trailing stop mechanisms providing fundamental risk control capabilities

Weaknesses

  • Trend-following strategy prone to frequent false signals and consecutive losses in ranging or choppy markets
  • Lacks market regime detection mechanism to automatically distinguish between trending and ranging conditions
  • Position sizing is relatively simplistic without volatility-adaptive lot adjustment based on market conditions

Improvements

  • Add a market regime filter (e.g., ADX indicator) to reduce trading frequency or pause trading during ranging markets
  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop-loss, take-profit, and position sizing for volatility-adaptive risk management
