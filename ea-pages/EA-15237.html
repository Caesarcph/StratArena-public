摘要

该EA基于StepMA_NRTR自定义指标进行交易，该指标是一种阶梯移动平均NRTR（无重绘追踪反转）系统。在向上阶梯信号时开多仓，向下阶梯信号时开空仓，支持可配置的资金管理、止损和止盈。通过新K线检测机制逐 bar 管理持仓，避免重复信号。

策略分析

该策略基于StepMA_NRTR指标的趋势跟踪信号进行交易，该指标结合了阶梯移动平均线和NRTR追踪止损逻辑。入场条件为：当指标的上行通道缓冲区（buffer 2）出现非空值时开多仓，当指标的下行通道缓冲区（buffer 3）出现非空值时开空仓。出场条件包括：反向信号触发时平仓（多仓在下行信号时平仓，空仓在上行信号时平仓），以及止损和止盈的硬性退出。仓位管理通过MarginMode枚举支持多种模式，包括固定手数、基于余额百分比和基于可用保证金百分比等方式。资金管理参数MM控制每笔交易的风险比例，默认为0.1。策略还包含一个额外的确认逻辑：在主信号判断之后，还会检查指标的趋势线缓冲区（buffer 0和1），如果趋势线也发出反向信号，则增强平仓决策。信号仅在每根新K线时评估一次，避免同一根K线内重复开仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on the StepMA_NRTR custom indicator, which is a step-moving average NRTR (No Repaint Trailing Reverse) system. It opens buy positions on upward step signals and sell positions on downward step signals, with configurable money management, stop loss, and take profit. Positions are managed per-bar using new bar detection to avoid redundant signals.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy trades based on trend-following signals from the StepMA_NRTR indicator, which combines a step-moving average with NRTR (No Repaint Trailing Reverse) logic. Entry conditions: a buy position is opened when the indicator's upper channel buffer (buffer 2) shows a non-empty value, and a sell position is opened when the lower channel buffer (buffer 3) shows a non-empty value. Exit conditions include closing on reverse signals (buy close on down signal, sell close on up signal), plus hard stop loss and take profit exits. Position sizing supports multiple modes via the MarginMode enum, including fixed lot size, percentage of balance, and percentage of free margin. The money management parameter MM controls the risk ratio per trade, defaulting to 0.1. An additional confirmation logic checks the trend line buffers (buffers 0 and 1) after the primary signal, reinforcing close decisions if the trend line also signals a reversal. Signals are evaluated only once per new bar via the CIsNewBar class, preventing redundant entries within the same bar.

优势

  • 使用新K线检测机制，避免同一根K线内重复开仓，减少过度交易
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，灵活适应不同交易者需求
  • 止损止盈参数可配置，提供基本的风险控制框架
  • 买卖方向可独立开关，便于单向交易或策略测试
  • 信号确认逻辑双重验证，先检查通道信号再检查趋势线信号

劣势

  • 止损设置为1000点、止盈2000点，对于H1周期可能过大，容易承受较大浮亏
  • 缺少移动止损或追踪止损机制，无法锁定浮动利润
  • 依赖自定义指标StepMA_NRTR，指标文件缺失时EA无法运行
  • 没有点差过滤机制，在高点差环境下可能不利
  • OnInit中存在冗余的return语句（第85行），代码不够严谨

改进建议

  • 增加追踪止损功能，在趋势运行中逐步锁定利润
  • 添加点差过滤和时间交易过滤，避免高成本和高波动时段入场

Strengths

  • New bar detection prevents redundant entries within the same bar, reducing overtrading
  • Supports multiple money management modes, flexibly adapting to different trader needs
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit parameters provide a basic risk control framework
  • Buy and sell directions can be independently toggled, facilitating one-direction trading or strategy testing
  • Dual signal confirmation logic checks channel signals first, then trend line signals for validation

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points may be excessively large for H1 timeframe, risking significant drawdowns
  • Lacks trailing stop mechanism to lock in floating profits as trend progresses
  • Depends on the custom StepMA_NRTR indicator file; EA cannot run if the indicator is missing
  • No spread filter, which may cause unfavorable entries in high-spread conditions
  • Redundant return statement in OnInit (line 85), indicating sloppy code structure

Improvements

  • Add a trailing stop feature to progressively lock in profits as the trend develops
  • Add spread filter and session/time filter to avoid entries during high-cost and high-volatility periods
