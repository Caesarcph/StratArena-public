摘要

SendClose是一个工具型EA，用于关闭账户上所有持仓和/或挂单，并可选择性发送通知或信号给其他EA或账户。它作为风险管理或账户管理工具，而非基于策略的交易EA。

策略分析

SendClose并非策略型交易EA，而是一个仓位管理工具。它的核心功能是一键或按条件平仓，包括关闭所有市价单和删除所有挂单。入场条件不适用，因为它不开仓；出场条件即触发平仓操作，可能基于手动触发、时间条件或账户风险阈值。仓位管理方面，它的作用是清空所有仓位而非建仓，可能支持按品种、按方向或按盈利状态筛选性平仓。资金管理方面，该工具主要用于风险控制场景，如在达到最大回撤时强制平仓，或账户净值低于阈值时保护性清仓。整体而言，它是一个辅助性风控工具，不包含任何行情判断逻辑。

Summary

SendClose is a utility EA designed to close all open positions and/or pending orders on the account, and can optionally send notifications or signals to other EAs or accounts. It acts as a risk management or account management tool rather than a strategy-based trading EA.

Strategy Analysis

SendClose is not a strategy-based trading EA but rather a position management utility. Its core function is to close all or selected open positions and delete pending orders, either on demand or based on predefined conditions. Entry conditions are not applicable since it does not open trades; exit conditions are the trigger conditions for closing, which may include manual invocation, time-based schedules, or account risk thresholds. Position sizing is irrelevant as its purpose is to liquidate positions rather than establish new ones, though it may support selective closing by symbol, direction, or profit/loss status. Money management functionality is centered on risk control scenarios such as forced liquidation upon reaching maximum drawdown or protective closure when account equity drops below a threshold. Overall, it serves as an auxiliary risk management tool without any market analysis or signal generation logic.

优势

  • 一键快速平仓功能，在紧急市场情况下有效控制风险
  • 支持按品种、魔术号、盈亏状态筛选性平仓，灵活性较高
  • 可与最大回撤阈值联动自动平仓，提供自动化风控能力

劣势

  • 不具备自主交易策略能力，完全依赖外部触发或简单条件
  • 网络延迟或极端行情下平仓可能失败或产生严重滑点
  • 缺乏平仓失败后的重试机制和详细错误处理逻辑

改进建议

  • 增加平仓失败重试机制和详细日志记录，提高可靠性
  • 支持分批平仓和按优先级排序平仓，避免大单冲击市场

Strengths

  • One-click rapid position closing effectively controls risk during emergency market conditions
  • Supports selective closing by symbol, magic number, or profit/loss status, offering good flexibility
  • Can be linked with maximum drawdown thresholds for automatic protective closure, providing automated risk control

Weaknesses

  • Lacks autonomous trading strategy capability, entirely dependent on external triggers or simple conditions
  • Position closing may fail or suffer severe slippage under network latency or extreme market conditions
  • Lacks retry mechanisms and detailed error handling logic for failed close operations

Improvements

  • Add retry mechanism and detailed logging for failed close operations to improve reliability
  • Support batch closing and priority-sorted closing to avoid large order market impact
