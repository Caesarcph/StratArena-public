摘要

SendClose是一个工具类EA，用于关闭当前图表品种上的所有持仓或挂单。它提供了一种通过程序或按钮触发的方式来清算指定品种的所有敞口。该EA不会产生自己的交易信号或开立新仓位。

策略分析

该EA不包含任何交易策略逻辑，其核心功能是批量平仓和删除挂单。入场条件不适用，因为该EA不会开仓。出场条件为触发关闭操作时，遍历当前品种的所有持仓并逐一平仓，同时删除所有相关挂单。仓位管理方面，该EA仅处理已有仓位的关闭，不涉及新建仓位的大小计算。资金管理方面，该EA不涉及资金分配或风险比例控制。其主要用途是作为紧急平仓工具或与其他策略EA配合使用，实现一键平仓功能。

Summary

SendClose is a utility Expert Advisor designed to close open positions or pending orders on the current chart symbol. It provides a programmatic or button-triggered way to liquidate all exposure for a given instrument. The EA does not generate its own trade signals or open new positions.

Strategy Analysis

This EA does not contain any trading strategy logic; its core functionality is batch closing of positions and deletion of pending orders. Entry conditions are not applicable since the EA does not open trades. Exit conditions consist of iterating through all open positions for the current symbol and closing them one by one, while also deleting all related pending orders. For position sizing, the EA only handles the closure of existing positions and does not calculate lot sizes for new entries. Money management is not involved as the EA does not allocate capital or control risk ratios. Its primary purpose is to serve as an emergency close-all tool or to work alongside other strategy EAs for one-click liquidation.

优势

  • 一键平仓操作简便快捷，适合紧急情况使用
  • 可按品种和魔术号过滤，灵活控制平仓范围
  • 同时处理持仓和挂单，清理彻底不留残留订单

劣势

  • 无确认机制，误触可能导致意外平仓
  • 缺乏部分平仓功能，无法精细控制平仓比例
  • 未处理平仓失败的重试逻辑，网络不稳定时可能遗漏仓位

改进建议

  • 增加平仓前的确认对话框或二次确认机制，防止误操作
  • 添加平仓失败的重试机制和日志记录，提高可靠性

Strengths

  • One-click close operation is simple and fast, suitable for emergency use
  • Filtering by symbol and magic number provides flexible control over which positions to close
  • Handles both open positions and pending orders simultaneously for thorough cleanup

Weaknesses

  • No confirmation mechanism; accidental trigger may cause unintended position closure
  • Lacks partial close functionality; cannot fine-tune the proportion of positions to close
  • No retry logic for failed close operations; positions may be missed during network instability

Improvements

  • Add a confirmation dialog or double-confirmation mechanism before closing to prevent accidental operations
  • Implement retry logic and logging for failed close operations to improve reliability
