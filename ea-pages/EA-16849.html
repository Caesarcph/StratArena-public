摘要

该EA实现双EMA交叉策略：短期EMA上穿长期EMA时做多，下穿时做空。包含固定止盈止损和移动止损功能以保护持仓利润。Reverse参数允许反转信号方向进行逆向交易。

策略分析

策略概念基于双指数移动平均线交叉，短期EMA(9)与长期EMA(45)的交叉作为趋势转折信号。入场条件为：当短EMA上穿长EMA时开多单，下穿时开空单，Reverse参数可反转信号方向使交叉信号含义对调。出场条件包括固定止盈(25点)和止损(105点)，以及移动止损(20点)跟踪保护利润。仓位管理采用固定手数(0.5手)，没有任何动态仓位调整或资金管理逻辑，代码中TODO注释也提到需要添加资金管理模块。风险管理方面，止损设置大于止盈(105 vs 25)，风险回报比约4:1不利于单笔交易，但依赖趋势延续来弥补。移动止损仅修改止损位而不修改止盈位，且只允许向有利方向移动。

Summary

This EA implements a dual EMA crossover strategy that opens buy positions when the short EMA crosses above the long EMA and sell positions when it crosses below. It includes fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels plus a trailing stop feature to protect profits on open positions. A Reverse input allows inverting the signal direction for contrarian trading.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a dual exponential moving average crossover, where the crossover of the short EMA(9) and long EMA(45) serves as a trend reversal signal. Entry conditions: a buy position is opened when the short EMA crosses above the long EMA, and a sell position when it crosses below; the Reverse parameter inverts signal direction for contrarian trading. Exit conditions include a fixed take-profit (25 points) and stop-loss (105 points), plus a trailing stop (20 points) that follows the price to protect accumulated profits. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (0.5 lots) with no dynamic sizing or money management logic; the code's TODO comment explicitly notes the need for a money management routine. Risk management features a stop-loss much larger than take-profit (105 vs 25), yielding an unfavorable risk-reward ratio of approximately 4:1 per trade, relying on trend continuation to compensate. The trailing stop only adjusts the stop-loss toward profit and never modifies the take-profit level, and it only moves in the favorable direction.

优势

  • 逻辑简单清晰，双EMA交叉是经典可靠的趋势跟踪方法
  • 包含移动止损功能，能有效锁定已有浮盈
  • Reverse参数提供灵活性，可根据市场状态切换顺势或逆势交易
  • 代码结构清晰，使用MQL5标准交易类库(CTrade/CPositionInfo/CSymbolInfo)，可维护性好

劣势

  • 固定手数无资金管理，无法根据账户余额或波动率动态调整仓位
  • 风险回报比差(止盈25点vs止损105点)，单笔亏损远大于单笔盈利
  • 无新K线检测机制，每个tick都会执行信号判断，可能导致重复入场或过度交易
  • Crossed函数使用静态变量，EA重启后状态丢失，可能错过首次交叉信号
  • 无点差过滤，在点差较大时小止盈(25点)极易被吞没

改进建议

  • 添加基于账户余额或ATR的动态仓位管理模块
  • 增加新K线检测避免每个tick重复计算，提高效率并防止重复交易
  • 添加点差过滤，当点差超过阈值时暂停开仓，保护小止盈策略
  • 改进Crossed函数，持久化上次方向状态避免EA重启后状态丢失
  • 优化风险回报比，考虑将止盈止损比例调整至更合理的水平

Strengths

  • Simple and clear logic; dual EMA crossover is a classic and reliable trend-following method
  • Includes trailing stop functionality to effectively lock in unrealized profits
  • Reverse parameter provides flexibility to switch between trend-following and contrarian modes based on market conditions
  • Clean code structure using MQL5 standard trade libraries (CTrade/CPositionInfo/CSymbolInfo) for good maintainability

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot size with no money management; cannot dynamically adjust position based on account balance or volatility
  • Poor risk-reward ratio (TP 25 points vs SL 105 points); single loss far exceeds single gain
  • No new bar detection; signal evaluation runs on every tick, potentially causing redundant entries or over-trading
  • Crossed function uses static variables that reset on EA restart, potentially missing the first crossover signal
  • No spread filter; the small take-profit (25 points) can easily be swallowed by wide spreads

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account balance or ATR for proper money management
  • Add new bar detection to avoid redundant calculations on every tick, improving efficiency and preventing duplicate trades
  • Add spread filtering to pause new entries when spread exceeds a threshold, protecting the small take-profit strategy
  • Improve the Crossed function by persisting the last direction state to prevent signal loss on EA restart
  • Optimize the risk-reward ratio by adjusting the take-profit to stop-loss proportion to more reasonable levels
