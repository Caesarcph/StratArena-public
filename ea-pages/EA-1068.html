摘要

该EA在三个不同周期（H6、H3、H1）上使用抛物线SAR指标生成趋势跟踪交易信号。买入信号需要三个周期的SAR均在收盘价下方且最快周期出现看涨反转，卖出信号反之。持仓以固定止损止盈管理。

策略分析

策略概念基于多周期抛物线SAR共振确认趋势方向，只有在三个时间框架的趋势一致时才开仓。入场条件为：买入需SAR1<SAR2<SAR3均低于各自收盘价，且第三周期SAR从上方穿越到下方（Sar_3[0]>Close_3[0]且Sar_3[1]<Close_3[1]）；卖出条件反之。出场条件包括SAR反转触发的方向性平仓信号以及固定点数的止损止盈。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值为固定手数，正值为按存款比例计算。资金管理依赖外部TradeAlgorithms.mqh库函数实现，包括BuyPositionOpen和SellPositionOpen等。该策略使用新柱检测机制避免重复信号，但在信号柱索引为0时会在每个tick都进行计算，可能增加不必要的运算负担。

Summary

This EA uses three Parabolic SAR indicators on different timeframes (H6, H3, H1) to generate trend-following trade signals. A buy signal occurs when all three SAR values are below their respective close prices, with the fastest timeframe showing a fresh bullish reversal. Sell signals are the inverse. Positions are managed with fixed stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on multi-timeframe Parabolic SAR confluence to confirm trend direction, only opening positions when all three timeframes align. Entry conditions: a buy requires all three SAR values below their respective close prices, with the fastest timeframe showing a fresh bullish reversal (Sar_3[0]>Close_3[0] and Sar_3[1]<Close_3[1]); sell conditions are the inverse. Exit conditions include SAR reversal-triggered directional close signals and fixed stop loss/take profit in points. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the deposit. Money management relies on external TradeAlgorithms.mqh library functions such as BuyPositionOpen and SellPositionOpen. The strategy uses a new bar detection mechanism to avoid repeated signals, but when SignalBar is 0, calculations occur on every tick, potentially increasing unnecessary computational overhead.

优势

  • 多周期共振确认趋势，减少假信号
  • 三重时间框架过滤提高了入场质量
  • 新柱检测机制避免同一信号重复开仓
  • 买卖开平仓权限可独立控制，灵活性好
  • 使用抛物线SAR自带跟踪止损特性，趋势跟踪能力较强

劣势

  • 三重SAR共振信号较少，可能长时间无交易机会
  • SAR在震荡市中频繁反转产生亏损信号
  • 缺少震荡市场过滤机制，横盘时容易反复开平仓
  • 止损1000点止盈2000点的固定比例不适合所有品种
  • 依赖外部TradeAlgorithms.mqh库，核心交易逻辑不可见

改进建议

  • 增加ADX或波动率过滤器，避免在震荡市场中频繁交易
  • 增加追踪止损功能，让盈利仓位获得更多趋势空间
  • 优化MM参数说明，明确正负值的计算逻辑
  • 添加最大回撤保护和最大连续亏损限制

Strengths

  • Multi-timeframe confluence confirmation reduces false signals
  • Triple timeframe filtering improves entry quality
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate signal entries
  • Buy/sell open/close permissions can be controlled independently for flexibility
  • Parabolic SAR inherently provides trailing stop-like behavior for strong trend following

Weaknesses

  • Triple SAR confluence signals are infrequent, potentially leading to long periods without trades
  • SAR frequently reverses in ranging markets, generating losing signals
  • No ranging market filter, prone to repeated open/close cycles during sideways movement
  • Fixed 1000-point SL and 2000-point TP ratio is not suitable for all instruments
  • Core trading logic depends on external TradeAlgorithms.mqh library, making it opaque

Improvements

  • Add ADX or volatility filter to avoid frequent trading in ranging markets
  • Add trailing stop functionality to let winning positions capture more trend movement
  • Clarify MM parameter documentation to explicitly explain positive vs negative value calculation logic
  • Add maximum drawdown protection and maximum consecutive loss limits
