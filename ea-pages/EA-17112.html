摘要

该EA基于Heiken Ashi蜡烛方向与StepUpDown趋势过滤器（由快速SMA(2)和慢速SMMA(30)关系推导）的共振信号进行交易。当两个指标同时确认上升趋势时开多仓，同时确认下降趋势时开空仓，支持可配置的移动止损、基于风险的仓位计算和反向信号平仓功能。

策略分析

策略概念基于Heiken Ashi平滑K线与StepUpDown趋势指标的双重确认，属于典型的趋势跟踪系统。入场条件为：Heiken Ashi蜡烛方向（haOpen<haClose为多头，haOpen>haClose为空头）与StepUpDown趋势方向（基于SMA(2)与SMMA(30)的偏离及高低点位置判断）必须一致才开仓。出场条件包括三种方式：反向信号平仓（CloseByOppositeSignal）、移动止损（TrailingStop）跟踪出场、以及无移动止损时基于盈利和反向StepUpDown信号的获利平仓。仓位管理支持固定手数和动态手数两种模式，动态手数根据账户余额、风险百分比、杠杆和合约大小计算，并除以持仓数量+1进行分配。资金管理通过RiskPercentage参数控制每笔交易的风险比例，止损距离由资金风险公式计算得出，止盈设为止损的TPvsSLRatio倍数，最低止损距离为38个点。此外EA还设置了同一小时内不重复开仓的限制，通过检查历史订单关闭时间来避免过度交易。

Summary

This EA trades based on the confluence of Heiken Ashi candle direction and a StepUpDown trend filter derived from the relationship between a fast SMA(2) and a slow SMMA(30). It opens buy positions when both indicators confirm an uptrend and sell positions when both confirm a downtrend, with configurable trailing stops, risk-based lot sizing, and opposite-signal position closing.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on dual confirmation from Heiken Ashi smoothed candles and a StepUpDown trend filter, constituting a typical trend-following system. Entry conditions require both Heiken Ashi candle direction (haOpen<haClose for bullish, haOpen>haClose for bearish) and StepUpDown trend direction (determined by SMA(2) vs SMMA(30) divergence and highest/lowest point positions) to align before opening a position. Exit conditions include three methods: closing on opposite signal (CloseByOppositeSignal), trailing stop tracking for exit, and profitable exit based on current profit and reverse StepUpDown signal when trailing stop is disabled. Position sizing supports both fixed lot and dynamic lot modes, with the dynamic mode calculating lots based on account balance, risk percentage, leverage, and contract size, then dividing by the number of open positions plus one. Money management controls per-trade risk via the RiskPercentage parameter, with stop loss distance calculated from a risk formula and take profit set as a multiple (TPvsSLRatio) of the stop loss, with a minimum stop distance of 38 pips. Additionally, the EA implements a same-hour re-entry restriction by checking historical order close times to avoid overtrading.

优势

  • 双指标共振确认机制降低了虚假信号入场的概率
  • 支持动态手数计算和风险百分比控制，资金管理较为完善
  • 提供移动止损功能，能有效锁定盈利并跟踪趋势
  • 设置了同小时不开重复仓的限制，避免过度交易
  • 止损距离有最低38点保护，防止止损过小被扫损

劣势

  • SMA(2)周期极短，StepUpDown趋势判断容易产生频繁的虚假反转信号
  • 定时器每分钟触发但交易逻辑依赖分钟变化，在快速行情中可能反应滞后
  • 对象指针（CTrade等）在OnTimer中每分钟new但从未delete，存在内存泄漏
  • ModifySLTP中使用break跳出循环，只修改第一个符合条件的持仓，多仓场景下处理不完整
  • CheckOrderCloseTime中HistoryOrderGetInteger使用order_ID作为ticket参数，存在逻辑错误
  • ClosePosition中PositionGetInteger在循环外调用，可能获取到错误的持仓信息

改进建议

  • 修复内存泄漏：将CTrade等对象的创建移至OnInit，或在使用后及时delete
  • 优化StepUpDown趋势判断逻辑，增加SMA周期或添加趋势强度过滤条件以减少虚假信号
  • 修复CheckOrderCloseTime中HistoryOrderGetInteger的错误参数传递
  • ModifySLTP中移除break语句，使其能遍历并修改所有符合条件的持仓
  • 添加点差过滤和滑点保护，避免在高点差环境下开仓

Strengths

  • Dual indicator confluence confirmation reduces the probability of entering on false signals
  • Supports dynamic lot calculation and risk percentage control with relatively sound money management
  • Provides trailing stop functionality to effectively lock in profits and follow trends
  • Implements same-hour re-entry restriction to avoid overtrading
  • Minimum 38-pip stop loss distance protects against stops being too tight and getting swept

Weaknesses

  • SMA(2) period is extremely short, making StepUpDown trend judgment prone to frequent false reversal signals
  • Timer triggers every minute but trading logic depends on minute change, potentially lagging in fast markets
  • Object pointers (CTrade etc.) are created with 'new' every minute in OnTimer but never deleted, causing memory leaks
  • ModifySLTP uses 'break' to exit the loop, only modifying the first matching position, which is incomplete for multi-position scenarios
  • CheckOrderCloseTime uses order_ID as the ticket parameter in HistoryOrderGetInteger, which is a logical error
  • ClosePosition calls PositionGetInteger outside the loop, potentially retrieving incorrect position information

Improvements

  • Fix memory leaks: move CTrade etc. object creation to OnInit, or delete them promptly after use
  • Optimize StepUpDown trend logic by increasing SMA period or adding trend strength filters to reduce false signals
  • Fix the incorrect parameter passing of HistoryOrderGetInteger in CheckOrderCloseTime
  • Remove break statements in ModifySLTP to allow iterating and modifying all matching positions
  • Add spread filter and slippage protection to avoid opening positions in high-spread environments

  • Memory leak issue can cause resource exhaustion during long-term operation
  • Logical error in CheckOrderCloseTime may cause the same-hour re-entry restriction to fail
