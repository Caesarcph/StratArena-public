摘要

ExpHAWaves是一个基于MT5的趋势跟踪EA，结合了Heiken Ashi蜡烛方向和StepUpDown指标（基于SMMA背离）产生交易信号。它支持基于风险百分比的动态或固定手数、追踪止损以及反向信号平仓功能。EA基于定时器每分钟运行而非每个tick。

策略分析

策略概念是结合Heiken Ashi蜡烛图方向判断与StepUpDown趋势指标的双重确认系统。入场条件要求两个信号一致：当Heiken Ashi为看涨（haOpen<haClose）且StepUpDown为上升趋势时做多，反之为看跌且下降趋势时做空。StepUpDown指标通过快速SMA(2)与慢速SMMA(30)的背离以及价格相对高低点位置来判断趋势方向。出场条件包括：反向信号平仓、追踪止损触发、以及非追踪止损模式下获利后趋势反转平仓。仓位管理支持固定手数和动态手数两种模式，动态模式根据账户余额和风险百分比计算，并除以持仓数+1进行调整。资金管理通过CalculateSLTP函数计算止损距离，考虑了杠杆风险、tick价值和点差，并设有最低38点pip的止损下限，止盈为止损乘以TPvsSLRatio倍数。

Summary

ExpHAWaves is a trend-following Expert Advisor for MT5 that combines Heiken Ashi candlestick direction with a StepUpDown indicator (based on SMMA divergence) to generate trade signals. It employs dynamic or fixed lot sizing based on risk percentage, supports trailing stops, and can close positions on opposite signals. The EA operates on a timer-based minute interval rather than every tick.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is a dual-confirmation system combining Heiken Ashi candlestick direction with a StepUpDown trend indicator. Entry conditions require both signals to align: a buy signal is generated when Heiken Ashi is bullish (haOpen<haClose) AND StepUpDown indicates uptrend, while a sell signal requires both to be bearish. The StepUpDown indicator determines trend direction by analyzing the divergence between a fast SMA(2) and slow SMMA(30), along with price position relative to recent highs and lows over a lookback window. Exit conditions include closing on opposite signals (configurable), trailing stop activation, and profit-taking when trend reverses without trailing stops enabled. Position sizing supports both fixed lot and dynamic lot modes, with the dynamic mode calculating lots based on account balance and risk percentage, divided by the number of open positions plus one. Money management calculates stop loss distance via the CalculateSLTP function, accounting for leverage risk, tick value, and spread, with a minimum SL of 38 pips, while take profit is set as the SL multiplied by the configurable TPvsSLRatio.

优势

  • 双重信号确认机制（Heiken Ashi + StepUpDown）减少假信号
  • 支持动态手数和固定手数两种资金管理模式，灵活性好
  • 内置追踪止损功能，能有效锁定盈利
  • 止损计算考虑了杠杆风险和tick价值，相对合理
  • 同一小时内不重复开仓的防护机制避免过度交易

劣势

  • Heiken Ashi计算仅使用2根K线，信号可能过于敏感或不稳定
  • StepUpDown指标使用SMA(2)周期极短，容易产生频繁信号切换
  • 内存泄漏：每分钟在OnTimer中new创建对象但从未delete释放
  • 止损最低38点pip的硬编码限制不适合所有品种和交易风格
  • CloseOrderProfit函数中仅凭趋势反转且浮盈为正就平仓，逻辑过于简单

改进建议

  • 修复OnTimer中的内存泄漏问题，将对象创建移至OnInit并在OnDeinit中释放
  • 将Heiken Ashi的计算扩展到更多K线以获得更稳定的信号，并加入新K线检测避免重复计算
  • 增加点差过滤和最大持仓数限制以增强风险控制

Strengths

  • Dual signal confirmation (Heiken Ashi + StepUpDown) reduces false signals
  • Supports both dynamic and fixed lot sizing for flexible money management
  • Built-in trailing stop functionality effectively locks in profits
  • Stop loss calculation accounts for leverage risk and tick value, relatively sound
  • Same-hour no-repeat-entry guard prevents overtrading

Weaknesses

  • Heiken Ashi calculation uses only 2 bars, signals may be overly sensitive or unstable
  • StepUpDown indicator uses extremely short SMA(2) period, prone to frequent signal flipping
  • Memory leak: objects created with 'new' in OnTimer every minute are never deleted
  • Hardcoded minimum SL of 38 pips is unsuitable for all instruments and trading styles
  • CloseOrderProfit exits solely on trend reversal with positive floating profit, logic is oversimplified

Improvements

  • Fix the memory leak in OnTimer by moving object creation to OnInit and releasing in OnDeinit
  • Extend Heiken Ashi calculation to more bars for more stable signals, and add new bar detection to avoid redundant processing
  • Add spread filter and maximum position count limit for enhanced risk control

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • OnTimer中每分钟new创建CTrade等对象但从不delete，长期运行会导致严重内存泄漏和最终崩溃
  • 动态手数计算除以持仓数+1，但默认最小为5，可能导致手数过小或计算不符合预期
  • 追踪止损中break语句导致只修改第一个符合条件的持仓，多持仓场景下存在风险

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Objects created with 'new' in OnTimer every minute are never deleted, causing severe memory leak and eventual crash in long-running sessions
  • Dynamic lot calculation divides by max(PositionsTotal+1, 5), which may result in unexpectedly small lots or deviate from user expectation
  • The 'break' statement in trailing stop loop only modifies the first matching position, creating risk in multi-position scenarios
