摘要

该EA结合Laguerre指标、CCI和MA交叉产生交易信号。当Laguerre为零、MA上升且CCI低于负阈值时开多仓，相反条件开空仓。支持虚拟止损/止盈，并在连续亏损后减少仓位。

策略分析

策略概念基于三个指标的组合信号：Laguerre指标判断趋势方向（0为多头区域，1为空头区域），MA交叉确认动量方向，CCI作为超买超卖过滤。入场条件：做多需Laguerre等于0、当前MA值大于前一根MA值且CCI低于-CCILevel；做空需Laguerre等于1、当前MA值小于前一根MA值且CCI高于CCILevel。出场条件：多头在Laguerre大于0.9时平仓，空头在Laguerre小于0.1时平仓。仓位管理支持固定手数或基于风险的动态手数计算，还有连续亏损后的减仓机制。资金管理方面，当Lots为0时根据自由保证金和MaximumRisk计算手数，DecreaseFactor参数控制亏损后的减仓幅度。虚拟止损止盈功能允许在不出示真实SL/TP的情况下按价位平仓。

Summary

This EA uses a combination of the Laguerre indicator, CCI, and MA crossover to generate trade signals. It opens buy positions when Laguerre is zero, MA is rising, and CCI is below a negative threshold, and sell positions under opposite conditions. The EA supports virtual stop-loss/take-profit and includes lot reduction after consecutive losses.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy combines three indicators: the Laguerre indicator for trend direction (0 = bullish zone, 1 = bearish zone), MA crossover for momentum confirmation, and CCI as an overbought/oversold filter. Entry conditions: buy requires Laguerre equal to 0, current MA above previous MA, and CCI below -CCILevel; sell requires Laguerre equal to 1, current MA below previous MA, and CCI above CCILevel. Exit conditions: close buy when Laguerre exceeds 0.9, close sell when Laguerre drops below 0.1. Position sizing supports fixed lots or risk-based dynamic calculation using free margin and MaximumRisk. The DecreaseFactor mechanism reduces lot size after consecutive losing trades to preserve capital. Virtual SL/TP allows the EA to close positions at specified price levels without placing actual stop orders on the broker side.

优势

  • 多指标组合过滤，减少虚假信号
  • 虚拟止损止盈保护策略隐私，避免经纪商猎杀止损
  • 连续亏损减仓机制有助于资金保护
  • 支持固定手数和动态风险手数两种模式
  • 信号逻辑清晰，Laguerre提供趋势方向判断
  • 有止损检查机制，避免不合理的止损设置

劣势

  • Laguerre等于0或1的精确匹配条件过于严格，可能错过信号
  • Shift=0时在未完成K线上交易，信号可能频繁变化
  • 无点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能不利
  • SolveLots计算公式简化，未考虑杠杆和品种规范
  • 多头开仓时错误使用了SolveBuySL和SolveBuyTP而非Sell版本
  • 无新K线检查当Shift=0时可能导致重复交易

改进建议

  • 修复卖出仓位使用了SolveBuySL/SolveBuyTP而非SolveSellSL/SolveSellTP的bug
  • 增加新K线检查机制避免Shift=0时的重复信号
  • 增加点差过滤条件，避免高点差时开仓
  • 将Laguerre条件从精确等于0/1改为阈值比较以增加交易机会

Strengths

  • Multi-indicator combination filters reduce false signals
  • Virtual SL/TP protects strategy privacy and avoids broker stop-hunting
  • Decrease lot mechanism after consecutive losses helps capital preservation
  • Supports both fixed lots and dynamic risk-based lot sizing
  • Clear signal logic with Laguerre providing directional judgment
  • Stop level validation prevents unreasonable SL/TP placement

Weaknesses

  • Laguerre exact equality to 0 or 1 is overly strict and may miss signals
  • Trading on incomplete bar when Shift=0 can cause signal flickering
  • No spread filter may be detrimental in high-spread environments
  • Simplified lot calculation without considering leverage and symbol specifications
  • Bug: sell entry incorrectly uses SolveBuySL/SolveBuyTP instead of Sell versions
  • No new bar check when Shift=0 may cause repeated trades on the same bar

Improvements

  • Fix the bug where sell positions use SolveBuySL/SolveBuyTP instead of SolveSellSL/SolveSellTP
  • Add new bar check mechanism to prevent repeated signals when Shift=0
  • Add spread filter to avoid opening positions during high-spread conditions
  • Change Laguerre condition from exact equality to threshold comparison for more trading opportunities
