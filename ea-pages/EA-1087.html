EA-1087 · Momentum

starter.mq5

This EA trades based on the Laguerre indicator combined with CCI and MA crossover signals. It opens buy positions when Laguerre is zero, MA is rising, and CCI is below a negative threshold, and sell positions on the mirror conditions. Positions are closed when Laguerre reaches opposite extreme levels, with optional virtual stop-loss/take-profit functionality.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on the Laguerre indicator's overbought/oversold states combined with CCI momentum confirmation and MA trend direction filtering. Entry conditions: a buy requires Laguerre at 0 (extreme oversold), MA current value above previous value (uptrend confirmation), and CCI below the negative threshold (momentum at downward extreme); sell conditions are the mirror image with Laguerre at 1 (extreme overbought), MA declining, and CCI above the positive threshold. Exit conditions: buy positions close when Laguerre exceeds 0.9, sell positions close when Laguerre drops below 0.1. Position sizing supports fixed lots or proportional risk calculation based on free margin, with a lot decrease factor after consecutive losses (DecreaseFactor). Money management calculates lots as free margin multiplied by risk percentage divided by 1000, with lot normalization to satisfy broker minimum, maximum, and step requirements. The virtual SL/TP feature simulates stop-loss and take-profit by checking position P&L on every tick rather than placing actual SL/TP orders on the server.

Strengths

  • Triple indicator filtering (Laguerre+CCI+MA) reduces false signals
  • Virtual SL/TP hides trading intentions from brokers who might otherwise see strategy levels
  • Lot decrease mechanism after losses helps control risk exposure during losing streaks
  • Lot normalization is thorough, accounting for broker minimum/maximum/step constraints

Weaknesses

  • Sell opening incorrectly calls SolveBuySL and SolveBuyTP instead of SolveSellSL and SolveSellTP, a critical logic bug
  • CCI level default of 5 is extremely low, making the CCI filter virtually useless as nearly all market conditions satisfy it
  • Shift default of 0 means trading on an uncompleted bar, prone to false signals and repainting issues
  • No spread filter or trading session filter, will trade even during extreme spreads or inactive hours

Possible improvements

  • Fix the critical bug where sell opening calls SolveBuySL/SolveBuyTP instead of SolveSellSL/SolveSellTP
  • Change Shift default to 1 to avoid trading on uncompleted bars and repainting issues
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter functionality
  • Raise CCILevel default to a more reasonable value (e.g., 50-100) so the CCI filter is actually effective

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Incorrect SL/TP function calls for sell direction may place stops in the wrong direction
  • Trading on uncompleted bars with Shift=0 risks signal flickering and repeated entries
  • Virtual SL/TP depends on client-side execution; disconnection means stops won't trigger
