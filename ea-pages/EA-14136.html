摘要

该EA使用自定义DigitalF-T01振荡器指标，基于两个振荡器缓冲区之间的交叉逻辑生成买卖信号。根据信号线交叉开仓和平仓，支持可配置的资金管理、止损和止盈参数。

策略分析

策略概念基于DigitalF-T01自定义振荡器指标的两条信号线交叉。入场条件：当Osc1从下方穿越Osc2上方时产生买入信号，当Osc1从上方穿越Osc2下方时产生卖出信号，仅在上一根K线两条线处于相反方向关系时才触发信号，确认交叉发生。出场条件：买入信号的交叉同时触发卖出平仓，卖出信号的交叉同时触发买入平仓，也支持止损和止盈出场。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，支持固定手数或基于保证金的仓位模式，由MMMode参数决定使用LOT还是其他模式。资金管理使用TradeAlgorithms.mqh库中的标准函数处理开平仓逻辑。策略使用新K线检测机制避免同一根K线内重复计算信号，仅在新K线出现时重新评估交易信号。

Summary

This EA uses the custom DigitalF-T01 oscillator indicator to generate buy and sell signals based on crossover logic between two oscillator buffers. It opens and closes positions based on signal line crossings, with configurable money management, stop loss, and take profit parameters.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the crossover of two signal lines from the custom DigitalF-T01 oscillator indicator. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when Osc1 crosses above Osc2, and a sell signal is generated when Osc1 crosses below Osc2, with the requirement that the previous bar showed the opposite directional relationship to confirm the crossover. Exit conditions: the crossover generating a buy signal simultaneously triggers sell position closure, and vice versa for buy position closure; stop loss and take profit levels also serve as exit mechanisms. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, supporting either fixed lot size or margin-based position mode as determined by the MMMode parameter choosing between LOT and other margin modes. Money management leverages the standard functions from the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library to handle opening and closing logic. The strategy employs a new bar detection mechanism via CIsNewBar to avoid redundant signal calculations within the same bar, recalculating trade signals only when a new bar appears.

优势

  • 使用振荡器交叉逻辑，策略概念清晰简洁
  • 具备止损和止盈保护，风险可控
  • 支持买入和卖出方向独立开关配置，灵活性高
  • 使用新K线检测避免重复信号，减少不必要的交易
  • 信号确认要求前一根K线方向相反，降低假信号概率

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标DigitalF-T01，指标逻辑不透明且不可审查
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值过大，可能导致较大回撤
  • 没有点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • 没有持仓检查逻辑，可能在已有同向持仓时重复开仓
  • 缺少市场状态过滤（如波动率、交易时段），在任何市场条件下都会交易

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤，避免在点差过大时开仓
  • 增加持仓方向检查，防止同向重复开仓
  • 增加波动率或交易时段过滤，减少不利市场条件下的交易

Strengths

  • Clear and concise strategy concept using oscillator crossover logic
  • Includes stop loss and take profit protection for risk control
  • Independent enable/disable switches for buy and sell directions offer high flexibility
  • New bar detection prevents duplicate signals and reduces unnecessary trades
  • Signal confirmation requiring opposite direction on previous bar reduces false signals

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom DigitalF-T01 indicator whose internal logic is opaque and not auditable
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are very wide, potentially causing large drawdowns
  • No spread filter, which may result in unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • No position check logic, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Lacks market state filtering (volatility, trading sessions), trades under all market conditions

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter to avoid opening positions when spread is excessively wide
  • Add position direction checking to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Add volatility or trading session filters to reduce trades in unfavorable market conditions
