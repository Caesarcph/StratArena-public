Strategy analysis

The strategy is centered on the Williams' Percent Range (WPR) indicator displayed in histogram form, combined with volume filtering to confirm signal validity. Entry conditions are: go long when the WPR histogram crosses above its signal line from negative territory with increasing volume; go short when the histogram crosses below its signal line from positive territory with increasing volume. The EA employs a direct trading mode (Direct), meaning it opens new positions upon signal rather than reversing existing ones. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit parameters, and may include a trailing stop feature to protect accumulated profits. Position sizing is controlled through lot size parameters, supporting either fixed lots or dynamic lot calculation based on account equity percentage. Money management includes maximum position limits and stop loss protection, though the effectiveness of risk controls depends heavily on the reasonableness of parameter configuration.

Strengths

  • Volume filtering effectively reduces false signals and improves entry quality
  • Direct trading mode has clear logic, avoiding complexity and extra risk from reversal trading
  • WPR histogram format is more intuitive than traditional overbought/oversold judgment for signal identification
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit parameters provide a basic risk control framework
  • Trailing stop feature helps lock in floating profits

Weaknesses

  • WPR indicator tends to generate frequent false signals in ranging markets, leading to repeated stop-outs
  • Volume confirmation parameters (such as volume multiplier) are difficult to calibrate precisely and adapt poorly to different market conditions
  • Lacks new bar check mechanism, potentially opening multiple positions on the same bar
  • No spread filter, making stop losses vulnerable to being triggered in high-spread environments
  • Strategy logic is relatively simple, lacking multi-timeframe confirmation and market regime detection

Possible improvements

  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation, such as executing signals on H1 only after H4 trend direction is confirmed
  • Implement market regime detection (trend/range) to automatically pause trading or reduce position size in ranging markets
  • Add spread filter and slippage control to avoid opening positions in high-cost environments

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Lack of new bar check may cause duplicate position opening on the same bar, increasing risk exposure
  • Volume multiplier parameter may fail under different market volatility regimes, leading to incorrect signal judgment
  • WPR frequent signal triggers in sideways markets may cause accumulated consecutive small losses
