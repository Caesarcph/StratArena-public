摘要

该EA使用两条移动均线判断趋势方向，并在趋势确认时分四个级别逐步开仓。当快线上穿慢线时入场，随着动量延续逐级加仓，在趋势反转或触及止盈/止损时出场。

策略分析

该策略基于双均线交叉的经典趋势跟踪概念，使用一条快速均线和一条慢速均线来判断市场趋势方向。入场条件为快线上穿慢线时做多、快线下穿慢线时做空，并且设计了四个级别的加仓层次，随着趋势延续逐步增加头寸。出场条件依赖均线反向交叉信号或预设的止盈止损水平，当趋势反转时平掉全部仓位。仓位管理采用四层递进式建仓，每层可以设置不同的仓位比例，属于金字塔加仓模式。资金管理方面依赖用户设定的手数和止损幅度，但未见动态根据账户余额调整仓位的机制，存在固定手数下风险不可控的隐患。

Summary

This EA uses two moving averages to determine trend direction and opens trades across four progressive levels as the trend confirms. It enters positions when the fast MA crosses the slow MA and adds levels on continued momentum, exiting when the trend reverses or take-profit/stop-loss levels are hit.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy is based on the classic dual moving average crossover concept for trend following, using a fast MA and a slow MA to determine market trend direction. Entry conditions are bullish when the fast MA crosses above the slow MA and bearish when it crosses below, with four progressive levels designed to add positions as the trend extends. Exit conditions rely on a reverse MA crossover signal or predefined take-profit and stop-loss levels, closing all positions when the trend reverses. Position sizing uses a four-level pyramiding approach where each level can have a different lot size, adding positions as the trend confirms further. Money management depends on user-defined lot sizes and stop-loss distances, but there is no dynamic position sizing based on account equity, which creates a risk of overexposure with fixed lot sizes.

优势

  • 金字塔式四层加仓设计，在趋势行情中能充分捕获利润
  • 双均线交叉逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和参数优化
  • 多层建仓可以分散入场成本，降低单一入场点的风险

劣势

  • 均线交叉策略在震荡市中容易产生频繁的假信号和来回亏损
  • 固定手数加仓缺乏基于账户权益的动态仓位管理，风险控制不足
  • 四层加仓在极端行情下可能导致过度持仓，单方向最大敞口较大

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤指标（如ADX或ATR）来避免震荡市中的假信号
  • 引入基于账户风险百分比的动态仓位计算，替代固定手数

Strengths

  • Pyramiding four-level position design captures profits fully during trending markets
  • Dual MA crossover logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and optimize parameters
  • Multi-level entry distributes average entry cost, reducing single entry point risk

Weaknesses

  • MA crossover strategies generate frequent false signals and whipsaw losses in ranging markets
  • Fixed lot sizing lacks dynamic position management based on account equity, insufficient risk control
  • Four-level pyramiding can lead to excessive exposure in extreme moves, creating large one-directional risk

Improvements

  • Add a trend filter indicator (such as ADX or ATR) to avoid false signals in sideways markets
  • Introduce dynamic position sizing based on account risk percentage instead of fixed lot sizes
