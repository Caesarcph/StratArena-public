摘要

RiskSizer Panel Lite是一款风险管理工具面板，用于根据用户设定的风险比例和止损距离计算仓位大小。它提供可视化界面，帮助交易者在下单前快速确定合适的交易手数，确保每笔交易的风险一致。该EA本身不自动开仓，而是作为手动或半自动交易的辅助工具。

策略分析

该EA并非策略型交易程序，而是一个仓位计算工具。其核心概念是根据账户余额或净值的风险比例和止损点数来计算建议手数，实现风险可控的资金管理。入场和出场条件不适用，因为它不产生交易信号。仓位管理方面，它将风险金额除以每手每点的价值来得出建议手数，确保每笔交易的潜在亏损不超过预设比例。资金管理方面，它允许用户设定风险百分比（如1%-3%），从而标准化每笔交易的风险敞口。该工具的价值在于将心理上的风险控制转化为量化的计算流程，减少手动计算错误和情绪化交易。

Summary

RiskSizer Panel Lite is a risk management utility panel for MetaTrader 5 that calculates position sizes based on user-defined risk percentage and stop-loss distance. It provides a visual interface for traders to quickly determine appropriate lot sizes before placing trades, ensuring consistent risk per trade. The EA does not open positions itself but serves as a companion tool for manual or semi-automated trading.

Strategy Analysis

This EA is not a strategy-based trading program but rather a position sizing calculator utility. Its core concept is to calculate suggested lot sizes by dividing the risked dollar amount (based on account balance/equity risk percentage) by the per-lot per-pip value, ensuring controlled risk exposure. Entry and exit conditions are not applicable since it does not generate trade signals. Position sizing is the primary function: it determines the appropriate volume so that the potential loss on any single trade does not exceed the user-specified percentage of account capital. Money management is standardized through a configurable risk percentage (typically 1%-3%), which enforces consistent risk per trade across all instruments. The tool's value lies in converting psychological risk control into a quantified calculation workflow, reducing manual computation errors and emotional trading decisions.

优势

  • 将风险控制量化，避免过度下注导致账户爆仓
  • 提供直观的图形面板界面，操作便捷，减少手动计算错误
  • 适用于所有交易品种，灵活设定风险比例和止损距离

劣势

  • 仅计算手数，不包含实际下单功能，需要手动执行交易
  • 不考虑点差、滑点和佣金等交易成本对实际风险的影响
  • 缺乏对账户整体风险敞口的监控，仅关注单笔交易风险

改进建议

  • 增加一键下单按钮，将计算结果直接应用到交易执行中
  • 添加账户整体风险监控功能，显示总敞口和最大回撤估算

Strengths

  • Quantifies risk control to prevent over-leveraging and account blow-ups
  • Provides an intuitive graphical panel interface for easy operation and reduced manual calculation errors
  • Works across all trading instruments with flexible risk percentage and stop-loss distance settings

Weaknesses

  • Only calculates lot size without actual order placement capability, requiring manual trade execution
  • Does not account for trading costs such as spread, slippage, and commission impact on actual risk
  • Lacks monitoring of overall account risk exposure, focusing only on per-trade risk

Improvements

  • Add one-click order button to directly apply calculated lot size to trade execution
  • Implement account-level risk monitoring showing total exposure and maximum drawdown estimation
