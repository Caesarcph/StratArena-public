摘要

RiskCalc是一个用于风险计算和仓位管理的工具型EA。它根据账户余额、风险百分比和止损距离帮助交易者确定合适的仓位大小，而不会自动下单交易。该工具作为手动交易决策的资金管理辅助工具。

策略分析

RiskCalc的核心功能是风险计算与仓位管理辅助，而非自动交易策略。它根据用户设定的风险百分比、止损点数和账户余额来计算建议的开仓手数，确保每笔交易的风险在可控范围内。入场和出场条件不适用于此工具，因为它不执行任何交易操作。仓位管理方面，它提供基于固定风险比例的仓位计算，将风险量化为具体的合约规模。资金管理方面，它支持百分比风险模型和固定金额风险模型，帮助交易者在每笔交易前评估风险敞口。该工具的核心价值在于将风险管理前置化，使交易者在下单前就能清楚知道潜在亏损金额。

Summary

RiskCalc is a utility EA designed for risk calculation and position sizing in MetaTrader 5. It helps traders determine appropriate lot sizes based on account balance, risk percentage, and stop-loss distance, without placing any trades on its own. The tool serves as a money management assistant for manual trading decisions.

Strategy Analysis

RiskCalc's core functionality is risk calculation and position sizing assistance rather than an automated trading strategy. It calculates suggested lot sizes based on user-defined risk percentage, stop-loss distance in points, and current account balance, ensuring each trade's risk stays within acceptable limits. Entry and exit conditions are not applicable since this utility does not execute any trades. For position sizing, it provides calculations based on a fixed percentage risk model, translating risk tolerance into concrete contract sizes. For money management, it supports both percentage-based and fixed-amount risk models, helping traders assess risk exposure before each trade. The tool's core value lies in front-loading risk management, enabling traders to know their potential loss amount before placing orders.

优势

  • 将风险管理前置化，在交易前明确风险敞口
  • 支持百分比和固定金额两种风险计算模式
  • 简单直观的参数设置，易于使用和理解

劣势

  • 仅提供计算功能，无法与交易执行流程直接集成
  • 缺乏对多品种同时交易的整体风险敞口评估
  • 没有考虑点差和滑点对实际风险的影响

改进建议

  • 增加多品种组合风险敞口计算功能
  • 集成自动下单选项，实现风险计算到执行的闭环
  • 增加基于ATR的动态止损建议功能

Strengths

  • Front-loads risk management by clarifying risk exposure before trading
  • Supports both percentage-based and fixed-amount risk calculation modes
  • Simple and intuitive parameter settings that are easy to use and understand

Weaknesses

  • Only provides calculation functionality without direct integration into trade execution workflow
  • Lacks overall risk exposure assessment for simultaneous multi-instrument trading
  • Does not account for the impact of spread and slippage on actual risk

Improvements

  • Add multi-instrument portfolio risk exposure calculation capability
  • Integrate optional auto-execution to close the loop from risk calculation to order placement
  • Add dynamic stop-loss suggestion based on ATR
