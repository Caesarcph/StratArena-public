摘要

该EA基于自定义ADXDMI指标进行交易，当指标线上穿信号线时产生买入信号，下穿时产生卖出信号。支持可配置的资金管理模式、止损止盈，以及买卖双向开仓和平仓的独立开关。

策略分析

策略概念基于ADXDMI指标的线与信号线的交叉来判断趋势方向。入场条件为：当指标线Ind上穿信号线Sign时开多单，当Ind下穿Sign时开空单，使用SignalBar参数决定使用哪根K线的值进行判断。出场条件包括：趋势反转时平仓（多单在Ind下穿Sign时平仓，空单在Ind上穿Sign时平仓），以及固定的止损止盈出场。仓位管理提供了五种模式：按可用保证金比例、按余额比例、按可用保证金扣除亏损后比例、按余额扣除亏损后比例、固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例或固定手数，MMMode选择具体的计算模式。策略使用新K线检测机制避免同一根K线内重复开仓，并通过Recount机制在数据不足时重新计算。

Summary

This EA trades based on the ADXDMI custom indicator, generating buy signals when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell signals when it crosses below. It supports configurable money management modes, stop loss/take profit, and separate toggles for opening and closing positions on both sides.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the crossover of the ADXDMI indicator line and its signal line to determine trend direction. Entry conditions: a buy position opens when the Ind line crosses above the Sign line, and a sell position opens when Ind crosses below Sign, using the SignalBar parameter to specify which bar's values are used for evaluation. Exit conditions include closing positions on trend reversal (long positions close when Ind crosses below Sign, short positions close when Ind crosses above Sign) and fixed stop loss/take profit exits. Position sizing offers five modes: percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin minus floating loss, percentage of balance minus floating loss, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter for risk percentage or fixed lot, with MMMode selecting the specific calculation mode. The strategy uses a new bar detection mechanism to prevent repeated entries within the same bar and employs a Recount flag to re-fetch data when buffer reads fail.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简洁清晰，基于指标交叉的趋势跟踪方法经典可靠
  • 提供五种仓位管理模式，灵活适应不同交易者的风险偏好
  • 买卖方向的开仓和平仓可独立控制，便于只做单边或仅用于平仓
  • 使用新K线检测机制避免同根K线重复交易，减少过度交易风险
  • 止损止盈参数可配置，有基本的风险控制框架

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标ADXDMI但未内嵌其源码，指标缺失时EA无法运行
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值过大，对大多数品种可能不合适
  • 没有考虑点差和交易成本的过滤，在小周期交易时可能因成本导致亏损
  • 缺少对现有持仓的检查，可能在同方向重复开仓
  • 指标缓冲区读取失败时仅设置Recount标志，没有更完善的错误处理机制

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓导致仓位过重
  • 增加点差过滤和交易成本考虑，提高实盘交易的净盈利能力
  • 将ADXDMI指标逻辑内嵌或提供更完善的指标缺失检测和提示

Strengths

  • Clean and straightforward strategy logic based on a classic trend-following crossover method
  • Five position sizing modes provide flexibility for different risk preferences
  • Independent toggles for buy/sell opening and closing allow one-direction-only or close-only usage
  • New bar detection prevents duplicate entries within the same bar, reducing overtrading risk
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit parameters provide a basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Depends on a custom ADXDMI indicator without embedding its source code; EA fails if the indicator is missing
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments
  • No spread or trading cost filter, which may cause losses on lower timeframes due to transaction costs
  • No check for existing positions before opening new ones, potentially leading to duplicate same-direction entries
  • Buffer read failures only set a Recount flag without more robust error handling or retry logic

Improvements

  • Add position existence checks before opening new trades to avoid overloading same-direction exposure
  • Add spread filtering and transaction cost considerations to improve net profitability in live trading
  • Embed the ADXDMI indicator logic internally or provide better detection and user notification for missing indicators
