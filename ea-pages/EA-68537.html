摘要

该EA使用ONNX机器学习模型检测盘整市场状态，在包络线指标边界开仓。当交易浮亏时，采用马丁格尔策略以倍数加仓并在固定点距补仓，当总盈利达到美元目标时全部平仓。

策略分析

策略核心概念是结合AI盘整检测与包络线均值回归入场：ONNX模型输出三个概率（盘整、看涨、看跌），当盘整概率超过阈值且价格触及包络线下轨时做多，触及上轨时做空。出场条件为组合持仓总盈利达到TakeProfitTargetUSD（12美元）即全平，或保证金亏损比例超过StopLossByMarginPercent时强制全平。仓位管理采用马丁格尔递增：首次入场用InpLotSize，后续每达到DistanceMartingalePips点距即以LotMultiplier倍数加仓，最多MaxTradesInSeries层。资金管理方面有保证金检查（CheckMargin）防止余额不足开仓，以及账户权益百分比止损。AI安全门控在马丁加仓时生效：若持仓为多但看跌概率超阈值则不再加多，空单同理。策略整体风险极高，马丁格尔在8层倍数下可导致指数级仓位膨胀，且无止损单保护。

Summary

This EA uses an ONNX machine learning model to detect sideways market conditions and opens trades at Envelopes indicator boundaries. When trades go against it, it employs a martingale recovery strategy by multiplying lot size and adding positions at fixed pip distances, closing all positions when total profit reaches a USD target.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept combines AI sideways detection with Envelopes mean-reversion entry: the ONNX model outputs three probabilities (sideways, bullish, bearish), and a buy is triggered when the sideways probability exceeds the threshold and price touches the lower Envelope band, with sells at the upper band. Exit conditions are: total portfolio profit reaches TakeProfitTargetUSD (12 USD) for full close, or margin drawdown percentage exceeds StopLossByMarginPercent for forced liquidation. Position sizing uses martingale progression: the first entry uses InpLotSize, and subsequent layers are added at LotMultiplier times the previous lot once price moves DistanceMartingalePips against the position, up to MaxTradesInSeries layers. Money management includes margin checking (CheckMargin) to prevent opening positions with insufficient funds, and an account equity percentage stop-loss. An AI safety gate applies during martingale additions: if holding a long but bearish probability exceeds InpAISafetyThreshold, no further buy additions are made, and vice versa for shorts. The overall risk is extremely high since martingale with 8 layers and a 2x multiplier causes exponential position growth, and there are no individual stop-loss orders on any trade.

优势

  • AI模型提供市场状态判断，避免在趋势行情中做均值回归
  • 有保证金检查和权益百分比止损，提供一定资金保护
  • AI安全门控在加仓时检查反向概率，减少不利加仓
  • 包络线入场逻辑清晰，在盘整市中具有均值回归优势
  • 代码结构清晰，函数分工合理，可读性较好

劣势

  • 马丁格尔策略8层2倍加仓可导致仓位指数膨胀，极端行情下可爆仓
  • 无单笔止损单，完全依赖组合盈利目标或保证金止损出场
  • 包络线偏差仅0.06%，过窄的通道在波动加大的盘整中频繁触发虚假信号
  • ONNX模型输出依赖未经公开验证的自训练模型，存在过拟合风险
  • 使用PositionSelect按品种选择持仓，多EA同品种运行时会冲突
  • 止损仅基于账户权益百分比，未考虑单品种风险隔离

改进建议

  • 添加单笔止损或组合止损点数限制，防止无限浮亏积累
  • 降低最大马丁层数或使用加法递增代替倍数递增，控制仓位指数增长
  • 增加点差过滤和波动率过滤，避免高成本或低质量交易环境
  • 将PositionSelect改为遍历所有持仓按Magic+Symbol精确匹配，支持多EA同品种运行
  • 添加最大浮亏金额限制作为额外风控层

Strengths

  • AI model provides market regime detection, avoiding mean-reversion trades during trending markets
  • Margin checking and equity percentage stop-loss provide some capital protection
  • AI safety gate checks counter-direction probability before adding martingale layers, reducing adverse additions
  • Envelopes entry logic is clear and exploits mean-reversion advantage in sideways markets
  • Code structure is clean with well-separated functions and good readability

Weaknesses

  • Martingale with 8 layers and 2x multiplier causes exponential position growth that can blow the account in extreme markets
  • No individual stop-loss orders, completely relying on portfolio profit target or margin stop-loss for exits
  • Envelope deviation of only 0.06% creates an extremely narrow band that generates frequent false signals during volatile sideways periods
  • ONNX model relies on an unverified self-trained model with overfitting risk
  • PositionSelect by symbol causes conflicts when multiple EAs run on the same instrument
  • Stop-loss is based only on account equity percentage without per-symbol risk isolation

Improvements

  • Add individual trade stop-loss or a maximum floating loss pip limit to prevent unlimited drawdown accumulation
  • Reduce maximum martingale layers or use additive lot increase instead of multiplicative to control exponential growth
  • Add spread filter and volatility filter to avoid high-cost or low-quality trading environments
  • Change position selection to iterate all positions matching Magic+Symbol exactly, supporting multiple EAs on the same instrument
  • Add a maximum floating loss amount limit as an additional risk control layer

Risk Assessment

Overall:DANGEROUS

Risk Points

  • Martingale 8-layer 2x progression means the final layer is 256x the initial lot, extreme drawdown can blow the account
  • No individual stop-loss; all positions rely solely on portfolio profit target or margin percentage stop for exit
  • 0.06% envelope deviation is extremely narrow, generating numerous false signals in volatile sideways markets that trigger martingale sequences
  • 50% margin stop-loss means half the account is already lost before triggering, providing severely delayed protection
