摘要

该EA使用ONNX神经网络模型检测横盘市场状态，在包络线指标上下轨处进行均值回归入场。当持仓浮亏时，采用马丁格尔恢复策略，按固定点距加仓并倍增手数，当所有持仓总盈利达到美元目标时一次性平仓。

策略分析

策略概念：结合AI横盘检测与包络线指标的均值回归策略，在横盘市场中捕捉价格触及包络带后的回归机会。入场条件：当AI模型预测横盘概率≥0.70且收盘价触及包络线下轨时做多，触及上轨时做空。出场条件：所有同方向持仓总盈利达到12美元目标时全部平仓，或账户保证金水平跌破50%时强制清仓。仓位管理：首仓使用固定手数0.08，亏损后按2倍乘数递增手数，最大允许8层加仓，加仓间距为15点。资金管理：通过保证金占比止损（50%）和单笔下单前保证金检查来控制风险，但单个持仓没有止损和止盈。AI安全过滤：在加仓时如果反向趋势概率超过0.75则跳过加仓，提供了一定的趋势保护。

Summary

This EA uses an ONNX neural network model to detect sideways market conditions and trades mean-reversion entries at Envelopes indicator bands. When a position goes against it, the EA applies a Martingale recovery strategy by multiplying the lot size and adding positions at fixed pip distances, closing all positions when the combined profit reaches a USD target.

Strategy Analysis

Strategy concept: A mean-reversion approach combining an ONNX neural network for sideways market detection with the Envelopes indicator, capturing price回归 opportunities when price touches envelope bands during ranging conditions. Entry conditions: Buy when the AI model predicts sideways probability ≥ 0.70 and close price touches the lower envelope band; Sell when sideways probability is high and close price touches the upper envelope band. Exit conditions: All positions in the series close simultaneously when combined unrealized profit reaches the $12 USD target, or the margin stop-loss triggers at 50% equity drawdown from balance. Position sizing: Initial lot is 0.08, with subsequent recovery trades doubling the lot size (2x multiplier) up to a maximum of 8 trades in the series, spaced 15 pips apart. Money management: A margin-based stop-loss at 50% equity drawdown and pre-trade margin check provide risk control, but individual positions have no stop-loss or take-profit. AI safety filter: During Martingale recovery additions, if the opposing trend probability exceeds 0.75, the add-on trade is skipped, providing some directional protection against trending markets.

优势

  • 使用ONNX机器学习模型智能判断市场状态，避免在趋势行情中开仓
  • AI安全过滤在加仓阶段提供趋势方向保护，减少逆势加仓风险
  • 包含保证金检查和账户级别止损，有基本的风控框架
  • 新K线检查避免同一根K线重复交易，减少过度交易
  • 手数精度处理和经纪商限制检查较为完善

劣势

  • 马丁格尔策略本身具有指数级风险增长特性，8层2倍加仓最大手数可达初始的256倍
  • 单个持仓没有止损，完全依赖账户级保证金止损，在极端行情下可能爆仓
  • 包络线偏差0.06极小，入场信号可能过于频繁且在波动稍大的横盘中频繁触发
  • 15点加仓间距在5位数报价中仅1.5点实际距离，加仓过于密集
  • ONNX模型文件未内置，缺失时EA直接初始化失败，缺乏降级方案
  • 12美元固定止盈目标不随持仓规模调整，大仓位可能过早止盈

改进建议

  • 为每个持仓设置独立止损，至少设置一个基于ATR的动态止损作为最后防线
  • 将马丁格尔加仓间距改为基于ATR动态调整，避免在低波动品种上加仓过密
  • 止盈目标应随持仓总规模动态调整，而非固定美元金额
  • 添加ONNX模型缺失时的降级策略，例如仅使用包络线指标进行交易
  • 添加点差过滤，避免在点差过大时开仓，减少滑点损失
  • 限制马丁格尔最大总仓位而非仅限制层数，防止手数指数膨胀

Strengths

  • Uses ONNX machine learning model to intelligently detect market regime, avoiding entries during trending conditions
  • AI safety filter provides directional protection during Martingale recovery, reducing counter-trend add-on risk
  • Includes margin check and account-level stop-loss, providing a basic risk management framework
  • New bar check prevents duplicate trades within the same candle, reducing overtrading
  • Lot precision handling and broker limit checks (min/max/step/volume limit) are thorough

Weaknesses

  • Martingale strategy inherently carries exponential risk growth; 8 levels at 2x multiplier means the last trade is 256x the initial lot size
  • Individual positions have no stop-loss, relying entirely on account-level margin stop-loss which may fail in extreme gap scenarios
  • Envelope deviation of 0.06 is extremely small, likely generating too many signals and triggering frequently even in mild volatility
  • 15-pip Martingale distance translates to only 1.5 pips on 5-digit brokers, making add-on positions dangerously close together
  • ONNX model file is not embedded; missing file causes INIT_FAILED with no fallback strategy
  • Fixed $12 profit target doesn't scale with position size, potentially causing premature exits on large Martingale positions

Improvements

  • Add individual stop-loss per position, at minimum an ATR-based dynamic stop as a last line of defense
  • Make Martingale add-on distance ATR-adaptive instead of fixed pips, preventing overly dense positions in low-volatility instruments
  • Profit target should dynamically scale with total position size rather than using a fixed USD amount
  • Implement a fallback strategy when the ONNX model is missing, such as trading with envelopes-only logic
  • Add a spread filter to avoid opening positions during high-spread conditions, reducing slippage losses
  • Cap maximum total exposure by lot size or margin percentage, not just the number of Martingale layers

风险点

  • 马丁格尔8层2倍加仓可导致最大手数为初始的256倍，极端风险
  • 单个持仓无止损，完全依赖账户级止损，跳空行情下可能穿仓
  • 15点加仓间距在5位数报价中仅1.5点实际距离，仓位过于集中
  • 包络线偏差0.06极小导致频繁入场，增加在假突破中启动马丁序列的概率

Risk Assessment

Overall:DANGEROUS

Risk Points

  • Martingale with 8 levels at 2x multiplier can reach 256x initial lot size on the final trade, representing extreme risk
  • No individual stop-loss on positions, relying solely on account-level margin stop which can be gapped through in fast markets
  • 15-pip distance on 5-digit brokers equals only 1.5 actual pips, concentrating positions dangerously close together
  • Extremely small envelope deviation (0.06) causes frequent entries, increasing probability of triggering Martingale sequences on false signals
