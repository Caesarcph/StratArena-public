摘要

该EA使用ONNX机器学习模型检测盘整行情，在布林包络线边界入场。当持仓浮亏时，采用马丁格尔策略加倍手数并在固定点距加仓。当所有持仓总浮盈达到目标美元金额或保证金回撤超过阈值时平仓出场。

策略分析

策略概念：结合AI趋势识别（ONNX模型三分类：盘整/看涨/看跌）与包络线指标，在盘整行情中于价格触及包络线上下轨时反向入场，期望价格回归均值。入场条件：无持仓时，收盘价触及下轨且AI盘整概率≥0.70则买入，触及上轨且盘整概率≥0.70则卖出。出场条件：所有持仓总浮盈≥12美元时全部平仓，或保证金回撤≥50%时强制清仓止损。仓位管理：首仓使用固定手数0.08，亏损加仓时手数乘以2.0倍（马丁格尔），加仓距离为15点，最多叠加8层仓位。资金管理：通过保证金回撤百分比进行全局风控，每笔订单前检查可用保证金是否充足，并有手数上限和交易量限制检查。AI安全阈值：在马丁格尔加仓阶段，如果AI检测到与当前持仓方向相反的强趋势（概率≥0.75），则阻止加仓以避免逆势加码。

Summary

This EA uses an ONNX-based machine learning model to detect sideways market conditions and enters trades at Envelope band boundaries. When a position goes against it, it applies a Martingale recovery strategy by multiplying lot size and adding positions at fixed pip distances. It exits when the total floating profit of all positions reaches a USD target or when margin drawdown exceeds a threshold.

Strategy Analysis

Strategy concept: Combines an ONNX machine learning model (3-class: sideways/bullish/bearish) with Envelope bands to trade mean-reversion entries during sideways markets, expecting price to revert to the mean. Entry conditions: When no positions are open, a buy is triggered if close price touches the lower Envelope band and the AI sideways probability is ≥0.70; a sell is triggered at the upper band under the same sideways condition. Exit conditions: All positions are closed when total floating profit reaches $12 USD, or a forced liquidation occurs when margin drawdown exceeds 50% of balance. Position sizing: The initial trade uses a fixed lot of 0.08; on losing positions, lot size is multiplied by 2.0 (Martingale doubling), with additional entries placed every 15 pips away, up to a maximum of 8 layers. Money management: Global risk control via margin drawdown percentage, pre-trade margin adequacy checks, and volume limit compliance. AI safety gate: During Martingale recovery additions, if the AI detects a strong trend opposing the current position direction (probability ≥0.75), the add-on trade is blocked to prevent adding to a losing position against a trending market.

优势

  • AI模型识别盘整行情，避免在趋势行情中逆势交易，提升入场质量
  • AI安全阈值在加仓阶段检测反向强趋势并阻止加仓，减少逆势加码风险
  • 保证金回撤百分比止损提供全局风控底线，防止账户爆仓

劣势

  • 马丁格尔策略在极端单边行情中可能叠加至8层仓位，风险呈指数级增长，50%回撤止损可能触发过晚
  • 缺乏点差过滤，在点差较大的品种或时段交易可能导致入场即亏损
  • 包络线周期仅8且偏差0.06极窄，容易产生频繁假信号和过度交易
  • 无硬性止损（每笔订单SL=0），完全依赖浮盈止盈和保证金回撤止损，单笔亏损不可控

改进建议

  • 为每笔订单设置硬性止损，限制单笔最大亏损，同时保留总浮盈止盈机制
  • 增加点差过滤和波动率过滤，避免在高点差或低流动性时段入场
  • 限制马丁格尔最大总手数或总风险敞口，而非仅限制层数，防止8层加仓后总仓位过大

Strengths

  • ONNX model identifies sideways conditions, avoiding counter-trend entries in trending markets and improving entry quality
  • AI safety threshold blocks Martingale add-ons when a strong opposing trend is detected, reducing the risk of stacking losing positions
  • Margin drawdown percentage stop-loss provides a global risk floor, preventing account blow-up

Weaknesses

  • Martingale strategy can stack up to 8 layers in extreme trending markets with exponential risk growth; the 50% drawdown stop may trigger too late
  • No spread filter — trading on high-spread instruments or during wide-spread sessions can result in immediate losses on entry
  • Envelope period of 8 with 0.06 deviation is extremely narrow, leading to frequent false signals and over-trading
  • No hard stop-loss per order (SL=0), relying entirely on floating profit targets and margin drawdown stop — individual trade loss is unbounded

Improvements

  • Add a hard stop-loss per order to cap individual trade loss, while keeping the total floating profit take-profit mechanism
  • Add spread and volatility filters to avoid entries during high-spread or low-liquidity periods
  • Cap the maximum total lot size or total risk exposure instead of only capping the number of Martingale layers, preventing excessive total exposure after 8 layers

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:DANGEROUS

风险点

  • 马丁格尔策略加倍手数，8层加仓后总手数为初始的255倍，极端行情下可能导致巨额亏损
  • 每笔订单无硬性止损（SL=0），单笔亏损完全不受控
  • 50%保证金回撤止损阈值过高，触发时账户已损失一半资金

Risk Assessment

Overall:DANGEROUS

Risk Points

  • Martingale doubles lot size each layer; after 8 layers total lots are 255x the initial, potentially causing catastrophic losses in extreme moves
  • No hard stop-loss per order (SL=0), individual trade losses are completely unbounded
  • 50% margin drawdown stop threshold is too high — by the time it triggers, half the account is already lost
