摘要

这是一个网格交易EA，当价格偏离近期高低点足够距离时开仓，随后在固定间距放置最多4个限价单，手数按2的幂次递增（马丁格尔风格）。当总浮动盈利达到目标金额时全部平仓，并带有可选的保本止损功能。

策略分析

策略概念是基于价格偏离极值的回归预期，结合网格加仓实现盈利。入场条件：当最高价减去当前Bid价超过ExtLimit点数时做多，当当前Bid价减去最低价超过ExtLimit点数时做空。初始仓位大小为账户余额除以100再除以1000，后续限价单手数按2的i次方递增（1x, 2x, 4x, 8x, 16x），这是典型的马丁格尔加倍下注模式。出场条件为所有持仓总盈利达到Amount参数设定的金额时全部平仓，同时删除所有挂单。仓位管理通过网格间距（ExtGrid）在价格不利方向布置限价单，每层间距固定。资金管理方面，锁利功能（LockDown）在持仓盈利超过一定点数后将止损移至保本位，但初始开仓无止损设置，风险较大。

Summary

A grid trading EA that opens an initial market order when price deviates significantly from recent highs/lows, then places up to 4 limit orders at fixed grid intervals with doubling lot sizes (Martingale-style). It closes all positions and pending orders when total floating profit reaches a target amount, with an optional breakeven stop-loss feature.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on expecting price reversion from extreme deviations, combined with grid pyramiding to achieve profit. Entry conditions: a buy is triggered when the highest price minus the current Bid exceeds ExtLimit points, and a sell is triggered when the current Bid minus the lowest price exceeds ExtLimit points. Initial lot size is calculated as account balance divided by 100 then by 1000, and subsequent limit order lots increase by powers of 2 (1x, 2x, 4x, 8x, 16x), which is a classic Martingale doubling pattern. Exit conditions trigger when the total floating profit across all positions reaches the Amount parameter, at which point all positions are closed and all pending orders are deleted. Position management uses a grid spacing (ExtGrid) to place limit orders in the adverse price direction at fixed intervals. The LockDown feature moves the stop-loss to breakeven once a position is profitable by a set number of points, but the initial entry has no stop-loss, creating significant risk exposure.

优势

  • 结构清晰，代码逻辑易于理解和维护
  • 使用了标准库类（CTrade、CPositionInfo等）处理交易操作，减少出错概率
  • 包含LotCheck函数进行手数规范化，确保符合经纪商的最小/最大手数和步长要求
  • LockDown保本止损功能可为盈利持仓提供保护

劣势

  • 马丁格尔加倍手数模式在连续亏损时会导致仓位指数级增长，存在爆仓风险
  • 初始开仓无止损设置，仅依赖盈利目标平仓，极端行情下可能造成巨大亏损
  • IsTradeAllowed函数中存在重复检查TERMINAL_TRADE_ALLOWED的代码冗余
  • 网格限价单数量固定为4层，无法根据市场波动率自适应调整
  • 未设置最大持仓限制，多组网格可能同时运行导致过度敞口

改进建议

  • 增加硬止损设置，防止单边极端行情导致的无限亏损
  • 将马丁格尔加倍改为固定比例加仓或使用ATR动态调整网格间距和加仓倍数
  • 增加最大同时运行网格组数限制和总仓位上限控制
  • 修复IsTradeAllowed中的重复检查代码

Strengths

  • Clean and well-structured code that is easy to understand and maintain
  • Uses standard library classes (CTrade, CPositionInfo, etc.) for trade operations, reducing error probability
  • Includes LotCheck function for lot normalization, ensuring compliance with broker min/max volume and step requirements
  • LockDown breakeven feature provides protection for profitable positions

Weaknesses

  • Martingale lot doubling causes exponential position growth during consecutive losses, creating account blow-up risk
  • No stop-loss on initial entry — relies solely on profit-target exit, which can lead to massive losses in extreme moves
  • Redundant duplicate check of TERMINAL_TRADE_ALLOWED in IsTradeAllowed function
  • Grid order count is fixed at 4 levels with no adaptive adjustment based on market volatility
  • No maximum position limit — multiple grid sets could run simultaneously leading to excessive exposure

Improvements

  • Add hard stop-loss to prevent unlimited losses during one-sided extreme market moves
  • Replace Martingale doubling with fixed-ratio pyramiding or use ATR to dynamically adjust grid spacing and multiplier
  • Add limits on maximum concurrent grid sets and total position exposure
  • Fix the duplicate TERMINAL_TRADE_ALLOWED check in IsTradeAllowed
