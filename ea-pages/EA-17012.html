Very Blonde System

This EA opens an initial market order when the price deviates significantly from the recent high/low, then places a series of 4 BuyLimit or SellLimit orders at fixed grid intervals with doubling lot sizes (Martingale-style). It closes all positions and pending orders when total floating profit reaches a target amount, and optionally moves stop loss to breakeven after a LockDown threshold is reached.

摘要

该EA在价格偏离近期高低点一定幅度后开仓，并在固定网格间距处放置4个限价挂单，仓位按2的幂次递增（马丁格尔式）。当浮动总盈利达到目标金额时平掉所有仓位和挂单，并在价格达到LockDown阈值后将止损移至保本。

策略分析

策略概念：这是一个基于价格偏离极值的网格+马丁格尔混合策略。入场条件：当最高价减去当前Bid价超过ExtLimit*Point时做多，当当前Bid价减去最低价超过ExtLimit*Point时做空。入场后以ExtGrid为间距放置4个限价挂单，每个挂单手数为前一个的2倍（2^i*Lots），属于典型的马丁格尔加仓模式。出场条件：所有持仓浮动盈利达到Amount参数设定的金额时全部平仓并删除挂单。仓位管理：初始手数根据账户余额计算（Balance/100/1000），后续挂单手量按2的幂次倍增，这种几何级数增长在没有止损保护的情况下风险极大。资金管理：有LockDown保本机制，当持仓浮盈超过ExtLockDown点数时将止损移至开仓价±1点，但仅对未设置止损的仓位生效。整体而言，该策略依赖盈利覆盖亏损的思路，但在极端行情下多级加仓可能导致爆仓。

Summary

This EA opens an initial market order when the price deviates significantly from the recent high/low, then places a series of 4 BuyLimit or SellLimit orders at fixed grid intervals with doubling lot sizes (Martingale-style). It closes all positions and pending orders when total floating profit reaches a target amount, and optionally moves stop loss to breakeven after a LockDown threshold is reached.

Strategy Analysis

Strategy concept: This is a hybrid Grid + Martingale strategy based on price deviation from recent extremes. Entry conditions: Buy when the highest price minus current Bid exceeds ExtLimit*Point, Sell when current Bid minus the lowest price exceeds ExtLimit*Point. After entry, 4 limit pending orders are placed at ExtGrid intervals, with each order's volume doubling exponentially (2^i*Lots), which is a classic Martingale scaling-in pattern. Exit conditions: All positions and pending orders are closed when total floating profit reaches the Amount parameter threshold. Position sizing: Initial lot size is calculated from account balance (Balance/100/1000), and subsequent pending order volumes increase by powers of 2, creating geometric growth that carries extreme risk without proper stop loss protection. Money management: A LockDown breakeven mechanism exists — when floating profit exceeds ExtLockDown points, stop loss is moved to entry price ±1 point, but only for positions that have no stop loss already set. Overall, the strategy relies on the assumption that profits will eventually cover losses, but multi-level scaling in during extreme market conditions can lead to account blowout.

优势

  • 网格加仓策略在震荡行情中能通过多层级限价单捕捉回调利润
  • 有浮盈目标自动平仓机制，锁定利润时同时清除所有挂单，避免残留订单
  • LockDown保本机制能在趋势有利时将止损移至保本位，降低风险敞口
  • 手数经过LotCheck规范化处理，遵守品种最小最大手数和手数步长限制

劣势

  • 马丁格尔倍量加仓在单边行情中会导致仓位和亏损呈指数级增长，极易爆仓
  • 没有任何硬性止损保护，LockDown默认值为0即不启用，即使启用也仅移至保本而非真正止损
  • IsTradeAllowed函数中重复检查了TERMINAL_TRADE_ALLOWED，存在冗余代码
  • iLowest/iHighest返回的是索引而非价格值，但代码将其直接与Bid比较，存在逻辑错误
  • 没有新K线检测机制，每个tick都可能触发逻辑判断，增加不必要的计算负担

改进建议

  • 修复iLowest/iHighest返回值问题，应使用iLow()/iHigh()获取价格而非索引
  • 增加硬性止损保护，避免单笔或累计亏损无限扩大
  • 添加新K线检测，避免每个tick重复计算
  • 限制最大加仓层数和最大总仓位，防止极端行情下过度暴露
  • 添加点差过滤，避免在大点差时开仓

Strengths

  • Grid scaling-in captures pullback profits in ranging markets through multi-level limit orders
  • Automatic close-all on profit target hit also removes all pending orders, preventing orphan orders
  • LockDown breakeven mechanism reduces risk exposure by moving stop loss to entry when price moves favorably
  • Lot sizes are normalized via LotCheck, respecting symbol volume min/max and step constraints

Weaknesses

  • Martingale lot doubling causes exponential position and loss growth in trending markets,easilyleading to account blowout
  • No hard stop loss protection — LockDown defaults to 0 (disabled), and even when enabled it only moves to breakeven not a true stop
  • IsTradeAllowed has a redundant duplicate check of TERMINAL_TRADE_ALLOWED (lines 230 and 235)
  • iLowest/iHighest return bar indices not price values, but code compares them directly with Bid — this is a critical logic bug
  • No new bar detection mechanism, every tick triggers logic evaluation, adding unnecessary computational overhead

Improvements

  • Fix iLowest/iHighest return value issue — should use iLow()/iHigh() to get price values, not bar indices
  • Add hard stop loss protection to prevent unlimited single-trade or cumulative losses
  • Add new bar detection to avoid redundant calculations on every tick
  • Limit maximum grid levels and total exposure to prevent over-leveraging in extreme markets
  • Add spread filter to avoid opening positions during wide spread conditions

风险评估

总体:DANGEROUS

风险点

  • 马丁格尔倍量加仓无止损保护，单边行情可能导致账户爆仓
  • iLowest/iHighest返回索引而非价格，与Bid比较属于逻辑错误，可能导致错误开仓
  • LockDown保本机制默认关闭（值为0），即使启用也仅在浮盈时生效，不提供亏损保护
  • 没有最大持仓限制，4级网格加仓后总手数为基础手数的15倍(1+2+4+8+16)，杠杆风险极高

Risk Assessment

Overall:DANGEROUS

Risk Points

  • Martingale doubling with no stop loss — trending markets can blow the account
  • iLowest/iHighest returns bar index not price — comparison with Bid is a logic bug causing potentially wrong entries
  • LockDown breakeven defaults to 0 (disabled), and even enabled only protects profitable positions, not losing ones
  • No maximum position limit — 4 grid levels create 15x base lot exposure (1+2+4+8+16), extremely high leverage risk
