摘要

Firebird v0.60是一个均值回归EA，基于价格偏离均线的程度进行交易。当价格过度偏离均线时入场，预期价格将回归均值，并使用可配置的手数管理及可选的马丁格尔式恢复机制。

策略分析

Firebird v0.60的核心策略是均值回归，通过计算价格与移动平均线之间的偏差来识别超买超卖机会。入场条件为价格偏离均线超过设定的阈值倍数的标准差或ATR值，做多时价格需远低于均线，做空时价格需远高于均线。出场条件包括价格回归均线、固定止盈止损触发，或反向信号出现。仓位管理支持固定手数模式，也支持在亏损后加倍手数的马丁格尔恢复模式。资金管理方面提供了最大持仓限制和账户权益保护机制，但止损设置可能不够完善，在强趋势市场中面临持续逆势加仓的风险。

Summary

Firebird v0.60 is a mean reversion EA that trades based on price deviation from a moving average. It enters positions when price moves excessively away from its MA, expecting a reversion to the mean, and uses configurable lot sizing with optional martingale-like recovery.

Strategy Analysis

Firebird v0.60 employs a mean reversion strategy that identifies trading opportunities by measuring the deviation of price from its moving average. Entry conditions trigger when price moves beyond a configurable threshold multiple of standard deviation or ATR away from the MA — long positions when price is significantly below the MA and short positions when price is significantly above it. Exit conditions include price reverting back to the mean line, fixed take-profit or stop-loss being hit, or an opposing signal appearing. Position sizing supports both fixed lot mode and an optional martingale-style recovery mode that increases lot size after losses. Money management incorporates maximum position limits and account equity protection, however stop-loss settings may be inadequate and the strategy faces the risk of continuously adding positions against strong trends.

优势

  • 均值回归策略在震荡市中表现良好，逻辑清晰易懂
  • 提供了可配置的偏差阈值和MA周期，适应性强
  • 包含最大持仓限制和权益保护，有一定的风险控制意识

劣势

  • 默认止损为零，在强趋势行情中可能遭受巨大亏损
  • 马丁格尔恢复模式在连续亏损时会导致手数指数级增长，风险极高
  • 缺乏市场状态过滤机制，在趋势行情中仍会逆势开仓

改进建议

  • 增加市场状态识别过滤器，在趋势行情中暂停交易或切换策略
  • 强制设置止损，移除马丁格尔恢复模式或限制最大加倍次数
  • 增加波动率和点差过滤器，避免在高波动和宽点差环境下入场

Strengths

  • Mean reversion strategy performs well in ranging markets with clear and understandable logic
  • Configurable deviation threshold and MA period provide good adaptability to different instruments
  • Includes maximum position limits and equity protection showing some risk management awareness

Weaknesses

  • Default stop-loss is zero which can lead to catastrophic losses in strong trending markets
  • Martingale recovery mode causes exponential lot size growth during consecutive losses, extremely risky
  • Lacks market regime filter, continues to open counter-trend positions during trending conditions

Improvements

  • Add market regime identification filter to pause trading or switch strategy during trending conditions
  • Enforce mandatory stop-loss and remove martingale recovery mode or cap the maximum multiplier iterations
  • Add volatility and spread filters to avoid entry during high volatility and wide spread environments
