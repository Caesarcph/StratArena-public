摘要

该EA每天只交易一次，逆着最近N小时的趋势方向开仓，目标获利10点。它通过可配置的回溯期识别当前主导趋势，然后反向开仓捕捉短期回撤。这是一个典型的日内一次均值回归剥头皮策略。

策略分析

策略概念是均值回归，逆势交易，基于最近N小时的趋势方向进行反向操作。入场条件为：计算最近N小时的 价格走势判断趋势方向，如果是上升趋势则做空，如果是下降趋势则做多，且每天仅交易一次。出场条件为：达到10点止盈目标后平仓，同时设有止损保护。仓位管理方面，支持固定手数交易，部分版本可能支持基于资金比例的仓位计算。资金管理较为简单，缺乏动态调整机制，且每日一次交易限制了过度交易风险。该策略依赖市场在短期趋势后的回撤现象，但在强趋势市场中可能连续止损。

Summary

This EA opens one trade per day against the trend of the last N hours, targeting a small 10-pip profit. It identifies the prevailing trend over a configurable lookback period and enters a contrarian position, aiming to capture a quick retracement. The strategy is a classic once-a-day scalping mean-reversion system.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is mean reversion, trading against the prevailing trend of the last N hours. Entry conditions: the EA evaluates the price movement over the last N hours to determine the dominant trend direction, then opens a contrarian position — selling if the trend is up, buying if the trend is down — with a strict one-trade-per-day limit. Exit conditions: positions are closed upon reaching a 10-pip take profit target, with a stop loss in place for capital protection. Position sizing typically uses fixed lots, though some versions may support percentage-based sizing. Money management is relatively simplistic without dynamic adjustment mechanisms. The daily trade limit prevents overtrading, but the strategy relies on the assumption that short-term retracements follow trending moves, which can fail in strong trending markets leading to consecutive losses.

优势

  • 每天仅交易一次，有效避免过度交易和情绪化操作
  • 逆势策略在震荡行情和趋势反转时表现较好，能捕捉短期回撤
  • 策略逻辑简单明了，参数少，易于理解和调优

劣势

  • 逆势交易在强趋势市场中容易连续止损，风险较大
  • 10点止盈目标过小，容易被点差和滑点侵蚀实际利润
  • 缺乏动态仓位和风险管理机制，无法根据市场波动率自适应调整

改进建议

  • 增加趋势强度过滤器，在强趋势行情中暂停交易或减少仓位
  • 加入动态止盈止损机制，根据ATR或波动率自动调整目标
  • 增加点差过滤器，避免在点差过大时开仓以保护利润

Strengths

  • One trade per day limit effectively prevents overtrading and emotional decision-making
  • Contrarian approach performs well in ranging markets and during trend reversals, capturing short-term retracements
  • Simple and clear strategy logic with few parameters, easy to understand and optimize

Weaknesses

  • Counter-trend trading is prone to consecutive stop losses in strong trending markets, posing significant risk
  • 10-pip take profit target is very small and can be easily eroded by spread and slippage
  • Lacks dynamic position sizing and risk management mechanisms, unable to adapt to changing market volatility

Improvements

  • Add a trend strength filter to pause trading or reduce position size during strong trending conditions
  • Implement dynamic take profit and stop loss based on ATR or volatility for adaptive targets
  • Add a spread filter to avoid opening positions when spreads are too wide, protecting thin profit margins

风险点

  • 逆势交易在强单边行情中可能造成连续止损
  • 10点止盈较小，点差和滑点对实际收益影响显著
  • 每日一次交易限制虽降低频率但无法防止单笔大亏损

Risk Points

  • Small 10-pip target makes the strategy highly sensitive to spread and slippage costs
  • Daily trade limit reduces frequency but does not prevent large single-trade losses
