摘要

Bruno是一个趋势跟踪型EA，利用均线交叉逻辑结合动量过滤器来识别并跟随市场趋势。当快慢均线在主趋势方向上交叉并得到动量指标确认时开仓。该EA使用移动止损和固定止盈来管理持仓。

策略分析

策略概念基于均线交叉与动量确认的趋势跟踪系统。入场条件为快均线上穿慢均线且动量指标（如RSI或MACD）确认趋势方向时做多，反之为做空。出场条件包括达到固定止盈目标、移动止损被触发或均线反向交叉时平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数或基于账户余额的百分比计算手数。资金管理方面设置了最大持仓限制和每日最大亏损额度来控制风险。整体策略逻辑清晰，但在震荡市中容易产生频繁的虚假信号导致连续亏损。

Summary

Bruno is a trend-following Expert Advisor that uses moving average crossover logic combined with momentum filters to identify and ride market trends. It enters positions when fast and slow moving averages cross in the direction of the prevailing trend, confirmed by momentum indicators. The EA applies trailing stops and fixed take-profit levels to manage open positions.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a trend-following system using moving average crossover with momentum confirmation. Entry conditions require the fast MA crossing above the slow MA with momentum indicators (such as RSI or MACD) confirming the trend direction for longs, and vice versa for shorts. Exit conditions include reaching a fixed take-profit target, trailing stop being triggered, or an opposing MA crossover signaling trend reversal. Position sizing uses either fixed lots or a percentage of account balance to calculate lot size. Money management incorporates maximum position limits and daily maximum loss thresholds to control overall risk exposure. The strategy logic is coherent but is prone to generating frequent false signals in ranging or choppy markets, leading to consecutive losses during sideways conditions.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和维护
  • 趋势行情中能够有效捕捉中长期波段利润
  • 设有移动止损和固定止盈，具备基本的风险管理框架

劣势

  • 震荡行情中容易产生大量虚假信号导致频繁亏损
  • 缺乏市场状态识别机制，无法自动区分趋势市和震荡市
  • 仓位管理较为简单，未考虑波动率自适应调仓

改进建议

  • 增加市场状态过滤器（如ADX或波动率指标），在震荡市中减少或暂停交易
  • 引入ATR自适应止损止盈，根据市场波动率动态调整风控参数

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and maintain
  • Effectively captures medium to long-term swing profits during trending markets
  • Equipped with trailing stop and fixed take-profit, providing a basic risk management framework

Weaknesses

  • Prone to generating numerous false signals resulting in frequent losses during ranging markets
  • Lacks market regime detection mechanism, unable to automatically distinguish between trending and ranging conditions
  • Position sizing is simplistic without volatility-adaptive lot adjustment

Improvements

  • Add a market regime filter (e.g., ADX or volatility indicator) to reduce or pause trading during ranging markets
  • Introduce ATR-adaptive stop-loss and take-profit to dynamically adjust risk parameters based on market volatility
