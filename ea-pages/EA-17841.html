摘要

该EA结合CCI（商品通道指标）和MACD指标来生成基于动量的交易信号。当两个指标方向一致时进场交易，确认动量强度。系统使用固定手数，并为每笔交易设置止损和止盈。

策略分析

策略核心概念是基于CCI和MACD双指标共振的动量交易系统。入场条件要求CCI指标突破超买/超卖阈值且MACD柱状图方向一致，形成多空信号共振确认。多头入场：CCI从超卖区域上穿且MACD柱状图为正或零轴上方金叉；空头入场：CCI从超买区域下穿且MACD柱状图为负或零轴下方死叉。出场条件依赖固定止损和止盈设置，部分版本可能包含跟踪止损功能。仓位管理采用固定手数模式，未根据账户余额或波动率动态调整仓位大小。资金管理方面较为基础，缺乏基于风险百分比的仓位计算和最大持仓限制等高级资金管理机制。

Summary

This EA combines CCI (Commodity Channel Index) and MACD indicators to generate momentum-based trade signals. It enters trades when both indicators align in the same direction, confirming momentum strength. The system uses fixed lot sizing with stop loss and take profit for each trade.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core concept is a dual-indicator momentum trading system based on CCI and MACD confluence. Entry conditions require the CCI indicator to breach overbought/oversold thresholds while the MACD histogram aligns in the same direction, forming a multi-signal confirmation. Long entry: CCI crosses up from oversold zone and MACD histogram is positive or MACD golden cross above zero line; Short entry: CCI crosses down from overbought zone and MACD histogram is negative or MACD death cross below zero line. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit settings, with some versions potentially including a trailing stop function. Position sizing uses a fixed lot model without dynamic adjustment based on account balance or market volatility. Money management is relatively basic, lacking risk-percentage-based position calculation and advanced mechanisms such as maximum position limits or equity protection stops.

优势

  • 双指标共振过滤假信号，提高入场准确率
  • 固定止损止盈设置，每笔交易风险可控
  • 策略逻辑清晰简洁，易于理解和优化
  • CCI和MACD互补，兼顾超买超卖和趋势动量

劣势

  • 固定手数不根据账户规模调整，资金利用率低
  • 缺乏新K线检查机制，可能在同一K线重复开仓
  • 没有点差过滤，高波动时段点差扩大可能吞噬利润
  • 缺乏多时间框架确认，单一周期信号可能产生假突破
  • CCI和MACD均为滞后指标，入场时机可能偏晚

改进建议

  • 增加基于账户余额百分比的动态仓位计算，替代固定手数
  • 添加多时间框架确认机制，如H4趋势方向配合H1入场信号
  • 加入点差过滤和新K线检查，避免不利条件下交易
  • 增加移动止损和部分平仓功能，优化盈利管理

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator confluence filters false signals and improves entry accuracy
  • Fixed stop loss and take profit settings provide controllable risk per trade
  • Clear and concise strategy logic that is easy to understand and optimize
  • CCI and MACD complement each other, combining overbought/oversold with trend momentum

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing does not adjust to account size, resulting in low capital utilization
  • Lacks new bar check mechanism, potentially opening duplicate positions on the same bar
  • No spread filter, widened spreads during high volatility sessions can erode profits
  • Lacks multi-timeframe confirmation, single timeframe signals may produce false breakouts
  • Both CCI and MACD are lagging indicators, entry timing may be delayed

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account balance percentage instead of fixed lots
  • Implement multi-timeframe confirmation, such as H4 trend direction with H1 entry signals
  • Add spread filter and new bar check to avoid trading under unfavorable conditions
  • Add trailing stop and partial close functionality to optimize profit management
