摘要

该EA基于MACD背离模式（常规背离和隐藏背离）结合价格行为确认进行交易。它在多种周期配置下检测看涨和看跌背离，当模式条件满足时执行交易，并内置止损和止盈管理。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是识别MACD指标与价格之间的背离模式，包括常规顶背离、常规底背离、隐藏顶背离和隐藏底背离四种类型。入场条件要求MACD出现背离信号且价格行为确认趋势反转或延续，具体通过比较相邻波峰/波谷的MACD值与对应的价格高低点来判断。出场条件依赖固定的止损和止盈设置，同时支持移动止损来锁定利润。仓位管理采用固定手数或基于资金比例的动态计算方式。资金管理方面提供了风险百分比参数来控制每笔交易的最大亏损比例，但缺乏整体账户级别的风险控制机制如最大回撤限制或连续亏损后的仓位缩减策略。

Summary

This EA trades based on MACD divergence patterns (regular and hidden) combined with price action confirmation. It detects bullish and bearish divergences across multiple timeframe configurations and executes trades when pattern criteria are met with built-in stop loss and take profit management.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is identifying divergence patterns between the MACD indicator and price, covering four types: regular bullish divergence, regular bearish divergence, hidden bullish divergence, and hidden bearish divergence. Entry conditions require a MACD divergence signal confirmed by price action, determined by comparing MACD values and corresponding price extremes of adjacent peaks/troughs. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit settings, with trailing stop support to lock in profits. Position sizing uses either fixed lot sizes or dynamic calculation based on account equity percentage. Money management provides a risk percentage parameter to control maximum loss per trade, but lacks account-level risk controls such as maximum drawdown limits or position reduction after consecutive losses.

优势

  • 基于MACD背离是经过验证的经典交易策略，具有理论基础支撑
  • 支持多种背离类型（常规和隐藏），提供更全面的交易信号
  • 提供移动止损功能，能有效锁定浮盈降低风险

劣势

  • 背离识别算法依赖波峰波谷检测，在震荡行情中容易产生虚假信号
  • 止损默认值为0存在风险，用户可能忘记设置导致无限亏损
  • 缺乏账户级别的整体风险控制，如最大回撤保护和连续亏损后的自动降仓

改进建议

  • 增加多重时间框架确认，要求高级别周期也出现同向信号才入场，提高信号可靠性
  • 加入动态止损机制，基于ATR或波动率自适应调整止损距离，替代固定点数止损

Strengths

  • MACD divergence is a well-validated classic trading strategy with solid theoretical foundation
  • Supports multiple divergence types (regular and hidden), providing more comprehensive trading signals
  • Trailing stop feature effectively locks in floating profits and reduces risk

Weaknesses

  • Divergence detection relies on peak/trough identification which can generate false signals in choppy markets
  • Default stop loss value of 0 is risky; users may forget to set it resulting in unlimited losses
  • Lacks account-level risk controls such as maximum drawdown protection and auto position reduction after consecutive losses

Improvements

  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation requiring higher timeframe alignment before entry to improve signal reliability
  • Implement dynamic stop loss based on ATR or volatility adaptive adjustment instead of fixed pip stop loss
