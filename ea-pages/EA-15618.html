摘要

该EA基于Q2MA自定义指标的交叉信号进行交易，使用可配置的平滑算法（SMA、EMA、JJMA、T3等）。当上线穿越下线时开多仓，反向穿越时开空仓，并可选择在信号反转时平仓。资金管理支持多种保证金模式，包括固定手数。

策略分析

该EA采用双线交叉的趋势跟踪策略，核心依赖Q2MA自定义指标的两条平滑线进行方向判断。入场条件为：上线从下方穿越下线时产生买入信号，上线从上方穿越下线时产生卖出信号，支持信号反转(Invert)功能。出场条件包括止损/止盈预设值以及信号反转时的反向平仓功能。仓位管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，支持五种资金管理模式：自由保证金、余额、扣除亏损后的自由保证金、扣除亏损后的余额及固定手数。策略在每个新K线形成时检测信号变化，使用SignalBar参数控制信号延迟，避免在未完成K线上产生错误信号。该EA的平滑算法选择灵活，支持JJMA、JurX、ParMA、T3、VIDYA、AMA等高级平滑方法，提升了信号质量。

Summary

This EA trades based on crossover signals from the Q2MA custom indicator, which uses configurable smoothing algorithms (SMA, EMA, JJMA, T3, etc.). It opens buy positions when the upper indicator line crosses above the lower line and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with optional position closing on signal reversal. Money management supports multiple margin modes including fixed lot sizing.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a dual-line crossover trend-following strategy, relying on two smoothed lines from the Q2MA custom indicator to determine market direction. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the upper line crosses above the lower line, and a sell signal is generated on the opposite crossover, with an optional inversion feature. Exit conditions include configurable stop-loss and take-profit levels, plus optional closing of opposite positions upon signal reversal. Position sizing is controlled via the MM parameter with five money management modes: free margin-based, balance-based, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot sizing. The strategy detects signal changes only on new bar formation using the CIsNewBar class, with a SignalBar parameter controlling signal lag to avoid false signals on incomplete bars. The smoothing algorithm is highly configurable, supporting advanced methods like JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, and AMA for improved signal quality.

优势

  • 支持多种高级平滑算法（JJMA、T3、VIDYA等），信号质量可调优
  • 设有止损止盈和滑点控制，风险参数完整
  • 新K线检测机制避免重复信号，信号延迟参数防止虚假触发
  • 买卖方向独立开关，灵活性高
  • 信号反转平仓功能可减少持仓风险

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值偏大，在短线交易中可能不合适
  • 缺少加仓和移动止损（追踪止损）机制，盈利时无法锁定利润
  • 依赖外部自定义指标Q2MA和头文件，部署复杂度增加
  • 无交易时间过滤和点差过滤，可能在不利时段开仓
  • 仓位管理中MM=0.1在LOT模式下无实际意义，参数设计有歧义

改进建议

  • 增加移动止损（Trailing Stop）功能以在趋势延续时锁定利润
  • 添加交易时间过滤和点差过滤，避免在高点差或低流动性时段开仓
  • 增加持仓检查和最大持仓限制，防止过度集中风险

Strengths

  • Supports multiple advanced smoothing algorithms (JJMA, T3, VIDYA, etc.) for tunable signal quality
  • Complete risk parameters with stop-loss, take-profit, and slippage control
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate signals; SignalBar parameter avoids false triggers on incomplete bars
  • Independent buy/sell direction switches provide high flexibility
  • Opposite position closing on signal reversal reduces holding risk

Weaknesses

  • Default SL=1000 and TP=2000 points are large and may be unsuitable for shorter-term trading
  • No trailing stop or position addition mechanism, limiting profit-locking capability during favorable moves
  • Depends on external custom indicator Q2MA and include files, increasing deployment complexity
  • No trading session or spread filter, potentially opening positions during unfavorable conditions
  • MM=0.1 has no practical effect in LOT mode, creating parameter ambiguity

Improvements

  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in profits during extended trends
  • Add trading session filter and spread filter to avoid entries during high spread or low liquidity periods
  • Add position count check and maximum position limits to prevent over-concentration risk
