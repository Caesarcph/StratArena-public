摘要

一个半自动交易面板EA，通过图表上的按钮下单买入和卖出市价单。它应用固定止损并自动适配5位报价经纪商，使用固定手数且不设止盈。

策略分析

该EA是一个手动交易辅助工具，不具备自动策略逻辑。入场条件完全由交易者点击图表上的Buy或Sell按钮触发，没有基于指标的自动信号。出场条件仅有固定止损，无止盈设置，交易者需手动平仓。仓位管理使用固定手数（Lot参数），没有根据账户余额或风险比例进行动态调整。资金管理方面完全没有考虑，不存在最大持仓限制、账户风险控制或资金分配逻辑。止损计算对5位和3位报价经纪商做了×10调整，但整体风控机制非常薄弱。

Summary

A semi-automated trading panel EA that places Buy and Sell market orders via on-chart buttons. It applies a fixed stop loss with automatic 5-digit broker adjustment and uses a fixed lot size without take profit.

Strategy Analysis

This EA is a manual trading assistant with no automated strategy logic. Entry conditions are entirely driven by the trader clicking Buy or Sell buttons on the chart, with no indicator-based automatic signals. Exit conditions consist solely of a fixed stop loss with no take profit, requiring the trader to manually close positions. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (Lot parameter) with no dynamic adjustment based on account balance or risk percentage. Money management is completely absent, with no maximum position limits, account risk controls, or capital allocation logic. The stop loss calculation includes a ×10 adjustment for 5-digit and 3-digit brokers, but overall risk management is extremely weak.

优势

  • 界面简洁，仅两个按钮实现快速下单，操作直观
  • 自动适配5位和3位报价经纪商的止损计算
  • 使用Magic Number标记订单，便于区分其他EA的交易

劣势

  • 无止盈设置，盈利交易完全依赖人工手动平仓
  • 没有持仓检查，重复点击按钮可能开出多个同方向仓位
  • 固定手数无资金管理，不根据账户规模调整风险敞口

改进建议

  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，防止重复开仓导致过度暴露
  • 增加止盈参数和追踪止损功能，完善出场机制

Strengths

  • Simple and intuitive interface with just two buttons for quick order placement
  • Automatic stop loss adjustment for 5-digit and 3-digit brokers
  • Uses Magic Number to tag orders for differentiation from other EA trades

Weaknesses

  • No take profit setting, profitable trades rely entirely on manual closing by the trader
  • No position check, repeated button clicks may open multiple positions in the same direction
  • Fixed lot size with no money management, risk exposure is not adjusted based on account size

Improvements

  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate orders and overexposure
  • Add take profit parameter and trailing stop functionality for better exit management

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:HIGH

风险点

  • 没有持仓检查，可能导致重复开仓和过度杠杆
  • 无止盈且无自动出场机制，盈利可能因市场反转而回撤
  • 滑点参数设为0在高波动市场可能导致订单无法成交

Risk Assessment

Overall:HIGH

Risk Points

  • No position check may lead to duplicate orders and excessive leverage
  • No take profit or automatic exit mechanism, profits may retrace on market reversal
  • Slippage parameter set to 0 may cause order rejection in high volatility markets
