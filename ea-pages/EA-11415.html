摘要

该EA基于BvsB（多空对比）自定义指标进行交易，当指标线从上方穿越信号线下方时开多仓，从下方穿越信号线上方时开空仓。它支持可配置的平滑方法和适用价格类型，并提供基于保证金模式的资金管理选项。EA包含止损止盈设置，以及独立的开仓和平仓开关。

策略分析

该EA的策略核心是基于BvsB（多空对比）指标的趋势跟踪交易系统。入场条件为：当上一根K线的指标线（Ind）高于信号线（Sign）且当前K线指标线跌破信号线时，触发买入开仓信号；反之，当上一根K线指标线低于信号线且当前K线指标线上穿信号线时，触发卖出开仓信号。出场条件包括：买入信号出现时同时关闭卖出仓位，卖出信号出现时同时关闭买入仓位，以及固定的止损和止盈。仓位管理通过MM参数和MMMode模式控制，支持按自由保证金、余额、亏损保证金、亏损余额比例计算仓位，也支持固定手数。EA使用IsNewBar机制确保每个K线只计算一次信号，避免重复交易。指标平滑方法支持SMA、EMA、JJMA、JurX、ParMA、T3、VIDYA、AMA等多种算法，灵活度较高。

Summary

This EA trades based on the BvsB (Bulls vs Bears) custom indicator, opening buy positions when the indicator line crosses below the signal line from above, and sell positions when it crosses above from below. It uses configurable smoothing methods and applied price types with money management options including lot sizing based on margin modes. The EA includes stop loss and take profit settings along with separate toggles for opening and closing positions.

Strategy Analysis

The EA's strategy is centered on a trend-following trading system based on the BvsB (Bulls vs Bears) indicator. Entry conditions are: when the previous bar's indicator line (Ind) is above the signal line (Sign) and the current bar's indicator line crosses below the signal line, a buy open signal is triggered; conversely, when the previous bar's indicator line is below the signal line and the current bar's indicator line crosses above the signal line, a sell open signal is triggered. Exit conditions include: closing sell positions when a buy signal appears, closing buy positions when a sell signal appears, and fixed stop loss and take profit levels. Position sizing is controlled through the MM parameter and MMMode, supporting calculations based on free margin, balance, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance percentages, or fixed lot size. The EA uses the IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are calculated only once per bar, preventing repeated trades. The smoothing method supports multiple algorithms including SMA, EMA, JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, and AMA, providing significant flexibility.

优势

  • 支持多种平滑算法（SMA、EMA、JJMA、JurX等），策略灵活度高
  • 具有IsNewBar新K线检测机制，避免同根K线重复开仓
  • 开仓和平仓信号独立可控，可以只开不关或只关不开
  • 止损止盈参数完整，风险控制有基本保障
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，适应不同风险偏好

劣势

  • 依赖外部自定义指标BvsB和库文件，缺乏独立性和可移植性
  • 止损设置为1000点、止盈2000点，数值偏大，可能导致较大亏损
  • 没有点差过滤和波动性过滤，在剧烈波动时可能产生不利成交
  • 信号反转时直接反手平仓再开仓，没有考虑市场震荡的假信号问题
  • 缺少最大持仓和最大回撤等高级风险控制机制

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和波动性过滤，避免在不利条件下开仓
  • 增加追踪止损功能，在趋势行情中锁定更多利润
  • 增加最大回撤和最大持仓限制，完善资金管理安全网

Strengths

  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithms (SMA, EMA, JJMA, JurX, etc.) providing high strategy flexibility
  • Implements IsNewBar mechanism to prevent repeated entries on the same bar
  • Independent toggles for opening and closing positions allow granular control
  • Complete stop loss and take profit parameters provide basic risk control
  • Multiple money management modes accommodate different risk preferences

Weaknesses

  • Depends on external custom indicator BvsB and library files, lacking independence and portability
  • Stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are large values that may lead to significant losses
  • No spread filter or volatility filter, potentially resulting in unfavorable fills during volatile conditions
  • Reversal signals directly close and open opposite positions without considering false signals in ranging markets
  • Lacks advanced risk controls such as maximum position limits or maximum drawdown protection

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and volatility filter to avoid opening positions under unfavorable conditions
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in more profits during trending markets
  • Add maximum drawdown and maximum position limits to improve the money management safety net
