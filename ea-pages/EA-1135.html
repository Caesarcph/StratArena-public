摘要

Combo_Right是一个多感知机交易EA，将基于CCI的基础信号与最多三个单层感知机（卖出、买入和通用）相结合来生成买卖信号。感知机计算可配置历史周期内价格差的加权和，监督函数根据运行模式参数决定遵循哪个信号源。

策略分析

该EA采用单层感知机（一种最简单的神经网络模型）作为核心策略引擎，结合CCI指标作为基础交易系统。策略概念是通过三个独立的感知机分别学习卖出信号、买入信号和综合信号，每个感知机使用4个权重对5个历史价格差值进行加权求和来产生输出。入场条件为：在pass=4模式下，若通用感知机输出>0且买入感知机输出>0则做多，若通用感知机输出<0且卖出感知机输出<0则做空；其他模式下感知机信号不满足时回退到CCI信号。出场条件完全依赖固定点数的止盈和止损，没有动态出场机制。仓位管理非常简单，直接使用固定的手数参数lots，没有根据账户余额或风险比例进行动态调整。资金管理方面缺乏保护机制，没有最大回撤限制、没有仓位缩放、没有资金曲线监控。感知机的权重需要通过外部优化来设定，EA本身不具备在线学习能力。

Summary

Combo_Right is a multi-perceptron trading EA that combines a basic CCI-based signal with up to three single-layer perceptrons (sale, buying, and general) to generate buy/sell signals. The perceptrons compute weighted sums of price differences across configurable historical periods, and a supervisor function decides which signal source to follow based on the operating mode parameter.

Strategy Analysis

The EA employs single-layer perceptrons—the simplest neural network model—as its core strategy engine, combined with a CCI indicator as the basic trading system. The strategy concept uses three independent perceptrons to learn sell signals, buy signals, and general signals respectively, each computing a weighted sum of 4 price differences across 5 historical price points. Entry conditions: in pass=4 mode, it goes long if the general perceptron output>0 and the buying perceptron output>0, or goes short if the general perceptron output<0 and the sale perceptron output<0; in other modes, it falls back to the CCI signal when perceptron conditions are not met. Exit conditions rely entirely on fixed-point take-profit and stop-loss values with no dynamic exit mechanism. Position sizing is extremely simple, using a fixed lot size parameter with no dynamic adjustment based on account balance or risk ratio. Money management lacks protective mechanisms—there is no maximum drawdown limit, no position scaling, and no equity curve monitoring. The perceptron weights must be set through external optimization, as the EA itself has no online learning capability.

优势

  • 使用感知机模型实现了一种简单的机器学习方法，概念新颖
  • 提供多种运行模式（pass参数），可以灵活测试不同信号组合
  • 有完整的止损止盈检查机制，防止在止损/止盈过近时下单
  • 使用已形成K线（Shift=1）避免未来数据偏差，支持回测可靠性
  • 代码结构清晰，Supervisor模式将信号逻辑分层组织

劣势

  • 单层感知机表达能力极弱，本质上只是线性加权的价格差求和，无法捕捉非线性市场规律
  • 感知机权重需要外部优化器调参，EA本身没有在线学习能力，容易过拟合历史数据
  • 固定手数没有资金管理策略，缺乏根据账户净值动态调整仓位大小的机制
  • 出场完全依赖固定止损止盈，没有移动止损、部分平仓或信号反转出场的灵活机制
  • 仅在同一时间周期上操作，没有多周期确认，容易产生虚假信号
  • CCI作为基础信号过于简单，单独使用时在震荡市和趋势市中都容易产生亏损信号

改进建议

  • 引入多层感知机或更复杂的神经网络结构以增强非线性模式识别能力，同时加入在线学习机制使权重能随市场变化自适应更新
  • 添加资金管理模块，根据账户净值和风险比例动态计算仓位大小，并加入最大回撤保护和最大持仓限制
  • 实现移动止损和信号反转出场机制，替代纯固定止损止盈的出场方式
  • 加入多周期确认机制，要求至少两个以上时间周期的信号一致才开仓

Strengths

  • Implements a simple machine learning approach using perceptron models, offering a novel concept for EA trading
  • Provides multiple operating modes via the pass parameter, allowing flexible testing of different signal combinations
  • Has a complete stop-loss and take-profit validation mechanism to prevent placing orders when SL/TP are too close
  • Uses formed bars (Shift=1) by default to avoid future data bias, supporting backtest reliability
  • Clean code structure with the Supervisor pattern organizing signal logic in a layered fashion

Weaknesses

  • Single-layer perceptrons have extremely limited expressive power—essentially just linearly weighted sums of price differences, unable to capture nonlinear market dynamics
  • Perceptron weights require external optimizer tuning; the EA has no online learning capability, making it prone to overfitting historical data
  • Fixed lot sizing with no money management strategy, lacking dynamic position sizing based on account equity
  • Exits rely entirely on fixed stop-loss and take-profit, with no trailing stop, partial close, or signal-reversal exit flexibility
  • Operates on a single timeframe only, lacking multi-timeframe confirmation, which increases the risk of false signals
  • CCI as the basic signal is overly simplistic and prone to generating losing signals in both ranging and trending markets when used alone

Improvements

  • Introduce multi-layer perceptrons or more complex neural network architectures to enhance nonlinear pattern recognition, and add online learning mechanisms so weights can adaptively update as market conditions change
  • Add a money management module that dynamically calculates position size based on account equity and risk ratio, with maximum drawdown protection and maximum position limits
  • Implement trailing stop and signal-reversal exit mechanisms to replace the purely fixed stop-loss/take-profit exit approach
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation, requiring at least two timeframes to agree before opening a position

  • 感知机权重依赖外部优化，存在过拟合风险，实盘可能与回测表现差异巨大
  • 没有最大回撤限制或紧急平仓机制，持续亏损时无法自动停止交易

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed lot sizing with no money management may cause excessive per-trade risk on small accounts
  • Perceptron weights rely on external optimization, creating overfitting risk where live performance may differ significantly from backtest results
  • No maximum drawdown limit or emergency closing mechanism, meaning the EA cannot automatically stop trading during sustained losses
