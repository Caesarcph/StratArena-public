摘要

该EA使用双RSI和双MA交叉系统来识别趋势方向变化并生成交易信号。它将短期RSI与长期RSI比较、短期MA与长期MA比较，结合两个信号确认趋势转变。仅在趋势反转且两组指标一致指向新方向时才开仓。

策略分析

策略概念基于双指标确认的趋势跟踪系统，通过短期RSI与长期RSI的相对位置以及短期MA与长期MA的交叉来判断趋势方向。入场条件为：当信号柱上短期MA大于长期MA且短期RSI大于长期RSI时，趋势得分Trend为正（最大+2），若上一趋势方向为负则触发买入开仓；反之，当Trend为负且上一趋势为正时触发卖出开仓。出场条件依赖反向信号：当趋势反转为空头时平多头仓位，趋势反转为多头时平空头仓位，同时设有固定的止损和止盈点数。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值表示固定手数，正值表示存款比例。资金管理采用百分比或固定手数模式，止损1000点、止盈2000点，风险回报比为1:2。策略还使用IsNewBar机制确保仅在新区块时计算信号，避免重复交易。

Summary

This EA uses a dual RSI and dual MA crossover system to identify trend direction changes and generate trading signals. It compares a short-period RSI against a long-period RSI and a short-period MA against a long-period MA, combining both signals to confirm trend shifts. Trades are opened only on trend reversals where both indicator pairs agree on the new direction.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a dual-indicator-confirmed trend following system that determines trend direction by comparing the relative positions of short-period vs long-period RSI and short-period vs long-period MA. Entry conditions: on the signal bar, if the short MA is above the long MA and the short RSI is above the long RSI, the Trend score is positive (max +2); if the previous trend was negative, a buy entry is triggered. Conversely, when Trend is negative and the previous trend was positive, a sell entry is triggered. Exit conditions rely on reversal signals: long positions are closed when the trend turns bearish, and short positions are closed when the trend turns bullish, with fixed stop loss and take profit in points. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the deposit. Money management uses percentage or fixed lot mode with a 1000-point stop loss and 2000-point take profit, yielding a risk-reward ratio of 1:2. The strategy also uses the IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are only calculated on new bars, preventing repeated trades within the same bar.

优势

  • 双指标确认机制减少假信号，RSI和MA必须同时同方向才确认趋势
  • 具有独立的买卖开仓和平仓权限控制，灵活性好
  • 使用IsNewBar机制防止同一柱内重复交易，逻辑严谨
  • 止损和止盈明确设定，风险回报比1:2合理
  • 代码结构清晰，包含外部交易算法库复用

劣势

  • 短期RSI周期仅为3，噪声极大，容易产生频繁虚假交叉信号
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点在H4周期上固定不变，未考虑市场波动率动态调整
  • 缺少点差过滤和滑点保护机制，在流动性差的市场中可能承受额外成本
  • 趋势搜索循环遍历历史柱可能导致信号延迟或在高波动时频繁重算
  • 没有多空持仓检查，可能在已有持仓时重复开仓

改进建议

  • 增加ATR动态止损止盈，根据市场波动率自适应调整止损距离
  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓
  • 引入点差过滤和交易时段限制，减少低流动性时段的交易成本

Strengths

  • Dual-indicator confirmation reduces false signals by requiring both RSI and MA to agree on direction
  • Independent buy/sell open and close permission controls provide good flexibility
  • IsNewBar mechanism prevents repeated trades within the same bar, ensuring logical rigor
  • Fixed stop loss and take profit with 1:2 risk-reward ratio is reasonable
  • Clean code structure with external trading algorithm library reuse

Weaknesses

  • Short RSI period of only 3 is extremely noisy and prone to frequent false crossover signals
  • Fixed 1000-point SL and 2000-point TP on H4 do not adapt to market volatility changes
  • Lacks spread filter and slippage protection, potentially incurring extra costs in low-liquidity markets
  • The trend search loop through historical bars may cause signal delays or frequent recalculations during high volatility
  • No position check before opening, risking duplicate positions on the same direction

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit to adapt stop distances to market volatility
  • Add position check logic before opening trades to avoid duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Introduce spread filter and trading session restrictions to reduce costs during low-liquidity periods

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 固定止损未考虑波动率，在极端行情中止损可能过近或过远
  • 缺少持仓检查可能导致同方向重复开仓，放大风险敞口
  • 短期RSI=3噪声大，可能在震荡市中频繁触发错误信号

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed stop loss ignores volatility, potentially too close or too far during extreme market conditions
  • Lack of position check may lead to duplicate openings in the same direction, amplifying risk exposure
  • Short RSI=3 is noisy and may frequently trigger incorrect signals in ranging markets
