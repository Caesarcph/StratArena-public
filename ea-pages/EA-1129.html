摘要

该EA使用双信号系统交易趋势反转，结合两个RSI指标和两个移动平均线指标。当快速MA/RSI对上穿慢速对且前一个趋势为看跌时做多，反之做空。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，支持固定比例或固定手数方式。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于双重确认的趋势反转交易系统。入场条件要求快速MA与慢速MA的交叉方向和快速RSI与慢速RSI的交叉方向一致，形成一个综合信号值res（范围-2到+2）。当信号bar上Trend为正且上一趋势LastTrend为负时开多仓，反之开空仓。出场条件包括止损和止盈的硬性设置，以及反向信号出现时平仓（如做多时出现看跌信号则平多仓）。仓位管理通过MM参数实现，负值代表固定手数，正值代表存款比例。资金管理依赖传统的止损止盈机制，止损1000点、止盈2000点，风险回报比为1:2。策略还通过遍历历史bar来确定上一个趋势方向，确保只在趋势反转时入场，而非趋势延续时重复开仓。

Summary

This EA trades trend reversals using a dual-signal system combining two RSI indicators and two Moving Average indicators. It enters long when the fast MA/RSI pair crosses above the slow pair and the previous trend was bearish, and vice versa for shorts. Position sizing uses a fixed fractional or fixed lot approach controlled by the MM parameter.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is a dual-confirmation trend reversal trading system. Entry conditions require that the crossover direction of the fast MA versus the slow MA and the fast RSI versus the slow RSI align, producing a composite signal value 'res' ranging from -2 to +2. A long position is opened when Trend at the signal bar is positive and LastTrend was negative, and vice versa for short positions. Exit conditions include hard stop loss and take profit settings, as well as closing positions when a reversal signal appears (e.g., closing longs when a bearish signal emerges). Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the deposit. Money management relies on traditional stop loss and take profit mechanisms, with a 1000-point stop loss and 2000-point take profit, yielding a risk-reward ratio of 1:2. The strategy also iterates through historical bars to determine the previous trend direction, ensuring entries only occur on trend reversals rather than repeated entries during trend continuation.

优势

  • 双重确认机制（MA+RSI）降低了假信号的概率
  • 只在趋势反转时入场，避免了趋势延续中的重复开仓
  • 止损止盈设置合理，风险回报比为1:2
  • 支持灵活的仓位管理方式（固定比例或固定手数）
  • 提供买卖开关控制，可以单独启用或禁用多空方向

劣势

  • 快速RSI周期仅为3，极易产生频繁的假信号
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点对于H4周期可能过大，未考虑市场波动性
  • 遍历历史bar寻找上一趋势方向可能导致性能问题和延迟
  • 缺少点差过滤，在点差较大时可能造成不必要的亏损
  • 没有追踪止损或动态止损机制，无法锁定浮动利润

改进建议

  • 增加ATR动态止损止盈，根据市场波动性自适应调整止损距离
  • 添加点差过滤条件，避免在点差过大时开仓
  • 优化历史趋势搜索算法，限制搜索范围以提高性能

Strengths

  • Dual confirmation mechanism (MA + RSI) reduces probability of false signals
  • Entries only on trend reversals avoid repeated opening during trend continuation
  • Reasonable stop loss and take profit settings with 1:2 risk-reward ratio
  • Flexible position sizing supporting both fixed fraction and fixed lot approaches
  • Buy/sell permission switches allow enabling or disabling each direction independently

Weaknesses

  • Fast RSI period of only 3 is highly prone to frequent false signals
  • Stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points may be excessive for H4 timeframe without considering market volatility
  • Iterating through historical bars to find the previous trend direction may cause performance issues and delay
  • No spread filter, which can lead to unnecessary losses when spreads are wide
  • No trailing stop or dynamic stop mechanism to lock in floating profits

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit to adapt stop distances to market volatility
  • Add a spread filter condition to avoid opening positions when the spread is too wide
  • Optimize the historical trend search algorithm by limiting the search range for better performance
