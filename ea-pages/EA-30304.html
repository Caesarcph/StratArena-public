摘要

该EA监控日内价格波动幅度与ATR的关系，当价格突破平均日波幅的触发百分比时入场交易。交易方向由当前价格距日内高低点的远近决定，止损止盈基于ATR计算，并在交易日结束时自动平仓。

策略分析

策略概念是基于ATR的日内区间突破交易，当日内价格波动超过ATR均值的触发百分比时入场。入场条件为：若当前价格距日内低点的幅度超过触发阈值则做多，若距日内高点的幅度超过触发阈值则做空。出场条件包括：止盈设置为入场价加减ATR剩余波幅（atr_rest），止损设置为入场价加减ATR触发波幅（atr_trigger），以及每日结束时的定时器强制平仓。仓位管理仅支持固定手数输入，并通过SetVolume函数进行最小最大手数校验及步长对齐。资金管理方面仅检查了自由保证金是否大于1000倍手数，但没有设置最大持仓限制或风险百分比控制。该策略的止损止盈比约为(100-perTrigger)/perTrigger，当perTrigger为60时，盈亏比约为0.67，风险收益比不佳。

Summary

This EA monitors the daily price range relative to the ATR and enters a trade when the price moves beyond a configurable trigger percentage of the average daily range. It determines trade direction based on which side of the daily range is closer to the current price, using ATR-based values for stop loss and take profit. All open positions are automatically closed at the end of the trading day.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is an intraday range breakout system based on ATR, entering trades when the daily price movement exceeds a configurable trigger percentage of the average daily ATR range. Entry conditions: a buy is triggered when the distance from the current bid to the daily low exceeds the ATR trigger threshold, and a sell is triggered when the distance from the daily high to the current ask exceeds the threshold. Exit conditions include a take profit set at the ATR remainder (atr_rest = total ATR range minus trigger), a stop loss set at the ATR trigger value, and a mandatory end-of-day close via the timer. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size input with basic validation against broker minimum/maximum/step constraints. Money management only checks if free margin exceeds 1000 times the lot size, lacking percentage-based risk control or maximum position limits. The risk-reward ratio is approximately (100-perTrigger)/perTrigger, which at the default 60% trigger yields a ratio of about 0.67, meaning the stop loss is larger than the take profit.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰简洁，基于ATR动态调整止损止盈，适应不同品种波动特性
  • 每日定时平仓机制避免了隔夜风险和持仓过夜的资金占用
  • 日内波幅已达到阈值时拒绝入场，避免追高杀低

劣势

  • 默认参数下盈亏比低于1（止损大于止盈），长期期望值为负
  • 每日仅允许开仓一次且无加仓逻辑，错失日内趋势延续的机会
  • 缺少点差过滤、滑点保护和重连处理，实盘环境可能产生异常订单
  • OnInit中触发率校验提示20到90但实际允许10到90，提示信息与代码不一致

改进建议

  • 调整止损止盈计算逻辑使盈亏比大于1，或允许用户独立设置止损和止盈比例
  • 增加点差过滤和最大滑点限制，避免在高点差时段入场
  • 增加新K线检测避免同一根K线重复触发，并修复OnInit中错误提示信息的不一致

Strengths

  • Clear and concise strategy logic with ATR-based dynamic SL/TP that adapts to different instrument volatilities
  • End-of-day forced close mechanism avoids overnight risk and capital tie-up
  • Refuses entry when daily range threshold is already reached, avoiding chasing price at extremes

Weaknesses

  • Default parameters produce a risk-reward ratio below 1 (SL larger than TP), leading to negative long-term expectancy
  • Only one trade per day with no pyramiding logic, missing opportunities from sustained intraday trends
  • Lacks spread filter, slippage protection, and reconnection handling, risking abnormal orders in live trading
  • OnInit trigger validation alert says 20-90 but actual code checks 10-90, inconsistent error message

Improvements

  • Adjust SL/TP calculation to achieve a risk-reward ratio above 1, or allow independent user-configurable SL/TP percentages
  • Add spread filter and maximum slippage limit to avoid entering during high-spread periods
  • Add new bar detection to prevent duplicate triggers on the same bar, and fix the inconsistent error message in OnInit

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 止损大于止盈，单笔亏损风险大于盈利
  • 无点差过滤，高波动时段点差扩大可能造成不利入场
  • 定时器平仓依赖本地时间，平台断线或重启可能导致仓位无法按时关闭
  • 仅用布尔标志trade_opened控制单日单笔，EA重启后标志重置可能重复开仓

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Stop loss larger than take profit, meaning single trade loss exceeds potential gain
  • No spread filter, high-volatility period spread widening can cause unfavorable entry prices
  • Timer-based close depends on local time; platform disconnection or restart may prevent timely position closure
  • Boolean flag trade_opened resets on EA restart, potentially causing duplicate daily entries
