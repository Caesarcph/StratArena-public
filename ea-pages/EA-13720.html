摘要

该EA基于DecEMA自定义指标进行交易，当指标向上拐头时做多，向下拐头时做空。它使用信号柱来检测趋势方向变化，并支持可配置的止损、止盈和资金管理选项。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于DecEMA指标的方向变化来进行趋势跟踪交易。入场条件为：当指标值从下降转为上升（Value[0]>Value[1]且Value[1]<Value[2]）时开多仓，当指标值从上升转为下降（Value[0]<Value[1]且Value[1]>Value[2]）时开空仓。出场条件包括止损和止盈的硬性设定，以及反向信号触发时的平仓（BuyPosClose/SellPosClose）。仓位管理提供了五种模式，包括按自由保证金比例、按余额比例、按亏损后自由保证金比例、按亏损后余额比例以及固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认为0.1。EA在每根新K线或指定信号柱上计算信号，避免逐tick重复交易，但仅在SignalBar为0时才逐tick计算。

Summary

This EA trades based on the DecEMA (Decomposition EMA) custom indicator, entering long when the indicator turns upward and short when it turns downward. It uses a signal bar to detect trend direction changes and executes trades with configurable stop loss, take profit, and money management options.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is trend following based on direction changes of the DecEMA indicator. Entry conditions: open a long position when the indicator turns upward (Value[0]>Value[1] and Value[1]<Value[2]), and open a short position when it turns downward (Value[0]<Value[1] and Value[1]>Value[2]). Exit conditions include hard stop loss and take profit levels, as well as closing positions on reverse signals (BuyPosClose/SellPosClose). Position sizing offers five modes: percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin after losses, percentage of balance after losses, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter, defaulting to 0.1. The EA calculates signals on each new bar or the specified signal bar, avoiding redundant per-tick trading, but only calculates per-tick when SignalBar is set to 0.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简洁清晰，基于指标拐点入场，易于理解和维护
  • 提供多种仓位管理模式，灵活性较高
  • 支持分别控制多空的开仓和平仓，便于精细化管理
  • 有新K线检测机制，避免重复信号
  • 止损止盈参数可配置，基础风险控制到位

劣势

  • 依赖自定义DecEMA指标，指标源码不可见，黑盒风险较高
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值过大，可能不适用于所有品种
  • 缺少点差过滤，在点差较大的品种上可能造成不必要损失
  • 没有持仓检查逻辑，可能重复开仓
  • 信号仅基于三个连续指标值比较，缺乏确认机制，容易产生虚假信号

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查，避免同方向重复开仓
  • 增加点差过滤和最小波动阈值，过滤低质量信号

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is clean and simple, based on indicator turning points, easy to understand and maintain
  • Multiple position sizing modes available, offering good flexibility
  • Separate controls for buy/sell opening and closing allow fine-grained management
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate signals
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit provide basic risk control

Weaknesses

  • Depends on custom DecEMA indicator whose source is unavailable, posing black-box risk
  • Default stop loss of 1000 and take profit of 2000 points are excessive for many instruments
  • No spread filter, which may cause unnecessary losses on high-spread instruments
  • No position check before opening, risk of duplicate positions
  • Signal based only on three consecutive indicator values without confirmation, prone to false signals

Improvements

  • Add position check to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Add spread filter and minimum movement threshold to filter low-quality signals
