摘要

该EA基于OsHMA（赫尔移动平均线振荡器）指标进行交易，支持两种入场模式：零线突破和柱状图方向变化（扭转）。它使用可配置的止损和止盈开立多空仓位，并可独立控制多空方向的入场和出场权限。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略基于OsHMA振荡器，即赫尔移动平均线（HMA）的差值振荡器，本质是一种平滑的动量指标。入场条件有两种模式：breakdown模式要求振荡器穿越零线，即从负转正时做多、从正转负时做空；twist模式要求振荡器柱状图方向发生扭转，即当前柱值大于前一柱值且前两柱呈下降趋势时做多，反之做空。出场条件与入场信号对称，当反向信号出现时平仓，同时设置了固定的止损和止盈点数。仓位管理通过MM参数实现，负值表示固定手数，正值表示按存款比例计算手数。资金管理方面，EA依赖TradeAlgorithms.mqh库中的标准化函数执行开平仓操作，包含价格偏差控制（Deviation参数）以应对滑点。策略未包含加仓、减仓或追踪止损等高级资金管理功能，属于较为基础的动量跟踪系统。

Summary

This EA trades based on the OsHMA (Oscillator of Hull Moving Average) indicator, supporting two entry modes: zero-line breakout and histogram direction change (twist). It opens buy/sell positions with configurable stop loss and take profit, and can independently control entry and exit permissions for long and short positions.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the OsHMA oscillator, which is a difference oscillator of Hull Moving Averages (HMA), essentially a smoothed momentum indicator. Entry conditions offer two modes: the breakdown mode requires the oscillator to cross the zero line — going long when it turns from negative to positive and short when it turns from positive to negative; the twist mode requires the histogram direction to reverse — going long when the current bar value exceeds the previous bar while the prior two bars showed declining values, and vice versa. Exit conditions are symmetric to entry signals, closing positions when a reverse signal appears, with fixed stop loss and take profit in points. Position sizing is handled via the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a deposit share percentage. Money management relies on standardized functions from the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library for order execution, including price deviation control (Deviation parameter) to handle slippage. The strategy lacks advanced money management features such as position scaling, partial closes, or trailing stops, making it a relatively basic momentum-following system.

优势

  • 双模式入场（零线突破与柱状图扭转）提供了灵活的交易策略选择
  • HMA振荡器相比简单MA振荡器具有更低的延迟和更好的平滑性
  • 多空方向的开仓和平仓权限可独立控制，便于灵活组合策略
  • 使用固定止损止盈提供基本的风险保护
  • 新K线检测机制避免同根K线重复交易

劣势

  • 缺少追踪止损功能，盈利仓位可能因回撤而大幅缩水
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点的默认值过大，可能不适合所有品种
  • 未对交易品种的点差进行过滤，高波动或高点差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • 依赖外部iCustom指标，若OsHMA指标未安装则EA无法运行
  • 缺乏多时间周期确认或其他指标的信号过滤，假信号可能导致频繁亏损交易

改进建议

  • 增加追踪止损功能以保护已获利润，降低盈利回撤风险
  • 添加点差过滤和波动率过滤，避免在高成本环境下入场
  • 增加多时间周期确认机制，例如更高周期趋势方向一致时才允许入场

Strengths

  • Dual entry modes (zero-line breakout and histogram twist) provide flexible strategy choices
  • HMA oscillator has lower latency and better smoothing compared to simple MA oscillators
  • Independent buy/sell open and close permissions allow flexible strategy combinations
  • Fixed stop loss and take profit provide basic risk protection
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated trading on the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Lacks trailing stop functionality, so profitable positions may suffer significant drawdowns on pullbacks
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large for some instruments
  • No spread filter means unfavorable fills may occur in high-volatility or high-spread environments
  • Depends on an external iCustom indicator — EA cannot run if OsHMA indicator is not installed
  • Lacks multi-timeframe confirmation or additional indicator filtering, so false signals may cause frequent losing trades

Improvements

  • Add trailing stop functionality to protect realized profits and reduce drawdown risk
  • Add spread filter and volatility filter to avoid entering trades in high-cost environments
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation, e.g., only allow entries when the higher timeframe trend aligns

风险点

  • 默认止损止盈点数偏大，小账户可能因单笔亏损占比较高而承受较大风险
  • 缺乏加仓检查机制，可能在已有同向持仓时重复开仓导致仓位过重
  • MM参数为负值时使用固定手数，未根据账户余额动态调整，存在过度杠杆风险

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Default stop loss and take profit points are large, posing significant risk for small accounts where a single loss represents a high percentage
  • Lacks position check before opening, potentially duplicating same-direction positions and causing excessive exposure
  • When MM is negative, fixed lot size is used without dynamic adjustment based on account balance, creating over-leveraging risk
