EA-20705 Mean Reversion / 均值回归 MEDIUM 交易 EA ★★☆☆☆

RSI Bollinger Bands EA

This EA combines RSI and Bollinger Bands to identify mean reversion trading opportunities. It enters long positions when RSI is oversold and price touches the lower Bollinger Band, and enters short positions when RSI is overbought and price touches the upper Bollinger Band. Exits are triggered when price reverts to the Bollinger Band middle line or by stop loss/take profit levels.

摘要

该EA结合RSI和布林带指标识别均值回归交易机会。当RSI超卖且价格触及布林带下轨时做多，当RSI超买且价格触及布林带上轨时做空。当价格回归布林带中轨或触发止损/止盈时平仓。

策略分析

策略概念基于均值回归原理，利用布林带衡量价格偏离程度，RSI确认超买超卖状态作为辅助过滤。入场条件为多头：价格触及或跌破布林带下轨且RSI低于超卖阈值；空头：价格触及或突破布林带上轨且RSI高于超买阈值。出场条件包括价格回归布林带中轨时平仓，以及预设的止损止盈水平。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，未见到动态仓位调整机制。资金管理方面缺乏基于账户余额或风险比例的动态计算，仅依赖固定手数和止损止盈控制风险。整体策略逻辑清晰，但缺少对市场趋势环境的过滤，可能在强趋势行情中频繁逆势交易导致连续亏损。

Summary

This EA combines RSI and Bollinger Bands to identify mean reversion trading opportunities. It enters long positions when RSI is oversold and price touches the lower Bollinger Band, and enters short positions when RSI is overbought and price touches the upper Bollinger Band. Exits are triggered when price reverts to the Bollinger Band middle line or by stop loss/take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on mean reversion principles, using Bollinger Bands to measure price deviation and RSI to confirm overbought/oversold conditions as an auxiliary filter. Entry conditions for longs require price touching or breaking below the lower Bollinger Band with RSI below the oversold threshold; for shorts, price touching or breaking above the upper Bollinger Band with RSI above the overbought threshold. Exit conditions include closing positions when price reverts to the Bollinger Band middle line, as well as preset stop loss and take profit levels. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic lot adjustment mechanisms. Money management lacks dynamic calculation based on account balance or risk percentage, relying solely on fixed lots and stop loss/take profit to control risk. The overall strategy logic is clear but lacks a trend environment filter, which may lead to frequent counter-trend trades and consecutive losses during strong trending markets.

优势

  • 双指标过滤减少虚假信号，RSI与布林带结合提高入场质量
  • 均值回归策略在震荡行情中表现稳健，逻辑清晰易懂
  • 设有止损止盈机制，风险有基本控制

劣势

  • 缺乏趋势过滤机制，在强趋势行情中容易逆势交易连续亏损
  • 固定手数仓位管理未考虑账户风险比例，无法根据市场波动动态调整
  • 未包含新K线检查机制，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤指标（如均线方向或ATR），在顺势方向才允许均值回归交易
  • 实现基于账户风险比例的动态仓位管理，根据ATR调整止损距离和手数

Strengths

  • Dual indicator filtering reduces false signals, RSI combined with Bollinger Bands improves entry quality
  • Mean reversion strategy performs steadily in ranging markets with clear and understandable logic
  • Stop loss and take profit mechanisms provide basic risk control

Weaknesses

  • Lacks trend filtering mechanism, prone to consecutive counter-trend losses in strong trending markets
  • Fixed lot position sizing does not consider account risk ratio and cannot dynamically adjust based on market volatility
  • Missing new bar check mechanism, may repeatedly open positions on the same bar

Improvements

  • Add a trend filter indicator (e.g., moving average direction or ADX) to allow mean reversion trades only in the direction of the broader trend
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account risk percentage, adjusting stop loss distance and lot size according to ATR
