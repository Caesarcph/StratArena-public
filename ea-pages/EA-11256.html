Exp_TSI_DeMarker

This EA trades based on the crossover signals of the TSI_DeMarker custom indicator, which combines DeMarker oscillator values with True Strength Index (TSI) smoothing. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with configurable position closing and money management options.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on the crossover of the TSI_DeMarker indicator's main line and signal line, essentially a momentum reversal strategy. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when the indicator line is above the signal line and the previous bar's indicator line crossed from below the signal line; conversely, a sell signal triggers when the indicator line is below and crosses from above. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit in points, plus closing positions on reverse crossover signals when the corresponding close flags are enabled. Position sizing supports four modes: free margin percentage, balance percentage, loss-adjusted free margin percentage, loss-adjusted balance percentage, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter defaulting to 0.1 (10% capital allocation). The strategy employs multiple smoothing algorithms (XMA) on DeMarker values, using three different smoothing lengths to generate the main and signal lines, filtering noise and capturing momentum shifts.

Strengths

  • Multi-layer smoothing algorithm filters noise and reduces false signals
  • Four position sizing modes offer flexibility for different risk preferences
  • Buy/sell open and close can be independently controlled for granular strategy configuration
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate signal execution
  • Stop loss and take profit are properly set, keeping risk manageable

Weaknesses

  • Depends on custom indicator TSI_DeMarker; EA fails if indicator file is missing
  • Default SL 1000 and TP 2000 points are large and may not suit all instruments
  • No check for existing positions in the same direction, potentially causing duplicate entries
  • No spread filter, which could be detrimental during high-spread periods
  • Signals only detected on new bar formation, potentially missing entries in fast-moving markets

Possible improvements

  • Add position existence check to avoid duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Add spread filter to pause trading when spread exceeds a threshold

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No same-direction position check may lead to duplicate entries and overexposure
  • Depends on external custom indicator; handle failure only logs but doesn't prevent subsequent execution attempts
  • Fixed stop loss/take profit not dynamically adjusted for market volatility
