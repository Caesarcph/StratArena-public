摘要

该EA基于TSI_DeMarker自定义指标的交叉信号进行交易，该指标结合了DeMarker震荡指标值与真实强度指数平滑算法。当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿时开空仓，并具有可配置的平仓逻辑。

策略分析

策略核心是基于TSI_DeMarker指标的交叉信号进行动量交易。入场条件为：当指标线（Ind）大于信号线（Sign）时，若前一根K线上指标线小于等于信号线（即上穿），则触发买入开仓；反之，当指标线小于信号线且前一根K线上指标线大于等于信号线（即下穿），则触发卖出开仓。出场条件分为两类：一是止损/止盈固定点数出场（StopLoss_=1000, TakeProfit_=2000），二是基于反向信号平仓（BuyPosClose/SellPosClose可独立控制）。仓位管理采用基于资金比例的动态计算（MM=0.1），支持四种资金管理模式：自由保证金、余额、扣除亏损后的自由保证金、扣除亏损后的余额，以及固定手数模式。指标使用三重平滑处理（XLength1, XLength2, XLength3），支持9种平滑算法选择，增强了信号的稳定性。策略在H8时间周期运行，通过IsNewBar检查确保每个K线只计算一次信号，避免了逐tick重复计算的问题。

Summary

This EA trades based on crossover signals from the TSI_DeMarker custom indicator, which combines DeMarker oscillator values with True Strength Index smoothing. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with configurable position closing logic.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is momentum trading based on crossover signals from the TSI_DeMarker indicator. Entry conditions: when the indicator line (Ind) is above the signal line (Sign) and the previous bar had Ind <= Sign (upward crossover), a buy position is triggered; conversely, when Ind is below Sign and the previous bar had Ind >= Sign (downward crossover), a sell position is triggered. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss (1000 points) and take profit (2000 points), plus optional signal-based closing where buy and sell close logic can be independently toggled. Position sizing uses dynamic calculation based on the MM ratio (0.1), supporting four money management modes: free margin, balance, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot mode. The indicator applies triple smoothing (XLength1, XLength2, XLength3) with 9 selectable smoothing algorithms (SMA, EMA, SMMA, LWMA, JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, AMA), enhancing signal stability. The strategy operates on the H8 timeframe and uses IsNewBar checks to ensure signal calculation occurs only once per bar, preventing redundant per-tick recalculations.

优势

  • 多模式资金管理，灵活性高，适应不同风险偏好
  • 买卖开仓和平仓逻辑可独立控制，策略配置灵活
  • 三重平滑算法有效过滤市场噪音，信号质量较高
  • 使用IsNewBar机制避免逐tick重复计算，执行效率好
  • 支持9种平滑算法切换，可针对不同市场特性优化

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点对于H8周期而言偏大，可能导致单笔亏损较大
  • 缺少持仓检查逻辑，可能在已有同向仓位时重复开仓
  • 无滑点/点差过滤，在高波动时段可能以不利价格成交
  • 依赖自定义指标TSI_DeMarker，增加了部署复杂性和依赖性
  • 没有追踪止损或移动止损功能，无法锁定浮动盈利

改进建议

  • 增加持仓方向检查，避免同向重复开仓
  • 添加点差过滤条件，在点差过大时延迟开仓以改善成交质量
  • 增加追踪止损功能，在盈利达到一定阈值后自动锁定部分利润

Strengths

  • Multi-mode money management offers high flexibility for different risk appetites
  • Independent toggles for buy/sell opening and closing allow flexible strategy configuration
  • Triple smoothing effectively filters market noise for higher quality signals
  • IsNewBar mechanism prevents redundant per-tick calculations, improving execution efficiency
  • Nine selectable smoothing algorithms enable optimization for different market characteristics

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are large for H8 timeframe, potentially causing significant single-trade losses
  • No position check logic; may open duplicate positions in the same direction
  • No spread filter; may execute at unfavorable prices during high-volatility periods
  • Depends on custom TSI_DeMarker indicator, increasing deployment complexity and dependency
  • No trailing stop functionality to lock in floating profits

Improvements

  • Add position direction check to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Add spread filter to delay entries when spread is excessively wide, improving execution quality
  • Add trailing stop functionality to automatically lock in partial profits after reaching a profit threshold
