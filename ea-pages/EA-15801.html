摘要

该EA基于iDeMarkerSign自定义指标进行交易，该指标使用DeMarker振荡器配合可配置的上下阈值产生超买超卖信号。当DeMarker跌破下限时开多单，突破上限时开空单，支持反向仓位平仓及多种资金管理模式。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是利用DeMarker振荡器的超买超卖区域进行均值回归交易。入场条件为：当指标上轨信号线（buffer 1）在指定K线位置产生非零非空值时，视为超买信号，开多单并可选平空单；当指标下轨信号线（buffer 0）产生有效值时，视为超卖信号，开空单并可选平多单。出场条件包括止损和止盈的固定点数设置，以及反向信号触发时的平仓机制。仓位管理通过MMMode枚举支持五种模式：自由保证金比例、余额比例、扣除亏损后的自由保证金比例、扣除亏损后的余额比例、以及固定手数模式。EA使用IsNewBar机制确保每个时间周期只在新的K线形成时执行一次交易逻辑，避免重复开仓。额外的反向信号扫描逻辑会在历史K线中向前搜索，确认是否存在更早的反向信号以决定是否平仓，这在同时启用开仓和平仓信号时生效。

Summary

This EA trades based on the iDeMarkerSign custom indicator, which generates overbought/oversold signals using the DeMarker oscillator with configurable upper and lower threshold levels. It opens buy positions when the DeMarker crosses below the lower level and sell positions when it crosses above the upper level, with optional counter-position closing and configurable money management modes.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is mean reversion trading using overbought/oversold zones of the DeMarker oscillator. Entry conditions: when the upper signal line (buffer 1) of the iDeMarkerSign indicator produces a non-zero, non-EMPTY_VALUE at the specified signal bar, it is interpreted as an oversold bounce signal, triggering a buy open and optionally a sell close; when the lower signal line (buffer 0) produces a valid value, it signals overbought conditions, triggering a sell open and optionally a buy close. Exit conditions include fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit in points, as well as counter-signal-based position closing. Position sizing supports five money management modes via MMMode: free margin percentage, balance percentage, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. The EA employs the IsNewBar mechanism to ensure trading logic executes only once per bar formation, preventing duplicate entries. An additional historical signal scan logic searches forward through past bars to find earlier counter-signals for closing decisions, which activates when both opening and closing signals are enabled simultaneously.

优势

  • 采用IsNewBar机制避免同一K线内重复交易，逻辑严谨
  • 提供五种资金管理模式，灵活适应不同风险偏好
  • 止损止盈参数齐全，风险控制手段基本完备
  • 支持独立控制开仓和平仓信号，策略配置灵活度高
  • 使用已收盘K线（SignalBar=1）读取信号，避免未收盘K线信号漂移

劣势

  • ATR_Period变量未声明为input，用户无法在参数面板中修改，属于代码缺陷
  • 历史K线反向信号扫描逻辑使用循环遍历所有历史K线，在K线数量较多时可能导致性能问题
  • 缺乏持仓检查逻辑，可能在已有同方向持仓时重复开仓
  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未考虑市场波动率（ATR）动态调整，在不同品种上适配性差
  • 缺乏点差过滤和交易时段过滤，可能在不适宜的时段或高点差时开仓

改进建议

  • 将ATR_Period声明为input变量，使其可在参数面板中配置
  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，在开仓前确认不存在同方向持仓，避免重复开仓
  • 将止损止盈改为基于ATR的动态计算，提高对不同波动率品种的适应性
  • 优化历史信号扫描逻辑，设置最大回溯K线数量上限，避免性能瓶颈

Strengths

  • Employs IsNewBar mechanism to prevent duplicate trades within the same bar, ensuring logical rigor
  • Provides five money management modes, flexibly accommodating different risk preferences
  • Stop loss and take profit parameters are complete, providing basic risk control measures
  • Supports independent control of opening and closing signals, offering high configuration flexibility
  • Uses closed bar (SignalBar=1) for signal reading, avoiding signal repainting on unclosed bars

Weaknesses

  • ATR_Period variable is not declared as input, preventing users from modifying it in the parameter panel, which is a code defect
  • The historical bar counter-signal scan logic iterates through all historical bars, potentially causing performance issues with large bar counts
  • Lacks position check logic, which may lead to duplicate entries in the same direction when a position already exists
  • Stop loss and take profit use fixed points without dynamic adjustment based on ATR volatility, resulting in poor adaptability across instruments
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, which may lead to entries during unsuitable sessions or high-spread conditions

Improvements

  • Declare ATR_Period as an input variable to make it configurable from the parameter panel
  • Add position check logic before opening to confirm no same-direction position exists, preventing duplicate entries
  • Convert stop loss and take profit to ATR-based dynamic calculation for better adaptability to instruments with varying volatility
  • Optimize historical signal scan logic by adding a maximum lookback bar limit to avoid performance bottlenecks
