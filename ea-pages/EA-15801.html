摘要

该EA基于自定义iDeMarkerSign指标的交易信号进行操作，该指标通过DeMarker震荡器的超买超卖水平来识别交易机会。当指标在低位发出向上信号时开多仓，在高位发出向下信号时开空仓，并支持反向信号平仓逻辑。仓位管理通过支持多种保证金模式的资金管理模块实现。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是利用DeMarker指标的极值区域反转信号进行交易。入场条件为：当iDeMarkerSign指标在超卖区域（DnLevel=0.3以下）产生上穿信号时开多仓，当在超买区域（UpLevel=0.7以上）产生下穿信号时开空仓。出场条件包括止损和止盈的固定点数设置，以及反向信号出现时的平仓机制——代码中有一段逻辑会在当前无反向信号时回溯历史K线寻找更早的平仓信号。仓位管理支持五种模式：固定手数、基于自由保证金、基于余额、基于净自由保证金和基于净余额的比例计算。资金管理通过TradeAlgorithms.mqh库函数实现，包含滑点容忍度设置。策略默认在H4周期运行，使用SignalBar=1即前一根已收盘K线的信号来确认交易，这是一种避免未来数据的合理做法。

Summary

This EA trades based on signals from the custom iDeMarkerSign indicator, which identifies overbought and oversold conditions using DeMarker oscillator levels. It opens buy positions when the indicator signals an upward crossover above the lower level and sell positions when it signals a downward crossover below the upper level, with configurable close-on-opposite-signal logic. Position sizing is managed via a money management module supporting multiple margin modes.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA trades on DeMarker indicator reversal signals from extreme zones. Entry conditions: a buy position is opened when iDeMarkerSign generates an upward signal in the oversold zone (below DnLevel=0.3), and a sell position is opened when a downward signal appears in the overbought zone (above UpLevel=0.7). Exit conditions include fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit in points, plus an opposite-signal close mechanism — the code includes logic that retrospectively scans historical bars to find earlier closing signals when no current reverse signal exists. Position sizing supports five modes: fixed lot, free margin-based, balance-based, net free margin-based, and net balance-based proportional calculation. Money management is implemented via the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library functions, including a deviation/slippage tolerance setting. The strategy defaults to the H4 timeframe and uses SignalBar=1, meaning it confirms trades on the previous closed bar, which is a sound practice to avoid repainting issues.

优势

  • 使用已收盘K线（SignalBar=1）确认信号，避免未来数据重绘问题
  • 支持五种仓位计算模式，资金管理灵活度高
  • 反向信号平仓机制提供了除止损止盈外的额外退出策略
  • 设有滑点容忍度参数，增强了实际交易中的执行鲁棒性
  • 买卖开关独立控制，可灵活配置只做单方向交易

劣势

  • ATR_Period变量未声明为input类型，用户无法在参数面板中修改
  • 回溯历史K线查找平仓信号的逻辑效率低且可能导致不一致的平仓判断
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时间过滤，在恶劣条件下可能仍会开仓
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点的默认值过大，对大多数品种不实用
  • 未检查是否已有同方向持仓就直接开仓，可能导致重复建仓

改进建议

  • 将ATR_Period改为input类型并添加点差过滤和交易时段过滤功能
  • 增加同方向持仓检查逻辑，避免重复开仓，并优化回溯平仓信号搜索的效率和可靠性

Strengths

  • Uses closed bar (SignalBar=1) for signal confirmation, avoiding repainting issues from future data
  • Supports five position sizing modes, providing high flexibility in money management
  • Opposite-signal closing mechanism adds an extra exit strategy beyond fixed SL/TP
  • Includes slippage tolerance parameter, improving execution robustness in live trading
  • Independent buy/sell toggle controls allow flexible unidirectional trading configuration

Weaknesses

  • ATR_Period variable is not declared as input, preventing users from modifying it in the parameters panel
  • The historical bar retrospective scanning logic for finding close signals is inefficient and may lead to inconsistent closing decisions
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, potentially opening positions under unfavorable conditions
  • Default StopLoss of 1000 points and TakeProfit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments
  • No check for existing same-direction positions before opening new ones, risking duplicate positions

Improvements

  • Change ATR_Period to input type and add spread filter and trading session filter functionality
  • Add same-direction position check logic to prevent duplicate entries, and optimize the efficiency and reliability of retrospective close-signal searching
