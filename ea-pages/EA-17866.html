Summary

20PRExp-3 is a scalping expert advisor that targets approximately 20-pip price ranges, likely entering on pullbacks within established ranges or on breakout attempts from narrow consolidation zones. It employs a systematic approach with predefined take-profit and stop-loss levels calibrated to short-term price movements. The EA appears to be a variant (version 3) of the 20-Pip Range Expert series.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a scalping strategy centered on trading within 20-pip price ranges. Entry conditions likely trigger when price reaches range boundaries or moves from the mid-range toward the edges, potentially using Bollinger Bands or ATR-based range detection. Exit conditions rely on fixed pip-based take-profit (approximately 20 pips) and corresponding stop-loss levels, aiming for high win rates to compensate for smaller per-trade profits. Position sizing likely uses fixed lots or a simple account-balance percentage calculation without dynamic adjustment mechanisms. Money management does not incorporate advanced Kelly criterion or risk budget allocation, resulting in an overall aggressive short-term style. The strategy lacks broader market trend assessment, which may lead to frequent stop-outs during trending markets.

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and debug
  • Fixed TP/SL provides a defined risk-reward ratio
  • Short-term trading reduces overnight risk and capital occupation time
  • Few parameters mean lower risk of overfitting
  • Suitable for capturing small price swings in ranging markets

Weaknesses

  • Lacks trend filter, prone to consecutive losses in trending markets

  • Fixed TP/SL does not account for market volatility changes, poor adaptability across different conditions

Improvements
