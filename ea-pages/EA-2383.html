Exp_CronexMFI

This EA trades based on the crossover signals of the CronexMFI custom indicator, which applies double smoothing to the Market Facilitation Index (MFI). It opens buy positions when the main MFI line crosses above the signal line and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with configurable position closing on signal reversal.

Strategy analysis

The EA's strategy concept is based on crossover signals from the CronexMFI indicator, which applies double smoothing (configurable smoothing method for fast and slow periods) to the Market Facilitation Index, classifying it as a momentum strategy. Entry conditions: when the main indicator line (Ind) is above the signal line (Sign) and the previous bar showed the main line at or below the signal line (upward crossover), a buy position is opened; conversely, when the main line is below the signal line and a downward crossover occurred, a sell position is opened. Exit conditions include configurable Stop Loss (default 1000 points) and Take Profit (default 2000 points), plus signal-reversal-based closing with independent enable/disable switches for buy and sell position closures. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter (default 0.1), supporting multiple margin modes including fixed lot size (LOT) mode. Money management supports four modes: percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin accounting for floating loss, and percentage of balance accounting for floating loss, plus a fixed lot mode. The strategy also employs the IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signal calculation happens only once per bar, preventing duplicate entries.

Strengths

  • Uses double-smoothed MFI indicator which reduces false signals compared to raw MFI, improving signal quality
  • Supports 10 different smoothing algorithms, allowing flexible adaptation to different market characteristics
  • Buy and sell opening and closing can be independently controlled, providing flexible strategy configuration
  • Employs IsNewBar mechanism to limit signal calculation to once per bar, preventing duplicate entries
  • Complete stop loss and take profit configuration with default TP at 2x SL, implying a positive reward-to-risk ratio

Weaknesses

  • Lacks spread filter mechanism, which may lead to unnecessary losing entries during high spread conditions
  • No multi-timeframe confirmation, single indicator crossover signals may generate numerous false signals
  • Default SL of 1000 and TP of 2000 points are large values, may not suit all instruments and volatility environments
  • No check for existing same-direction positions, potentially leading to repeated position stacking
  • Depends on external custom indicator CronexMFI and two include files, making deployment and portability complex

Possible improvements

  • Add spread filter to pause opening positions when spread exceeds a threshold, avoiding high slippage costs
  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate same-direction entries that lead to excessive leverage
  • Add multi-timeframe trend confirmation, e.g., only allow entries when the higher timeframe trend direction aligns

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No check for existing same-direction positions, potentially stacking positions during persistent signals
  • Default 1000-point SL is large, single trade loss could be significant in high volatility markets
  • Depends on external indicator file; if missing, initialization fails but existing positions are unaffected
