EA-327 · Mean Reversion

Expert_CP_Stoch

This EA uses a Stochastic oscillator combined with a moving average filter to generate trading signals within the MQL5 Standard Library Expert Advisor framework. It employs fixed-lot money management and no trailing stop, relying on fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels for trade exits. The signal threshold mechanism aggregates weighted filter signals before opening or closing positions.

Strategy analysis

The strategy uses a Stochastic oscillator as the primary signal generator, combined with a moving average (MA) as an auxiliary filter. Entry conditions rely on Stochastic %K/%D line crossovers in overbought/oversold zones, confirmed by the MA for trend direction, and the aggregated signal must reach the opening threshold (Signal_ThresholdOpen=10) before a position is opened. Exit conditions include the reverse signal reaching the closing threshold (Signal_ThresholdClose=10), stop-loss (50 points) or take-profit (50 points) being hit. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach (Money_FixLot_Lots=0.1), which does not dynamically adjust based on account balance or volatility. Money management employs the CMoneyFixedLot class with a fixed percentage of 10% and a fixed lot size of 0.1, lacking the ability to dynamically size positions based on volatility or risk. The overall strategy is mean-reversion in nature, exploiting the Stochastic indicator's tendency to revert from overbought/oversold levels, but the relatively tight stop-loss and take-profit (50 points each) may result in frequent stop-outs in volatile markets.

Strengths

  • Uses the MQL5 Standard Library framework with clean, modular code structure
  • Stochastic oscillator combined with MA provides dual filtering to reduce false signals
  • Signal threshold mechanism offers flexible open/close control, preventing overtrading
  • Fixed lot sizing reduces position management complexity, suitable for beginners

Weaknesses

  • Fixed 50-point stop-loss and take-profit without ATR-based dynamic adjustment, leading to inconsistent performance across varying market conditions
  • Fixed lot sizing instead of dynamic position management cannot adapt to account size and risk
  • No trailing stop used (CTrailingNone), potentially missing profits from extended trends
  • Lacks new bar check mechanism despite EveryTick=false, risking duplicate operations on the same bar
  • No spread filter included, which may cause unnecessary losses on high-spread instruments

Possible improvements

  • Replace fixed stop-loss/take-profit with ATR-based dynamic calculation to adapt to varying market volatility
  • Add a trailing stop module to replace CTrailingNone, protecting profits and following trends
  • Add spread filter and new bar check mechanism to improve trade execution reliability

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Fixed 50-point stop-loss may be too wide in low-volatility markets and too tight in high-volatility markets, creating asymmetric risk
  • Lack of new bar check and spread filter creates risk of duplicate entries and slippage
  • Fixed lot sizing ignores account equity changes, maintaining constant risk exposure during losing streaks
