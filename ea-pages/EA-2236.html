摘要

该EA基于FisherCGOscillator自定义指标进行交易，当振荡器线上穿信号线时产生买入信号，下穿时产生卖出信号。支持可配置的资金管理模式、止损止盈，以及独立的多空开平仓权限控制。

策略分析

策略概念基于FisherCGOscillator振荡器的趋势动量交叉信号，属于典型的动量跟踪策略。入场条件为：振荡器线(Ind)上穿信号线(Sign)时开多仓，下穿时开空仓，仅在信号确认后的新K线触发交易。出场条件包括固定点数的止损和止盈，以及反向信号触发的平仓（多仓在卖出信号时平仓，空仓在买入信号时平仓）。仓位管理通过MM参数控制存款份额，支持五种模式：自由保证金、余额、损失自由保证金、损失余额和固定手数。资金管理使用TradeAlgorithms.mqh库封装的标准化交易函数，包含最大价格偏差控制和信号时间戳追踪以防止重复交易。策略默认使用H8周期和10周期的指标参数，SignalBar=1表示使用前一根已闭合K线的信号，避免未完成K线的虚假信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on the FisherCGOscillator custom indicator, generating buy signals when the oscillator line crosses above its signal line and sell signals when it crosses below. It supports configurable money management modes, stop loss/take profit, and permissions for opening and closing positions independently for long and short sides.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on crossover signals from the FisherCGOscillator, a momentum-based oscillator that applies Fisher transform to the center of gravity oscillator for smoother signals. Entry conditions: a buy is triggered when the oscillator line (Ind) crosses above the signal line (Sign), and a sell is triggered when Ind crosses below Sign; trades are only executed on new bar formation to avoid intra-bar noise. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss (1000 points) and take profit (2000 points), plus directional close on opposing signals — long positions close on sell signals and short positions close on buy signals. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter (default 0.1 = 10% of deposit) with five calculation modes: free margin-based, balance-based, loss share of free margin, loss share of balance, and fixed lot. Money management is delegated to the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library which provides standardized BuyPositionOpen/SellPositionOpen and BuyPositionClose/SellPositionClose functions with deviation control and signal timestamp tracking to prevent duplicate entries on the same bar. The default H8 timeframe with SignalBar=1 ensures signals are taken from the last closed bar, adding robustness against repaint and incomplete bar issues.

优势

  • 使用Fisher变换平滑振荡器信号，减少虚假交叉信号，提高交易质量
  • 多空开平仓权限独立控制，灵活性高，可单独禁用某个方向的交易
  • 采用新K线检测机制和SignalBar=1默认值，有效避免未闭合K线的信号重绘问题
  • 五种资金管理模式可选，适应不同风险偏好和账户类型
  • 止损止盈参数明确，默认2:1的盈亏比合理

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标FisherCGOscillator，如果指标文件缺失则EA无法运行且错误处理不充分
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时段过滤，在低流动性时段可能产生不利成交
  • 固定点数止损止盈未考虑不同品种的波动率差异，可能对某些品种不适用
  • OnInit中指标句柄获取失败仅打印警告未返回错误码，EA会继续运行但无法正常交易
  • 未检测是否已有同方向持仓，可能在已有持仓时重复开仓

改进建议

  • 在OnInit中指标句柄无效时应返回INIT_FAILED而非继续运行，并在OnTick中增加句柄有效性检查
  • 增加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免高点差和非活跃时段的不利成交
  • 考虑使用ATR动态调整止损止盈，适应不同品种和波动率环境

Strengths

  • Fisher transform applied to the CG oscillator produces smoother signals with fewer false crossovers, improving trade quality
  • Independent permission toggles for buy/sell open/close provide high flexibility to configure directional exposure
  • New bar detection (CIsNewBar) and default SignalBar=1 effectively prevent repainting and intra-bar signal noise
  • Five money management modes accommodate different risk appetites and account types
  • Explicit stop loss and take profit with a default 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio is sensible

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom FisherCGOscillator indicator file; if missing, the EA runs but trades silently fail with only a Print warning in OnInit
  • No spread filter or trading session filter, which can lead to unfavorable fills during low-liquidity periods
  • Fixed point-based SL/TP does not account for varying volatility across instruments, making it unsuitable for some symbols
  • OnInit returns 0 (success) even when the indicator handle is INVALID_HANDLE, allowing the EA to run in a broken state
  • No check for existing same-direction positions, potentially leading to duplicate entries on repeated signals

Improvements

  • Return INIT_FAILED from OnInit when the indicator handle is invalid, and add handle validity checks in OnTick before attempting CopyBuffer
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid entering trades during high-spread or low-liquidity periods
  • Consider ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit to adapt to different instruments and volatility regimes

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 指标句柄获取失败时EA仍继续运行但不产生交易信号，可能导致错过交易机会而用户不知情
  • 固定点数止损未根据品种波动率调整，高波动品种可能频繁止损，低波动品种止损可能过大
  • 无持仓检查机制，可能在已有同方向仓位时重复开仓导致过度敞口

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • EA continues running with an invalid indicator handle, silently failing to trade without user awareness
  • Fixed-point SL/TP without volatility adjustment can cause frequent stop-outs on volatile instruments or excessive risk on calm ones
  • No existing position check before opening new trades, risking overexposure through duplicate same-direction entries
