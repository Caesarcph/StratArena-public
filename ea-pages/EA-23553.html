摘要

该EA基于图表上绘制的水平线进行交易，当价格与用户定义的水平线交互时触发买入或卖出订单。它充当基于水平的交易助手，交易者手动放置水平线，EA自动执行订单。

策略分析

策略概念基于支撑阻力水平线，交易者在图表上手动绘制水平线作为关键价格位，EA监控价格与这些水平线的交互来决定入场。入场条件是价格触及或突破水平线时自动开仓，可在水平线上方设置买入、下方设置卖出，具体方向取决于线的属性或命名约定。出场条件通常依赖止盈止损参数，也可能在价格到达另一条水平线时平仓。仓位管理通过固定手数或基于资金的动态手数参数控制，没有看到马丁格尔或网格机制。资金管理方面提供止损和止盈参数，但依赖于交易者正确放置水平线，缺乏自动化的水平线生成逻辑，整体策略高度依赖交易者对关键价格水平的判断能力。

Summary

This EA trades based on horizontal line levels drawn on the chart, triggering buy or sell orders when price interacts with user-defined horizontal lines. It acts as a level-based trading assistant where traders manually place horizontal lines and the EA automates order execution at those levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on support and resistance horizontal levels, where traders manually draw horizontal lines on the chart as key price levels and the EA monitors price interaction with these lines to determine entries. Entry conditions trigger when price touches or breaks through a horizontal line, with buy orders placed above the line and sell orders below, depending on line attributes or naming conventions. Exit conditions typically rely on take-profit and stop-loss parameters, or may close positions when price reaches another horizontal line. Position sizing is controlled through fixed lot or risk-based dynamic lot parameters, with no martingale or grid mechanisms observed. Money management provides stop-loss and take-profit parameters but depends heavily on the trader correctly placing horizontal lines, lacking automated level generation logic. The overall strategy is highly dependent on the trader's ability to identify key price levels correctly.

优势

  • 策略逻辑直观易懂，基于支撑阻力水平进行交易是经典有效的方法
  • 交易者可以灵活控制入场点位，根据自己的分析放置水平线
  • 自动化执行减少人为延迟，在价格触及关键水平时快速下单
  • 支持魔术号码区分订单，可与其他EA共存运行

劣势

  • 高度依赖交易者手动绘制水平线，缺乏自动化水平识别能力
  • 止损默认值为0，如果不手动设置则存在无限亏损风险
  • 没有新K线检查机制，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓
  • 缺乏点差过滤，在大幅点差时仍会执行交易
  • 没有回撤控制或最大持仓限制等资金管理保护

改进建议

  • 增加自动识别关键支撑阻力水平的功能，如使用分形或前期高低点
  • 添加新K线检查和点差过滤机制以防止重复交易和高成本入场
  • 增加基于账户风险百分比的动态仓位计算替代固定手数

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is intuitive and easy to understand, trading based on support/resistance levels is a classic and effective approach
  • Traders have flexible control over entry points, placing horizontal lines based on their own analysis
  • Automated execution reduces human delay, placing orders quickly when price touches key levels
  • Magic number support distinguishes orders, allowing coexistence with other EAs

Weaknesses

  • Highly dependent on traders manually drawing horizontal lines, lacking automated level identification capability
  • Default stop-loss value is 0, posing unlimited loss risk if not manually configured
  • No new bar check mechanism, potentially opening duplicate positions on the same bar
  • Lack of spread filter, executing trades even during wide spread conditions
  • No drawdown control or maximum position limits for money management protection

Improvements

  • Add automatic identification of key support/resistance levels using fractals or previous highs/lows
  • Implement new bar check and spread filter mechanisms to prevent duplicate trades and costly entries
  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account risk percentage as an alternative to fixed lots
