摘要

Kloss是一个多指标动量EA，结合LWMA、CCI和随机震荡器产生买卖信号。当CCI和随机指标确认超买/超卖极端区域且价格穿越移动平均线时开仓，具有反向平仓逻辑和延迟止损止盈修改模式以规避经纪商止损级别限制。

策略分析

策略概念基于三重指标确认的动量反转交易：使用LWMA判断价格方向，CCI识别超买超卖极端区域，随机指标确认动量偏离。入场条件为做多时CCI低于-CCIDiffer、随机指标主线上穿50-StDiffer、收盘价高于MA；做空时CCI高于CCIDiffer、随机指标主线下穿50+StDiffer、收盘价低于MA。出场条件包括止损止盈触发，以及当RevClose开启时由反向信号平仓。仓位管理支持固定手数或基于账户自由保证金和风险比例的动态手数计算，手数经过规范化处理以符合经纪商最小最大手数和步长限制。资金管理通过MaximumRisk参数控制每笔交易的风险敞口，仅使用账户自由保证金的千分之几作为仓位大小依据。MWMode机制允许先无止损止盈开仓再事后修改，规避部分经纪商开仓时止损级别限制过严的问题。

Summary

Kloss is a multi-indicator momentum EA that combines LWMA, CCI, and Stochastic oscillator to generate buy and sell signals. It opens positions when CCI and Stochastic confirm overbought/oversold extremes alongside price crossing the moving average, with optional reverse-close logic and a delayed SL/TP modification mode to work around broker stop-level restrictions.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on triple-indicator confirmed momentum reversal trading: using LWMA for price direction, CCI for identifying overbought/oversold extremes, and Stochastic for confirming momentum divergence. Entry conditions for buy require CCI below -CCIDiffer, Stochastic main line below 50-StDiffer, and close price above MA; for sell, CCI above CCIDiffer, Stochastic main line above 50+StDiffer, and close price below MA. Exit conditions include stop loss and take profit triggers, and when RevClose is enabled, positions are closed by opposite trade signals. Position sizing supports fixed lots or dynamic lot calculation based on account free margin and risk percentage, with lot normalization to comply with broker minimum/maximum volume and step size constraints. Money management controls risk exposure per trade via the MaximumRisk parameter, using only a fraction of a thousand of account free margin as position size basis. The MWMode mechanism allows opening positions without SL/TP first and then modifying them afterward, working around brokers that have strict stop-level requirements at order placement time.

优势

  • 三重指标确认机制减少了虚假信号，提高入场质量
  • 止损止盈对称设置（均550点），风险收益比合理
  • MWMode机制巧妙规避了部分经纪商的止损级别限制问题
  • 动态手数计算考虑了经纪商手数规范（最小值、步长、最大值）
  • RevClose反向平仓功能提供额外的出场保护

劣势

  • MAPeriod设为1导致MA几乎没有平滑作用，等于直接用价格比较，失去均线意义
  • 动态手数计算公式（自由保证金×风险/1000）缺乏严格的风险控制逻辑，未考虑止损距离
  • 没有点差过滤机制，在宽点差环境下可能产生不利入场
  • 仅支持单品种单仓位交易，无法进行多品种分散
  • 全局变量存储止损止盈值的方式不够健壮，终端重启可能导致数据丢失

改进建议

  • 将动态手数计算改为基于止损距离的风险百分比模型，使每笔交易的最大亏损金额可控
  • 增加点差过滤条件，当点差超过阈值时暂停交易以避免不利入场
  • 增加新K线检查机制（目前仅在ZeroBar为true时使用零号K线），避免同一K线重复计算
  • 将MWMode的止损止盈存储从全局变量改为更持久的持久化方案，防止终端意外重启丢失数据

Strengths

  • Triple indicator confirmation mechanism reduces false signals and improves entry quality
  • Symmetric stop loss and take profit (both 550 points) provides a reasonable risk-reward ratio
  • MWMode mechanism cleverly works around some brokers' stop-level placement restrictions
  • Dynamic lot calculation accounts for broker lot normalization (min, step, max)
  • RevClose reverse-close feature provides additional exit protection

Weaknesses

  • MAPeriod set to 1 makes the MA nearly useless as it essentially compares price to itself, losing the moving average's purpose
  • Dynamic lot formula (free margin × risk / 1000) lacks rigorous risk control logic, not accounting for stop loss distance
  • No spread filter mechanism, may produce unfavorable entries in wide-spread environments
  • Single-instrument single-position trading only, no multi-instrument diversification
  • Global variable storage for SL/TP values is not robust; terminal restart may cause data loss

Improvements

  • Change dynamic lot calculation to a stop-loss-distance-based risk percentage model so maximum loss per trade is controllable
  • Add a spread filter condition to pause trading when spread exceeds a threshold, avoiding unfavorable entries
  • Add proper new-bar checking mechanism (currently only checks zero bar when ZeroBar is true) to prevent repeated calculations on the same bar
  • Replace MWMode global variable storage for SL/TP with a more persistent mechanism to prevent data loss on unexpected terminal restart

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 动态手数计算未考虑止损距离，在高杠杆下可能产生超出预期的亏损
  • MWMode依赖全局变量存储止损止盈值，终端异常关闭可能导致仓位无止损保护
  • 无点差过滤机制，在新闻事件或低流动性时段可能遭遇滑点和宽点差

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Dynamic lot calculation does not account for stop loss distance, potentially causing larger-than-expected losses under high leverage
  • MWMode relies on global variables to store SL/TP values; abnormal terminal shutdown may leave positions without stop loss protection
  • No spread filter, may encounter slippage and wide spreads during news events or low liquidity periods
