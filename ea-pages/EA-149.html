摘要

该EA基于自定义iMA指标的相对变化率进行交易，当指标变化率超过阈值时开多仓，低于负阈值时开空仓。对盈利仓位采用追踪止损，对亏损仓位采用基于tick价值的止损，同时根据风险比例动态计算仓位大小并设有最大手数限制。

策略分析

策略概念基于自定义移动平均线指标(ima)的相对变化率，通过计算当前值与前一个值的相对差值来衡量趋势动量。入场条件为：无持仓且新K线形成时，若变化率k1大于等于信号阈值k则开多仓，若k1小于等于负k则开空仓。出场条件分为两部分：盈利仓位使用追踪止损，当浮盈点数超过take且当前止损距离也超过take时，将止损上移至当前价格减去take个点（多仓）或当前价格加上take个点（空仓）；亏损仓位则根据浮亏换算为tick点数，若超过drop阈值则强制平仓。仓位管理采用基于风险比例r和止损距离drop的动态手数计算，将自由保证金按风险比例分配后除以每点价值和止损点数，再向下取整至最小手数倍数。资金管理还包含最大手数限制和保证金预检循环递减，确保下单手数不超过账户承受能力。

Summary

This EA trades based on the rate of change of a custom iMA indicator, opening buy positions when the indicator's relative change exceeds a threshold and sell positions when it drops below the negative threshold. It employs a trailing stop mechanism for profitable positions and a tick-value-based stop loss for losing ones, with dynamic lot sizing based on risk percentage and a configurable maximum lot cap.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the relative rate of change of a custom moving average indicator (ima), measuring trend momentum by computing the relative difference between the current and previous indicator values. Entry conditions require no open position and a new bar formation; a buy signal triggers when the rate of change k1 is greater than or equal to the signal threshold k, while a sell signal triggers when k1 is less than or equal to negative k. Exit conditions are split into two parts: profitable positions use a trailing stop that shifts the stop loss when floating profit in points exceeds the take parameter and the current stop distance also exceeds take; losing positions are forcibly closed when the floating loss, converted to tick points, exceeds the drop threshold. Position sizing dynamically calculates lot size based on the risk ratio r and stop loss distance drop, distributing free margin proportionally and dividing by tick value and drop points, then rounding down to the nearest minimum lot increment. Money management also incorporates a maximum lot cap and a margin pre-check loop that decrements the lot size until sufficient free margin is confirmed, ensuring the order size stays within account capacity.

优势

  • 动态仓位管理基于风险比例，每笔交易风险可控
  • 追踪止损机制有效保护已有盈利
  • 新K线检查避免在同一根K线上重复开仓
  • 手数计算包含保证金预检和最大手数限制，防止过度杠杆
  • 使用指标变化率而非价格交叉，能更灵敏地捕捉趋势动量

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标ima，若指标文件缺失则EA无法运行且仅打印警告
  • 追踪止损和止损逻辑互斥，盈利仓位没有硬性止盈目标
  • 信号标志f_buy和f_sell在开仓后通过fdrop()手动清除，但若开仓失败则信号不会重置，可能重复尝试开仓
  • 亏损止损基于浮亏金额换算而非固定点数，在波动率变化时止损距离不稳定
  • 持仓检查仅用PositionSelect按品种选择，多EA同品种运行时可能冲突

改进建议

  • 将开仓后信号清除逻辑改为根据交易结果判断，避免开仓失败时信号残留导致重复下单
  • 增加对自定义指标ima的更严格检查和错误处理，包括指标缓冲区数据有效性验证
  • 为亏损仓位添加基于点数的硬性止损，而非仅依赖浮亏金额换算，提高止损的确定性
  • 增加MagicNumber以区分不同EA实例的持仓，避免多EA同品种运行冲突

Strengths

  • Dynamic position sizing based on risk ratio keeps per-trade risk controlled
  • Trailing stop mechanism effectively protects existing profits
  • New bar check prevents repeated entries on the same candle
  • Lot calculation includes margin pre-check and max lot cap, preventing excessive leverage
  • Using indicator rate of change rather than price crossover captures trend momentum more sensitively

Weaknesses

  • Depends on custom indicator ima; if the indicator file is missing the EA fails with only a print warning
  • Trailing stop and stop loss logic are mutually exclusive; profitable positions have no hard take-profit target
  • Signal flags f_buy and f_sell are manually cleared via fdrop() after open attempt, but if the open fails the signals persist and may cause repeated attempts
  • Loss stop is based on floating P&L conversion rather than fixed points, making the stop distance unstable under changing volatility
  • Position check uses PositionSelect by symbol only, which may conflict if multiple EAs run on the same symbol

Improvements

  • Change post-open signal clearing logic to check trade result, preventing signal persistence on failed orders
  • Add stricter validation and error handling for the custom ima indicator, including buffer data validity checks
  • Add a fixed point-based hard stop loss for losing positions instead of relying solely on floating P&L conversion for more deterministic stops
  • Add a MagicNumber to distinguish positions from different EA instances, preventing conflicts when multiple EAs run on the same symbol

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 开仓失败后信号不清除，可能在同一K线反复尝试开仓导致多次滑点损失
  • 止损基于浮亏换算而非固定点数，极端行情下实际止损距离可能远超预期
  • 无MagicNumber管理，多EA同品种运行时持仓和止损逻辑可能互相干扰
  • 追踪止损仅修改止损不设止盈，大幅盈利可能因回调而回吐

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Signals are not cleared on open failure, potentially causing repeated entry attempts on the same bar with slippage accumulation
  • Stop loss based on P&L conversion rather than fixed points means actual stop distance may far exceed expectations in extreme markets
  • No MagicNumber management means multiple EAs on the same symbol may interfere with each other's position and stop logic
  • Trailing stop only modifies stop loss without setting take-profit, meaning significant gains may be given back on pullbacks
