摘要

该EA基于自定义均线指标(ima)的变化率进行交易。当均线相对变化率超过阈值时做多，低于负阈值时做空，使用固定点数止损和对盈利仓位实施追踪止损。仓位大小根据账户可用保证金和风险参数动态计算。

策略分析

策略概念是基于自定义均线指标ima的变化率来捕捉趋势动量。入场条件为：当无持仓且新K线形成时，计算ima指标相邻两值的相对变化率k1=(aIMA[0]-aIMA[1])/|aIMA[1]|，若k1≥k则开多单，若k1≤-k则开空单。出场条件包括两个方面：一是追踪止损——当持仓盈利且价格超过take点数时，将止损移动到距当前价格take点数的位置；二是亏损止损——当持仓亏损且亏损点数换算达到drop值时强制平仓。仓位管理采用动态计算，根据账户可用保证金乘以风险比例r再除以drop和tick价值来计算手数，然后向下取整到最小手数倍数，并通过FreeMarginCheck逐步缩减直到保证金充足，同时限制最大手数。资金管理方面使用了风险比例参数r控制每笔交易的风险敞口，但止损逻辑存在严重缺陷——开仓时并未设置实际止损价格（传入0），而是依赖OnTick中实时检测亏损来平仓，这在服务器断连或行情跳空时会导致巨大亏损。

Summary

This EA trades based on the rate of change of a custom moving average indicator (ima). It opens buy positions when the MA's relative slope exceeds a threshold and sell positions when it drops below a negative threshold, using a fixed stop-loss in ticks and a trailing stop for profitable positions. Lot sizing is dynamically calculated based on account free margin and risk parameters.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on capturing trend momentum through the rate of change of a custom moving average indicator (ima). Entry conditions: when no position is open and a new bar forms, the EA calculates the relative rate of change k1=(aIMA[0]-aIMA[1])/|aIMA[1]| from the ima indicator buffer; a buy signal triggers when k1>=k and a sell signal when k1<=-k. Exit conditions are twofold: a trailing stop mechanism that moves the stop loss to maintain a 'take' point distance from current price when the position is profitable and has moved beyond 'take' points, and a loss cutoff that forcefully closes the position when the calculated loss in ticks exceeds the 'drop' threshold. Position sizing dynamically calculates lot size as FreeMargin*r/drop/TickValue, rounds down to the nearest minimum lot step, then iteratively reduces until FreeMarginCheck passes, with additional caps at max_lots and SYMBOL_VOLUME_MAX. Money management uses the risk ratio parameter r to control exposure per trade, but has a critical flaw—positions are opened with zero stop loss price (sl=0 passed to PositionOpen), relying entirely on the OnTick loop to detect and close losing positions, which creates severe risk during disconnections or price gaps.

优势

  • 动态仓位计算根据账户保证金自动调整，有效控制每笔风险敞口
  • 追踪止损机制能锁定盈利，随着价格有利移动不断上移止损
  • 使用新K线检测避免同根K线重复开仓，减少过度交易
  • 通过FreeMarginCheck验证保证金充足性，增加了一层安全保障

劣势

  • 开仓时未设置实际止损价格（sl=0），完全依赖OnTick实时检测亏损平仓，断连或跳空时风险巨大
  • 止损逻辑以盈亏金额换算而非点数，在tick价值变化时可能不准确
  • 信号标志f_buy/f_sell在开仓后仅通过fdrop()简单清零，但在K线未更新时可能残留旧信号
  • 依赖自定义指标ima，若指标不存在则直接返回初始化失败，缺乏容错处理
  • 追踪止损逻辑中take同时承担止盈距离和追踪距离的角色，逻辑混淆且缺少TakeProfit设置

改进建议

  • 开仓时应直接设置止损价格（将drop转换为价格距离），而非依赖OnTick循环检测，确保服务器端止损生效
  • 将追踪止损距离和止盈距离分离为独立参数，并在开仓时设置合理的TakeProfit价格
  • 增加spread过滤机制，避免在高点差时段开仓，提升成交质量

Strengths

  • Dynamic lot sizing based on account free margin automatically adjusts position size to control risk exposure per trade
  • Trailing stop mechanism locks in profits by moving the stop loss as price moves favorably
  • New bar detection prevents repeated entries on the same bar, reducing overtrading
  • FreeMarginCheck validation adds a safety layer ensuring sufficient margin before opening positions

Weaknesses

  • Positions are opened with zero stop loss (sl=0), relying entirely on OnTick monitoring to close losers—disconnections or price gaps could cause catastrophic losses
  • The loss cutoff uses profit amount divided by tick value rather than actual price distance, which may be inaccurate when tick value fluctuates
  • Signal flags f_buy/f_sell are only cleared by fdrop() after position open, but stale signals could persist across ticks if no new position is opened
  • Depends on a custom indicator 'ima' with no fallback; initialization fails outright if the indicator is missing
  • The 'take' parameter conflates trailing stop distance and take-profit distance, creating confusing logic with no actual TakeProfit price set on orders

Improvements

  • Set a real stop loss price on PositionOpen (converting drop to price distance) instead of relying on OnTick loop monitoring, ensuring server-side SL is always active
  • Separate trailing stop distance and take-profit distance into independent parameters and set a proper TakeProfit price on order placement
  • Add a spread filter to avoid opening positions during high-spread periods, improving trade execution quality
