摘要

该EA使用双EMA交叉系统产生交易信号。当短周期EMA上穿长周期EMA时买入，反之则卖出。每笔交易使用固定手数、止损和止盈水平。

策略分析

该EA采用双指数移动平均线（EMA）交叉策略作为核心交易逻辑，属于典型的趋势跟踪系统。入场条件为：当短周期EMA（周期=Periods）从下方穿越长周期EMA（周期=Periods+2）时开多单，反之下穿时开空单。出场条件完全依赖固定点数的止损和止盈，没有移动止损或信号反转平仓机制。仓位管理采用固定0.1手手数，不根据账户余额或波动性动态调整。资金管理方面，止损和止盈为固定点数设定，在5位小数账户上会自动乘以10以适应报价精度。策略的EMA周期差仅为2，这意味着两根均线非常接近，容易产生频繁的虚假信号。

Summary

This EA uses a dual EMA crossover system to generate trade signals. It buys when a shorter-period EMA crosses above a longer-period EMA and sells on the opposite crossover. Each trade is placed with fixed lot size, stop loss, and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a dual Exponential Moving Average (EMA) crossover strategy as its core trading logic, which is a classic trend-following system. Entry conditions: a buy is triggered when the shorter-period EMA (period=Periods) crosses above the longer-period EMA (period=Periods+2), and a sell is triggered on the opposite crossover. Exit conditions rely entirely on fixed-pip stop loss and take profit levels, with no trailing stop or signal-reversal exit mechanism. Position sizing is fixed at 0.1 lots with no dynamic adjustment based on account balance or market volatility. Money management uses hardcoded SL/TP pip values that are scaled by 10x on 5-digit broker accounts for price precision adaptation. The EMA period difference is only 2, meaning the two moving averages are very close together, which tends to produce frequent false signals in ranging markets.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单明了，易于理解和维护
  • 设有固定的止损和止盈，每笔交易的风险可控
  • 使用魔术编号区分订单，避免与其他EA冲突
  • 支持5位小数报价的自动适配

劣势

  • 没有检查是否已有持仓，可能重复开仓导致过度敞口
  • 没有新K线检查，每个tick都会评估交叉信号，可能重复下单
  • EMA周期差仅为2，两根均线过于接近，在震荡市中会产生大量虚假信号
  • 固定0.1手手数不考虑账户规模，不适合所有资金量的账户
  • 没有点差过滤，在点差较大时仍可能开仓

改进建议

  • 添加持仓检查和新K线检查，避免重复开仓
  • 增加点差过滤和波动性过滤，提高信号质量
  • 将手数改为基于账户余额或风险的动态计算
  • 考虑增大EMA周期差或添加第三根EMA作为趋势过滤器

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic that is easy to understand and maintain
  • Fixed stop loss and take profit provide defined risk per trade
  • Uses magic number to distinguish orders and avoid conflicts with other EAs
  • Automatic adaptation for 5-digit price quotes

Weaknesses

  • No existing position check, potentially opening duplicate trades leading to excessive exposure
  • No new bar check, evaluating crossover signals on every tick can cause repeated order submissions
  • EMA period difference of only 2 makes the lines too close, generating many false signals in ranging markets
  • Fixed 0.1 lot size ignores account equity, unsuitable for all account sizes
  • No spread filter, may enter trades even when spread is wide

Improvements

  • Add position check and new bar check to prevent duplicate trade openings
  • Add spread filter and volatility filter to improve signal quality
  • Change lot size to dynamic calculation based on account balance or risk percentage
  • Consider increasing the EMA period difference or adding a third EMA as a trend filter
