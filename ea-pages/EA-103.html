摘要

该EA基于双EMA交叉策略进行交易，当较快EMA上穿较慢EMA时做多，反之做空。使用固定0.1手下单，并配置了可调的止损和止盈水平。策略在每次交叉信号时直接入场，不检查现有持仓。

策略分析

策略概念基于双指数移动平均线(EMA)交叉，使用周期为Periods和Periods+2的两条EMA线判断趋势方向。入场条件为：当较快EMA(周期17)从下方穿越较慢EMA(周期19)时开多单，当较快EMA从上方穿越较慢EMA时开空单。出场条件完全依赖止损和止盈订单，没有移动止损或信号反转平仓机制。仓位管理采用固定0.1手，不根据账户余额或风险比例动态调整。资金管理方面，止损和止盈以点数设置，并针对5位小数报价进行了乘以10的适配处理。整体策略逻辑简单直接，但缺乏对现有持仓的检查，可能在同方向上重复开仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on a dual EMA crossover strategy, going long when a faster EMA crosses above a slower EMA and going short on the opposite crossover. It uses fixed lot sizing of 0.1 lots with configurable stop loss and take profit levels. The strategy enters on every crossover signal without checking for existing open positions.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a dual exponential moving average (EMA) crossover, using two EMA lines with periods of Periods and Periods+2 to determine trend direction. Entry conditions are: open a buy when the faster EMA (period 17) crosses above the slower EMA (period 19), and open a sell when the faster EMA crosses below the slower EMA. Exit conditions rely entirely on stop loss and take profit orders, with no trailing stop or signal-reversal close mechanism. Position sizing uses a fixed 0.1 lots, with no dynamic adjustment based on account balance or risk percentage. For money management, SL and TP are set in points with a x10 multiplier adaptation for 5-decimal pricing. The overall strategy logic is simple and direct, but it lacks checks for existing open positions, potentially leading to repeated entries in the same direction.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单明了，易于理解和维护
  • 使用了EMA交叉这种经典且经过验证的趋势跟踪方法
  • 对5位小数报价做了适配处理，兼容性较好

劣势

  • 没有检查现有持仓，可能同方向重复开仓导致过度暴露
  • 固定手数0.1手，缺乏动态仓位管理，无法适应不同账户规模
  • 仅依赖止损止盈出场，没有移动止损或信号反转平仓机制
  • 没有新K线检查，每个tick都会评估交叉条件可能导致误判

改进建议

  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，防止同方向重复开仓
  • 实现新K线检测，仅在K线收盘时评估交叉信号
  • 加入动态仓位管理，根据账户余额和风险比例调整手数
  • 增加移动止损功能，在趋势延续时锁定利润

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic that is easy to understand and maintain
  • Uses EMA crossover, a classic and well-validated trend following method
  • Adapts for 5-decimal pricing, providing good broker compatibility

Weaknesses

  • No check for existing open positions, potentially causing repeated entries in the same direction and overexposure
  • Fixed 0.1 lot size with no dynamic position sizing, unable to adapt to different account sizes
  • Relies solely on SL/TP for exits, lacking trailing stop or signal-reversal close mechanisms
  • No new bar check, evaluating crossover conditions on every tick may lead to false signals

Improvements

  • Add position check logic to prevent repeated entries in the same direction
  • Implement new bar detection to evaluate crossover signals only on bar close
  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account balance and risk ratio
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in profits as the trend continues
