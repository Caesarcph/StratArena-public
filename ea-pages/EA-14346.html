摘要

该EA基于i-GAP自定义指标进行交易，该指标检测连续K线之间的价格跳空。在向上跳空时开多仓，在向下跳空时开空仓，并支持可配置的止损和止盈水平。当反向跳空信号出现时，EA还会平掉反向持仓。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念是基于价格跳空（Gap）现象进行交易，认为跳空代表市场强劲的动能方向，大概率会延续。入场条件为：当i-GAP指标在指定信号K线上检测到向上跳空且UpValue非零时开多仓，检测到向下跳空且DnValue非零时开空仓。出场条件包括固定点数的止损（1000点）和止盈（2000点），以及反向信号平仓——当检测到反向跳空信号时平掉原有持仓。仓位管理通过MM参数和MMMode模式控制，支持按自由保证金、余额、亏损后保证金、亏损后余额和固定手数五种模式计算仓位。资金管理方面，止损与止盈比例为1:2，风险回报比较为合理。EA还包含历史回溯机制，在开仓和平仓同时启用时，会向前搜索历史K线以寻找反向信号来决定是否需要平仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on the i-GAP custom indicator, which detects price gaps between consecutive bars. It opens buy positions on upward gaps and sell positions on downward gaps, with configurable stop-loss and take-profit levels. The EA also supports closing opposite positions when a gap signal occurs in the reverse direction.

Strategy Analysis

The EA's strategy concept is based on trading price gaps, interpreting them as strong momentum signals likely to continue in the gap direction. Entry conditions: when the i-GAP indicator detects an upward gap on the specified signal bar (UpValue is non-zero), a buy position is opened; when a downward gap is detected (DnValue is non-zero), a sell position is opened. Exit conditions include fixed stop-loss (1000 points) and take-profit (2000 points) levels, plus reverse-signal closing where opposite positions are closed when a gap signal occurs in the reverse direction. Position sizing is controlled through the MM parameter and MMMode enum, supporting five modes: free margin-based, balance-based, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. Money management features a stop-loss to take-profit ratio of 1:2, providing a reasonable risk-reward profile. The EA also includes a historical lookback mechanism that scans forward through historical bars to find reverse gap signals when both opening and closing are enabled, determining whether existing positions should be closed.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于跳空这种有统计学支撑的市场现象
  • 止损止盈比为1:2，风险回报比合理
  • 提供五种仓位管理模式，灵活性高
  • 支持独立控制开仓和平仓方向，配置灵活
  • 使用IsNewBar机制避免同根K线重复交易

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点过大，对大多数品种而言风险较高
  • 缺少点差过滤，跳空交易在低流动性时段点差可能极大
  • 历史回溯平仓逻辑遍历所有K线，效率低下且可能导致意外平仓
  • 依赖自定义指标i-GAP，指标不可用时策略完全失效
  • 缺少持仓数量限制，可能在连续跳空时过度开仓

改进建议

  • 添加点差和流动性过滤，避免在点差过大时入场
  • 增加最大持仓数量限制和最大同方向持仓限制
  • 优化历史回溯平仓逻辑，限制搜索范围而非遍历全部K线

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on price gaps, a market phenomenon with statistical support
  • Stop-loss to take-profit ratio of 1:2, providing a reasonable risk-reward profile
  • Five position sizing modes offering high flexibility
  • Independent control over opening and closing directions for each position type
  • Uses IsNewBar mechanism to prevent duplicate trades within the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Stop-loss of 1000 points and take-profit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments, creating high risk
  • No spread filter — gap trading during low-liquidity sessions can suffer from extremely wide spreads
  • Historical lookback closing logic iterates through all bars, which is inefficient and may cause unexpected closures
  • Depends entirely on the custom i-GAP indicator — strategy completely fails if the indicator is unavailable
  • No position count limit, potentially over-opening positions on consecutive gap signals

Improvements

  • Add spread and liquidity filters to avoid entering trades when spreads are excessively wide
  • Add maximum position count limits and maximum same-direction position limits
  • Optimize the historical lookback closing logic by limiting the search range instead of iterating through all bars
