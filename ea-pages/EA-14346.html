摘要

该EA基于i-GAP自定义指标进行交易，该指标用于检测指定时间周期上的价格跳空缺口。当检测到向上缺口时开多仓，向下缺口时开空仓，并支持可配置的仓位大小、止损和止盈。当反向缺口信号出现时，还支持平掉反向持仓。

策略分析

策略核心概念是利用价格跳空缺口（GAP）作为交易信号，属于突破类策略。入场条件为：当i-GAP指标在指定信号K线上检测到向上缺口时开多仓，检测到向下缺口时开空仓，缺口大小需超过SizeGAP参数设定的阈值。出场条件包括：固定点数的止损（StopLoss_=1000）和止盈（TakeProfit_=2000），以及反向缺口信号触发时平掉对应持仓。仓位管理通过MarginMode枚举支持多种模式，包括固定手数、按余额比例、按净值比例等。资金管理参数MM控制仓位比例，默认0.1。该EA还实现了历史回溯检查机制——当开仓和平仓条件同时启用但当前无平仓信号时，会遍历历史K线寻找更早的反向缺口信号以决定是否平仓。新K线检测机制确保信号只在每根K线开始时计算一次，避免重复交易。

Summary

This EA trades based on the i-GAP custom indicator, which detects price gaps on a specified timeframe. It opens buy positions on upward gaps and sell positions on downward gaps, with configurable lot sizing, stop loss, and take profit. It also supports closing opposite positions when a gap signal is detected in the reverse direction.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of this strategy is to exploit price gaps as trading signals, classifying it as a breakout-type strategy. Entry conditions are: when the i-GAP indicator detects an upward gap on the specified signal bar, a buy position is opened; when a downward gap is detected, a sell position is opened, with the gap size required to exceed the SizeGAP parameter threshold. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss (StopLoss_=1000 points) and take profit (TakeProfit_=2000 points), as well as closing opposite positions when a reverse gap signal is triggered. Position sizing supports multiple modes via the MarginMode enumeration, including fixed lot, balance-proportional, and equity-proportional sizing. The money management parameter MM controls the position ratio, defaulting to 0.1. The EA also implements a historical lookback mechanism — when both opening and closing conditions are enabled but no current close signal exists, it iterates through historical bars to find earlier reverse gap signals to decide whether to close positions. The new bar detection mechanism ensures signals are calculated only once at the start of each bar, preventing duplicate trades.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于价格跳空缺口这一经典技术分析概念，具有较强的市场意义
  • 提供灵活的资金管理模式，支持固定手数和按比例计算等多种方式
  • 开仓和平仓方向均可独立配置，策略灵活性高
  • 实现了新K线检测机制，避免同一根K线内重复交易
  • 止损和止盈参数可配置，具备基本风险控制框架

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点设置过大，可能导致单笔亏损金额过高
  • 缺少点差过滤机制，在跳空缺口处点差往往扩大，容易造成不利成交
  • 未检查当前是否已有持仓就直接开仓，可能导致重复开仓
  • 历史回溯平仓逻辑遍历所有历史K线，在数据量大时可能影响性能
  • 依赖外部自定义指标i-GAP和TradeAlgorithms.mqh库，独立性较差

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓
  • 添加点差过滤和最大滑点保护，在跳空缺口处提升成交质量
  • 优化历史回溯平仓逻辑，限制最大回溯K线数量以提升性能
  • 增加追踪止损功能，在有利方向移动时锁定利润

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on price gaps — a classic technical analysis concept with strong market significance
  • Flexible money management modes supporting fixed lot and proportional calculation methods
  • Opening and closing directions can be configured independently, providing high strategy flexibility
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate trades within the same bar
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit parameters provide a basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large, potentially leading to high per-trade losses
  • No spread filter — spreads often widen around gaps, leading to unfavorable fills
  • No check for existing positions before opening new ones, potentially causing duplicate positions
  • Historical lookback closing logic iterates all historical bars, which may impact performance with large datasets
  • Depends on external custom indicator i-GAP and TradeAlgorithms.mqh library, reducing independence

Improvements

  • Add position check logic to avoid duplicate openings in the same direction
  • Add spread filter and maximum slippage protection to improve fill quality around gaps
  • Optimize historical lookback closing logic by limiting maximum lookback bar count for better performance
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in profits when price moves favorably
