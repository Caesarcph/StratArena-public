摘要

该EA基于RD-TrendTrigger自定义振荡指标进行交易，支持两种入场模式：振荡器方向变化（twist）和水平突破（disposition）。当振荡器发出动量转变信号时开仓，通过止损、止盈及指标平仓信号管理出场。

策略分析

策略基于RD-TrendTrigger振荡器，该指标结合线性回归和T3平滑算法生成动量信号。入场有两种模式：twist模式检测振荡器方向变化，当连续三个值形成先上升后下降（或先下降后上升）时产生卖出（或买入）信号；disposition模式检测振荡器突破高/低水平线（+50/-50）时产生信号。出场方面，twist模式下方向反转时触发平仓信号，disposition模式下振荡器回升至低水平线以上时平空仓，同时所有仓位都设有止损和止盈保护。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值代表固定手数，正值代表存款比例，由TradeAlgorithms.mqh库函数处理。该EA仅在新的K线形成时检测信号（SignalBar=0除外），避免同一根K线重复交易，但对disposition模式下的多头平仓信号缺失是一个逻辑缺陷。

Summary

This EA trades based on the RD-TrendTrigger custom oscillator indicator, supporting two entry modes: oscillator direction change (twist) and level breakthrough (disposition). It opens buy/sell positions when the oscillator signals momentum shifts and manages exits via stop loss, take profit, and closing signals from the indicator.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy is based on the RD-TrendTrigger oscillator, which combines linear regression with T3 smoothing to generate momentum signals. Entry has two modes: twist mode detects oscillator direction changes, generating sell signals when three consecutive values form a rise-then-fall pattern (or buy signals for fall-then-rise); disposition mode generates signals when the oscillator breaks through the high/low levels (+50/-50). For exits, twist mode triggers closing on direction reversal, while disposition mode closes short positions when the oscillator rises above the low level; all positions have stop loss and take profit protection. Position sizing is controlled via the MM parameter, where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent deposit share percentages, handled by the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library. The EA only checks signals on new bar formation (except when SignalBar=0), preventing repeated trades on the same bar, but there is a logic gap in disposition mode where buy position closing signals are missing.

优势

  • 双模式入场设计灵活，适应不同市场状态
  • 有新K线检测机制，避免重复信号
  • 止损止盈设置完善，保护资金安全
  • 买卖开关独立控制，便于灵活调试

劣势

  • disposition模式缺少多头平仓信号逻辑，存在明显缺陷
  • 依赖外部TradeAlgorithms.mqh库，核心交易逻辑不透明
  • 无点差过滤机制，高波动时滑点风险较大
  • 止损1000点过大，可能导致单笔亏损过高

改进建议

  • 补充disposition模式下多头平仓信号逻辑
  • 增加点差过滤和最大持仓检查机制
  • 将止损参数缩小并加入动态止损功能

Strengths

  • Dual-mode entry design is flexible, adapting to different market conditions
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate signal execution
  • Stop loss and take profit are properly configured, protecting capital
  • Independent buy/sell permission switches allow flexible testing and control

Weaknesses

  • Disposition mode lacks buy position closing signal logic, a clear defect
  • Depends on external TradeAlgorithms.mqh library, core trading logic is opaque
  • No spread filter mechanism, significant slippage risk during high volatility
  • Stop loss of 1000 points is excessively large, potentially causing oversized single-trade losses

Improvements

  • Add buy position closing signal logic in disposition mode
  • Add spread filter and maximum position check mechanisms
  • Reduce stop loss parameter and add dynamic trailing stop functionality

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • disposition模式多头仓位无平仓信号，可能长期持有亏损仓位
  • 止损1000点/止盈2000点的风险收益比虽为1:2但绝对值过大
  • 依赖外部库的仓位管理逻辑不透明，难以审计风险

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Disposition mode has no buy closing signal, potentially holding losing long positions indefinitely
  • Stop loss 1000 points / take profit 2000 points has 1:2 ratio but absolute values are excessively large
  • Position management relies on opaque external library, making risk auditing difficult
