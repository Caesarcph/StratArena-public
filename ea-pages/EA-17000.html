Strategy analysis

VR STEALS 2's core strategy is scalping, relying on low-spread environments to execute rapid trades on small timeframes like M1 or M5. Entry conditions are based on tick-level price dynamics analysis, opening positions when the spread is below a configured threshold and price momentum meets criteria. Exit conditions use fixed take-profit targets (typically a few pips) and stop-loss, pursuing a high-win-rate small-profit trading model. Position sizing supports both fixed lots and balance-based dynamic lot calculation. For money management, the incorporates spread filtering and trading session restrictions to avoid news periods and low-liquidity times. The overall strategy design emphasizes frequent trading with small profit accumulation, but is highly sensitive to broker execution quality (spread, slippage, latency).

Strengths

  • Built-in spread filter avoids trading during high-spread periods, reducing trading costs
  • Trading session restrictions allow avoiding news and high-volatility periods
  • Clear take-profit and stop-loss settings make per-trade risk controllable

Weaknesses

  • Scalping strategy is extremely sensitive to broker execution quality; spread and slippage changes directly affect profitability
  • Frequent trading generates high commission costs that erode profits over the long term
  • Poor risk-reward ratio with small take-profit and larger stop-loss requires extremely high win rate to maintain positive expectation

Possible improvements

  • Add dynamic take-profit mechanism that adapts targets based on market volatility
  • Incorporate economic news event filtering to automatically avoid major data release periods

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Caution

  • Scalping strategy is highly dependent on broker trading environment; results may vary significantly across different brokers
  • Small TP large SL pattern can rapidly deplete account equity during losing streaks
