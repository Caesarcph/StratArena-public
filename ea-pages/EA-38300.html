摘要

TurnGrid是一种基于网格策略的智能交易系统，在当前市场价格上下以固定间隔放置挂单。它通过在价格反转时平仓来捕获市场震荡利润，有效调整网格以适应市场条件变化。

策略分析

TurnGrid策略的核心概念是在价格上下方按固定间距布置网格挂单，利用市场的震荡特性获利。入场条件是当价格达到网格线时，对应的限价或止损挂单被触发开仓。出场条件依赖于价格反转信号——当检测到价格转向时，该方向的仓位被平仓获利。仓位管理采用固定手数或可配置的手数大小，每个网格层级使用相同的交易量。资金管理方面，EA通过网格间距和总订单数控制风险敞口，但缺乏传统的止损机制，完全依赖价格回归来平仓。网格策略在震荡行情中表现良好，但在单边趋势行情中可能导致累积亏损，尤其是没有全局止损保护时风险较大。

Summary

TurnGrid is a grid-based Expert Advisor that places pending orders at fixed price intervals around the current market price. It aims to capture profits from market oscillations by closing positions when price reverses direction, effectively turning the grid to adapt to changing market conditions.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of the TurnGrid strategy is to place pending grid orders at fixed intervals above and below the current price, capitalizing on market oscillations to generate profit. Entry conditions are triggered when price reaches a grid level, activating the corresponding limit or stop pending order. Exit conditions rely on a price reversal signal — when a turn in price direction is detected, positions in that direction are closed for profit. Position sizing employs a fixed or configurable lot size, with the same volume applied at each grid level. Money management controls risk exposure through grid spacing and total order count, but lacks traditional stop-loss mechanisms, relying entirely on price mean-reversion for exits. The grid approach performs well in ranging markets but can accumulate significant losses during strong trending conditions, especially without a global stop-loss safeguard.

优势

  • 震荡行情中表现优异，能持续从小幅波动中获利
  • 策略逻辑清晰，参数可配置性强，便于优化和调整
  • 无需预测市场方向，通过网格结构自动捕捉双向波动

劣势

  • 单边趋势行情中网格仓位会不断累积，导致严重浮亏
  • 缺乏全局止损保护，极端行情下可能爆仓
  • 资金利用率低，大量保证金被挂单占用，影响交易效率

改进建议

  • 增加全局止损和最大浮亏限制，防止单边行情下爆仓
  • 加入趋势过滤机制，在强趋势时减少或暂停网格开仓

Strengths

  • Performs excellently in ranging markets, continuously profiting from small price oscillations
  • Clear strategy logic with highly configurable parameters for optimization and adjustment
  • No need to predict market direction — the grid structure automatically captures bidirectional fluctuations

Weaknesses

  • Grid positions continuously accumulate during strong trending markets, leading to significant floating losses
  • Lacks a global stop-loss mechanism, risking account blowout under extreme market conditions
  • Low capital efficiency — large amounts of margin are tied up by pending orders, reducing trading efficiency

Improvements

  • Add a global stop-loss and maximum drawdown limit to prevent account blowout during trending markets
  • Incorporate a trend filter to reduce or pause grid order placement during strong trending conditions
