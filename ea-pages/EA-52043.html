This EA trades two currency pairs based on their statistical correlation, opening opposing positions when the pairs diverge from their historical correlation relationship and closing when correlation normalizes. It exploits temporary mispricing between correlated instruments using a pairs trading approach. The strategy assumes mean reversion of the correlation spread between the two selected symbols.

摘要

该EA基于两个货币对之间的统计相关性进行交易，当两对货币偏离历史相关关系时开立对冲头寸，待相关性回归正常时平仓。它利用配对交易方法挖掘相关工具之间的暂时错误定价。策略假设两个选定品种之间的相关性价差具有均值回归特性。

策略分析

该EA采用统计套利/配对交易策略，核心概念是两个高度相关货币对的价格偏离最终会回归均值。入场条件为当两对货币的价差或相关性偏离历史正常水平超过设定阈值时，在一对货币上做多同时在另一对上做空，以对冲市场方向性风险。出场条件为当相关性恢复到正常水平或价差收敛时平仓获利，也可能设有止损在偏离继续扩大时止损。仓位管理方面，通常会根据两个品种的波动率比率和点值差异来调整手数，以确保两边的货币风险敞口相等。资金管理方面，该EA可能使用固定手数或基于账户余额的比例仓位，但配对交易天然具有市场中性特性，降低了方向性风险。整体策略依赖相关性的稳定性假设，在市场结构性变化时可能面临较大回撤风险。

Summary

This EA trades two currency pairs based on their statistical correlation, opening opposing positions when the pairs diverge from their historical correlation relationship and closing when correlation normalizes. It exploits temporary mispricing between correlated instruments using a pairs trading approach. The strategy assumes mean reversion of the correlation spread between the two selected symbols.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a statistical arbitrage/pairs trading strategy, with the core concept that price divergence between two highly correlated currency pairs will eventually revert to the mean. Entry conditions trigger when the spread or correlation between the two pairs deviates beyond a configured threshold from historical norms, opening a long position on one pair and a short position on the other to hedge directional market risk. Exit conditions close both positions when the correlation normalizes or the spread converges, capturing the reversion profit, with stop-losses potentially set for when divergence continues to widen. Position sizing typically adjusts lot sizes based on the volatility ratio and pip value differences between the two instruments to ensure equal monetary exposure on both sides. Money management may use fixed lots or balance-proportional sizing, while the pair trading structure inherently provides market-neutral characteristics that reduce directional risk. The overall strategy relies on the stability assumption of correlation, which can face significant drawdown during structural market regime changes.

优势

  • 市场中性策略，对冲了方向性风险，在涨跌市中均可获利
  • 利用统计关系进行交易，具有数学和统计学基础支撑
  • 配对交易天然限制了单边敞口风险，回撤相对可控

劣势

  • 依赖相关性稳定性假设，市场结构性变化时相关性可能崩溃导致巨大亏损
  • 两个品种的点值、波动率和交易时间可能不同，造成对冲不完全
  • 需要同时管理两个品种的订单，增加了执行风险和滑点成本

改进建议

  • 增加动态相关性监控和自适应阈值调整，在相关性减弱时自动降低仓位或暂停交易
  • 加入协整检验替代简单相关性分析，更准确地判断价差的均值回归特性

Strengths

  • Market-neutral strategy that hedges directional risk, capable of profiting in both rising and falling markets
  • Trading based on statistical relationships provides mathematical and empirical foundation
  • Pairs trading inherently limits directional exposure, making drawdowns relatively more controlled

Weaknesses

  • Relies on correlation stability assumption; correlation breakdowns during structural market shifts can cause severe losses
  • Different pip values, volatility, and trading sessions between the two instruments can result in imperfect hedging
  • Requires simultaneously managing orders on two symbols, increasing execution risk and slippage costs

Improvements

  • Add dynamic correlation monitoring with adaptive threshold adjustment, automatically reducing position size or pausing trading when correlation weakens
  • Replace simple correlation analysis with cointegration testing to more accurately assess the mean-reversion property of the spread

  • 执行风险：两个品种的订单无法同时成交可能导致对冲缺口，尤其在流动性不足时

Risk Points

  • Execution risk: Inability to fill both symbols' orders simultaneously can create hedge gaps, especially during low liquidity conditions
