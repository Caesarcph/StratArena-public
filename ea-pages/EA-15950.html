This EA trades based on the Aroon Oscillator indicator with alert signals. It opens buy positions on upward crossover signals and sell positions on downward crossover signals, with configurable stop-loss, take-profit, and money management options.

Strategy analysis

This EA uses the Aroon Oscillator indicator (custom AroonOscillatorSignAlert indicator) as its core signal source. Entry conditions: when the indicator's upper buffer (buffer 1) produces a non-zero, non-empty value on the configured signal bar, it triggers a buy open and sell close signal; when the lower buffer (buffer 0) produces a valid signal, it triggers a sell open and buy close signal. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop-loss and take-profit settings, and also support reverse-signal closing — when both buy and sell open/close are enabled, the EA retrospects historical bars to find opposing signals to determine whether to close positions. Position sizing supports five modes via the MM parameter and MMMode enumeration: by free margin percentage, by balance percentage, by net free margin percentage, by net balance percentage, and fixed lot size. Money management uses fixed stop-loss and take-profit in points with no dynamic adjustment mechanism, lacking the ability to adapt stops and targets based on market volatility. The overall strategy is a typical momentum-following system relying on the Aroon Oscillator crossing threshold levels to detect trend momentum changes.

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on the Aroon Oscillator momentum signals with solid theoretical foundation
  • Five position sizing modes offer good flexibility to suit different risk preferences
  • Independent buy/sell open/close switches enable long-only, short-only, or bidirectional trading
  • SignalBar parameter controls signal confirmation timing, avoiding false signals from uncompleted bars
  • New bar detection mechanism (CIsNewBar) prevents duplicate entries on the same bar

Weaknesses

  • ATR_Period is not declared as an input parameter, making it non-optimizable in the strategy tester — a code defect
  • Fixed stop-loss and take-profit in points ignores market volatility changes (e.g., ATR-based dynamic stops), leading to inconsistent performance across varying volatility regimes
  • The historical bar loop for finding reverse closing signals (lines 142-163) can be inefficient with large bar counts, posing potential performance issues
  • No spread filter mechanism — trades open even with wide spreads, potentially resulting in excessive entry costs
  • No position count check or maximum position limit, which may lead to excessive positions from dense signal clusters
  • Comments are garbled (encoding issue), severely harming code readability and maintainability

Possible improvements

  • Declare ATR_Period as an input parameter for strategy tester optimization, and fix file encoding to restore Chinese comments
  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss/take-profit options, a spread filter, and maximum position limits to improve risk management adaptability

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Fixed stop-loss/take-profit doesn't adapt to volatility changes — stops too wide in low volatility, targets hard to reach in high volatility
  • No spread filter — entries execute even when spreads widen during volatile periods
  • No maximum position limit — dense signals may accumulate excessive exposure
  • Historical bar loop has no upper bound protection, potentially iterating too many bars in extreme cases
