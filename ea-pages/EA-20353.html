摘要

该EA使用布林带进行均值回归交易，当价格触及或突破布林带上下轨时最多可开N个仓位。入场逻辑基于价格穿透轨道后预期回归中轨，并支持配置最大持仓数量来管理多个仓位。

策略分析

策略核心是基于布林带的均值回归交易，当价格触及或突破上轨时做空，触及或突破下轨时做多，预期价格将回归布林带中轨。入场条件依赖于布林带轨道的穿透，允许在同一方向上累计开立最多N个仓位以摊薄成本并增加获利概率。出场条件通常设定为止盈到达布林带中轨或设定的固定止盈点，同时配合止损来控制单笔亏损。仓位管理采用分批建仓方式，每次信号触发时增加一个仓位直到达到最大持仓数N，每个仓位的手数可以独立配置。资金管理方面依赖止损和最大仓位数限制来控制总体风险敞口，但多仓位叠加在极端趋势行情中可能造成较大累积亏损。

Summary

This EA trades mean reversion signals using Bollinger Bands, opening up to N positions when price touches or exceeds the bands. It enters on band penetration expecting price to revert to the mean (middle band), and manages multiple positions with configurable position limits.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy is mean reversion based on Bollinger Bands, going short when price touches or breaches the upper band and going long when it touches or breaches the lower band, expecting price to revert to the middle band. Entry conditions rely on Bollinger Band penetration, with the system allowing accumulation of up to N positions in the same direction to average the entry price and improve profit probability. Exit conditions are typically set as take-profit at the Bollinger middle band or a fixed take-profit level, combined with stop-loss to control per-trade losses. Position sizing employs a scaled entry approach where each signal triggers an additional position up to the maximum N positions, with each position's lot size independently configurable. Money management relies on stop-loss and the maximum position count limit to control overall risk exposure, though stacked positions in strong trending markets can result in significant cumulative losses.

优势

  • 布林带均值回归逻辑经典可靠，在震荡市中表现良好
  • 支持N个仓位分批建仓，能够摊薄入场成本提高回归获利概率
  • 参数可配置性强，布林带周期、偏差倍数、最大仓位数均可调整

劣势

  • 均值回归策略在强趋势行情中容易连续亏损，多仓位叠加会放大风险
  • 止损和止盈默认值可能为零或不够精细，缺少严格的风控保护
  • 缺少趋势过滤器，无法区分震荡市和趋势市导致在趋势行情中反复逆势开仓

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤器（如ADX或大周期均线）避免在趋势行情中逆势开仓
  • 实现动态止损移动和盈亏平衡功能以更好保护浮盈
  • 加入持仓间相关性检查和最大回撤控制，限制极端情况下的总风险敞口

Strengths

  • Bollinger Band mean reversion logic is classic and reliable, performing well in ranging markets
  • Supports N-position scaling to average entry price and improve mean-reversion profit probability
  • Highly configurable parameters for BB period, deviation multiplier, and maximum position count

Weaknesses

  • Mean reversion strategy prone to consecutive losses in strong trends, with multiple positions amplifying risk
  • Default stop-loss and take-profit values may be zero or insufficiently refined, lacking strict risk protection
  • Missing trend filter to distinguish ranging from trending markets, causing repeated counter-trend entries in trends

Improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., ADX or higher-timeframe MA) to avoid counter-trend entries in trending markets
  • Implement dynamic trailing stop and breakeven functionality to better protect floating profits
  • Add inter-position correlation checks and maximum drawdown controls to limit total risk exposure in extreme scenarios
