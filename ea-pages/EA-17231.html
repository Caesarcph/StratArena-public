摘要

Gazonkos是一个基于网格策略的智能交易系统，在中心价格上下按固定间距挂单，利用市场波动获利。它系统性地在预定距离放置买卖订单，通过价格振荡捕捉小额利润。该EA依赖网格间距和仓位管理来产生收益，不具有方向性偏见。

策略分析

该EA采用网格交易策略，在当前价格上下按固定间距布置买卖挂单网格。入场条件为价格到达预设的网格水平时自动触发挂单成交，无需趋势判断或指标信号。出场条件通常为每个网格单设置固定止盈目标，当价格回撤到获利水平时平仓。仓位管理方面，部分网格EA采用等量手数，也有采用递增手数（马丁格尔变体）以摊平成本。资金管理依赖于网格间距和止盈比例的合理配置，间距过小可能导致密集持仓风险。整体策略在震荡行情中表现良好，但在单边趋势中可能因连续建仓而面临大幅浮亏风险。

Summary

Gazonkos is a grid-based Expert Advisor that places pending orders at fixed price intervals around a central price level, aiming to profit from market fluctuations within a range. It systematically opens buy and sell orders at predetermined distances, capturing small profits as price oscillates. The EA relies on grid spacing and lot management to generate returns without directional bias.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a grid trading strategy that places buy and sell pending orders at fixed intervals above and below the current price. Entry conditions are purely mechanical: orders are triggered when price reaches predefined grid levels, requiring no trend analysis or indicator signals. Exit conditions typically involve fixed take-profit targets for each grid order, closing positions when price reverts to profitable levels. Position sizing varies: some grid EAs use uniform lot sizes per level, while others employ increasing lots (Martingale variant) to average down entry prices. Money management depends on proper configuration of grid spacing and take-profit ratios; insufficient spacing can lead to excessive position accumulation. The strategy performs well in ranging markets but faces significant drawdown risk during strong trending conditions due to continuous position building against the trend.

优势

  • 网格策略在震荡市场中能稳定获利，无需判断趋势方向
  • 系统化交易逻辑清晰，参数可调，便于优化和回测
  • 挂单方式确保在价格到达目标位时精确入场，避免滑点问题

劣势

  • 单边趋势行情中网格持仓会持续增加，浮亏可能急剧扩大
  • 若无止损保护，极端行情可能导致爆仓风险
  • 网格间距和手数设置需要根据品种波动率精细调整，通用性较差

改进建议

  • 增加全局止损和最大浮亏限制，防止单边行情下爆仓
  • 加入趋势过滤器，在强趋势期间暂停网格或减少仓位
  • 实现动态网格间距，根据ATR等波动率指标自适应调整

Strengths

  • Grid strategy generates consistent profits in ranging markets without requiring trend direction judgment
  • Systematic trading logic is clear with adjustable parameters, facilitating optimization and backtesting
  • Pending order approach ensures precise entries at target price levels, avoiding slippage issues

Weaknesses

  • During strong trending markets, grid positions accumulate continuously and floating losses can expand rapidly
  • Without stop-loss protection, extreme market conditions can lead to account blowout risk
  • Grid spacing and lot sizing require fine-tuning per instrument volatility, limiting general applicability

Improvements

  • Add global stop-loss and maximum drawdown limits to prevent account blowout during trending markets
  • Implement a trend filter to pause grid operations or reduce exposure during strong trend conditions
  • Implement dynamic grid spacing that adapts based on volatility indicators such as ATR
