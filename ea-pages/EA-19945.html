摘要

该EA基于EMA交叉信号进行交易，可选MACD过滤，入场时同时下1个市价单和4个间隔排列的对冲挂单。对已持仓位使用移动止损跟踪利润，并在信号反转时可选平掉反向仓位。对冲机制通过在趋势方向上布置阶梯式止损挂单来逐步建仓。

策略分析

策略概念基于短期EMA与长期EMA的交叉判断趋势方向，当短EMA上穿长EMA时视为上升趋势，下穿时视为下降趋势。入场条件为EMA交叉且可选MACD过滤（做多需MACD主线>=0，做空需MACD主线<=0），入场时除市价单外还会在距离当前价格1-4倍HedgeLevel处放置4个同方向止损挂单，形成阶梯式加仓。出场条件包括固定止损止盈以及移动止损跟踪，移动止损仅在利润超过TrailingStop+TrailingStep时才启动调整。仓位管理采用固定手数，未根据账户资金动态调整。资金管理方面没有最大持仓限制，4个挂单加上市价单意味着单次信号可能同时持有5个仓位，风险敞口较大。

Summary

This EA trades EMA crossover signals with an optional MACD filter, placing a main market order plus 4 hedge pending orders at fixed intervals. It uses trailing stops for open positions and optionally closes opposite positions on signal reversal. The hedging mechanism creates a ladder of stop orders in the direction of the trend to scale into positions.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the crossover of a short-period EMA and a long-period EMA to determine trend direction, with an uptrend signaled when the short EMA crosses above the long EMA and a downtrend when it crosses below. Entry conditions require an EMA crossover plus an optional MACD filter (long requires MACD main line >= 0, short requires MACD main line <= 0), and upon entry the EA places a market order along with 4 stop orders spaced at 1x to 4x the HedgeLevel distance, creating a ladder of scaling-in positions. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit levels, as well as a trailing stop that only activates when unrealized profit exceeds TrailingStop plus TrailingStep. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size with no dynamic adjustment based on account equity or risk percentage. Money management lacks a maximum position limit, and the combination of a market order plus 4 pending stop orders means a single signal can result in up to 5 simultaneous positions, creating significant risk exposure.

优势

  • 使用EMA交叉结合MACD双重过滤，信号质量优于单一指标
  • 移动止损机制可以锁定浮动利润，减少回撤
  • 挂单有过期时间设置，避免过期信号导致的无效入场
  • 支持3/5位小数点自动调整，兼容不同品种
  • 有成交量合法性检查，防止无效手数下单

劣势

  • 单次信号可产生5个仓位(1市价+4挂单)，风险敞口过大且无最大持仓控制
  • 移动止损步进仅1点且移动止损仅30点，在波动较大市场中容易被扫出
  • 挂单间隔仅6点过于密集，在剧烈行情中可能快速全部成交导致大量亏损
  • 没有持仓数量检查，不同信号可能累积大量同向仓位
  • 止损140点止盈120点，盈亏比小于1，长期期望值不利

改进建议

  • 增加最大同时持仓数量限制，防止风险敞口失控
  • 将HedgeLevel与ATR动态挂钩，使挂单间距适应市场波动性
  • 增加盈亏比控制，使止盈至少大于止损或采用动态止盈

Strengths

  • Uses EMA crossover combined with optional MACD filter for higher signal quality than a single indicator
  • Trailing stop mechanism locks in floating profits and reduces drawdowns
  • Pending orders have expiration times, preventing invalid entries from stale signals
  • Automatic 3/5 digit point adjustment ensures compatibility with different symbol formats
  • Volume validation check prevents orders with invalid lot sizes

Weaknesses

  • A single signal can create 5 positions (1 market + 4 pending), with no maximum position control creating excessive risk exposure
  • Trailing stop of only 30 pips with 1 pip step is too tight and likely to get stopped out in volatile markets
  • Hedge level of only 6 pips between pending orders is too dense, all 4 could fill rapidly in fast markets causing large losses
  • No total position count check means multiple signals can accumulate excessive directional exposure
  • Stop loss of 140 pips vs take profit of 120 pips gives a risk-reward ratio below 1, which is unfavorable long-term

Improvements

  • Add a maximum simultaneous position count limit to prevent uncontrolled risk exposure
  • Link HedgeLevel dynamically to ATR so that pending order spacing adapts to market volatility
  • Improve risk-reward ratio by setting take profit larger than stop loss or using dynamic take profit targets

风险评估

总体:HIGH

风险点

  • 单次信号最多开5个仓位，无最大仓位限制，风险敞口可能急剧扩大
  • 挂单间距仅6点过于密集，极端行情下可能全部快速成交导致巨额亏损
  • 盈亏比小于1，长期统计学上不利
  • 未检查已有挂单数量，重复信号可能产生更多挂单叠加风险

Risk Assessment

Overall:HIGH

Risk Points

  • Single signal can open up to 5 positions with no maximum position limit, risk exposure can expand rapidly
  • Pending order spacing of only 6 pips is too dense, all could fill rapidly in extreme markets causing massive losses
  • Risk-reward ratio below 1 is statistically unfavorable long-term
  • No check on existing pending orders, repeated signals could stack even more pending orders increasing risk
