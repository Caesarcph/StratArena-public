Exp_ColorJFatl_StDev

This EA trades based on the ColorJFatl_StDev custom indicator, which combines a JFATL (JMA-smoothed FATL) moving average with Standard Deviation channels. It opens and closes positions using three signal modes: point-based (dot signals), directional (line direction change), or disabled, allowing flexible entry and exit logic on trend reversals or continuations.

Strategy analysis

The strategy core is trend-following based on the JFATL indicator (a JMA-smoothed FATL digital filter) combined with Standard Deviation channels. Entry conditions operate in two modes: POINT mode triggers trades when dot signals appear in the indicator buffers (non-EMPTY_VALUE values); DIRECT mode triggers on JFATL line direction changes such as bottom reversals (Line[0]>Line[1] && Line[1]<Line[2]) or top reversals. Exit conditions mirror entry logic with two modes: POINT mode uses opposite-direction dot signals for closing; DIRECT mode closes when the JFATL line direction reverses. Position sizing is controlled via the MM parameter and MMMode enum, supporting five modes including percentage of free margin, balance, post-loss margin/balance, or fixed lot size. Money management leverages the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library with configurable stop loss, take profit, and slippage deviation control. The strategy allows signal verification on non-zero bars via SignalBar and uses an IsNewBar mechanism to prevent redundant calculations within the same bar.

Strengths

  • Flexible signal mode design allowing independent selection of dot or directional signals for entry and exit, adapting to different trading styles
  • JFATL digital filter combined with Standard Deviation channels provides both trend-following and volatility-adaptive capabilities
  • Built-in IsNewBar detection and history loading mechanism prevents redundant calculations and signal repainting
  • Five position sizing modes offer flexible money management options
  • Recount mechanism ensures safe retry on indicator data read failures rather than executing erroneous trades

Weaknesses

  • Depends on external custom indicator ColorJFatl_StDev; EA fails to run if the indicator is not properly installed
  • Default SL of 1000 points and TP of 2000 points may be too wide for H4 timeframe; risk-reward ratio needs adjustment per instrument
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, potentially leading to unfavorable fills during high-spread or inactive periods
  • DIRECT mode exit compares only two bars of line values, which may generate frequent false exit signals in choppy markets
  • No explicit position check logic; relies entirely on TradeAlgorithms.mqh library functions for position management

Possible improvements

  • Add spread filter and trading session control to avoid opening positions under unfavorable conditions
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in floating profits and reduce profit giveback risk
  • Add position count checks and duplicate same-direction entry protection logic
  • Add confirmation conditions for DIRECT mode exits (e.g., two consecutive bars confirming direction change) to reduce false signals

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Depends on external custom indicator; missing or version-mismatched indicator can cause EA initialization failure
  • No spread filter; may result in unfavorable slippage fills during high-spread market conditions
  • Weak DIRECT mode exit conditions may cause frequent entries and exits in choppy markets leading to consecutive losses
  • No explicit position count limit; may over-commit in extreme conditions
