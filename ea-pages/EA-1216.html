摘要

该EA基于MA圆整通道进行突破交易，通道由圆整移动平均线与ATR带宽构成。价格收盘突破上轨时做多，跌破下轨时做空，同时触发反向仓位平仓信号。

策略分析

策略概念基于MA圆整通道突破，通道由圆整后的移动平均线加上ATR倍数构成上下轨。入场条件为：当已完成K线同时存在上下通道值时，收盘价突破上轨则开多单并平空单，收盘价跌破下轨则开空单并平多单。若已完成K线不存在通道（通道消失），则回溯寻找最近的通道上轨来判断是否需要平仓。出场条件包括止损止盈点数设置，以及通道消失时的条件平仓逻辑。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，负值代表固定手数，正值代表按存款比例计算手数。资金管理采用固定止损止盈方式，止损1000点、止盈2000点，风险回报比为1:2。

Summary

This EA trades breakouts from an MA Rounding Channel, which combines a rounded moving average with ATR-based channel bands. It opens buy positions when price closes above the upper channel and sell positions when price closes below the lower channel, with corresponding exit signals for opposite positions.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on breakout from an MA Rounding Channel, where the channel consists of a rounded moving average plus ATR-multiple bands forming upper and lower boundaries. Entry conditions: when a closed bar has both upper and lower channel values present, a close above the upper channel triggers a buy entry and short position close, while a close below the lower channel triggers a sell entry and long position close. When the channel disappears on the closed bar (either band value is zero), the EA searches backward through history to find the most recent upper channel value and determines if exit signals should be generated based on price relative to that level. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit in points, plus conditional closes when price breaks the opposite channel band or channel continuity is lost. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter: negative values represent fixed lot sizes while positive values represent a share of the deposit. Money management uses a fixed stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points, yielding a risk-reward ratio of 1:2.

优势

  • 支持10种平滑算法，策略灵活性高，可适应不同市场环境
  • 通道消失时具有回溯查找机制，避免在无通道时持仓风险失控
  • 风险回报比为1:2，止盈大于止损，具备正期望值潜力
  • 提供买卖开仓和平仓的独立开关控制，仓位管理粒度细致
  • 新K线检测机制避免同一K线内重复交易

劣势

  • 通道消失时的回溯查找逻辑存在潜在问题：仅查找上轨而未对称查找下轨用于平空仓判断
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下突破信号可能为假突破
  • MM参数正负值含义不同（负值=固定手数、正值=存款比例），设计不够直观易误用
  • 无持仓检查机制，可能在新信号出现时重复开仓
  • 回溯查找遍历所有K线，在历史数据量大时可能导致性能问题

改进建议

  • 通道消失时增加对称的下轨回溯查找逻辑，使多空平仓判断更均衡
  • 增加持仓检查和最大持仓数限制，避免重复开仓和过度暴露风险
  • 增加点差过滤和滑点保护机制，提高实盘交易的鲁棒性

Strengths

  • Supports 10 smoothing algorithms providing high strategy flexibility to adapt to different market conditions
  • Backward search mechanism when channel disappears prevents uncontrolled position risk during channel-less periods
  • Risk-reward ratio of 1:2 with take profit larger than stop loss, offering positive expectancy potential
  • Independent toggle controls for buy/sell open and close operations provide granular position management
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated trading within the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Channel disappearance backtracking logic is asymmetric: only searches for upper band but not lower band for short position close decisions
  • No spread filter, breakout signals may produce false breakouts in high-spread environments
  • MM parameter dual-meaning design (negative=fixed lot, positive=deposit share) is unintuitive and error-prone
  • No position check before opening new trades, potentially leading to duplicate positions on repeated signals
  • Backward search iterates through all bars which may cause performance issues with large history data

Improvements

  • Add symmetric lower band backward search when channel disappears for balanced long/short close decisions
  • Add position check and maximum position limit to prevent duplicate entries and overexposure
  • Add spread filter and slippage protection mechanisms to improve robustness in live trading

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 缺少持仓检查，可能在已有同向仓位时重复开仓导致风险加倍
  • 通道消失时回溯查找逻辑不对称，空单平仓判断可能遗漏
  • MM参数负值为固定手数的设计可能导致不合理的仓位大小
  • 无点差过滤，高波动时点差扩大可能导致不利成交

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Missing position check may cause duplicate entries in same direction, doubling risk exposure
  • Asymmetric channel disappearance backtracking may miss short position close signals
  • MM parameter negative value as fixed lot size may result in unreasonable position sizing
  • No spread filter, widening spreads during high volatility may cause unfavorable fills
