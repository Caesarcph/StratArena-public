摘要

该EA基于MA舍入通道指标的突破进行交易。当价格收盘突破通道上轨时开多仓，跌破下轨时开空仓，当价格重新回到通道内或通道消失时触发平仓信号。仓位管理通过MM参数控制存款份额或固定手数。

策略分析

策略核心是基于MA舍入通道的突破交易，通道由一条经舍入处理的移动平均线加上ATR倍数构建上下轨形成。入场条件为：当通道存在于已收盘K线上时，若收盘价突破上轨则产生买入信号，若跌破下轨则产生卖出信号；同时检查前一根K线是否无通道或价格未突破，以确认突破的有效性。出场条件包括：当通道消失时，通过回溯历史K线寻找最近的通道上轨或下轨，判断价格是否已穿越从而触发平仓；也可以通过固定点数的止损和止盈出场。仓位管理方面，MM参数为负值时表示固定手数，为正值时表示按存款比例计算手数。止损设为1000点，止盈设为2000点，风险回报比为1:2。该策略还提供了买入和卖出的开仓/平仓权限开关，允许灵活控制交易方向。

Summary

This EA trades breakouts from an MA Rounding Channel indicator. It opens buy positions when price closes above the upper channel band and sell positions when price closes below the lower band, with exit signals triggered when price re-enters or when the channel disappears. Position sizing uses a deposit share or fixed lot mechanism controlled by the MM parameter.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy is a breakout system based on the MA Rounding Channel, which consists of a rounded moving average line with ATR-based upper and lower bands. Entry conditions: when the channel exists on the closed bar, a buy signal triggers if the close breaks above the upper band, and a sell signal triggers if the close breaks below the lower band; the EA also validates breakout freshness by checking whether the previous bar lacked a channel or the price had not yet broken through. Exit conditions include: when the channel disappears, the EA backtracks through historical bars to find the most recent channel boundary and determines if the price has crossed it to trigger position closure; fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels also serve as exits. Position sizing uses the MM parameter where negative values represent fixed lot sizes and positive values represent a share of the deposit. Stop-loss is set at 1000 points and take-profit at 2000 points, yielding a 1:2 risk-reward ratio. The EA also provides permission switches for buy/sell opening and closing, allowing flexible directional control.

优势

  • 通道基于ATR自适应调整宽度，能根据市场波动率动态适应
  • 止损1000点/止盈2000点的1:2风险回报比设计合理
  • 支持10种平滑方法和9种价格类型，参数灵活性高
  • 提供买卖方向独立开关，可灵活控制单向或双向交易
  • 通道消失时仍能通过回溯寻找最近的通道边界来管理平仓

劣势

  • 未检查持仓是否存在就直接发送平仓指令，可能产生无效请求
  • 缺少点差过滤，突破信号可能因大点差而失效
  • 通道消失时的回溯搜索效率低，最坏情况需遍历所有历史K线
  • 止损1000点较大，在H4周期上可能承受较大浮亏
  • MM参数的正负值逻辑不够直观，容易混淆存款份额与固定手数

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免在无持仓时发送平仓指令
  • 添加点差过滤和最大滑点检查，提高突破信号的可靠性
  • 优化通道消失时的回溯搜索逻辑，设置最大回溯K线数限制以提升性能

Strengths

  • Channel width adapts dynamically to market volatility via ATR-based bands
  • Reasonable 1:2 risk-reward ratio with 1000-point SL and 2000-point TP
  • High parameter flexibility with 10 smoothing methods and 9 price types
  • Independent buy/sell permission switches allow flexible directional control
  • Robust exit logic that backtracks to find the most recent channel boundary when the channel disappears

Weaknesses

  • No position existence check before sending close orders, potentially generating invalid requests
  • No spread filter, breakout signals may be invalidated by wide spreads
  • Inefficient historical bar backtracking when channel disappears—worst case iterates through all bars
  • 1000-point stop-loss is relatively large, potentially allowing significant drawdown on H4 timeframe
  • MM parameter's positive/negative logic is unintuitive, easily confusing deposit share with fixed lot size

Improvements

  • Add position existence checks before sending close orders to avoid invalid requests
  • Add spread filter and maximum slippage checks to improve breakout signal reliability
  • Optimize channel disappearance backtracking logic by setting a maximum lookback bar limit to improve performance
