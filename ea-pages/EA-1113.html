摘要

这是一个多信号马丁格尔风格EA，基于六种可选信号类型之一（MACD、支点、支撑阻力、i_Trend+RSI、i_Trend+RSI+随机指标、i_Trend+RSI+随机指标+MFI）开仓。当价格逆向波动时，通过后续加仓时翻倍手数来摊低成本，超过设定次数后切换为1.5倍乘数，并使用移动止盈进行出场管理。

策略分析

策略概念基于多指标信号确认开仓，支持六种不同的信号源，包括MACD动量、支点水平、支撑阻力突破以及i_Trend指标与RSI、随机指标、MFI的多种组合。入场条件为选定信号源发出买入或卖出信号，且无反向信号时开仓。出场条件包括初始止盈、加仓后重新计算的加权平均止盈，以及可选的移动止损。仓位管理采用马丁格尔式递增加仓：前DoubleCount次加仓手数为起始手数乘以2的幂次，之后改为1.5倍递增，每次加仓间距为Pips点数。资金管理方面，当Lots设为0时根据账户自由保证金和MaximumRisk参数计算手数。加仓后止盈按持仓加权均价加上TakeProfit2点数重新计算，最后一次加仓时设置止损。移动止损功能在价格朝有利方向移动后将止损移至保本以上。

Summary

A multi-signal martingale-style EA that opens positions based on one of six selectable signal types (MACD, Pivot, Support/Resistance, i_Trend+RSI, i_Trend+RSI+Stochastic, i_Trend+RSI+Stochastic+MFI). It employs aggressive position scaling by doubling lots on subsequent entries when price moves against the position, then switching to 1.5x multiplication after a configurable count, with a trailing stop for exit management.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on multi-indicator signal confirmation for trade entry, supporting six different signal sources including MACD momentum, Pivot levels, Support/Resistance breakouts, and various combinations of i_Trend with RSI, Stochastic, and MFI. Entry conditions trigger when the selected signal source produces a buy or sell signal without a conflicting opposite signal. Exit conditions include initial take-profit, recalculated weighted-average take-profit after additional entries, and an optional trailing stop. Position sizing employs martingale-style progressive scaling: the first DoubleCount additional entries multiply the starting lot by powers of 2, then switching to 1.5x multiplication thereafter, with each add-on requiring a minimum adverse price movement of Pips points. Money management calculates lot size from account free margin and MaximumRisk when the Lots parameter is set to zero. After adding positions, take-profit is recalculated as the volume-weighted average entry price plus TakeProfit2 points, and a stop-loss is only set on the final MaxCount entry. The trailing stop function moves the stop-loss to breakeven or better once price moves favorably.

优势

  • 支持六种信号源切换，策略灵活性高，可适应不同市场环境
  • 采用面向对象设计，信号类继承结构清晰，便于扩展新的信号类型
  • 加仓后重新计算加权均价止盈，逻辑合理，确保整体持仓盈利目标
  • 包含止损检查、最小止损距离验证等风控措施

劣势

  • 马丁格尔式加仓策略风险极高，连续逆向波动会导致手数指数级增长，可能爆仓
  • 止损仅在最后一次加仓时设置，前期加仓均无止损保护，面临单边行情巨大风险
  • MaxCount设为-1时无加仓次数限制，极端行情下亏损无上限
  • CSigi_TrendRSISto类中m_buy被错误地赋值为卖出条件（复制粘贴bug），导致该信号类型完全失效
  • SolveLots函数的风险计算方式过于简化，未考虑杠杆和品种点值

改进建议

  • 修复CSigi_TrendRSISto中m_buy的赋值bug，将第741行的m_buy改为正确的买入条件而非复制m_sell的条件
  • 为每次加仓都设置止损，而非仅在最后一次加仓时设置，降低无保护持仓风险
  • 加入最大账户风险限制和最大总持仓手数限制，防止马丁格尔加仓失控
  • 加仓逻辑中应考虑点差和手续费成本，当前加权均价止盈未计入交易成本

Strengths

  • Supports six switchable signal sources, providing high strategy flexibility for different market conditions
  • Uses object-oriented design with clean signal class inheritance, making it easy to extend with new signal types
  • Recalculates volume-weighted average take-profit after adding positions, ensuring coherent profit targets for the overall position
  • Includes stop-loss validation and minimum stop-level checks as risk control measures

Weaknesses

  • Martingale-style position scaling carries extreme risk; consecutive adverse moves cause exponential lot growth that can blow the account
  • Stop-loss is only set on the final MaxCount entry, leaving all prior additions without stop-loss protection against strong trends
  • MaxCount=-1 allows unlimited additions, creating unbounded loss potential in extreme market conditions
  • CSigi_TrendRSISto class has a copy-paste bug where m_buy is assigned the sell condition, making that signal type completely non-functional
  • SolveLots risk calculation is oversimplified, not accounting for leverage and tick value per symbol

Improvements

  • Fix the m_buy assignment bug in CSigi_TrendRSISto where line 741 incorrectly assigns the sell condition to m_buy instead of the proper buy condition
  • Set stop-loss on every additional entry rather than only on the final MaxCount entry, reducing unprotected position risk
  • Add maximum account risk limits and maximum total position lot caps to prevent martingale scaling from spiraling out of control
  • Incorporate spread and commission costs into the weighted-average take-profit calculation, as the current formula ignores transaction costs
