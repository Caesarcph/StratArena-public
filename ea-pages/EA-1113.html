摘要

这是一个多信号EA，根据六种可选信号类型之一（MACD、支点、支撑阻力、i_Trend+RSI、i_Trend+RSI+随机指标、i_Trend+RSI+随机指标+MFI）开仓，然后采用激进的马丁格尔式加仓策略。它在固定点数间隔处同方向加仓，手数按指数增长（前DoubleCount次为2倍，之后为1.5倍），直到达到可配置的最大加仓次数。还提供简单移动止损功能。

策略分析

策略概念：该EA的核心是在趋势信号方向上不断加仓，通过马丁格尔式倍增手数来摊薄成本，期待市场回归时获利。入场条件：首次入场依赖所选信号类型（如MACD金叉做多、死叉做空），后续加仓需价格朝不利方向移动Pips点数且信号仍然有效。出场条件：通过TakeProfit2计算加权均价加利润目标出场，或通过移动止损保护利润；最后一次加仓时才设置止损。仓位管理：初始手数由Lots或MaximumRisk决定，后续加仓手数按2的幂次（前DoubleCount次）和1.5的幂次（之后）递增，这是一种指数级增长的仓位策略。资金管理：仅基于可用保证金和风险比例计算初始手数，但加仓时手数增长远超风控原则，MaxCount为10时最大手数可达初始手数的数百倍。存在一个严重bug：CSigi_TrendRSISto类的第743行将m_buy重复赋值为卖出条件而非m_sell，导致该信号模式下买入信号永远被覆盖为卖出条件。

Summary

A multi-signal EA that opens initial positions based on one of six selectable signal types (MACD, Pivot, Support/Resistance, i_Trend+RSI, i_Trend+RSI+Stochastic, i_Trend+RSI+Stochastic+MFI), then employs an aggressive martingale-style scaling-in strategy. It adds positions in the same direction at fixed pip intervals with exponentially increasing lot sizes (2x for the first DoubleCount entries, then 1.5x), up to a configurable maximum count. A simple trailing stop is also available for profit protection.

Strategy Analysis

Strategy concept: The EA's core approach is scaling into positions in the signal direction using a martingale-style lot multiplication, averaging down the entry price and relying on market reversion for profit. Entry conditions: The first entry depends on the selected signal type (e.g., MACD rising for buy, falling for sell), and subsequent additions require price moving against the position by Pips points while the signal remains active. Exit conditions: TakeProfit2 calculates a volume-weighted average price plus profit target for exit, or a simple trailing stop protects profits; stoploss is only set on the final addition when MaxCount is reached. Position sizing: Initial lot size is determined by Lots or MaximumRisk, and subsequent additions multiply lots by powers of 2 (for the first DoubleCount entries) then by powers of 1.5, creating an exponentially growing position. Money management: While the initial lot uses free margin and a risk percentage, the martingale scaling far exceeds any prudent risk principle — with MaxCount=10, the maximum lot can reach hundreds of times the initial lot. There is a critical bug: in the CSigi_TrendRSISto class, line 743 assigns m_buy instead of m_sell for the sell condition, meaning in that signal mode the buy signal is always overwritten by the sell condition.

优势

  • 支持六种不同的信号模式，用户可根据市场环境灵活切换
  • 采用OOP设计模式（CTradeSignals基类+派生类），信号模块化且易于扩展新的信号类型
  • 加仓时使用加权均价计算止盈，逻辑上保证了整体仓位达到目标利润时才平仓
  • 包含止损止盈合法性检查（CheckBuySL/TP等），防止因止损太近被拒单
  • 手数规范化函数考虑了最小手数、步长和最大手数限制

劣势

  • 马丁格尔式加仓策略风险极高，逆势加仓可导致指数级亏损，10次加仓后手数为初始的数百倍
  • CSigi_TrendRSISto类存在严重bug：第743行将卖出条件错误赋值给m_buy而非m_sell，导致该模式下买入信号永远无法正确触发
  • 止损仅在最后一次加仓（Index+1==MaxCount）时才设置，中间加仓过程完全无止损保护
  • FindStartLots函数在没有找到历史入场交易时仍返回true但aLots可能不是正确值
  • 没有点差过滤和滑点保护机制，在点差扩大时仍可能开仓
  • 加仓逻辑仅检查当前是否持仓但不区分是否为本EA所开仓位，多EA并行时会冲突

改进建议

  • 修复CSigi_TrendRSISto类第743行的严重bug，将m_buy改为m_sell
  • 为每次加仓都设置止损，而不是仅在最后一次加仓时设置，以控制单笔加仓风险
  • 增加点差过滤和最大持仓风险检查，防止在极端行情下过度加仓
  • 增加EA魔术号（Magic Number）来区分本EA和其他EA的仓位，避免多EA冲突
  • 将马丁格尔倍增策略改为更温和的线性或对数增长方式，降低爆仓风险

Strengths

  • Supports six different signal modes, allowing users to flexibly switch based on market conditions
  • Uses OOP design pattern (CTradeSignals base class + derived classes), making signals modular and easy to extend with new signal types
  • Uses volume-weighted average price for take-profit calculation when scaling in, ensuring the overall position exits only when the profit target is met
  • Includes stop-loss/take-profit validity checks (CheckBuySL/TP etc.) to prevent order rejection due to stops being too close
  • The lot normalization function accounts for minimum lot, volume step, and maximum lot limits

Weaknesses

  • The martingale-style scaling strategy carries extreme risk — adding positions against the trend can lead to exponential losses, with lots reaching hundreds of times the initial size after 10 additions
  • Critical bug in CSigi_TrendRSISto class: line 743 assigns the sell condition to m_buy instead of m_sell, meaning buy signals are always overwritten by sell logic in that signal mode
  • Stop-loss is only set on the final addition (Index+1==MaxCount), leaving all intermediate scaling-in positions completely unprotected by any stop-loss
  • FindStartLots returns true even when no historical entry deal is found, potentially returning an incorrect aLots value
  • No spread filter or slippage protection mechanism — positions can be opened even when spreads are widened
  • The scaling-in logic only checks if a position exists but doesn't distinguish whether it was opened by this EA, causing conflicts when multiple EAs run on the same symbol

Improvements

  • Fix the critical bug in CSigi_TrendRSISto class line 743 by changing m_buy to m_sell
  • Set stop-loss for every addition rather than only the final one, to control per-entry risk
  • Add spread filtering and maximum position risk checks to prevent excessive scaling during extreme market conditions
  • Add an EA magic number to distinguish this EA's positions from others, avoiding multi-EA conflicts
  • Replace the martingale doubling with a more moderate linear or logarithmic lot growth scheme to reduce blow-up risk

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:DANGEROUS

风险点

  • 马丁格尔式手数倍增加仓策略，10次加仓后手数可达初始的约860倍，极端行情下可快速爆仓
  • 加仓过程中无止损保护，仅最后一次加仓设置止损，中间任何一笔都面临无限风险
  • MaxCount=-1时允许无限加仓，无上限的仓位增长是致命风险
  • 没有整体仓位风险限制或保证金检查，可能因保证金不足导致强平

Risk Assessment

Overall:DANGEROUS

Risk Points

  • Martingale-style lot multiplication scaling — after 10 additions the lot can reach ~860x the initial size, capable of blowing the account rapidly in extreme markets
  • No stop-loss protection during scaling-in process — only the final addition has a stop-loss, leaving all intermediate entries with unlimited risk
  • MaxCount=-1 allows unlimited additions, creating a fatally unbounded position growth scenario
  • No overall position risk limit or margin check, potentially leading to margin call forced liquidation
