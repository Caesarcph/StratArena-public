摘要

该EA基于日线支点突破信号交易，价格收于支点上方时开多单，收于下方时开空单。止损和止盈设置在支点支撑/阻力位，提供三个目标层级，并可在达到第一目标时将止损移至保本。

策略分析

策略核心基于经典支点计算，利用前一日最高价、最低价和收盘价计算支点及三层支撑阻力位。入场条件为价格穿越支点：前一根K线收盘价在支点一侧、当前K线收盘价穿越到另一侧即触发信号，本质上是一个支点突破策略。出场条件包括三层目标：第一目标为S1/R1，第二目标为S2/R2，第三目标为S3/R3，止损对应设在同层支撑/阻力位。仓位管理支持固定手数和基于可用保证金比例的动态手数两种模式，并引入亏损递减因子DcF，在连续亏损时逐步缩小仓位以控制风险。资金管理方面，MaxR参数控制最大风险占比，亏损递减机制防止连亏时过度暴露。此外还支持日内交易模式（23:00强制平仓）和第一目标达成后移止损至保本的功能。

Summary

This EA trades pivot point breakout signals, opening buy positions when price closes above the daily pivot and sell positions when it closes below. Stop loss and take profit are set at pivot support/resistance levels, with three target tiers and an optional breakeven feature when the first target is reached.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is based on classic pivot point calculation, deriving the pivot and three levels of support/resistance from the previous day's high, low, and close prices. Entry conditions rely on price crossing the pivot: a buy signal triggers when the previous bar closes below the pivot and the current bar closes above it, while a sell signal is the inverse — this is fundamentally a pivot breakout strategy. Exit conditions offer three target tiers: Target 1 uses S1/R1, Target 2 uses S2/R2, and Target 3 uses S3/R3, with stop loss placed at the corresponding support/resistance level. Position sizing supports both fixed lots and dynamic lots based on a percentage of free margin (MaxR), plus a loss reduction factor (DcF) that progressively decreases lot size after consecutive losses. Money management is reinforced by the MaxR parameter controlling maximum risk exposure and the DcF mechanism preventing overexposure during losing streaks. Additional features include an intraday-only mode that forces position closure at 23:00 and a ModSL option that moves the stop loss to breakeven once the first target is reached.

优势

  • 基于经典支点理论，策略逻辑清晰易懂，支撑阻力位有统计学依据
  • 具备亏损递减机制（DcF），连续亏损时自动缩小仓位，有助于控制回撤
  • 提供三档止盈目标选择和保本止损功能，交易者可根据风险偏好灵活配置
  • 设有最小止损/止盈距离检查，防止因止损过近被拒绝成交

劣势

  • 支点计算仅使用前一日数据，在波动剧烈的市场中支撑阻力位可能不够精准
  • 缺少滑点和点差过滤机制，在点差较大时支点突破信号可能产生虚假入场
  • 入场信号过于简单（仅收盘价穿越支点），没有成交量确认或其他过滤条件，容易产生频繁的假突破信号
  • 保本止损功能使用全局变量存储目标价，EA重启后可能丢失状态导致功能异常

改进建议

  • 增加多重确认条件，如加入成交量过滤、ATR波动率过滤或移动平均线趋势过滤，减少假突破信号
  • 增加点差过滤机制，当点差超过阈值时暂停开仓，避免在高点差环境下入场

Strengths

  • Based on classic pivot point theory with clear and understandable logic; support/resistance levels have statistical basis
  • Includes a loss reduction mechanism (DcF) that automatically decreases position size after consecutive losses, helping control drawdowns
  • Offers three target profit tiers and a breakeven stop loss feature, giving traders flexibility based on risk appetite
  • Implements minimum stop loss/take profit distance checks to prevent order rejection due to stops being too close

Weaknesses

  • Pivot calculation uses only the previous day's data, which may not provide precise support/resistance levels in highly volatile markets
  • Lacks spread and slippage filtering; pivot breakout signals can produce false entries when spreads are wide
  • Entry signals are overly simplistic (only close price crossing the pivot) without volume confirmation or additional filters, leading to frequent false breakouts
  • Breakeven stop loss feature relies on global variables to store target prices; EA restart may lose state and cause feature malfunction

Improvements

  • Add multiple confirmation conditions such as volume filtering, ATR volatility filtering, or moving average trend filtering to reduce false breakout signals
  • Add a spread filter mechanism that pauses new entries when the spread exceeds a configurable threshold, avoiding entries in high-spread environments
