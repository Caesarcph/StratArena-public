摘要

一个夜间剥头皮EA，仅在亚洲时段（服务器时间21:00-06:00）使用随机指标的超买/超卖信号在M15周期上进行交易。当随机指标低于超卖水平时开多仓，高于超买水平时开空仓，采用固定止损止盈和基于可用保证金的自动仓位计算。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是时段交易配合均值回归，仅在夜间时段（21:00至次日6:00）交易，利用亚洲市场波动较小的特点进行剥头皮操作。入场条件为：随机指标主线值低于30（超卖）时做多，高于70（超买）时做空，且仅在当前无持仓时下单。出场条件依赖固定止损40点（4位报价）或40点*10（5位报价）和止盈20点，没有移动止损或条件出场机制。仓位管理采用基于账户可用保证金的动态计算公式：可用保证金/100000*10，并限制在0.1到5手之间，同时根据手数大小进行不同精度的标准化处理。资金管理方面，止损和止盈根据经纪商报价位数（3/5位）自动乘以10进行调整，但没有考虑点差过滤、最大回撤限制或风险比例控制。整体策略简单直接，但在风险控制和信号过滤方面存在明显不足。

Summary

A night scalping EA that trades only during the Asian session (21:00-06:00 server time) using Stochastic oscillator overbought/oversold signals on the M15 timeframe. It opens buy positions when Stochastic is below the oversold level and sell positions when above the overbought level, with fixed stop loss and take profit, and auto-calculated lot sizing based on free margin.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy combines session trading with mean reversion, operating only during the Asian session (21:00-06:00 server time) to exploit the typically low volatility environment for scalping. Entry conditions are straightforward: buy when the Stochastic main line falls below 30 (oversold) and sell when it rises above 70 (overbought), with trades only placed when no existing position is held on the symbol. Exit conditions rely solely on fixed stop loss of 40 pips (or 400 points for 5-digit brokers) and take profit of 20 pips, with no trailing stop, partial close, or conditional exit mechanism. Position sizing uses a dynamic formula based on free margin: AccountFreeMargin/100000*10, clamped between 0.1 and 5 lots, with normalization adjusted by lot size tier. Money management adjusts SL/TP for 3/5-digit brokers but lacks spread filtering, maximum drawdown limits, or risk-per-trade percentage controls, making the approach simplistic and potentially dangerous under adverse market conditions.

优势

  • 夜间时段交易有效利用了亚洲市场低波动特性，适合剥头皮策略
  • 支持3/5位报价经纪商自动调整，兼容性较好
  • 有持仓检查避免重复开仓，防止同方向过度暴露
  • 仓位大小有上下限限制（0.1-5手），防止过度杠杆

劣势

  • 没有点差过滤，夜间低流动性时段点差可能很大，导致止盈无法覆盖交易成本
  • 止损大于止盈（40:20），盈亏比仅1:2，需要超过66%胜率才能盈利
  • 仅检查单线随机指标值，没有确认信号（如K线交叉、多周期确认），容易产生假信号
  • 仓位计算基于全部可用保证金而非风险比例，可能导致过度交易
  • 没有新K线检查，每个tick都会评估交易条件，可能频繁尝试下单

改进建议

  • 添加点差过滤器，当点差超过止盈的一定比例时禁止交易，避免在高点差环境下入场
  • 增加随机指标K线与D线交叉确认，而非仅依赖主线数值，提高信号可靠性
  • 添加新K线检查机制，只在M15新K线形成时评估交易条件，减少不必要的计算和订单尝试
  • 改进仓位管理为基于风险比例的计算方式（如每笔交易风险不超过账户余额的1-2%）
  • 添加移动止损或保本止损机制，在价格朝有利方向移动时锁定利润

Strengths

  • Night session trading effectively exploits the low volatility characteristic of Asian markets, suitable for scalping
  • Automatic 3/5-digit price adjustment ensures compatibility with various brokers
  • Position check prevents duplicate entries, avoiding overexposure in the same direction
  • Lot size is clamped between 0.1 and 5 lots, preventing excessive leverage

Weaknesses

  • No spread filter — low liquidity during night sessions can cause wide spreads, making the small TP insufficient to cover trading costs
  • Stop loss exceeds take profit (40:20), giving a risk-reward ratio of only 1:2, requiring over 66% win rate to be profitable
  • Only checks Stochastic value level without confirmation signals (crossover, multi-TF confirmation), prone to false signals
  • Position sizing based on total free margin rather than risk percentage, potentially leading to overleveraging
  • No new bar check means trade conditions are evaluated every tick, potentially generating excessive order attempts

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter to block trading when spread exceeds a proportion of the take profit, preventing entries during wide spread conditions
  • Add Stochastic K/D crossover confirmation instead of relying solely on the main line value, improving signal reliability
  • Implement new bar detection to evaluate trade conditions only on M15 bar formation, reducing unnecessary computation and order attempts
  • Refactor position sizing to risk-based calculation (e.g., risk no more than 1-2% of account balance per trade)
  • Add trailing stop or breakeven mechanism to lock in profits when price moves favorably
