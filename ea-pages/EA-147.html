摘要

这是一个夜间时段交易EA，利用随机指标识别超买超卖条件，仅在夜间时段（21:00-06:00）执行买入或卖出交易。它具有基于可用保证金动态计算手数的功能，以及固定止损止盈和5位数经纪商归一化处理。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念是基于夜间时段的均值回归思路，利用随机指标在超卖区域（低于30）产生买入信号，在超买区域（高于70）产生卖出信号。入场条件为随机指标前一根K线值穿越超卖/超买阈值，且当前时间处于21:00至06:00的夜间窗口。出场条件依赖固定止损（40点）和止盈（20点），止损大于止盈意味着盈亏比为1:0.5，需要较高胜率才能盈利。仓位管理采用动态手数计算，根据账户可用保证金除以100000再乘以10来确定，并限制在0.1到5手之间。资金管理方面存在缺陷，没有考虑最大回撤限制，且盈亏比不对称，止损是止盈的两倍，单笔亏损可能较大。此外，EA仅在夜间交易，试图利用夜间市场波动较小、均值回归概率较高的特性。

Summary

A night-session trading EA that uses the Stochastic oscillator to identify overbought and oversold conditions, placing buy or sell trades only during a defined night window (21:00–06:00). It features dynamic lot sizing based on free margin and fixed stop-loss/take-profit levels with 5-digit broker normalization.

Strategy Analysis

The EA's strategy concept is a mean-reversion approach applied during the night session, using the Stochastic oscillator to generate buy signals when it falls below the oversold level (30) and sell signals when it rises above the overbought level (70). Entry conditions require the Stochastic value of the previous bar to cross the oversold/overbought threshold, and the current hour must be within the 21:00-06:00 night window. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop-loss (40 pips) and take-profit (20 pips), meaning the risk-reward ratio is 1:0.5, which requires a high win rate to be profitable. Position sizing uses dynamic lot calculation based on free margin divided by 100,000 multiplied by 10, clamped between 0.1 and 5 lots. Money management has flaws — there is no maximum drawdown limit, and the asymmetric risk-reward ratio means the stop-loss is double the take-profit, so each losing trade erases two winning trades. The EA restricts trading to the night session, attempting to exploit the tendency of lower volatility and higher mean-reversion probability during off-hours.

优势

  • 夜间时段过滤减少了低流动性假信号的风险
  • 动态手数计算根据账户规模自适应调整仓位
  • 5位数经纪商自动适配，兼容性较好
  • 使用随机指标超买超卖逻辑，概念清晰易懂

劣势

  • 止损是止盈的两倍，盈亏比1:0.5极不合理，需要超过66%胜率才能盈利
  • 输入参数Lot完全被动态计算覆盖，造成用户困惑
  • 没有新K线检查，同一根K线上可能重复发单
  • 缺少点差过滤，夜间时段点差可能扩大导致不利成交
  • PositionSelect只能管理单品种一个持仓，多品种或多仓位场景无法处理

改进建议

  • 增加新K线检测机制，避免同一根K线重复开仓
  • 调整止损止盈比例至少为1:1或更高，或引入追踪止损改善盈亏比
  • 添加点差过滤，当点差超过阈值时跳过交易信号
  • 使Lot输入参数生效，提供固定手数和动态手数两种模式选择

Strengths

  • Night session filter reduces the risk of false signals during low liquidity periods
  • Dynamic lot sizing adapts position size to account equity automatically
  • Automatic 5-digit broker normalization improves compatibility across brokers
  • Stochastic overbought/oversold logic is clear and conceptually straightforward

Weaknesses

  • Stop-loss is double the take-profit, yielding a 1:0.5 risk-reward ratio that requires over 66% win rate to break even
  • The Lot input parameter is completely overridden by dynamic calculation, causing user confusion
  • No new bar check means the EA could send duplicate orders on the same bar
  • No spread filter — night session spreads often widen, leading to unfavorable fills
  • PositionSelect only manages a single position per symbol, unable to handle multi-position or multi-symbol scenarios

Improvements

  • Add a new bar detection mechanism to prevent duplicate order submissions on the same bar
  • Adjust stop-loss to take-profit ratio to at least 1:1 or higher, or introduce a trailing stop to improve the risk-reward ratio
  • Add a spread filter to skip trade signals when the spread exceeds a configurable threshold
  • Make the Lot input parameter functional by offering both fixed lot and dynamic lot sizing modes
