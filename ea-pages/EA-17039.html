摘要

这是一个剥头皮EA，每笔交易目标仅为10点利润。它基于短期动量信号开仓，并在达到 modest 止盈目标后迅速平仓，依靠高胜率频繁交易来积累利润。

策略分析

策略概念是典型的剥头皮交易，追求每笔交易仅10点的微小利润，通过高频交易积累收益。入场条件基于短期动量或价格突破，在价格出现短暂方向性移动时快速进场。出场条件采用固定止盈（10点）和止损的组合，止盈距离非常小，期望通过高胜率弥补风险。仓位管理方面，代码中可能使用固定手数或基于资金比例的简单仓位计算，缺乏动态调整机制。资金管理较为简单，未发现复杂的资金曲线调整或风险比例控制逻辑，存在连续亏损时风险放大的隐患。整体策略依赖市场在短时间内的微小波动，在低流动性和高点差环境下表现可能不佳。

Summary

This is a scalping EA that aims for small pip targets (10 pips) per trade. It opens positions based on short-term momentum signals and closes them quickly once the modest take-profit is reached, relying on high win-rate frequency to accumulate profits.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is classic scalping, targeting a mere 10 pips per trade and accumulating profits through high-frequency trading. Entry conditions are based on short-term momentum or brief price breakouts, quickly entering when a directional move is detected. Exit conditions use a fixed take-profit (10 pips) paired with a stop-loss, with the very small TP distance relying on a high win rate to offset losses. Position sizing likely uses fixed lots or a simple account-balance ratio without dynamic adjustment. Money management is relatively simplistic, lacking equity curve adjustments or proportional risk control, which poses a risk of amplified drawdown during consecutive losses. The overall strategy depends on capturing micro price movements and may underperform in low-liquidity or high-spread environments.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和调整参数
  • 固定小目标止盈在高波动市场容易快速成交
  • 剥头皮策略在趋势明确时胜率较高
  • 代码结构简单，适合初学者学习EA开发

劣势

  • 10点止盈在很多货币对上甚至不够覆盖点差成本
  • 缺乏点差过滤，高点差时段交易会严重侵蚀利润
  • 未发现新K线检查机制，可能同一根K线重复开仓
  • 仓位管理缺乏动态调整，无法根据市场波动自适应
  • 剥头皮策略对经纪商执行质量高度敏感，滑点影响大

改进建议

  • 添加点差过滤器，当点差超过阈值时禁止开仓
  • 增加新K线检查，防止同一根K线重复进场
  • 引入ATR动态调整止盈止损距离，适应市场波动变化

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and adjust parameters
  • Fixed small TP target is easily hit in volatile markets for quick exits
  • Scalping strategy can achieve high win rates during clear directional moves
  • Straightforward code structure suitable for beginners learning EA development

Weaknesses

  • 10-pip TP is often insufficient to cover spread costs on many currency pairs
  • No spread filter means trading during high-spread periods severely erodes profit
  • No new bar check detected, risk of repeated entries on the same bar
  • Position sizing lacks dynamic adjustment and cannot adapt to market volatility
  • Scalping is highly sensitive to broker execution quality; slippage impact is significant

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter to prohibit trading when spread exceeds a threshold
  • Implement new bar checking to prevent duplicate entries on the same candle
  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic TP/SL to adapt to changing market volatility
