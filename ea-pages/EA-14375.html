摘要

该EA基于LeManTrendHist自定义指标进行交易，该指标是一个使用多重平滑移动平均的趋势跟踪直方图指标。当直方图上升时开多仓，下降时开空仓，并支持对多空方向的入场和出场进行独立配置。

策略分析

该EA使用LeManTrendHist直方图指标判断趋势方向，指标基于三条不同周期（Min=13, Midle=21, Max=34）的平滑均线计算得出。入场条件为：当Value[1]<Value[2]（直方图上升）且Value[0]>Value[1]（持续上升）时开多仓；当Value[1]>Value[2]（直方图下降）且Value[0]<Value[1]（持续下降）时开空仓。出场条件为：趋势反转时分别平多仓或平空仓，即直方图方向变化时触发对应方向的平仓信号。仓位管理支持五种模式，包括固定手数、按余额比例、按可用保证金比例等，通过MM和MMMode参数控制。止损和止盈分别默认设为1000点和2000点，提供了基本的风险控制机制。EA使用IsNewBar机制确保每个K线只在首次跳价时计算信号，避免重复交易。

Summary

This EA trades based on the LeManTrendHist custom indicator, which is a trend-following histogram indicator using multiple smoothed moving averages. It opens buy positions when the histogram rises and sell positions when the histogram falls, with configurable options for entry and exit on each side.

Strategy Analysis

The EA uses the LeManTrendHist histogram indicator to determine trend direction, which is calculated from three smoothed moving averages with different periods (Min=13, Midle=21, Max=34). Entry conditions: a buy position opens when Value[1]<Value[2] (histogram rising) and Value[0]>Value[1] (continued rise); a sell position opens when Value[1]>Value[2] (histogram falling) and Value[0]<Value[1] (continued fall). Exit conditions: positions close on trend reversal, meaning a histogram direction change triggers the corresponding close signal for the opposite direction. Position sizing supports five margin modes including fixed lot, balance percentage, and free margin percentage, controlled by the MM and MMMode parameters. Stop loss and take profit default to 1000 and 2000 points respectively, providing basic risk control. The EA uses the IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are calculated only on the first tick of each new bar, preventing repeated trades.

优势

  • 支持多种仓位管理模式，灵活性高
  • 多空方向的入场和出场可独立控制，便于精细调优
  • 使用IsNewBar机制避免同根K线重复交易
  • 止损止盈参数齐全，基本风险控制到位
  • 指标平滑算法可选（JJMA/JurX/ParMA/T3/VIDYA/AMA等），适应性强

劣势

  • 缺少点差过滤，在点差较大时可能产生不利入场
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值过大，可能不适合所有品种
  • 没有追踪止损功能，无法锁定浮动利润
  • 指标依赖iCustom调用外部自定义指标，部署时需确保指标文件存在
  • 没有最大持仓或最大回撤限制，资金管理不够完善

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤，避免在点差过大时开仓
  • 增加追踪止损功能，在趋势延续时保护利润
  • 增加最大回撤和最大持仓数量限制

Strengths

  • Supports multiple position sizing modes with high flexibility
  • Buy/sell entry and exit can be independently controlled for fine-tuning
  • Uses IsNewBar mechanism to prevent repeated trades on the same bar
  • Complete stop loss and take profit parameters for basic risk control
  • Selectable smoothing algorithms (JJMA, JurX, ParMA, T3, VIDYA, AMA etc.) for adaptability

Weaknesses

  • No spread filter, may enter at unfavorable prices during high spread
  • Default SL 1000 and TP 2000 points are too large for some instruments
  • No trailing stop feature to lock in floating profits
  • Depends on iCustom calling an external custom indicator, requiring the indicator file at deployment
  • No maximum position or drawdown limit, money management is incomplete

Improvements

  • Add spread filter to avoid opening positions during high spread
  • Add trailing stop functionality to protect profits during trend continuation
  • Add maximum drawdown and maximum position count limits
