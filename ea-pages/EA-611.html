摘要

该EA基于MACD柱状图零轴穿越和信号线背离模式进行交易。它采用与账户权益增长挂钩的动态仓位模型，相对初始存款计算手数。在新K线出现时检测到动量转变后开仓，支持可选的止盈止损。

策略分析

策略概念基于MACD指标的动量反转交易，通过检测MACD柱状图穿越零轴来判断趋势方向切换，同时结合信号线的极值点（信号线拐头）作为辅助入场信号。入场条件有两个：一是MACD柱状图从正转负（做空信号）或从负转正（做多信号），二是当MACD柱状图保持同符号但信号线出现拐头时也触发信号。出场条件仅依赖固定的TP止盈点数和SL止损点数，SL默认为0表示不设止损。仓位管理采用反向加仓逻辑——如果已有同方向持仓则减量开仓，反方向则增量反转仓位。资金管理基于账户累计盈亏动态调整手数，公式为Risk×Money/Deposit，手数会在最小手数和最大手数限制之间规范化。策略仅在每根新K线时计算信号，避免重复交易，但存在SL=0时无止损保护的显著风险。

Summary

This EA trades based on MACD histogram zero-crossings and signal line divergence patterns. It uses a dynamic lot sizing model tied to account equity growth relative to an initial deposit. Positions are opened on new bars when momentum shifts are detected, with optional take-profit and stop-loss levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on MACD momentum reversal trading, detecting MACD histogram zero-line crossovers to identify trend direction shifts, supplemented by signal line extremum points (signal line turning) as auxiliary entry signals. Entry conditions are twofold: first, MACD histogram crossing from positive to negative (sell signal) or negative to positive (buy signal); second, when the MACD histogram maintains the same sign but the signal line shows a turning point. Exit conditions rely solely on fixed TP take-profit points and SL stop-loss points, with SL defaulting to 0 meaning no stop-loss protection. Position management uses a reversal logic — if a same-direction position already exists, the lot size is reduced; if opposite, the lot size is increased to reverse the position. Money management dynamically adjusts lot size based on cumulative account profit/loss using the formula Risk×Money/Deposit, with lots normalized between minimum and maximum volume limits. The strategy only calculates signals on each new bar to avoid repeated trading, but the default SL=0 creates significant risk exposure with no stop-loss protection.

优势

  • 动态资金管理根据账户盈亏自动调整仓位，盈利时加大仓位、亏损时缩小仓位
  • 双重入场信号（零轴穿越+信号线拐头）提高了入场时机的可靠性
  • 新K线判断机制避免了同一根K线内重复计算信号和频繁交易

劣势

  • SL默认为0，没有止损保护，单笔交易可能承受无限亏损
  • 反向加仓逻辑在反转时可能大幅增加仓位，放大风险敞口
  • MACD参数(30/500/36)周期过长，信号滞后严重，可能错过最佳入场时机
  • 没有持仓时间限制或跟踪止损，盈利可能大幅回撤

改进建议

  • 增加强制止损逻辑，即使SL=0也应设置基于ATR或固定百分比的兜底止损
  • 增加最大持仓时间或跟踪止损机制，保护已有浮盈不被大幅回撤吞噬

Strengths

  • Dynamic money management automatically adjusts position size based on account equity, increasing lots on profit and decreasing on loss
  • Dual entry signals (zero-line crossover plus signal line turning point) improve entry timing reliability
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents redundant signal calculation and over-trading within the same bar

Weaknesses

  • SL defaults to 0 with no stop-loss protection, exposing trades to unlimited potential loss on a single position
  • The reversal position logic can significantly increase lot size when reversing direction, amplifying risk exposure
  • MACD parameters (30/500/36) are extremely long-period, causing severe signal lag and potentially missing optimal entry timing
  • No maximum holding time or trailing stop, meaning profits can experience significant drawdowns before TP is hit

Improvements

  • Add mandatory stop-loss logic — even when SL=0, a fallback stop based on ATR or a fixed percentage should be implemented
  • Add maximum holding time or trailing stop mechanism to protect floating profits from significant drawdowns
