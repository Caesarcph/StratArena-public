摘要

该EA使用自定义的ZigZag斐波那契指标识别关键斐波那契价位，在K线形态确认突破后入场交易。持仓期间采用类抛物线SAR追踪止损系统，随趋势发展动态调整止损位。

策略分析

策略概念基于ZigZag斐波那契回撤水平识别支撑阻力位，当价格突破某个斐波那契水平后等待确认再入场。入场条件要求：价格突破斐波那契水平、当前K线方向与交易方向一致、前一根K线收盘价在突破水平另一侧、最新K线收盘价回到突破水平的交易方向一侧。出场条件依赖类抛物线SAR追踪止损系统，该系统使用加速因子（AF）逐步收紧止损，加速因子从AFStep开始每次新bar增加，上限为AFCap。仓位管理方面，固定手数为1手，没有动态仓位调整机制。资金管理仅做了保证金检查（CheckMargin函数），确保账户有足够保证金才下单，但缺少最大风险敞口控制和亏损保护机制。

Summary

This EA uses a custom ZigZag Fibonacci indicator to identify key Fibo price levels, then enters trades on breakouts confirmed by candlestick patterns. It manages open positions with a parabolic SAR-style trailing stop loss system, dynamically adjusting the stop as the trend progresses.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on identifying support and resistance levels via ZigZag Fibonacci retracement levels, entering trades after breakout confirmation by candlestick patterns. Entry conditions require: price breaking through a Fibonacci level, the current bar direction aligning with the trade direction, the previous bar closing on the opposite side of the broken level, and the latest bar closing on the trade-direction side of the level. Exit conditions rely on a parabolic SAR-style trailing stop system, which uses an acceleration factor (AF) that starts at AFStep and increments each new bar up to AFCap, progressively tightening the stop loss. Position sizing is fixed at 1 lot with no dynamic lot calculation based on account equity or risk percentage. Money management only includes a margin check via the CheckMargin function to ensure sufficient free margin before opening a position, but lacks max drawdown controls, equity protection, or risk-based position sizing.

优势

  • 结合斐波那契水平与K线形态确认突破，入场逻辑较为严谨，减少假突破风险
  • 采用类抛物线SAR追踪止损，能随趋势发展动态保护利润，让利润奔跑
  • 每笔交易都有止损设置，不存在无止损的裸露仓位
  • 入场前执行保证金检查，避免因保证金不足导致订单失败

劣势

  • 固定1手交易没有动态仓位管理，无法根据账户规模或风险承受能力调整仓位
  • 没有获利目标（Take Profit），完全依赖追踪止损出场，可能在震荡行情中频繁止损
  • 缺少点差过滤和滑点保护，在市场流动性差时可能导致不利成交
  • OnTrade函数中仅检测止损平仓来更新CloseBarTime，但没有处理其他退出情况如手动平仓
  • 指标数据复制失败时仅打印警告但继续执行，可能导致使用过期或无效数据做出交易决策

改进建议

  • 增加基于账户净值和风险百分比的动态仓位管理，替代固定1手交易
  • 添加点差过滤和最大滑点限制，避免在高点差时段不利成交
  • 增加对指标数据复制失败的严格检查，失败时跳过当次交易逻辑而非继续执行
  • 添加最大回撤保护和每日亏损限制，增强资金安全
  • 优化OnTrade函数处理所有类型的平仓事件，包括手动平仓和部分平仓

Strengths

  • Combines Fibonacci level breakout with candlestick pattern confirmation for more reliable entries, reducing false breakout risk
  • Employs a parabolic SAR-style trailing stop that dynamically protects profits as the trend develops, allowing winners to run
  • Every trade has a stop loss set at entry, ensuring no naked positions without risk protection
  • Performs a margin check before order placement, avoiding failed orders due to insufficient margin

Weaknesses

  • Fixed 1-lot position sizing with no dynamic lot calculation, unable to adjust position based on account size or risk tolerance
  • No take profit target; relies entirely on trailing stop for exit, which may cause frequent stop-outs in choppy/ranging markets
  • Lacks spread filter and slippage protection, potentially leading to unfavorable fills during low liquidity periods
  • OnTrade function only detects stop-loss closures to update CloseBarTime but does not handle other exit scenarios like manual closes
  • Indicator buffer copy failures only print warnings but execution continues, potentially using stale or invalid data for trading decisions

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account equity and risk percentage instead of fixed 1-lot trading
  • Add spread filter and maximum slippage limits to avoid unfavorable fills during high-spread periods
  • Add strict checks for indicator buffer copy failures, skipping trading logic when data is unavailable rather than continuing
  • Add maximum drawdown protection and daily loss limits to enhance capital safety
  • Improve OnTrade function to handle all types of position closure events, including manual and partial closes
