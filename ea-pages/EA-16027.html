摘要

该EA基于自定义ZigZag+Fibonacci指标进行斐波那契水平突破交易。当价格突破Fibo水平并以趋势K线形态确认后入场，随后使用抛物线SAR式移动止损系统管理出场。

策略分析

策略概念基于ZigZag指标识别的斐波那契回撤水平突破交易。入场条件为：价格突破某个Fibo水平超过Breakout点数，等待下一根K线收盘确认，且该K线为与交易方向一致的趋势K线（收盘价高于开盘价做多，反之做空），同时前一根K线收盘价在Fibo水平的另一侧。出场条件完全依赖Parabolic SAR式移动止损系统，随着价格有利移动，止损逐步跟进，没有固定止盈。仓位管理采用固定1手交易，没有任何动态仓位调整或加仓逻辑。资金管理方面仅调用CheckMargin函数检查可用保证金是否充足，但未做任何比例仓位计算。整体策略逻辑清晰，但缺乏风控多样性，出场机制单一，且固定手数忽略了账户规模变化。

Summary

This EA trades Fibonacci level breakouts based on a custom ZigZag+Fibonacci indicator. It enters positions when price breaks a Fibo level and confirms with a trend bar pattern, then manages exits using a Parabolic SAR-style trailing stop system.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on trading breakouts of Fibonacci retracement levels identified by a custom ZigZag+Fibonacci indicator. Entry conditions require price to break a Fibo level by more than the Breakout parameter in points, then wait for the next bar to close as a confirmation trend bar (close above open for buys, opposite for sells), with the previous bar having closed beyond the level and the confirming bar closing on the trade direction side of the level. Exit conditions rely entirely on a Parabolic SAR-style trailing stop system that incrementally moves the stop loss as the position moves favorably, with no fixed take profit target. Position sizing is hardcoded to 1 lot with no dynamic lot calculation or scaling logic. Money management only consists of a CheckMargin function that verifies sufficient free margin before entry, but does not perform any proportional sizing based on account equity or risk percentage. The overall strategy logic is coherent but lacks diversity in risk controls, relies on a single exit mechanism, and the fixed lot size ignores account size variability.

优势

  • 结合ZigZag与斐波那契水平的突破策略有明确的入场逻辑，技术依据充分
  • 使用Parabolic SAR式移动止损系统能有效锁定利润，让利润奔跑
  • 入场确认要求趋势K线形态过滤假突破，降低错误入场概率
  • 等待新K线收盘确认机制避免了盘中噪音干扰

劣势

  • 固定1手交易不根据账户规模调整，资金管理粗糙，大账户风险过小、小账户风险过大
  • 没有固定止盈目标，完全依赖移动止损出场，在震荡行情中可能频繁被止损
  • Parabolic SAR参数AFStep和AFCap默认值偏保守，可能导致止损跟进过慢错过利润保护窗口
  • 缺少点差过滤和滑点保护，在高点差环境下突破交易容易亏损
  • OnTrade中检测止损平仓的逻辑仅通过注释字符串匹配'sl'，不够可靠

改进建议

  • 增加基于账户净值比例的动态仓位计算（如风险1-2%的开仓手数），替代固定1手
  • 添加点差过滤条件，当点差超过阈值时不入场，避免高点差环境下的不利成交
  • 增加固定止盈选项作为移动止损的补充，提供更灵活的出场策略
  • 改进止损检测逻辑，使用DEAL_REASON_SL而非字符串匹配来识别止损平仓事件

Strengths

  • Combining ZigZag with Fibonacci level breakouts provides a well-grounded entry logic with solid technical basis
  • The Parabolic SAR-style trailing stop system effectively locks in profits while allowing winners to run
  • Entry confirmation requiring a trend bar candle pattern filters out false breakouts and reduces erroneous entries
  • Waiting for a new bar close confirmation mechanism avoids intrabar noise interference

Weaknesses

  • Fixed 1 lot trading without account-size-based adjustment makes money management crude — oversized risk for small accounts, undersized for large accounts
  • No fixed take profit target means exit relies entirely on the trailing stop, which may trigger frequently in choppy markets
  • The default Parabolic SAR parameters (AFStep=0.01, AFCap=0.4) are conservative, potentially causing the trailing stop to lag and miss profit protection windows
  • Lacks spread filter and slippage protection, making breakout trades vulnerable to loss in high-spread environments
  • The stop-loss detection in OnTrade relies on matching the 'sl' substring in deal comments, which is fragile and unreliable

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account equity percentage (e.g., 1-2% risk per trade) to replace the fixed 1 lot size
  • Add a spread filter condition that prevents entry when spread exceeds a threshold, avoiding unfavorable fills in high-spread conditions
  • Add a fixed take profit option as a complement to the trailing stop for a more flexible exit strategy
  • Improve stop-loss detection logic by using DEAL_REASON_SL instead of substring matching in deal comments to identify stop-out events

风险点

  • 固定1手仓位不考虑账户规模，小资金账户可能面临过高风险
  • 没有最大回撤控制或每日亏损限制机制
  • 止损检测依赖注释字符串匹配，可能漏检导致交易逻辑异常
  • 缺少点差和滑点保护，突破策略在高点差品种上风险较大

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed 1 lot position sizing regardless of account size poses excessive risk for small accounts
  • No maximum drawdown control or daily loss limit mechanism exists
  • Stop-loss detection relies on comment string matching which may miss events and cause logic anomalies
  • Lack of spread and slippage protection makes the breakout strategy risky on high-spread instruments
