摘要

该EA使用单一EMA指标结合时间过滤条件进行动量反转交易。买入条件为价格低于下降中的EMA且低于前一根K线收盘价并在新K线开启指定分钟时触发，卖出反之。采用固定手数，无止损止盈，新信号会反向平仓后开仓。

策略分析

策略概念基于EMA动量反转：当价格低于当前EMA且EMA在下降（MA_current<MA_previous），同时价格低于前一根K线收盘价时，认为下跌动量确立，在K线开启后第N分钟买入做空头反转；卖出条件对称。入场条件严格依赖三个条件同时满足：价格与EMA的位置关系、EMA方向、价格与前一根收盘价的关系，加上时间窗口过滤（InpTimeBarOpen）限制信号只在K线开启后特定分钟触发以避免重复交易。出场条件仅依靠反向信号触发平仓，没有独立的止损或止盈逻辑，这意味着仓位可能长时间持有并承受大幅浮亏。仓位管理采用固定手数（InpLots），没有任何基于账户余额或风险比例的动态仓位调整。资金管理方面，使用CheckVolume检查保证金是否充足，但没有最大持仓限制、回撤控制或任何资金保护机制。整体策略逻辑简单，风险控制严重不足。

Summary

This EA trades momentum reversals using a single EMA indicator combined with a time-based candle filter. It opens buy positions when the price is below a falling EMA and below the previous candle close at a specific minute after bar opening, and vice versa for sells. Positions are reversed—existing opposite positions are closed before opening new ones—with fixed lot sizing and no stop loss or take profit.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on EMA momentum reversal: a buy signal triggers when the price is below the current EMA, the EMA is declining (MA_current<MA_previous), and the price is below the previous candle's close, interpreted as an oversold momentum reversal opportunity; sell conditions are the exact mirror. Entry conditions require three simultaneous criteria—price relative to EMA, EMA direction, and price relative to prior close—plus a time window filter (InpTimeBarOpen) that restricts signals to a specific minute after bar opening to prevent duplicate trades within the same candle. Exit conditions rely solely on reverse signals closing existing positions, with no independent stop loss or take profit logic, meaning positions can endure prolonged and significant drawdowns. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (InpLots) with no dynamic adjustment based on account equity, risk percentage, or volatility. Money management includes a margin sufficiency check via CheckVolume before order placement, but lacks maximum exposure limits, drawdown controls, equity protection, or any portfolio-level risk safeguards. The overall strategy is logically simple but critically deficient in risk management.

优势

  • 代码结构清晰，使用MQL5标准交易类库（CTrade/CPositionInfo/CSymbolInfo），封装良好
  • 有时间窗口过滤机制（InpTimeBarOpen），避免同一根K线重复开仓
  • 开仓前检查保证金充足性（CheckVolume），避免因资金不足导致订单失败

劣势

  • 完全没有止损和止盈，仓位可能承受无限亏损
  • 固定手数没有动态仓位管理，不适应不同账户规模和波动环境
  • 仅依赖单一EMA指标和前一根K线收盘价，信号过于简单且容易产生虚假信号
  • 没有新K线检查（new bar check），时间窗口过滤不如标准新K线检测可靠
  • 没有点差过滤，在大幅点差时仍可能开仓导致不利成交

改进建议

  • 添加止损和止盈逻辑，建议基于ATR动态设置止损距离
  • 增加新K线检测机制替代时间窗口过滤，确保每根K线只交易一次
  • 加入点差过滤和波动率过滤，避免不利市场条件下开仓

Strengths

  • Clean code structure using MQL5 standard trade libraries (CTrade/CPositionInfo/CSymbolInfo) with proper encapsulation
  • Time window filter (InpTimeBarOpen) prevents duplicate entries within the same candle
  • Pre-trade margin check (CheckVolume) prevents order failures due to insufficient funds

Weaknesses

  • No stop loss or take profit whatsoever—positions can suffer unlimited drawdown
  • Fixed lot sizing with no dynamic position management, unsuitable for varying account sizes and volatility regimes
  • Relies on only a single EMA and previous candle close, producing overly simplistic signals prone to false entries
  • No proper new bar check mechanism—the minute-based filter is less reliable than standard new bar detection
  • No spread filter—trades can open during wide spreads resulting in unfavorable fills

Improvements

  • Add stop loss and take profit logic, ideally using ATR-based dynamic stop distances
  • Replace the minute-based time filter with a proper new bar detection mechanism to ensure one trade per candle
  • Add spread filter and volatility filter to avoid opening positions under unfavorable market conditions

  • 没有新K线检测机制，时间窗口过滤可能失效导致同一K线多次开仓

  • No new bar check mechanism; the minute-based filter may fail and allow multiple entries per candle
  • No spread filter; entries during widened spreads result in slippage losses
  • Fixed lot size ignores account risk capacity, large lots can lead to margin call
