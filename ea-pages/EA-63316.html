摘要

该EA是一个风险计算工具，帮助交易者根据账户净值、风险百分比和止损距离来确定合适的仓位大小。它本身不执行任何开仓、平仓或管理交易的操作，而是提供计算出的手数建议以辅助手动交易决策。该工具作为资金管理辅助工具，用于规范仓位管理。

策略分析

该EA本身不包含交易策略，它是一个纯粹的风险管理计算工具。其核心功能是根据用户设定的风险比例（占账户净值的百分比）和止损点数，自动计算出建议的开仓手数。入场条件和出场条件完全由用户自行决定，该EA不提供任何信号或交易触发机制。仓位管理方面，它通过公式将风险金额除以每点价值来得出合理的交易量，确保每笔交易的风险暴露在可控范围内。资金管理是其唯一的功能重点，强调固定比例风险模型，有助于交易者保持一致性并避免过度交易。该工具不涉及任何技术指标、信号生成或自动化执行逻辑。

Summary

This EA is a risk calculation utility that helps traders determine appropriate position sizes based on their account equity, risk percentage, and stop-loss distance. It does not open, close, or manage any trades on its own; instead, it provides computed lot size recommendations to assist with manual trading decisions. The tool serves as a money management aid for disciplined position sizing.

Strategy Analysis

This EA does not contain a trading strategy itself; it is a pure risk management calculation utility. Its core function is to automatically compute a suggested lot size based on the user-defined risk percentage (of account equity) and stop-loss distance in points. Entry and exit conditions are entirely left to the user's discretion, as the EA provides no signals or trade triggers whatsoever. In terms of position sizing, it uses a formula that divides the risked dollar amount by the per-point value to derive a reasonable trade volume, ensuring each trade's risk exposure stays within controllable bounds. Money management is its sole functional focus, emphasizing a fixed-fractional risk model that helps traders maintain consistency and avoid over-leveraging. The tool does not involve any technical indicators, signal generation, or automated execution logic.

优势

  • 帮助交易者严格执行固定比例风险管理，避免情绪化过度下单
  • 计算逻辑简单透明，易于理解和使用，降低了仓位管理的人为错误
  • 不涉及自动交易，完全由用户掌控交易决策，安全性高

劣势

  • 功能单一，仅提供手数计算，缺少其他风险管理工具如盈亏比计算或账户整体风险概览
  • 未考虑点差和滑点对实际风险的影响，计算结果可能与实际执行存在偏差
  • 没有提供多品种或跨货币对的风险汇总，无法评估账户整体敞口

改进建议

  • 增加盈亏比计算功能，帮助交易者在入场前评估潜在收益与风险的比值
  • 添加账户整体风险概览面板，显示所有持仓的总风险敞口和相关性

Strengths

  • Helps traders strictly enforce fixed-fractional risk management, avoiding emotional over-positioning
  • Simple and transparent calculation logic that is easy to understand and use, reducing human error in position sizing
  • No automated trading involved; the user retains full control over trading decisions, making it very safe

Weaknesses

  • Single-function utility that only provides lot size calculation, lacking other risk management tools such as risk-reward ratio calculation or account-level risk overview
  • Does not account for spread and slippage impact on actual risk, so calculated results may deviate from real execution
  • No multi-symbol or cross-pair risk aggregation, unable to evaluate overall account exposure

Improvements

  • Add risk-reward ratio calculation to help traders evaluate potential return versus risk before entering a trade
  • Add an account-level risk overview panel displaying total risk exposure and correlation across all open positions
