摘要

该EA基于MACD背离模式在所有交易品种上进行交易，通过识别价格与MACD指标之间的看涨和看跌背离来生成交易信号。当确认出现背离模式时开仓，并使用固定手数和止损/止盈管理交易。该策略是一个完全系统化的基于经典MACD背离检测的动量反转方法。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于MACD背离模式识别趋势反转点。入场条件为：当价格创新低但MACD指标未创新低时形成看涨背离，产生买入信号；当价格创新高但MACD指标未创新高时形成看跌背离，产生卖出信号。出场条件依赖预设的止损和止盈水平，也可能在反向背离信号出现时平仓。仓位管理采用固定0.01手数，不考虑账户余额或波动率动态调整。资金管理方面较为简单，没有复合仓位计算或风险比例控制机制。策略优势在于背离是经典的反转信号，但缺乏过滤机制可能导致在强趋势中频繁逆势交易。

Summary

This EA trades MACD divergence patterns across all symbols, identifying bullish and bearish divergences between price and the MACD indicator to generate trade signals. It enters positions when a confirmed divergence pattern is detected and manages trades with fixed lot sizing and stop-loss/take-profit levels. The strategy is a fully systematic momentum-reversal approach based on classic MACD divergence detection.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on MACD divergence pattern recognition to identify trend reversal points. Entry conditions: a bullish divergence forms when price makes a new low but MACD does not, generating a buy signal; a bearish divergence forms when price makes a new high but MACD does not, generating a sell signal. Exit conditions rely on preset stop-loss and take-profit levels, and positions may also close when an opposing divergence signal appears. Position sizing uses a fixed 0.01 lot size without dynamic adjustment based on account balance or volatility. Money management is simplistic, lacking compound position calculation or proportional risk control mechanisms. The strategy's strength lies in divergence being a classic reversal signal, but the lack of filtering mechanisms may cause frequent counter-trend trades during strong trends.

优势

  • MACD背离是经过验证的经典反转交易信号，具有理论依据
  • 固定0.01小手数降低了单笔交易风险，适合测试和小资金账户
  • 支持所有交易品种，通用性强，可在多个市场应用
  • 策略逻辑清晰，背离模式易于理解和验证
  • 全自动系统化交易，无需人工干预

劣势

  • 固定手数不考虑账户规模和波动率，资金管理不够灵活
  • 缺乏趋势过滤器，可能在强趋势中频繁产生逆势背离信号导致连续亏损
  • 背离判断可能因参数设置不同而产生不同结果，存在主观性
  • 没有加仓或减仓机制，无法在有利时扩大盈利
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时段过滤，可能在不利条件下开仓

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤器（如大周期均线方向），避免在强趋势中逆势交易
  • 实现动态仓位管理，根据账户余额和ATR波动率计算风险比例手数
  • 加入点差过滤和交易时段限制，避免在高点差和低流动性时段交易

Strengths

  • MACD divergence is a proven classic reversal trading signal with solid theoretical basis
  • Fixed 0.01 small lot size reduces per-trade risk, suitable for testing and small accounts
  • Supports all trading symbols with strong versatility across multiple markets
  • Clear strategy logic with divergence patterns that are easy to understand and verify
  • Fully automated systematic trading without manual intervention required

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot size does not account for account size or volatility, making money management inflexible
  • Lacks trend filter, may generate frequent counter-trend divergence signals during strong trends leading to consecutive losses
  • Divergence detection may produce different results with different parameter settings, introducing subjectivity
  • No position scaling mechanism, unable to increase profits when trades move favorably
  • Missing spread filter and trading session filter, may open positions under unfavorable conditions

Improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., higher timeframe MA direction) to avoid counter-trend trades during strong trends
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account balance and ATR volatility for proportional risk management
  • Add spread filter and trading session restrictions to avoid trading during high spread and low liquidity periods
