MACD EA

This EA trades based on MACD crossover signals, entering long positions when MACD crosses above its signal line and entering short positions when MACD crosses below. It uses configurable lot sizing and stop loss/take profit levels for risk management. The strategy is a classic momentum-following approach relying on MACD histogram and signal line interactions.

Strategy analysis

This EA employs the classic MACD momentum crossover strategy, with the core concept of using MACD golden cross and death cross signals to determine market trend direction. Entry conditions are based on MACD main line crossing the signal line — golden cross for long entries, death cross for short entries. Exit conditions rely on preset stop loss and take profit parameters, and may also close positions when an opposing crossover signal appears. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach where the user sets the lot size through the InpLotSize parameter. Money management includes a stop loss parameter to cap per-trade losses, but lacks dynamic position sizing based on account equity or balance. The overall strategy logic is simple and clear, but is prone to frequent false signals in ranging markets leading to consecutive losses.

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and optimize
  • Uses the well-established MACD indicator with solid theoretical foundation widely used in technical analysis
  • Provides stop loss and take profit parameters with basic risk control capability

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot position sizing cannot dynamically adjust risk exposure based on account equity
  • MACD tends to generate numerous false signals in ranging markets leading to frequent losses
  • Lacks new bar check mechanism, potentially opening multiple positions on the same bar
  • Lacks spread filter, entry costs may be too high in high-spread environments

Possible improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing to automatically calculate lot size based on account balance and risk ratio
  • Add trend filter conditions (e.g., higher timeframe MA direction) to avoid frequent trading in ranging markets
  • Add new bar check and spread filter to improve trade quality and reduce false signals

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Lacks new bar check, potentially opening duplicate positions on the same bar leading to overexposure
  • Fixed lot size cannot adapt to different account sizes, risk may be too high for small accounts
  • MACD lag causes delayed entries, stop losses may be frequently triggered
