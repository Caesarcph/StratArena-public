摘要

N Candles v4是一个基于连续N根K线形态识别的交易EA，当检测到连续N根同方向K线时入场，并根据可配置的止盈、止损或形态反转条件出场。该EA在亏损后采用马丁格尔式的仓位加码来尝试回本。

策略分析

策略核心概念是识别连续N根同方向K线（连续阳线或连续阴线），将其视为趋势过度延伸的信号，从而进行反转交易。入场条件为当市场出现用户指定数量的连续同方向K线收盘时开仓做多或做空。出场条件包括固定点数的止盈和止损，以及可选的K线反转出场。仓位管理方面，EA在连续亏损后采用马丁格尔策略加倍手数，试图通过加大仓位来弥补前期损失。资金管理依赖于初始手数和乘数因子的配置，但没有账户净值比例的动态仓位控制。整体策略逻辑简单直观，但马丁格尔加仓机制在极端行情下存在爆仓风险。

Summary

N Candles v4 is an Expert Advisor that identifies and trades based on N consecutive candlestick patterns (e.g., N consecutive bullish or bearish candles). It enters positions when a streak of same-direction candles is detected and exits based on configurable conditions such as take-profit, stop-loss, or pattern reversal. The EA applies martingale-style position sizing after losses to recover equity.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy concept is identifying N consecutive candles in the same direction (consecutive bullish or bearish candles), interpreting this as an overextension of trend, and entering a reversal trade. Entry conditions trigger when the market produces a user-specified number of consecutive same-direction candle closes, opening a buy after N bearish candles or a sell after N bullish candles. Exit conditions include fixed pip-based take-profit and stop-loss levels, with optional reversal-based exit when an opposite candle appears. Position sizing employs a martingale approach that multiplies lot size after consecutive losses, attempting to recover equity through increased exposure. Money management relies on the initial lot size and a multiplier factor configuration, but lacks dynamic equity-proportional position sizing. The overall strategy logic is simple and intuitive, but the martingale lot escalation mechanism poses a significant blow-up risk during trending markets with sustained directional moves.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，基于连续K线形态的过度延伸反转概念易于理解
  • 参数可配置性强，用户可以调整K线数量、止盈止损和加仓倍数
  • 设有最大加仓倍数限制，一定程度上控制了马丁格尔策略的极端风险

劣势

  • 马丁格尔加仓机制在强趋势行情中可能导致指数级亏损甚至爆仓
  • 缺乏点差过滤和滑点保护，在流动性差的市场环境下容易被止损扫出
  • 没有账户净值比例的动态仓位管理，固定手数加码方式不够灵活

改进建议

  • 用固定风险比例替代马丁格尔加仓，每次交易风险不超过账户净值的1-2%
  • 增加点差和波动率过滤条件，避免在高点差或低流动性时段开仓

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic based on the overextension reversal concept of consecutive candle patterns, easy to understand
  • Highly configurable parameters allowing users to adjust candle count, TP/SL, and lot multiplier
  • Maximum multiplier cap provides some control over the extreme risk of the martingale progression

Weaknesses

  • Martingale lot escalation can lead to exponential losses or account blow-up during strong trending markets
  • Lack of spread filter and slippage protection makes the EA vulnerable to stop-hunting in low-liquidity conditions
  • No dynamic equity-proportional position sizing; the fixed lot multiplication approach is inflexible

Improvements

  • Replace martingale lot progression with fixed risk-per-trade sizing, risking no more than 1-2% of account equity per trade
  • Add spread and volatility filters to avoid opening positions during high-spread or low-liquidity periods
