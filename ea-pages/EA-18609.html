摘要

N Candles v4 是一个MetaTrader 5平台上的工具型指标/EA，用于计数和显示图表上N根连续K线（如连续阳线或阴线）的信息。它作为可视化分析工具帮助交易者识别K线序列中的连续形态，而非自动执行交易。

策略分析

该EA本质上是一个K线连续形态识别工具，而非自动交易策略。它通过统计连续同向K线（连续阳线或连续阴线）的数量来辅助交易者判断市场趋势强度和潜在反转点。入场条件方面，该EA不自行开仓，而是提供连续K线计数信息供交易者参考决策。出场条件同样不适用，因为该EA不管理任何持仓。仓位管理不涉及，因其仅作为信息展示工具。资金管理方面，该EA不涉及任何资金管理逻辑，仅专注于K线形态的统计与展示。

Summary

N Candles v4 is a utility indicator/EA for MetaTrader 5 that counts and displays information about N consecutive candles (e.g., consecutive bullish or bearish candles) on the chart. It serves as a visual analytical tool to help traders identify streak patterns in candle sequences rather than executing trades automatically.

Strategy Analysis

This EA is fundamentally a candlestick streak pattern identification tool rather than an automated trading strategy. It counts consecutive same-direction candles (consecutive bullish or bearish candles) to help traders gauge trend strength and potential reversal points. Regarding entry conditions, the EA does not open positions on its own but provides consecutive candle count information for traders to make reference decisions. Exit conditions are likewise not applicable since the EA does not manage any positions. Position sizing is not involved as it functions purely as an informational display tool. In terms of money management, the EA contains no money management logic whatsoever, focusing exclusively on the statistics and display of candlestick patterns.
