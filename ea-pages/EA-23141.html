EA-23141 · Mean Reversion

Exp_Slow-Stoch_Duplex

This EA trades a duplex (dual) Slow Stochastic strategy, generating buy signals when the stochastic is oversold and crosses upward, and sell signals when it is overbought and crosses downward. It operates systematically on the Slow Stochastic indicator with configurable overbought/oversold thresholds and applies to any symbol and timeframe. The EA opens positions based on stochastic crossover signals in overbought/oversold zones.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on the mean reversion principle of the Slow Stochastic indicator, assuming that prices in overbought and oversold zones will revert to the mean. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the %K line crosses above the %D line in the oversold zone (default below 20), and a sell signal is generated when the %K line crosses below the %D line in the overbought zone (default above 80). Exit conditions rely on fixed take-profit and stop-loss settings, or closing positions when a reverse signal appears. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach where the user directly sets the trade volume via the Money parameter. Money management lacks dynamic position calculation with no automatic adjustment based on account balance or risk percentage. The strategy performs well in ranging markets but may suffer frequent stop-outs during strong trending conditions.

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic based on classic stochastic signals, easy to understand and verify
  • Uses Slow Stochastic to reduce false signals, more stable than fast stochastic
  • Has explicit stop-loss and take-profit settings with controllable risk
  • Adjustable overbought/oversold thresholds to adapt to different market conditions

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing lacks dynamic money management, cannot automatically adjust positions based on account size
  • Mean reversion strategy prone to consecutive losses during strong trending markets
  • No new bar check mechanism, potentially opening multiple positions on the same bar
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, may open positions under unfavorable conditions

Possible improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing that automatically calculates trade lots based on account balance and risk percentage
  • Add a trend filter (e.g., MA direction) to avoid counter-trend trades during strong trends

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No new bar check, may repeatedly open positions on the same bar causing position overlap
  • Mean reversion strategy can produce consecutive losses in one-directional markets
  • Fixed lot size does not consider account risk tolerance
