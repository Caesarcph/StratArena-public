摘要

该EA使用经过移动平均线平滑的RSI指标来生成基于动量的交易信号，基于MQL5标准库专家框架运行。它采用阈值信号系统来开仓和平仓，使用固定手数且无追踪止损。策略完全依赖CH_HM_RSI自定义信号过滤器进行入场和出场决策。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略概念是基于RSI指标的动量交易，通过一个周期为33的RSI指标再叠加周期为2的移动平均线进行信号平滑处理。入场条件由信号阈值控制，当综合信号值超过开仓阈值（默认40）时开仓，信号值低于平仓阈值（默认20）时平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数模式（默认0.1手），不根据账户资金动态调整仓位大小。资金管理使用MoneyFixedLot模块，仅依据设定的固定百分比和固定手数进行交易。出场条件除了信号阈值平仓外，还支持止损和止盈设置，但默认值均为0，意味着未设置止损止盈。策略没有使用追踪止损，完全依赖信号反转来平仓，这在趋势行情中可能导致利润回吐较大。

Summary

This EA uses an RSI indicator smoothed by a moving average to generate momentum-based trade signals within the MQL5 Standard Library expert framework. It employs a threshold-based signal system to open and close positions, with fixed lot sizing and no trailing stop. The strategy relies on the CH_HM_RSI custom signal filter for all entry and exit decisions.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy concept is momentum trading based on the RSI indicator, smoothed by a 2-period moving average applied to a 33-period RSI. Entry conditions are governed by signal thresholds: a position is opened when the composite signal exceeds the open threshold (default 40), and closed when it falls below the close threshold (default 20). Position sizing uses a fixed lot model (default 0.1 lots), which does not dynamically adjust based on account equity or volatility. Money management is handled by the MoneyFixedLot module, trading only with the configured fixed percentage and fixed lot size. Exit conditions include signal-based closing via thresholds, plus optional stop loss and take profit levels — however both default to 0, meaning no SL/TP is set. The strategy employs no trailing stop, relying entirely on signal reversal for exits, which can lead to significant profit giveback during trending markets.

优势

  • 使用MQL5标准库框架，代码结构清晰规范，模块化程度高
  • RSI叠加MA平滑处理可以减少虚假信号，提高信号质量
  • 信号阈值可调，允许用户灵活控制开仓平仓灵敏度
  • 挂单有过期时间设置，避免过时挂单长期占用保证金
  • 框架支持多信号过滤器扩展，便于增加额外策略逻辑

劣势

  • 止损和止盈默认值为0，存在无限亏损风险，不设置止损极为危险
  • 固定手数不根据账户资金动态调整，无法适应不同规模账户
  • 无追踪止损，在趋势行情中利润回吐严重
  • 缺乏点差过滤，在窄阈值条件下容易被高点差吃掉利润
  • RSI周期33搭配MA周期2的参数组合未经充分论证，可能过拟合特定行情

改进建议

  • 设置合理的默认止损止盈值，例如根据ATR动态计算止损距离，避免无限风险敞口
  • 增加追踪止损模块替代TrailingNone，在趋势行情中保护浮盈
  • 增加点差过滤和 新K线检查 机制，避免高滑点和重复信号
  • 将固定手数改为动态仓位管理，根据账户风险承受能力调整手数

Strengths

  • Uses the MQL5 Standard Library expert framework, resulting in clean, well-structured, and highly modular code
  • RSI smoothed by MA reduces false signals and improves signal quality
  • Adjustable signal thresholds allow users to flexibly control entry and exit sensitivity
  • Pending orders have expiration settings, preventing stale orders from occupying margin indefinitely
  • The framework supports multiple signal filters, making it easy to extend with additional strategy logic

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss and take profit default to 0, creating unlimited loss risk — trading without SL is extremely dangerous
  • Fixed lot sizing does not adapt to account equity, making it unsuitable for different account sizes
  • No trailing stop means significant profit giveback during trending markets
  • No spread filter, which can erode profits under narrow threshold conditions with high spreads
  • The RSI(33)+MA(2) parameter combination is not well-justified and may be overfitted to specific market conditions

Improvements

  • Set reasonable default SL/TP values, e.g. using ATR-based dynamic stop loss calculation to avoid unlimited risk exposure
  • Replace TrailingNone with a proper trailing stop module to protect floating profits in trending markets
  • Add spread filter and new bar check mechanism to avoid high slippage and duplicate signals
  • Replace fixed lot sizing with dynamic position sizing that adjusts based on account risk tolerance
