Multi Divergence EA

This EA detects divergence patterns across three momentum indicators (RSI, MACD, Stochastic) and enters trades when a configurable minimum number of confirmations align. It applies trend and volume filters before opening positions, and uses fixed lot sizing or risk-based money management with configurable stop loss and take profit.

Strategy analysis

The strategy core is a multi-indicator divergence detection system for momentum reversal trading. Entry conditions require divergence between price and indicator (e.g., price making lower low while indicator makes higher low signals bullish divergence), with at least MinConfirmations indicators confirming the signal, plus optional trend filter requiring price to be on the correct side of EMA50, and optional volume filter requiring recent volume to exceed 1.2x the average. Exit conditions rely on fixed point-based stop loss and take profit, with no trailing stop or dynamic exit mechanism. Position sizing supports both fixed lot size and risk-based calculation using account balance percentage, where the latter dynamically computes lot size based on stop loss points and tick value with normalization. The money management module respects minimum/maximum lot and step constraints but lacks margin verification. Overall strategy logic is clear, but divergence detection only compares the two most recent extrema, potentially missing more complex divergence patterns.

Strengths

  • Multi-indicator divergence confirmation mechanism improves signal reliability and reduces false signal risk
  • Multiple entry filters including spread filter, trend filter, and volume filter enhance trade quality
  • Money management module respects instrument specifications such as min/max lot and lot step constraints
  • Clean code structure with well-separated functional modules makes it easy to understand and maintain

Weaknesses

  • Ask and Bid variables are incorrectly assigned SYMBOL_ASK and SYMBOL_BID constants instead of real-time prices, causing critical errors in spread calculation and order pricing
  • Divergence detection only compares the two most recent extrema, cannot detect complex multi-point divergence patterns, and unsorted extrema arrays may compare mismatched pairs
  • CalculateDivergenceStrength formula has logic flaws: simply adding price and indicator changes doesn't properly measure divergence strength, and using MathAbs in denominator may cause division by zero
  • Lacks margin check, order fill type setting, and slippage control, which may cause real trading failures due to insufficient margin or excessive slippage
  • Trend filter logic contradicts divergence nature: bullish divergence requires price above EMA, but as a reversal signal, price is typically below EMA at that point

Possible improvements

  • Fix Ask/Bid variables: should use SymbolInfoDouble to get real-time bid/ask prices instead of assigning constants to double variables
  • Improve divergence strength calculation: use normalized directional divergence between price and indicator changes instead of simple addition
  • Add multi-point divergence detection and time-sorted extrema pairing to improve accuracy and capture complex patterns
  • Add margin check (OrderCalcCheck), configurable order fill type, and slippage tolerance parameters
  • Reconsider trend filter logic: for divergence reversal strategies, entry should be allowed when price is on the opposite side of EMA

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Ask/Bid variable assignment bug is critical, causing all trades to execute at wrong prices and spread calculation to completely fail
  • CalculateDivergenceStrength may cause division by zero errors leading to EA crash or invalid signals
  • Lack of margin check may attempt orders when account has insufficient funds, causing errors
  • Trend filter logic contradicts divergence reversal strategy, potentially filtering out valid signals
