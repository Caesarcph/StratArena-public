摘要

BITEX.ONE MarketMaker是一个做市商类型的EA，通过在当前价格上下同时挂买入和卖出限价单来赚取买卖价差和短期价格波动的利润。订单成交后会持续补单，维持双边市场报价。该EA依赖可配置的网格间距和手数设置，在区间内从价格波动中获取小额利润。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是做市商策略，属于均值回归的一种变体。入场条件是在当前价格上下按固定间距分别挂买入限价单和卖出限价单，当价格触及挂单价位时成交入场。出场条件是当挂单成交后，通过止盈目标平仓获利，止盈间距通常较小，依赖频繁交易累积利润。仓位管理采用固定手数或可配置手数，每次补单使用相同手数，未发现明显的加仓或减仓逻辑。资金管理方面缺乏硬性止损设置，若市场单边走势剧烈，挂单可能持续成交导致持仓方向一致且亏损扩大。整体而言，该策略在震荡市中表现良好，但在趋势市中存在较大风险，因为双边挂单在单边行情中会持续被成交造成累积亏损。

Summary

BITEX.ONE MarketMaker is an Expert Advisor that functions as a market maker by placing both buy and sell limit orders around the current price, profiting from the bid-ask spread and short-term price oscillations. It continuously replenishes orders as they are filled, maintaining a persistent two-sided presence in the market. The EA relies on configurable grid spacing and lot sizing to capture small profits from price fluctuation within a range.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is a market-making approach, a variant of mean reversion. Entry conditions involve placing buy limit orders below and sell limit orders above the current price at fixed intervals, executing when price touches the order level. Exit conditions are met when filled orders reach a small take-profit target, relying on high trade frequency to accumulate profits. Position sizing uses fixed or configurable lot sizes with the same lot per replenishment, with no progressive sizing or reduction logic detected. Money management lacks hard stop-loss settings, meaning sustained one-directional market moves can cause consecutive order fills accumulating loss in the adverse direction. The strategy performs well in ranging markets but carries significant risk in trending markets where bilateral orders are continuously filled, building a large directional loss position.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中可以稳定获利，利用买卖价差反复交易
  • 双边挂单策略逻辑清晰，实现简单，易于理解和调优
  • 频繁交易可在低波动市场中持续产生现金流

劣势

  • 缺乏止损保护，单边趋势行情可能导致严重亏损
  • 双边挂单在极端行情下会持续成交，仓位风险难以控制
  • 做市策略对点差和手续费高度敏感，高成本环境下盈利困难

改进建议

  • 增加硬性止损和最大回撤限制，防止单边行情下的灾难性亏损
  • 加入趋势过滤机制，在检测到强趋势时暂停一侧挂单或减少仓位

Strengths

  • Generates steady profits in ranging markets by repeatedly capturing the bid-ask spread
  • Clear bilateral order logic that is simple to implement, understand, and tune
  • High trade frequency can produce consistent cash flow in low-volatility environments

Weaknesses

  • No stop-loss protection, sustained trending moves can cause severe losses
  • Bilateral orders fill consecutively in extreme markets, making position risk hard to control
  • Market-making strategy is highly sensitive to spread and commission costs; profitability is difficult under high-cost conditions

Improvements

  • Add hard stop-loss and maximum drawdown limits to prevent catastrophic losses during trending markets
  • Incorporate a trend filter to pause one-sided order placement or reduce position size when a strong trend is detected

  • 双边挂单持续成交会累积方向性风险敞口
  • 未检测市场波动率变化，极端行情下缺乏自适应保护

  • No volatility adaptation; lacks self-protective measures during extreme market conditions
