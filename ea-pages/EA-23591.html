摘要

该EA实现了做市商策略，在当前价格上下以固定间隔布置买卖限价挂单网格。通过在价格震荡区间内捕捉买卖订单之间的价差获利，并在订单成交后持续补充网格以维持结构完整。

策略分析

该EA采用做市商风格的网格策略，核心概念是在当前市场价格上下对称地放置限价买单和限价卖单，形成网格结构。入场条件是当价格触及网格中的某个挂单价位时自动成交，无需额外的技术指标信号驱动。出场条件依赖于每个网格层级的固定止盈目标，当订单盈利达到预设点数时平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数或递增手数方式，每层网格可以使用相同或递增的成交量。资金管理方面，EA通过网格间距和网格层数来控制风险敞口，但缺少全局止损机制。策略在震荡行情中表现优异，能够持续从价格波动中获利，但在趋势行情中可能因单边成交而导致浮亏累积。

Summary

This EA implements a market-making strategy using a grid of pending buy and sell limit orders placed at fixed intervals around the current price. It profits from price oscillations within a range by capturing the spread between buy and sell orders. The strategy continuously replaces filled orders to maintain the grid structure.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a market-making style grid strategy, with the core concept of placing symmetric buy limit and sell limit orders above and below the current market price to form a grid structure. Entry conditions are triggered automatically when price reaches a pending order level in the grid, requiring no additional technical indicator signals. Exit conditions rely on fixed take-profit targets at each grid level, closing positions when profit reaches a preset number of points. Position sizing uses either fixed lot sizes or incrementing lot sizes, where each grid layer can use the same or increasing volume. Money management is controlled through grid spacing and number of grid layers to manage risk exposure, but the EA lacks a global stop-loss mechanism. The strategy performs excellently in ranging markets by continuously profiting from price oscillations, but may accumulate significant floating losses in trending markets due to one-sided order fills.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中能持续稳定盈利，利用价格往返波动捕捉利润
  • 做市商逻辑清晰，买卖对称布局，无需复杂技术指标判断方向
  • 网格层级和间距可调，适应不同品种和波动率环境

劣势

  • 无全局止损机制，在单边趋势行情中浮亏可能无限累积
  • 网格策略属于逆势加仓类型，行情持续单边运行时风险极大
  • 缺乏趋势过滤机制，无法识别并避开趋势行情以减少亏损

改进建议

  • 添加全局止损或最大回撤限制，防止单边趋势中浮亏过大
  • 增加趋势过滤指标（如均线或ADX），在趋势行情中暂停或减少网格挂单

Strengths

  • Consistently profits in ranging markets by capturing gains from price oscillations back and forth
  • Clear market-making logic with symmetric buy/sell layout requiring no complex technical indicators for direction
  • Adjustable grid levels and spacing to adapt to different instruments and volatility environments

Weaknesses

  • No global stop-loss mechanism, floating losses can accumulate indefinitely during strong trending markets
  • Grid strategy is inherently counter-trend with increasing exposure, posing extreme risk during sustained directional moves
  • Lacks trend filter mechanism to identify and avoid trending markets to reduce losses

Improvements

  • Add a global stop-loss or maximum drawdown limit to prevent excessive floating losses in trending markets
  • Incorporate a trend filter indicator (such as moving averages or ADX) to pause or reduce grid orders during trending conditions

风险评估

总体:HIGH

风险点

  • 网格策略在单边趋势中浮亏持续累积，缺少全局止损保护
  • 逆势加仓机制导致持仓风险随行情偏离线性增长
  • 无新K线检查机制，可能在同一K线内重复下单

Risk Assessment

Overall:HIGH

Risk Points

  • Grid strategy accumulates floating losses continuously in trending markets with no global stop-loss protection
  • Counter-trend position sizing causes portfolio risk to grow linearly as price deviates from the grid center
  • No new bar check mechanism, potentially placing duplicate orders within the same bar
