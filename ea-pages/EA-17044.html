ExpPricePosition

This Expert Advisor trades based on a multi-indicator confluence system combining PricePosition and StepUpDown indicators across multiple timeframes. It uses SMMA/SMA moving average crossovers and price position relative to signal lines to generate buy/sell signals, with dynamic lot sizing, trailing stops, and opposite-signal closing capabilities.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on a dual-confirmation mechanism using the PricePosition indicator for price direction and the StepUpDown indicator for trend direction. Entry conditions require multiple confluences: PricePosition direction alignment (1 or -1), StepUpDown trend agreement, current candle being bullish (for buy) or bearish (for sell), price pulling back to the fast SMMA line, fast MA direction confirmation, and multi-timeframe momentum comparison (pwr0 > pwr1). Exit conditions include: trailing stop activation, closing on opposite signal (CloseByOppositeSignal), and profitable closing when StepUpDown direction reverses. Position sizing supports both fixed lot and dynamic lot modes, where the dynamic mode calculates lot size based on account balance and risk percentage, distributed across open positions. Money management calculates stop loss based on risk percentage and tick value, with take profit set as a multiple (TPvsSLRatio, default 3x) of the stop loss, and a minimum 38-pip stop loss distance as protection.

Strengths

  • Multi-indicator multi-timeframe confluence entry provides strong false signal filtering with strict entry conditions
  • Supports dynamic position sizing based on account balance and risk percentage, keeping risk controlled
  • Dual exit mechanism with trailing stop and opposite signal closing provides solid profit protection

Weaknesses

  • Severe memory leak: CTrade, CSymbolInfo, CPositionInfo, CAccountInfo objects are created with 'new' in OnTimer every minute but never deleted
  • Uses timer-driven trading (OnTimer) instead of tick-driven (OnTick), potentially missing fast market opportunities
  • The pwr calculation compares daily close with hourly close but doesn't account for gaps or cross-session discontinuities
  • CheckOpenPosition calls PositionSelect before iterating PositionsTotal, which may cause logical inconsistency

Possible improvements

  • Fix memory leak: move CTrade and other object creation to OnInit or add proper delete calls; best to use global objects initialized once
  • Migrate trading logic from OnTimer to OnTick with new bar detection to ensure timely response to market changes
  • Add spread filter to avoid opening positions during high spread periods; add maximum position limit to prevent over-concentration on a single symbol

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Memory leak may cause EA crash or platform slowdown during extended runtime
  • Timer-driven trading (checking every minute) is slow to respond in volatile markets, potentially increasing slippage
  • Default trailing stop of only 10 pips may be too tight for volatile instruments, causing premature stops
  • Hardcoded minimum 38-pip stop loss in CalculatePips may not suit all trading instruments
