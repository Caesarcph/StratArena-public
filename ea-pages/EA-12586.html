摘要

该EA基于TrendlessAG_Hist自定义指标进行交易，该指标对去趋势价格振荡器进行双重平滑处理。当直方图上升时开多仓，下降时开空仓，具有可配置的信号K线和平滑参数。

策略分析

策略概念基于TrendlessAG_Hist指标的双重平滑去趋势振荡器，通过第一层平滑去除价格趋势，再通过百分比归一化和第二层平滑生成交易信号。入场条件为：当直方图值从下方穿越前一周期值时开多仓（Value[0]>Value[1]且Value[1]<Value[2]），当直方图值从上方穿越前一周期值时开空仓（Value[0]<Value[1]且Value[1]>Value[2]）。出场条件为反向信号触发平仓，即多头信号时平空仓，空头信号时平多仓，同时设有固定点数的止损和止盈。仓位管理采用基于账户保证金的百分比计算手数，支持固定手数和保证金比例两种模式。资金管理通过MM参数控制仓位大小，但缺乏最大持仓限制和账户权益保护机制。

Summary

This EA trades based on the TrendlessAG_Hist custom indicator, which applies double smoothing to a detrended price oscillator. It opens buy positions when the histogram rises and sell positions when it falls, with configurable signal bar and smoothing parameters.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the TrendlessAG_Hist indicator, a double-smoothed detrended oscillator that first removes price trend via smoothing, then normalizes by percentage and applies a second smoothing layer to generate signals. Entry conditions: buy when the histogram rises above its previous value (Value[0]>Value[1] and Value[1]<Value[2]), and sell when the histogram falls below its previous value (Value[0]<Value[1] and Value[1]>Value[2]). Exit conditions are triggered by reverse signals — closing sell positions on buy signals and closing buy positions on sell signals, with fixed StopLoss and TakeProfit in points. Position sizing uses a margin-based percentage calculation controlled by the MM parameter, supporting both fixed lot and margin ratio modes via MMMode. Money management is basic, lacking maximum position limits, equity protection, or drawdown controls, relying solely on the MM fraction and fixed SL/TP for risk containment.

优势

  • 双重平滑设计有效减少虚假信号，第一层去趋势后第二层JMA平滑增强信号稳定性
  • 支持多种平滑算法和价格类型，参数灵活可调适应不同市场环境
  • 买卖开关独立控制，可灵活配置只做多或只做空方向
  • 新K线检测机制避免同根K线重复交易

劣势

  • OnInit函数末尾错误返回INIT_FAILED导致EA永远无法正常启动运行
  • 缺乏点差过滤和最大持仓控制，在高点差环境下可能产生不利交易
  • 无回撤保护和账户权益检查机制，连续亏损时风险不可控
  • 依赖自定义指标TrendlessAG_Hist和外部头文件，部署复杂且可移植性差

改进建议

  • 修复OnInit函数末尾的INIT_FAILED为INIT_SUCCEEDED，使EA能够正常启动运行
  • 添加点差过滤、最大持仓限制和账户回撤保护机制
  • 增加移动止损和保本止损功能以改善盈利交易的风险管理
  • 将指标计算内嵌到EA中减少外部依赖

Strengths

  • Double smoothing design effectively reduces false signals — first detrending layer then JMA smoothing enhances signal stability
  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithms and price types with flexible parameters adaptable to different market conditions
  • Independent buy/sell switches allow flexible configuration for long-only or short-only trading
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated trading on the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Critical bug: OnInit returns INIT_FAILED at line 95, making the EA completely non-functional — it will never start trading
  • Lacks spread filter and maximum position controls, potentially entering unfavorable trades in high-spread conditions
  • No drawdown protection or account equity check, risk is uncontrolled during consecutive losses
  • Depends on custom indicator TrendlessAG_Hist and external include files, making deployment complex and portability poor

Improvements

  • Fix the critical bug: change INIT_FAILED to INIT_SUCCEEDED at the end of OnInit so the EA can actually start running
  • Add spread filter, maximum position limits, and account drawdown protection mechanisms
  • Implement trailing stop and break-even functionality to improve risk management on profitable trades
  • Embed indicator calculation within the EA to reduce external dependencies and improve portability
