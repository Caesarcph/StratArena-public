摘要

该EA利用ZigZag指标摆动点结合快慢EMA交叉判断趋势方向，然后在最近ZigZag波动的57%斐波那契回撤位挂限价单。未成交的挂单会在可配置的K线数量后自动删除，并包含星期几的交易过滤功能。

策略分析

策略概念基于ZigZag摆动点与EMA趋势方向结合的斐波那契回撤入场。当快EMA在慢EMA之上且ZigZag出现新高点时，在57%回撤位挂Buy Limit单；当快EMA在慢EMA之下且ZigZag出现新低点时，在57%回撤位挂Sell Limit单。止损设在ZigZag极值点（买单止损在低点，卖单止损在高点），止盈为止损距离乘以TPFromSLMultiplier倍数。仓位管理使用固定手数InitialLot，无加仓或减仓逻辑。资金管理通过CheckMargin函数检查可用保证金是否充足，并在PendingOrders中验证手数符合经纪商的最小/最大/步长限制。挂单在WaitNewSignalZigZagBarCount根K线后若未成交则自动删除，等待下一个信号。

Summary

This EA uses ZigZag indicator swing points combined with fast/slow EMA crossover to identify trend direction, then places pending limit orders at the 57% Fibonacci retracement level of the last ZigZag swing. It manages orders by deleting unfilled pending orders after a configurable number of bars, and includes day-of-week trading filters.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept combines ZigZag swing points with EMA trend direction to enter at Fibonacci retracement levels. When the fast EMA is above the slow EMA and a new ZigZag high forms, a Buy Limit order is placed at the 57% Fibonacci retracement of the swing; conversely, when the fast EMA is below the slow EMA and a new ZigZag low forms, a Sell Limit order is placed at the 57% retracement. Stop loss is set at the ZigZag extreme (buy SL at the swing low, sell SL at the swing high), and take profit is calculated as the SL distance multiplied by the TPFromSLMultiplier. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (InitialLot) with no scaling or pyramiding logic. Money management checks free margin via OrderCalcMargin before placing orders and validates lot size against broker minimum, maximum, and step constraints. Unfilled pending orders are automatically deleted after WaitNewSignalZigZagBarCount bars, resetting the system for the next signal.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，结合趋势判断（EMA交叉）与关键回撤位（斐波那契57%）入场，具有较高的盈亏比设计
  • 包含完整的止损止盈逻辑，止损设在ZigZag极值点具有技术依据，止盈通过倍数关系保证正期望
  • 实现了挂单超时删除机制，避免过期信号导致不利入场，同时包含交易日过滤和保证金检查
  • 使用限价单而非市价单入场，寻求更优的入场价格，符合趋势回撤交易理念

劣势

  • GetLastZigZagPoints函数逻辑混乱：前半部分从索引5开始搜索并设置lastZigZagHigh/Low，后半部分又从索引0遍历覆盖了前面的结果，导致前半部分代码完全无效
  • 仅允许同时持有一个仓位（PositionSelect检查），且不区分EA实例，在多品种运行时会互相干扰
  • BreakoutDistancePips参数已声明但未使用，属于冗余代码；变量命名混用印尼语和英语，可读性差
  • 没有点差过滤、滑点保护或市场波动率检查，在高波动或大点差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • onTick中使用TimeCurrent()==lastCheck限制每秒仅执行一次，但新K线检测应使用Bars或iBars来判断而非时间戳

改进建议

  • 修复GetLastZigZagPoints函数：移除重复遍历逻辑，明确区分最近和前一个ZigZag点的获取，确保prevZigZagHigh/Low正确更新
  • 添加点差过滤和波动率检查（如ATR），避免在大点差或极端波动时入场；使用IsNewBar函数替代当前的时间戳判断方式
  • 移除未使用的BreakoutDistancePips参数，统一变量命名为英语，添加对多实例运行的支持（通过MagicNumber和Symbol组合过滤）
  • 增加追踪止损或移动止盈功能以保护浮盈，同时添加最大回撤限制和每日亏损限制等资金管理保护机制

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic combining trend identification (EMA crossover) with key retracement entry (57% Fibonacci), designed for favorable risk-reward ratio
  • Complete stop loss and take profit logic with SL at ZigZag extremes providing technical justification, and TP calculated via multiplier for positive expectancy
  • Implements pending order timeout deletion mechanism to avoid stale signals, along with trading day filters and margin checks
  • Uses limit orders instead of market orders for better entry prices, aligning with trend retracement trading philosophy

Weaknesses

  • GetLastZigZagPoints function has contradictory logic: the first half searches from index 5 and sets lastZigZagHigh/Low, but the second half iterates from index 0 and overwrites those results, rendering the first half completely useless
  • Only allows one position at a time (PositionSelect check) and does not distinguish between EA instances, causing interference when running on multiple symbols
  • BreakoutDistancePips parameter is declared but never used (dead code); variable naming mixes Indonesian and English, reducing readability
  • No spread filter, slippage protection, or volatility check — unfavorable fills may occur in high-volatility or wide-spread environments
  • OnTick uses TimeCurrent()==lastCheck to limit execution to once per second, but new bar detection should use Bars or iBars rather than timestamp comparison

Improvements

  • Fix GetLastZigZagPoints function: remove redundant iteration logic, clearly separate retrieval of the most recent and previous ZigZag points, ensure prevZigZagHigh/Low are correctly updated
  • Add spread filter and volatility check (e.g., ATR) to avoid entries during wide spreads or extreme volatility; replace the current timestamp-based check with a proper IsNewBar function
  • Remove unused BreakoutDistancePips parameter, unify variable naming to English, add support for multiple EA instances via MagicNumber+Symbol combination filtering
  • Add trailing stop or moving take-profit functionality to protect floating profits, and implement drawdown limits and daily loss caps as money management safeguards

  • 没有点差过滤，大点差环境下限价单可能在不利价格成交
  • 单一持仓限制且使用PositionSelect(_Symbol)不考虑MagicNumber，可能与其他EA冲突
  • 固定手数无动态调整，没有最大回撤或连续亏损保护机制

  • No spread filter — limit orders may fill at unfavorable prices in wide-spread environments
  • Single position limit using PositionSelect(_Symbol) without MagicNumber filtering may conflict with other EAs
  • Fixed lot sizing with no dynamic adjustment, no maximum drawdown or consecutive loss protection mechanisms
