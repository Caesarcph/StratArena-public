摘要

该EA结合斐波那契回撤水平与抛物线SAR指标（iSAR）来识别趋势跟踪入场机会。当价格与关键斐波那契水平对齐且抛物线SAR确认趋势方向时开仓。该策略旨在捕捉回调至斐波那契区域后的趋势延续。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是将斐波那契回撤水平与抛物线SAR指标相结合，构成一个趋势跟踪系统。入场条件是价格回撤至关键斐波那契水平（如38.2%、50%、61.8%）附近，同时抛物线SAR指标翻转确认趋势方向，此时顺势入场。出场条件依赖抛物线SAR的反转信号或固定的止盈止损水平。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，未发现动态仓位调整机制。资金管理方面缺乏风险比例控制，没有根据账户余额或波动率动态计算仓位大小。整体策略逻辑清晰，但风控措施较为薄弱，缺少追踪止损和分批出场机制。

Summary

This EA combines Fibonacci retracement levels with the Parabolic SAR indicator (iSAR) to identify trend-following entry opportunities. It opens trades when price aligns with key Fibonacci levels and the Parabolic SAR confirms trend direction. The strategy aims to capture trend continuations after pullbacks to Fibonacci zones.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA combines Fibonacci retracement levels with the Parabolic SAR indicator to form a trend-following system. Entry conditions require price to pull back to key Fibonacci levels (such as 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%) while the Parabolic SAR flips to confirm trend direction, at which point a trade is opened in the trend's direction. Exit conditions rely on Parabolic SAR reversal signals or fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels. Position sizing uses fixed lots without any dynamic adjustment mechanism. Money management lacks risk-proportion control, with no position sizing based on account balance or volatility. The overall strategy logic is clear, but risk management measures are relatively weak, lacking trailing stops and partial exit mechanisms.

优势

  • 斐波那契与SAR双重确认减少假信号，提高入场质量
  • 趋势跟踪策略在明确趋势行情中表现良好，盈亏比合理
  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和参数优化
  • 抛物线SAR自带追踪功能，能较好跟随趋势发展

劣势

  • 固定手数缺乏动态仓位管理，无法根据市场波动率调整风险敞口
  • 在震荡行情中容易产生频繁的虚假信号导致连续亏损
  • 缺乏追踪止损机制，盈利头寸可能在回撤中大幅回吐利润
  • 斐波那契水平的选择主观性较强，不同行情可能需要不同参数

改进建议

  • 增加ATR动态止损和追踪止损，根据市场波动率自适应调整止损距离
  • 引入震荡/趋势行情过滤器（如ADX），在震荡市中暂停交易或减小仓位
  • 加入基于账户风险比例的动态仓位计算，替代固定手数

Strengths

  • Dual confirmation from Fibonacci and SAR reduces false signals and improves entry quality
  • Trend-following strategy performs well in clear trending markets with reasonable risk-reward ratio
  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and optimize parameters
  • Parabolic SAR has built-in trailing function that follows trend development well

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing lacks dynamic position management, unable to adjust risk exposure based on market volatility
  • Prone to frequent false signals in ranging markets leading to consecutive losses
  • Lacks trailing stop mechanism, profitable positions may give back significant profits during pullbacks
  • Fibonacci level selection is subjective, different market conditions may require different parameters

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and trailing stop to adapt stop distances to market volatility
  • Introduce ranging/trending market filter (e.g., ADX) to pause trading or reduce position size in sideways markets
  • Implement risk-percentage-based dynamic position sizing instead of fixed lots
