Exp_Fractal_ADX_Cloud

This EA trades breakout signals based on the Fractal indicator combined with ADX trend strength filtering and an Ichimoku Cloud confirmation. It enters long when price breaks above a fractal high with ADX confirming trend strength and price above the Cloud, and enters short on the mirror condition. Exits are triggered by opposite signals, ADX weakness, or Cloud penetration.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is built on a dual-confirmation mechanism combining Bill Williams Fractal breakouts with ADX trend strength filtering. Entry conditions: a long trade requires price breaking above the most recent upper fractal, ADX main line exceeding a threshold (default 25) with +DI above -DI, and price positioned above the Ichimoku Cloud; a short trade requires price breaking below the most recent lower fractal, ADX above threshold with -DI above +DI, and price below the Cloud. Exit conditions include: hitting stop loss or take profit levels, ADX main line dropping below the threshold indicating trend weakness, or price crossing the Cloud in the opposite direction. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach where the user specifies the trade volume via input parameter. Money management provides configurable stop loss and take profit parameters but lacks dynamic position sizing or risk-percentage calculation based on account equity.

Strengths

  • English strength 1: Triple filter of Fractal breakout + ADX + Ichimoku Cloud significantly reduces false signals
  • English strength 2: Ichimoku Cloud provides dynamic support/resistance levels that adapt to market volatility
  • English strength 3: ADX filtering ensures entries only during confirmed trends, avoiding losses in ranging markets
  • English strength 4: Parameterized stop loss and take profit design allows flexible risk control adjustment

Weaknesses

  • English weakness 1: Fixed lot position sizing is inflexible and does not account for account risk percentage
  • English weakness 2: Fractal signals are inherently lagging, breakout signals may arrive late
  • English weakness 3: No spread filter, potentially leading to unfavorable entries in high-spread environments
  • English weakness 4: No new bar check, potentially opening duplicate positions within the same candle

Possible improvements

  • English improvement 1: Add risk-based position sizing that calculates lot size as a percentage of account equity
  • English improvement 2: Add new bar detection logic to prevent duplicate position openings within the same candle

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • English risk point 1: Fixed lot size does not scale with account size, large accounts may have concentrated risk
  • English risk point 2: No new bar check may cause repeated position opening on the same signal, increasing exposure
