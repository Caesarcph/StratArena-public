Expert_AH_HM_Stoch

This EA uses a custom Stochastic oscillator signal filter (CH_HM_Stoch) combined with a moving average to generate buy/sell signals within the MQL5 Expert Advisor framework. It employs fixed lot sizing for money management and no trailing stop. Trade entries and exits are governed by configurable signal threshold values for opening and closing positions.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the custom CH_HM_Stoch signal filter, which integrates the Stochastic oscillator with a Moving Average (MA) indicator. Entry conditions are triggered when the Stochastic indicator value crosses or deviates from the MA beyond the configured opening threshold (Signal_ThresholdOpen=40). Exit conditions occur when the signal strength falls below the closing threshold (Signal_ThresholdClose=20). Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach (Money_FixLot_Lots=0.1) without dynamic adjustment based on account balance. Money management employs the CMoneyFixedLot class, trading with a fixed percentage (Money_FixLot_Percent=10%) and fixed lot size. The strategy falls under the mean reversion category, leveraging the overbought/oversold characteristics of the Stochastic combined with MA trend assessment to identify price reversion opportunities. Notably, no explicit Stop Loss or Take Profit levels are set (Signal_StopLevel=0, Signal_TakeLevel=0), relying entirely on the signal filter's closing logic for risk control.

Strengths

  • Built on the standard MQL5 Expert framework with clean, well-structured code that is easy to maintain and extend
  • The Stochastic combined with MA signal filtering mechanism can effectively filter out false signals and improve entry quality
  • Signal threshold-based entry/exit control provides flexible strategy tuning space
  • Pending orders have expiration time (Signal_Expiration=4 bars), preventing long-term margin occupation

Weaknesses

  • No Stop Loss (Signal_StopLevel=0) or Take Profit (Signal_TakeLevel=0) set, lacking hard risk protection when signals fail
  • Fixed lot sizing cannot dynamically adjust position size based on market volatility and account risk
  • The D-period is set to 49, far exceeding conventional values (typically 3-5), which may cause severe signal lag and missed optimal entry timing
  • No trailing stop mechanism (TrailingNone), profitable positions cannot lock in floating profits
  • Relies on a single signal filter without multi-dimensional confirmation, potentially generating frequent false signals in choppy markets

Possible improvements

  • Add non-zero default values for Stop Loss and Take Profit parameters to provide a baseline risk protection floor
  • Replace fixed lot sizing with dynamic position management (e.g., ATR-based or risk-percentage mode) for market-volatility-adaptive adjustment
  • Introduce a trailing stop mechanism to replace TrailingNone, enabling profit locking in trending markets while letting profits run

Risk assessment

Overall risk: High risk

  • Stop Loss and Take Profit are both 0, which could cause massive losses in extreme market conditions with no hard exit protection
  • Fixed lot mode does not adjust with account size, potentially leading to over-leveraging in small accounts
  • Stochastic D-period of 49 is excessively long; signal lag may cause severe losses during rapid market reversals
