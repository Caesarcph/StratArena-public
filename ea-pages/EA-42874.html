摘要

DD Relative是一个监控工具型EA，用于追踪和显示交易账户的相对回撤指标。它计算回撤相对于余额或净值的百分比，并在回撤超过可配置阈值时提供视觉警报。

策略分析

该EA属于工具类别，不执行自主交易。其核心概念是监控账户的相对回撤水平，即将当前浮亏与历史最高净值或当前余额进行对比计算。入场条件不适用，因为该EA不开仓交易。出场条件同样不适用。仓位管理不涉及，EA仅监控现有头寸的浮盈浮亏情况。资金管理方面，该EA提供回撤百分比计算和阈值警报，帮助交易者控制风险敞口，但本身不执行任何资金管理操作。主要功能包括实时回撤百分比显示、回撤预警通知、以及回撤历史统计记录。

Summary

DD Relative is a monitoring utility EA that tracks and displays relative drawdown metrics for the trading account. It calculates drawdown as a percentage relative to balance or equity and provides visual alerts when drawdown exceeds configurable thresholds.

Strategy Analysis

This EA falls under the utility category and does not execute autonomous trades. Its core concept is monitoring the account's relative drawdown level by comparing current floating loss against historical peak equity or current balance. Entry conditions are not applicable since this EA does not open positions. Exit conditions are similarly not applicable. Position sizing is not involved; the EA only monitors the floating profit and loss of existing positions. For money management, the EA provides drawdown percentage calculation and threshold alerts to help traders control risk exposure, but does not itself execute any money management operations. Key features include real-time drawdown percentage display, drawdown warning notifications, and drawdown history statistics recording.

优势

  • 实时监控账户回撤状态，帮助交易者及时察觉风险
  • 提供可配置的回撤阈值警报，支持多种通知方式
  • 作为辅助工具不影响主策略EA的运行

劣势

  • 仅监控回撤不主动采取风控措施，如不平仓或锁仓
  • 无法区分不同EA或品种的回撤贡献占比
  • 缺乏历史回撤图表功能，仅有数值显示

改进建议

  • 增加自动风控功能，如回撤超限时自动平仓
  • 增加按EA和品种分类的回撤统计
  • 增加回撤历史图表可视化功能

Strengths

  • Real-time monitoring of account drawdown status helps traders detect risk promptly
  • Configurable drawdown threshold alerts with multiple notification methods supported
  • Runs as a supplementary tool without interfering with main strategy EAs

Weaknesses

  • Only monitors drawdown without actively taking risk control measures such as closing or hedging positions
  • Cannot distinguish drawdown contribution ratios from different EAs or instruments
  • Lacks historical drawdown charting functionality, only numerical display

Improvements

  • Add automatic risk control features such as auto-closing positions when drawdown exceeds limits
  • Add drawdown statistics categorized by EA and instrument
  • Add historical drawdown chart visualization functionality
