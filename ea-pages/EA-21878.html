摘要

Gordago EA是一个趋势跟踪型专家顾问，使用均线交叉逻辑配合动量确认来沿趋势方向入场交易。它采用固定手数和止损/止盈水平的系统化方法，目标是捕捉中期价格波动。该EA专为趋势市场设计，在震荡或盘整行情中可能表现不佳。

策略分析

Gordago EA的核心策略是基于移动平均线交叉的趋势跟踪系统，当快速均线上穿慢速均线且动量指标确认时做多，反之下空。入场条件依赖均线交叉信号配合动量过滤，避免在无趋势行情中频繁交易。出场条件包括固定止损和止盈，以及均线反向交叉时的信号平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，没有根据账户余额或风险比例进行动态调整。资金管理方面较为简单，缺乏加仓、减仓或追踪止损等高级功能，整体风险控制依赖于固定止损但缺乏对市场波动率的适应性调整。

Summary

Gordago EA is a trend-following expert advisor that uses moving average crossover logic combined with momentum confirmation to enter trades in the direction of the prevailing trend. It employs a systematic approach with fixed lot sizing and stop-loss/take-profit levels, targeting medium-term price movements. The EA is designed for trending markets and may struggle in ranging or choppy conditions.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of Gordago EA is a trend-following system based on moving average crossover, going long when the fast MA crosses above the slow MA with momentum confirmation, and vice versa for short entries. Entry conditions rely on MA crossover signals filtered by momentum indicators to avoid excessive trading in trendless markets. Exit conditions include fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels, as well as signal-based exits when the MAs cross in the opposite direction. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic adjustment based on account balance or risk percentage. Money management is relatively simplistic, lacking advanced features such as position scaling, partial closing, or trailing stops, and overall risk control depends on fixed stops without adaptive adjustments for market volatility.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰简洁，基于经典均线交叉系统，易于理解和验证
  • 使用动量指标进行过滤确认，减少假信号和无效交易
  • 每笔交易设有固定止损，有效控制单笔最大亏损
  • 采用魔术编号管理订单，避免与其他EA冲突
  • 系统化执行消除了情绪干扰，严格按照信号交易

劣势

  • 固定手数不做动态调整，无法根据账户风险比例自适应仓位大小
  • 缺乏追踪止损功能，在强趋势行情中可能过早获利了结
  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未考虑ATR等波动率指标进行动态调整
  • 趋势跟踪策略在震荡市场中容易产生连续亏损
  • 缺少新K线检查机制，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓

改进建议

  • 引入ATR动态止损止盈，根据市场波动率自适应调整出场水平
  • 添加追踪止损功能，在趋势行情中锁定更多利润
  • 实现基于风险百分比的动态仓位管理，替代固定手数方式

Strengths

  • Clear and concise strategy logic based on classic MA crossover, easy to understand and verify
  • Uses momentum indicator for confirmation filtering, reducing false signals and ineffective trades
  • Fixed stop-loss on every trade effectively controls maximum per-trade loss
  • Employs magic number for order management, avoiding conflicts with other EAs
  • Systematic execution eliminates emotional interference, strictly following signals

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing without dynamic adjustment cannot adapt position size to account risk ratio
  • Lacks trailing stop functionality, may exit too early in strong trending markets
  • Stop-loss and take-profit are fixed pip values without ATR-based volatility adjustment
  • Trend-following strategy prone to consecutive losses in ranging/choppy markets
  • Missing new bar check mechanism, potentially opening duplicate positions on the same bar

Improvements

  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop-loss/take-profit for volatility-adaptive exit levels
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in more profit during trending moves
  • Implement risk-percentage-based dynamic position sizing instead of fixed lots
