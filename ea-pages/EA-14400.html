摘要

该EA基于自定义MACD-2指标进行交易，提供三种趋势检测模式：柱状图方向变化、云图交叉和MACD零线穿越。当选定的趋势信号触发时，开仓买入/卖出并平掉反向持仓，支持可配置的手数、止损和止盈。

策略分析

策略概念基于MACD-2指标的三种趋势判断模式：柱状图模式通过检测MACD柱状图值的递增/递减方向变化来捕捉动量转折；云图模式通过指标上下轨的交叉来判断趋势切换；零线模式通过MACD值穿越零轴来判断多空转换。入场条件方面，柱状图模式下当柱状图从下降转为上升时开多仓、从上升转为下降时开空仓；云图模式下当上线从下方穿越下线时开多仓、反之为空仓；零线模式下当MACD从负转正时开多仓、从正转负时开空仓。出场条件依赖固定止损止盈以及反向信号触发时平掉对向持仓，没有移动止损或追踪止盈机制。仓位管理通过MarginMode枚举支持五种模式：按可用保证金比例、按余额比例、按净可用保证金比例、按净余额比例和固定手数。资金管理使用MM参数控制风险比例或固定手数大小，但缺乏根据波动率动态调整仓位的机制。

Summary

This EA trades based on the MACD-2 custom indicator with three trend detection modes: histogram direction change, cloud crossover, and MACD zero-line crossover. It opens buy/sell positions and closes opposite positions when the selected trend signal triggers, using configurable lot sizing, stop loss, and take profit.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept revolves around three trend detection modes of the MACD-2 custom indicator: histogram mode detects momentum turning points by checking whether MACD histogram values are increasing or decreasing; cloud mode identifies trend shifts through crossovers of the indicator's upper and lower bands; zero-line mode determines bullish/bearish transitions by detecting MACD value crossing the zero axis. For entry conditions, in histogram mode, a buy signal triggers when the histogram shifts from declining to rising, and a sell signal triggers when it shifts from rising to declining; in cloud mode, crossovers of the Up and Dn buffers signal entries; in zero-line mode, MACD crossing above zero triggers buys and crossing below zero triggers sells. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss and take profit values, plus closing opposite positions when a reverse signal occurs, with no trailing stop or dynamic profit-taking mechanism. Position sizing supports five modes via the MarginMode enum: percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of net free margin, percentage of net balance, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter for risk ratio or fixed lot size, but lacks volatility-based dynamic position adjustment.

优势

  • 提供三种趋势判断模式，灵活适应不同市场环境
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，用户可根据风险偏好选择
  • 有新K线检测机制避免同根K线重复交易
  • 买入和卖出方向可独立开关控制，策略配置灵活
  • 止损止盈参数可配置，基本风险控制到位

劣势

  • 依赖自定义MACD-2指标，指标文件缺失则EA无法运行
  • 缺少移动止损/追踪止盈机制，盈利单可能大幅回吐
  • 没有点差过滤，在点差较大的品种上可能产生虚假信号
  • 没有持仓数量检查，可能在同一方向重复开仓
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点的默认值偏大，可能不适合所有品种
  • 缺乏行情波动率（如ATR）自适应的仓位调整

改进建议

  • 增加移动止损功能，保护已获利润并让盈利单持续运行
  • 增加点差过滤器，避免在点差过大时开仓
  • 增加持仓数量检查，防止同方向重复开仓
  • 使用ATR动态调整止损止盈距离，适应不同品种的波动特性
  • 增加指标数据有效性校验和更完善的错误恢复机制

Strengths

  • Offers three trend detection modes for flexibility across different market conditions
  • Supports multiple money management modes allowing users to choose based on risk preference
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated trading on the same bar
  • Buy and sell directions can be independently enabled or disabled for flexible strategy configuration
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit parameters provide basic risk control

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom MACD-2 indicator file; missing indicator causes EA failure
  • Lacks trailing stop or dynamic take-profit mechanism, profitable positions may give back significant gains
  • No spread filter, which may produce false signals on high-spread instruments
  • No position count check, potentially opening multiple positions in the same direction
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points may be too wide for some instruments
  • Lacks volatility-adaptive (e.g., ATR-based) position sizing

Improvements

  • Add trailing stop functionality to protect profits and let winning positions run
  • Add a spread filter to avoid opening positions when spread is excessively wide
  • Add position count check to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Use ATR for dynamic stop loss and take profit adjustment to adapt to different instruments' volatility
  • Add indicator data validity checks and more robust error recovery mechanisms
