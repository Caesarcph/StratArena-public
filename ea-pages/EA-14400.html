摘要

该EA基于自定义MACD-2指标进行交易，支持三种趋势检测模式：直方图方向变化、云图（上下线交叉）和零线穿越。在信号确认时开仓买入/卖出，并同时平仓反向头寸，支持可配置的资金管理和止损/止盈设置。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略基于自定义MACD-2指标的三种趋势判断模式。第一种直方图模式（HISTOGRAM）通过检测MACD直方图的方向变化来产生信号——当直方图由下降转为上升时产生买入信号，由上升转为下降时产生卖出信号。第二种云图模式（CLOUD）通过指标的上轨与下轨交叉来判断趋势——当上轨从下方穿越下轨时买入，从上方穿越时卖出。第三种零线模式（ZERO）通过MACD直方图穿越零线来产生信号——从负转正时买入，从正转负时卖出。入场条件要求前一周期与当前周期之间发生方向性变化，确保趋势反转的确认。出场条件包括反向信号触发平仓以及固定止损/止盈。仓位管理通过MarginMode枚举支持多种模式，包括按自由保证金、余额、亏损自由保证金、亏损余额比例计算，以及固定手数模式。EA仅在新的K线出现时（通过IsNewBar检测）重新计算信号，避免重复开仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on a custom MACD-2 indicator with three trend detection modes: histogram direction change, cloud (up/down line crossover), and zero-line crossover. It opens buy/sell positions on signal confirmation and closes opposite positions accordingly, with configurable money management and stop-loss/take-profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on three trend detection modes of the custom MACD-2 indicator. The first histogram mode (HISTOGRAM) generates signals by detecting directional changes in the MACD histogram — a buy signal occurs when the histogram transitions from declining to rising, and a sell signal when it transitions from rising to declining. The second cloud mode (CLOUD) determines trend direction through crossovers of the indicator's upper and lower lines — buying when the upper line crosses above the lower line and selling when it crosses below. The third zero-line mode (ZERO) generates signals when the MACD histogram crosses the zero line — buying on a cross from negative to positive and selling on a cross from positive to negative. Entry conditions require a directional change between the previous and current periods, ensuring confirmation of a trend reversal. Exit conditions include closing on opposite signals and fixed stop-loss/take-profit levels. Position sizing supports multiple modes via the MarginMode enumeration, including calculation by free margin, balance, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance percentages, and fixed lot mode. The EA recalculates signals only on new bar appearance (via IsNewBar check), preventing redundant trade entries.

优势

  • 三种趋势判断模式提供灵活性，适应不同市场环境
  • 使用SignalBar=1确保只在已收盘K线上产生信号，避免未完成K线的虚假信号
  • 通过IsNewBar机制限制每根K线只计算一次信号，防止重复开仓
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，从固定手数到按余额比例分配
  • 买卖开仓和平仓可独立开关，便于单向交易或仅信号提示

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标MACD-2而非内置指标，增加了部署复杂性和依赖风险
  • 没有点差过滤，在宽点差环境下可能产生不利入场
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值过大，对小周期交易不合适
  • 没有多空同时持仓检查，可能在震荡行情中频繁开平仓
  • 缺少追踪止损功能，无法锁定浮动利润

改进建议

  • 添加点差过滤器，在点差过大时禁止开仓以保护交易质量
  • 增加追踪止损功能，在趋势有利时自动移动止损锁定利润
  • 添加最大同时持仓数量限制，防止震荡市中过度交易

Strengths

  • Three trend detection modes provide flexibility to adapt to different market conditions
  • Using SignalBar=1 ensures signals are generated only on closed bars, avoiding false signals from incomplete bars
  • IsNewBar mechanism limits signal calculation to once per bar, preventing duplicate trade entries
  • Multiple money management modes supported, from fixed lots to balance-proportional allocation
  • Buy/sell opening and closing can be independently toggled, facilitating one-directional trading or signal-only use

Weaknesses

  • Depends on custom MACD-2 indicator rather than built-in, increasing deployment complexity and dependency risk
  • No spread filter, which may lead to unfavorable entries in wide-spread environments
  • Default stop-loss of 1000 points and take-profit of 2000 points are excessively large for lower timeframes
  • No check for simultaneous long/short positions, which may cause frequent open/close cycles in choppy markets
  • Lacks trailing stop functionality, unable to lock in floating profits

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter to prohibit entry when spread exceeds a threshold, protecting trade quality
  • Implement trailing stop functionality to automatically move stop-loss and lock in profits when the trend moves favorably
  • Add a maximum simultaneous position limit to prevent overtrading in choppy markets

风险点

  • 自定义指标依赖——若MACD-2指标文件缺失，EA将初始化失败无法运行
  • 缺少点差过滤，高波动时段可能以不利价格成交
  • 止损止盈固定点数未根据ATR或波动率动态调整，在不同品种和周期上可能不适配
  • 没有最大持仓限制，震荡行情中可能产生频繁信号导致过度交易和手续费损耗

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Custom indicator dependency — if the MACD-2 indicator file is missing, EA initialization will fail
  • No spread filter, potentially executing at unfavorable prices during high-volatility periods
  • Fixed stop-loss/take-profit points not dynamically adjusted by ATR or volatility, may be unsuitable across different instruments and timeframes
  • No maximum position limit, choppy markets may generate frequent signals leading to overtrading and commission drain
