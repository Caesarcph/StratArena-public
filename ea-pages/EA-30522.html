摘要

该EA在每个新K线出现时识别反转K线形态（锤子线、倒锤子线、射击之星、上吊线）和动量持续形态，然后根据形态极值设置止损并开仓。它分为多头趋势（买入信号）和空头趋势（卖出信号）两种模式，仅使用最近两根K线进行形态识别。

策略分析

策略概念基于经典K线反转形态识别，通过分析最近两根K线的实体、上下影线比例关系来判断潜在入场点。入场条件分为看涨和看跌两组：看涨组包括看涨锤子线（实体小于下影线一半，下影线大于上影线3倍）、看涨倒锤线（实体小于上影线一半，上影线大于下影线3倍）、以及看涨动量持续（连续两根阳线且当前实体和范围均大于前一根）；看跌组包括射击之星、上吊线和看跌动量持续，条件逻辑与看涨组镜像对称。出场条件仅设置了止损位（多头为止于两根K线最低价，空头为止于两根K线最高价），但未设置止盈目标，依赖止损被动出场。仓位管理方面仅使用固定手数输入参数dVol，没有任何动态仓位调整或资金管理逻辑。资金管理完全缺失，没有账户余额比例计算、风险百分比控制或最大回撤限制，交易手数完全由用户手动输入。

Summary

This EA identifies candlestick reversal patterns (Hammer, Inverted Hammer, Shooting Star, Hanging Man) and momentum continuation patterns on each new bar, then opens market orders with stop-loss levels based on the pattern's extremes. It operates in two modes: bullish trend (buy signals) and bearish trend (sell signals), using only the two most recent candles for pattern detection.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on classic candlestick reversal pattern recognition, analyzing the proportional relationships between body, upper wick, and lower wick of the two most recent candles to identify potential entry points. Entry conditions are divided into bullish and bearish groups: the bullish group includes Bullish Hammer (body less than half the lower wick, lower wick more than 3x upper wick), Bullish Inverted Hammer (body less than half the upper wick, upper wick more than 3x lower wick), and Bullish Momentum Continuation (two consecutive bullish candles with current body at least 2x previous body and current range larger than previous). The bearish group mirrors the logic with Shooting Star, Hanging Man, and Bearish Momentum patterns. Exit conditions only set stop-loss levels (long SL at the minimum low of the two candles, short SL at the maximum high), with no take-profit targets, relying solely on stop-loss for trade closure. Position sizing uses only a fixed lot size input parameter dVol, with no dynamic position adjustment or money management logic whatsoever. Money management is completely absent—there is no account balance percentage calculation, risk percentage control, or maximum drawdown limit; the lot size is entirely manually specified by the user.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于经典K线形态理论，易于理解和验证
  • 每个形态都设置了基于形态极值的止损位，提供了基本的风险保护
  • 使用新K线检测机制避免同一K线内重复开仓，减少了过度交易风险

劣势

  • 完全没有止盈目标，盈利交易只能靠止损被触发才能平仓或手动平仓
  • 没有检查是否已存在同方向持仓，可能在同一趋势中反复开仓导致过度暴露
  • K线形态识别条件过于简单且缺乏确认机制（如成交量、趋势背景、支撑阻力位），容易产生大量假信号
  • 没有滑点控制和点差过滤，在流动性差的市场中可能以极差价格成交
  • 使用MQL4兼容函数（iTimeMQL4、ObjectCreateMQL4等），代码迁移性差且可能导致兼容性问题

改进建议

  • 增加止盈目标设置和追踪止损功能，让盈利交易有合理的退出机制
  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓，并加入最大持仓数限制
  • 增加多时间框架确认（如更高周期趋势方向一致才入场）和支撑阻力位过滤，减少假信号
  • 实现基于账户风险百分比的动态仓位计算，替代固定手数输入
  • 添加点差过滤、交易时段过滤和最大回撤保护等风险控制模块

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on classic candlestick pattern theory, easy to understand and verify
  • Each pattern sets a stop-loss based on the pattern's price extremes, providing basic risk protection
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate entries within the same bar, reducing overtrading risk

Weaknesses

  • No take-profit target at all, profitable trades can only close via stop-loss trigger or manual intervention
  • No check for existing positions in the same direction, may repeatedly open positions leading to overexposure
  • Candlestick pattern recognition conditions are overly simplistic with no confirmation mechanism (volume, trend context, support/resistance), generating many false signals
  • No slippage control or spread filter, may execute at very poor prices in low-liquidity markets
  • Uses MQL4 compatibility functions (iTimeMQL4, ObjectCreateMQL4, etc.), poor code portability and potential compatibility issues

Improvements

  • Add take-profit target settings and trailing stop functionality for reasonable exit mechanisms on profitable trades
  • Add position check logic to avoid duplicate entries in the same direction, and implement maximum position limits
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation (e.g., only enter when higher timeframe trend aligns) and support/resistance filtering to reduce false signals
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account risk percentage instead of fixed lot size input
  • Add spread filter, trading session filter, and maximum drawdown protection as risk control modules
