OsMA Four Colors Arrow EA

This EA trades based on the OsMA (Oscillator of Moving Averages) indicator using a four-color arrow system to visualize bullish and bearish momentum shifts. It generates buy signals when OsMA turns positive from negative and sell signals when OsMA turns negative from positive, with color coding for momentum strength. The EA automates entries and exits based on these momentum crossover signals.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on momentum shift signals from the OsMA indicator. OsMA represents the difference between the MACD histogram and its signal line, reflecting the direction and strength of price momentum. Entry conditions are: a buy position is opened when OsMA crosses from negative to positive territory, signaling bullish momentum acceleration; a sell position is opened when OsMA crosses from positive to negative territory, signaling bearish momentum acceleration. The four-color arrow system differentiates momentum strength through color coding — for example, darker colors indicate stronger momentum while lighter colors indicate weaker momentum, helping traders visually assess signal quality. Exit conditions typically involve closing positions when a reverse signal appears, or through configurable stop-loss and take-profit parameters. Position sizing allows users to set fixed lot sizes or dynamic calculation based on account equity. Money management relies on proper stop-loss and take-profit configuration, but the overall strategy lacks adaptive volatility-based position adjustment mechanisms.

Strengths

  • Based on the well-established OsMA momentum indicator with clear and understandable signal logic, suitable for trending markets
  • The four-color arrow system provides intuitive momentum strength visualization for better signal quality assessment
  • Flexible parameterized design allows adjustment of EMA periods and stop-loss/take-profit to adapt to different market conditions

Weaknesses

  • Single-indicator strategy lacks filtering mechanisms, prone to false signals in ranging/choppy markets
  • No spread filter or trading session filter included, may enter positions under unfavorable conditions
  • Lacks dynamic position sizing; fixed lot mode cannot adapt to changing market volatility

Possible improvements

  • Add a trend filter indicator (such as ADX or higher timeframe moving average) to reduce false signals in ranging markets
  • Implement dynamic position sizing module that automatically adjusts lot size based on ATR or account risk percentage

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Single OsMA indicator prone to consecutive false signals in ranging markets leading to multiple stop-loss hits
  • No spread filter mechanism may trigger trades under high spread conditions unreasonably
  • Lack of new bar check may cause repeated position opening on the same bar
