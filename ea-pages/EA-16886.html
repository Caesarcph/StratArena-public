摘要

该EA使用CCI和MACD指标产生交易信号，在两个指标都深度超卖时买入，深度超买时卖出。它采用马丁格尔仓位管理方案，每次亏损后通过m_pos计数器加倍手数，盈利后重置为基础手数。EA还包含对盈利超过120点的仓位进行平仓的机制。

策略分析

策略概念是基于CCI和MACD双重超买超卖的反转交易，当CCI和MACD同时超过±230的阈值时触发入场信号。入场条件为：CCI和MACD均大于230时卖出，均小于-230时买入，这是一个逆势操作策略。出场条件有两个：一是开反向仓位时平掉所有同向仓位（closeAll函数），二是盈利仓位价格偏离开仓价超过120点时平仓（close函数）。仓位管理采用马丁格尔策略，m_pos变量在每次亏损平仓时递增，开仓手数为基础手数乘以2的m_pos次方，实现亏损后加倍下注。资金管理方面，基础手数根据账户余额动态计算（余额/50001），最小0.1手。整体策略逻辑存在明显风险：马丁格尔加倍在连续亏损时会导致手数指数级增长，且没有止损保护，可能在极端行情中造成巨大亏损。

Summary

This EA uses CCI and MACD indicators to generate trading signals, entering buy positions when both indicators are deeply oversold and sell positions when both are deeply overbought. It employs a Martingale position sizing scheme, doubling the lot size after each losing trade (via the m_pos counter), and resetting to base lot after a winning trade. The EA also includes a closing mechanism for profitable positions that have moved 120 points in favor.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is a mean-reversion approach using dual CCI and MACD overbought/oversold signals, triggering entries when both indicators simultaneously exceed the ±230 threshold. Entry conditions: sell when both CCI and MACD are above 230, buy when both are below -230, which is a counter-trend approach. Exit conditions are twofold: first, all positions of the same direction are closed when opening an opposite direction (closeAll function), and second, profitable positions are closed when price deviation from entry exceeds 120 points (close function). Position sizing follows a Martingale scheme where the m_pos counter increments on each losing close, and the lot size is calculated as base lot multiplied by 2 raised to the power of m_pos, effectively doubling down after losses. Money management calculates the base lot dynamically from account balance (balance/50001), with a minimum of 0.1 lots. The overall strategy carries significant risk: Martingale doubling causes exponential lot growth during consecutive losses, and there is no stop-loss protection, which can lead to catastrophic losses in extreme market conditions.

优势

  • 双重指标过滤（CCI+MACD）可以减少虚假信号，提高入场质量
  • 动态基础手数根据账户余额调整，有一定的资金管理意识
  • 逆势交易逻辑在震荡市中可能捕捉到较好的反转机会

劣势

  • 马丁格尔仓位加倍策略在连续亏损时风险极高，可能导致爆仓
  • 没有止损（SL=0.0），单笔亏损没有上限保护
  • close()函数中使用OrdersTotal()遍历但用Position方法处理，逻辑错误可能导致功能失效
  • m_pos+=2在盈利平仓时递增而非重置，与马丁格尔逻辑矛盾，可能导致手数异常增长
  • 没有新K线检查，每个tick都会评估入场条件，可能导致频繁交易

改进建议

  • 添加止损和止盈参数，限制单笔交易的最大亏损
  • 修复close()函数中OrdersTotal/PositionsTotal的混用bug，并修正m_pos在盈利平仓时的逻辑（应重置而非递增）
  • 添加新K线检查机制，避免每个tick重复入场，以及添加最大马丁格尔层数限制防止手数无限增长

Strengths

  • Dual indicator filter (CCI+MACD) reduces false signals and improves entry quality
  • Dynamic base lot sizing based on account balance shows awareness of money management
  • Counter-trend logic can capture good reversal opportunities in ranging markets

Weaknesses

  • Martingale position doubling carries extreme risk during consecutive losses, potentially causing account blowup
  • No stop-loss (SL=0.0) means unlimited downside exposure per trade
  • The close() function iterates OrdersTotal() but uses Position methods, a logic error that may cause it to malfunction
  • m_pos+=2 on profitable close increments instead of resetting, contradicting the Martingale logic and causing abnormal lot growth
  • No new bar check means entry conditions are evaluated on every tick, potentially causing excessive trading

Improvements

  • Add stop-loss and take-profit parameters to limit maximum loss per trade
  • Fix the OrdersTotal/PositionsTotal bug in close() and correct the m_pos logic on profitable closes (should reset, not increment)
  • Add a new bar check to prevent repeated entries on every tick, and implement a maximum Martingale level cap to prevent unlimited lot growth
