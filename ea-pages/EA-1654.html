EA-1654 · Multi-Timeframe

Exp_F2a_AO

This EA uses a multi-timeframe approach combining a higher-timeframe trend filter (daily candlestick direction) with the F2a_AO custom indicator signals on a lower timeframe (H12) to generate buy and sell entries. It opens long positions when the trend is bullish and the F2a_AO oscillator gives an up signal, and short positions when the trend is bearish and a down signal appears, with configurable stop-loss and take-profit levels.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy is a multi-timeframe trend-following system that uses the difference between the daily candle's close and open prices to determine the macro trend direction. The F2a_AO indicator is a custom oscillator based on triple MA filtering (MA_Filtr=3, MA_Fast=13, MA_Slow=144), generating buy signals when the upper signal buffer has a value and sell signals when the lower signal buffer has a value. Entry conditions require alignment between the macro trend and oscillator direction — longs are only opened when Trend>0 and shorts only when Trend<0. Exit conditions include hard stop-loss and take-profit levels, oscillator reversal signals triggering position closes, and a supplementary lookback mechanism that scans historical bars to find the most recent opposite signal for closing. Position sizing supports five modes: fixed lot, balance-based, equity-based, loss-share-based, and free-margin-based, defaulting to fixed lot mode (LOT). Money management is controlled via the MM parameter with a default of 0.1 share of deposit.

Strengths

  • Multi-timeframe trend filter effectively reduces counter-trend trades and improves win rate
  • Five position sizing modes provide flexibility for different money management approaches
  • Explicit stop-loss and take-profit with default 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio has positive expectancy potential
  • Buy and sell directions can be independently toggled for different market conditions
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate trades on the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Trend determination relies solely on a single bar's open-close difference, lacking trend confirmation and prone to whipsaws in ranging markets
  • The unlimited bar lookback loop for finding closing signals may cause performance issues, especially during backtesting
  • No spread filter or volatility filter — trades are executed even during high-spread or volatile conditions
  • The custom F2a_AO indicator is an external dependency, making it impossible to audit its signal logic reliability
  • No position check or maximum position limit, potentially leading to repeated entries in the same direction

Possible improvements

  • Add trend confirmation mechanism such as multi-bar trend or ADX filter to reduce false signals in ranging markets
  • Add a maximum bar lookback limit for the closing signal search loop to prevent performance degradation
  • Add spread filter and maximum position count limits to improve capital safety

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Unlimited bar lookback in closing signal search poses performance risk and potential infinite loop
  • Lack of position check may cause repeated entries in the same direction leading to over-concentration
  • Oversimplified trend determination can cause frequent whipsaw entries and consecutive losses in ranging markets
