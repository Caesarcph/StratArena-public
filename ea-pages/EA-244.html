EA-244 · Breakout

grr-al

This EA monitors each new bar's opening price and trades on a breakout of DELTA points from that level within the same bar. It enters a buy when price moves above the bar open by DELTA points, or a sell when it moves below by DELTA points, with fixed SL/TP and no position management. The strategy is a simple intrabar breakout system with no trailing stops or multi-position logic.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is an intrabar breakout trading system that uses the new bar's opening price as a reference level, entering when price breaks away from that level by a defined DELTA within the same bar. Entry conditions: after a new bar forms, if the current bid price exceeds the bar open by DELTA points, a SELL order is placed; if the bid falls below the bar open by DELTA points, a BUY order is placed — notably the logic appears inverted, selling on upward breakouts and buying on downward breakouts, which suggests a possible logic error. Exit conditions use fixed stop loss of 100 points and take profit of 700 points, yielding a risk-reward ratio of 1:7. Position sizing uses a fixed BASELOT of 0.1 when RISK is 0, otherwise calculates lot size based on 90% of free margin multiplied by the RISK factor, capped at the broker's maximum lot. Money management has redundant code (duplicate RISK==0 check) and lacks any check for existing open positions, which could lead to over-trading on strong trending moves.

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic that is easy to understand and modify
  • Take profit is much larger than stop loss (1:7 risk-reward ratio), where a single win can cover multiple losses
  • Uses magic number to identify orders, preventing conflicts with other EAs
  • Has OrderCheck pre-validation to avoid sending invalid orders
  • Each bar only triggers one breakout signal (br flag control), preventing repeated entries within the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Entry logic appears inverted: sell triggered on price rise and buy triggered on price decline, contradicting typical breakout strategy convention
  • No position check exists, potentially opening duplicate positions in the same direction leading to overexposure
  • Stop loss and take profit use fixed point values without considering ATR or market volatility, causing inconsistent performance across different instruments
  • Duplicate RISK==0 check indicates redundant code
  • Using ORDER_FILLING_FOK may cause full order rejection in illiquid markets
  • Does not account for spread, swap, or other trading costs that impact strategy profitability

Possible improvements

  • Fix the entry logic direction: upward breakout should trigger buy not sell, downward breakout should trigger sell not buy, or explicitly document this as a reversal strategy
  • Add position count checks to limit same-direction or total open positions, preventing overexposure risk
  • Change stop loss and take profit to ATR-based dynamic calculation so the strategy adapts to different instruments and market volatility conditions

Risk assessment

Overall risk: High risk

  • No position check, potentially opening excessive positions during strong trends leading to severe overexposure
  • Entry direction appears inverted, potentially causing positions in the opposite of intended direction and major losses
  • Fixed 100-point stop loss may be too tight for high-volatility instruments and easily triggered by noise
  • Using 90% of free margin for lot calculation when RISK is non-zero can lead to excessive leverage
