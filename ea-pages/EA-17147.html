摘要

该EA实现了一种骑墙突破策略，配合移动止损管理。它在定义的价格水平（通常是时段开盘或区间）上下放置挂单（买入止损和卖出止损），并在价格有利方向移动时跟踪止损，以锁定利润同时让突破交易继续运行。

策略分析

该EA采用骑墙突破策略，核心概念是在关键价格水平两侧同时放置买入止损和卖出止损挂单，等待价格突破任一方向后入场。入场条件是价格突破预设的区间上下轨，触发挂单成交。出场条件依赖于移动止损机制——当价格朝有利方向移动时，止损会跟随移动以锁定浮盈，若价格回撤则触及移动止损平仓。仓位管理方面，EA使用固定手数或基于资金的仓位计算方式下单。资金管理上，通过移动止损替代固定止盈，让利润奔跑的同时控制回撤风险。该策略适合在市场波动性增加的时段（如新闻发布或开盘时段）使用，因为此时更容易出现方向性突破。

Summary

This EA implements a straddle breakout strategy with trailing stop management. It places pending buy-stop and sell-stop orders around a defined price level (typically session open or range) and trails the stop loss as price moves in favor. The trailing mechanism locks in profits while allowing the breakout trade to run.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a straddle breakout strategy where the core concept is placing both buy-stop and sell-stop pending orders on either side of a key price level, waiting for price to break out in either direction to trigger entry. Entry conditions are based on price breaking above or below a predefined range, which activates the corresponding pending order. Exit conditions rely on a trailing stop mechanism — as price moves favorably, the stop loss trails behind to lock in floating profit, and if price reverses, the trailing stop is hit to close the position. Position sizing uses either fixed lots or an equity-based calculation method for order placement. Money management is handled through trailing stops instead of fixed take-profit levels, allowing profits to run while controlling drawdown risk. This strategy is best suited for periods of increased market volatility (such as news releases or session opens) when directional breakouts are more likely to occur.

优势

  • 骑墙策略不需要预测市场方向，突破任一方向均可获利
  • 移动止损机制能有效锁定浮盈，让利润最大化
  • 策略逻辑简洁明了，参数较少，易于理解和调优

劣势

  • 在震荡行情中容易被双杀，上下挂单均被触发后止损出场
  • 缺乏波动性过滤机制，在低波动时段可能频繁无效突破
  • 没有考虑点差和滑点成本，在新闻时段点差扩大可能导致额外亏损

改进建议

  • 增加波动性过滤器（如ATR），仅在波动性足够时放置挂单
  • 添加时段过滤功能，避免在低流动性时段交易

Strengths

  • Straddle strategy does not require predicting market direction — profit possible from breakout in either direction
  • Trailing stop mechanism effectively locks in floating profit and maximizes gains
  • Strategy logic is clean and simple with few parameters, making it easy to understand and optimize

Weaknesses

  • Vulnerable to whipsaws in ranging markets — both pending orders can be triggered and stopped out
  • Lacks volatility filtering, leading to frequent false breakouts during low-volatility periods
  • Does not account for spread widening and slippage costs, which can cause additional losses during news events

Improvements

  • Add a volatility filter (e.g., ATR-based) to only place orders when volatility is sufficient
  • Add session filtering to avoid trading during low-liquidity periods
