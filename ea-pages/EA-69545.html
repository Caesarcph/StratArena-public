VR Breakdown level

This EA implements a simple breakout strategy that trades when price breaks above the previous bar's high (BUY) or below the previous bar's low (SELL). It monitors open positions on each tick and sets Stop Loss and Take Profit levels if they are missing or need updating. The strategy resets breakout levels after a trade is triggered, waiting for the next new bar to establish fresh levels.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on trading breakouts of the previous bar's high and low, which is a classic breakout approach. Entry conditions: a BUY is triggered when the Ask price reaches or exceeds the previous bar's high, and a SELL is triggered when the Bid price reaches or falls below the previous bar's low. Exit conditions rely on fixed Stop Loss and Take Profit values in points (SL=200 points, TP=400 points), giving a risk-reward ratio of 1:2. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (0.01 lots), corrected during initialization to conform to the symbol's volume step, but if the corrected lot falls below the minimum allowed volume it is set to 0, which could prevent the EA from trading entirely. Money management lacks dynamic position sizing or percentage-of-equity calculation, relying entirely on a user-specified static lot value. The strategy resets breakout levels after a successful trade, preventing repeated entries at the same level until a new bar forms and establishes fresh reference levels.

Strengths

  • Simple and clean strategy logic, code is easy to understand and maintain
  • Stop Loss to Take Profit ratio of 1:2 provides a positive risk-reward expectation
  • Breakout levels reset after each trade, avoiding repeated entries at the same level
  • Uses magic number to filter positions, preventing interference with other EAs or manual trades
  • Includes broker compliance check (CheckSL_TP) respecting minimum stop level requirements

Weaknesses

  • No check for existing positions in the same direction, potentially opening multiple positions on the same breakout and overexposing the account
  • Fixed lot size with no dynamic position sizing, unable to adjust risk based on account equity or volatility
  • Breakout strategy generates many false signals in ranging markets, leading to consecutive losses
  • No trade count limit or maximum position limit, risk exposure is uncontrolled
  • If corrected lot size falls below minimum volume it is silently set to 0 with no warning, making the EA unable to trade without user awareness

Possible improvements

  • Add position count limits and same-direction position checks to prevent overexposure
  • Implement dynamic position sizing (e.g., percentage of account equity or ATR-based) instead of fixed lots
  • Add a range/choppy market filter (e.g., ADX or ATR threshold) to pause trading during low-volatility or sideways conditions
  • When corrected lot size falls below minimum, OnInit should return INIT_FAILED and log an error message
  • Add maximum drawdown protection or daily loss limits to prevent catastrophic losses in extreme market conditions

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • No position count limit, potentially opening multiple positions in the same direction leading to overexposure
  • Breakout strategy generates many false signals and consecutive losses in ranging markets
  • No maximum drawdown or daily loss protection mechanism
  • Lot correction failure silently sets lots to 0, user may be unaware the EA cannot trade
