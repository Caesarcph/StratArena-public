摘要

该EA实现了达维斯箱体交易系统，通过识别箱体整理形态并在箱体突破时进行交易。当价格向上突破箱体时产生买入信号，向下突破时产生卖出信号。仓位管理支持止损、止盈及多种资金管理模式。

策略分析

该EA基于达维斯箱体理论，该理论由尼古拉斯·达瓦斯提出，核心思想是在价格形成箱体整理后，等待突破方向确认再入场。入场条件方面，当指标缓冲区6的第1根K线值大于2且第0根K线值小于3时触发买入信号，当值小于2且大于1时触发卖出信号，这本质上是检测箱体上轨或下轨的突破。出场条件包括止损和止盈的固定点数设置，以及反向信号触发平仓——买入信号出现时平卖仓，卖出信号出现时平买仓。仓位管理支持五种模式：按可用保证金比例、按余额比例、按扣除浮亏后的保证金比例、按扣除浮亏后的余额比例、以及固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1手。策略仅在新的K线形成时计算信号，避免逐tick重复计算，但在SignalBar为0时也会每tick计算，这是一个设计考量。

Summary

This EA implements the Darvas Box trading system, which identifies box-like consolidation patterns and trades breakouts from those boxes. It uses a custom DarvasBoxes_System indicator to generate buy signals when price breaks above a box and sell signals when price breaks below. Positions are managed with configurable stop loss, take profit, and money management options.

Strategy Analysis

This EA is based on the Darvas Box theory proposed by Nicolas Darvas, which trades breakouts from box-like consolidation patterns. Entry conditions are determined by reading the custom indicator's buffer 6: when the signal bar value is greater than 2 and the current bar value is less than 3, a buy signal is triggered indicating an upward box breakout; when the signal bar value is less than 2 and the current bar value is greater than 1, a sell signal is triggered indicating a downward box breakout. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit in points, as well as opposite signal closes — buy signals close sell positions and sell signals close buy positions. Position sizing supports five margin modes: percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin minus floating loss, percentage of balance minus floating loss, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter with a default of 0.1. The strategy only recalculates signals on new bar formation to avoid redundant per-tick computation, though when SignalBar is 0, it recalculates every tick which is a notable design choice.

优势

  • 基于经典达维斯箱体理论，策略逻辑清晰且有历史依据
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，灵活适应不同风险偏好
  • 新K线过滤机制减少冗余计算，提高执行效率
  • 止损止盈可配置，风险控制参数完备
  • 买卖方向可独立开关，便于单向交易或对冲测试

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标DarvasBoxes_System，指标逻辑不透明，无法独立验证信号质量
  • 止损和止盈为固定点数，未根据箱体高度或ATR动态调整，可能不适配市场波动
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时段过滤，在不利条件下可能产生滑点较大的成交
  • SignalBar为0时每tick都重新计算信号，可能导致频繁交易
  • 指标缓冲区6的魔法数字2和3含义不明，代码可读性差

改进建议

  • 增加ATR动态止损止盈，根据市场波动自动调整止损距离
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段控制，避免在高点差或非活跃时段交易
  • 用枚举常量替代魔法数字2和3，提高指标信号状态的可读性
  • 增加最大持仓数量限制和最大回撤保护，增强风险控制

Strengths

  • Based on the well-established Darvas Box theory with clear and historically validated logic
  • Supports multiple money management modes for flexible risk preference adaptation
  • New bar filtering reduces redundant calculations and improves execution efficiency
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit with complete risk control parameters
  • Buy and sell directions can be independently toggled for directional or hedging testing

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom DarvasBoxes_System indicator whose internal logic is opaque and unverifiable
  • Fixed point stop loss and take profit do not adapt to market volatility via box height or ATR
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, potentially executing in unfavorable conditions
  • When SignalBar is 0, signals recalculate every tick which may cause excessive trading
  • Magic numbers 2 and 3 from indicator buffer 6 are unexplained, hurting code readability

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit to adapt to market volatility automatically
  • Implement spread filter and trading session controls to avoid high-spread or low-liquidity periods
  • Replace magic numbers 2 and 3 with named enum constants for better signal state readability
  • Add maximum position count limits and maximum drawdown protection for enhanced risk control
