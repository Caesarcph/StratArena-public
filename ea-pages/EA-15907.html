摘要

该EA实现了Darvas箱体交易系统，通过识别箱体形态的交易区间并在突破时进行交易。它使用自定义的DarvasBoxes_System指标来检测向上和向下突破信号，然后相应地开立买入或卖出仓位。系统包含可配置的资金管理、止损止盈以及双边开仓和平仓的独立控制。

策略分析

该EA基于Darvas箱体理论，这是一种经典的突破交易策略，由Nicolas Darvas提出。策略通过DarvasBoxes_System自定义指标识别价格形成的箱体区间，当价格向上突破箱体顶部时产生买入信号（Col[1]>2且Col[0]<3），当价格向下突破箱体底部时产生卖出信号（Col[1]<2且Col[0]>1）。入场条件完全依赖指标的缓冲区数值判断，指标的第6个缓冲区提供信号数据，Col[1]判断突破方向，Col[0]确认突破强度。出场方面，EA使用固定的止损和止盈（默认1000和2000点），同时当反向突破信号出现时会主动平仓。仓位管理通过MM参数和MMMode枚举支持多种模式，包括按自由保证金、余额、扣除亏损后的保证金比例以及固定手数。EA还实现了新K线检测机制避免重复信号，并通过SignalBar参数允许用户选择信号触发位置。

Summary

This EA implements the Darvas Boxes trading system, which identifies box-shaped trading ranges and trades breakouts from those ranges. It uses the custom DarvasBoxes_System indicator to detect upward and downward breakout signals, then opens buy or sell positions accordingly. The system includes configurable money management, stop loss/take profit, and separate controls for opening and closing positions on both sides.

Strategy Analysis

This EA is based on the Darvas Box theory, a classic breakout trading strategy proposed by Nicolas Darvas. The strategy identifies box-shaped trading ranges through the DarvasBoxes_System custom indicator and trades breakouts from those ranges. When price breaks above the box top, a buy signal is generated (Col[1]>2 and Col[0]<3), and when price breaks below the box bottom, a sell signal is generated (Col[1]<2 and Col[0]>1). Entry conditions rely entirely on the indicator's buffer values, where buffer index 6 provides signal data with Col[1] determining breakout direction and Col[0] confirming breakout strength. For exits, the EA uses fixed stop loss and take profit levels (default 1000 and 2000 points respectively), and also actively closes positions when opposing breakout signals appear. Position sizing supports multiple modes through the MM parameter and MMMode enumeration, including percentage of free margin, balance, margin after deducting losses, and fixed lot size. The EA implements a new bar detection mechanism to avoid duplicate signals and allows users to select the signal trigger bar via the SignalBar parameter.

优势

  • Darvas箱体是经典且经过长期验证的突破策略，逻辑清晰可靠
  • 提供多种资金管理模式，灵活性高，适应不同风险偏好
  • 买入和卖出的开仓平仓可独立控制，便于精细化管理策略
  • 实现了新K线检测机制，避免同一根K线上重复开仓
  • 使用已完成K线（SignalBar=1）作为信号源，减少虚假信号

劣势

  • 完全依赖自定义指标DarvasBoxes_System，指标算法不透明且不可审查
  • 止损和止盈为固定点数，未考虑市场波动性（如ATR），在不同行情下可能不合适
  • 缺少加仓和减仓逻辑，仓位管理较为简单
  • 没有点差过滤和交易时段过滤，在高点差或低流动性时段可能造成不利成交
  • 信号判断使用硬编码的数值阈值（2和3），缺乏自适应能力

改进建议

  • 增加基于ATR的动态止损止盈，使风险管理适应市场波动性变化
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免不利交易环境下的入场

Strengths

  • The Darvas Box is a classic and well-validated breakout strategy with clear and reliable logic
  • Multiple money management modes provide flexibility to suit different risk preferences
  • Independent control of buy/sell opening and closing enables fine-grained strategy management
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents duplicate position openings on the same bar
  • Using completed bars (SignalBar=1) as signal source reduces false signals

Weaknesses

  • Complete dependency on the custom DarvasBoxes_System indicator whose algorithm is opaque and not auditable
  • Fixed stop loss and take profit in points without considering market volatility (e.g. ATR), which may be unsuitable across different market conditions
  • Lacks position scaling logic (add/reduce), making position management relatively simplistic
  • No spread filter or trading session filter, potentially leading to unfavorable fills during high spread or low liquidity periods
  • Signal judgment uses hardcoded numeric thresholds (2 and 3), lacking adaptability to changing market conditions

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit to adapt risk management to changing market volatility
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid entries during unfavorable trading conditions
