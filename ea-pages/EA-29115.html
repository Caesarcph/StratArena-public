CoupleHedgeEA v3.0

A multi-currency couple hedging EA that uses 3-5 currencies to form 3-30 pair groups, opening correlated hedge positions (plus/minus suites) on each group. It manages positions with configurable step progressions (statical/geometrical/exponential), multiple close-in-profit modes (side-by-side, both-sides, hybrid), and includes session control, spread filtering, and auto lot sizing.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on correlation hedging between currency pairs, combining 3-5 currencies into multiple pair groups (couples), each containing two pairs. Entry conditions open positions simultaneously on both pairs in a group — if the two pairs share the same base or quote currency, one buys and one sells; otherwise both trade in the same direction, forming Plus andMinus hedging suites. Exit conditions support five profit-close modes (side-by-side, both-sides, hybrid 1, hybrid 2, and hybrid 1+2) along with configurable loss-close modes (whole side, partial side, or no loss close). Position sizing uses progressive scaling with three step progression modes (statical, geometrical, exponential) and four lot progression modes (static, geometrical, exponential, decreasing lot), adding positions at set step intervals when in loss. Money management offers auto lot sizing with a risk factor, maximum lot size limits, maximum order count limits, spread filtering, and session control (waiting after Monday open, stopping before Friday close) to manage overall risk exposure.

Strengths

  • Multi-currency hedging design leverages currency correlations to diversify directional risk
  • Flexible step and lot progression modes (statical/geometrical/exponential) adapt to different volatility environments
  • Session control (Monday open wait, Friday close stop) and spread filtering reduce low-liquidity period risks
  • Supports up to 5 currencies forming 30 hedge groups, offering rich combination flexibility
  • Dual close mechanism for both profit and loss with multiple hybrid close modes available

Weaknesses

  • Geometrical/exponential step progression causes position size and risk to escalate rapidly during sustained losses, exhibiting implicit martingale characteristics
  • Heavy use of global variables and fixed-size arrays results in poor maintainability and limited extensibility
  • Default configuration does not close in loss (Not_Close_In_Loss), potentially allowing floating losses to grow indefinitely
  • Currency pair combination logic is hardcoded and cannot dynamically adapt to different broker symbol lists
  • No stop-loss mechanism — relies entirely on hedging combinations and profit/loss targets for risk control, risking account blow-up in extreme markets

Possible improvements

  • Add hard stop-loss mechanism with maximum floating loss limit per hedge group to prevent account blow-up in extreme markets
  • Refactor hardcoded currency pair combination logic into a dynamic generation algorithm for better maintainability and extensibility
  • Implement new bar detection to avoid executing full logic on every tick, reducing CPU and server load

Risk assessment

Overall risk: High risk

  • Geometrical/exponential step progression causes exponential position growth during consecutive losses, creating implicit martingale risk
  • Default no-loss-close setting allows floating losses to grow indefinitely until margin call
  • No hard stop-loss — relies entirely on hedge pair profits for closure, which fails in extreme trending markets
  • No new bar check mechanism may lead to over-trading in high-frequency tick environments
