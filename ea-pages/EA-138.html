摘要

Puria是一个趋势跟踪EA，使用三重移动平均线交叉系统结合MACD确认来生成买卖信号。当快速EMA(5)上穿两条慢速LWMA(75,85)且MACD大于零时做多，反之做空。仓位使用固定止损和止盈管理，同一时间只允许持有一个头寸。

策略分析

该EA采用三重移动平均线交叉策略作为核心概念，结合MACD指标进行趋势确认。入场条件为：做多时，EMA(5)必须同时高于LWMA(75)和LWMA(85)，前一根K线收盘价高于EMA(5)，且MACD值大于零；做空条件则完全相反。出场条件完全依赖固定的止损和止盈水平，没有追踪止损或其他动态出场机制。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，不根据账户余额或风险比例动态调整。资金管理方面存在明显不足，没有最大回撤控制、没有仓位比例计算，且止损止盈点数较小，在5位报价平台上会乘以10但整体风险敞口仍然较大。

Summary

Puria is a trend-following EA that uses a triple Moving Average crossover system combined with MACD confirmation to generate buy and sell signals. It enters long when the fast EMA(5) crosses above two slow LWMA(75,85) with MACD above zero, and enters short on the inverse condition. Positions are managed with fixed Stop Loss and Take Profit levels, and only one position at a time is allowed.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a triple moving average crossover strategy as its core concept, combined with MACD confirmation for trend validation. Entry conditions for a buy require EMA(5) to be above both LWMA(75) and LWMA(85), the previous bar's close price above EMA(5), and MACD above zero; sell conditions are the exact inverse. Exit conditions rely entirely on fixed Stop Loss and Take Profit levels, with no trailing stop or other dynamic exit mechanisms. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size approach without any dynamic adjustment based on account balance or risk percentage. Money management is noticeably deficient—there is no maximum drawdown control, no risk-based position sizing, and the relatively small SL/TP values (14/15 pips) combined with the one-position-at-a-time constraint limit both risk and reward potential.

优势

  • 使用多重指标确认信号，EMA与LWMA结合加MACD过滤，减少假信号
  • 每笔交易都有固定止损和止盈，风险可控
  • 仅允许同时持有一个头寸，避免过度交易和仓位叠加风险
  • 新K线检测机制避免同一K线内重复开仓

劣势

  • 固定手数不做动态仓位调整，无法根据账户风险比例自适应
  • 止损止盈点数较小（14/15点），容易被市场噪音触发止损
  • 没有追踪止损机制，无法锁定浮动利润
  • PositionSelect仅按Symbol选择，多EA同品种运行会冲突
  • 缺少点差过滤，在小止损情况下点差扩大可能导致不利成交
  • MA参数硬编码在OnInit中与input参数不一致，input参数实际未被使用

改进建议

  • 修复input参数未使用的问题，在iMA调用中使用MA1_Period、MA2_Period、MA3_Period替代硬编码值
  • 添加动态仓位计算，根据账户余额和风险比例自动调整手数
  • 增加追踪止损功能，在盈利达到一定点数后自动移动止损以锁定利润
  • 添加点差过滤，当点差超过止损的一定比例时跳过交易信号

Strengths

  • Uses multiple indicator confirmation combining EMA, LWMA, and MACD filter to reduce false signals
  • Every trade has a fixed Stop Loss and Take Profit, keeping risk bounded
  • Only one position allowed at a time, preventing overtrading and position stacking
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated entries within the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing with no dynamic position adjustment based on account risk percentage
  • Small SL/TP values (14/15 pips) make the strategy vulnerable to market noise and spread widening
  • No trailing stop mechanism to lock in floating profits as the trade moves favorably
  • PositionSelect uses Symbol only, causing conflicts when multiple EAs run on the same instrument
  • No spread filter, which is problematic given the tight stop loss that can be easily overcome by spread expansion
  • MA period parameters are hardcoded in OnInit (75, 85, 5) while input parameters (MA1_Period, MA2_Period, MA3_Period) are never used in indicator creation

Improvements

  • Fix the unused input parameters by replacing hardcoded values (75, 85, 5) in iMA calls with MA1_Period, MA2_Period, and MA3_Period
  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account balance and risk percentage to automatically adjust lot sizes
  • Implement a trailing stop feature that moves the stop loss to lock in profits after a threshold is reached
  • Add a spread filter to skip trade signals when the spread exceeds a certain percentage of the stop loss
