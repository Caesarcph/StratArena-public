摘要

该EA基于TSI_CCI自定义指标的交叉信号进行交易，该指标结合了真实强度指数平滑与CCI动量。当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿时开空仓，并支持可配置的平仓和资金管理选项。

策略分析

策略概念基于TSI_CCI指标的双重平滑CCI动量交叉信号。入场条件为：当指标线(Ind)上穿信号线(Sign)时触发买入开仓，当指标线下穿信号线时触发卖出开仓，同时要求前一根K线上指标线已处于信号线另一侧以确保交叉确认。出场条件包括：指标线上穿信号线时平卖仓，下穿信号线时平买仓，同时可配置固定点数的止损(1000点)和止盈(2000点)。仓位管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，支持五种资金管理模式(自由保证金、余额、亏损后自由保证金、亏损后余额、固定手数)。资金管理采用MarginMode枚举选择模式，默认为固定手数(LOT)模式，MM参数0.1代表10%的资金使用比例。策略使用IsNewBar机制确保每个K线只计算一次信号，避免重复入场，同时通过Recount标志处理指标数据加载失败的重试逻辑。

Summary

This EA trades based on crossover signals from the TSI_CCI custom indicator, which combines True Strength Index smoothing with CCI momentum. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions on the opposite crossover, with configurable position closing and money management options.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on crossover signals from the TSI_CCI indicator, which applies triple smoothed moving average processing to CCI momentum values. Entry conditions: a buy signal triggers when the indicator line (Ind) crosses above the signal line (Sign) with confirmation that the previous bar had Ind below Sign, and vice versa for sell signals. Exit conditions: sell positions close when the indicator crosses above the signal line, buy positions close on the opposite crossover, supplemented by configurable Stop Loss (1000 points) and Take Profit (2000 points). Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter with five money management modes: Free Margin, Balance, Loss Free Margin, Loss Balance, and Fixed Lot, defaulting to Fixed Lot mode. The money management allows risk allocation of 10% (MM=0.1) by default across the selected margin mode. The strategy uses an IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are calculated only once per bar, preventing duplicate entries, and employs a Recount flag to handle indicator data loading failures with automatic retry logic.

优势

  • 使用三重平滑CCI动量指标，信号经过充分过滤减少假突破
  • 支持10种平滑算法选择，策略灵活性高，可适应不同市场环境
  • 包含IsNewBar新K线检测机制，避免同一K线重复开仓
  • 提供完整的买卖开平仓开关，可灵活控制交易方向
  • 有Recount重试机制处理指标数据未就绪的情况，增强鲁棒性

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点相对较大，在低波动市场可能等待时间过长
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差品种上可能造成不必要的损失
  • 依赖外部自定义指标TSI_CCI，部署时需确保指标文件存在
  • 没有持仓数量限制，在极端行情下可能连续开仓导致过度暴露
  • 指标参数较多(3个平滑长度+相位+方法)，优化难度大且容易过拟合

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤机制，当点差超过阈值时暂停开仓
  • 增加最大持仓数量限制和每日最大交易次数限制，控制风险暴露
  • 将止损止盈改为ATR动态计算，适应不同波动率环境

Strengths

  • Triple-smoothed CCI momentum indicator provides well-filtered signals reducing false breakouts
  • Supports 10 smoothing algorithms offering high strategy flexibility for different market conditions
  • IsNewBar mechanism prevents duplicate entries within the same bar
  • Complete buy/sell open/close toggles allow flexible directional control
  • Recount retry mechanism handles indicator data unavailability, improving robustness

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss 1000 and take profit 2000 points are relatively large, may require long wait times in low volatility markets
  • No spread filter, which can cause unnecessary losses on high-spread instruments
  • Depends on external custom indicator TSI_CCI, deployment requires ensuring the indicator file exists
  • No position count limit, potentially leading to overexposure in extreme market conditions
  • Many indicator parameters (3 smoothing lengths + phase + method) make optimization difficult and prone to overfitting

Improvements

  • Add a spread filter mechanism to pause trading when spread exceeds a threshold
  • Add maximum position count and daily trade count limits to control risk exposure
  • Convert fixed SL/TP to ATR-based dynamic calculation to adapt to different volatility environments
