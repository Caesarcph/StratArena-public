摘要

该EA使用移动平均线实现逆势均值回归策略。当价格显著偏离移动平均线时，它逆势开仓，预期价格将回归均值。策略在回撤时建仓，并通过止盈止损水平管理出场。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略概念是逆势交易，即当价格偏离移动平均线达到一定幅度时，反向开仓预期价格回归均值。入场条件是价格相对于均线的偏离度超过预设阈值，可能在均线上方过度延伸时做空，在均线下方过度偏离时做多。出场条件依赖固定点数的止盈和止损，止盈设于均线附近或回归目标位，止损设于偏离继续扩大的位置。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，未见动态仓位调整或基于ATR的自适应手数计算。资金管理方面缺乏风险百分比控制，没有根据账户余额或波动率动态调整仓位大小的机制，整体风控较为简单。

Summary

This EA implements a contrarian mean-reversion strategy using moving averages. It enters trades against the prevailing trend when price deviates significantly from a moving average, anticipating a reversion to the mean. The strategy opens positions on pullbacks and manages exits via take-profit and stop-loss levels.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy concept is contrarian trading, entering positions against the prevailing move when price deviates significantly from a moving average, anticipating a mean reversion. Entry conditions are triggered when price deviation from the MA exceeds a predefined threshold — going short when price is overextended above the MA and going long when price is oversold below the MA. Exit conditions rely on fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels in points, with TP set near the mean reversion target and SL placed where the deviation would continue to widen. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach with no dynamic lot calculation or ATR-based adaptive sizing. Money management lacks percentage-based risk control and does not adjust position size based on account balance or volatility, making the overall risk management relatively simplistic.

优势

  • 逆势交易逻辑清晰，在震荡市中有较好表现
  • 使用均线偏离度作为入场依据，概念直观易懂
  • 设定了明确的止盈止损，有基本的风控框架

劣势

  • 固定手数无动态仓位管理，无法根据账户风险调整暴露
  • 逆势策略在强趋势市场中会持续亏损，缺乏趋势过滤机制
  • 缺少新K线检查，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓
  • 无点差过滤，在高点差环境下不利

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤器（如ADX或大周期均线方向），避免在强趋势中逆势开仓
  • 引入基于账户余额百分比或ATR的动态仓位管理
  • 添加新K线检查和点差过滤以提升执行质量

Strengths

  • Clear contrarian logic that performs well in ranging markets
  • Uses MA deviation as entry signal, concept is intuitive and easy to understand
  • Has defined TP/SL providing a basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing without dynamic position management cannot adjust exposure to account risk
  • Contrarian strategy suffers continuous losses in strong trending markets, lacks trend filter
  • Missing new bar check may cause repeated entries on the same candle
  • No spread filter makes it vulnerable in high-spread conditions

Improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., ADX or higher timeframe MA direction) to avoid counter-trend entries during strong trends
  • Introduce dynamic position sizing based on account equity percentage or ATR
  • Add new bar check and spread filter to improve execution quality
