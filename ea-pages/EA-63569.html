MartingalePulse

A multi-strategy Expert Advisor that combines four price action entry methods (HFT tick momentum, engulfing candlestick, RSI reversal, EMA crossover) with a Martingale position sizing system. It features advanced risk management including daily profit/drawdown limits, trading session filters, trailing stops, and margin checks. The EA increases lot size after losses using configurable Martingale types and resets on profit or when the maximum order count per round is reached.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy concept combines technical analysis entry signals with a Martingale money management system. Entry conditions offer four options: HFT tick momentum requires N consecutive ticks moving in the same direction; engulfing pattern requires the previous candle to be fully engulfed by the current one; RSI reversal requires RSI crossing back from overbought/oversold zones; EMA crossover requires the close price crossing the EMA line. Exit conditions rely on a fixed-point stop loss and a take profit calculated from the risk-reward ratio, with an optional trailing stop. Position sizing uses a Martingale approach that increases lot size after losses via three modes—classic multiplier, multiplier plus initial lot, or additive increment—and resets on profit. Money management supports fixed lot sizing or risk-percentage-based initial lot calculation, with daily profit caps and drawdown protection. The EA also supports three entry methods—market orders, stop orders, and limit orders—and trading session control configurable per day of the week.

Strengths

  • Offers four different entry strategy options, providing good flexibility
  • Includes daily profit cap and drawdown protection, showing risk awareness
  • Supports trading session filtering to avoid unfavorable time periods
  • Martingale rounds have a maximum order count limit, preventing infinite doubling
  • Well-structured code with good modularity and margin sufficiency checks

Weaknesses

  • Martingale strategy is inherently high-risk; lot sizes grow exponentially during losing streaks, rapidly draining the account
  • Market orders are placed without SL/TP first then modified, risking naked positions if modification fails due to slippage
  • EMA crossover and some strategies lack new-bar checks, potentially triggering repeated signals on the same bar
  • No spread filter is implemented, which can cause losses during high-spread periods especially on smaller timeframes
  • Reversal logic closes the current position and waits for the next tick to open a new one, potentially missing optimal entry prices in fast markets

Possible improvements

  • Add direct SL/TP setting for market order entries to avoid the risk window of opening then modifying
  • Implement consistent new-bar checks across all entry strategies to prevent repeated signals on the same bar
  • Add a spread filter to pause entries when spread exceeds a configurable threshold
  • Consider adding a total monetary loss limit for Martingale cycles, complementing the order count limit with a capital dimension
  • Improve reversal logic to open the opposite position immediately after close confirmation instead of waiting for the next tick

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Dangerous

  • Martingale strategy causes exponential lot size growth during consecutive losses, potentially leading to account blowout
  • Market orders are opened first then SL/TP is set via modification, risking unprotected positions if modification fails
  • Default Martingale multiplier of 2.0 with 5 rounds maximum can reach 32x the initial lot size in extreme cases
  • No spread filter means entries during high-spread periods can generate unnecessary losses
  • Waiting for the next tick after reversal close may result in worse entry prices during volatile markets
