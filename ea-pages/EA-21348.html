Cloud's Trade 2

Cloud's Trade 2 is a trend-following Expert Advisor that uses Ichimoku Cloud (Kumo) as its primary indicator to identify trend direction and generate trade signals. It enters long positions when price breaks above the cloud and short positions when price breaks below, using the Tenkan-sen/Kijun-sen crossover and cloud color for confirmation. The EA includes basic stop loss and take profit management with position sizing controls.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the Ichimoku Kinko Hyo system, using the Kumo (Cloud) as the primary trend determination tool. For entry conditions: a long signal requires price breaking above the cloud from below, the Tenkan-sen crossing above the Kijun-sen, and the cloud being bullish (rising cloud); a short signal is the inverse, requiring price breaking below the cloud from above, Tenkan-sen crossing below Kijun-sen, and a bearish (falling) cloud. Exit conditions include fixed stop loss and take profit levels, as well as closing positions when price re-enters the cloud. Position sizing uses either fixed lots or a dynamic lot calculation based on account balance percentage. Money management incorporates maximum position count limits and per-trade risk controls, but does not employ sophisticated money management algorithms. The overall strategy logic is clear and follows a classic trend-following paradigm suited for medium-to-long-term trending markets, though it may generate numerous false signals in ranging/choppy conditions.

Strengths

  • Uses the well-established Ichimoku Kinko Hyo system with multi-dimensional trend confirmation, reducing false signals
  • Entry requires triple confirmation (price breakout, line crossover, cloud color), providing robust and logically sound trade signals
  • Includes complete parameter design with stop loss, take profit, and position sizing controls for basic risk management

Weaknesses

  • Lacks a ranging market filter, prone to frequent false breakout signals and consecutive losses in choppy conditions
  • Stop loss and take profit use fixed pip values without ATR-based dynamic adjustment, limiting adaptability across varying volatility regimes
  • Missing new bar check mechanism, potentially triggering multiple signals within the same bar leading to duplicate position openings

Possible improvements

  • Add an ADX or volatility filter to reduce trade frequency or pause trading during ranging markets, improving overall win rate
  • Replace fixed stop loss/take profit with ATR-based dynamic levels to make risk management adaptive to different market volatility environments

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Missing new bar check, potentially opening duplicate positions within the same bar and increasing risk exposure
  • Fixed stop loss/take profit does not account for market volatility, potentially resulting in stops that are too tight during high-volatility periods
