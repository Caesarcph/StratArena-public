EA-16786 · Mean Reversion

exp_centerofgravitycandle

This EA trades based on the Center of Gravity indicator applied to candle data, entering positions when price deviates from the calculated center of gravity and expecting a reversion to the mean. It generates buy signals when the COG value is at the lower band and sell signals when at the upper band, typically using crossover or extreme-level detection for entries. The strategy systematically opens and closes positions based on these oscillator-style signals without discretionary input.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the Center of Gravity (COG) indicator, an oscillator developed by John Ehlers that uses polynomial regression to calculate the center of gravity of price, determining overbought and oversold market conditions. Entry conditions: buy when the COG indicator crosses upward from the lower band area, indicating price is reverting from oversold territory; sell when the COG indicator crosses downward from the upper band area, indicating price is reverting from overbought territory. Exit conditions typically rely on the COG returning to or crossing the zero line, though stop-loss and take-profit levels may also be configured. Position sizing uses either fixed lots or an equity-proportional calculation depending on parameter settings. Money management may lack a dynamic position-sizing mechanism, with risk control primarily dependent on stop-loss settings. The overall strategy is a classic mean reversion type that performs well in ranging markets but may suffer consecutive losses in strong trending conditions.

Strengths

  • Based on a mathematically grounded polynomial regression model with solid statistical foundation
  • Mean reversion strategy performs stably in ranging markets with relatively high win rates
  • COG indicator is sensitive to price turning points, providing timely entry signals
  • Clear strategy logic with few parameters, easy to understand and optimize

Weaknesses

  • Mean reversion strategy is prone to consecutive losses in strong trending markets, lacking a trend filter
  • Default parameters may not include stop-loss, posing significant capital risk
  • Lacks dynamic position sizing mechanism, unable to adapt to market volatility changes
  • COG indicator has repainting issues, making backtesting results potentially unreliable
  • No multi-timeframe confirmation, single timeframe signals are prone to false breakouts

Possible improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., moving average direction) to pause trading or switch strategy during trending markets
  • Implement ATR-based dynamic stop-loss, take-profit, and position sizing for better risk adaptation
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation to reduce false signals
  • Add repainting detection or use a non-repainting COG variant to ensure backtesting reliability

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Default stop-loss may be zero, creating unlimited loss potential
  • COG indicator repainting causes discrepancy between backtest and live performance
  • Mean reversion strategy lacks protection in trending markets, risking consecutive counter-trend entries
