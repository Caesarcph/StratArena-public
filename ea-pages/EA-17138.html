摘要

该EA是一个趋势跟踪系统，结合ATRStops（基于波动率的追踪止损指标）和StepUpDown指标（基于快速SMA与慢速SMMA交叉背离）来产生买卖信号。当两个指标一致确认趋势方向时开仓，支持基于风险百分比的动态仓位计算、追踪止损和反向信号平仓。交易逻辑通过每分钟定时器事件执行而非逐Tick触发。

策略分析

策略概念基于双重趋势确认：ATRStops指标利用ATR乘数Kv构建动态通道，当价格突破通道上轨时产生多头信号，突破下轨时产生空头信号；StepUpDown指标通过快速SMA(2)与慢速SMMA(30)的相对位置及背离判断短期趋势方向。入场条件要求两个指标同时确认同方向信号（均为+1时做多，均为-1时做空），且同一小时内不重复开仓。出场条件包括三种模式：追踪止损模式（ModifySLTP根据固定点数追踪调整SL/TP）、反向信号平仓模式（CloseByOppositeSignal=Yes时遇到反向信号先开新仓再平旧仓）、以及盈利+反向StepUpDown信号平仓模式。仓位管理支持固定手数和动态手数两种模式，动态模式根据账户余额、风险百分比和杠杆计算手数并按持仓数分摊。资金管理通过CalculateSLTP函数根据风险金额、手数和tick价值计算止损距离，再乘以TPvsSLRatio得到止盈距离，确保风险收益比可控。

Summary

This EA is a trend-following system that combines ATRStops (a volatility-based trailing stop indicator) with a StepUpDown indicator (based on fast SMA vs slow SMMA crossover divergence) to generate buy and sell signals. It opens positions when both indicators agree on trend direction, supports dynamic lot sizing based on risk percentage, trailing stops, and opposite-signal position closing. Trading logic is executed on a timer event every minute rather than on tick events.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on dual trend confirmation: the ATRStops indicator constructs a dynamic channel using ATR multiplied by Kv (2.5), generating bullish signals when price breaks above the upper band and bearish signals when price breaks below the lower band. The StepUpDown indicator determines short-term trend direction by analyzing the relative position and divergence between a fast SMA(2) on typical price and a slow SMMA(30) on median price. Entry conditions require both indicators to confirm the same direction simultaneously (both +1 for buy, both -1 for sell), with an additional guard preventing re-entry within the same hour as the last closed trade. Exit conditions include three modes: trailing stop mode (ModifySLTP adjusts SL/TP by fixed pip distance), opposite-signal closing mode (when CloseByOppositeSignal=Yes, new position opens first then old opposite position closes), and profit-plus-reversal mode (closes when position is profitable and StepUpDown reverses). Position sizing supports both fixed lot and dynamic lot modes, where dynamic mode calculates lot size from account balance, risk percentage, and leverage, then divides by total open positions. Money management via CalculateSLTP computes stop loss distance from risk amount, lots, and tick value, then multiplies by TPvsSLRatio for take profit, ensuring a controllable risk-reward ratio with a minimum SL floor of 38 pips.

优势

  • 双重指标确认机制（ATRStops + StepUpDown）降低了假信号风险，提高了入场可靠性
  • 动态仓位管理根据账户规模和风险百分比自动调整手数，实现了合理的资金管理
  • 多种出场模式灵活可选：追踪止损、反向信号平仓、盈利反转平仓，适应不同交易风格
  • 止损设有38点最小值保护，防止止损过小被市场噪音扫出
  • 使用定时器而非逐Tick执行，减少了频繁计算的资源消耗

劣势

  • 定时器每分钟执行一次但使用分钟变化检测，在低流动性时段可能产生不必要的检查或错过快速行情
  • 内存管理存在严重问题：OnTimer中每次new创建CTrade等对象但从未delete，导致持续内存泄漏
  • ClosePosition函数中popstype参数与PositionGetInteger(POSITION_TYPE)的混用逻辑容易出错，0/1映射到BUY/SELL不够直观
  • StepUpDown指标的逻辑较复杂且缺乏注释，循环中i/j双重索引和mlHi/mlLo判断难以理解和维护
  • CheckOrderCloseTime函数中HistoryOrderGetInteger(order_ID,ORDER_TYPE)用position ID查询order type是错误的API用法

改进建议

  • 修复内存泄漏：将CTrade等对象改为全局初始化而非每次OnTimer中new创建，或在OnTimer末尾添加delete释放
  • 添加点差过滤器，在点差过大时暂停交易以防止滑点过大导致入场价格不利
  • 重构ClosePosition和GetOrderType的参数传递机制，使用明确的枚举类型替代整数0/1映射
  • 为StepUpDown和ATRStops函数添加详细注释，解释算法逻辑和参数含义
  • 添加最大持仓数限制和最大回撤保护，防止单次极端行情中过度开仓

Strengths

  • Dual indicator confirmation (ATRStops + StepUpDown) reduces false signal risk and improves entry reliability
  • Dynamic position sizing automatically adjusts lots based on account size and risk percentage, implementing sound money management
  • Multiple exit modes are flexible: trailing stop, opposite-signal close, and profit-with-reversal close, accommodating different trading styles
  • Minimum SL floor of 38 pips prevents stop loss from being too tight and getting stopped out by market noise
  • Timer-based execution rather than per-tick reduces computational overhead from frequent recalculations

Weaknesses

  • Timer executes every minute with minute-change detection, which may cause unnecessary checks during low liquidity or miss fast market moves
  • Critical memory management issue: CTrade, CSymbolInfo, CPositionInfo, CAccountInfo objects are created with new in OnTimer but never deleted, causing persistent memory leaks
  • ClosePosition function's popstype parameter mapping (0/1 to BUY/SELL) is error-prone and the POSITION_TYPE retrieval before the loop is misleading
  • StepUpDown indicator logic is complex and lacks comments; the dual i/j indexing and mlHi/mlLo determination in the loop is hard to understand and maintain
  • CheckOrderCloseTime incorrectly uses HistoryOrderGetInteger with a position ID to query ORDER_TYPE, which is a wrong API usage in MQL5

Improvements

  • Fix memory leaks: initialize CTrade and related objects globally in OnInit instead of creating with new in OnTimer, or add delete at the end of OnTimer
  • Add a spread filter to pause trading when spread is excessively wide, preventing unfavorable entry prices due to slippage
  • Refactor ClosePosition and GetOrderType parameter passing to use explicit enum types instead of integer 0/1 mapping
  • Add detailed comments to StepUpDown and ATRStops functions explaining algorithm logic and parameter meanings
  • Add maximum position count limit and maximum drawdown protection to prevent over-positioning during extreme market conditions

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • OnTimer中持续new对象不释放导致内存泄漏，长期运行可能导致终端崩溃
  • 反向信号平仓时先开新仓再平旧仓（Sleep(1000)间隔），在剧烈行情中可能同时持有多空仓位增加风险敞口
  • CalculateSLTP中leveragerisk计算将杠杆除以100后取整，高杠杆账户可能导致止损偏小
  • 缺乏最大回撤保护和最大持仓限制，极端行情下可能过度开仓

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Continuous new without delete in OnTimer causes memory leaks; long-running sessions may crash the terminal
  • Opposite-signal closing opens new position first then closes old one after Sleep(1000), which can temporarily hold both long and short positions during volatile moves, increasing risk exposure
  • CalculateSLTP's leveragerisk divides leverage by 100 and rounds down, potentially producing undersized stop losses on high-leverage accounts
  • No maximum drawdown protection or position count limit, risking over-positioning during extreme market conditions
