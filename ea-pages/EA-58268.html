摘要

这是一个布林带指标实现，而非EA交易程序。它根据指定周期和偏差倍数计算并显示上轨、中轨和下轨布林带，作为技术分析工具用于识别超买超卖状态。

策略分析

该文件是一个布林带技术指标而非自动交易EA，因此不具备自动入场和出场逻辑。布林带中轨通常为简单移动平均线(SMA)，上下轨为中轨加减N倍标准差。交易者可手动根据价格触及上轨做空、触及下轨做多来实现均值回归策略。该指标本身不包含仓位管理和资金管理功能，仅提供可视化参考。标准差倍数和周期参数允许用户调整带的宽窄和灵敏度。作为纯指标工具，它不执行任何交易操作，风险完全取决于使用者的手动决策。

Summary

This is a Bollinger Bands indicator implementation for MetaTrader 5, not an Expert Advisor. It calculates and displays the upper, middle, and lower Bollinger Bands on the chart based on a specified period and deviation multiplier, serving as a technical analysis tool for identifying overbought and oversold conditions.

Strategy Analysis

This file is a Bollinger Bands technical indicator rather than an automated trading EA, so it does not contain automatic entry and exit logic. The middle band is typically a simple moving average (SMA), with upper and lower bands set at N standard deviations above and below. Traders can manually implement mean reversion strategies by selling when price touches the upper band and buying when it touches the lower band. The indicator itself does not include position sizing or money management features, providing only visual reference. The deviation multiplier and period parameters allow users to adjust band width and sensitivity. As a pure indicator tool, it executes no trades and risk depends entirely on the user's manual decisions.
