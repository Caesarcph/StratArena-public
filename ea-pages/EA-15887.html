摘要

该EA基于Fisher_org_v1_Sign自定义指标进行交易，在向上信号时开多仓，在向下信号时开空仓。它支持可配置的资金管理，具有多种保证金模式，并分别控制入场和出场信号。仓位通过止损和止盈水平进行管理。

策略分析

该EA使用Fisher变换指标(Fisher_org_v1_Sign)生成交易信号，Fisher变换将价格分布转换为近似正态分布，适合识别超买超卖区域。入场条件：当指标上轨缓冲区(UpValue)出现非零且非EMPTY_VALUE的值时开多仓，当指标下轨缓冲区(DnValue)出现非零且非EMPTY_VALUE的值时开空仓。出场条件：指标出现反向信号时平仓，即上轨信号平空仓，下轨信号平多仓；同时支持止损和止盈出场。仓位管理：支持五种资金管理模式，包括按自由保证金比例、按余额比例、按亏损后自由保证金比例、按亏损后余额比例和固定手数。资金管理：通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1，结合MMMode选择计算模式。该EA还包含一个额外的逻辑——当买入和卖出同时启用且没有反向平仓信号时，会回溯历史K线查找较早的平仓信号。

Summary

This EA trades based on the Fisher_org_v1_Sign custom indicator, opening buy positions on upward signals and sell positions on downward signals. It uses configurable money management with multiple margin modes and supports separate controls for entry and exit signals. Positions are managed with stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

This EA uses the Fisher Transform indicator (Fisher_org_v1_Sign) to generate trading signals. The Fisher Transform converts price distributions into approximately Gaussian normal distributions, which is effective for identifying overbought and oversold conditions. Entry conditions: a buy position is opened when the upper buffer (UpValue) shows a non-zero and non-EMPTY_VALUE value, and a sell position is opened when the lower buffer (DnValue) shows a non-zero and non-EMPTY_VALUE value. Exit conditions: positions are closed on reverse signals — upper buffer signals close sell positions, and lower buffer signals close buy positions; stop loss and take profit levels also serve as exit mechanisms. Position sizing: the EA supports five money management modes including percentage of free margin, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin after losses, percentage of balance after losses, and fixed lot size. Money management: the MM parameter controls the risk ratio (default 0.1) combined with the MMMode selection for calculation mode. The EA also includes an additional logic — when both buy and sell are enabled and no reverse close signal is detected on the current bar, it scans historical bars backward to find an earlier close signal.

优势

  • 策略基于Fisher变换，具有统计学基础，能有效识别超买超卖区域
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，灵活适应不同风险偏好
  • 入场和出场控制独立可配置，允许只入场或只出场等灵活组合
  • 包含新K线检测机制，避免同一K线内重复交易
  • 止损止盈参数可配置，具备基本风险控制

劣势

  • ATR_Period未声明为input变量，无法在策略测试器中优化
  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值过大，可能不适合大多数品种
  • 回溯历史K线查找平仓信号的循环没有上限保护，可能造成性能问题
  • 缺乏点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生不利入场
  • 未检查当前是否已有持仓就开新仓，可能导致重复开仓

改进建议

  • 将ATR_Period改为input变量以便参数优化和界面配置
  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，避免在同一方向重复开仓
  • 为历史K线回溯循环添加最大迭代次数限制，防止性能问题

Strengths

  • The strategy is based on the Fisher Transform with a statistical foundation, effectively identifying overbought and oversold zones
  • Supports multiple money management modes, flexibly adapting to different risk preferences
  • Entry and exit controls are independently configurable, allowing flexible combinations such as entry-only or exit-only
  • Includes a new bar detection mechanism to prevent repeated trades within the same bar
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit parameters provide basic risk control

Weaknesses

  • ATR_Period is not declared as an input variable, preventing optimization in the strategy tester
  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments
  • The loop scanning historical bars for close signals has no upper bound protection, potentially causing performance issues
  • Lacks spread filtering, which may result in unfavorable entries in high-spread environments
  • Does not check for existing positions before opening new ones, potentially causing duplicate positions

Improvements

  • Change ATR_Period to an input variable to enable parameter optimization and UI configuration
  • Add position check logic to avoid opening duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Add a maximum iteration limit to the historical bar scanning loop to prevent performance issues
