摘要

该EA基于BnB（多空力量）自定义指标进行交易，使用平滑动量方法检测趋势方向变化。当指标线上穿信号线时开多仓，下穿时开空仓，并配有可配置的资金管理和仓位关闭逻辑。

策略分析

该EA的策略核心是基于BnB自定义指标的多空力量动量判断，BnB指标通过可配置的平滑算法（支持SMA、EMA、JJMA、T3等多种方法）对成交量加权的多空力量进行平滑处理。入场条件为：当指标线Ind高于信号线Sign时为多头趋势，若前一根K线Ind下穿Sign则触发买入信号；反之当Ind低于Sign时为空头趋势，若前一根K线Ind上穿Sign则触发卖出信号。出场条件通过趋势反转自动触发平仓——多头趋势中触发卖出平仓，空头趋势中触发买入平仓，同时配合StopLoss和TakeProfit参数进行止损止盈。仓位管理支持五种模式：基于可用保证金、余额、扣除亏损后可用保证金、扣除亏损后余额以及固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，MMMode参数选择计算模式。策略默认使用H4周期，SignalBar=1表示使用前一根已收盘K线的信号，避免了未收盘K线的信号漂移问题。

Summary

This EA trades based on the BnB (Bulls and Bears) custom indicator, using a smoothed momentum approach to detect trend direction changes. It opens buy positions when the indicator line crosses above the signal line and sell positions on the reverse crossover, with configurable money management and position closing logic.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the BnB (Bulls and Bears) custom indicator, which uses a configurable smoothing algorithm (supporting SMA, EMA, JJMA, T3, and other methods) to smooth volume-weighted bullish and bearish momentum. Entry conditions: when the indicator line Ind is above the signal line Sign, it indicates a bullish trend, and if the previous bar shows Ind crossing below Sign, a buy signal is triggered; conversely, when Ind is below Sign indicating a bearish trend, if Ind crosses above Sign on the previous bar, a sell signal is triggered. Exit conditions are automatically triggered by trend reversal—sell position closing occurs in bullish trends and buy position closing in bearish trends, supplemented by configurable StopLoss and TakeProfit parameters. Position sizing supports five modes: based on free margin, balance, loss-adjusted free margin, loss-adjusted balance, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter for risk ratio and the MMMode parameter for calculation mode. The strategy defaults to H4 timeframe with SignalBar=1, using the previously closed bar's signal to avoid signal drift from unclosed bars.

优势

  • 使用多种平滑算法（T3/JJMA等），灵活适应不同市场环境
  • SignalBar=1使用已收盘K线信号，避免信号漂移和频繁重算
  • 内置五种资金管理模式，仓位控制灵活多样
  • 支持独立控制买入/卖出的开仓和平仓，策略可灵活裁剪
  • 有NewBar检测机制，避免同一根K线内重复交易

劣势

  • 止损1000点和止盈2000点默认值过大，对多数品种不适用
  • 没有持仓检查机制，可能在已有同向仓位时重复开仓
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • 依赖自定义BnB指标文件，缺少该指标则EA无法运行
  • 没有移动止损或保本止损机制，盈利保护能力不足

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓导致仓位过重
  • 添加点差过滤和移动止损功能，提高交易质量和盈利保护能力

Strengths

  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithms (T3, JJMA, etc.) for flexible adaptation to different market conditions
  • Uses SignalBar=1 with closed bar signals to avoid signal drift and frequent recalculation
  • Built-in five money management modes providing flexible position sizing options
  • Allows independent control of buy/sell opening and closing, enabling flexible strategy customization
  • Implements NewBar detection to prevent repeated trading within the same bar

Weaknesses

  • Default StopLoss of 1000 points and TakeProfit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments
  • No position check mechanism—may open duplicate positions in the same direction
  • Lacks spread filtering, which could lead to unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • Depends on a custom BnB indicator file; the EA cannot run without it
  • No trailing stop or break-even mechanism, limiting profit protection capability

Improvements

  • Add position checking logic to avoid duplicate openings in the same direction that lead to overexposure
  • Add spread filtering and trailing stop functionality to improve trade quality and profit protection
