摘要

MARSi EA是一个结合移动平均线和RSI指标的均值回归策略EA，利用RSI识别超买超卖状态，同时用移动平均线确认趋势方向。当RSI达到极端值且价格触及或穿越移动平均线包络线时入场，旨在捕捉价格回归均值的波动。该EA采用系统化的入场和出场规则，两个指标的参数均可配置。

策略分析

该EA的策略核心是均值回归，结合移动平均线（MA）和相对强弱指数（RSI）两个指标产生交易信号。入场条件方面，当RSI进入超卖区域（低于设定阈值）且价格低于移动平均线下轨时做多；当RSI进入超买区域（高于设定阈值）且价格高于移动平均线上轨时做空。出场条件依赖于RSI回归中性区域或价格回到移动平均线附近，同时可能设置止损和止盈进行风险管理。仓位管理方面，EA支持固定手数交易，参数中可配置交易量大小。资金管理方面，该EA缺乏动态仓位调整机制，未根据账户余额或风险比例自动计算手数，存在过度交易风险。整体策略逻辑清晰但较为简单，依赖指标极端值回归的假设在强趋势市场中可能失效。

Summary

MARSi EA is a Moving Average and RSI combined strategy expert advisor that identifies overbought and oversold market conditions using RSI while confirming trend direction with moving averages. It enters trades on RSI extremes when price is at or beyond moving average envelopes, aiming to capture mean-reversion moves. The EA applies systematic entry and exit rules with configurable parameters for both indicators.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is mean reversion, combining two indicators — Moving Average (MA) and Relative Strength Index (RSI) — to generate trading signals. For entry conditions, the EA goes long when RSI enters the oversold zone (below a configured threshold) and price falls below the lower MA envelope, and goes short when RSI enters the overbought zone (above a configured threshold) and price rises above the upper MA envelope. Exit conditions rely on RSI returning to a neutral zone or price reverting back toward the moving average, with possible stop-loss and take-profit levels for risk management. Position sizing supports fixed lot trading with configurable volume parameters, but lacks dynamic lot calculation based on account equity or risk percentage. Money management is a notable weakness as the EA does not auto-adjust position sizes, creating potential over-trading risk. The overall strategy logic is clear but relatively simple, and the assumption that indicator extremes will revert can fail in strong trending markets.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，双指标组合提供了一定的信号过滤效果，减少虚假信号
  • 均值回归策略在震荡行情中表现较好，能捕捉超买超卖的回归机会
  • 参数可配置性强，用户可根据不同品种和周期调整MA和RSI参数
  • MA和RSI是经典指标的组合，交易者容易理解策略原理

劣势

  • 均值回归策略在强趋势市场中会连续亏损，RSI极端值可长期维持而不回归
  • 缺乏动态资金管理机制，固定手数无法根据账户风险自适应调整
  • 止损默认值为0，意味着若用户不手动设置止损，将面临无限亏损风险
  • 缺少新K线检测机制，可能在同一K线上重复开仓造成过度交易
  • 没有点差过滤，在点差较大的品种上可能导致入场成本过高侵蚀利润

改进建议

  • 增加动态仓位计算功能，根据账户余额和风险比例自动调整交易手数
  • 添加新K线检测机制和点差过滤，防止过度交易和高成本入场
  • 设置合理的止损默认值而非0，并添加最大持仓数量限制以增强风险控制

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic with dual-indicator combination providing signal filtering to reduce false signals
  • Mean reversion strategy performs well in ranging markets, capturing overbought/oversold reversion opportunities
  • Highly configurable parameters allowing users to adjust MA and RSI settings for different instruments and timeframes
  • Classic MA+RSI combination is well-understood by traders, making the strategy rationale easy to grasp

Weaknesses

  • Mean reversion strategy suffers consecutive losses in strong trending markets where RSI extremes can persist without reverting
  • Lacks dynamic money management — fixed lot size cannot auto-adjust based on account risk
  • Stop-loss default is 0, meaning unlimited loss risk if users don't manually configure it
  • No new bar detection mechanism, potentially causing repeated entries on the same bar and over-trading
  • No spread filter, which can erode profits on high-spread instruments where entry costs are excessive

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing that auto-calculates lot size based on account equity and risk percentage
  • Implement new bar detection and spread filter to prevent over-trading and costly entries
  • Set reasonable default stop-loss values instead of 0, and add maximum open position limits for enhanced risk control
