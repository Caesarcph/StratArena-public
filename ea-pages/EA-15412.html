摘要

一个多货币交易面板，支持EURUSD、USDJPY和GBPUSD三个品种的手动和自动交易。自动交易器通过比较最近两根K线的开盘价、最高价、最低价和收盘价来判断动量方向，在7项检查中买入或卖出信号较多时开仓或反转仓位。

策略分析

策略概念基于简单动量比较，通过比较当前K线与前一根K线的Open、High、Low、Close以及它们的组合均值（(H+L)/2、(H+L+C)/3、(H+L+C+C)/4）共7项指标，统计买入和卖出信号数量。入场条件为：当买入信号多于卖出信号时做多，反之作空；若无持仓则新开仓，若有反向持仓则先平仓再反转。出场条件仅通过手动点击CLOSE按钮或CLOSE ALL按钮平仓，自动模式下只在信号反转时才会平仓并反向开仓。仓位管理使用固定手数，可通过面板的加减按钮调整，最小为0.01手，无止损止盈设置。资金管理完全缺失，没有风险控制机制，没有最大持仓限制，没有回撤保护，每品种只允许持有一个仓位。

Summary

A multicurrency trading panel for EURUSD, USDJPY, and GBPUSD that provides both manual and automated trading. The auto-trader generates buy/sell signals by comparing the last two bars' Open, High, Low, and Close prices to determine momentum direction. It opens or reverses positions based on which direction has more momentum signals out of 7 checks.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on simple momentum comparison, comparing the current bar versus the previous bar across Open, High, Low, Close and their derived averages ((H+L)/2, (H+L+C)/3, (H+L+C+C)/4) for a total of 7 signal checks. Entry conditions: when buy signals exceed sell signals, a long position is opened; conversely for short; if no position exists a new one is opened, if an opposite position exists it is closed and reversed. Exit conditions are only through manual CLOSE/CLOSE ALL buttons; in auto mode positions are only closed when the signal reverses direction. Position sizing uses a fixed lot adjustable via panel +/- buttons with a minimum of 0.01 lots, with no stop-loss or take-profit. Money management is completely absent — there is no risk control mechanism, no maximum position limit, no drawdown protection, and only one position per symbol is allowed.

优势

  • 多品种同时监控和交易，提供分散化机会
  • 手动与自动交易模式可切换，灵活性高
  • 面板界面直观，实时显示信号强度百分比和持仓盈亏

劣势

  • 无止损止盈设置，风险完全不可控
  • 策略逻辑过于简单，仅比较两根K线缺乏统计优势
  • 无点差过滤和滑点保护，在M5周期高频交易成本高
  • 自动模式下信号反转立即反向开仓，频繁交易产生大量手续费
  • 仓位管理仅有固定手数加减，无根据账户余额或波动率的动态调整

改进建议

  • 添加止损止盈参数，并设置最大回撤保护机制
  • 增加点差过滤和新K线检查，避免在同一个K线上重复交易
  • 优化信号逻辑，增加更多技术指标确认或延长比较周期

Strengths

  • Multi-symbol monitoring and trading provides diversification opportunities
  • Switchable manual and auto trading modes offer high flexibility
  • Intuitive panel interface with real-time signal strength percentage and position P&L display

Weaknesses

  • No stop-loss or take-profit, risk is completely uncontrolled
  • Strategy logic is overly simplistic, comparing only two bars lacks statistical edge
  • No spread filter or slippage protection, high-frequency trading on M5 incurs significant costs
  • Auto mode immediately reverses on signal flip, causing frequent trades and excessive commission fees
  • Position sizing only has fixed lot increment/decrement, no dynamic adjustment based on account balance or volatility

Improvements

  • Add stop-loss and take-profit parameters with maximum drawdown protection
  • Add spread filter and new bar check to prevent repeated trading on the same bar
  • Improve signal logic with additional technical indicator confirmation or extend the comparison period
