摘要

该EA通过计算综合GO指标来交易，GO指标由开盘价、最高价、最低价和收盘价的移动平均偏差加权和再乘以成交量得到。当GO值超过开仓阈值时做多，低于负阈值时做空，支持自定义开平仓级别。策略使用EMA平滑后的价格而非原始价格来生成动量信号。

策略分析

策略核心是GO指标，其公式为GO=((C-O)+(H-O)+(L-O)+(C-L)+(C-H))*V，其中C、O、H、L分别为收盘价、开盘价、最高价、最低价的移动平均值，V为信号K线的成交量。入场条件：GO值大于OpenLevel时开多仓，GO值小于-OpenLevel时开空仓。出场条件：持有多仓时GO值降至OpenLevel-CloseLevelDif以下则平多仓，持有空仓时GO值升至-(OpenLevel-CloseLevelDif)以上则平空仓。仓位管理方面，支持固定手数或基于账户自由保证金和风险比例的动态手数计算，手数经过规范化处理以符合品种的最小/最大手数和步长要求。资金管理较为简单，仅通过MaximumRisk参数控制风险敞口，但止损和止盈参数默认为0，即不设止损止盈。策略在开仓时会检查止损止盈是否满足品种的最小止损距离要求，但由于StopLoss和TakeProfit硬编码为0，该检查实际不会触发。

Summary

This EA calculates a composite GO indicator from the weighted sum of moving average deviations across Open, High, Low, and Close prices, multiplied by volume. It opens buy positions when GO exceeds a threshold and sell positions when GO falls below a negative threshold, with configurable open/close levels. The strategy uses EMA-smoothed prices rather than raw prices to generate momentum-based signals.

Strategy Analysis

The core of the strategy is the GO indicator, calculated as GO=((C-O)+(H-O)+(L-O)+(C-L)+(C-H))*V, where C, O, H, L are moving average values of Close, Open, High, Low prices respectively, and V is the signal bar's volume. Entry conditions: a buy position opens when GO exceeds OpenLevel, and a sell position opens when GO falls below -OpenLevel. Exit conditions: a buy position closes when GO drops below OpenLevel minus CloseLevelDif, and a sell position closes when GO rises above -(OpenLevel minus CloseLevelDif). Position sizing supports either fixed lots or dynamic lot calculation based on account free margin and the MaximumRisk parameter, with normalization to comply with the symbol's minimum/maximum volume and step requirements. Money management is simplistic, controlling risk exposure only through the MaximumRisk parameter, while StopLoss and TakeProfit are hardcoded to 0, meaning no stop-loss or take-profit is placed by default. The EA includes minimum stop-level checks before opening, but since StopLoss and TakeProfit are always 0, these checks never actually prevent a trade.

优势

  • GO指标综合了多个价格维度的偏差，比单一价格指标提供更全面的市场动量视图
  • 支持固定手数和动态风险比例两种仓位管理模式，灵活性较好
  • 包含最小止损距离检查机制，防止因止损过近被交易所拒绝
  • 使用已确认K线（Shift=1）作为默认信号源，避免未成形K线的信号漂移

劣势

  • 止损和止盈硬编码为0，交易完全依赖信号平仓，存在大幅亏损风险
  • 平仓操作使用3倍点差的滑点容忍度，在流动性差的市场中可能导致不利成交
  • 卖出开仓时错误调用了SolveBuySL和SolveBuyTP函数而非对应的Sell函数，这是严重的逻辑错误
  • GO指标的数学公式中(C-L)+(C-H)与(C-O)+(H-O)+(L-O)存在冗余计算，且物理意义不明确
  • 没有新K线检查机制（Shift=0时每个tick都会执行），可能导致频繁交易

改进建议

  • 修复卖出开仓时错误调用SolveBuySL/SolveBuyTP的bug，应改为SolveSellSL/SolveSellTP
  • 将StopLoss和TakeProfit改为可配置的输入参数，而非硬编码为0
  • 添加新K线检查逻辑，确保即使Shift=0也只在K线变化时执行交易逻辑
  • 添加最大持仓数量和最大回撤限制等资金管理保护机制

Strengths

  • The GO indicator synthesizes deviations across multiple price dimensions, providing a more comprehensive momentum view than single-price indicators
  • Supports both fixed lots and dynamic risk-based position sizing, offering good flexibility
  • Includes minimum stop-level checks to prevent order rejection due to stops being too close
  • Defaults to using confirmed bars (Shift=1) for signals, avoiding signal drift from unconfirmed bars

Weaknesses

  • StopLoss and TakeProfit are hardcoded to 0, leaving positions entirely dependent on signal-based exits with significant loss risk
  • Close operations use a 3-spread slippage tolerance, which may result in unfavorable fills in low-liquidity markets
  • When opening sell positions, the code incorrectly calls SolveBuySL and SolveBuyTP instead of the corresponding Sell functions - a critical logic bug
  • The GO formula has redundant terms since (C-L)+(C-H) overlaps with (C-O)+(H-O)+(L-O), and the physical meaning is unclear
  • No new-bar check mechanism when Shift=0, meaning every tick triggers trading logic and potential excessive trading

Improvements

  • Fix the bug where sell opening incorrectly calls SolveBuySL/SolveBuyTP instead of SolveSellSL/SolveSellTP
  • Make StopLoss and TakeProfit configurable input parameters instead of hardcoding them to 0
  • Add new-bar detection logic so that trading logic only executes on bar changes even when Shift=0
  • Add money management protections such as maximum position count and maximum drawdown limits
