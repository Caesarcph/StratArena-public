摘要

该EA实现了止损反转策略，当价格触及止损水平时反转持仓方向，将每次止损转化为反向新入场信号。它作为一种系统性均值回归策略，始终持仓，基于价格达到预定义反转阈值在多头和空头之间交替。

策略分析

止损反转策略的核心概念是当当前持仓被止损出场时，立即在相反方向开立新仓位，本质上将止损转化为反转信号。入场条件为：当多头仓位被止损平仓后立即开空，当空头仓位被止损平仓后立即开多，始终在市场中保持持仓。出场条件仅依赖止损触发，没有独立的止盈逻辑，盈利完全依赖价格在下次反转前朝有利方向移动足够距离。仓位管理通常使用固定手数或基于资金比例的手数计算，缺乏根据市场波动率动态调整仓位的机制。资金管理方面存在明显不足，因为策略持续持仓且每次反转都产生新交易，在单边趋势行情中会连续止损并反复反转，导致累积亏损风险极大。该策略在震荡行情中表现较好，但在趋势行情中会产生连续亏损，属于典型的均值回归策略特征。

Summary

This EA implements a stop-reversal strategy that reverses position direction when price hits a stop level, effectively turning every stop-loss into a new entry signal in the opposite direction. It operates as a systematic mean-reversion system that continuously stays in the market, alternating between long and short positions based on price reaching predefined reversal thresholds.

Strategy Analysis

The stop-reversal strategy's core concept is that when the current position is stopped out, a new position is immediately opened in the opposite direction, effectively converting each stop-loss into a reversal entry signal. Entry conditions are straightforward: when a long position is stopped out, a short is immediately opened, and vice versa, ensuring the EA is always in the market. Exit conditions rely solely on stop-loss triggers with no independent take-profit logic, meaning profitability depends entirely on price moving favorably enough before the next reversal. Position sizing typically uses fixed lots or balance-proportional sizing without dynamic adjustment based on market volatility or ATR. Money management is notably lacking as the strategy maintains continuous exposure and generates a new trade on every reversal, creating significant cumulative loss risk during trending markets where consecutive stop-outs and reversals erode capital rapidly. The strategy performs reasonably well in ranging or choppy markets but suffers significant drawdowns in strong trending conditions, which is characteristic of mean-reversion systems.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和实现
  • 在震荡行情中能够捕捉反复波动带来的利润
  • 始终持仓避免了错过行情的问题，确保持续参与市场

劣势

  • 在强趋势行情中会连续止损反转导致严重亏损
  • 缺乏趋势过滤机制，无法识别当前市场状态是否适合该策略
  • 没有最大连续亏损次数限制或整体回撤控制，资金安全风险高

改进建议

  • 增加趋势过滤器（如均线方向或ADX指标），在强趋势行情中暂停反转交易或调整止损距离
  • 引入最大连续亏损次数限制和整体回撤保护，达到阈值后停止交易

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and implement
  • Can capture profits from repetitive price swings in ranging markets
  • Always-in-market approach ensures continuous market participation without missing moves

Weaknesses

  • Suffers severe consecutive losses from repeated stop-and-reverse cycles in strong trending markets
  • Lacks trend filtering mechanism to identify whether current market conditions suit the strategy
  • No maximum consecutive loss limit or overall drawdown control, posing high capital safety risk

Improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., moving average direction or ADX indicator) to pause reversal trading or adjust stop distances during strong trends
  • Introduce maximum consecutive loss limits and overall drawdown protection that halts trading when thresholds are reached
