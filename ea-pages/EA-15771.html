摘要

该EA基于iWPRSign自定义指标进行交易，该指标通过威廉百分比（WPR）穿越超买/超卖水平生成买卖信号。它在新K线信号上系统性地开仓和平仓，支持可配置的资金管理模式和固定止损/止盈。

策略分析

策略概念基于威廉百分比范围（WPR）指标的超买超卖反转信号，当WPR穿越上轨（-30）时产生买入信号，穿越下轨（-70）时产生卖出信号。入场条件为：WPR上穿-30水平时做多，WPR下穿-70水平时做空，仅在指定信号K线上触发。出场条件包括固定止损和止盈，以及反向信号平仓——买入信号同时可平卖仓，卖出信号同时可平买仓。仓位管理支持五种模式：自由保证金比例、余额比例、含亏损自由保证金比例、含亏损余额比例及固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，默认0.1手或10%资金。EA还包含历史K线回溯检测逻辑，当同时允许开仓和平仓时，会从信号K线向前搜索最近的反向信号以确认平仓条件。

Summary

This EA trades based on the iWPRSign custom indicator, which generates buy and sell signals from Williams' Percent Range (WPR) crossing overbought/oversold levels. It opens and closes positions systematically on new bar signals, with configurable money management modes and fixed stop-loss/take-profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on Williams' Percent Range (WPR) overbought/oversold reversal signals, generating buy signals when WPR crosses above the -30 upper level and sell signals when WPR crosses below the -70 lower level. Entry conditions are: buy when WPR breaks above -30, sell when WPR breaks below -70, triggered only on the specified signal bar. Exit conditions include fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels, as well as reverse-signal closing where a buy signal can close a sell position and a sell signal can close a buy position. Position sizing supports five modes: free margin percentage, balance percentage, loss-inclusive free margin percentage, loss-inclusive balance percentage, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled by the MM parameter, defaulting to 0.1 lots or 10% of capital. The EA also includes a historical bar lookback detection logic that searches forward from the signal bar for the nearest reverse signal to confirm closing conditions when both opening and closing are enabled simultaneously.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于经典WPR超买超卖反转信号，易于理解和验证
  • 提供多种资金管理模式选择，灵活适应不同风险偏好
  • 支持独立控制开仓和平仓方向，可灵活组合做多做空策略
  • 止损止盈比例1:2，具有正向期望值潜力
  • 使用新K线检测机制避免同根K线重复交易

劣势

  • ATR_Period被硬编码为全局变量而非input参数，无法在策略测试器中优化
  • 缺乏点差过滤机制，在高位点差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • 历史K线回溯搜索平仓逻辑效率低，在K线数量多时可能造成性能问题
  • 未检查当前是否已有同方向持仓，可能重复开仓
  • WPR水平参数固定为-30/-70，缺乏自适应或动态水平调整机制

改进建议

  • 将ATR_Period改为input参数以支持策略优化
  • 添加点差过滤和持仓检查逻辑，避免不利成交和重复开仓
  • 优化历史K线回溯搜索逻辑，限制搜索范围或缓存结果以提高性能

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on classic WPR overbought/oversold reversal signals, easy to understand and verify
  • Multiple money management modes available, flexibly adapting to different risk preferences
  • Independent control of opening and closing directions, allowing flexible long/short strategy combinations
  • Stop-loss to take-profit ratio of 1:2, providing positive expectancy potential
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents repeated trading on the same bar

Weaknesses

  • ATR_Period is hardcoded as a global variable instead of an input parameter, preventing optimization in the strategy tester
  • No spread filter mechanism, potentially leading to unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • Historical bar lookback search for closing logic is inefficient, potentially causing performance issues with many bars
  • No check for existing positions in the same direction, potentially causing duplicate entries
  • WPR level parameters are fixed at -30/-70, lacking adaptive or dynamic level adjustment mechanism

Improvements

  • Change ATR_Period to an input parameter to support strategy optimization
  • Add spread filter and position check logic to avoid unfavorable fills and duplicate entries
  • Optimize historical bar lookback search logic by limiting search range or caching results to improve performance
