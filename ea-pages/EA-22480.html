EA-22480 · Momentum

Exp_XWPR_Histogram_Vol

This EA trades based on the XWPR histogram indicator, which is an enhanced Williams' Percent Range displayed as a histogram around a zero line. Volume confirmation is required before entry, ensuring that momentum signals are supported by actual market participation. The EA opens positions when the WPR histogram crosses the zero line and volume conditions are met.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on the Williams Percent Range (WPR) indicator displayed in histogram mode, transforming the traditional overbought/oversold oscillator into a zero-centered momentum histogram for more intuitive trend identification. Entry conditions require the XWPR histogram to cross above the zero line from negative territory for long positions, and below the zero line from positive territory for short positions, with volume confirmation mandatory to validate the momentum signal. Exit conditions rely on the appearance of a reverse signal, meaning positions are closed when the histogram crosses the zero line in the opposite direction, or through user-configured stop-loss and take-profit parameters. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach controlled by the InpLot parameter, without any dynamic lot sizing based on account equity or risk percentage. Money management provides stop-loss and take-profit parameters but lacks comprehensive account-level risk controls such as maximum drawdown limits, equity protection, or trailing stop mechanisms. The core strength of this strategy lies in combining momentum and volume dual filtering, which effectively reduces false breakout signals that plague single-indicator systems.

Strengths

  • Dual momentum and volume filtering effectively reduces false signals
  • Histogram mode makes WPR signals more intuitive with clear zero-line crossover logic
  • Systematic execution without emotional interference, clear entry and exit rules
  • Volume confirmation increases signal reliability, avoiding misleading signals in low-liquidity markets
  • Simple parameter design allows users to quickly understand and adjust settings

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing lacks dynamic risk management, cannot auto-adjust based on account size
  • Stop-loss and take-profit default to 0, beginners may overlook setting them leading to unlimited risk
  • Single-indicator strategy prone to frequent false signals in ranging markets
  • No trailing stop mechanism, profitable positions may give back significant gains
  • No account-level risk controls such as maximum position limits or drawdown protection

Possible improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account equity and ATR for adaptive risk management
  • Implement trailing stop and break-even functionality to protect accumulated profits
  • Add market regime filter (e.g., ADX) to reduce trading frequency in ranging markets
  • Implement multi-timeframe confirmation mechanism to improve signal reliability

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Stop-loss and take-profit default to zero, unlimited risk exposure when not set
  • Fixed lot sizing ignores account size, small accounts may be over-leveraged
  • Single momentum indicator in sideways markets may cause consecutive losses from frequent trading
