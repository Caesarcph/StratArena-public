EA-1048 · Momentum

MA2CCI

This EA uses a dual moving average crossover combined with a CCI zero-line cross to generate trend-following entry signals. ATR is used for dynamic stop loss placement, and lot sizing is based on either fixed lots or risk-based percentage of free margin with a drawdown reduction mechanism. Positions are closed on the opposite MA crossover signal.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on a dual moving average crossover confirmed by CCI zero-line crosses: a buy signal occurs when the fast MA crosses above the slow MA and CCI crosses above zero simultaneously, while a sell signal requires the fast MA crossing below the slow MA with CCI crossing below zero. Entry conditions demand both the MA crossover and CCI zero-line cross to align, providing a dual filter that reduces false signals in ranging markets. Exit conditions rely on the opposite MA crossover to close positions, with no take-profit level set, depending solely on ATR-based dynamic stop loss for risk control. Position sizing supports two modes: fixed lot size or risk-based lot calculation using a percentage of free margin. Money management includes a drawdown reduction mechanism via the DcF parameter, which progressively decreases lot size after consecutive losses, but only activates when DcF is greater than zero. Stop loss is calculated from the ATR value and validated against the broker's minimum stop level, with orders rejected if the stop loss would be too close. Overall, this is a classic trend-following system that uses CCI to filter MA crossover signals and reduce whipsaw trades in sideways markets.

Strengths

  • Dual signal filtering (MA crossover + CCI confirmation) reduces false signals in ranging markets
  • ATR-based dynamic stop loss adapts to market volatility changes, more rational than fixed stops
  • Drawdown reduction mechanism (DcF parameter) automatically decreases lot size after consecutive losses
  • New bar detection via LastTime prevents multiple trades within the same bar
  • Stop level validation ensures orders comply with broker minimum stop distance requirements

Weaknesses

  • Extremely short MA and CCI periods (default 4/8/4) lead to frequent false signals and overtrading
  • No take-profit level means profits depend entirely on stop loss or opposing signal exits, potentially missing optimal exit points
  • Position check is per-symbol only, lacking global risk control for multi-symbol concurrent operation
  • Email notification uses SendMail with only basic error checking, lacking retry or fallback notification mechanisms
  • The lot reduction formula (aLots-aLots*losses/DcF) can reduce lot size to extremely small values after many consecutive losses

Possible improvements

  • Add take-profit parameter or trailing stop mechanism to lock in profits and optimize risk-reward ratio
  • Add trading session filter and spread filter to avoid trading during unfavorable hours or high-spread conditions
  • Add maximum drawdown limit and maximum consecutive loss count limit as hard risk control floors
  • Optimize default parameter values — 4/8 period MAs and CCI are overly sensitive; consider more robust defaults like 10/20/14

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Extremely short-period MA and CCI parameters generate frequent and unreliable signals, increasing overtrading risk
  • No take-profit mechanism means winning trades may give back significant gains while waiting for opposing signals
  • Drawdown reduction mechanism is completely disabled when DcF=0, providing no consecutive-loss protection
  • Only checks single-symbol positions without global position or risk control
