摘要

该EA使用双均线交叉结合CCI零线交叉来产生趋势跟踪交易信号。ATR用于动态止损设置，仓位管理包含亏损递减因子，在连续亏损后减小仓位。入场要求均线交叉和CCI交叉在同一方向上同时确认。

策略分析

策略核心是双均线交叉确认趋势方向，快线(FMa=4)上穿慢线(SMa=8)为多头信号，下穿为空头信号。CCI指标作为二次过滤，要求CCI同时穿越零线才触发入场，这减少了假突破信号。出场条件同样是均线反向交叉，不设止盈仅靠止损和反向信号平仓。止损基于ATR值动态计算，买仓止损为Bid减ATR，卖仓止损为Ask加ATR，并验证止损距离不小于经纪商最小止损水平。仓位管理方面，当Lots设为0时根据自由保证金和最大风险比例(MaxR)计算仓位，DcF参数在连续亏损时递减手数，起到类似反马丁格尔的资金保护作用。整体策略简洁明确，属于典型的趋势跟踪系统，适合趋势明显的市场环境。

Summary

This EA uses a dual moving average crossover combined with a CCI zero-line crossover to generate trend-following trade signals. ATR is used for dynamic stop-loss placement, and lot sizing incorporates a drawdown reduction factor that decreases position size after consecutive losses. Entries require both the MA crossover and CCI crossover to align in the same direction.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy uses a dual moving average crossover to confirm trend direction, with the fast MA (FMa=4) crossing above the slow MA (SMa=8) as a bullish signal and crossing below as a bearish signal. The CCI indicator serves as a secondary filter, requiring CCI to simultaneously cross the zero line to trigger entry, which reduces false breakout signals. Exit conditions rely on the MA crossover reversing direction, with no take-profit level—positions are closed only by stop-loss or opposing signal. Stop-loss is dynamically calculated based on ATR, with buy stop-loss at Bid minus ATR and sell stop-loss at Ask plus ATR, and the EA validates that the stop distance meets the broker's minimum stop level. For position sizing, when Lots is set to 0, the lot size is calculated from free margin and the MaxR risk ratio; the DcF parameter reduces lot size after consecutive losses, acting as an anti-martingale capital protection mechanism. The overall strategy is clean and straightforward, representing a typical trend-following system best suited for markets with clear directional movement.

优势

  • 双指标过滤（均线+CCI）减少假信号，提高入场质量
  • ATR动态止损适应市场波动性，比固定点数止损更合理
  • DcF亏损递减机制在连续亏损时保护资金，属于良好的资金管理实践
  • 有新K线检查（LastTime机制），避免同一根K线内重复交易
  • 止损距离验证（与StopsLevel比较），防止因止损过近被拒绝

劣势

  • MA和CCI周期都极短（4和8），在震荡市中容易频繁产生虚假交叉信号
  • 没有止盈设置，完全依赖反向信号平仓，可能在趋势反转时回吐大量利润
  • 只允许持有单一品种的单一仓位，无法多品种同时运行
  • 邮件通知使用SendMail但仅打印错误而不做重试或备用处理
  • LotsOptimized中亏损计算逻辑仅统计连续亏损笔数，不考虑亏损幅度

改进建议

  • 增加止盈逻辑（如ATR倍数止盈或追踪止损），避免趋势反转时利润全部回吐
  • 增加更大时间周期的趋势过滤（如H4趋势方向），避免在主趋势相反方向开仓
  • 将极短的默认参数（4/8）调整为更常规的值（如10/20），减少噪音信号

Strengths

  • Dual indicator filter (MA crossover + CCI zero-line cross) reduces false signals and improves entry quality
  • ATR-based dynamic stop-loss adapts to market volatility, more sensible than fixed-point stops
  • DcF drawdown reduction mechanism protects capital during consecutive losing streaks, representing sound money management
  • New bar check (LastTime mechanism) prevents multiple trades within the same bar
  • Stop-loss distance validation against broker StopsLevel prevents rejected orders due to stops too close

Weaknesses

  • Extremely short MA and CCI periods (4 and 8) lead to frequent false crossover signals in ranging markets
  • No take-profit level—positions rely entirely on opposing signal or stop-loss, potentially giving back significant profits during trend reversals
  • Only supports a single position per symbol, cannot run across multiple instruments simultaneously
  • Email notification uses SendMail with only error printing, no retry or fallback mechanism
  • The LotsOptimized loss calculation only counts consecutive losing deals without considering loss magnitude

Improvements

  • Add take-profit logic (e.g., ATR-multiple TP or trailing stop) to avoid giving back all profits during trend reversals
  • Add higher-timeframe trend filter (e.g., H4 trend direction) to avoid trading against the dominant trend
  • Adjust the extremely short default periods (4/8) to more conventional values (e.g., 10/20) to reduce noise signals

风险评估

总体:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 超短周期参数导致高交易频率，在震荡市中可能产生大量亏损交易
  • 没有止盈机制，盈利仓位可能因趋势缓慢反转而变为亏损
  • 仓位递减逻辑在极端情况下（如连续多笔大亏）可能将手数降至最小值但仍持续交易

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Ultra-short period parameters cause high trading frequency, potentially generating many losing trades in ranging markets
  • No take-profit mechanism means profitable positions may turn into losses during slow trend reversals
  • Lot reduction logic may reduce size to minimum but continue trading even during extreme drawdown streaks
