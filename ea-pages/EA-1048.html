摘要

该EA使用双均线交叉结合CCI零线交叉来产生交易信号，并以ATR为基础动态设置止损。包含连亏后减仓逻辑和可选的邮件通知功能。

策略分析

策略概念基于快慢两条简单移动平均线（SMA）的交叉信号，并结合CCI指标穿越零线作为确认条件。入场条件为：快均线上穿慢均线且CCI从负值穿越至正值时做多，快均线下穿慢均线且CCI从正值穿越至负值时做空。出场条件为：持有多头时快均线下穿慢均线平仓，持有空头时快均线上穿慢均线平仓。仓位管理方面，当Lots参数为0时根据账户可用保证金和最大风险比例自动计算仓位，否则使用固定手数。资金管理包含连亏减仓机制：遍历历史成交记录统计连续亏损次数，亏损越多则递减手数越大，递减系数由DcF参数控制。止损基于ATR指标动态计算，并检查止损距离是否满足经纪商的最小止损水平要求。

Summary

This EA uses a dual moving average crossover combined with a CCI zero-line crossover to generate trade signals, with ATR-based dynamic stop loss placement. It includes lot reduction logic after consecutive losses and optional email notifications for trade events.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on a crossover of fast and slow Simple Moving Averages (SMA), confirmed by a CCI zero-line crossover. Entry conditions: go long when the fast SMA crosses above the slow SMA and CCI crosses above zero; go short when the fast SMA crosses below the slow SMA and CCI crosses below zero. Exit conditions: close a long position when the fast SMA crosses below the slow SMA, and close a short position when the fast SMA crosses above the slow SMA. Position sizing uses a fixed lot when the Lots parameter is non-zero, or calculates lot size dynamically based on free margin and maximum risk percentage when Lots is zero. Money management includes a loss-reduction mechanism: the EA iterates through historical deals counting consecutive losses, reducing the lot size proportionally based on the number of consecutive losses and the DcF reduction factor. Stop loss is dynamically calculated using the ATR indicator value, and the EA verifies that the stop loss distance satisfies the broker's minimum stop level requirement before placing an order.

优势

  • 双指标确认机制（均线交叉+CCI确认）减少假信号
  • ATR动态止损适应市场波动性变化
  • 连亏减仓机制有效控制回撤风险
  • 检查止损是否满足经纪商最小止损水平，避免订单被拒
  • 新K线检测避免同一根K线重复交易

劣势

  • 所有指标周期默认为4，过于敏感容易产生频繁信号和噪音交易
  • 止损计算对买入使用Bid-ATR但对卖出使用Ask+ATR，方向逻辑正确但未考虑点差成本
  • 减仓公式在极端连亏情况下可能将手数降至极低值
  • 缺少止盈逻辑，完全依赖反向信号平仓，可能错过利润锁定机会
  • 邮件通知功能依赖终端配置，错误处理仅打印日志无重试机制
  • 仅处理当前品种的单持仓，不支持多品种或加仓策略

改进建议

  • 增加止盈逻辑或追踪止损，以锁定利润并改善风险收益比
  • 优化指标默认周期参数，4周期过短建议至少使用10以上以减少噪音
  • 添加点差过滤条件，避免在点差过大时入场

Strengths

  • Dual indicator confirmation (MA crossover + CCI confirmation) reduces false signals
  • ATR-based dynamic stop loss adapts to market volatility changes
  • Consecutive loss lot reduction mechanism effectively controls drawdown risk
  • Checks stop level against broker minimum stop distance to avoid order rejection
  • New bar detection prevents duplicate trades on the same bar

Weaknesses

  • All indicator periods default to 4, which is overly sensitive and prone to noise and frequent signals
  • Stop loss calculation does not account for spread costs which can affect actual SL distance
  • The lot reduction formula may reduce lot size to extremely low values after extreme consecutive losses
  • No take-profit logic; relies entirely on opposing signal for exit, potentially missing profit locking opportunities
  • Email notification depends on terminal configuration; error handling only logs without retry mechanism
  • Only handles single position on current symbol; no multi-symbol or scale-in strategy support

Improvements

  • Add take-profit logic or trailing stop to lock in profits and improve risk-reward ratio
  • Optimize default indicator period parameters; period 4 is too short, suggest at least 10 to reduce noise
  • Add spread filter to avoid entering trades when spread is excessively wide

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:LOW

风险点

  • 连亏减仓机制可能导致手数降至极低值后长期无法恢复盈利能力
  • 缺少止盈意味着盈利交易可能因市场反转而变为亏损
  • 非常短的指标周期（4）在高波动市场可能产生频繁的错误信号

Risk Assessment

Overall:LOW

Risk Points

  • The consecutive loss reduction mechanism may reduce lot size to extremely low values, making recovery of profitability difficult over long periods
  • Lack of take-profit means winning trades may reverse into losses before the opposing signal triggers exit
  • Very short indicator periods (4) may generate frequent false signals in highly volatile markets
