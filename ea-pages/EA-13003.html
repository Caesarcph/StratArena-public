摘要

该EA基于William Blau的BlauTVI（ ticks量指标）进行交易，利用三重平滑的tick量动量产生买卖信号。当指标方向改变且动量加速时开仓，在反向信号时平仓。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于BlauTVI指标，这是一个由William Blau提出的三重平滑tick量动量指标。入场条件为：当BlauTVI值从上升转为下降（Value[1]>Value[2]）且当前值低于前值（Value[0]<Value[1]）时开空仓；当BlauTVI值从下降转为上升（Value[1]<Value[2]）且当前值高于前值（Value[0]>Value[1]）时开多仓。出场条件为反向信号触发时平仓，即持多仓时出现空头信号则平多仓，持空仓时出现多头信号则平空仓。仓位管理通过MM参数控制，支持固定手数和固定保证金两种模式，由MMMode参数决定。资金管理方面设有StopLoss和TakeProfit参数，但默认值较大（止损1000点，止盈2000点），风险收益比为1:2。策略还使用了IsNewBar机制确保每个K线只计算一次信号，避免重复开仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on the BlauTVI (Tick Volume Indicator) by William Blau, using triple-smoothed tick volume momentum to generate buy and sell signals. It opens positions when the indicator direction changes and the momentum is accelerating, while closing positions on opposing signals.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the BlauTVI indicator, a triple-smoothed tick volume momentum indicator proposed by William Blau. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when BlauTVI transitions from declining to rising (Value[1]<Value[2]) and current momentum is accelerating (Value[0]>Value[1]); a sell signal is generated when BlauTVI transitions from rising to declining (Value[1]>Value[2]) and current momentum is decelerating (Value[0]<Value[1]). Exit conditions are triggered by opposing signals — long positions are closed when a bearish signal appears and short positions are closed when a bullish signal appears. Position sizing is controlled by the MM parameter, supporting both fixed lot size and fixed margin modes as determined by the MMMode parameter. Money management includes StopLoss and TakeProfit parameters, with default values of 1000 and 2000 points respectively, yielding a risk-reward ratio of 1:2. The strategy also employs an IsNewBar mechanism to ensure signals are calculated only once per bar, preventing duplicate entries.

优势

  • 使用三重平滑算法有效过滤市场噪音，信号质量较高
  • 提供完整的买卖开仓和平仓开关，灵活控制交易方向
  • 采用IsNewBar机制防止同一K线重复开仓
  • 止损止盈参数可配置，默认风险收益比为1:2较为合理
  • 支持多种平滑算法和成交量类型，适应不同市场环境

劣势

  • 默认止损1000点和止盈2000点过大，对大多数品种不适用
  • 没有点差过滤，短线交易可能因点差过大而亏损
  • 没有持仓检查，可能重复开仓导致仓位过大
  • 依赖自定义指标BlauTVI，若指标文件缺失则EA无法运行
  • 信号逻辑较简单，仅基于指标方向变化，缺乏趋势过滤

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓
  • 增加点差过滤和最大持仓数量限制，提升风控能力

Strengths

  • Triple smoothing algorithm effectively filters market noise, producing higher quality signals
  • Provides complete buy/sell open and close switches for flexible trade direction control
  • Uses IsNewBar mechanism to prevent duplicate entries on the same bar
  • Configurable stop loss and take profit with a default risk-reward ratio of 1:2
  • Supports multiple smoothing algorithms and volume types, adaptable to different market conditions

Weaknesses

  • Default stop loss of 1000 points and take profit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments
  • No spread filter, short-term trades may suffer losses due to wide spreads
  • No position check before opening, potentially leading to duplicate positions and oversized exposure
  • Depends on custom BlauTVI indicator, EA fails to run if the indicator file is missing
  • Signal logic is simplistic, based only on indicator direction change without trend filtering

Improvements

  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Add spread filter and maximum position count limit to improve risk management
