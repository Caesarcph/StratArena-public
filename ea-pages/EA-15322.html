摘要

该EA基于Bezier_StDev自定义指标进行交易，利用贝塞尔曲线平滑配合标准差通道来识别均值回归的入场和出场点。它支持三种信号模式（点信号、直接信号、无信号），买入和卖出方向的开仓与平仓信号可独立配置。

策略分析

策略核心基于贝塞尔曲线平滑叠加标准差通道，属于均值回归类型。入场条件分三种模式：POINT模式通过读取指标缓冲区3（买入）和缓冲区2（卖出）的信号点判断；DIRECT模式通过判断贝塞尔曲线的拐点（V形底买入、倒V形顶卖出）确认入场；WITHOUT模式则不产生入场信号。出场条件同样支持三种模式：POINT模式读取信号点，DIRECT模式通过曲线斜率变化判断趋势反转，WITHOUT模式不平仓。仓位管理通过MM参数和MMMode枚举支持多种资金管理模式，包括固定手数、按余额百分比、按可用保证金百分比等。止损和止盈以点数设定，默认分别为1000和2000点。策略仅在新的K线出现时计算信号，避免重复交易，并通过IsNewBar机制控制交易频率。

Summary

This EA trades based on the Bezier_StDev custom indicator, using Bezier curve smoothing with standard deviation bands to identify mean reversion entry and exit points. It supports three signal modes (point, direct, and without) for both opening and closing positions independently for buy and sell sides.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is based on Bezier curve smoothing overlaid with standard deviation bands, classifying it as a mean reversion type. Entry conditions support three modes: POINT mode reads signal dots from indicator buffer 3 (buy) and buffer 2 (sell); DIRECT mode identifies Bezier curve inflection points (V-bottom for buy, inverted-V top for sell) to confirm entries; WITHOUT mode generates no entry signals. Exit conditions similarly support three modes: POINT mode reads signal dots, DIRECT mode detects trend reversal via curve slope changes, and WITHOUT mode does not close positions. Position sizing supports multiple money management modes via the MM parameter and MMMode enumeration, including fixed lot, percentage of balance, percentage of free margin, and others. Stop loss and take profit are set in points with defaults of 1000 and 2000 points respectively. The strategy only calculates signals on new bar formation to avoid redundant trades, controlling trade frequency through the IsNewBar mechanism.

优势

  • 信号模式灵活可配置，开仓和平仓信号可独立设置不同模式
  • 基于贝塞尔曲线平滑能有效过滤市场噪音，减少虚假信号
  • 使用新K线检测机制避免同一K线内重复开仓，控制交易频率
  • 支持多种资金管理模式，适应不同风险偏好的交易者
  • 止损止盈参数明确，风险可控

劣势

  • 依赖自定义指标Bezier_StDev，指标源码不可见时难以验证策略逻辑完整性
  • 默认止损1000点和止盈2000点差距较大，可能导致单笔亏损金额偏高
  • 未包含点差过滤机制，在点差较大的品种上可能产生不利成交
  • DIRECT模式使用3根K线判断拐点，滞后性较强可能错过最佳入场时机
  • 缺乏对持仓数量的限制，可能在震荡行情中连续开仓导致过度交易

改进建议

  • 增加点差过滤和最大持仓数量限制，防止高滑点和过度交易
  • 增加追踪止损功能，在趋势有利时锁定利润而非仅依赖固定止盈

Strengths

  • Flexible signal mode configuration with independent settings for open and close signals
  • Bezier curve smoothing effectively filters market noise and reduces false signals
  • New bar detection mechanism prevents redundant trades within the same bar, controlling trade frequency
  • Supports multiple money management modes adaptable to different risk preferences
  • Explicit stop loss and take profit parameters provide controllable risk

Weaknesses

  • Depends on custom indicator Bezier_StDev; without source code, strategy logic completeness is hard to verify
  • Default 1000-point SL and 2000-point TP spread is large, potentially leading to high per-trade losses
  • No spread filter mechanism, which may result in unfavorable fills on high-spread instruments
  • DIRECT mode uses 3 bars to detect inflection points, causing significant lag that may miss optimal entries
  • Lacks position count limits, potentially leading to over-trading in choppy markets

Improvements

  • Add spread filter and maximum position count limits to prevent high slippage and over-trading
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in profits during favorable trends rather than relying solely on fixed take profit
