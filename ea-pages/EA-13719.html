摘要

HVR（历史波动率比率）是一个MT5自定义技术指标，计算6期短期与100期长期对数收益率历史波动率的比值。它在独立窗口中绘制该比率，帮助交易者识别波动率收缩或扩张阶段。HTF版本增加了多周期功能，允许在低于源数据的周期图表上显示指标。

策略分析

HVR指标的核心概念是比较短期（6期）和长期（100期）的历史波动率，通过计算对数收益率的样本标准差来衡量波动性。短期波动率除以长期波动率得到比率，当比率上升时表示近期波动加剧，比率下降时表示近期波动收敛。入场条件方面，该指标本身不产生交易信号，但交易者常用波动率收缩（低HVR值）作为突破行情的前兆，用波动率扩张（高HVR值）作为趋势确认。出场条件同样不直接由指标给出，需要结合其他指标判断。仓位管理和资金管理方面，该指标不涉及任何仓位或资金管理逻辑，仅作为辅助分析工具。支持多种应用价格类型（收盘价、开盘价、最高价、最低价、中位数、典型价、加权收盘价等），增加了灵活性。HTF版本通过iCustom调用基础指标并支持跨周期显示，方便在不同时间框架上观察波动率变化。

Summary

HVR (Historical Volatility Ratio) is a custom technical indicator for MetaTrader 5 that computes the ratio of short-term (6-period) to long-term (100-period) historical volatility of log price returns. It plots this ratio in a separate window, helping traders identify volatility contraction or expansion regimes. The HTF version adds multi-timeframe capability, allowing the indicator to be displayed on charts with lower timeframes than the source data.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of the HVR indicator is comparing short-term (6-period) and long-term (100-period) historical volatility by computing the sample standard deviation of log returns. The ratio of short-term to long-term volatility rises when recent volatility increases and falls when recent volatility contracts relative to the longer-term baseline. For entry conditions, the indicator itself does not generate trading signals, but traders commonly use volatility contraction (low HVR values) as a precursor to breakout moves and volatility expansion (high HVR values) as trend confirmation. Exit conditions are likewise not directly provided by the indicator and require complementary analysis tools. Regarding position sizing and money management, the indicator has no position or money management logic whatsoever, serving purely as an analytical aid. It supports multiple applied price types (close, open, high, low, median, typical, weighted close, and several custom price constructions), adding flexibility for different analysis approaches. The HTF version calls the base indicator via iCustom and supports cross-timeframe display, enabling volatility observation across different timeframes on a single chart.

优势

  • 波动率比率可有效识别市场波动收缩与扩张阶段，为突破策略提供早期信号
  • 支持12种应用价格类型，包括自定义的TrendFollow和Demark价格，灵活性高
  • HTF版本支持多周期显示，方便跨时间框架分析波动率变化

劣势

  • 短期和长期周期（6和100）硬编码，无法通过参数调整，缺乏灵活性
  • 指标不产生直接交易信号，仅作为辅助工具，对新手交易者不够直观
  • 使用循环缓冲区（Count数组）实现复杂且难以维护，代码可读性差

改进建议

  • 将6和100周期改为可配置输入参数，允许用户根据不同市场和周期调整
  • 添加波动率比率的阈值线或区域着色，便于直观判断波动率状态

Strengths

  • The volatility ratio effectively identifies volatility contraction and expansion regimes, providing early signals for breakout strategies
  • Supports 12 applied price types including custom TrendFollow and Demark prices, offering high flexibility
  • The HTF version enables multi-timeframe display, facilitating cross-timeframe volatility analysis

Weaknesses

  • Short-term and long-term periods (6 and 100) are hardcoded with no parameter adjustment, limiting flexibility
  • The indicator does not generate direct trading signals and serves only as an auxiliary tool, making it unintuitive for novice traders
  • The circular buffer implementation using the Count array is complex and hard to maintain, resulting in poor code readability

Improvements

  • Make the 6 and 100 periods configurable input parameters, allowing users to adjust for different markets and timeframes
  • Add threshold lines or zone coloring for the volatility ratio to facilitate intuitive volatility state identification
