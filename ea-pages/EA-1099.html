摘要

该EA基于自定义BrakeExp指标进行交易，该指标生成上/下趋势和信号值。它在上升趋势信号时开多仓，在下降趋势信号时开空仓，并根据趋势读数平仓反向持仓，配有可配置的资金管理和止损/止盈设置。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略是基于BrakeExp自定义指标的趋势跟踪系统。入场条件为：当UpSignal信号触发时开多仓，当DnSignal信号触发时开空仓。出场条件包括：当UpTrend持续但无新信号时平空仓，当DnTrend持续但无新信号时平多仓，以及通过固定点数的止损和止盈来管理出场。仓位管理方面，MM参数支持两种模式：负值直接指定手数，正值按存款比例计算仓位。资金管理依赖TradeAlgorithms.mqh库中的标准化交易函数。策略仅在新K线出现时检测信号（SignalBar=0除外），避免重复计算，但也通过Recount机制在数据不足时重新计算。

Summary

This EA trades based on the custom BrakeExp indicator, which generates up/down trend and signal values. It opens buy positions on upward signals and sell positions on downward signals, while closing opposite positions based on trend readings, with configurable money management and stop-loss/take-profit settings.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of this EA is a trend-following system based on the custom BrakeExp indicator. Entry conditions: opens long positions when UpSignal triggers and short positions when DnSignal triggers. Exit conditions include closing short positions when UpTrend persists without new signals, closing long positions when DnTrend persists without new signals, and using fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels for trade management. Position sizing supports two modes via the MM parameter: negative values specify lot size directly, while positive values calculate position size as a share of the deposit. Money management relies on standardized trading functions from the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library. The strategy only detects signals on new bars (unless SignalBar=0), avoiding redundant calculations, and uses a Recount mechanism to recalculate when data is insufficient.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，趋势跟踪加信号确认的双层判断机制减少了假信号
  • 支持灵活的资金管理方式，可按固定手数或存款比例下单
  • 设有完整的开关控制，可独立控制多空开仓和平仓权限
  • 仅在新K线时检测信号，减少计算开销和重复交易风险
  • 使用止损止盈保护每笔交易，风险控制基础完备

劣势

  • 依赖自定义BrakeExp指标，缺乏对该指标内部逻辑的透明度
  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未考虑市场波动率（ATR）动态调整
  • 缺少点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能不利
  • SignalBar=0时会在未完成K线上交易，可能产生虚假信号
  • 缺少最大持仓限制和连续亏损保护机制

改进建议

  • 建议增加ATR动态止损止盈，根据市场波动率自动调整保护距离
  • 增加点差过滤和最大持仓控制，提升在不利市场条件下的稳健性

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic with dual-layer judgment of trend following and signal confirmation reduces false signals
  • Flexible money management supporting both fixed lot size and deposit percentage modes
  • Complete permission switches allowing independent control of long/short open and close operations
  • Signal detection only on new bars reduces computational overhead and repeated trade risk
  • Stop-loss and take-profit protect every trade, providing a solid risk control foundation

Weaknesses

  • Depends on the custom BrakeExp indicator without transparency into its internal logic
  • Fixed stop-loss and take-profit in points, not dynamically adjusted for market volatility (ATR)
  • No spread filter, which may be detrimental in high-spread environments
  • Trading on SignalBar=0 (unfinished bar) may generate false signals
  • Lacks maximum position limits and consecutive loss protection mechanisms

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic stop-loss and take-profit to automatically adjust protection distance based on market volatility
  • Add spread filter and maximum position control to improve robustness under adverse market conditions
