摘要

该EA使用三条不同周期的简单移动平均线(SMA)来判断趋势方向并生成交易信号。通常在快线在中线之上、中线在慢线之上时做多，反之做空。当SMA排列被打乱或反向交叉时触发出场。

策略分析

3SMA策略的核心概念是通过三条不同周期的简单移动平均线（短期、中期、长期）的排列顺序来判断市场趋势的方向和强度。入场条件为：当三条SMA呈现多头排列（短>中>长）时开多单，呈现空头排列（短<中<长）时开空单。出场条件通常为SMA排列被打乱，即短期均线上穿或下穿中期均线时平仓。仓位管理一般采用固定手数或基于账户余额的百分比计算手数。资金管理方面，该EA可能包含止损和止盈设置，但具体的止损止盈逻辑依赖于参数配置。该策略的优点是逻辑简单清晰，能有效捕捉中长期趋势，但在震荡市中容易产生频繁的虚假信号和反复亏损。

Summary

This EA uses three Simple Moving Averages (SMA) of different periods to determine trend direction and generate trading signals. It typically enters long when the fast SMA is above the medium SMA which is above the slow SMA, and short when the reverse alignment occurs. Exits are triggered when the SMA alignment breaks or crosses in the opposite direction.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of the 3SMA strategy is to determine market trend direction and strength through the alignment order of three Simple Moving Averages with different periods (short-term, medium-term, long-term). Entry conditions are: open long when the three SMAs show bullish alignment (short > medium > long), and open short when they show bearish alignment (short < medium < long). Exit conditions are typically triggered when the SMA alignment is broken, i.e., when the short-term MA crosses the medium-term MA in the opposite direction. Position sizing generally uses fixed lots or calculates lot size based on a percentage of account balance. Money management may include stop-loss and take-profit settings, but the specific SL/TP logic depends on parameter configuration. The strategy's strength is its simple and clear logic that can effectively capture medium to long-term trends, but it is prone to frequent false signals and repeated losses in ranging markets.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和调试
  • 能有效捕捉中长期趋势行情，趋势市中盈利可观
  • 三条均线过滤减少了单均线交叉的虚假信号数量

劣势

  • 震荡市中频繁产生虚假信号导致反复亏损
  • 均线系统本质上是滞后指标，入场和出场都存在明显延迟
  • 默认参数可能缺少止损设置，存在较大的资金风险

改进建议

  • 增加ADX或波动率过滤器，在震荡行情中减少开仓信号
  • 加入动态止损如ATR止损或移动止损，改善风险管理和盈利保护

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and debug
  • Effectively captures medium to long-term trending markets with substantial profits in trends
  • Triple MA filtering reduces the number of false signals compared to single or dual MA crossovers

Weaknesses

  • Generates frequent false signals with repeated losses in ranging/choppy markets
  • Moving averages are inherently lagging indicators, causing significant delay in both entries and exits
  • Default parameters may lack stop-loss settings, posing significant capital risk

Improvements

  • Add ADX or volatility filter to reduce trade signals during ranging market conditions
  • Implement dynamic stop-loss such as ATR-based stops or trailing stops to improve risk management and profit protection
