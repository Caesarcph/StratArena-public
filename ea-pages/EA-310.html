Strategy analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the CBH_BH_Stoch signal filter, which combines a Stochastic oscillator with a Moving Average to determine market momentum direction. Entry conditions rely on Stochastic K-line (period 47) and D-line (period 9) crossover or overbought/oversold zone signals, confirmed by a 5-period MA for trend direction. Exit conditions are governed by signal thresholds: the open threshold is 40 and the close threshold is 20, triggering position closure when signal strength drops below the close threshold. Position sizing uses a fixed lot model (default 0.1 lots) with no dynamic adjustment. Money management employs the CMoneyFixedLot class based on a fixed percentage (10%) and fixed lot size, but no stop loss or take profit levels are set (both at 0.0), meaning exits depend entirely on signal reversal without hard risk protection. Furthermore, the EA does not use trailing stops, so profitable positions cannot automatically lock in gains.

Strengths

  • Uses the standard MetaQuotes EA framework with clean code structure, making it easy to maintain and extend
  • Stochastic oscillator combined with Moving Average provides dual confirmation, reducing false signals
  • Configurable signal thresholds allow flexible adjustment of entry and exit sensitivity

Weaknesses

  • No stop loss or take profit set (both at 0.0), lacking hard risk protection when signals fail
  • No trailing stop used, so profitable positions cannot lock in gains before trend reversal
  • Fixed lot sizing does not account for account size or volatility changes, making money management overly simplistic

Possible improvements

  • Add stop loss and take profit parameters to provide hard risk protection and prevent excessive losses on single trades
  • Introduce dynamic position sizing that adjusts lot size based on account balance and market volatility (e.g., ATR)

Risk assessment

Overall risk: High risk

  • Stop loss and take profit are both zero, trades rely entirely on signal-based exits which can cause massive losses in extreme market conditions
  • No new bar check mechanism, potentially trading at non-optimal price points
  • Fixed lot size does not adjust with account changes, leading to potential mismatch between position size and account size over long-term operation
