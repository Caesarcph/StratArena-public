Exp_MACDCandle

This EA trades based on the MACDCandle custom indicator, generating buy signals when the MACD histogram transitions to bullish (value 2) and sell signals when it transitions to bearish (value 0). It supports two operating modes: histogram crossover and signal line crossover, with configurable position sizing, stop loss, and take profit levels.

Strategy analysis

The strategy concept is based on MACD momentum changes, using a custom MACDCandle indicator that encodes MACD states into discrete values (0, 2, etc.) to generate trading signals. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when indicator buffer 4 transitions from non-2 to 2, and a sell signal when it transitions from non-0 to 0, essentially capturing shifts in MACD momentum direction. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss (1000 points) and take profit (2000 points), while also closing positions on reverse signals — closing sells on buy signals and closing buys on sell signals. Position sizing supports two modes: fixed lot size (LOT) or margin-based percentage (MM parameter defaults to 0.1), implemented through the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library functions. Money management features a 1:2 risk-reward ratio, but lacks trailing stops and dynamic position adjustment, making risk control relatively static and inflexible.

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic based on the classic MACD momentum indicator with solid theoretical foundation
  • Provides stop loss and take profit protection with a 1:2 risk-reward ratio, favorable for long-term expectancy
  • Independent on/off switches for buy and sell directions offer high trading flexibility
  • Uses IsNewBar check to prevent repeated trades within the same bar, reducing overtrading risk
  • Clean code structure with modular use of TradeAlgorithms library, easy to maintain

Weaknesses

  • Fixed stop loss and take profit in points without considering market volatility (ATR), poor adaptability across instruments and market conditions
  • Lacks trailing stop mechanism, potentially losing most profits during strong trending moves due to retracements
  • Depends on a custom MACDCandle indicator, increasing deployment complexity and dependency risk
  • No trading session filter, may generate unfavorable signals during low-liquidity periods
  • No spread filter or margin sufficiency check, which may cause anomalies during high spread or insufficient margin conditions

Possible improvements

  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit to adapt risk management to different instruments' volatility characteristics
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in more profits during trending markets
  • Add trading session filter and spread filter to avoid trading under unfavorable market conditions

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Fixed 1000-point stop loss may be too large or too small during significant volatility changes, failing to effectively protect capital
  • No spread filter means high-spread environments could trigger stops with expanded slippage
  • Dependency on external custom indicator means missing indicator file will cause EA initialization failure
