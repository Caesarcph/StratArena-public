摘要

该EA实现了针对FORTS（俄罗斯期货）市场的高频做市策略。当价差足够大时，在订单簿两侧同时挂出买入和卖出限价单，并动态管理挂单价格以维持最优报价地位，从而捕获价差利润。

策略分析

该EA采用做市商策略，核心概念是在订单簿的买卖两侧同时挂限价单来赚取价差。入场条件为：当市场价差大于等于SpreadToPutOrders乘以最小变动价位时，同时在买侧和卖侧各挂Lots手限价单，价格距离当前买价/卖价一个最小变动价位。若已有持仓但无挂单，则在持仓方向的对侧挂双倍数量的限价单作为平仓单。出场条件依赖限价单被成交后，通过反向限价单平仓，或通过OnDeinit中强制平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数，但持仓后挂反向单时使用双倍手数（CurrentPosition.Volume()*2），这可能意味着同时平仓和开新仓。资金管理方面完全缺失，没有止损止盈设置，也没有最大持仓限制或资金比例控制。策略通过实时监控订单簿变化来删除价格不合理的挂单并重新挂单，保持最优报价地位。

Summary

This EA implements a high-frequency market-making spreader strategy for the FORTS (Russian futures) market. It places buy and sell limit orders on both sides of the order book when the spread is wide enough, and dynamically manages order positions to maintain best bid/offer status while capturing the spread.

Strategy Analysis

This EA implements a market-making strategy whose core concept is to simultaneously place limit orders on both bid and ask sides of the order book to capture the spread. Entry conditions: when the market spread is greater than or equal to SpreadToPutOrders multiplied by the minimum tick size, it places buy and sell limit orders of Lots volume on each side, priced one tick away from the current bid/ask. If a position already exists but no pending orders are present, it places a reverse limit order at double the current position volume on the opposite side for closing. Exit conditions rely on limit orders being filled and then closed via the reverse order, or forced closure in OnDeinit. Position sizing uses fixed lots, but reverse orders after a fill use double volume (CurrentPosition.Volume()*2), which implies simultaneously closing and opening a new position. Money management is completely absent with no stop-loss or take-profit, no maximum position limits, and no equity-based sizing. The strategy monitors order book changes in real-time to delete mispriced pending orders and re-place them to maintain best bid/offer status.

优势

  • 利用订单簿深度数据进行精确报价，比仅使用价格数据的策略更有优势
  • 动态删除不合理的挂单并重新挂单，保持最优报价地位，提高成交率
  • 做市策略在震荡市场中表现良好，能持续从价差中获利
  • 代码结构清晰，逻辑模块化，函数分工明确

劣势

  • 完全没有止损止盈机制，单边行情下可能造成巨大亏损
  • 双倍手数反向挂单逻辑（Volume()*2）存在风险，可能在连续成交时迅速放大仓位
  • 缺乏资金管理和仓位控制，没有最大持仓限制
  • 高频依赖订单簿更新，在流动性不足或延迟较高时策略失效
  • 删除和重挂订单频繁，可能导致交易成本增加和被交易所限制

改进建议

  • 增加止损止盈机制和最大持仓限制，防止单边行情下无限扩大亏损
  • 将双倍手数逻辑改为仅平仓手数，避免仓位非线性增长风险
  • 增加价差过滤和流动性检查，在市场条件不适宜时暂停交易
  • 添加滑点控制和重试机制，提高订单执行的鲁棒性

Strengths

  • Utilizes order book depth data for precise quoting, giving an advantage over strategies using only price data
  • Dynamically deletes mispriced pending orders and replaces them to maintain best bid/offer status, improving fill rates
  • Market-making strategy performs well in range-bound markets, continuously profiting from the spread
  • Clean code structure with modular logic and well-separated function responsibilities

Weaknesses

  • Completely lacks stop-loss and take-profit mechanisms, which can cause massive losses in trending markets
  • The double-volume reverse order logic (Volume()*2) is risky and can rapidly amplify positions on consecutive fills
  • No money management or position control with no maximum position limits
  • High-frequency dependency on order book updates means the strategy fails under low liquidity or high latency
  • Frequent order deletion and replacement may increase transaction costs and trigger exchange rate limits

Improvements

  • Add stop-loss/take-profit mechanisms and maximum position limits to prevent unlimited losses in trending markets
  • Change the double-volume logic to only close the existing position volume, avoiding non-linear position growth risk
  • Add spread filtering and liquidity checks to pause trading when market conditions are unfavorable
  • Add slippage control and retry mechanisms to improve order execution robustness
