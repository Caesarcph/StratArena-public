摘要

该EA基于Delta RSI指标进行交易，通过计算两个不同周期RSI的差值来捕捉动量变化。当Delta RSI上穿阈值时开多仓，下穿阈值时开空仓，采用固定手数并设置止损止盈。

策略分析

该策略的核心概念是Delta RSI，即两个不同周期RSI的差值，用于衡量短期与中期动量之间的偏离程度。入场条件为：当Delta RSI从下方向上穿越买入阈值时开多仓，从上方向下穿越卖出阈值时开空仓。出场条件依赖预设的止损和止盈水平，同时可能包含基于指标反转的平仓信号。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，没有根据账户资金动态调整的机制。资金管理方面缺乏风险百分比计算，每笔交易的风险敞口不随账户余额变化。整体策略逻辑简单直接，适合动量明显的趋势行情，但在震荡市中可能频繁止损。

Summary

This EA trades based on the Delta RSI indicator, which measures the difference between two RSI periods to detect momentum shifts. It opens buy positions when the Delta RSI crosses above a threshold and sell positions when it crosses below, using fixed lot sizing with stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of this strategy is Delta RSI, which calculates the difference between two RSI periods to measure the divergence between short-term and medium-term momentum. Entry conditions are: open a buy position when Delta RSI crosses above the buy threshold from below, and open a sell position when it crosses below the sell threshold from above. Exit conditions rely on preset stop loss and take profit levels, and may also include signal-based closing when the indicator reverses. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic adjustment based on account equity. Money management lacks risk-percentage calculation, meaning the risk exposure per trade does not scale with account balance. The overall strategy logic is straightforward and suits strong momentum trending markets, but may suffer frequent stop-outs in choppy sideways conditions.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰简洁，Delta RSI概念直观易懂，适合快速理解和使用
  • 同时设置了止损和止盈，具备基本的风险控制框架，不会出现无限亏损
  • 双RSI差值方法能有效捕捉短期动量相对于中期动量的变化，在趋势启动阶段信号较好
  • 使用MagicNumber区分订单，避免与其他EA冲突，支持多EA并行运行

劣势

  • 固定手数缺乏动态资金管理，无法根据账户规模自动调整风险敞口，大资金时风险过于集中
  • 没有新增K线检查机制，同一根K线内可能多次触发信号导致重复开仓
  • 缺乏点差过滤和交易时段过滤，在点差扩大或低流动性时段可能产生不利成交
  • Delta RSI阈值固定为0，没有根据市场波动率自适应调整，在不同市场环境下表现差异大

改进建议

  • 增加基于账户余额百分比的动态仓位计算，例如每笔交易风险不超过账户的1-2%，实现更科学的资金管理
  • 添加新增K线检查和点差过滤机制，防止重复开仓和高点差时段的不利成交，提升策略稳健性

Strengths

  • Clear and concise strategy logic with an intuitive Delta RSI concept, easy to understand and use quickly
  • Both stop loss and take profit are set, providing a basic risk control framework that prevents unlimited losses
  • The dual-RSI difference method effectively captures shifts in short-term momentum relative to medium-term momentum, generating good signals at trend onset
  • MagicNumber is used to distinguish orders, avoiding conflicts with other EAs and supporting multi-EA parallel operation

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing lacks dynamic money management, cannot automatically adjust risk exposure based on account size, concentrating risk excessively with larger accounts
  • No new bar check mechanism exists, potentially allowing multiple signals within the same bar leading to duplicate position openings
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, which may result in unfavorable fills during wide spread or low liquidity periods
  • Delta RSI threshold is fixed at zero without volatility-adaptive adjustment, leading to significantly different performance across varying market conditions

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account balance percentage, such as risking no more than 1-2% per trade, for more scientific money management
  • Add new bar check and spread filter mechanisms to prevent duplicate entries and unfavorable fills during high-spread periods, improving strategy robustness
