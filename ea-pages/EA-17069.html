摘要

Ilan1.4是一个经典的马丁格尔策略EA，每次亏损后将手数翻倍，同时追求小幅止盈收益。它基于简单的动量信号入场，依靠在亏损方向不断加仓并倍增仓位来等待回调回本。在持续单边行情中存在爆仓的极端风险。

策略分析

Ilan1.4的核心策略概念是马丁格尔加仓法：初始以较小手数开仓，如果市场朝不利方向移动达到设定的间距，则以倍增的手数再次开仓同方向头寸，从而摊薄均价。入场条件通常基于简单的指标信号（如RSI或动量偏移）在首次交易时触发，后续加仓则纯粹基于价格距离触发而非新的信号确认。出场条件是当整体持仓的浮盈达到设定的净利润目标（以金额计）时一次性平仓所有头寸。仓位管理采用典型的倍增模式（1, 2, 4, 8...），每次加仓手数乘以一个倍数系数（默认为2倍）。资金管理方面，该EA没有设置止损，完全依赖价格回调来回本，最大持仓层数由参数限制但实际效果有限。在单边趋势行情中，加仓层数会迅速耗尽保证金，导致强制平仓或爆仓，这是该策略最致命的缺陷。

Summary

Ilan1.4 is a classic Martingale-based Expert Advisor that doubles the lot size after each losing trade while targeting small take-profit gains. It enters positions based on a simple momentum signal and relies on averaging down with exponentially increasing position sizes to recover losses. The strategy carries extreme risk of account blow-up during sustained trending markets.

Strategy Analysis

Ilan1.4's core strategy concept is Martingale position scaling: it opens an initial trade with a small lot size, and if the market moves against the position by a configured pip distance, it opens another position in the same direction with a multiplied lot size, thereby averaging down the entry price. Entry conditions for the first trade are typically triggered by a simple indicator signal such as RSI or momentum shift, while subsequent add-on positions are triggered purely by price distance from the last entry rather than any new signal confirmation. Exit conditions are met when the floating profit of the entire position basket reaches a configured net profit target in monetary terms, at which point all positions are closed simultaneously. Position sizing follows a classic doubling pattern (1, 2, 4, 8...) where each new layer multiplies the previous lot by a coefficient (default 2x). Money management is essentially non-existent in terms of protective stops—the EA relies entirely on price reversion to recover losses, with a maximum number of layers parameter that provides only marginal protection. During sustained trending markets, the exponentially growing position sizes rapidly deplete margin, leading to margin calls or account liquidation, which is the strategy's most fatal flaw.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中能稳定盈利，小利润频繁兑现
  • 逻辑简单直观，参数少易于理解和调试
  • 加仓间距和倍数系数可调，灵活性较高

劣势

  • 无止损机制，单边趋势中面临爆仓风险
  • 马丁格尔倍增仓位在连续亏损时呈指数级增长，资金消耗极快
  • 加仓不基于新的信号确认，纯粹依赖价格距离，属于盲目的摊薄策略
  • 没有考虑点差、隔夜费等交易成本对净值的影响

改进建议

  • 增加硬止损机制，在最大加仓层数达到后设置全局止损以控制最大回撤
  • 在加仓前加入趋势过滤指标（如均线方向），避免在强趋势中逆势加仓
  • 引入动态仓位管理，根据账户净值和波动率调整初始手数和加仓间距

Strengths

  • Can generate steady small profits in ranging/sideways markets with frequent take-profit hits
  • Simple and intuitive logic with few parameters, easy to understand and debug
  • Configurable pip step distance and lot multiplier coefficient provide flexibility

Weaknesses

  • No stop-loss mechanism exposes the account to blow-up risk during sustained trending markets
  • Martingale lot doubling causes exponential position growth during consecutive losses, rapidly depleting capital
  • Add-on positions are not based on new signal confirmation but purely on price distance, making it a blind averaging-down strategy
  • Does not account for the impact of spreads, swap fees, and other trading costs on net equity

Improvements

  • Add a hard stop-loss mechanism—set a global stop when the maximum layer count is reached to cap maximum drawdown
  • Incorporate a trend filter indicator (e.g., moving average direction) before adding positions to avoid counter-trend scaling in strong trends
  • Introduce dynamic position sizing that adjusts initial lots and pip step distance based on account equity and market volatility
