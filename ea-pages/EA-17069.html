摘要

Ilan1.4是经典的马丁格尔策略EA，基于简单动量信号开仓，每次亏损后将手数翻倍以图回本。它采用类网格方式递增手数，目标止盈较小。在趋势行情中，由于仓位指数级增长，该EA具有极高风险。

策略分析

Ilan1.4的核心策略概念是马丁格尔加仓法：首次入场基于价格动量或指标信号（通常为RSI或随机振荡器超买超卖），当市场逆向波动达到设定间距时，以倍数手数加仓。入场条件通常是指标超卖区做多、超买区做空，加仓间距由Delimiter参数控制。出场条件为整个仓位组合达到设定的总盈利目标（TakeProfit参数）时全部平仓，没有独立止损机制。仓位管理采用倍数递增方式，每加一次仓手数乘以LotExponent（通常为1.5-2.0），导致持仓量呈指数级增长。资金管理方面没有最大持仓限制或账户风险控制，仅依赖总盈利目标平仓，这在连续逆向行情中可能导致爆仓。该策略在震荡行情中表现良好，但在单边趋势中风险极大。

Summary

Ilan1.4 is a classic Martingale-style Expert Advisor that opens initial positions based on a simple momentum signal and doubles the lot size after each consecutive loss. It uses a grid-like approach with increasing lot multipliers to recover losses, targeting small take-profit levels. The EA is highly dangerous in trending markets due to its exponential position sizing.

Strategy Analysis

Ilan1.4's core strategy concept is a Martingale position-scaling approach: the initial entry is based on a simple momentum or oscillator signal (typically RSI or Stochastic overbought/oversold levels), and when the market moves against the position by a configured distance, the EA adds another position with multiplied lot size. Entry conditions generally involve buying in oversold zones and selling in overbought zones, with the spacing between additional positions controlled by the Delimiter parameter. Exit conditions are met when the net profit of the entire position group reaches a configured TakeProfit target, at which point all positions in the group are closed simultaneously; there is no individual stop-loss mechanism. Position sizing follows an exponential scaling model where each subsequent level multiplies the lot size by LotExponent (typically 1.5-2.0), causing total exposure to grow exponentially. Money management lacks maximum position limits or account-level risk controls, relying solely on the aggregate profit target for exit, which can lead to margin calls during sustained trending moves. The strategy performs well in ranging markets but carries extreme risk in trending conditions.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中能稳定盈利，小仓位组合容易达到盈利目标
  • 参数可配置性强，交易者可调整加仓间距和倍数以适应不同品种
  • 代码逻辑简单直观，便于理解马丁格尔策略的运作机制

劣势

  • 马丁格尔加仓导致风险指数级增长，单边行情中极易爆仓
  • 没有止损机制，亏损头寸没有保护，仅依赖总盈利目标平仓
  • 没有账户资金检查和最大回撤限制，资金管理严重不足

改进建议

  • 增加全局止损和最大回撤限制，防止账户爆仓
  • 加入趋势过滤指标（如均线方向），在强趋势行情中暂停或减少加仓
  • 限制最大总持仓量和单品种最大风险暴露比例

Strengths

  • Generates consistent profits in ranging markets where small position groups easily reach profit targets
  • Highly configurable parameters allow traders to adjust spacing and multiplier for different instruments
  • Simple and straightforward code logic makes it easy to understand how Martingale strategies operate

Weaknesses

  • Martingale position scaling causes exponential risk growth; sustained trends can easily blow the account
  • No stop-loss mechanism means losing positions have no protection, relying solely on aggregate profit target for exit
  • Lacks account equity checks and maximum drawdown limits, making money management severely inadequate

Improvements

  • Add a global stop-loss and maximum drawdown limit to prevent account blow-up
  • Incorporate a trend filter (e.g., moving average direction) to pause or reduce position scaling during strong trends
  • Cap maximum total exposure and limit per-instrument risk as a percentage of account equity
