摘要

Ilan 1.4是一个经典的马丁格尔风格EA，开仓后若价格反向运行，则按固定间距加仓并递增手数。它使用简单的动量入场信号，依赖均价回归策略在市场回调时全部平仓获利。该EA无止损，完全依赖市场回撤来恢复，在单边趋势行情中风险极高。

策略分析

Ilan 1.4的核心策略是马丁格尔加仓法：初始开仓基于简单动量指标判断方向，若价格逆向波动超过设定的网格间距（Delta参数），则以倍增或递增的手数再次同向开仓，拉低均价。出场条件为当所有持仓的加权平均价格回到总浮盈达到TakeProfit设定的点数时一次性全部平仓。仓位管理采用手数倍增系数（LotExponent），每次加仓手数为前一次手数乘以该系数，实现指数级增长。资金管理方面没有硬性止损，最大回撤取决于账户余额能承受多少次连续加仓。该策略在震荡行情中表现稳定，但在单边趋势中可能因无限加仓而爆仓，风险极高。

Summary

Ilan 1.4 is a classic Martingale-style Expert Advisor that opens a position and, if price moves against it, opens additional positions at fixed pip intervals with progressively increasing lot sizes. It uses a simple momentum-based entry signal and relies on an averaging strategy to eventually close all positions at a net profit when the market reverts. The EA has no stop loss and depends entirely on market retracement to recover, making it highly risky in trending markets.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy of Ilan 1.4 is a Martingale averaging-down method: the initial entry uses a simple momentum indicator to determine direction, and if price moves adversely beyond a configured grid interval (Delta parameter), it opens another position in the same direction with an increased lot size, thereby lowering the average entry price. Exit occurs when the weighted average price of all open positions reaches a total floating profit equal to the TakeProfit setting, at which point all positions close simultaneously. Position sizing uses a lot multiplication coefficient (LotExponent), where each successive position's lot size equals the previous lot multiplied by this factor, resulting in exponential lot growth. Money management lacks a hard stop loss, meaning maximum drawdown depends entirely on how many consecutive additions the account balance can sustain. The strategy performs reliably in ranging markets but risks account blowout during strong trending moves due to unlimited position accumulation.

优势

  • 在震荡行情中盈利稳定，回撤后容易回本
  • 参数灵活可调，网格间距和手数系数均可自定义
  • 逻辑简单直观，易于理解和修改
  • 可实现小资金快速翻倍（在有利行情下）
  • 历史悠久，经过大量实盘验证和社区优化

劣势

  • 无止损机制，单边行情可能导致爆仓
  • 手数指数级增长，对资金要求极高
  • 不设最大持仓限制时可能无限加仓
  • 严重依赖市场回调，趋势行情中无法退出
  • 缺乏市场状态判断，不会在不利条件下停止交易

改进建议

  • 增加硬性止损和最大回撤保护机制
  • 加入趋势过滤指标（如大周期均线），在单边行情中暂停加仓
  • 实现动态手数调整，根据账户余额比例计算加仓手数而非固定倍增

Strengths

  • Steady profit in ranging markets with easy recovery after drawdown
  • Flexible parameters allow customization of grid spacing and lot multiplier
  • Simple and intuitive logic, easy to understand and modify
  • Can double small accounts quickly under favorable market conditions
  • Long history with extensive live testing and community optimization

Weaknesses

  • No stop loss mechanism, strong trends can cause account blowout
  • Exponential lot growth demands extremely high capital reserves
  • Without MaxTrades limit, positions can accumulate indefinitely
  • Heavily reliant on market retracement, unable to exit in trending markets
  • Lacks market regime detection, continues trading in adverse conditions

Improvements

  • Add hard stop loss and maximum drawdown protection mechanism
  • Incorporate trend filter indicators (e.g., higher timeframe MA) to pause additions during strong trends
  • Implement dynamic lot sizing based on account balance percentage rather than fixed multiplication
