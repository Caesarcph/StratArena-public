Ilan1.4

Ilan1.4 is a classic Martingale-style Expert Advisor that opens initial positions based on a simple momentum signal and doubles the lot size after each consecutive loss. It uses a grid-like approach with increasing lot multipliers to recover losses, targeting small take-profit levels. The EA is highly dangerous in trending markets due to its exponential position sizing.

摘要

Ilan1.4是一个经典的马丁格尔策略EA，基于简单的动量/RSI信号开仓，每次亏损后在固定价格间隔加倍手数。它假设价格最终会回归，从而以小额净利润平掉所有仓位。该策略没有止损，使用指数级加仓来弥补亏损，在趋势行情中风险极大。

策略分析

Ilan1.4的核心策略概念是马丁格尔加倍法，即在每次浮亏时以更大的手数逆势加仓，期望价格回归后一次性获利了结。入场条件通常基于RSI超买超卖或简单的价格动量反转信号，首次开仓使用基础手数。出场条件是当所有持仓的加权平均价格回到一定利润水平时全部平仓，整体止盈金额很小。仓位管理采用固定间隔加仓，每次加仓距离前一笔为设定的点数（Del参数），手数则按乘数倍增（通常为2倍）。资金管理方面没有止损机制，完全依赖价格回归来解套，账户风险随加仓次数呈指数级增长。在强趋势行情中，连续加仓会迅速耗尽保证金导致爆仓，这是该策略最致命的缺陷。

Summary

Ilan1.4 is a classic Martingale-based Expert Advisor that opens initial positions based on a simple momentum/RSI signal and then doubles the lot size on each subsequent losing trade at fixed price intervals. It relies on the assumption that price will eventually revert, allowing all positions to close at a small net profit. The strategy has no stop-loss and uses an exponential lot increase to recover losses, making it extremely risky in trending markets.

Strategy Analysis

Ilan1.4's core strategy concept is the Martingale doubling method, where it increases lot size on each losing position at fixed price intervals, expecting price reversion to close all positions at a small net profit. Entry conditions are typically based on RSI overbought/oversold levels or simple price momentum reversal signals for the initial trade. Exit conditions trigger when the weighted average price of all open positions reaches a predefined profit target, closing the entire basket at once. Position sizing follows a fixed-interval grid with exponential lot multiplication (typically 2x) on each subsequent level, while the price distance between levels is defined by the Del parameter. Money management lacks any stop-loss mechanism, relying entirely on mean reversion to escape drawdowns, which means account risk grows exponentially with each averaging level. In strong trending markets, consecutive martingale additions can rapidly deplete margin and cause a margin call, which is the strategy's most critical flaw.

优势

  • 逻辑简单直观，易于理解和修改参数
  • 在震荡行情中表现稳定，能持续获取小额利润
  • 价格回归时可以一次性解套所有仓位，实现盈利

劣势

  • 无止损机制，趋势行情中加仓呈指数增长，极易爆仓
  • 马丁格尔策略本质上具有无限风险，资金需求呈几何级数增长
  • 依赖价格回归假设，在单边行情中可能持续加仓直到保证金耗尽
  • 缺乏市场状态过滤，不论趋势或震荡都同样操作

改进建议

  • 增加整体账户止损或最大回撤限制，防止爆仓
  • 加入趋势过滤指标（如均线方向），在趋势行情中暂停加仓或减少倍增系数
  • 限制最大加仓手数占总资金的比例，实现动态仓位管理
