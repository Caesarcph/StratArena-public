摘要

RabbitM2是一个在M1时间周期上运行的剥头皮EA，结合移动平均线交叉和RSI指标作为入场信号。它通过较小的止盈目标和适度的止损来捕捉微小的价格波动。该EA在开仓前使用基础趋势过滤和动量确认机制。

策略分析

RabbitM2策略基于短期移动平均线交叉与RSI超买超卖区域的组合信号进行交易。入场条件为快速均线上穿慢速均线且RSI处于特定阈值范围内时做多，反之为做空。出场条件包括固定的止盈和止损水平，以及均线下穿时的趋势反转出场。仓位管理采用固定手数模式，未根据账户余额或波动率进行动态调整。资金管理方面缺乏风险比例计算，每笔交易使用相同手数，未考虑账户净值变化。整体策略设计较为简单，适合低波动市场环境下的短线交易，但在高波动或震荡剧烈的市场中可能面临连续止损的风险。

Summary

RabbitM2 is a scalping EA that trades on the M1 timeframe using a combination of moving average crossovers and RSI-based entry signals. It aims to capture small price movements with tight take-profit targets and moderate stop-losses. The EA implements a basic trend filter alongside momentum confirmation before opening positions.

Strategy Analysis

RabbitM2 strategy is based on a combination of short-term moving average crossover and RSI overbought/oversold zone signals for trade entries. Entry conditions trigger a buy when the fast MA crosses above the slow MA and RSI is within a specific threshold range, with the reverse for sell entries. Exit conditions include fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels, as well as trend reversal exits when the MAs cross in the opposite direction. Position sizing uses a fixed lot model without dynamic adjustment based on account balance or volatility. Money management lacks risk-proportional calculation, using the same lot size for every trade regardless of account equity changes. The overall strategy design is relatively simple, suitable for short-term trading in low-volatility market environments, but may face consecutive stop-losses in highly volatile or violently oscillating markets.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单明了，易于理解和调整参数
  • 结合趋势和动量双重过滤，减少虚假信号
  • 固定止盈止损有助于控制单笔交易风险
  • M1时间周期适合快速进出的短线交易需求
  • 魔术号码机制可避免与其他EA订单冲突

劣势

  • 固定手数不考虑账户资金变化，风险暴露不灵活
  • 缺乏点差过滤机制，在高点差环境下容易亏损
  • 未实现新K线检查，可能在同一根K线上重复开仓
  • 没有追踪止损或动态止盈机制，无法锁定浮动利润
  • RSI阈值固定不变，无法适应不同市场波动环境

改进建议

  • 增加动态仓位计算功能，根据账户余额和风险比例自动调整手数
  • 添加点差过滤和新K线检查机制，提高交易安全性和避免重复开仓

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and adjust parameters
  • Dual filtering with both trend and momentum reduces false signals
  • Fixed take-profit and stop-loss helps control per-trade risk
  • M1 timeframe suits quick in-and-out short-term trading needs
  • Magic number mechanism prevents order conflicts with other EAs

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot size ignores account equity changes, inflexible risk exposure
  • Lacks spread filter, prone to losses in high-spread environments
  • No new bar check implemented, may repeatedly open positions on the same bar
  • No trailing stop or dynamic take-profit mechanism to lock in floating profits
  • Fixed RSI thresholds cannot adapt to different market volatility conditions

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing that automatically adjusts lots based on account balance and risk ratio
  • Add spread filter and new bar check mechanism to improve trading safety and avoid duplicate entries
