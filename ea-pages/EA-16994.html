摘要

该EA基于布林带均值回归信号交易，价格突破上轨时卖出，突破下轨时买入。采用固定的止盈止损点数距离，并提供基于账户余额的动态仓位调整选项。每个tick检查持仓盈亏，达到阈值时平仓。

策略分析

策略概念是典型的布林带均值回归：当价格偏离布林带上轨一定距离时做空，偏离下轨一定距离时做多，假设价格会回归均值。入场条件为收盘价高于上轨加额外偏移量时SELLme=true触发卖出，收盘价低于下轨减额外偏移量时BUYme=true触发买入。出场条件有两种：当持仓浮盈达到ExtProfitMade点数时止盈平仓，当浮亏达到ExtLossLimit点数时止损平仓，同时也设置了SL/TP挂单保护。仓位管理方面，支持固定手数和基于余额的动态手数两种模式，动态模式下手数随账户余额线性增减，上限500手。资金管理较为粗糙，仅基于余额比例调整手数，没有最大回撤控制或风险百分比计算。策略使用PRICE_OPEN计算布林带，周期仅4，属于非常短的参数设置，容易产生频繁信号和过度交易。

Summary

This EA trades Bollinger Bands mean-reversion signals, selling when price breaks above the upper band and buying when it breaks below the lower band. It uses a fixed take-profit and stop-loss based on point distances, with an optional lot-sizing mechanism that scales position size proportionally to account balance. Order management is performed on each tick, closing positions when profit or loss thresholds are hit.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is classic Bollinger Band mean reversion: it sells when price breaks above the upper band by a threshold distance and buys when price breaks below the lower band, assuming price will revert to the mean. Entry conditions trigger SELLme when close price exceeds upper band plus ExtBDistance points, and BUYme when close falls below lower band minus ExtBDistance points. Exit conditions are dual: positions are closed in-code when unrealized profit reaches ExtProfitMade points or loss reaches ExtLossLimit points, while SL/TP orders are also placed at order open for broker-side protection. Position sizing offers two modes: fixed lots or dynamic lots that scale linearly with account balance relative to starting balance, capped at 500 lots. Money management is rudimentary, relying solely on a balance-ratio lot calculation without drawdown limits or percentage-of-equity risk sizing. The Bollinger Band uses PRICE_OPEN with a very short period of 4, which is likely to generate excessive signals and lead to overtrading.

优势

  • 使用布林带均值回归逻辑，策略思路清晰易懂
  • 同时设置了代码内止盈止损和挂单SL/TP，双重保护机制
  • 提供OneOrderOnly模式限制同时只持一仓，有助于控制风险敞口
  • 日志记录功能完善，便于回测分析和问题排查
  • digits_adjust机制自动适配3/5位小数品种

劣势

  • 布林带周期仅4，信号过于频繁，属于过度敏感的参数设置
  • 止损20点止盈3点的盈亏比极差（约1:0.15），长期必然亏损
  • 动态手数随余额线性增长，盈利时会加速放大仓位，回撤时风险失控
  • 没有点差过滤，布林带突破策略在宽点差品种上容易被止损
  • 代码中SL/TP计算额外加了10点偏移，但缺乏注释解释原因，维护困难
  • 每tick都执行完整循环检查所有持仓，效率低下

改进建议

  • 提高布林带周期至20以上，并增加信号确认机制（如RSI超买超卖或成交量确认）以减少虚假信号
  • 改善盈亏比，将止盈提高至至少与止损1:1或更高，或改用移动止损锁定利润
  • 加入最大回撤限制和基于权益风险百分比的仓位管理，替代简单的余额比例计算
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，避免在高成本时段入场

Strengths

  • Clear and understandable Bollinger Band mean-reversion logic
  • Dual exit protection with both in-code profit/loss checks and broker-side SL/TP orders
  • OneOrderOnly mode restricts to a single position, helping control risk exposure
  • Comprehensive logging functionality aids backtesting analysis and troubleshooting
  • Automatic digits_adjust mechanism for 3/5 digit symbol compatibility

Weaknesses

  • BB period of 4 is extremely short, generating overly frequent signals and likely overtrading
  • Risk-reward ratio is terrible at roughly 1:0.15 (20 point SL vs 3 point TP), virtually guaranteeing long-term losses
  • Dynamic lot sizing scales linearly with balance, accelerating position size during wins and risking blow-up during drawdowns
  • No spread filter means the strategy is vulnerable to wide-spread instruments eating into the tiny profit target
  • SL/TP calculations add an unexplained 10-point offset, making maintenance difficult without documentation
  • Full position loop runs every tick rather than on new bars only, wasting computational resources

Improvements

  • Increase BB period to 20+ and add signal confirmation (e.g., RSI overbought/oversold or volume) to reduce false signals
  • Improve risk-reward ratio by raising take-profit to at least 1:1 with stop-loss, or implement trailing stop to lock profits
  • Add maximum drawdown limits and equity-percentage risk sizing instead of simple balance-ratio lot calculation
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to avoid entries during high-cost periods
