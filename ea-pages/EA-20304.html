EA Stop Order

This EA places stop orders (buy stop and sell stop) at a configurable distance from the current price, aiming to capture breakout momentum when price moves beyond a defined range. It automatically manages pending orders with stop loss and take profit levels, and can trail stops or delete unfilled orders after a specified period. The strategy is a classic stop-order breakout system suitable for volatile market conditions.

Strategy analysis

EA Stop Order employs a classic stop-order breakout strategy by placing a Buy Stop above the current price and a Sell Stop below it. The entry condition is triggered when price breaks through a pre-configured distance threshold, confirming the breakout direction before entering in the trend's direction. Exit conditions are controlled through stop loss and take profit parameters, ensuring each trade has a defined risk-reward ratio. Position sizing is based on a fixed lot parameter, with some versions supporting dynamic lot calculation based on account balance. Money management provides stop loss distance parameters to control per-trade risk, but lacks comprehensive account-level risk controls such as maximum drawdown limits or consecutive loss protection. The EA also features pending order timeout deletion to avoid frequent false breakout triggers during ranging markets.

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and optimize, suitable for beginners
  • Stop order breakout entry effectively captures momentum at trend initiation
  • Each trade has defined stop loss and take profit, maintaining controllable risk-reward ratio
  • Pending order timeout deletion reduces unnecessary losses from false breakouts
  • Bi-directional pending orders ensure no breakout opportunity is missed in either direction

Weaknesses

  • Lacks account-level money management with no maximum drawdown or daily loss limits
  • Fixed lot sizing ignores account balance changes, potentially leading to overtrading or underutilization of capital
  • Prone to frequent false breakout triggers during ranging markets, causing consecutive stop losses
  • No new bar check mechanism, may attempt to place orders every tick, wasting resources
  • Lacks volatility filter (e.g., ATR), using identical parameters across different volatility regimes produces inconsistent results

Possible improvements

  • Add ATR-based adaptive stop distance and order placement distance, enabling the strategy to auto-adjust parameters based on market volatility
  • Implement account-level risk controls including maximum drawdown limits, daily maximum loss limits, and trading pause after consecutive losses
  • Implement dynamic position sizing that auto-calculates lot size based on account balance and risk percentage

Risk assessment

Overall risk: Medium

  • Bi-directional pending orders in ranging markets can trigger both stop losses simultaneously, causing double losses
  • No new bar check may lead to duplicate order placement or resource waste
  • Lacks market regime filter, continues trading even in unsuitable market conditions
