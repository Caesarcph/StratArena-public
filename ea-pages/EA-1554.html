摘要

该EA利用快慢均线差的立方放大器生成动量信号，配合高低阈值判别器确定交易方向。当放大信号突破上阈值时做多，突破下阈值时做空，信号翻转时反转持仓。

策略分析

策略核心概念是对快慢均线差值进行立方放大（cubic amplifier），将微小的均线偏差非线性放大，使得趋势动量变化更加敏感。入场条件为：当放大后的信号值px突破动态高点hi时标记为买入（trade=1），当px跌破动态低点lo时标记为卖出（trade=-1），高低阈值之间维持固定间距Delta（即通道宽度）。出场条件隐含在反转逻辑中——当已有持仓且信号方向相反时，会平掉当前仓位并开反向仓，本质上是一个永远在场的反转系统。仓位管理采用基于可用保证金的自适应手数计算，公式为AccountFreeMargin/2000并限制最大15手。资金管理方面没有止损和止盈设置，完全依赖信号反转来退出交易，风险控制较弱。

Summary

This EA uses a cubic amplifier of the difference between fast and slow moving averages to generate momentum signals, with a high-low threshold discriminator for trade direction. It enters long when the amplified signal breaks above the upper threshold and short when it breaks below the lower threshold, reversing positions when the signal flips direction.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept applies a cubic amplifier (pow(m*(mf-ms),3)) to the difference between fast and slow moving averages, non-linearly magnifying small deviations to make momentum shifts more sensitive. Entry conditions: when the amplified signal px breaks above the dynamic high threshold hi, it flags a buy (trade=1); when px breaks below the dynamic low threshold lo, it flags a sell (trade=-1), with the hi-lo channel maintaining a fixed width of Delta pips. Exit conditions are implicit in the reversal logic — when a position exists and the signal flips direction, the EA closes the current position and opens the opposite, making this an always-in-the-market reversal system. Position sizing uses an adaptive lot calculation based on free margin (FreeMargin/2000), capped at 15 lots maximum. Money management lacks explicit stop-loss and take-profit levels, relying entirely on signal reversal for exits, which presents significant risk exposure.

优势

  • 立方放大器设计巧妙，能将微小趋势信号非线性放大，提高灵敏度
  • 动态高低阈值通道机制有效过滤市场噪音，避免频繁交易
  • 自适应仓位计算基于可用保证金，能在不同账户规模下自动调整

劣势

  • 完全没有止损和止盈设置，单笔交易风险敞口无限
  • 永远在场的反转策略在震荡市中会产生连续亏损
  • Delta值使用硬编码的0.00001乘数，不适应不同报价精度（如JPY对）
  • 缺乏新K线检查，每个tick都会评估交易条件，可能导致过度交易

改进建议

  • 添加止损和止盈参数，控制单笔交易最大亏损
  • 加入新K线检测逻辑，避免同一根K线内重复交易
  • 根据品种的小数位数自动调整Delta乘数，兼容不同报价精度
  • 增加震荡市过滤条件（如ADX指标），避免在横盘时反复反转

Strengths

  • The cubic amplifier design is ingenious, non-linearly magnifying small trend signals for improved sensitivity
  • The dynamic high-low threshold channel mechanism effectively filters market noise and avoids excessive trading
  • Adaptive position sizing based on free margin automatically adjusts for different account sizes

Weaknesses

  • No stop-loss or take-profit at all, exposing each trade to unlimited risk
  • The always-in reversal strategy generates consecutive losses in ranging markets
  • Delta uses a hardcoded 0.00001 multiplier, not adapting to different quote precisions (e.g., JPY pairs)
  • Lacks new bar check, evaluating trade conditions on every tick which may lead to over-trading

Improvements

  • Add stop-loss and take-profit parameters to control maximum loss per trade
  • Implement new bar detection to prevent repeated trading within the same bar
  • Auto-adjust the Delta multiplier based on symbol digits for compatibility with different quote precisions
  • Add a ranging market filter (e.g., ADX indicator) to avoid repeated reversals in sideways markets
