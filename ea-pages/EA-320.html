摘要

该EA使用经过移动平均平滑处理的MFI（市场促进指数）指标作为动量信号过滤器，在MetaQuotes标准EA框架内运行。它根据聚合信号强度的阈值开仓和平仓，采用固定手数且无追踪止损。策略依赖单一自定义信号过滤器（CML_MFI）来判断交易方向。

策略分析

该策略基于MFI（市场促进指数）指标，通过对其施加移动平均平滑处理来识别市场动量变化。入场条件为当CML_MFI过滤器的加权信号值超过开盘阈值（默认40）时开仓，低于平仓阈值（默认20）时平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数方式（默认0.1手），不根据账户资金动态调整。资金管理使用CMoneyFixedLot类，设定百分比为10%，但实际采用固定手数交易，百分比参数在此模式下未实质生效。该策略没有设置止损和止盈（均为0.0），也没有追踪止损，这意味着持仓完全依赖信号反转来平仓，存在较大风险。挂单有效期为4根K线，超时后挂单自动取消。

Summary

This EA uses the MFI (Market Facilitation Index) indicator smoothed by a Moving Average as a momentum signal filter within the MetaQuotes standard Expert Advisor framework. It opens and closes positions based on threshold values for the aggregated signal strength, with fixed lot sizing and no trailing stop. The strategy relies on a single custom signal filter (CML_MFI) to determine trade direction.

Strategy Analysis

This strategy is based on the MFI (Market Facilitation Index) indicator, applying a Moving Average smoothing to identify momentum shifts in the market. Entry conditions trigger when the weighted signal value from the CML_MFI filter exceeds the open threshold (default 40), and exit conditions trigger when the signal falls below the close threshold (default 20). Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach (default 0.1 lots) without dynamic adjustment based on account equity. Money management employs the CMoneyFixedLot class with a percentage setting of 10%, though the percentage parameter is not effectively used in fixed-lot mode. The strategy has no stop loss or take profit configured (both set to 0.0), and no trailing stop is applied, meaning positions rely entirely on signal reversal for closure, which introduces significant risk. Pending orders have an expiration of 4 bars, after which they are automatically cancelled.

优势

  • 基于MFI指标的动量策略逻辑清晰，指标平滑处理有助于减少假信号
  • 使用MetaQuotes标准EA框架，代码结构规范，模块化程度高
  • 信号阈值可调，开仓和平仓阈值分离，提供灵活的交易控制

劣势

  • 没有设置止损和止盈，持仓完全依赖信号反转，可能导致大幅亏损
  • 仅依赖单一信号过滤器，缺乏多维度确认，策略鲁棒性不足
  • 固定手数不根据市场波动或账户风险动态调整，资金管理过于简单

改进建议

  • 增加止损和止盈设置，防止单笔交易出现过大亏损
  • 添加额外的信号过滤器（如趋势过滤器或波动率过滤器）以提高信号的可靠性
  • 将固定手数改为基于ATR或账户风险的动态仓位管理

Strengths

  • Clear momentum strategy logic based on MFI indicator with MA smoothing helps reduce false signals
  • Uses MetaQuotes standard EA framework with well-structured code and high modularity
  • Adjustable signal thresholds with separate open/close thresholds provide flexible trade control

Weaknesses

  • No stop loss or take profit configured; positions rely entirely on signal reversal, which may lead to significant drawdowns
  • Relies on a single signal filter without multi-dimensional confirmation, lacking strategy robustness
  • Fixed lot sizing does not adjust dynamically based on market volatility or account risk, making money management overly simplistic

Improvements

  • Add stop loss and take profit settings to prevent excessive losses on individual trades
  • Add additional signal filters (e.g., trend or volatility filters) to improve signal reliability
  • Replace fixed lot sizing with dynamic position sizing based on ATR or account risk
