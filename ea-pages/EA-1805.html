摘要

IREA（逆向反应EA）基于异常价格冲击将被反向修正的理念进行交易。它使用自定义InverseReaction指标检测绝对变动超过置信阈值的价格棒，然后开反向单预期均值回归。入场受最小和最大棒尺寸条件过滤，每笔交易设有固定止损和止盈。

策略分析

IREA的策略概念是均值回归：当价格出现异常大幅波动时，预期市场会产生反向修正。入场条件由自定义InverseReaction指标生成信号——当最近一根K线的绝对变化量（IRC）大于置信水平（IRL）且处于MinCriteria和MaxCriteria之间时，视为有效信号。具体逻辑要求第一根K线（sign[0]）必须满足条件，且两根K线不能同时满足（避免连续异常），同时IRC为负时做多、为正时做空，即逆势交易。出场条件依赖固定的止损（1000点）和止盈（250点），止盈远小于止损，说明策略依赖较高的胜率来弥补不利的盈亏比。仓位管理采用固定手数（TrVol=1.0），没有动态仓位调整或资金管理逻辑。资金管理方面完全缺失，没有回撤控制、没有最大持仓限制、没有资金百分比下单机制。

Summary

IREA (Inverse Reaction EA) trades the idea that unusual price impulses will be corrected by an inverse reaction. It uses a custom InverseReaction indicator to detect bars whose absolute change exceeds a confidence threshold, then opens a counter-direction trade expecting mean reversion. Entries are filtered by minimum and maximum bar size criteria, and each trade has a fixed stop loss and take profit.

Strategy Analysis

IREA's strategy concept is mean reversion: when price exhibits an unusually large impulse move, the market is expected to produce an inverse corrective reaction. Entry conditions are generated by a custom InverseReaction indicator — a signal is valid when the absolute bar change (IRC) exceeds the confidence level (IRL) and falls between MinCriteria and MaxCriteria. The specific logic requires the first bar (sign[0]) to satisfy the condition while both bars must not simultaneously satisfy it (avoiding consecutive anomalies), and a negative IRC triggers a buy while a positive IRC triggers a sell, trading against the impulse direction. Exit conditions rely on fixed stop loss (1000 points) and take profit (250 points), with the TP being much smaller than the SL, meaning the strategy depends on a high win rate to compensate for the unfavorable risk-reward ratio. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size (TrVol=1.0) with no dynamic position adjustment or money management logic. Money management is completely absent — there is no drawdown control, no maximum position limit, and no percentage-of-equity sizing mechanism.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰简洁，基于均值回归理念有理论依据
  • 设有最小和最大K线尺寸双重过滤，避免在极端行情和微小波动中入场
  • 仅在新区块时交易且无持仓时才开仓，避免重复入场和过度交易
  • 使用自定义指标置信阈值（基于1.618系数），信号有统计学基础

劣势

  • 盈亏比极差（止盈250点vs止损1000点，1:4），需要超过80%胜率才能盈利
  • 固定手数无资金管理，无法根据账户规模和风险动态调整仓位
  • 无回撤控制机制，连续亏损时不会停止交易
  • 止损1000点过大，单笔亏损可能占账户很大比例
  • 依赖自定义指标InverseReaction，指标缺失时EA无法运行

改进建议

  • 增加动态仓位管理，根据账户余额百分比或ATR计算手数
  • 改善盈亏比，考虑缩小止损或采用移动止损/分批止盈策略
  • 增加最大回撤限制和最大连续亏损次数限制，保护账户资金
  • 增加点差过滤，避免在高点差时段入场影响均值回归策略的微利特性

Strengths

  • Clear and concise strategy logic based on the well-established mean reversion concept
  • Dual filtering by minimum and maximum bar size prevents entries during extreme moves and insignificant fluctuations
  • Trades only on new bars and when no positions are open, avoiding repeated entries and overtrading
  • Custom indicator confidence threshold based on the golden ratio (1.618) provides a statistical basis for signals

Weaknesses

  • Extremely poor risk-reward ratio (TP 250 vs SL 1000, 1:4 ratio), requiring over 80% win rate to be profitable
  • Fixed lot sizing with no money management, unable to dynamically adjust position based on account size and risk
  • No drawdown control mechanism — the EA will continue trading through consecutive losses without stopping
  • Stop loss of 1000 points is excessively large, a single loss could represent a significant portion of the account
  • Depends on a custom InverseReaction indicator; the EA cannot function if the indicator is missing

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing based on account equity percentage or ATR-based calculation
  • Improve risk-reward ratio by tightening the stop loss or implementing trailing stops / partial profit taking
  • Add maximum drawdown limit and maximum consecutive loss count to protect account capital
  • Add spread filtering to avoid entries during high-spread periods that erode the narrow profit margins of mean reversion
