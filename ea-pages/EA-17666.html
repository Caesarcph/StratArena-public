摘要

该EA基于CG振荡器（重心振荡器）信号进行交易，当振荡器向下穿越下阈值时开多仓，向上穿越上阈值时开空仓。它采用双信号X2确认方法，使用两个不同周期的CG振荡器实例来过滤虚假入场信号。EA实现了固定手数的仓位管理以及止损/止盈设置。

策略分析

策略概念基于重心振荡器（CG Oscillator），该指标通过线性回归加权方法计算价格的重心位置，属于均值回归类策略。入场条件为：当快速CG振荡器和慢速CG振荡器同时发出超卖信号并反转时做多，当两者同时发出超买信号并反转时做空，X2双确认机制有效减少了单指标假信号。出场条件包括止损、止盈触发，以及反向信号出现时平仓。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，未根据账户余额动态调整。资金管理方面，止损和止盈由用户设定参数控制，但缺乏浮亏比例限制和最大持仓数量控制。

Summary

This EA trades based on the CG Oscillator (Center of Gravity Oscillator) signals, entering buy positions when the oscillator crosses below a lower threshold and sell positions when it crosses above an upper threshold. It uses a dual-signal X2 confirmation approach with two CG Oscillator instances of different periods to filter false entries. The EA implements position management with fixed lot sizing and stop-loss/take-profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the Center of Gravity (CG) Oscillator, which calculates the price's center of gravity through linear regression weighting, classifying it as a mean reversion strategy. Entry conditions require both a fast CG oscillator and a slow CG oscillator to simultaneously issue oversold signals and reverse for buy entries, and overbought signals and reverse for sell entries, with the X2 dual-confirmation mechanism effectively reducing false signals from a single indicator. Exit conditions include stop-loss and take-profit triggers, as well as position closing when a reverse signal appears. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic adjustment based on account balance. In terms of money management, stop-loss and take-profit are controlled by user-set parameters, but there is no floating loss percentage limit or maximum position count control.

优势

  • 双CG振荡器X2确认机制有效过滤假信号，提高入场质量
  • 策略逻辑清晰，基于均值回归原理具有统计学依据
  • 支持止损止盈设置，具备基本的风险控制框架

劣势

  • 固定手数交易不考虑账户规模变化，资金管理不够灵活
  • 缺乏最大持仓限制和浮亏比例控制，极端行情下风险敞口不可控
  • 均值回归策略在强趋势行情中容易连续止损，缺乏趋势过滤机制

改进建议

  • 增加动态仓位管理，根据账户余额和波动率自动调整手数
  • 添加趋势过滤指标（如ADX或长期均线），在强趋势行情中暂停或调整均值回归交易

Strengths

  • Dual CG Oscillator X2 confirmation mechanism effectively filters false signals and improves entry quality
  • Clear strategy logic based on mean reversion principles with statistical justification
  • Supports stop-loss and take-profit settings, providing a basic risk control framework

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing does not adapt to account size changes, making money management inflexible
  • Lacks maximum position limits and floating loss percentage controls, leaving risk exposure uncontrolled in extreme markets
  • Mean reversion strategy is prone to consecutive stop-losses in strong trending markets, lacking a trend filter mechanism

Improvements

  • Add dynamic position sizing that automatically adjusts lot size based on account balance and volatility
  • Add a trend filter indicator (such as ADX or long-term moving average) to pause or adjust mean reversion trades during strong trending markets
