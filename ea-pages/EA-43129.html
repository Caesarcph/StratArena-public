摘要

该EA检测价格与振荡指标（可能是RSI或MACD）之间的背离，并结合布林带进行入场过滤。当背离信号暗示潜在反转时开仓，利用布林带确认超买或超卖状态。带点数止损止盈的变体提供固定风险管理，而警报版本仅通知不下单。

策略分析

策略核心是背离交易，即价格创新高/新低而指标未创新高/新低时，预示趋势可能反转。入场条件：检测到看涨背离（价格新低+指标更高低点）时做多，检测到看跌背离（价格新高+指标更低高点）时做空，同时结合布林带确认价格处于极端位置（触及上轨做空、下轨做多）。出场条件：使用固定点数的止损和止盈，止损设在背离极值点之外，止盈根据风险回报比设定。仓位管理采用固定手数或基于账户余额的比例手数，部分变体可能支持追踪止损。资金管理方面，依赖布林带收窄和背离的双重确认来过滤假信号，但缺少动态仓位调整机制。

Summary

This EA detects divergence between price and an oscillator indicator (likely RSI or MACD) combined with Bollinger Bands for entry filtering. It opens trades when divergence signals a potential reversal, using the Bollinger Bands to confirm overbought or oversold conditions. The variant with pip-based SL/TP provides fixed risk management while the alert version only notifies without placing trades.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy is divergence trading, where a new price high/low not confirmed by the oscillator signals a potential trend reversal. Entry conditions: go long on bullish divergence (price lower low + indicator higher low) and go short on bearish divergence (price higher high + indicator lower high), with Bollinger Bands confirming extreme price levels as an additional filter. Exit conditions use fixed pip-based stop loss and take profit values, with stops placed beyond the divergence extremum and take profit set according to a risk-reward ratio. Position sizing employs fixed lots or balance-proportional lots, with some variants potentially supporting trailing stops. Money management relies on the dual confirmation of Bollinger Band contraction and divergence to filter false signals, but lacks dynamic position adjustment based on volatility or account equity drawdown limits.

优势

  • 背离是高胜率的反转信号，结合布林带过滤提高了入场质量
  • 固定止损止盈使风险可控且易于回测优化
  • 多文件变体显示策略在持续迭代改进

劣势

  • 背离检测在震荡市中容易产生假信号导致频繁亏损
  • 固定点数止损未考虑市场波动率变化，在高波动时段可能被轻易扫到
  • 缺少最大回撤保护和连续亏损后的仓位缩减机制

改进建议

  • 加入ATR动态止损止盈，根据市场波动率自动调整风险参数
  • 增加多时间周期确认，要求高一级时间周期趋势方向一致才入场

Strengths

  • Divergence is a high-probability reversal signal; Bollinger Band filter improves entry quality
  • Fixed SL/TP makes risk controllable and easy to backtest and optimize
  • Multiple file variants indicate ongoing iterative strategy improvement

Weaknesses

  • Divergence detection generates false signals in choppy markets leading to frequent losses
  • Fixed pip stops ignore volatility changes and can be easily swept during high-volatility periods
  • Lacks maximum drawdown protection and position reduction after consecutive losses

Improvements

  • Add ATR-based dynamic SL/TP to automatically adjust risk parameters based on market volatility
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation requiring higher timeframe trend alignment before entry
