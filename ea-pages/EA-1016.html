摘要

该EA使用ATR_Trailing自定义指标对已有持仓进行移动止损管理。它不会开立新仓位，仅根据ATR衍生的追踪止损水平修改已有多单或空单的止损。EA按K线周期运行，仅在新K线出现时重新计算追踪止损位。

策略分析

该EA的策略概念是基于ATR（平均真实波幅）指标来动态调整持仓的止损位，实现追踪止损功能。入场条件方面，该EA本身不具备入场逻辑，它只管理已有持仓的止损，不负责开仓。出场条件是通过ATR_Trailing指标计算出的上轨（UpValue）和下轨（DnValue）来设置止损位：空头持仓以上轨为止损，多头持仓以下轨为止损。仓位管理方面，EA不涉及开仓手数的计算，仅对已存在仓位进行止损调整。资金管理方面，该EA没有任何资金管理逻辑，不计算风险比例或仓位大小。当持仓没有止损时，EA会根据指标值立即设置初始止损；当已有止损时，仅在新的止损位对持仓更有利时才修改（买止损只能上移，卖止损只能下移）。

Summary

This EA trails the stop-loss of an existing position using the ATR_Trailing custom indicator. It does not open new positions—it only modifies the SL of already-open buy or sell positions based on ATR-derived trailing levels. The EA operates on a per-bar basis, recalculating the trailing stop only when a new bar appears.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept of this EA is to dynamically adjust the stop-loss of existing positions based on the ATR (Average True Range) indicator, implementing a trailing stop mechanism. Regarding entry conditions, the EA itself has no entry logic—it only manages the stop-loss of existing positions and does not open trades. Exit conditions are determined by the ATR_Trailing indicator's upper band (UpValue) and lower band (DnValue): short positions use the upper band as stop-loss, while long positions use the lower band. In terms of position sizing, the EA does not calculate lot sizes as it never opens positions—it only modifies stop-loss levels of existing ones. For money management, the EA has no money management logic whatsoever, calculating neither risk ratios nor position sizes. When a position has no stop-loss, the EA immediately sets an initial stop based on indicator values; when a stop-loss already exists, the EA only modifies it when the new stop level is more favorable (buy stops can only move up, sell stops can only move down).

优势

  • 基于ATR的追踪止损能自适应市场波动率变化，在波动大时止损宽、波动小时止损紧
  • 只在NewBar时计算止损调整，避免了逐Tick计算的过度交易和资源浪费
  • 止损只能朝有利方向移动的设计（买止损只上移、卖止损只下移），符合追踪止损的核心逻辑
  • 代码结构清晰，买卖持仓的止损修改逻辑分离为独立函数

劣势

  • EA不具备开仓功能，完全依赖外部手动开仓或其他EA开仓，使用场景受限
  • 订单填充类型硬编码为ORDER_FILLING_FOK，不兼容所有券商，可能导致某些环境下修改失败
  • 缺少对品种交易状态和连接状态的检查，在市场关闭或断线时仍可能尝试下单
  • dSellPositionModify函数中使用Ask价格而非Bid价格来计算空头止损距离，逻辑不够精确
  • OnDeinit函数未释放指标句柄和CChart对象，存在资源泄漏

改进建议

  • 在OnDeinit中添加IndicatorRelease释放指标句柄，并调用cchart.Detach()分离图表对象，避免资源泄漏
  • 将ORDER_FILLING_FOK改为根据品种动态选择填充类型，提高券商兼容性
  • 在空头止损计算中使用Bid价格替代Ask价格，使止损距离计算更准确

Strengths

  • ATR-based trailing stop adapts to market volatility—wider stops in volatile markets and tighter stops in calm markets
  • Stop recalculation only on new bars avoids excessive per-tick computation and unnecessary trade requests
  • Stop-loss can only move in the favorable direction (buy stops only move up, sell stops only move down), which is the core logic of trailing stops
  • Clean code structure with buy and sell position modification logic separated into independent functions

Weaknesses

  • The EA cannot open positions and relies entirely on manual opening or other EAs, limiting its use cases
  • Order filling type is hardcoded to ORDER_FILLING_FOK, which is incompatible with some brokers and may cause modification failures
  • No checks for symbol trading status or connection state—may attempt to modify positions when the market is closed or connection is lost
  • The dSellPositionModify function uses Ask price instead of Bid price to calculate short position stop distance, which is logically imprecise
  • OnDeinit does not release the indicator handle or detach the CChart object, causing resource leaks

Improvements

  • Add IndicatorRelease in OnDeinit to free the indicator handle and call cchart.Detach() to detach the chart object, preventing resource leaks
  • Replace ORDER_FILLING_FOK with dynamically selected filling type based on the symbol, improving broker compatibility
  • Use Bid price instead of Ask price in short position stop-loss calculation for more accurate stop distance computation

风险点

  • EA本身不设止损也不开仓，若持仓由其他方式开立且无初始止损，在EA启动前存在无止损风险
  • 硬编码FOK填充类型可能在某些券商导致止损修改持续失败，持仓将失去追踪止损保护
  • 缺少交易状态和连接检查，断线或市场关闭期间止损无法及时调整

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • The EA itself does not set stops or open positions—if a position is opened without an initial stop before the EA starts, there is a no-stop-risk window
  • Hardcoded FOK filling type may cause persistent stop modification failures on some brokers, leaving positions without trailing stop protection
  • No trading status or connection checks—stops cannot be adjusted in time during disconnection or market closure
