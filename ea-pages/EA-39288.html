摘要

这是一个追踪止损工具EA，用于激活和管理已有持仓的追踪止损功能。它本身不开仓交易，而是监控并调整止损水平以在价格有利移动时保护利润。

策略分析

从文件名'Trailing Activate'可以判断，这是一个追踪止损管理工具而非自主交易策略EA。其主要功能是在已有持仓上激活追踪止损，当价格朝有利方向移动时，止损会跟随价格移动以锁定利润。该工具不会自行产生入场信号或开仓，而是依赖于用户手动或其他EA建立的持仓。仓位管理方面，它仅修改已有订单的止损水平，不涉及新仓位的开立。资金管理方面，追踪止损本身就是一种资金保护机制，通过逐步上移止损来减少回撤风险。由于无法读取源代码，具体参数如追踪距离、激活阈值、追踪步长等无法确认。

Summary

This EA is a trailing stop utility that activates and manages trailing stops on existing open positions. It does not open trades on its own but instead monitors and adjusts stop loss levels to protect profits as price moves favorably.

Strategy Analysis

Based on the filename 'Trailing Activate', this is a trailing stop management utility rather than an autonomous trading strategy EA. Its primary function is to activate trailing stops on existing positions, moving the stop loss to lock in profits as price moves favorably. This tool does not generate its own entry signals or open trades; it depends on positions established manually or by other EAs. In terms of position management, it only modifies stop loss levels of existing orders without opening new positions. Regarding money management, trailing stop is itself a capital protection mechanism that reduces drawdown risk by gradually moving the stop loss forward. Without access to the source code, specific parameters such as trailing distance, activation threshold, and trailing step size cannot be confirmed.
