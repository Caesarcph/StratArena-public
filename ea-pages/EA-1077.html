摘要

这是一个三均线交叉趋势跟踪EA，使用快、中、慢三条SMA均线生成交易信号。当快均线上穿中均线且两者均在慢均线下方时做多，反之做空。EA包含基于账户风险和连续亏损的仓位优化功能，以及持仓追踪止损功能。

策略分析

策略概念基于三条简单移动平均线（SMA）的交叉关系判断趋势方向，属于经典的多均线趋势跟踪系统。入场条件：做多信号为快均线从前一根K线低于中均线变为当前K线高于中均线（金叉），同时快均线和中均线当前值均低于慢均线，确保在大趋势向下的情况下捕捉反弹或趋势转折；做空信号为相反的死叉条件，且快中均线均在慢均线上方。出场条件包括固定点数的止盈和止损，以及基于追踪止损的动态出场机制，追踪止损只在价格超过开仓价后激活。仓位管理方面，当Lots参数为0时根据账户可用保证金和MaximumRisk计算动态仓位，否则使用固定手数。资金管理引入了DecreaseFactor参数，在连续亏损后按比例递减仓位大小，降低连续亏损期间的风险敞口，但不存在加仓或马丁格尔机制。

Summary

A triple moving average crossover EA that uses three SMA lines (fast, middle, slow) to generate trend-following trade signals. It enters buy when the fast MA crosses above the middle MA while both remain below the slow MA, and sells on the inverse condition. The EA includes lot optimization based on account risk and consecutive losses, plus a trailing stop for open positions.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the crossover relationship of three Simple Moving Averages (SMA) to determine trend direction, representing a classic multi-MA trend-following system. Entry conditions: a buy signal is generated when the fast MA crosses above the middle MA from the previous bar to the current bar (golden cross), while both the fast and middle MA values remain below the slow MA, ensuring trades align with the broader trend context; sell signals use the inverse dead cross condition with both fast and middle MAs above the slow MA. Exit conditions include fixed take-profit and stop-loss in points, plus a dynamic trailing stop mechanism that only activates when price moves beyond the entry price. Position sizing uses dynamic lot calculation based on account free margin and MaximumRisk when Lots is set to 0, otherwise uses a fixed lot size. Money management incorporates a DecreaseFactor parameter that proportionally reduces lot size after consecutive losing trades, lowering risk exposure during drawdown periods, though there is no position-adding or martingale mechanism.

优势

  • 使用三均线过滤系统，要求快中均线均在慢均线同一侧才能入场，有效减少震荡行情中的虚假信号
  • 内置连续亏损递减仓位机制，在连亏期间自动降低风险敞口，有助于控制回撤
  • 追踪止损机制仅在盈利超过开仓价后激活，并能正确检查StopsLevel避免被经纪商拒绝

劣势

  • SMA均线本身滞后性严重，三条SMA组合进一步加剧信号延迟，容易错过趋势初期或在高点低点附近才入场
  • 仅支持单品种单持仓，在趋势行情中无法加仓扩大盈利，且不区分多空持仓类型
  • 缺少市场波动率过滤（如ATR），在低波动震荡市中仍会产生交叉信号导致频繁止损

改进建议

  • 将SMA替换为EMA或DEMA以减少滞后性，或加入ATR波动率过滤在低波动时暂停交易
  • 增加多时间框架确认逻辑，例如在更高周期趋势方向一致时才允许入场，提高信号质量

Strengths

  • Triple MA filter system requiring both fast and middle MAs to be on the same side of the slow MA reduces false signals in ranging markets
  • Built-in consecutive loss lot reduction mechanism automatically decreases risk exposure during drawdown periods, helping control equity decline
  • Trailing stop only activates after price exceeds entry and correctly checks StopsLevel to prevent broker rejection of modification requests

Weaknesses

  • SMA indicators are inherently lagging, and combining three SMAs further delays signal generation, often causing late entries near trend peaks or troughs
  • Supports only single symbol and single position, preventing pyramiding in trending markets, and does not distinguish between long and short position types
  • Lacks volatility filtering (e.g., ATR), so crossover signals still occur in low-volatility ranging markets leading to frequent stop-loss triggers

Improvements

  • Replace SMA with EMA or DEMA to reduce lag, or add ATR volatility filtering to pause trading during low-volatility periods
  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation logic, such as only allowing entries when the higher timeframe trend direction aligns, to improve signal quality

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 追踪止损和开仓逻辑中的Pos.Select仅按品种选择，如果同品种有多个持仓则可能无法正确处理
  • DecreaseFactor递减机制在极端连亏情况下可能将手数降至最小值但仍持续交易，无法自动停止交易
  • 缺少点差过滤和滑点保护，在高点差环境下止盈止损可能被轻易触发

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Pos.Select by symbol only cannot correctly handle multiple positions on the same symbol, which may cause trailing stop or opening logic errors
  • The DecreaseFactor reduction mechanism may reduce lots to minimum but continue trading during extreme consecutive losses without auto-stopping
  • No spread filter or slippage protection, causing take-profit and stop-loss to be easily triggered in high-spread environments
