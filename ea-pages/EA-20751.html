摘要

该EA基于K线实体相对于历史平均值的偏离程度进行交易。当当前K线实体显著超过历史平均K线实体大小时，将其视为动量信号，沿K线方向入场交易。

策略分析

策略概念基于K线实体变化率，即当前K线实体与历史平均K线实体的比值作为交易信号。入场条件为当前K线实体大小超过平均值的某个倍数阈值时开仓：阳线（收盘价高于开盘价）做多，阴线（收盘价低于开盘价）做空。出场条件依赖固定止盈止损或反向信号触发平仓。仓位管理通常采用固定手数方式，未根据账户资金动态调整。资金管理方面缺乏复合仓位控制，风险主要取决于止损设置和信号过滤的可靠性。整体策略逻辑简单直观，属于基于价格行为统计偏离的动量型策略。

Summary

This EA trades based on the average change of candle bodies compared to historical norms. It identifies when the current candle body significantly deviates from the average candle body size, treating above-average bodies as momentum signals for entry in the direction of the candle.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the candle body change ratio, comparing the current candle body size to the historical average as a trading signal. Entry conditions trigger when the current candle body exceeds a multiple threshold of the historical average: a bullish candle (close above open) signals a buy, while a bearish candle (close below open) signals a sell. Exit conditions rely on fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels or opposing signals to close positions. Position sizing typically uses fixed lots without dynamic adjustment based on account equity. Money management lacks compound position control, and risk depends primarily on stop-loss settings and signal filtering reliability. The overall strategy logic is simple and intuitive, representing a momentum-type approach based on statistical deviation of price behavior.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简洁直观，易于理解和验证
  • 基于统计偏离的动量概念具有合理的市场逻辑支撑
  • 参数较少，减少了过拟合的风险

劣势

  • 固定手数缺乏根据账户风险动态调整的仓位管理
  • 未考虑交易成本（点差、佣金）对短线策略的侵蚀效应
  • 单根K线信号缺乏多时间框架确认，容易受市场噪音干扰产生虚假信号

改进建议

  • 增加多时间框架确认机制，例如在H1信号方向与H4趋势一致时才入场
  • 引入基于ATR或账户余额的动态仓位管理，替代固定手数

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is simple and intuitive, easy to understand and verify
  • The momentum concept based on statistical deviation has reasonable market logic support
  • Few parameters reduce the risk of overfitting

Weaknesses

  • Fixed lot sizing lacks dynamic position management based on account risk
  • Does not account for trading costs (spread, commission) erosion on short-term strategies
  • Single candle signal lacks multi-timeframe confirmation, prone to false signals from market noise

Improvements

  • Add multi-timeframe confirmation, e.g., only enter when H1 signal aligns with H4 trend direction
  • Introduce dynamic position sizing based on ATR or account balance instead of fixed lots

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 单一K线信号缺乏过滤机制，在震荡市中容易频繁开平仓
  • 固定止盈止损未考虑市场波动率变化，低波动期止损可能过大而高波动期止盈可能不足

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Single candle signal lacks filtering mechanism, prone to frequent opening and closing in ranging markets
  • Fixed stop-loss and take-profit do not adapt to market volatility changes; stop-loss may be too wide in low volatility and take-profit insufficient in high volatility
