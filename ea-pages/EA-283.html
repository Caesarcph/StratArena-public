Expert_ABC_WS_Stoch

This EA uses a Stochastic oscillator combined with a moving average filter (CBC_WS_Stoch) to generate trading signals within the MQL5 Standard Library Expert Advisor framework. It employs fixed lot sizing for money management and no trailing stop, relying on the signal thresholds to open and close positions based on overbought/oversold mean reversion logic.

Strategy analysis

The core strategy of this EA is based on the CBC_WS_Stoch signal filter, which combines the Stochastic oscillator with a short-period moving average (MA period=5). Entry conditions rely on the Stochastic indicator's overbought/oversold zones, with the MA serving as an auxiliary filter; a trade is opened when the composite signal strength exceeds the open threshold (default 40) and closed when it falls below the close threshold (default 20). Position sizing uses a fixed lot model (MoneyFixedLot) with a default of 0.1 lots, which does not dynamically adjust based on account balance or equity. Money management sets a 10% percent parameter but effectively uses fixed lots, making risk control relatively simplistic. Notably, there is no stop loss (Signal_StopLevel=0) and no take profit (Signal_TakeLevel=0), meaning the EA relies entirely on signal reversal for exits, which can lead to significant losses if signals fail to reverse in time. No trailing stop is used (TrailingNone), and exits are triggered solely by signal changes or pending order expiration (4 bars), reflecting a mean reversion philosophy overall.

Strengths

  • Built on the MQL5 Standard Library Expert framework with clean, modular code structure that is easy to extend
  • Stochastic + MA combination filter reduces false signals; the non-standard K period (47) avoids common parameter congestion
  • Signal threshold mechanism (open=40/close=20) provides flexible signal sensitivity control adaptable to different market conditions

Weaknesses

  • No stop loss or take profit configured (both set to 0), exposing each trade to unlimited risk
  • Fixed lot money management does not dynamically adjust with account growth, preventing compounding and not adapting to different account sizes
  • No trailing stop and pending order expiration of only 4 bars may cause missed exit opportunities in volatile markets or frequent order expirations

Possible improvements

  • Add stop loss and take profit parameter settings with reasonable default values to control maximum per-trade loss
  • Replace fixed lot money management with dynamic proportional position sizing (e.g., MoneyFixedMargin or MoneyFixedPercent) so lot sizes auto-adjust with account equity

Risk assessment

Overall risk: High risk

  • Stop loss and take profit are both 0, relying entirely on signal reversal for exits, facing huge loss risk in extreme market conditions
  • No new bar check mechanism (Expert_EveryTick=false but not based on new bar logic), which may cause repeated signals within the same bar leading to anomalous behavior
