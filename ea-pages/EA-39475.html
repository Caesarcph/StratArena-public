摘要

Lotsizer是一个仓位计算工具型EA，根据账户余额、净值、风险百分比和止损距离来计算最优手数。它不会自行开仓，而是辅助交易者确定正确的交易量，以防止过度杠杆。该工具专注于基于风险的仓位计算。

策略分析

Lotsizer并非自主交易EA，而是一个仓位管理辅助工具。它的核心功能是根据用户设定的风险比例和止损点数，自动计算出建议的下单手数，确保每笔交易的风险金额在可控范围内。入场和出场条件由用户自行决定，该工具不产生任何交易信号。仓位管理是其主要功能，通过账户余额或净值乘以风险百分比得到风险金额，再除以止损点数对应的每点价值，得出推荐手数。资金管理方面，它帮助交易者严格遵守单笔风险上限，避免因手数过大导致账户爆仓。该工具通常还提供账户信息显示，如余额、净值、已用保证金和可用保证金等，方便交易者全面了解账户状态。

Summary

Lotsizer is a position sizing utility EA that calculates optimal lot sizes based on account balance, equity, risk percentage, and stop loss distance. It does not open trades on its own but assists traders in determining the correct volume for manual or semi-automated trading decisions. The tool focuses on risk-based position calculation to prevent over-leveraging.

Strategy Analysis

Lotsizer is not an autonomous trading EA but a position sizing assistant utility. Its core function is to calculate the recommended lot size based on the user-specified risk percentage and stop loss distance, ensuring the risk amount per trade stays within controllable limits. Entry and exit conditions are determined entirely by the user, as this tool does not generate any trading signals. Position sizing is its primary function: it multiplies account balance or equity by the risk percentage to obtain the risk amount, then divides by the per-point value corresponding to the stop loss distance to derive the recommended lot size. In terms of money management, it helps traders strictly adhere to per-trade risk limits, preventing account blowouts from excessively large positions. The tool typically also displays account information such as balance, equity, used margin, and free margin, enabling traders to have a comprehensive view of their account status.

优势

  • 帮助交易者严格控制每笔交易的风险金额，防止过度杠杆
  • 使用简单直观，只需输入风险比例和止损距离即可获得推荐手数
  • 实时显示账户关键信息，便于交易决策
  • 可根据账户余额或净值灵活切换计算基准

劣势

  • 不产生交易信号，完全依赖交易者自身的判断
  • 缺少对点差和手续费的考虑，实际风险可能略高于计算值
  • 没有历史交易统计功能，无法评估整体风险暴露
  • 仅在本地运行，无法跨账户统一管理仓位

改进建议

  • 增加对点差和佣金的考虑，使风险计算更精确
  • 加入多仓位总风险计算功能，显示当前所有持仓的总风险暴露百分比

Strengths

  • Helps traders strictly control risk amount per trade, preventing over-leveraging
  • Simple and intuitive to use - just input risk percentage and stop loss distance to get recommended lot size
  • Displays key account information in real-time for informed trading decisions
  • Flexible switching between balance and equity as the calculation basis

Weaknesses

  • Does not generate trading signals, entirely dependent on the trader's own judgment
  • Does not account for spread and commission costs, so actual risk may be slightly higher than calculated
  • Lacks historical trade statistics, unable to assess overall risk exposure
  • Runs locally only, cannot manage position sizing across multiple accounts uniformly

Improvements

  • Add consideration for spread and commission to make risk calculation more accurate
  • Add multi-position aggregate risk calculation to display total risk exposure percentage across all open positions
