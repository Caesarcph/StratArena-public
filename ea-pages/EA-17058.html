摘要

该EA通过识别日线级别的关键支撑和阻力水平，在价格突破这些水平时入场交易。它基于日线高低点突破策略，沿突破方向下单，并设有可配置的止损和止盈水平。

策略分析

策略核心概念是基于日线级别的支撑阻力位突破交易。EA在每个交易日开始时计算前一日的高点和低点作为关键水平，当价格突破前日高点时做多，突破前日低点时做空。入场条件是价格收盘突破关键水平，而非仅仅刺穿，这有助于过滤假突破。出场条件采用固定止损止盈方式，止损设置在突破位的反方向一定距离，止盈则根据风险回报比设定。仓位管理采用固定手数方式，没有根据账户余额动态调整的机制。资金管理方面缺乏马丁格尔或网格等危险策略，但也没有使用百分比风险模型来控制每笔交易的风险敞口。整体策略逻辑清晰，属于经典的日内突破交易系统。

Summary

This EA identifies key support and resistance levels from daily price data and enters trades when price breaks through those levels. It uses a breakout strategy based on daily high/low breakdowns, placing orders in the direction of the breakout with configurable stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of this strategy is based on daily-level support and resistance breakout trading. The EA calculates the previous day's high and low as key levels at the start of each trading day, going long when price breaks above the previous day's high and going short when price breaks below the previous day's low. Entry conditions require a price close beyond the key level rather than just a wick penetration, which helps filter out false breakouts. Exit conditions use fixed stop loss and take profit, with the stop loss placed a configurable distance opposite the breakout level and take profit set based on a risk-reward ratio. Position sizing uses a fixed lot approach without dynamic adjustment based on account balance. Money management lacks dangerous strategies like martingale or grid, but also does not employ a percentage-risk model to control risk exposure per trade. Overall, the strategy logic is clear and belongs to the classic intraday breakout trading system category.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简洁清晰，基于经典的日线突破原理，易于理解和验证
  • 使用收盘价确认突破而非简单刺穿，有效减少假突破带来的亏损
  • 不使用马丁格尔或网格等危险策略，风险相对可控

劣势

  • 仓位管理采用固定手数，未根据账户规模和波动性动态调整，不利于长期资金增长
  • 缺乏移动止损或部分平仓机制，无法在盈利交易中锁定部分利润
  • 没有考虑交易时段和市场波动性过滤，在低流动性时段可能产生不利入场
  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未根据ATR等波动性指标动态调整，在不同市场环境下适应性不足

改进建议

  • 引入ATR动态止损止盈机制，使风险管理参数能根据市场波动性自动调整
  • 增加移动止损功能，在价格朝有利方向移动时逐步锁定利润
  • 添加交易时段过滤和波动性过滤，避免在低流动性或窄幅震荡时段入场

Strengths

  • Strategy logic is clean and clear, based on classic daily breakout principles, easy to understand and verify
  • Uses closing price confirmation for breakouts rather than simple wick penetration, effectively reducing losses from false breakouts
  • Does not use dangerous strategies like martingale or grid, keeping risk relatively controlled

Weaknesses

  • Position sizing uses fixed lots without dynamic adjustment based on account size and volatility,不利于长期资金增长
  • Lacks trailing stop or partial close mechanisms, unable to lock in partial profits on winning trades
  • Does not consider trading sessions or market volatility filters, may produce unfavorable entries during low liquidity periods
  • Stop loss and take profit are fixed pip values without dynamic adjustment using ATR or similar volatility indicators, lacking adaptability across different market conditions

Improvements

  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit mechanism so risk management parameters auto-adjust to market volatility
  • Add trailing stop functionality to progressively lock in profits as price moves favorably
  • Add trading session and volatility filters to avoid entries during low liquidity or narrow range periods

总体 / Overall:MEDIUM

风险点

  • 固定止损止盈不适应市场波动性变化，在高波动期可能止损过近导致频繁止损
  • 没有持仓检查和重复下单防护机制，可能存在同一信号多次开仓的风险
  • 固定手数仓位管理不随账户余额调整，长期可能导致过度交易或资金利用不足

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Fixed stop loss and take profit do not adapt to changing market volatility; during high volatility periods stops may be too tight causing frequent stop-outs
  • Lacks position check and duplicate order protection, potentially risking multiple opens on the same signal
  • Fixed lot position sizing does not adjust with account balance, potentially leading to overtrading or underutilization of capital over time
