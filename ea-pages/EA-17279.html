摘要

21hour EA是一个基于21周期移动均线的趋势跟踪专家顾问。当价格上穿21均线时做多，下穿时做空，持仓时间随趋势延续而延长。该策略旨在捕捉中长期趋势走势，同时过滤短期噪音。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略概念是利用21周期移动均线判断趋势方向，当价格从下方突破均线时开多仓，从上方跌破均线时开空仓。入场条件依赖于价格与均线的交叉关系，属于典型的单均线趋势跟踪系统。出场条件为价格反向穿越均线时平仓，没有设置独立的止损或止盈逻辑，完全依赖均线信号来决定持仓时长。仓位管理方面，EA使用固定手数进行交易，没有根据账户资金或波动率进行动态仓位调整。资金管理方面较为薄弱，缺乏最大回撤控制、资金比例仓位计算等风险控制机制。整体来看，这是一个简洁的趋势跟踪系统，但风险管理和仓位管理设计不足，在震荡行情中容易产生频繁的假信号亏损。

Summary

The 21hour EA is a trend-following Expert Advisor that trades based on a 21-period moving average applied to the H1 timeframe. It enters long positions when price crosses above the 21 MA and short positions when price crosses below, holding positions for extended periods as dictated by the trend. The strategy aims to capture medium-to-long-term trend movements while filtering out short-term noise.

Strategy Analysis

The core strategy concept relies on a 21-period moving average to determine trend direction, entering long when price crosses above the MA and short when price crosses below. Entry conditions are based purely on price-MA crossover signals, representing a classic single-MA trend following system. Exit conditions trigger when price reverses back across the MA, with no independent stop-loss or take-profit logic—position duration is entirely dictated by the moving average signal. Position sizing uses a fixed lot size without dynamic adjustment based on account equity or market volatility. Money management is relatively weak, lacking drawdown controls, percentage-based sizing, or other risk management mechanisms. Overall, this is a minimalist trend following system with insufficient risk and position management design, making it vulnerable to frequent whipsaw losses in ranging markets.

优势

  • 策略逻辑简单清晰，易于理解和维护
  • 趋势行情中能够有效捕捉中长期走势
  • 单均线系统参数少，减少过拟合风险

劣势

  • 单均线系统在震荡市中频繁产生假信号
  • 缺乏止损止盈机制，亏损可能无限扩大
  • 固定手数未考虑账户资金比例，风险控制不足
  • 没有新K线检查机制，可能重复开仓

改进建议

  • 增加止损止盈机制，控制单笔最大亏损
  • 添加趋势过滤指标（如ADX或大周期MA），减少震荡市假信号
  • 实现基于账户资金比例的动态仓位管理

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and maintain
  • Effectively captures medium-to-long-term trends in trending markets
  • Single MA system with few parameters reduces overfitting risk

Weaknesses

  • Single MA system generates frequent false signals in ranging markets
  • Lack of stop-loss and take-profit mechanisms allows unlimited loss exposure
  • Fixed lot size ignores account equity proportion, insufficient risk control
  • No new bar check mechanism may cause repeated position opening

Improvements

  • Add stop-loss and take-profit mechanisms to control maximum loss per trade
  • Add trend filter indicators (e.g., ADX or higher-period MA) to reduce ranging market false signals
  • Implement dynamic position sizing based on account equity percentage
