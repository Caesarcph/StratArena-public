摘要

这是一个通道突破EA，当价格突破 configurable 回溯期的最高价或最低价时，分别下BuyStop和SellStop挂单，止损基于通道对侧边界进行追踪。同时内置自营公司挑战辅助功能，监控每日亏损限额和盈利目标，当触及任一阈值时自动平仓和撤单。

策略分析

策略核心是Donchian通道突破：使用过去N根K线的最高价和最低价形成交易通道，价格突破通道上轨时做多，突破下轨时做空。入场条件为：无同方向持仓且挂单价位与当前价的距离满足经纪商止损级别要求时，放置BuyStop或SellStop挂单，触发价位为通道边界加/减一个tick。出场条件采用双重机制：一是追踪止损，使用出场周期的最低价（多单）或最高价（空单）作为动态止损位；二是当价格反向穿越追踪止损位时直接平仓。仓位管理基于风险百分比模型：根据每笔交易的风险占权益的百分比（默认1%）计算手数，使用OrderCalcProfit估算每手止损金额，再反算合适手数，同时考虑手数步长、最小/最大手数限制以及可用保证金。资金管理方面，EA内置自营公司挑战辅助功能，通过isPassed检查权益是否达到目标，通过isDailyLimit检查当日累计亏损是否超过限额，二者均调用ClearAll函数关闭所有持仓和挂单。

Summary

A channel breakout EA that enters BuyStop/SellStop orders when price breaks above the highest high or below the lowest low of a configurable lookback period, with a trailing stop based on the opposite channel boundary. It includes a prop firm challenge helper that monitors daily loss limits and profit targets, automatically closing all positions and orders when either threshold is breached.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy core is a Donchian-style channel breakout: it uses the highest high and lowest low over the past N bars to form a trading channel, going long when price breaks above the upper channel and short when it breaks below the lower channel. Entry conditions require no existing position in the same direction and that the pending order price is at least the broker's minimum stop level away from the current price, placing BuyStop or SellStop orders at the channel boundary plus/minus one tick. Exit conditions use a dual mechanism: a trailing stop that dynamically adjusts to the lowest low (for longs) or highest high (for shorts) over the exit period, and an immediate market close when price crosses against the trailing level. Position sizing is based on a risk-percentage model: it calculates lot size from RISK_PER_TRADE percent of equity divided by the per-lot stop-loss value estimated via OrderCalcProfit, then floors to the volume step and clamps to broker min/max limits while checking available margin. Money management includes a prop firm challenge helper that monitors equity against a profit target (isPassed) and daily accumulated losses against a loss limit (isDailyLimit), both triggering ClearAll to close all positions and cancel all pending orders when breached.

优势

  • 风险百分比仓位管理模型科学合理，每笔交易风险可控
  • 内置自营公司挑战辅助功能，自动监控盈利目标和每日亏损限额
  • 追踪止损机制灵活，使用通道对侧边界作为动态止损位
  • 考虑了经纪商交易限制，包括止损级别、手数限制和保证金检查
  • 使用新K线检测避免同一K线内重复交易

劣势

  • ATR指标句柄在PositionManaging中每次调用都重新创建，未在OnInit中缓存，存在资源泄漏风险
  • OrderManaging函数返回的orders变量始终为0，逻辑存在缺陷
  • 每日亏损计算假设历史交易按时间倒序排列并使用break退出，可能遗漏当日早期交易
  • ClearAll函数未过滤魔术号和交易品种，可能关闭其他EA的持仓和挂单
  • 缺乏点差过滤，在高点差环境下突破信号可能被虚假触发
  • 追踪止损修改条件'0.1*ATR'硬编码，无法通过参数调整

改进建议

  • 将ATR指标句柄在OnInit中创建并缓存，在OnDeinit中释放，避免每次调用时重复创建造成资源泄漏
  • ClearAll函数应增加魔术号和交易品种过滤，避免误关闭其他EA的订单和持仓
  • 每日亏损计算应遍历所有历史交易而非依赖倒序break逻辑，确保准确统计当日所有盈亏

Strengths

  • Risk-percentage position sizing model is sound and keeps per-trade risk controlled
  • Built-in prop firm challenge helper automatically monitors profit targets and daily loss limits
  • Flexible trailing stop mechanism using the opposite channel boundary as a dynamic stop level
  • Accounts for broker trading constraints including stop levels, volume limits, and margin checks
  • New bar detection prevents redundant trading within the same bar

Weaknesses

  • ATR indicator handle is created fresh on every PositionManaging call instead of being cached in OnInit, risking resource leaks
  • OrderManaging function always returns 0 due to the orders variable never being incremented, a logical flaw
  • Daily loss calculation assumes history deals are reverse-chronological and uses break to exit, potentially missing earlier same-day trades
  • ClearAll does not filter by magic number or symbol, potentially closing positions and orders from other EAs
  • No spread filter, so breakout signals may be falsely triggered in high-spread environments
  • Trailing stop adjustment threshold of 0.1*ATR is hardcoded and not configurable via input parameters

Improvements

  • Create and cache the ATR indicator handle in OnInit and release it in OnDeinit to avoid resource leaks from repeated creation on every call
  • Add magic number and symbol filtering to ClearAll to prevent accidentally closing other EAs' orders and positions
  • Daily loss calculation should iterate through all history deals instead of relying on reverse-chronological break logic to ensure accurate same-day P&L accounting
