摘要

这是一个多货币智能交易系统，同时交易最多12个货币对，使用SMA斜率方向作为唯一信号。当SMA持续上升时开多仓，SMA持续下降时开空仓，SMA斜率反转时平仓。仓位大小根据可用保证金动态计算，但止损和止盈输入参数在订单逻辑中被忽略。

策略分析

该策略基于简单移动平均线(SMA)的斜率方向判断趋势。入场条件为：当SMA在最近两个区间（bar 1-2和bar 2-3）的差值均为正时，认为趋势向下而发出买入信号（DnSignal）；当差值均为负时，认为趋势向上而发出卖出信号（UpSignal）。出场条件为：当SMA最近一个区间（bar 1-2）的差值符号反转时平仓。仓位管理方面，Money_M()函数根据账户可用保证金动态计算手数，公式为自由保证金/100000*10，并限制在0.1到5手之间。但存在严重缺陷：BuyPositionOpen和SellPositionOpen函数中request.volume直接调用Money_M()而非使用传入的Lots参数，且止损(StLoss)和止盈(TkProfit)参数在订单请求中被硬编码为0，完全忽略了用户输入。策略同时在最多12个货币对上运行，每个货币对独立产生信号和执行交易。

Summary

A multicurrency Expert Advisor that trades up to 12 currency pairs simultaneously using SMA slope direction as its sole signal. It opens buy positions when the SMA is consistently rising and sell positions when the SMA is consistently falling, with exit signals triggered on any SMA slope reversal. Position sizing is dynamically calculated from free margin but stop-loss and take-profit input parameters are ignored in the order logic.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy is based on the slope direction of a Simple Moving Average (SMA) to determine trend direction. Entry conditions: when the SMA differences over the last two consecutive periods (bar 1-2 and bar 2-3) are both positive, a buy signal is generated (DnSignal=true); when both differences are negative, a sell signal is generated (UpSignal=true). Note that the signal naming is counterintuitive and appears inverted — DnSignal triggers buys and UpSignal triggers sells. Exit conditions: when the SMA difference for the most recent period (bar 1-2) reverses sign, the corresponding position is closed. Position sizing is dynamically calculated by the Money_M() function using the formula FreeMargin/100000*10, clamped between 0.1 and 5 lots, with increment normalization based on lot size. A critical flaw exists: BuyPositionOpen and SellPositionOpen use Money_M() for request.volume instead of the passed Lots parameter, and StopLoss/TakeProfit are hardcoded to 0 in the order request, completely ignoring user input. The EA operates on up to 12 currency pairs simultaneously, each generating independent signals and executing trades.

优势

  • 支持多达12个货币对同时交易，实现多货币分散化
  • 使用新K线检测(IsNewBar)避免同一根K线重复交易
  • 信号和执行逻辑分离(TradeSignalCounter与TradePerformer)，代码结构清晰
  • 动态仓位管理根据可用保证金自动调整手数

劣势

  • 止损和止盈参数完全无效，订单中硬编码为0，交易无保护
  • Lots参数被Money_M()覆盖，用户输入的手数设置完全无效
  • 信号命名逻辑混乱：DnSignal触发买入，UpSignal触发卖出，容易造成理解错误
  • MaMethod参数传入但iMA调用中硬编码MODE_SMA，参数形同虚设
  • 没有点差过滤，短线反转信号可能被高点差吞噬利润
  • iMA handle在初始化时创建但OnDeinit中没有释放，造成资源泄漏

改进建议

  • 修复BuyPositionOpen和SellPositionOpen中request.volume应使用传入的Lots参数，request.sl和request.tp应使用传入的StLoss和TkProfit参数计算实际价位
  • 在OnDeinit中释放所有iMA指标句柄(Handle)以防止资源泄漏
  • 添加点差过滤和最大持仓检查，增强风险控制
  • 修正信号变量命名使其更直观（BuySignal/SellSignal替代DnSignal/UpSignal）
  • 使用PositionSelect后应考虑用PositionGetTicket和PositionSelectByTicket替代，因PositionSelect在多仓位对冲账户中可能不可靠

Strengths

  • Supports simultaneous trading on up to 12 currency pairs for diversification
  • Uses new bar detection (IsNewBar) to prevent duplicate trades within the same bar
  • Clean separation of signal generation (TradeSignalCounter) and execution (TradePerformer)
  • Dynamic position sizing automatically adjusts lot size based on free margin

Weaknesses

  • Stop-loss and take-profit parameters are completely non-functional — orders hardcode SL=0 and TP=0, leaving trades unprotected
  • The Lots parameter is overridden by Money_M(), making user-configured lot sizes entirely ineffective
  • Signal naming is confusing and inverted: DnSignal triggers buy orders and UpSignal triggers sell orders
  • MaMethod parameter is passed but iMA() call hardcodes MODE_SMA, rendering the parameter useless
  • No spread filter exists, so small reversal signals can be easily consumed by high spreads
  • iMA indicator handles are created in OnInit but never released in OnDeinit, causing resource leaks

Improvements

  • Fix BuyPositionOpen/SellPositionOpen to use the passed Lots parameter for request.volume and calculate actual SL/TP price levels from StLoss/TkProfit instead of hardcoding 0
  • Release all iMA indicator handles in OnDeinit to prevent resource leaks
  • Add spread filter and maximum position check for better risk control
  • Rename signal variables to be more intuitive (BuySignal/SellSignal instead of DnSignal/UpSignal)
  • Replace PositionSelect with PositionSelectByTicket for reliability on hedging accounts

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:HIGH

风险点

  • 止损和止盈完全无效，所有订单以SL=0和TP=0发出，交易无任何价格保护
  • 仓位大小由Money_M()自动计算，在账户保证金充足时会开出较大仓位，缺乏手数上限控制
  • 多货币对同时交易可能导致相关性风险， EURUSD和GBPUSD等正相关货币对同时开仓会放大风险

Risk Assessment

Overall:HIGH

Risk Points

  • Stop-loss and take-profit are completely non-functional — all orders are sent with SL=0 and TP=0, providing zero price protection
  • Position sizing by Money_M() can open large positions when account margin is sufficient, with only a 5-lot cap and no per-symbol risk limit
  • Simultaneous multi-currency trading creates correlation risk — correlated pairs like EURUSD and GBPUSD opening together amplify exposure
