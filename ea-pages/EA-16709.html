摘要

e-Regr是一个基于线性回归通道分析的均值回归交易EA。它在价格偏离回归线到达通道边界时开仓，预期价格将回归均值。该EA利用回归通道斜率过滤趋势方向，并通过可配置的止损止盈管理仓位。

策略分析

e-Regr策略的核心概念是线性回归通道的均值回归，它通过计算指定周期内的线性回归线及其上下通道边界来判断价格偏离程度。入场条件为：当价格触及或穿越回归通道上轨时做空，触及或穿越下轨时做多，同时会结合通道斜率方向进行过滤，只有在斜率支持交易方向时才开仓。出场条件包括：价格回归到回归线附近时平仓获利，或触及止损位时止损退出。仓位管理采用固定手数或基于资金比例的动态手数计算方式。资金管理方面提供了最大持仓限制和连续亏损后的暂停机制，但止损设置相对固定，未考虑市场波动率的动态调整。

Summary

e-Regr is an Expert Advisor based on linear regression channel analysis for mean reversion trading. It identifies price deviations from the regression line and enters trades when price reaches the channel boundaries, expecting a return to the mean. The EA uses the regression channel slope to filter trend direction and manages positions with configurable stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of e-Regr is mean reversion based on linear regression channel analysis, calculating the regression line and its upper/lower channel boundaries over a specified period to measure price deviation. Entry conditions: short when price touches or crosses the upper regression band, long when price touches or crosses the lower band, with an optional filter using the channel slope to confirm trade direction alignment. Exit conditions include taking profit when price reverts to the regression mean line, or cutting losses at a configurable stop loss level. Position sizing uses either fixed lot sizes or an equity-proportional dynamic lot calculation. Money management features include maximum position limits and a pause mechanism after consecutive losses, though stop loss levels are relatively static without adapting to market volatility changes.

优势

  • 线性回归通道提供了统计学上有依据的均值回归参考，比简单移动平均线更具鲁棒性
  • 斜率过滤机制有效避免逆趋势交易，减少了在强趋势行情中的亏损
  • 参数结构清晰，回归周期和通道宽度可调，适应不同品种和时间周期

劣势

  • 均值回归策略在强趋势行情中容易连续亏损，即使有斜率过滤也无法完全避免
  • 止损止盈为固定点数，未根据ATR或通道宽度动态调整，在不同波动率环境下表现不一致
  • 缺乏多时间周期确认机制，单一周期信号容易被噪音干扰产生虚假信号

改进建议

  • 引入ATR动态止损止盈，根据当前市场波动率自动调整风险参数
  • 增加多时间周期回归通道确认，提高信号可靠性并减少虚假交易

Strengths

  • Linear regression channel provides a statistically grounded mean reversion reference, more robust than simple moving averages
  • Slope filter mechanism effectively avoids counter-trend trades, reducing losses during strong trending markets
  • Clean parameter structure with adjustable regression period and channel width, adaptable to different instruments and timeframes

Weaknesses

  • Mean reversion strategies are prone to consecutive losses during strong trending markets, even with slope filtering
  • Fixed stop loss and take profit in points without ATR-based dynamic adjustment leads to inconsistent performance across varying volatility regimes
  • Lacks multi-timeframe confirmation, making single-timeframe signals susceptible to noise-induced false entries

Improvements

  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit to automatically adjust risk parameters based on current market volatility
  • Add multi-timeframe regression channel confirmation to improve signal reliability and reduce false trades

  • 均值回归策略在极端趋势行情中可能承受连续亏损，通道会随趋势偏移而无法提供有效支撑阻力

Risk Assessment

Overall:MEDIUM

Risk Points

  • Mean reversion strategy may suffer consecutive losses in extreme trending markets as the channel shifts with the trend, failing to provide effective support/resistance
  • Fixed-point stop loss ignores market volatility changes — too wide in low volatility wasting profit potential, too tight in high volatility getting stopped out easily
