摘要

Billy Expert是一个基于布林带均值回归策略的EA，当价格触及下轨时买入，触及上轨时卖出，预期价格回归中轨。包含仓位管理和止损止盈设置。

策略分析

该EA采用布林带均值回归策略，核心概念是价格偏离均值后会回归。入场条件为：当价格触及或突破布林带下轨时开多单，当价格触及或突破布林带上轨时开空单。出场条件包括：价格回归至布林带中轨时平仓，或触及止损/止盈水平。仓位管理方面，支持固定手数交易，部分版本支持基于资金比例的动态手数计算。资金管理方面，设置了止损和止盈参数，但具体的风险控制力度取决于参数配置。整体策略逻辑简单直观，但在强趋势行情中可能面临连续亏损的风险。

Summary

Billy Expert is a Mean Reversion EA that trades based on Bollinger Bands deviation from the mean. It enters buy positions when price touches the lower band and sell positions when price touches the upper band, expecting price to revert to the middle band. The EA includes lot sizing and stop loss/take profit management.

Strategy Analysis

This EA employs a Bollinger Bands mean reversion strategy, based on the concept that prices deviating from the mean will eventually revert. Entry conditions: open buy positions when price touches or breaks below the lower Bollinger Band, and open sell positions when price touches or breaks above the upper Bollinger Band. Exit conditions include closing positions when price reverts to the middle Bollinger Band, or when stop loss/take profit levels are hit. Position sizing supports fixed lot trading, with some versions offering dynamic lot calculation based on account balance percentage. Money management includes configurable stop loss and take profit parameters, though the effectiveness of risk control depends on parameter settings. The overall strategy logic is simple and intuitive, but it faces the risk of consecutive losses during strong trending markets where mean reversion fails.

  • 增加持仓时间限制，避免长期套牢

Strengths

  • Simple and clear strategy logic, easy to understand and debug
  • Bollinger Bands is a classic technical indicator validated over long market history
  • Mean reversion strategy performs well in ranging/sideways markets

Weaknesses

  • May sustain continuous losses in strong trending markets when price does not revert to mean
  • Lacks trend filtering mechanism, prone to counter-trend trading
  • Missing advanced money management features like dynamic position sizing or trailing stop

Improvements

  • Add a trend filter (e.g., ADX or higher timeframe MA) to avoid counter-trend entries during strong trends
  • Add trailing stop functionality to lock in floating profits
  • Add maximum position holding time limit to avoid prolonged drawdowns
