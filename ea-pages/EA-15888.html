摘要

该EA基于JFatl_Digit_System自定义指标进行交易，该指标结合了JMA（Jurik移动平均线）与FATL（快速自适应趋势线）数字滤波。当指标发出上升趋势信号时开多仓，发出下降趋势信号时开空仓，并配有可配置的止损止盈水平。

策略分析

策略概念基于JFatl_Digit_System指标，该指标融合了JMA平滑与FATL数字滤波技术，通过数字量化方式判断趋势方向。入场条件为：当指标缓冲区6的值大于2且缓冲区0的值小于3时开多仓；当缓冲区6的值小于2且缓冲区0的值大于1时开空仓。出场条件通过独立的买卖平仓信号控制，与开仓信号分离，允许灵活的平仓逻辑。仓位管理通过MM参数控制下单量，支持五种资金管理模式，包括固定手数、按余额比例、按可用保证金比例等。止损设为1000点，止盈设为2000点，盈亏比为1:2，体现了趋势跟踪策略让利润奔跑的理念。策略仅在新增K线时计算信号，避免了逐笔计算的噪音干扰。

Summary

This EA trades based on the JFatl_Digit_System custom indicator, which combines a JMA (Jurik Moving Average) with FATL (Fast Adaptive Trend Line) digital filtering. It opens buy positions when the indicator signals an uptrend and sell positions when it signals a downtrend, with configurable stop loss and take profit levels.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on the JFatl_Digit_System indicator, which fuses JMA smoothing with FATL digital filtering to determine trend direction through digital quantization. Entry conditions: a buy position opens when indicator buffer 6 value is greater than 2 and buffer 0 value is less than 3; a sell position opens when buffer 6 value is less than 2 and buffer 0 value is greater than 1. Exit conditions are controlled by independent buy/sell close signals, decoupled from entry signals, allowing flexible closing logic. Position sizing is managed via the MM parameter, supporting five money management modes including fixed lots, balance-based, and free margin-based sizing. Stop loss is set at 1000 points and take profit at 2000 points, yielding a 1:2 risk-reward ratio that reflects the trend-following philosophy of letting profits run. The strategy only calculates signals on new bars, avoiding noise from tick-by-tick computation.

优势

  • 策略逻辑清晰，使用JMA与FATL数字滤波结合，信号平滑且噪音较低
  • 止损1000点配合止盈2000点，盈亏比1:2，符合趋势跟踪策略的核心原则
  • 支持五种资金管理模式，灵活适配不同风险偏好
  • 开仓与平仓信号独立控制，可分别启用或禁用买卖方向
  • 仅在新K线时计算信号，减少虚假触发和资源消耗

劣势

  • 高度依赖自定义指标JFatl_Digit_System，缺少该指标时EA无法运行
  • min_rates_total变量被覆盖为3+SignalBar，原始计算39+30+Shift的逻辑被丢弃，可能是编程错误
  • 没有点差过滤，在高点差环境下可能产生不利成交
  • SignalBar为0时的特殊处理逻辑不明确，可能导致意外行为
  • 止损和止盈为固定点数，未考虑市场波动率变化，在低波动或高波动行情中可能不适用

改进建议

  • 修复min_rates_total被覆盖的bug，应合并两行逻辑或使用正确的最小K线数计算
  • 增加点差过滤机制，避免在点差过大时入场
  • 引入ATR动态止损止盈，使风险管理适应市场波动率

Strengths

  • Clear strategy logic combining JMA with FATL digital filtering for smooth, low-noise signals
  • Stop loss of 1000 points with take profit of 2000 points yields a 1:2 risk-reward ratio, aligning with trend-following principles
  • Supports five money management modes for flexible risk preference adaptation
  • Independent open/close signal control allows enabling/disabling buy and sell directions separately
  • Signal calculation only on new bars reduces false triggers and resource consumption

Weaknesses

  • Highly dependent on the custom JFatl_Digit_System indicator; EA cannot run without it
  • min_rates_total variable is overwritten from 39+30+Shift to 3+SignalBar, discarding the original calculation — likely a programming bug
  • No spread filter, potentially leading to unfavorable fills in high-spread environments
  • Special handling logic when SignalBar equals 0 is unclear and may cause unexpected behavior
  • Fixed stop loss and take profit in points do not adapt to market volatility changes, potentially unsuitable in low or high volatility conditions

Improvements

  • Fix the min_rates_total overwrite bug; merge the two lines or use correct minimum bar count calculation
  • Add a spread filter to prevent entry when spread is excessively wide
  • Introduce ATR-based dynamic stop loss and take profit for volatility-adaptive risk management
