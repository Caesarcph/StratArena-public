摘要

一个周期性区间突破EA，基于ATR、百分比或固定范围在定时（每周或每日）设定价格边界（高/低），当价格突破边界时开仓交易。支持多种交易模式（止损/限价/随机）、手数管理策略（固定/线性/马丁格尔/斐波那契）和可配置的止盈/止损百分比。EA在StandBy、Setup、Trade和Deal四个阶段之间循环，每笔交易完成后重置。

策略分析

策略概念是基于时间周期（每周指定日期或每日指定时间）建立价格区间，等待价格突破区间边界后入场。入场条件为：当Ask价突破区间上轨时，根据交易模式决定方向——Stop模式下做多，Limit模式下做空；当Bid价跌破区间下轨时反之。出场条件依赖于设置的止盈和止损百分比，分别基于区间范围计算。仓位管理支持四种模式：固定手数、线性递增（亏损后手数线性增加）、马丁格尔（亏损后手数倍增）和斐波那契（亏损后手数按斐波那契数列递增）。资金管理通过保证金百分比控制基础手数，并使用OrderCalcMargin检查是否有足够资金开仓。区间范围可由ATR指标、价格百分比或固定点数三种方式计算，并支持范围乘数在亏损后扩大区间。

Summary

A periodic range breakout EA that sets up price boundaries (High/Low) based on ATR, percentage, or fixed range at scheduled times (weekly or daily), then opens breakout trades when price crosses those boundaries. It supports multiple trade modes (stop/limit/random), lot management strategies (constant/linear/martingale/fibonacci), and configurable take-profit/stop-loss percentages. The EA cycles through StandBy, Setup, Trade, and Deal phases, resetting after each completed deal.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is to establish a price range at scheduled times (weekly on a specified day or daily at a specified hour), then enter trades when price breaks out of the range boundaries. Entry conditions: when Ask breaks above the upper boundary, direction depends on trade mode — Stop mode goes long, Limit mode goes short; conversely when Bid breaks below the lower boundary. Exit conditions rely on configurable take-profit and stop-loss percentages, both calculated as proportions of the range. Position sizing supports four modes: constant lot size, linear increment (lot increases linearly after losses), martingale (lot doubles after losses with configurable multiplier), and Fibonacci (lot follows Fibonacci sequence after consecutive losses). Money management controls base lot size via a margin percentage and uses OrderCalcMargin to verify sufficient funds before opening positions. Range can be calculated via ATR indicator, price percentage, or fixed points, with a range multiplier to widen the range after losses.

优势

  • 多模式区间计算（ATR/百分比/固定）适应不同市场波动环境
  • 完整的四阶段状态机设计（StandBy/Setup/Trade/Deal）逻辑清晰
  • 支持多种手数管理策略，用户可根据风险偏好灵活选择
  • 输入参数分组清晰，带有范围校验，防止误配置
  • 可视化辅助（水平线/垂直线）方便用户理解当前区间设置

劣势

  • 默认马丁格尔手数管理在连续亏损时会指数级增大仓位，存在爆仓风险
  • Trade阶段为空实现，无法处理已开仓单的中间状态（如移动止损、部分平仓等）
  • 随机交易模式（Random）缺乏实际策略意义，且switch语句缺少break导致case穿透
  • 没有检查是否已有持仓，可能重复开仓导致风险敞口失控
  • SetupCondition中的小时换算逻辑有潜在问题，hour>=23时强制归零可能跳过正确时间窗口

改进建议

  • 添加持仓检查逻辑，防止在已有同方向仓位时重复开仓
  • 修复CheckRandom()中switch语句缺失的break，并考虑移除Random模式或赋予其更合理的策略含义
  • 在Trade阶段实现移动止损或保本止损逻辑，提升已开仓单的风险管理能力

Strengths

  • Multiple range calculation modes (ATR/Percent/Fixed) adapt to different market volatility environments
  • Clean four-phase state machine design (StandBy/Setup/Trade/Deal) with clear logic flow
  • Supports multiple lot management strategies, allowing users to choose based on risk preference
  • Well-organized input parameters with range validation to prevent misconfiguration
  • Visual aids (horizontal/vertical lines) help users understand the current range setup

Weaknesses

  • Default martingale lot management exponentially increases position size after consecutive losses, creating account blowup risk
  • Trade phase is empty — no handling of open positions mid-trade (e.g., trailing stop, partial close)
  • Random trade mode lacks strategic purpose, and the switch statement is missing break clauses causing fall-through
  • No check for existing open positions, potentially allowing duplicate entries and uncontrolled risk exposure
  • Hour conversion logic in SetupCondition has potential issues — forcing hour=0 when >=23 may skip the correct time window

Improvements

  • Add position check logic to prevent duplicate entries when an existing position in the same direction is already open
  • Fix the missing break clauses in CheckRandom() switch statement, and consider removing Random mode or giving it meaningful strategy logic
  • Implement trailing stop or breakeven logic in the Trade phase to improve risk management for open positions
