摘要

该EA利用RSI超买超卖水平，在动量方向上放置挂单买入止损和卖出止损。每次开仓三笔订单，手数根据可用保证金动态计算，同时对持仓进行移动止损并跟踪修改挂单价格。止盈和止损为固定点数。

策略分析

策略概念基于RSI动量反转：当RSI低于30时认为市场超卖即将上涨，放置3笔BuyStop挂单；当RSI高于70时认为市场超买即将下跌，放置3笔SellStop挂单。入场条件是RSI穿越阈值水平，通过挂单方式在价格突破当前价位时入场。出场条件依赖固定的止盈（150点）和止损（50点），盈亏比为3:1。仓位管理采用三笔分批挂单，每笔手数为总计算手数的1/3。资金管理根据账户自由保证金动态计算手数，每2000保证金0.1手，上限15手，下限0.1手。此外，EA对持仓执行移动止损跟踪，并将远离当前价格超过20点的挂单价格拉回至距当前价20点处。

Summary

This EA uses RSI overbought/oversold levels to place pending Buy Stop and Sell Stop orders in the direction of momentum. It opens three orders at a time with dynamic lot sizing based on free margin, and trails stop losses while modifying pending order prices to follow the market. Take profit and stop loss are fixed pip-based values.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is based on RSI momentum reversal: when RSI falls below 30, the market is considered oversold and about to rise, so 3 BuyStop pending orders are placed; when RSI rises above 70, the market is considered overbought and about to fall, so 3 SellStop pending orders are placed. Entry conditions rely on RSI crossing threshold levels, entering via pending stop orders when price breaks beyond the current level. Exit conditions depend on fixed take profit (150 pips) and stop loss (50 pips), giving a risk-reward ratio of 3:1. Position sizing uses three split pending orders, each with 1/3 of the total calculated lot size. Money management dynamically calculates lots based on account free margin, at 0.1 lots per 2000 margin, capped at 15 lots with a floor of 0.1 lots. Additionally, the EA trails stop losses on open positions and pulls back pending orders that are more than 20 pips away from the current price to within 20 pips.

优势

  • 盈亏比为3:1，具有正期望值的潜力
  • 动态手数计算根据账户余额自适应调整仓位
  • 移动止损和挂单跟踪机制帮助锁定浮盈
  • 三笔分批挂单实现部分仓位进场的分散化

劣势

  • 止损50点远小于止盈150点但缺乏回撤保护机制，市场剧烈波动可能直接止损出局
  • 一次开3笔同方向订单增加了集中风险敞口
  • 没有新K线检查，每个tick都可能重复触发逻辑导致资源浪费
  • SellStop修改逻辑中存在重复OrderSend调用的bug
  • 挂单距离仅依赖StopLevel而没有考虑Spread变化，在滑点大时可能失败

改进建议

  • 添加新K线检查避免每个tick重复执行交易逻辑
  • 修复SellStop修改部分重复OrderSend的bug（第207行）
  • 添加最大持仓数限制和单日最大亏损限制以增强风险控制
  • 将挂单数量和距离参数外部化，允许用户自定义调整

Strengths

  • Risk-reward ratio of 3:1 provides potential for positive expectancy
  • Dynamic lot sizing adapts position size to account balance automatically
  • Trailing stop and pending order tracking help lock in floating profits
  • Three split pending orders provide diversification through partial position entries

Weaknesses

  • Stop loss of 50 pips is much smaller than take profit of 150 pips but lacks drawdown protection; volatile markets may trigger stops immediately
  • Opening 3 same-direction orders at once increases concentrated risk exposure
  • No new bar check means logic may redundantly trigger on every tick wasting resources
  • Bug in SellStop modification logic with duplicate OrderSend call on lines 204 and 207
  • Pending order distance relies only on StopLevel without considering spread changes, which may fail with large slippage

Improvements

  • Add new bar check to avoid redundant trade logic execution on every tick
  • Fix the duplicate OrderSend bug in SellStop modification section (line 207)
  • Add maximum position limit and daily max loss limit for enhanced risk control
  • Externalize pending order count and distance parameters for user customization
