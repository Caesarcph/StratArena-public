摘要

该EA基于ASCtrend自定义指标信号进行交易，在上升趋势信号时开多仓，在下降趋势信号时开空仓。支持可配置的资金管理模式、止损止盈，以及买卖双向的开仓和平仓独立开关。

策略分析

策略核心基于ASCtrend自定义指标，该指标根据RISK参数计算趋势方向并输出上下信号值。入场条件为：当指标上轨缓冲区（buffer 1）在指定信号柱上产生非零且非EMPTY_VALUE的值时，触发买入开仓和卖出平仓信号；当指标下轨缓冲区（buffer 0）产生有效值时，触发卖出开仓和买入平仓信号。出场条件分为两种：一是反向信号触发平仓，二是止损止盈触发平仓。仓位管理支持五种模式：按可用保证金比例、按余额比例、按含浮亏的可用保证金比例、按含浮亏的余额比例、以及固定手数。资金管理通过MM参数控制风险比例，MMMode选择计算方式。此外，EA在新K线出现时才重新计算信号，避免重复交易，并通过回溯历史柱来检测是否已存在反向信号以决定是否需要平仓。

Summary

This EA trades based on the ASCtrend custom indicator signals, opening buy positions on upward signals and sell positions on downward signals. It supports configurable money management modes, stop-loss/take-profit, and separate toggles for opening and closing positions on each side.

Strategy Analysis

The strategy is centered on the ASCtrend custom indicator, which calculates trend direction based on the RISK parameter and outputs upward and downward signal values. Entry conditions: when the indicator's upper buffer (buffer 1) produces a non-zero, non-EMPTY_VALUE value on the specified signal bar, it triggers buy-open and sell-close signals; when the lower buffer (buffer 0) produces a valid value, it triggers sell-open and buy-close signals. Exit conditions come in two forms: reverse signal triggers position closure, and stop-loss/take-profit levels trigger closure. Position sizing supports five modes: by free margin percentage, by balance percentage, by free margin including floating loss, by balance including floating loss, and fixed lot size. Money management is controlled via the MM parameter for risk proportion and MMMode for calculation method. Additionally, the EA only recalculates signals on new bars to avoid repeated trades, and it looks back through historical bars to check for existing reverse signals to determine if position closing is warranted.

优势

  • 信号计算仅在新生成K线时执行，避免逐tick重复计算和重复开仓
  • 买卖开仓和平仓均设有独立开关，灵活性高，可单独启用或禁用
  • 支持五种仓位管理模式，适应不同风险偏好和交易风格
  • 设有止损和止盈参数，具备基本的风险控制机制
  • 使用信号柱索引参数化，可灵活选择信号确认时机

劣势

  • ASCtrend为自定义指标，核心算法不透明，无法直接评估信号质量
  • 止损设为1000点、止盈设为2000点，默认值偏大，在多数品种上风险较高
  • 缺少点差过滤和交易时段过滤，可能在不利条件下开仓
  • 回溯历史柱查找反向信号的循环可能效率低下，且没有上限保护
  • 未检测是否已存在同向持仓，可能导致重复开仓增加风险敞口

改进建议

  • 增加持仓检查逻辑，避免同方向重复开仓
  • 添加点差过滤和交易时段过滤，减少不利条件下的交易
  • 为历史柱回溯循环添加上限保护，防止极端情况下性能问题
  • 增加追踪止损功能，在趋势行情中锁定浮动利润

Strengths

  • Signal calculation only executes on new bar formation, avoiding per-tick recalculation and repeated trade entries
  • Independent toggles for buy/sell opening and closing provide high flexibility for customizing trading behavior
  • Supports five position sizing modes to accommodate different risk preferences and trading styles
  • Configurable stop-loss and take-profit parameters provide basic risk control mechanisms
  • Signal bar index is parameterized, allowing flexible selection of signal confirmation timing

Weaknesses

  • ASCtrend is a custom indicator with opaque internal logic, making it impossible to directly evaluate signal quality
  • Default stop-loss of 1000 points and take-profit of 2000 points are excessively large for most instruments, posing high risk
  • Lacks spread filter and trading session filter, potentially opening positions under unfavorable conditions
  • The loop that searches historical bars for reverse signals may be inefficient and lacks an upper bound safeguard
  • Does not check for existing positions in the same direction, potentially causing duplicate entries and increased risk exposure

Improvements

  • Add position existence check to prevent duplicate entries in the same direction
  • Add spread filter and trading session filter to reduce trading under unfavorable conditions
  • Add an upper bound safeguard to the historical bar lookback loop to prevent performance issues in extreme cases
  • Add a trailing stop feature to lock in floating profits during trending markets
