摘要

该EA实现了双向马丁格尔策略，同时在当前价格上下方放置买入限价和卖出限价订单。当价格对一侧不利时，它通过马丁格尔式的加仓来恢复亏损，并将挂单重新定位到更接近当前价格的位置（收缩范围）。

策略分析

策略概念是双向挂单马丁格尔：同时在价格上方放置卖出限价单、下方放置买入限价单，形成对称布局。入场条件为：初始时直接市价买入和卖出各一手，同时在上下方各挂一个限价单；当一侧挂单被取消或不存在时，通过ShrinkRange()重新计算仓位并重建挂单。出场条件隐含在挂单的止盈价格中——当挂单成交时即获利出场，因为挂单价格设在TP距离处。仓位管理采用马丁格尔递增方式：当浮亏增加时，NewTotalVol按(OldLOSS+WinPerTrade)/TP公式计算，确保新仓位能覆盖旧亏损并盈利。资金管理通过PercentSame参数控制多空方向的仓位比例分配，VolumeLimitOrder限制单笔订单最大手数以规避经纪商限制。该策略没有止损机制，完全依赖马丁格尔加仓来回本，存在爆仓风险。

Summary

This EA implements a dual-direction martingale strategy that places both BUY and SELL limit orders simultaneously around the current price. When price moves against one side, it recalculates position sizes using a martingale-like volume increase to recover losses, and repositions pending orders closer to the current price (shrinking the range).

Strategy Analysis

The strategy concept is a dual-direction pending order martingale: it places SELL limit orders above and BUY limit orders below the current price simultaneously, creating a symmetrical layout. Entry conditions: on initialization, it opens market BUY and SELL orders plus pending limit orders at TP distance from price; when one side's pending order is missing, ShrinkRange() recalculates positions and rebuilds orders. Exit conditions are implicit in the pending order take-profit prices—when a limit order fills, profit is realized since the order price is set at TP distance from entry. Position sizing uses martingale escalation: as unrealized losses increase, NewTotalVol is calculated via (OldLOSS+WinPerTrade)/TP formula, ensuring new positions can cover old losses and still profit. Money management uses the PercentSame parameter to control volume allocation ratio between long and short sides, while VolumeLimitOrder caps individual order size to comply with broker limits. The strategy has no stop-loss mechanism and relies entirely on martingale position increases to recover losses, creating significant account blow-up risk.

优势

  • 双向同时布局，在震荡行情中可以双向获利
  • 通过VolumeLimitOrder拆分大单，避免经纪商拒绝
  • 包含重试机制处理REQUOTE，提高订单成交率
  • ShrinkRange机制能在亏损后自动调整挂单距离，加速回本

劣势

  • 无止损机制，单边趋势行情中亏损会无限放大
  • 马丁格尔加仓在极端行情下可能导致账户爆仓
  • 使用全局变量而非局部变量存储状态，代码可读性和安全性差
  • Sleep(12345)在OnTick中阻塞线程，可能导致错过行情
  • MqlTradeRequest结构体R被多个函数共享使用，存在字段残留风险

改进建议

  • 添加止损机制和最大亏损限制，防止账户爆仓
  • 将MqlTradeRequest在每次使用前完整初始化，避免字段残留导致错误
  • 用定时器或非阻塞方式替代Sleep重试机制
  • 添加持仓检查和最大持仓量限制

Strengths

  • Dual-direction layout allows profiting from both sides in ranging markets
  • VolumeLimitOrder splits large orders to avoid broker rejection
  • Retry mechanism handles REQUOTE scenarios, improving order fill rate
  • ShrinkRange mechanism automatically adjusts pending order distance after losses, accelerating recovery

Weaknesses

  • No stop-loss mechanism; losses amplify infinitely in trending markets
  • Martingale position sizing can lead to account blow-up in extreme markets
  • Uses global variables instead of local variables for state, harming readability and safety
  • Sleep(12345) blocks the OnTick thread, potentially missing market events
  • Shared MqlTradeRequest struct R across functions risks residual field contamination

Improvements

  • Add stop-loss mechanism and maximum drawdown limit to prevent account blow-up
  • Fully initialize MqlTradeRequest before each use to avoid residual field errors
  • Replace Sleep retry mechanism with timer-based or non-blocking approach
  • Add position checking and maximum position limits

风险评估 / Risk Assessment

总体 / Overall:DANGEROUS

风险点

  • 马丁格尔无止损加仓，极端单边行情会爆仓
  • 双向同时市价开仓，初始即暴露双向风险
  • ShrinkRange将挂单移近当前价格，增加了被触发后再次亏损的概率
  • 没有最大回撤或最大手数保护机制

Risk Assessment

Overall:DANGEROUS

Risk Points

  • Martingale position increase without stop-loss; extreme one-way trends cause account blow-up
  • Simultaneous dual-direction market orders expose risk on both sides from the start
  • ShrinkRange moves pending orders closer to price, increasing probability of triggering and re-loss
  • No maximum drawdown or maximum lot size protection mechanism
