摘要

一个双向马丁格尔EA，同时在当前价格上下方放置BUY_LIMIT和SELL_LIMIT挂单。当一侧被触发且价格逆向运动时，EA通过马丁格尔逻辑重新计算总仓位以回收亏损并获利，然后将两侧重单移近当前价格并加大手数。它持续缩小挂单范围以迫使价格回到盈利区间。

策略分析

策略概念：这是一个双向马丁格尔策略，核心思想是同时在不同方向下限价单，利用价格回归获利。入场条件：初始时在当前买价上方放置SELL_LIMIT单，在当前卖价下方放置BUY_LIMIT单，距离由KTP参数计算的TP值决定。出场条件：没有显式止损，仅依赖反向挂单被触发后的整体获利出场，通过TP距离确保每笔交易盈利点差以上。仓位管理：当一侧订单被触发后价格继续远离，ShrinkRange函数根据当前浮亏重新计算总仓位量，公式为NewTotalVol=(OldLOSS+WinPerTrade)/TP，其中OldLOSS是两侧未实现亏损之和。资金管理：使用PercentSame参数控制主方向占总仓位的比例，剩余部分分配给反向对冲单，VolumeLimitOrder限制单笔订单最大手数。该策略没有止损保护，完全依赖价格回归来平仓，在单边行情中浮亏将无限扩大，属于极高风险的马丁格尔策略。

Summary

A bi-directional martingale EA that simultaneously places BUY_LIMIT and SELL_LIMIT pending orders at a fixed TP distance from the current price. When one side is triggered and price moves against it, the EA recalculates total volume using martingale logic to recover losses plus profit, then repositions both orders closer to the current price with increased lot sizes. It continuously shrinks the order range to force price back into the profitable zone.

Strategy Analysis

Strategy concept: This is a bi-directional martingale system that places pending limit orders on both sides of the current price, aiming to profit from price reversion. Entry conditions: On initialization, the EA places a BUY_LIMIT below the bid price and a SELL_LIMIT above the ask price, with distance determined by the TP value calculated from the KTP parameter percentage of price. Exit conditions: There is no explicit stop-loss; the strategy relies on overall profit when price reverts to the opposite pending order's take-profit level, with each cycle targeting at least the spread as profit. Position sizing: When one side is triggered and price moves away, the ShrinkRange function recalculates total volume using the formula NewTotalVol=(OldLOSS+WinPerTrade)/TP, where OldLOSS is the sum of unrealized losses from both sides. Money management: The PercentSame parameter controls what percentage of total volume goes to the dominant direction, while the remainder is allocated to the hedging opposite side; VolumeLimitOrder caps individual order size to avoid broker rejection. The strategy has no stop-loss protection and depends entirely on price mean reversion, meaning floating losses will grow indefinitely in trending markets, making this an extremely high-risk martingale approach.

优势

  • 双向挂单设计能在震荡行情中持续获利，利用价格来回波动的特性
  • 有单笔手数上限VolumeLimitOrder控制，避免单次下单量过大被经纪商拒绝
  • 自动缩小挂单范围的ShrinkRange机制能加速亏损回本，在价格回归时更快实现盈利

劣势

  • 无止损保护，在单边行情中浮亏将无限放大，可能导致账户爆仓
  • 马丁格尔加仓逻辑在极端行情下手数增长极快，资金消耗速度呈指数级
  • 没有新K线检查也没有持仓检查，在快速行情中可能重复下单导致仓位失控

改进建议

  • 添加最大浮亏止损线，当亏损超过账户余额一定比例时强制全部平仓
  • 加入新K线检查机制，避免同一根K线内重复触发交易逻辑

Strengths

  • Bi-directional pending order design can continuously profit in ranging markets by exploiting price oscillation
  • VolumeLimitOrder cap prevents individual orders from being too large and rejected by the broker
  • The ShrinkRange mechanism accelerates loss recovery by repositioning orders closer to current price for faster breakeven

Weaknesses

  • No stop-loss protection means floating losses grow indefinitely in trending markets, potentially blowing the account
  • Martingale lot escalation is extremely rapid in extreme moves, draining capital at an exponential rate
  • No new bar check and insufficient position verification may cause duplicate orders in fast markets leading to uncontrolled exposure

Improvements

  • Add a maximum drawdown stop-loss that force-closes all positions when losses exceed a percentage of account equity
  • Implement a new bar check mechanism to prevent redundant trade triggers within the same candle
