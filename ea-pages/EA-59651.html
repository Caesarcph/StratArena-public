Basket Manager EA

This EA monitors open positions and closes all positions as a basket when the total count reaches a threshold and the account equity exceeds the balance. On the nth deal entry, it also closes all other positions leaving only the newly opened one. It does not open any positions on its own.

摘要

该EA监控持仓数量，当持仓数达到阈值且账户净值大于余额时，一次性平掉所有仓位作为一篮子获利了结。当第n笔成交为入场交易时，它还会平掉除该笔之外的所有其他仓位。它本身不主动开仓。

策略分析

该EA的核心策略概念是'篮子管理'——将所有持仓视为一个整体组合来管理。入场条件方面，EA本身不执行任何入场操作，它依赖外部信号或其他EA来开仓。出场条件分为两种：第一种是在OnTick中，当持仓数达到或超过参数n且账户净值大于余额时，关闭全部仓位实现整体盈利出场；第二种是在OnTradeTransaction中，当持仓数刚好等于n且净值不低于余额时，如果检测到新的入场成交，则保留该成交对应的仓位，关闭其余所有仓位。仓位管理方面，EA不做任何仓位大小调整或加仓逻辑。资金管理方面，仅以净值与余额的比较作为盈利判断标准，没有设置止损止盈或回撤控制。整体逻辑简单但存在严重缺陷：用while循环逐个平仓可能因平仓失败导致无限循环，且没有考虑滑点、重新报价等交易异常情况。

Summary

This EA monitors open positions and closes all positions as a basket when the total count reaches a threshold and the account equity exceeds the balance. On the nth deal entry, it also closes all other positions leaving only the newly opened one. It does not open any positions on its own.

Strategy Analysis

The core concept of this EA is 'basket management' — treating all open positions as a single portfolio. For entry conditions, the EA does not execute any entries itself; it relies on external signals or other EAs to open positions. Exit conditions are twofold: first, in OnTick, when the position count reaches or exceeds parameter n and account equity exceeds balance, all positions are closed for an overall profit; second, in OnTradeTransaction, when position count exactly equals n and equity is not below balance, if a new entry deal is detected, all other positions are closed while keeping only the newly opened one. For position sizing, the EA does not adjust lot sizes or add positions. For money management, it only uses the equity-versus-balance comparison as the profit criterion, without setting stop-loss, take-profit, or drawdown controls. The overall logic is simple but has serious flaws: the while loop for closing positions one by one could lead to infinite loops if a close fails, and there is no handling for slippage, requotes, or other trading exceptions.

优势

  • 概念简单明了，易于理解和部署
  • 篮子平仓理念有助于锁定整体组合盈利
  • OnTradeTransaction事件驱动响应及时，能迅速清理多余仓位

劣势

  • while循环平仓无失败退出条件，可能造成无限循环甚至账户爆仓
  • 没有止损和止盈设置，浮亏时不会止损
  • 用PositionGetTicket(0)在循环中索引可能因仓位变化而跳过或重复
  • 不检查平仓返回值，无法知道平仓是否成功
  • 没有区分不同品种或魔法编号，会关闭所有仓位包括其他EA的仓位

改进建议

  • 为while循环添加最大迭代次数限制和每次平仓后的失败重试/退出机制
  • 添加魔术编号和品种过滤，避免误关其他EA的仓位
  • 添加止损和最大回撤保护机制

Strengths

  • Simple and clear concept, easy to understand and deploy
  • Basket closing approach helps lock in overall portfolio profit
  • OnTradeTransaction event-driven response is timely, quickly clearing excess positions

Weaknesses

  • While loop for closing positions has no failure exit condition, potentially causing infinite loops or even account blowup
  • No stop-loss or take-profit set, floating losses are never cut
  • Using PositionGetTicket(0) in a loop may skip or repeat positions as the list shifts after each close
  • No check on PositionClose return value, impossible to know if close succeeded
  • No filtering by symbol or magic number, closes all positions including those from other EAs

Improvements

  • Add a maximum iteration limit to the while loop and a retry/exit mechanism after each close attempt
  • Add magic number and symbol filtering to avoid accidentally closing other EAs' positions
  • Add stop-loss and maximum drawdown protection mechanisms

  • 平仓失败未处理可能导致仓位无法及时关闭
  • 无魔术编号过滤会关闭所有EA的仓位造成意外损失

  • No magic number filter closes all EAs' positions causing unintended losses
