How regimes are defined

  • Steady uptrend Close above the 200-day average, 50-day above 200-day, volatility not elevated.
  • Volatile uptrend Uptrend, but 20-day volatility is in the top fifth of its last three years.
  • Mixed / turning Price and the two averages disagree: the trend is turning or absent.
  • Downtrend Close below the 200-day average and 50-day below 200-day, volatility not elevated.
  • Stress Downtrend with volatility in the top fifth of its last three years.

A regime uses closing prices up to that day only. Volatility is the annualized standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns, ranked against the previous three years of the same market. Strategy counts use the engine's position for each strategy at the last close. Descriptive statistics from historical data. Not a forecast and not investment advice.
