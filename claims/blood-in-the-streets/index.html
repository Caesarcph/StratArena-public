Claim check: Buy when there is blood in the streets

“Buy when there is blood in the streets: a 20% crash is the time to buy.”

What the data says

Not enough data Not enough data to say: no market had more than 9 independent events after the signal, fewer than the 10 needed even to start judging, so any pattern is an anecdote.

Too few independent events or observations to judge either way.

Who says it

Attributed to Baron Rothschild; repeated by Warren Buffett fans and Reddit.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Drawdown is the close divided by the highest close so far, minus one. Event: the first day it reaches -20% after having been above -10%. We record the 250-trading-day return from that close and compare it with the average 250-day return of the same market. Such events are rare, so a milder -10% version is shown for contrast.
