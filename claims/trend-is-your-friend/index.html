Claim check: The trend is your friend

“The trend is your friend: buy what is going up, sell what is going down.”

What the data says

Partly Partly: the rule gave a higher Sharpe ratio than buy-and-hold in 6 of 10 markets over 2010-12-21 to 2026-11-10 and in both halves of the history in 3; the median CAGR was +3.9% against +2.3% for buy-and-hold and the median maximum drawdown -46.1% against -71.2% (before costs). It holds in some markets, not across the board.

It holds in some markets or periods, leans the right way without proof, or helps on risk but not on return.

Who says it

Wall Street proverb popularised by Ed Seykota and Jesse Livermore lore; the core pitch of trend-following CTAs.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Time-series momentum: long from the day after the 252-day return is positive, cash otherwise. The claim counts as true if the Sharpe ratio beats buy-and-hold in the full history and in both halves. Shorter lookbacks are shown as a robustness check.
