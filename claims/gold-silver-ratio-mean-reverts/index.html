Claim check: The gold/silver ratio reverts

“The gold/silver ratio reverts: when it is high, silver is the buy.”

What the data says

Does not hold Not supported by the data: in Gold/silver ratio, the 63-day return after “Gold/silver ratio more than 2 standard deviations above its 252-day average” averaged -1.4% against +0.7% for all days, from 17 independent events (t = -1.4, likely noise). The direction points the claimed way, but the result cannot be told apart from chance.

No effect we can tell from chance, or the opposite effect.

Who says it

Precious-metals dealers and Reddit r/Silverbugs.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Ratio = GLD close divided by SLV close. Event: the log ratio is more than two standard deviations above its trailing 252-day mean. We record the change in the ratio over the next 63 trading days (events at least 63 days apart) and compare it with the average 63-day change. If the ratio reverts, it should fall by more than usual, which would make silver the better buy.
