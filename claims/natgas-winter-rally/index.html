Claim check: The natural gas winter rally

“Natural gas rallies into winter: buy in autumn, sell in winter.”

What the data says

Does not hold Not supported by the data: September to December returned -3.7% a month on average against -0.4% for the other months in natural gas (n = 66 months, 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12; t = -1.6, likely noise). We see no sign of the claimed effect.

No effect we can tell from chance, or the opposite effect.

Who says it

Energy traders and commodity seasonality guides.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Monthly returns of the natural gas ETF UNG. The trade buys at the end of August and sells at the end of December, so it earns the September to December monthly returns; these are compared with the other eight months (Welch t-stat). UNG holds futures and loses value as contracts are rolled, which affects every month.
