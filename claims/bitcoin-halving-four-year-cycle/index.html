Claim check: Bitcoin four-year halving cycle

“Bitcoin follows a four-year cycle around the halving: prices peak 12-18 months after each halving.”

What the data says

Not enough data Not enough data to say: no market had more than 3 independent events after the signal, fewer than the 10 needed even to start judging, so any pattern is an anecdote.

Too few independent events or observations to judge either way.

Who says it

Crypto Twitter and YouTube analysts; PlanB's stock-to-flow narrative.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Halving dates 2016-07-09, 2020-05-11 and 2024-04-20 (first close on or after each). For Bitcoin we record the return over the next 90, 180 and 365 days and the number of days from the halving to the highest close within the following 600 days, and compare the returns with the average for all days. With three events there is no statistical test to run; the page lists them for context.
