Claim check: Gold as a safe haven

“Gold is a safe haven: it goes up when stocks crash.”

What the data says

Does not hold Not supported by the data: on the 137 days when S&P 500 fell 2% or more, gold returned +0.01% on average against +0.03% on all days (t = -0.2, likely noise); in the 11 worst months for S&P 500 it averaged -0.3% a month. We see no sign of the claimed effect.

No effect we can tell from chance, or the opposite effect.

Who says it

Standard line from gold dealers and financial media during every sell-off.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

On the dates both SPY and GLD traded, we pick the days when SPY fell 2% or more and look at the same-day return of GLD, comparing its average with GLD's average on all days (t-stat on the event days). The worst 5% of SPY months are listed as a second look. The claim holds if gold is clearly higher than usual on those days, in both halves.
