Claim check: Bounce after three down days

“After three down days in a row, the market bounces.”

What the data says

Does not hold Not supported by the data: after “Three down closes in a row”, the average 3-day return was 0.0% against +0.1% for all days (medians over 10 markets); it was higher than usual in 3 of 10 markets and statistically clear, with at least 20 independent events and the same sign in both halves, in 0. The sign mostly points the other way, and the result cannot be told apart from chance.

No effect we can tell from chance, or the opposite effect.

Who says it

Larry Connors's short-term mean reversion research.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Event: three consecutive daily closes lower than the previous close. We record the return over the next three trading days (events at least 3 days apart) and compare it with the market's average 3-day return. The same pattern above the 200-day average is shown for contrast.
