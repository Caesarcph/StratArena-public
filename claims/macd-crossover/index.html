Claim check: MACD crossover

“Buy when MACD crosses above its signal line, sell when it crosses below.”

What the data says

Partly Partly: the rule gave a higher Sharpe ratio than buy-and-hold in 6 of 10 markets over 2010-03-02 to 2026-11-11 and in both halves of the history in 2; the median CAGR was +4.0% against +4.4% for buy-and-hold and the median maximum drawdown -41.6% against -71.2% (before costs). It holds in some markets, not across the board.

It holds in some markets or periods, leans the right way without proof, or helps on risk but not on return.

Who says it

Gerald Appel's indicator (1979); the textbook example on Investopedia and TradingView.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

MACD line = 12-day EMA minus 26-day EMA of the close; signal line = 9-day EMA of the MACD. Long from the day after MACD is above its signal line, cash otherwise. The claim holds if the Sharpe ratio beats buy-and-hold in the full history and in both halves on most markets.
