Claim check: September is the worst month

“September is the worst month of the year for stocks.”

What the data says

Does not hold Not supported by the data: September returned -0.5% a month on average against +1.4% for the other months in S&P 500 (n = 17 months, 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10; t = -1.7, likely noise; rank 1 of 12 by average return). The direction points the claimed way, but the result cannot be told apart from chance.

No effect we can tell from chance, or the opposite effect.

Who says it

Stock Trader's Almanac repeats it every August.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Month-end to month-end returns for SPY. September is compared with the other eleven months (Welch t-stat), must be among the two lowest of the twelve calendar months by average return, and negative relative to the rest in both halves of the history.
