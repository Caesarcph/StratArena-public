Claim check: New highs lead to more highs

“Buying at new highs works: new highs lead to more new highs.”

What the data says

Partly Partly: after “Close above all closes of the previous 252 days”, the average 63-day return was +3.6% against +2.2% for all days (medians over 9 markets); it was higher than usual in 7 of 9 markets and statistically clear, with at least 20 independent events and the same sign in both halves, in 0. The direction mostly points the claimed way, but not strongly enough to be sure.

It holds in some markets or periods, leans the right way without proof, or helps on risk but not on return.

Who says it

George and Hwang (2004) 52-week-high momentum; frequently quoted when indices make records.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Event: the close is above every close of the previous 252 trading days. We record the 63-trading-day return from that close (events at least 63 days apart) and compare it with the average 63-day return of the same market.

MarketsS&P 500, Gold, Silver, Oil, Natural gas, Commodities, Agriculture, EUR/USD, Bitcoin, Ether
