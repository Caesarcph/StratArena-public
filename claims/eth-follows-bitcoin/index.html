Claim check: Ether follows Bitcoin

“Ethereum follows Bitcoin: when BTC moves, alts follow a few days later.”

What the data says

Does not hold Not supported by the data: the strongest correlation between Bitcoin's return and Ether's return on a later day was 0.049 at a lag of 2 days (t = 2.8), against 0.78 on the same day; that is too small to matter, since a correlation of 0.10 would explain only 1% of the variation.

No effect we can tell from chance, or the opposite effect.

Who says it

Alt season narratives on YouTube.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Correlation between Bitcoin's return on day t and Ether's return on day t+1, t+2 and t+3, on common dates. A real lag effect needs a correlation of at least 0.10 with a t-stat above 2.4 (slightly stricter than 2 because three lags are examined) and a positive sign in both halves.
