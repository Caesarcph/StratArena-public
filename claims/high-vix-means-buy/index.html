Claim check: High volatility means buy

“When the VIX is high, it is time to buy (be greedy when others are fearful).”

What the data says

Does not hold Not supported by the data: after “20-day realized volatility in its top 10% of the last 5 years”, the average 63-day return was +5.7% against +1.2% for all days (medians over 9 markets); it was higher than usual in 6 of 9 markets and statistically clear, with at least 20 independent events and the same sign in both halves, in 1. Results are mixed across markets and cannot be told apart from chance.

No effect we can tell from chance, or the opposite effect.

Who says it

Common saying on fintwit; also Warren Buffett's 'be greedy when others are fearful'.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

The VIX is not in our data, so we use 20-day realized volatility of each market as a stand-in (annualised). Event: it ranks in the top 10% of its own last five years (at least one year of history needed). We record the 63-trading-day return from that close (events at least 63 days apart) and compare it with the average 63-day return of the same market. This is realized, not implied, volatility.
