Claim check: Buy the dip

“Buy the dip: after a down day or pullback, prices bounce.”

What the data says

Partly Partly: after “5-day fall of 3% or more, above the 200-day average”, the average 21-day return was +1.0% against +0.7% for all days (medians over 9 markets); it was higher than usual in 8 of 9 markets and statistically clear, with at least 20 independent events and the same sign in both halves, in 1. The direction mostly points the claimed way, but not strongly enough to be sure.

It holds in some markets or periods, leans the right way without proof, or helps on risk but not on return.

Who says it

Retail rallying cry on Reddit's WallStreetBets and everywhere in the 2010-2021 bull market.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Event: the close is down 3% or more over five trading days while it is above its 200-day average (a dip inside an uptrend). We record the 21-trading-day return from that close (events at least 21 days apart) and compare it with the average 21-day return of the same market. The same fall below the 200-day average is shown for contrast and does not count towards the verdict.
