Claim check: Bitcoin as digital gold

“Bitcoin is digital gold: it holds up when stocks fall.”

What the data says

Does not hold Not supported by the data: on the 99 days when S&P 500 fell 2% or more, Bitcoin returned -3.07% on average against +0.25% on all days (t = -4.6); in the 8 worst months for S&P 500 it averaged -5.1% a month. The effect runs the other way.

No effect we can tell from chance, or the opposite effect.

Who says it

Michael Saylor, Grayscale and many crypto influencers.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

On the dates both SPY and Bitcoin have a close (weekdays), we pick the days when SPY fell 2% or more and look at the same-day return of Bitcoin against its average on all days. A safe haven has to be clearly higher than usual on those days, in both halves. Correlations with gold and with stocks are shown for context.
