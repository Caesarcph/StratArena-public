Claim check: The turn-of-the-month effect

“The best days for stocks are around the turn of the month.”

What the data says

Does not hold Not supported by the data: The turn-of-month window (the last trading day plus the first three of the next month) returned +0.07% a day on average against +0.05% for all other days in S&P 500 (n = 203 windows, 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10; t = 0.5, likely noise). The direction points the claimed way, but the result cannot be told apart from chance.

No effect we can tell from chance, or the opposite effect.

Who says it

Documented by Ariel (1987) and Lakonishok and Smidt (1988); popular with Quantpedia and r/algotrading.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

The window is the last trading day of a month plus the first three trading days of the next. We compare the average daily return inside the window with all other days (Welch t-stat), in the full history and in both halves.
