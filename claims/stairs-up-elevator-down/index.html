Claim check: Stairs up, elevator down

“Markets take the stairs up and the elevator down.”

What the data says

Holds Yes, in this data: daily returns had negative skew in 5 of 6 markets and statistically clear negative skew with the same sign in both halves in 4; in S&P 500 skewness was -0.34, the worst day -10.9% and the best day +10.5%.

The effect shows up in most markets, passes the significance guard, and has the same sign in both halves of the history.

Who says it

Wall Street saying often attributed to traders; cited in crash commentary.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Skewness of daily returns: a negative value means the biggest moves are down moves (slow climbs, sudden drops). The t-stat of skewness is skew / sqrt(6 / n); it must be below -2 and negative in both halves. We also report the average down day and up day and the worst and best day. Only daily returns are tested, not the length of rallies and declines.
