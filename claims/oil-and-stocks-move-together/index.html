Claim check: Oil and stocks move together

“Oil and stocks move together: oil falling means stocks fall.”

What the data says

Partly Partly: the daily returns of oil and S&P 500 had a correlation of 0.298 over 2010-01-05 to 2026-11-10 (0.43 in the first half and 0.22 in the second), which is a link in the claimed direction, but weaker than the 0.30 we require for a clear one. The link is real but weaker than the saying implies.

It holds in some markets or periods, leans the right way without proof, or helps on risk but not on return.

Who says it

Media line since 2014-2016 and the 2020 oil crash.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Correlation of daily returns between the oil ETF USO and SPY on common dates, for the full history and each half, with a 126-day rolling line. A correlation of at least 0.30 in the right direction in all three periods counts as moving together; between 0.10 and 0.30 as a weak link.
