Claim check: Weak dollar, strong gold

“When the dollar falls, gold rises.”

What the data says

Does not hold Not supported by the data: the daily returns of EUR/USD and gold had a correlation of 0.02 over 2010-01-05 to 2026-11-11 (0.02 in the first half and 0.01 in the second), which is essentially no link. We see no sign of the claimed effect.

No effect we can tell from chance, or the opposite effect.

Who says it

Macro commentators.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

We use EUR/USD as an inverse dollar proxy (euro up means dollar down). Correlation of daily returns between EUR/USD and GLD on common dates, for the full history and each half. A positive correlation of at least 0.30 in all three periods counts as the dollar and gold moving oppositely; 0.10 to 0.30 as a weak link.
