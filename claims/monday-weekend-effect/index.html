Claim check: The Monday effect

“Monday is the worst day of the week for stocks.”

What the data says

Does not hold Not supported by the data: Monday returned +0.06% a day on average against +0.06% for other days in S&P 500 (n = 793 days, 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10; t = 0.2, likely noise; rank 3 of 5 by average return). We see no sign of the claimed effect.

No effect we can tell from chance, or the opposite effect.

Who says it

Documented by Frank Cross (1973) and French (1980) 'weekend effect'.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Daily close-to-close returns of SPY grouped by weekday. Monday is compared with all other days (Welch t-stat), must have the lowest average of the weekdays and a negative difference in both halves of the history. A Monday return covers the weekend, so it is the previous Friday close to Monday close.
