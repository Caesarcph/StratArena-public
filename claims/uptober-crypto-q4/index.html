Claim check: Uptober and the crypto Q4

“Bitcoin pumps in October and Q4 ('Uptober').”

What the data says

Not enough data Not enough data to say: the longest history gives only 13 observations of the period in question, fewer than the 15 needed.

Too few independent events or observations to judge either way.

Who says it

Crypto Twitter meme; BTC's Octobers were mostly green.

Origins are as commonly cited and were not independently verified.

How we tested it

Month-end to month-end returns for Bitcoin and Ether. October is compared with the other eleven months (Welch t-stat), in the full history and in both halves; the whole fourth quarter is shown as a second look. Bitcoin has about a dozen Octobers and Ether fewer, so the significance guard is hard to pass.
