Stop Loss Overlay vs Parabolic SAR on Agriculture: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-24: 4250 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Agriculture are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Stop Loss Overlay had the higher CAGR (+3.6% vs +2.3% for Parabolic SAR). On risk-adjusted return Stop Loss Overlay led, with a Sharpe of 0.43 against 0.29. Maximum drawdowns were similar (-25.3% vs -26.1%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Agriculture on CAGR (+0.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
