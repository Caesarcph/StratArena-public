Detrended Price Oscillator vs Darvas Box on Agriculture: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-11: 4241 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Agriculture are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+2.5% for Detrended Price Oscillator, +2.2% for Darvas Box). On risk-adjusted return Detrended Price Oscillator led, with a Sharpe of 0.53 against 0.28. Detrended Price Oscillator had the shallower maximum drawdown (-12.3% vs -27.2%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Agriculture on CAGR (+0.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
