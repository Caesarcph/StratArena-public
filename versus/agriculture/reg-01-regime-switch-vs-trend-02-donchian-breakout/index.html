Regime Switch vs Donchian Breakout on Agriculture: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-24: 4250 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Agriculture are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+2.4% for Regime Switch, +2.4% for Donchian Breakout). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.29 vs 0.36, a gap under 0.1. Donchian Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-17.8% vs -31.3%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Agriculture on CAGR (+0.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
