Detrended Price Oscillator vs Stop Loss Overlay on Agriculture: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-13: 4243 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Agriculture are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Stop Loss Overlay had the higher CAGR (+3.6% vs +2.5% for Detrended Price Oscillator). On risk-adjusted return Detrended Price Oscillator led, with a Sharpe of 0.53 against 0.43. Detrended Price Oscillator had the shallower maximum drawdown (-12.3% vs -25.3%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Agriculture on CAGR (+0.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
