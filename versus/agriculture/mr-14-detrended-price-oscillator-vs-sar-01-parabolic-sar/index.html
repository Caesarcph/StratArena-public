Detrended Price Oscillator vs Parabolic SAR on Agriculture: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-13: 4243 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Agriculture are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+2.5% for Detrended Price Oscillator, +2.0% for Parabolic SAR). On risk-adjusted return Detrended Price Oscillator led, with a Sharpe of 0.53 against 0.26. Detrended Price Oscillator had the shallower maximum drawdown (-12.3% vs -28.6%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Agriculture on CAGR (+0.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.41. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +2.3% with a Sharpe of 0.42 and a maximum drawdown of -19.3%. The mix did not beat the better strategy on Sharpe (0.42 vs 0.53).
