Donchian Breakout vs Darvas Box on Agriculture: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-20: 4248 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Agriculture are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Donchian Breakout had the higher CAGR (+2.4% vs +1.8% for Darvas Box). On risk-adjusted return Donchian Breakout led, with a Sharpe of 0.36 against 0.24. Donchian Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-17.8% vs -29.7%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Agriculture on CAGR (+0.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
