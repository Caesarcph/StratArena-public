Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze vs Ichimoku Cloud on Agriculture: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-13: 4243 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Agriculture are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+1.5% for Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze, +1.8% for Ichimoku Cloud). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.28 vs 0.26, a gap under 0.1. Ichimoku Cloud had the shallower maximum drawdown (-21.5% vs -23.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Agriculture on CAGR (+0.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.38. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +1.7% with a Sharpe of 0.32 and a maximum drawdown of -15.8%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.32 vs 0.28).
