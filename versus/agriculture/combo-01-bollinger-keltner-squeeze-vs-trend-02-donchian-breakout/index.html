Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze vs Donchian Breakout on Agriculture: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-11: 4241 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Agriculture are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Donchian Breakout had the higher CAGR (+2.4% vs +1.5% for Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.28 vs 0.36, a gap under 0.1. Donchian Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-17.8% vs -23.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Agriculture on CAGR (+0.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.35. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +2.0% with a Sharpe of 0.39 and a maximum drawdown of -13.1%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.39 vs 0.36).
