Parabolic SAR vs Volatility Breakout on Agriculture: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-23: 4249 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Agriculture are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+1.7% for Parabolic SAR, +1.4% for Volatility Breakout). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.23 vs 0.32, a gap under 0.1. Volatility Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-13.6% vs -30.5%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Agriculture on CAGR (+0.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
