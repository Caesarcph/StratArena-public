Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze vs Detrended Price Oscillator on Agriculture: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-23: 4249 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Agriculture are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Detrended Price Oscillator had the higher CAGR (+2.4% vs +1.6% for Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze). On risk-adjusted return Detrended Price Oscillator led, with a Sharpe of 0.53 against 0.31. Detrended Price Oscillator had the shallower maximum drawdown (-12.3% vs -20.6%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Agriculture on CAGR (+0.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
