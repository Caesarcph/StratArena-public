Trend + Macro Filter vs Volatility Breakout on Agriculture: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-13: 4243 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Agriculture are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Trend + Macro Filter had the higher CAGR (+2.4% vs +1.4% for Volatility Breakout). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.30 vs 0.33, a gap under 0.1. Volatility Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-13.6% vs -29.2%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Agriculture on CAGR (+0.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.24. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +2.0% with a Sharpe of 0.38 and a maximum drawdown of -12.1%. A correlation below 0.3 means the two often did different things, which is the case where mixing helps most. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.38 vs 0.33).
