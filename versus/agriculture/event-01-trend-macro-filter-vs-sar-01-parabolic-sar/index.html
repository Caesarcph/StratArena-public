Trend + Macro Filter vs Parabolic SAR on Agriculture: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10: 4240 trading days (16.8 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Agriculture are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+2.3% for Trend + Macro Filter, +2.4% for Parabolic SAR). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.30 vs 0.30, a gap under 0.1. Maximum drawdowns were similar (-29.2% vs -28.6%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Agriculture on CAGR (+0.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.48. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +2.4% with a Sharpe of 0.35 and a maximum drawdown of -25.9%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.35 vs 0.30).
