Trend + Macro Filter vs Detrended Price Oscillator on Agriculture: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12: 4242 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Agriculture are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+2.3% for Trend + Macro Filter, +2.5% for Detrended Price Oscillator). On risk-adjusted return Detrended Price Oscillator led, with a Sharpe of 0.53 against 0.30. Detrended Price Oscillator had the shallower maximum drawdown (-12.3% vs -29.2%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Agriculture on CAGR (+0.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.29. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +2.5% with a Sharpe of 0.46 and a maximum drawdown of -17.1%. A correlation below 0.3 means the two often did different things, which is the case where mixing helps most. The mix did not beat the better strategy on Sharpe (0.46 vs 0.53).
