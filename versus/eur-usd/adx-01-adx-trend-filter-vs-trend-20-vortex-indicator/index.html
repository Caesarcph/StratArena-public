ADX Trend Filter vs Vortex Indicator on EUR/USD: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-01 to 2026-11-13: 4391 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of EUR/USD are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Vortex Indicator had the higher CAGR (+4.9% vs +1.8% for ADX Trend Filter). On risk-adjusted return Vortex Indicator led, with a Sharpe of 0.83 against 0.48. ADX Trend Filter had the shallower maximum drawdown (-5.7% vs -13.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of EUR/USD on CAGR (-1.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
