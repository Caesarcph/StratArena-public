Parabolic SAR vs Vortex Indicator on EUR/USD: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-01 to 2026-11-12: 4390 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of EUR/USD are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Parabolic SAR had the higher CAGR (+7.1% vs +4.9% for Vortex Indicator). On risk-adjusted return Parabolic SAR led, with a Sharpe of 1.16 against 0.83. Parabolic SAR had the shallower maximum drawdown (-10.8% vs -13.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of EUR/USD on CAGR (-1.5%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
