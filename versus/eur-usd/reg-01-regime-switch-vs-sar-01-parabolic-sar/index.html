Regime Switch vs Parabolic SAR on EUR/USD: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-01 to 2026-11-26: 4400 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of EUR/USD are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Parabolic SAR had the higher CAGR (+7.0% vs +0.2% for Regime Switch). On risk-adjusted return Parabolic SAR led, with a Sharpe of 1.16 against 0.07. Parabolic SAR had the shallower maximum drawdown (-10.8% vs -14.6%). Both beat buy-and-hold of EUR/USD on CAGR (-1.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
