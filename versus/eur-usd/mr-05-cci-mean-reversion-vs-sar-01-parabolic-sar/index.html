CCI Mean Reversion vs Parabolic SAR on EUR/USD: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-01 to 2026-11-10: 4388 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of EUR/USD are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Parabolic SAR had the higher CAGR (+7.1% vs +0.1% for CCI Mean Reversion). On risk-adjusted return Parabolic SAR led, with a Sharpe of 1.17 against 0.04. Parabolic SAR had the shallower maximum drawdown (-10.8% vs -23.3%). Both beat buy-and-hold of EUR/USD on CAGR (-1.5%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.19. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +3.6% with a Sharpe of 0.83 and a maximum drawdown of -12.1%. A correlation below 0.3 means the two often did different things, which is the case where mixing helps most. The mix did not beat the better strategy on Sharpe (0.83 vs 1.17).
