Trend + Mean Reversion Hybrid vs Vortex Indicator on EUR/USD: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-01 to 2026-11-11: 4389 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of EUR/USD are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Vortex Indicator had the higher CAGR (+4.9% vs +0.5% for Trend + Mean Reversion Hybrid). On risk-adjusted return Vortex Indicator led, with a Sharpe of 0.83 against 0.21. Trend + Mean Reversion Hybrid had the shallower maximum drawdown (-5.7% vs -13.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of EUR/USD on CAGR (-1.5%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.08. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +2.7% with a Sharpe of 0.83 and a maximum drawdown of -6.9%. A correlation below 0.3 means the two often did different things, which is the case where mixing helps most. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.83 vs 0.83).
