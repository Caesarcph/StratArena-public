ADX Trend Filter vs Parabolic SAR on EUR/USD: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-01 to 2026-11-13: 4391 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of EUR/USD are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Parabolic SAR had the higher CAGR (+7.1% vs +1.8% for ADX Trend Filter). On risk-adjusted return Parabolic SAR led, with a Sharpe of 1.16 against 0.48. ADX Trend Filter had the shallower maximum drawdown (-5.7% vs -10.8%). Both beat buy-and-hold of EUR/USD on CAGR (-1.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
