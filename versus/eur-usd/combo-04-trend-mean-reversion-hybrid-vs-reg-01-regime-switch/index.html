Trend + Mean Reversion Hybrid vs Regime Switch on EUR/USD: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-01 to 2026-11-11: 4389 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of EUR/USD are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+0.5% for Trend + Mean Reversion Hybrid, +0.2% for Regime Switch). On risk-adjusted return Trend + Mean Reversion Hybrid led, with a Sharpe of 0.21 against 0.07. Trend + Mean Reversion Hybrid had the shallower maximum drawdown (-5.7% vs -14.6%). Both beat buy-and-hold of EUR/USD on CAGR (-1.5%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
