CCI Mean Reversion vs Z-Score Mean Reversion on EUR/USD: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-01 to 2026-11-11: 4389 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of EUR/USD are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Z-Score Mean Reversion had the higher CAGR (+0.6% vs +0.1% for CCI Mean Reversion). On risk-adjusted return Z-Score Mean Reversion led, with a Sharpe of 0.30 against 0.04. Z-Score Mean Reversion had the shallower maximum drawdown (-6.3% vs -23.3%). Both beat buy-and-hold of EUR/USD on CAGR (-1.5%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
