Residual Momentum vs Parabolic SAR on EUR/USD: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-01 to 2026-11-11: 4389 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of EUR/USD are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Parabolic SAR had the higher CAGR (+7.1% vs +0.2% for Residual Momentum). On risk-adjusted return Parabolic SAR led, with a Sharpe of 1.17 against 0.06. Parabolic SAR had the shallower maximum drawdown (-10.8% vs -13.5%). Both beat buy-and-hold of EUR/USD on CAGR (-1.5%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
