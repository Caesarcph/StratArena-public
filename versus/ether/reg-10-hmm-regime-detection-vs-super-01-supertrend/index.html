HMM Regime Detection vs Supertrend on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-21: 3300 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Supertrend had the higher CAGR (+69.2% vs +44.1% for HMM Regime Detection). On risk-adjusted return Supertrend led, with a Sharpe of 1.00 against 0.79. Supertrend had the shallower maximum drawdown (-55.0% vs -58.2%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+20.1%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
