Volatility Regime Switch vs Chandelier Exit on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-10: 3289 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Chandelier Exit had the higher CAGR (+45.5% vs +39.4% for Volatility Regime Switch). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.79 vs 0.78, a gap under 0.1. Volatility Regime Switch had the shallower maximum drawdown (-57.4% vs -60.1%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+22.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.64. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +46.0% with a Sharpe of 0.87 and a maximum drawdown of -47.2%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.87 vs 0.79).
