Volatility Regime Switch vs Trailing Stop Strategy on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-12: 3291 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Trailing Stop Strategy had the higher CAGR (+61.3% vs +39.3% for Volatility Regime Switch). On risk-adjusted return Trailing Stop Strategy led, with a Sharpe of 0.91 against 0.79. Volatility Regime Switch had the shallower maximum drawdown (-57.4% vs -68.7%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+22.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.67. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +53.7% with a Sharpe of 0.94 and a maximum drawdown of -55.6%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.94 vs 0.91).
