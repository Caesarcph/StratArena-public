HMM Regime Detection vs Trailing Stop Strategy on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-10: 3289 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Trailing Stop Strategy had the higher CAGR (+61.4% vs +44.3% for HMM Regime Detection). On risk-adjusted return Trailing Stop Strategy led, with a Sharpe of 0.91 against 0.79. HMM Regime Detection had the shallower maximum drawdown (-58.2% vs -68.7%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+22.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.76. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +55.5% with a Sharpe of 0.91 and a maximum drawdown of -56.6%. The mix did not beat the better strategy on Sharpe (0.91 vs 0.91).
