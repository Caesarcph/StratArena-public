Volatility Regime Switch vs Supertrend on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-11: 3290 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Supertrend had the higher CAGR (+69.5% vs +39.4% for Volatility Regime Switch). On risk-adjusted return Supertrend led, with a Sharpe of 1.00 against 0.79. Supertrend had the shallower maximum drawdown (-55.0% vs -57.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+22.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
