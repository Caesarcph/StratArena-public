Stop Loss Overlay vs Trailing Stop Strategy on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-11: 3290 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Trailing Stop Strategy had the higher CAGR (+61.3% vs +60.3% for Stop Loss Overlay). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.93 vs 0.91, a gap under 0.1. Stop Loss Overlay had the shallower maximum drawdown (-50.3% vs -68.7%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+22.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.76. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +64.2% with a Sharpe of 0.98 and a maximum drawdown of -54.6%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.98 vs 0.93).
