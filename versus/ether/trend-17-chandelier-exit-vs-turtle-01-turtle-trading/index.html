Chandelier Exit vs Turtle Trading on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-12: 3291 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Chandelier Exit had the higher CAGR (+45.5% vs +43.8% for Turtle Trading). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.78 vs 0.79, a gap under 0.1. Turtle Trading had the shallower maximum drawdown (-52.0% vs -60.1%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+22.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.81. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +46.7% with a Sharpe of 0.83 and a maximum drawdown of -46.7%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.83 vs 0.79).
