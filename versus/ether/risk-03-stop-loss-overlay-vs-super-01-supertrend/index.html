Stop Loss Overlay vs Supertrend on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-13: 3292 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Supertrend had the higher CAGR (+69.4% vs +60.3% for Stop Loss Overlay). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.93 vs 1.00, a gap under 0.1. Stop Loss Overlay had the shallower maximum drawdown (-50.3% vs -55.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+22.0%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.80. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +67.5% with a Sharpe of 1.02 and a maximum drawdown of -44.9%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (1.02 vs 1.00).
