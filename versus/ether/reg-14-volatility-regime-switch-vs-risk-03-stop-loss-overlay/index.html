Volatility Regime Switch vs Stop Loss Overlay on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-10: 3289 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Stop Loss Overlay had the higher CAGR (+60.3% vs +39.4% for Volatility Regime Switch). On risk-adjusted return Stop Loss Overlay led, with a Sharpe of 0.93 against 0.79. Stop Loss Overlay had the shallower maximum drawdown (-50.3% vs -57.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+22.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.74. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +52.2% with a Sharpe of 0.93 and a maximum drawdown of -52.9%. The mix did not beat the better strategy on Sharpe (0.93 vs 0.93).
