HMM Regime Detection vs Stop Loss Overlay on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-13: 3292 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Stop Loss Overlay had the higher CAGR (+60.3% vs +44.2% for HMM Regime Detection). On risk-adjusted return Stop Loss Overlay led, with a Sharpe of 0.93 against 0.79. Stop Loss Overlay had the shallower maximum drawdown (-50.3% vs -58.2%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+22.0%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
