HMM Regime Detection vs Volatility Regime Switch on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-10: 3289 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

HMM Regime Detection had the higher CAGR (+44.3% vs +39.4% for Volatility Regime Switch). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.79 vs 0.79, a gap under 0.1. Maximum drawdowns were similar (-58.2% vs -57.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+22.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.69. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +44.6% with a Sharpe of 0.86 and a maximum drawdown of -46.7%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.86 vs 0.79).
