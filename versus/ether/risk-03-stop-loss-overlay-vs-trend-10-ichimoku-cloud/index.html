Stop Loss Overlay vs Ichimoku Cloud on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-11: 3290 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Stop Loss Overlay had the higher CAGR (+60.3% vs +40.1% for Ichimoku Cloud). On risk-adjusted return Stop Loss Overlay led, with a Sharpe of 0.93 against 0.79. Stop Loss Overlay had the shallower maximum drawdown (-50.3% vs -55.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+22.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.81. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +51.9% with a Sharpe of 0.91 and a maximum drawdown of -48.0%. The mix did not beat the better strategy on Sharpe (0.91 vs 0.93).
