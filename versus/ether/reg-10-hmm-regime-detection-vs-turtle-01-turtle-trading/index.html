HMM Regime Detection vs Turtle Trading on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-13: 3292 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+44.2% for HMM Regime Detection, +43.8% for Turtle Trading). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.79 vs 0.79, a gap under 0.1. Turtle Trading had the shallower maximum drawdown (-52.0% vs -58.2%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+22.0%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.75. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +46.5% with a Sharpe of 0.85 and a maximum drawdown of -53.6%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.85 vs 0.79).
