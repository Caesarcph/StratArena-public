Supertrend vs Turtle Trading on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-13: 3292 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Supertrend had the higher CAGR (+69.4% vs +43.8% for Turtle Trading). On risk-adjusted return Supertrend led, with a Sharpe of 1.00 against 0.79. Turtle Trading had the shallower maximum drawdown (-52.0% vs -55.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+22.0%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.86. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +57.8% with a Sharpe of 0.94 and a maximum drawdown of -52.9%. The mix did not beat the better strategy on Sharpe (0.94 vs 1.00).
