Trailing Stop Strategy vs Supertrend on Ether: which did better?

Common history 2017-11-09 to 2026-11-24: 3303 trading days (9.0 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Ether are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Supertrend had the higher CAGR (+69.1% vs +61.0% for Trailing Stop Strategy). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.91 vs 1.00, a gap under 0.1. Supertrend had the shallower maximum drawdown (-55.0% vs -68.7%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Ether on CAGR (+20.1%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
