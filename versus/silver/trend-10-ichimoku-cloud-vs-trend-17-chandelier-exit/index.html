Ichimoku Cloud vs Chandelier Exit on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-13: 4243 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Chandelier Exit had the higher CAGR (+13.7% vs +7.9% for Ichimoku Cloud). On risk-adjusted return Chandelier Exit led, with a Sharpe of 0.68 against 0.48. Ichimoku Cloud had the shallower maximum drawdown (-40.4% vs -41.5%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+5.7%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
