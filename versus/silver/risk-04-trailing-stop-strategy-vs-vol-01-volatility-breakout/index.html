Trailing Stop Strategy vs Volatility Breakout on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12: 4242 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Trailing Stop Strategy had the higher CAGR (+8.2% vs +5.1% for Volatility Breakout). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.46 vs 0.49, a gap under 0.1. Volatility Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-20.5% vs -40.9%). Only Trailing Stop Strategy beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+5.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.43. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +7.3% with a Sharpe of 0.54 and a maximum drawdown of -26.2%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.54 vs 0.49).
