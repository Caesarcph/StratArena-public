HMA Crossover vs Ichimoku Cloud on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-13: 4243 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

HMA Crossover had the higher CAGR (+9.6% vs +7.9% for Ichimoku Cloud). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.51 vs 0.48, a gap under 0.1. Ichimoku Cloud had the shallower maximum drawdown (-40.4% vs -52.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+5.7%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.50. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +9.4% with a Sharpe of 0.57 and a maximum drawdown of -39.8%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.57 vs 0.51).
