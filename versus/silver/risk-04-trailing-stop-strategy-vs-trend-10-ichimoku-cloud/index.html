Trailing Stop Strategy vs Ichimoku Cloud on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-20: 4248 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+8.2% for Trailing Stop Strategy, +7.9% for Ichimoku Cloud). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.46 vs 0.48, a gap under 0.1. Maximum drawdowns were similar (-40.9% vs -40.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+4.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.71. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +8.4% with a Sharpe of 0.50 and a maximum drawdown of -38.2%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.50 vs 0.48).
