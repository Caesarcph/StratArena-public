Chandelier Exit vs Volatility Breakout on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-25: 4251 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Chandelier Exit had the higher CAGR (+13.7% vs +4.8% for Volatility Breakout). On risk-adjusted return Chandelier Exit led, with a Sharpe of 0.68 against 0.46. Volatility Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-20.5% vs -41.5%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+4.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.43. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +9.7% with a Sharpe of 0.70 and a maximum drawdown of -19.8%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.70 vs 0.68).
