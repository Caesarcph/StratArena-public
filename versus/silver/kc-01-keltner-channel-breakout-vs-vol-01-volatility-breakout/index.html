Keltner Channel Breakout vs Volatility Breakout on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-20: 4248 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Keltner Channel Breakout had the higher CAGR (+7.5% vs +5.1% for Volatility Breakout). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.48 vs 0.49, a gap under 0.1. Volatility Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-20.5% vs -38.9%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+4.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix
