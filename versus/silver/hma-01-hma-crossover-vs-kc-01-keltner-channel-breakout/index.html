HMA Crossover vs Keltner Channel Breakout on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-11: 4241 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

HMA Crossover had the higher CAGR (+9.9% vs +7.5% for Keltner Channel Breakout). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.53 vs 0.48, a gap under 0.1. Keltner Channel Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-38.9% vs -49.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+6.1%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.56. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +9.3% with a Sharpe of 0.57 and a maximum drawdown of -43.3%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.57 vs 0.53).
