Detrended Price Oscillator vs Trailing Stop Strategy on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-24: 4250 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+8.1% for Detrended Price Oscillator, +8.2% for Trailing Stop Strategy). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.49 vs 0.46, a gap under 0.1. Trailing Stop Strategy had the shallower maximum drawdown (-40.9% vs -48.2%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+4.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.66. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +8.6% with a Sharpe of 0.52 and a maximum drawdown of -42.9%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.52 vs 0.49).
