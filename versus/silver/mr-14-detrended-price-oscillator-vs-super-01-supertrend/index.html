Detrended Price Oscillator vs Supertrend on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10: 4240 trading days (16.8 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+8.4% for Detrended Price Oscillator, +8.1% for Supertrend). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.50 vs 0.46, a gap under 0.1. Supertrend had the shallower maximum drawdown (-40.5% vs -44.5%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+6.1%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.67. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +8.7% with a Sharpe of 0.52 and a maximum drawdown of -41.7%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.52 vs 0.50).
