HMA Crossover vs Chandelier Exit on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-13: 4243 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Chandelier Exit had the higher CAGR (+13.7% vs +9.6% for HMA Crossover). On risk-adjusted return Chandelier Exit led, with a Sharpe of 0.68 against 0.51. Chandelier Exit had the shallower maximum drawdown (-41.5% vs -52.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+5.7%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix
