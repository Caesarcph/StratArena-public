Ichimoku Cloud vs Vortex Indicator on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-23: 4249 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Vortex Indicator had the higher CAGR (+10.3% vs +7.9% for Ichimoku Cloud). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.48 vs 0.54, a gap under 0.1. Ichimoku Cloud had the shallower maximum drawdown (-40.4% vs -43.3%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+4.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.70. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +9.5% with a Sharpe of 0.55 and a maximum drawdown of -35.1%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.55 vs 0.54).
