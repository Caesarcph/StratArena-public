Keltner Channel Breakout vs Ichimoku Cloud on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12: 4242 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+7.5% for Keltner Channel Breakout, +7.9% for Ichimoku Cloud). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.48 vs 0.48, a gap under 0.1. Keltner Channel Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-38.9% vs -40.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+5.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
