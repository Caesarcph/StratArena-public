Detrended Price Oscillator vs Volatility Breakout on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10: 4240 trading days (16.8 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Detrended Price Oscillator had the higher CAGR (+8.4% vs +5.1% for Volatility Breakout). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.50 vs 0.49, a gap under 0.1. Volatility Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-20.5% vs -44.5%). Only Detrended Price Oscillator beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+6.1%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.27. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +7.4% with a Sharpe of 0.61 and a maximum drawdown of -20.6%. A correlation below 0.3 means the two often did different things, which is the case where mixing helps most. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.61 vs 0.50).
