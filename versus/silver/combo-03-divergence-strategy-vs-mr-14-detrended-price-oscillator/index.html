Divergence Strategy vs Detrended Price Oscillator on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-16: 4244 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+8.4% for Divergence Strategy, +8.4% for Detrended Price Oscillator). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.47 vs 0.50, a gap under 0.1. Detrended Price Oscillator had the shallower maximum drawdown (-44.5% vs -56.5%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+5.5%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix
