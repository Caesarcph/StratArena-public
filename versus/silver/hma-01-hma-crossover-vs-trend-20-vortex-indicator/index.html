HMA Crossover vs Vortex Indicator on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-13: 4243 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Vortex Indicator had the higher CAGR (+10.3% vs +9.6% for HMA Crossover). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.51 vs 0.54, a gap under 0.1. Vortex Indicator had the shallower maximum drawdown (-43.3% vs -52.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+5.7%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.66. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +10.5% with a Sharpe of 0.58 and a maximum drawdown of -44.7%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.58 vs 0.54).
