Supertrend vs Vortex Indicator on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12: 4242 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Vortex Indicator had the higher CAGR (+10.3% vs +7.9% for Supertrend). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.45 vs 0.54, a gap under 0.1. Supertrend had the shallower maximum drawdown (-41.6% vs -43.3%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+5.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix
