Chandelier Exit vs Vortex Indicator on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10: 4240 trading days (16.8 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Chandelier Exit had the higher CAGR (+13.7% vs +10.3% for Vortex Indicator). On risk-adjusted return Chandelier Exit led, with a Sharpe of 0.68 against 0.54. Chandelier Exit had the shallower maximum drawdown (-41.5% vs -43.3%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+6.1%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.87. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +12.2% with a Sharpe of 0.63 and a maximum drawdown of -40.5%. The mix did not beat the better strategy on Sharpe (0.63 vs 0.68).
