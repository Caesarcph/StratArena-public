Ichimoku Cloud vs Volatility Breakout on Silver: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-24: 4250 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Silver are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Ichimoku Cloud had the higher CAGR (+7.9% vs +4.8% for Volatility Breakout). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.48 vs 0.46, a gap under 0.1. Volatility Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-20.5% vs -40.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Silver on CAGR (+4.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.32. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +6.9% with a Sharpe of 0.57 and a maximum drawdown of -26.8%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.57 vs 0.48).
