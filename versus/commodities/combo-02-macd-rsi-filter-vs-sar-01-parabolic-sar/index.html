MACD + RSI Filter vs Parabolic SAR on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-24: 4250 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

MACD + RSI Filter had the higher CAGR (+4.8% vs +4.2% for Parabolic SAR). On risk-adjusted return MACD + RSI Filter led, with a Sharpe of 0.55 against 0.41. MACD + RSI Filter had the shallower maximum drawdown (-31.0% vs -37.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+1.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix
