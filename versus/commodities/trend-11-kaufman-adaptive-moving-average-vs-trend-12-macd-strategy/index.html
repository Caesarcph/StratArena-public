Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average vs MACD Strategy on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10: 4240 trading days (16.8 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average had the higher CAGR (+5.2% vs +4.3% for MACD Strategy). On risk-adjusted return Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average led, with a Sharpe of 0.53 against 0.42. Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average had the shallower maximum drawdown (-28.0% vs -34.1%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+2.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
