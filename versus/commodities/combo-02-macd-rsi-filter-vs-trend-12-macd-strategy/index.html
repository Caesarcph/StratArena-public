MACD + RSI Filter vs MACD Strategy on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10: 4240 trading days (16.8 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+4.8% for MACD + RSI Filter, +4.3% for MACD Strategy). On risk-adjusted return MACD + RSI Filter led, with a Sharpe of 0.55 against 0.42. MACD + RSI Filter had the shallower maximum drawdown (-31.0% vs -34.1%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+2.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.77. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +4.7% with a Sharpe of 0.51 and a maximum drawdown of -32.1%. The mix did not beat the better strategy on Sharpe (0.51 vs 0.55).
