Donchian Breakout vs Vortex Indicator on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10: 4240 trading days (16.8 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Vortex Indicator had the higher CAGR (+5.3% vs +3.6% for Donchian Breakout). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.40 vs 0.48, a gap under 0.1. Donchian Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-29.4% vs -31.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+2.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.69. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +4.6% with a Sharpe of 0.49 and a maximum drawdown of -25.3%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.49 vs 0.48).
