HMA Crossover vs Parabolic SAR on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12: 4242 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+5.1% for HMA Crossover, +4.8% for Parabolic SAR). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.48 vs 0.45, a gap under 0.1. Maximum drawdowns were similar (-33.2% vs -33.2%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+2.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.73. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +5.1% with a Sharpe of 0.50 and a maximum drawdown of -29.9%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.50 vs 0.48).
