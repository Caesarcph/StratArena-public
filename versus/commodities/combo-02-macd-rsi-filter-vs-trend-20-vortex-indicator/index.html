MACD + RSI Filter vs Vortex Indicator on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10: 4240 trading days (16.8 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Vortex Indicator had the higher CAGR (+5.4% vs +4.8% for MACD + RSI Filter). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.55 vs 0.49, a gap under 0.1. MACD + RSI Filter had the shallower maximum drawdown (-31.0% vs -32.5%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+2.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.51. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +5.3% with a Sharpe of 0.59 and a maximum drawdown of -24.3%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.59 vs 0.55).
