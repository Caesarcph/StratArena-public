MACD + RSI Filter vs Donchian Breakout on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10: 4240 trading days (16.8 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

MACD + RSI Filter had the higher CAGR (+4.8% vs +3.6% for Donchian Breakout). On risk-adjusted return MACD + RSI Filter led, with a Sharpe of 0.55 against 0.40. Donchian Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-29.4% vs -31.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+2.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.12. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +4.4% with a Sharpe of 0.63 and a maximum drawdown of -23.3%. A correlation below 0.3 means the two often did different things, which is the case where mixing helps most. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.63 vs 0.55).
