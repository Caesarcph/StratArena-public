Trailing Stop Strategy vs Donchian Breakout on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-13: 4243 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Trailing Stop Strategy had the higher CAGR (+5.7% vs +3.6% for Donchian Breakout). On risk-adjusted return Trailing Stop Strategy led, with a Sharpe of 0.51 against 0.40. Maximum drawdowns were similar (-29.7% vs -29.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+2.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix
