Trailing Stop Strategy vs Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-25: 4251 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Trailing Stop Strategy had the higher CAGR (+6.0% vs +5.2% for Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.53 vs 0.52, a gap under 0.1. Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average had the shallower maximum drawdown (-28.0% vs -29.7%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+1.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix
