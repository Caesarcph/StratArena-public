MACD Strategy vs Vortex Indicator on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-23: 4249 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Vortex Indicator had the higher CAGR (+5.4% vs +4.3% for MACD Strategy). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.42 vs 0.49, a gap under 0.1. Vortex Indicator had the shallower maximum drawdown (-32.5% vs -34.1%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+1.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
