Trailing Stop Strategy vs MACD Strategy on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-11: 4241 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Trailing Stop Strategy had the higher CAGR (+5.7% vs +4.3% for MACD Strategy). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.51 vs 0.42, a gap under 0.1. Trailing Stop Strategy had the shallower maximum drawdown (-29.7% vs -34.1%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+2.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
