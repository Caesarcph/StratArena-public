HMA Crossover vs MACD Strategy on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-23: 4249 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

HMA Crossover had the higher CAGR (+4.8% vs +4.3% for MACD Strategy). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.46 vs 0.42, a gap under 0.1. Maximum drawdowns were similar (-33.2% vs -34.1%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+1.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.70. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +4.7% with a Sharpe of 0.48 and a maximum drawdown of -31.5%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.48 vs 0.46).
