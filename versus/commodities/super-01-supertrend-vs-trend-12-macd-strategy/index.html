Supertrend vs MACD Strategy on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-11: 4241 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+4.4% for Supertrend, +4.3% for MACD Strategy). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.43 vs 0.42, a gap under 0.1. Supertrend had the shallower maximum drawdown (-29.1% vs -34.1%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+2.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.75. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +4.5% with a Sharpe of 0.45 and a maximum drawdown of -29.7%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.45 vs 0.43).
