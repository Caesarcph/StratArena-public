Parabolic SAR vs Vortex Indicator on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-24: 4250 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Vortex Indicator had the higher CAGR (+5.3% vs +4.2% for Parabolic SAR). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.41 vs 0.48, a gap under 0.1. Vortex Indicator had the shallower maximum drawdown (-32.5% vs -37.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+1.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.76. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +4.9% with a Sharpe of 0.48 and a maximum drawdown of -34.5%. The mix did not beat the better strategy on Sharpe (0.48 vs 0.48).
