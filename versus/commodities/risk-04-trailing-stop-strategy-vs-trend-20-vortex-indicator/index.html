Trailing Stop Strategy vs Vortex Indicator on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-23: 4249 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+5.7% for Trailing Stop Strategy, +5.4% for Vortex Indicator). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.51 vs 0.49, a gap under 0.1. Trailing Stop Strategy had the shallower maximum drawdown (-29.7% vs -32.5%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+1.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix
