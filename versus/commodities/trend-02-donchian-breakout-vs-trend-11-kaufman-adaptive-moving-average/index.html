Donchian Breakout vs Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average on Commodities: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-24: 4250 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Commodities are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average had the higher CAGR (+5.2% vs +3.6% for Donchian Breakout). On risk-adjusted return Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average led, with a Sharpe of 0.52 against 0.40. Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average had the shallower maximum drawdown (-28.0% vs -29.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Commodities on CAGR (+1.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
