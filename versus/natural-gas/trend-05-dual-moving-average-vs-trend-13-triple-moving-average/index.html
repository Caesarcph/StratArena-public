Dual Moving Average vs Triple Moving Average on Natural gas: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-11: 4241 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Natural gas are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Dual Moving Average had the higher CAGR (-2.0% vs -5.1% for Triple Moving Average). On risk-adjusted return Dual Moving Average led, with a Sharpe of 0.06 against -0.05. Dual Moving Average had the shallower maximum drawdown (-49.7% vs -70.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Natural gas on CAGR (-25.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
