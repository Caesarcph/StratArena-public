ADX Trend Filter vs Z-Score Mean Reversion on Natural gas: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-23: 4249 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Natural gas are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Z-Score Mean Reversion had the higher CAGR (+0.3% vs -3.3% for ADX Trend Filter). On risk-adjusted return Z-Score Mean Reversion led, with a Sharpe of 0.08 against -0.03. Z-Score Mean Reversion had the shallower maximum drawdown (-23.0% vs -52.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Natural gas on CAGR (-25.3%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

A correlation below 0.3 means the two often did different things, which is the case where mixing helps most. The mix did not beat the better strategy on Sharpe (-0.01 vs 0.08).
