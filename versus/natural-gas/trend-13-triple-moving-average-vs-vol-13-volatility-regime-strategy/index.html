Triple Moving Average vs Volatility Regime Strategy on Natural gas: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12: 4242 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Natural gas are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Volatility Regime Strategy had the higher CAGR (-0.5% vs -5.1% for Triple Moving Average). On risk-adjusted return Volatility Regime Strategy led, with a Sharpe of 0.10 against -0.05. Volatility Regime Strategy had the shallower maximum drawdown (-53.9% vs -70.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Natural gas on CAGR (-25.1%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
