ADX Trend Filter vs Dual Moving Average on Natural gas: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12: 4242 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Natural gas are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Dual Moving Average had the higher CAGR (-2.0% vs -3.0% for ADX Trend Filter). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe -0.01 vs 0.06, a gap under 0.1. Dual Moving Average had the shallower maximum drawdown (-49.7% vs -52.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Natural gas on CAGR (-25.1%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
