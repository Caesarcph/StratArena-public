Dual Moving Average vs Volatility Regime Strategy on Natural gas: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-16: 4244 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Natural gas are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Volatility Regime Strategy had the higher CAGR (-0.5% vs -2.0% for Dual Moving Average). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.06 vs 0.09, a gap under 0.1. Dual Moving Average had the shallower maximum drawdown (-49.7% vs -53.9%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Natural gas on CAGR (-25.4%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
