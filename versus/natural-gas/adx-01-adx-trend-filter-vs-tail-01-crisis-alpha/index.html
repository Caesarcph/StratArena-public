ADX Trend Filter vs Crisis Alpha on Natural gas: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-11: 4241 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Natural gas are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Crisis Alpha had the higher CAGR (+3.3% vs -3.1% for ADX Trend Filter). On risk-adjusted return Crisis Alpha led, with a Sharpe of 0.31 against -0.02. ADX Trend Filter had the shallower maximum drawdown (-52.0% vs -84.7%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Natural gas on CAGR (-25.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was -0.47. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +4.9% with a Sharpe of 0.34 and a maximum drawdown of -37.8%. A correlation below 0.3 means the two often did different things, which is the case where mixing helps most. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.34 vs 0.31).
