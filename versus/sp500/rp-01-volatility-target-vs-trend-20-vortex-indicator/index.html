Volatility Target vs Vortex Indicator on S&P 500: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-11: 4241 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of S&P 500 are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Volatility Target had the higher CAGR (+10.6% vs +8.3% for Vortex Indicator). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.92 vs 0.84, a gap under 0.1. Volatility Target had the shallower maximum drawdown (-16.6% vs -27.6%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of S&P 500 on CAGR (+13.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix
