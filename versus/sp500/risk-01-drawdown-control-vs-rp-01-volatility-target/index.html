Drawdown Control vs Volatility Target on S&P 500: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12: 4242 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of S&P 500 are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Drawdown Control had the higher CAGR (+11.4% vs +10.6% for Volatility Target). On risk-adjusted return Volatility Target led, with a Sharpe of 0.92 against 0.80. Volatility Target had the shallower maximum drawdown (-16.6% vs -28.0%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of S&P 500 on CAGR (+13.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
