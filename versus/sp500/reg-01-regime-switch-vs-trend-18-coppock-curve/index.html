Regime Switch vs Coppock Curve on S&P 500: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12: 4242 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of S&P 500 are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Regime Switch had the higher CAGR (+9.9% vs +8.1% for Coppock Curve). On risk-adjusted return Regime Switch led, with a Sharpe of 0.91 against 0.79. Maximum drawdowns were similar (-17.7% vs -18.2%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of S&P 500 on CAGR (+13.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.58. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +9.2% with a Sharpe of 0.96 and a maximum drawdown of -14.8%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.96 vs 0.91).
