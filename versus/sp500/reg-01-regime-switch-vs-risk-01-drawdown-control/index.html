Regime Switch vs Drawdown Control on S&P 500: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-13: 4243 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of S&P 500 are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Drawdown Control had the higher CAGR (+11.3% vs +9.9% for Regime Switch). On risk-adjusted return Regime Switch led, with a Sharpe of 0.90 against 0.80. Regime Switch had the shallower maximum drawdown (-17.7% vs -28.0%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of S&P 500 on CAGR (+13.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix
