Connors RSI vs Volatility Target on S&P 500: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-11: 4241 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of S&P 500 are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Volatility Target had the higher CAGR (+10.6% vs +6.0% for Connors RSI). On risk-adjusted return Volatility Target led, with a Sharpe of 0.92 against 0.80. Maximum drawdowns were similar (-16.8% vs -16.6%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of S&P 500 on CAGR (+13.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.34. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +8.4% with a Sharpe of 1.06 and a maximum drawdown of -12.7%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (1.06 vs 0.92).
