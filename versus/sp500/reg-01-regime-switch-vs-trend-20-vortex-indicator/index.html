Regime Switch vs Vortex Indicator on S&P 500: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-25: 4251 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of S&P 500 are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Regime Switch had the higher CAGR (+9.5% vs +8.9% for Vortex Indicator). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.87 vs 0.89, a gap under 0.1. Regime Switch had the shallower maximum drawdown (-17.7% vs -27.6%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of S&P 500 on CAGR (+13.5%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.51. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +9.4% with a Sharpe of 1.02 and a maximum drawdown of -17.0%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (1.02 vs 0.89).
