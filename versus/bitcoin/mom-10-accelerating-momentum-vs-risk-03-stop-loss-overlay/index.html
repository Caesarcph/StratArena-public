Accelerating Momentum vs Stop Loss Overlay on Bitcoin: which did better?

Common history 2014-09-17 to 2026-11-10: 4438 trading days (12.1 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Bitcoin are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Stop Loss Overlay had the higher CAGR (+74.9% vs +64.0% for Accelerating Momentum). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 1.11 vs 1.19, a gap under 0.1. Maximum drawdowns were similar (-61.8% vs -61.8%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Bitcoin on CAGR (+52.0%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.84. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +70.8% with a Sharpe of 1.20 and a maximum drawdown of -61.1%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (1.20 vs 1.19).
