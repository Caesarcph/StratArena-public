Ichimoku Cloud vs Turtle Trading on Bitcoin: which did better?

Common history 2014-09-17 to 2026-11-11: 4439 trading days (12.2 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Bitcoin are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Ichimoku Cloud had the higher CAGR (+60.0% vs +54.1% for Turtle Trading). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 1.11 vs 1.05, a gap under 0.1. Ichimoku Cloud had the shallower maximum drawdown (-48.5% vs -63.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Bitcoin on CAGR (+52.0%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.81. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +58.3% with a Sharpe of 1.14 and a maximum drawdown of -53.8%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (1.14 vs 1.11).
