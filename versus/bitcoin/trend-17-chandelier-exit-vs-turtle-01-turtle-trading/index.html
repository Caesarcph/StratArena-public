Chandelier Exit vs Turtle Trading on Bitcoin: which did better?

Common history 2014-09-17 to 2026-11-10: 4438 trading days (12.1 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Bitcoin are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Chandelier Exit had the higher CAGR (+64.6% vs +54.1% for Turtle Trading). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 1.09 vs 1.05, a gap under 0.1. Chandelier Exit had the shallower maximum drawdown (-52.1% vs -63.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Bitcoin on CAGR (+52.0%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.81. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +60.7% with a Sharpe of 1.13 and a maximum drawdown of -51.3%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (1.13 vs 1.09).
