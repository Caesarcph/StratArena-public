Stop Loss Overlay vs Trailing Stop Strategy on Bitcoin: which did better?

Common history 2014-09-17 to 2026-11-15: 4443 trading days (12.2 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Bitcoin are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Stop Loss Overlay had the higher CAGR (+74.8% vs +64.4% for Trailing Stop Strategy). On risk-adjusted return Stop Loss Overlay led, with a Sharpe of 1.19 against 1.09. Maximum drawdowns were similar (-61.8% vs -61.1%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Bitcoin on CAGR (+50.4%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.82. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +71.2% with a Sharpe of 1.19 and a maximum drawdown of -53.3%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (1.19 vs 1.19).
