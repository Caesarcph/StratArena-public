Keltner Channel Breakout vs Trailing Stop Strategy on Bitcoin: which did better?

Common history 2014-09-17 to 2026-11-13: 4441 trading days (12.2 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Bitcoin are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Trailing Stop Strategy had the higher CAGR (+64.5% vs +53.8% for Keltner Channel Breakout). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 1.13 vs 1.09, a gap under 0.1. Keltner Channel Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-45.5% vs -61.1%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Bitcoin on CAGR (+50.9%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.74. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +60.9% with a Sharpe of 1.19 and a maximum drawdown of -50.6%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (1.19 vs 1.13).
