Stop Loss Overlay vs Turtle Trading on Bitcoin: which did better?

Common history 2014-09-17 to 2026-11-12: 4440 trading days (12.2 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Bitcoin are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Stop Loss Overlay had the higher CAGR (+74.9% vs +54.1% for Turtle Trading). On risk-adjusted return Stop Loss Overlay led, with a Sharpe of 1.19 against 1.05. Stop Loss Overlay had the shallower maximum drawdown (-61.8% vs -63.4%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Bitcoin on CAGR (+51.5%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.80. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +65.8% with a Sharpe of 1.18 and a maximum drawdown of -56.0%. The mix did not beat the better strategy on Sharpe (1.18 vs 1.19).
