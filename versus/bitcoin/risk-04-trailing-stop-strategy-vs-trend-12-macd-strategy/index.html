Trailing Stop Strategy vs MACD Strategy on Bitcoin: which did better?

Common history 2014-09-17 to 2026-11-12: 4440 trading days (12.2 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Bitcoin are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Trailing Stop Strategy had the higher CAGR (+64.5% vs +59.0% for MACD Strategy). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 1.09 vs 1.05, a gap under 0.1. MACD Strategy had the shallower maximum drawdown (-57.9% vs -61.1%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Bitcoin on CAGR (+51.5%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.81. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +63.3% with a Sharpe of 1.12 and a maximum drawdown of -57.6%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (1.12 vs 1.09).
