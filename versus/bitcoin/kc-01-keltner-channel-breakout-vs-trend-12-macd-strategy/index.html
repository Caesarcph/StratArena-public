Keltner Channel Breakout vs MACD Strategy on Bitcoin: which did better?

Common history 2014-09-17 to 2026-11-10: 4438 trading days (12.1 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Bitcoin are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

MACD Strategy had the higher CAGR (+59.0% vs +53.9% for Keltner Channel Breakout). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 1.13 vs 1.05, a gap under 0.1. Keltner Channel Breakout had the shallower maximum drawdown (-45.5% vs -57.9%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Bitcoin on CAGR (+52.0%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.69. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +58.5% with a Sharpe of 1.18 and a maximum drawdown of -48.7%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (1.18 vs 1.13).
