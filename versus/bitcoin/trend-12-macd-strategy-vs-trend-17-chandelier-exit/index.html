MACD Strategy vs Chandelier Exit on Bitcoin: which did better?

Common history 2014-09-17 to 2026-11-15: 4443 trading days (12.2 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Bitcoin are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Chandelier Exit had the higher CAGR (+64.5% vs +58.9% for MACD Strategy). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 1.05 vs 1.09, a gap under 0.1. Chandelier Exit had the shallower maximum drawdown (-52.1% vs -57.9%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Bitcoin on CAGR (+50.4%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.78. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +63.5% with a Sharpe of 1.13 and a maximum drawdown of -47.3%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (1.13 vs 1.09).
