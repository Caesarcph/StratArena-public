Stop Loss Overlay vs Chandelier Exit on Bitcoin: which did better?

Common history 2014-09-17 to 2026-11-11: 4439 trading days (12.2 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Bitcoin are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Stop Loss Overlay had the higher CAGR (+74.9% vs +64.6% for Chandelier Exit). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 1.19 vs 1.09, a gap under 0.1. Chandelier Exit had the shallower maximum drawdown (-52.1% vs -61.8%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Bitcoin on CAGR (+52.0%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.80. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +71.5% with a Sharpe of 1.20 and a maximum drawdown of -56.3%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (1.20 vs 1.19).
