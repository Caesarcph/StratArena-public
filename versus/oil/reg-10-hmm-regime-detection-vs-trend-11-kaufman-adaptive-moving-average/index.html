HMM Regime Detection vs Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average on Oil: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-11: 4241 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Oil are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+5.6% for HMM Regime Detection, +5.4% for Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.35 vs 0.36, a gap under 0.1. Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average had the shallower maximum drawdown (-46.8% vs -52.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Oil on CAGR (-4.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
