HMM Regime Detection vs Aroon Indicator on Oil: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-13: 4243 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Oil are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+5.6% for HMM Regime Detection, +5.6% for Aroon Indicator). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.35 vs 0.38, a gap under 0.1. Aroon Indicator had the shallower maximum drawdown (-31.2% vs -52.0%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Oil on CAGR (-4.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix
