Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average vs Coppock Curve on Oil: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10: 4240 trading days (16.8 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Oil are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Coppock Curve had the higher CAGR (+6.3% vs +5.4% for Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.36 vs 0.38, a gap under 0.1. Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average had the shallower maximum drawdown (-46.8% vs -57.3%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Oil on CAGR (-4.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
