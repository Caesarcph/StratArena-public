Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average vs Vortex Indicator on Oil: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12: 4242 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Oil are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+5.4% for Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average, +5.4% for Vortex Indicator). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.36 vs 0.34, a gap under 0.1. Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average had the shallower maximum drawdown (-46.8% vs -54.8%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Oil on CAGR (-4.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.78. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +5.7% with a Sharpe of 0.37 and a maximum drawdown of -43.6%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.37 vs 0.36).
