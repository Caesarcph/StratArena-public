Aroon Indicator vs Vortex Indicator on Oil: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-25: 4251 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Oil are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+5.5% for Aroon Indicator, +5.4% for Vortex Indicator). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.38 vs 0.34, a gap under 0.1. Aroon Indicator had the shallower maximum drawdown (-31.2% vs -54.8%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Oil on CAGR (-4.8%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.65. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +5.9% with a Sharpe of 0.39 and a maximum drawdown of -42.5%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.39 vs 0.38).
