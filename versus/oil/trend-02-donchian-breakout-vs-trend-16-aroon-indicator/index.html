Donchian Breakout vs Aroon Indicator on Oil: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-24: 4250 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Oil are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Aroon Indicator had the higher CAGR (+5.6% vs +4.3% for Donchian Breakout). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.32 vs 0.38, a gap under 0.1. Aroon Indicator had the shallower maximum drawdown (-31.2% vs -35.5%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Oil on CAGR (-5.0%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
