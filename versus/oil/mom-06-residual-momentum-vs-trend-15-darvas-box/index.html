Residual Momentum vs Darvas Box on Oil: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-24: 4250 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Oil are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Darvas Box had the higher CAGR (+5.4% vs +4.9% for Residual Momentum). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.33 vs 0.34, a gap under 0.1. Darvas Box had the shallower maximum drawdown (-50.6% vs -54.2%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Oil on CAGR (-5.0%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.56. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +5.7% with a Sharpe of 0.38 and a maximum drawdown of -40.8%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.38 vs 0.34).
