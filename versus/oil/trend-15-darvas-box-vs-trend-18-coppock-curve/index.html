Darvas Box vs Coppock Curve on Oil: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12: 4242 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Oil are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Coppock Curve had the higher CAGR (+6.3% vs +5.4% for Darvas Box). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.34 vs 0.38, a gap under 0.1. Darvas Box had the shallower maximum drawdown (-50.6% vs -57.3%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Oil on CAGR (-4.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
