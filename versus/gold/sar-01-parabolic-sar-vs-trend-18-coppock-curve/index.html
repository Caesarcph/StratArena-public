Parabolic SAR vs Coppock Curve on Gold: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-24: 4250 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Gold are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+6.5% for Parabolic SAR, +6.7% for Coppock Curve). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.59 vs 0.58, a gap under 0.1. Coppock Curve had the shallower maximum drawdown (-22.2% vs -27.5%). Only Coppock Curve beat buy-and-hold of Gold on CAGR (+6.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.77. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +6.7% with a Sharpe of 0.62 and a maximum drawdown of -21.3%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.62 vs 0.59).
