CCI Mean Reversion vs Parabolic SAR on Gold: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-10: 4240 trading days (16.8 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Gold are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Parabolic SAR had the higher CAGR (+6.7% vs +5.3% for CCI Mean Reversion). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.63 vs 0.61, a gap under 0.1. CCI Mean Reversion had the shallower maximum drawdown (-17.0% vs -26.6%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of Gold on CAGR (+7.5%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.07. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +6.3% with a Sharpe of 0.84 and a maximum drawdown of -18.8%. A correlation below 0.3 means the two often did different things, which is the case where mixing helps most. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.84 vs 0.63).
