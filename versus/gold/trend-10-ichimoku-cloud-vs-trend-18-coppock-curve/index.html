Ichimoku Cloud vs Coppock Curve on Gold: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-25: 4251 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Gold are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+6.9% for Ichimoku Cloud, +6.7% for Coppock Curve). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.65 vs 0.58, a gap under 0.1. Ichimoku Cloud had the shallower maximum drawdown (-17.4% vs -22.2%). Both beat buy-and-hold of Gold on CAGR (+6.7%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
