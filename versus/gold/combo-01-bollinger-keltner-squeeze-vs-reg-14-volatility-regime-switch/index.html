Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze vs Volatility Regime Switch on Gold: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-20: 4248 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Gold are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+4.7% for Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze, +4.6% for Volatility Regime Switch). On risk-adjusted return Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze led, with a Sharpe of 0.64 against 0.52. Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze had the shallower maximum drawdown (-10.9% vs -29.4%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of Gold on CAGR (+6.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
