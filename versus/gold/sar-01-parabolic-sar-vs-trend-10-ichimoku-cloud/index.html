Parabolic SAR vs Ichimoku Cloud on Gold: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-11: 4241 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Gold are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+6.7% for Parabolic SAR, +6.9% for Ichimoku Cloud). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.60 vs 0.65, a gap under 0.1. Ichimoku Cloud had the shallower maximum drawdown (-17.4% vs -26.6%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of Gold on CAGR (+7.4%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.67. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +6.9% with a Sharpe of 0.68 and a maximum drawdown of -18.7%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.68 vs 0.65).
