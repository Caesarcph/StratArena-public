Parabolic SAR vs Volatility Regime Strategy on Gold: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12: 4242 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Gold are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+6.5% for Parabolic SAR, +6.1% for Volatility Regime Strategy). On risk-adjusted return Volatility Regime Strategy led, with a Sharpe of 0.75 against 0.59. Volatility Regime Strategy had the shallower maximum drawdown (-15.3% vs -27.5%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of Gold on CAGR (+7.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.45. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +6.5% with a Sharpe of 0.76 and a maximum drawdown of -14.9%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.76 vs 0.75).
