Stop Loss Overlay vs Ichimoku Cloud on Gold: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-24: 4250 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Gold are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Ichimoku Cloud had the higher CAGR (+6.9% vs +6.1% for Stop Loss Overlay). On risk-adjusted return Ichimoku Cloud led, with a Sharpe of 0.65 against 0.53. Ichimoku Cloud had the shallower maximum drawdown (-17.4% vs -36.1%). Only Ichimoku Cloud beat buy-and-hold of Gold on CAGR (+6.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
