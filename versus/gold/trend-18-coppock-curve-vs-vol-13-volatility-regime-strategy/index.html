Coppock Curve vs Volatility Regime Strategy on Gold: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-24: 4250 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Gold are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Coppock Curve had the higher CAGR (+6.7% vs +5.9% for Volatility Regime Strategy). On risk-adjusted return Volatility Regime Strategy led, with a Sharpe of 0.72 against 0.58. Volatility Regime Strategy had the shallower maximum drawdown (-15.3% vs -22.2%). Only Coppock Curve beat buy-and-hold of Gold on CAGR (+6.6%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
