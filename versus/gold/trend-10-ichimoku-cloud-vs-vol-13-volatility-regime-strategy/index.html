Ichimoku Cloud vs Volatility Regime Strategy on Gold: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-16: 4244 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Gold are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Ichimoku Cloud had the higher CAGR (+6.9% vs +6.1% for Volatility Regime Strategy). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.65 vs 0.75, a gap under 0.1. Volatility Regime Strategy had the shallower maximum drawdown (-15.3% vs -17.4%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of Gold on CAGR (+7.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
