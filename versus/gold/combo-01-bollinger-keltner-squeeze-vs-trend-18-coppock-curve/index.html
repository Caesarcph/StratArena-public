Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze vs Coppock Curve on Gold: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-11: 4241 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Gold are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Coppock Curve had the higher CAGR (+6.7% vs +4.7% for Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.64 vs 0.58, a gap under 0.1. Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze had the shallower maximum drawdown (-10.9% vs -22.2%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of Gold on CAGR (+7.4%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
