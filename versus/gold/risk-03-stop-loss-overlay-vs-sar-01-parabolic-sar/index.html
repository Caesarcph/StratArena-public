Stop Loss Overlay vs Parabolic SAR on Gold: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-12: 4242 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Gold are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+6.1% for Stop Loss Overlay, +6.5% for Parabolic SAR). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.54 vs 0.59, a gap under 0.1. Parabolic SAR had the shallower maximum drawdown (-27.5% vs -36.1%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of Gold on CAGR (+7.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.72. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +6.4% with a Sharpe of 0.60 and a maximum drawdown of -28.9%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.60 vs 0.59).
