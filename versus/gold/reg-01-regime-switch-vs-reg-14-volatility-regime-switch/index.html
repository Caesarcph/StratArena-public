Regime Switch vs Volatility Regime Switch on Gold: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-11: 4241 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Gold are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

Regime Switch had the higher CAGR (+5.9% vs +4.7% for Volatility Regime Switch). On risk-adjusted return it is too close to call: Sharpe 0.55 vs 0.54, a gap under 0.1. Maximum drawdowns were similar (-29.2% vs -29.4%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of Gold on CAGR (+7.4%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Correlation and a 50/50 mix

The daily-return correlation of the two strategies was 0.65. Holding half of each, rebalanced daily, would have returned CAGR +5.5% with a Sharpe of 0.60 and a maximum drawdown of -27.6%. The mix had a higher Sharpe than either strategy alone (0.60 vs 0.55).
