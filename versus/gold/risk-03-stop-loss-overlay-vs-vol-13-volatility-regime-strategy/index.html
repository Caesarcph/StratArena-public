Stop Loss Overlay vs Volatility Regime Strategy on Gold: which did better?

Common history 2010-01-04 to 2026-11-13: 4243 trading days (16.9 years). Both strategies and buy-and-hold of Gold are measured over exactly this window, before trading costs.

Verdict from the numbers

The two had about the same CAGR (+6.1% for Stop Loss Overlay, +6.1% for Volatility Regime Strategy). On risk-adjusted return Volatility Regime Strategy led, with a Sharpe of 0.75 against 0.53. Volatility Regime Strategy had the shallower maximum drawdown (-15.3% vs -36.1%). Neither beat buy-and-hold of Gold on CAGR (+7.2%). This describes past results over this window, before costs; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
