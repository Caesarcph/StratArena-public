  1. Long Trend

    A faster average above a slower average identifies a persistent upward price trend.

    Exit

  2. Channel Breakout

    New closing highs may signal continuation, while a shorter closing-price breakdown signals an exit.

    Enter
    Close > Rolling maximum(55) · 1 bar earlier
    Exit
    Close < Rolling minimum(20) · 1 bar earlier

  3. Oversold Recovery

    An unusually low RSI may precede a rebound toward neutral momentum.

    Enter
    RSI(14) < 30
    Exit
    RSI(14) ≥ 55

  4. Six Month Momentum

    Positive medium-term price momentum may persist until the trailing price change becomes nonpositive.

    Enter
    ROC(126) > 0
    Exit
    ROC(126) ≤ 0

  5. Trend Pullback

    A short-term momentum pullback within an established upward trend may offer a subsequent recovery.

    Enter
    (EMA(50) > EMA(200) AND RSI(5) < 35)
    Exit
    (RSI(5) ≥ 65 OR EMA(50) ≤ EMA(200))

  6. Quiet Uptrend

    An upward price trend with low realized volatility may persist until price weakness or elevated volatility appears.

    Enter
    (Close > SMA(100) AND Annualized volatility(20) < 0.15)
    Exit
    (Close ≤ SMA(100) OR Annualized volatility(20) ≥ 0.25)

Frozen rules (6)

  1. SMA Crossover

    Go long when short-term average trend turns up relative to long-term average, exit when it reverses.

    Enter
    SMA(20) crosses above SMA(100)
    Exit
    SMA(20) crosses below SMA(100)

  2. EMA Trend Filter

    Stay long only while price is above a slow EMA, indicating an established uptrend.

    Enter
    Close > EMA(50)
    Exit
    Close < EMA(50)

  3. RSI Mean Reversion

    Buy after oversold conditions and exit once momentum recovers to neutral/overbought territory.

    Enter
    RSI(14) < 30
    Exit
    RSI(14) > 55

  4. Rolling Breakout

    Enter on a new 55-day high breakout and exit on a new 20-day low breakdown, a classic trend-following channel.

    Enter
    Close > Rolling maximum(55) · 1 bar earlier
    Exit
    Close < Rolling minimum(20) · 1 bar earlier

  5. ROC Momentum

    Hold when positive 60-day momentum confirms an uptrend, exit when momentum turns negative.

    Enter
    ROC(60) > 0
    Exit
    ROC(60) < 0

  6. Low Volatility Trend

    Combine a price-above-SMA uptrend with a calm volatility regime, exiting on trend break or volatility spike.

    Enter
    (Close > SMA(100) AND Annualized volatility(20) < 0.25)
    Exit
    (Close < SMA(100) OR Annualized volatility(20) > 0.45)

How the season is measured

  • A decision after close t executes at close t+1, then first earns the return from close t+1 to t+2. Every rule starts in cash; positions are either cash or fully long.
  • The 0, 5, 10 and 25 bp scenarios charge the absolute executed exposure change against post-return, pre-fee equity. This is a synthetic exposure-change cost model, not broker fills or exact traded notional.
  • Buy-and-hold uses the same market dates, initial cash, next-close entry and cost scenario. There is no forced final liquidation. The six rule accounts are not rebalanced against one another each day.
  • All six submitted rules are retained, including duplicates. No rule is selected using its season result.
  • Client system prompts, reasoning allocation and auxiliary model calls can differ. Receipt notes disclose observed differences; inference compute is not normalized across providers.
