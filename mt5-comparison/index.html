Native MT5 and Python: a historical comparison

15 EAs across 4 markets: original MQL5 programs in the MT5 tester, alongside retained Python-conversion results. The figures describe this captured batch; later EA results on this site may differ.

These runs use different price feeds, execution models and costs. Agreement does not establish faithful replication, and a difference alone does not identify a conversion bug.

How to read the differences

  • Native: original MQL5, broker real ticks, historical spread and broker contract constraints; requested starting balance USD 10,000 and leverage 1:400. The quarantined capture did not establish that configuration.
  • Python: translated programs on H1 bars. The frozen records declare commission 0.00002 and no spread model. Data feeds, intrabar execution, sizing and metric definitions differ.
  • The Python files are frozen retained snapshots. Their hashes establish file integrity, not which source and inputs were actually executed: the old run records did not capture execution-time hashes.
  • The zero-trade and timeout groups describe missing comparison coverage. Neither should be treated as a successful replication or a strategy quality rating.
