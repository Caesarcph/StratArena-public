Does Turtle Trading Still Work? Ten Markets, 2021–2026

Over the five years to November 2026 the 20/10 Turtle breakout (TURTLE-01) made 6.0% a year on US stocks against 11.9% for holding, with a smaller drawdown (-13.3% against -24.5%). It beat holding on return on four of ten markets, and the slower Donchian Breakout (TREND-02) had the higher return and Sharpe ratio on eight.

The rules as coded

Turtle Trading (TURTLE-01) works on daily closing prices. It goes long when the close is above the highest close of the previous 20 days, and goes flat when the close is below the lowest close of the previous 10 days. Otherwise it keeps its position. It is long only, always fully invested when long, and does not add to winners. The strategy page mentions position sizing by risk; the code does not do it. The original Turtle system of the 1980s also sold short and added to positions, and this version does neither.

In the walk-forward test the entry window was chosen from 16, 20 and 24 days and the exit window from 8, 10 and 12, re-selected every six months. Donchian Breakout (TREND-02) is the slower relative: long above the previous 55-day high, flat below the previous 20-day low. It has no parameters to tune, and the ATR sizing, trade limit and trailing stop on its page are not in the code. All results are before trading costs, and the site counts each entry and each exit as a trade.

Five years against the full history

Over the five years to 11 November 2026 TURTLE-01 had a positive annual return on 7 of 10 markets (not on silver, natural gas or ether) and a positive Sharpe ratio on 9. Against holding it earned a higher return on 4 markets (natural gas, EUR/USD, bitcoin and ether), a higher Sharpe ratio on 2 (EUR/USD and bitcoin) and a smaller maximum drawdown on 8 (not oil or broad commodities). On US stocks it made 6.0% a year with a Sharpe of 0.63 and a -13.3% drawdown; holding made 11.9%, 0.74 and -24.5%.

Compared with its own full history, the last five years were better on only three markets. Gold made 6.4% a year against 3.8%, agriculture 1.5% against 0.5% and EUR/USD 0.3% against -1.3%. On the others the recent record is weaker, and the gap is widest in crypto: bitcoin 8.0% a year (Sharpe 0.34) against 54.1% (1.05) since 2014, and ether -4.1% against 43.9% since 2017. Silver went from 4.5% to -1.0% and natural gas from -13.6% to -21.9%. Over the full history the rule beat holding on return on 6 markets and had a smaller drawdown on 9.

Year by year, 2022 to 2026

The calendar years show why the five-year number is modest. For each year, the median market return is given for Turtle, Donchian and holding.

  • 2022: -4.2%, +3.4% and +0.8%. Turtle beat holding on 5 of 10 markets; it made 32.4% on natural gas and lost 46.4% on bitcoin (holding lost 64.3%). US stocks: -5.6% against -18.2% for holding.
  • 2023: +0.5%, +1.2% and +5.7%. It beat holding on 3 of 10. Bitcoin made 64.8% against 155.4% for holding; US stocks 17.7% against 26.2%.
  • 2024: +3.5%, +10.4% and +22.9%. It beat holding on 2 of 10. US stocks made 6.3% against 24.9%.
  • 2025: +7.3%, +4.5% and +3.8%. It beat holding on 2 of 10. Silver made 56.6% against 144.7%.
  • 2026 to 11 November: +1.9%, +2.3% and -6.4%. It beat holding on 6 of 10; oil was its best market at 45.5% (holding 104.6%).

In the 12 calendar years 2010 to 2021, Turtle beat holding in 57 of 109 market-years (the first years of bitcoin and ether are partial). In 2022 to 2026 it did so in 18 of 50. Rising markets, where holding earned its keep, were the hardest comparison.

Where the breakouts failed

We split each market's five-year record into round trips, from entry to exit. TURTLE-01 closed 340 of them and 36% made money. Winners averaged +8.8% and lasted a median 24 days; losers averaged -4.2% and lasted a median 8 days, and 208 of the 218 losers were over within 20 days. The win rate was lowest on natural gas (23%), bitcoin (29%), agriculture (30%) and silver (31%) and highest on gold and EUR/USD (48% each). This is the usual breakout trade: many small losses, few large gains.

The worst single trades were short, sharp reversals: natural gas lost 21.1% in 10 days in March 2023, ether 15.6% in 10 days in December 2021 and silver 15.1% in 16 days in May to June 2026.

Oil shows what whipsaws cost. From 8 March 2022 to 1 October 2025 TURTLE-01 fell 51.9%, while oil itself fell 14.4% over the same dates; Donchian fell 24.7%. On natural gas, TURTLE-01 lost 85.0% between 6 June 2022 and 1 October 2026, the fund lost 92.0% and Donchian 65.4%. On silver, after the 2025 rally, TURTLE-01 fell 45.5% between 28 January and 18 August 2026, almost exactly the 45.6% fall in the metal.

The 2022 bear market

Between 3 January and 12 October 2022 US stocks fell 24.5%. TURTLE-01 lost 9.7% on US stocks, and Donchian 8.7%. Turtle finished positive on only 3 of 10 markets: natural gas +51.2%, broad commodities +13.1% and oil +4.3%. Donchian was positive on 5: oil +37.3%, broad commodities +28.9%, natural gas +8.8%, agriculture +2.6% and gold +1.9%. Both lost on bitcoin, Turtle 37.9% and Donchian 26.4% against 58.8% for holding. The slower rule caught more of the oil and broad-commodity trends; the faster one caught more of natural gas.

Against Donchian Breakout

Donchian traded far less: 258 trades over five years across the ten markets against 675 for Turtle, and was invested on 36% of days on average against 41%. It beat holding's Sharpe ratio on 6 markets (silver, natural gas, broad commodities, EUR/USD, bitcoin and ether), against 2 for Turtle, and had a smaller drawdown than holding on 9 of 10. Turtle beat Donchian on return and Sharpe on only two markets, US stocks and EUR/USD. Donchian's round trips were fewer but bigger: 132 closed, a 41% win rate, winners +16.1% over a median 58 days and losers -5.7% over 17.

Over the full history the order changes in crypto. Turtle made 54.1% a year on bitcoin (Sharpe 1.05, drawdown -63.4%) against 48.2% (0.98, -56.9%) for Donchian, and 43.9% on ether against 32.7%. Turtle also led on US stocks, 6.7% against 4.4%.

What the numbers cannot tell you

Most of these samples are small: on US stocks, TURTLE-01 closed 29 round trips in five years. The probabilistic Sharpe ratio on US stocks was 0.92 for Turtle and 0.85 for Donchian; the deflated Sharpe ratio, which allows for the number of strategies compared, was 0.48 and 0.34. Neither is close to a conventional 0.95. Costs matter more for the faster rule: at the site's assumption of 10 basis points per unit of turnover, the average drag over the ten markets was 1.2% a year for Turtle and 0.5% for Donchian. Both are long-only on closing prices with no position sizing, so the results say little about the original multi-market system.

Takeaways

  • On a single market, breakouts rarely beat holding on return: Turtle did on 4 of 10 markets over five years, and Donchian on 4.
  • The benefit is drawdown: Turtle's was smaller on 8 of 10 markets and Donchian's on 9.
  • The faster 20/10 rule made 340 round trips in five years and won 36% of them; the 55/20 rule made 132 and won 41%, with bigger winners.
  • The last five years were weaker than the full history for Turtle on seven of ten markets, with the widest gap in crypto.
