Four fees, six fixed pilots: exact cost sensitivity

All 72 historical scenarios, including 20 negative net returns and 15 pairs without an observed sampled excess crossing.

ReproducibilityCostsFixed sample

Hypothesis: absolute losses and relative ranks differ

With fixed recorded decisions and positive turnover, higher positive fees should reduce terminal wealth. Relative rank may nevertheless improve when other paths lose more to costs. We test 0, 5, 10 and 25 basis points without selecting a best fee or dropping negative results.

All 72 scenarios replayed. 20 rows have negative net total return; 15 of 18 pairs have no observed matched-benchmark excess crossing at the sampled fees. These absent crossings are retained, not treated as missing data.

Negative findings and sampled crossings

MR-01/SPY illustrates why rank and wealth must be read separately: its rank improves by one at 25 bp even though return falls from 28.787% to 10.827%. MR-16/SPY falls from 46.219% to −0.054%. TREND-14 is gross-negative in all three markets. RISK-04/BTC-USD falls from 31.674% to −22.741%. None is excluded.

The only sampled excess crossings occur on BTC-USD: MR-01 and TURTLE-01 between 10 and 25 bp, and RISK-04 between 0 and 5 bp. A bracket is neither a fitted root nor evidence of a unique break-even fee. Positive rank change means zero-fee rank minus current rank; it never means higher net return.

Fixed evidence, rules and reproduction

The six original pilots are MR-01 (RSI Mean Reversion), MR-16 (Connors RSI), TURTLE-01 (Turtle Trading), TREND-14 (Linear Regression Channel), RP-01 (Volatility Target) and RISK-04 (Trailing Stop Strategy). RP-01 is not the separate risk-parity basket. All 18 strategy-market pairs and 72 fee scenarios are retained. This retrospective selection was fixed before table extraction; it is not a prospective preregistration.

The cost and cross-market panels use 2021-11-05 through 2026-11-05, with 1,255 closes for SPY/GLD and 1,827 for BTC-USD. Return intervals are one fewer. The calendar audit uses each run’s full native dates. Daily site updates do not replace this frozen evidence.

Failure conditions and interpretation

Fees use drift-adjusted one-way risky turnover and are charged after each gross return: (1 + grossReturn) × (1 − turnover × bps / 10000). Strategy and matched benchmark are charged separately. The start-close anchor excludes fees already paid at the first included close. Fees do not feed back into targets or drift decisions. Paid fees are start-close capital units, not terminal wealth drag.

Financing, borrow, funding, tax, impact, broker fills and forced terminal liquidation are excluded. These are separate strategy paths, not a pooled or independent-sleeve portfolio. Reject comparisons with changed dates, input, rule, model, anchor or fee units. Historical outcomes do not establish future returns or causal strategy-family superiority. MT5 parity remains deferred.
