TREND-10 · Trend Following

Ichimoku Cloud

The backtest builds the Ichimoku lines from rolling closing-price extremes. It goes long above the cloud when Tenkan is above Kijun and uses the lower cloud boundary and line relationship to exit.

Rules

Entry

Go long when the close is above the cloud top and Tenkan is above Kijun.

Exit

Go to cash when the close is below the cloud bottom or Tenkan is at or below Kijun. Otherwise keep the previous target if the long condition does not trigger.

Indicators

Tenkan and Kijun are midpoints between the highest and lowest closes in their windows.
Span A is the midpoint of Tenkan and Kijun. Span B is the midpoint of highest and lowest closes over 52 bars. Both use values from kijun bars earlier to form the current cloud.

Inputs

Default: tenkan = 9, kijun = 26, senkou = 52.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
