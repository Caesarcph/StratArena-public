TREND-13 · Trend Following

Triple Moving Average

Uses short, medium, and long SMAs to confirm trend alignment. Long when the fast SMA is above the medium SMA and the medium SMA is above the slow SMA. Exit when the stack reverses.

What the backtest shows

  • Beat buy-and-hold on 2 of 10 markets by annual return over 5 years.
  • Had a smaller maximum drawdown than buy-and-hold on 9 of 10 markets.
  • Best market by Sharpe ratio: GLD (Sharpe 0.81, CAGR 13.0%).
  • Weakest market: ETH-USD (Sharpe -0.28, CAGR -21.7%).
  • Few or no trades in the window on SPY, DBA, UNG — treat those rows as a sign the rules rarely triggered, not as an edge.
