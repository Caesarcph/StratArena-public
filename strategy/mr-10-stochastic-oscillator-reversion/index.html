MR-10 · Mean Reversion

Stochastic Oscillator Reversion

Uses Stochastic Oscillator (%K and %D) to identify oversold conditions. Enter long when %K drops below 20, exit when rising above 50. Classic oscillator mean reversion.

Rules

Entry

Buy when %K < 20 (oversold)

Exit

Exit when %K > 50 (neutral)

Indicators

%K = (Close - Low14) / (High14 - Low14) * 100
%D = SMA(%K, 3)

Inputs

K_Period = 14
D_Period = 3
Oversold = 20
Exit = 50

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Max hold period optional
