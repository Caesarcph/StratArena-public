Multi-Factor Composite

Ranks assets across multiple factors: Value (price relative to long-term average), Momentum (recent returns), and Quality (low volatility, low drawdown). Combines factor scores with configurable weights to select top-ranked assets.

Rules

Entry

Allocate to assets with highest composite score

Exit

Monthly rebalance based on updated scores

Indicators

Value: Price / 252-day MA (lower = better value)
Momentum: 6-month return
Quality: Inverse volatility + low drawdown
Composite z-score

Inputs

Value_Lookback = 252
Mom_Lookback = 126
Vol_Lookback = 60
Weights = [0.3, 0.4, 0.3]

Position sizing

Proportional to factor scores

Risk controls

Volatility adjustment

Notes

  • Monthly decisions use the preceding completed observation at the first observed new-month session; this changes trading month-end timing too.
  • Partial-month terminal rows do not trigger rebalancing; targets carry unchanged between monthly decisions.
  • Factor diversification reduces single-factor risk
  • Momentum tends to be strongest factor
  • Quality provides downside protection
