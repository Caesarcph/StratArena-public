Rules

Inputs

Value_Lookback = 252
Mom_Lookback = 126
Vol_Lookback = 60
Weights = [0.3, 0.4, 0.3]

Indicators

Value: Price / 252-day MA (lower = better value)
Momentum: 6-month return
Quality: Inverse volatility + low drawdown
Composite z-score

Entry

Allocate to assets with highest composite score

Exit

Monthly rebalance based on updated scores

Position sizing

Proportional to factor scores

Risk controls

Volatility adjustment

Notes

  • Factor diversification reduces single-factor risk
  • Momentum tends to be strongest factor
  • Quality provides downside protection
