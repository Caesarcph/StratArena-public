Cross-Asset Momentum

Rules

Entry

Multiply inverse volatility for SPY and USO by 1.5 for a positive score and 0.3 otherwise. Multiply GLD by 1.5 for a negative score and 0.5 otherwise.

Exit

Recompute targets daily after 36 observed rows of warmup.

Indicators

Compute 100 × mean SPY return minus 50 × mean GLD return from the lagged history. Use current 20-return annualized sample volatility and substitute 15% if the estimate is unavailable or below 1%.

Inputs

Default momentum lookback 21 daily returns, signal lag 5 sessions, volatility lookback 20 returns.

Position sizing

All other assets use 0.5 times inverse volatility. Normalize the whole basket to 100% long.
