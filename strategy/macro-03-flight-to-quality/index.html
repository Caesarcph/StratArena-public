Flight to Quality

The daily rule holds an equal-weight basket outside stress and switches to GLD when either SPY stress measure triggers. It waits for a sustained clear period before restoring the risk basket.

Rules

Entry

After the 30-row warmup, set the target to 100% GLD when either threshold is exceeded.

Exit

Start the recovery timer on the first clear observation and return to the risk basket 10 observations later if stress stays clear. A new trigger resets the timer.

Indicators

Measure drawdown from SPY's highest closing price observed so far. Use the sample standard deviation of 20 daily returns multiplied by √252 for annualized volatility.

Inputs

Default drawdown threshold 5%, annualized volatility threshold 25%, volatility lookback 20 daily returns, recovery timer 10 row intervals.

Position sizing

Use 100% GLD under stress and equal weights across the nine other assets outside stress.
