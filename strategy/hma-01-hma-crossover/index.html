Rules

Entry

Go long when the fast HMA is above the slow HMA. Go short when the fast HMA is below the slow HMA if allow_short is enabled.

Exit

Go flat when the averages are equal or unavailable. With default settings, a fast HMA below the slow HMA also means cash.

Indicators

HMA(n) is WMA(2 × WMA(close, floor(n/2)) − WMA(close, n), floor(√n)).

Inputs

fast_period = 9, slow_period = 21, allow_short = false.

Position sizing

100% long or cash by default. Optional short target −100%.
