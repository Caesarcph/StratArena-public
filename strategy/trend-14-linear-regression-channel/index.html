TREND-14 · Trend Following

Linear Regression Channel

The channel uses the fitted endpoint of a rolling linear regression and the dispersion of its price residuals. Each close is evaluated against the previous bar's channel.

Rules

Entry

Close strictly above the previous upper band sets long. Close strictly below the previous lower band sets short.

Exit

Inside the outer bands, an existing long exits strictly below the previous fitted endpoint, and an existing short exits strictly above it. Otherwise hold.

Indicators

Fit ordinary least squares to the last period closes. Channel bands are the fitted endpoint ± std_dev × population standard deviation of residuals.

Inputs

period = 50, std_dev = 2.

Position sizing

100% long, 100% short or cash.
