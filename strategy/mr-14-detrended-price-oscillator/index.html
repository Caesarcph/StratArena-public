MR-14 · Mean Reversion

Detrended Price Oscillator

Detrended Price Oscillator (DPO) removes longer-term trend by comparing a shifted price to its moving average. This strategy buys when DPO is deeply negative and exits near the zero line.

Rules

Entry

Go long when DPO% < Entry_Level

Exit

Exit when DPO% > Exit_Level

Indicators

SMA(Period)
DPO = Close.shift(Period/2 + 1) - SMA

Inputs

Period = 20
Entry_Level = -2.0
Exit_Level = 0.0

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Detrending reduces long-cycle bias
