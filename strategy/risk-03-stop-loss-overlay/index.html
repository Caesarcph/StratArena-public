RISK-03

Stop Loss Overlay

Overlays a stop loss on a base trend strategy. When price drops below entry by stop percentage, exit and wait for cooldown period before re-entering. Protects gains and limits losses.

Rules

Inputs

MA_Period = 50
Stop_Pct = 5%
Cooldown = 5 days

Indicators

SMA for trend
Entry price tracking

Entry

Base signal: Price > SMA
Only if not in cooldown

Exit

Price drops Stop_Pct from entry
Or base signal exits

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Hard stop loss
Cooldown prevents whipsaws

Notes

  • Stop loss is essential risk management
  • Cooldown period prevents re-entry whipsaws
  • Can be overlaid on any strategy
