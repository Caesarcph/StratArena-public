VOL-13 · Volatility

Volatility Regime Strategy

Dynamically switches between trend following in low volatility regimes and mean reversion in high volatility regimes. Low vol environments favor trends; high vol environments favor mean reversion as prices overshoot.

Indicators

Realized volatility
Volatility percentile
Trend MA for low vol

Entry

Low vol: trend following (price > MA)
High vol: mean reversion (RSI oversold)

Exit

Regime change or signal reversal

Position sizing

Full position in clear regimes

Risk controls

Reduce size in transition zones

Notes

  • Low vol = trending markets
  • High vol = mean reverting markets
  • Adaptive approach captures both
