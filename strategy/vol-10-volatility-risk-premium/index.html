VOL-10 · Volatility

Volatility Risk Premium

Volatility Risk Premium (VRP) is the difference between implied and realized volatility. When VRP is high (implied > realized), sell volatility by holding the underlying. When VRP is negative, stay out as markets may be turbulent.

Rules

Entry

VRP > threshold (implied > realized)

Exit

VRP < 0 (realized catching up)

Indicators

Realized Vol = Std(returns) * sqrt(252)
Implied Vol proxy = Recent vol range
VRP = Implied - Realized

Inputs

Realized_Lookback = 20
VRP_Threshold = 0.02

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Exit when VRP turns negative
