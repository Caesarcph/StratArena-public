Rules

Inputs

Lookback = 60

Indicators

Covariance matrix

Entry

Solve min w'Σw s.t. sum(w)=1, w>=0

Exit

Monthly rebalance

Risk

Minimum achievable portfolio variance

Notes

  • No return forecasts needed
  • Often outperforms due to low volatility anomaly
  • Tends to concentrate in low-vol assets
