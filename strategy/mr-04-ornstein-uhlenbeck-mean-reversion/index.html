MR-04 · Mean Reversion

Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Mean Reversion

Models price as an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process and trades when price deviates from estimated long-term mean. Uses half-life to filter for tradeable mean-reversion speed.

Rules

Inputs

Lookback = 60
Entry_Std = 1.5
Exit_Std = 0.5
Min/Max Halflife = 1-30

Indicators

OU parameters (mu, theta, sigma)
Half-life
Z-score

Entry

Buy when z < -Entry_Std and halflife in valid range

Exit

Exit when |z| < Exit_Std

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Only trade when half-life between 1-30 days

Notes

  • Based on OU stochastic differential equation: dX = theta*(mu-X)*dt + sigma*dW
  • Half-life provides guidance on expected holding period
  • Parameter estimation via OLS regression
