MR-04 · Mean Reversion

Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Mean Reversion

Fit an intercept and lag coefficient to the previous 60 closes, excluding the decision close. Only a valid mean-reverting fit with an eligible half-life can hold a position.

Rules

Entry

Valid fit, half-life within inclusive[1,30], and current z<−1.5.

Exit

Exit if z>−.5, the fit becomes invalid, or its half-life leaves the allowed range.

Indicators

OLS slope uses matched covariance/variance normalization. For0<b<1, theta=−log(b), mu=a/(1−b), half-life=log(2)/theta; z uses fitted equilibrium volatility.

Inputs

lookback=60, entry_std=1.5, exit_std=.5, min_halflife=1, max_halflife=30.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
