Rules

Entry

Long when fast average>slow average AND monthly row rank≥3.

Exit

Cash when trend condition fails or monthly row rank<3.

Indicators

SMA50 and SMA200 of close; zero-based observed-row rank within each input month.

Inputs

fast_window=50, slow_window=200, event_days=3.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
