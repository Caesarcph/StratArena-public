MR-15 · Mean Reversion

Ultimate Oscillator

Ultimate Oscillator blends three horizons of buying pressure to reduce false signals. This implementation uses close-only data to approximate the classic BP/TR calculation, entering on oversold readings and exiting near neutral.

Rules

Inputs

Short = 7
Medium = 14
Long = 28
Oversold = 30
Exit = 50

Indicators

BP = Close - min(Close, Close[-1])
TR = max(Close, Close[-1]) - min(Close, Close[-1])
AvgShort = sum(BP, Short) / sum(TR, Short)
AvgMedium = sum(BP, Medium) / sum(TR, Medium)
AvgLong = sum(BP, Long) / sum(TR, Long)
UO = 100 * (4*AvgShort + 2*AvgMedium + AvgLong) / 7

Entry

Buy when UO < 30 (oversold)

Exit

Exit when UO > 50 (neutral)

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Max hold period of 20 days

Notes

  • Uses three lookback windows to reduce whipsaws
  • Close-only approximation for BP/TR without full OHLC data
