MR-13 · Mean Reversion

TRIX Reversion

TRIX is the rate of change of a triple-smoothed EMA. This strategy buys when TRIX is deeply negative and exits when momentum mean-reverts toward zero.

Rules

Inputs

Period = 15
Signal = 9
Entry_Level = -0.5
Exit_Level = 0.0

Indicators

TRIX = ROC(EMA(EMA(EMA(close, Period))))
Signal = EMA(TRIX, Signal)

Entry

Go long when TRIX < Entry_Level

Exit

Exit when TRIX > Exit_Level or TRIX > Signal

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Momentum filter avoids chasing peaks

Notes

  • TRIX is smoother than standard ROC
  • Extremes are rare on daily data
