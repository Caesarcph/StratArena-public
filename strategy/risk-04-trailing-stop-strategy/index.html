RISK-04 · Trend Following

Trailing Stop Strategy

The strategy enters above a closing-price moving average. Its trailing stop uses the highest close since entry and an ATR approximation based on absolute changes between successive closes.

Rules

Entry

When flat, go long if the close is above the entry SMA. Initialize the stop using the entry close.

Exit

Go to cash if the close is below the updated stop.

Indicators

Entry SMA from 20 closes. The range proxy is the 14-day simple average of absolute close-to-close price changes.
Stop: the maximum of the previous stop and highest close since entry minus 2.5 times the range proxy.

Inputs

Default: atr_period = 14, multiplier = 2.5, entry_ma = 20.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
