Rules

Entry

Target1 when momentum>0.

Exit

Target0 when momentum≤0 or unavailable.

Indicators

Momentum=close/close252 observations earlier−1.

Inputs

Fixed lookback252; config does not alter this rule.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
