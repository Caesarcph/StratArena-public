Rules

Inputs

FastMA = 50
SlowMA = 200
Risk = 1%

Indicators

SMA(50)
SMA(200)
ATR(14)

Entry

Go long when SMA(50) > SMA(200)

Exit

Exit when SMA(50) < SMA(200)

Position sizing

Position = Risk / ATR

Risk controls

Hold cash when volatility spikes

Notes

  • Best in long trends
  • Late exits in sharp reversals
