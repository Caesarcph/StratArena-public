What the backtest shows

  • Beat buy-and-hold on 3 of 10 markets by annual return over 5 years.
  • Had a smaller maximum drawdown than buy-and-hold on 8 of 10 markets.
  • Best market by Sharpe ratio: GLD (Sharpe 0.76, CAGR 12.1%).
  • Weakest market: EURUSD=X (Sharpe -0.21, CAGR -1.1%).
