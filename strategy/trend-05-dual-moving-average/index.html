Dual Moving Average

This fixed two-average rule sets a fully long target whenever the 50-day simple moving average exceeds the 200-day average. All other bars target cash.

Rules

Entry

Set the target to 100% long while the 50-day average exceeds the 200-day average.

Exit

Use cash when the averages are equal, the fast average is below the slow average or the averages are unavailable.

Indicators

Calculate simple moving averages of the daily close over both windows.

Inputs

Fixed closing-price SMA windows of 50 and 200 days.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
