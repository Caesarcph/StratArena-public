Rules

Entry

When flat, z below −2 opens long SPY and short GLD. z above +2 opens short SPY and long GLD.

Exit

Close when absolute z is at or below the exit band or the holding count reaches 20. A default zero-width band requires z to equal zero. A sign change can leave the trade open.

Indicators

Compute log(SPY) − log(GLD) and standardize it using its rolling sample mean and standard deviation.

Inputs

Default lookback 60 sessions, entry z-score 2, exit band 0 and maximum holding count 20 observations.

Position sizing

Each leg is 50% of equity in opposite directions, for zero net dollar exposure and 100% gross.
