STAT-10

Dynamic Hedge Ratio Pairs

A one-coefficient Kalman filter estimates SPY's relationship to GLD in log prices. The strategy standardizes the fitted residual and updates the hedge weight while a trade is open.

Rules

Entry

After the 110-row warmup, z below −1.5 opens a long residual. z above +1.5 opens a short residual.

Exit

On valid signal rows, exit the long when z is above −0.3, or exit the short when z is below +0.3. The 20-observation holding limit also closes the position on a valid signal row.

Indicators

After updating beta each session, compute log(SPY) − beta × log(GLD) and use the 60-session sample mean and standard deviation to calculate its z-score.

Inputs

Default process noise Q 0.001, measurement noise R 1, z-score window 60, entry z-score 1.5, exit z-score 0.3 and holding limit 20 observations.

Position sizing

Normalize the signed weights [1, −beta] for long or [−1, beta] for short to 100% gross exposure.

Notes

  • The site backtest aligns SPY, GLD, USO, EURUSD=X, DBC, SLV, DBA, UNG, BTC-USD and ETH-USD on their common completed sessions. Its SPY display label identifies the basket.
  • The rule trades the first two aligned columns, SPY and GLD in the site configuration. The eight other inputs affect the common calendar and carry zero target weights.
  • Daily targets apply on the next common return interval. A missing z-score or hedge ratio carries the previous target.
