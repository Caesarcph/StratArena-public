REG-01 · Regime Detection

Regime Switch

The close must exceed its 200-day simple moving average and annualized 60-day return volatility must be below 25%. The model moves between full long exposure and cash.

Rules

Entry

Go long when the close is above the SMA and annualized volatility is below 25%.

Exit

Go to cash whenever either condition fails.

Indicators

200-day closing-price SMA.
60-day sample standard deviation of daily returns multiplied by √252.

Inputs

Fixed: trend SMA = 200 days, volatility lookback = 60 days, volatility threshold = 25% annualized.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
