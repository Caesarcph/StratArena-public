RISK-01

Drawdown Control

The rule measures the tested instrument's drawdown from its rolling peak. The daily long target decreases linearly above the drawdown threshold, subject to a minimum allocation.

Rules

Entry

At d ≤ max_drawdown set the long target to 100%. Start the first bar fully long.

Exit

At d > max_drawdown use max(min_position, 1 − (d − max_drawdown) × scale / max_drawdown). Recompute as the close or rolling high changes.

Indicators

Drawdown d is the magnitude of the close's relative shortfall from the rolling maximum close. Early windows use the available closes.

Inputs

max_drawdown = 0.10, scale = 0.5, lookback = 252, min_position = 0.10.

Position sizing

Long allocation ranges from 10% to 100% with the defaults.
