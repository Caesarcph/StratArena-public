Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average

Perry Kaufman's Adaptive Moving Average (KAMA). Uses Efficiency Ratio to dynamically adjust smoothing. Fast in trends, slow in chop. Reduces whipsaws compared to fixed MAs.

Rules

Inputs

ER_Period = 10
Fast = 2
Slow = 30

Indicators

Efficiency Ratio = |Price Change| / Sum of |Daily Changes|
Smoothing Constant = (ER * (Fast - Slow) + Slow)^2
KAMA = KAMA[-1] + SC * (Price - KAMA[-1])

Entry

Buy when Price > KAMA and KAMA rising

Exit

Sell when Price < KAMA

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Adaptive nature reduces false signals

Notes

  • Perry Kaufman's Trading Systems and Methods
  • Efficiency Ratio measures trend vs noise
  • ER = 1 means perfect trend, ER = 0 means pure noise
