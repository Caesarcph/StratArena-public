Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average

The smoothing rate adapts to the ratio of absolute net price change to the total absolute change. Price relative to KAMA and the average's five-bar slope drive the target.

Rules

Entry

After er_period + slope_period bars, set long when the close exceeds KAMA and KAMA is higher than slope_period bars before.

Exit

Set cash when price is at or below KAMA. Otherwise, if price is above but the slope is nonpositive, carry the prior target.

Indicators

ER is absolute price change over er_period divided by the sum of absolute changes. A zero denominator gives ER = 0.
Let f = 2/(fast + 1), s = 2/(slow + 1). Add [ER × (f − s) + s]² times (current close − previous KAMA) to the previous KAMA.

Inputs

er_period = 10, fast = 2, slow = 30, slope_period = 5.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
