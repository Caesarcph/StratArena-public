Rules

Entry

When flat, enter if z<−entry_z.

Exit

When held, exit if z>−exit_z; otherwise retain the position.

Indicators

After 20 observations, Q adapts to the population standard deviation of the prior 20 log returns. Divide price-minus-estimate by the 20-row residual sample standard deviation, floored at 1e-8.

Inputs

Q=.001, R=1, entry_z=2, exit_z=.5;20-observation warmup.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
