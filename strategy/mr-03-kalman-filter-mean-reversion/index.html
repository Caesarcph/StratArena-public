MR-03 · Mean Reversion

Kalman Filter Mean Reversion

Uses Kalman Filter to dynamically estimate the 'fair value' of an asset. Enters long when price deviates significantly below the estimated value, expecting reversion. The filter adapts to changing market conditions.

Rules

Entry

Buy when z-score < -Entry_Z (price undervalued)

Exit

Exit when |z-score| < Exit_Z

Indicators

Kalman state estimate
Standardized residual (z-score)

Inputs

Q = 0.001 (process noise)
R = 1.0 (observation noise)
Entry_Z = 2.0
Exit_Z = 0.5

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Adaptive Q based on recent volatility
