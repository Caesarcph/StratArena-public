MOM-06 · Momentum

Residual Momentum

Residual Momentum is the historical label. This single-series rule does not load SPY or estimate beta: it subtracts a 60-observation mean from each return, then sums earlier demeaned returns.

Rules

Entry

Long when the residual sum is positive and indicator history is available.

Exit

Cash when the sum is nonpositive or unavailable.

Indicators

r=one-row close return; residual=r−SMA60(r). Sum momentum_lookback−skip_period residuals after shifting them skip_period rows.

Inputs

momentum_lookback=252, skip_period=21; trailing mean fixed60; beta_window=252 is accepted but unused.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
