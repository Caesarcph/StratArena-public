MOM-06 · Momentum

Residual Momentum

Based on Blitz et al. (2011). Calculates momentum from residual returns after regressing out market factor (SPY). Captures 'pure' idiosyncratic momentum rather than beta-driven returns.

Rules

Inputs

Beta_Window = 252
Momentum_Lookback = 252
Skip_Period = 21

Indicators

Rolling beta to SPY
Residual return = Actual - Beta * Market
Cumulative residual momentum

Entry

Buy when residual momentum > 0 (positive alpha)

Exit

Exit when residual momentum <= 0

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Skip most recent month to avoid reversal

Notes

  • Based on Blitz, Huij & Martens (2011) research
  • Removes systematic risk, captures idiosyncratic momentum
  • Uses simplified CAPM residual (SPY as market proxy)
