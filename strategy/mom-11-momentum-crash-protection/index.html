Rules

Entry

After the 272-row warmup, positive momentum and baseline drawdown of 10% or less set the target to 100%.

Exit

Go to cash if current momentum is zero or negative, or if baseline drawdown is greater than 20%.

Indicators

Accumulate the instrument's daily returns on rows where prior momentum was positive. Measure drawdown from that baseline equity curve's highest level so far.

Inputs

Default momentum lookback 252 sessions, first drawdown threshold 10%, severe threshold 20% and lower exposure at the severe threshold 50%.

Position sizing

For positive momentum and a drawdown above 10% through 20%, scale linearly from 100% to 50%.
