MOM-11 · Momentum

Momentum Crash Protection

Follows momentum but monitors the momentum factor's own drawdown. When momentum is experiencing a crash (reversal), reduces position size to protect against momentum crashes that historically cause severe losses.

Rules

Entry

Momentum positive
Scale by momentum drawdown

Exit

Momentum negative or severe drawdown

Indicators

12-month momentum
Momentum factor drawdown
Rolling high of momentum signal

Inputs

Mom_Lookback = 252
DD_Threshold = 0.10
Scale_Factor = 0.5

Position sizing

Full position if no drawdown
Scaled if momentum crashing

Risk controls

Exit completely if drawdown > 20%
