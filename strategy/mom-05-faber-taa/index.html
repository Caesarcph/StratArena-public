Rules

Entry

At a monthly decision, activate an asset's sleeve if its latest completed close is above its SMA.

Exit

Move the sleeve to cash when the close is at or below its SMA.

Indicators

Take the simple moving average of each asset's closing prices.

Inputs

Default SMA window 200 trading sessions, used as a daily-data approximation of the 10-month trend rule.

Position sizing

The four assets get one 25% sleeve each. When one asset qualifies, 25% is invested and the other 75% is cash.
