MOM-05 · Trend Following

Faber TAA

Meb Faber's Tactical Asset Allocation from 2007 paper. Simple rule: hold an asset only when price is above its 10-month (200-day) moving average. Equal weight across all assets in uptrend.

Rules

Inputs

MA_Period = 200 (10 months)

Indicators

200-day SMA for each asset

Entry

Hold asset if Price > SMA(200)

Exit

Exit asset if Price <= SMA(200)

Position sizing

Equal weight across assets in uptrend

Risk controls

Full cash position when all assets below MA

Notes

  • From Meb Faber's 2007 SSRN paper
  • Extremely simple trend-following rule
  • Monthly rebalancing for practical implementation
