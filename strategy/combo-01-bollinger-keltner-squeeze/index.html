Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze

Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze is the historical name. This implementation detects a squeeze from Bollinger bandwidth relative to its own history and waits for positive price momentum before going long.

Rules

Entry

Go long when a squeeze has been recorded, the current bar is out of the squeeze and momentum is positive.

Exit

Exit when momentum is negative, the close is more than 5% below the entry close or the holding count exceeds 20 bars.

Indicators

The Bollinger bands are the 20-bar closing-price SMA plus or minus 2 sample standard deviations. Divide their difference by the middle band plus a small offset to get bandwidth.
A squeeze occurs at or below the current 50-bar 25th percentile of bandwidth. Momentum is close minus its 12-bar simple average.

Inputs

Default: bb_period = 20, bb_std = 2, momentum_period = 12, squeeze_lookback = 50, squeeze_percentile = 25, max_hold = 20.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
