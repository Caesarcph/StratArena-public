Bollinger-Keltner Squeeze

When Bollinger Bands contract inside Keltner Channel, volatility is compressed (squeeze). When squeeze releases, trade in direction of momentum. Captures explosive moves.

Rules

Inputs

BB_Period = 20
BB_Std = 2.0
KC_Period = 20
KC_Mult = 1.5

Indicators

Bollinger Bands = SMA ± 2*StdDev
Keltner Channel = EMA ± 1.5*ATR
Squeeze = BB inside KC
Momentum = Price - Average(High+Low+Close)/3

Entry

Squeeze releases (BB outside KC)
Buy if momentum positive, sell if negative

Exit

Opposite signal or momentum fades

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Wait for confirmed squeeze release

Notes

  • John Carter's TTM Squeeze concept
  • Low volatility precedes high volatility
  • Squeeze duration indicates potential move size
