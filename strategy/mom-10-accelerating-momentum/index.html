Accelerating Momentum

Combines momentum across multiple timeframes (1M, 3M, 6M, 12M) with higher weights on recent periods. Captures accelerating trends where short-term momentum aligns with longer-term direction.

Rules

Inputs

Indicators

Weighted composite score

Entry

Composite score > 0
All periods positive (confirmation)

Exit

Composite score < 0
Or short-term momentum turns negative

Position sizing

Based on momentum strength

Risk controls

Reduce position if acceleration slows

Notes

  • Short-term momentum predicts near-term returns
  • Multi-period confirmation reduces whipsaws
  • Weights emphasize recent performance
