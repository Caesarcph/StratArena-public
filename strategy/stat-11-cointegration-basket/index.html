Cointegration Basket

Cointegration Basket is the strategy name. The rule fits a rolling OLS regression of SPY against the rest of the configured basket and trades standardized one-step residuals.

Rules

Entry

After the 130-row warmup and with a valid residual window, z below −2 opens a long residual and z above +2 opens a short residual.

Exit

On valid score rows, exit the long when z is above −0.5, or exit the short when z is below +0.5. The 20-observation holding limit also closes a trade on a valid score row.

Indicators

On each eligible day fit OLS to the preceding 60 completed log-price rows. Evaluate the fit on the current row and standardize its residual against the rolling sample of real one-step residuals.

Inputs

Default regression lookback 60 sessions, z-score lookback 60 earlier residuals plus the current residual, entry z-score 2, exit z-score 0.5 and holding limit 20 observations.

Position sizing

For a long residual, take +1 in the target and the negative of each fitted coefficient in the other assets. Reverse the signs for a short residual and scale to 100% gross.

Notes

  • The site backtest aligns SPY, GLD, USO, EURUSD=X, DBC, SLV, DBA, UNG, BTC-USD and ETH-USD on their common completed sessions. Its SPY display label identifies the basket.
  • The published rule fits OLS with an intercept and has no formal stationarity test.
  • Signal fits update daily. The hedge weights of an open trade remain at their entry values, and daily targets take effect on the next common return interval.
