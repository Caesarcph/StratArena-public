TREND-12 · Trend Following

MACD Strategy

Uses MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) line crossing the Signal line to identify trend changes. MACD above Signal indicates bullish momentum.

Rules

Entry

Buy when MACD crosses above Signal line

Exit

Sell when MACD crosses below Signal line

Indicators

MACD = EMA(12) - EMA(26)
Signal Line = EMA(MACD, 9)
Histogram = MACD - Signal

Inputs

Fast = 12
Slow = 26
Signal = 9

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

None - pure trend following
