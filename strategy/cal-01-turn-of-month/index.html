CAL-01

Turn of Month

Turn of Month effect: stocks tend to perform better around month boundaries. Hold during last N days of month and first M days of next month. Cash otherwise.

What the backtest shows

  • Beat buy-and-hold on 1 of 10 markets by annual return over 5 years.
  • Had a smaller maximum drawdown than buy-and-hold on 8 of 10 markets.
  • Best market by Sharpe ratio: GLD (Sharpe 0.63, CAGR 6.5%).
  • Weakest market: EURUSD=X (Sharpe -0.53, CAGR -2.4%).

Rules

Inputs

End_Days = 3
Start_Days = 3

Indicators

Trading day of month
Days until month end

Entry

Hold last 3 trading days of month
Hold first 3 trading days of new month

Exit

Exit outside turn-of-month window

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Simple calendar-based rule

Notes

  • Institutional rebalancing at month-end
  • Payroll and pension fund flows
  • Well-documented calendar anomaly
