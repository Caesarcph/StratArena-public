CAL-01

Turn of Month

Long in the first/last configured weekday-count windows of each calendar month. The Monday-Friday calendar is known without future price rows; it is an approximation, not an exchange holiday calendar.

Rules

Entry

Hold when at most Start_Days calendar weekdays have elapsed
Hold when fewer than End_Days calendar weekdays remain

Exit

Exit outside turn-of-month window

Indicators

Elapsed/remaining Monday-Friday calendar weekdays

Inputs

End_Days = 3
Start_Days = 3

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Simple calendar-based rule

Notes

  • Version 2 uses a fixed Monday-Friday calendar and ignores exchange holidays; weekend observations can stay inside a contiguous month-turn window.
  • The earlier version counted future rows in the downloaded month and could revise earlier targets as new observations arrived.
  • Institutional rebalancing at month-end
  • Payroll and pension fund flows
  • Well-documented calendar anomaly
