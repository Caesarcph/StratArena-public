Trend + Mean Reversion Hybrid

The trend filter gates entry and exits holdings when the close reaches or falls below the long average. A recovery threshold and time limit also close positions.

Rules

Entry

After 200 rows, close>SMA200 AND RSI<35.

Exit

RSI>50, close≤SMA200, or holding age≥20 observations.

Indicators

SMA200 of closes; RSI14 from simple mean gains/losses with denominator offset1e-10.

Inputs

trend_ma=200, rsi_period=14, rsi_oversold=35, rsi_exit=50, max_hold=20.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
