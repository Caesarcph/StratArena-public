COMBO-04 · Trend Following

Trend + Mean Reversion Hybrid

Combines trend following with mean reversion. First confirms long-term trend direction, then enters on short-term oversold pullbacks within that trend. Captures trend moves at better entry points.

Rules

Entry

Price > 200 MA (uptrend)
AND RSI < 35 (pullback)

Exit

RSI > 50 (recovered)
OR Price < 200 MA (trend break)

Indicators

Long-term MA for trend
RSI for mean reversion

Inputs

Trend_MA = 200
Short_MA = 20
RSI_Period = 14
RSI_Oversold = 35

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Only trade in direction of trend
