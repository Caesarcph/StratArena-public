52-Week High Momentum

Based on George & Hwang (2004) research. Stocks near their 52-week highs tend to outperform due to investor underreaction (anchoring bias). Enter when price reaches 95% of 52-week high, exit when falling below 85%.

Rules

Inputs

Lookback = 252 (52 weeks)
Entry_Threshold = 0.95
Exit_Threshold = 0.85

Indicators

52-week high
High ratio = Price / 52W High

Entry

Buy when high ratio >= 0.95 (near 52W high)

Exit

Exit when high ratio < 0.85

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Exit on significant pullback from high

Notes

  • Anchoring bias: investors underreact to prices near historical highs
  • George & Hwang (2004) academic research
  • Works best in uptrending markets
