52-Week High Momentum

The52-week label uses252 observations of closing prices, not calendar weeks or intraday highs. Entry and exit thresholds create a holding band.

Rules

Entry

After 252 rows, when flat enter if ratio≥.95.

Exit

When held, exit if ratio<.85; otherwise keep holding.

Indicators

Ratio=current close/maximum close over the current252-row window.

Inputs

lookback=252, entry_threshold=.95, exit_threshold=.85.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
