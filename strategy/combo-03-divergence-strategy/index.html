COMBO-03

Divergence Strategy

A higher price low with a lower nearby RSI/MACD low enters long; a lower price high with a higher nearby indicator high exits. Pivots require order observations on each side; targets change only at confirmation and persist between events.

What the backtest shows

  • Beat buy-and-hold on 2 of 10 markets by annual return over 5 years.
  • Had a smaller maximum drawdown than buy-and-hold on 8 of 10 markets.
  • Best market by Sharpe ratio: DBC (Sharpe 0.81, CAGR 9.5%).
  • Weakest market: ETH-USD (Sharpe -0.34, CAGR -22.0%).
  • Few or no trades in the window on GLD, USO, EURUSD=X, DBA, UNG, BTC-USD — treat those rows as a sign the rules rarely triggered, not as an edge.

Notes

  • Version 2 preserves the implemented hidden-divergence direction, rather than the old regular-divergence description.
  • At confirmation, the latest pivot must be within lookback observations and the preceding pivot within 2 * lookback; lookback must exceed order for events.
  • RSI uses simple rolling means; MACD uses indicator_period for its fast EMA and 26 for its slow EMA.
  • Version 1 revised historical signals and did not hold between events; its results are not comparable to version 2.
