Rules

Entry

After atr_period + 3 bars, initialize as an uptrend if the close exceeds (high + low)/2, else as a downtrend. Later a close above the upper trailing band turns a downtrend upward.

Exit

A close below the lower trailing band turns an uptrend downward. Otherwise preserve direction.

Indicators

ATR is an EMA of OHLC true range with span atr_period. Raw bands are (high + low)/2 ± multiplier × ATR.
Trail or reset the bands with the previous trailing bands and previous close.

Inputs

atr_period = 10, multiplier = 2.5, allow_short = false.

Position sizing

100% long or cash by default. Optional short target −100%.
