MR-12 · Mean Reversion

Money Flow Index Reversion

Uses Money Flow Index (volume-weighted RSI) to identify oversold conditions. MFI < 20 indicates oversold with volume confirmation. Exit when MFI recovers to neutral.

Rules

Inputs

Period = 14
Oversold = 30
Exit = 50

Indicators

Typical Price = (High + Low + Close) / 3
Raw Money Flow = Typical Price * Volume
MFI = 100 - 100 / (1 + Positive MF / Negative MF)

Entry

Buy when MFI < 30 (oversold)

Exit

Exit when MFI > 50 (neutral)

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Max hold period of 15 days

Notes

  • Also known as volume-weighted RSI
  • Combines price and volume for stronger signals
  • More reliable than pure price oscillators
