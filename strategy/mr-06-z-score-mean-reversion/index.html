MR-06 · Mean Reversion

Z-Score Mean Reversion

Basic mean reversion using price z-score with dual filters: volatility must be in reasonable range and price must be near 200-day MA (no strong trend). Trades only when conditions favor mean reversion.

What the backtest shows

  • Beat buy-and-hold on 4 of 10 markets by annual return over 5 years.
  • Had a smaller maximum drawdown than buy-and-hold on 10 of 10 markets.
  • Best market by Sharpe ratio: SPY (Sharpe 0.89, CAGR 8.0%).
  • Weakest market: ETH-USD (Sharpe -0.06, CAGR -2.1%).
  • Few or no trades in the window on UNG — treat those rows as a sign the rules rarely triggered, not as an edge.

Rules

Entry

Buy when Z < -Entry_Z
Volatility between 10%-50% annualized
Price within 10% of 200-day MA

Exit

Exit when |Z| < Exit_Z

Indicators

Z-Score = (Close - SMA) / StdDev
Annualized Volatility
200-day MA distance

Lookback = 20
Entry_Z = 2.0
Exit_Z = 0.5
