Keltner Channel Breakout

The channel centers on a closing-price EMA and uses a simple average of OHLC true range for its width. Outside-band signals take precedence over midline exits.

Rules

Entry

Close above the upper band sets the long target. Below the lower band, set a short target if allow_short is enabled or go flat otherwise.

Exit

Within the outer bands, exit a long below the EMA and exit a short above it. At the EMA the prior target carries forward.

Indicators

Middle = EMA of closes. ATR = simple moving average of true range. Upper and lower bands = middle ± multiplier × ATR.

Inputs

ema_period = 20, atr_period = 20, multiplier = 2, allow_short = false.

Position sizing

100% long or cash by default. Optional short target −100%.
