TREND-16 · Trend Following

Aroon Indicator

Aroon indicator measures how long since the highest high and lowest low. Aroon Up > 70 with Aroon Down < 30 indicates strong uptrend. Captures trend emergence and strength.

Rules

Inputs

Period = 25
Up_Threshold = 70
Down_Threshold = 30

Indicators

Aroon Up = ((Period - Days since highest high) / Period) * 100
Aroon Down = ((Period - Days since lowest low) / Period) * 100

Entry

Buy when Aroon Up > 70 and Aroon Down < 30

Exit

Sell when Aroon Up < 50 or Aroon Down > 50

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Exit on trend weakness

Notes

  • Developed by Tushar Chande in 1995
  • Name means 'dawn's early light' in Sanskrit
  • Measures trend strength and potential reversals
