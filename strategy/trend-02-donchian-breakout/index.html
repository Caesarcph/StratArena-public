Donchian Breakout

A close-based Donchian rule with fixed windows. A breakout above the long window establishes full long exposure. A break below the short window moves to cash.

Rules

Entry

Go long when the close strictly exceeds the upper channel.

Exit

Go to cash when the close is strictly below the lower channel.

Indicators

Highest close of the previous 55 sessions and lowest close of the previous 20 sessions.

Inputs

Fixed: breakout lookback = 55, exit lookback = 20.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
