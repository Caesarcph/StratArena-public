Rules

Entry

If close > DonchianHigh then enter long

Exit

If close < DonchianLow then exit

Indicators

DonchianHigh
DonchianLow
ATR(20)

Inputs

BreakoutLookback = 55
ExitLookback = 20
Risk = 1%

Position sizing

Position = Risk / ATR

Risk controls

Max 3 concurrent trades
Trailing stop at 2x ATR
