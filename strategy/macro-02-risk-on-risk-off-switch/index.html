Risk-On/Risk-Off Switch

Constructs a composite risk sentiment indicator from multiple signals (volatility, momentum, correlation). Risk-On regime favors equities and commodities. Risk-Off regime shifts to gold and reduces exposure. Dynamic allocation based on regime probability.
