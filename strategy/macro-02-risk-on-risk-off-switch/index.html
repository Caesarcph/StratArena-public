Rules

Entry

After the 262-row warmup, an average above 0.3 sets 100% to the risk group. An average below −0.3 sets 100% to GLD.

Exit

Recalculate daily. Scores between those thresholds, including equality, split 50% risk and 50% GLD.

Indicators

Volatility percentile below 30 adds 1 and above 70 subtracts 1. A close above the SMA adds 1 and otherwise subtracts 1.
Momentum above 0 adds 1 and below −5% subtracts 1. Average the available component scores.

Inputs

Default volatility lookback 20 daily returns, volatility percentile lookback 252 estimates, trend SMA 50 sessions and momentum lookback 63 sessions.

Position sizing

Divide the risk allocation equally among its nine members.

Notes

  • The site backtest aligns SPY, GLD, USO, EURUSD=X, DBC, SLV, DBA, UNG, BTC-USD and ETH-USD on their common completed sessions. Its SPY display label identifies the basket.
  • The risk group consists of every configured asset except GLD. The published results apply targets to the next common return interval.
  • The legacy vol_threshold parameter has no effect. Volatility enters the score through its percentile.
