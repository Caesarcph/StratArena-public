MR-11 · Mean Reversion

Williams %R Reversion

Uses rolling closing-price extrema instead of OHLC highs and lows. The exit threshold is the overbought zone, not neutral.

Rules

Entry

After 14 observations, enter when R<−80.

Exit

Exit when R>−20 or holding age≥20 observations.

Indicators

R=−100×(highestClose−close)/(highestClose−lowestClose+1e-10).

Inputs

period=14, oversold=−80, overbought=−20, max_hold=20.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
