MR-11 · Mean Reversion

Williams %R Reversion

Uses Williams %R oscillator to identify oversold conditions. %R ranges from -100 to 0. Enter long when %R < -80 (oversold), exit when %R > -20 (overbought). Classic momentum oscillator reversion.

Rules

Inputs

Period = 14
Oversold = -80
Overbought = -20

Indicators

%R = (Highest High - Close) / (Highest High - Lowest Low) * -100

Entry

Buy when %R < -80 (oversold)

Exit

Exit when %R > -20 (overbought)

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Max hold period of 20 days

Notes

  • Developed by Larry Williams
  • Similar to Stochastic but inverted scale
  • Ranges from -100 (oversold) to 0 (overbought)
