MACD + RSI Filter

A bullish crossover below the RSI overbought level or an already-bullish MACD with oversold RSI can enter. A bearish crossover exits without an oversold veto.

Rules

Entry

After 40 rows: bullish crossover AND RSI<70, OR MACD already above signal AND RSI<30.

Exit

Bearish crossover, RSI>80, or MACD not above signal while RSI>70.

Indicators

MACD uses recursive EMA12−EMA26 and EMA9 signal. RSI uses EMA span14 of gains/losses with denominator offset1e-10.

Inputs

macd_fast=12, macd_slow=26, macd_signal=9, rsi_period=14, rsi_overbought=70, rsi_oversold=30, rsi_extreme=80.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
