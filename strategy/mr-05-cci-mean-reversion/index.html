Rules

Entry

When flat, enter if CCI<−100.

Exit

While held, exit if CCI≥0, holding age≥15 observations, or CCI<−200.

Indicators

CCI=(close−SMA20)/(.015×20-row mean absolute deviation from that window mean).

Inputs

period=20, oversold=−100, exit_level=0, max_hold=15, extreme_stop=−200.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
