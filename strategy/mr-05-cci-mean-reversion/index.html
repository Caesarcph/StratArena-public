MR-05 · Mean Reversion

CCI Mean Reversion

Uses Commodity Channel Index to identify oversold conditions. Enters long when CCI falls below -100 (oversold) and exits when CCI returns to neutral (0). Includes max holding period and extreme stop.

Rules

Entry

Buy when CCI < -100 (oversold)

Exit

Exit when CCI >= 0
Or max holding period reached
Or CCI < -200 (extreme stop)

Indicators

Typical Price = (H+L+C)/3
CCI = (TP - SMA) / (0.015 * Mean Deviation)

Inputs

Period = 20
Oversold = -100
Exit_Level = 0
Max_Hold = 15

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Stop at CCI < -200
Max hold 15 days

Strategy evidence

Evidence is not available for this strategy in the current release. A displayed backtest is not a verified evidence bundle.

Notes

  • Donald Lambert's 1980 indicator
  • Constant 0.015 ensures ~70-80% of CCI values fall within [-100, +100]
  • Works best in sideways markets
