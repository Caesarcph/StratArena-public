MR-07 · Mean Reversion

Hurst-Filtered Mean Reversion

Uses Hurst exponent to detect mean-reverting regimes (H < 0.5) before applying z-score mean reversion. Only trades when statistical evidence supports mean reversion, avoiding trend-following periods.

Rules

Entry

Only trade when H < 0.55 (mean-reverting)
Buy when Z < -1.5

Exit

Exit when Z > -0.3
Or when trending and Z > 0
