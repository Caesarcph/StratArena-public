TREND-18 · Trend Following

Coppock Curve

Coppock Curve is a weighted moving average of two rate-of-change series. It is designed as a long-term trend indicator that turns positive near major bottoms.

Rules

Entry

Go long when Coppock > 0

Exit

Exit when Coppock < 0

Indicators

ROC1 = (P / P[-ROC1]) - 1
ROC2 = (P / P[-ROC2]) - 1
Coppock = WMA(ROC1 + ROC2, WMA)

Inputs

ROC1 = 14
ROC2 = 11
WMA = 10

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation
