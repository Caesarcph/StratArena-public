TREND-15 · Trend Following

Darvas Box

This Darvas-style rule uses a rolling OHLC price box. Its upper and lower limits are the highest high and lowest low from the prior complete window.

Rules

Entry

Go long if the close strictly exceeds the previous box high.

Exit

Go flat if the close is strictly below the previous box low, otherwise keep the prior target.

Indicators

Build the box from the previous box_period highs and lows. The current bar is outside the calculation window.

Inputs

box_period = 20.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
