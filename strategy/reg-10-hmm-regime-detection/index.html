REG-10 · Regime Detection

HMM Regime Detection

Hidden Markov Model identifies latent market states (bull/bear). Uses Gaussian emissions and Baum-Welch for parameter estimation. Trades based on inferred regime.

Rules

Inputs

Indicators

Returns as observations
HMM with Gaussian emissions
Infer most likely state sequence

Entry

Buy when in bullish regime (higher mean state)

Exit

Sell when in bearish regime

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Regime probability threshold

Notes

  • Simplified HMM without external libraries
  • Uses rolling window for regime detection
  • Bull regime = positive mean returns, Bear = negative
