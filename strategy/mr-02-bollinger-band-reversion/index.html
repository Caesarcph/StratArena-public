MR-02 · Mean Reversion

Bollinger Band Reversion

Classic mean reversion using Bollinger Bands. Enters long when price touches the lower band (oversold) and exits when price reverts to the middle band. Includes bandwidth filter to trade only during volatility expansion.

Rules

Inputs

Period = 20
Num_Std = 2.0
Max_Hold = 10

Indicators

SMA(20)
StdDev(20)
Upper/Lower Bands
Bandwidth

Entry

Buy when Close <= Lower Band and Bandwidth expanding

Exit

Exit when Close >= Middle Band
Or max holding period reached

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Stop loss at 3% below lower band
Max hold 10 days

Notes

  • Based on John Bollinger's 1980s indicator
  • Assumes ~95% of prices fall within 2 standard deviations
  • Best in sideways markets, underperforms in strong trends
