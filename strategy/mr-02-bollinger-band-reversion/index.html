MR-02 · Mean Reversion

Bollinger Band Reversion

Uses closing-price Bollinger bands and a bandwidth filter. The stop follows the current lower band; it is not a fixed loss from the entry price.

Rules

Entry

Close≤lower band AND bandwidth>its moving average.

Exit

Close≥middle, holding age≥10 observations, or close<0.97×current lower band.

Indicators

SMA20 ±2 sample standard deviations; bandwidth=(upper−lower)/middle, compared with its 20-row SMA.

Inputs

period=20, num_std=2, max_hold=10; decisions start at row40 with defaults.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
