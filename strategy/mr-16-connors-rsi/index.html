MR-16 · Mean Reversion

Connors RSI

Connors RSI combines three components: RSI of price, RSI of streak duration, and Percentile Rank of returns. It is designed to be more robust and responsive than standard RSI. Enters long when CRSI is extremely low and exits when it recovers.

Rules

Entry

Buy when CRSI < 10

Exit

Exit when CRSI > 50 or holding days >= 10

Indicators

Price RSI(3)
Streak RSI(2)
Percent Rank(100)
CRSI = (Price RSI + Streak RSI + Rank) / 3

Inputs

Price_RSI_Period = 3
Streak_RSI_Period = 2
Rank_Period = 100
Entry_Threshold = 10
Exit_Threshold = 50
Max_Hold = 10

Risk controls

None (time-based exit)
