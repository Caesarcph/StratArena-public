MR-16 · Mean Reversion

Connors RSI

This Connors-style variant averages price RSI, signed-streak RSI and return rank. Corrected RSI maps positive gains without losses to100, losses without gains to0, and flat or unavailable windows to50.

Rules

Entry

After 100 observations, enter when CRSI<10.

Exit

Exit when CRSI>50 or holding age≥10 observations.

Indicators

Both RSIs use simple rolling mean gains/losses. Rank is the current one-row return within 100 returns including itself, with average ranks for ties. CRSI averages the three components.

Inputs

price_rsi_period=3, streak_rsi_period=2, rank_period=100, entry_threshold=10, exit_threshold=50, max_hold=10.

Position sizing

100% long or cash.
