2011 gold peak and bubble burst

Gold set a record in early September on fears about sovereign debt and currency debasement. It then slid into year-end as the dollar firmed and the crisis-driven bid faded.

Dates checked against the price data: researched window 2011-09-05 to 2011-12-30; measured 2011-08-22 to 2011-12-29 (start shifted by -14 days, end by -1 days).
