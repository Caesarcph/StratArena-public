April 2013 gold crash

Gold lost its footing in April with a very heavy one-day drop and kept sliding to multi-year lows, with heavy ETF selling. A firmer dollar, better US data and forced liquidations drove it.

Dates checked against the price data: researched window 2013-04-11 to 2013-04-16; measured 2013-03-21 to 2013-04-15 (start shifted by -21 days, end by -1 days).
