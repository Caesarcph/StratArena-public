Glossary

Win Rate

In Quant Arena: the share of trading days with a positive strategy return. Not the share of winning trades.

FormulaWin rate = days with return > 0 / all days in the window

Read this before comparing

Because days spent in cash have a zero return, they count as non-winning days. A strategy that is invested only 40% of the time will therefore show a low win rate even if its trades are very good. Use it to compare strategies with similar exposure, and look at the profit factor and the drawdown at the same time.

Win rate is not profitability

Trend-following systems often win on fewer than half of their days or trades and still make money through a few large gains. Martingale and grid systems show the opposite pattern: very high win rates until one loss wipes out months of gains.
