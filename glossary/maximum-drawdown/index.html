Glossary

Maximum Drawdown

The largest fall from a previous peak in the equity curve. The single best measure of how painful a strategy was to hold.

FormulaMax drawdown = min over t of (Equity_t / max(Equity_0..t) − 1)

Why it matters

A 50% drawdown needs a 100% gain to recover. Most people abandon a strategy during a deep drawdown, so a system with a slightly lower return and a much smaller drawdown is often the one that actually gets followed.

Read it with duration

Depth is only half of the story. A 20% drawdown that recovers in two months feels very different from one that lasts three years. The drawdown chart on every strategy page shows both depth and how long the equity stayed under water.
