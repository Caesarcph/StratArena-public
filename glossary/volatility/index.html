Glossary

Annualized Volatility

How much daily returns swing around their average, scaled to a yearly figure.

FormulaVolatility = std(daily return) × √252

How to read it

A US equity index typically runs at 15–20% annualized volatility; bitcoin often runs above 60%. Timing strategies that spend part of the time in cash usually show lower volatility than buy-and-hold on the same asset, which is one of the main reasons they exist.
