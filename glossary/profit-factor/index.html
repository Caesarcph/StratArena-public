Profit Factor

Total gains divided by total losses. Above 1 means the strategy made more than it lost.

FormulaProfit factor = Σ positive daily returns / |Σ negative daily returns|

How Quant Arena calculates it

We use daily strategy returns rather than individual trades, so the number is comparable across strategies with very different trade counts. Values between 1.1 and 1.5 are typical for robust daily strategies; much higher values on a long history deserve a closer look for look-ahead bias or a very small sample.
