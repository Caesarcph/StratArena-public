趋势 + 均值回归混合

交易规则

Inputs

Trend_MA = 200
Short_MA = 20
RSI_Period = 14
RSI_Oversold = 35

Indicators

Long-term MA for trend
RSI for mean reversion

Entry

Price > 200 MA (uptrend)
AND RSI < 35 (pullback)

Exit

RSI > 50 (recovered)
OR Price < 200 MA (trend break)

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Only trade in direction of trend

备注

  • 上升趋势中逢低买入
  • 趋势过滤避免逆势操作
  • 入场点比纯趋势跟踪更好
