交易规则

Inputs

Max_Drawdown = 0.10
Scale = 0.5
Recovery_Threshold = 0.5

Indicators

Rolling maximum price
Current drawdown

Entry

Full position when drawdown < threshold
Reduce by scale factor when drawdown exceeds

Exit

Gradually restore as drawdown recovers

Position sizing

Dynamic based on drawdown

Risk controls

Hard drawdown limit
