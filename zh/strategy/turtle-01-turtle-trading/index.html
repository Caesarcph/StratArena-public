交易规则

Inputs

EntryLookback = 20
ExitLookback = 10
Risk = 1%

Indicators

DonchianHigh(20)
DonchianLow(10)
ATR(20)

Entry

Go long when close > prior 20-day high

Exit

Exit when close < prior 10-day low

Position sizing

Position = Risk / ATR (tracked externally)

Risk controls

Trade only after breakout confirmation

备注

  • 能抓住持续的趋势
  • 区间震荡中容易被来回打脸
