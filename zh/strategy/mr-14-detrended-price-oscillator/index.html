去趋势价格震荡指标

去趋势价格震荡指标（DPO）通过比较平移后的价格和它的均线，去掉较长期的趋势。DPO 深度为负时买入，回到 0 附近时离场。

交易规则

Inputs

Entry_Level = -2.0

Indicators

SMA(Period)
DPO = Close.shift(Period/2 + 1) - SMA

Entry

Go long when DPO% < Entry_Level

Exit

Exit when DPO% > Exit_Level

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Detrending reduces long-cycle bias
