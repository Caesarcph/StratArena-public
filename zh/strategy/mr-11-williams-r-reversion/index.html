交易规则

Inputs

Period = 14
Oversold = -80
Overbought = -20

Indicators

%R = (Highest High - Close) / (Highest High - Lowest Low) * -100

Entry

Buy when %R < -80 (oversold)

Exit

Exit when %R > -20 (overbought)

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Max hold period of 20 days

备注

  • 由 Larry Williams 提出
  • 和随机指标类似，但刻度是反的
  • 范围从 −100（超卖）到 0（超买）
