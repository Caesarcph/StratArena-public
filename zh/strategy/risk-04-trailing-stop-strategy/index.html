移动止损策略

使用随价格上移的 ATR 移动止损，止损位 = 最高价 − ATR 倍数。上涨趋势中锁定利润，同时给正常波动留出空间。

交易规则

Entry

Price > MA (trend confirmation)

Exit

Price < Trailing Stop (Highest High - ATR * Mult)

Indicators

ATR for stop distance
Trailing high
MA for entry

Inputs

ATR_Period = 14
Multiplier = 2.5
Entry_MA = 20

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Trailing stop protects gains
