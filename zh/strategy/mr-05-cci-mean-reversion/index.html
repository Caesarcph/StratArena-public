交易规则

Inputs

Period = 20
Oversold = -100
Exit_Level = 0
Max_Hold = 15

Indicators

Typical Price = (H+L+C)/3
CCI = (TP - SMA) / (0.015 * Mean Deviation)

Entry

Buy when CCI < -100 (oversold)

Exit

Exit when CCI >= 0
Or max holding period reached
Or CCI < -200 (extreme stop)

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Stop at CCI < -200
Max hold 15 days

备注

  • Donald Lambert 在 1980 年提出
  • 常数 0.015 让 70%–80% 的 CCI 值落在 [−100, +100] 之间
  • 横盘市场中效果最好
