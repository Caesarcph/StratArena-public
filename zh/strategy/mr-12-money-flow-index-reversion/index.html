交易规则

Entry

Buy when MFI < 30 (oversold)

Exit

Exit when MFI > 50 (neutral)

Indicators

Typical Price = (High + Low + Close) / 3
Raw Money Flow = Typical Price * Volume
MFI = 100 - 100 / (1 + Positive MF / Negative MF)

Inputs

Period = 14
Oversold = 30
Exit = 50

Position sizing

Fixed 100% allocation

Risk controls

Max hold period of 15 days
