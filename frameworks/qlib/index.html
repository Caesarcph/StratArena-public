Overview

Qlib covers the whole research loop for machine-learning stock selection: a fast data layer, feature engineering, a model zoo (gradient boosting, LSTM, transformers and more), portfolio construction and backtesting. Its examples are built mostly around Chinese A-share data.

Best for: Researchers building cross-sectional stock-selection models with machine learning.

Strengths

  • End-to-end ML research workflow
  • Many reference models and benchmarks
  • Backed by Microsoft Research, MIT license

Limitations

  • Equity factor research, not trading execution
  • Default data focuses on China A-shares
  • Needs solid ML background
