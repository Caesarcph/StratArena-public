How to choose

  • Learning, or porting rule-based systems with detailed orders: an event-driven library such as Backtrader or Backtesting.py.
  • Testing many parameter combinations quickly: vectorbt. Then confirm the final rules in an event-driven engine.
  • Going live with a broker across asset classes: LEAN or NautilusTrader; for Chinese futures and stocks, vn.py.
  • Crypto: Freqtrade for directional strategies, Hummingbot for market making and arbitrage.
  • Machine learning: Qlib for stock selection models, FinRL for reinforcement learning, TradingAgents for language-model agents.
  • Whatever you pick, test parameters out of sample. Across our own 486 walk-forward tests the median Sharpe ratio fell from 0.32 on the training data to 0.19 on new data.
