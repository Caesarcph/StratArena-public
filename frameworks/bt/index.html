Overview

bt describes a strategy as a stack of small algorithms (select assets, weigh them, rebalance on a schedule) arranged in a tree, so strategies can hold other strategies. It is built on the ffn performance library and is well suited to monthly or weekly allocation rules rather than intraday trading.

Best for: Asset allocation, risk parity, momentum rotation and other portfolio-level strategies.

Strengths

  • Natural fit for multi-asset allocation
  • Composable building blocks
  • Clean comparison of several strategies at once

Limitations

  • Not designed for order-level or intraday logic
  • Smaller community than Backtrader or vectorbt
