Overview

yfinance is the most common way to get free daily and intraday price history for stocks, ETFs, currencies and crypto in Python. It is unofficial: Yahoo can change its site or rate-limit requests, and the data is meant for personal use under Yahoo's terms.

Best for: Personal research and prototypes on daily data.

Strengths

  • Free and easy
  • Broad coverage of tickers
  • Adjusted prices for dividends and splits

Limitations

  • Unofficial; can break or be rate-limited without notice
  • Adjusted history changes after dividends, so old backtests shift slightly
  • Not licensed for redistribution

From our own use

Quant Arena's daily strategy data comes from yfinance. Between two builds a day apart we found that 198 of 576 equity curves had small changes deep in their history, partly because Yahoo revises adjusted prices after dividends.
