Overview

TradingAgents splits a trading decision across several LLM agents: analysts read fundamentals, news, sentiment and technicals, bullish and bearish researchers debate, a trader proposes a position and a risk team reviews it. It runs on commercial or local language-model APIs, so each decision costs tokens, and it is presented by its authors as a research framework rather than investment advice.

Best for: Exploring how LLM agents reason about stocks; research and demos.

Strengths

  • Readable reasoning for every decision
  • Modular agent roles and model choice
  • Huge community interest

Limitations

  • Hard to backtest over long periods: LLM calls are slow and costly, and models may already know the outcome of past events
  • Results vary between runs and models
  • Not a substitute for a systematic, testable rule

From our own use

The look-ahead problem is real: a language model trained on data up to a recent date may already know what happened in the period you backtest. Any LLM strategy result needs a test period after the model's training cutoff.
