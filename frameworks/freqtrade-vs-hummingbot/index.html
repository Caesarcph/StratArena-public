Freqtrade vs Hummingbot

They solve different problems. Freqtrade runs directional strategies on candles (trend, mean reversion, ML signals). Hummingbot provides liquidity: market making and arbitrage, including on DEXs.

Strengths · Limitations

Freqtrade

Strengths

  • Complete toolkit from backtest to live
  • Large community and many example strategies
  • Very active development

Limitations

  • Crypto only
  • Hyperopt makes overfitting easy; check results out of sample
  • GPL-3.0 license

Hummingbot

Strengths

  • Strong exchange and DEX connectivity
  • Purpose-built market-making strategies
  • Active open-source foundation behind it

Limitations

  • Backtesting of market making is limited compared with candle strategies
  • Market making needs capital and careful risk control
  • Not aimed at directional trading

Which one to choose

They solve different problems. Freqtrade runs directional strategies on candles (trend, mean reversion, ML signals). Hummingbot provides liquidity: market making and arbitrage, including on DEXs.
