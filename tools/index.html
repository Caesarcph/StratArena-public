Recommended tools

Tools for running these strategies yourself

Quant Arena shows what a rule did in the past. To try one on a live chart you need a few tools. These are the ones we point readers to. Some links are affiliate links, clearly marked.

Partner

TradingView

Charting and strategy testing in the browser

  • Paste any of our Pine Scripts into the Pine Editor and see the trades drawn on the chart.
  • The built-in Strategy Tester reports returns, drawdown and a trade list for the market and period you choose.
  • A free plan covers the basics; paid plans add more indicators per chart, alerts and longer history.
  • TradingView says people who sign up through a partner link get a $15 coupon toward a paid plan. The offer and its terms are theirs and can change.

How to use it with this site

  1. Pick a strategy on the strategy code page and copy or download its Pine Script.
  2. Open a chart on TradingView, set the timeframe to 1 day, and open the Pine Editor at the bottom.
  3. Paste the script, click Add to chart, then open the Strategy Tester tab.
  4. Compare the result with the backtest on our strategy page. Small differences in indicator smoothing are normal; the Python file is the reference.
